# C2+ Vocabulary — Vector-borne disease ecology

This lesson follows how vectors, climate, habitat, and control programs shape transmission risk. The network is **vectorial capacity → extrinsic incubation period → biting rate → host-seeking behavior → blood-meal preference → larval habitat → breeding-site reduction → insecticide-treated net → residual spraying → insecticide resistance → resistance management → entomological inoculation rate → human biting rate → temperature suitability → degree-day accumulation → seasonal forcing → climate suitability envelope → vector surveillance trap → ovitrap index → parity rate → mosquito pool testing → arbovirus amplification → spillover hotspot → integrated vector management → vector-control threshold**.

## 1. vectorial capacity /vɛkˈtɔriəl kəˈpæsəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the potential of a vector population to transmit a pathogen, considering abundance, survival, biting, and pathogen development.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** vectorial capacity là tổng “khả năng truyền” của cả vector population, không chỉ một con insect.
**Grammar & collocations:** `vectorial-capacity estimate` — estimate; `calculate vectorial capacity` — calculate; `transmission potential` — potential.
**Examples:** `Vectorial capacity increased when warm nights improved mosquito survival.` → Survival dài hơn làm capacity tăng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `매개체 전파 능력` — vectorial capacity.

## 2. extrinsic incubation period /ˈɛkstrɪnsɪk ˌɪŋkjəˈbeɪʃən ˈpɪriəd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the time a pathogen needs to develop inside a vector before that vector can transmit it.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** extrinsic incubation period là thời gian pathogen “ủ” trong vector trước khi truyền được.
**Grammar & collocations:** `extrinsic-incubation period` — period; `shorten the incubation period` — shorten; `infectious vector` — vector.
**Examples:** `A shorter extrinsic incubation period allowed more mosquitoes to become infectious during their lifetime.` → Ủ ngắn làm transmission dễ hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `외부 잠복기` — extrinsic incubation period.

## 3. biting rate /ˈbaɪtɪŋ reɪt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the frequency at which a vector takes blood meals from hosts.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** biting rate đo vector bite bao nhiêu lần và tạo bao nhiêu cơ hội truyền.
**Grammar & collocations:** `vector-biting rate` — rate; `estimate biting frequency` — estimate; `blood-feeding event` — event.
**Examples:** `The biting rate rose when people spent longer evenings outdoors.` → Behavior của người đổi exposure.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `흡혈률` — biting rate.

## 4. host-seeking behavior /hoʊst ˈsiːkɪŋ bɪˈheɪvjər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the sensory and movement behavior a vector uses to locate and approach a blood host.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** host-seeking behavior là cách vector tìm người/animal để blood-feed.
**Grammar & collocations:** `host-seeking behavior` — behavior; `alter host seeking` — alter; `odor cue` — cue.
**Examples:** `Host-seeking behavior changed when indoor lighting and ventilation altered odor cues.` → Environment ảnh hưởng cách vector tìm host.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `숙주 탐색 행동` — host-seeking behavior.

## 5. blood-meal preference /ˈblʌd miːl ˈprɛfərəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the tendency of a vector to feed on particular host species or host types.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** blood-meal preference cho biết vector thích bite người, chim hay animal nào hơn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `blood-meal preference` — preference; `identify a host blood meal` — identify; `host choice` — choice.
**Examples:** `A shift in blood-meal preference increased contact with humans near expanding settlements.` → Preference đổi thì risk đổi.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `혈액 식사 선호` — blood-meal preference.

## 6. larval habitat /ˈlɑrvəl ˈhæbɪˌtæt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the water, soil, plant, or built environment where immature vector stages develop.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** larval habitat là nơi mosquito larva lớn lên trước adult stage.
**Grammar & collocations:** `larval-habitat mapping` — mapping; `locate larval habitat` — locate; `breeding water` — water.
**Examples:** `Construction containers became productive larval habitats after heavy rain.` → Nước đọng tạm thời cũng tạo risk.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `유충 서식지` — larval habitat.

## 7. breeding-site reduction /ˈbriːdɪŋ saɪt rɪˈdʌkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** removal, drainage, treatment, or redesign of places where vectors reproduce.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** breeding-site reduction đánh vào nơi sinh sản thay vì chỉ diệt adult.
**Grammar & collocations:** `breeding-site reduction campaign` — campaign; `remove standing water` — remove; `habitat management` — management.
**Examples:** `Breeding-site reduction focused on blocked drains and uncovered storage tanks.` → Small site changes có thể giảm larva.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `번식지 감소` — breeding-site reduction.

## 8. insecticide-treated net /ɪnˈsɛktəˌsaɪd ˈtriːtɪd nɛt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a bed net treated with insecticide to repel, disable, or kill mosquitoes that contact it.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** insecticide-treated net tạo barrier khi người ngủ và mosquito hoạt động.
**Grammar & collocations:** `insecticide-treated net` — net; `sleep under a treated net` — sleep; `net coverage` — coverage.
**Examples:** `Insecticide-treated nets reduced nighttime exposure when households used them consistently.` → Coverage và use đều quan trọng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `살충제 처리 모기장` — insecticide-treated net.

## 9. residual spraying /rɪˈzɪdʒuəl ˈspreɪɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** application of a long-lasting insecticide to indoor surfaces where vectors rest.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** residual spraying để lại lớp insecticide trên wall nhằm tác động sau khi spray xong.
**Grammar & collocations:** `indoor-residual spraying` — spraying; `spray a dwelling` — spray; `resting surface` — surface.
**Examples:** `Residual spraying protected houses where mosquitoes rested on treated walls after feeding.` → Resting behavior quyết định hiệu quả.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `잔류 살충제 살포` — residual spraying.

## 10. insecticide resistance /ɪnˈsɛktəˌsaɪd rɪˈzɪstəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** reduced susceptibility of a vector population to an insecticide that previously controlled it.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** insecticide resistance làm cùng dose nhưng vector sống nhiều hơn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `insecticide-resistance profile` — profile; `monitor resistance` — monitor; `susceptibility bioassay` — bioassay.
**Examples:** `Insecticide resistance reduced the impact of the original spraying program.` → Resistance làm cần đổi strategy.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `살충제 내성` — insecticide resistance.

## 11. resistance management /rɪˈzɪstəns ˈmænɪdʒmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** coordinated use and rotation of control methods to delay or reduce the spread of resistance.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** resistance management tránh dùng một mechanism mãi đến khi mất tác dụng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `resistance-management plan` — plan; `rotate insecticides` — rotate; `preserve susceptibility` — preserve.
**Examples:** `Resistance management combined insecticide rotation with breeding-site reduction.` → Multi-method làm chậm resistance.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `내성 관리` — resistance management.

## 12. entomological inoculation rate /ˌɛntəməˈlɑdʒɪkəl ɪˌnɑkjəˈleɪʃən reɪt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the estimated number of infectious bites received by a person in a given period.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** entomological inoculation rate nối vector data với số infectious bite mà người có thể nhận.
**Grammar & collocations:** `entomological-inoculation rate` — rate; `estimate infectious bites` — estimate; `transmission intensity` — intensity.
**Examples:** `The entomological inoculation rate fell after targeted nighttime control began.` → Rate giảm cho thấy exposure giảm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `곤충학적 접종률` — entomological inoculation rate.

## 13. human biting rate /ˈhjuːmən ˈbaɪtɪŋ reɪt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the frequency with which vectors bite humans in a defined place and period.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** human biting rate tập trung vào bite lên người, không phải mọi host.
**Grammar & collocations:** `human-biting rate` — rate; `measure human biting` — measure; `human exposure` — exposure.
**Examples:** `The human biting rate was highest near unlit outdoor kitchens.` → Location và behavior cùng quan trọng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `사람 흡혈률` — human biting rate.

## 14. temperature suitability /ˈtɛmprətʃər ˌsuːtəˈbɪləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the degree to which local temperatures support vector survival, pathogen development, and transmission.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** temperature suitability hỏi nhiệt độ có hợp cho cả vector lẫn pathogen không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `temperature-suitability map` — map; `assess thermal suitability` — assess; `transmission window` — window.
**Examples:** `Temperature suitability expanded toward higher elevations during unusually warm months.` → Climate đổi vùng risk.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `온도 적합성` — temperature suitability.

## 15. degree-day accumulation /dɪˈɡri deɪ əˌkjuːməˈleɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** accumulated temperature exposure above a threshold, used to estimate development timing of vectors or pathogens.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** degree-day accumulation cộng “nhiệt hữu dụng” để dự đoán stage phát triển.
**Grammar & collocations:** `degree-day accumulation` — accumulation; `accumulate thermal time` — accumulate; `development threshold` — threshold.
**Examples:** `Degree-day accumulation predicted an earlier mosquito season than the historical average.` → Thermal time giúp forecast season.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `도일 누적` — degree-day accumulation.

## 16. seasonal forcing /ˈsiːzənəl ˈfɔrsɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** recurring seasonal changes in climate, behavior, or ecology that drive predictable variation in transmission.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** seasonal forcing là lực lặp theo mùa làm transmission lên xuống.
**Grammar & collocations:** `seasonal-forcing pattern` — pattern; `model seasonal forcing` — model; `seasonal amplitude` — amplitude.
**Examples:** `Seasonal forcing explained why transmission peaked before the hottest month.` → Season effect có thể có lag.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `계절 강제력` — seasonal forcing.

## 17. climate suitability envelope /ˈklaɪmət ˌsuːtəˈbɪləti ˈɛnvəˌloʊp/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the range of climate conditions within which a vector or pathogen can persist and transmit.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** climate suitability envelope là “vùng điều kiện” cho phép system tồn tại.
**Grammar & collocations:** `climate-suitability envelope` — envelope; `map suitable climate` — map; `range expansion` — expansion.
**Examples:** `The climate suitability envelope moved northward as average temperatures changed.` → Envelope đổi theo climate.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `기후 적합성 범위` — climate suitability envelope.

## 18. vector surveillance trap /ˈvɛktər sərˈveɪləns træp/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a device placed to collect vectors for counting, identification, infection testing, or behavior measurement.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** vector surveillance trap là công cụ lấy mẫu vector ngoài field.
**Grammar & collocations:** `vector-surveillance trap network` — network; `deploy a trap` — deploy; `trap catch` — catch.
**Examples:** `The vector surveillance trap network compared mosquito density across neighborhoods.` → Trap giúp map density theo space.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `매개체 감시 포획기` — vector surveillance trap.

## 19. ovitrap index /ˈoʊvɪˌtræp ˈɪndɛks/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an index based on the proportion of ovitraps containing mosquito eggs or evidence of oviposition.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** ovitrap index dùng trap để đo female mosquito activity qua egg-laying.
**Grammar & collocations:** `ovitrap-index survey` — survey; `calculate the ovitrap index` — calculate; `egg-laying activity` — activity.
**Examples:** `The ovitrap index rose after rain and guided localized control.` → Egg signal đi trước adult count.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `산란 유인기 지수` — ovitrap index.

## 20. parity rate /ˈpærəti reɪt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the proportion of female mosquitoes that have laid eggs at least once, indicating age structure and biting history.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** parity rate cho biết female đã bite/đẻ ít nhất một lần, giúp ước vector “già” và có thể infectious.
**Grammar & collocations:** `mosquito parity rate` — rate; `estimate parity` — estimate; `older female vector` — vector.
**Examples:** `A high parity rate suggested that many older mosquitoes had survived long enough to transmit infection.` → Age của vector quan trọng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `산란 경험률` — parity rate.

## 21. mosquito pool testing /məˈskiːtoʊ puːl ˈtɛstɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** testing groups of collected mosquitoes together to detect pathogen material in the vector population.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mosquito pool testing gộp nhiều mosquito để tìm signal pathogen hiệu quả hơn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `mosquito-pool testing` — testing; `test a mosquito pool` — test; `pool positivity` — positivity.
**Examples:** `Mosquito pool testing detected viral RNA before human cases increased.` → Vector surveillance có thể báo sớm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `모기 집단 검사` — mosquito pool testing.

## 22. arbovirus amplification /ˈɑrbəˌvaɪrəs ˌæmpləfəˈkeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an increase in arbovirus circulation through interactions among vectors, animal hosts, and environmental conditions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** arbovirus amplification là pathogen tăng trong vector–host cycle trước khi human cases bùng lên.
**Grammar & collocations:** `arbovirus-amplification cycle` — cycle; `amplify viral circulation` — amplify; `vector–host system` — system.
**Examples:** `Arbovirus amplification followed heavy rainfall and a rise in competent mosquito habitat.` → Rain mở habitat và amplification.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `아르보바이러스 증폭` — arbovirus amplification.

## 23. spillover hotspot /ˈspɪlˌoʊvər ˈhɑtˌspɑt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a location or setting where conditions make pathogen transmission from animals or vectors to humans especially likely.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** spillover hotspot là nơi nhiều risk factor chồng lên nhau.
**Grammar & collocations:** `spillover-hotspot map` — map; `identify a hotspot` — identify; `interface risk` — risk.
**Examples:** `The spillover hotspot combined dense housing, livestock markets, and seasonal flooding.` → Hotspot là combination, không chỉ một factor.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `종간 전파 위험 지역` — spillover hotspot.

## 24. integrated vector management /ˈɪntəˌɡreɪtɪd ˈvɛktər ˈmænɪdʒmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** coordinated use of environmental, chemical, biological, and behavioral methods to reduce vector-borne disease.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** integrated vector management gộp nhiều method theo ecology và local context.
**Grammar & collocations:** `integrated-vector-management program` — program; `combine vector-control methods` — combine; `ecological management` — management.
**Examples:** `Integrated vector management combined drainage, nets, targeted spraying, and community action.` → Integration giảm phụ thuộc một tool.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `통합 매개체 관리` — integrated vector management.

## 25. vector-control threshold /ˈvɛktər kənˈtroʊl ˈθrɛʃhoʊld/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a predefined vector abundance, infection, or risk level that triggers control action.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** vector-control threshold biến trap data thành rule để bắt đầu intervention.
**Grammar & collocations:** `vector-control threshold` — threshold; `trigger control action` — trigger; `risk-based response` — response.
**Examples:** `The vector-control threshold was crossed when pool positivity and biting rates rose together.` → Hai signal cùng tăng tạo trigger mạnh.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `매개체 방제 임계치` — vector-control threshold.

## Review in context — Part 1

**Vectorial capacity** depends on the **extrinsic incubation period**, **biting rate**, **host-seeking behavior**, and **blood-meal preference**. A productive **larval habitat** can be reduced through **breeding-site reduction**. **Insecticide-treated nets** and **residual spraying** lower exposure, but **insecticide resistance** requires **resistance management**. The **entomological inoculation rate** and **human biting rate** summarize infectious and human contact intensity.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 1:**

**Vectorial capacity** phụ thuộc **extrinsic incubation period**, **biting rate**, **host-seeking behavior** và **blood-meal preference**. **Larval habitat** hiệu quả có thể giảm bằng **breeding-site reduction**. **Insecticide-treated net** và **residual spraying** hạ exposure, nhưng **insecticide resistance** cần **resistance management**. **Entomological inoculation rate** và **human biting rate** tóm tắt cường độ bite infectious và bite lên người.

## Review in context — Part 2

**Temperature suitability**, **degree-day accumulation**, and **seasonal forcing** shape a **climate suitability envelope**. A **vector surveillance trap**, **ovitrap index**, and **parity rate** describe vector abundance and age. **Mosquito pool testing** can reveal **arbovirus amplification** before a **spillover hotspot** becomes obvious. **Integrated vector management** combines methods, while a **vector-control threshold** turns surveillance into timely action.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 2:**

**Temperature suitability**, **degree-day accumulation** và **seasonal forcing** định hình **climate suitability envelope**. **Vector surveillance trap**, **ovitrap index** và **parity rate** mô tả abundance và age của vector. **Mosquito pool testing** có thể phát hiện **arbovirus amplification** trước khi **spillover hotspot** rõ ràng. **Integrated vector management** kết hợp nhiều method, còn **vector-control threshold** biến surveillance thành action đúng lúc.
