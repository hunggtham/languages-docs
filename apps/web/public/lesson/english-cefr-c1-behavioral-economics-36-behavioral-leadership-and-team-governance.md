# C1 Vocabulary 36 — Behavioral leadership and team governance

This lesson follows how teams interpret authority, delegation, accountability, voice, and leadership change when coordination and trust are uncertain.

Flow: **leader prototypicality → authority trust → delegation reluctance → decision ownership → managerial listening → psychological contract → role ambiguity perception → team voice climate → coordination belief → leadership framing → servant leadership cue → succession anxiety → status quo leadership → power distance expectation → accountability salience → charismatic confidence signal → ethical leadership credibility → leader-member reciprocity → strategic patience → organizational memory cue**.

## 1. leader prototypicality /ˈliːdər ˌproʊtəˌtɪpəˈkæləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the extent to which a leader appears to represent the traits and values a group associates with its ideal member.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mức leader represent trait và value group associate với member ideal.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high leader prototypicality` — prototypicality; `increase leader prototypicality` — increase.
**Examples:** `Leader prototypicality increased when the director shared the same operational problems as the team.` → Prototypicality leader tăng khi director share problem operational giống team.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `리더 원형성`.

## 2. authority trust /əˈθɔrəti trʌst/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** confidence that a person with formal power will use it competently, fairly, and within legitimate limits.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** confidence người có power formal use nó competent, fair và within limit legitimate.
**Grammar & collocations:** `fragile authority trust` — trust; `build authority trust` — build.
**Examples:** `Authority trust weakened after the manager changed priorities without explaining the reason.` → Trust authority yếu sau manager change priority không explain reason.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `권위 신뢰`.

## 3. delegation reluctance /ˌdɛləˈɡeɪʃən rɪˈlʌktəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** unwillingness to give responsibility to others because control, quality, or credit may be lost.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** không willing give responsibility vì sợ mất control, quality hoặc credit.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong delegation reluctance` — reluctance; `reduce delegation reluctance` — reduce.
**Examples:** `Delegation reluctance kept the senior analyst approving every small decision.` → Reluctance delegation khiến analyst senior approve mọi decision nhỏ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `위임 주저`.

## 4. decision ownership /dɪˈsɪʒən ˈoʊnərˌʃɪp/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the sense that a person or group is responsible for understanding, making, and following through on a decision.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** sense person hoặc group responsible understand, make và follow-through decision.
**Grammar & collocations:** `clear decision ownership` — ownership; `assign decision ownership` — assign.
**Examples:** `Decision ownership was unclear, so each department assumed another team would implement the change.` → Ownership decision không clear nên mỗi department assume team khác implement change.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `의사결정 주인의식`.

## 5. managerial listening /ˌmænəˈdʒɪriəl ˈlɪsənɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the deliberate effort by managers to understand concerns, information, and suggestions from employees.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** effort deliberate manager để understand concern, information và suggestion employee.
**Grammar & collocations:** `active managerial listening` — listening; `practice managerial listening` — practice.
**Examples:** `Managerial listening revealed a scheduling problem that the performance dashboard could not show.` → Listening managerial reveal problem schedule dashboard performance không show.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `관리자 경청`.

## 6. psychological contract /ˌsaɪkəˈlɑdʒɪkəl ˈkɑntrækt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the unwritten expectations employees and organizations hold about mutual promises, support, loyalty, and fairness.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** expectation unwritten giữa employee và organization về promise, support, loyalty và fairness.
**Grammar & collocations:** `broken psychological contract` — contract; `repair the psychological contract` — repair.
**Examples:** `The psychological contract weakened when flexibility promised during hiring disappeared after onboarding.` → Contract psychological yếu khi flexibility promise lúc hiring biến sau onboarding.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `심리적 계약`.

## 7. role ambiguity perception /roʊl ˌæmbɪˈɡjuəti pərˈsɛpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a judgment that responsibilities, authority, priorities, or success criteria are unclear.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** judgment responsibility, authority, priority hoặc success criteria không clear.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high role ambiguity perception` — perception; `reduce role ambiguity perception` — reduce.
**Examples:** `Role ambiguity perception increased when two managers gave incompatible instructions.` → Perception ambiguity role tăng khi hai manager give instruction incompatible.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `역할 모호성 인식`.

## 8. team voice climate /tiːm vɔɪs ˈklaɪmət/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the shared atmosphere determining whether team members can raise concerns, ideas, and disagreement safely.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** atmosphere shared quyết định member raise concern, idea và disagreement safe không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `open team voice climate` — climate; `build a team voice climate` — build.
**Examples:** `A strong team voice climate helped the intern question a missing safety check.` → Climate voice team mạnh giúp intern question safety check thiếu.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `팀 발언 분위기`.

## 9. coordination belief /koʊˌɔrdəˈneɪʃən bɪˈliːf/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a belief that people and units can align timing, information, and effort to achieve a shared result.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** belief people và unit align timing, information và effort để achieve result shared.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong coordination belief` — belief; `strengthen coordination beliefs` — strengthen.
**Examples:** `Coordination belief improved after the teams agreed on one shared status board.` → Belief coordination improve sau team agree một status board shared.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `조정 신념`.

## 10. leadership framing /ˈliːdərˌʃɪp ˈfreɪmɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the way leadership is presented as command, service, expertise, protection, or collective responsibility.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cách present leadership như command, service, expertise, protection hoặc responsibility collective.
**Grammar & collocations:** `inclusive leadership framing` — framing; `change leadership framing` — change.
**Examples:** `Leadership framing shifted from heroic rescue to shared problem solving.` → Framing leadership shift từ heroic rescue sang solve problem shared.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `리더십 프레이밍`.

## 11. servant leadership cue /ˈsɝvənt ˈliːdərˌʃɪp kjuː/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a signal that a leader prioritizes enabling, protecting, and developing others rather than displaying status.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** signal leader prioritize enable, protect và develop người khác thay display status.
**Grammar & collocations:** `credible servant leadership cue` — cue; `provide a servant leadership cue` — provide.
**Examples:** `A servant leadership cue appeared when the director removed a barrier before requesting higher performance.` → Cue leadership servant xuất hiện khi director remove barrier trước request performance cao.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `서번트 리더십 단서`.

## 12. succession anxiety /səkˈsɛʃən æŋˈzaɪəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** fear and uncertainty about who will lead, what will change, and whether stability will continue after a leader leaves.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** fear và uncertainty ai lead, change gì và stability continue sau leader leave.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high succession anxiety` — anxiety; `reduce succession anxiety` — reduce.
**Examples:** `Succession anxiety grew because the organization had no visible transition plan.` → Anxiety succession tăng vì organization không có transition plan visible.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `승계 불안`.

## 13. status quo leadership /ˈsteɪtəs kwoʊ ˈliːdərˌʃɪp/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** leadership that protects familiar arrangements even when evidence suggests change is needed.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** leadership protect arrangement quen dù evidence suggest need change.
**Grammar & collocations:** `rigid status quo leadership` — leadership; `challenge status quo leadership` — challenge.
**Examples:** `Status quo leadership kept the team using a failing process because it had once produced success.` → Leadership status quo giữ process fail vì từng produce success.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `현상 유지 리더십`.

## 14. power distance expectation /ˈpaʊər ˈdɪstəns ˌɛkspɛkˈteɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a belief about how unequal authority should be and how freely junior people may question senior people.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** belief authority unequal tới đâu và junior question senior tự do thế nào.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high power distance expectation` — expectation; `challenge power distance expectations` — challenge.
**Examples:** `Power distance expectation made the visitor wait for permission to offer a useful correction.` → Expectation distance power khiến visitor đợi permission để offer correction hữu ích.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `권력 거리 기대`.

## 15. accountability salience /əˌkaʊntəˈbɪləti ˈseɪliəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** how visible and motivating responsibility for an outcome is to the people making a decision.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mức responsibility outcome visible và motivating với người make decision.
**Grammar & collocations:** `low accountability salience` — salience; `increase accountability salience` — increase.
**Examples:** `Accountability salience rose when the project record named who approved each exception.` → Salience accountability tăng khi record project name ai approve exception.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `책임 현저성`.

## 16. charismatic confidence signal /ˌkærɪzˈmætɪk ˈkɑnfədəns ˈsɪɡnəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a visible expression of certainty and energy that makes a leader seem unusually persuasive or capable.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** expression certainty và energy visible làm leader trông persuasive hoặc capable bất thường.
**Grammar & collocations:** `powerful charismatic confidence signal` — signal; `interpret a charismatic confidence signal` — interpret.
**Examples:** `A charismatic confidence signal made the proposal seem safer than its evidence justified.` → Signal confidence charismatic làm proposal trông safe hơn evidence justify.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `카리스마적 자신감 신호`.

## 17. ethical leadership credibility /ˈɛθɪkəl ˈliːdərˌʃɪp krɛdəˈbɪləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** confidence that a leader's decisions, standards, and treatment of people are genuinely ethical.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** confidence decision, standard và treatment people của leader genuinely ethical.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high ethical leadership credibility` — credibility; `protect ethical leadership credibility` — protect.
**Examples:** `Ethical leadership credibility fell when the director applied a rule to staff but exempted personal allies.` → Credibility leadership ethical giảm khi director apply rule cho staff nhưng exempt ally personal.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `윤리적 리더십 신뢰성`.

## 18. leader-member reciprocity /ˈliːdər ˈmɛmbər ˌrɛsəˈprɑsəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the expectation that leaders and members will exchange effort, protection, information, and loyalty fairly.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** expectation leader và member exchange effort, protection, information và loyalty fair.
**Grammar & collocations:** `healthy leader-member reciprocity` — reciprocity; `strengthen leader-member reciprocity` — strengthen.
**Examples:** `Leader-member reciprocity improved when extra effort was met with honest information about future roles.` → Reciprocity leader-member improve khi effort extra gặp information honest về role future.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `리더-구성원 상호성`.

## 19. strategic patience /strəˈtiːdʒɪk ˈpeɪʃəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** willingness to delay visible results while protecting a long-term plan under uncertainty.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** willingness delay result visible để protect plan long-term khi uncertainty.
**Grammar & collocations:** `disciplined strategic patience` — patience; `maintain strategic patience` — maintain.
**Examples:** `Strategic patience kept the team investing in training before demanding immediate output.` → Patience strategic giữ team đầu tư training trước demand output immediate.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `전략적 인내`.

## 20. organizational memory cue /ˌɔrɡənəˈzeɪʃənəl ˈmɛməri kjuː/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a record, story, or practice that reminds an organization what it learned from earlier decisions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** record, story hoặc practice nhắc organization đã learn gì từ decision cũ.
**Grammar & collocations:** `useful organizational memory cue` — cue; `preserve an organizational memory cue` — preserve.
**Examples:** `An organizational memory cue prevented the team from repeating a documented procurement mistake.` → Cue memory organizational ngăn team lặp mistake procurement đã document.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `조직 기억 단서`.

## Review in context

A leader's **leader prototypicality** may build initial trust, but durable **authority trust** depends on conduct. **Delegation reluctance** limits learning, while clear **decision ownership** makes follow-through visible. **Managerial listening** can repair a **psychological contract**, and lower **role ambiguity perception** helps people act. A strong **team voice climate** supports a shared **coordination belief**; **leadership framing** determines whether power looks like command or service. A **servant leadership cue** can reduce **succession anxiety**, while **status quo leadership** resists necessary change. **Power distance expectation** shapes who speaks, and **accountability salience** makes responsibility harder to hide. A **charismatic confidence signal** may persuade beyond evidence, so **ethical leadership credibility** matters. **Leader-member reciprocity** sustains cooperation, **strategic patience** protects long-term work, and an **organizational memory cue** keeps lessons available after people move on.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

**Leader prototypicality** có thể build trust ban đầu, nhưng **authority trust** lâu dài phụ thuộc conduct. **Delegation reluctance** limit learning, còn **decision ownership** rõ làm follow-through visible. **Managerial listening** có thể repair **psychological contract**, và **role ambiguity perception** thấp giúp act. **Team voice climate** mạnh hỗ trợ **coordination belief**, còn **leadership framing** quyết định power trông như command hay service. **Servant leadership cue** giảm **succession anxiety**, trong khi **status quo leadership** resist change cần. **Power distance expectation** shape ai speak, còn **accountability salience** làm responsibility khó hide. **Charismatic confidence signal** có thể persuade vượt evidence, nên **ethical leadership credibility** quan trọng. **Leader-member reciprocity** sustain cooperation, **strategic patience** protect work long-term, và **organizational memory cue** giữ lesson available khi người cũ move.
