# C2+ Vocabulary — Disability inclusion and independent living

This lesson follows how services remove disabling barriers, support autonomy, and design participation around real lives rather than assumed norms. The network is **inclusive design → reasonable accommodation → adaptive device → functional limitation → activity restriction → social participation barrier → independent living → personal assistance → supported employment → caregiver respite → institutionalization → deinstitutionalization → guardianship → supported housing → accessibility review → easy-read format → augmentative communication → sign-language access → disability-adjusted service → coercive control → ableism → disability justice → intersectional disability → age-friendly design → continuity support**.

## 1. inclusive design /ɪnˈkluːsɪv dɪˈzaɪn/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** design that anticipates diverse bodies, senses, communication styles, and circumstances so people can use a service without special exclusion.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** inclusive design xây từ đầu cho nhiều người, không đợi user bị loại rồi mới sửa.
**Grammar & collocations:** `inclusive-design standard` — standard; `design for diverse users` — design; `access need` — need.
**Examples:** `Inclusive design put captions, readable contrast, and keyboard access into the service from the start.` → Access được xây sẵn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `포용적 설계` — inclusive design.

## 2. reasonable accommodation /ˈriːzənəbəl əˌkɑməˈdeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a practical change that enables a person with a disability to access work, education, care, or a public service without imposing disproportionate burden.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** reasonable accommodation là chỉnh hợp lý để person tham gia bình đẳng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `reasonable-accommodation request` — request; `provide an accommodation` — provide; `equal access` — access.
**Examples:** `A reasonable accommodation moved the appointment to a quieter room and allowed extra processing time.` → Chỉnh nhỏ nhưng tạo access.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `합리적 편의 제공` — reasonable accommodation.

## 3. adaptive device /əˈdæptɪv dɪˈvaɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a tool or equipment modified or selected to help a person perform an activity despite a disability or functional difference.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** adaptive device là tool được điều chỉnh theo body/need cụ thể.
**Grammar & collocations:** `adaptive-device assessment` — assessment; `use an adaptive device` — use; `functional support` — support.
**Examples:** `An adaptive device let the patient control the wheelchair with a small head movement.` → Device mở rộng independence.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `적응형 보조기기` — adaptive device.

## 4. functional limitation /ˈfʌŋkʃənəl ˌlɪməˈteɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** difficulty performing a physical, sensory, cognitive, or mental function in a particular context.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** functional limitation mô tả việc gì khó làm, không định nghĩa toàn bộ con người.
**Grammar & collocations:** `functional-limitation assessment` — assessment; `experience a limitation` — experience; `daily function` — function.
**Examples:** `The assessment described a functional limitation in fine-motor tasks but not in communication.` → Limitation có phạm vi cụ thể.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `기능 제한` — functional limitation.

## 5. activity restriction /ækˈtɪvəti rɪˈstrɪkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** reduced ability to carry out a daily or valued activity because of a health condition, environment, or lack of support.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** activity restriction là activity bị giới hạn, có thể do environment chứ không chỉ body.
**Grammar & collocations:** `activity-restriction measure` — measure; `restrict daily activity` — restrict; `environmental barrier` — barrier.
**Examples:** `The activity restriction came from inaccessible transport rather than from the person's medical condition alone.` → Transport tạo restriction.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `활동 제한` — activity restriction.

## 6. social participation barrier /ˈsoʊʃəl pɑrˌtɪsəˈpeɪʃən ˈbæriər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a social, physical, economic, or institutional obstacle that prevents a person from joining community, work, education, or civic life.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** social participation barrier chặn person khỏi việc thuộc về và tham gia.
**Grammar & collocations:** `social-participation-barrier audit` — audit; `remove a participation barrier` — remove; `community inclusion` — inclusion.
**Examples:** `A social participation barrier was the absence of an accessible route to the evening class.` → Không tới được lớp nên bị loại khỏi participation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `사회 참여 장벽` — social participation barrier.

## 7. independent living /ˌɪndɪˈpɛndənt ˈlɪvɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the right and practical ability to choose where and how to live with the support needed to participate in ordinary community life.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** independent living không nhất thiết “tự làm mọi thứ”; là tự chọn đời sống với support phù hợp.
**Grammar & collocations:** `independent-living support` — support; `live in the community` — live; `personal choice` — choice.
**Examples:** `Independent living allowed the client to choose a shared apartment with scheduled personal support.` → Tự chọn nơi sống và nhận support.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `자립 생활` — independent living.

## 8. personal assistance /ˈpɜrsənəl əˈsɪstəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** individualized support that helps a person carry out daily activities, work, study, or community participation according to their own choices.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** personal assistance đưa support theo lịch và preference của person.
**Grammar & collocations:** `personal-assistance budget` — budget; `provide personal assistance` — provide; `user-directed support` — support.
**Examples:** `Personal assistance helped the student attend lectures and manage medication without entering an institution.` → Support giữ student ở community.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `개인 지원` — personal assistance.

## 9. supported employment /səˈpɔrtɪd ɪmˈplɔɪmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** paid work supported by individualized coaching, workplace adjustments, or ongoing assistance for a person with a disability.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** supported employment mở việc thật cùng support, không tách person khỏi labor market.
**Grammar & collocations:** `supported-employment program` — program; `support a worker` — support; `workplace adjustment` — adjustment.
**Examples:** `Supported employment paired a real job with a coach who gradually reduced assistance.` → Coach giảm dần khi worker tự tin.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `지원 고용` — supported employment.

## 10. caregiver respite /ˈkɛrˌɡɪvər ˈrɛspɪt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** temporary relief or replacement care that gives an unpaid caregiver time to rest, work, attend appointments, or recover.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** caregiver respite cho người chăm sóc một khoảng nghỉ có tổ chức.
**Grammar & collocations:** `caregiver-respite service` — service; `provide respite care` — provide; `unpaid caregiver` — caregiver.
**Examples:** `Caregiver respite prevented exhaustion by arranging trained support two afternoons each week.` → Nghỉ đều giúp tránh kiệt sức.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `돌봄 제공자 휴식 지원` — caregiver respite.

## 11. institutionalization /ˌɪnstəˌtuːʃənələˈzeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** placement or long-term life in an institution, often accompanied by loss of ordinary community choice, relationships, or autonomy.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** institutionalization là đời sống bị chuyển vào institution và mất nhiều choice đời thường.
**Grammar & collocations:** `institutionalization risk` — risk; `institutionalize a person` — institutionalize; `loss of autonomy` — loss.
**Examples:** `Institutionalization increased when community housing and personal support were cut.` → Thiếu support community đẩy person vào institution.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `시설화` — institutionalization.

## 12. deinstitutionalization /ˌdiːɪnstəˌtuːʃənələˈzeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** movement from institutional care toward ordinary community living with adequate services, rights, and support.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** deinstitutionalization không chỉ đóng institution; cần xây support community thật.
**Grammar & collocations:** `deinstitutionalization plan` — plan; `move into community care` — move; `community support system` — system.
**Examples:** `Deinstitutionalization failed when people were discharged without housing, income, or clinical follow-up.` → Ra institution mà thiếu support thì fail.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `탈시설화` — deinstitutionalization.

## 13. guardianship /ˈɡɑrdiənˌʃɪp/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a legal arrangement in which another person or body is authorized to make specified decisions for someone judged unable to make them independently.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** guardianship chuyển một phần hoặc toàn bộ decision power sang guardian.
**Grammar & collocations:** `guardianship order` — order; `appoint a guardian` — appoint; `legal decision-maker` — decision-maker.
**Examples:** `The court limited guardianship to financial decisions and preserved the person's control over daily care.` → Guardian chỉ có scope hẹp.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `후견` — guardianship.

## 14. supported housing /səˈpɔrtɪd ˈhaʊzɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** ordinary housing combined with individualized support for health, daily living, safety, or tenancy.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** supported housing là nhà bình thường + support cần thiết.
**Grammar & collocations:** `supported-housing program` — program; `maintain a tenancy` — maintain; `housing support` — support.
**Examples:** `Supported housing reduced hospital use by giving residents stable homes and on-call assistance.` → Nhà ổn định giúp health ổn định.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `지원 주거` — supported housing.

## 15. accessibility review /əkˌsɛsəˈbɪləti rɪˈvjuː/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** structured examination of whether a place, service, document, or technology can be used by people with diverse disabilities.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** accessibility review tìm điểm loại user trước khi service launch.
**Grammar & collocations:** `accessibility-review checklist` — checklist; `review a service for access` — review; `barrier finding` — finding.
**Examples:** `The accessibility review found that the online form timed out before screen-reader users could finish it.` → Form không phù hợp screen reader.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `접근성 검토` — accessibility review.

## 16. easy-read format /ˈiːzi riːd ˈfɔrmæt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** information presented with plain language, short sentences, clear layout, and supportive images for people who need easier processing.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** easy-read format biến instruction phức tạp thành text dễ xử lý.
**Grammar & collocations:** `easy-read-format leaflet` — leaflet; `write in plain language` — write; `support comprehension` — support.
**Examples:** `The clinic's easy-read format explained consent with pictures and one decision per page.` → Mỗi page một decision.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `읽기 쉬운 형식` — easy-read format.

## 17. augmentative communication /ɔɡˌmɛntətɪv kəˌmjuːnəˈkeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** methods and tools that supplement or replace speech, including symbols, devices, gestures, and text.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** augmentative communication mở nhiều đường giao tiếp ngoài speech.
**Grammar & collocations:** `augmentative-communication device` — device; `augment speech` — augment; `communication aid` — aid.
**Examples:** `Augmentative communication let the patient describe pain before the examination began.` → Patient có cách nói khác.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `보완대체 의사소통` — augmentative communication.

## 18. sign-language access /saɪn ˈlæŋɡwɪdʒ ˈæksɛs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** reliable access to communication, interpretation, and information in a person's sign language.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** sign-language access bảo đảm person giao tiếp trực tiếp bằng ngôn ngữ của mình.
**Grammar & collocations:** `sign-language-access policy` — policy; `arrange an interpreter` — arrange; `accessible consultation` — consultation.
**Examples:** `Sign-language access allowed the patient to discuss consent without relying on a child to interpret.` → Không bắt trẻ làm interpreter.

## 19. disability-adjusted service /dɪsəˈbɪləti əˈdʒʌstɪd ˈsɜrvɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a service modified in its communication, environment, schedule, or delivery to meet disability-related access needs.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** disability-adjusted service thay đổi service để equal access thành thật.
**Grammar & collocations:** `disability-adjusted-service standard` — standard; `adapt service delivery` — adapt; `access requirement` — requirement.
**Examples:** `The disability-adjusted service offered home visits when the clinic building could not be reached safely.` → Delivery đổi để patient access được.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `장애 조정 서비스` — disability-adjusted service.

## 20. coercive control /koʊˈɜrsɪv kənˈtroʊl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a pattern of threats, monitoring, isolation, or manipulation used to dominate another person's choices and freedom.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** coercive control không chỉ một hành vi; là pattern làm person mất autonomy.
**Grammar & collocations:** `coercive-control assessment` — assessment; `control a partner's choices` — control; `isolation tactic` — tactic.
**Examples:** `The clinician screened for coercive control before assuming that the patient freely refused support.` → Refusal có thể không tự do.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `강압적 통제` — coercive control.

## 21. ableism /ˈeɪbəlˌɪzəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** beliefs, practices, or structures that treat nondisabled ways of functioning as superior and disadvantage disabled people.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** ableism biến “chuẩn không khuyết tật” thành tiêu chuẩn duy nhất.
**Grammar & collocations:** `ableism-awareness training` — training; `challenge ableist assumptions` — challenge; `structural disadvantage` — disadvantage.
**Examples:** `The service audit exposed ableism in its assumption that every patient could use a phone app.` → App-only là assumption ableist.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `장애인 차별주의` — ableism.

## 22. disability justice /dɪsəˈbɪləti ˈdʒʌstɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a movement and framework linking disability rights with racial, economic, gender, environmental, and bodily justice.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** disability justice nhìn disability cùng power và nhiều bất bình đẳng khác.
**Grammar & collocations:** `disability-justice framework` — framework; `advance disability justice` — advance; `interlocking oppression` — oppression.
**Examples:** `Disability justice asked whether the emergency plan protected people with the least transport and income.` → Justice nhìn group chịu burden lớn nhất.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `장애 정의` — disability justice.

## 23. intersectional disability /ˌɪntərˈsɛkʃənəl dɪsəˈbɪləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** disability understood together with other identities and structures, such as race, class, gender, age, migration, or sexuality.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** intersectional disability cho thấy disability experience đổi theo nhiều identity cùng lúc.
**Grammar & collocations:** `intersectional-disability analysis` — analysis; `intersect with social identity` — intersect; `compounded barrier` — barrier.
**Examples:** `Intersectional disability analysis found that migrant women with impairments faced language, income, and mobility barriers together.` → Nhiều barrier chồng nhau.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `교차적 장애` — intersectional disability.

## 24. age-friendly design /eɪdʒ ˈfrɛndli dɪˈzaɪn/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** design that supports safety, dignity, mobility, communication, and participation across older people's diverse abilities.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** age-friendly design làm environment dễ dùng cho người lớn tuổi mà không infantilize họ.
**Grammar & collocations:** `age-friendly-design standard` — standard; `design for older users` — design; `safe mobility` — mobility.
**Examples:** `Age-friendly design added seating, contrast, clear signs, and rest points along the clinic route.` → Small design giúp đi lại an toàn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `고령친화적 설계` — age-friendly design.

## 25. continuity support /ˌkɑntəˈnuəti səˈpɔrt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** ongoing assistance that preserves relationships, routines, services, and access when a person's circumstances or providers change.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** continuity support giữ service không đứt khi patient chuyển nơi, đổi staff hoặc đổi phase.
**Grammar & collocations:** `continuity-support plan` — plan; `maintain service continuity` — maintain; `transition support` — support.
**Examples:** `Continuity support kept the same communication aid and care coordinator during the move to adult services.` → Đổi service nhưng không mất support.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `연속성 지원` — continuity support.

## Review in context — Part 1

**Inclusive design** anticipates difference, while **reasonable accommodation** and an **adaptive device** respond to a person's specific need. A **functional limitation** may create an **activity restriction** or a **social participation barrier**, but **independent living** can be supported through **personal assistance** and **supported employment**. **Caregiver respite** protects unpaid carers. **Institutionalization** can remove choice, so **deinstitutionalization**, carefully limited **guardianship**, and **supported housing** should be planned together.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 1:**

**Inclusive design** dự đoán khác biệt, còn **reasonable accommodation** và **adaptive device** đáp ứng need cụ thể. **Functional limitation** có thể tạo **activity restriction** hoặc **social participation barrier**, nhưng **independent living** có thể được hỗ trợ bằng **personal assistance** và **supported employment**. **Caregiver respite** bảo vệ người chăm sóc không lương. **Institutionalization** có thể lấy mất choice, nên **deinstitutionalization**, **guardianship** có giới hạn và **supported housing** cần được plan cùng nhau.

## Review in context — Part 2

An **accessibility review** can require an **easy-read format**, **augmentative communication**, or **sign-language access**. A **disability-adjusted service** changes delivery rather than blaming the user. Screening for **coercive control** protects genuine choice. **Ableism** is challenged by a **disability justice** framework that recognizes **intersectional disability**. **Age-friendly design** and **continuity support** help people remain safe, connected, and included as needs change.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 2:**

**Accessibility review** có thể yêu cầu **easy-read format**, **augmentative communication** hoặc **sign-language access**. **Disability-adjusted service** thay đổi delivery thay vì đổ lỗi user. Screening **coercive control** bảo vệ choice thật. **Ableism** được challenge bằng framework **disability justice**, framework này nhận biết **intersectional disability**. **Age-friendly design** và **continuity support** giúp people safe, connected và included khi need đổi.
