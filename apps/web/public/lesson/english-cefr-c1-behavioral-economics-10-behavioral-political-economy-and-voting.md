# C1 Vocabulary 10 — Behavioral political economy and voting

This lesson follows how identity, attention, framing, and social beliefs shape political judgments and participation.

Flow: **partisan perception → motivated reasoning politics → identity-protective cognition → voter turnout salience → political efficacy → policy polarization → ideological sorting → affective polarization → status quo policy → issue framing → loss framing politics → retrospective voting → prospective voting → economic voting belief → political trust → institutional legitimacy → corruption perception → social desirability voting → bandwagon belief → political participation cost**.

## 1. partisan perception /ˈpɑrtɪzən pərˈsɛpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the way a person's political affiliation influences how they interpret facts, candidates, or institutions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cách political affiliation influence interpretation fact, candidate hoặc institution.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong partisan perception` — perception; `shape partisan perceptions` — shape.
**Examples:** `Partisan perception made identical economic statistics seem different to the two groups.` → Perception partisan làm statistic economic giống nhau trông khác với hai group.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `당파적 인식`.

## 2. motivated reasoning politics /ˈmoʊtəˌveɪtɪd ˈriːzənɪŋ ˈpɑlətɪks/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** interpreting political information in a way that protects a desired identity, belief, or conclusion.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** interpret political information để protect identity, belief hoặc conclusion desired.
**Grammar & collocations:** `motivated-reasoning politics` — reasoning; `study motivated reasoning in politics` — study.
**Examples:** `Motivated reasoning politics led supporters to excuse the same behavior they condemned in opponents.` → Reasoning motivated politics làm supporter excuse behavior họ condemn ở opponent.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `정치적 동기화 추론`.

## 3. identity-protective cognition /aɪˈdɛntəti prəˈtɛktɪv kɑɡˈnɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** thinking that selectively accepts or rejects information to preserve a valued group identity.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** thinking select accept/reject information để preserve group identity valued.
**Grammar & collocations:** `identity-protective cognition bias` — bias; `trigger identity-protective cognition` — trigger.
**Examples:** `Identity-protective cognition made the climate report feel like an attack on the community.` → Cognition identity-protective làm climate report trông như attack vào community.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `정체성 보호 인지`.

## 4. voter turnout salience /ˈvoʊtər ˈtɝnˌaʊt ˈseɪliəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** how visible and personally important voting feels when a person decides whether to participate.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mức voting visible và personally important khi decide participate.
**Grammar & collocations:** `low voter-turnout salience` — salience; `increase voter turnout salience` — increase.
**Examples:** `Voter turnout salience rose when the election determined the future of the local hospital.` → Salience turnout voter tăng khi election quyết định future hospital local.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `투표 참여 현저성`.

## 5. political efficacy /pəˈlɪtɪkəl ˈɛfəkəsi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the belief that one's political actions can influence public decisions or that institutions will respond.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** belief action political của mình influence decision public hoặc institution respond.
**Grammar & collocations:** `low political efficacy` — efficacy; `strengthen political efficacy` — strengthen.
**Examples:** `Political efficacy increased after residents saw their petition change the budget proposal.` → Efficacy political tăng sau resident thấy petition đổi budget proposal.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `정치적 효능감`.

## 6. policy polarization /ˈpɑləsi ˌpoʊlərəˈzeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** increasing separation between groups in their preferred public policies.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** separation tăng giữa group về public policy preference.
**Grammar & collocations:** `sharp policy polarization` — polarization; `measure policy polarization` — measure.
**Examples:** `Policy polarization made a modest tax proposal appear like a fundamental ideological choice.` → Polarization policy làm tax proposal modest trông như ideological choice fundamental.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `정책 양극화`.

## 7. ideological sorting /ˌaɪdiəˈlɑdʒɪkəl ˈsɔrtɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the tendency for people to organize their political identities and social relationships around aligned ideologies.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** people organize identity political và relationship social theo ideology aligned.
**Grammar & collocations:** `increasing ideological sorting` — sorting; `study ideological sorting` — study.
**Examples:** `Ideological sorting changed which neighbors residents trusted for political information.` → Sorting ideological đổi neighbor mà resident trust cho information political.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `이념적 분류`.

## 8. affective polarization /əˈfɛktɪv ˌpoʊlərəˈzeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** emotional dislike and distrust between supporters of opposing political groups.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** dislike và distrust emotional giữa supporter của group political đối lập.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high affective polarization` — polarization; `reduce affective polarization` — reduce.
**Examples:** `Affective polarization made people reject a useful idea after learning which party proposed it.` → Polarization affective làm người reject idea hữu ích sau biết party propose.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `정서적 양극화`.

## 9. status quo policy /ˈsteɪtəs kwoʊ ˈpɑləsi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an existing policy that people continue to support or tolerate because change feels uncertain or costly.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** policy cũ tiếp tục support/tolerate vì change uncertain hoặc costly.
**Grammar & collocations:** `defend the status quo policy` — policy; `challenge a status quo policy` — challenge.
**Examples:** `The status quo policy survived because voters feared an unfamiliar replacement.` → Policy status quo survive vì voter fear replacement unfamiliar.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `현상 유지 정책`.

## 10. issue framing /ˈɪʃuː ˈfreɪmɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the way a public problem or policy choice is presented to guide attention and interpretation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cách present public problem hoặc policy choice để guide attention và interpretation.
**Grammar & collocations:** `security-based issue framing` — framing; `change issue framing` — change.
**Examples:** `Issue framing as neighborhood safety gained more support than framing as administrative reform.` → Framing issue như neighborhood safety gain support hơn administrative reform.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `쟁점 프레이밍`.

## 11. loss framing politics /lɔs ˈfreɪmɪŋ ˈpɑlətɪks/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** presenting a political choice in terms of losses that will occur if an option is rejected.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** present political choice theo loss xảy ra nếu reject option.
**Grammar & collocations:** `negative loss-framing politics` — framing; `use loss framing in politics` — use.
**Examples:** `Loss framing politics made voters focus on what the reform might take away.` → Framing loss politics làm voter focus reform có thể take away gì.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `정치적 손실 프레이밍`.

## 12. retrospective voting /ˌrɛtrəˈspɛktɪv ˈvoʊtɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** voting based mainly on judgments about an incumbent's past performance.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** voting dựa chủ yếu judgment về past performance của incumbent.
**Grammar & collocations:** `retrospective economic voting` — voting; `model retrospective voting` — model.
**Examples:** `Retrospective voting rewarded the mayor after employment improved.` → Voting retrospective reward mayor sau employment improve.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `회고적 투표`.

## 13. prospective voting /prəˈspɛktɪv ˈvoʊtɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** voting based on expectations about what candidates or parties will do in the future.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** voting dựa expectation candidate hoặc party sẽ do gì future.
**Grammar & collocations:** `forward-looking prospective voting` — voting; `study prospective voting` — study.
**Examples:** `Prospective voting favored the candidate promising faster housing construction.` → Voting prospective favor candidate promise housing construction nhanh.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `전망적 투표`.

## 14. economic voting belief /ˌiːkəˈnɑmɪk ˈvoʊtɪŋ bɪˈliːf/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a belief that personal or national economic performance should determine political support.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** belief economic performance cá nhân hoặc national nên determine political support.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong economic-voting belief` — belief; `test an economic voting belief` — test.
**Examples:** `Economic voting belief weakened when voters blamed global conditions rather than the government.` → Belief voting economic yếu khi voter blame global condition chứ không phải government.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `경제 투표 믿음`.

## 15. political trust /pəˈlɪtɪkəl trʌst/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** confidence that political institutions, leaders, or processes will act competently and fairly.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** confidence institution, leader hoặc process political act competent và fair.
**Grammar & collocations:** `declining political trust` — trust; `restore political trust` — restore.
**Examples:** `Political trust recovered after the agency published the full procurement record.` → Trust political recover sau agency publish procurement record đầy đủ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `정치적 신뢰`.

## 16. institutional legitimacy /ˌɪnstəˈtuːʃənəl ləˈdʒɪtəməsi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the belief that an institution has a rightful authority to make and enforce decisions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** belief institution có authority rightful để make và enforce decision.
**Grammar & collocations:** `perceived institutional legitimacy` — legitimacy; `undermine institutional legitimacy` — undermine.
**Examples:** `Transparent appeals strengthened institutional legitimacy after the contested vote.` → Legitimacy institutional tăng sau appeal minh bạch sau vote tranh chấp.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `제도적 정당성`.

## 17. corruption perception /kəˈrʌpʃən pərˈsɛpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a person's belief about how common or serious misuse of public power is.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** belief corruption hoặc misuse public power common/serious thế nào.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high corruption perception` — perception; `change corruption perceptions` — change.
**Examples:** `Corruption perception reduced willingness to pay for a new public service.` → Perception corruption giảm willingness pay cho public service mới.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `부패 인식`.

## 18. social desirability voting /ˈsoʊʃəl dɪˌzaɪrəˈbɪləti ˈvoʊtɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** reporting or choosing a vote in a way that presents oneself favorably to others.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** report hoặc choose vote để present self favorably với người khác.
**Grammar & collocations:** `social-desirability voting bias` — bias; `detect social desirability voting` — detect.
**Examples:** `Social desirability voting made respondents overreport support for a civically admired candidate.` → Voting desirability social làm respondent overreport support candidate admired.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `사회적 바람직성 투표`.

## 19. bandwagon belief /ˈbændˌwæɡən bɪˈliːf/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the belief that a person should support an option because many other people appear to support it.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** belief nên support option vì nhiều người khác appear support.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong bandwagon belief` — belief; `activate a bandwagon belief` — activate.
**Examples:** `A bandwagon belief grew after the poll showed one candidate far ahead.` → Belief bandwagon grow sau poll show candidate lead xa.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `편승 믿음`.

## 20. political participation cost /pəˈlɪtɪkəl pɑrˌtɪsəˈpeɪʃən kɔst/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the time, effort, money, or social risk required to vote, organize, contact officials, or attend civic activities.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** time, effort, money hoặc social risk cần để vote, organize, contact official hoặc civic activity.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high political participation cost` — cost; `lower political participation costs` — lower.
**Examples:** `A mobile polling station lowered the political participation cost for shift workers.` → Polling station mobile giảm cost participation political cho shift worker.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `정치 참여 비용`.

## Review in context

**Partisan perception**, **motivated reasoning politics**, and **identity-protective cognition** can change **voter turnout salience** and perceived **political efficacy**. **Policy polarization**, **ideological sorting**, and **affective polarization** reinforce a **status quo policy** through **issue framing** and **loss framing politics**. **Retrospective voting** differs from **prospective voting**, while an **economic voting belief** shapes both. **Political trust** and **institutional legitimacy** can fall with high **corruption perception**. **Social desirability voting**, a **bandwagon belief**, and **political participation cost** also influence what citizens report and do.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:** **Partisan perception**, **motivated reasoning politics** và **identity-protective cognition** có thể đổi **voter turnout salience** và **political efficacy**. **Policy polarization**, **ideological sorting** và **affective polarization** reinforce **status quo policy** qua **issue framing** và **loss framing politics**. **Retrospective voting** khác **prospective voting**, còn **economic voting belief** shape cả hai. **Political trust** và **institutional legitimacy** có thể giảm khi **corruption perception** cao. **Social desirability voting**, **bandwagon belief** và **political participation cost** cũng influence điều citizen report và do.
