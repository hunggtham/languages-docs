# C2 Vocabulary — Film and broadcast production robotics

This lesson follows a production from repeatable camera movement and virtual capture to live replay and studio-floor coordination. The network is **film production robot → motion-control camera rig → robotic focus puller → automated camera slider → virtual production tracking → cinema lighting robot → remote pan-tilt head → robotic dolly shot → drone cinematography → lens calibration robot → automated boom camera → motion-control repeatability → set continuity capture → digital crowd simulation → facial capture rig → stunt camera robot → remote production control → broadcast replay robot → teleprompter tracking robot → studio floor robot**.

## 1. film production robot /fɪlm prəˈdʌkʃən ˈroʊbɑːt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a robot used to capture images, move equipment, manage a set, or repeat a film-production action.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** robot dùng để capture image, move equipment, manage set hoặc lặp film-production action.
**Grammar & collocations:** `film-production-robot workflow` — workflow; `repeat a complex shot` — lặp complex shot.
**Examples:** `The film production robot repeated the camera move for three visual effects passes.` → Film production robot lặp camera move cho ba visual-effects pass.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `영화 제작 로봇`.

## 2. motion-control camera rig /ˈmoʊʃən kənˈtroʊl ˈkæmrə rɪɡ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a programmable mechanical rig that moves a camera along a precise, repeatable path.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** rig cơ khí lập trình được, di chuyển camera theo path precise và repeatable.
**Grammar & collocations:** `motion-control-camera-rig path` — path; `store a camera trajectory` — lưu camera trajectory.
**Examples:** `The motion-control camera rig matched yesterday’s tracking shot.` → Motion-control camera rig khớp tracking shot của hôm qua.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `모션 컨트롤 카메라 리그`.

## 3. robotic focus puller /roʊˈbɑːtɪk ˈfoʊkəs ˈpʊlər/

**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** system motor chỉnh lens focus khi subject hoặc camera di chuyển.
**Grammar & collocations:** `robotic-focus-puller motor` — motor; `hold focus on a moving face` — giữ focus trên mặt đang move.
**Examples:** `The robotic focus puller kept the actor sharp during the fast approach.` → Robotic focus puller giữ actor sharp trong lúc camera approach nhanh.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `로봇 포커스 풀러`.

## Review in context

A **film production robot** built around a **motion-control camera rig** can use a **robotic focus puller** and **automated camera slider** for repeatable images. **Virtual production tracking**, a **cinema lighting robot**, and a **remote pan-tilt head** shape the shot, while a **robotic dolly shot**, **drone cinematography**, and a **lens calibration robot** extend camera movement. An **automated boom camera** depends on **motion-control repeatability**; **set continuity capture** preserves matching details and **digital crowd simulation** fills the background. A **facial capture rig** and **stunt camera robot** record specialized performances. **Remote production control**, a **broadcast replay robot**, a **teleprompter tracking robot**, and a **studio floor robot** keep live production coordinated.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:** **Film production robot** dựa trên **motion-control camera rig** có thể dùng **robotic focus puller** và **automated camera slider** để tạo image lặp lại. **Virtual production tracking**, **cinema lighting robot** và **remote pan-tilt head** định hình shot, còn **robotic dolly shot**, **drone cinematography** và **lens calibration robot** mở rộng camera movement. **Automated boom camera** dựa vào **motion-control repeatability**; **set continuity capture** giữ detail nhất quán và **digital crowd simulation** lấp background. **Facial capture rig** và **stunt camera robot** ghi performance chuyên biệt. **Remote production control**, **broadcast replay robot**, **teleprompter tracking robot** và **studio floor robot** giữ live production phối hợp.
