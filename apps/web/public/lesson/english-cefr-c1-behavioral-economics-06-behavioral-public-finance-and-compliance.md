# C1 Vocabulary 06 — Behavioral public finance and compliance

This lesson follows how people interpret taxes, fairness, enforcement, and social responsibility when deciding whether to comply or contribute.

Flow: **tax morale → fairness perception → compliance norm → enforcement cue → tax framing → withholding illusion → tax complexity → mental accounting tax → public-good contribution → free-riding belief → social image concern → warm-glow giving → crowding-out effect → charitable giving motive → prosocial preference → corruption norm → bribe aversion → honesty prime → ethical fading → behavioral public finance**.

## 1. tax morale /tæks məˈræl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the willingness to pay taxes because doing so is viewed as a civic duty or legitimate contribution.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** willingness pay tax vì xem đó là civic duty hoặc contribution legitimate.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high tax morale` — morale cao; `strengthen tax morale` — strengthen.
**Examples:** `Tax morale rose when residents could see how revenue improved local clinics.` → Morale tax tăng khi resident thấy revenue improve clinic local.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `납세 의식`.

## 2. fairness perception /ˈfɛrnəs pərˈsɛpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a person's judgment about whether a rule, payment, or distribution treats people appropriately.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** judgment xem rule, payment hoặc distribution đối xử với người có appropriate không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `subjective fairness perception` — perception; `shape fairness perceptions` — shape.
**Examples:** `Fairness perception fell when small businesses faced the same paperwork as large firms.` → Perception fairness giảm khi small business face paperwork như large firm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `공정성 인식`.

## 3. compliance norm /kəmˈplaɪəns nɔrm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a shared expectation that people in a group should follow a rule or fulfill an obligation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** expectation shared rằng người trong group nên follow rule hoặc fulfill obligation.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong compliance norm` — norm; `reinforce a compliance norm` — reinforce.
**Examples:** `A compliance norm made late filing seem unusual among local contractors.` → Norm compliance làm late filing trông unusual giữa contractor local.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `준수 규범`.

## 4. enforcement cue /ɪnˈfɔrsmənt kjuː/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a signal that reminds people that a rule is monitored and violations may have consequences.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** signal nhắc rằng rule được monitor và violation có consequence.
**Grammar & collocations:** `visible enforcement cue` — cue; `provide an enforcement cue` — provide.
**Examples:** `A simple enforcement cue on the form increased accurate reporting.` → Cue enforcement đơn giản trên form tăng accurate reporting.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `집행 신호`.

## 5. tax framing /tæks ˈfreɪmɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the way a tax or payment is described, which can influence how people understand and respond to it.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cách describe tax hoặc payment ảnh hưởng cách người understand và respond.
**Grammar & collocations:** `revenue-based tax framing` — framing; `change tax framing` — change.
**Examples:** `Tax framing as a contribution to emergency care produced more support than framing as a charge.` → Framing tax như contribution cho emergency care tạo support hơn framing như charge.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `세금 프레이밍`.

## 6. withholding illusion /wɪðˈhoʊldɪŋ ɪˈluːʒən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the tendency to underestimate taxes when they are deducted before income is received.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** underestimate tax khi tax deduct trước khi nhận income.
**Grammar & collocations:** `withholding-illusion effect` — effect; `reduce withholding illusion` — reduce.
**Examples:** `Withholding illusion made employees less aware of the total annual tax they paid.` → Illusion withholding làm employee ít aware total tax annual.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `원천징수 환상`.

## 7. tax complexity /tæks kəmˈplɛksəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the difficulty created by complicated tax rules, forms, calculations, and eligibility conditions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** difficulty do tax rule, form, calculation và eligibility condition phức tạp.
**Grammar & collocations:** `administrative tax complexity` — complexity; `reduce tax complexity` — reduce.
**Examples:** `Tax complexity discouraged eligible families from claiming the credit.` → Complexity tax discourage family eligible claim credit.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `세금 복잡성`.

## 8. mental accounting tax /ˈmɛntl əˈkaʊntɪŋ tæks/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a tax response shaped by treating a payment as belonging to a particular mental budget or category.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** tax response shape bởi xem payment thuộc mental budget hoặc category cụ thể.
**Grammar & collocations:** `mental-accounting tax response` — response; `study mental accounting tax` — study.
**Examples:** `The mental accounting tax response differed when the bill was labeled as a service fee.` → Response tax mental accounting khác khi bill label service fee.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `정신 회계 세금`.

## 9. public-good contribution /ˈpʌblɪk ɡʊd ˌkɑntrəˈbjuːʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a payment, effort, or resource offered to support a benefit that many people can share.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** payment, effort hoặc resource support benefit nhiều người share.
**Grammar & collocations:** `voluntary public-good contribution` — contribution; `increase public-good contributions` — increase.
**Examples:** `The public-good contribution funded maintenance of the shared flood barrier.` → Contribution public-good fund maintenance flood barrier chung.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `공공재 기여`.

## 10. free-riding belief /ˈfriː ˌraɪdɪŋ bɪˈliːf/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the belief that others will provide a shared benefit, allowing oneself to enjoy it without contributing.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** belief người khác provide shared benefit nên mình enjoy mà không contribute.
**Grammar & collocations:** `widespread free-riding belief` — belief; `challenge a free-riding belief` — challenge.
**Examples:** `A free-riding belief reduced donations when residents thought the target would be reached anyway.` → Belief free-riding giảm donation khi resident nghĩ target vẫn đạt.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `무임승차 믿음`.

## 11. social image concern /ˈsoʊʃəl ˈɪmɪdʒ kənˈsɜrn/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** worry about how one's actions will be judged or interpreted by other people.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** worry người khác judge hoặc interpret action của mình thế nào.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong social image concern` — concern; `activate social image concerns` — activate.
**Examples:** `Social image concern encouraged businesses to display their environmental donations.` → Concern image social encourage business display environmental donation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `사회적 이미지 우려`.

## 12. warm-glow giving /ˈwɔrm ɡloʊ ˈɡɪvɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** giving motivated partly by the personal satisfaction of being generous, not only by the benefit received by others.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** giving do satisfaction cá nhân khi generous, không chỉ benefit người khác.
**Grammar & collocations:** `warm-glow giving motive` — motive; `encourage warm-glow giving` — encourage.
**Examples:** `Warm-glow giving increased when donors received a personal thank-you message.` → Giving warm-glow tăng khi donor nhận thank-you message cá nhân.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `따뜻한 만족 기부`.

## 13. crowding-out effect /ˈkraʊdɪŋ aʊt ɪˈfɛkt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a reduction in one contribution or behavior caused by an increase in another source of support.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** một contribution hoặc behavior giảm vì source support khác tăng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `partial crowding-out effect` — effect; `measure a crowding-out effect` — measure.
**Examples:** `A government grant created a crowding-out effect by reducing private donations.` → Grant government tạo effect crowding-out làm private donation giảm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `구축 효과`.

## 14. charitable giving motive /ˈtʃɛrətəbəl ˈɡɪvɪŋ ˈmoʊtɪv/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a reason why a person donates, such as compassion, reputation, reciprocity, or personal satisfaction.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** reason người donate như compassion, reputation, reciprocity hoặc satisfaction.
**Grammar & collocations:** `mixed charitable giving motive` — motive; `identify a charitable giving motive` — identify.
**Examples:** `The survey found that each charitable giving motive predicted a different response to publicity.` → Survey thấy mỗi motive giving charitable predict response khác với publicity.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `자선 기부 동기`.

## 15. prosocial preference /proʊˈsoʊʃəl ˈprɛfərəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a preference for actions that benefit other people or a group, even when they carry a personal cost.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** preference action benefit người khác hoặc group dù có personal cost.
**Grammar & collocations:** `stable prosocial preference` — preference; `measure prosocial preferences` — measure.
**Examples:** `A prosocial preference made commuters accept a small fee for cleaner air.` → Preference prosocial làm commuter accept fee nhỏ cho air sạch hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `친사회적 선호`.

## 16. corruption norm /kəˈrʌpʃən nɔrm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a shared expectation about whether using public power for private benefit is normal or tolerated.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** expectation shared xem dùng public power cho private benefit là normal hoặc tolerated.
**Grammar & collocations:** `permissive corruption norm` — norm; `change a corruption norm` — change.
**Examples:** `A permissive corruption norm made small unofficial payments seem unavoidable.` → Norm corruption permissive làm payment unofficial nhỏ trông unavoidable.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `부패 규범`.

## 17. bribe aversion /braɪb əˈvɝʒən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a strong reluctance to offer, request, or accept an improper payment.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** reluctance mạnh với offer, request hoặc accept improper payment.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong bribe aversion` — aversion; `measure bribe aversion` — measure.
**Examples:** `Bribe aversion increased when the service displayed a clear complaint channel.` → Aversion bribe tăng khi service display complaint channel rõ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `뇌물 회피`.

## 18. honesty prime /ˈɑnəsti praɪm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a reminder or prompt designed to activate honest behavior before a decision.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** reminder hoặc prompt activate honest behavior trước decision.
**Grammar & collocations:** `brief honesty prime` — prime; `use an honesty prime` — use.
**Examples:** `An honesty prime asked applicants to sign a truth statement before completing the form.` → Prime honesty yêu cầu applicant sign truth statement trước complete form.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `정직성 점화`.

## 19. ethical fading /ˈɛθɪkəl ˈfeɪdɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the process by which the moral dimension of a decision becomes less visible as attention shifts to targets or financial outcomes.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** moral dimension của decision mờ đi khi attention shift sang target hoặc financial outcome.
**Grammar & collocations:** `ethical-fading risk` — risk; `prevent ethical fading` — prevent.
**Examples:** `Ethical fading made the sales team describe misleading fees as a harmless optimization.` → Fading ethical làm sales team describe fee misleading như optimization harmless.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `윤리적 희미해짐`.

## 20. behavioral public finance /bɪˈheɪvjərəl ˈpʌblɪk faɪˌnæns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the study of how psychological biases, social preferences, and limited attention shape public revenue and spending.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** study bias psychological, social preference và limited attention shape public revenue và spending.
**Grammar & collocations:** `behavioral-public-finance policy` — policy; `study behavioral public finance` — study.
**Examples:** `Behavioral public finance compared tax designs by how people actually perceived their costs.` → Finance public behavioral compare tax design theo cách người thực sự perceive cost.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `행동 공공재정`.

## Review in context

**Tax morale** depends on **fairness perception**, a shared **compliance norm**, and an **enforcement cue**. **Tax framing**, **withholding illusion**, **tax complexity**, and **mental accounting tax** shape how people understand payment. A **public-good contribution** can fall when a **free-riding belief** spreads, but **social image concern** and **warm-glow giving** may encourage support. A **crowding-out effect** can weaken a **charitable giving motive**, while a **prosocial preference** can resist it. A **corruption norm**, **bribe aversion**, an **honesty prime**, and protection against **ethical fading** all matter in **behavioral public finance**.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:** **Tax morale** phụ thuộc **fairness perception**, **compliance norm** chung và **enforcement cue**. **Tax framing**, **withholding illusion**, **tax complexity** và **mental accounting tax** shape cách người hiểu payment. **Public-good contribution** có thể giảm khi **free-riding belief** lan rộng, nhưng **social image concern** và **warm-glow giving** có thể encourage support. **Crowding-out effect** có thể weaken **charitable giving motive**, còn **prosocial preference** có thể resist. **Corruption norm**, **bribe aversion**, **honesty prime** và bảo vệ khỏi **ethical fading** đều quan trọng trong **behavioral public finance**.
