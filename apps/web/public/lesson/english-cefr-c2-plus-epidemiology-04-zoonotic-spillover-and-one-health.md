# C2+ Vocabulary — Zoonotic spillover and One Health

This lesson follows how pathogens move across animal, human, and environmental systems and how One Health teams assess and reduce that risk. The network is **spillover event → reservoir host → bridge host → amplification host → host-range expansion → vector competence → enzootic cycle → epizootic wave → reverse zoonosis → wildlife interface → land-use transition → ecological niche overlap → pathogen reservoir → cross-species transmission → serologic sentinel → animal sentinel → syndromic veterinary surveillance → One Health coordination → risk-based culling → biosecurity perimeter → farm-to-farm spread → carcass disposal → wildlife trade pathway → spillover risk assessment → zoonotic emergence**.

## 1. spillover event /ˈspɪlˌoʊvər ɪˈvɛnt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an episode in which a pathogen passes from an animal or environmental source into a new host species.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** spillover event là khoảnh khắc pathogen vượt species barrier.
**Grammar & collocations:** `spillover-event investigation` — investigation; `detect a spillover` — detect; `cross-species jump` — jump.
**Examples:** `The spillover event followed repeated contact between workers and infected animals.` → Contact lặp lại mở cơ hội jump.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `종간 전파 사건` — spillover event.

## 2. reservoir host /ˈrɛzərˌvwɑr hoʊst/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a host species in which a pathogen can persist and circulate over time, often with limited disease.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** reservoir host giữ pathogen trong ecosystem và tiếp tục cung cấp source.
**Grammar & collocations:** `reservoir-host population` — population; `identify a reservoir host` — identify; `pathogen maintenance` — maintenance.
**Examples:** `The investigation compared bat colonies as possible reservoir hosts.` → Reservoir cần evidence dài hạn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `병원체 저장 숙주` — reservoir host.

## 3. bridge host /brɪdʒ hoʊst/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a species that connects a pathogen reservoir with people or another host population.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** bridge host là “cầu nối” giữa wildlife reservoir và human contact.
**Grammar & collocations:** `bridge-host investigation` — investigation; `identify a bridge host` — identify; `host-interface pathway` — pathway.
**Examples:** `Domestic animals may act as bridge hosts when they enter both forests and villages.` → Bridge host chạm hai network.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `교량 숙주` — bridge host.

## 4. amplification host /ˌæmpləfəˈkeɪʃən hoʊst/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a host in which a pathogen reaches high abundance or prevalence, increasing the chance of onward transmission.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** amplification host làm signal pathogen “to” lên trong network.
**Grammar & collocations:** `amplification-host species` — species; `amplify transmission` — amplify; `high pathogen load` — load.
**Examples:** `Dense livestock housing created conditions in which the species became an amplification host.` → Density làm load tăng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `증폭 숙주` — amplification host.

## 5. host-range expansion /hoʊst reɪndʒ ɪkˈspænʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an increase in the number or diversity of host species that a pathogen can infect or transmit through.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** host-range expansion là pathogen mở rộng “danh sách host” có thể infect.
**Grammar & collocations:** `host-range expansion` — expansion; `expand the host range` — expand; `species susceptibility` — susceptibility.
**Examples:** `Genetic change alone did not prove host-range expansion; field transmission also had to be shown.` → Range cần evidence thực địa.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `숙주 범위 확대` — host-range expansion.

## 6. vector competence /ˈvɛktər ˈkɑmpətəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the ability of a vector to acquire, support, and transmit a pathogen.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** vector competence hỏi insect/tick có thể giữ và truyền pathogen không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `vector-competence assay` — assay; `assess vector competence` — assess; `pathogen transmission` — transmission.
**Examples:** `Warming temperatures altered vector competence and lengthened the seasonal transmission window.` → Climate có thể đổi competence.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `매개체 감염능` — vector competence.

## 7. enzootic cycle /ˌɛnzoʊˈɑtɪk ˈsaɪkəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a continuing transmission cycle of a pathogen within an animal population or ecosystem.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** enzootic cycle là background circulation trong animal system.
**Grammar & collocations:** `enzootic-cycle model` — model; `maintain an enzootic cycle` — maintain; `animal transmission network` — network.
**Examples:** `The pathogen remained in an enzootic cycle despite few reported human cases.` → Human case ít không có nghĩa pathogen biến mất.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `동물군 내 유행 주기` — enzootic cycle.

## 8. epizootic wave /ˌɛpɪzoʊˈɑtɪk weɪv/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a marked increase in disease transmission or illness across an animal population.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** epizootic wave là “wave” bệnh trong animal population.
**Grammar & collocations:** `epizootic-wave alert` — alert; `track an epizootic wave` — track; `animal morbidity` — morbidity.
**Examples:** `An epizootic wave in poultry preceded infections among farm workers.` → Animal wave có thể là early warning.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `동물 전염병 파동` — epizootic wave.

## 9. reverse zoonosis /rɪˈvɜrs zoʊəˈnoʊsɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** transmission of a pathogen from humans back into an animal population.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** reverse zoonosis đi ngược chiều human → animal và có thể tạo reservoir mới.
**Grammar & collocations:** `reverse-zoonosis event` — event; `transmit back to animals` — transmit; `animal reintroduction` — reintroduction.
**Examples:** `Reverse zoonosis raised concern that the virus could establish in urban wildlife.` → Human infection có thể quay lại wildlife.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `역인수공통감염` — reverse zoonosis.

## 10. wildlife interface /ˈwaɪldˌlaɪf ˈɪntərˌfeɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a place or activity where people, domestic animals, and wildlife come into contact.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** wildlife interface là điểm giao giữa nhiều host network.
**Grammar & collocations:** `wildlife-interface assessment` — assessment; `map a wildlife interface` — map; `human–animal contact` — contact.
**Examples:** `The market was mapped as a wildlife interface because several species shared holding areas.` → Interface có nhiều route transmission.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `야생동물 접점` — wildlife interface.

## 11. land-use transition /ˈlænd juːs trænˈzɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a change in how land is used that alters contact among wildlife, livestock, vectors, and people.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** land-use transition đổi habitat và contact pattern cùng lúc.
**Grammar & collocations:** `land-use transition` — transition; `convert forest to farmland` — convert; `contact opportunity` — opportunity.
**Examples:** `Land-use transition increased human movement through a previously undisturbed wildlife habitat.` → Land change mở thêm contact.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `토지 이용 전환` — land-use transition.

## 12. ecological niche overlap /ˌiːkəˈlɑdʒɪkəl nɪtʃ ˈoʊvərˌlæp/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the extent to which two species share habitat, resources, or conditions that enable pathogen exchange.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** ecological niche overlap cho biết hai species sống “chồng” nhau ở đâu.
**Grammar & collocations:** `ecological-niche overlap` — overlap; `estimate niche overlap` — estimate; `shared habitat` — habitat.
**Examples:** `Ecological niche overlap was highest around seasonal water points used by several mammals.` → Shared resource tạo contact.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `생태적 지위 중첩` — ecological niche overlap.

## 13. pathogen reservoir /ˈpæθədʒən ˈrɛzərˌvwɑr/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an animal, human, or environmental source in which a pathogen persists and from which exposure can occur.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** pathogen reservoir là nơi pathogen “trú” và tiếp tục xuất hiện trong system.
**Grammar & collocations:** `pathogen-reservoir mapping` — mapping; `identify a reservoir` — identify; `environmental persistence` — persistence.
**Examples:** `The team sampled soil and water to determine whether the farm environment was a pathogen reservoir.` → Reservoir có thể là environment.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `병원체 저장소` — pathogen reservoir.

## 14. cross-species transmission /krɔs ˈspiːʃiːz trænzˈmɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** movement of a pathogen from one species into another species.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cross-species transmission là process vượt species boundary, chưa chắc tạo sustained spread.
**Grammar & collocations:** `cross-species transmission event` — event; `document cross-species transmission` — document; `species barrier` — barrier.
**Examples:** `Cross-species transmission was supported by matching sequences and a plausible contact route.` → Cần cả genetic và ecological evidence.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `종간 전파` — cross-species transmission.

## 15. serologic sentinel /ˌsɪrəˈlɑdʒɪk ˈsɛntənəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a person or animal monitored through antibody testing to provide an early signal of circulation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** serologic sentinel dùng antibody như “cảm biến” exposure trong system.
**Grammar & collocations:** `serologic-sentinel survey` — survey; `sample sentinel animals` — sample; `antibody signal` — signal.
**Examples:** `Serologic sentinels detected exposure among workers before severe cases were reported.` → Antibody signal có thể đến sớm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `혈청학적 감시 개체` — serologic sentinel.

## 16. animal sentinel /ˈænəməl ˈsɛntənəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an animal monitored because its exposure or illness can signal an environmental or zoonotic hazard.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** animal sentinel báo động risk trong environment mà human sampling chưa thấy.
**Grammar & collocations:** `animal-sentinel program` — program; `monitor sentinel animals` — monitor; `early-warning species` — species.
**Examples:** `Dead birds served as animal sentinels for intensified mosquito-borne disease surveillance.` → Animal signal dẫn đường cho human health.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `동물 감시 개체` — animal sentinel.

## 17. syndromic veterinary surveillance /sɪnˈdrɑmɪk ˈvɛtərɪˌnɛri sərˈveɪləns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** monitoring animal symptoms, deaths, production changes, or treatment patterns before a specific diagnosis is confirmed.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** syndromic veterinary surveillance nhìn pattern trong đàn trước lab confirmation.
**Grammar & collocations:** `syndromic-veterinary surveillance` — surveillance; `monitor livestock syndromes` — monitor; `unusual mortality signal` — signal.
**Examples:** `Syndromic veterinary surveillance flagged reduced egg production before the pathogen was identified.` → Production change là signal sớm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `증후군 기반 수의 감시` — syndromic veterinary surveillance.

## 18. One Health coordination /wʌn hɛlθ koʊˌɔrdəˈneɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** structured collaboration among human-health, animal-health, environmental, and community authorities.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** One Health coordination nối các agency có cùng risk nhưng khác data và mandate.
**Grammar & collocations:** `One-Health coordination platform` — platform; `coordinate across sectors` — coordinate; `shared risk assessment` — assessment.
**Examples:** `One Health coordination aligned veterinary sampling with hospital testing and water monitoring.` → Coordination ghép các mảnh evidence.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `원헬스 협력` — One Health coordination.

## 19. risk-based culling /rɪsk beɪst ˈkʌlɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** removal of animals according to infection probability, transmission role, welfare, and proportionality rather than indiscriminate slaughter.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** risk-based culling nhắm vào risk cao với tiêu chí minh bạch và giảm harm không cần thiết.
**Grammar & collocations:** `risk-based-culling policy` — policy; `cull an infected group` — cull; `proportional response` — response.
**Examples:** `Risk-based culling protected unaffected herds while removing animals with confirmed infection.` → Culling phải proportional.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `위험 기반 살처분` — risk-based culling.

## 20. biosecurity perimeter /ˌbaɪoʊsɪˈkjʊrəti pəˈrɪmətər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a defined boundary with controls that limit pathogen movement into or out of a facility or animal population.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** biosecurity perimeter là ranh giới có rule cho người, vehicle, equipment và animal.
**Grammar & collocations:** `biosecurity-perimeter control` — control; `secure the perimeter` — secure; `movement restriction` — restriction.
**Examples:** `The farm strengthened its biosecurity perimeter with vehicle disinfection and visitor logs.` → Perimeter cần cả physical lẫn administrative control.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `생물보안 경계` — biosecurity perimeter.

## 21. farm-to-farm spread /fɑrm tə fɑrm sprɛd/

**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** farm-to-farm spread đi theo network logistics chứ không chỉ direct animal contact.

## 23. wildlife trade pathway /ˈwaɪldˌlaɪf treɪd ˈpæθweɪ/

**Core meaning (English):** a chain of capture, transport, sale, or handling through which wildlife and pathogens can move.

## 24. spillover risk assessment /ˈspɪlˌoʊvər rɪsk əˈsɛsmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a structured evaluation of the likelihood and consequences of pathogen transmission across species or interfaces.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** spillover risk assessment ghép probability, exposure và consequence để ưu tiên action.
**Grammar & collocations:** `spillover-risk assessment` — assessment; `rank transmission risk` — rank; `interface hazard` — hazard.
**Examples:** `The spillover risk assessment prioritized markets with dense multi-species contact.` → Risk cao khi contact dày và control yếu.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `종간 전파 위험 평가` — spillover risk assessment.

## 25. zoonotic emergence /zoʊəˈnɑtɪk ɪˈmɜrdʒəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the appearance and establishment of a zoonotic pathogen in a new host population or geographic setting.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** zoonotic emergence là lúc zoonotic pathogen không chỉ jump mà còn bắt đầu establish.
**Grammar & collocations:** `zoonotic-emergence scenario` — scenario; `monitor emergence` — monitor; `new host population` — population.
**Examples:** `Zoonotic emergence requires evidence of sustained circulation, not just one unusual infection.` → Emergence cao hơn một spillover đơn lẻ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `인수공통감염병 출현` — zoonotic emergence.

## Review in context — Part 1

A **spillover event** may begin in a **reservoir host**, pass through a **bridge host**, and increase in an **amplification host**. **Host-range expansion**, **vector competence**, an **enzootic cycle**, and an **epizootic wave** help explain why risk changes. **Reverse zoonosis** can send infection back into animals. Teams map the **wildlife interface**, study **land-use transition** and **ecological niche overlap**, and identify a **pathogen reservoir** before claiming **cross-species transmission**.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 1:**

Một **spillover event** có thể bắt đầu ở **reservoir host**, đi qua **bridge host** và tăng lên trong **amplification host**. **Host-range expansion**, **vector competence**, **enzootic cycle** và **epizootic wave** giúp giải thích vì sao risk thay đổi. **Reverse zoonosis** có thể đưa infection trở lại animal. Nhóm điều tra lập bản đồ **wildlife interface**, nghiên cứu **land-use transition** và **ecological niche overlap**, rồi xác định **pathogen reservoir** trước khi kết luận có **cross-species transmission**.

## Review in context — Part 2

A **serologic sentinel** or **animal sentinel** can provide an early warning, while **syndromic veterinary surveillance** catches unusual illness before confirmation. **One Health coordination** links the evidence. A **risk-based culling** plan, **biosecurity perimeter**, and controls on **farm-to-farm spread** can reduce amplification. Safe **carcass disposal** and scrutiny of the **wildlife trade pathway** close further routes. A **spillover risk assessment** distinguishes a single jump from sustained **zoonotic emergence**.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 2:**

**Serologic sentinel** hoặc **animal sentinel** có thể cung cấp cảnh báo sớm, còn **syndromic veterinary surveillance** phát hiện bệnh bất thường trước khi confirmation. **One Health coordination** nối các evidence. Kế hoạch **risk-based culling**, **biosecurity perimeter** và kiểm soát **farm-to-farm spread** có thể giảm amplification. **Carcass disposal** an toàn và giám sát **wildlife trade pathway** đóng các route còn lại. **Spillover risk assessment** phân biệt một jump đơn lẻ với **zoonotic emergence** kéo dài.
