# C2 Vocabulary — Environmental-monitoring robotics and climate sensing

This lesson follows autonomous platforms that sample air, water, habitats, and climate variables, then turn observations into environmental decisions. The network is **environmental monitoring robot → air-quality monitoring robot → water-quality robot → sampling robot → autonomous buoy → weather station robot → atmospheric sensing drone → greenhouse robot → pollination robot → forest monitoring robot → wildlife monitoring robot → biodiversity survey robot → invasive species detection → illegal dumping detection → shoreline mapping → coastal erosion monitoring → glacier monitoring robot → permafrost monitoring → methane leak detection → carbon flux measurement**.

## 1. environmental monitoring robot /ɪnˌvaɪrənˈmentəl ˈmɑːnətərɪŋ ˈroʊbɑːt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a robot that measures, maps, or observes environmental conditions over time.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** robot đo, map hoặc quan sát environmental condition theo time.
**Grammar & collocations:** `environmental-monitoring-robot route` — route; `sample a remote wetland` — lấy sample wetland xa.
**Examples:** `The environmental monitoring robot surveyed the wetland after the flood receded.` → Environmental monitoring robot khảo sát wetland sau khi nước lũ rút.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `환경 모니터링 로봇`.

**Grammar & collocations:** `air-quality-monitoring-robot sensor` — sensor; `map a pollution plume` — map pollution plume.

## Review in context

An **environmental monitoring robot**, **air-quality monitoring robot**, **water-quality robot**, or **sampling robot** can work with an **autonomous buoy**, **weather station robot**, or **atmospheric sensing drone**. A **greenhouse robot** and **pollination robot** support controlled crops, while a **forest monitoring robot**, **wildlife monitoring robot**, and **biodiversity survey robot** observe ecosystems. **Invasive species detection** and **illegal dumping detection** protect habitats; **shoreline mapping** and **coastal erosion monitoring** track change. A **glacier monitoring robot** and **permafrost monitoring** measure frozen ground, while **methane leak detection** and **carbon flux measurement** connect local observations to climate decisions.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:** **Environmental monitoring robot**, **air-quality monitoring robot**, **water-quality robot** hoặc **sampling robot** có thể làm việc với **autonomous buoy**, **weather station robot** hay **atmospheric sensing drone**. **Greenhouse robot** và **pollination robot** hỗ trợ crop kiểm soát, còn **forest monitoring robot**, **wildlife monitoring robot** và **biodiversity survey robot** quan sát ecosystem. **Invasive species detection** và **illegal dumping detection** bảo vệ habitat; **shoreline mapping** và **coastal erosion monitoring** theo dõi thay đổi. **Glacier monitoring robot** và **permafrost monitoring** đo ground đóng băng, còn **methane leak detection** và **carbon flux measurement** nối observation local với climate decision.
