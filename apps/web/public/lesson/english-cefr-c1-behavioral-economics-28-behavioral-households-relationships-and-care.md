# C1 Vocabulary 28 — Behavioral households, relationships, and care

This lesson follows how households and close relationships coordinate money, time, support, privacy, and repair under unequal information and competing needs.

Flow: **household coordination cost → domestic labor norm → care reciprocity expectation → emotional labor visibility → parenting time scarcity → sibling fairness perception → couple financial transparency → relationship risk perception → partner choice framing → trust repair routine → household bargaining anchor → shared goal salience → conflict cooling norm → apology timing effect → gift reciprocity expectation → friendship maintenance friction → social support availability belief → loneliness risk perception → intimacy privacy boundary → relationship investment framing**.

## 1. household coordination cost /ˈhaʊsˌhoʊld koʊˌɔrdəˈneɪʃən kɔst/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the time, effort, and mental work needed for household members to align plans, tasks, and decisions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** time, effort và mental work để member household align plan, task và decision.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high household coordination cost` — cost; `reduce household coordination costs` — reduce.
**Examples:** `Household coordination cost rose when work schedules changed without a shared calendar.` → Cost coordination household tăng khi schedule work đổi mà không có calendar shared.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `가정 조정 비용`.

## 2. domestic labor norm /dəˈmɛstɪk ˈleɪbər nɔrm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a shared expectation about who should perform, notice, and organize unpaid work at home.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** expectation shared ai perform, notice và organize unpaid work ở nhà.
**Grammar & collocations:** `unequal domestic labor norm` — norm; `challenge a domestic labor norm` — challenge.
**Examples:** `A domestic labor norm made meal planning invisible even though it consumed every evening.` → Norm labor domestic làm meal planning invisible dù consume mỗi evening.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `가사 노동 규범`.

## 3. care reciprocity expectation /kɛr ˌrɛsəˈprɑsəti ˌɛkspɛkˈteɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a belief that people who provide care will receive attention, help, or respect when they later need it.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** belief người provide care sẽ receive attention, help hoặc respect khi cần sau.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong care reciprocity expectation` — expectation; `meet care reciprocity expectations` — meet.
**Examples:** `Care reciprocity expectation motivated the daughter to support a parent while balancing her own work.` → Expectation reciprocity care motivate daughter support parent và balance work.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `돌봄 상호성 기대`.

## 4. emotional labor visibility /ɪˈmoʊʃənəl ˈleɪbər ˌvɪzəˈbɪləti/

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `감정 노동 가시성`.

## 5. parenting time scarcity /ˈpɛrəntɪŋ taɪm ˈskɛrsəti/

## 6. sibling fairness perception /ˈsɪblɪŋ ˈfɛrnəs pərˈsɛpʃən/

## Review in context

Close relationships require managing **household coordination cost** and negotiating a **domestic labor norm**. A **care reciprocity expectation** can motivate support, but low **emotional labor visibility** hides who is carrying the relationship. **Parenting time scarcity** makes shared routines valuable, while **sibling fairness perception** shapes reactions to unequal care. **Couple financial transparency** reduces surprise, and **relationship risk perception** influences whether difficult topics are raised. **Partner choice framing** can emphasize status or cooperation; a **trust repair routine** keeps mistakes from becoming identity judgments. A **household bargaining anchor** may preserve an unfair assumption, while **shared goal salience** helps people revise plans. A **conflict cooling norm** and an appropriate **apology timing effect** create room for repair. **Gift reciprocity expectation** and **friendship maintenance friction** shape social exchange, while **social support availability belief** protects against **loneliness risk perception**. A clear **intimacy privacy boundary** and humane **relationship investment framing** make commitment sustainable rather than invisible.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

Relationship close cần manage **household coordination cost** và negotiate **domestic labor norm**. **Care reciprocity expectation** có thể motivate support, nhưng **emotional labor visibility** thấp che ai đang carry relationship. **Parenting time scarcity** làm routine shared valuable, còn **sibling fairness perception** shape reaction với care unequal. **Couple financial transparency** giảm surprise, và **relationship risk perception** influence việc có raise topic khó không. **Partner choice framing** có thể nhấn status hay cooperation; **trust repair routine** giữ mistake không thành judgment identity. **Household bargaining anchor** có thể giữ assumption unfair, còn **shared goal salience** giúp revise plan. **Conflict cooling norm** và **apology timing effect** phù hợp tạo room repair. **Gift reciprocity expectation** và **friendship maintenance friction** shape social exchange, còn **social support availability belief** bảo vệ khỏi **loneliness risk perception**. **Intimacy privacy boundary** rõ và **relationship investment framing** humane làm commitment sustainable thay vì invisible.
