# C2+ Vocabulary — Values, ethics, and technology governance

This lesson examines how values enter inquiry, how ethical safeguards shape research, and how societies govern uncertain technologies. The network is **value-free ideal → contextual value → epistemic value → pragmatic value → constitutive value → regulative value → inductive policy → risk acceptance threshold → evidential threshold → ethical permissibility → research ethics review → equipoise → clinical uncertainty → consent capacity → incidental result → data minimization principle → benefit-sharing → dual-use concern → responsible innovation → anticipatory governance → technology assessment → social license → public justification → value conflict**.

## 1. value-free ideal /ˈvælju friː aɪˈdiəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the aspiration that scientific inquiry can be conducted without ethical, social, or political values influencing its choices.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** value-free ideal là aspiration mạnh; cần phân biệt nó với thực tế value có thể vào qua question, threshold và application.
**Grammar & collocations:** `value-free-ideal debate` — debate; `aspire to value-free inquiry` — aspire; `value-laden choice` — choice.
**Examples:** `The committee questioned whether the value-free ideal could survive choices about which harms to measure.` → Committee hỏi value-free ideal có survive choice về harm cần đo không.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `가치 중립 이상` — value-free ideal.

## 2. contextual value /kənˈtɛkstʃuəl ˈvæljuː/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a value whose relevance depends on the social, historical, practical, or institutional context of inquiry.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** contextual value đổi theo context, như urgency hoặc fairness trong một decision cụ thể.
**Grammar & collocations:** `contextual-value judgment` — judgment; `identify contextual values` — identify; `context-sensitive choice` — choice.
**Examples:** `The contextual value of rapid reporting increased during the outbreak.` → Contextual value của rapid reporting tăng trong outbreak.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `맥락적 가치` — contextual value.

## 3. epistemic value /ɪˌpɪstəmɪk ˈvæljuː/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a value that supports the production, assessment, or communication of knowledge, such as accuracy, coherence, or explanatory power.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** epistemic value giúp knowledge tốt hơn: accurate, coherent, explanatory và open to checking.
**Grammar & collocations:** `epistemic-value criterion` — criterion; `value accuracy` — value; `explanatory virtue` — virtue.
**Examples:** `Replication has epistemic value because it tests whether an effect survives new data.` → Replication có epistemic value vì test effect có survive data mới không.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `인식론적 가치` — epistemic value.

## 4. pragmatic value /præɡˈmætɪk ˈvæljuː/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a value based on practical usefulness, problem-solving capacity, or consequences for action.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** pragmatic value hỏi knowledge/model giúp làm gì, quyết định gì, giảm harm nào.
**Grammar & collocations:** `pragmatic-value assessment` — assessment; `serve a practical purpose` — serve; `action-guiding value` — value.
**Examples:** `The simple forecast retained pragmatic value even after a more realistic model was published.` → Simple forecast vẫn có pragmatic value dù model realistic hơn đã publish.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `실용적 가치` — pragmatic value.

## 5. constitutive value /ˈkɑnstəˌtjuːtɪv ˈvæljuː/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a value that helps define what counts as a legitimate question, object, method, or outcome in an inquiry.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** constitutive value tham gia “định hình” field: outcome nào được coi là relevant, harm nào được tính.
**Grammar & collocations:** `constitutive-value assumption` — assumption; `constitute a research object` — constitute; `define relevance` — define.
**Examples:** `A constitutive value of fairness shaped which groups the sampling plan had to include.` → Constitutive value của fairness định hình group phải include.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `구성적 가치` — constitutive value.

## 6. regulative value /ˈrɛɡjəˌleɪtɪv ˈvæljuː/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a value that guides inquiry and decision-making without fully determining the result.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** regulative value làm compass cho method/decision, nhưng không tự tạo ra evidence.
**Grammar & collocations:** `regulative-value principle` — principle; `guide inquiry` — guide; `regulate a choice` — regulate.
**Examples:** `Transparency served as a regulative value throughout the evidence review.` → Transparency là regulative value trong evidence review.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `규제적 가치` — regulative value.

## 7. inductive policy /ɪnˈdʌktɪv ˈpɑləsi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a policy governing how much evidence, uncertainty, and error risk are acceptable when drawing or acting on empirical generalizations.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** inductive policy quy định threshold và error cost khi đi từ evidence chưa chắc sang action.
**Grammar & collocations:** `inductive-policy choice` — choice; `set an evidential threshold` — set; `tolerate a false positive` — tolerate.
**Examples:** `The regulator adopted a conservative inductive policy because missed hazards would be costly.` → Regulator adopt inductive policy conservative vì bỏ sót hazard cost cao.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `귀납 정책` — inductive policy.

## 8. risk acceptance threshold /rɪsk əkˈsɛptəns ˈθrɛʃhoʊld/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the level of estimated risk below which an organization is willing to proceed under stated conditions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** risk acceptance threshold là line quyết định “rủi ro đủ thấp để proceed” theo context và safeguard.
**Grammar & collocations:** `risk-acceptance threshold` — threshold; `set a threshold` — set; `acceptable residual risk` — risk.
**Examples:** `The hospital lowered its risk acceptance threshold for the vulnerable ward.` → Hospital hạ risk acceptance threshold cho vulnerable ward.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `위험 수용 임계값` — risk acceptance threshold.

## 9. evidential threshold /ˌɛvɪˈdɛnʃəl ˈθrɛʃhoʊld/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the amount or quality of evidence required before accepting a claim, changing a policy, or taking an action.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** evidential threshold đổi theo consequence, reversibility và burden of error.
**Grammar & collocations:** `evidential-threshold rule` — rule; `meet a threshold` — meet; `sufficient evidence` — evidence.
**Examples:** `The agency set a higher evidential threshold for an irreversible intervention.` → Agency đặt evidential threshold cao hơn cho intervention irreversible.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `증거 임계값` — evidential threshold.

## 10. ethical permissibility /ˈɛθɪkəl pərˌmɪsəˈbɪləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the status of being morally allowed under relevant principles, duties, rights, and protections.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** ethical permissibility hỏi action có được phép về mặt moral không, không chỉ feasible hay legal.
**Grammar & collocations:** `ethical-permissibility assessment` — assessment; `judge permissibility` — judge; `moral constraint` — constraint.
**Examples:** `Technical feasibility did not settle the ethical permissibility of the experiment.` → Technical feasibility không quyết định ethical permissibility của experiment.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `윤리적 허용 가능성` — ethical permissibility.

## 11. research ethics review /rɪˈsɜrtʃ ˈɛθɪks rɪˈvjuː/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** formal evaluation of a study's risks, consent, privacy, fairness, and participant protections before or during research.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** research ethics review kiểm tra risk, consent, privacy, fairness và protection.
**Grammar & collocations:** `research-ethics-review board` — board; `submit a protocol` — submit; `participant protection` — protection.
**Examples:** `Research ethics review required a clearer plan for handling distress during interviews.` → Review yêu cầu plan rõ hơn để xử lý distress.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `연구 윤리 심사` — research ethics review.

## 12. equipoise /ɪˈkwɪpɔɪz/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a state of genuine uncertainty within the expert community about which of two or more interventions is better.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** equipoise là uncertainty thật sự giữa treatment option, tạo ethical basis cho trial.
**Grammar & collocations:** `clinical equipoise` — equipoise; `maintain equipoise` — maintain; `genuine uncertainty` — uncertainty.
**Examples:** `The trial was ethically defensible while clinical equipoise remained.` → Trial defendable khi clinical equipoise còn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `임상적 균형` — equipoise.

## 13. clinical uncertainty /ˈklɪnɪkəl ʌnˈsɜrtənti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** unresolved uncertainty about diagnosis, prognosis, treatment effect, or patient response in clinical practice.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** clinical uncertainty chỉ rõ điều chưa biết trong care, không đồng nghĩa với careless practice.
**Grammar & collocations:** `clinical-uncertainty assessment` — assessment; `resolve uncertainty` — resolve; `treatment uncertainty` — uncertainty.
**Examples:** `The guideline marked the remaining clinical uncertainty around long-term side effects.` → Guideline đánh dấu clinical uncertainty về long-term side effect.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `임상적 불확실성` — clinical uncertainty.

## 14. consent capacity /kənˈsɛnt kəˈpæsəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the ability to understand relevant information, appreciate consequences, reason about options, and communicate a voluntary choice.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** consent capacity không chỉ là ký form; là hiểu, cân nhắc consequence và communicate choice.
**Grammar & collocations:** `assess consent capacity` — assess; `capacity assessment` — assessment; `voluntary decision` — decision.
**Examples:** `The investigator used a short comprehension check to assess consent capacity.` → Investigator dùng comprehension check để assess consent capacity.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `동의 능력` — consent capacity.

## 15. incidental result /ˌɪnsəˈdɛntl rɪˈzʌlt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an unexpected finding discovered during a study that may have significance beyond the original research question.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** incidental result là finding ngoài question ban đầu nhưng có thể quan trọng cho participant hoặc science.
**Grammar & collocations:** `incidental-result policy` — policy; `disclose an unexpected finding` — disclose; `clinically significant result` — result.
**Examples:** `The imaging protocol specified when an incidental result would be returned to the participant.` → Imaging protocol quy định khi nào incidental result được trả.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `우연한 결과` — incidental result.

## 16. data minimization principle /ˈdeɪtə ˌmɪnəməˈzeɪʃən ˈprɪnsəpəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the principle that data collection and retention should be limited to what is necessary for a legitimate purpose.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** data minimization principle giảm collect/retain data không cần, bảo vệ privacy và limit misuse.
**Grammar & collocations:** `data-minimization principle` — principle; `collect only necessary data` — collect; `limit retention` — limit.
**Examples:** `The survey followed the data minimization principle by omitting exact birth dates.` → Survey theo data minimization principle bằng cách bỏ exact birth date.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `데이터 최소화 원칙` — data minimization principle.

## 17. benefit-sharing /ˈbɛnəfɪt ˌʃɛrɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the fair distribution of benefits arising from research, biological resources, data, or technological development.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** benefit-sharing hỏi ai đóng góp resource/knowledge thì ai nhận benefit và theo điều kiện nào.
**Grammar & collocations:** `benefit-sharing agreement` — agreement; `share research benefits` — share; `equitable distribution` — distribution.
**Examples:** `The community requested benefit-sharing before granting access to the traditional seed collection.` → Community yêu cầu benefit-sharing trước khi cho access seed collection.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `이익 공유` — benefit-sharing.

## 18. dual-use concern /ˈduːəl juːs kənˈsɜrn/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** concern that knowledge, materials, or tools developed for beneficial purposes could also enable harm.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** dual-use concern xuất hiện khi same tool có thể dùng tốt hoặc gây harm.
**Grammar & collocations:** `dual-use concern in biotechnology` — concern; `mitigate misuse` — mitigate; `beneficial application` — application.
**Examples:** `The review treated the gene-editing protocol as a dual-use concern and restricted certain details.` → Review xem protocol là dual-use concern và restrict detail.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `이중 용도 우려` — dual-use concern.

## 19. responsible innovation /rɪˈspɑnsəbəl ˌɪnəˈveɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** innovation pursued with attention to social desirability, ethical acceptability, sustainability, and the ability to respond to concerns.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** responsible innovation đưa ethics, sustainability và stakeholder concern vào ngay design, không đợi hậu quả.
**Grammar & collocations:** `responsible-innovation framework` — framework; `anticipate impacts` — anticipate; `respond to concern` — respond.
**Examples:** `Responsible innovation required testing accessibility before scaling the platform.` → Responsible innovation yêu cầu test accessibility trước scale platform.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `책임 있는 혁신` — responsible innovation.

## 20. anticipatory governance /ænˌtɪsəˈpətɔri ˈɡʌvərnəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** governance that explores possible futures and steers emerging technologies before their impacts become fixed.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** anticipatory governance nghĩ trước scenario, risk và rule khi technology còn đang shape.
**Grammar & collocations:** `anticipatory-governance exercise` — exercise; `anticipate emerging impacts` — anticipate; `steer development` — steer.
**Examples:** `Anticipatory governance brought affected workers into the design of the automation policy.` → Anticipatory governance đưa affected worker vào design automation policy.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `선제적 거버넌스` — anticipatory governance.

## 21. technology assessment /tɛkˈnɑlədʒi əˈsɛsmənt/

**Core meaning (English):** systematic evaluation of a technology's capabilities, risks, social effects, distributional consequences, and alternatives.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** technology assessment nhìn capability, risk, social effect, distribution và alternative cùng lúc.

## 22. social license /ˈsoʊʃəl ˈlaɪsəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** continuing public acceptance and trust that allow an organization or technology to operate beyond formal legal permission.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** social license là sự chấp nhận xã hội, khác với legal license và có thể mất khi trust giảm.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `사회적 허가` — social license.

## 23. public justification /ˈpʌblɪk ˌdʒʌstəfəˈkeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a reason for a policy or action that can be explained and defended to the people affected by it.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** public justification đòi lý do có thể giải thích và defend trước người bị tác động.
**Grammar & collocations:** `public-justification requirement` — requirement; `justify a policy publicly` — justify; `share reasons` — share.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `공적 정당화` — public justification.

## 24. value conflict /ˈvælju ˈkɑnflɪkt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a situation in which two or more legitimate values support incompatible choices or priorities.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** value conflict không phải một bên chắc chắn sai; là trade-off giữa values như privacy, safety, speed và fairness.
**Grammar & collocations:** `value-conflict analysis` — analysis; `resolve a conflict` — resolve; `competing values` — values.
**Examples:** `The policy revealed a value conflict between rapid access and strict privacy protection.` → Policy lộ value conflict giữa rapid access và privacy protection.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `가치 충돌` — value conflict.

## Review in context

The **value-free ideal** is challenged by **contextual value**, **epistemic value**, and **pragmatic value**. **Constitutive value** helps define a research object, while **regulative value** guides inquiry. An **inductive policy** sets a **risk acceptance threshold** and an **evidential threshold**. **Ethical permissibility** is examined through **research ethics review**; **equipoise** and **clinical uncertainty** can justify a trial. Researchers assess **consent capacity** and plan how to handle an **incidental result** under a **data minimization principle**. **Benefit-sharing** addresses fairness, while a **dual-use concern** asks how helpful knowledge could be misused. **Responsible innovation** and **anticipatory governance** shape a **technology assessment** before impacts become fixed. A technology also needs a **social license**, a defensible **public justification**, and an honest response to **value conflict**.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

**Value-free ideal** bị thách thức bởi **contextual value**, **epistemic value** và **pragmatic value**. **Constitutive value** giúp định nghĩa research object, còn **regulative value** hướng dẫn inquiry. **Inductive policy** đặt **risk acceptance threshold** và **evidential threshold**. **Ethical permissibility** được xem xét qua **research ethics review**; **equipoise** và **clinical uncertainty** có thể biện minh cho trial. Researcher đánh giá **consent capacity** và plan xử lý **incidental result** theo **data minimization principle**. **Benefit-sharing** xử lý fairness, còn **dual-use concern** hỏi knowledge hữu ích có thể bị misuse ra sao. **Responsible innovation** và **anticipatory governance** định hình **technology assessment** trước khi impact cố định. Technology cũng cần **social license**, **public justification** có thể defend và phản hồi trung thực với **value conflict**.
