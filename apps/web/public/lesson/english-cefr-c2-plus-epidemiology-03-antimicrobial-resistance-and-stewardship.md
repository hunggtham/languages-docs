# C2+ Vocabulary — Antimicrobial resistance and stewardship

This lesson follows how resistance emerges, is measured, and is managed through prescribing, laboratory interpretation, and infection prevention. The network is **antimicrobial stewardship → minimum inhibitory concentration breakpoint → broth microdilution → phenotypic resistance → genotypic resistance → mobile resistance element → gene transfer event → plasmid-mediated resistance → hospital colonization burden → ecological antibiotic pressure → drug-resistant pathogen → extensively drug-resistant → pan-drug-resistant → carbapenemase → extended-spectrum beta-lactamase → therapy de-escalation decision → empiric regimen → facility antibiogram → antibiotic timeout review → infection prevention bundle design → contact precaution → screening culture → decolonization protocol → stewardship metric → antimicrobial consumption**.

## 1. antimicrobial stewardship /ˌæntɪmaɪˈkroʊbiəl ˈstjuərdʃɪp/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** coordinated action to use antimicrobial medicines effectively, safely, and only when their likely benefits justify their harms.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** antimicrobial stewardship cân benefit của treatment với resistance, toxicity và unnecessary exposure.
**Grammar & collocations:** `antimicrobial-stewardship program` — program; `promote stewardship` — promote; `appropriate prescribing` — prescribing.
**Examples:** `Antimicrobial stewardship paired prescribing review with rapid diagnostic support.` → Stewardship cần data và workflow.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `항생제 관리` — antimicrobial stewardship.

## 2. minimum inhibitory concentration breakpoint /ˈmɪnəməm ɪnˈhɪbətɔri ˌkɑnsənˈtreɪʃən ˈbreɪkˌpɔɪnt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a concentration threshold used to interpret whether an organism is susceptible, intermediate, or resistant to a drug.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** minimum inhibitory concentration breakpoint biến lab measurement thành clinical category.
**Grammar & collocations:** `minimum-inhibitory-concentration breakpoint` — breakpoint; `interpret a susceptibility result` — interpret; `clinical threshold` — threshold.
**Examples:** `The laboratory updated the minimum inhibitory concentration breakpoint after a standards revision.` → Breakpoint có thể đổi theo evidence.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `최소억제농도 기준점` — minimum inhibitory concentration breakpoint.

## 3. broth microdilution /brɔθ ˌmaɪkroʊdaɪˈluːʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a laboratory method that tests serial drug concentrations in broth to determine antimicrobial susceptibility.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** broth microdilution tạo dãy concentration để xem growth bị inhibit ở mức nào.
**Grammar & collocations:** `broth-microdilution panel` — panel; `perform broth microdilution` — perform; `susceptibility testing` — testing.
**Examples:** `Broth microdilution confirmed the unusual resistance pattern seen in the screening assay.` → Method confirm pattern đáng ngờ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `액체배지 미량희석법` — broth microdilution.

## 4. phenotypic resistance /ˌfiːnoʊˈtɪpɪk rɪˈzɪstəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** observed failure of an organism to respond to a drug in a susceptibility or treatment test.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** phenotypic resistance là behavior thực tế của organism khi gặp drug.
**Grammar & collocations:** `phenotypic-resistance profile` — profile; `observe phenotypic resistance` — observe; `drug-response phenotype` — phenotype.
**Examples:** `Phenotypic resistance prompted confirmatory testing before the result was released.` → Phenotype cần confirm khi bất thường.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `표현형 내성` — phenotypic resistance.

## 5. genotypic resistance /ˌdʒiːnoʊˈtɪpɪk rɪˈzɪstəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** resistance inferred from genetic variants known to affect antimicrobial action or uptake.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** genotypic resistance đọc mutation hoặc gene để dự đoán drug response.
**Grammar & collocations:** `genotypic-resistance marker` — marker; `detect a resistance gene` — detect; `genetic prediction` — prediction.
**Examples:** `Genotypic resistance was detected before a full phenotypic panel became available.` → Gene signal có thể đến sớm hơn phenotype.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `유전형 내성` — genotypic resistance.

## 6. mobile resistance element /ˈmoʊbəl rɪˈzɪstəns ˈɛləmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a transferable genetic structure that carries one or more antimicrobial-resistance determinants between cells.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mobile resistance element là “gói gene” có thể đi giữa bacterial cells.
**Grammar & collocations:** `mobile-resistance element` — element; `carry a resistance determinant` — carry; `transferable DNA` — DNA.
**Examples:** `The mobile resistance element carried several genes on a single transferable structure.` → Một element có thể mang nhiều resistance gene.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `이동성 내성 유전 요소` — mobile resistance element.

## 7. gene transfer event /dʒiːn ˈtrænsfər ɪˈvɛnt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an episode in which genetic material moves between organisms or cells and changes the recipient's traits.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** gene transfer event là một lần DNA đổi chủ và có thể đổi resistance profile.
**Grammar & collocations:** `gene-transfer event` — event; `infer horizontal transfer` — infer; `recipient cell` — cell.
**Examples:** `The investigation reconstructed a gene transfer event across bacteria sharing the same ward environment.` → Environment tạo cơ hội gene exchange.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `유전자 전달 사건` — gene transfer event.

## 8. plasmid-mediated resistance /ˈplæzmɪd ˈmiːdieɪtɪd rɪˈzɪstəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** resistance caused by genes carried on plasmids that can replicate separately from the bacterial chromosome.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** plasmid-mediated resistance đi trên plasmid, nên có thể lan nhanh giữa bacteria.
**Grammar & collocations:** `plasmid-mediated-resistance gene` — gene; `carry resistance on a plasmid` — carry; `plasmid transfer` — transfer.
**Examples:** `Plasmid-mediated resistance complicated control because the determinant moved across species.` → Plasmid có thể vượt species boundary.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `플라스미드 매개 내성` — plasmid-mediated resistance.

## 9. hospital colonization burden /ˈhɑspɪtəl ˌkɑlənəˈzeɪʃən ˈbɜrdən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the amount of resistant-organism carriage present among patients or staff in a healthcare facility.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** hospital colonization burden là “background reservoir” không nhất thiết gây symptom nhưng tạo risk transmission.
**Grammar & collocations:** `hospital-colonization burden` — burden; `measure carriage prevalence` — measure; `reservoir pressure` — pressure.
**Examples:** `The hospital colonization burden fell after screening and environmental cleaning were strengthened.` → Burden giảm khi nhiều layer cùng làm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `병원 집락화 부담` — hospital colonization burden.

## 10. ecological antibiotic pressure /ˌiːkəˈlɑdʒɪkəl ˌæntɪbaɪˈɑtɪk ˈprɛʃər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the selective pressure that antimicrobial exposure places on microbial communities, favoring resistant organisms.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** ecological antibiotic pressure làm ecosystem vi sinh chọn lọc organism chịu drug.
**Grammar & collocations:** `ecological-antibiotic pressure` — pressure; `reduce selective exposure` — reduce; `microbial community` — community.
**Examples:** `Shortening unnecessary courses reduced ecological antibiotic pressure without compromising treatment.` → Giảm exposure thừa giúp ecosystem.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `생태적 항생제 압력` — ecological antibiotic pressure.

## 11. drug-resistant pathogen /drʌɡ rɪˈzɪstənt ˈpæθədʒən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a disease-causing organism that is not adequately controlled by one or more drugs normally used against it.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** drug-resistant pathogen là pathogen làm treatment options hẹp hơn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `drug-resistant pathogen` — pathogen; `contain a resistant pathogen` — contain; `resistance profile` — profile.
**Examples:** `The reference laboratory confirmed a drug-resistant pathogen in the bloodstream isolate.` → Confirmation giúp chọn therapy đúng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `약제 내성 병원체` — drug-resistant pathogen.

## 12. extensively drug-resistant /ɪkˈstɛnsɪvli drʌɡ rɪˈzɪstənt/
**Part of speech:** adjective
**Core meaning (English):** resistant to most available antimicrobial classes, leaving only a limited number of active treatment options.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** extensively drug-resistant nghĩa là hầu hết class đã mất tác dụng, nhưng có thể còn vài option.
**Grammar & collocations:** `extensively drug-resistant isolate` — isolate; `treat an extensively drug-resistant infection` — treat; `limited options` — options.
**Examples:** `An extensively drug-resistant isolate required specialist review and prolonged monitoring.` → XDR cần expertise và follow-up.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `광범위 약제 내성` — extensively drug-resistant.

## 13. pan-drug-resistant /pæn drʌɡ rɪˈzɪstənt/
**Part of speech:** adjective
**Core meaning (English):** resistant to all or nearly all antimicrobial agents tested or clinically available for the organism.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** pan-drug-resistant là mức cực đoan khi therapeutic options gần như bằng zero.
**Grammar & collocations:** `pan-drug-resistant organism` — organism; `investigate pan-drug resistance` — investigate; `last-resort option` — option.
**Examples:** `The suspected pan-drug-resistant organism was referred to a national reference service.` → Suspected case cần confirm cấp cao.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `범약제 내성` — pan-drug-resistant.

## 14. carbapenemase /ˌkɑrbəˈpɛnəmeɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an enzyme that breaks down carbapenem antibiotics and can confer high-level resistance.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** carbapenemase phá drug mạnh, làm mất một nhóm “last-line” quan trọng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `carbapenemase-producing organism` — organism; `detect carbapenemase` — detect; `enzyme-mediated resistance` — resistance.
**Examples:** `The assay detected carbapenemase and triggered enhanced infection-control precautions.` → Enzyme result đổi ngay control response.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `카바페넴분해효소` — carbapenemase.

## 15. extended-spectrum beta-lactamase /ɪkˈstɛndɪd ˈspɛktrəm ˌbeɪtə lækˈteɪmeɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an enzyme that hydrolyzes many penicillins and cephalosporins, reducing their clinical activity.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** extended-spectrum beta-lactamase mở rộng resistance qua nhiều beta-lactam.
**Grammar & collocations:** `extended-spectrum beta-lactamase producer` — producer; `screen for ESBL` — screen; `beta-lactam resistance` — resistance.
**Examples:** `The culture grew an extended-spectrum beta-lactamase producer, so the regimen was reassessed.` → ESBL result yêu cầu reassessment.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `광범위 베타락타마제` — extended-spectrum beta-lactamase.

## 16. therapy de-escalation decision /ˈθɛrəpi diˌɛskəˈleɪʃən dɪˈsɪʒən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a decision to narrow or simplify antimicrobial treatment once evidence identifies a safer effective option.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** therapy de-escalation decision giảm broad coverage khi pathogen và susceptibility đã rõ.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `치료 단계 축소 결정` — therapy de-escalation decision.

## 17. empiric regimen /ɛmˈpɪrɪk ˈrɛdʒəmən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an initial treatment plan selected before the causative organism and full susceptibility profile are known.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** empiric regimen là treatment dựa trên probability và risk trước khi có full data.
**Grammar & collocations:** `empiric-regimen choice` — choice; `start empiric therapy` — start; `local resistance pattern` — pattern.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `경험적 치료 요법` — empiric regimen.

## Review in context — Part 1

An **antimicrobial stewardship** team interprets a **minimum inhibitory concentration breakpoint** and may confirm it with **broth microdilution**. **Phenotypic resistance** and **genotypic resistance** can provide complementary evidence. A **mobile resistance element** may support a **gene transfer event**, including **plasmid-mediated resistance**. In a hospital, the **hospital colonization burden** and **ecological antibiotic pressure** shape the spread of a **drug-resistant pathogen**. Labels such as **extensively drug-resistant**, **pan-drug-resistant**, **carbapenemase**, and **extended-spectrum beta-lactamase** describe increasingly difficult treatment problems.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 1:**

Nhóm **antimicrobial stewardship** diễn giải **minimum inhibitory concentration breakpoint** và có thể xác nhận bằng **broth microdilution**. **Phenotypic resistance** và **genotypic resistance** cung cấp evidence bổ sung cho nhau. **Mobile resistance element** có thể hỗ trợ một **gene transfer event**, trong đó có **plasmid-mediated resistance**. Trong hospital, **hospital colonization burden** và **ecological antibiotic pressure** định hình sự lan rộng của **drug-resistant pathogen**. Các nhãn **extensively drug-resistant**, **pan-drug-resistant**, **carbapenemase** và **extended-spectrum beta-lactamase** mô tả các vấn đề treatment ngày càng khó.

## Review in context — Part 2

A **therapy de-escalation decision** narrows treatment after an **empiric regimen** is reassessed with the **facility antibiogram** and patient data. An **antibiotic timeout review** can identify unnecessary coverage. **Infection prevention bundle design**, **contact precaution**, **screening culture**, and a **decolonization protocol** reduce opportunities for transmission. A **stewardship metric** shows whether these changes improve practice, while **antimicrobial consumption** reveals the broader exposure pattern.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 2:**

**Therapy de-escalation decision** thu hẹp treatment sau khi **empiric regimen** được đánh giá lại bằng **facility antibiogram** và data của patient. **Antibiotic timeout review** có thể phát hiện coverage không cần thiết. **Infection prevention bundle design**, **contact precaution**, **screening culture** và **decolonization protocol** giảm cơ hội transmission. **Stewardship metric** cho thấy practice có cải thiện không, còn **antimicrobial consumption** cho thấy exposure ở phạm vi rộng hơn.
