# C2+ Vocabulary — Environmental health equity and exposure

This lesson follows how communities understand environmental hazards, measure unequal exposure, and choose protective or remedial action. The network is **contaminant route → cumulative burden → environmental fairness → vulnerability index → hazard map → risk attribution → source apportionment → dose-effect curve → exposure labeling error → sentinel species → biomonitoring → occupational exposure → precautionary zoning → buffer zone → remediation threshold → polluter pays → fair transition → heat inequity → adaptation shortfall → cumulative impact assessment → community consent → participatory monitoring → environmental racism → sacrifice zone → health impact assessment**.

## 1. contaminant route /kənˈtæmənənt ruːt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the physical or biological route by which a hazardous substance travels from its source to people, animals, or ecosystems.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** contaminant route là đường hazard đi từ source tới body/community.
**Grammar & collocations:** `contaminant-route diagram` — diagram; `trace a contaminant route` — trace; `exposure point` — point.
**Examples:** `The investigation traced the contaminant route from the factory drain to household wells.` → Trace từ drain tới well.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `오염물질 경로` — contaminant route.

## 2. cumulative burden /ˈkjuːmjələtɪv ˈbɜrdən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the combined load of multiple exposures, disadvantages, stressors, or harms experienced over time.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cumulative burden là nhiều burden chồng lên nhau, không phải một exposure đơn lẻ.
**Grammar & collocations:** `cumulative-burden estimate` — estimate; `accumulate environmental harms` — accumulate; `multiple stressor` — stressor.
**Examples:** `Cumulative burden was high where traffic pollution, heat, and poor housing occurred together.` → Nhiều stressor cùng một nơi.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `누적 부담` — cumulative burden.

## 3. environmental fairness /ɪnˌvaɪrənˈmɛntl ˈfɛrnəs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** fair distribution of environmental benefits, hazards, decision power, and protection across communities.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** environmental fairness hỏi ai chịu hazard, ai nhận benefit và ai có voice.
**Grammar & collocations:** `environmental-fairness review` — review; `distribute protection fairly` — distribute; `affected community` — community.
**Examples:** `Environmental fairness required the city to prioritize cleanup where residents had carried pollution for decades.` → Cleanup ưu tiên nơi chịu pollution lâu.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `환경 공정성` — environmental fairness.

## 4. vulnerability index /ˌvʌlnərəˈbɪləti ˈɪnˌdɛks/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a combined measure of conditions that make a population more likely to experience harm or less able to respond and recover.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** vulnerability index gom age, income, housing, health và exposure thành risk profile.
**Grammar & collocations:** `vulnerability-index map` — map; `rank community vulnerability` — rank; `coping capacity` — capacity.
**Examples:** `The vulnerability index identified neighborhoods where heat warnings would need door-to-door outreach.` → Index chỉ nơi cần outreach trực tiếp.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `취약성 지수` — vulnerability index.

## 5. hazard map /ˈhæzərd mæp/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a map showing the location, intensity, probability, or spread of an environmental danger.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** hazard map biến risk không gian thành hình để thấy nơi nào nguy hiểm.
**Grammar & collocations:** `hazard-map layer` — layer; `map a hazard` — map; `risk zone` — zone.
**Examples:** `The hazard map overlaid flood depth with locations of nursing homes.` → Map nối hazard với vulnerable facility.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `위험 지도` — hazard map.

## 6. risk attribution /rɪsk ˌætrəˈbjuʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** analysis of how much of an observed health outcome can reasonably be attributed to a particular exposure or source.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** risk attribution hỏi outcome đến từ exposure này bao nhiêu phần.
**Grammar & collocations:** `risk-attribution estimate` — estimate; `attribute an outcome to exposure` — attribute; `causal contribution` — contribution.
**Examples:** `Risk attribution estimated how many asthma admissions were linked to traffic emissions.` → Estimate phần asthma do traffic.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `위험 귀속` — risk attribution.

## 7. source apportionment /sɔrs əˈpɔrʃənmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** estimation of how much of a measured pollutant or hazard comes from each contributing source.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** source apportionment chia pollutant theo từng source.
**Grammar & collocations:** `source-apportionment model` — model; `apportion pollution` — apportion; `emission source` — source.
**Examples:** `Source apportionment showed that freight traffic contributed more particulate matter than household heating.` → Freight traffic đóng góp nhiều hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `오염원 기여도 분석` — source apportionment.

## 8. dose-effect curve /doʊs ɪˈfɛkt kɜrv/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a representation of how the magnitude or probability of an effect changes as exposure dose changes.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** dose-effect curve nối lượng exposure với mức effect.
**Grammar & collocations:** `dose-effect-curve model` — model; `plot exposure against effect` — plot; `response pattern` — pattern.
**Examples:** `The dose-effect curve rose sharply after particulate exposure passed a critical range.` → Effect tăng mạnh sau một range.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `용량-효과 곡선` — dose-effect curve.

## 9. exposure labeling error /ɪkˈspoʊʒər ˈleɪbəlɪŋ ˈɛrər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** incorrect classification of a person's or place's exposure level, timing, source, or status.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** exposure labeling error gắn nhãn exposure sai, làm risk estimate lệch.
**Grammar & collocations:** `exposure-labeling-error audit` — audit; `misclassify exposure` — misclassify; `measurement record` — record.
**Examples:** `Exposure labeling error hid the fact that many workers changed shifts during the monitoring period.` → Label không phản ánh shift change.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `노출 분류 오류` — exposure labeling error.

## 10. sentinel species /ˈsɛntənəl ˈspiːʃiːz/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a species whose health or behavior provides an early signal of environmental change or contamination that may also affect humans.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** sentinel species là “loài canh gác” báo hazard trước khi human signal rõ.
**Grammar & collocations:** `sentinel-species study` — study; `detect environmental change` — detect; `ecological signal` — signal.
**Examples:** `Fish served as a sentinel species for contamination entering the river system.` → Fish báo contamination sớm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `지표종` — sentinel species.

## 11. biomonitoring /ˌbaɪoʊˈmɑnətərɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** measurement of chemicals, metabolites, or biological responses in human or animal tissues to assess exposure.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** biomonitoring đo exposure bên trong body, không chỉ đo ngoài môi trường.
**Grammar & collocations:** `biomonitoring program` — program; `measure a biomarker` — measure; `biological sample` — sample.
**Examples:** `Biomonitoring found elevated lead levels in children living near the recycling site.` → Sample body cho thấy lead cao.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `생체 모니터링` — biomonitoring.

## 12. occupational exposure /ˌɑkjəˈpeɪʃənəl ɪkˈspoʊʒər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** contact with a chemical, biological, physical, or ergonomic hazard as a result of work.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** occupational exposure là exposure gắn với job và workplace.
**Grammar & collocations:** `occupational-exposure limit` — limit; `control workplace exposure` — control; `protective equipment` — equipment.
**Examples:** `Occupational exposure monitoring led the laboratory to improve ventilation and protective equipment.` → Monitor dẫn tới control tốt hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `직업적 노출` — occupational exposure.

## 13. precautionary zoning /prɪˈkɔʃənˌɛri ˈzoʊnɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** land-use planning that separates people or sensitive activities from a plausible hazard before complete evidence of harm is available.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** precautionary zoning dùng khoảng cách/quy hoạch để giảm risk khi evidence chưa đủ.
**Grammar & collocations:** `precautionary-zoning rule` — rule; `separate sensitive land uses` — separate; `protective setback` — setback.
**Examples:** `Precautionary zoning kept new housing away from the uncertain contamination plume.` → Chưa chắc hết contamination nhưng vẫn giữ khoảng cách.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `예방적 용도지역 지정` — precautionary zoning.

## 14. buffer zone /ˈbʌfər zoʊn/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an area maintained between a hazard and people, ecosystems, facilities, or activities to reduce contact or spread.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** buffer zone là vùng đệm giảm contact.
**Grammar & collocations:** `buffer-zone requirement` — requirement; `maintain a buffer zone` — maintain; `protective distance` — distance.
**Examples:** `A buffer zone separated the chemical storage area from the village water intake.` → Vùng đệm bảo vệ water intake.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `완충 지대` — buffer zone.

## 15. remediation threshold /rɪˌmiːdiˈeɪʃən ˈθrɛʃˌhoʊld/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the contamination or risk level at which cleanup, containment, relocation, or another corrective action is required.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** remediation threshold là ngưỡng kích hoạt cleanup/action.
**Grammar & collocations:** `remediation-threshold value` — value; `trigger a cleanup` — trigger; `contamination limit` — limit.
**Examples:** `The soil concentration exceeded the remediation threshold, so the city removed the top layer.` → Vượt ngưỡng nên phải xử lý soil.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `정화 기준치` — remediation threshold.

## 16. polluter pays /pəˈluːtər peɪz/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a principle that assigns the costs of preventing, controlling, and repairing pollution to the party responsible for causing it.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** polluter pays bắt nguồn gây pollution chịu cost xử lý.
**Grammar & collocations:** `polluter-pays principle` — principle; `recover cleanup costs` — recover; `responsible operator` — operator.
**Examples:** `The polluter pays rule funded river restoration without reducing the community's right to compensation.` → Polluter trả cleanup và community vẫn có quyền compensation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `오염자 부담 원칙` — polluter pays.

## 17. fair transition /fɛr trænˈzɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a change toward a safer or lower-carbon economy that shares benefits and protects workers and communities facing disruption.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** fair transition đổi system nhưng không bỏ lại worker/community chịu cost.
**Grammar & collocations:** `fair-transition plan` — plan; `protect affected workers` — protect; `skills retraining` — retraining.
**Examples:** `A fair transition paired coal-plant closure with retraining, income support, and local investment.` → Đóng plant đi cùng support thật.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `공정한 전환` — fair transition.

## 18. heat inequity /hiːt ɪnˈɛkwəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** unequal exposure to dangerous heat and unequal access to cooling, shade, health care, or adaptive resources.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** heat inequity là nóng không đều và khả năng tránh nóng cũng không đều.
**Grammar & collocations:** `heat-inequity map` — map; `reduce heat exposure` — reduce; `cooling access` — access.
**Examples:** `Heat inequity was greatest where tree cover was low and older residents lived alone.` → Ít cây và người già sống một mình làm risk cao.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `열 불평등` — heat inequity.

## 19. adaptation shortfall /ˌædæpˈteɪʃən ˈʃɔrtˌfɔl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the gap between the protective adaptation needed for a hazard and the adaptation actually available or delivered.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** adaptation shortfall là thiếu hụt giữa cần làm và đã làm.
**Grammar & collocations:** `adaptation-shortfall estimate` — estimate; `close an adaptation gap` — close; `protective capacity` — capacity.
**Examples:** `The adaptation shortfall remained large because cooling centers were too far from high-risk blocks.` → Có center nhưng không tới được.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `적응 부족` — adaptation shortfall.

## 20. cumulative impact assessment /ˈkjuːmjələtɪv ˈɪmpækt əˈsɛsmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** evaluation of the combined effects of multiple projects, hazards, or decisions on people and environments over time.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cumulative impact assessment nhìn nhiều project/hazard cùng lúc, không xét từng cái riêng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `cumulative-impact-assessment report` — report; `assess combined effects` — assess; `development pressure` — pressure.
**Examples:** `The cumulative impact assessment considered the port, refinery, highway, and housing expansion together.` → Không tách từng project.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `누적 영향 평가` — cumulative impact assessment.

## 21. community consent /kəˈmjuːnəti kənˈsɛnt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** informed and freely expressed agreement by an affected community to a project, study, intervention, or data use.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** community consent là agreement của cộng đồng sau khi hiểu risk/benefit và có quyền nói không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `community-consent process` — process; `obtain informed consent` — obtain; `affected residents` — residents.
**Examples:** `Community consent was required before researchers installed air monitors on private land.` → Cài monitor cần consent.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `지역사회 동의` — community consent.

## 22. participatory monitoring /pɑrˈtɪsəpəˌtɔri ˈmɑnətərɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** monitoring in which affected residents help decide what to measure, collect observations, interpret results, and act on findings.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** participatory monitoring để community cùng đo và hiểu data, không chỉ bị đo.
**Grammar & collocations:** `participatory-monitoring protocol` — protocol; `collect community observations` — collect; `citizen data` — data.
**Examples:** `Participatory monitoring helped residents document odors that official sensors had missed.` → Resident ghi nhận signal ngoài sensor.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `참여형 모니터링` — participatory monitoring.

## 23. environmental racism /ɪnˌvaɪrənˈmɛntl ˈreɪsɪzəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** unequal environmental hazards or protection that reflect and reinforce racialized power, exclusion, or discrimination.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** environmental racism là phân bổ hazard/protection gắn với race và power structure.
**Grammar & collocations:** `environmental-racism analysis` — analysis; `expose racialized inequality` — expose; `discriminatory siting` — siting.
**Examples:** `The environmental-racism analysis compared decades of industrial siting with neighborhood demographics.` → Phân tích nối siting và demographic.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `환경 인종차별` — environmental racism.

## 24. sacrifice zone /ˈsækrəˌfaɪs zoʊn/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an area treated as acceptable to damage or burden for broader economic, military, or infrastructural goals.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** sacrifice zone là nơi bị coi như có thể hy sinh để nơi khác hưởng lợi.
**Grammar & collocations:** `sacrifice-zone narrative` — narrative; `burden a community` — burden; `industrial sacrifice` — sacrifice.
**Examples:** `Residents rejected the sacrifice-zone narrative and demanded the same clean-air standard as wealthier districts.` → Community từ chối bị hy sinh.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `희생 지역` — sacrifice zone.

## 25. health impact assessment /hɛlθ ˈɪmpækt əˈsɛsmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a systematic assessment of how a proposed policy, project, or decision may affect population health and health equity.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** health impact assessment đưa health và equity vào decision trước khi project chạy.
**Grammar & collocations:** `health-impact-assessment report` — report; `assess population-health effects` — assess; `policy proposal` — proposal.
**Examples:** `The health impact assessment predicted respiratory and mental-health effects before the highway expansion was approved.` → Assessment đi trước approval.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `건강 영향 평가` — health impact assessment.

## Review in context — Part 1

A **contaminant route** can create **cumulative burden**, so **environmental fairness** requires more than a single average exposure. A **vulnerability index** and **hazard map** show who is at risk; **risk attribution** and **source apportionment** estimate where the risk comes from. A **dose-effect curve** can be distorted by an **exposure labeling error**. **Sentinel species**, **biomonitoring**, and **occupational exposure** data provide different views of harm. **Precautionary zoning**, a **buffer zone**, and a **remediation threshold** turn evidence into protection.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 1:**

**Contaminant route** có thể tạo **cumulative burden**, nên **environmental fairness** cần nhìn hơn một exposure average. **Vulnerability index** và **hazard map** cho thấy ai đang risk; **risk attribution** và **source apportionment** ước tính risk đến từ đâu. **Dose-effect curve** có thể bị méo bởi **exposure labeling error**. **Sentinel species**, **biomonitoring** và data về **occupational exposure** cho các góc nhìn khác nhau về harm. **Precautionary zoning**, **buffer zone** và **remediation threshold** biến evidence thành protection.

## Review in context — Part 2

The **polluter pays** principle assigns cleanup responsibility, while a **fair transition** protects people affected by economic change. **Heat inequity** reveals unequal exposure and an **adaptation shortfall** reveals missing protection. A **cumulative impact assessment** combines projects and hazards; **community consent** and **participatory monitoring** give residents decision power. **Environmental racism** describes racialized unequal burden, and a **sacrifice zone** describes the logic that normalizes it. A **health impact assessment** brings these consequences into policy before approval.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 2:**

Nguyên tắc **polluter pays** giao responsibility cleanup cho bên gây pollution, còn **fair transition** bảo vệ người chịu disruption kinh tế. **Heat inequity** cho thấy exposure không đều và **adaptation shortfall** cho thấy protection còn thiếu. **Cumulative impact assessment** gộp nhiều project/hazard; **community consent** và **participatory monitoring** cho resident quyền tham gia. **Environmental racism** mô tả burden bất bình đẳng gắn với race, còn **sacrifice zone** mô tả logic coi một nơi có thể bị hy sinh. **Health impact assessment** đưa các consequence này vào policy trước approval.
