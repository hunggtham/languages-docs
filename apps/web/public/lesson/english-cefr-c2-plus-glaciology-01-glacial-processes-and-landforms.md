# C2+ Vocabulary — Glacial processes and landforms

This lesson follows a glacier from snow accumulation and internal deformation through meltwater, erosion, deposition, and postglacial adjustment. The network is **firn layer → névé → ablation zone → accumulation zone → bergschrund → crevasse field → moulin glaciology → nunatak landscape → roche moutonnée → drumlin → esker → kame → glacial till → varve geology → isostatic rebound → glacial erratic → arête glaciology → cirque glaciology → horn glaciology → solifluction**.

## 1. firn layer /fɜːrn ˈleɪər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a compacted layer of old snow that is denser than fresh snow but has not yet become glacier ice.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** layer snow cũ compact, dense hơn fresh snow nhưng chưa thành glacier ice.
**Grammar & collocations:** `thick firn layer` — layer dày; `measure a firn layer` — measure.
**Examples:** `A thick firn layer insulated the glacier from short-term temperature changes.` → Firn layer dày insulate glacier khỏi temperature change ngắn hạn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `빙설층`.

## 2. névé /neɪˈveɪ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** granular, partially compacted snow that persists through at least one summer.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** snow dạng grain, compact một phần và survive ít nhất một summer.
**Grammar & collocations:** `persistent névé` — névé tồn tại; `cross a névé` — cross.
**Examples:** `The climbers crossed persistent névé before reaching the icefall.` → Climbers cross névé tồn tại trước khi tới icefall.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `만년설`.

## 3. ablation zone /əˈbleɪʃən zoʊn/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the lower part of a glacier where ice loss from melting, sublimation, or calving exceeds snow gain.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** phần lower glacier nơi ice loss vượt snow gain.
**Grammar & collocations:** `expanding ablation zone` — zone expand; `map the ablation zone` — map.
**Examples:** `The expanding ablation zone exposed dark rock at the glacier's edge.` → Ablation zone expand expose dark rock ở edge glacier.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `삭마대`.

## 4. accumulation zone /əˌkjuːmjəˈleɪʃən zoʊn/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the part of a glacier where snowfall and other gains exceed ice loss.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** phần glacier nơi snowfall/gain vượt ice loss.
**Grammar & collocations:** `upper accumulation zone` — zone upper; `survey the accumulation zone` — survey.
**Examples:** `The upper accumulation zone retained snow through the warm summer.` → Accumulation zone upper giữ snow qua summer ấm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `집적대`.

## 5. bergschrund /ˈbɜːrɡʃrʊnd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a deep crevasse separating moving glacier ice from the stagnant ice or rock at its head.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** crevasse sâu tách moving ice khỏi stagnant ice/rock ở head glacier.
**Grammar & collocations:** `open bergschrund` — bergschrund mở; `cross a bergschrund` — cross.
**Examples:** `The team crossed the open bergschrund before dawn.` → Team cross bergschrund mở trước dawn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `빙하 상단 균열`.

## 6. crevasse field /krəˈvæs fiːld/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a broad area of glacier ice broken by numerous deep cracks.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** area rộng glacier ice bị break bởi nhiều crack sâu.
**Grammar & collocations:** `unstable crevasse field` — field không ổn định; `cross a crevasse field` — cross.
**Examples:** `The route avoided the unstable crevasse field on the steep slope.` → Route tránh crevasse field không ổn định trên slope dốc.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `빙하 균열 지대`.

## 7. moulin glaciology /muːˈlɑːn ˌɡleɪsiˈɑːlədʒi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the study of a vertical shaft or conduit through glacier ice that carries surface meltwater downward.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** study shaft/conduit vertical qua ice đưa meltwater surface xuống.
**Grammar & collocations:** `deep moulin glaciology` — moulin sâu; `map a moulin` — map.
**Examples:** `Moulin glaciology tracked meltwater into the glacier's interior.` → Moulin glaciology track meltwater vào interior glacier.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `빙하 수직 수로 연구`.

## 8. nunatak landscape /ˈnuːnətæk ˈlændskeɪp/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a landscape containing an isolated mountain peak or ridge projecting above an ice sheet.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** landscape có peak/ridge mountain cô lập nhô trên ice sheet.
**Grammar & collocations:** `exposed nunatak landscape` — landscape lộ; `photograph a nunatak` — photograph.
**Examples:** `The exposed nunatak landscape supported plants absent from the surrounding ice.` → Nunatak landscape lộ support plant không có trong ice quanh.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `빙원 돌출 산지`.

## 9. roche moutonnée /rɑːʃ muːtəˈneɪ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a streamlined bedrock hill with a gentle stoss side and a steep lee side shaped by moving ice.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** bedrock hill streamline, stoss side gentle và lee side steep do ice move shape.
**Grammar & collocations:** `smoothed roche moutonnée` — roche moutonnée smooth; `identify a roche moutonnée` — identify.
**Examples:** `The smoothed roche moutonnée recorded the former ice-flow direction.` → Roche moutonnée smooth ghi hướng ice-flow cũ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `빙하마모 암구`.

## 10. drumlin /ˈdrʌmlɪn/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an elongated hill of glacial sediment aligned with the direction of ice movement.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** hill dài bằng glacial sediment align theo hướng ice movement.
**Grammar & collocations:** `parallel drumlin` — drumlin song song; `map a drumlin field` — map field.
**Examples:** `The parallel drumlin field revealed the glacier's former flow.` → Drumlin field song song reveal flow cũ của glacier.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `드럼린`.

## 11. esker /ˈɛskər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a winding ridge of sand and gravel deposited by a stream flowing within or beneath a glacier.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** ridge uốn lượn sand/gravel deposit bởi stream trong/dưới glacier.
**Grammar & collocations:** `sinuous esker` — esker uốn; `trace an esker` — trace.
**Examples:** `The sinuous esker marked an ancient subglacial drainage route.` → Esker uốn đánh dấu drainage route dưới glacier cổ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `에스커`.

## 12. kame /keɪm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a mound or irregular hill of sand and gravel deposited by meltwater near a melting glacier.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mound/hill không đều sand/gravel deposit bởi meltwater gần glacier tan.
**Grammar & collocations:** `isolated kame` — kame cô lập; `survey a kame` — survey.
**Examples:** `An isolated kame rose above the outwash plain.` → Kame cô lập rise trên outwash plain.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `케임`.

## 13. glacial till /ˈɡleɪʃəl tɪl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** unsorted sediment deposited directly by glacier ice.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** sediment không sort deposit trực tiếp bởi glacier ice.
**Grammar & collocations:** `compact glacial till` — till compact; `sample glacial till` — sample.
**Examples:** `The foundation rested on compact glacial till rather than bedrock.` → Foundation nằm trên glacial till compact thay vì bedrock.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `빙하 퇴적물`.

## 14. varve geology /vɑːrv dʒiˈɑːlədʒi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the study of paired sediment layers deposited in an annual cycle, often in glacial lakes.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** study layer sediment đôi deposit theo annual cycle, thường trong glacial lake.
**Grammar & collocations:** `lacustrine varve geology` — geology varve lake; `count varves` — count.
**Examples:** `Varve geology provided a year-by-year record of glacier retreat.` → Varve geology cung cấp record glacier retreat từng năm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `연층 지질학`.

## 15. isostatic rebound /ˌaɪsəˈstætɪk rɪˈbaʊnd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the gradual uplift of land after the removal of a heavy ice sheet.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** land uplift dần sau khi ice sheet nặng remove.
**Grammar & collocations:** `postglacial isostatic rebound` — rebound postglacial; `measure isostatic rebound` — measure.
**Examples:** `Postglacial isostatic rebound raised the former shoreline above sea level.` → Isostatic rebound postglacial nâng former shoreline trên sea level.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `빙하성 지각 반발`.

## 16. glacial erratic /ˈɡleɪʃəl ɪˈrætɪk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a large rock transported and deposited far from its original geological source by glacier ice.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** rock lớn transport/deposit xa source geology bởi glacier ice.
**Grammar & collocations:** `granite glacial erratic` — erratic granite; `trace a glacial erratic` — trace.
**Examples:** `The granite glacial erratic came from a mountain range far to the north.` → Glacial erratic granite đến từ mountain range xa về north.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `빙하 표석`.

## 17. arête glaciology /əˈrɛt ˌɡleɪsiˈɑːlədʒi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the study of a sharp ridge carved between two glacial valleys.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** study ridge sắc carve giữa hai glacial valley.
**Grammar & collocations:** `knife-edge arête glaciology` — arête sharp; `survey an arête` — survey.
**Examples:** `Arête glaciology explained the sharp ridge between the two cirques.` → Arête glaciology giải thích ridge sắc giữa hai cirque.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `빙하 칼날능선 연구`.

## 18. cirque glaciology /sɜːrk ˌɡleɪsiˈɑːlədʒi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the study of a bowl-shaped hollow eroded by glacier ice near a mountain headwall.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** study hollow hình bowl erode bởi glacier gần mountain headwall.
**Grammar & collocations:** `amphitheater cirque glaciology` — cirque bowl; `map a cirque` — map.
**Examples:** `Cirque glaciology linked the amphitheater hollow to an older glacier.` → Cirque glaciology link hollow bowl với glacier cũ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `빙하권곡 연구`.

## 19. horn glaciology /hɔːrn ˌɡleɪsiˈɑːlədʒi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the study of a sharp pyramidal peak carved by several glaciers eroding a mountain.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** study peak pyramid sharp carve bởi nhiều glacier erode mountain.
**Grammar & collocations:** `pyramidal horn glaciology` — horn pyramid; `interpret a horn` — interpret.
**Examples:** `Pyramidal horn glaciology revealed erosion from three separate cirques.` → Horn glaciology pyramid reveal erosion từ ba cirque.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `빙하 첨봉 연구`.

## 20. solifluction /ˌsɑːlɪˈflʌkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the slow downslope movement of waterlogged soil, especially in cold regions with frozen ground.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** soil waterlogged move chậm xuống slope, nhất là vùng lạnh có frozen ground.
**Grammar & collocations:** `seasonal solifluction` — solifluction theo mùa; `monitor solifluction` — monitor.
**Examples:** `Seasonal solifluction distorted the road across the tundra slope.` → Solifluction theo mùa làm distort road trên tundra slope.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `토양 포행`.

## Review in context

A glacier develops a **firn layer**, **névé**, an **ablation zone**, and an **accumulation zone**; a **bergschrund** and **crevasse field** expose hazards, while a **moulin glaciology** survey tracks meltwater. An exposed **nunatak landscape**, **roche moutonnée**, **drumlin**, **esker**, **kame**, and **glacial till** record ice movement and deposition. **Varve geology**, **isostatic rebound**, and a **glacial erratic** extend the history after retreat. **Arête glaciology**, **cirque glaciology**, **horn glaciology**, and **solifluction** describe mountain erosion and cold-ground movement.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:** Glacier phát triển **firn layer**, **névé**, **ablation zone** và **accumulation zone**; **bergschrund** và **crevasse field** tạo hazard, còn survey **moulin glaciology** theo dõi meltwater. **Nunatak landscape** lộ, **roche moutonnée**, **drumlin**, **esker**, **kame** và **glacial till** ghi lại ice movement/deposition. **Varve geology**, **isostatic rebound** và **glacial erratic** mở rộng history sau retreat. **Arête glaciology**, **cirque glaciology**, **horn glaciology** và **solifluction** mô tả mountain erosion và cold-ground movement.
