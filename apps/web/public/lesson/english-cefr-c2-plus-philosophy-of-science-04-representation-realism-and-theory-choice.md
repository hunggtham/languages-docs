# C2+ Vocabulary — Representation, realism, and theory choice

This lesson examines how scientific models represent targets, how theories gain credibility, and why rival frameworks can remain in play. The network is **representational adequacy → semantic view of theories → syntactic view of theories → model-theoretic interpretation → scientific representation → target system → source system → analogical model → scale model → mechanistic idealization → abstraction level → explanatory depth → causal relevance → intervention invariance → causal completeness → causal isolation → confounding structure → evidence underdetermination → theory equivalence → semantic incommensurability → scientific pluralism → epistemic virtue → consilience of evidence → predictive success → theory-mediated observation**.

## 1. representational adequacy /ˌrɛprɪzɛnˈteɪʃənəl ˈædəkwəsi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the degree to which a representation captures the features of a target that matter for a specified purpose.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** representation không cần giống target mọi mặt; nó phải đủ đúng ở feature relevant cho task.
**Grammar & collocations:** `representational-adequacy criterion` — criterion; `assess adequacy` — assess; `adequate representation` — representation.
**Examples:** `The simulation had representational adequacy for flood timing but not for household evacuation behavior.` → Simulation có representational adequacy cho flood timing nhưng không cho household evacuation behavior.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `표상적 적합성` — representational adequacy.

## 2. semantic view of theories /sɪˈmæntɪk vjuː əv ˈθɪəriz/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an account that treats theories primarily as families of models or structures that satisfy certain conditions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** semantic view xem theory qua model và structure mà nó cho phép, không chỉ qua danh sách câu lệnh.
**Grammar & collocations:** `semantic-view account` — account; `interpret a theory semantically` — interpret; `family of models` — family.
**Examples:** `The semantic view of theories compared model structures rather than only textbook axioms.` → Semantic view of theories so sánh model structure thay vì chỉ textbook axiom.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `이론의 의미론적 관점` — semantic view of theories.

## 3. syntactic view of theories /sɪnˈtæktɪk vjuː əv ˈθɪəriz/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an account that characterizes a theory as a formal system of sentences, axioms, and derivation rules.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** syntactic view tập trung vào formal sentence, axiom và rule suy diễn của theory.
**Grammar & collocations:** `syntactic-view formulation` — formulation; `formal axiom system` — system; `derive a theorem` — derive.
**Examples:** `The syntactic view of theories made the derivation rules explicit but obscured practical model use.` → Syntactic view of theories làm derivation rule rõ nhưng che khuất practical model use.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `이론의 통사론적 관점` — syntactic view of theories.

## 4. model-theoretic interpretation /ˈmɑdəl θiˈɛrətɪk ˌɪntərprəˈteɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an interpretation that studies which structures make a set of theoretical statements true.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** model-theoretic interpretation hỏi structure nào satisfy statement của theory.
**Grammar & collocations:** `model-theoretic interpretation of a theory` — interpretation; `satisfy a sentence` — satisfy; `admissible structure` — structure.
**Examples:** `A model-theoretic interpretation clarified which idealized systems satisfied the equations.` → Model-theoretic interpretation làm rõ system idealized nào satisfy equation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `모형 이론적 해석` — model-theoretic interpretation.

## 5. scientific representation /ˌsaɪənˈtɪfɪk ˌrɛprɪzɛnˈteɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the practice of using models, diagrams, equations, simulations, or other artifacts to stand for scientific targets.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** scientific representation dùng nhiều artifact để “đứng thay” target và hỗ trợ inference.
**Grammar & collocations:** `scientific-representation practice` — practice; `represent a target` — represent; `representational device` — device.
**Examples:** `A causal diagram is a form of scientific representation that highlights dependency structure.` → Causal diagram là scientific representation làm nổi dependency structure.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `과학적 표상` — scientific representation.

## 6. target system /ˈtɑrɡət ˈsɪstəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the real or intended system, process, or phenomenon that a model is designed to represent or investigate.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** target system là “đích” ngoài đời mà model muốn hiểu, dự đoán hoặc can thiệp.
**Grammar & collocations:** `target-system behavior` — behavior; `define the target system` — define; `model a target` — model.
**Examples:** `The target system was a coastal wetland exposed to seasonal salinity changes.` → Target system là coastal wetland chịu seasonal salinity change.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `대상 시스템` — target system.

## 7. source system /sɔrs ˈsɪstəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a system whose structure or behavior is used as a basis for representing another system.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** source system cung cấp pattern hoặc mechanism để xây representation cho target.
**Grammar & collocations:** `source-system analogy` — analogy; `map from source to target` — map; `source behavior` — behavior.
**Examples:** `The hydraulic source system helped students visualize electric current in the target circuit.` → Hydraulic source system giúp học viên hình dung electric current trong target circuit.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `원천 시스템` — source system.

## 8. analogical model /ˌænəˈlɑdʒɪkəl ˈmɑdəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a model that represents a target by exploiting a relevant structural or relational similarity with another system.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** analogical model mượn relation tương tự, nhưng analogy chỉ hữu ích trong feature đã map đúng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `analogical-model reasoning` — reasoning; `draw an analogy` — draw; `structural similarity` — similarity.
**Examples:** `The network analogy was an analogical model for understanding disease transmission.` → Network analogy là analogical model để hiểu disease transmission.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `유추 모형` — analogical model.

## 9. scale model /skeɪl ˈmɑdəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a representation built at a different physical or temporal scale while preserving selected relations to the target.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** scale model đổi kích thước hoặc tốc độ nhưng giữ relation cần study.
**Grammar & collocations:** `scaled-model experiment` — experiment; `scale down a system` — scale; `preserve geometric similarity` — preserve.
**Examples:** `The wind-tunnel aircraft was a scale model designed to preserve aerodynamic similarity.` → Aircraft trong wind tunnel là scale model giữ aerodynamic similarity.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `축척 모형` — scale model.

## 10. mechanistic idealization /ˌmɛkəˈnɪstɪk aɪˌdiələˈzeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a purposeful simplification that isolates a proposed mechanism by suppressing other interactions or complications.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mechanistic idealization tắt bớt interaction để nhìn causal mechanism rõ hơn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `mechanistic-idealization assumption` — assumption; `isolate a mechanism` — isolate; `suppress a complication` — suppress.
**Examples:** `The mechanistic idealization ignored background noise to test the proposed feedback loop.` → Mechanistic idealization bỏ qua background noise để test feedback loop.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `기계론적 이상화` — mechanistic idealization.

## 11. abstraction level /əbˈstrækʃən ˈlɛvəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the degree of detail and generality at which a system or phenomenon is described.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** abstraction level quyết định ta nhìn micro detail hay macro pattern.
**Grammar & collocations:** `choose an abstraction level` — choose; `higher-level abstraction` — abstraction; `shift levels` — shift.
**Examples:** `At a citywide abstraction level, individual routing choices appeared as aggregate congestion.` → Ở abstraction level toàn city, routing choice cá nhân hiện thành aggregate congestion.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `추상화 수준` — abstraction level.

## 12. explanatory depth /ɪkˈsplænəˌtɔri dɛpθ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the extent to which an explanation identifies underlying structures, processes, or conditions rather than merely describing a pattern.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** explanatory depth đi từ “pattern là gì” xuống “vì sao và qua process nào”.
**Grammar & collocations:** `explanatory depth of a theory` — depth; `deepen an explanation` — deepen; `underlying process` — process.
**Examples:** `The mechanistic account offered greater explanatory depth than the purely statistical summary.` → Mechanistic account có explanatory depth lớn hơn statistical summary thuần túy.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `설명적 깊이` — explanatory depth.

## 13. causal relevance /ˈkɔzəl ˈrɛləvəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the extent to which a variable, process, or factor makes a difference to the outcome under consideration.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** causal relevance hỏi factor có thật sự make a difference cho outcome không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `causal relevance of a variable` — relevance; `assess relevance` — assess; `relevant causal factor` — factor.
**Examples:** `The experiment tested the causal relevance of humidity rather than its simple correlation with yield.` → Experiment test causal relevance của humidity, không chỉ correlation với yield.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `인과적 관련성` — causal relevance.

## 14. intervention invariance /ˌɪntərˈvɛnʃən ɪnˈvɛriəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** stability of a relationship or model under the interventions it is intended to represent.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** model giữ quan hệ khi intervention trong phạm vi hợp lệ; đây là basis cho transport và policy use.
**Grammar & collocations:** `intervention-invariance assumption` — assumption; `test invariance under intervention` — test; `stable policy relation` — relation.
**Examples:** `The forecast failed an intervention-invariance test when the subsidy rule changed.` → Forecast fail intervention invariance test khi subsidy rule đổi.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `개입 불변성` — intervention invariance.

## 15. causal completeness /ˈkɔzəl kəmˈpliːtnəs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the condition that a causal model includes all variables or pathways needed for the question and scope being analyzed.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** causal completeness là đủ pathway liên quan cho question, không có nghĩa model chứa toàn bộ universe.
**Grammar & collocations:** `causal-completeness assumption` — assumption; `check completeness` — check; `omitted pathway` — pathway.
**Examples:** `The team questioned causal completeness after discovering an unmeasured seasonal pathway.` → Team nghi ngờ causal completeness sau khi phát hiện seasonal pathway chưa đo.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `인과적 완전성` — causal completeness.

## 16. causal isolation /ˈkɔzəl ˌaɪsəˈleɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the separation of a causal pathway from competing influences so that its effect can be identified.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** causal isolation tách pathway muốn study khỏi competing cause.
**Grammar & collocations:** `achieve causal isolation` — achieve; `isolate an effect` — isolate; `competing influence` — influence.
**Examples:** `Random assignment improved causal isolation by balancing many background factors.` → Random assignment cải thiện causal isolation bằng cách balance nhiều background factor.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `인과적 고립` — causal isolation.

## 17. confounding structure /kənˈfaʊndɪŋ ˈstrʌktʃər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the arrangement of common causes or selection processes that mixes the association of interest with other pathways.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** confounding structure là geometry của các common cause làm association bị trộn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `confounding-structure diagnosis` — diagnosis; `map a confounder` — map; `open a backdoor path` — open.
**Examples:** `The causal graph exposed a confounding structure created by selective enrollment.` → Causal graph lộ confounding structure do selective enrollment tạo ra.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `교란 구조` — confounding structure.

## 18. evidence underdetermination /ˈɛvɪdəns ˌʌndərˌdɪtərməˈneɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the condition in which available evidence is compatible with more than one serious theoretical explanation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cùng evidence có thể fit nhiều theory; data chưa tự chọn một explanation duy nhất.
**Grammar & collocations:** `evidence-underdetermination problem` — problem; `underdetermine a theory` — underdetermine; `rival explanation` — explanation.
**Examples:** `Evidence underdetermination persisted because both models reproduced the observed trend.` → Evidence underdetermination tiếp tục vì cả hai model đều reproduce observed trend.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `증거의 미결정성` — evidence underdetermination.

## 19. theory equivalence /ˈθɪəri ɪˈkwɪvələns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a relation in which different formulations yield the same relevant predictions or structures under a translation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** hai theory có thể khác language nhưng equivalent cho prediction hoặc structure được xét.
**Grammar & collocations:** `theory-equivalence claim` — claim; `establish equivalence` — establish; `equivalent formulation` — formulation.
**Examples:** `The proof established theory equivalence for the two coordinate descriptions.` → Proof establish theory equivalence cho hai coordinate description.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `이론 동치` — theory equivalence.

## 20. semantic incommensurability /sɪˈmæntɪk ˌɪnkəˌmɛnʃərəˈbɪləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** difficulty in translating key concepts across frameworks because their meanings depend on different conceptual structures.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** semantic incommensurability không chỉ là khác từ; concept được neo trong framework khác nên translation không hoàn toàn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `semantic-incommensurability thesis` — thesis; `translate across frameworks` — translate; `conceptual scheme` — scheme.
**Examples:** `Semantic incommensurability made the older classification difficult to compare with the molecular framework.` → Semantic incommensurability làm classification cũ khó compare với molecular framework.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `의미론적 공약불가능성` — semantic incommensurability.

## 21. scientific pluralism /ˌsaɪənˈtɪfɪk ˈplʊərəlɪzəm/

## 22. epistemic virtue /ɪˌpɪstəmɪk ˈvɜrtʃuː/

**Core meaning (English):** a trait or practice, such as honesty, explanatory power, or caution, that supports reliable inquiry.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** epistemic virtue là phẩm chất giúp inquiry đáng tin, như honesty, clarity, humility và courage.

**Core meaning (English):** the convergence of independent lines of evidence on the same conclusion.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** evidence từ source khác nhau hội tụ về cùng conclusion, tăng confidence mà không biến thành certainty.

## 24. predictive success /prɪˈdɪktɪv səkˈsɛs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** accurate and reproducible prediction of relevant observations by a theory or model.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** predictive success là model dự đoán đúng observation mới một cách lặp lại, không chỉ fit dữ liệu cũ.
**Grammar & collocations:** `predictive-success argument` — argument; `achieve predictive success` — achieve; `out-of-sample prediction` — prediction.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `예측 성공` — predictive success.

## 25. theory-mediated observation /ˈθɪəri ˈmiːdieɪtɪd ˌɑbzərˈveɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** observation whose identification or interpretation depends on theoretical concepts, instruments, or background assumptions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** observation đi qua theory, instrument và category; “thấy” một phenomenon đã có mediation.
**Grammar & collocations:** `theory-mediated observation claim` — claim; `mediate observation` — mediate; `background assumption` — assumption.
**Examples:** `A particle track was a theory-mediated observation requiring an instrument model and classification rule.` → Particle track là theory-mediated observation cần instrument model và classification rule.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `이론 매개 관찰` — theory-mediated observation.

## Review in context

A model can have **representational adequacy** for a purpose. The **semantic view of theories** emphasizes families of models, while the **syntactic view of theories** emphasizes formal sentences; a **model-theoretic interpretation** asks which structures satisfy them. **Scientific representation** connects a **target system** with a **source system** through an **analogical model** or **scale model**. A **mechanistic idealization** works at a chosen **abstraction level** and may provide explanatory depth. Researchers ask about **causal relevance**, **intervention invariance**, **causal completeness**, and **causal isolation**, while mapping **confounding structure**. **Evidence underdetermination** can leave rival accounts alive; **theory equivalence** may show that different formulations agree, whereas **semantic incommensurability** can block direct translation. **Scientific pluralism** treats several approaches as useful, and **epistemic virtue** supports careful inquiry. **Consilience of evidence** and **predictive success** strengthen a claim, even though **theory-mediated observation** reminds us that evidence is interpreted through concepts and instruments.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

Một model có thể đạt **representational adequacy** cho một purpose. **Semantic view of theories** nhấn mạnh family của model, còn **syntactic view of theories** nhấn mạnh formal sentence; **model-theoretic interpretation** hỏi structure nào satisfy chúng. **Scientific representation** nối **target system** với **source system** qua **analogical model** hoặc **scale model**. **Mechanistic idealization** hoạt động ở một **abstraction level** được chọn và có thể tạo explanatory depth. Researcher hỏi về **causal relevance**, **intervention invariance**, **causal completeness** và **causal isolation**, đồng thời map **confounding structure**. **Evidence underdetermination** có thể giữ nhiều account cạnh tranh; **theory equivalence** cho thấy các formulation khác nhau vẫn agreement, còn **semantic incommensurability** có thể chặn translation trực tiếp. **Scientific pluralism** xem nhiều approach là hữu ích, còn **epistemic virtue** hỗ trợ inquiry cẩn trọng. **Consilience of evidence** và **predictive success** củng cố claim, dù **theory-mediated observation** nhắc rằng evidence luôn được diễn giải qua concept và instrument.
