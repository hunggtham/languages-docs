# C1 Vocabulary 53 — Interference, spillovers, and network exposure

This lesson follows how causal effects change when one unit's treatment can influence other units through shared places, relationships, or networks.

Flow: **cluster treatment → peer exposure → social treatment effect → interference graph → neighborhood exposure → interference mechanism → network interference → exposure contrast → cluster-level treatment → peer treatment → interference bound → spillover estimand → social network exposure → treatment diffusion → contagion effect → equilibrium exposure → interference adjustment → network potential outcome → peer effect model → exposure saturation**.

## 1. cluster treatment /ˈklʌstər ˈtriːtmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an intervention assigned to a group of connected units rather than to each unit separately.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** intervention assign cho group unit connected thay vì từng unit riêng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `randomized cluster treatment` — treatment; `assign cluster treatment` — assign.
**Examples:** `Cluster treatment gave every school in the district the same training package.` → Treatment cluster cho mọi school trong district cùng training package.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `군집 처치`.

## 2. peer exposure /pɪr ɪkˈspoʊʒər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the treatment or condition experienced by people connected to a focal unit.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** treatment hoặc condition người connected với focal unit trải nghiệm.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high peer exposure` — exposure; `measure peer exposure` — measure.
**Examples:** `Peer exposure to the program increased even for students not directly assigned to it.` → Exposure peer với program tăng cả ở student không được assign trực tiếp.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `동료 노출`.

## 3. social treatment effect /ˈsoʊʃəl ˈtriːtmənt ɪˈfɛkt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a causal effect that includes how treatment changes behavior or outcomes through social connections.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** causal effect gồm treatment đổi behavior hoặc outcome qua social connection.
**Grammar & collocations:** `positive social treatment effect` — effect; `estimate a social treatment effect` — estimate.
**Examples:** `The social treatment effect extended beyond the people who received the message.` → Effect treatment social extend ngoài người receive message.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `사회적 처치 효과`.

## 4. interference graph /ˌɪntərˈfɪrəns ɡræf/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a graph showing which units can affect one another's outcomes or treatment responses.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** graph show unit nào có thể affect outcome hoặc treatment response của nhau.
**Grammar & collocations:** `sparse interference graph` — graph; `construct an interference graph` — construct.
**Examples:** `The interference graph connected households that shared a water system.` → Graph interference connect household share water system.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `간섭 그래프`.

## 5. neighborhood exposure /ˈneɪbərˌhʊd ɪkˈspoʊʒər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the treatment conditions present among people or units located near a focal unit.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** treatment condition hiện diện ở người hoặc unit gần focal unit.
**Grammar & collocations:** `local neighborhood exposure` — exposure; `calculate neighborhood exposure` — calculate.
**Examples:** `Neighborhood exposure to the cooling program lowered heat-related visits.` → Exposure neighborhood với cooling program giảm visit liên quan heat.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `지역 노출`.

## 6. interference mechanism /ˌɪntərˈfɪrəns ˈmɛkəˌnɪzəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the process through which one unit's treatment changes another unit's outcome.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** process treatment của một unit đổi outcome unit khác.
**Grammar & collocations:** `plausible interference mechanism` — mechanism; `identify an interference mechanism` — identify.
**Examples:** `The interference mechanism was information sharing between neighboring clinics.` → Mechanism interference là việc clinic lân cận share information.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `간섭 메커니즘`.

## 7. network interference /ˈnɛtˌwɜrk ˌɪntərˈfɪrəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** interference in which treatment effects travel through a network of social, economic, or physical connections.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** interference effect treatment travel qua network social, economic hoặc physical.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong network interference` — interference; `model network interference` — model.
**Examples:** `Network interference made one person's vaccination relevant to nearby residents.` → Interference network làm vaccination của một người relevant với resident gần.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `네트워크 간섭`.

## 8. exposure contrast /ɪkˈspoʊʒər ˈkɑntræst/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the defined difference between two levels or patterns of treatment exposure being compared.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** difference define giữa hai level hoặc pattern treatment exposure compare.
**Grammar & collocations:** `sharp exposure contrast` — contrast; `define an exposure contrast` — define.
**Examples:** `The exposure contrast compared no treated neighbors with half the neighborhood treated.` → Contrast exposure compare không có neighbor treated với half neighborhood treated.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `노출 대비`.

## 9. cluster-level treatment /ˈklʌstər ˈlɛvəl ˈtriːtmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** treatment assigned at the level of a group whose members may influence one another.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** treatment assign ở level group nơi member có thể influence nhau.
**Grammar & collocations:** `cluster-level treatment assignment` — assignment; `analyze cluster-level treatment` — analyze.
**Examples:** `Cluster-level treatment prevented untreated neighbors from serving as a clean comparison.` → Treatment cluster-level prevent untreated neighbor làm comparison sạch.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `군집 수준 처치`.

## 10. peer treatment /pɪr ˈtriːtmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** treatment received by a person's peers or connected units rather than by the person directly.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** treatment peer hoặc unit connected receive, không phải person trực tiếp.
**Grammar & collocations:** `indirect peer treatment` — treatment; `account for peer treatment` — account.
**Examples:** `Peer treatment changed the information available to untreated workers.` → Treatment peer đổi information available cho worker untreated.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `동료 처치`.

## 11. interference bound /ˌɪntərˈfɪrəns baʊnd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a range limiting plausible spillover effects when the exact pattern of interference is not identified.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** range limit spillover effect plausible khi pattern interference exact không identify.
**Grammar & collocations:** `sharp interference bound` — bound; `derive an interference bound` — derive.
**Examples:** `The interference bound showed that the indirect effect could not be arbitrarily large.` → Bound interference show indirect effect không thể lớn tùy ý.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `간섭 경계`.

## 12. spillover estimand /ˈspɪlˌoʊvər ˈɛstəmənd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a precisely defined causal quantity measuring an effect transmitted from treated units to other units.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** causal quantity define rõ đo effect truyền từ treated unit tới unit khác.
**Grammar & collocations:** `network spillover estimand` — estimand; `identify a spillover estimand` — identify.
**Examples:** `The spillover estimand measured the change among neighbors who were not directly treated.` → Estimand spillover measure change ở neighbor không được treat trực tiếp.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `파급효과 추정 대상`.

## 13. social network exposure /ˈsoʊʃəl ˈnɛtˌwɜrk ɪkˈspoʊʒər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the treatment-related information or conditions reaching a person through their social network.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** information hoặc condition liên quan treatment reach person qua social network.
**Grammar & collocations:** `measured social network exposure` — exposure; `map social network exposure` — map.
**Examples:** `Social network exposure increased when friends shared the public-health video.` → Exposure network social tăng khi friend share public-health video.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `사회 네트워크 노출`.

## 14. treatment diffusion /ˈtriːtmənt dɪˈfjuːʒən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the spread of a treatment, practice, or behavior from initially treated units to others.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** spread treatment, practice hoặc behavior từ treated unit ban đầu tới người khác.
**Grammar & collocations:** `rapid treatment diffusion` — diffusion; `model treatment diffusion` — model.
**Examples:** `Treatment diffusion made the control villages gradually adopt the farming practice.` → Diffusion treatment làm control village dần adopt farming practice.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `처치 확산`.

## 15. contagion effect /kənˈteɪdʒən ɪˈfɛkt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a change that spreads from one unit to connected units through contact, imitation, or shared exposure.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** change spread từ unit này sang unit connected qua contact, imitation hoặc exposure chung.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong contagion effect` — effect; `measure a contagion effect` — measure.
**Examples:** `The contagion effect carried the rumor beyond the original online group.` → Effect contagion carry rumor ra ngoài online group original.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `전염 효과`.

## 16. equilibrium exposure /ˌiːkwəˈlɪbriəm ɪkˈspoʊʒər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** exposure that results after connected units' decisions and treatments settle into a stable pattern.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** exposure xảy ra sau decision và treatment của connected unit settle pattern stable.
**Grammar & collocations:** `stable equilibrium exposure` — exposure; `solve for equilibrium exposure` — solve.
**Examples:** `Equilibrium exposure depended on how quickly firms copied one another's prices.` → Exposure equilibrium phụ thuộc firm copy price nhau nhanh thế nào.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `균형 노출`.

**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an analytical adjustment that accounts for outcomes being affected by treatments received by connected units.

**Grammar & collocations:** `network interference adjustment` — adjustment; `apply an interference adjustment` — apply.

## 18. network potential outcome /ˈnɛtˌwɜrk pəˈtɛnʃəl ˈaʊtˌkʌm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an outcome defined for a unit under a specified assignment of treatment across its network.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** outcome của unit dưới treatment assignment cụ thể trong network.
**Grammar & collocations:** `network potential-outcome model` — model; `define a network potential outcome` — define.
**Examples:** `The network potential outcome changed when two neighboring clinics received the program.` → Outcome potential network đổi khi hai clinic neighbor nhận program.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `네트워크 잠재 결과`.

## 19. peer effect model /pɪr ɪˈfɛkt ˈmɑdəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a model in which one unit's outcome or decision depends partly on the behavior of connected peers.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** model outcome hoặc decision unit phụ thuộc một phần behavior peer connected.
**Grammar & collocations:** `linear peer effect model` — model; `estimate a peer effect model` — estimate.
**Examples:** `The peer effect model separated classmates' influence from shared classroom resources.` → Model effect peer tách influence classmate khỏi resource classroom chung.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `동료 효과 모형`.

## 20. exposure saturation /ɪkˈspoʊʒər ˌsætʃəˈreɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a condition in which additional treated or exposed neighbors add little further influence.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** condition thêm treated hoặc exposed neighbor tạo ít influence thêm.
**Grammar & collocations:** `early exposure saturation` — saturation; `test for exposure saturation` — test.
**Examples:** `Exposure saturation appeared after most households had already received the warning.` → Saturation exposure xuất hiện sau khi hầu hết household đã receive warning.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `노출 포화`.

## Review in context

A **cluster treatment** can create **peer exposure** and a **social treatment effect** when an **interference graph** connects units. **Neighborhood exposure** reveals an **interference mechanism**, so **network interference** must be described through an **exposure contrast** and a **cluster-level treatment** rule. Untreated units may still receive **peer treatment**; an **interference bound** can limit the **spillover estimand**. **Social network exposure** may produce **treatment diffusion** or a **contagion effect**, and connected decisions can create **equilibrium exposure**. An **interference adjustment** uses a **network potential outcome** and a **peer effect model**, while **exposure saturation** describes the point at which additional neighbors add little influence.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:** **Cluster treatment** có thể tạo **peer exposure** và **social treatment effect** khi **interference graph** connect các unit. **Neighborhood exposure** reveal **interference mechanism**, nên **network interference** phải được describe qua **exposure contrast** và rule **cluster-level treatment**. Untreated unit vẫn có thể receive **peer treatment**; **interference bound** có thể limit **spillover estimand**. **Social network exposure** có thể tạo **treatment diffusion** hoặc **contagion effect**, còn decision connected có thể tạo **equilibrium exposure**. **Interference adjustment** dùng **network potential outcome** và **peer effect model**, trong khi **exposure saturation** mô tả point thêm neighbor tạo ít influence.
