# C2+ Vocabulary — Shipping contracts, claims, and sustainability

This lesson extends container logistics into allocation of trade responsibilities, maritime claims, customs evidence, and emissions accounting. The network is **incoterm allocation → export-control classification → dual-use item → certificate of origin → returns disposition → scope-three emissions → carbon border adjustment → embedded emissions → mass-balance certification → chain-of-custody certificate → free-time agreement → general average → particular average → marine cargo insurance → force majeure clause → laytime calculation → notice of readiness → demurrage dispute → deadfreight claim → cargo misdeclaration**.

## 1. incoterm allocation /ˈɪnkoʊtɜrm ˌæləˈkeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the assignment of transport, cost, risk, insurance, customs, and delivery responsibilities under a chosen Incoterms rule.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** Incoterm chia “ai trả, ai chịu risk, ai làm thủ tục” ở từng chặng của shipment.
**Grammar & collocations:** `incoterm allocation of risk` — allocation; `review an Incoterm` — review; `allocate delivery obligations` — allocate.
**Examples:** `The contract's incoterm allocation placed import clearance and inland risk on the buyer.` → Incoterm allocation của contract đặt import clearance và inland risk lên buyer.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `인코텀즈 책임 배분` — incoterm allocation.

## 2. export-control classification /ˈɛkspɔrt kənˈtroʊl ˌklæsəfəˈkeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** classification of a product, technology, software, or technical data under applicable export-control rules.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** xác định hàng thuộc control list nào, cần license hay exception nào trước khi export.
**Grammar & collocations:** `export-control classification number` — number; `classify an item` — classify; `classification ruling` — ruling.
**Examples:** `The manufacturer requested an export-control classification before sending the design files overseas.` → Manufacturer xin export-control classification trước khi gửi design file ra nước ngoài.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `수출 통제 분류` — export-control classification.

## 3. dual-use item /ˌduːəl juːs ˈɑbdʒɛkt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a product, technology, or component that can serve civilian purposes but also has potential military or security applications.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** item có use dân sự nhưng có thể chuyển sang military/security, nên export review cần kỹ hơn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `dual-use item control` — control; `dual-use export` — export; `identify a dual-use item` — identify.
**Examples:** `The sensor was treated as a dual-use item because its resolution supported military imaging.` → Sensor được xem là dual-use item vì resolution phù hợp military imaging.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `이중용도 품목` — dual-use item.

## 4. certificate of origin /sərˈtɪfɪkət əv ˈɔrədʒɪn/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a document certifying the country in which goods were produced, manufactured, or substantially transformed.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** giấy chứng minh “hàng có origin ở đâu”, ảnh hưởng tariff, preference và customs treatment.
**Grammar & collocations:** `preferential certificate of origin` — certificate; `issue a certificate` — issue; `verify origin` — verify.
**Examples:** `The exporter attached a certificate of origin to claim the reduced tariff.` → Exporter đính certificate of origin để claim tariff giảm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `원산지 증명서` — certificate of origin.

## 5. returns disposition /rɪˈtɜrnz ˌdɪspəˈzɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the decision about what to do with returned goods, such as restock, repair, refurbish, liquidate, recycle, or destroy them.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** sau khi hàng return, nó đi về nhánh nào trong reverse network.
**Grammar & collocations:** `returns-disposition decision` — decision; `assign a disposition` — assign; `disposition code` — code.
**Examples:** `The returns disposition sent unopened units back to stock and damaged units to refurbishment.` → Returns disposition đưa unit unopened về stock và unit damaged sang refurbishment.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `반품 처분` — returns disposition.

## 6. scope-three emissions /skoʊp θriː ɪˈmɪʃənz/
**Part of speech:** plural noun
**Core meaning (English):** indirect greenhouse-gas emissions occurring across an organization's upstream and downstream value chain rather than from its own operations or purchased energy.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** emissions “ngoài tường công ty”: supplier, transport, use phase và end-of-life.
**Grammar & collocations:** `scope-three emissions inventory` — inventory; `value-chain emissions` — emissions; `report scope three` — report.
**Examples:** `Ocean freight was included in the manufacturer's scope-three emissions inventory.` → Ocean freight được đưa vào inventory scope-three emissions của manufacturer.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `스코프 3 배출` — scope-three emissions.

## 7. carbon border adjustment /ˈkɑrbən ˈbɔrdər əˈdʒʌstmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a trade measure that adjusts the carbon cost of imported goods to reflect emissions associated with their production.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** import goods phải chịu carbon cost tương ứng để tránh production chuyển sang jurisdiction có rule nhẹ hơn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `carbon-border-adjustment mechanism` — mechanism; `calculate a border adjustment` — calculate; `embedded-carbon reporting` — reporting.
**Examples:** `The supplier needed verified emissions data for the carbon border adjustment filing.` → Supplier cần emissions data được verify cho carbon border adjustment filing.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `탄소 국경 조정` — carbon border adjustment.

## 8. embedded emissions /ɛmˈbɛdɪd ɪˈmɪʃənz/
**Part of speech:** plural noun
**Core meaning (English):** greenhouse-gas emissions released during the production and processing of a good before it reaches the buyer or user.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** emissions “nằm trong” hàng hóa qua material, energy và manufacturing history.
**Grammar & collocations:** `embedded-emissions calculation` — calculation; `report embedded emissions` — report; `emissions intensity` — intensity.
**Examples:** `The steel importer calculated embedded emissions from the furnace and electricity mix.` → Steel importer tính embedded emissions từ furnace và electricity mix.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `내재 배출` — embedded emissions.

## 9. mass-balance certification /mæs ˈbæləns ˌsɜrtəfəˈkeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** certification that tracks the amount of a claimed sustainable input through a mixed production system using documented input–output accounting.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** material sustainable có thể trộn physically, nhưng accounting phải cân input và output có claim.
**Grammar & collocations:** `mass-balance certification scheme` — scheme; `certified sustainable feedstock` — feedstock; `audit the balance` — audit.
**Examples:** `Mass-balance certification allowed the refinery to claim renewable content without segregating every molecule.` → Mass-balance certification cho refinery claim renewable content mà không cần segregate từng molecule.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `물질수지 인증` — mass-balance certification.

## 10. chain-of-custody certificate /tʃeɪn əv ˈkʌstədi sərˈtɪfɪkət/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a document or certification record showing how a product or material was controlled and transferred from source through each relevant stage.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** giấy nối từng mắt xích custody, giúp chứng minh material không bị tráo hoặc claim bị đứt.
**Grammar & collocations:** `chain-of-custody certificate` — certificate; `maintain chain of custody` — maintain; `custody record` — record.
**Examples:** `The auditor checked the chain-of-custody certificate from the forest source to the mill.` → Auditor check chain-of-custody certificate từ forest source tới mill.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `관리 연계 인증서` — chain-of-custody certificate.

## 11. free-time agreement /ˈfriː taɪm əˈɡriːmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a negotiated period during which a container can remain at a terminal or with a customer before storage, demurrage, or detention charges begin.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** “đồng hồ miễn phí” được agreement cho phép trước khi fee bắt đầu chạy.
**Grammar & collocations:** `extended free-time agreement` — agreement; `negotiate free time` — negotiate; `free-time expiry` — expiry.
**Examples:** `The importer negotiated a free-time agreement to cover the seasonal customs backlog.` → Importer negotiate free-time agreement để cover customs backlog theo mùa.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `무료 기간 계약` — free-time agreement.

## 12. general average /ˈdʒɛnərəl ˈævərɪdʒ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a maritime principle under which extraordinary sacrifices or expenses made to save a voyage are shared proportionally by the parties with interests in the voyage.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** một sacrifice bất thường để cứu cả voyage được phân bổ giữa cargo, vessel và freight interests theo rule.
**Grammar & collocations:** `declare general average` — declare; `general-average adjustment` — adjustment; `general-average guarantee` — guarantee.
**Examples:** `The carrier declared general average after cargo was sacrificed to stabilize the vessel.` → Carrier declare general average sau khi sacrifice cargo để stabilize vessel.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `공동해손` — general average.

## 13. particular average /pərˈtɪkjələr ˈævərɪdʒ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a partial loss affecting a particular insured interest rather than a shared extraordinary sacrifice for the whole maritime venture.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** loss riêng của cargo/vessel interest, không phải sacrifice chia cho toàn voyage.
**Grammar & collocations:** `particular-average loss` — loss; `claim a particular average` — claim; `particular-average damage` — damage.
**Examples:** `Water damage to one consignee's cargo was treated as a particular average claim.` → Water damage của cargo một consignee được xem là particular average claim.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `단독해손` — particular average.

## 14. marine cargo insurance /məˈriːn ˈkɑrɡoʊ ɪnˈʃʊrəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** insurance covering specified risks of physical loss or damage to goods during maritime or connected transport.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** policy bảo vệ cargo trên sea và chặng liên quan, tùy exclusions, valuation và insured interest.
**Grammar & collocations:** `marine-cargo insurance policy` — policy; `insure cargo` — insure; `cargo-insurance claim` — claim.
**Examples:** `Marine cargo insurance covered the water damage after the container shifted in the storm.` → Marine cargo insurance cover water damage sau khi container shift trong storm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `적하 해상보험` — marine cargo insurance.

## 15. force majeure clause /fɔrs mæˈʒɜr klɔz/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a contract provision addressing extraordinary events beyond a party's reasonable control that may excuse, suspend, or modify performance.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** clause xử lý event như war, port closure, disaster; effect phụ thuộc wording và notice requirement.
**Grammar & collocations:** `force-majeure clause` — clause; `invoke force majeure` — invoke; `force-majeure notice` — notice.
**Examples:** `The carrier invoked the force majeure clause after the port was closed by the earthquake.` → Carrier invoke force majeure clause sau khi port đóng do earthquake.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `불가항력 조항` — force majeure clause.

## 16. laytime calculation /ˈleɪtaɪm ˌkælkjəˈleɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** calculation of the contractual time allowed for loading or discharging cargo and of any time used beyond that allowance.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** tính “đồng hồ” cargo operation theo charter: time allowed, interruptions và excess.
**Grammar & collocations:** `laytime-calculation dispute` — dispute; `calculate laytime` — calculate; `laytime allowance` — allowance.
**Examples:** `The parties disagreed about whether weather interruptions counted in the laytime calculation.` → Các party bất đồng weather interruption có tính trong laytime calculation không.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `하역 시간 계산` — laytime calculation.

## 17. notice of readiness /ˈnoʊtɪs əv ˈrɛdinəs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a formal notice from a vessel that it has arrived and is ready, subject to the contract, to load or discharge cargo.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** thông báo “tàu đã sẵn sàng”, thường khởi động các mốc laytime nếu điều kiện contract thỏa.
**Grammar & collocations:** `valid notice of readiness` — notice; `tender readiness` — tender; `accept a notice` — accept.
**Examples:** `The charterer rejected the notice of readiness because the berth was not accessible.` → Charterer reject notice of readiness vì berth chưa accessible.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `준비 완료 통지` — notice of readiness.

## 18. demurrage dispute /dɪˈmɜrɪdʒ dɪˈspjuːt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a disagreement about whether time or circumstances triggered charges for keeping a vessel, container, or cargo beyond the contractual allowance.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** hai bên tranh luận “đồng hồ fee” có chạy không, vì delay, free time, notice hoặc exception.
**Grammar & collocations:** `demurrage-dispute resolution` — resolution; `contest a charge` — contest; `demurrage evidence` — evidence.
**Examples:** `The demurrage dispute centered on whether customs inspection suspended the free-time clock.` → Demurrage dispute tập trung customs inspection có suspend free-time clock không.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `체선료 분쟁` — demurrage dispute.

## 19. deadfreight claim /ˈdɛdˌfreɪt kleɪm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a claim for payment when a charterer fails to provide the contracted quantity of cargo or space and the carrier loses expected freight.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** space đã book nhưng cargo không đủ; carrier claim freight cho phần “trống”.
**Grammar & collocations:** `deadfreight claim` — claim; `deadfreight liability` — liability; `short-ship cargo` — cargo.
**Examples:** `The carrier filed a deadfreight claim after the charterer canceled half the booked volume.` → Carrier file deadfreight claim sau khi charterer cancel nửa volume đã book.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `미선적 운임 청구` — deadfreight claim.

## 20. cargo misdeclaration /ˈkɑrɡoʊ ˌmɪsdɛkləˈreɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an inaccurate, incomplete, or false declaration of cargo identity, quantity, weight, value, classification, or dangerous properties.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cargo được khai sai về what/how much/how dangerous, tạo safety, customs và contract risk.
**Grammar & collocations:** `dangerous-cargo misdeclaration` — misdeclaration; `detect misdeclaration` — detect; `misdeclaration penalty` — penalty.
**Examples:** `Cargo misdeclaration concealed lithium batteries and triggered an emergency inspection.` → Cargo misdeclaration che lithium battery và trigger emergency inspection.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `화물 허위 신고` — cargo misdeclaration.

## Review in context

An **incoterm allocation**, **export-control classification**, **dual-use item** review, and **certificate of origin** determine who carries trade obligations. A **returns disposition** can affect **scope-three emissions**, **carbon border adjustment**, and **embedded emissions** claims; **mass-balance certification** and a **chain-of-custody certificate** support sustainability evidence. In shipping operations, a **free-time agreement** defines the clock, while **general average**, **particular average**, and **marine cargo insurance** address different losses. A **force majeure clause**, **laytime calculation**, and **notice of readiness** shape performance disputes. A **demurrage dispute** may follow a delay, a **deadfreight claim** may follow missing cargo, and **cargo misdeclaration** can create safety and liability consequences.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

**Incoterm allocation**, **export-control classification**, review **dual-use item** và **certificate of origin** quyết định ai chịu trade obligation. **Returns disposition** có thể ảnh hưởng claim về **scope-three emissions**, **carbon border adjustment** và **embedded emissions**; **mass-balance certification** và **chain-of-custody certificate** hỗ trợ evidence sustainability. Trong shipping operation, **free-time agreement** định nghĩa clock, còn **general average**, **particular average** và **marine cargo insurance** xử lý các loại loss khác nhau. **Force majeure clause**, **laytime calculation** và **notice of readiness** định hình performance dispute. **Demurrage dispute** có thể xuất hiện sau delay, **deadfreight claim** sau cargo thiếu, còn **cargo misdeclaration** tạo safety và liability consequence.
