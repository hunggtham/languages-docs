# C1 Vocabulary 17 — Reader subscriptions and newsletter lifecycle

This lesson follows how a reader moves from first contact to sustained support through newsletters, subscriptions, and membership benefits.

Flow: **onboarding sequence → welcome email → churn signal → retention offer → cancellation survey → newsletter segmentation → subject line test → delivery rate → subscriber cohort → paywall meter → registration wall → premium tier → archive access → bundle offer → subscriber journey → conversion event → retention campaign → newsletter preference → subscriber benefit → trial conversion**.

## 1. onboarding sequence /ˈɑnˌbɔrdɪŋ ˈsiːkwəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a planned series of messages or steps that helps a new reader understand and begin using a service.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** series message hoặc step được plan giúp reader mới hiểu và bắt đầu use service.
**Grammar & collocations:** `personalized onboarding sequence` — sequence; `design an onboarding sequence` — design.
**Examples:** `The onboarding sequence showed new members how to save articles and manage alerts.` → Sequence onboarding chỉ member mới cách save article và manage alert.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `온보딩 순서`.

## 2. welcome email /ˈwɛlkəm ˈiːmeɪl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the first email sent to a new subscriber or member to introduce a service and explain what to expect.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** email đầu gửi subscriber hoặc member mới để giới thiệu service và explain điều sẽ expect.
**Grammar & collocations:** `personalized welcome email` — email; `send a welcome email` — gửi.
**Examples:** `The welcome email linked to the publication's correction policy and contact page.` → Email welcome link tới correction policy và contact page của publication.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `환영 이메일`.

## 3. churn signal /ˈtʃɜrn ˈsɪɡnəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a behavior or change that suggests a subscriber may soon stop paying for or using a service.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** behavior hoặc change gợi ý subscriber sắp stop pay hoặc use service.
**Grammar & collocations:** `early churn signal` — signal sớm; `detect a churn signal` — detect.
**Examples:** `A long period without opening the newsletter became a churn signal.` → Khoảng thời gian dài không mở newsletter thành signal churn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `이탈 신호`.

## 4. retention offer /rɪˈtɛnʃən ˈɔfər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a price, benefit, or service option offered to persuade an existing subscriber not to leave.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** price, benefit hoặc service option offer để persuade subscriber hiện tại không leave.
**Grammar & collocations:** `targeted retention offer` — offer; `make a retention offer` — đưa.
**Examples:** `The retention offer gave long-term readers a lower rate without removing archive access.` → Offer retention cho reader lâu năm rate thấp hơn mà không remove archive access.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `구독 유지 제안`.

## 5. cancellation survey /ˌkænsəˈleɪʃən ˈsɜrveɪ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a questionnaire asking people why they ended a subscription or membership.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** questionnaire hỏi vì sao người end subscription hoặc membership.
**Grammar & collocations:** `short cancellation survey` — survey; `complete a cancellation survey` — complete.
**Examples:** `The cancellation survey found that confusing billing was a larger problem than price.` → Survey cancellation thấy billing confusing là problem lớn hơn price.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `해지 설문`.

## 6. newsletter segmentation /ˈnuːzˌlɛtər ˌsɛɡmənˈteɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** dividing a newsletter audience into groups so that messages can match different interests or behaviors.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** chia audience newsletter thành group để message match interest hoặc behavior khác nhau.
**Grammar & collocations:** `behavior-based newsletter segmentation` — segmentation; `use newsletter segmentation` — use.
**Examples:** `Newsletter segmentation gave climate readers a different weekly briefing from arts readers.` → Segmentation newsletter cho climate reader briefing khác arts reader.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `뉴스레터 세분화`.

## 7. subject line test /ˈsʌbdʒɪkt laɪn tɛst/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a comparison of alternative email subject lines to learn which wording attracts more useful attention.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** compare subject line email khác nhau để learn wording nào attract attention hữu ích hơn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `simple subject line test` — test; `run a subject line test` — run.
**Examples:** `A subject line test showed that a specific question outperformed a vague promise.` → Test subject line cho thấy question cụ thể outperform promise vague.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `제목줄 테스트`.

## 8. delivery rate /dɪˈlɪvəri reɪt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the percentage of sent messages that successfully reach recipients' mail systems.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** percentage message gửi đi reach mail system của recipient thành công.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high delivery rate` — rate cao; `monitor delivery rate` — monitor.
**Examples:** `The team improved delivery rate by cleaning inactive addresses.` → Team cải thiện rate delivery bằng cách clean address inactive.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `전달률`.

## 9. subscriber cohort /səbˈskraɪbər ˈkoʊˌhɔrt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a group of subscribers who joined during the same period or under the same conditions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** group subscriber join cùng period hoặc điều kiện.
**Grammar & collocations:** `new subscriber cohort` — cohort; `compare subscriber cohorts` — compare.
**Examples:** `The spring subscriber cohort stayed longer than the group acquired during the election.` → Cohort subscriber spring stay lâu hơn group acquire trong election.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `구독자 코호트`.

## 10. paywall meter /ˈpeɪˌwɔl ˈmiːtər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a system that counts how much free content a reader has used before asking for registration or payment.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** system count free content reader use trước khi ask registration hoặc payment.
**Grammar & collocations:** `flexible paywall meter` — meter; `adjust a paywall meter` — adjust.
**Examples:** `The paywall meter allowed occasional readers to sample several investigations each month.` → Meter paywall cho occasional reader sample vài investigation mỗi tháng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `유료벽 계수기`.

## 11. registration wall /ˌrɛdʒəˈstreɪʃən wɔl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a requirement to create an account before accessing content that is still free of charge.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** requirement create account trước khi access content vẫn free.
**Grammar & collocations:** `light registration wall` — wall nhẹ; `place content behind a registration wall` — đặt.
**Examples:** `The registration wall helped the newsroom understand which public-service stories people returned to.` → Wall registration giúp newsroom hiểu story public-service nào người quay lại.

## 16. conversion event /kənˈvɜrʒən ɪˈvɛnt/

**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** action observable cho thấy person tiến tới relationship desired như register hoặc subscribe.

**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the change from a temporary or introductory user into a paying subscriber.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** change từ temporary hoặc introductory user thành paying subscriber.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `체험판 전환`.

## Review in context

An **onboarding sequence** begins with a **welcome email** and explains the service before a **churn signal** appears. A **retention offer** or **cancellation survey** can reveal why people leave, while **newsletter segmentation** and a **subject line test** improve the **delivery rate** for each **subscriber cohort**. A **paywall meter** may lead to a **registration wall**, a **premium tier**, or wider **archive access**. A **bundle offer** should fit the reader's **subscriber journey** and make each **conversion event** understandable. A **retention campaign** can respect each **newsletter preference**, explain every **subscriber benefit**, and improve **trial conversion** without hiding the price.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:** **Onboarding sequence** bắt đầu bằng **welcome email** và explain service trước khi xuất hiện **churn signal**. **Retention offer** hoặc **cancellation survey** có thể reveal vì sao người leave, còn **newsletter segmentation** và **subject line test** cải thiện **delivery rate** cho từng **subscriber cohort**. **Paywall meter** có thể dẫn tới **registration wall**, **premium tier** hoặc **archive access** rộng hơn. **Bundle offer** nên fit **subscriber journey** và làm mỗi **conversion event** dễ hiểu. **Retention campaign** có thể respect từng **newsletter preference**, explain mỗi **subscriber benefit** và improve **trial conversion** mà không hide price.
