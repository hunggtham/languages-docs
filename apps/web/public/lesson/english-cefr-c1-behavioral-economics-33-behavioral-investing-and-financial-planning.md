# C1 Vocabulary 33 — Behavioral investing and financial planning

This lesson follows how people choose investments, savings, debt, and advice when uncertainty, attention, fees, and long-term goals interact.

Flow: **portfolio salience → fee illusion → return expectation → risk capacity perception → investment default → diversification friction → loss realization aversion → market news attention → volatility normalization → dividend framing → liquidity preference → emergency fund belief → debt repayment motivation → financial advice trust → peer investing cue → capital gains salience → goal-based saving → spending rebound → financial self-efficacy → long-term wealth framing**.

## 1. portfolio salience /pɔrtˈfoʊlioʊ ˈseɪliəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** how noticeable and emotionally important the current composition and value of an investment portfolio are.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mức composition và value portfolio investment noticeable và emotional important.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high portfolio salience` — salience; `increase portfolio salience` — increase.
**Examples:** `Portfolio salience rose after the app displayed a daily loss beside the long-term target.` → Salience portfolio tăng sau khi app display loss daily cạnh target long-term.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `포트폴리오 현저성`.

## 2. fee illusion /fiː ɪˈluːʒən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** underestimating the long-term effect of fees because a small recurring charge seems harmless.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** underestimate effect long-term của fee vì charge recurring nhỏ trông harmless.
**Grammar & collocations:** `dangerous fee illusion` — illusion; `correct a fee illusion` — correct.
**Examples:** `Fee illusion hid how a small annual charge could reduce retirement savings over decades.` → Illusion fee che charge annual nhỏ reduce saving retirement qua decade.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `수수료 착시`.

## 3. return expectation /rɪˈtɝn ˌɛkspɛkˈteɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a belief about the profit, income, or growth an investment will produce.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** belief investment produce profit, income hoặc growth bao nhiêu.
**Grammar & collocations:** `optimistic return expectation` — expectation; `revise return expectations` — revise.
**Examples:** `Return expectation remained high even after several comparable assets had weakened.` → Expectation return vẫn cao dù nhiều asset comparable yếu.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `수익 기대`.

## 4. risk capacity perception /rɪsk kəˈpæsəti pərˈsɛpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a judgment about how much financial loss a person can actually absorb without harming essential plans.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** judgment person absorb loss financial bao nhiêu mà không harm plan essential.
**Grammar & collocations:** `low risk capacity perception` — perception; `improve risk capacity perception` — improve.
**Examples:** `Risk capacity perception changed after the family calculated its emergency expenses honestly.` → Perception capacity risk đổi sau khi family calculate expense emergency honest.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `위험 감내 능력 인식`.

## 5. investment default /ɪnˈvɛstmənt dɪˈfɔlt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a preselected investment contribution, fund, or allocation that remains unless a person actively changes it.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** contribution, fund hoặc allocation investment preselect giữ nguyên nếu không change.
**Grammar & collocations:** `sensible investment default` — default; `review an investment default` — review.
**Examples:** `An investment default kept the new employee saving a small amount until she chose a higher rate.` → Default investment giữ employee mới save amount nhỏ tới khi choose rate cao.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `투자 기본값`.

## 6. diversification friction /daɪˌvɝsəfəˈkeɪʃən ˈfrɪkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** effort, confusion, or cost that makes it difficult to spread investments across different assets.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** effort, confusion hoặc cost làm khó spread investment qua asset khác.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high diversification friction` — friction; `reduce diversification friction` — reduce.
**Examples:** `Diversification friction kept the investor concentrated in a familiar employer stock.` → Friction diversification giữ investor tập trung vào stock employer quen.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `분산 투자 마찰`.

## 7. loss realization aversion /lɔs ˌriːələˈzeɪʃən əˈvɝʒən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** reluctance to sell an asset at a loss because making the loss official feels especially painful.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** reluctance sell asset loss vì make loss official feel painful.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong loss realization aversion` — aversion; `manage loss realization aversion` — manage.
**Examples:** `Loss realization aversion kept the portfolio exposed to a company whose prospects had changed.` → Aversion realization loss giữ portfolio expose company prospects đã đổi.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `손실 확정 회피`.

## 8. market news attention /ˈmɑrkɪt nuːz əˈtɛnʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the amount and direction of attention given to financial news when making investment decisions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** amount và direction attention dành cho news financial khi decide investment.
**Grammar & collocations:** `excessive market news attention` — attention; `limit market news attention` — limit.
**Examples:** `Market news attention made the investor trade after every dramatic headline.` → Attention news market khiến investor trade sau mỗi headline dramatic.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `시장 뉴스 주의`.

## 9. volatility normalization /ˌvɑləˈtɪləti ˌnɔrmələˈzeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** becoming accustomed to price swings so that unusual risk starts to feel ordinary.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** quen price swing tới mức risk unusual feel ordinary.
**Grammar & collocations:** `dangerous volatility normalization` — normalization; `question volatility normalization` — question.
**Examples:** `Volatility normalization made the trader ignore a level of movement that exceeded the original plan.` → Normalization volatility khiến trader ignore movement vượt plan gốc.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `변동성 정상화`.

## 10. dividend framing /ˈdɪvəˌdɛnd ˈfreɪmɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the way dividends are presented as income, reward, safety, or money separated from the asset's value.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cách present dividend như income, reward, safety hoặc money tách khỏi asset value.
**Grammar & collocations:** `income-focused dividend framing` — framing; `change dividend framing` — change.
**Examples:** `Dividend framing made the investor prefer a lower-growth fund because the payment felt tangible.` → Framing dividend khiến investor prefer fund growth thấp vì payment tangible.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `배당 프레이밍`.

## 11. liquidity preference /lɪˈkwɪdəti ˈprɛfərəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a preference for assets that can be converted to cash quickly, even when less liquid assets may earn more.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** preference asset convert cash nhanh dù asset illiquid có thể earn hơn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong liquidity preference` — preference; `balance liquidity preference` — balance.
**Examples:** `Liquidity preference increased before the family expected a large medical payment.` → Preference liquidity tăng trước khi family expect payment medical lớn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `유동성 선호`.

## 12. emergency fund belief /ɪˈmɝdʒənsi fʌnd bɪˈliːf/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a belief about how much readily available savings is necessary to absorb unexpected expenses.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** belief saving available ngay cần bao nhiêu để absorb expense unexpected.
**Grammar & collocations:** `realistic emergency fund belief` — belief; `update an emergency fund belief` — update.
**Examples:** `Emergency fund belief changed after the household reviewed how long unemployment benefits would last.` → Belief fund emergency đổi sau khi household review benefit unemployment kéo dài bao lâu.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `비상 자금 신념`.

## 13. debt repayment motivation /dɛt rɪˈpeɪmənt ˌmoʊtəˈveɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the desire and energy to reduce borrowed money despite delayed benefits and competing spending needs.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** desire và energy reduce debt dù benefit delay và spending need cạnh tranh.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong debt repayment motivation` — motivation; `sustain debt repayment motivation` — sustain.
**Examples:** `Debt repayment motivation rose when the borrower could see one account disappear completely.` → Motivation repayment debt tăng khi borrower thấy một account biến mất hoàn toàn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `부채 상환 동기`.

## 14. financial advice trust /fəˈnænʃəl ədˈvaɪs trʌst/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** confidence that financial guidance is competent, impartial, understandable, and aligned with the client's interests.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** confidence advice financial competent, impartial, understandable và align interest client.
**Grammar & collocations:** `low financial advice trust` — trust; `build financial advice trust` — build.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `재정 조언 신뢰`.

## 15. peer investing cue /pɪr ɪnˈvɛstɪŋ kjuː/

**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** information suggest friend, colleague hoặc group social invest theo cách cụ thể.

## 16. capital gains salience /ˈkæpətəl ɡeɪnz ˈseɪliəns/

**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mức tax hoặc reward từ price increase asset noticeable và influential.
**Grammar & collocations:** `low capital gains salience` — salience; `increase capital gains salience` — increase.

## 17. goal-based saving /ɡoʊl beɪst ˈseɪvɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** saving organized around a clear future purpose, amount, and time rather than around an abstract intention.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** saving organize theo purpose, amount và time future rõ thay vì intention abstract.
**Grammar & collocations:** `effective goal-based saving` — saving; `encourage goal-based saving` — encourage.
**Examples:** `Goal-based saving helped the couple protect a deposit from everyday spending.` → Saving goal-based giúp couple protect deposit khỏi spending hằng ngày.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `목표 기반 저축`.

## 18. spending rebound /ˈspɛndɪŋ ˈriːbaʊnd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an increase in spending after a period of restraint, saving, or perceived financial improvement.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** spending increase sau period restraint, saving hoặc financial improvement perceived.
**Grammar & collocations:** `rapid spending rebound` — rebound; `anticipate spending rebound` — anticipate.
**Examples:** `Spending rebound followed the debt payoff when the family treated the freed payment as disposable income.` → Rebound spending xảy ra sau payoff debt khi family xem payment freed là income disposable.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `지출 반등`.

## 19. financial self-efficacy /fəˈnænʃəl ˌsɛlfˈɛfəkəsi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** confidence that one can understand, plan, and manage personal financial decisions effectively.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** confidence hiểu, plan và manage financial decision personal effective.
**Grammar & collocations:** `low financial self-efficacy` — self-efficacy; `strengthen financial self-efficacy` — strengthen.
**Examples:** `Financial self-efficacy improved after the student practiced comparing loan offers.` → Self-efficacy financial improve sau khi student practice compare loan offer.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `재정 자기효능감`.

## 20. long-term wealth framing /ˌlɔŋ ˈtɝm wɛlθ ˈfreɪmɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** presenting financial decisions as building future security and options rather than chasing immediate performance.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** present decision financial như build security và option future thay vì chase performance immediate.
**Grammar & collocations:** `constructive long-term wealth framing` — framing; `use long-term wealth framing` — use.
**Examples:** `Long-term wealth framing helped the investor ignore a daily price change and follow the plan.` → Framing wealth long-term giúp investor ignore price change daily và follow plan.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `장기 부 프레이밍`.

## Review in context

Investors often react to **portfolio salience** while underestimating fees through a **fee illusion**. **Return expectation** should be compared with **risk capacity perception**, and an **investment default** can either help or trap a saver. **Diversification friction** and **loss realization aversion** keep people concentrated, while **market news attention** and **volatility normalization** encourage reactive trades. **Dividend framing** makes cash payments feel separate from total return, and **liquidity preference** rises when a household expects uncertainty. A realistic **emergency fund belief** supports **debt repayment motivation**, while **financial advice trust** determines whether guidance is used. A **peer investing cue** can overwhelm analysis, and **capital gains salience** changes after-tax choices. **Goal-based saving** protects a purpose, but **spending rebound** may follow a successful payoff. Stronger **financial self-efficacy** and **long-term wealth framing** help people stay with a plan through short-term noise.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

Investor thường react với **portfolio salience** trong khi underestimate fee qua **fee illusion**. **Return expectation** nên compare với **risk capacity perception**, và **investment default** có thể help hoặc trap saver. **Diversification friction** và **loss realization aversion** giữ người ta concentrate, còn **market news attention** và **volatility normalization** khuyến khích trade reactive. **Dividend framing** làm cash payment trông tách khỏi total return, và **liquidity preference** tăng khi household expect uncertainty. **Emergency fund belief** realistic hỗ trợ **debt repayment motivation**, còn **financial advice trust** quyết định guidance có dùng không. **Peer investing cue** có thể overwhelm analysis, và **capital gains salience** đổi lựa chọn after-tax. **Goal-based saving** bảo vệ purpose, nhưng **spending rebound** có thể theo sau payoff thành công. **Financial self-efficacy** mạnh hơn và **long-term wealth framing** giúp stay plan qua noise ngắn hạn.
