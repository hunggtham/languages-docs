# C2+ Vocabulary — Maternal, newborn, and reproductive autonomy

This lesson follows how services protect pregnant people, newborns, families, and reproductive decision-making across the care journey. The network is **preconception care → antenatal risk stratification → respectful maternity care → birth preparedness → obstetric early warning → maternal near miss → postpartum hemorrhage → hypertensive disorder of pregnancy → perinatal mental health → kangaroo mother care → neonatal sepsis → skin-to-skin contact → breastfeeding support → lactation counseling → rooming-in → newborn screening → stillbirth review → perinatal mortality → midwifery continuity → birth companion → obstetric violence → reproductive autonomy → contraceptive counseling → postpartum contraception → maternal transport**.

## 1. preconception care /ˌpriːkənˈsɛpʃən kɛr/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** health care and support provided before pregnancy to improve health, review risks, discuss medicines, and plan for conception if desired.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** preconception care chuẩn bị sức khỏe trước pregnancy, không mặc định ai cũng muốn có con.
**Grammar & collocations:** `preconception-care visit` — visit; `review pregnancy risk` — review; `optimize health` — optimize.
**Examples:** `Preconception care reviewed diabetes control, medicines, nutrition, and the person's reproductive goals.` → Care bắt đầu từ goal và health trước pregnancy.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `임신 전 관리` — preconception care.

## 2. antenatal risk stratification /ˌæntɪˈneɪtəl rɪsk ˌstrætəfəˈkeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** classification of pregnancy-related risk using medical, social, and contextual information to guide monitoring and referral.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** antenatal risk stratification chia mức risk để care intensity phù hợp.
**Grammar & collocations:** `antenatal-risk-stratification tool` — tool; `stratify pregnancy risk` — stratify; `referral threshold` — threshold.
**Examples:** `Antenatal risk stratification identified the need for closer monitoring without labeling the pregnancy as predetermined to fail.` → Stratify để support, không fatalism.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `산전 위험 층화` — antenatal risk stratification.

## 3. respectful maternity care /rɪˈspɛktfəl məˈtɜrnəti kɛr/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** maternity care that protects dignity, privacy, informed choice, communication, and freedom from mistreatment or discrimination.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** respectful maternity care coi pregnant person là decision-maker có dignity.
**Grammar & collocations:** `respectful-maternity-care standard` — standard; `protect dignity in childbirth` — protect; `informed choice` — choice.
**Examples:** `Respectful maternity care explained each examination and asked permission before proceeding.` → Giải thích và xin permission.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `존중하는 모성 돌봄` — respectful maternity care.

## 4. birth preparedness /bɜrθ prɪˈpɛrədnəs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** practical preparation for birth and possible complications, including transport, contacts, supplies, finances, and preferred support.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** birth preparedness biến birth plan thành logistics thực tế.
**Grammar & collocations:** `birth-preparedness plan` — plan; `prepare for delivery` — prepare; `emergency contact` — contact.
**Examples:** `Birth preparedness included a transport plan for nighttime referral and a person authorized to make calls.` → Chuẩn bị cả transport và authority.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `출산 준비` — birth preparedness.

## 5. obstetric early warning /əbˈstɛtrɪk ˈɜrli ˈwɔrnɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a structured system for recognizing physiological deterioration during pregnancy, labor, or after birth and escalating care promptly.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** obstetric early warning bắt deterioration trước khi thành emergency lớn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `obstetric-early-warning chart` — chart; `escalate abnormal observations` — escalate; `maternal deterioration` — deterioration.
**Examples:** `The obstetric early warning system flagged a rising respiratory rate before the patient became unstable.` → Signal sớm giúp response.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `산과 조기 경보` — obstetric early warning.

## 6. maternal near miss /ˈmətɜrnəl nɪr mɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a severe pregnancy or childbirth complication from which the mother survives, often revealing preventable weaknesses in care.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** maternal near miss là case rất nặng nhưng sống sót, cần học để ngăn death.
**Grammar & collocations:** `maternal-near-miss review` — review; `survive a life-threatening complication` — survive; `preventable factor` — factor.
**Examples:** `The maternal near miss review found delays in recognizing bleeding and arranging transport.` → Review tìm delay system.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `산모 중증 생존 사례` — maternal near miss.

## 7. postpartum hemorrhage /ˌpoʊstˈpɑrtəm ˈhɛmərɪdʒ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** heavier-than-expected bleeding after childbirth that can become a life-threatening emergency.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** postpartum hemorrhage là bleeding sau birth cần recognition và response nhanh.
**Grammar & collocations:** `postpartum-hemorrhage protocol` — protocol; `recognize heavy bleeding` — recognize; `emergency response` — response.
**Examples:** `The postpartum hemorrhage protocol clarified who called for blood, transport, and senior help.` → Protocol chia role trong emergency.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `산후 출혈` — postpartum hemorrhage.

## 8. hypertensive disorder of pregnancy /ˌhaɪpərˈtɛnsɪv dɪˈsɔrdər əv ˈprɛɡnənsi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a pregnancy-related condition involving elevated blood pressure and, in some cases, organ or placental complications.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** hypertensive disorder of pregnancy là nhóm condition cần monitoring và timely assessment.
**Grammar & collocations:** `hypertensive-disorder-of-pregnancy pathway` — pathway; `monitor blood pressure` — monitor; `organ involvement` — involvement.
**Examples:** `The pathway linked home blood-pressure monitoring with rapid review of warning symptoms.` → Home monitor nối với referral.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `임신성 고혈압 질환` — hypertensive disorder of pregnancy.

## 9. perinatal mental health /ˌpɛrəˈneɪtəl ˈmɛntl hɛlθ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** mental health during pregnancy and the period around and after birth, including prevention, assessment, and treatment.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** perinatal mental health nhìn parent và baby trong cùng care journey.
**Grammar & collocations:** `perinatal-mental-health service` — service; `screen for depression` — screen; `perinatal support` — support.
**Examples:** `Perinatal mental health support included confidential screening and a referral that did not separate the parent from the baby.` → Care giữ connection parent-baby.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `주산기 정신 건강` — perinatal mental health.

## 10. kangaroo mother care /kæŋɡəˈruː ˈmʌðər kɛr/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** care for a small or premature newborn involving prolonged skin-to-skin contact, breastfeeding support, and close monitoring.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** kangaroo mother care dùng warmth, contact và feeding như một phần neonatal care.
**Grammar & collocations:** `kangaroo-mother-care program` — program; `provide skin-to-skin care` — provide; `preterm newborn` — newborn.
**Examples:** `Kangaroo mother care supported temperature regulation and bonding while the newborn gained strength.` → Contact hỗ trợ nhiều function.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `캥거루 케어` — kangaroo mother care.

## 11. neonatal sepsis /ˌniːoʊˈneɪtəl ˈsɛpsɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a serious systemic infection occurring in a newborn period and requiring urgent assessment and treatment.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** neonatal sepsis là infection nặng ở newborn, cần detection nhanh.
**Grammar & collocations:** `neonatal-sepsis pathway` — pathway; `assess newborn infection` — assess; `urgent treatment` — treatment.
**Examples:** `The neonatal sepsis pathway linked temperature change, feeding difficulty, and breathing signs to urgent review.` → Nhiều sign cùng quan trọng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `신생아 패혈증` — neonatal sepsis.

## 12. skin-to-skin contact /skɪn tə skɪn ˈkɑntækt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** placing a newborn directly against a parent's bare chest to support warmth, bonding, regulation, and early feeding.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** skin-to-skin contact tạo warmth và connection trực tiếp.
**Grammar & collocations:** `skin-to-skin-contact practice` — practice; `place a newborn skin to skin` — place; `early bonding` — bonding.
**Examples:** `Skin-to-skin contact began when the newborn was stable and continued during the first feeding attempt.` → Contact đi cùng stability và feeding.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `피부 대 피부 접촉` — skin-to-skin contact.

## 13. breastfeeding support /ˈbrɛstˌfiːdɪŋ səˈpɔrt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** practical, skilled, and noncoercive help with feeding decisions, positioning, supply concerns, and infant-parent goals.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** breastfeeding support giúp parent theo goal, không biến feeding thành phán xét đạo đức.
**Grammar & collocations:** `breastfeeding-support service` — service; `support feeding goals` — support; `feeding position` — position.
**Examples:** `Breastfeeding support addressed pain and positioning while respecting the parent's informed choice.` → Support không coercion.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `모유 수유 지원` — breastfeeding support.

## 14. lactation counseling /lækˈteɪʃən ˈkaʊnsəlɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** specialized guidance about milk production, feeding technique, infant behavior, and alternatives when feeding is difficult.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** lactation counseling là counseling chuyên môn, không chỉ lời khuyên chung.
**Grammar & collocations:** `lactation-counseling visit` — visit; `address milk-supply concerns` — address; `feeding assessment` — assessment.
**Examples:** `Lactation counseling identified a latch problem and arranged follow-up instead of blaming the parent.` → Tìm kỹ thuật, không blame.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `수유 상담` — lactation counseling.

## 15. rooming-in /ˈruːmɪŋ ɪn/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** practice of keeping a newborn in the same room as the parent rather than routinely separating them.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** rooming-in giữ parent và baby gần nhau để bonding và responsive care.
**Grammar & collocations:** `rooming-in policy` — policy; `keep a newborn nearby` — keep; `parent-infant contact` — contact.
**Examples:** `Rooming-in allowed the parent to recognize feeding cues while staff remained available for support.` → Gần nhau nhưng vẫn có staff support.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `모자동실` — rooming-in.

## 16. newborn screening /ˈnuːbɔrn ˈskriːnɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** testing or examination shortly after birth to identify certain conditions before symptoms become obvious.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** newborn screening tìm condition sớm để follow-up kịp.
**Grammar & collocations:** `newborn-screening panel` — panel; `screen for a condition` — screen; `follow-up test` — test.
**Examples:** `Newborn screening detected a treatable metabolic condition and triggered confirmatory testing.` → Screen mở đường cho confirm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `신생아 선별검사` — newborn screening.

## 17. stillbirth review /ˈstɪlˌbɜrθ rɪˈvjuː/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** structured, compassionate examination of a stillbirth to identify clinical, social, and system factors and support learning.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** stillbirth review học từ loss mà không đổ lỗi gia đình.
**Grammar & collocations:** `stillbirth-review panel` — panel; `review contributing factors` — review; `bereavement-sensitive process` — process.
**Examples:** `The stillbirth review found gaps in follow-up and communication while offering the family a separate explanation meeting.` → Review vừa learning vừa care.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `사산 검토` — stillbirth review.

## 18. perinatal mortality /ˌpɛrəˈneɪtəl mɔrˈtæləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** deaths occurring around birth, usually including late fetal deaths and early newborn deaths according to a defined measure.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** perinatal mortality là measure population quanh birth, cần định nghĩa rõ.
**Grammar & collocations:** `perinatal-mortality rate` — rate; `measure perinatal deaths` — measure; `defined time window` — window.
**Examples:** `The district tracked perinatal mortality by referral delay, birth setting, and socioeconomic group.` → Rate được phân tích theo context.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `주산기 사망률` — perinatal mortality.

## 19. midwifery continuity /ˌmɪdˈwaɪfəri ˌkɑntəˈnuəti/

**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** midwifery continuity giữ relationship và information liền mạch.
**Grammar & collocations:** `midwifery-continuity model` — model; `provide continuity of midwifery care` — provide; `known care team` — team.
**Examples:** `Midwifery continuity made it easier to notice subtle changes and discuss preferences early.` → Known team nhận ra change sớm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `조산사 연속 돌봄` — midwifery continuity.

## 20. birth companion /bɜrθ kəmˈpænjən/

## 21. obstetric violence /əbˈstɛtrɪk ˈvaɪələns/

**Core meaning (English):** physical, verbal, psychological, or procedural mistreatment during reproductive or maternity care, including care without meaningful consent.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** obstetric violence là mistreatment trong care sinh sản, làm mất dignity và autonomy.
**Grammar & collocations:** `obstetric-violence complaint` — complaint; `prevent mistreatment` — prevent; `nonconsensual procedure` — procedure.
**Examples:** `The review addressed obstetric violence by examining consent, language, restraint, and unequal treatment.` → Review nhìn cả procedure và power.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `산과 폭력` — obstetric violence.

## 22. reproductive autonomy /rɪˈprəˌdʌktɪv ɔˈtɑnəmi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a person's ability to make voluntary, informed decisions about pregnancy, contraception, birth, and reproductive health.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** reproductive autonomy là quyền quyết định về reproduction không bị ép hay thao túng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `reproductive-autonomy principle` — principle; `protect reproductive choice` — protect; `informed decision` — decision.
**Examples:** `Reproductive autonomy required presenting contraception and pregnancy options without steering the patient toward one choice.` → Cung cấp option không steering.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `생식 자율성` — reproductive autonomy.

## 23. contraceptive counseling /ˌkɑntrəˈsɛptɪv ˈkaʊnsəlɪŋ/

## 24. postpartum contraception /ˌpoʊstˈpɑrtəm ˌkɑntrəˈsɛpʃən/

## 25. maternal transport /məˈtɜrnəl ˈtrænspɔrt/

**Core meaning (English):** organized movement of a pregnant or postpartum person to a facility capable of providing needed assessment or treatment.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** maternal transport nối patient với facility đúng capability khi cần referral.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `산모 이송` — maternal transport.

## Review in context — Part 1

**Preconception care** can lead to **antenatal risk stratification** and **respectful maternity care**. **Birth preparedness** includes transport and contacts, while **obstetric early warning** identifies deterioration. A **maternal near miss**, **postpartum hemorrhage**, or a **hypertensive disorder of pregnancy** requires learning as well as clinical response. **Perinatal mental health** support belongs in the same journey. For newborns, **kangaroo mother care**, **neonatal sepsis** awareness, **skin-to-skin contact**, **breastfeeding support**, **lactation counseling**, and **rooming-in** can be coordinated with safety needs.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 1:**

**Preconception care** có thể dẫn tới **antenatal risk stratification** và **respectful maternity care**. **Birth preparedness** gồm transport và contact, còn **obstetric early warning** phát hiện deterioration. **Maternal near miss**, **postpartum hemorrhage** hoặc **hypertensive disorder of pregnancy** cần learning cùng clinical response. Support **perinatal mental health** thuộc cùng journey. Với newborn, **kangaroo mother care**, nhận biết **neonatal sepsis**, **skin-to-skin contact**, **breastfeeding support**, **lactation counseling** và **rooming-in** có thể được phối hợp với safety.

## Review in context — Part 2

**Newborn screening** identifies conditions early, while a compassionate **stillbirth review** supports learning after loss. **Perinatal mortality** should be examined by context and inequity. **Midwifery continuity** and a chosen **birth companion** can strengthen communication; preventing **obstetric violence** protects **reproductive autonomy**. **Contraceptive counseling** and **postpartum contraception** must remain voluntary, and a reliable **maternal transport** plan connects people to appropriate care.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 2:**

**Newborn screening** tìm condition sớm, còn **stillbirth review** giàu compassion hỗ trợ learning sau loss. **Perinatal mortality** nên được phân tích theo context và inequity. **Midwifery continuity** và **birth companion** do patient chọn có thể tăng communication; ngăn **obstetric violence** bảo vệ **reproductive autonomy**. **Contraceptive counseling** và **postpartum contraception** phải voluntary, còn plan **maternal transport** đáng tin nối patient với facility phù hợp.
