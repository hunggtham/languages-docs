# Writing: My career plan

Target: about 150 words.

## Questions to answer

- What is your current role?
- What role or responsibility do you want next?
- Which skills do you need?
- What will you do during the next one to three years?

## Suggested structure

1. Current situation.
2. Future goal and motivation.
3. Two concrete actions and a realistic timeline.

## Useful sentence starters

- I currently work as …
- My long-term goal is to …
- To achieve this goal, I need to improve …
- First, I am going to …
- I also plan to …
- If I follow this plan, I will be able to …
