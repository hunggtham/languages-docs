# C2 Vocabulary — Educational and social robotics

This lesson follows a robot from classroom support and tutoring to socially assistive interaction and respectful dialogue. The network is **educational robot → classroom robot → tutoring robot → teaching assistant robot → social robot → conversational robot → emotion-aware robot → affect recognition → facial expression recognition → prosody recognition → engagement estimation → attention estimation → learning progress estimation → adaptive tutoring → personalized instruction → robot-mediated learning → collaborative learning robot → peer robot → robot ethics curriculum → robot programming education**.

## 1. educational robot /ˌedʒəˈkeɪʃənəl ˈroʊbɑːt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a robot used to support teaching, practice, demonstration, or exploration of ideas.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** robot hỗ trợ teaching, practice, demonstration hoặc khám phá ý tưởng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `educational-robot activity` — activity; `demonstrate a science concept` — demonstrate concept khoa học.
**Examples:** `The educational robot demonstrated orbital motion with a small moving model.` → Educational robot minh họa orbital motion bằng model nhỏ chuyển động.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `교육 로봇`.

## 2. classroom robot /ˈklæsruːm ˈroʊbɑːt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a robot that operates in a classroom to assist teachers, students, or activities.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** robot hoạt động trong classroom để hỗ trợ teacher, student hoặc activity.
**Grammar & collocations:** `classroom-robot routine` — routine; `circulate among student groups` — di chuyển giữa group học sinh.
**Examples:** `The classroom robot delivered materials to each group during the experiment.` → Classroom robot giao material tới từng group trong experiment.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `교실 로봇`.

## 3. tutoring robot /ˈtuːtərɪŋ ˈroʊbɑːt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a robot that provides guided practice, hints, feedback, or explanation in a subject.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** robot cung cấp practice, hint, feedback hoặc explanation có hướng dẫn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `tutoring-robot prompt` — prompt; `offer a hint without giving the answer` — đưa hint mà không cho answer.
**Examples:** `The tutoring robot asked the learner to explain the next algebra step.` → Tutoring robot yêu cầu learner giải thích bước algebra tiếp.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `튜터링 로봇`.

## 4. teaching assistant robot /ˈtiːtʃɪŋ əˈsɪstənt ˈroʊbɑːt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a robot that supports a teacher with demonstrations, reminders, materials, or routine questions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** robot hỗ trợ teacher bằng demonstration, reminder, material hoặc routine question.
**Grammar & collocations:** `teaching-assistant-robot dashboard` — dashboard; `answer a procedural question` — trả procedural question.
**Examples:** `The teaching assistant robot reminded the class to record the measurement units.` → Teaching assistant robot nhắc class ghi unit measurement.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `교육 조교 로봇`.

## 5. social robot /ˈsoʊʃəl ˈroʊbɑːt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a robot designed to interact with people through communication, social cues, and shared activities.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** robot thiết kế tương tác với người qua communication, social cue và activity chung.
**Grammar & collocations:** `social-robot behavior` — behavior; `respond to a greeting` — phản hồi greeting.
**Examples:** `The social robot greeted each visitor and adjusted its distance to the conversation.` → Social robot chào visitor và chỉnh distance theo conversation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `소셜 로봇`.

## 6. conversational robot /ˌkɑːnvərˈseɪʃənəl ˈroʊbɑːt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a robot that maintains spoken or text interaction over multiple turns.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** robot duy trì interaction spoken/text qua nhiều lượt.
**Grammar & collocations:** `conversational-robot policy` — policy; `maintain dialogue context` — giữ context dialogue.
**Examples:** `The conversational robot remembered the visitor’s earlier question about the exhibit.` → Conversational robot nhớ câu hỏi trước của visitor về exhibit.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `대화형 로봇`.

## 7. emotion-aware robot /ɪˈmoʊʃən əˈwer ˈroʊbɑːt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a robot that adapts interaction using estimates of a person’s emotional state.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** robot adapt interaction dựa trên estimate emotional state của người.
**Grammar & collocations:** `emotion-aware-robot response` — response; `reduce conversational pressure` — giảm pressure hội thoại.
**Examples:** `The emotion-aware robot slowed its explanation when the learner appeared frustrated.` → Emotion-aware robot làm chậm explanation khi learner có vẻ frustrated.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `감정 인지 로봇`.

## 8. affect recognition /əˈfekt ˌrekəɡˈnɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** estimating a person’s emotional expression or affective state from observable signals.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** estimate emotional expression hoặc affective state từ signal quan sát.
**Grammar & collocations:** `affect-recognition model` — model; `estimate uncertainty in emotion` — estimate uncertainty cảm xúc.
**Examples:** `Affect recognition treated the tired expression as uncertain rather than as a definite mood.` → Affect recognition coi expression mệt là uncertain chứ không phải mood chắc chắn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `정서 인식`.

## 9. facial expression recognition /ˈfeɪʃəl ɪkˈspreʃən ˌrekəɡˈnɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** interpreting visible facial movements as possible signals of expression or attention.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** diễn giải facial movement thấy được thành signal expression/attention có thể.
**Grammar & collocations:** `facial-expression-recognition confidence` — confidence; `detect a smile or frown` — detect smile/frown.
**Examples:** `Facial expression recognition noticed a smile but did not assume agreement.` → Facial expression recognition nhận thấy smile nhưng không giả định đồng ý.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `얼굴 표정 인식`.

## 10. prosody recognition /ˈprɑːsədi ˌrekəɡˈnɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** interpreting pitch, rhythm, stress, and timing in speech as interaction signals.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** diễn giải pitch, rhythm, stress và timing trong speech thành interaction signal.
**Grammar & collocations:** `prosody-recognition feature` — feature; `detect an emphasized warning` — detect warning được nhấn.
**Examples:** `Prosody recognition detected hesitation before the student answered.` → Prosody recognition detect hesitation trước khi student trả lời.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `운율 인식`.

## 11. engagement estimation /ɪnˈɡeɪdʒmənt ˌestəˈmeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** estimating how actively and continuously a person is participating in an activity.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** estimate mức người tham gia active và liên tục trong activity.
**Grammar & collocations:** `engagement-estimation signal` — signal; `detect declining participation` — detect participation giảm.
**Examples:** `Engagement estimation prompted the robot to vary the practice activity.` → Engagement estimation khiến robot thay đổi practice activity.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `참여도 추정`.

## 12. attention estimation /əˈtenʃən ˌestəˈmeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** estimating whether and where a person is attending to a task, object, or speaker.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** estimate người có đang chú ý và chú ý vào task/object/speaker nào.
**Grammar & collocations:** `attention-estimation cue` — cue; `detect gaze away from the lesson` — detect gaze rời lesson.
**Examples:** `Attention estimation suggested that the learner was looking at the wrong diagram.` → Attention estimation cho thấy learner nhìn nhầm diagram.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `주의 추정`.

## 13. learning progress estimation /ˈlɜːrnɪŋ ˈprɑːɡres ˌestəˈmeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** estimating how a learner’s knowledge or skill changes across practice.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** estimate knowledge/skill learner thay đổi qua practice.
**Grammar & collocations:** `learning-progress-estimation model` — model; `track mastery over attempts` — theo dõi mastery qua attempt.
**Examples:** `Learning progress estimation showed that the learner needed another example of proportional reasoning.` → Learning progress estimation cho thấy learner cần example khác về proportional reasoning.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `학습 진도 추정`.

## 14. adaptive tutoring /əˈdæptɪv ˈtuːtərɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** tutoring that changes explanation, difficulty, pacing, or practice based on learner state.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** tutoring đổi explanation, difficulty, pace hoặc practice theo learner state.
**Grammar & collocations:** `adaptive-tutoring policy` — policy; `adjust problem difficulty` — chỉnh difficulty problem.
**Examples:** `Adaptive tutoring returned to a simpler example after two failed attempts.` → Adaptive tutoring quay lại example đơn giản sau hai attempt fail.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `적응형 튜터링`.

## 15. personalized instruction /ˈpɜːrsənəlaɪzd ɪnˈstrʌkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** instruction adjusted to a particular learner’s goals, prior knowledge, pace, or needs.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** instruction chỉnh theo goal, prior knowledge, pace hoặc need của learner cụ thể.
**Grammar & collocations:** `personalized-instruction plan` — plan; `select a relevant example` — chọn example relevant.
**Examples:** `Personalized instruction used sports data because the learner had asked for that context.` → Personalized instruction dùng sports data vì learner yêu cầu context đó.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `개인 맞춤형 교육`.

## 16. robot-mediated learning /ˈroʊbɑːt ˈmiːdieɪtɪd ˈlɜːrnɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** learning in which a robot structures, supports, or delivers interaction between learners and content.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** learning nơi robot structure, support hoặc deliver interaction giữa learner và content.
**Grammar & collocations:** `robot-mediated-learning activity` — activity; `mediate peer practice` — trung gian peer practice.
**Examples:** `Robot-mediated learning gave the remote student a physical partner for the experiment.` → Robot-mediated learning cho remote student một partner physical cho experiment.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `로봇 매개 학습`.

## 17. collaborative learning robot /kəˈlæbərətɪv ˈlɜːrnɪŋ ˈroʊbɑːt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a robot that helps learners coordinate, explain, and solve a problem together.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** robot giúp learner coordinate, explain và solve problem cùng nhau.
**Grammar & collocations:** `collaborative-learning-robot prompt` — prompt; `balance speaking turns` — cân lượt nói.
**Examples:** `The collaborative learning robot asked each partner to justify a different solution.` → Collaborative learning robot yêu cầu mỗi partner justify solution khác nhau.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `협력 학습 로봇`.

## 18. peer robot /pɪr ˈroʊbɑːt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a robot presented as a fellow learner or partner rather than only as an instructor.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** robot được trình bày như fellow learner/partner thay vì chỉ instructor.
**Grammar & collocations:** `peer-robot interaction` — interaction; `share an uncertain answer` — chia sẻ answer chưa chắc.
**Examples:** `The peer robot made a deliberate mistake so the learner could correct it.` → Peer robot cố ý mắc lỗi để learner sửa.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `또래 로봇`.

## 19. robot ethics curriculum /ˈroʊbɑːt ˈeθɪks kəˈrɪkjələm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a planned set of lessons about responsible design, use, and governance of robots.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** curriculum lesson về design, use và governance robot có trách nhiệm.
**Grammar & collocations:** `robot-ethics-curriculum module` — module; `discuss accountability in automation` — thảo luận accountability automation.
**Examples:** `The robot ethics curriculum asked students to compare convenience with privacy.` → Robot ethics curriculum yêu cầu student so sánh convenience với privacy.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `로봇 윤리 교육과정`.

## 20. robot programming education /ˈroʊbɑːt ˈproʊɡræmɪŋ ˌedʒəˈkeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** teaching people to design, code, test, and debug robot behavior.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** dạy người design, code, test và debug robot behavior.
**Grammar & collocations:** `robot-programming-education platform` — platform; `debug a sensor callback` — debug sensor callback.
**Examples:** `Robot programming education used a simulator before students connected real motors.` → Robot programming education dùng simulator trước khi student nối motor thật.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `로봇 프로그래밍 교육`.

## Review in context

An **educational robot**, **classroom robot**, **tutoring robot**, or **teaching assistant robot** supports learning; a **social robot** and **conversational robot** make interaction natural. An **emotion-aware robot** may use **affect recognition**, **facial expression recognition**, and **prosody recognition**, while **engagement estimation** and **attention estimation** track participation. **Learning progress estimation** guides **adaptive tutoring** and **personalized instruction**. **Robot-mediated learning**, a **collaborative learning robot**, and a **peer robot** structure shared practice, while a **robot ethics curriculum** and **robot programming education** teach responsible creation.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:** **Educational robot**, **classroom robot**, **tutoring robot** hoặc **teaching assistant robot** hỗ trợ learning; **social robot** và **conversational robot** làm interaction tự nhiên. **Emotion-aware robot** có thể dùng **affect recognition**, **facial expression recognition** và **prosody recognition**, còn **engagement estimation** và **attention estimation** theo dõi participation. **Learning progress estimation** dẫn **adaptive tutoring** và **personalized instruction**. **Robot-mediated learning**, **collaborative learning robot** và **peer robot** tổ chức practice chung, còn **robot ethics curriculum** và **robot programming education** dạy việc tạo robot có trách nhiệm.
