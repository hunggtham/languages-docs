# C1 Vocabulary 27 — Behavioral sport and performance

This lesson follows how athletes, coaches, officials, and fans interpret effort, risk, fairness, identity, and uncertainty in competitive performance.

Flow: **competition motivation → home advantage perception → referee trust → effort substitution → performance pressure → team cohesion belief → fan identity cue → ticket price salience → loss aversion match → coaching feedback framing → injury risk perception → recovery expectation sport → talent belief → selection bias perception → rule fairness perception → crowd contagion → rivalry intensity → sportsmanship norm → performance variance tolerance → athlete role identity**.

## 1. competition motivation /ˌkɑmpəˈtɪʃən ˌmoʊtəˈveɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the desire to improve or perform that arises from comparing one's result with others.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** desire improve hoặc perform từ việc compare result mình với người khác.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong competition motivation` — motivation; `channel competition motivation` — channel.
**Examples:** `Competition motivation pushed the runner to practice consistently without turning every session into a crisis.` → Motivation competition thúc runner practice consistent mà không biến session thành crisis.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `경쟁 동기`.

## 2. home advantage perception /hoʊm ədˈvæntɪdʒ pərˈsɛpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a belief that performing at a familiar venue provides a systematic benefit over visiting competitors.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** belief venue quen tạo benefit systematic so với competitor visiting.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong home advantage perception` — perception; `test home advantage perception` — test.
**Examples:** `Home advantage perception made the new team expect support before the first whistle.` → Perception advantage home khiến team mới expect support trước whistle đầu.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `홈 어드밴티지 인식`.

## 3. referee trust /ˈrɛfəri trʌst/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** confidence that officials apply rules competently, consistently, and without favoritism.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** confidence official apply rule competent, consistent và không favoritism.
**Grammar & collocations:** `low referee trust` — trust; `restore referee trust` — restore.
**Examples:** `Referee trust fell after two similar challenges received visibly different decisions.` → Trust referee giảm sau hai challenge giống nhau nhận decision khác rõ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `심판 신뢰`.

## 4. effort substitution /ˈɛfərt ˌsʌbstəˈtuːʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** replacing one kind of effort with another when the original route becomes costly, difficult, or blocked.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** replace loại effort này bằng loại khác khi route gốc costly, difficult hoặc blocked.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strategic effort substitution` — substitution; `observe effort substitution` — observe.
**Examples:** `Effort substitution led the player to study opponents more carefully after speed training stalled.` → Substitution effort khiến player study opponent kỹ hơn sau khi speed training stall.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `노력 대체`.

## 5. performance pressure /pərˈfɔrməns ˈprɛʃər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** psychological pressure created by high expectations, evaluation, or consequences attached to performance.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** pressure psychological do expectation cao, evaluation hoặc consequence gắn với performance.
**Grammar & collocations:** `intense performance pressure` — pressure; `manage performance pressure` — manage.
**Examples:** `Performance pressure made the skilled goalkeeper choose a safe pass when an attacking move was needed.` → Pressure performance khiến goalkeeper giỏi choose pass safe khi cần attack.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `수행 압박`.

## 6. team cohesion belief /tiːm koʊˈhiːʒən bɪˈliːf/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a belief that teammates trust one another, coordinate reliably, and remain committed under stress.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** belief teammate trust, coordinate reliable và remain committed khi stress.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong team cohesion belief` — belief; `strengthen team cohesion beliefs` — strengthen.
**Examples:** `Team cohesion belief helped the substitutes communicate clearly after the starting captain left injured.` → Belief cohesion team giúp substitute communicate rõ sau captain start injured.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `팀 결속 신념`.

## 7. fan identity cue /fæn aɪˈdɛntəti kjuː/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a symbol or experience that makes a supporter feel connected to a team and its community.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** symbol hoặc experience làm supporter feel connected với team và community.
**Grammar & collocations:** `powerful fan identity cue` — cue; `create a fan identity cue` — create.
**Examples:** `A fan identity cue turned a shared scarf into a reminder of family tradition.` → Cue identity fan biến scarf shared thành reminder tradition family.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `팬 정체성 단서`.

## 8. ticket price salience /ˈtɪkɪt praɪs ˈseɪliəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** how noticeable and psychologically important the price of attending an event is.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mức ticket price noticeable và psychologically important khi attend event.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high ticket price salience` — salience; `reduce ticket price salience` — reduce.
**Examples:** `Ticket price salience increased when service fees appeared only at the final payment step.` → Salience price ticket tăng khi fee service chỉ appear ở payment cuối.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `티켓 가격 현저성`.

## 9. loss aversion match /lɔs əˈvɝʒən mætʃ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a tendency for fear of losing a particular contest to outweigh the pleasure of winning it.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** fear lose contest cụ thể outweigh pleasure win contest.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong loss aversion match` — aversion; `manage loss aversion match` — manage.
**Examples:** `Loss aversion match made the leading team defend too cautiously in the final minutes.` → Aversion loss match khiến team lead defend quá cautious phút cuối.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `경기 손실 회피`.

## 10. coaching feedback framing /ˈkoʊtʃɪŋ ˈfiːdbæk ˈfreɪmɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the way coaching information is presented as correction, progress, threat, or opportunity.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cách present coaching information như correction, progress, threat hoặc opportunity.
**Grammar & collocations:** `constructive coaching feedback framing` — framing; `change coaching feedback framing` — change.
**Examples:** `Coaching feedback framing that named a next action helped the athlete use criticism immediately.` → Framing feedback coaching name next action giúp athlete use criticism ngay.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `코칭 피드백 프레이밍`.

## 11. injury risk perception /ˈɪndʒəri rɪsk pərˈsɛpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a judgment about how likely and serious physical harm is during a sport or training activity.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** judgment physical harm likely và serious khi sport hoặc training.
**Grammar & collocations:** `elevated injury risk perception` — perception; `calibrate injury risk perception` — calibrate.
**Examples:** `Injury risk perception rose after the surface became slippery during repeated drills.` → Perception risk injury tăng sau khi surface slippery trong drill lặp.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `부상 위험 인식`.

## 12. recovery expectation sport /rɪˈkʌvəri ˌɛkspɛkˈteɪʃən spɔrt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a belief about how quickly an athlete will regain strength, confidence, and performance after injury or exhaustion.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** belief athlete regain strength, confidence và performance nhanh thế nào sau injury hoặc exhaustion.
**Grammar & collocations:** `realistic recovery expectation sport` — expectation; `revise recovery expectations sport` — revise.
**Examples:** `Recovery expectation sport became more realistic after the coach explained the stages of rehabilitation.` → Expectation recovery sport realistic hơn sau khi coach explain stage rehabilitation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `스포츠 회복 기대`.

## 13. talent belief /ˈtælənt bɪˈliːf/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a belief about whether performance mainly reflects fixed natural ability or learnable practice.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** belief performance reflect ability fixed hay practice learnable chủ yếu.
**Grammar & collocations:** `fixed talent belief` — belief; `challenge a talent belief` — challenge.
**Examples:** `A fixed talent belief made the beginner interpret one poor result as proof of permanent inability.` → Belief talent fixed khiến beginner xem result kém là proof inability permanent.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `재능 신념`.

## 14. selection bias perception /sɪˈlɛkʃən baɪəs pərˈsɛpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a judgment that visible performers are not representative because selection has favored certain backgrounds or opportunities.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** judgment performer visible không representative vì selection favor background hoặc opportunity.
**Grammar & collocations:** `accurate selection bias perception` — perception; `explain selection bias perception` — explain.
**Examples:** `Selection bias perception made the scout question whether the trial reflected skill or access to elite coaching.` → Perception bias selection khiến scout question trial reflect skill hay access coaching elite.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `선발 편향 인식`.

## 15. rule fairness perception /ruːl ˈfɛrnəs pərˈsɛpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a judgment about whether rules are clear, consistently applied, and respectful to all competitors.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** judgment rule clear, consistently apply và respectful với competitor.
**Grammar & collocations:** `low rule fairness perception` — perception; `improve rule fairness perception` — improve.
**Examples:** `Rule fairness perception fell when the tournament changed eligibility after matches had begun.` → Perception fairness rule giảm khi tournament đổi eligibility sau match bắt đầu.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `규칙 공정성 인식`.

## 16. crowd contagion /kraʊd kənˈteɪdʒən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the spread of emotion, attention, or behavior through a crowd as people respond to one another.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** emotion, attention hoặc behavior spread qua crowd khi người ta respond lẫn nhau.
**Grammar & collocations:** `rapid crowd contagion` — contagion; `monitor crowd contagion` — monitor.
**Examples:** `Crowd contagion turned a doubtful reaction into loud support after one section began chanting.` → Contagion crowd biến reaction doubtful thành support loud sau khi một section chant.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `군중 전염`.

## 17. rivalry intensity /ˈraɪvəlri ɪnˈtɛnsəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the emotional and competitive strength of an ongoing contest between teams, individuals, or groups.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** strength emotional và competitive của contest kéo dài giữa team, individual hoặc group.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high rivalry intensity` — intensity; `manage rivalry intensity` — manage.
**Examples:** `Rivalry intensity increased ticket demand but also made minor mistakes feel like insults.` → Intensity rivalry tăng ticket demand nhưng làm mistake nhỏ trông như insult.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `라이벌 관계 강도`.

## 18. sportsmanship norm /ˈspɔrtsmənˌʃɪp nɔrm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a shared expectation that competitors should show respect, restraint, and fairness regardless of the result.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** expectation competitor show respect, restraint và fairness dù result nào.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong sportsmanship norm` — norm; `reinforce a sportsmanship norm` — reinforce.
**Examples:** `A sportsmanship norm encouraged the captain to check on an injured opponent before celebrating.` → Norm sportsmanship khuyến khích captain check opponent injured trước celebrate.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `스포츠맨십 규범`.

## 19. performance variance tolerance /pərˈfɔrməns ˈvɛriəns ˈtɑlərəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** willingness to accept natural fluctuations in performance without treating every dip as failure.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** willingness accept fluctuation natural performance mà không xem dip nào cũng là failure.
**Grammar & collocations:** `low performance variance tolerance` — tolerance; `build performance variance tolerance` — build.
**Examples:** `Performance variance tolerance helped the coach evaluate a season rather than one unlucky match.` → Tolerance variance performance giúp coach evaluate season thay vì một match unlucky.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `성과 변동 허용`.

## 20. athlete role identity /ˈæθliːt roʊl aɪˈdɛntəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the extent to which being an athlete forms a central part of a person's self-concept and daily choices.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mức being athlete là phần central của self-concept và daily choice.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong athlete role identity` — identity; `rebuild athlete role identity` — rebuild.
**Examples:** `Athlete role identity made retirement emotionally difficult even after the body could no longer compete.` → Identity role athlete khiến retirement emotional khó dù body không compete được.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `선수 역할 정체성`.

## Review in context

**Competition motivation** can improve effort, but **home advantage perception** and **referee trust** shape whether performance feels controllable. **Effort substitution** helps when one training route fails, while **performance pressure** can make a skilled decision too cautious. **Team cohesion belief** supports coordination, and a **fan identity cue** turns attendance into belonging. **Ticket price salience** affects access, while **loss aversion match** can make a leading team protect a narrow advantage. **Coaching feedback framing** determines whether criticism becomes a next step. **Injury risk perception** and **recovery expectation sport** shape persistence, while a fixed **talent belief** limits learning. **Selection bias perception** reminds observers that visible success may reflect opportunity, and **rule fairness perception** keeps competition legitimate. **Crowd contagion** and **rivalry intensity** amplify emotion, but a **sportsmanship norm** can constrain harm. **Performance variance tolerance** protects long-term development, and **athlete role identity** shapes how people adapt when competition ends.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

**Competition motivation** có thể improve effort, nhưng **home advantage perception** và **referee trust** shape performance có cảm thấy controllable không. **Effort substitution** giúp khi training route này fail, còn **performance pressure** khiến decision skilled quá cautious. **Team cohesion belief** hỗ trợ coordination, và **fan identity cue** biến attendance thành belonging. **Ticket price salience** ảnh hưởng access, còn **loss aversion match** làm team lead bảo vệ advantage narrow. **Coaching feedback framing** quyết định criticism thành next step hay không. **Injury risk perception** và **recovery expectation sport** shape persistence, còn **talent belief** fixed limit learning. **Selection bias perception** nhắc visible success có thể reflect opportunity, và **rule fairness perception** giữ competition legitimate. **Crowd contagion** và **rivalry intensity** amplify emotion, nhưng **sportsmanship norm** constrain harm. **Performance variance tolerance** bảo vệ development dài hạn, còn **athlete role identity** shape cách adapt khi competition end.
