# Virology: Infection Dynamics and Phage Biology

## Contextual Network
This lesson explores the intricate lifecycle and impact of viruses, moving from initial exposure and **viremia** to **viral shedding** and ultimate **viral clearance**. It covers varied viral behaviors, such as establishing a **latent infection** in a **viral reservoir**, experiencing **viral uncoating** and **viral egress**, and preventing competitors through **superinfection exclusion**. It contrasts destructive pathways like a **lytic cycle** ending in a massive **burst size** and a **cytocidal infection**, against integrated states like a **lysogenic cycle** harboring a **prophage**, or an incomplete **abortive infection**. We examine unique viral forms including a **defective interfering particle**, an **endogenous retrovirus**, a naked plant-infecting **viroid**, and an engineered **pseudovirus** or **viral vector**. It touches upon structural components like the **nucleocapsid**, and population-level concepts such as an **oncogenic virus**, a **neurotropic virus**, the broad **virome**, and a bacteria-killing **bacteriophage**, often quantified by a **hemagglutination assay**.

## 1. viremia /vaɪˈriːmiə/

**Core meaning (English):** the presence of viruses in the bloodstream.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** sự hiện diện của virus trong bloodstream.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `바이러스혈증`.

## Review in Context

Following exposure, a host may experience significant **viremia**, later followed by **viral shedding** until immune mechanisms achieve **viral clearance**. A **neurotropic virus** or an **oncogenic virus** might establish a **latent infection** within a persistent **viral reservoir**. During a productive infection, **viral uncoating** releases the **nucleocapsid** contents; later, **viral egress** disperses new virions. However, a cell might only suffer an **abortive infection**, or it could harbor a **defective interfering particle** that exploits replication machinery. Sometimes, a prior infection triggers **superinfection exclusion**. In bacteria, a **bacteriophage** can enter a **lytic cycle**, generating a huge **burst size** and a rapid **cytocidal infection**, or a **lysogenic cycle** where a **prophage** quietly integrates. Researchers explore the vast **virome** and ancient **endogenous retrovirus** sequences, use a **hemagglutination assay** for quantification, discover RNA-only pathogens like a **viroid**, and construct a safe **pseudovirus** or **viral vector** for gene delivery.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:** Sau khi phơi nhiễm, host có thể trải qua **viremia** đáng kể, sau đó là **viral shedding** cho đến khi immune mechanism đạt được **viral clearance**. Một **neurotropic virus** hoặc **oncogenic virus** có thể thiết lập **latent infection** trong một **viral reservoir** dai dẳng. Trong productive infection, **viral uncoating** release thành phần **nucleocapsid**; sau đó, **viral egress** phát tán virion mới. Tuy nhiên, một cell có thể chỉ bị **abortive infection**, hoặc chứa **defective interfering particle** lợi dụng replication machinery. Đôi khi, infection trước đó trigger **superinfection exclusion**. Ở vi khuẩn, một **bacteriophage** có thể vào **lytic cycle**, tạo **burst size** khổng lồ và **cytocidal infection** nhanh, hoặc **lysogenic cycle** nơi một **prophage** âm thầm integrate. Các researcher khám phá **virome** bao la và trình tự **endogenous retrovirus** cổ đại, dùng **hemagglutination assay** để định lượng, phát hiện pathogen chỉ có RNA như **viroid**, và chế tạo **pseudovirus** hoặc **viral vector** an toàn để phân phối gene.
