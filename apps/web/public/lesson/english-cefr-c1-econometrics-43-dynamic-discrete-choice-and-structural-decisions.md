# C1 Vocabulary 43 — Dynamic discrete choice and structural decisions

This lesson follows how economists model choices that depend on current states, future consequences, and the information available to decision-makers.

Flow: **dynamic discrete choice → state dependence → forward-looking agent → Bellman equation → inclusive value → choice-specific value → transition kernel → dynamic decision problem → finite dependence → conditional choice probability → policy function → structural estimation → simulated likelihood → reservation value → dynamic choice model → entry decision → exit decision → discrete state → optimal stopping → choice-specific shock**.

## 1. dynamic discrete choice /daɪˈnæmɪk dɪˈskriːt tʃɔɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a model of decisions among separate alternatives when today's choice changes future opportunities or conditions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** model decision giữa alternatives tách biệt khi choice hôm nay đổi opportunity hoặc condition future.
**Grammar & collocations:** `dynamic discrete-choice model` — model; `estimate dynamic discrete choice` — estimate.
**Examples:** `Dynamic discrete choice captured how replacing a car changed the value of later options.` → Choice discrete dynamic capture việc replace car đổi value của option sau.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `동적 이산 선택`.

## 2. state dependence /steɪt dɪˈpɛndəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the idea that a person's current choice depends partly on their past choices or current state.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** choice hiện tại phụ thuộc một phần vào choice past hoặc state hiện tại.
**Grammar & collocations:** `genuine state dependence` — dependence; `test for state dependence` — test.
**Examples:** `State dependence meant that a previous subscription made renewal more likely.` → Dependence state nghĩa là subscription trước làm renewal likely hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `상태 의존성`.

## 3. forward-looking agent /ˈfɔrwərd ˈlʊkɪŋ ˈeɪdʒənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a decision-maker who considers how current actions will affect future benefits, costs, and opportunities.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** decision-maker cân nhắc action hiện tại ảnh hưởng future benefit, cost và opportunity thế nào.
**Grammar & collocations:** `rational forward-looking agent` — agent; `model a forward-looking agent` — model.
**Examples:** `A forward-looking agent may accept a short-term loss to secure a better future position.` → Agent forward-looking có thể accept loss ngắn hạn để secure position future tốt hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `미래 지향적 행위자`.

## 4. Bellman equation /ˈbɛlmən ɪˈkweɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a recursive equation that expresses the value of a current state as the best current payoff plus expected future value.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** equation recursive express value state hiện tại bằng payoff hiện tại tốt nhất cộng expected value future.
**Grammar & collocations:** `solve the Bellman equation` — solve; `dynamic Bellman equation` — equation.
**Examples:** `The Bellman equation linked today's investment decision to future profits.` → Equation Bellman link investment hôm nay với profit future.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `벨만 방정식`.

## 5. inclusive value /ɪnˈkluːsɪv ˈvælju/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a summary of the expected value of a set of available choices, including the uncertainty about which one will be selected.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** summary expected value của set choice available, gồm uncertainty về choice được select.
**Grammar & collocations:** `choice-set inclusive value` — value; `calculate the inclusive value` — calculate.
**Examples:** `The inclusive value rose when the commuter gained a reliable alternative route.` → Value inclusive tăng khi commuter có route alternative đáng tin.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `포괄 가치`.

## 6. choice-specific value /tʃɔɪs spəˈsɪfɪk ˈvælju/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the part of an option's value that depends on choosing that particular alternative in a given state.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** phần value của option phụ thuộc việc choose alternative cụ thể trong state.
**Grammar & collocations:** `choice-specific value function` — function; `compare choice-specific values` — compare.
**Examples:** `The subsidy increased the choice-specific value of the cleaner technology.` → Subsidy tăng value choice-specific của technology sạch hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `선택별 가치`.

## 7. transition kernel /trænˈzɪʃən ˈkɜrnəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a rule that gives the probability distribution of the next state conditional on the current state and action.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** rule give probability distribution của state tiếp theo theo state và action hiện tại.
**Grammar & collocations:** `estimated transition kernel` — kernel; `specify a transition kernel` — specify.
**Examples:** `The transition kernel described how a firm's productivity changed after investment.` → Kernel transition describe productivity firm đổi thế nào sau investment.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `전이 커널`.

## 8. dynamic decision problem /daɪˈnæmɪk dɪˈsɪʒən ˈprɑbləm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a choice problem in which today's action changes the information, resources, or options available later.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** problem choice nơi action hôm nay đổi information, resource hoặc option later.
**Grammar & collocations:** `finite dynamic decision problem` — problem; `formulate a dynamic decision problem` — formulate.
**Examples:** `Choosing when to search for work is a dynamic decision problem because time changes future offers.` → Chọn khi nào search work là problem decision dynamic vì time đổi offer future.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `동적 의사결정 문제`.

## 9. finite dependence /ˈfaɪnaɪt dɪˈpɛndəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a property that lets a dynamic model be simplified because only a limited history affects the current choice.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** property simplify model vì chỉ history giới hạn ảnh hưởng choice hiện tại.
**Grammar & collocations:** `finite-dependence assumption` — assumption; `exploit finite dependence` — exploit.
**Examples:** `Finite dependence reduced the computation needed to estimate the replacement model.` → Dependence finite giảm computation cần để estimate model replacement.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `유한 의존성`.

## 10. conditional choice probability /kənˈdɪʃənəl tʃɔɪs prəˌbəbəˈləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the probability of selecting an option given the observed state, alternatives, and model assumptions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** probability select option theo state observe, alternative và assumption model.
**Grammar & collocations:** `estimated conditional choice probability` — probability; `derive a conditional choice probability` — derive.
**Examples:** `The conditional choice probability increased when the waiting time fell.` → Probability choice conditional tăng khi waiting time giảm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `조건부 선택 확률`.

## 11. policy function /ˈpɑləsi ˈfʌŋkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a rule that maps a current state and information set to the action a model predicts.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** rule map state và information hiện tại tới action model predict.
**Grammar & collocations:** `optimal policy function` — function; `recover the policy function` — recover.
**Examples:** `The policy function predicted when a firm would expand capacity.` → Function policy predict khi firm expand capacity.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `정책 함수`.

## 12. structural estimation /ˈstrʌktʃərəl ˌɛstəˈmeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** estimation that uses an explicit behavioral or economic model to recover parameters with interpretable meanings.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** estimation dùng model behavioral hoặc economic explicit để recover parameter có meaning interpret.
**Grammar & collocations:** `dynamic structural estimation` — estimation; `perform structural estimation` — perform.
**Examples:** `Structural estimation separated the value of flexibility from the value of immediate income.` → Estimation structural tách value flexibility khỏi value income immediate.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `구조적 추정`.

## 13. simulated likelihood /ˈsɪmjəˌleɪtɪd ˈlaɪkliˌhʊd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a likelihood calculation approximated with simulated draws when the exact probability is difficult to compute.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** likelihood calculation approximate bằng simulated draw khi exact probability khó compute.
**Grammar & collocations:** `simulated-likelihood estimator` — estimator; `maximize simulated likelihood` — maximize.
**Examples:** `Simulated likelihood made the model feasible when future shocks were complicated.` → Likelihood simulated làm model feasible khi future shock phức tạp.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `시뮬레이션 우도`.

## 14. reservation value /ˌrɛzərˈveɪʃən ˈvælju/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the minimum benefit or maximum cost at which a person is just willing to accept an option.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** benefit minimum hoặc cost maximum tại đó person vừa đủ willing accept option.
**Grammar & collocations:** `estimated reservation value` — value; `exceed the reservation value` — exceed.
**Examples:** `The offer exceeded the worker's reservation value and was accepted.` → Offer vượt value reservation của worker nên được accept.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `유보 가치`.

## 15. dynamic choice model /daɪˈnæmɪk tʃɔɪs ˈmɑdəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a model in which choices evolve over time because current decisions affect future states and payoffs.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** model choice evolve theo time vì decision hiện tại ảnh hưởng state và payoff future.
**Grammar & collocations:** `rich dynamic choice model` — model; `calibrate a dynamic choice model` — calibrate.
**Examples:** `The dynamic choice model allowed households to postpone a purchase.` → Model choice dynamic cho household postpone purchase.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `동적 선택 모형`.

## 18. discrete state /dɪˈskriːt steɪt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a distinct, countable condition used to represent the situation of an agent or system.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** condition distinct và countable represent situation agent hoặc system.

## 19. optimal stopping /ˈɑptəməl ˈstɑpɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the problem of choosing when to end a process to maximize expected value.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** problem choose khi nào end process để maximize expected value.
**Grammar & collocations:** `optimal-stopping rule` — rule; `solve an optimal stopping problem` — solve.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `최적 정지`.

## 20. choice-specific shock /tʃɔɪs spəˈsɪfɪk ʃɑk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an unobserved factor that changes the attractiveness of one option relative to other options.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** factor unobserved đổi attractiveness của một option so với option khác.
**Grammar & collocations:** `independent choice-specific shock` — shock; `integrate over choice-specific shocks` — integrate.
**Examples:** `A choice-specific shock captured a private preference that the survey could not measure.` → Shock choice-specific capture preference riêng survey không measure được.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `선택별 충격`.

## Review in context

A **dynamic discrete choice** model allows **state dependence** and treats the decision-maker as a **forward-looking agent**. A **Bellman equation** summarizes future value, while **inclusive value** and **choice-specific value** describe the alternatives. A **transition kernel** defines the next state in a **dynamic decision problem**, and **finite dependence** can simplify computation. The researcher estimates a **conditional choice probability**, recovers a **policy function**, and uses **structural estimation** with **simulated likelihood**. A **dynamic choice model** can use a **reservation value** to determine an **entry decision** or **exit decision**. Each **discrete state** can support an **optimal stopping** rule, while a **choice-specific shock** captures unobserved differences between options.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:** Model **dynamic discrete choice** cho phép có **state dependence** và xem decision-maker là **forward-looking agent**. **Bellman equation** tóm tắt future value, còn **inclusive value** và **choice-specific value** mô tả alternative. **Transition kernel** define state tiếp theo trong **dynamic decision problem**, và **finite dependence** có thể simplify computation. Researcher estimate **conditional choice probability**, recover **policy function** và dùng **structural estimation** với **simulated likelihood**. **Reservation value** có thể quyết định **entry decision** hoặc **exit decision**. Mỗi **discrete state** có thể support rule **optimal stopping**, còn **choice-specific shock** capture difference unobserved giữa option.
