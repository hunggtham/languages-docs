# Writing: My work

Target: 100–150 words.

## Questions to answer

- What is your job?
- What do you normally do during a workday?
- Which part is easy or difficult?
- What skill do you want to improve?

## Suggested structure

1. Introduce the job and workplace.
2. Describe two regular responsibilities.
3. Explain one challenge and one goal.

## Useful sentence starters

- I currently work as …
- A typical workday starts with …
- One part of my job that I enjoy is …
- The most challenging part is … because …
- In the future, I would like to …
