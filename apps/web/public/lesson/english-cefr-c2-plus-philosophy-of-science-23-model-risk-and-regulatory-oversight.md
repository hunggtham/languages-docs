# C2+ Vocabulary — Model risk and regulatory oversight

This lesson follows how institutions govern models, anticipate failure, and assign responsibility when automated systems affect the public. The network is **model risk → risk tolerance posture → validation independence → comparator model → adverse-scenario testing → counterfactual scenario analysis → capital buffer → governance perimeter → regulatory sandbox → supervisory technology → compliance by design → auditability requirement → incident disclosure → near miss → harm taxonomy → severity scale → corrective-action plan → accountability void → responsibility diffusion → oversight failure → regulatory arbitrage → jurisdictional mismatch → cross-border governance → standard-setting body → enforcement discretion**.

## 1. model risk /ˈmɑdəl rɪsk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the possibility that a model is wrong, misused, poorly implemented, or applied outside the conditions where it is reliable.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** model risk không chỉ là model sai; còn gồm misuse, implementation lỗi và out-of-scope use.
**Grammar & collocations:** `model-risk framework` — framework; `manage model risk` — manage; `model limitation` — limitation.
**Examples:** `The bank treated model risk as a governance issue rather than a purely technical defect.` → Bank xem risk là governance issue.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `모형 위험` — model risk.

## 2. risk tolerance posture /rɪsk ˈtɑlərəns ˈpɑstʃər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an institution's stated willingness to accept particular levels and types of risk under defined controls.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** risk tolerance posture nói institution chấp nhận risk nào, mức nào, có control gì.
**Grammar & collocations:** `risk-tolerance posture` — posture; `set a tolerance` — set; `acceptable residual risk` — risk.
**Examples:** `The regulator required a conservative risk tolerance posture for automated credit decisions.` → Regulator yêu cầu posture conservative.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `위험 허용 태세` — risk tolerance posture.

## 3. validation independence /ˌvæləˈdeɪʃən ˌɪndɪˈpɛndəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** separation between the people who build or sponsor a model and those who evaluate whether it is fit for use.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** validation independence tránh builder tự chấm model của mình.
**Grammar & collocations:** `validation-independence requirement` — requirement; `independent validator` — validator; `separate oversight` — oversight.
**Examples:** `Validation independence was weakened when the development team approved its own exception.` → Team tự approve exception làm independence yếu.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `검증 독립성` — validation independence.

## 4. comparator model /kəmˈpærətər ˈmɑdəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an alternative model used to challenge, benchmark, or test the assumptions and performance of a primary model.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** comparator model là model cạnh tranh để challenge/benchmark model chính.
**Grammar & collocations:** `comparator-model benchmark` — benchmark; `challenge a model` — challenge; `alternative specification` — specification.
**Examples:** `The comparator model exposed a performance drop hidden by the preferred specification.` → Model cạnh tranh lộ performance drop.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `비교 모형` — comparator model.

## 5. adverse-scenario testing /ˈædvɜrs səˈnɛrioʊ ˈtɛstɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** testing a system under deliberately unfavorable but plausible combinations of conditions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** adverse-scenario testing đặt system vào combination xấu nhưng plausible để xem fail ra sao.
**Grammar & collocations:** `adverse-scenario-testing suite` — suite; `test under adverse conditions` — test; `plausible stress case` — case.
**Examples:** `Adverse-scenario testing revealed that the service failed when demand and latency rose together.` → Testing lộ fail khi demand/latency cùng tăng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `불리한 시나리오 테스트` — adverse-scenario testing.

## 6. counterfactual scenario analysis /ˌkaʊntərˈfæktʃuəl səˈnɛrioʊ əˈnæləsɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** analysis of what might happen under specified alternative decisions, conditions, or policy choices.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** counterfactual scenario analysis hỏi “nếu policy/condition khác thì outcome ra sao?”.
**Grammar & collocations:** `counterfactual-scenario analysis` — analysis; `compare an alternative` — compare; `policy counterfactual` — counterfactual.
**Examples:** `Counterfactual scenario analysis estimated losses if the safety warning had been delayed.` → Analysis estimate loss nếu warning delay.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `반사실적 시나리오 분석` — counterfactual scenario analysis.

## 7. capital buffer /ˈkæpɪtəl ˈbʌfər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** reserve capacity, money, staff, time, or other resources held to absorb unexpected losses or shocks.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** capital buffer là reserve để absorb shock/loss thay vì fail ngay.
**Grammar & collocations:** `capital-buffer requirement` — requirement; `maintain a buffer` — maintain; `absorb a loss` — absorb.
**Examples:** `The hospital kept a staffing capital buffer for sudden surges in demand.` → Hospital giữ staff reserve.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `자본 완충` — capital buffer.

## 8. governance perimeter /ˈɡʌvərnəns pəˈrɪmətər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the boundary defining which activities, systems, entities, or risks fall within an oversight regime.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** governance perimeter vạch cái gì nằm trong scope oversight và cái gì bị bỏ ngoài.
**Grammar & collocations:** `governance-perimeter review` — review; `fall inside the perimeter` — fall; `regulated activity` — activity.
**Examples:** `The new rule expanded the governance perimeter to include third-party scoring services.` → Rule mở scope tới scoring service bên ngoài.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `거버넌스 경계` — governance perimeter.

## 9. regulatory sandbox /ˈrɛɡjəˌlətɔri ˈsændˌbɑks/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a supervised environment where an innovative product or process can be tested under temporary, limited, or tailored rules.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** regulatory sandbox cho innovation test trong boundary và safeguard đặc biệt.
**Grammar & collocations:** `regulatory-sandbox application` — application; `test under supervision` — test; `temporary rule` — rule.
**Examples:** `The startup entered a regulatory sandbox before offering the automated advice service nationally.` → Startup test dưới supervision trước rollout.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `규제 샌드박스` — regulatory sandbox.

## 10. supervisory technology /ˌsuːpərˈvaɪzəri tɛkˈnɑlədʒi/

## 11. compliance by design /kəmˈplaɪəns baɪ dɪˈzaɪn/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** designing a system so that legal, ethical, security, and reporting requirements are built into its operation from the start.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** compliance by design đưa rule/ethics/security vào architecture, không vá sau.
**Grammar & collocations:** `compliance-by-design framework` — framework; `build compliance into a system` — build; `design control` — control.
**Examples:** `Compliance by design required the service to log every high-impact recommendation automatically.` → Service tự log recommendation high-impact.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `설계에 의한 규정 준수` — compliance by design.

## 12. auditability requirement /ˌɔːdətəˈbɪləti rɪˈkwaɪərmənt/

**Core meaning (English):** a rule requiring that decisions, inputs, changes, and controls be recorded well enough for later independent examination.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** auditability requirement bắt system giữ record đủ để reconstruct và inspect.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `감사 가능성 요구사항` — auditability requirement.

## 13. incident disclosure /ˈɪnsədənt dɪˈskloʊʒər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** formal communication about a harmful, unsafe, unlawful, or materially anomalous event involving a system.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** incident disclosure công khai event harm/unsafe/anomaly đủ material.
**Grammar & collocations:** `incident-disclosure protocol` — protocol; `disclose an incident` — disclose; `material event` — event.
**Examples:** `Incident disclosure was required within seventy-two hours of the data exposure.` → Cần disclose trong 72 giờ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `사고 공개` — incident disclosure.

## 14. near miss /nɪr mɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an event that could have caused serious harm but did not, often because of chance or timely intervention.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** near miss là suýt harm; học từ nó trước khi thành incident thật.
**Grammar & collocations:** `near-miss report` — report; `record a near miss` — record; `prevent a serious incident` — prevent.
**Examples:** `The near miss revealed that two independent safeguards could fail at the same time.` → Near miss lộ hai safeguard cùng fail.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `아차 사고` — near miss.

## 15. harm taxonomy /hɑrm tæˈsɑnəmi/

## 16. severity scale /səˈvɛrəti skeɪl/

## 17. corrective-action plan /kəˈrɛktɪv ˈækʃən plæn/

**Core meaning (English):** a documented plan specifying how an identified problem will be contained, fixed, monitored, and prevented from recurring.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** corrective-action plan không chỉ sửa ngay; còn contain, monitor, prevent recurrence.

## 18. accountability void /əˌkaʊntəˈbɪləti vɔɪd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a situation in which no person, organization, or process clearly bears responsibility for a harmful decision or failure.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** accountability void là khoảng trống “không ai chịu trách nhiệm”.
**Grammar & collocations:** `accountability-void diagnosis` — diagnosis; `assign responsibility` — assign; `responsibility gap` — gap.
**Examples:** `The procurement chain created an accountability void when every vendor blamed the next system.` → Vendor đổ lỗi lẫn nhau.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `책임 공백` — accountability void.

## 19. responsibility diffusion /rɪˌspɑnsəˈbɪləti dɪˈfjuːʒən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** spreading responsibility across many actors so that each appears only partly accountable for the outcome.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** responsibility diffusion làm mỗi actor chỉ nhận một phần nhỏ, cuối cùng không ai own outcome.
**Grammar & collocations:** `responsibility-diffusion effect` — effect; `diffuse accountability` — diffuse; `distributed responsibility` — responsibility.
**Examples:** `Responsibility diffusion delayed the decision to suspend the unsafe model.` → Diffusion làm delay suspension.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `책임 분산` — responsibility diffusion.

## 20. oversight failure /ˈoʊvərˌsaɪt ˈfeɪljər/

## 22. jurisdictional mismatch /ˌdʒʊrəsˈdɪkʃənəl mɪsˈmætʃ/

**Core meaning (English):** a misalignment between where an activity operates, where harm occurs, and which authority has legal power to oversee it.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** jurisdictional mismatch là nơi operation/harm/authority không trùng nhau.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `관할권 불일치` — jurisdictional mismatch.

## Review in context

**Model risk** is governed by a **risk tolerance posture** and **validation independence**; a **comparator model**, **adverse-scenario testing**, and **counterfactual scenario analysis** expose failure. A **capital buffer** and a clear **governance perimeter** support safe experimentation in a **regulatory sandbox** with **supervisory technology** and **compliance by design**. An **auditability requirement** supports **incident disclosure** and learning from a **near miss**. A **harm taxonomy** and **severity scale** guide a **corrective-action plan**. Without clear ownership, an **accountability void** and **responsibility diffusion** can produce **oversight failure**. **Regulatory arbitrage** and **jurisdictional mismatch** require **cross-border governance**. A **standard-setting body** can coordinate expectations, while **enforcement discretion** determines how rules are applied.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

**Model risk** được quản bởi **risk tolerance posture** và **validation independence**; **comparator model**, **adverse-scenario testing** và **counterfactual scenario analysis** lộ failure. **Capital buffer** và **governance perimeter** rõ hỗ trợ thử nghiệm an toàn trong **regulatory sandbox** với **supervisory technology** và **compliance by design**. **Auditability requirement** hỗ trợ **incident disclosure** và học từ **near miss**. **Harm taxonomy** và **severity scale** hướng dẫn **corrective-action plan**. Nếu ownership không rõ, **accountability void** và **responsibility diffusion** có thể tạo **oversight failure**. **Regulatory arbitrage** và **jurisdictional mismatch** cần **cross-border governance**. **Standard-setting body** phối hợp expectation, còn **enforcement discretion** quyết định rule được áp dụng ra sao.
