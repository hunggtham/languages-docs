# C2+ Vocabulary — Pedogenesis and soil function

This lesson follows soil formation from profile development and horizon movement through salinity, structure, carbon cycling, and plant–soil feedback. The network is **pedogenesis profile → eluviation → illuviation → spodic horizon → argillic horizon → cambic horizon → vertisol → lateritic horizon → gleization → calcification process → salinization risk → sodicity hazard → cation-exchange capacity measurement → base saturation → bulk density → aggregate stability index → soil respiration → rhizodeposition → soil priming effect → soil mineralization**.

## 1. pedogenesis profile /ˌpiːdoʊˈdʒɛnəsɪs ˈproʊfaɪl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a vertical description of how soil horizons formed and changed over time.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** description dọc về cách soil horizon hình thành và đổi theo time.
**Grammar & collocations:** `complete pedogenesis profile` — profile đầy đủ; `describe a pedogenesis profile` — describe.
**Examples:** `The complete pedogenesis profile showed centuries of clay movement.` → Pedogenesis profile đầy đủ cho thấy clay movement nhiều thế kỷ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `토양 생성 단면`.

## 2. eluviation /ɪˌluːviˈeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the removal of clay, iron, or organic material from an upper soil horizon by moving water.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** clay, iron hoặc organic material bị remove khỏi upper horizon bởi water move.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong eluviation` — eluviation mạnh; `measure eluviation` — measure.
**Examples:** `Strong eluviation left the upper horizon pale and sandy.` → Eluviation mạnh làm upper horizon nhạt và sandy.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `용탈`.

## 3. illuviation /ɪˌluːviˈeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the accumulation of material washed down from an upper soil horizon into a lower one.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** material từ upper horizon wash xuống và accumulate ở lower horizon.
**Grammar & collocations:** `clay illuviation` — illuviation clay; `identify illuviation` — identify.
**Examples:** `Clay illuviation produced a dense subsurface layer.` → Illuviation clay tạo subsurface layer dense.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `집적`.

## 4. spodic horizon /ˈspoʊdɪk həˈraɪzən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a subsurface soil horizon enriched with organic matter, aluminum, and often iron.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** soil horizon dưới surface giàu organic matter, aluminum và thường iron.
**Grammar & collocations:** `dark spodic horizon` — horizon tối; `sample a spodic horizon` — sample.
**Examples:** `The dark spodic horizon formed beneath the conifer litter.` → Spodic horizon tối hình thành dưới litter conifer.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `스포딕 층위`.

## 5. argillic horizon /ɑːrˈdʒɪlɪk həˈraɪzən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a subsurface horizon containing more clay than the material above it.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** horizon dưới surface có nhiều clay hơn material ở trên.
**Grammar & collocations:** `thick argillic horizon` — horizon dày; `map an argillic horizon` — map.
**Examples:** `The thick argillic horizon slowed downward water movement.` → Argillic horizon dày làm chậm water movement xuống.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `점토집적층`.

## 6. cambic horizon /ˈkæmbɪk həˈraɪzən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a weakly developed subsurface horizon showing alteration but not enough evidence for a more specialized horizon.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** horizon dưới surface phát triển yếu, có alteration nhưng chưa đủ cho horizon chuyên hóa.
**Grammar & collocations:** `young cambic horizon` — horizon trẻ; `describe a cambic horizon` — describe.
**Examples:** `The young cambic horizon showed early mineral alteration.` → Cambic horizon trẻ cho thấy mineral alteration sớm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `변성층`.

## 7. vertisol /ˈvɜːrtɪsɔːl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a clay-rich soil that repeatedly swells, shrinks, and cracks as its moisture changes.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** soil giàu clay liên tục swell/shrink/crack khi moisture đổi.
**Grammar & collocations:** `cracking vertisol` — vertisol nứt; `cultivate a vertisol` — cultivate.
**Examples:** `The cracking vertisol required careful timing for planting.` → Vertisol nứt cần timing cẩn thận khi planting.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `버티솔`.

## 8. lateritic horizon /ˌlætəˈrɪtɪk həˈraɪzən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a strongly weathered horizon enriched in iron and aluminum under warm, wet conditions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** horizon weathering mạnh, giàu iron/aluminum trong warm wet condition.
**Grammar & collocations:** `iron-rich lateritic horizon` — horizon giàu iron; `sample a lateritic horizon` — sample.
**Examples:** `The iron-rich lateritic horizon hardened when exposed to air.` → Lateritic horizon giàu iron harden khi expose air.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `라테라이트 층위`.

## 9. gleization /ɡliːəˈzeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** soil development under prolonged saturation that produces gray colors and reduced iron patterns.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** soil develop khi saturation lâu, tạo gray color và iron reduction pattern.
**Grammar & collocations:** `wetland gleization` — gleization wetland; `recognize gleization` — recognize.
**Examples:** `Wetland gleization produced gray mottles below the surface.` → Gleization wetland tạo mottle gray dưới surface.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `글레이화`.

## 10. calcification process /ˌkælsɪfɪˈkeɪʃən ˈprɑːses/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** accumulation of calcium carbonate in a soil profile.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** calcium carbonate accumulate trong soil profile.
**Grammar & collocations:** `secondary calcification process` — process thứ cấp; `document calcification` — document.
**Examples:** `The secondary calcification process created white nodules in the subsoil.` → Calcification process thứ cấp tạo nodule trắng trong subsoil.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `석회화 과정`.

## 11. salinization risk /ˌsælənəˈzeɪʃən rɪsk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the likelihood that soluble salts will accumulate enough to damage soil productivity.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** likelihood soluble salt accumulate đủ để damage productivity soil.
**Grammar & collocations:** `irrigation salinization risk` — risk do irrigation; `reduce salinization risk` — reduce.
**Examples:** `Poor drainage increased irrigation salinization risk in the dry basin.` → Drainage kém tăng salinization risk do irrigation trong basin khô.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `염류화 위험`.

## 12. sodicity hazard /soʊˈdɪsəti ˈhæzərd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the risk that excessive exchangeable sodium will destroy soil structure and reduce infiltration.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** risk sodium exchangeable quá nhiều phá soil structure và giảm infiltration.
**Grammar & collocations:** `severe sodicity hazard` — hazard nặng; `manage sodicity hazard` — manage.
**Examples:** `A severe sodicity hazard caused the clay soil to disperse after irrigation.` → Sodicity hazard nặng làm clay soil disperse sau irrigation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `나트륨화 위험`.

## 13. cation-exchange capacity measurement /ˈkætaɪən ɪksˈtʃeɪndʒ kəˈpæsəti ˈmɛʒərmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** measuring how much positively charged nutrient a soil can hold and exchange.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** đo lượng nutrient positive charge soil có thể hold/exchange.
**Grammar & collocations:** `laboratory cation-exchange capacity measurement` — measurement lab; `perform a measurement` — perform.
**Examples:** `Laboratory cation-exchange capacity measurement showed that the clay could retain potassium.` → Measurement CEC lab cho thấy clay giữ được potassium.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `양이온 교환 용량 측정`.

## 14. base saturation /beɪs ˌsætʃəˈreɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the proportion of soil exchange sites occupied by basic cations such as calcium, magnesium, and potassium.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** tỷ lệ exchange site được chiếm bởi basic cation như calcium, magnesium, potassium.
**Grammar & collocations:** `low base saturation` — saturation thấp; `calculate base saturation` — calculate.
**Examples:** `Low base saturation indicated a strongly leached soil.` → Base saturation thấp cho thấy soil bị leach mạnh.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `염기 포화도`.

## 15. bulk density /bʌlk ˈdɛnsəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the dry mass of soil per unit of total volume, including its pore space.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** dry mass soil trên total volume, bao gồm pore space.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high bulk density` — density cao; `measure bulk density` — measure.
**Examples:** `High bulk density restricted root growth in the compacted layer.` → Bulk density cao hạn chế root growth trong layer compact.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `용적 밀도`.

## 16. aggregate stability index /ˈæɡrɪɡət stəˈbɪləti ˈɪndɛks/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a measure of how well soil aggregates resist breakdown when wetted or disturbed.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** measure soil aggregate chống breakdown khi wet/disturb tốt thế nào.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high aggregate stability index` — index cao; `calculate an index` — calculate.
**Examples:** `A high aggregate stability index showed that cover crops protected the soil.` → Aggregate stability index cao cho thấy cover crop bảo vệ soil.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `토양 입단 안정성 지수`.

## 17. soil respiration /sɔɪl ˌrɛspəˈreɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the release of carbon dioxide from soil organisms and plant roots.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** CO2 release từ soil organism và plant root.
**Grammar & collocations:** `nighttime soil respiration` — respiration ban đêm; `measure soil respiration` — measure.
**Examples:** `Warm moisture increased nighttime soil respiration.` → Warm moisture tăng soil respiration ban đêm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `토양 호흡`.

## 18. rhizodeposition /ˌraɪzoʊˌdɛpəˈzɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the release of organic compounds, cells, and residues from living plant roots into soil.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** organic compound, cell và residue release từ living root vào soil.
**Grammar & collocations:** `root rhizodeposition` — deposition từ root; `measure rhizodeposition` — measure.
**Examples:** `Root rhizodeposition fed microbes near the growing root tip.` → Rhizodeposition từ root feed microbe gần root tip grow.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `근권 분비물 방출`.

## 19. soil priming effect /sɔɪl ˈpraɪmɪŋ ɪˈfɛkt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a change in decomposition of existing soil organic matter after new carbon enters the soil.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** decomposition của soil organic matter cũ đổi sau khi carbon mới vào soil.
**Grammar & collocations:** `positive soil priming effect` — effect dương; `measure a priming effect` — measure.
**Examples:** `A positive soil priming effect accelerated the loss of older carbon.` → Soil priming effect dương accelerate loss carbon cũ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `토양 프라이밍 효과`.

## 20. soil mineralization /sɔɪl ˌmɪnərələˈzeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** microbial conversion of organic nutrients into inorganic forms available to plants.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** microbe convert organic nutrient thành inorganic form plant dùng được.
**Grammar & collocations:** `nitrogen soil mineralization` — mineralization nitrogen; `stimulate mineralization` — stimulate.
**Examples:** `Warm moisture stimulated nitrogen soil mineralization after the rain.` → Warm moisture stimulate soil mineralization nitrogen sau rain.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `토양 무기화`.

## Review in context

A **pedogenesis profile** records **eluviation**, **illuviation**, a **spodic horizon**, **argillic horizon**, **cambic horizon**, or **vertisol**; a **lateritic horizon**, **gleization**, and **calcification process** reflect climate and drainage. Management evaluates **salinization risk**, **sodicity hazard**, **cation-exchange capacity measurement**, **base saturation**, and **bulk density**. A high **aggregate stability index** protects structure, while **soil respiration**, **rhizodeposition**, a **soil priming effect**, and **soil mineralization** connect roots, microbes, and carbon cycling.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:** **Pedogenesis profile** ghi nhận **eluviation**, **illuviation**, **spodic horizon**, **argillic horizon**, **cambic horizon** hoặc **vertisol**; **lateritic horizon**, **gleization** và **calcification process** phản ánh climate/drainage. Management đánh giá **salinization risk**, **sodicity hazard**, **cation-exchange capacity measurement**, **base saturation** và **bulk density**. **Aggregate stability index** cao bảo vệ structure, còn **soil respiration**, **rhizodeposition**, **soil priming effect** và **soil mineralization** nối root, microbe với carbon cycling.
