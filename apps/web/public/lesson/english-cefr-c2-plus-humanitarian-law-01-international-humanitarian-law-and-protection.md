# C2+ Vocabulary — International humanitarian law and protection

This lesson follows the rules that limit conduct during armed conflict, protect civilians and detainees, and connect battlefield facts to accountability and humanitarian access. The network is **distinction principle → proportionality in attack → military necessity → precaution in attack → hors de combat → protected person → command responsibility → joint criminal enterprise → complementarity principle → aut dedere aut judicare → temporary protected status → internally displaced person → humanitarian corridor → safe passage → siege warfare → dual-use object → direct participation in hostilities → common article three → grave breach → war crime nexus**.

## 1. distinction principle /dɪˈstɪŋkʃən ˈprɪnsəpəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the rule requiring parties to distinguish at all times between civilians and combatants, and between civilian objects and military objectives.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** luôn tách civilian khỏi combatant và civilian object khỏi military target trước khi attack.
**Grammar & collocations:** `principle of distinction` — principle; `apply distinction` — apply; `distinction failure` — failure.
**Examples:** `The distinction principle prohibited treating an entire neighborhood as a military target.` → Distinction principle cấm coi cả neighborhood là military target.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `구별 원칙` — distinction principle.

## 2. proportionality in attack /prəˌpɔrʃəˈnæləti ɪn əˈtæk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the rule prohibiting an attack expected to cause civilian harm excessive in relation to the concrete and direct military advantage anticipated.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cân civilian harm dự kiến với military advantage cụ thể; không phải mọi collateral harm đều tự động unlawful, nhưng excessiveness bị cấm.
**Grammar & collocations:** `proportionality-in-attack assessment` — assessment; `anticipated military advantage` — advantage; `assess proportionality` — assess.
**Examples:** `The commander documented the proportionality in attack analysis before authorizing the strike.` → Commander ghi lại proportionality in attack trước khi authorize strike.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `공격 비례성` — proportionality in attack.

## 3. military necessity /ˈmɪləˌtɛri nəˈsɛsəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the principle that force or measures in armed conflict must be directed toward a legitimate military purpose and not exceed what is needed for it.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** military action phải phục vụ legitimate military purpose và không vượt mức cần thiết.
**Grammar & collocations:** `military-necessity argument` — argument; `invoke military necessity` — invoke; `necessity assessment` — assessment.
**Examples:** `Military necessity did not justify attacking a hospital that was not being used for military purposes.` → Military necessity không biện minh attack hospital không dùng cho military purpose.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `군사적 필요성` — military necessity.

## 4. precaution in attack /prɪˈkɔʃən ɪn əˈtæk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the obligation to take feasible steps to verify targets, minimize civilian harm, and cancel or suspend an attack when the expected conditions change.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** trước và trong attack phải verify, chọn means ít harm hơn, warn khi possible và dừng nếu target assessment sai.
**Grammar & collocations:** `precaution-in-attack obligation` — obligation; `feasible precaution` — precaution; `take precautions` — take.
**Examples:** `The unit changed its timing as a precaution in attack when civilians entered the area.` → Unit đổi timing như precaution in attack khi civilian vào area.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `공격 시 예방조치` — precaution in attack.

## 5. hors de combat /ɔr də kəmˈbɑː/
**Part of speech:** adjective phrase
**Core meaning (English):** no longer able to fight because a person is wounded, sick, detained, or otherwise incapacitated, and is therefore protected from attack under applicable rules.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** “out of the fight”; người wounded, surrendered hoặc detained không còn là lawful target chỉ vì trước đó họ combat.
**Grammar & collocations:** `rendered hors de combat` — rendered; `person hors de combat` — person; `hors-de-combat protection` — protection.
**Examples:** `The wounded fighter was hors de combat and had to be evacuated rather than attacked.` → Fighter bị thương hors de combat và phải được evacuate thay vì attack.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `전투불능 상태` — hors de combat.

## 6. protected person /prəˈtɛktɪd ˈpɜrsən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a person who falls within a legal category receiving special protection under humanitarian law, such as certain civilians, wounded persons, detainees, or medical personnel.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** category pháp lý được protection đặc biệt; status phụ thuộc context và instrument áp dụng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `protected-person status` — status; `protected person under the Convention` — person; `treat a person as protected` — treat.
**Examples:** `The detainee's protected-person status required humane treatment and information about the detention.` → Protected-person status của detainee yêu cầu humane treatment và information về detention.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `보호 대상자` — protected person.

## 7. command responsibility /kəˈmænd rɪˌspɑnsəˈbɪləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a doctrine under which a commander may be responsible for crimes by subordinates when the commander knew or should have known and failed to prevent or punish them.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** responsibility không chỉ vì ra lệnh trực tiếp; nó có thể đến từ knowledge, control và failure to act.
**Grammar & collocations:** `command-responsibility doctrine` — doctrine; `effective command and control` — control; `establish responsibility` — establish.
**Examples:** `The investigation examined command responsibility rather than assuming every subordinate act was directly ordered.` → Investigation xem command responsibility thay vì mặc định mọi act của subordinate đều được order trực tiếp.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `지휘관 책임` — command responsibility.

## 8. joint criminal enterprise /dʒɔɪnt ˈkrɪmənəl ˈɛntərˌpraɪz/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a legal theory alleging that participants share a common criminal plan or purpose and can be responsible for crimes carried out through that common enterprise.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** nhiều người cùng một common criminal plan; analysis tập trung vào contribution, shared purpose và foreseeable crimes.
**Grammar & collocations:** `joint-criminal-enterprise theory` — theory; `common criminal purpose` — purpose; `participate in an enterprise` — participate.
**Examples:** `The indictment alleged a joint criminal enterprise organized to remove the civilian population.` → Indictment cáo buộc joint criminal enterprise nhằm remove civilian population.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `공동 범죄 기업` — joint criminal enterprise.

## 9. complementarity principle /ˌkɑmpləˌmɛntəˈrɪti ˈprɪnsəpəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the principle that an international criminal court acts only when a state with primary jurisdiction is unwilling or unable genuinely to investigate or prosecute.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** international court là “last resort”, bổ sung cho national justice chứ không tự động thay thế nó.
**Grammar & collocations:** `complementarity principle` — principle; `genuine national proceedings` — proceedings; `challenge admissibility` — challenge.
**Examples:** `The defense invoked the complementarity principle by pointing to a genuine domestic investigation.` → Defense viện complementarity principle và chỉ ra domestic investigation genuine.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `보충성 원칙` — complementarity principle.

## 10. aut dedere aut judicare /aʊt dɪˈdɪəri aʊt ˌdʒuːdɪˈkɛri/
**Part of speech:** legal phrase
**Core meaning (English):** the obligation to extradite a person or prosecute that person under specified treaty or domestic-law conditions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** “extradite hoặc prosecute”; state không được biến territory thành nơi trú ẩn cho một số serious crimes.
**Grammar & collocations:** `aut-dedere-aut-judicare obligation` — obligation; `extradite or prosecute` — phrase; `invoke the obligation` — invoke.
**Examples:** `The treaty's aut dedere aut judicare clause required the state to choose prosecution or extradition.` → Clause aut dedere aut judicare yêu cầu state chọn prosecution hoặc extradition.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `인도 또는 기소 원칙` — aut dedere aut judicare.

## 11. temporary protected status /ˈtɛmpəˌrɛri prəˈtɛktɪd ˈsteɪtəs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a time-limited legal status allowing eligible people from a designated crisis area to remain and receive protection from removal under specified conditions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** status tạm thời cho người không thể safely return vì conflict/disaster; không tự động là permanent residence.
**Grammar & collocations:** `temporary-protected-status designation` — designation; `grant protected status` — grant; `renew the status` — renew.
**Examples:** `Temporary protected status allowed the displaced family to work legally while return remained unsafe.` → Temporary protected status cho family displaced làm việc hợp pháp khi return vẫn unsafe.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `임시 보호 지위` — temporary protected status.

## 12. internally displaced person /ɪnˈtɜrnəli dɪˈspleɪst ˈpɜrsən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a person forced to leave home because of conflict, violence, disaster, or persecution but who remains within the country's borders.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** bị displacement nhưng chưa cross international border, nên vẫn nằm trong jurisdiction của own state.
**Grammar & collocations:** `internally displaced person camp` — camp; `IDP protection` — protection; `assist displaced people` — assist.
**Examples:** `The internally displaced person needed shelter, documents, and safe access to health care.` → Internally displaced person cần shelter, documents và access an toàn tới healthcare.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `국내실향민` — internally displaced person.

## 13. humanitarian corridor /hjuˌmænəˈtɛriən ˈkɔrədɔr/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an agreed route or area intended to allow civilians, aid, or evacuations to move more safely through or away from a conflict zone.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** “hành lang” được agree để civilian/aid đi qua, cần clarity, monitoring và respect từ các party.
**Grammar & collocations:** `humanitarian corridor agreement` — agreement; `open a corridor` — open; `corridor monitoring` — monitoring.
**Examples:** `The humanitarian corridor allowed medical supplies to reach the besieged town.` → Humanitarian corridor cho medical supplies tới town bị siege.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `인도주의 통로` — humanitarian corridor.

## 14. safe passage /seɪf ˈpæsɪdʒ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an arrangement or guarantee intended to let specified people or goods travel without attack or obstruction along an agreed route.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** permission/guarantee cho movement an toàn trong time/route cụ thể, không nhất thiết là unrestricted movement.
**Grammar & collocations:** `negotiate safe passage` — negotiate; `safe-passage guarantee` — guarantee; `allow safe passage` — allow.
**Examples:** `The parties negotiated safe passage for wounded civilians and medical vehicles.` → Các party negotiate safe passage cho civilian bị thương và medical vehicle.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `안전 통행` — safe passage.

## 15. siege warfare /siːdʒ ˈwɔrfɛr/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the use of encirclement, blockade, or sustained pressure to isolate a place and compel surrender, with serious implications for civilian protection and humanitarian access.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** warfare bằng bao vây/cắt supply; analysis phải tách military objective khỏi starvation hoặc harm tới civilian.
**Grammar & collocations:** `prolonged siege warfare` — warfare; `siege conditions` — conditions; `lift a siege` — lift.
**Examples:** `Siege warfare restricted food and medicine, making humanitarian access urgent.` → Siege warfare hạn chế food và medicine, khiến humanitarian access cấp thiết.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `포위전` — siege warfare.

## 16. dual-use object /ˌduːəl juːs ˈɑbdʒɛkt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an object that serves both civilian and military purposes, requiring careful assessment before it can be treated as a military objective.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** object có hai vai: civilian utility và possible military use; không thể label military chỉ vì có khả năng dual use.
**Grammar & collocations:** `dual-use object assessment` — assessment; `civilian dual-use infrastructure` — infrastructure; `identify a military use` — identify.
**Examples:** `The bridge was a dual-use object, so the analysis examined its concrete military contribution.` → Bridge là dual-use object nên analysis xem military contribution cụ thể.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `이중용도 물체` — dual-use object.

## 17. direct participation in hostilities /dəˈrɛkt pɑrˌtɪsəˈpeɪʃən ɪn hɑˈstɪlətiz/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** conduct that directly causes harm to the opposing party's military operations or personnel and may affect a civilian's protection from attack for the duration of that conduct.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** civilian thực hiện conduct trực tiếp gây harm tới enemy operation; status protection trong period đó được phân tích theo facts.
**Grammar & collocations:** `direct-participation assessment` — assessment; `participate directly` — participate; `temporary loss of protection` — loss.
**Examples:** `The report distinguished direct participation in hostilities from political support or ordinary work.` → Report phân biệt direct participation in hostilities với political support hoặc ordinary work.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `적대행위 직접 참여` — direct participation in hostilities.

## 18. common article three /ˈkɑmən ˈɑrtɪkəl θriː/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the shared minimum humanitarian provision in Article 3 common to the four Geneva Conventions for certain non-international armed conflicts.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** “common floor” tối thiểu về humane treatment, protection của wounded và prohibition violence trong internal conflict.
**Grammar & collocations:** `Common Article 3 protection` — protection; `invoke Common Article 3` — invoke; `non-international armed conflict` — conflict.
**Examples:** `Common Article 3 prohibits cruel treatment of persons taking no active part in the hostilities.` → Common Article 3 cấm cruel treatment với người không còn active part trong hostilities.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `공통 제3조` — Common Article 3.

## 19. grave breach /ɡreɪv briːtʃ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a particularly serious violation of specified humanitarian-law protections that triggers special legal consequences under applicable instruments.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** violation ở mức “grave” được treaty xác định, không phải mọi breach đều tự động là grave breach.
**Grammar & collocations:** `grave-breach provision` — provision; `allege a grave breach` — allege; `grave-breach prosecution` — prosecution.
**Examples:** `The prosecutor examined whether the conduct met the treaty's definition of a grave breach.` → Prosecutor xem conduct có đáp ứng definition grave breach của treaty không.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `중대한 위반` — grave breach.

## 20. war crime nexus /wɔr kraɪm ˈnɛksəs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the required connection between an alleged criminal act and an armed conflict for the act to qualify as a war crime under the applicable legal framework.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** phải nối act với armed conflict bằng một nexus đủ rõ; ordinary crime trong wartime không tự động là war crime.
**Grammar & collocations:** `war-crime nexus requirement` — requirement; `establish the nexus` — establish; `conflict-related conduct` — conduct.
**Examples:** `The court required evidence of a war crime nexus rather than relying only on the timing of the incident.` → Court yêu cầu evidence của war crime nexus thay vì chỉ dựa timing incident.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `전쟁범죄 연계성` — war crime nexus.

## Review in context

Humanitarian law begins with the **distinction principle**, **proportionality in attack**, **military necessity**, and **precaution in attack**. A wounded person **hors de combat** may be a **protected person**, and **command responsibility** or a **joint criminal enterprise** can shape accountability. At the international level, the **complementarity principle** and **aut dedere aut judicare** determine how national and international jurisdiction interact. Civilians may receive **temporary protected status** or remain an **internally displaced person**. A **humanitarian corridor** and **safe passage** address movement, while **siege warfare** raises urgent access concerns. A **dual-use object** requires careful targeting analysis, and **direct participation in hostilities** can affect a civilian's protection during specific conduct. **Common Article 3**, also called **common article three**, a **grave breach**, and the **war crime nexus** connect minimum protections to legal classification.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

Humanitarian law bắt đầu với **distinction principle**, **proportionality in attack**, **military necessity** và **precaution in attack**. Người bị thương **hors de combat** có thể là **protected person**, còn **command responsibility** hoặc **joint criminal enterprise** định hình accountability. Ở cấp international, **complementarity principle** và **aut dedere aut judicare** quyết định quan hệ giữa national và international jurisdiction. Civilian có thể nhận **temporary protected status** hoặc vẫn là **internally displaced person**. **Humanitarian corridor** và **safe passage** xử lý movement, còn **siege warfare** tạo access concern cấp thiết. **Dual-use object** cần targeting analysis cẩn thận, và **direct participation in hostilities** có thể ảnh hưởng protection của civilian trong conduct cụ thể. **Common Article 3**, **grave breach** và **war crime nexus** nối minimum protection với legal classification.
