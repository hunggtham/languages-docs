# C1 Vocabulary 55 — Causal survival and competing risks

This lesson follows how time-to-event analyses describe treatment effects when censoring and several possible event types complicate follow-up.

Flow: **censoring distribution → administrative censoring → informative censoring → survival estimand → RMST contrast → time-to-event outcome → cause-specific effect → survival intervention → dynamic survival → at-risk population → risk difference survival → survival treatment effect → cause-specific cumulative incidence → event-specific hazard → survival contrast → interval censoring → failure function → risk prediction → competing-risk prediction → cause-specific survival**.

## 1. censoring distribution /ˈsɛnsərɪŋ ˌdɪstrəˈbjuːʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the probability pattern describing when follow-up ends before the event of interest is observed.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** probability pattern describe khi follow-up end trước khi observe event interest.
**Grammar & collocations:** `estimated censoring distribution` — distribution; `model the censoring distribution` — model.
**Examples:** `The censoring distribution differed between hospitals with different follow-up schedules.` → Distribution censoring khác giữa hospital có follow-up schedule khác.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `검열 분포`.

## 2. administrative censoring /ədˈmɪnəˌstreɪtɪv ˈsɛnsərɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** censoring that occurs because a study reaches its planned end date rather than because of a participant's event or withdrawal.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** censoring vì study tới end date plan, không phải event hoặc withdrawal participant.
**Grammar & collocations:** `planned administrative censoring` — censoring; `account for administrative censoring` — account.
**Examples:** `Administrative censoring ended observation for patients still event-free at the study close.` → Censoring administrative end observation patient vẫn event-free lúc study close.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `행정적 검열`.

## 3. informative censoring /ɪnˈfɔrmətɪv ˈsɛnsərɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** censoring related to a person's event risk or unobserved condition rather than occurring independently of it.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** censoring liên quan event risk hoặc condition unobserved, không independent.
**Grammar & collocations:** `possible informative censoring` — censoring; `adjust for informative censoring` — adjust.
**Examples:** `Informative censoring was plausible when people with worsening symptoms stopped attending visits.` → Censoring informative plausible khi người symptom worse stop attend visit.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `정보성 검열`.

## 4. survival estimand /sərˈvaɪvəl ˈɛstəmənd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a precisely defined causal quantity describing survival or event risk under specified conditions and a time horizon.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** causal quantity define rõ về survival hoặc event risk dưới condition và time horizon.
**Grammar & collocations:** `five-year survival estimand` — estimand; `define a survival estimand` — define.
**Examples:** `The survival estimand compared event-free probability at three years.` → Estimand survival compare event-free probability ở năm thứ ba.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `생존 추정 대상`.

## 5. RMST contrast /ˌɑr ɛm ɛs ˈtiː ˈkɑntræst/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the difference in restricted mean survival time between two treatment conditions over a chosen period.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** difference restricted mean survival time giữa hai treatment condition trong period chọn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `adjusted RMST contrast` — contrast; `estimate an RMST contrast` — estimate.
**Examples:** `The RMST contrast indicated two additional event-free months under the new program.` → Contrast RMST indicate thêm hai tháng event-free dưới program mới.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `제한 평균 생존시간 대비`.

## 6. time-to-event outcome /taɪm tə ɪˈvɛnt ˈaʊtˌkʌm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an outcome defined by whether and when a specified event occurs.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** outcome define bởi event có xảy ra và xảy ra khi nào.
**Grammar & collocations:** `primary time-to-event outcome` — outcome; `analyze a time-to-event outcome` — analyze.
**Examples:** `The time-to-event outcome was hospital readmission within twelve months.` → Outcome time-to-event là hospital readmission trong twelve tháng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `사건 발생 시간 결과`.

## 7. cause-specific effect /kɔz spəˈsɪfɪk ɪˈfɛkt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a treatment effect defined for one particular type of event while treating other event types as competing processes.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** treatment effect define cho một event type, event khác là competing process.
**Grammar & collocations:** `estimated cause-specific effect` — effect; `interpret a cause-specific effect` — interpret.
**Examples:** `The cause-specific effect concerned cardiovascular death rather than all-cause mortality.` → Effect cause-specific concern cardiovascular death chứ không phải all-cause mortality.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `원인별 효과`.

## 8. survival intervention /sərˈvaɪvəl ˌɪntərˈvɛnʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a specified treatment or policy intervention evaluated by its effect on survival or event timing.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** treatment hoặc policy intervention evaluate theo effect lên survival hoặc event timing.
**Grammar & collocations:** `hypothetical survival intervention` — intervention; `evaluate a survival intervention` — evaluate.
**Examples:** `The survival intervention assigned follow-up calls after every abnormal test.` → Intervention survival assign follow-up call sau mỗi test abnormal.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `생존 개입`.

## 9. dynamic survival /daɪˈnæmɪk sərˈvaɪvəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** survival analysis in which treatment or covariate information changes as follow-up proceeds.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** survival analysis nơi treatment hoặc covariate đổi khi follow-up tiến triển.
**Grammar & collocations:** `dynamic survival model` — model; `study dynamic survival` — study.
**Examples:** `Dynamic survival reflected dose changes after each clinical assessment.` → Survival dynamic reflect dose change sau mỗi clinical assessment.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `동적 생존`.

## 10. at-risk population /æt rɪsk ˌpɑpjəˈleɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the units still eligible to experience and be observed for the event at a particular time.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** unit còn eligible experience và observe event ở time cụ thể.
**Grammar & collocations:** `restricted at-risk population` — population; `define the at-risk population` — define.
**Examples:** `The at-risk population excluded patients who had already experienced a competing event.` → Population at-risk exclude patient đã experience competing event.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `위험 집단`.

## 11. risk difference survival /rɪsk ˈdɪfərəns sərˈvaɪvəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the difference in survival or event probability between two treatment conditions at a specified time.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** difference survival hoặc event probability giữa treatment condition ở time cụ thể.
**Grammar & collocations:** `absolute risk difference survival` — difference; `report risk difference survival` — report.
**Examples:** `The risk difference survival favored screening at the five-year point.` → Difference risk survival favor screening ở mốc five-year.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `생존 위험 차이`.

## 12. survival treatment effect /sərˈvaɪvəl ˈtriːtmənt ɪˈfɛkt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the causal effect of treatment on survival time or the chance of avoiding an event.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** causal effect treatment lên survival time hoặc chance avoid event.
**Grammar & collocations:** `long-term survival treatment effect` — effect; `estimate a survival treatment effect` — estimate.
**Examples:** `The survival treatment effect was strongest during the first year.` → Effect treatment survival mạnh nhất trong năm đầu.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `생존 치료 효과`.

## 13. cause-specific cumulative incidence /kɔz spəˈsɪfɪk ˈkjuːmjələtɪv ˈɪnsədəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the probability of experiencing one specified event by a time while accounting for competing events.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** probability experience event cụ thể trước time, account competing event.
**Grammar & collocations:** `estimated cause-specific cumulative incidence` — incidence; `plot cause-specific cumulative incidence` — plot.
**Examples:** `Cause-specific cumulative incidence of relapse fell when maintenance therapy continued.` → Incidence cumulative cause-specific của relapse giảm khi maintenance therapy continue.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `원인별 누적 발생률`.

## 14. event-specific hazard /ɪˈvɛnt spəˈsɪfɪk ˈhæzərd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the instantaneous risk of one specified event among units currently free of that event and relevant competing events.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** instantaneous risk của event cụ thể trong unit chưa experience event hoặc competing event liên quan.
**Grammar & collocations:** `time-varying event-specific hazard` — hazard; `model an event-specific hazard` — model.
**Examples:** `The event-specific hazard for relapse was highest soon after discharge.` → Hazard event-specific của relapse cao nhất ngay sau discharge.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `사건별 위험률`.

## 15. survival contrast /sərˈvaɪvəl ˈkɑntræst/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a defined comparison between survival outcomes under two treatments, policies, or exposure conditions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** comparison define giữa survival outcome dưới treatment, policy hoặc exposure condition khác.
**Grammar & collocations:** `clinically relevant survival contrast` — contrast; `define a survival contrast` — define.
**Examples:** `The survival contrast compared immediate surgery with monitored care.` → Contrast survival compare immediate surgery với monitored care.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `생존 대비`.

## 16. interval censoring /ˈɪntərvəl ˈsɛnsərɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** censoring in which the event is known to have occurred between two observation times rather than at an exact time.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** event biết xảy ra giữa hai observation time, không biết exact time.
**Grammar & collocations:** `substantial interval censoring` — censoring; `handle interval censoring` — handle.
**Examples:** `Interval censoring occurred because patients were tested only at quarterly visits.` → Censoring interval xảy ra vì patient chỉ được test ở visit quarterly.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `구간 검열`.

## 17. failure function /ˈfeɪljər ˈfʌŋkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a function describing the accumulated probability that an event or failure has occurred by a given time.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** function describe probability tích lũy event hoặc failure đã xảy ra tới time.
**Grammar & collocations:** `estimated failure function` — function; `plot a failure function` — plot.
**Examples:** `The failure function rose slowly at first and then accelerated after year four.` → Function failure tăng chậm ban đầu rồi accelerate sau year four.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `고장 함수`.

## 18. risk prediction /rɪsk prɪˈdɪkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an estimate of an individual's or group's probability of experiencing an event within a defined period.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** estimate probability person hoặc group experience event trong period define.
**Grammar & collocations:** `individual risk prediction` — prediction; `validate risk prediction` — validate.
**Examples:** `Risk prediction identified patients who needed a closer follow-up schedule.` → Prediction risk identify patient cần follow-up close hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `위험 예측`.

## 19. competing-risk prediction /kəmˈpiːtɪŋ rɪsk prɪˈdɪkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** prediction of an event probability while recognizing that other event types can occur first.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** predict event probability nhưng recognize event type khác có thể xảy ra trước.
**Grammar & collocations:** `calibrated competing-risk prediction` — prediction; `evaluate competing-risk prediction` — evaluate.
**Examples:** `Competing-risk prediction lowered the estimated relapse chance when death was common.` → Prediction competing-risk giảm relapse chance estimate khi death common.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `경쟁 위험 예측`.

## 20. cause-specific survival /kɔz spəˈsɪfɪk sərˈvaɪvəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** survival defined with respect to one event type while distinguishing it from other possible events.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** survival define theo một event type, phân biệt với event khác possible.
**Grammar & collocations:** `cause-specific survival estimate` — estimate; `compare cause-specific survival` — compare.
**Examples:** `Cause-specific survival was better for the treatment when relapse, not death, was the outcome.` → Survival cause-specific tốt hơn khi outcome là relapse, không phải death.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `원인별 생존`.

## Review in context

A **censoring distribution** describes follow-up loss, including **administrative censoring** and possible **informative censoring**. The analyst defines a **survival estimand**, perhaps an **RMST contrast**, for a **time-to-event outcome**. A **cause-specific effect** can be evaluated under a **survival intervention** or **dynamic survival** rule for the **at-risk population**. A **risk difference survival** and a **survival treatment effect** summarize the comparison, while **cause-specific cumulative incidence** and an **event-specific hazard** describe competing events. A **survival contrast** must address **interval censoring**, the **failure function**, **risk prediction**, **competing-risk prediction**, and **cause-specific survival**.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:** **Censoring distribution** mô tả follow-up loss, gồm **administrative censoring** và có thể có **informative censoring**. Analyst define **survival estimand**, có thể là **RMST contrast**, cho **time-to-event outcome**. **Cause-specific effect** có thể evaluate dưới **survival intervention** hoặc rule **dynamic survival** cho **at-risk population**. **Risk difference survival** và **survival treatment effect** summarize comparison, còn **cause-specific cumulative incidence** và **event-specific hazard** describe competing event. **Survival contrast** phải xử lý **interval censoring**, **failure function**, **risk prediction**, **competing-risk prediction** và **cause-specific survival**.
