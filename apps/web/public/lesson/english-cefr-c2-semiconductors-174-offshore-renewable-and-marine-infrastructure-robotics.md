# C2 Vocabulary — Offshore renewable and marine-infrastructure robotics

This lesson follows offshore energy and marine infrastructure from inspection and sensing to maintenance, environmental survey, and decommissioning. The network is **offshore wind inspection drone → subsea cable inspection robot → wind blade cleaning robot → floating platform inspection drone → marine turbine maintenance robot → mooring line inspection robot → underwater foundation robot → offshore substation robot → corrosion monitoring drone → wave energy converter robot → tidal turbine inspection robot → marine debris collection robot → aquaculture platform robot → buoy maintenance robot → coastal erosion survey drone → seabed mapping robot → subsea valve robot → marine sensor deployment robot → offshore safety patrol robot → decommissioning support robot**.

## 1. offshore wind inspection drone /ˈɔːfʃɔːr wɪnd ɪnˈspekʃən droʊn/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a drone that inspects offshore wind turbines, blades, towers, or platforms.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** drone inspect offshore wind turbine, blade, tower hoặc platform.
**Grammar & collocations:** `offshore-wind-inspection-drone camera` — camera; `inspect a blade edge` — inspect blade edge.
**Examples:** `The offshore wind inspection drone photographed a crack near the blade root.` → Drone inspection offshore wind chụp crack gần blade root.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `해상 풍력 검사 드론`.

## Review in context

An **offshore wind inspection drone**, **subsea cable inspection robot**, and **wind blade cleaning robot** inspect renewable assets, while a **floating platform inspection drone**, **marine turbine maintenance robot**, and **mooring line inspection robot** support floating structures. An **underwater foundation robot**, **offshore substation robot**, and **corrosion monitoring drone** protect foundations and power equipment. A **wave energy converter robot**, **tidal turbine inspection robot**, **marine debris collection robot**, and **aquaculture platform robot** work in active water. A **buoy maintenance robot**, **coastal erosion survey drone**, **seabed mapping robot**, **subsea valve robot**, **marine sensor deployment robot**, **offshore safety patrol robot**, and **decommissioning support robot** connect monitoring, maintenance, safety, and end-of-life work.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:** **Offshore wind inspection drone**, **subsea cable inspection robot** và **wind blade cleaning robot** inspect renewable asset, còn **floating platform inspection drone**, **marine turbine maintenance robot** và **mooring line inspection robot** hỗ trợ floating structure. **Underwater foundation robot**, **offshore substation robot** và **corrosion monitoring drone** bảo vệ foundation/power equipment. **Wave energy converter robot**, **tidal turbine inspection robot**, **marine debris collection robot** và **aquaculture platform robot** làm việc trong water. **Buoy maintenance robot**, **coastal erosion survey drone**, **seabed mapping robot**, **subsea valve robot**, **marine sensor deployment robot**, **offshore safety patrol robot** và **decommissioning support robot** nối monitoring, maintenance, safety với end-of-life work.
