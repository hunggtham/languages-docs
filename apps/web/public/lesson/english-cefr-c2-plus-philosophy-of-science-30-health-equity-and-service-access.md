# C2+ Vocabulary — Health equity and service access

This lesson follows how health systems distribute access, recognize unequal burdens, and design care around patient needs. The network is **horizontal equity → vertical equity → needs-based allocation → proportionate universalism → inverse care law → care desert → access friction → cost-related care burden → catastrophic health expenditure → unmet need → delayed care → continuity gap → diagnostic odyssey → care cascade → service integration → multimorbidity → medication burden → integrated care pathway → patient navigation → shared decision-making → health literacy barrier → cultural safety → trauma-informed care → community paramedicine → mobile clinic**.

## 1. horizontal equity /ˌhɔrəˈzɑntəl ˈɛkwəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the principle that people with comparable health needs should receive comparable access, treatment, and respect.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** horizontal equity đối xử tương đương với những người có need tương đương.
**Grammar & collocations:** `horizontal-equity standard` — standard; `apply equal treatment` — apply; `comparable need` — need.
**Examples:** `Horizontal equity required clinics to offer the same appointment rules to patients with similar needs.` → Need tương đương thì rule tương đương.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `수평적 형평성` — horizontal equity.

## 2. vertical equity /ˈvɜrtɪkəl ˈɛkwəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the principle that people with different levels of need may require different resources or forms of support to reach a fair outcome.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** vertical equity cho người có barrier lớn hơn nhiều support hơn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `vertical-equity framework` — framework; `target additional support` — target; `different level of need` — need.
**Examples:** `Vertical equity justified transport vouchers for patients who could not safely reach a distant hospital.` → Barrier khác nhau cần support khác nhau.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `수직적 형평성` — vertical equity.

## 3. needs-based allocation /niːdz beɪst ˌæləˈkeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** distribution of services, funding, or staff according to measured health needs rather than equal shares alone.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** needs-based allocation phân bổ theo need thật, không chỉ chia đều.
**Grammar & collocations:** `needs-based-allocation formula` — formula; `allocate by need` — allocate; `population health profile` — profile.
**Examples:** `Needs-based allocation directed more community nurses to districts with high chronic-disease prevalence.` → Nurse được đưa tới nơi need cao.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `필요 기반 배분` — needs-based allocation.

## 4. proportionate universalism /prəˈpɔrʃənət ˌjuːnəˈvɜrsəlɪzəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an approach that provides universal services while varying their intensity and support in proportion to different levels of disadvantage.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** proportionate universalism là universal nhưng intensity tăng theo disadvantage.
**Grammar & collocations:** `proportionate-universalism policy` — policy; `scale support proportionately` — scale; `universal service` — service.
**Examples:** `Proportionate universalism kept vaccination free for everyone but added outreach in underserved neighborhoods.` → Free cho all, outreach nhiều hơn nơi thiếu.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `비례적 보편주의` — proportionate universalism.

## 5. inverse care law /ˈɪnvɜrs kɛr lɔ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the tendency for access to good medical care to be worst where the need for it is greatest.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** inverse care law là nơi cần care nhất lại access kém nhất.
**Grammar & collocations:** `inverse-care-law pattern` — pattern; `reverse an access gap` — reverse; `high-need population` — population.
**Examples:** `The inverse care law appeared when specialist clinics clustered in affluent districts far from high-need communities.` → Specialist tập trung nơi ít need hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `역의료 법칙` — inverse care law.

## 6. care desert /kɛr ˈdɛzərt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a geographic or social area where essential health services are scarce, distant, or persistently unavailable.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** care desert là vùng “sa mạc” thiếu clinic, provider hoặc transport.
**Grammar & collocations:** `care-desert map` — map; `serve a care desert` — serve; `provider shortage` — shortage.
**Examples:** `A care desert left older residents dependent on long journeys for routine dental treatment.` → Người già phải đi xa chỉ để khám răng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `의료 사막` — care desert.

## 7. access friction /ˈæksɛs ˈfrɪkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** practical difficulty, delay, cost, confusion, or effort that makes it harder for a person to obtain care.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** access friction là ma sát nhỏ nhưng tích lũy làm patient bỏ cuộc.
**Grammar & collocations:** `access-friction audit` — audit; `reduce administrative friction` — reduce; `appointment barrier` — barrier.
**Examples:** `Online forms, repeated identity checks, and limited hours created access friction for shift workers.` → Nhiều friction khiến worker khó đi khám.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `의료 접근 마찰` — access friction.

## 8. cost-related care burden /kɔst rɪˈleɪtɪd kɛr ˈbɜrdən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the financial pressure created by fees, transport, medicines, time away from work, and other expenses required to obtain care.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cost-related care burden gồm cả tiền trực tiếp và chi phí cơ hội khi đi khám.
**Grammar & collocations:** `cost-related-care burden survey` — survey; `carry a care burden` — carry; `transport expense` — expense.
**Examples:** `Cost-related care burden caused patients to skip follow-up visits even when consultation fees were low.` → Chi phí tổng thể vẫn làm bỏ follow-up.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `비용 관련 의료 부담` — cost-related care burden.

## 9. catastrophic health expenditure /ˌkætəˈstrɑfɪk hɛlθ ɪkˈspɛndɪtʃər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** health spending so large relative to a household's resources that it threatens basic living or financial stability.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** catastrophic health expenditure là medical bill vượt sức chịu đựng của household.
**Grammar & collocations:** `catastrophic-health-expenditure threshold` — threshold; `incur catastrophic spending` — incur; `household budget` — budget.
**Examples:** `Catastrophic health expenditure pushed the family to borrow money after a prolonged hospitalization.` → Hospitalization dài tạo debt.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `재난적 의료비` — catastrophic health expenditure.

## 10. unmet need /ʌnˈmɛt niːd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a health problem for which a person needs care but does not receive an appropriate service within the needed time.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** unmet need là need đã rõ nhưng care chưa tới hoặc chưa đủ.
**Grammar & collocations:** `unmet-need indicator` — indicator; `measure unmet need` — measure; `needed treatment` — treatment.
**Examples:** `The survey revealed unmet need for mental-health counseling among unemployed adults.` → Need counseling tồn tại nhưng chưa được đáp ứng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `미충족 의료 필요` — unmet need.

## 11. delayed care /dɪˈleɪd kɛr/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** care that is postponed beyond the clinically or practically appropriate time.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** delayed care là care đến muộn, có thể làm condition nặng hơn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `delayed-care consequence` — consequence; `delay treatment` — delay; `time-sensitive condition` — condition.
**Examples:** `Delayed care converted a manageable infection into a complicated admission.` → Delay làm infection nặng hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `지연된 진료` — delayed care.

## 12. continuity gap /ˌkɑntəˈnuəti ɡæp/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a break in information, responsibility, treatment, or follow-up as a patient moves between services or stages of care.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** continuity gap là đoạn đứt giữa provider, visit hoặc treatment phase.
**Grammar & collocations:** `continuity-gap analysis` — analysis; `close a continuity gap` — close; `care handoff` — handoff.
**Examples:** `A continuity gap emerged when the emergency department discharge note never reached primary care.` → Note không tới primary nên gap.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `진료 연속성 공백` — continuity gap.

## 13. diagnostic odyssey /ˌdaɪəɡˈnɑstɪk ˈɑdəsi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a prolonged, uncertain journey through multiple consultations and tests before a correct diagnosis is reached.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** diagnostic odyssey là patient đi qua nhiều cửa mà chưa có diagnosis đúng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `diagnostic-odyssey burden` — burden; `reach a diagnosis` — reach; `specialist referral` — referral.
**Examples:** `The diagnostic odyssey lasted years because symptoms crossed several specialties.` → Symptoms giao nhiều specialty làm diagnosis lâu.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `진단 여정` — diagnostic odyssey.

## 14. care cascade /kɛr kæˈskeɪd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a chain of tests, referrals, treatments, and follow-up actions triggered by an initial finding or intervention.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** care cascade là một finding kéo theo chuỗi action liên tiếp.
**Grammar & collocations:** `care-cascade review` — review; `trigger a cascade` — trigger; `downstream intervention` — intervention.
**Examples:** `An incidental scan finding started a care cascade that produced anxiety, procedures, and costs.` → Finding tình cờ kéo chuỗi care.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `의료 연쇄` — care cascade.

## 15. service integration /ˈsɜrvɪs ˌɪntəˈɡreɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** coordinated organization of previously separate health or social services around shared goals and patient pathways.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** service integration nối các service rời thành một pathway chung.
**Grammar & collocations:** `service-integration model` — model; `integrate social and clinical care` — integrate; `shared care plan` — plan.
**Examples:** `Service integration connected housing support, primary care, and addiction treatment.` → Housing và medical cùng plan.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `서비스 통합` — service integration.

## 16. multimorbidity /ˌmʌltiˈmɔrbədəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the presence of two or more long-term health conditions in the same person.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** multimorbidity là nhiều chronic condition cùng tồn tại, không chỉ một disease.
**Grammar & collocations:** `multimorbidity care plan` — plan; `manage multiple conditions` — manage; `complex patient` — patient.
**Examples:** `Multimorbidity made single-disease guidelines difficult to apply without adjustment.` → Nhiều disease làm guideline đơn lẻ thiếu phù hợp.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `다중질환` — multimorbidity.

## 17. medication burden /ˌmɛdɪˈkeɪʃən ˈbɜrdən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the combined practical, financial, cognitive, and physical burden of taking and managing medicines.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** medication burden không chỉ số viên; còn lịch uống, side effects và cost.
**Grammar & collocations:** `medication-burden assessment` — assessment; `reduce pill burden` — reduce; `adherence difficulty` — difficulty.
**Examples:** `Medication burden increased when each specialist added a new prescription without reconciliation.` → Mỗi specialist thêm thuốc làm burden tăng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `약물 부담` — medication burden.

## 18. integrated care pathway /ˈɪntəˌɡreɪtɪd kɛr ˈpæθweɪ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a planned sequence of coordinated actions across professionals and settings for a defined patient group or condition.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** integrated care pathway mô tả ai làm gì, khi nào, qua các setting.
**Grammar & collocations:** `integrated-care-pathway map` — map; `follow a pathway` — follow; `multidisciplinary team` — team.
**Examples:** `The integrated care pathway linked screening, diagnosis, rehabilitation, and community follow-up.` → Pathway nối cả journey.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `통합 진료 경로` — integrated care pathway.

## 19. patient navigation /ˈpeɪʃənt ˌnævəˈɡeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** practical guidance that helps patients understand, schedule, reach, and complete a complex care journey.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** patient navigation là người/bộ phận giúp patient không lạc trong hệ thống.
**Grammar & collocations:** `patient-navigation service` — service; `navigate a referral` — navigate; `appointment coordination` — coordination.
**Examples:** `Patient navigation helped a new immigrant arrange interpretation, imaging, and specialist review.` → Navigator nối nhiều bước.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `환자 네비게이션` — patient navigation.

## 20. shared decision-making /ʃɛrd dɪˈsɪʒən ˌmeɪkɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a process in which clinicians and patients combine clinical evidence with the patient's values, goals, and preferences to choose care.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** shared decision-making chia sẻ evidence và preference để chọn treatment.
**Grammar & collocations:** `shared-decision-making aid` — aid; `make an informed choice` — make; `patient preference` — preference.
**Examples:** `Shared decision-making supported a less invasive option that matched the patient's priorities.` → Chọn option hợp priority patient.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `공동 의사결정` — shared decision-making.

## 21. health literacy barrier /hɛlθ ˈlɪtərəsi ˈbæriər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** difficulty understanding, evaluating, or using health information well enough to make or carry out care decisions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** health literacy barrier khiến information không biến thành action đúng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `health-literacy barrier assessment` — assessment; `explain in plain language` — explain; `care instruction` — instruction.
**Examples:** `A health literacy barrier led the patient to double a dose after misreading the label.` → Hiểu sai label gây dùng sai dose.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `건강 문해 장벽` — health literacy barrier.

## 22. cultural safety /ˈkʌltʃərəl ˈseɪfti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** care designed so patients are not demeaned, stereotyped, or made unsafe by cultural power differences or institutional practices.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cultural safety đòi hỏi patient cảm thấy được tôn trọng và không bị áp đặt.
**Grammar & collocations:** `cultural-safety training` — training; `provide culturally safe care` — provide; `power imbalance` — imbalance.
**Examples:** `Cultural safety required the clinic to examine its interpreter policy and assumptions about family roles.` → Clinic phải xem lại policy và assumption.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `문화적 안전` — cultural safety.

## 23. trauma-informed care /ˈtrɔmə ɪnˈfɔrmd kɛr/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** care that recognizes how trauma may affect behavior and trust, and avoids practices that reproduce fear, shame, or loss of control.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** trauma-informed care tạo safety, choice và control thay vì ép buộc.
**Grammar & collocations:** `trauma-informed-care approach` — approach; `avoid retraumatization` — avoid; `restore patient control` — restore.
**Examples:** `Trauma-informed care offered a private waiting space and explained every examination step.` → Giải thích và choice làm patient an toàn hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `트라우마 인지 진료` — trauma-informed care.

## 24. community paramedicine /kəˈmjuːnəti ˌpærəˈmɛdəsən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a model in which paramedics provide expanded assessment, prevention, follow-up, or referral services in community settings.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** community paramedicine đưa năng lực paramedic ra ngoài ambulance và ER.
**Grammar & collocations:** `community-paramedicine program` — program; `conduct a home assessment` — conduct; `prevent an emergency visit` — prevent.
**Examples:** `Community paramedicine checked frail patients at home and escalated warning signs early.` → Paramedic theo dõi tại nhà.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `지역사회 구급의료` — community paramedicine.

## 25. mobile clinic /ˈmoʊbəl ˈklɪnɪk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a movable facility or vehicle that brings selected health services to locations without convenient fixed clinics.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mobile clinic mang service tới nơi patient sống thay vì đợi patient đi xa.
**Grammar & collocations:** `mobile-clinic schedule` — schedule; `deploy a mobile clinic` — deploy; `underserved settlement` — settlement.
**Examples:** `The mobile clinic delivered screening and vaccinations to remote settlements each month.` → Clinic di động lấp khoảng trống địa lý.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `이동 진료소` — mobile clinic.

## Review in context

**Horizontal equity** treats comparable needs comparably, while **vertical equity** and **needs-based allocation** provide more support where barriers are greater. **Proportionate universalism** can reduce the **inverse care law**, especially when a **care desert** creates **access friction**. A **cost-related care burden** may become **catastrophic health expenditure**, leaving **unmet need** and **delayed care**. A **continuity gap** can send a patient into a **diagnostic odyssey** or an unnecessary **care cascade**. **Service integration**, attention to **multimorbidity**, and reduction of **medication burden** support an **integrated care pathway**. **Patient navigation** and **shared decision-making** address a **health literacy barrier**. **Cultural safety** and **trauma-informed care** build trust, while **community paramedicine** and a **mobile clinic** bring coordinated care closer to underserved communities.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

**Horizontal equity** đối xử tương đương với need tương đương, còn **vertical equity** và **needs-based allocation** cung cấp nhiều support hơn nơi barrier lớn hơn. **Proportionate universalism** có thể giảm **inverse care law**, nhất là khi **care desert** tạo **access friction**. **Cost-related care burden** có thể thành **catastrophic health expenditure**, để lại **unmet need** và **delayed care**. **Continuity gap** có thể đẩy patient vào **diagnostic odyssey** hoặc **care cascade** không cần thiết. **Service integration**, chú ý tới **multimorbidity** và giảm **medication burden** hỗ trợ **integrated care pathway**. **Patient navigation** và **shared decision-making** xử lý **health literacy barrier**. **Cultural safety** và **trauma-informed care** xây trust, còn **community paramedicine** và **mobile clinic** đưa care phối hợp tới gần cộng đồng thiếu dịch vụ hơn.
