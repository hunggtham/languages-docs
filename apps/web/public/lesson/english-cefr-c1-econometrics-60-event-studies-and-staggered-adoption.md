# C1 Vocabulary 60 — Event studies and staggered adoption

This lesson follows how policy effects evolve around different adoption dates and how event-study designs avoid misleading comparisons.

Flow: **event-time coefficient → relative-time indicator → staggered adoption → treatment timing → cohort-specific effect → cohort-time effect → pretrend diagnostic → lead coefficient → lag coefficient → event-study plot → never-treated group → not-yet-treated group → treatment cohort → adoption cohort → calendar-time effect → event-time effect → two-way fixed effect → imputation estimator → interaction-weighted estimator → group-time average effect**.

## 1. event-time coefficient /ɪˈvɛnt taɪm koʊəˈfɪʃənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a coefficient describing an outcome difference at a particular time before or after treatment begins.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** coefficient describe outcome difference ở time cụ thể trước hoặc sau treatment start.
**Grammar & collocations:** `dynamic event-time coefficient` — coefficient; `plot event-time coefficients` — plot.
**Examples:** `The event-time coefficient became positive two years after adoption.` → Coefficient event-time positive hai năm sau adoption.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `사건 시점 계수`.

## 2. relative-time indicator /ˈrɛlətɪv taɪm ˈɪndəˌkeɪtər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an indicator identifying how far an observation is from its unit's treatment date.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** indicator identify observation cách treatment date của unit bao xa.
**Grammar & collocations:** `relative-time indicator variable` — variable; `construct a relative-time indicator` — construct.
**Examples:** `The relative-time indicator marked the year before a district adopted the policy.` → Indicator relative-time mark year trước district adopt policy.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `상대 시간 지표`.

## 3. staggered adoption /ˈstæɡərd əˈdɑpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** implementation of a treatment or policy at different times for different units.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** treatment hoặc policy implement ở time khác nhau giữa unit.
**Grammar & collocations:** `staggered-policy adoption` — adoption; `analyze staggered adoption` — analyze.
**Examples:** `Staggered adoption allowed the analyst to compare early and late municipalities.` → Adoption staggered cho analyst compare municipality early và late.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `시차 도입`.

## 4. treatment timing /ˈtriːtmənt ˈtaɪmɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the date or schedule at which a unit receives an intervention.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** date hoặc schedule unit receive intervention.
**Grammar & collocations:** `unknown treatment timing` — timing; `condition on treatment timing` — condition.
**Examples:** `Treatment timing depended on when each hospital completed the upgrade.` → Timing treatment phụ thuộc khi hospital complete upgrade.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `처치 시점`.

## 5. cohort-specific effect /ˈkoʊˌhɔrt spəˈsɪfɪk ɪˈfɛkt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a treatment effect estimated for units that first receive treatment in the same period.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** treatment effect estimate cho unit receive treatment lần đầu cùng period.
**Grammar & collocations:** `cohort-specific effect estimate` — estimate; `compare cohort-specific effects` — compare.
**Examples:** `The cohort-specific effect was larger for regions adopting the program during the recession.` → Effect cohort-specific lớn hơn ở region adopt program trong recession.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `코호트별 효과`.

## 6. cohort-time effect /ˈkoʊˌhɔrt taɪm ɪˈfɛkt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an effect defined jointly for a treatment cohort and a calendar or relative time.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** effect define cùng lúc theo treatment cohort và calendar hoặc relative time.
**Grammar & collocations:** `cohort-time effect estimate` — estimate; `aggregate cohort-time effects` — aggregate.
**Examples:** `The cohort-time effect showed that early adopters improved slowly but steadily.` → Effect cohort-time show adopter early improve chậm nhưng steady.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `코호트-시간 효과`.

## 7. pretrend diagnostic /ˈpriːtrɛnd ˌdaɪəɡˈnɑstɪk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a check of whether treated and comparison units followed similar patterns before treatment.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** check treated và comparison unit có follow pattern tương tự trước treatment không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `visual pretrend diagnostic` — diagnostic; `run a pretrend diagnostic` — run.
**Examples:** `The pretrend diagnostic revealed a widening gap before the policy began.` → Diagnostic pretrend reveal gap widen trước policy start.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `사전 추세 진단`.

## 8. lead coefficient /liːd koʊəˈfɪʃənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an event-study coefficient for a period before treatment, often used to examine pretrends or anticipation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** coefficient event-study cho period trước treatment, dùng examine pretrend hoặc anticipation.
**Grammar & collocations:** `insignificant lead coefficient` — coefficient; `interpret a lead coefficient` — interpret.
**Examples:** `The lead coefficient was close to zero, supporting parallel pre-treatment movement.` → Coefficient lead gần zero, support movement parallel trước treatment.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `선행 계수`.

## 9. lag coefficient /læɡ koʊəˈfɪʃənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an event-study coefficient for a period after treatment, showing how an effect evolves.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** coefficient event-study cho period sau treatment, show effect evolve.
**Grammar & collocations:** `delayed lag coefficient` — coefficient; `estimate lag coefficients` — estimate.
**Examples:** `The lag coefficient peaked after three years and then declined.` → Coefficient lag peak sau ba năm rồi decline.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `시차 계수`.

## 10. event-study plot /ɪˈvɛnt ˌstʌdi plɑt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a graph displaying estimated effects across periods relative to treatment.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** graph display effect estimate qua period relative với treatment.
**Grammar & collocations:** `annotated event-study plot` — plot; `inspect an event-study plot` — inspect.
**Examples:** `The event-study plot showed no clear pre-treatment trend but a persistent post-treatment rise.` → Plot event-study show không pretrend rõ nhưng rise post-treatment persistent.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `사건 연구 도표`.

## 11. never-treated group /ˈnɛvər ˈtriːtɪd ɡruːp/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** units that do not receive the intervention during the study period.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** unit không receive intervention trong study period.
**Grammar & collocations:** `stable never-treated group` — group; `use a never-treated group` — use.
**Examples:** `The never-treated group supplied a comparison for regions that adopted the reform.` → Group never-treated cung cấp comparison cho region adopt reform.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `비처치 집단`.

## 12. not-yet-treated group /nɑt jɛt ˈtriːtɪd ɡruːp/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** units that will receive treatment later but remain untreated at a particular comparison time.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** unit sẽ receive treatment later nhưng còn untreated ở comparison time.
**Grammar & collocations:** `appropriate not-yet-treated group` — group; `compare with the not-yet-treated group` — compare.
**Examples:** `The not-yet-treated group was useful before its own adoption date.` → Group not-yet-treated hữu ích trước adoption date của nó.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `아직 처치되지 않은 집단`.

## 13. treatment cohort /ˈtriːtmənt ˈkoʊˌhɔrt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** units that first receive treatment during the same period.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** unit first receive treatment trong cùng period.
**Grammar & collocations:** `early treatment cohort` — cohort; `define a treatment cohort` — define.
**Examples:** `The treatment cohort from 2018 had four years of follow-up.` → Cohort treatment từ 2018 có four năm follow-up.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `처치 코호트`.

## 14. adoption cohort /əˈdɑpʃən ˈkoʊˌhɔrt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a group of units classified by the period in which they adopt a policy or technology.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** group unit classify theo period adopt policy hoặc technology.
**Grammar & collocations:** `late adoption cohort` — cohort; `compare adoption cohorts` — compare.
**Examples:** `The late adoption cohort faced a different economic environment.` → Cohort adoption late face economic environment khác.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `도입 코호트`.

## 15. calendar-time effect /ˈkæləndər taɪm ɪˈfɛkt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an outcome change associated with a particular calendar period rather than with time since treatment.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** outcome change liên quan calendar period cụ thể, không phải time since treatment.
**Grammar & collocations:** `common calendar-time effect` — effect; `separate calendar-time effects` — separate.
**Examples:** `The calendar-time effect captured a national recession affecting all regions.` → Effect calendar-time capture recession national affect mọi region.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `달력 시간 효과`.

## 16. event-time effect /ɪˈvɛnt taɪm ɪˈfɛkt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an effect associated with time elapsed since a unit received treatment.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** effect liên quan time elapsed từ khi unit receive treatment.
**Grammar & collocations:** `dynamic event-time effect` — effect; `estimate event-time effects` — estimate.
**Examples:** `The event-time effect grew with each year after implementation.` → Effect event-time grow mỗi year sau implementation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `사건 시간 효과`.

## 17. two-way fixed effect /tuː weɪ fɪkst ɪˈfɛkt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a panel specification with unit effects and time effects, often used to compare changes across units and periods.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** panel specification có unit effect và time effect để compare change.
**Grammar & collocations:** `two-way fixed-effects regression` — regression; `fit a two-way fixed effect` — fit.
**Examples:** `The two-way fixed effect absorbed permanent regional differences and common yearly shocks.` → Effect fixed two-way absorb difference region permanent và shock yearly chung.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `양방향 고정효과`.

## 18. imputation estimator /ˌɪmpjəˈteɪʃən ˈɛstəˌmeɪtər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an estimator that predicts untreated potential outcomes and uses the predictions to calculate treatment effects.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** estimator predict untreated potential outcome rồi dùng prediction calculate treatment effect.
**Grammar & collocations:** `outcome-model imputation estimator` — estimator; `use an imputation estimator` — use.
**Examples:** `The imputation estimator predicted each treated unit's untreated outcome from comparison periods.` → Estimator imputation predict untreated outcome của treated unit từ comparison period.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `대체 추정량`.

## 19. interaction-weighted estimator /ˌɪntərˈækʃən ˈweɪtɪd ˈɛstəˌmeɪtər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an estimator that combines treatment-by-time effects with weights reflecting their relative contribution.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** estimator combine treatment-by-time effect với weight reflect contribution relative.
**Grammar & collocations:** `interaction-weighted event study` — study; `calculate an interaction-weighted estimator` — calculate.
**Examples:** `The interaction-weighted estimator gave later cohorts less influence on early event times.` → Estimator interaction-weighted give cohort later ít influence hơn ở event time sớm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `상호작용 가중 추정량`.

## 20. group-time average effect /ɡruːp taɪm ˈævərɪdʒ ɪˈfɛkt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an average treatment effect for a treatment group at a particular calendar time.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** average treatment effect cho treatment group ở calendar time cụ thể.
**Grammar & collocations:** `group-time average treatment effect` — effect; `aggregate group-time average effects` — aggregate.
**Examples:** `The group-time average effect measured the policy's impact on the 2020 adopters in 2022.` → Effect average group-time measure impact policy với adopter 2020 trong 2022.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `집단-시간 평균 효과`.

## Review in context

An **event-time coefficient** uses a **relative-time indicator** in a setting of **staggered adoption** and varying **treatment timing**. A **cohort-specific effect** and a **cohort-time effect** can be checked with a **pretrend diagnostic**, a **lead coefficient**, a **lag coefficient**, and an **event-study plot**. A **never-treated group** or a **not-yet-treated group** may provide comparisons for each **treatment cohort** or **adoption cohort**. Analysts separate a **calendar-time effect** from an **event-time effect**, question a **two-way fixed effect**, and consider an **imputation estimator**, an **interaction-weighted estimator**, and a **group-time average effect**.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:** **Event-time coefficient** dùng **relative-time indicator** trong setting **staggered adoption** và **treatment timing** khác nhau. **Cohort-specific effect** và **cohort-time effect** có thể check bằng **pretrend diagnostic**, **lead coefficient**, **lag coefficient** và **event-study plot**. **Never-treated group** hoặc **not-yet-treated group** có thể làm comparison cho **treatment cohort** hoặc **adoption cohort**. Analyst tách **calendar-time effect** khỏi **event-time effect**, question **two-way fixed effect**, và cân nhắc **imputation estimator**, **interaction-weighted estimator** cùng **group-time average effect**.
