# C2+ Vocabulary — Epistemic infrastructure and data stewardship

This lesson follows the infrastructure that makes research traceable, reusable, durable, and accountable. The network is **epistemic infrastructure → data infrastructure → research commons → open notebook → auditability → chain traceability → reproducible workflow → computational notebook → revision control → metadata standard → concept alignment → data interoperability → FAIR principle → repository access policy → data stewardship practice → archival persistence → media decay → provenance chain → curation burden → data rescue → legacy dataset → schema versioning → meaning drift → institutional recall → research data lifecycle**.

## 1. epistemic infrastructure /ɪˌpɪstɛmɪk ˈɪnfrəˌstrʌktʃər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the people, tools, standards, records, institutions, and routines that make knowledge production possible and accountable.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** epistemic infrastructure là nền móng gồm người, tool, standard, record và routine của knowledge.
**Grammar & collocations:** `epistemic-infrastructure investment` — investment; `maintain an infrastructure` — maintain; `knowledge-producing system` — system.
**Examples:** `Reliable calibration labs are part of the epistemic infrastructure of environmental monitoring.` → Calibration lab là infrastructure của monitoring.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `인식론적 인프라` — epistemic infrastructure.

## 2. data infrastructure /ˈdeɪtə ˈɪnfrəˌstrʌktʃər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** systems and standards for collecting, storing, securing, processing, sharing, and preserving data.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** data infrastructure bao phủ từ collect/store đến secure/process/share/preserve.
**Grammar & collocations:** `data-infrastructure layer` — layer; `build data infrastructure` — build; `storage and processing system` — system.
**Examples:** `The observatory upgraded its data infrastructure before adding more sensors.` → Observatory upgrade infrastructure trước thêm sensor.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `데이터 인프라` — data infrastructure.

## 3. research commons /rɪˈsɜrtʃ ˈkɑmənz/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** shared research resources governed for collective access, contribution, maintenance, and reuse.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** research commons là shared resource có rule để access, contribute, maintain và reuse.
**Grammar & collocations:** `research-commons governance` — governance; `contribute to a commons` — contribute; `shared research asset` — asset.
**Examples:** `The genomic repository functioned as a research commons with clear reuse conditions.` → Repository là commons với rule reuse rõ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `연구 공유지` — research commons.

## 4. open notebook /ˈoʊpən ˈnoʊtˌbʊk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a publicly accessible record of ongoing research decisions, analyses, observations, and revisions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** open notebook mở process đang diễn ra, không chỉ final paper.
**Grammar & collocations:** `open-notebook practice` — practice; `document an ongoing analysis` — document; `public research log` — log.
**Examples:** `The open notebook recorded why the team changed its sampling schedule.` → Notebook ghi lý do đổi schedule.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `공개 연구 노트` — open notebook.

## 5. auditability /ˌɔːdətəˈbɪləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the degree to which a process, result, or decision can be inspected and reconstructed by an independent reviewer.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** auditability cho phép reviewer đi ngược từ result về data, code, decision và assumption.
**Grammar & collocations:** `auditability requirement` — requirement; `make a workflow auditable` — make; `reconstruct a decision` — reconstruct.
**Examples:** `Versioned scripts increased the auditability of the cost estimate.` → Script versioned tăng auditability của estimate.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `감사 가능성` — auditability.

## 6. chain traceability /tʃeɪn ˌtreɪsəˈbɪləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the ability to follow a result through every documented transformation back to its source materials and decisions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** chain traceability nối result qua từng transformation về source/decision.
**Grammar & collocations:** `chain-traceability record` — record; `trace a value backward` — trace; `documented transformation` — transformation.
**Examples:** `Chain traceability showed which raw files contributed to the published figure.` → Traceability chỉ raw file nào tạo figure.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `연쇄 추적성` — chain traceability.

## 7. reproducible workflow /rɪˌproʊdjuːsəbəl ˈwɜrkˌfloʊ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an ordered process in which another person can rerun the same inputs, code, and steps to obtain comparable outputs.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** reproducible workflow là quy trình người khác rerun được với input/code/step rõ.
**Grammar & collocations:** `reproducible-workflow template` — template; `rerun a workflow` — rerun; `document dependencies` — document.
**Examples:** `The reproducible workflow rebuilt every table from the archived raw data.` → Workflow rebuild mọi table từ raw data.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `재현 가능한 작업 흐름` — reproducible workflow.

## 8. computational notebook /ˌkɑmpjəˈteɪʃənəl ˈnoʊtˌbʊk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an interactive document combining executable code, data, narrative, visualizations, and results.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** computational notebook gộp code, data, prose, plot và result trong một artifact.
**Grammar & collocations:** `computational-notebook environment` — environment; `execute a cell` — execute; `embedded visualization` — visualization.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `계산 노트북` — computational notebook.

## 9. revision control /rɪˈvɪʒən kənˈtroʊl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** tracking and managing successive versions of code, data, documents, or model specifications.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** revision control giữ history version để biết ai đổi gì, khi nào và revert được.

## 11. concept alignment /ˈkɑnsɛpt əˈlaɪnmənt/

## 12. data interoperability /ˈdeɪtə ˌɪntərˌɑpərəˈbɪləti/

## 13. FAIR principle /fɛr ˈprɪnsəpəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the principle that research data should be findable, accessible, interoperable, and reusable under appropriate conditions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** FAIR = Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable; không đồng nghĩa mọi data phải public vô điều kiện.
**Grammar & collocations:** `FAIR-principle checklist` — checklist; `make data reusable` — make; `appropriate access condition` — condition.
**Examples:** `The repository applied the FAIR principle while keeping sensitive records under controlled access.` → Repository theo FAIR nhưng giữ record nhạy cảm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `FAIR 원칙` — FAIR principle.

## 17. media decay /ˈmiːdiə dɪˈkeɪ/

**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** media decay làm storage/file format xuống cấp, data không đọc được dù chưa bị xóa.

## Review in context

An **epistemic infrastructure** depends on **data infrastructure**, a **research commons**, and practices such as an **open notebook**. **Auditability** and **chain traceability** make a **reproducible workflow** inspectable; a **computational notebook** and **revision control** preserve steps. A **metadata standard**, **concept alignment**, and **data interoperability** support the **FAIR principle**, while a **repository access policy** and **data stewardship practice** govern responsible reuse. **Archival persistence** guards against **media decay**; a **provenance chain** records custody, but the **curation burden** remains. **Data rescue** can preserve a **legacy dataset** if **schema versioning** documents change and prevents **meaning drift**. **Institutional recall** keeps decisions visible across the full **research data lifecycle**.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

**Epistemic infrastructure** dựa vào **data infrastructure**, **research commons** và practice như **open notebook**. **Auditability** và **chain traceability** làm **reproducible workflow** inspectable; **computational notebook** và **revision control** giữ step. **Metadata standard**, **concept alignment** và **data interoperability** hỗ trợ **FAIR principle**, còn **repository access policy** và **data stewardship practice** quản lý reuse có trách nhiệm. **Archival persistence** chống **media decay**; **provenance chain** ghi custody, nhưng **curation burden** vẫn còn. **Data rescue** có thể giữ **legacy dataset** nếu **schema versioning** document change và ngăn **meaning drift**. **Institutional recall** giữ decision visible trong toàn **research data lifecycle**.
