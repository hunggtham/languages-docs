# C2+ Vocabulary — Science communication and public reasoning

This lesson follows how scientific claims move into public discussion, how audiences interpret uncertainty, and how trust can be earned or distorted. The network is **deficit model → dialogue model → public understanding → scientific literacy → public epistemic trust → credibility calibration → misinformation inoculation → calibrated uncertainty communication → hazard communication → issue framing effect → narrative framing → directional reasoning → group-defensive cognition → epistemic vigilance → source-reliability judgment → provenance cue → correction efficacy → belief polarization → opinion dynamics → consensus heuristic → false balance → sensational framing → click-driven amplification → public engagement → citizen science**.

## 1. deficit model /ˈdɛfəsɪt ˈmɑdəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the assumption that public disagreement with science mainly results from a lack of information that more information will fix.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** deficit model coi public như thiếu knowledge; chỉ cần “đổ thêm facts” là đổi belief.
**Grammar & collocations:** `deficit-model assumption` — assumption; `fill an information deficit` — fill; `one-way transmission` — transmission.
**Examples:** `The campaign moved beyond the deficit model after facts alone failed to change behavior.` → Campaign vượt deficit model khi facts không đủ đổi behavior.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `결핍 모형` — deficit model.

## 2. dialogue model /ˈdaɪəˌlɔɡ ˈmɑdəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an approach that treats communication as two-way exchange in which researchers listen to public questions, experience, and values.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** dialogue model coi communication là hai chiều: researcher nghe experience/value của public.
**Grammar & collocations:** `dialogue-model forum` — forum; `exchange questions` — exchange; `listen to community concerns` — listen.
**Examples:** `The dialogue model invited residents to redefine which outcomes mattered.` → Model mời resident cùng define outcome.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `대화 모형` — dialogue model.

## 3. public understanding /ˈpʌblɪk ˌʌndərˈstændɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the knowledge, interpretation, and practical sense that non-specialist audiences develop about scientific ideas and evidence.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** public understanding không chỉ nhớ fact; còn hiểu evidence, limit và implication.
**Grammar & collocations:** `public-understanding survey` — survey; `improve understanding` — improve; `non-specialist audience` — audience.
**Examples:** `The exhibit improved public understanding by showing uncertainty rather than hiding it.` → Exhibit cải thiện understanding bằng cách cho uncertainty.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `대중의 이해` — public understanding.

## 4. scientific literacy /ˌsaɪənˈtɪfɪk ˈlɪtərəsi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the ability to interpret scientific claims, evidence, uncertainty, methods, and implications well enough for informed participation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** scientific literacy là đọc claim/evidence/uncertainty để tham gia decision, không cần thành specialist.
**Grammar & collocations:** `scientific-literacy program` — program; `interpret evidence` — interpret; `evaluate a claim` — evaluate.
**Examples:** `Scientific literacy helped voters distinguish a confidence interval from a guaranteed range.` → Literacy giúp phân biệt interval với guarantee.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `과학적 소양` — scientific literacy.

## 5. public epistemic trust /ˈpʌblɪk ˌɛpɪˈstɛmɪk trʌst/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** public confidence that an institution or expert community is a reliable source of knowledge.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** public epistemic trust là trust vào competence, honesty và correction của knowledge source.
**Grammar & collocations:** `public-epistemic-trust survey` — survey; `earn public trust` — earn; `reliable knowledge source` — source.
**Examples:** `Public epistemic trust recovered after the agency published the raw data and correction process.` → Trust phục hồi sau raw data và correction process.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `대중의 인식론적 신뢰` — public epistemic trust.

## 6. credibility calibration /ˈkrɛdəbɪləti ˌkælɪˈbreɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** adjustment of trust in a source so that confidence matches the source's demonstrated competence, limits, and incentives.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** credibility calibration là tin vừa đủ: không blind trust, không cynical distrust.
**Grammar & collocations:** `credibility-calibration cue` — cue; `calibrate trust` — calibrate; `source track record` — record.
**Examples:** `Transparent uncertainty helped audiences perform credibility calibration instead of treating the expert as infallible.` → Uncertainty giúp audience calibrate trust.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `신뢰성 보정` — credibility calibration.

## 7. misinformation inoculation /ˌmɪsɪnfərˈmeɪʃən ɪˌnɑkjəˈleɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** preparation that exposes people to weakened examples of misleading tactics and gives them tools to recognize those tactics later.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** misinformation inoculation “tiêm” warning + tactic recognition trước khi gặp misinformation mạnh.
**Grammar & collocations:** `misinformation-inoculation intervention` — intervention; `prebunk a tactic` — prebunk; `recognize manipulation` — recognize.
**Examples:** `Misinformation inoculation taught students how false experts manufacture certainty.` → Inoculation dạy tactic tạo certainty giả.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `허위 정보 면역` — misinformation inoculation.

## 8. calibrated uncertainty communication /ˈkælɪˌbreɪtɪd ʌnˈsɜrtənti kəˌmjuːnəˈkeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** communication that expresses uncertainty with enough precision to guide interpretation without creating false confidence or paralysis.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** calibrated uncertainty communication nói uncertainty vừa đủ rõ để action, không overclaim hoặc làm người nghe tê liệt.
**Grammar & collocations:** `calibrated-uncertainty message` — message; `communicate confidence levels` — communicate; `avoid false precision` — avoid.
**Examples:** `Calibrated uncertainty communication explained what was known, probable, and still open.` → Communication tách known/probable/open.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `보정된 불확실성 소통` — calibrated uncertainty communication.

## 9. hazard communication /ˈhæzərd kəˌmjuːnəˈkeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** communication about the nature, likelihood, severity, and protective implications of a danger.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** hazard communication cho biết hazard gì, likely/severe ra sao và bảo vệ thế nào.
**Grammar & collocations:** `hazard-communication label` — label; `communicate protective guidance` — communicate; `risk audience` — audience.
**Examples:** `The hazard communication notice explained both exposure routes and practical precautions.` → Notice giải thích exposure route và precaution.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `위험 소통` — hazard communication.

## 10. issue framing effect /ˈɪʃu ˈfreɪmɪŋ ɪˈfɛkt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a change in interpretation or support caused by presenting the same issue through different emphases, categories, or reference points.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** issue framing effect làm reaction đổi vì cách đặt issue, dù underlying fact giống.
**Grammar & collocations:** `issue-framing effect` — effect; `frame a policy question` — frame; `reference point` — point.
**Examples:** `The issue framing effect was visible when the same statistic was described as lives saved or lives at risk.` → Cùng statistic nhưng framing đổi reaction.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `쟁점 프레이밍 효과` — issue framing effect.

## 11. narrative framing /ˈnærətɪv ˈfreɪmɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** selection and arrangement of events, characters, causes, and consequences to guide how an audience interprets evidence.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** narrative framing chọn event/character/cause để dẫn audience vào một meaning.
**Grammar & collocations:** `narrative-framing strategy` — strategy; `frame a story` — frame; `causal storyline` — storyline.
**Examples:** `The narrative framing centered local adaptation rather than technological failure.` → Story frame center local adaptation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `서사적 프레이밍` — narrative framing.

## 12. directional reasoning /dəˈrɛkʃənəl ˈriːzənɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** reasoning that selectively favors evidence or interpretations supporting a desired conclusion.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** directional reasoning đi về conclusion mong muốn, khác với truth-seeking open update.
**Grammar & collocations:** `directional-reasoning bias` — bias; `favor confirming evidence` — favor; `desired conclusion` — conclusion.
**Examples:** `Directional reasoning led the team to explain away every unfavorable trial result.` → Reasoning làm team dismiss result bất lợi.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `방향성 추론` — directional reasoning.

## 13. group-defensive cognition /ɡruːp dɪˈfɛnsɪv kɑɡˈnɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** reasoning shaped by a desire to protect the status, identity, or moral standing of one's group.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** group-defensive cognition bảo vệ identity/status của group trước evidence đe dọa.
**Grammar & collocations:** `group-defensive-cognition pattern` — pattern; `protect group identity` — protect; `identity threat` — threat.
**Examples:** `Group-defensive cognition made the community reject a safety finding associated with a rival institution.` → Group reject finding vì identity threat.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `집단 방어적 인지` — group-defensive cognition.

## 14. epistemic vigilance /ɪˌpɪstɛmɪk ˈvɪdʒələns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** active monitoring for unreliable testimony, faulty reasoning, manipulation, or unsupported claims.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** epistemic vigilance là “bộ lọc cảnh giác” với source, logic và incentive.
**Grammar & collocations:** `epistemic-vigilance practice` — practice; `screen a claim` — screen; `detect manipulation` — detect.
**Examples:** `Epistemic vigilance prompted readers to check the denominator before sharing the headline.` → Vigilance khiến reader check denominator.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `인식론적 경계` — epistemic vigilance.

## 15. source-reliability judgment /sɔrs rɪˌlaɪəˈbɪləti ˈdʒʌdʒmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** assessment of whether a source is likely to provide accurate, competent, and appropriately qualified information.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** source-reliability judgment đánh giá accuracy, competence, qualification của source.
**Grammar & collocations:** `source-reliability judgment` — judgment; `assess source reliability` — assess; `qualified source` — source.
**Examples:** `The source-reliability judgment changed after the outlet corrected two major errors.` → Judgment đổi sau correction.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `출처 신뢰성 판단` — source-reliability judgment.

## 16. provenance cue /ˈprɑvənəns kjuː/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a signal about where information came from, how it was produced, or whether its chain of custody is traceable.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** provenance cue cho biết source/process/history của information để assess trust.
**Grammar & collocations:** `provenance-cue label` — label; `signal data origin` — signal; `traceable source` — source.
**Examples:** `A provenance cue showed that the image had been generated rather than photographed in the field.` → Cue cho biết image generated.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `출처 단서` — provenance cue.

## 17. correction efficacy /kəˈrɛkʃən ˈɛfɪkəsi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the extent to which a correction reduces belief in a false claim without creating new confusion or backfire.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** correction efficacy hỏi correction có sửa belief thật không hay gây confusion/backfire.
**Grammar & collocations:** `correction-efficacy study` — study; `correct a false belief` — correct; `backfire effect` — effect.
**Examples:** `Correction efficacy improved when the message supplied a clear replacement explanation.` → Correction hiệu quả hơn khi có replacement explanation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `정정 효과` — correction efficacy.

## 18. belief polarization /bɪˈliːf ˌpoʊlərəˈzeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** movement of groups toward more extreme and opposed positions after processing the same or similar information.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** belief polarization làm group đi xa nhau hơn thay vì hội tụ.
**Grammar & collocations:** `belief-polarization dynamic` — dynamic; `polarize a debate` — polarize; `extreme position` — position.
**Examples:** `Belief polarization increased when each group consumed only identity-confirming sources.` → Polarization tăng khi source chỉ confirm identity.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `신념 양극화` — belief polarization.

## 19. opinion dynamics /əˈpɪnjən daɪˈnæmɪks/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** study of how individual views change, persist, cluster, or spread through social interaction and information.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** opinion dynamics xem belief lan/đổi/cluster qua social interaction.
**Grammar & collocations:** `opinion-dynamics model` — model; `update an opinion` — update; `social influence` — influence.
**Examples:** `The opinion dynamics model showed how a small activist group could shift the public conversation.` → Model cho activist group shift conversation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `의견 동학` — opinion dynamics.

## 20. consensus heuristic /kənˈsɛns hjʊˈrɪstɪk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a shortcut that treats apparent agreement among many people or institutions as evidence that a claim is reliable.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** consensus heuristic dùng agreement như shortcut cho trust, nhưng consensus có thể bị manufactured.
**Grammar & collocations:** `consensus-heuristic cue` — cue; `infer reliability from agreement` — infer; `apparent consensus` — consensus.
**Examples:** `The consensus heuristic was misleading when the same press release was copied across outlets.` → Heuristic sai khi outlets copy cùng release.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `합의 휴리스틱` — consensus heuristic.

## 21. false balance /fɔls ˈbæləns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** presentation that gives weak or unsupported positions equal weight with well-supported evidence in the name of neutrality.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** false balance “cân bằng giả” giữa evidence mạnh và claim yếu.
**Grammar & collocations:** `false-balance critique` — critique; `give equal weight` — give; `unsupported position` — position.
**Examples:** `The article avoided false balance by explaining why one claim lacked credible evidence.` → Article tránh cân bằng giả.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `거짓 균형` — false balance.

## 22. sensational framing /sɛnˈseɪʃənəl ˈfreɪmɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** presentation that emphasizes shock, fear, novelty, or conflict at the expense of proportion and context.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** sensational framing phóng đại shock/fear/novelty làm mất proportion.
**Grammar & collocations:** `sensational-framing tactic` — tactic; `amplify a dramatic angle` — amplify; `strip context` — strip.
**Examples:** `Sensational framing made a rare side effect sound inevitable.` → Framing làm side effect hiếm trông inevitable.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `선정적 프레이밍` — sensational framing.

## 23. click-driven amplification /klɪk ˈdrɪvən ˌæmpləfəˈkeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** enlargement of attention or reach caused by systems that reward content for attracting clicks rather than informing accurately.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** click-driven amplification đẩy content vì click/engagement, không vì accuracy.
**Grammar & collocations:** `click-driven-amplification loop` — loop; `reward engagement` — reward; `attention metric` — metric.
**Examples:** `Click-driven amplification pushed the alarming headline far beyond the cautious study.` → Amplification đẩy headline alarmist xa hơn study.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `클릭 기반 증폭` — click-driven amplification.

## 24. public engagement /ˈpʌblɪk ɛnˈɡeɪdʒmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** structured interaction between researchers and publics to exchange knowledge, questions, experience, and priorities.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** public engagement là interaction hai chiều với public, không chỉ broadcast.
**Grammar & collocations:** `public-engagement program` — program; `engage affected communities` — engage; `co-design a question` — co-design.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `대중 참여` — public engagement.

## 25. citizen science /ˈsɪtəzən ˈsaɪəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** research in which members of the public contribute to data collection, analysis, interpretation, or question design.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** citizen science đưa public vào data/question, cần training và quality control phù hợp.
**Grammar & collocations:** `citizen-science project` — project; `collect observations` — collect; `community-generated data` — data.
**Examples:** `Citizen science expanded the bird survey across neighborhoods that researchers could not visit daily.` → Citizen science mở rộng bird survey.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `시민 과학` — citizen science.

## Review in context

The **deficit model** treats communication as filling missing facts, whereas a **dialogue model** builds **public understanding** and **scientific literacy** through exchange. **Public epistemic trust** depends on **credibility calibration**, clear **misinformation inoculation**, and **calibrated uncertainty communication**. **Hazard communication** and an **issue framing effect** shape how people interpret danger; **narrative framing**, **directional reasoning**, and **group-defensive cognition** can pull interpretation away from evidence. **Epistemic vigilance**, a **source-reliability judgment**, and a **provenance cue** help audiences evaluate claims. **Correction efficacy** matters when beliefs persist or undergo **belief polarization**; **opinion dynamics** describes the spread. A **consensus heuristic** can mislead, while **false balance**, **sensational framing**, and **click-driven amplification** distort proportion. **Public engagement** and **citizen science** create more reciprocal participation.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

**Deficit model** coi communication là bù facts thiếu, còn **dialogue model** xây **public understanding** và **scientific literacy** qua exchange. **Public epistemic trust** dựa vào **credibility calibration**, **misinformation inoculation** rõ và **calibrated uncertainty communication**. **Hazard communication** và **issue framing effect** định hình cách hiểu danger; **narrative framing**, **directional reasoning** và **group-defensive cognition** có thể kéo interpretation khỏi evidence. **Epistemic vigilance**, **source-reliability judgment** và **provenance cue** giúp audience evaluate claim. **Correction efficacy** quan trọng khi belief persist hoặc bị **belief polarization**; **opinion dynamics** mô tả spread. **Consensus heuristic** có thể mislead, còn **false balance**, **sensational framing** và **click-driven amplification** làm méo proportion. **Public engagement** và **citizen science** tạo participation hai chiều hơn.
