# C2+ Vocabulary — Archaeobotany and zooarchaeology evidence

This lesson follows archaeological environmental evidence from plant microremains and flotation through animal bones, domestication, and taphonomic interpretation. The network is **palynological profile → diatom analysis → phytolith → charred macroremain → archaeobotanical → coprolite → coastal midden → flotation fraction → taphonomic signature → zooarchaeological quantification → faunal assemblage → cut mark archaeology → butchery mark → domestication syndrome → rachis → glume → chaff → carbonized seed → paleoethnobotany → starch grain analysis**.

## 1. palynological profile /ˌpælɪnəˈlɑːdʒɪkəl ˈproʊfaɪl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a record of preserved pollen types through a sediment sequence, used to reconstruct past vegetation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** record pollen type preserve qua sediment sequence để reconstruct vegetation quá khứ.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high-resolution palynological profile` — profile độ phân giải cao; `build a palynological profile` — build.
**Examples:** `The high-resolution palynological profile showed a shift from forest to grassland.` → Palynological profile độ phân giải cao cho thấy shift từ forest sang grassland.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `화분 분석 프로필`.

## 2. diatom analysis /ˈdaɪətəm əˈnæləsɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** studying microscopic algae in sediments to infer past water conditions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** study microscopic algae trong sediment để infer water condition quá khứ.
**Grammar & collocations:** `freshwater diatom analysis` — analysis diatom freshwater; `conduct diatom analysis` — conduct.
**Examples:** `Freshwater diatom analysis indicated that the ancient lake became more saline.` → Diatom analysis freshwater cho thấy ancient lake mặn hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `규조 분석`.

## 3. phytolith /ˈfaɪtəlɪθ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a microscopic silica body formed inside plant tissue and preserved after the plant decays.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** silica body microscopic hình thành trong plant tissue và preserve sau decay.
**Grammar & collocations:** `grass phytolith` — phytolith grass; `identify a phytolith` — identify.
**Examples:** `A grass phytolith survived where no visible plant fragment remained.` → Phytolith grass survive dù không còn plant fragment visible.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `식물규산체`.

## 4. charred macroremain /tʃɑːrd ˈmækroʊrɪmeɪn/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a visible plant fragment preserved through burning, such as a seed, wood piece, or nutshell.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** plant fragment visible preserve nhờ burning, như seed, wood hoặc nutshell.
**Grammar & collocations:** `charred seed macroremain` — macroremain seed charred; `recover a charred macroremain` — recover.
**Examples:** `The team recovered a charred macroremain from the hearth sediment.` → Team recover charred macroremain từ hearth sediment.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `탄화 식물 잔존물`.

## 5. archaeobotanical /ˌɑːrkiːoʊbəˈtænɪkəl/
**Part of speech:** adjective
**Core meaning (English):** relating to the study of ancient plant remains and their role in human activity.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** liên quan study plant remains cổ và role trong human activity.
**Grammar & collocations:** `archaeobotanical evidence` — evidence archaeobotanical; `archaeobotanical sampling` — sampling.
**Examples:** `Archaeobotanical evidence suggested that the settlement stored several cereals.` → Archaeobotanical evidence gợi ý settlement store nhiều cereal.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `고고식물학의`.

## 6. coprolite /ˈkɑːprəlaɪt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** fossilized or mineralized animal dung that can preserve evidence of diet and environment.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** dung animal fossilize/mineralize, preserve evidence diet và environment.
**Grammar & collocations:** `human coprolite` — coprolite human; `analyze a coprolite` — analyze.
**Examples:** `The human coprolite contained seeds and parasite eggs.` → Coprolite human chứa seed và parasite egg.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `분화석`.

## 7. coastal midden /ˈkoʊstəl ˈmɪdən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an archaeological refuse deposit near a coast, often containing shells, fish bones, and tools.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** refuse deposit archaeological gần coast, có shell, fish bone và tool.
**Grammar & collocations:** `shell-rich coastal midden` — midden nhiều shell; `excavate a coastal midden` — excavate.
**Examples:** `The shell-rich coastal midden showed repeated seasonal occupation.` → Coastal midden nhiều shell cho thấy occupation theo mùa lặp lại.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `해안 패총`.

## 8. flotation fraction /floʊˈteɪʃən ˈfrækʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the light material recovered by floating excavated sediment in water.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** material nhẹ recover bằng float excavated sediment trong water.
**Grammar & collocations:** `light flotation fraction` — fraction nhẹ; `sort the flotation fraction` — sort.
**Examples:** `The light flotation fraction contained tiny seeds and charcoal.` → Flotation fraction nhẹ chứa seed nhỏ và charcoal.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `부유 선별 분획`.

## 9. taphonomic signature /ˌtæfəˈnɑːmɪk ˈsɪɡnətʃər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the pattern of damage, burial, decay, and disturbance that affects remains after deposition.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** pattern damage, burial, decay và disturbance ảnh hưởng remain sau deposition.
**Grammar & collocations:** `distinctive taphonomic signature` — signature đặc trưng; `interpret a taphonomic signature` — interpret.
**Examples:** `The taphonomic signature showed that the bones had been exposed before burial.` → Taphonomic signature cho thấy bone expose trước burial.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `화석화 과정 흔적`.

## 10. zooarchaeological quantification /ˌzuːoʊˌɑːrkiəˈlɑːdʒɪkəl ˌkwɑːntəfɪˈkeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** measuring and comparing animal remains to estimate patterns of human use and site activity.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** measure/compare animal remain để estimate human use và site activity.
**Grammar & collocations:** `careful zooarchaeological quantification` — quantification cẩn thận; `perform quantification` — perform.
**Examples:** `Zooarchaeological quantification revealed a preference for young sheep.` → Zooarchaeological quantification reveal preference cho sheep non.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `동물고고학 정량화`.

## 11. faunal assemblage /ˈfɔːnəl əˈsɛmblɑːʒ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the collection of animal remains recovered from an archaeological context.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** collection animal remain recover từ archaeological context.
**Grammar & collocations:** `mixed faunal assemblage` — assemblage mixed; `analyze a faunal assemblage` — analyze.
**Examples:** `The mixed faunal assemblage included domestic and wild species.` → Faunal assemblage mixed gồm species domestic và wild.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `동물 유체 집합`.

## 12. cut mark archaeology /kʌt mɑːrk ˌɑːrkiˈɑːlədʒi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the archaeological analysis of fine marks made on bone by stone or metal tools.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** analysis archaeological của mark nhỏ do stone/metal tool tạo trên bone.
**Grammar & collocations:** `experimental cut mark archaeology` — archaeology cut mark experimental; `apply cut mark analysis` — apply.
**Examples:** `Cut mark archaeology distinguished skinning from marrow extraction.` → Cut mark archaeology phân biệt skinning với marrow extraction.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `절흔 고고학`.

## 13. butchery mark /ˈbʊtʃəri mɑːrk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a cut, chop, or scrape on an animal bone caused by processing a carcass.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cut, chop hoặc scrape trên bone do process carcass.
**Grammar & collocations:** `fresh butchery mark` — mark mới; `identify a butchery mark` — identify.
**Examples:** `The fresh butchery mark crossed the rib at a consistent angle.` → Butchery mark mới cross rib ở angle đều.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `도축흔`.

## 14. domestication syndrome /dəˌmɛstəˈkeɪʃən ˈsɪndroʊm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a cluster of physical and behavioral changes that often accompanies domestication.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cluster thay đổi physical/behavior thường đi cùng domestication.
**Grammar & collocations:** `domestication syndrome traits` — trait; `debate domestication syndrome` — debate.
**Examples:** `The researchers debated whether the smaller horns reflected domestication syndrome.` → Researchers debate horn nhỏ hơn có phản ánh domestication syndrome.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `가축화 증후군`.

## 15. rachis /ˈreɪkɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the central axis of a grass or cereal head to which spikelets are attached.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** central axis của grass/cereal head, nơi spikelet attach.
**Grammar & collocations:** `brittle rachis` — rachis dễ gãy; `examine the rachis` — examine.
**Examples:** `A brittle rachis made the wild cereal shed its seeds naturally.` → Rachis dễ gãy làm wild cereal shed seed tự nhiên.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `수축`.

## 16. glume /ɡluːm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** one of the bracts that encloses a grass spikelet or grain.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** bract bao quanh spikelet hoặc grain của grass.
**Grammar & collocations:** `wheat glume` — glume wheat; `remove the glume` — remove.
**Examples:** `The wheat glume remained attached after threshing.` → Glume wheat vẫn attach sau threshing.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `영`.

## 17. chaff /tʃæf/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the dry husks and other light plant material separated from grain during threshing or winnowing.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** husk khô và plant material nhẹ tách khỏi grain khi thresh/winnow.
**Grammar & collocations:** `grain chaff` — chaff grain; `separate chaff` — separate.
**Examples:** `Wind carried the chaff away from the heavier grain.` → Wind carry chaff đi khỏi grain nặng hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `겨`.

## 18. carbonized seed /ˈkɑːrbənaɪzd siːd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a seed preserved as carbon after exposure to fire or intense heat.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** seed preserve thành carbon sau fire hoặc intense heat.
**Grammar & collocations:** `carbonized seed assemblage` — assemblage; `recover a carbonized seed` — recover.
**Examples:** `A carbonized seed identified the crop stored in the ancient granary.` → Carbonized seed identify crop được store trong granary cổ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `탄화 종자`.

## 19. paleoethnobotany /ˌpeɪlioʊˌɛθnoʊˈbɑːtəni/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the study of past relationships between people and plants through archaeological evidence.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** study relation quá khứ giữa people và plant bằng archaeological evidence.
**Grammar & collocations:** `regional paleoethnobotany` — paleoethnobotany vùng; `research paleoethnobotany` — research.
**Examples:** `Paleoethnobotany connected the plant remains to cooking and ritual.` → Paleoethnobotany connect plant remains với cooking và ritual.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `고고민족식물학`.

## 20. starch grain analysis /stɑːrtʃ ɡreɪn əˈnæləsɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** examining microscopic starch residues to identify plants or food processing activities.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** examine starch residue microscopic để identify plant hoặc food processing.
**Grammar & collocations:** `microscopic starch grain analysis` — analysis microscopic; `conduct starch grain analysis` — conduct.
**Examples:** `Microscopic starch grain analysis found traces of tubers on the grinding stone.` → Starch grain analysis microscopic find trace tuber trên grinding stone.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `전분 입자 분석`.

## Review in context

A **palynological profile**, **diatom analysis**, **phytolith**, and **charred macroremain** contribute to an **archaeobotanical** interpretation; a **coprolite**, **coastal midden**, and **flotation fraction** add context. Researchers read a **taphonomic signature**, perform **zooarchaeological quantification**, and compare a **faunal assemblage**. **Cut mark archaeology** identifies a **butchery mark**, while **domestication syndrome** is tested against animal change. Plant evidence includes a **rachis**, **glume**, **chaff**, and **carbonized seed**; **paleoethnobotany** and **starch grain analysis** connect remains to human practice.
