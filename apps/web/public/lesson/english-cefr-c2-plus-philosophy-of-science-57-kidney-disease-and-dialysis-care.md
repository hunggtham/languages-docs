# C2+ Vocabulary — Kidney disease and dialysis care

This lesson follows how kidney disease is detected, slowed, replaced, or managed supportively through shared decisions and coordinated care. The network is **chronic kidney disease → filtration estimate → urinary albumin signal → sudden kidney injury → kidney replacement support → hemodialysis access → peritoneal dialysis → dialysis adequacy → fluid restriction → hyperkalemia → uremic symptom → conservative kidney management → kidney failure trajectory → home dialysis → vascular access → fistula maturation → dialysis disequilibrium → transplant referral → nephroprotection → urinary protein loss → renal anemia → mineral bone disorder → shared dialysis decision → modality education → kidney supportive care**.

## 1. chronic kidney disease /ˈkrɑnɪk ˈkɪdni dɪˈziːz/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** long-term abnormal kidney structure or function that can progress, remain stable, or require ongoing monitoring and support.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** chronic kidney disease là condition dài hạn của kidney, cần theo dõi trajectory chứ không chỉ một test.
**Grammar & collocations:** `chronic-kidney-disease pathway` — pathway; `monitor kidney function` — monitor; `disease progression` — progression.
**Examples:** `Chronic kidney disease management combined blood-pressure control, medication review, and regular laboratory monitoring.` → Quản lý nhiều layer.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `만성 신장 질환` — chronic kidney disease.

## 2. filtration estimate /fɪlˈtreɪʃən ˈɛstəmət/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** calculated estimate of how effectively the kidneys filter blood, derived from laboratory and patient information.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** filtration estimate là con số ước tính function lọc, không phải đo trực tiếp từng nephron.
**Grammar & collocations:** `filtration-estimate trend` — trend; `estimate kidney filtration` — estimate; `laboratory value` — value.
**Examples:** `The filtration estimate declined gradually, prompting a conversation about future care options.` → Trend quan trọng hơn một value.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `여과 추정치` — filtration estimate.

## 3. urinary albumin signal /ˈjʊrəˌnɛri ælˈbjuːmən ˈsɪɡnəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** albumin detected in urine as a signal of possible kidney filtration-barrier damage or elevated renal risk.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** urinary albumin signal là dấu hiệu albumin lọt qua filtration barrier.
**Grammar & collocations:** `urinary-albumin-signal measurement` — measurement; `detect albumin in urine` — detect; `renal risk marker` — marker.
**Examples:** `The urinary albumin signal remained elevated even while the filtration estimate was stable.` → Hai signal có thể không đổi cùng lúc.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `요 알부민 신호` — urinary albumin signal.

## 4. sudden kidney injury /ˈsʌdən ˈkɪdni ˈɪndʒəri/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** rapid decline in kidney function caused by illness, reduced blood flow, obstruction, toxins, or another acute stressor.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** sudden kidney injury là drop nhanh của kidney function, khác với chronic decline.
**Grammar & collocations:** `sudden-kidney-injury assessment` — assessment; `identify an acute decline` — identify; `kidney stressor` — stressor.
**Examples:** `The sudden kidney injury followed dehydration and medication exposure during a gastrointestinal illness.` → Acute context làm function drop.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `급성 신장 손상` — sudden kidney injury.

## 5. kidney replacement support /ˈkɪdni rɪˈpleɪsmənt səˈpɔrt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** treatment that performs some functions of failing kidneys through dialysis or transplantation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** kidney replacement support thay một phần function kidney khi kidney không đủ.
**Grammar & collocations:** `kidney-replacement-support decision` — decision; `replace filtration function` — replace; `dialysis option` — option.
**Examples:** `Kidney replacement support was discussed alongside conservative care rather than presented as the only path.` → Có nhiều pathway.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `신장 대체 지원` — kidney replacement support.

## 6. hemodialysis access /ˌhiːmoʊdaɪˈæləsɪs ˈæksɛs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a surgically created or placed route through which blood can safely leave and return during hemodialysis.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** hemodialysis access là “cửa” nối blood với dialysis machine.
**Grammar & collocations:** `hemodialysis-access plan` — plan; `protect vascular access` — protect; `dialysis circuit` — circuit.
**Examples:** `The team protected the hemodialysis access by avoiding unnecessary blood pressure measurements in that arm.` → Access cần được giữ lâu dài.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `혈액투석 혈관통로` — hemodialysis access.

## 7. peritoneal dialysis /ˌpɛrɪtəˈniəl daɪˈæləsɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** dialysis that uses the lining of the abdomen and dialysate to remove waste and excess fluid.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** peritoneal dialysis dùng peritoneum làm membrane lọc.
**Grammar & collocations:** `peritoneal-dialysis training` — training; `exchange dialysate` — exchange; `abdominal catheter` — catheter.
**Examples:** `Peritoneal dialysis allowed treatment at home after the family completed training and infection education.` → Home option cần training.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `복막 투석` — peritoneal dialysis.

## 8. dialysis adequacy /daɪˈæləsɪs ˈædəkwəsi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** assessment of whether dialysis removes enough waste and fluid while meeting clinical, nutritional, and patient-centered goals.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** dialysis adequacy hỏi treatment có đủ hiệu quả và phù hợp không, không chỉ số giờ.
**Grammar & collocations:** `dialysis-adequacy measure` — measure; `assess clearance` — assess; `treatment target` — target.
**Examples:** `Dialysis adequacy was reviewed after fatigue and fluid gain persisted despite regular sessions.` → Symptoms làm cần review.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `투석 적절성` — dialysis adequacy.

## 9. fluid restriction /ˈfluːɪd rɪˈstrɪkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** planned limitation of fluid intake when the body cannot remove fluid adequately or fluid balance creates risk.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** fluid restriction cần được cá nhân hóa theo kidney function, dialysis và context.
**Grammar & collocations:** `fluid-restriction plan` — plan; `limit fluid intake` — limit; `volume overload` — overload.
**Examples:** `The fluid restriction plan included practical advice about ice, soups, and social meals.` → Plan phải đi vào daily life.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `수분 제한` — fluid restriction.

## 10. hyperkalemia /ˌhaɪpərkəˈliːmiə/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an abnormally high level of potassium in the blood that can affect muscles and heart rhythm.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** hyperkalemia là potassium cao, cần đánh giá clinical và laboratory context.
**Grammar & collocations:** `hyperkalemia alert` — alert; `monitor serum potassium` — monitor; `cardiac risk` — risk.
**Examples:** `The hyperkalemia pathway connected laboratory alerts with rapid clinical review and medication assessment.` → Lab signal cần response.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `고칼륨혈증` — hyperkalemia.

## 11. uremic symptom /jʊˈriːmɪk ˈsɪmptəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a symptom associated with accumulation of waste products when kidney function is severely reduced.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** uremic symptom là body phản ứng với waste tích lũy khi kidney clearance kém.
**Grammar & collocations:** `uremic-symptom assessment` — assessment; `assess nausea and confusion` — assess; `waste accumulation` — accumulation.
**Examples:** `The team reviewed appetite loss, nausea, itching, and confusion as possible uremic symptoms.` → Nhiều symptom cần nhìn cùng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `요독 증상` — uremic symptom.

## 12. conservative kidney management /kənˈsɜrvətɪv ˈkɪdni ˈmænɪdʒmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** active treatment of symptoms, complications, function, and quality of life without dialysis or transplant as the primary plan.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** conservative kidney management là care chủ động, không phải “không làm gì”.
**Grammar & collocations:** `conservative-kidney-management plan` — plan; `manage symptoms without dialysis` — manage; `quality-of-life goal` — goal.
**Examples:** `Conservative kidney management focused on comfort, home support, and avoiding burdensome hospital trips.` → Plan có goals rõ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `보존적 신장 관리` — conservative kidney management.

## 13. kidney failure trajectory /ˈkɪdni ˈfeɪljər trəˈdʒɛktəri/

**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** kidney failure trajectory là đường đi theo thời gian, không phải một mốc duy nhất.
**Grammar & collocations:** `kidney-failure-trajectory discussion` — discussion; `map disease progression` — map; `future transition` — transition.
**Examples:** `The kidney failure trajectory discussion helped the family plan transport, work, and future treatment decisions.` → Planning trước giảm crisis.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `신부전 경과` — kidney failure trajectory.

## 14. home dialysis /hoʊm daɪˈæləsɪs/

## 15. vascular access /ˈvæskjələr ˈæksɛs/

## 16. fistula maturation /ˈfɪstʃələ ˌmætʃəˈreɪʃən/

**Core meaning (English):** development of a surgically created arteriovenous fistula until it is strong and usable for hemodialysis.

## 17. dialysis disequilibrium /daɪˈæləsɪs ˌdɪsɪˈkwɪləˌbriəm/

**Core meaning (English):** neurological symptoms caused by rapid shifts in fluid or solute balance during or shortly after dialysis.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** dialysis disequilibrium là body/brain chưa thích ứng với shift quá nhanh.
**Grammar & collocations:** `dialysis-disequilibrium risk` — risk; `adjust dialysis intensity` — adjust; `neurological symptom` — symptom.

## 18. transplant referral /ˈtrænsˌplænt rɪˈfɜrəl/

## 20. urinary protein loss /ˈjʊrəˌnɛri ˈproʊtiːn lɔs/

## Review in context — Part 1

**Chronic kidney disease** is tracked through a **filtration estimate** and a **urinary albumin signal**. A **sudden kidney injury** may change the course and require **kidney replacement support**. **Hemodialysis access**, **peritoneal dialysis**, and **dialysis adequacy** must fit clinical needs and daily life. **Fluid restriction**, **hyperkalemia**, and a **uremic symptom** require coordinated assessment. **Conservative kidney management** can be active and goal-focused when it matches the person's **kidney failure trajectory**.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 1:**

**Chronic kidney disease** được theo dõi bằng **filtration estimate** và **urinary albumin signal**. **Sudden kidney injury** có thể đổi trajectory và cần **kidney replacement support**. **Hemodialysis access**, **peritoneal dialysis** và **dialysis adequacy** phải hợp clinical need và daily life. **Fluid restriction**, **hyperkalemia** và **uremic symptom** cần assessment phối hợp. **Conservative kidney management** có thể chủ động và goal-focused khi phù hợp với **kidney failure trajectory**.

## Review in context — Part 2

**Home dialysis**, **vascular access**, **fistula maturation**, and **dialysis disequilibrium** shape treatment planning. **Transplant referral** may allow early education, while **nephroprotection** aims to preserve function and reduce **urinary protein loss**. **Renal anemia** and **mineral bone disorder** require ongoing review. A **shared dialysis decision** is supported by **modality education**. **Kidney supportive care** can address symptoms and quality of life whether or not dialysis or transplant is chosen.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 2:**

**Home dialysis**, **vascular access**, **fistula maturation** và **dialysis disequilibrium** định hình treatment plan. **Transplant referral** có thể mở education sớm, còn **nephroprotection** nhằm giữ function và giảm **urinary protein loss**. **Renal anemia** và **mineral bone disorder** cần review liên tục. **Shared dialysis decision** được hỗ trợ bằng **modality education**. **Kidney supportive care** xử lý symptom và quality of life dù patient chọn hay không chọn dialysis/transplant.
