# C2+ Vocabulary — Mental-health recovery and addiction care

This lesson follows how mental-health and addiction services assess risk, support autonomy, reduce harm, and sustain recovery. The network is **stepped care → measurement-based care → care pathway → suicide risk formulation → safety planning → means restriction → protective factor → trauma exposure → complex PTSD → psychological first aid → peer support specialist → assertive community treatment → involuntary treatment → capacity assessment → supported decision-making → recovery-oriented care → risk-minimizing care → opioid agonist therapy → overdose prevention site → naloxone distribution → contingency management → dual diagnosis → treatment retention → return-to-use prevention → crisis stabilization**.

## 1. stepped care /stɛpt kɛr/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an approach that begins with the least intensive effective support and increases treatment intensity when need or risk requires it.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** stepped care đi từ support nhẹ lên intensive care theo need, không mặc định ai cũng cần level cao nhất.
**Grammar & collocations:** `stepped-care pathway` — pathway; `step up treatment` — step up; `least intensive support` — support.
**Examples:** `Stepped care offered guided self-help first and specialist therapy when symptoms persisted.` → Treatment tăng theo need.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `단계적 치료` — stepped care.

## 2. measurement-based care /ˈmɛʒərmənt beɪst kɛr/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** routine use of standardized symptom and functioning measures to guide clinical decisions and track response.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** measurement-based care dùng score lặp lại để điều chỉnh treatment, không chỉ dựa vào impression.
**Grammar & collocations:** `measurement-based-care protocol` — protocol; `track symptom change` — track; `standardized scale` — scale.
**Examples:** `Measurement-based care showed that sleep improved while panic symptoms remained unchanged.` → Không chỉ nhìn một outcome.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `측정 기반 진료` — measurement-based care.

## 3. care pathway /kɛr ˈpæθweɪ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an organized sequence of assessment, treatment, referral, and follow-up steps for a patient or condition.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** care pathway vẽ journey từ assessment đến follow-up.
**Grammar & collocations:** `care-pathway map` — map; `follow a pathway` — follow; `referral step` — step.
**Examples:** `The care pathway connected emergency assessment, medication review, therapy, and housing support.` → Pathway nối clinical và social care.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `진료 경로` — care pathway.

## 4. suicide risk formulation /ˈsuːəˌsaɪd rɪsk ˌfɔrmjəˈleɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a structured, individualized explanation of a person's current suicide risk, drivers, protective factors, and changes over time.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** suicide risk formulation không chỉ cho score; nó giải thích risk động và context.
**Grammar & collocations:** `suicide-risk-formulation note` — note; `formulate current risk` — formulate; `dynamic factor` — factor.
**Examples:** `The suicide risk formulation linked recent loss, insomnia, access to medication, and a trusted sibling.` → Formulation nối nhiều factor.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `자살 위험 공식화` — suicide risk formulation.

## 5. safety planning /ˈseɪfti ˈplænɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** collaborative preparation of concrete steps, contacts, coping strategies, and environmental changes to use during a mental-health crisis.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** safety planning tạo plan dùng được trước khi crisis trở nên nguy hiểm.
**Grammar & collocations:** `safety-planning worksheet` — worksheet; `create a crisis plan` — create; `coping step` — step.
**Examples:** `Safety planning listed warning signs, two people to call, and a place to go before acting on suicidal thoughts.` → Plan rất cụ thể.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `안전 계획 수립` — safety planning.

## 6. means restriction /miːnz rɪˈstrɪkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** reduction of a person's immediate access to a method that could cause serious self-harm or suicide.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** means restriction làm phương tiện nguy hiểm khó tiếp cận trong thời điểm risk cao.
**Grammar & collocations:** `means-restriction intervention` — intervention; `restrict access to a method` — restrict; `temporary safeguard` — safeguard.
**Examples:** `Means restriction included secure medication storage and a trusted person holding the key.` → Giảm access vào phương tiện nguy hiểm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `수단 제한` — means restriction.

## 7. protective factor /prəˈtɛktɪv ˈfæktər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a condition, relationship, ability, belief, or resource that lowers risk or supports coping and recovery.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** protective factor là điểm tựa làm risk giảm hoặc recovery mạnh hơn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `protective-factor assessment` — assessment; `strengthen a protective factor` — strengthen; `social connection` — connection.
**Examples:** `A stable relationship became a protective factor during the patient's housing transition.` → Relationship giúp giữ stability.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `보호 요인` — protective factor.

## 8. trauma exposure /ˈtrɔmə ɪkˈspoʊʒər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** contact with an event or environment involving actual or threatened death, serious injury, violence, or violation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** trauma exposure là trải nghiệm/tiếp xúc vượt quá khả năng xử lý bình thường.
**Grammar & collocations:** `trauma-exposure history` — history; `assess trauma exposure` — assess; `threatening event` — event.
**Examples:** `The clinician asked about trauma exposure without forcing the patient to retell every detail.` → Hỏi an toàn, không ép kể lại.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `외상 노출` — trauma exposure.

## 9. complex PTSD /ˈkɑmplɛks ˌpiːtiːɛsˈdiː/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a trauma-related condition involving PTSD symptoms plus persistent problems with emotion regulation, self-concept, and relationships after prolonged or repeated trauma.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** complex PTSD rộng hơn classic PTSD vì ảnh hưởng identity và relationship lâu dài.
**Grammar & collocations:** `complex-PTSD presentation` — presentation; `treat prolonged trauma effects` — treat; `emotion regulation` — regulation.
**Examples:** `Complex PTSD required stabilization and relationship-based care before intensive trauma processing.` → Cần stabilization trước.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `복합 PTSD` — complex PTSD.

## 10. psychological first aid /ˌsaɪkəˈlɑdʒɪkəl fɜrst eɪd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** humane, practical support that promotes safety, calm, connection, and access to services after a distressing event.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** psychological first aid không phải therapy sâu; là support sớm, an toàn và thực tế.
**Grammar & collocations:** `psychological-first-aid training` — training; `provide immediate support` — provide; `connect to services` — connect.
**Examples:** `Psychological first aid gave displaced families information, water, calm contact, and referral options.` → Hỗ trợ nhu cầu trước mắt.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `심리적 응급처치` — psychological first aid.

## 11. peer support specialist /pɪr səˈpɔrt ˈspɛʃəlɪst/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a trained worker who uses lived experience of mental-health or substance-use recovery to support others.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** peer support specialist dùng lived experience đã được train để đồng hành.
**Grammar & collocations:** `peer-support-specialist role` — role; `provide peer support` — provide; `lived experience` — experience.
**Examples:** `The peer support specialist helped the client prepare questions for the psychiatrist.` → Peer giúp patient có voice.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `동료 지원 전문가` — peer support specialist.

## 12. assertive community treatment /əˈsɜrtɪv kəˈmjuːnəti ˈtriːtmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an intensive, team-based community service that brings treatment, support, and crisis response to people with complex mental-health needs.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** assertive community treatment đưa team tới community thay vì đợi patient tự tới clinic.
**Grammar & collocations:** `assertive-community-treatment team` — team; `deliver intensive community care` — deliver; `multidisciplinary outreach` — outreach.
**Examples:** `Assertive community treatment helped the patient maintain housing while reducing repeated hospital admissions.` → Team hỗ trợ housing và care.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `적극적 지역사회 치료` — assertive community treatment.

## 13. involuntary treatment /ɪnˈvɑləˌtɛri ˈtriːtmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** treatment provided without a person's current consent under specific legal and clinical conditions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** involuntary treatment là ngoại lệ nghiêm ngặt, cần legal safeguard và review.
**Grammar & collocations:** `involuntary-treatment order` — order; `authorize treatment without consent` — authorize; `legal safeguard` — safeguard.
**Examples:** `Involuntary treatment was considered only after immediate danger and lack of decision capacity were documented.` → Cần evidence cụ thể.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `비자발적 치료` — involuntary treatment.

## 14. capacity assessment /kəˈpæsəti əˈsɛsmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** evaluation of whether a person can understand, use, weigh, and communicate a decision about a specific matter at a specific time.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** capacity assessment hỏi decision cụ thể, không dán nhãn toàn bộ person.
**Grammar & collocations:** `capacity-assessment tool` — tool; `assess decision capacity` — assess; `specific decision` — decision.
**Examples:** `The capacity assessment found that the patient could decide about medication but not manage complex finances.` → Capacity phụ thuộc decision.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `의사결정 능력 평가` — capacity assessment.

## 15. supported decision-making /səˈpɔrtɪd dɪˈsɪʒən ˌmeɪkɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** assistance that helps a person understand options and communicate a choice while preserving the person's own authority to decide.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** supported decision-making thêm support, không lấy quyền decision của patient.
**Grammar & collocations:** `supported-decision-making plan` — plan; `support an informed choice` — support; `decision aid` — aid.
**Examples:** `Supported decision-making used pictures, extra time, and a trusted interpreter rather than substitute consent.` → Support thay vì quyết định hộ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `지원 의사결정` — supported decision-making.

## 16. recovery-oriented care /rɪˈkʌvəri ˈɔriˌɛntɪd kɛr/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** care that centers a person's goals, strengths, identity, hope, and participation in building a meaningful life.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** recovery-oriented care không chỉ giảm symptom mà giúp person sống theo goal của mình.
**Grammar & collocations:** `recovery-oriented-care plan` — plan; `center patient goals` — center; `meaningful life` — life.
**Examples:** `Recovery-oriented care treated employment and friendship as outcomes alongside symptom reduction.` → Life goal cũng là outcome.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `회복 지향적 진료` — recovery-oriented care.

## 17. risk-minimizing care /rɪsk ˈmɪnəˌmaɪzɪŋ kɛr/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** care that reduces immediate danger and improves health without requiring a person to achieve complete abstinence or ideal behavior first.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** risk-minimizing care giảm harm thực tế ngay cả khi behavior chưa hoàn hảo.
**Grammar & collocations:** `risk-minimizing-care service` — service; `reduce immediate harm` — reduce; `safer-use advice` — advice.
**Examples:** `Risk-minimizing care offered sterile supplies and medical advice before the patient was ready to stop using drugs.` → Giảm harm trước, không đợi hoàn hảo.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `위험 최소화 진료` — risk-minimizing care.

## 18. opioid agonist therapy /ˈoʊpiˌɔɪd ˈæɡənɪst ˈθɛrəpi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** treatment using a prescribed opioid medicine to reduce withdrawal, craving, illicit use, and overdose risk.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** opioid agonist therapy ổn định cơ thể và giảm risk bằng medicine có kiểm soát.
**Grammar & collocations:** `opioid-agonist-therapy clinic` — clinic; `treat opioid dependence` — treat; `reduce craving` — reduce.
**Examples:** `Opioid agonist therapy helped the patient remain in treatment while rebuilding stable housing.` → Treatment hỗ trợ recovery rộng hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `오피오이드 작용제 치료` — opioid agonist therapy.

## 19. overdose prevention site /ˈoʊvərˌdoʊs prɪˈvɛnʃən saɪt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a supervised or supported place where people can use drugs more safely and receive rapid response, supplies, and referral.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** overdose prevention site đưa supervision và cứu nguy tới nơi risk xảy ra.
**Grammar & collocations:** `overdose-prevention-site service` — service; `respond to an overdose` — respond; `supervised consumption` — consumption.
**Examples:** `The overdose prevention site reversed overdoses and connected visitors to primary care.` → Vừa cứu nguy vừa nối care.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `과다복용 예방 시설` — overdose prevention site.

## 20. naloxone distribution /nəˈlɑksˌoʊn ˌdɪstrəˈbjuːʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** organized provision of naloxone to people who use opioids, their families, workers, or community responders.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** naloxone distribution đưa thuốc reversal tới người có thể cần trước emergency.
**Grammar & collocations:** `naloxone-distribution program` — program; `distribute overdose-reversal medicine` — distribute; `community responder` — responder.
**Examples:** `Naloxone distribution reached shelters, pharmacies, and family caregivers rather than only clinics.` → Distribution qua nhiều kênh.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `날록손 배포` — naloxone distribution.

## 21. contingency management /kənˈtɪndʒənsi ˈmænɪdʒmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a treatment approach that provides tangible rewards when a person meets clearly defined health or recovery goals.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** contingency management gắn reward cụ thể với behavior/recovery target.
**Grammar & collocations:** `contingency-management program` — program; `reward a recovery behavior` — reward; `defined target` — target.
**Examples:** `Contingency management rewarded attendance and verified treatment goals without shaming missed visits.` → Reward behavior, không shame.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `조건부 관리` — contingency management.

## 22. dual diagnosis /ˈduəl ˌdaɪəɡˈnoʊsɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** co-occurring mental-health and substance-use conditions requiring coordinated assessment and treatment.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** dual diagnosis là hai nhóm condition cùng tồn tại, không nên treat tách rời.
**Grammar & collocations:** `dual-diagnosis service` — service; `treat co-occurring conditions` — treat; `integrated assessment` — assessment.
**Examples:** `The dual diagnosis clinic coordinated treatment for psychosis and stimulant use instead of sending the patient between services.` → Một clinic xử lý cả hai.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `이중 진단` — dual diagnosis.

## 23. treatment retention /ˈtriːtmənt rɪˈtɛnʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** continued participation in treatment long enough to receive a meaningful opportunity for benefit.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** treatment retention đo patient có ở lại treatment đủ lâu không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `treatment-retention rate` — rate; `retain a patient in care` — retain; `continuity barrier` — barrier.
**Examples:** `Treatment retention improved when appointments were flexible and transportation was reimbursed.` → Flexibility và transport giữ patient.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `치료 유지` — treatment retention.

## 24. return-to-use prevention /rɪˈtɜrn tə juːz prɪˈvɛnʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** planning and support intended to reduce the chance, severity, or danger of returning to substance use after a period of change.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** return-to-use prevention chuẩn bị cho risk quay lại use mà không coi đó là moral failure.
**Grammar & collocations:** `return-to-use-prevention plan` — plan; `prepare for recurrence risk` — prepare; `early warning sign` — sign.
**Examples:** `Return-to-use prevention included overdose education because tolerance could fall during abstinence.` → Tolerance giảm làm overdose risk tăng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `재사용 예방` — return-to-use prevention.

## 25. crisis stabilization /ˈkraɪsɪs ˌsteɪbəlɪˈzeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** short-term intervention that reduces immediate danger and restores enough safety and functioning for a next-step plan.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** crisis stabilization đưa person từ danger cao về mức có thể plan tiếp.
**Grammar & collocations:** `crisis-stabilization unit` — unit; `stabilize an acute crisis` — stabilize; `next-step plan` — plan.
**Examples:** `Crisis stabilization provided sleep, medication review, and a safe handoff to community care.` → Stabilize trước rồi handoff.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `위기 안정화` — crisis stabilization.

## Review in context — Part 1

**Stepped care** can begin with **measurement-based care** inside a coordinated **care pathway**. A **suicide risk formulation**, **safety planning**, and **means restriction** identify immediate danger, while a **protective factor** can support hope. Clinicians should ask about **trauma exposure** and recognize **complex PTSD**; **psychological first aid** offers early support. A **peer support specialist** and **assertive community treatment** can extend care into the community. **Involuntary treatment** requires strict safeguards, while **capacity assessment** and **supported decision-making** protect autonomy.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 1:**

**Stepped care** có thể bắt đầu bằng **measurement-based care** trong **care pathway** phối hợp. **Suicide risk formulation**, **safety planning** và **means restriction** xác định danger trước mắt, còn **protective factor** hỗ trợ hope. Clinician nên hỏi về **trauma exposure** và nhận biết **complex PTSD**; **psychological first aid** cung cấp support sớm. **Peer support specialist** và **assertive community treatment** mở rộng care ra community. **Involuntary treatment** cần safeguard nghiêm ngặt, còn **capacity assessment** và **supported decision-making** bảo vệ autonomy.

## Review in context — Part 2

**Recovery-oriented care** centers a meaningful life, and **risk-minimizing care** reduces immediate harm without demanding perfection. **Opioid agonist therapy**, an **overdose prevention site**, and **naloxone distribution** can prevent death. **Contingency management** supports defined goals, while a **dual diagnosis** service coordinates mental-health and substance-use treatment. **Treatment retention** improves when barriers are reduced; **return-to-use prevention** prepares for recurrence risk. **Crisis stabilization** restores safety before the next care step.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 2:**

**Recovery-oriented care** đặt meaningful life ở trung tâm, còn **risk-minimizing care** giảm harm trước mắt mà không đòi perfection. **Opioid agonist therapy**, **overdose prevention site** và **naloxone distribution** có thể ngăn tử vong. **Contingency management** hỗ trợ target rõ, còn service **dual diagnosis** phối hợp mental-health và substance-use care. **Treatment retention** tốt hơn khi barrier giảm; **return-to-use prevention** chuẩn bị cho risk quay lại. **Crisis stabilization** đưa person về safety trước bước care tiếp theo.
