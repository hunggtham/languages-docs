# C2+ Vocabulary — Patient safety and reliability science

This lesson follows how health organizations understand harm, learn from failure, and design safer systems rather than relying on individual vigilance alone. The network is **precursor event → never event → root cause analysis → systems engineering → human factors → latent condition → Swiss cheese model → just culture → safety culture → second victim → failure mode effects analysis → high reliability organization → safety-II → resilient performance → clinical microsystem → morbidity and mortality conference → harm surveillance → incident learning → open disclosure → duty of candor → patient safety indicator → care bundle → reliability science → standard work → safety net**.

## 1. precursor event /prɪˈkɜrsər ɪˈvɛnt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an occurrence or condition that signals a possible pathway to harm, whether or not harm has happened yet.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** precursor event là dấu hiệu đi trước harm, cho phép can thiệp sớm.
**Grammar & collocations:** `precursor-event log` — log; `identify a precursor event` — identify; `harm pathway` — pathway.
**Examples:** `The missing allergy alert was treated as a precursor event before any patient received the drug.` → Lỗi alert được xử lý trước khi harm xảy ra.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `전조 사건` — precursor event.

## 2. never event /ˈnɛvər ɪˈvɛnt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a serious, preventable incident that should not occur in a health system with appropriate safeguards.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** never event là harm nghiêm trọng lẽ ra phải ngăn được.
**Grammar & collocations:** `never-event policy` — policy; `prevent a never event` — prevent; `serious preventable harm` — harm.
**Examples:** `The wrong-site surgery was classified as a never event and triggered an external review.` → Sai vị trí phẫu thuật là event không thể chấp nhận.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `절대 발생해서는 안 되는 사건` — never event.

## 3. root cause analysis /ruːt kɔz əˈnæləsɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** structured investigation of the underlying conditions and system relationships that allowed an incident to occur.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** root cause analysis đi sâu vào system, không dừng ở người mắc lỗi cuối cùng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `root-cause-analysis report` — report; `analyze an incident` — analyze; `underlying condition` — condition.
**Examples:** `Root cause analysis found that the labeling error arose from similar packaging and a rushed handoff.` → Lỗi do packaging và handoff, không chỉ do cá nhân.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `근본 원인 분석` — root cause analysis.

## 4. systems engineering /ˈsɪstəmz ˌɛndʒəˈnɪrɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** disciplined design and analysis of interacting people, technologies, processes, and environments to achieve safe system performance.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** systems engineering thiết kế cả network người–tool–process, không chỉ một component.
**Grammar & collocations:** `systems-engineering approach` — approach; `design a safe system` — design; `interacting component` — component.
**Examples:** `Systems engineering redesigned the medication cabinet, software, and workflow together.` → Không sửa một phần riêng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `시스템 공학` — systems engineering.

## 5. human factors /ˈhjuːmən ˈfæktərz/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** study of how human abilities, limitations, cognition, behavior, and social interaction affect system performance and safety.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** human factors thiết kế theo cách người thật chú ý, nhớ, mệt và phối hợp.
**Grammar & collocations:** `human-factors review` — review; `account for human limitations` — account; `workload design` — design.
**Examples:** `Human factors review showed that the alarm was easy to miss during a noisy night shift.` → Alarm không hợp attention thực tế.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `인간공학적 요인` — human factors.

## 6. latent condition /ˈleɪtənt kənˈdɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a hidden design, policy, resource, or organizational condition that increases the chance of error or harm.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** latent condition nằm im trong system nhưng tạo điều kiện cho lỗi.
**Grammar & collocations:** `latent-condition analysis` — analysis; `create a latent risk` — create; `organizational weakness` — weakness.
**Examples:** `A latent condition was the staffing model that left one nurse covering two distant units.` → Staffing model tạo risk ẩn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `잠재적 조건` — latent condition.

## 7. Swiss cheese model /swɪs tʃiːz ˈmɑdəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a model in which multiple defensive layers contain weaknesses that can align and allow an incident to pass through.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** Swiss cheese model hình dung mỗi lớp bảo vệ có lỗ; khi lỗ thẳng hàng, harm lọt qua.
**Grammar & collocations:** `Swiss-cheese-model diagram` — diagram; `align defensive gaps` — align; `safety layer` — layer.
**Examples:** `The Swiss cheese model showed how a bad label, missing check, and rushed administration aligned.` → Nhiều lỗ cùng thẳng hàng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `스위스 치즈 모델` — Swiss cheese model.

## 8. just culture /dʒʌst ˈkʌltʃər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an organizational approach that distinguishes human error, risky behavior, and reckless conduct while promoting learning and fair accountability.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** just culture không đổ lỗi mù quáng nhưng cũng không bỏ qua reckless behavior.
**Grammar & collocations:** `just-culture policy` — policy; `distinguish error from recklessness` — distinguish; `fair accountability` — accountability.
**Examples:** `A just culture treated the honest slip as a learning opportunity but addressed deliberate concealment separately.` → Phân biệt slip và concealment.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `공정 문화` — just culture.

## 9. safety culture /ˈseɪfti ˈkʌltʃər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** shared values, expectations, behaviors, and practices that make safety a routine organizational priority.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** safety culture là cách organization thật sự đặt safety vào daily decision.
**Grammar & collocations:** `safety-culture survey` — survey; `strengthen safety culture` — strengthen; `shared expectation` — expectation.
**Examples:** `Safety culture improved when managers acted on staff concerns instead of merely praising reporting.` → Manager phải act chứ không chỉ praise.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `안전 문화` — safety culture.

## 10. second victim /ˈsɛkənd ˈvɪktəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a health worker who is emotionally or professionally harmed after being involved in an adverse patient event.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** second victim là staff cũng chịu trauma sau patient harm.
**Grammar & collocations:** `second-victim support` — support; `support an involved clinician` — support; `post-incident distress` — distress.
**Examples:** `The second victim received confidential peer support after the medication error.` → Staff được support sau error.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `제2의 피해자` — second victim.

## 11. failure mode effects analysis /ˈfeɪljər moʊd ɪˈfɛkts əˈnæləsɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** prospective examination of how a process might fail, what effects each failure could have, and which failures deserve priority prevention.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** failure mode effects analysis tìm failure trước khi nó xảy ra.
**Grammar & collocations:** `failure-mode-effects-analysis worksheet` — worksheet; `rank failure modes` — rank; `preventive control` — control.
**Examples:** `Failure mode effects analysis ranked a missing backup power test as the most urgent weakness.` → Phân tích xếp priority cho backup power.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `고장 형태 영향 분석` — failure mode effects analysis.

## 12. high reliability organization /haɪ rɪˌlaɪəˈbɪləti ˌɔrɡənəˈzeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an organization that performs complex, hazardous work with very few serious failures through constant attention to risk and learning.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** high reliability organization không nghĩ “đã an toàn rồi”, mà luôn tìm signal nhỏ.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high-reliability-organization principle` — principle; `preoccupy a team with failure` — preoccupy; `sensitive operation` — operation.
**Examples:** `A high reliability organization investigated small anomalies before they became service interruptions.` → Anomaly nhỏ cũng được xem.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `고신뢰 조직` — high reliability organization.

## 13. safety-II /ˈseɪfti tuː/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an approach that studies how everyday work usually succeeds and how normal variability can be used to improve resilience.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** safety-II học từ việc “đúng thường ngày”, không chỉ phân tích failure.
**Grammar & collocations:** `Safety-II perspective` — perspective; `study everyday success` — study; `normal variability` — variability.
**Examples:** `A Safety-II review examined how nurses adapted safely when the official workflow was impossible.` → Học từ adaptation thành công.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `Safety-II` — Safety-II.

## 14. resilient performance /rɪˈzɪliənt pərˈfɔrməns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the ability of a system or team to adjust to changing conditions while maintaining safe and effective functioning.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** resilient performance là adapt mà vẫn giữ function safe.
**Grammar & collocations:** `resilient-performance assessment` — assessment; `adapt under pressure` — adapt; `maintain safe function` — maintain.
**Examples:** `Resilient performance allowed the ward to preserve critical care during a sudden oxygen shortage.` → Ward adapt mà vẫn giữ care.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `회복탄력적 성과` — resilient performance.

## 15. clinical microsystem /ˈklɪnɪkəl ˈmaɪkroʊˌsɪstəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a small, interdependent unit of patients, professionals, processes, and information that produces care together.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** clinical microsystem là “tế bào” nhỏ nơi care thật sự diễn ra.
**Grammar & collocations:** `clinical-microsystem assessment` — assessment; `improve a care unit` — improve; `interdependent team` — team.
**Examples:** `The clinical microsystem included the dialysis patients, nurses, technicians, schedules, and supply process.` → Không chỉ tính staff, mà cả system quanh care.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `임상 미시체계` — clinical microsystem.

## 16. morbidity and mortality conference /mɔrˈbɪdəti ænd mɔrˈtæləti ˈkɑnfərəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a structured clinical meeting that reviews serious complications and deaths to learn and improve care.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** morbidity and mortality conference biến case nặng thành learning chung.
**Grammar & collocations:** `morbidity-and-mortality-conference review` — review; `discuss a complication` — discuss; `case learning` — learning.
**Examples:** `The morbidity and mortality conference focused on system contributors rather than humiliating the resident.` → Conference hướng vào system.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `사망 및 이환 회의` — morbidity and mortality conference.

## 17. harm surveillance /hɑrm sərˈveɪləns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** systematic monitoring for patient injury, unsafe conditions, or patterns of preventable harm.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** harm surveillance theo dõi harm như một signal liên tục.
**Grammar & collocations:** `harm-surveillance dashboard` — dashboard; `monitor patient harm` — monitor; `preventable injury` — injury.
**Examples:** `Harm surveillance detected a rise in pressure injuries after staffing patterns changed.` → Staffing đổi kéo pressure injury tăng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `위해 감시` — harm surveillance.

## 18. incident learning /ˈɪnsədənt ˈlɜrnɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** organized use of incident information to identify system improvements and prevent recurrence.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** incident learning biến incident thành change, không chỉ lưu report.
**Grammar & collocations:** `incident-learning system` — system; `learn from an incident` — learn; `corrective action` — action.
**Examples:** `Incident learning led to a quieter medication-storage area and a redesigned picking sequence.` → Report dẫn tới redesign cụ thể.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `사건 학습` — incident learning.

## 19. open disclosure /ˈoʊpən dɪˈskloʊʒər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** honest, timely communication with patients and families when care has caused or may have caused harm.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** open disclosure nói thật với patient/family về harm và next step.
**Grammar & collocations:** `open-disclosure policy` — policy; `disclose an adverse event` — disclose; `apology and explanation` — explanation.
**Examples:** `Open disclosure included an apology, the known facts, and a promise to investigate further.` → Nói thật và nói next step.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `공개 설명` — open disclosure.

## 20. duty of candor /ˈduːti əv ˈkændər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a professional or legal obligation to be truthful and transparent with patients about significant harm or risk.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** duty of candor là duty nói thật, không che giấu harm quan trọng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `duty-of-candor requirement` — requirement; `speak candidly to a patient` — speak; `material harm` — harm.
**Examples:** `The duty of candor required the surgeon to explain the retained instrument immediately.` → Surgeon phải explain ngay.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `진실 고지 의무` — duty of candor.

## 21. patient safety indicator /ˈpeɪʃənt ˈseɪfti ˈɪndəˌkeɪtər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a measure used to identify possible complications, adverse events, or unsafe patterns in health care.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** patient safety indicator biến safety concern thành metric theo dõi.
**Grammar & collocations:** `patient-safety-indicator set` — set; `track a complication rate` — track; `safety metric` — metric.
**Examples:** `The patient safety indicator flagged an unusual rise in postoperative infections.` → Metric làm infection rise nổi lên.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `환자 안전 지표` — patient safety indicator.

## 22. care bundle /kɛr ˈbʌndəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a small set of evidence-based practices that should be performed together to reduce a defined clinical risk.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** care bundle là package vài action phải làm cùng để giảm risk.
**Grammar & collocations:** `care-bundle checklist` — checklist; `complete a bundle` — complete; `evidence-based practice` — practice.
**Examples:** `The care bundle combined hand hygiene, line review, and daily removal checks.` → Nhiều action cùng giảm infection.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `진료 번들` — care bundle.

## 23. reliability science /rɪˌlaɪəˈbɪləti saɪəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** study and application of methods for making processes perform consistently under expected and changing conditions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** reliability science hỏi làm sao process chạy đúng lặp đi lặp lại.
**Grammar & collocations:** `reliability-science method` — method; `improve process reliability` — improve; `consistent performance` — performance.
**Examples:** `Reliability science helped the unit reduce missed doses by redesigning the refill process.` → Sửa process để dose không miss.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `신뢰성 과학` — reliability science.

## 24. standard work /ˈstændərd wɜrk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an agreed best-known sequence for performing a task safely and consistently, while allowing justified adaptation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** standard work là cách làm chuẩn hiện tại, không phải rule mù quáng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `standard-work instruction` — instruction; `follow standard work` — follow; `best-known process` — process.
**Examples:** `Standard work clarified who checked the specimen label and when the result was escalated.` → Role và timing rõ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `표준 작업` — standard work.

## 25. safety net /ˈseɪfti nɛt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a backup safeguard or support that catches failures, uncertainty, or deterioration before serious harm occurs.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** safety net là lớp backup khi first-line defense không đủ.
**Grammar & collocations:** `safety-net instruction` — instruction; `provide a safety net` — provide; `backup safeguard` — safeguard.
**Examples:** `The discharge call served as a safety net for patients whose symptoms might worsen at home.` → Call follow-up bắt deterioration.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `안전망` — safety net.

## Review in context — Part 1

A **precursor event** may reveal a pathway to a **never event**, so **root cause analysis**, **systems engineering**, and **human factors** examine more than the final error. A **latent condition** can align with other weaknesses in the **Swiss cheese model**. **Just culture** distinguishes error from recklessness, while **safety culture** makes reporting normal. Support for a **second victim** matters after harm, and **failure mode effects analysis** looks ahead. A **high reliability organization** studies small anomalies; **Safety-II** and **resilient performance** study how ordinary work succeeds.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 1:**

**Precursor event** có thể lộ đường dẫn tới **never event**, nên **root cause analysis**, **systems engineering** và **human factors** xem nhiều hơn lỗi cuối cùng. **Latent condition** có thể thẳng hàng với các điểm yếu khác trong **Swiss cheese model**. **Just culture** phân biệt error với recklessness, còn **safety culture** làm reporting trở thành bình thường. Support cho **second victim** quan trọng sau harm, và **failure mode effects analysis** nhìn trước failure. **High reliability organization** nghiên cứu anomaly nhỏ; **Safety-II** và **resilient performance** nghiên cứu cách work thường ngày thành công.

## Review in context — Part 2

A **clinical microsystem** can learn through a **morbidity and mortality conference**, **harm surveillance**, and **incident learning**. **Open disclosure** and a **duty of candor** protect honest communication with patients. A **patient safety indicator** makes patterns visible, while a **care bundle** turns evidence into a repeatable set of actions. **Reliability science** improves consistency through **standard work**, and a **safety net** catches deterioration when the first layer fails.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 2:**

**Clinical microsystem** có thể học qua **morbidity and mortality conference**, **harm surveillance** và **incident learning**. **Open disclosure** và **duty of candor** bảo vệ communication trung thực với patient. **Patient safety indicator** làm pattern hiện rõ, còn **care bundle** biến evidence thành nhóm action lặp lại được. **Reliability science** tăng consistency qua **standard work**, và **safety net** bắt deterioration khi lớp đầu tiên fail.
