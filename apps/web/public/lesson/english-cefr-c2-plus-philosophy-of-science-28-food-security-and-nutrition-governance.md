# C2+ Vocabulary — Food security and nutrition governance

This lesson follows how societies secure food, protect nutrition, and absorb shocks across supply chains and households. The network is **community food sovereignty → food availability → food access → food utilization → food stability → nutrition security → dietary diversity → hidden hunger → micronutrient deficiency → stunting → wasting → food price volatility → buffer stock → strategic grain reserve → import dependency → supply shock → harvest failure → postharvest infrastructure → cold-chain capacity → social protection floor → school feeding → cash transfer → nutrition-sensitive intervention → crop nutrient enhancement → fortification standard**.

## 1. community food sovereignty /kəˈmjuːnəti fuːd ˈsɑvrənti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a community's capacity and right to shape how food is produced, distributed, valued, and accessed.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** community food sovereignty nhấn quyền community định hình food system của mình.
**Grammar & collocations:** `community-food-sovereignty framework` — framework; `shape a food system` — shape; `local control` — control.
**Examples:** `Community food sovereignty supported local seed saving and shorter food chains.` → Community giữ seed và chain ngắn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `공동체 식량 주권` — community food sovereignty.

## 2. food availability /fuːd əˌveɪləˈbɪləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the presence of sufficient food in a region through production, storage, trade, or transfers.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** food availability hỏi food có tồn tại đủ trong region không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `food-availability estimate` — estimate; `ensure food availability` — ensure; `regional supply` — supply.
**Examples:** `Food availability fell after the port closure interrupted grain deliveries.` → Port đóng làm availability giảm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `식량 가용성` — food availability.

## 3. food access /fuːd ˈæksɛs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the ability of people to obtain adequate food through income, markets, social support, transport, or other means.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** food access không chỉ food có; household phải afford/reach/obtain được.
**Grammar & collocations:** `food-access barrier` — barrier; `improve access` — improve; `afford nutritious food` — afford.
**Examples:** `Food access weakened when fuel prices made the weekly market unreachable.` → Fuel đắt làm market khó reach.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `식량 접근성` — food access.

## 4. food utilization /fuːd ˌjuːtələˈzeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the extent to which people can safely consume food and convert it into nutrition, given health, sanitation, knowledge, and care.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** food utilization hỏi ăn food có convert thành nutrition không, phụ thuộc health/sanitation/care.
**Grammar & collocations:** `food-utilization indicator` — indicator; `utilize nutrients` — utilize; `safe preparation` — preparation.
**Examples:** `Unsafe water reduced food utilization even when households had enough calories.` → Nước bẩn làm nutrition giảm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `식량 이용` — food utilization.

## 5. food stability /fuːd stəˈbɪləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** continuity of food availability, access, and utilization over time despite seasonal, economic, climatic, or political shocks.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** food stability là food security giữ được qua season/shock, không chỉ một thời điểm.
**Grammar & collocations:** `food-stability assessment` — assessment; `maintain stable access` — maintain; `seasonal shock` — shock.
**Examples:** `Food stability improved when the cooperative stored grain for the lean season.` → Cooperative store grain cho lean season.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `식량 안정성` — food stability.

## 6. nutrition security /nuˈtrɪʃən səˈkjʊrəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** sustained access to adequate, safe, diverse, and culturally appropriate nutrition needed for health and development.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** nutrition security rộng hơn calories: đủ, safe, diverse và phù hợp health/culture.
**Grammar & collocations:** `nutrition-security strategy` — strategy; `secure adequate nutrition` — secure; `health-supporting diet` — diet.
**Examples:** `Nutrition security required affordable protein and clean water, not just more grain.` → Cần protein/water sạch, không chỉ grain.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `영양 안보` — nutrition security.

## 7. dietary diversity /ˈdaɪəˌtɛri daɪˈvɜrsəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** variety across food groups in a diet, often used as an indicator of nutritional adequacy.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** dietary diversity đo variety của food group, không chỉ tổng calorie.
**Grammar & collocations:** `dietary-diversity score` — score; `increase food-group variety` — increase; `diverse diet` — diet.
**Examples:** `The program improved dietary diversity by adding legumes, eggs, and leafy vegetables.` → Program thêm nhiều food group.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `식이 다양성` — dietary diversity.

## 8. hidden hunger /ˈhɪdən ˈhʌŋɡər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** micronutrient deficiency that may occur even when people consume enough calories or appear adequately fed.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** hidden hunger là thiếu micronutrient dù calories đủ và không nhìn thấy ngay.
**Grammar & collocations:** `hidden-hunger prevalence` — prevalence; `address micronutrient gaps` — address; `calorie-sufficient diet` — diet.
**Examples:** `Hidden hunger persisted because the staple diet supplied calories but little iron or vitamin A.` → Staple đủ calorie nhưng thiếu iron/vitamin A.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `숨은 기아` — hidden hunger.

## 9. micronutrient deficiency /ˌmaɪkroʊˈnuːtriənt dɪˈfɪʃənsi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** inadequate intake, absorption, or availability of vitamins and minerals required for health.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** micronutrient deficiency là thiếu vitamin/mineral vì intake/absorption/availability.
**Grammar & collocations:** `micronutrient-deficiency screening` — screening; `correct a deficiency` — correct; `vitamin and mineral intake` — intake.
**Examples:** `Micronutrient deficiency was common among children whose diets relied on one staple crop.` → Trẻ ăn một crop staple dễ deficiency.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `미량영양소 결핍` — micronutrient deficiency.

## 10. stunting /ˈstʌntɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** impaired linear growth in children associated with chronic undernutrition, illness, or adverse early-life conditions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** stunting phản ánh thiếu nutrition/chronic illness kéo dài trong early life.
**Grammar & collocations:** `childhood-stunting rate` — rate; `prevent stunting` — prevent; `linear growth` — growth.
**Examples:** `The first thousand days were targeted because nutrition interventions can reduce stunting risk.` → Intervention sớm giảm stunting risk.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `발육 부진` — stunting.

## 11. wasting /ˈweɪstɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** low weight for height in children, often reflecting acute undernutrition or recent illness.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** wasting phản ánh acute undernutrition/illness gần đây, khác chronic stunting.
**Grammar & collocations:** `childhood-wasting treatment` — treatment; `screen for wasting` — screen; `acute undernutrition` — undernutrition.
**Examples:** `The clinic expanded screening for wasting after the flood disrupted food access.` → Flood làm access giảm, clinic screen wasting.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `소모증` — wasting.

## 12. food price volatility /fuːd praɪs ˌvɑləˈtɪləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** rapid or unpredictable changes in food prices that disrupt household planning, access, and producer decisions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** food price volatility làm price nhảy khó predict, household khó plan.
**Grammar & collocations:** `food-price-volatility index` — index; `stabilize prices` — stabilize; `price shock` — shock.
**Examples:** `Food price volatility pushed low-income families toward cheaper, less diverse diets.` → Price volatility làm diet kém diverse.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `식품 가격 변동성` — food price volatility.

## 13. buffer stock /ˈbʌfər stɑk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** stored commodity held to reduce shortages or price spikes when supply is disrupted.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** buffer stock là kho dự trữ để absorb supply shock và price spike.
**Grammar & collocations:** `buffer-stock policy` — policy; `release stock` — release; `stabilize a market` — stabilize.
**Examples:** `The agency released buffer stock when imports were delayed by a storm.` → Agency release stock khi import delay.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `완충 재고` — buffer stock.

## 14. strategic grain reserve /strəˈtiːdʒɪk ɡreɪn rɪˈzɜrv/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** publicly managed grain storage held for severe shortages, emergencies, or supply disruptions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** strategic grain reserve là grain store của public cho emergency/shortage.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strategic-grain-reserve release` — release; `maintain a reserve` — maintain; `emergency grain` — grain.
**Examples:** `The strategic grain reserve covered school meals during the harvest failure.` → Reserve cover school meal.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `전략 곡물 비축` — strategic grain reserve.

## 15. import dependency /ˈɪmpɔrt dɪˈpɛndənsi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** reliance on imported food, inputs, or energy such that external disruption can threaten domestic supply or prices.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** import dependency làm food system vulnerable với border/trade/foreign supply shock.
**Grammar & collocations:** `import-dependency ratio` — ratio; `reduce import dependence` — reduce; `external supply` — supply.
**Examples:** `Import dependency made the island vulnerable when shipping insurance became unavailable.` → Island vulnerable khi shipping insurance mất.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `수입 의존성` — import dependency.

## 16. supply shock /səˈplaɪ ʃɑk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** sudden disruption or change in the amount, cost, or reliability of goods available for a market or population.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** supply shock làm quantity/cost/reliability đổi nhanh.
**Grammar & collocations:** `supply-shock response` — response; `absorb a shock` — absorb; `supply disruption` — disruption.
**Examples:** `The fertilizer supply shock raised production costs before the next planting season.` → Fertilizer shock làm cost tăng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `공급 충격` — supply shock.

## 17. harvest failure /ˈhɑrvəst ˈfeɪljər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a crop harvest substantially below expected production because of weather, pests, disease, conflict, or management failure.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** harvest failure là crop yield fail vì weather/pest/disease/conflict/management.
**Grammar & collocations:** `harvest-failure estimate` — estimate; `suffer crop failure` — suffer; `expected yield` — yield.
**Examples:** `Harvest failure forced the cooperative to draw down its grain reserve.` → Failure buộc draw reserve.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `수확 실패` — harvest failure.

## 18. postharvest infrastructure /ˌpoʊstˈhɑrvəst ˈɪnfrəˌstrʌktʃər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** facilities, equipment, roads, storage, processing, and systems that preserve food after harvest.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** postharvest infrastructure giữ food sau harvest qua road/storage/processing.
**Grammar & collocations:** `postharvest-infrastructure investment` — investment; `preserve a harvest` — preserve; `storage facility` — facility.
**Examples:** `Postharvest infrastructure reduced losses by connecting small farms to dry storage.` → Infrastructure giảm loss.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `수확 후 인프라` — postharvest infrastructure.

## 19. cold-chain capacity /koʊld tʃeɪn kəˈpæsəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the ability of refrigerated storage and transport systems to keep temperature-sensitive food safe and usable.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cold-chain capacity giữ temperature từ farm tới market.
**Grammar & collocations:** `cold-chain-capacity gap` — gap; `maintain refrigeration` — maintain; `temperature-sensitive food` — food.
**Examples:** `Cold-chain capacity determined whether vaccines and fresh produce reached remote clinics.` → Cold chain quyết định vaccine/produce tới nơi.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `콜드체인 역량` — cold-chain capacity.

## 20. social protection floor /ˈsoʊʃəl prəˈtɛkʃən flɔr/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a minimum level of income, services, or support that protects people from severe deprivation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** social protection floor là mức minimum support để tránh deprivation nghiêm trọng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `social-protection-floor policy` — policy; `guarantee a minimum` — guarantee; `income support` — support.
**Examples:** `The social protection floor kept households from selling productive assets during the food crisis.` → Floor giúp household không bán asset.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `사회 보호 최저선` — social protection floor.

## 21. school feeding /skuːl ˈfiːdɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** organized provision of meals or snacks to children through schools or education programs.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** school feeding vừa support nutrition vừa giữ attendance và learning.

## 22. cash transfer /kæʃ ˈtrænsfər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** direct payment to people or households to help them meet needs, manage shocks, or invest in wellbeing.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cash transfer đưa money trực tiếp để household mua food/manage shock.

## 23. nutrition-sensitive intervention /nuˈtrɪʃən ˈsɛnsətɪv ˌɪntərˈvɛnʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an intervention in another sector designed to improve nutrition through effects on food, health, care, sanitation, or livelihoods.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** nutrition-sensitive intervention không chỉ là supplement; policy sector khác nhưng có nutrition pathway.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `영양 민감형 개입` — nutrition-sensitive intervention.

## 24. crop nutrient enhancement /krɑp ˈnuːtriənt ɪnˈhɑnsmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** breeding, agronomic, or processing approaches that increase the nutritional value of a crop.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** crop nutrient enhancement làm crop có nhiều vitamin/mineral/protein hơn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `crop-nutrient-enhancement program` — program; `increase micronutrient content` — increase; `nutrient-dense variety` — variety.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `작물 영양 강화` — crop nutrient enhancement.

## 25. fortification standard /ˌfɔrtəfəˈkeɪʃən ˈstændərd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a requirement specifying which nutrients, amounts, products, and quality controls must be used when foods are fortified.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** fortification standard quy định nutrient/amount/product/quality khi enrich food.
**Grammar & collocations:** `fortification-standard compliance` — compliance; `fortify a staple` — fortify; `nutrient specification` — specification.
**Examples:** `The fortification standard required flour mills to monitor iron levels in every batch.` → Mill phải monitor iron mỗi batch.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `강화 기준` — fortification standard.

## Review in context

**Community food sovereignty** asks who shapes the food system, while **food availability**, **food access**, **food utilization**, and **food stability** describe different dimensions of security. **Nutrition security** depends on **dietary diversity**; otherwise **hidden hunger**, **micronutrient deficiency**, **stunting**, or **wasting** may persist. **Food price volatility**, a **buffer stock**, and a **strategic grain reserve** shape resilience to **import dependency**, **supply shock**, and **harvest failure**. **Postharvest infrastructure** and **cold-chain capacity** protect food after harvest. A **social protection floor**, **school feeding**, and a **cash transfer** support households, while a **nutrition-sensitive intervention**, **crop nutrient enhancement**, and a **fortification standard** address nutrition through different pathways.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

**Community food sovereignty** hỏi ai định hình food system, còn **food availability**, **food access**, **food utilization** và **food stability** mô tả các mặt khác nhau của security. **Nutrition security** cần **dietary diversity**; nếu không, **hidden hunger**, **micronutrient deficiency**, **stunting** hoặc **wasting** có thể còn. **Food price volatility**, **buffer stock** và **strategic grain reserve** định hình resilience trước **import dependency**, **supply shock** và **harvest failure**. **Postharvest infrastructure** và **cold-chain capacity** bảo vệ food sau harvest. **Social protection floor**, **school feeding** và **cash transfer** hỗ trợ household, còn **nutrition-sensitive intervention**, **crop nutrient enhancement** và **fortification standard** xử lý nutrition qua pathway khác nhau.
