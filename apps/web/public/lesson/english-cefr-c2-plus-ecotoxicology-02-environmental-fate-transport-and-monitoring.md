# C2+ Vocabulary — Environmental fate, transport, and monitoring

This lesson follows what happens to a contaminant after release: how it partitions, transforms, travels, accumulates, and is measured in realistic ecosystems. The network is **environmental fate → sorption-desorption → volatilization flux → hydrolysis rate → biodegradation half-life → persistence criterion → long-range transport → depuration → bioconcentration factor → octanol-water partition coefficient → sediment toxicity → mesocosm experiment → environmental transformation product → degradation pathway → Henry law constant → vapor pressure → aqueous solubility → fugacity model → passive sampler → sediment-water partitioning**.

## 1. environmental fate /ɪnˌvaɪrənˈmɛntl feɪt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the movement, transformation, storage, and ultimate distribution of a substance in environmental compartments.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** “hành trình” của chemical sau release: air, water, soil, sediment, biota và transformation.
**Grammar & collocations:** `environmental-fate assessment` — assessment; `fate and transport` — phrase; `model environmental fate` — model.
**Examples:** `The environmental fate assessment tracked the compound from runoff into groundwater and fish tissue.` → Environmental fate assessment theo compound từ runoff vào groundwater và fish tissue.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `환경 거동` — environmental fate.

## 2. sorption-desorption /ˈsɔrpʃən dɪˈsɔrpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the reversible attachment of a substance to a solid surface and its subsequent release back into water or air.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** chemical bám vào particle rồi có thể nhả ra; hai chiều này quyết định mobility và exposure.
**Grammar & collocations:** `sorption-desorption isotherm` — isotherm; `sorption-desorption kinetics` — kinetics; `measure sorption` — measure.
**Examples:** `Sorption-desorption controlled how quickly the pesticide left the sediment during the flood.` → Sorption-desorption kiểm soát pesticide rời sediment nhanh thế nào trong flood.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `흡착-탈착` — sorption-desorption.

## 3. volatilization flux /ˌvɑləˌtəlaɪˈzeɪʃən flʌks/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the rate at which a substance transfers from a liquid or solid surface into the atmosphere as vapor.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** tốc độ chemical “bay” khỏi soil/water vào air, thường tăng khi warm, windy hoặc concentration surface cao.
**Grammar & collocations:** `surface volatilization flux` — flux; `estimate vapor flux` — estimate; `volatilization loss` — loss.
**Examples:** `Warm temperatures increased the volatilization flux from the contaminated soil.` → Nhiệt độ ấm làm tăng volatilization flux từ soil contaminated.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `휘발 플럭스` — volatilization flux.

## 4. hydrolysis rate /haɪˈdrɑləsɪs reɪt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the rate at which a compound reacts with water and breaks into different chemical products.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** water tham gia reaction và cắt molecule; rate phụ thuộc pH, temperature và structure.
**Grammar & collocations:** `pH-dependent hydrolysis rate` — rate; `hydrolysis half-life` — half-life; `measure hydrolysis` — measure.
**Examples:** `The hydrolysis rate increased in alkaline water, shortening the compound's persistence.` → Hydrolysis rate tăng trong alkaline water, làm persistence compound ngắn hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `가수분해 속도` — hydrolysis rate.

## 5. biodegradation half-life /ˌbaɪoʊdɪɡrəˈdeɪʃən hæf laɪf/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the time required for biological processes to reduce the amount or concentration of a substance by half under specified conditions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** microbe cần bao lâu để “ăn” mất một nửa chemical; half-life thay đổi theo oxygen, temperature, substrate và community.
**Grammar & collocations:** `long biodegradation half-life` — half-life; `estimate a half-life` — estimate; `biodegradation kinetics` — kinetics.
**Examples:** `The cold sediment produced a long biodegradation half-life for the solvent.` → Sediment lạnh tạo biodegradation half-life dài cho solvent.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `생분해 반감기` — biodegradation half-life.

## 6. persistence criterion /pərˈsɪstəns kraɪˈtɪriən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a defined rule or threshold used to classify how long a substance remains in an environmental compartment or resists transformation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** rule quyết định chemical có “persistent” không dựa trên half-life, compartment và condition cụ thể.
**Grammar & collocations:** `regulatory persistence criterion` — criterion; `meet a persistence criterion` — meet; `persistence classification` — classification.
**Examples:** `The compound met the persistence criterion because it remained in sediment for several seasons.` → Compound đạt persistence criterion vì ở lại sediment nhiều season.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `잔류성 기준` — persistence criterion.

## 7. long-range transport /lɔŋ reɪndʒ ˈtrænspɔrt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** movement of a contaminant across large geographic distances through atmospheric, oceanic, or other environmental pathways.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** chemical đi xa khỏi source nhờ wind, current hoặc aerosol, khiến vùng remote vẫn exposure.
**Grammar & collocations:** `atmospheric long-range transport` — transport; `long-range-transport model` — model; `track transport` — track.
**Examples:** `Long-range transport carried the pollutant from industrial regions into the mountain lake.` → Long-range transport mang pollutant từ industrial region tới mountain lake.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `장거리 이동` — long-range transport.

## 8. depuration /dɪˌpjʊrəˈteɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the loss or elimination of a substance from an organism after exposure is reduced or removed.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** organism “tự làm sạch” chemical qua metabolism, excretion hoặc dilution khi exposure source dừng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `depuration phase` — phase; `depuration rate` — rate; `measure depuration` — measure.
**Examples:** `Fish showed rapid depuration after they were moved to clean water.` → Fish depuration nhanh sau khi chuyển sang clean water.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `정화 배출` — depuration.

## 9. bioconcentration factor /ˌbaɪoʊkɑnsənˈtreɪʃən ˈfæktər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the ratio of a substance's concentration in an organism to its concentration in the surrounding water when uptake occurs directly from water.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** BCF đo organism concentrate chemical từ water bao nhiêu lần, chưa tính ăn prey.
**Grammar & collocations:** `bioconcentration factor estimate` — estimate; `high BCF` — factor cao; `calculate a factor` — calculate.
**Examples:** `The high bioconcentration factor indicated strong uptake through the fish's gills.` → Bioconcentration factor cao cho thấy uptake mạnh qua gill của fish.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `생물농축계수` — bioconcentration factor.

## 10. octanol-water partition coefficient /ɑkˈteɪnəl ˈwɔtər pɑrˈtɪʃən ˌkoʊəˈfɪʃənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a ratio describing how a substance distributes between octanol and water, commonly used as an indicator of hydrophobicity and partitioning behavior.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** chemical “thích oil hay water” đến đâu; coefficient cao thường gợi hydrophobicity và affinity với organic matter.
**Grammar & collocations:** `octanol-water partition coefficient` — coefficient; `log Kow` — log value; `estimate hydrophobicity` — estimate.
**Examples:** `A high octanol-water partition coefficient suggested that the compound would associate with organic sediment.` → Coefficient octanol-water cao gợi compound gắn với organic sediment.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `옥탄올-물 분배계수` — octanol-water partition coefficient.

## 11. sediment toxicity /ˈsɛdəmənt tɑkˈsɪsəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** harmful effects on organisms living in or contacting sediment contaminated with chemical or biological stressors.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** toxicity không chỉ trong water column; benthic organism chạm, ăn hoặc sống trong sediment contaminated.
**Grammar & collocations:** `sediment-toxicity test` — test; `sediment toxicity threshold` — threshold; `assess toxicity` — assess.
**Examples:** `Sediment toxicity reduced survival in the amphipod test even when overlying water was clear.` → Sediment toxicity giảm survival của amphipod dù water bên trên trong.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `퇴적물 독성` — sediment toxicity.

## 12. mesocosm experiment /ˈmɛzəˌkɑzəm ɪkˈspɛrəmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a controlled experiment using a relatively large, semi-natural enclosure that preserves several interacting ecological components.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** “mini ecosystem” đủ lớn để giữ interaction thật hơn lab microcosm nhưng vẫn controlled.
**Grammar & collocations:** `outdoor mesocosm experiment` — experiment; `mesocosm enclosure` — enclosure; `run a mesocosm` — run.
**Examples:** `The mesocosm experiment captured plant–microbe interactions that a single-species assay missed.` → Mesocosm experiment capture interaction plant–microbe mà single-species assay bỏ sót.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `메조코즘 실험` — mesocosm experiment.

## 13. environmental transformation product /ɪnˌvaɪrənˈmɛntl ˌtrænsfərˈmeɪʃən ˈprɑdʌkt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a chemical formed when an environmental process changes the structure of an original substance.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** parent chemical biến thành product mới do light, water, oxygen hoặc microbe; product mới có thể toxic hơn/ít hơn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `environmental-transformation product` — product; `identify transformation products` — identify; `parent compound` — compound.
**Examples:** `The environmental transformation product persisted longer than the parent pesticide.` → Environmental transformation product persistent lâu hơn parent pesticide.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `환경 변환 생성물` — environmental transformation product.

## 14. degradation pathway /ˌdɛɡrəˈdeɪʃən ˈpæθweɪ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a sequence of chemical, photochemical, or biological reactions through which a substance is transformed or broken down.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** “con đường phân hủy” qua nhiều intermediate, không chỉ một half-life đơn giản.
**Grammar & collocations:** `microbial degradation pathway` — pathway; `map a pathway` — map; `degradation intermediate` — intermediate.
**Examples:** `The researchers mapped a degradation pathway that ended in harmless inorganic products.` → Researcher map degradation pathway kết thúc bằng inorganic product không harmful.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `분해 경로` — degradation pathway.

## 15. Henry law constant /ˈhɛnri lɔ ˈkɑnstənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a parameter describing the equilibrium tendency of a dissolved substance to partition into the gas phase above a liquid.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** constant cho biết chemical thích rời water lên air đến mức nào ở equilibrium.
**Grammar & collocations:** `temperature-dependent Henry law constant` — constant; `Henry's-law partitioning` — partitioning; `estimate volatilization` — estimate.
**Examples:** `The high Henry law constant predicted rapid transfer from surface water to air.` → Henry law constant cao dự đoán transfer nhanh từ surface water sang air.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `헨리 법칙 상수` — Henry law constant.

## 16. vapor pressure /ˈveɪpər ˈprɛʃər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the pressure exerted by a substance's vapor when it is in equilibrium with its condensed phase at a specified temperature.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mức chemical muốn bay thành vapor ở nhiệt độ đó; vapor pressure cao thường tăng volatilization.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high vapor pressure` — pressure; `vapor-pressure estimate` — estimate; `temperature-dependent vapor pressure` — pressure.
**Examples:** `The solvent's high vapor pressure required sealed sampling containers.` → Vapor pressure cao của solvent cần sampling container kín.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `증기압` — vapor pressure.

## 17. aqueous solubility /ˈeɪkwiəs ˌsɑljəˈbɪləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the maximum amount of a substance that dissolves in water under specified temperature, pH, and chemical conditions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** chemical hòa tan vào water được bao nhiêu; solubility quyết định mobility và exposure aqueous.
**Grammar & collocations:** `aqueous-solubility limit` — limit; `water solubility` — solubility; `measure solubility` — measure.
**Examples:** `Low aqueous solubility caused the compound to remain associated with suspended particles.` → Aqueous solubility thấp khiến compound gắn với suspended particle.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `수용해도` — aqueous solubility.

## 18. fugacity model /ˈfjuːɡæsəti ˈmɑdəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a multimedia environmental model that estimates how a substance partitions and moves among air, water, soil, sediment, and biota using fugacity-based relationships.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** model theo dõi “chemical pressure” giữa compartments để dự đoán hướng partition và steady state.
**Grammar & collocations:** `level-IV fugacity model` — model; `multimedia fugacity` — fugacity; `run a model` — run.
**Examples:** `The fugacity model predicted that the compound would leave soil and accumulate in sediment.` → Fugacity model dự đoán compound rời soil và accumulate trong sediment.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `퓨가시티 모델` — fugacity model.

## 19. passive sampler /ˈpæsɪv ˈsæmplər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a device that accumulates dissolved or vapor-phase chemicals over time without active pumping.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** sampler “ngồi yên” nhưng hút chemical dần, cho time-integrated exposure thay vì một snapshot.
**Grammar & collocations:** `passive-sampler deployment` — deployment; `deploy a sampler` — deploy; `time-integrated concentration` — concentration.
**Examples:** `The passive sampler integrated low concentrations that grab samples repeatedly missed.` → Passive sampler tích hợp low concentration mà grab sample lặp lại bỏ sót.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `수동 채취기` — passive sampler.

## 20. sediment-water partitioning /ˈsɛdəmənt ˈwɔtər pɑrˈtɪʃənɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the distribution of a substance between sediment solids and the water occupying or flowing over the sediment.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** chemical chia giữa solid sediment và pore/overlying water; disturbance có thể shift balance và release chemical.
**Grammar & collocations:** `sediment-water-partitioning coefficient` — coefficient; `model partitioning` — model; `partition into sediment` — partition.
**Examples:** `Sediment-water partitioning shifted toward the water phase when organic matter was disturbed.` → Sediment-water partitioning shift về water phase khi organic matter bị disturb.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `퇴적물-물 분배` — sediment-water partitioning.

## Review in context

An **environmental fate** assessment follows **sorption-desorption**, **volatilization flux**, **hydrolysis rate**, and **biodegradation half-life** before applying a **persistence criterion**. **Long-range transport** can move a contaminant far from its source, while **depuration**, the **bioconcentration factor**, and the **octanol-water partition coefficient** describe uptake and loss in organisms. **Sediment toxicity** can be studied in a **mesocosm experiment** that preserves ecological interactions. An **environmental transformation product** emerges through a **degradation pathway**. **Henry law constant**, **vapor pressure**, and **aqueous solubility** describe phase transfer, and a **fugacity model** combines those properties across compartments. A **passive sampler** measures time-integrated exposure, while **sediment-water partitioning** explains how disturbance can release a chemical again.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

Assessment **environmental fate** theo dõi **sorption-desorption**, **volatilization flux**, **hydrolysis rate** và **biodegradation half-life** trước khi áp dụng **persistence criterion**. **Long-range transport** có thể đưa contaminant xa khỏi source, còn **depuration**, **bioconcentration factor** và **octanol-water partition coefficient** mô tả uptake/loss trong organism. **Sediment toxicity** có thể study bằng **mesocosm experiment** giữ interaction ecology. **Environmental transformation product** xuất hiện qua **degradation pathway**. **Henry law constant**, **vapor pressure** và **aqueous solubility** mô tả phase transfer, còn **fugacity model** kết hợp các property qua nhiều compartment. **Passive sampler** đo exposure tích hợp theo time, trong khi **sediment-water partitioning** giải thích disturbance có thể release chemical trở lại.
