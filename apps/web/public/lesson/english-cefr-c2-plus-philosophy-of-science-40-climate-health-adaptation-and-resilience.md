# C2+ Vocabulary — Climate, health adaptation, and resilience

This lesson follows how health systems anticipate climate hazards, protect vulnerable communities, and learn from adaptation limits. The network is **early warning threshold → heat action plan → cooling refuge → heat-island effect → wet-bulb temperature → thermal stress → vector suitability → climate-sensitive disease → seasonal forecast → attribution science → climate migration → planned relocation → co-benefit of resilience → response capacity → harmful adaptation → residual climate harm → climate services → downscaled projection → exposure scenario → compound hazard → chain failure → lifeline coupling → health co-benefit → ecosystem-based measure → climate budget**.

## 1. early warning threshold /ˈɜrli ˈwɔrnɪŋ ˈθrɛʃˌhoʊld/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a predefined level of observed or forecast risk that triggers an alert, protective action, or escalation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** early warning threshold là ngưỡng biến signal thành action trước khi harm lớn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `early-warning threshold` — threshold; `trigger an alert` — trigger; `forecast risk` — risk.
**Examples:** `The early warning threshold triggered outreach before temperatures reached the dangerous range.` → Action đi trước peak heat.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `조기 경보 임계값` — early warning threshold.

## 2. heat action plan /hiːt ˈækʃən plæn/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a coordinated plan for reducing illness and death during periods of unusually high heat.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** heat action plan nối forecast, communication, cooling, outreach và clinical readiness.
**Grammar & collocations:** `heat-action-plan protocol` — protocol; `activate a heat plan` — activate; `heat-health warning` — warning.
**Examples:** `The heat action plan opened cooling sites, checked isolated residents, and adjusted outdoor work.` → Plan có nhiều action cùng lúc.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `폭염 행동 계획` — heat action plan.

## 3. cooling refuge /ˈkuːlɪŋ ˈrɛfjuːdʒ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a publicly accessible place where people can escape dangerous heat and obtain water, rest, or basic support.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cooling refuge là nơi trú nóng có thể tới được và an toàn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `cooling-refuge network` — network; `open a cooling refuge` — open; `heat shelter` — shelter.
**Examples:** `A cooling refuge in the library served people who lacked air conditioning at home.` → Library thành nơi trú nóng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `냉방 대피소` — cooling refuge.

## 4. heat-island effect /hiːt ˈaɪlənd ɪˈfɛkt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** extra warming in built-up areas caused by heat-absorbing surfaces, limited vegetation, and human activity.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** heat-island effect làm city nóng hơn vùng xung quanh.
**Grammar & collocations:** `heat-island-effect map` — map; `intensify urban heat` — intensify; `tree canopy` — canopy.
**Examples:** `The heat-island effect was strongest where asphalt covered former green space.` → Asphalt và thiếu cây làm heat tăng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `열섬 효과` — heat-island effect.

## 5. wet-bulb temperature /ˈwɛt bʌlb ˈtɛmprətʃər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a temperature measure that combines heat and humidity to indicate how difficult it is for the body to cool through evaporation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** wet-bulb temperature phản ánh heat + humidity và giới hạn cooling của body.
**Grammar & collocations:** `wet-bulb-temperature alert` — alert; `measure evaporative cooling limits` — measure; `humid heat` — heat.
**Examples:** `Wet-bulb temperature rose even after the air temperature stabilized because humidity remained high.` → Humidity làm body khó cool.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `습구 온도` — wet-bulb temperature.

## 6. thermal stress /ˈθɜrməl strɛs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** physiological strain caused when environmental heat or cold overwhelms the body's ability to regulate temperature.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** thermal stress là body bị ép vượt khả năng điều nhiệt.
**Grammar & collocations:** `thermal-stress exposure` — exposure; `experience heat stress` — experience; `thermoregulatory strain` — strain.
**Examples:** `Thermal stress increased among outdoor workers who lacked shaded rest breaks.` → Thiếu shade làm worker stress hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `열 스트레스` — thermal stress.

## 7. vector suitability /ˈvɛktər ˌsuːtəˈbɪləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the degree to which environmental conditions support the survival, reproduction, and disease transmission of a vector.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** vector suitability cho biết climate có hợp với mosquito/tick truyền disease không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `vector-suitability model` — model; `support vector transmission` — support; `mosquito habitat` — habitat.
**Examples:** `Warming increased vector suitability at higher elevations where the mosquito had rarely survived before.` → Mosquito lên vùng cao.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `매개체 적합성` — vector suitability.

## 8. climate-sensitive disease /ˈklaɪmət ˈsɛnsətɪv dɪˈziːz/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a disease whose incidence, severity, distribution, or transmission changes with climate conditions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** climate-sensitive disease đổi theo heat, rainfall, humidity hoặc vector.
**Grammar & collocations:** `climate-sensitive-disease surveillance` — surveillance; `track climate-related incidence` — track; `transmission season` — season.
**Examples:** `The surveillance system extended its dengue season because the climate-sensitive disease was appearing earlier.` → Dengue season dài hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `기후 민감성 질병` — climate-sensitive disease.

## 9. seasonal forecast /ˈsiːzənəl ˈfɔrˌkæst/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a prediction of likely climate conditions over the coming months, used to prepare services and communities.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** seasonal forecast cho health system nhìn trước vài tháng để chuẩn bị.
**Grammar & collocations:** `seasonal-forecast bulletin` — bulletin; `forecast a wet season` — forecast; `preparedness decision` — decision.
**Examples:** `The seasonal forecast prompted earlier cholera-supply planning in flood-prone districts.` → Forecast giúp chuẩn bị supply.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `계절 예보` — seasonal forecast.

## 10. attribution science /ˌætrəˈbjuʃən saɪəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** scientific analysis of how climate change affected the probability, intensity, or duration of an observed event.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** attribution science hỏi climate change đã làm event khác đi bao nhiêu.
**Grammar & collocations:** `attribution-science estimate` — estimate; `attribute an extreme event` — attribute; `counterfactual climate` — climate.
**Examples:** `Attribution science estimated that warming made the heat wave more likely and longer.` → Warming tăng probability và duration.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `기후 귀속 과학` — attribution science.

## 11. climate migration /ˈklaɪmət maɪˈɡreɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** movement of people influenced partly or substantially by climate-related changes, hazards, or loss of livelihood.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** climate migration là migration có climate làm một phần nguyên nhân.
**Grammar & collocations:** `climate-migration pathway` — pathway; `move after repeated hazards` — move; `receiving community` — community.
**Examples:** `Climate migration placed new pressure on housing, clinics, and sanitation in receiving towns.` → Nơi nhận người cần thêm service.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `기후 이주` — climate migration.

## 12. planned relocation /plænd ˌriːloʊˈkeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** organized movement of people, assets, or services away from an area facing intolerable or persistent hazard.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** planned relocation là move có plan, resource và consent hơn là chạy trốn tự phát.
**Grammar & collocations:** `planned-relocation framework` — framework; `relocate a settlement` — relocate; `receiving-site capacity` — capacity.
**Examples:** `Planned relocation included a new clinic, school, transport route, and livelihood support.` → Relocation phải gồm service và sinh kế.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `계획 이주` — planned relocation.

## 13. co-benefit of resilience /ˈkoʊˌbɛnəfɪt əv rɪˈzɪliəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an additional health, social, or economic benefit produced by an action intended primarily to strengthen resilience.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** co-benefit of resilience là lợi ích phụ nhưng thật của investment resilience.
**Grammar & collocations:** `resilience-co-benefit estimate` — estimate; `generate a co-benefit` — generate; `health gain` — gain.
**Examples:** `Tree planting produced a co-benefit of resilience by reducing heat while improving air quality.` → Cây vừa giảm heat vừa làm sạch air.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `회복탄력성 공동 편익` — co-benefit of resilience.

## 14. response capacity /rɪˈspɑns kəˈpæsəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the resources, skills, institutions, and relationships available to act effectively when a hazard occurs.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** response capacity là khả năng biến warning thành action thật.
**Grammar & collocations:** `response-capacity assessment` — assessment; `build response capacity` — build; `emergency resource` — resource.
**Examples:** `Response capacity was limited by weak transport, staff shortages, and unreliable power.` → Capacity yếu vì nhiều bottleneck.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `대응 역량` — response capacity.

## 15. harmful adaptation /ˈhɑrmfəl ˌædæpˈteɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an adaptation that reduces risk in one place or time while increasing vulnerability, inequality, or harm elsewhere.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** harmful adaptation giải quyết risk trước mắt nhưng đẩy harm sang người/nơi khác.
**Grammar & collocations:** `harmful-adaptation review` — review; `shift risk elsewhere` — shift; `unintended consequence` — consequence.
**Examples:** `A flood wall became harmful adaptation when it diverted water toward a poorer neighborhood.` → Wall bảo vệ một nơi nhưng đẩy nước sang nơi nghèo.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `유해한 적응` — harmful adaptation.

## 16. residual climate harm /rɪˈzɪdʒuəl ˈklaɪmət hɑrm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** climate-related damage or loss that remains after feasible mitigation and adaptation measures have been taken.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** residual climate harm là phần harm còn lại dù đã adapt hết mức khả thi.
**Grammar & collocations:** `residual-climate-harm estimate` — estimate; `address remaining harm` — address; `unavoidable loss` — loss.
**Examples:** `The fund was designed to address residual climate harm after repeated coastal flooding.` → Fund xử lý loss còn lại.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `잔여 기후 피해` — residual climate harm.

## 17. climate services /ˈklaɪmət ˈsɜrvəsɪz/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** usable climate information, forecasts, tools, and advice designed to support decisions in a specific sector or place.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** climate services biến climate data thành advice dùng được cho health/planning.
**Grammar & collocations:** `climate-services platform` — platform; `translate forecasts into decisions` — translate; `decision support` — support.
**Examples:** `Climate services translated rainfall forecasts into malaria-prevention guidance for local clinics.` → Forecast thành advice cụ thể.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `기후 서비스` — climate services.

## 18. downscaled projection /ˈdaʊnˌskeɪld prəˈdʒɛkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a higher-resolution climate projection adjusted to describe likely conditions in a particular region or locality.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** downscaled projection kéo climate model lớn xuống scale địa phương.
**Grammar & collocations:** `downscaled-projection dataset` — dataset; `project local rainfall` — project; `regional climate model` — model.
**Examples:** `The downscaled projection helped planners estimate which neighborhoods would face longer heat seasons.` → Projection chỉ heat theo neighborhood.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `다운스케일링 전망` — downscaled projection.

## 19. exposure scenario /ɪkˈspoʊʒər səˈnɛrioʊ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a defined set of assumptions about how people, places, or systems may be exposed to a hazard under future conditions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** exposure scenario là “nếu điều kiện này xảy ra thì ai sẽ tiếp xúc hazard ra sao”.
**Grammar & collocations:** `exposure-scenario model` — model; `model future exposure` — model; `planning assumption` — assumption.
**Examples:** `The exposure scenario combined sea-level rise with projected population growth near the coast.` → Scenario gộp climate và population.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `노출 시나리오` — exposure scenario.

## 20. compound hazard /ˈkɑmˌpaʊnd ˈhæzərd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** two or more hazards that occur together or in sequence and create greater or different risk than either alone.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** compound hazard là nhiều hazard chồng nhau hoặc nối nhau.
**Grammar & collocations:** `compound-hazard scenario` — scenario; `combine climate hazards` — combine; `joint risk` — risk.
**Examples:** `A compound hazard of heat, smoke, and power failure threatened patients using home oxygen.` → Ba hazard cùng lúc tạo risk lớn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `복합 위험` — compound hazard.

## 21. chain failure /tʃeɪn ˈfeɪljər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a sequence in which one disruption causes another system or service to fail, producing cascading consequences.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** chain failure là failure truyền từ mắt xích này sang mắt xích khác.
**Grammar & collocations:** `chain-failure analysis` — analysis; `trigger a sequence of failures` — trigger; `cascading consequence` — consequence.
**Examples:** `The storm caused chain failure: power loss stopped pumps, which interrupted water treatment and dialysis.` → Một failure kéo nhiều service.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `연쇄 고장` — chain failure.

## 22. lifeline coupling /ˈlaɪfˌlaɪn ˈkʌplɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** dependence between essential infrastructure systems such as power, water, transport, communications, and health care.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** lifeline coupling cho thấy infrastructure sống nhờ nhau.
**Grammar & collocations:** `lifeline-coupling map` — map; `depend on critical infrastructure` — depend; `service interdependence` — interdependence.
**Examples:** `Lifeline coupling meant that a telecom outage disrupted both ambulance dispatch and pharmacy ordering.` → Mất telecom ảnh hưởng nhiều service.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `핵심 기반시설 결합` — lifeline coupling.

## 23. health co-benefit /hɛlθ ˈkoʊˌbɛnəfɪt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a positive health effect produced by an action whose main objective may lie in another sector.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** health co-benefit là lợi ích health đi kèm policy giao thông, năng lượng, housing…
**Grammar & collocations:** `health-co-benefit estimate` — estimate; `generate a health benefit` — generate; `cross-sector policy` — policy.
**Examples:** `Replacing diesel buses produced a health co-benefit through lower particulate exposure.` → Transport policy tạo health benefit.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `건강 공동 편익` — health co-benefit.

## 24. ecosystem-based measure /ˈiːkoʊˌsɪstəm beɪst ˈmɛʒər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a protective action that uses or restores ecological processes to reduce hazards and support human well-being.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** ecosystem-based measure dùng wetland, mangrove, tree cover… để giảm risk.
**Grammar & collocations:** `ecosystem-based-measure plan` — plan; `restore a wetland` — restore; `natural protection` — protection.
**Examples:** `An ecosystem-based measure restored wetlands that absorbed floodwater and improved water quality.` → Wetland vừa chống flood vừa lọc water.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `생태계 기반 조치` — ecosystem-based measure.

## 25. climate budget /ˈklaɪmət ˈbʌdʒət/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a quantified limit on cumulative greenhouse-gas emissions consistent with a chosen temperature or policy goal.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** climate budget là lượng emission “được phép tiêu” trước khi vượt goal.
**Grammar & collocations:** `climate-budget pathway` — pathway; `stay within a budget` — stay; `emissions limit` — limit.
**Examples:** `The climate budget forced the health authority to compare the long-term emissions of each infrastructure option.` → Budget giới hạn cách build.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `기후 예산` — climate budget.

## Review in context — Part 1

An **early warning threshold** can activate a **heat action plan**, including a **cooling refuge** in a neighborhood affected by the **heat-island effect**. **Wet-bulb temperature** and **thermal stress** show why humidity and body cooling matter. **Vector suitability** can expand the range of a **climate-sensitive disease**, so a **seasonal forecast** and **attribution science** help services prepare. **Climate migration** may require **planned relocation**, while a **co-benefit of resilience** can make protective investment useful in ordinary times. **Response capacity** determines whether warnings become action.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 1:**

**Early warning threshold** có thể kích hoạt **heat action plan**, gồm **cooling refuge** trong khu vực chịu **heat-island effect**. **Wet-bulb temperature** và **thermal stress** cho thấy humidity và khả năng body cooling quan trọng thế nào. **Vector suitability** có thể mở rộng vùng của **climate-sensitive disease**, nên **seasonal forecast** và **attribution science** giúp service chuẩn bị. **Climate migration** có thể cần **planned relocation**, còn **co-benefit of resilience** làm investment protection hữu ích cả trong thời gian bình thường. **Response capacity** quyết định warning có thành action không.

## Review in context — Part 2

A **harmful adaptation** may shift risk, leaving **residual climate harm** after other measures are taken. **Climate services**, a **downscaled projection**, and an **exposure scenario** support local planning for a **compound hazard**. A **chain failure** can spread through **lifeline coupling**, while a **health co-benefit** may come from an **ecosystem-based measure**. A **climate budget** limits long-term emissions and forces decision-makers to compare protection, health, and future risk together.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 2:**

**Harmful adaptation** có thể đẩy risk sang nơi khác, để lại **residual climate harm** dù đã làm các measure khác. **Climate services**, **downscaled projection** và **exposure scenario** hỗ trợ planning địa phương trước **compound hazard**. **Chain failure** có thể lan qua **lifeline coupling**, còn **health co-benefit** có thể đến từ **ecosystem-based measure**. **Climate budget** giới hạn emission dài hạn và buộc decision-maker so sánh protection, health và future risk cùng nhau.
