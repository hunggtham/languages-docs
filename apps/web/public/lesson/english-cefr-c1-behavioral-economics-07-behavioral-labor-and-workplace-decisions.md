# C1 Vocabulary 07 — Behavioral labor and workplace decisions

This lesson follows how beliefs about fairness, effort, status, and reciprocity shape workplace choices and labor-market behavior.

Flow: **effort aversion → reference wage → fair wage → reciprocity wage → gift exchange → shirking norm → workplace norm → promotion bias → career concern → status quo job → job search frictions → present bias labor → overconfidence labor → pay transparency → wage anchoring → effort choice → motivation crowding → performance salience → behavioral labor economics → labor supply framing**.

## 1. effort aversion /ˈɛfərt əˈvɝʒən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a dislike of exertion that makes a person prefer a less demanding task or schedule, even at some cost.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** không thích exertion khiến person prefer task hoặc schedule ít demanding dù có cost.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong effort aversion` — aversion; `measure effort aversion` — measure.
**Examples:** `Effort aversion made the worker choose a predictable shift over a higher-paid demanding shift.` → Aversion effort làm worker chọn shift predictable thay shift demanding pay cao.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `노력 회피`.

## 2. reference wage /ˈrɛfərəns weɪdʒ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the pay level a worker uses as a comparison point when judging a current or proposed wage.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** pay level worker dùng làm comparison point judge wage hiện tại hoặc đề xuất.
**Grammar & collocations:** `internal reference wage` — wage; `update a reference wage` — update.
**Examples:** `The reference wage came from the worker's previous job rather than the local average.` → Wage reference đến từ job trước chứ không phải local average.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `참조 임금`.

## 3. fair wage /fɛr weɪdʒ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a wage a worker considers appropriate given effort, treatment, market conditions, and what others receive.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** wage worker thấy appropriate theo effort, treatment, market condition và pay người khác.
**Grammar & collocations:** `perceived fair wage` — wage; `offer a fair wage` — offer.
**Examples:** `Employees viewed the raise as a fair wage after learning that responsibilities had expanded.` → Employee xem raise là wage fair sau biết responsibility expand.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `공정 임금`.

## 4. reciprocity wage /ˌrɛsəˈprɑsəti weɪdʒ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** pay that reflects an employer's wish to reward effort or loyalty and a worker's wish to respond in kind.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** pay reflect employer muốn reward effort/loyalty và worker muốn respond tương xứng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `reciprocity-wage contract` — contract; `set a reciprocity wage` — set.
**Examples:** `The reciprocity wage encouraged employees to stay late during the emergency.` → Wage reciprocity encourage employee stay late trong emergency.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `상호성 임금`.

## 5. gift exchange /ɡɪft ɪksˈtʃeɪndʒ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a workplace relationship in which an employer offers favorable treatment and workers respond with extra effort or loyalty.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** relationship employer offer treatment tốt và worker respond bằng effort hoặc loyalty thêm.
**Grammar & collocations:** `workplace gift exchange` — exchange; `model gift exchange` — model.
**Examples:** `The gift exchange began when the manager guaranteed schedules during a difficult season.` → Exchange gift bắt đầu khi manager guarantee schedule trong season khó.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `선물 교환`.

## 6. shirking norm /ˈʃɝkɪŋ nɔrm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a shared expectation about how acceptable it is to reduce effort or avoid responsibility at work.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** expectation shared về việc giảm effort hoặc tránh responsibility ở work có acceptable không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `permissive shirking norm` — norm; `change a shirking norm` — change.
**Examples:** `A permissive shirking norm spread when supervisors ignored repeated lateness.` → Norm shirking permissive lan khi supervisor ignore lateness lặp lại.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `근무 태만 규범`.

## 7. workplace norm /ˈwɝkˌpleɪs nɔrm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an informal expectation about how colleagues should behave, cooperate, or respond at work.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** expectation informal về colleague nên behave, cooperate hoặc respond ở work.
**Grammar & collocations:** `supportive workplace norm` — norm; `establish a workplace norm` — establish.
**Examples:** `The workplace norm was to help a colleague before asking a supervisor for permission.` → Norm workplace là help colleague trước khi ask supervisor.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `직장 규범`.

## 8. promotion bias /prəˈmoʊʃən baɪəs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a systematic tendency for promotion decisions to favor or disadvantage people for reasons unrelated to job performance.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** tendency systematic promotion decision favor/disadvantage người vì reason không liên quan performance.
**Grammar & collocations:** `implicit promotion bias` — bias; `detect promotion bias` — detect.
**Examples:** `Promotion bias appeared when identical records received different evaluations under different names.` → Bias promotion xuất hiện khi record giống nhau nhận evaluation khác theo name.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `승진 편향`.

## 9. career concern /kəˈrɪr kənˈsɝn/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** worry about how a current choice may affect future employment, status, income, or advancement.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** worry current choice affect employment, status, income hoặc advancement future.
**Grammar & collocations:** `long-term career concern` — concern; `voice a career concern` — voice.
**Examples:** `Career concern made the analyst avoid reporting a serious forecasting error.` → Concern career làm analyst tránh report forecasting error nghiêm trọng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `경력 우려`.

## 10. status quo job /ˈsteɪtəs kwoʊ dʒɑb/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a current job or arrangement that a person keeps because changing it feels costly, risky, or difficult.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** job hoặc arrangement hiện tại giữ vì change thấy cost, risk hoặc difficult.
**Grammar & collocations:** `comfortable status quo job` — job; `remain in a status quo job` — remain.
**Examples:** `The status quo job felt safer despite limited opportunities for training.` → Job status quo thấy safe hơn dù ít opportunity training.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `현상 유지 직업`.

## 11. job search frictions /dʒɑb sɝtʃ ˈfrɪkʃənz/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** practical, informational, or psychological obstacles that slow a person's search for suitable work.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** obstacle practical, informational hoặc psychological làm chậm search job phù hợp.
**Grammar & collocations:** `severe job-search frictions` — frictions; `reduce job search frictions` — reduce.
**Examples:** `Job search frictions kept qualified workers from applying to distant vacancies.` → Frictions search job giữ qualified worker không apply vacancy xa.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `구직 마찰`.

## 12. present bias labor /ˈprɛzənt baɪəs ˈleɪbər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a tendency in labor decisions to overweight immediate pay or comfort relative to future earnings or skills.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** labor decision weight pay/comfort immediate hơn future earning hoặc skill.
**Grammar & collocations:** `present-bias labor choice` — choice; `model present bias in labor` — model.
**Examples:** `Present bias labor made the worker postpone a course that would raise future wages.` → Bias present labor làm worker postpone course tăng wage future.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `노동 현재 편향`.

## 13. overconfidence labor /ˌoʊvərˈkɑnfədəns ˈleɪbər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** excessive confidence about one's productivity, prospects, or ability to judge a job market.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** confidence quá mức về productivity, prospect hoặc ability judge job market.
**Grammar & collocations:** `overconfidence labor belief` — belief; `correct overconfidence in labor` — correct.
**Examples:** `Overconfidence labor led the applicant to reject several realistic offers.` → Overconfidence labor làm applicant reject vài offer realistic.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `노동 과신`.

## 14. pay transparency /peɪ trænˈspɛrənsi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the availability of clear information about wages, pay ranges, or how compensation decisions are made.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** information clear về wage, pay range hoặc cách compensation decision được make.
**Grammar & collocations:** `greater pay transparency` — transparency; `introduce pay transparency` — introduce.
**Examples:** `Pay transparency reduced uncertainty about whether the offer was competitive.` → Transparency pay giảm uncertainty offer có competitive không.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `임금 투명성`.

## 15. wage anchoring /weɪdʒ ˈæŋkərɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the tendency for an initial wage figure to influence later judgments and negotiations.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** wage figure ban đầu influence judgment và negotiation sau.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong wage anchoring` — anchoring; `counter wage anchoring` — counter.
**Examples:** `Wage anchoring made the first salary number shape the entire negotiation.` → Anchoring wage làm salary number đầu shape cả negotiation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `임금 고정`.

## 16. effort choice /ˈɛfərt tʃɔɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a decision about how much energy, time, or attention to devote to a task.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** decision devote energy, time hoặc attention cho task bao nhiêu.
**Grammar & collocations:** `optimal effort choice` — choice; `model effort choice` — model.
**Examples:** `Effort choice changed when the manager explained how quality would be evaluated.` → Choice effort đổi khi manager explain quality evaluate thế nào.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `노력 선택`.

## 17. motivation crowding /ˌmoʊtəˈveɪʃən ˈkraʊdɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a change in internal motivation caused by an external reward, penalty, or monitoring system.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** internal motivation đổi vì reward, penalty hoặc monitoring external.
**Grammar & collocations:** `motivation-crowding effect` — effect; `test for motivation crowding` — test.
**Examples:** `A small bonus caused motivation crowding when volunteers felt their goodwill was being priced.` → Crowding motivation xảy ra khi volunteer feel goodwill bị price bằng bonus nhỏ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `동기 구축`.

## 18. performance salience /pərˈfɔrməns ˈseɪliəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** how noticeable the connection is between a person's effort and the performance outcome that follows.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mức connection giữa effort và performance outcome trở nên noticeable.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high performance salience` — salience; `increase performance salience` — increase.
**Examples:** `Performance salience increased when workers received immediate feedback.` → Salience performance tăng khi worker receive feedback immediate.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `성과 현저성`.

## 19. behavioral labor economics /bɪˈheɪvjərəl ˈleɪbər ˌiːkəˈnɑmɪks/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the study of how psychological biases, social preferences, and beliefs shape work and employment decisions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** study bias psychological, social preference và belief shape work/employment decision.
**Grammar & collocations:** `behavioral-labor-economics model` — model; `study behavioral labor economics` — study.
**Examples:** `Behavioral labor economics explains why identical incentives can produce different effort responses.` → Economics labor behavioral explain incentive giống nhau tạo effort response khác.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `행동 노동경제학`.

## 20. labor supply framing /ˈleɪbər səˈplaɪ ˈfreɪmɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the way a job, wage, or schedule is presented, which can change a person's willingness to work.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cách present job, wage hoặc schedule làm willingness work đổi.
**Grammar & collocations:** `flexible labor-supply framing` — framing; `change labor supply framing` — change.
**Examples:** `Labor supply framing as flexibility attracted more caregivers than framing as overtime.` → Framing supply labor như flexibility attract caregiver hơn framing như overtime.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `노동 공급 프레이밍`.

## Review in context

**Effort aversion** can make a worker compare a job with a **reference wage** and judge a **fair wage** through **reciprocity wage** and **gift exchange**. A **shirking norm** and a **workplace norm** shape daily behavior, while **promotion bias** and **career concern** affect advancement. A **status quo job** may persist because of **job search frictions**, **present bias labor**, or **overconfidence labor**. **Pay transparency** can weaken **wage anchoring**, while **effort choice**, **motivation crowding**, and **performance salience** are central to **behavioral labor economics** and **labor supply framing**.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:** **Effort aversion** khiến worker compare job với **reference wage** và judge **fair wage** qua **reciprocity wage** và **gift exchange**. **Shirking norm** và **workplace norm** shape behavior hàng ngày, còn **promotion bias** và **career concern** ảnh hưởng advancement. **Status quo job** có thể tồn tại vì **job search frictions**, **present bias labor** hoặc **overconfidence labor**. **Pay transparency** có thể weaken **wage anchoring**, còn **effort choice**, **motivation crowding** và **performance salience** là central trong **behavioral labor economics** và **labor supply framing**.
