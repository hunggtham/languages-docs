# C1 Vocabulary 42 — Behavioral work technology and automation

This lesson follows how workers and organizations interpret automation, monitoring, skills, collaboration, and changing work design.

Flow: **automation threat perception → algorithm trust work → human oversight norm → digital skill confidence → reskilling motivation → task automation default → monitoring intrusiveness perception → productivity metric salience → remote collaboration friction → notification boundary → platform learning curve → tool switching cost → workplace data privacy belief → automation fairness perception → job redesign expectation → human-machine complementarity belief → digital exclusion risk → technology adoption fatigue → workflow resilience belief → future of work framing**.

## 1. automation threat perception /ˌɔtəˈmeɪʃən θrɛt pərˈsɛpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a judgment about how likely technology is to reduce one's job security, status, income, or autonomy.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** judgment technology reduce job security, status, income hoặc autonomy likely thế nào.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high automation threat perception` — perception; `reduce automation threat perception` — reduce.
**Examples:** `Automation threat perception rose when the company showed a tool without explaining how roles would change.` → Perception threat automation tăng khi company show tool không explain role đổi.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `자동화 위협 인식`.

## 2. algorithm trust work /ˈælgəˌrɪðəm trʌst wɝk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** confidence that an algorithm used at work is accurate, explainable, and appropriate for its decision.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** confidence algorithm work accurate, explainable và appropriate cho decision.
**Grammar & collocations:** `low algorithm trust work` — trust; `build algorithm trust work` — build.
**Examples:** `Algorithm trust work improved when managers could review the factors behind a recommendation.` → Trust algorithm work improve khi manager review factor sau recommendation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `업무 알고리즘 신뢰`.

## 3. human oversight norm /ˈhjuːmən ˈoʊvərˌsaɪt nɔrm/

## Review in context

Workers respond to **automation threat perception** and **algorithm trust work** before accepting new systems. A **human oversight norm** protects judgment, while **digital skill confidence** and **reskilling motivation** shape learning. A **task automation default** can reduce routine effort, but **monitoring intrusiveness perception** and **productivity metric salience** affect dignity. **Remote collaboration friction** makes a clear **notification boundary** valuable, and a steep **platform learning curve** increases **tool switching cost**. **Workplace data privacy belief** and **automation fairness perception** determine legitimacy; **job redesign expectation** shapes preparation. A **human-machine complementarity belief** supports cooperation, while **digital exclusion risk** warns against access gaps. Repeated changes create **technology adoption fatigue**, but **workflow resilience belief** grows through practice. Finally, **future of work framing** determines whether change is experienced as shared design or unavoidable loss.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

Worker phản hồi **automation threat perception** và **algorithm trust work** trước accept system mới. **Human oversight norm** bảo vệ judgment, còn **digital skill confidence** và **reskilling motivation** shape learning. **Task automation default** giảm routine effort, nhưng **monitoring intrusiveness perception** và **productivity metric salience** ảnh hưởng dignity. **Remote collaboration friction** làm **notification boundary** rõ valuable, còn **platform learning curve** steep tăng **tool switching cost**. **Workplace data privacy belief** và **automation fairness perception** quyết định legitimacy; **job redesign expectation** shape preparation. **Human-machine complementarity belief** support cooperation, còn **digital exclusion risk** cảnh báo access gap. Change lặp tạo **technology adoption fatigue**, nhưng **workflow resilience belief** grow qua practice. Cuối cùng, **future of work framing** quyết định change được experience như design shared hay loss unavoidable.
