# C2+ Vocabulary — Policy instruments and implementation effects

This lesson follows how policies are designed, implemented, adapted, and reshaped by human behavior and institutional context. The network is **policy instrument → regulatory instrument → command-and-control → market-based instrument → outcome performance standard → technology standard → disclosure mandate → expiry clause → review clause → adaptive regulation → policy feedback → delivery fidelity → frontline discretion → procedural burden → compliance burden → policy response behavior → policy leakage → compensatory demand effect → incentive-induced risk → hidden-quality sorting → spillover consequence → distributional incidence → policy counterfactual → treated-unit effect → policy externality**.

## 1. policy instrument /ˈpɑləsi ˈɪnstrəmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a tool government or an institution uses to pursue a policy objective, such as a rule, tax, subsidy, service, or information requirement.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** policy instrument là tool cụ thể để đạt policy objective.
**Grammar & collocations:** `policy-instrument mix` — mix; `choose an instrument` — choose; `policy objective` — objective.
**Examples:** `The ministry compared a tax and a subsidy as alternative policy instruments.` → Ministry compare tax và subsidy.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `정책 수단` — policy instrument.

## 2. regulatory instrument /ˈrɛɡjəˌlətɔri ˈɪnstrəmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a rule, standard, licensing condition, inspection, or enforcement mechanism used to shape behavior.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** regulatory instrument là rule/standard/license/inspection dùng để shape behavior.
**Grammar & collocations:** `regulatory-instrument design` — design; `deploy an instrument` — deploy; `regulated actor` — actor.
**Examples:** `The disclosure rule was selected as a regulatory instrument because direct inspection was costly.` → Disclosure rule được chọn vì inspection đắt.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `규제 수단` — regulatory instrument.

## 3. command-and-control /kəˈmænd ænd kənˈtroʊl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a regulatory approach that prescribes required behavior, limits, technologies, or outcomes and enforces compliance.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** command-and-control nói rõ actor phải làm gì/không làm gì và enforce.
**Grammar & collocations:** `command-and-control regime` — regime; `prescribe a limit` — prescribe; `enforce compliance` — enforce.
**Examples:** `The command-and-control rule required factories to install a specified filter.` → Rule bắt factory install filter cụ thể.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `명령 및 통제` — command-and-control.

## 4. market-based instrument /ˈmɑrkət beɪst ˈɪnstrəmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a policy tool that changes prices, incentives, rights, or tradable obligations so actors adjust behavior through market choices.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** market-based instrument dùng price/incentive/right để actor tự điều chỉnh qua market.
**Grammar & collocations:** `market-based-instrument design` — design; `price an externality` — price; `trade an allowance` — trade.
**Examples:** `The carbon fee was a market-based instrument intended to change energy choices.` → Carbon fee là instrument dựa market.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `시장 기반 수단` — market-based instrument.

## 5. outcome performance standard /ˈaʊtˌkʌm pərˈfɔrməns ˈstændərd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a rule specifying the result or level of performance required while leaving actors flexibility about how to achieve it.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** outcome performance standard đặt result cần đạt, không ép technology cụ thể.
**Grammar & collocations:** `outcome-performance standard` — standard; `meet a performance level` — meet; `flexible compliance path` — path.
**Examples:** `The outcome performance standard limited emissions but allowed firms to choose their equipment.` → Standard giới hạn emission nhưng cho chọn equipment.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `성과 기준` — outcome performance standard.

## 6. technology standard /tɛkˈnɑlədʒi ˈstændərd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a rule requiring use of a specified technology, process, or technical specification.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** technology standard bắt dùng technology/process/technical spec cụ thể.
**Grammar & collocations:** `technology-standard requirement` — requirement; `specify a device` — specify; `technical compliance` — compliance.
**Examples:** `The technology standard required every facility to use the same leak detector.` → Standard bắt cùng leak detector.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `기술 기준` — technology standard.

## 7. disclosure mandate /dɪˈskloʊʒər ˈmændeɪt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a requirement to reveal specified information so that people, markets, or authorities can evaluate risk or conduct.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** disclosure mandate bắt reveal information để người khác evaluate risk/behavior.
**Grammar & collocations:** `disclosure-mandate rule` — rule; `mandate disclosure` — mandate; `public reporting` — reporting.
**Examples:** `The disclosure mandate required lenders to publish approval rates by neighborhood.` → Lender phải publish rate theo neighborhood.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `공시 의무` — disclosure mandate.

## 8. expiry clause /ɪkˈspaɪəri klɔz/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a provision that ends a rule, authorization, or program at a specified time unless it is renewed.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** expiry clause làm policy tự hết hiệu lực nếu không renew.
**Grammar & collocations:** `expiry-clause review` — review; `expire automatically` — expire; `renew an authorization` — renew.
**Examples:** `The emergency order included an expiry clause and a required evidence review.` → Emergency order có expiry và review.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `만료 조항` — expiry clause.

## 9. review clause /rɪˈvjuː klɔz/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a provision requiring a policy, rule, or program to be reassessed at a specified time or after specified evidence.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** review clause buộc policy quay lại evidence sau một time/trigger.
**Grammar & collocations:** `review-clause trigger` — trigger; `reassess a rule` — reassess; `scheduled review` — review.
**Examples:** `The review clause required reassessment after two years of implementation data.` → Clause yêu cầu reassess sau hai năm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `검토 조항` — review clause.

## 10. adaptive regulation /əˈdæptɪv ˌrɛɡjəˈleɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** regulation designed to learn from monitoring and revise requirements as evidence, technology, or conditions change.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** adaptive regulation học từ monitoring và đổi rule theo evidence/technology/condition.
**Grammar & collocations:** `adaptive-regulation framework` — framework; `revise a requirement` — revise; `learning-based governance` — governance.
**Examples:** `Adaptive regulation tightened the standard when monitoring showed unexpected exposure.` → Regulation tighten khi exposure unexpected.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `적응적 규제` — adaptive regulation.

## 11. policy feedback /ˈpɑləsi ˈfiːdbæk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a process in which an existing policy changes resources, interests, expectations, or institutions that then reshape later policy choices.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** policy feedback làm policy hiện tại đổi actor/incentive/institution, rồi ảnh hưởng policy sau.
**Grammar & collocations:** `policy-feedback mechanism` — mechanism; `reshape political incentives` — reshape; `feedback effect` — effect.
**Examples:** `The subsidy created policy feedback by building a powerful industry constituency.` → Subsidy tạo constituency mạnh.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `정책 피드백` — policy feedback.

## 12. delivery fidelity /dɪˈlɪvəri ˈfɪdəˌlɪti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the degree to which an intervention or program is implemented as its design specifies.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** delivery fidelity hỏi frontline có deliver program đúng design không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `delivery-fidelity measure` — measure; `implement a protocol faithfully` — implement; `program deviation` — deviation.
**Examples:** `Low delivery fidelity explained why the successful pilot produced weak results elsewhere.` → Deliver lệch làm result yếu.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `시행 충실도` — delivery fidelity.

## 13. frontline discretion /ˈfrʌntˌlaɪn dɪˈskrɛʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** judgment exercised by staff who apply a policy directly to individual cases under real-world constraints.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** frontline discretion là judgment của worker ở điểm tiếp xúc policy và case thật.
**Grammar & collocations:** `frontline-discretion study` — study; `exercise judgment` — exercise; `case-by-case decision` — decision.
**Examples:** `Frontline discretion helped staff adapt the rule to families facing an unusual emergency.` → Staff adapt rule cho case đặc biệt.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `일선 재량` — frontline discretion.

## 14. procedural burden /prəˈsiːdʒərəl ˈbɜrdən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** time, effort, paperwork, or complexity imposed by the steps required to obtain a service or comply with a rule.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** procedural burden là cost thủ tục mà user phải chịu để access service/compliance.
**Grammar & collocations:** `procedural-burden assessment` — assessment; `complete paperwork` — complete; `access cost` — cost.
**Examples:** `Procedural burden discouraged eligible households from applying for assistance.` → Burden làm household eligible không apply.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `절차적 부담` — procedural burden.

## 15. compliance burden /kəmˈplaɪəns ˈbɜrdən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the resources and effort organizations must spend to satisfy a rule's reporting, technical, monitoring, or documentation requirements.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** compliance burden là cost của organization khi meet rule/report/monitor/document.
**Grammar & collocations:** `compliance-burden estimate` — estimate; `meet a requirement` — meet; `reporting cost` — cost.
**Examples:** `The small supplier faced a compliance burden designed for much larger firms.` → Supplier nhỏ chịu burden của firm lớn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `준수 부담` — compliance burden.

## 16. policy response behavior /ˈpɑləsi rɪˈspɑns bɪˈheɪvjər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the ways people, firms, or institutions change their actions in response to a policy's incentives, restrictions, or signals.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** policy response behavior là behavior actor đổi sau policy, gồm intended và unexpected response.
**Grammar & collocations:** `policy-response behavior` — behavior; `respond to an incentive` — respond; `behavioral adaptation` — adaptation.
**Examples:** `Policy response behavior shifted when firms discovered a cheaper way to meet the reporting rule.` → Firm đổi behavior khi tìm cách rẻ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `정책 대응 행동` — policy response behavior.

## 17. policy leakage /ˈpɑləsi ˈliːkɪdʒ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** loss of a policy's intended effect because benefits, restrictions, emissions, activity, or behavior shift outside its targeted boundary.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** policy leakage làm effect “rò” ra ngoài boundary hoặc chuyển sang nơi khác.
**Grammar & collocations:** `policy-leakage estimate` — estimate; `leak across a boundary` — leak; `displacement effect` — effect.
**Examples:** `The emissions rule suffered policy leakage when production moved to an unregulated region.` → Production chuyển vùng làm effect leak.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `정책 누출` — policy leakage.

## 18. compensatory demand effect /kəmˈpɛnsəˌtɔri dɪˈmænd ɪˈfɛkt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an increase in use or consumption that offsets part of the savings or reduction produced by an efficiency improvement.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** compensatory demand effect làm efficiency saving bị bù lại vì dùng nhiều hơn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `compensatory-demand effect` — effect; `offset an efficiency gain` — offset; `increase consumption` — increase.
**Examples:** `The insulation program showed a compensatory demand effect as households heated more rooms.` → Household heat nhiều phòng hơn, bù saving.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `보상적 수요 효과` — compensatory demand effect.

## 19. incentive-induced risk /ɪnˈsɛntɪv ɪnˈduːst rɪsk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** risk created or increased when an incentive encourages behavior that shifts costs or dangers elsewhere.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** incentive-induced risk xuất hiện khi reward khuyến khích behavior nguy hiểm hoặc chuyển cost.
**Grammar & collocations:** `incentive-induced-risk analysis` — analysis; `shift risk to others` — shift; `perverse incentive` — incentive.
**Examples:** `The bonus created incentive-induced risk by rewarding speed without tracking defects.` → Bonus reward speed nhưng bỏ defect.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `인센티브 유발 위험` — incentive-induced risk.

## 20. hidden-quality sorting /ˈhɪdən ˈkwɑləti ˈsɔrtɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** sorting among participants or products according to unobserved quality or information that one side cannot see.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** hidden-quality sorting là selection theo quality ẩn, làm market/group composition lệch.
**Grammar & collocations:** `hidden-quality-sorting mechanism` — mechanism; `sort by unobserved quality` — sort; `information asymmetry` — asymmetry.
**Examples:** `The subsidy attracted low-quality contractors through hidden-quality sorting.` → Subsidy attract contractor quality thấp vì information asymmetry.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `숨은 품질 선별` — hidden-quality sorting.

## 21. spillover consequence /ˈspɪlˌoʊvər ˈkɑnsəˌkwɛns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an effect of a policy or intervention that falls on people, places, or systems outside its intended target.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** spillover consequence là consequence ngoài target ban đầu.
**Grammar & collocations:** `spillover-consequence analysis` — analysis; `affect a neighboring group` — affect; `unintended effect` — effect.
**Examples:** `The traffic restriction had a spillover consequence for small businesses on the adjacent street.` → Restriction ảnh hưởng business phố bên.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `파급 결과` — spillover consequence.

## 22. distributional incidence /ˌdɪstrəˈbjuːʃənəl ˈɪnsɪdəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** how the costs, benefits, or burdens of a policy are allocated across groups, regions, or income levels.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** distributional incidence map ai chịu cost/nhận benefit theo group/income/region.
**Grammar & collocations:** `distributional-incidence analysis` — analysis; `bear a burden` — bear; `benefit distribution` — distribution.
**Examples:** `The distributional incidence showed that the fee burden fell most heavily on renters.` → Burden fee rơi nặng lên renter.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `분배적 귀착` — distributional incidence.

## 23. policy counterfactual /ˈpɑləsi ˌkaʊntərˈfæktʃuəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the outcome that would be expected under a specified alternative policy or absence of the policy.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** policy counterfactual là outcome nếu policy khác hoặc không có policy.
**Grammar & collocations:** `policy-counterfactual estimate` — estimate; `compare policy paths` — compare; `alternative policy` — policy.
**Examples:** `The evaluation compared observed outcomes with a policy counterfactual based on untreated districts.` → Evaluation compare observed với district untreated.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `정책 반사실` — policy counterfactual.

## 24. treated-unit effect /ˈtriːtɪd ˈjuːnɪt ɪˈfɛkt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the causal effect of an intervention for the units that actually received it, under a defined outcome and time period.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** treated-unit effect hỏi effect thật trên unit đã nhận treatment.
**Grammar & collocations:** `treated-unit-effect estimate` — estimate; `estimate an effect for treated units` — estimate; `received intervention` — intervention.
**Examples:** `The treated-unit effect was larger than the population-wide effect because early adopters were especially exposed.` → Effect ở treated unit lớn hơn population-wide.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `처치 단위 효과` — treated-unit effect.

## 25. policy externality /ˈpɑləsi ˌɛkstərˈnæləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a cost or benefit imposed by a policy-induced action on people who were not part of the policy transaction.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** policy externality là cost/benefit policy đẩy sang người ngoài transaction.
**Grammar & collocations:** `policy-externality estimate` — estimate; `impose an external cost` — impose; `affected outsider` — outsider.
**Examples:** `The road subsidy created a policy externality through noise in nearby neighborhoods.` → Road subsidy tạo noise externality.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `정책 외부성` — policy externality.

## Review in context

A **policy instrument** can be a **regulatory instrument**, **command-and-control** rule, or **market-based instrument**. An **outcome performance standard** leaves implementation flexible, while a **technology standard** prescribes a device. A **disclosure mandate**, **expiry clause**, and **review clause** support **adaptive regulation**. **Policy feedback** changes later choices; **delivery fidelity**, **frontline discretion**, **procedural burden**, and **compliance burden** shape implementation. **Policy response behavior** may create **policy leakage**, a **compensatory demand effect**, or **incentive-induced risk**. **Hidden-quality sorting** and a **spillover consequence** can alter **distributional incidence**. Evaluation compares a **policy counterfactual**, estimates a **treated-unit effect**, and tracks a **policy externality**.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

**Policy instrument** có thể là **regulatory instrument**, rule **command-and-control** hoặc **market-based instrument**. **Outcome performance standard** để implementation linh hoạt, còn **technology standard** bắt device cụ thể. **Disclosure mandate**, **expiry clause** và **review clause** hỗ trợ **adaptive regulation**. **Policy feedback** làm choice sau đổi; **delivery fidelity**, **frontline discretion**, **procedural burden** và **compliance burden** định hình implementation. **Policy response behavior** có thể tạo **policy leakage**, **compensatory demand effect** hoặc **incentive-induced risk**. **Hidden-quality sorting** và **spillover consequence** đổi **distributional incidence**. Evaluation compare **policy counterfactual**, estimate **treated-unit effect** và theo dõi **policy externality**.
