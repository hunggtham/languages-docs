# Writing: Why I study English

Target: 120–150 words.

## Questions to answer

- Why is English important in your current life?
- How can it help your work or future?
- What is currently difficult?
- What is your three-month goal?

## Suggested structure

1. Main reason for studying English.
2. Work/life example.
3. Current difficulty and short-term plan.

## Useful sentence starters

- The main reason I study English is …
- English is useful for me because …
- For example, I often need to …
- At the moment, I find … difficult.
- Over the next three months, I plan to …
