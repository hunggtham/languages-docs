# C2 Vocabulary — Mining and mineral-processing robotics

This lesson follows a mine from extraction and geotechnical inspection to crushing, sampling, tailings, and rescue. The network is **ore crushing inspection robot → mineral sorting robot → conveyor belt inspection robot → mine ventilation robot → underground mapping robot → blast-hole drilling robot → rockfall monitoring drone → tailings dam inspection robot → ore sampling robot → flotation cell robot → smelter furnace inspection robot → rail haulage robot → dust suppression robot → acid mine drainage robot → mine rescue robot → geotechnical borehole robot → pit wall survey drone → autonomous mine haulage truck → ore stockpile mapping drone → mine water pumping robot**.

## 1. ore crushing inspection robot /ɔːr ˈkrʌʃɪŋ ɪnˈspekʃən ˈroʊbɑːt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a robot that inspects crushers, chutes, and feeds used to break mined ore.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** robot inspect crusher, chute và feed dùng break mined ore.
**Grammar & collocations:** `ore-crushing-inspection-robot camera` — camera; `inspect a worn liner` — inspect liner mòn.
**Examples:** `The ore crushing inspection robot found a worn liner inside the chute.` → Robot inspection ore crushing tìm liner mòn trong chute.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `광석 분쇄 검사 로봇`.

## Review in context

An **ore crushing inspection robot**, **mineral sorting robot**, and **conveyor belt inspection robot** support processing, while a **mine ventilation robot**, **underground mapping robot**, and **blast-hole drilling robot** work below ground. A **rockfall monitoring drone**, **tailings dam inspection robot**, **ore sampling robot**, and **flotation cell robot** check hazards and quality. A **smelter furnace inspection robot**, **rail haulage robot**, **dust suppression robot**, and **acid mine drainage robot** connect extraction with environmental control. A **mine rescue robot**, **geotechnical borehole robot**, **pit wall survey drone**, **autonomous mine haulage truck**, **ore stockpile mapping drone**, and **mine water pumping robot** support safety, logistics, and continuity.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:** **Ore crushing inspection robot**, **mineral sorting robot** và **conveyor belt inspection robot** hỗ trợ processing, còn **mine ventilation robot**, **underground mapping robot** và **blast-hole drilling robot** làm việc dưới ground. **Rockfall monitoring drone**, **tailings dam inspection robot**, **ore sampling robot** và **flotation cell robot** check hazard/quality. **Smelter furnace inspection robot**, **rail haulage robot**, **dust suppression robot** và **acid mine drainage robot** nối extraction với environmental control. **Mine rescue robot**, **geotechnical borehole robot**, **pit wall survey drone**, **autonomous mine haulage truck**, **ore stockpile mapping drone** và **mine water pumping robot** hỗ trợ safety, logistics với continuity.
