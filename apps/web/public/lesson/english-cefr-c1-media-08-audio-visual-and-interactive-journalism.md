# C1 Vocabulary 08 — Audio, visual, and interactive journalism

This lesson follows how reporters combine sound, imagery, maps, automation, and interactive design to tell complex stories.

Flow: **audio documentary → narrative podcast → sound design → ambient sound → voice-over → transcript sync → interactive graphic → scrollytelling → geospatial layer → annotation layer → immersive journalism → 360-degree video → computational journalism → automated transcription → synthetic media → provenance marker → content warning → interactive journalism → audio vignette → visual narrative**.

## 1. audio documentary /ˈɔdioʊ ˌdɑkjəˈmɛntəri/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a nonfiction audio work that uses reporting, interviews, narration, and sound to develop a sustained story.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** tác phẩm nonfiction audio dùng reporting, interview, narration và sound để develop story dài.
**Grammar & collocations:** `audio-documentary series` — series; `produce an audio documentary` — sản xuất.
**Examples:** `The audio documentary followed one coastal family through a year of changing fishing rules.` → Documentary audio theo một family ven biển qua một năm rule fishing đổi.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `오디오 다큐멘터리`.

## 2. narrative podcast /ˈnærətɪv ˈpɑdˌkæst/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a podcast organized around a developing story rather than a simple sequence of separate commentary segments.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** podcast tổ chức quanh story phát triển thay vì các segment commentary rời.
**Grammar & collocations:** `narrative-podcast episode` — episode; `listen to a narrative podcast` — nghe.
**Examples:** `The narrative podcast returned to the same witness as new evidence changed the investigation.` → Podcast narrative quay lại witness khi evidence mới đổi investigation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `내러티브 팟캐스트`.

## 3. sound design /saʊnd dɪˈzaɪn/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the deliberate selection, arrangement, and mixing of sound to shape a listener's understanding and emotional response.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** chọn, sắp xếp và mix sound có chủ ý để định hình understanding/emotion listener.
**Grammar & collocations:** `sound-design choice` — choice; `refine sound design` — tinh chỉnh.
**Examples:** `The sound design used silence before the interviewee described the loss.` → Design sound dùng silence trước khi interviewee mô tả loss.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `사운드 디자인`.

## 4. ambient sound /ˈæmbiənt saʊnd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** background sound from the recorded environment that helps establish place, activity, or atmosphere.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** sound nền từ environment record giúp establish place, activity hoặc atmosphere.
**Grammar & collocations:** `ambient-sound track` — track; `record ambient sound` — ghi.
**Examples:** `Ambient sound from the market made the interview's location clear without an explanation.` → Sound ambient từ market làm location interview rõ mà không cần explanation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `환경음`.

## 5. voice-over /ˈvɔɪs ˌoʊvər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** spoken narration heard over images, recordings, or other sound rather than produced by the visible speaker.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** narration spoken nghe trên image/recording/sound khác, không phải từ visible speaker.
**Grammar & collocations:** `voice-over narration` — narration; `record a voice-over` — thu.
**Examples:** `The voice-over explained the map while the animation showed the river's route.` → Narration voice-over giải thích map khi animation show route river.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `보이스오버`.

## 6. transcript sync /ˈtrænskrɪpt sɪŋk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the alignment of written words with the exact moments when they are spoken in audio or video.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** align written word với exact moment được nói trong audio/video.
**Grammar & collocations:** `transcript-sync interface` — interface; `check transcript sync` — kiểm tra.
**Examples:** `Transcript sync let readers jump directly to the sentence that mentioned the missing records.` → Sync transcript cho reader jump thẳng đến sentence nói về record missing.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `대본 동기화`.

## 7. interactive graphic /ˌɪntərˈæktɪv ˈɡræfɪk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a visual explanation that lets users change a view, select data, or explore relationships themselves.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** visual explanation cho user đổi view, select data hoặc explore relationship.
**Grammar & collocations:** `interactive-graphic control` — control; `build an interactive graphic` — xây.
**Examples:** `The interactive graphic let readers compare rent and income by neighborhood.` → Graphic interactive cho reader so rent và income theo neighborhood.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `인터랙티브 그래픽`.

## 8. scrollytelling /ˈskroʊliˌtɛlɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a digital storytelling format in which scrolling changes the text, images, data, or animation on the screen.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** format storytelling digital mà scroll đổi text, image, data hoặc animation.
**Grammar & collocations:** `scrollytelling feature` — feature; `design a scrollytelling story` — thiết kế.
**Examples:** `Scrollytelling revealed the forest loss step by step as readers moved down the page.` → Scrollytelling reveal forest loss từng bước khi reader scroll.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `스크롤텔링`.

## 9. geospatial layer /ˌdʒioʊˈspæʃəl ˈleɪər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a set of geographic data displayed over a map so users can compare locations or patterns.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** set geographic data display trên map để so location/pattern.
**Grammar & collocations:** `geospatial-layer control` — control; `toggle a geospatial layer` — bật/tắt.
**Examples:** `The geospatial layer showed which schools were within the flood zone.` → Layer geospatial cho thấy school nào trong flood zone.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `지리 공간 레이어`.

## 10. annotation layer /ˌænəˈteɪʃən ˈleɪər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** added labels, notes, or highlights that explain features in an image, map, chart, or document.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** label, note hoặc highlight thêm để explain feature image/map/chart/document.
**Grammar & collocations:** `annotation-layer toggle` — toggle; `add an annotation layer` — thêm.
**Examples:** `The annotation layer marked the dates when each section of the bridge was repaired.` → Layer annotation mark date từng section bridge được repair.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `주석 레이어`.

## 11. immersive journalism /ɪˈmɝsɪv ˈdʒɝnəˌlɪzəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** journalism designed to give audiences a strong sense of being present inside an event or environment.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** journalism design để audience cảm giác đang hiện diện trong event/environment.
**Grammar & collocations:** `immersive-journalism project` — project; `create immersive journalism` — tạo.
**Examples:** `Immersive journalism placed the audience inside the evacuation route without staging new events.` → Journalism immersive đặt audience vào route sơ tán mà không stage event mới.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `몰입형 저널리즘`.

## 12. 360-degree video /ˌθriː ˈsɪksti dɪˈɡriː ˈvɪdioʊ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** video captured in all directions so viewers can choose where to look around a scene.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** video capture mọi hướng để viewer chọn nhìn quanh scene.
**Grammar & collocations:** `360-degree-video player` — player; `record 360-degree video` — record.
**Examples:** `The 360-degree video let viewers inspect the damaged theater from the center aisle.` → Video 360 độ cho viewer inspect theater hỏng từ aisle trung tâm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `360도 동영상`.

## 13. computational journalism /ˌkɑmpjəˈteɪʃənəl ˈdʒɝnəˌlɪzəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** journalism that uses code, algorithms, databases, or automated analysis to investigate and explain public information.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** journalism dùng code, algorithm, database hoặc automated analysis để investigate/explain public information.
**Grammar & collocations:** `computational-journalism method` — method; `practice computational journalism` — làm.
**Examples:** `Computational journalism found a pattern in thousands of inspection records that manual reading had missed.` → Journalism computational tìm pattern trong hàng nghìn record inspection mà đọc tay bỏ sót.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `계산 저널리즘`.

## 14. automated transcription /ˈɔtəˌmeɪtəd trænˈskrɪpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** software-generated conversion of speech into written text.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** software tự chuyển speech thành written text.
**Grammar & collocations:** `automated-transcription draft` — draft; `review automated transcription` — review.
**Examples:** `The reporter reviewed the automated transcription because names and technical terms were uncertain.` → Reporter review transcription tự động vì name và technical term uncertain.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `자동 음성 전사`.

## 15. synthetic media /sɪnˈθɛtɪk ˈmiːdiə/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** audio, images, video, or text generated or substantially altered by computational systems.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** audio/image/video/text được generate hoặc alter đáng kể bởi computational system.
**Grammar & collocations:** `synthetic-media disclosure` — disclosure; `label synthetic media` — label.
**Examples:** `The newsroom labeled the synthetic media used to illustrate a hypothetical scene.` → Newsroom label media tổng hợp dùng minh họa scene hypothetical.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `합성 미디어`.

## 16. provenance marker /ˈprɑvənəns ˈmɑrkər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a visible or machine-readable indicator showing where a digital file came from and how it was changed.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** indicator visible/machine-readable cho biết file digital từ đâu và đổi ra sao.
**Grammar & collocations:** `provenance-marker standard` — standard; `attach a provenance marker` — gắn.
**Examples:** `A provenance marker showed that the image had been cropped but not generated.` → Marker provenance cho thấy image bị crop nhưng không generate.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `출처 표지`.

## 17. content warning /ˈkɑntɛnt ˈwɔrnɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a notice that tells audiences about potentially disturbing, graphic, or emotionally difficult material before they encounter it.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** notice báo trước material disturbing, graphic hoặc emotionally difficult.
**Grammar & collocations:** `content-warning label` — label; `add a content warning` — thêm.
**Examples:** `The content warning allowed listeners to decide whether they were ready for the survivor's account.` → Warning content cho listener quyết định đã sẵn sàng nghe account survivor chưa.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `콘텐츠 경고`.

## 18. interactive journalism /ˌɪntərˈæktɪv ˈdʒɝnəˌlɪzəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** journalism that lets audiences explore evidence, choices, or viewpoints through direct interaction with the presentation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** journalism cho audience explore evidence, choice hoặc viewpoint bằng interaction trực tiếp.
**Grammar & collocations:** `interactive-journalism project` — project; `design interactive journalism` — thiết kế.
**Examples:** `Interactive journalism let readers test how different assumptions changed the housing projection.` → Journalism interactive cho reader test assumption khác đổi housing projection thế nào.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `인터랙티브 저널리즘`.

## 19. audio vignette /ˈɔdioʊ vɪnˈjɛt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a short audio scene or portrait that conveys a focused moment, voice, or atmosphere.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** audio scene/portrait ngắn truyền một moment, voice hoặc atmosphere tập trung.
**Grammar & collocations:** `audio-vignette series` — series; `record an audio vignette` — thu.
**Examples:** `The audio vignette captured a bus driver's description of the first morning after the strike.` → Vignette audio ghi lời bus driver mô tả sáng đầu sau strike.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `오디오 비네트`.

## 20. visual narrative /ˈvɪʒuəl ˈnærətɪv/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a story whose sequence and meaning are carried primarily by photographs, video, graphics, or visual arrangement.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** story mà sequence/meaning chủ yếu do photo, video, graphic hoặc visual arrangement truyền.
**Grammar & collocations:** `visual-narrative structure` — structure; `build a visual narrative` — xây.
**Examples:** `The visual narrative followed the river from its source to the neighborhoods it supplied.` → Narrative visual theo river từ source đến neighborhood nó cung cấp.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `시각적 서사`.

## Review in context

An **audio documentary** or **narrative podcast** depends on **sound design**, **ambient sound**, **voice-over**, and **transcript sync**. An **interactive graphic**, **scrollytelling**, a **geospatial layer**, and an **annotation layer** support **immersive journalism** and a **360-degree video**. **Computational journalism** may use **automated transcription**, while **synthetic media** should carry a **provenance marker** and a **content warning** when appropriate. **Interactive journalism**, an **audio vignette**, and a **visual narrative** give audiences different ways to understand evidence.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

**Audio documentary** hoặc **narrative podcast** dựa vào **sound design**, **ambient sound**, **voice-over** và **transcript sync**. **Interactive graphic**, **scrollytelling**, **geospatial layer** và **annotation layer** hỗ trợ **immersive journalism** cùng **360-degree video**. **Computational journalism** có thể dùng **automated transcription**, còn **synthetic media** nên có **provenance marker** và **content warning** khi phù hợp. **Interactive journalism**, **audio vignette** và **visual narrative** cho audience nhiều cách hiểu evidence khác nhau.
