# C2 Vocabulary — Aquarium and aquatic-conservation robotics

This lesson follows aquarium operations from exhibit care and water testing to public education and coral restoration. The network is **aquarium cleaning robot → coral reef monitoring robot → fish tank feeding robot → aquatic exhibit guide robot → water chemistry sampling robot → jellyfish tank circulation robot → shark habitat inspection drone → marine life imaging robot → aquarium quarantine robot → touch pool sanitation robot → aquatic educator robot → visitor flow aquarium robot → coral restoration nursery robot**.

## 1. aquarium cleaning robot /əˈkweriəm ˈkliːnɪŋ ˈroʊbɑːt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a robot that cleans aquarium glass, floors, and exhibit surfaces.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** robot clean glass, floor và surface của aquarium.
**Grammar & collocations:** `aquarium-cleaning-robot brush` — brush; `remove algae from glass` — remove algae.
**Examples:** `The aquarium cleaning robot removed algae from the glass.` → Robot vệ sinh aquarium loại algae khỏi glass.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `수족관 청소 로봇`.
