# C2+ Vocabulary — Disaster-risk governance and emergency resilience

This lesson follows how institutions prepare for hazards, protect essential functions, finance recovery, and learn after emergencies. The network is **risk reduction → hazard mapping → early warning system → evacuation trigger → shelter capacity → evacuation bottleneck → last-mile communication → community resilience → social vulnerability → cascading hazard → compound event → systemic risk → critical infrastructure interdependence → recovery window → build-back-better → resilient reconstruction → risk transfer → parametric insurance → contingent credit → catastrophe bond → emergency procurement → mutual aid agreement → protected relief corridor → operational debrief → lessons-learned register**.

## 1. risk reduction /rɪsk rɪˈdʌkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** actions that lower the likelihood, exposure, severity, or consequences of a hazard before it causes harm.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** risk reduction giảm probability/exposure/severity/consequence trước hazard.
**Grammar & collocations:** `risk-reduction measure` — measure; `reduce exposure` — reduce; `prevent avoidable harm` — prevent.
**Examples:** `Risk reduction combined drainage upgrades with household evacuation training.` → Risk reduction gồm drainage và training.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `위험 감소` — risk reduction.

## 2. hazard mapping /ˈhæzərd ˈmæpɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** systematic representation of where a hazard may occur, how intense it may be, and which areas it may affect.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** hazard mapping map location/intensity/affected area của hazard.
**Grammar & collocations:** `hazard-mapping product` — product; `map an inundation zone` — map; `hazard intensity` — intensity.
**Examples:** `Hazard mapping showed that the proposed clinic sat inside a landslide corridor.` → Map cho thấy clinic nằm trong corridor.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `위험 지도화` — hazard mapping.

## 3. early warning system /ˈɜrli ˈwɔrnɪŋ ˈsɪstəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an arrangement that detects danger, forecasts its development, communicates warnings, and supports timely protective action.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** early warning system nối detect → forecast → communicate → act.
**Grammar & collocations:** `early-warning-system upgrade` — upgrade; `issue a warning` — issue; `protective action` — action.
**Examples:** `The early warning system failed at the communication stage even though the sensor worked.` → Sensor work nhưng communication fail.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `조기 경보 시스템` — early warning system.

## 4. evacuation trigger /ɪˌvækjuˈeɪʃən ˈtrɪɡər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a predefined observation or threshold that activates an evacuation decision or protocol.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** evacuation trigger là signal/threshold đã định để activate evacuation.
**Grammar & collocations:** `evacuation-trigger threshold` — threshold; `activate an evacuation` — activate; `warning level` — level.
**Examples:** `The river gauge reached the evacuation trigger two hours before the crest.` → Gauge đạt trigger trước crest hai giờ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `대피 발동 기준` — evacuation trigger.

## 5. shelter capacity /ˈʃɛltər kəˈpæsəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the number of people a shelter can safely accommodate with adequate space, supplies, sanitation, and services.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** shelter capacity không chỉ là số giường; còn space, food, sanitation và service.
**Grammar & collocations:** `shelter-capacity estimate` — estimate; `accommodate evacuees` — accommodate; `safe occupancy` — occupancy.
**Examples:** `Shelter capacity fell when one building lost water and accessible toilets.` → Capacity giảm khi mất water/toilet.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `대피소 수용 능력` — shelter capacity.

## 6. evacuation bottleneck /ɪˌvækjuˈeɪʃən ˈbɑtlˌnɛk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a point, route, service, or decision that limits the speed or scale of evacuation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** evacuation bottleneck là chỗ làm dòng evacuation chậm: bridge, bus, information hoặc decision.
**Grammar & collocations:** `evacuation-bottleneck analysis` — analysis; `clear a route` — clear; `traffic constraint` — constraint.
**Examples:** `A single bridge became the evacuation bottleneck when two districts left at once.` → Một bridge làm bottleneck.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `대피 병목` — evacuation bottleneck.

## 7. last-mile communication /læst maɪl kəˌmjuːnəˈkeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** delivery of a warning or instruction to the people who must act, especially those least reached by central communication channels.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** last-mile communication đưa warning tới người cuối cùng cần act, kể cả group khó reach.
**Grammar & collocations:** `last-mile-communication plan` — plan; `reach an isolated household` — reach; `warning delivery` — delivery.
**Examples:** `Last-mile communication required door-to-door volunteers after the cellular network failed.` → Cần volunteer door-to-door.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `라스트마일 소통` — last-mile communication.

## 8. community resilience /kəˈmjuːnəti rɪˈzɪliəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a community's capacity to anticipate, withstand, recover from, and adapt to disruptive events.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** community resilience gồm prepare, withstand, recover và adapt.
**Grammar & collocations:** `community-resilience strategy` — strategy; `recover from disruption` — recover; `local capacity` — capacity.
**Examples:** `Community resilience improved when local clinics shared backup generators and staff.` → Clinic share generator/staff.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `지역사회 회복탄력성` — community resilience.

## 9. social vulnerability /ˈsoʊʃəl ˌvʌlnərəˈbɪləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** susceptibility to harm shaped by social conditions such as income, health, housing, discrimination, access, and political power.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** social vulnerability do condition xã hội shape, không chỉ do hazard intensity.
**Grammar & collocations:** `social-vulnerability index` — index; `assess susceptibility` — assess; `unequal exposure` — exposure.
**Examples:** `The social-vulnerability map highlighted renters without transport during evacuation.` → Map highlight renter thiếu transport.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `사회적 취약성` — social vulnerability.

## 10. cascading hazard /kæˈskeɪdɪŋ ˈhæzərd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a hazard that triggers or intensifies further hazards through linked physical, social, or infrastructural processes.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cascading hazard lan từ hazard này sang hazard khác qua infrastructure/social link.
**Grammar & collocations:** `cascading-hazard scenario` — scenario; `trigger a secondary hazard` — trigger; `linked failure` — failure.
**Examples:** `The earthquake created a cascading hazard of landslides, fires, and water contamination.` → Earthquake kéo theo nhiều hazard.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `연쇄 재난` — cascading hazard.

## 11. compound event /ˈkɑmpaʊnd ɪˈvɛnt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an event involving multiple hazards or drivers whose combination produces greater or different impacts than either alone.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** compound event là nhiều hazard/driver cùng lúc, tạo impact không linear.
**Grammar & collocations:** `compound-event analysis` — analysis; `combine hazards` — combine; `joint impact` — impact.
**Examples:** `The compound event combined extreme heat, drought, and a power outage.` → Event gồm heat/drought/outage.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `복합 사건` — compound event.

## 12. systemic risk /sɪˈstɛmɪk rɪsk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** risk that spreads through interdependent components and threatens the functioning of an entire system rather than one isolated unit.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** systemic risk lan qua interdependence và đe dọa whole system.
**Grammar & collocations:** `systemic-risk assessment` — assessment; `spread through interdependence` — spread; `system-wide failure` — failure.
**Examples:** `The outage became systemic risk when hospitals, payment systems, and transport shared the same dependency.` → Shared dependency làm outage systemic.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `시스템적 위험` — systemic risk.

## 13. critical infrastructure interdependence /ˈkrɪtɪkəl ˈɪnfrəˌstrʌktʃər ˌɪntərdɪˈpɛndəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** mutual dependence among infrastructure systems whose failures can transmit across services.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** critical infrastructure interdependence làm water/power/telecom/health cùng phụ thuộc.
**Grammar & collocations:** `critical-infrastructure-interdependence map` — map; `model cross-service dependence` — model; `shared dependency` — dependency.
**Examples:** `Critical infrastructure interdependence made the hospital's backup plan dependent on fuel logistics.` → Hospital phụ thuộc fuel logistics.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `핵심 인프라 상호의존성` — critical infrastructure interdependence.

## 14. recovery window /rɪˈkʌvəri ˈwɪndoʊ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a limited period during which action can restore a function, prevent further damage, or influence long-term recovery.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** recovery window là khoảng thời gian nếu act đúng sẽ restore/prevent damage tốt hơn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `recovery-window estimate` — estimate; `act within a window` — act; `time-sensitive recovery` — recovery.
**Examples:** `The recovery window for vaccine refrigeration closed when fuel deliveries were delayed.` → Delay làm mất window.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `복구 시간 창` — recovery window.

## 15. build-back-better /bɪld bæk ˈbɛtər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** reconstruction that reduces future risk and improves resilience rather than merely restoring the pre-disaster condition.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** build-back-better rebuild tốt hơn trước, giảm future risk.
**Grammar & collocations:** `build-back-better program` — program; `rebuild more safely` — rebuild; `pre-disaster baseline` — baseline.
**Examples:** `The build-back-better program raised homes above the new flood standard.` → Rebuild nâng nhà theo standard mới.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `더 나은 재건` — build-back-better.

## 16. resilient reconstruction /rɪˈzɪliənt ˌriːkənˈstrʌkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** rebuilding that incorporates durability, redundancy, accessibility, and the ability to recover from future shocks.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** resilient reconstruction rebuild với durability/redundancy/accessibility và future shock.
**Grammar & collocations:** `resilient-reconstruction standard` — standard; `reconstruct infrastructure` — reconstruct; `future shock` — shock.
**Examples:** `Resilient reconstruction replaced the single road with two all-weather access routes.` → Rebuild thêm two access routes.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `회복탄력적 재건` — resilient reconstruction.

## 17. risk transfer /rɪsk ˈtrænsfər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** arrangement that shifts the financial or contractual consequences of a risk from one party to another.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** risk transfer chuyển financial consequence qua insurance/contract/market, không làm hazard biến mất.
**Grammar & collocations:** `risk-transfer mechanism` — mechanism; `transfer financial exposure` — transfer; `contractual protection` — protection.
**Examples:** `Risk transfer protected the municipality from the cost of a severe storm, but not from disruption itself.` → Transfer chỉ bảo vệ cost.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `위험 이전` — risk transfer.

## 18. parametric insurance /ˌpærəˈmɛtrɪk ɪnˈʃʊrəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** insurance that pays when a measurable index crosses a predefined threshold rather than after a full loss assessment.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** parametric insurance pay theo index/threshold như wind speed, rainfall, không cần assess từng loss.
**Grammar & collocations:** `parametric-insurance trigger` — trigger; `pay on an index` — pay; `threshold event` — event.
**Examples:** `Parametric insurance paid farmers when rainfall stayed below the agreed threshold.` → Insurance pay khi rainfall thấp.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `지수형 보험` — parametric insurance.

## 19. contingent credit /kənˈtɪndʒənt ˈkrɛdɪt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** financing that becomes available after a specified emergency, trigger, or disaster declaration.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** contingent credit là credit activate khi disaster/trigger xảy ra.
**Grammar & collocations:** `contingent-credit line` — line; `draw emergency funds` — draw; `trigger condition` — condition.
**Examples:** `Contingent credit gave the city cash before tax revenue recovered.` → City có cash sớm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `조건부 신용` — contingent credit.

## 20. catastrophe bond /kəˈtæstrəfi bɑnd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a financial instrument in which investors provide capital that may be used after a defined catastrophe, accepting loss of principal in exchange for return.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** catastrophe bond chuyển catastrophe risk sang investor theo trigger.
**Grammar & collocations:** `catastrophe-bond issuance` — issuance; `transfer catastrophe risk` — transfer; `trigger payout` — payout.
**Examples:** `The catastrophe bond released funds after the hurricane exceeded the defined wind speed.` → Bond release funds theo wind trigger.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `재난 채권` — catastrophe bond.

## 21. emergency procurement /ɪˈmɜrdʒənsi prəˈkjʊrmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** accelerated acquisition of goods or services during a crisis under special rules and controls.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** emergency procurement mua nhanh trong crisis nhưng vẫn cần transparency/control.
**Grammar & collocations:** `emergency-procurement rule` — rule; `acquire supplies rapidly` — acquire; `emergency contract` — contract.
**Examples:** `Emergency procurement delivered water tanks quickly but required a later price audit.` → Mua nhanh nhưng audit sau.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `긴급 조달` — emergency procurement.

## 22. mutual aid agreement /ˈmjuːtʃuəl eɪd əˈɡriːmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a formal arrangement for jurisdictions, agencies, or organizations to share personnel, equipment, supplies, or services during emergencies.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mutual aid agreement định trước ai hỗ trợ ai bằng người/gear/supply/service.
**Grammar & collocations:** `mutual-aid agreement` — agreement; `share emergency resources` — share; `request assistance` — request.
**Examples:** `The mutual aid agreement sent firefighters and pumps from neighboring counties.` → Counties hỗ trợ firefighter/pump.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `상호 지원 협정` — mutual aid agreement.

## 23. protected relief corridor /prəˈtɛktɪd rɪˈliːf ˈkɔrədɔr/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a safeguarded route through which humanitarian personnel, supplies, or civilians can move during conflict or disaster.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** protected relief corridor là route được bảo vệ để relief/civilian di chuyển.
**Grammar & collocations:** `protected-relief corridor` — corridor; `secure a route` — secure; `humanitarian passage` — passage.
**Examples:** `The protected relief corridor allowed medical supplies to reach the isolated district.` → Corridor đưa medical supply tới district.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `보호된 구호 통로` — protected relief corridor.

## 24. operational debrief /ˌɑpəˈreɪʃənəl diːˈbrɪf/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** structured discussion after an operation to reconstruct what happened, identify decisions, and improve future performance.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** operational debrief xem operation diễn ra sao, decision nào, cải thiện gì.
**Grammar & collocations:** `operational-debrief session` — session; `reconstruct an operation` — reconstruct; `identify a lesson` — identify.
**Examples:** `The operational debrief found that the shelter opened before transport was ready.` → Debrief lộ shelter mở sớm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `작전 사후 브리핑` — operational debrief.

## 25. lessons-learned register /ˈlɛsənz lɜrnd ˈrɛdʒɪstər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a maintained record of findings, decisions, failures, and recommendations intended to improve future operations.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** lessons-learned register biến debrief/failure thành record để lần sau dùng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `lessons-learned register` — register; `record a lesson` — record; `track implementation` — track.
**Examples:** `The lessons-learned register assigned an owner to each emergency improvement.` → Register assign owner cho improvement.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `교훈 등록부` — lessons-learned register.

## Review in context

**Risk reduction** begins with **hazard mapping** and an **early warning system** whose **evacuation trigger** must match **shelter capacity**, **evacuation bottleneck**, and **last-mile communication**. **Community resilience** depends on social conditions, so **social vulnerability** matters when a **cascading hazard**, **compound event**, or **systemic risk** meets **critical infrastructure interdependence**. A **recovery window** supports **build-back-better** and **resilient reconstruction**. Financial tools include **risk transfer**, **parametric insurance**, **contingent credit**, and a **catastrophe bond**. **Emergency procurement**, a **mutual aid agreement**, and a **protected relief corridor** support response. An **operational debrief** feeds a **lessons-learned register**.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

**Risk reduction** bắt đầu bằng **hazard mapping** và **early warning system**, trong đó **evacuation trigger** phải khớp **shelter capacity**, **evacuation bottleneck** và **last-mile communication**. **Community resilience** phụ thuộc social condition, nên **social vulnerability** quan trọng khi **cascading hazard**, **compound event** hoặc **systemic risk** gặp **critical infrastructure interdependence**. **Recovery window** hỗ trợ **build-back-better** và **resilient reconstruction**. Công cụ finance gồm **risk transfer**, **parametric insurance**, **contingent credit** và **catastrophe bond**. **Emergency procurement**, **mutual aid agreement** và **protected relief corridor** hỗ trợ response. **Operational debrief** đưa lesson vào **lessons-learned register**.
