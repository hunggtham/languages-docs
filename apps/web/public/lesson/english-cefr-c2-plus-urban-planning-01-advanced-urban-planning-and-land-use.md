# C2+ Vocabulary — Advanced urban planning and land use

This lesson follows how cities manage growth, mobility, housing supply, environmental burdens, and neighborhood form. The network is **induced demand → complete streets → road diet → form-based code → floor-area ratio → upzoning → downzoning → urban growth boundary → greenbelt policy → infill development → brownfield remediation → land-value capture → housing filtering → gentrification pressure → displacement risk → environmental justice mapping → walkability index → jobs-housing balance → polycentric urban form → urban heat island**.

## 1. induced demand /ɪnˈduːst dɪˈmænd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the tendency for expanding transport capacity to generate additional travel that consumes part or all of the expected congestion relief.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** thêm lane làm road hấp dẫn hơn, người đổi route/time/mode hoặc đi nhiều hơn, nên traffic quay lại.
**Grammar & collocations:** `induced-demand effect` — effect; `induced demand for driving` — demand; `measure induced demand` — measure.
**Examples:** `The highway expansion produced induced demand within a few years.` → Highway expansion tạo induced demand trong vài năm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `유발 수요` — induced demand.

## 2. complete streets /kəmˈpliːt striːts/
**Part of speech:** plural noun
**Core meaning (English):** a street-design approach that plans for safe use by pedestrians, cyclists, transit riders, drivers, and people with disabilities.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** street “đủ cho mọi người”, không chỉ tối ưu cho car.
**Grammar & collocations:** `complete-streets policy` — policy; `design complete streets` — design; `complete-streets standard` — standard.
**Examples:** `The complete streets plan added protected bike lanes and accessible crossings.` → Complete streets plan thêm bike lane bảo vệ và crossing accessible.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `완전한 거리` — complete streets.

## 3. road diet /roʊd ˈdaɪət/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the reallocation or reduction of motor-vehicle lanes to create space for safety, transit, cycling, or public areas.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** “ăn kiêng” cho road: bớt lane car để dành space cho mode khác và giảm speed.
**Grammar & collocations:** `four-to-three-lane road diet` — road diet; `road-diet project` — project; `implement a road diet` — implement.
**Examples:** `The road diet shortened crossing distances and reduced severe crashes.` → Road diet rút ngắn khoảng crossing và giảm crash nghiêm trọng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `도로 다이어트` — road diet.

## 4. form-based code /fɔrm beɪst koʊd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a land-use regulation that focuses on the physical form, frontage, scale, and public-realm relationship of buildings rather than only on separated uses.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** code quan tâm building “trông và đứng thế nào” trong street, không chỉ hỏi bên trong là retail hay office.
**Grammar & collocations:** `form-based code district` — district; `form-based-code standard` — standard; `adopt a code` — adopt.
**Examples:** `The form-based code required active frontages along the main avenue.` → Form-based code yêu cầu frontage hoạt động dọc đại lộ chính.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `형태 기반 코드` — form-based code.

## 5. floor-area ratio /ˈflɔr ˈɛriə ˈreɪʃioʊ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the ratio of a building's total floor area to the area of its lot.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** tổng diện tích sàn chia diện tích đất; FAR cao cho phép development dày hơn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `maximum floor-area ratio` — ratio; `FAR bonus` — bonus; `calculate floor-area ratio` — calculate.
**Examples:** `The transit district allowed a higher floor-area ratio near the station.` → Transit district cho FAR cao hơn gần station.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `용적률` — floor-area ratio.

## 6. upzoning /ˈʌpˌzoʊnɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a zoning change that permits greater density, building height, or a broader range of uses on a site.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** “nâng” quyền development của parcel, ví dụ từ low-density residential lên mixed-use nhiều tầng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `transit-area upzoning` — upzoning; `upzoning proposal` — proposal; `upzone a corridor` — upzone.
**Examples:** `Upzoning around the station created capacity for apartments above shops.` → Upzoning quanh station tạo capacity cho apartment trên shop.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `상향 용도지역 변경` — upzoning.

## 7. downzoning /ˈdaʊnˌzoʊnɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a zoning change that reduces permitted density, height, intensity, or range of uses.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** “hạ” development right của land, thường để bảo vệ character, infrastructure hoặc environmental constraints.
**Grammar & collocations:** `neighborhood downzoning` — downzoning; `downzoning ordinance` — ordinance; `downzone a parcel` — downzone.
**Examples:** `Downzoning limited new height but also reduced the number of homes that could be built.` → Downzoning giới hạn height mới nhưng cũng giảm số home có thể xây.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `하향 용도지역 변경` — downzoning.

## 8. urban growth boundary /ˈɜrbən ɡroʊθ ˈbaʊndəri/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a mapped limit beyond which urban development is restricted to contain sprawl and protect rural or ecological land.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** đường biên giữ city không lan vô hạn ra farmland, forest hoặc wetland.
**Grammar & collocations:** `urban-growth-boundary expansion` — expansion; `inside the boundary` — inside; `set a boundary` — set.
**Examples:** `The urban growth boundary concentrated new infrastructure inside the existing service area.` → Urban growth boundary tập trung infrastructure mới trong service area hiện hữu.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `도시 성장 경계` — urban growth boundary.

## 9. greenbelt policy /ˈɡriːnˌbɛlt ˈpɑləsi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a policy that protects a belt of open, agricultural, or ecological land around an urban area from development.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** vành đai xanh giữ land mở quanh city, tạo boundary nhưng có thể đẩy pressure vào vùng bên trong.
**Grammar & collocations:** `metropolitan greenbelt policy` — policy; `greenbelt protection` — protection; `enforce a greenbelt` — enforce.
**Examples:** `The greenbelt policy protected farmland but increased pressure for infill inside the boundary.` → Greenbelt policy bảo vệ farmland nhưng tăng pressure cho infill bên trong boundary.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `그린벨트 정책` — greenbelt policy.

## 10. infill development /ˈɪnfɪl dɪˈvɛləpmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** new development on vacant, underused, or previously developed land within an existing urban area.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** “lấp chỗ trống” trong city thay vì mở road và utility mới ra fringe.
**Grammar & collocations:** `urban infill development` — development; `infill parcel` — parcel; `promote infill` — promote.
**Examples:** `Infill development added homes near schools without extending the sewer network.` → Infill development thêm home gần school mà không kéo dài sewer network.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `도시 충전 개발` — infill development.

## 11. brownfield remediation /ˈbraʊnfiːld rɪˌmiːdiˈeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the assessment, cleanup, and risk management of previously used land contaminated by industrial or commercial activity.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** làm sạch và quản lý risk của land công nghiệp cũ trước khi tái sử dụng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `brownfield-remediation grant` — grant; `remediate a site` — remediate; `contaminated brownfield` — brownfield.
**Examples:** `Brownfield remediation made the former factory site safe for mixed-use redevelopment.` → Brownfield remediation làm site nhà máy cũ an toàn cho redevelopment mixed-use.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `브라운필드 정화` — remediation brownfield.

## 12. land-value capture /ˈlænd ˈvæljuː ˈkæptʃər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a public-finance strategy that recovers part of the increase in land value created by public investment or regulatory change.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** city “thu lại” một phần land value tăng nhờ station, road hoặc zoning để tái đầu tư hạ tầng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `land-value-capture mechanism` — mechanism; `capture uplift` — capture; `value-capture revenue` — revenue.
**Examples:** `Land-value capture helped finance the new transit extension.` → Land-value capture giúp finance transit extension mới.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `토지가치 환수` — land-value capture.

## 13. housing filtering /ˈhaʊzɪŋ ˈfɪltərɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the process by which older housing becomes relatively cheaper over time as it ages or loses amenities, potentially serving lower-income households.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** housing “lọc xuống” các price tier thấp hơn theo thời gian, nhưng không phải lúc nào cũng đủ nhanh hoặc đủ rẻ.
**Grammar & collocations:** `downward housing filtering` — filtering; `filtering process` — process; `filter into affordability` — filter.
**Examples:** `Housing filtering was too slow to offset the rapid loss of subsidized units.` → Housing filtering quá chậm để bù việc mất subsidized unit nhanh.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `주택 필터링` — housing filtering.

## 14. gentrification pressure /ˌdʒɛntrəfəˈkeɪʃən ˈprɛʃər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** economic and social pressure toward rising land values, higher-income in-migration, and neighborhood change in a lower-cost area.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** áp lực kéo giá và resident profile lên, có thể tạo investment nhưng cũng làm resident cũ bất an.
**Grammar & collocations:** `rising gentrification pressure` — pressure; `measure neighborhood pressure` — measure; `gentrification-risk indicator` — indicator.
**Examples:** `New rail access increased gentrification pressure around the station district.` → Rail access mới tăng gentrification pressure quanh district station.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `젠트리피케이션 압력` — gentrification pressure.

## 15. displacement risk /dɪˈspleɪsmənt rɪsk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the likelihood that residents, businesses, or community institutions will be forced or priced out by redevelopment, rent increases, or policy changes.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** risk người và business bị đẩy khỏi neighborhood, trực tiếp hoặc vì giá tăng dần.
**Grammar & collocations:** `residential displacement risk` — risk; `assess displacement risk` — assess; `displacement-risk mitigation` — mitigation.
**Examples:** `The plan paired new density with tenant protections to reduce displacement risk.` → Plan ghép density mới với tenant protection để giảm displacement risk.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `이주 위험` — displacement risk.

## 16. environmental justice mapping /ɪnˌvaɪrənˈmɛntl ˈdʒʌstɪs ˈmæpɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** mapping that combines environmental hazards with demographic and socioeconomic data to reveal unequal exposure or protection.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** chồng bản đồ pollution, income, race, health và infrastructure để thấy burden phân bổ có công bằng không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `environmental-justice mapping tool` — tool; `map cumulative burdens` — map; `justice-mapping layer` — layer.
**Examples:** `Environmental justice mapping showed that heat and traffic pollution overlapped in the same low-income census tracts.` → Mapping cho thấy heat và traffic pollution chồng lên các census tract low-income.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `환경 정의 지도화` — environmental justice mapping.

## 17. walkability index /ˌwɔkəˈbɪləti ˈɪndɛks/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a composite measure estimating how convenient and safe it is to reach destinations on foot in an area.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** điểm tổng hợp từ distance, connectivity, crossings, land use và street condition để đo “đi bộ có dễ không”.
**Grammar & collocations:** `neighborhood walkability index` — index; `calculate walkability` — calculate; `high-walkability area` — area.
**Examples:** `The walkability index rose after the city connected the block network and added shade.` → Walkability index tăng sau khi city nối block network và thêm shade.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `보행성 지수` — walkability index.

## 18. jobs-housing balance /dʒɑbz ˈhaʊzɪŋ ˈbæləns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the relationship between the number and type of jobs and the housing available to workers within a defined area.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** city có đủ housing gần nơi có job không; balance tốt có thể giảm commute dài nhưng không tự động bảo đảm affordability.
**Grammar & collocations:** `jobs-housing-balance target` — target; `improve the balance` — improve; `jobs-housing mismatch` — mismatch.
**Examples:** `The jobs-housing balance worsened when offices expanded without new affordable homes.` → Jobs-housing balance xấu đi khi office mở rộng mà không có affordable home mới.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `일자리-주택 균형` — jobs-housing balance.

## 19. polycentric urban form /ˌpɑliˈsɛntrɪk ˈɜrbən fɔrm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an urban structure organized around multiple employment, service, and activity centers rather than one dominant downtown.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** city có nhiều “trái tim” thay vì một center duy nhất, phân tán job và service.
**Grammar & collocations:** `polycentric metropolitan form` — form; `polycentric development` — development; `create multiple centers` — create.
**Examples:** `A polycentric urban form shortened some commutes by placing jobs near secondary centers.` → Polycentric urban form rút ngắn vài commute bằng cách đặt job gần secondary center.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `다핵 도시 형태` — polycentric urban form.

## 20. urban heat island /ˈɜrbən hiːt ˈaɪlənd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the tendency for built-up urban areas to be warmer than surrounding rural areas because of heat-absorbing surfaces, reduced vegetation, and waste heat.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** city thành “đảo nhiệt” do asphalt/concrete giữ heat, cây ít và waste heat nhiều.
**Grammar & collocations:** `urban-heat-island effect` — effect; `heat-island intensity` — intensity; `mitigate urban heat` — mitigate.
**Examples:** `Tree canopy and reflective roofs reduced the urban heat island during the heat wave.` → Tree canopy và reflective roof giảm urban heat island trong heat wave.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `도시 열섬` — urban heat island.

## Review in context

Transport planning must account for **induced demand**, while **complete streets** and a **road diet** can rebalance space among users. A **form-based code** shapes the public realm, and a **floor-area ratio** sets development intensity. **Upzoning** may add capacity, whereas **downzoning** reduces it. An **urban growth boundary** and **greenbelt policy** contain expansion, while **infill development** and **brownfield remediation** reuse land inside the city. **Land-value capture** can fund infrastructure. Housing supply changes through **housing filtering**, but **gentrification pressure** and **displacement risk** require safeguards. **Environmental justice mapping** reveals unequal burdens; a **walkability index** and **jobs-housing balance** measure access. A **polycentric urban form** distributes activity, while trees and reflective surfaces mitigate the **urban heat island**.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

Quy hoạch transport phải tính **induced demand**, còn **complete streets** và **road diet** có thể phân bổ lại space giữa các user. **Form-based code** định hình public realm, còn **floor-area ratio** đặt development intensity. **Upzoning** có thể thêm capacity, trong khi **downzoning** giảm nó. **Urban growth boundary** và **greenbelt policy** giới hạn expansion, còn **infill development** và **brownfield remediation** tái sử dụng land trong city. **Land-value capture** có thể finance infrastructure. Housing supply thay đổi qua **housing filtering**, nhưng **gentrification pressure** và **displacement risk** cần safeguard. **Environmental justice mapping** cho thấy burden không đều; **walkability index** và **jobs-housing balance** đo access. **Polycentric urban form** phân phối activity, còn tree và reflective surface giảm **urban heat island**.
