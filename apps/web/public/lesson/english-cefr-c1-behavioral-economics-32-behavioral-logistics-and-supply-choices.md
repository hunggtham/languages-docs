# C1 Vocabulary 32 — Behavioral logistics and supply choices

This lesson follows how customers and organizations respond to delivery promises, shortages, returns, uncertainty, and the social cost of moving goods.

Flow: **delivery time perception → shipment tracking salience → stockout disappointment → substitution acceptance → queueing fairness belief → last-mile trust → package theft perception → return friction → delivery default → inventory scarcity cue → service recovery expectation → order batching preference → convenience premium acceptance → warehouse automation trust → supply disruption memory → supplier reliability belief → demand forecast optimism → procurement anchoring → contract flexibility value → logistics sustainability framing**.

## 1. delivery time perception /dɪˈlɪvəri taɪm pərˈsɛpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a person's judgment about how long a delivery will take and how that delay feels.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** judgment delivery take long bao lâu và delay feel thế nào.
**Grammar & collocations:** `accurate delivery time perception` — perception; `change delivery time perception` — change.
**Examples:** `Delivery time perception worsened when the promised window narrowed without a clear update.` → Perception time delivery tệ hơn khi window promise narrow mà không update rõ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `배송 시간 인식`.

## 2. shipment tracking salience /ˈʃɪpmənt ˈtrækɪŋ ˈseɪliəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** how noticeable and important shipment progress is while a customer waits for an order.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mức shipment progress noticeable và important khi customer wait.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high shipment tracking salience` — salience; `increase shipment tracking salience` — increase.
**Examples:** `Shipment tracking salience reduced anxiety when each handoff appeared with a realistic time.` → Salience tracking shipment giảm anxiety khi mỗi handoff appear với time realistic.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `배송 추적 현저성`.

## 3. stockout disappointment /ˈstɑkˌaʊt ˌdɪsəˈpɔɪntmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** negative emotion caused by finding that a desired product is unavailable when expected.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** emotion negative khi product muốn không available như expected.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong stockout disappointment` — disappointment; `reduce stockout disappointment` — reduce.
**Examples:** `Stockout disappointment was lower when the store offered a credible restock date and alternatives.` → Disappointment stockout thấp hơn khi store offer date restock credible và alternative.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `품절 실망`.

## 4. substitution acceptance /ˌsʌbstəˈtuːʃən əkˈsɛptəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** willingness to accept a different product or service when the preferred option is unavailable.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** willingness accept product hoặc service khác khi option preferred unavailable.
**Grammar & collocations:** `low substitution acceptance` — acceptance; `increase substitution acceptance` — increase.
**Examples:** `Substitution acceptance rose when the replacement matched the original size and function.` → Acceptance substitution tăng khi replacement match size và function original.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `대체 수용`.

## 5. queueing fairness belief /ˈkjuːɪŋ ˈfɛrnəs bɪˈliːf/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a belief that orders are served according to clear and legitimate rules rather than hidden privilege.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** belief order serve theo rule clear và legitimate không phải privilege hidden.
**Grammar & collocations:** `low queueing fairness belief` — belief; `strengthen queueing fairness beliefs` — strengthen.
**Examples:** `Queueing fairness belief fell when late orders were moved ahead without explanation.` → Belief fairness queueing giảm khi order late move ahead không explain.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `대기열 공정성 신념`.

## 6. last-mile trust /læst maɪl trʌst/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** confidence that the final delivery stage will be secure, accurate, and respectful of the customer's instructions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** confidence final delivery stage secure, accurate và respect instruction customer.
**Grammar & collocations:** `fragile last-mile trust` — trust; `build last-mile trust` — build.
**Examples:** `Last-mile trust improved after drivers followed safe-drop instructions consistently.` → Trust last-mile improve sau khi driver follow instruction safe-drop consistent.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `라스트 마일 신뢰`.

## 7. package theft perception /ˈpækɪdʒ θɛft pərˈsɛpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a judgment about how likely and costly it is for a delivered package to be stolen.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** judgment package delivery bị steal likely và costly thế nào.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high package theft perception` — perception; `reduce package theft perception` — reduce.
**Examples:** `Package theft perception led the resident to choose a staffed pickup point.` → Perception theft package khiến resident choose pickup point có staff.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `소포 절도 인식`.

## 8. return friction /rɪˈtɝn ˈfrɪkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** effort, cost, uncertainty, or inconvenience involved in sending an unwanted product back.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** effort, cost, uncertainty hoặc inconvenience để send product không muốn back.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high return friction` — friction; `remove return friction` — remove.
**Examples:** `Return friction made the customer keep an unsuitable item instead of requesting a refund.` → Friction return khiến customer giữ item không phù hợp thay vì request refund.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `반품 마찰`.

## 9. delivery default /dɪˈlɪvəri dɪˈfɔlt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a preselected delivery speed, location, or instruction that remains unless the customer changes it.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** speed, location hoặc instruction delivery preselect giữ nguyên nếu customer không change.
**Grammar & collocations:** `convenient delivery default` — default; `change a delivery default` — change.
**Examples:** `A delivery default sent the package to a staffed locker rather than an exposed doorway.` → Default delivery send package tới locker có staff thay vì doorway exposed.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `배송 기본값`.

## 10. inventory scarcity cue /ˈɪnvənˌtɔri ˈskɛrsəti kjuː/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a message suggesting that available stock is limited and may disappear soon.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** message suggest stock available limited và sắp disappear.
**Grammar & collocations:** `artificial inventory scarcity cue` — cue; `respond to an inventory scarcity cue` — respond.
**Examples:** `An inventory scarcity cue pushed the buyer to order before checking whether the claim was accurate.` → Cue scarcity inventory thúc buyer order trước check claim accurate.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `재고 희소성 단서`.

## 11. service recovery expectation /ˈsɝvɪs rɪˈkʌvəri ˌɛkspɛkˈteɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a belief about how quickly and fairly a provider will repair a delivery failure.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** belief provider repair delivery failure nhanh và fair thế nào.
**Grammar & collocations:** `realistic service recovery expectation` — expectation; `raise service recovery expectations` — raise.
**Examples:** `Service recovery expectation improved when the carrier offered a replacement before asking for repeated evidence.` → Expectation recovery service improve khi carrier offer replacement trước yêu cầu evidence lặp.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `서비스 회복 기대`.

## 12. order batching preference /ˈɔrdər ˈbætʃɪŋ ˈprɛfərəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a preference for combining several deliveries into one shipment to reduce cost, clutter, or environmental impact.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** preference combine delivery thành shipment để reduce cost, clutter hoặc environmental impact.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong order batching preference` — preference; `respect order batching preferences` — respect.
**Examples:** `Order batching preference delayed one item but reduced four separate trips to the building.` → Preference batching order delay một item nhưng giảm bốn trip riêng tới building.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `주문 묶음 선호`.

## 13. convenience premium acceptance /kənˈviːnjəns ˈpriːmiəm əkˈsɛptəns/

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `편의 프리미엄 수용`.

## 15. supply disruption memory /səˈplaɪ dɪsˈrʌpʃən ˈmɛməri/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a remembered shortage or delay that changes how people plan for future supply problems.

## 17. demand forecast optimism /dɪˈmænd ˈfɔrˌkæst ˈɑptəˌmɪzəm/

**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** belief demand future cao, steady hoặc easy predict hơn evidence.

## 18. procurement anchoring /prəˈkjʊrmənt ˈæŋkərɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** relying too heavily on an initial price, supplier, or specification when making a purchasing decision.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** rely quá mức vào price, supplier hoặc specification đầu khi purchasing.
**Grammar & collocations:** `persistent procurement anchoring` — anchoring; `counter procurement anchoring` — counter.
**Examples:** `Procurement anchoring made the team compare every quote with an outdated contract price.` → Anchoring procurement khiến team compare mọi quote với contract price cũ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `조달 기준점 편향`.

## 19. contract flexibility value /ˈkɑntrækt ˌflɛksəˈbɪləti ˈvælju/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the value of being able to change quantities, timing, or terms when future conditions are uncertain.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** value ability change quantity, timing hoặc term khi future uncertain.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high contract flexibility value` — value; `estimate contract flexibility value` — estimate.
**Examples:** `Contract flexibility value was high because demand could change before the next production cycle.` → Value flexibility contract cao vì demand có thể đổi trước production cycle tiếp.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `계약 유연성 가치`.

## 20. logistics sustainability framing /ləˈdʒɪstɪks səˌsteɪnəˈbɪləti ˈfreɪmɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** presenting delivery and supply choices in terms of environmental impact, labor, resilience, and long-term community value.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** present delivery và supply choice theo impact environment, labor, resilience và value community long-term.
**Grammar & collocations:** `credible logistics sustainability framing` — framing; `use logistics sustainability framing` — use.
**Examples:** `Logistics sustainability framing encouraged customers to accept a slower consolidated delivery.` → Framing sustainability logistics khuyến khích customer accept delivery consolidated chậm hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `물류 지속 가능성 프레이밍`.

## Review in context

Customers first form a **delivery time perception**, then watch **shipment tracking salience** while waiting. **Stockout disappointment** can be reduced by honest alternatives, which raises **substitution acceptance**. **Queueing fairness belief** and **last-mile trust** shape whether the process feels legitimate; **package theft perception** can change the preferred location. High **return friction** discourages correction, while a safe **delivery default** protects attention. An **inventory scarcity cue** may accelerate purchase, but **service recovery expectation** determines how a failure is judged. **Order batching preference** trades speed for fewer trips, and **convenience premium acceptance** reflects the value of reduced effort. **Warehouse automation trust** depends on visible controls, while a **supply disruption memory** changes reserves. **Supplier reliability belief**, **demand forecast optimism**, and **procurement anchoring** shape planning. **Contract flexibility value** protects against uncertainty, and **logistics sustainability framing** connects supply choices to long-term social and environmental effects.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

Customer đầu tiên form **delivery time perception**, rồi theo dõi **shipment tracking salience** khi wait. **Stockout disappointment** giảm với alternative honest, nâng **substitution acceptance**. **Queueing fairness belief** và **last-mile trust** shape process có legitimate không; **package theft perception** đổi location preferred. **Return friction** cao làm discourage correction, còn **delivery default** safe bảo vệ attention. **Inventory scarcity cue** có thể accelerate purchase, nhưng **service recovery expectation** quyết định đánh giá failure. **Order batching preference** trade speed lấy ít trip, còn **convenience premium acceptance** reflect value effort giảm. **Warehouse automation trust** phụ thuộc control visible, còn **supply disruption memory** đổi reserve. **Supplier reliability belief**, **demand forecast optimism** và **procurement anchoring** shape planning. **Contract flexibility value** bảo vệ uncertainty, và **logistics sustainability framing** connect supply choice với effect social và environmental dài hạn.
