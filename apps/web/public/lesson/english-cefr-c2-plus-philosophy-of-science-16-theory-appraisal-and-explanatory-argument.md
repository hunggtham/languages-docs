# C2+ Vocabulary — Theory appraisal and explanatory argument

This lesson follows how researchers compare explanations, diagnose anomalies, and judge whether a theory is genuinely improving. The network is **explanatory scope → predictive scope → ad hoc adjustment → auxiliary assumption → protective belt → degenerating research programme → progressive research programme → anomalous data → recalcitrant anomaly → background assumption → robustness probe → cross-method corroboration → consilience → explanatory compression → unifying law → mechanism-based explanation → causal narrative → nomological relevance → explanatory contrast → why-question → contrastive explanation → pragmatic adequacy → explanatory target → explanandum → explanans**.

## 1. explanatory scope /ɪkˈsplænəˌtɔri skoʊp/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the range of phenomena a theory or explanation aims to account for.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** explanatory scope là vùng phenomenon mà theory claim giải thích.
**Grammar & collocations:** `explanatory-scope comparison` — comparison; `extend a theory's scope` — extend; `target phenomenon` — phenomenon.
**Examples:** `The revised theory increased explanatory scope by covering both laboratory and field observations.` → Theory mới mở scope cho lab và field.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `설명 범위` — explanatory scope.

## 2. predictive scope /prɪˈdɪktɪv skoʊp/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the range of conditions, cases, or future observations for which a theory makes testable predictions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** predictive scope là vùng case/condition mà theory đưa prediction testable.
**Grammar & collocations:** `predictive-scope boundary` — boundary; `extend predictive scope` — extend; `out-of-sample case` — case.
**Examples:** `The model had broad predictive scope but weak explanatory detail about the mechanism.` → Model predict rộng nhưng mechanism detail yếu.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `예측 범위` — predictive scope.

## 3. ad hoc adjustment /ˌæd hɑk əˈdʒʌstmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a modification introduced mainly to protect a theory from a counterexample without independent justification or new explanatory gain.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** ad hoc adjustment là patch thêm để cứu theory, nhưng thiếu lý do độc lập.
**Grammar & collocations:** `ad-hoc-adjustment criticism` — criticism; `patch a theory` — patch; `independent justification` — justification.
**Examples:** `The reviewer rejected the ad hoc adjustment because it fit one anomaly and predicted nothing new.` → Reviewer reject patch vì không predict mới.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `임시 조정` — ad hoc adjustment.

## 4. auxiliary assumption /ɔɡˈzɪljəri əˈsʌmpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a supporting assumption that connects a theory to a particular test, measurement, or application.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** auxiliary assumption nối core theory với test cụ thể, như calibration hoặc initial condition.
**Grammar & collocations:** `auxiliary-assumption set` — set; `add an assumption` — add; `test condition` — condition.
**Examples:** `The failed prediction could reflect a faulty auxiliary assumption about sensor response.` → Failed prediction có thể do auxiliary assumption về sensor.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `보조 가정` — auxiliary assumption.

## 5. protective belt /prəˈtɛktɪv bɛlt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a layer of adjustable auxiliary hypotheses surrounding a theory's more central commitments.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** protective belt là lớp assumption có thể chỉnh để bảo vệ core theory.
**Grammar & collocations:** `protective-belt hypothesis` — hypothesis; `revise an auxiliary claim` — revise; `core commitment` — commitment.
**Examples:** `The team revised the protective belt while preserving the central conservation principle.` → Team revise lớp ngoài, giữ conservation principle.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `보호 벨트` — protective belt.

## 6. degenerating research programme /dɪˈdʒɛnəˌreɪtɪŋ rɪˈsɜrtʃ ˈproʊɡræm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a research programme that mainly adds defensive adjustments without generating novel predictions or explanatory progress.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** degenerating programme chỉ thêm patch để defend, không tạo prediction/explanation mới.
**Grammar & collocations:** `degenerating-research-programme diagnosis` — diagnosis; `add defensive patches` — add; `lack progressive content` — lack.
**Examples:** `The historian argued that repeated rescue clauses made the programme degenerating.` → Historian cho rằng rescue clause lặp làm programme degenerating.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `퇴행적 연구 프로그램` — degenerating research programme.

## 7. progressive research programme /prəˈɡrɛsɪv rɪˈsɜrtʃ ˈproʊɡræm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a research programme that extends explanatory power and yields novel, corroborated predictions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** progressive programme vừa giải thích tốt hơn vừa predict điều mới được confirm.
**Grammar & collocations:** `progressive-research-programme assessment` — assessment; `generate a novel prediction` — generate; `extend a theory` — extend.
**Examples:** `The progressive research programme predicted a new spectral line before it was observed.` → Programme predict spectral line trước observation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `진보적 연구 프로그램` — progressive research programme.

## 8. anomalous data /əˈnɑmələs ˈdeɪtə/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** observations that conflict with the expected pattern, model, or current explanation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** anomalous data lệch khỏi pattern expected; cần xem đó là error, boundary hay discovery.
**Grammar & collocations:** `anomalous-data report` — report; `flag an anomaly` — flag; `expected pattern` — pattern.
**Examples:** `The anomalous data appeared only at high pressure, suggesting a boundary of the model.` → Data anomaly chỉ ở pressure cao.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `변칙 데이터` — anomalous data.

## 9. recalcitrant anomaly /rɪˈkælsətrənt əˈnɑməli/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a persistent anomaly that resists routine correction, reinterpretation, or adjustment within a framework.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** recalcitrant anomaly là anomaly lì, không biến mất sau correction nhỏ.
**Grammar & collocations:** `recalcitrant-anomaly case` — case; `resist adjustment` — resist; `persistent mismatch` — mismatch.
**Examples:** `The recalcitrant anomaly survived recalibration and independent replication.` → Anomaly survive recalibration và replication.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `완강한 변칙` — recalcitrant anomaly.

## 10. background assumption /ˈbækɡraʊnd əˈsʌmpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an often-unspoken belief about instruments, sampling, categories, or conditions that supports an inference.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** background assumption nằm phía sau inference, như sample representative hay instrument stable.
**Grammar & collocations:** `background-assumption audit` — audit; `make an assumption explicit` — make; `unstated premise` — premise.
**Examples:** `The analysis failed when its background assumption about stable demand stopped holding.` → Analysis fail khi demand không stable.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `배경 가정` — background assumption.

## 11. robustness probe /roʊˈbʌstnəs proʊb/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a deliberate test of whether a conclusion survives reasonable changes in data, model, specification, or assumptions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** robustness probe thử đổi assumption/model hợp lý để xem conclusion có đứng không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `robustness-probe analysis` — analysis; `probe a conclusion` — probe; `alternative specification` — specification.
**Examples:** `A robustness probe showed that the association persisted after excluding the largest site.` → Probe cho association vẫn giữ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `강건성 탐색` — robustness probe.

## 12. cross-method corroboration /krɔs ˈmɛθəd kəˌrɑbəˈreɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** support for a claim obtained when independent methods converge on compatible evidence.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cross-method corroboration là nhiều method độc lập cùng support claim.
**Grammar & collocations:** `cross-method-corroboration evidence` — evidence; `corroborate across methods` — corroborate; `independent method` — method.
**Examples:** `Cross-method corroboration linked the survey result to sensor and interview evidence.` → Ba method cùng support result.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `방법 간 보강` — cross-method corroboration.

## 13. consilience /kənˈsɪliəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** agreement among evidence from different fields, methods, or explanatory routes.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** consilience là hội tụ evidence độc lập về cùng conclusion.
**Grammar & collocations:** `consilience across disciplines` — consilience; `converge on a conclusion` — converge; `independent line of evidence` — line.
**Examples:** `The consilience between genetics and field ecology strengthened the explanation.` → Genetics và field ecology cùng củng cố explanation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `증거 수렴` — consilience.

## 14. explanatory compression /ɪkˈsplænəˌtɔri kəmˈprɛʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** reduction of many observations or regularities to a compact set of principles that preserves their explanatory relationships.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** explanatory compression nén nhiều pattern vào ít principle mà vẫn giữ relation quan trọng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `explanatory-compression argument` — argument; `compress regularities` — compress; `compact principle` — principle.
**Examples:** `The equation achieved explanatory compression by unifying three apparently separate trends.` → Equation nén ba trend thành một explanation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `설명적 압축` — explanatory compression.

## 15. unifying law /ˈjuːnəˌfaɪɪŋ lɔː/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a law or principle that accounts for multiple phenomena through a common structure or relation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** unifying law dùng một relation chung để account cho nhiều phenomenon.
**Grammar & collocations:** `unifying-law proposal` — proposal; `unify phenomena` — unify; `common relation` — relation.
**Examples:** `The unifying law connected local circulation with the observed global pattern.` → Law nối local circulation với global pattern.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `통합 법칙` — unifying law.

## 16. mechanism-based explanation /ˈmɛkəˌnɪzəm beɪst ˌɛkspləˈneɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** explanation that accounts for a phenomenon by describing the entities, activities, and interactions that produce it.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mechanism-based explanation đi qua entity/activity/interaction tạo outcome.
**Grammar & collocations:** `mechanism-based-explanation` — explanation; `identify a productive mechanism` — identify; `generative pathway` — pathway.
**Examples:** `The mechanism-based explanation linked the policy to behavior through changing incentives.` → Explanation nối policy với behavior qua incentive.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `메커니즘 기반 설명` — mechanism-based explanation.

## 17. causal narrative /ˈkɔzəl ˈnærətɪv/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an ordered account of how events, conditions, and mechanisms combine to produce an outcome.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** causal narrative sắp xếp sequence và mechanism thành câu chuyện cause → effect có kiểm tra.
**Grammar & collocations:** `causal-narrative reconstruction` — reconstruction; `trace a sequence` — trace; `causal storyline` — storyline.
**Examples:** `The causal narrative connected drought, migration, and labor-market change without treating them as a single cause.` → Narrative nối các stage, không giản lược thành một cause.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `인과 서사` — causal narrative.

## 18. nomological relevance /ˌnoʊməˈlɑdʒɪkəl ˈrɛləvəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** relevance to a law-like regularity that helps explain why a phenomenon occurs under specified conditions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** nomological relevance là relevance của factor đối với law/regularity đang giải thích.
**Grammar & collocations:** `nomological-relevance criterion` — criterion; `invoke a regularity` — invoke; `law-relevant condition` — condition.
**Examples:** `The variable had nomological relevance because it entered the growth law, not merely the dataset.` → Variable relevant vì nằm trong growth law.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `법칙적 관련성` — nomological relevance.

## 19. explanatory contrast /ɪkˈsplænəˌtɔri ˈkɑntræst/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the difference between what happened and a relevant alternative that an explanation is expected to account for.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** explanatory contrast hỏi “tại sao A chứ không phải B?” thay vì chỉ “tại sao A?”.
**Grammar & collocations:** `explanatory-contrast question` — question; `contrast actual and alternative` — contrast; `relevant alternative` — alternative.
**Examples:** `The explanatory contrast was why one district flooded while a similar district did not.` → Contrast là vì sao district này flood district kia không.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `설명적 대조` — explanatory contrast.

## 20. why-question /waɪ ˈkwɛstʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a question asking for a reason, cause, mechanism, purpose, or contrast behind a fact or event.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** why-question có thể hỏi cause, mechanism, purpose hoặc contrast; không chỉ một loại answer.
**Grammar & collocations:** `why-question taxonomy` — taxonomy; `ask why` — ask; `reason-seeking question` — question.
**Examples:** `The team clarified whether its why-question concerned mechanism or policy justification.` → Team clarify hỏi mechanism hay policy justification.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `왜 질문` — why-question.

## 21. contrastive explanation /kənˈtræstɪv ˌɛkspləˈneɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** explanation directed at why one outcome occurred instead of a specified alternative.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** contrastive explanation trả lời “A thay vì B” và chọn difference relevant.
**Grammar & collocations:** `contrastive-explanation framework` — framework; `explain why A rather than B` — explain; `contrast condition` — condition.
**Examples:** `The contrastive explanation focused on why the vaccine worked in one age group but not another.` → Explanation focus vào age-group difference.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `대조적 설명` — contrastive explanation.

## 22. pragmatic adequacy /præɡˈmætɪk ˌædəˈkwəsi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the sufficiency of an explanation or model for guiding action, prediction, or problem-solving in a practical setting.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** pragmatic adequacy hỏi explanation có đủ dùng cho action/decision không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `pragmatic-adequacy standard` — standard; `guide a decision` — guide; `fit a practical purpose` — fit.
**Examples:** `The simplified rule had pragmatic adequacy for emergency triage despite its theoretical limits.` → Rule đơn giản đủ dùng cho triage.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `실용적 적합성` — pragmatic adequacy.

## 23. explanatory target /ɪkˈsplænəˌtɔri ˈtɑrɡət/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the phenomenon, pattern, event, or contrast that an explanation is intended to account for.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** explanatory target là cái explanation phải account, cần nêu rõ scope.
**Grammar & collocations:** `explanatory-target definition` — definition; `identify the target` — identify; `target phenomenon` — phenomenon.
**Examples:** `The explanatory target was the sudden migration spike, not the long-term population trend.` → Target là migration spike đột ngột.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `설명 대상` — explanatory target.

## 24. explanandum /ˌɛkspləˈnændəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the fact, event, pattern, or proposition that is to be explained.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** explanandum là “điều cần giải thích”; nó là target của explanation.
**Grammar & collocations:** `define the explanandum` — define; `explanandum statement` — statement; `fact to be explained` — fact.
**Examples:** `The explanandum was the repeated timing pattern observed across three storms.` → Explanandum là timing pattern lặp.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `피설명항` — explanandum.

## 25. explanans /ˌɛkspləˈnænz/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the set of laws, conditions, mechanisms, or reasons offered to explain an explanandum.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** explanans là phần “dùng để giải thích”: law, condition, mechanism và reason.
**Grammar & collocations:** `explanans structure` — structure; `supply an explanans` — supply; `explanatory premises` — premises.
**Examples:** `The explanans combined a transport law with the storm's initial conditions.` → Explanans gồm transport law và initial condition.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `설명항` — explanans.

## Review in context

**Explanatory scope** and **predictive scope** describe what a theory covers. An **ad hoc adjustment** may protect a claim through an **auxiliary assumption** or **protective belt**, but a **degenerating research programme** differs from a **progressive research programme** that produces new success. **Anomalous data** can become a **recalcitrant anomaly** when it survives an audit of the **background assumption**. A **robustness probe**, **cross-method corroboration**, and **consilience** test whether the claim is stable. **Explanatory compression** and a **unifying law** offer economy; a **mechanism-based explanation** and **causal narrative** show how production occurs. **Nomological relevance** helps define an **explanatory contrast** and a **why-question**. A **contrastive explanation** serves a practical **explanatory target**, whose **explanandum** is addressed by an **explanans** with sufficient **pragmatic adequacy**.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

**Explanatory scope** và **predictive scope** mô tả theory cover gì. **Ad hoc adjustment** có thể bảo vệ claim qua **auxiliary assumption** hoặc **protective belt**, nhưng **degenerating research programme** khác **progressive research programme** tạo success mới. **Anomalous data** thành **recalcitrant anomaly** khi survive audit của **background assumption**. **Robustness probe**, **cross-method corroboration** và **consilience** test claim có stable không. **Explanatory compression** và **unifying law** tạo economy; **mechanism-based explanation** và **causal narrative** cho thấy process tạo outcome. **Nomological relevance** giúp define **explanatory contrast** và **why-question**. **Contrastive explanation** phục vụ **explanatory target** thực tế, trong đó **explanandum** được xử lý bởi **explanans** có **pragmatic adequacy** đủ.
