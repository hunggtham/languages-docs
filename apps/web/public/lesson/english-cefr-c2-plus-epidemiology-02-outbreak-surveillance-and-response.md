# C2+ Vocabulary — Outbreak surveillance and response

This lesson follows how public-health teams detect, verify, contain, and learn from an outbreak. The network is **genomic lineage tracking → variant nomenclature decision → sentinel-site calibration → event-based signal triage → clinical syndrome surveillance → working case definition → line-list reconciliation → epidemic-curve reconstruction → incubation-period distribution → attack-rate denominator → exposure window → isolation adherence → source-control protocol → ring-immunization strategy → prophylaxis eligibility window → community mitigation → nonpharmaceutical intervention → risk-stratified testing → diagnostic turnaround → laboratory surge capacity → biosafety level → specimen referral network → outbreak threshold → incident command → outbreak debrief cycle**.

## 1. genomic lineage tracking /dʒiˈnoʊmɪk ˈliːniɪdʒ ˈtrækɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** monitoring genetic lineages of a pathogen to identify introductions, transmission chains, replacement patterns, and emerging mutations.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** genomic lineage tracking nối sequence với đường truyền trong population.
**Grammar & collocations:** `genomic-lineage-tracking pipeline` — pipeline; `track a lineage` — track; `lineage replacement` — replacement.
**Examples:** `Genomic lineage tracking linked several apparently separate cases to one introduction.` → Sequence giúp nối các case rời.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `유전체 계통 추적` — genomic lineage tracking.

## 2. variant nomenclature decision /ˈvɛriənt noʊmənˈkleɪtʃər dɪˈsɪʒən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a documented decision about how a newly observed pathogen variant should be named and classified.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** variant nomenclature decision giữ tên gọi nhất quán khi evidence thay đổi.
**Grammar & collocations:** `variant-nomenclature decision` — decision; `assign a lineage name` — assign; `classification criteria` — criteria.
**Examples:** `The variant nomenclature decision recorded both the genetic evidence and the uncertainty.` → Naming cần để lại reasoning.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `변이 명명 결정` — variant nomenclature decision.

## 3. sentinel-site calibration /ˈsɛntənəl saɪt ˌkælɪˈbreɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** adjustment and validation of selected surveillance sites so their signals can be interpreted consistently over time.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** sentinel-site calibration làm rõ signal do disease hay do site đổi cách báo cáo.
**Grammar & collocations:** `sentinel-site calibration exercise` — exercise; `calibrate a surveillance site` — calibrate; `reporting baseline` — baseline.
**Examples:** `Sentinel-site calibration showed that the apparent rise partly reflected a new testing protocol.` → Calibration tách signal thật khỏi artefact.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `감시 거점 보정` — sentinel-site calibration.

## 4. event-based signal triage /ɪˈvɛnt beɪst ˈsɪɡnəl triˈɑːʒ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** rapid sorting of informal reports, rumors, or alerts to decide which signals require epidemiological verification.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** event-based signal triage biến rumor thành queue để verify theo risk.
**Grammar & collocations:** `event-based signal triage` — triage; `triage an alert` — triage; `signal verification` — verification.
**Examples:** `Event-based signal triage prioritized the unexplained cluster in a hospital ward.` → Triage giúp dùng resource đúng chỗ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `사건 기반 신호 분류` — event-based signal triage.

## 5. clinical syndrome surveillance /ˈklɪnɪkəl ˈsɪndroʊm sərˈveɪləns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** monitoring patterns of symptoms or syndromes before a definitive laboratory diagnosis is available.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** clinical syndrome surveillance nhìn pattern symptom sớm hơn test confirmation.
**Grammar & collocations:** `clinical-syndrome surveillance system` — system; `monitor respiratory syndromes` — monitor; `syndrome signal` — signal.
**Examples:** `Clinical syndrome surveillance detected an unusual fever pattern before confirmatory results arrived.` → Syndrome signal đi trước lab result.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `임상 증후군 감시` — clinical syndrome surveillance.

## 6. working case definition /ˈwɜrkɪŋ keɪs ˌdɛfəˈnɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a provisional set of clinical, laboratory, and exposure criteria used to identify possible cases during an evolving investigation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** working case definition đủ rõ để hành động nhưng có thể sửa khi evidence mới đến.
**Grammar & collocations:** `working-case definition` — definition; `revise the case definition` — revise; `probable case` — case.
**Examples:** `The working case definition was broadened after interviews revealed an atypical symptom pattern.` → Definition phải học từ field evidence.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `잠정 사례 정의` — working case definition.

## 7. line-list reconciliation /ˈlaɪn lɪst ˌrɛkənˌsɪliˈeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** systematic comparison and correction of case records from different reporting systems so each person is represented accurately.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** line-list reconciliation dọn duplicate, missing field và conflicting dates trong case list.
**Grammar & collocations:** `line-list reconciliation meeting` — meeting; `reconcile duplicate records` — reconcile; `case-record discrepancy` — discrepancy.
**Examples:** `Line-list reconciliation removed duplicate admissions and corrected several onset dates.` → Data sạch giúp curve đáng tin.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `사례 목록 대조` — line-list reconciliation.

## 8. epidemic-curve reconstruction /ˌɛpɪˈdɛmɪk kɜrv ˌriːkənˈstrʌkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** rebuilding an outbreak curve from incomplete onset, report, or exposure dates to infer timing and transmission pattern.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** epidemic-curve reconstruction lấp khoảng trống dữ liệu để nhìn shape của outbreak.
**Grammar & collocations:** `epidemic-curve reconstruction` — reconstruction; `reconstruct onset dates` — reconstruct; `propagated outbreak` — outbreak.
**Examples:** `Epidemic-curve reconstruction suggested several overlapping transmission waves rather than one point-source event.` → Shape phân biệt nhiều kiểu outbreak.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `유행 곡선 재구성` — epidemic-curve reconstruction.

## 9. incubation-period distribution /ˌɪŋkjəˈbeɪʃən ˈpɪriəd ˌdɪstrəˈbjuːʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the distribution of time between infection and symptom onset across infected people.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** incubation-period distribution cho biết symptom thường xuất hiện sau exposure bao lâu và tail dài tới đâu.
**Grammar & collocations:** `incubation-period distribution` — distribution; `estimate the incubation period` — estimate; `right-skewed distribution` — distribution.
**Examples:** `The incubation-period distribution informed the length of symptom monitoring after exposure.` → Distribution hướng dẫn follow-up window.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `잠복기 분포` — incubation-period distribution.

## 11. exposure window /ɪkˈspoʊʒər ˈwɪndoʊ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the period during which a person could plausibly have encountered the infectious source or environment.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** exposure window khoanh khoảng thời gian cần truy event, contact và movement.
**Grammar & collocations:** `exposure-window reconstruction` — reconstruction; `narrow the exposure window` — narrow; `plausible exposure` — exposure.
**Examples:** `Interviewers narrowed the exposure window by comparing travel records with symptom onset.` → Window hẹp hơn giúp tìm source.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `노출 기간` — exposure window.

## 12. isolation adherence /ˌaɪsəˈleɪʃən ədˈhɪrəns/

## 13. source-control protocol /sɔrs kənˈtroʊl ˈproʊtəkɔl/

**Core meaning (English):** a set of measures that reduces infectious emissions at their source, such as masking, ventilation, or containment.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** source-control protocol giảm pathogen rời khỏi source trước khi lan sang người khác.
**Grammar & collocations:** `source-control protocol` — protocol; `implement source control` — implement; `infectious emission` — emission.
**Examples:** `The source-control protocol combined respirators, room ventilation, and rapid separation of symptomatic patients.` → Source control thường là một package.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `발원지 통제 지침` — source-control protocol.

## 14. ring-immunization strategy /rɪŋ ˌɪmjənəˈzeɪʃən ˈstrætədʒi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a strategy that immunizes contacts and contacts of contacts around an identified case to create a protective ring.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** ring-immunization strategy bao quanh case bằng một ring immune thay vì vaccinate ngẫu nhiên toàn population.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `환형 면역 전략` — ring-immunization strategy.

## Review in context — Part 1

**Genomic lineage tracking** supports a **variant nomenclature decision**, while **sentinel-site calibration** prevents ordinary reporting changes from being mistaken for transmission. **Event-based signal triage** can activate **clinical syndrome surveillance** before a laboratory result is available. Investigators use a **working case definition**, **line-list reconciliation**, and **epidemic-curve reconstruction** to organize evidence. The **incubation-period distribution**, **attack-rate denominator**, and **exposure window** shape how cases and contacts are counted.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 1:**

**Genomic lineage tracking** hỗ trợ **variant nomenclature decision**, còn **sentinel-site calibration** ngăn thay đổi báo cáo thông thường bị nhầm là transmission. **Event-based signal triage** có thể kích hoạt **clinical syndrome surveillance** trước khi có lab result. Điều tra viên dùng **working case definition**, **line-list reconciliation** và **epidemic-curve reconstruction** để sắp xếp evidence. **Incubation-period distribution**, **attack-rate denominator** và **exposure window** định hình cách đếm case và contact.

## Review in context — Part 2

**Isolation adherence** improves when a **source-control protocol** is practical. A **ring-immunization strategy** must respect the **prophylaxis eligibility window**, while **community mitigation** can combine several **nonpharmaceutical intervention** layers. **Risk-stratified testing** is more useful when **diagnostic turnaround** is short. During a surge, **laboratory surge capacity**, the correct **biosafety level**, and a reliable **specimen referral network** protect both speed and safety. An **outbreak threshold** can activate **incident command**, and an **outbreak debrief cycle** turns lessons into corrective action.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 2:**

**Isolation adherence** tốt hơn khi **source-control protocol** khả thi. **Ring-immunization strategy** phải tôn trọng **prophylaxis eligibility window**, còn **community mitigation** có thể kết hợp nhiều lớp **nonpharmaceutical intervention**. **Risk-stratified testing** hữu ích hơn khi **diagnostic turnaround** ngắn. Trong surge, **laboratory surge capacity**, **biosafety level** phù hợp và **specimen referral network** đáng tin bảo vệ cả tốc độ lẫn an toàn. **Outbreak threshold** có thể kích hoạt **incident command**, còn **outbreak debrief cycle** biến bài học thành corrective action.
