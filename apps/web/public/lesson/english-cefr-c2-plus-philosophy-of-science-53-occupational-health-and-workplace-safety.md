# C2+ Vocabulary — Occupational health and workplace safety

This lesson follows how workplaces identify hazards, protect workers, support recovery, and design safer conditions. The network is **occupational hygiene → hierarchy of controls → exposure limit → fit testing → respiratory protection → ergonomic risk → work-related musculoskeletal disorder → presenteeism → sickness absence → return-to-work plan → fitness-for-work assessment → workplace accommodation → occupational injury → needlestick injury → post-exposure prophylaxis → industrial toxicology → psychosocial hazard → ethical injury → burnout prevention → workplace violence → violence prevention plan → shift work disorder → circadian misalignment → heat stress → occupational epidemiology**.

## 1. occupational hygiene /ˌɑkjəˈpeɪʃənəl ˈhaɪdʒiːn/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** science and practice of anticipating, recognizing, evaluating, and controlling workplace exposures that may harm health.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** occupational hygiene tìm hazard trong workplace rồi đo và control.
**Grammar & collocations:** `occupational-hygiene survey` — survey; `evaluate workplace exposure` — evaluate; `hazard control` — control.
**Examples:** `Occupational hygiene identified solvent vapor as the main exposure requiring engineering control.` → Đo exposure trước khi chọn control.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `산업 위생` — occupational hygiene.

## 2. hierarchy of controls /ˈhaɪəˌrɑrki əv kənˈtroʊlz/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** ordered approach that prioritizes eliminating a hazard, then substituting, engineering, administrating, or using personal protection.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** hierarchy of controls ưu tiên bỏ hazard trước khi bắt worker tự bảo vệ.
**Grammar & collocations:** `hierarchy-of-controls framework` — framework; `eliminate a hazard` — eliminate; `engineering control` — control.
**Examples:** `The hierarchy of controls favored enclosing the chemical process over relying only on masks.` → Engineering tốt hơn chỉ dựa vào mask.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `위험 통제 계층` — hierarchy of controls.

## 3. exposure limit /ɪkˈspoʊʒər ˈlɪmət/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a defined maximum concentration, intensity, or duration of workplace exposure considered acceptable under specified conditions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** exposure limit là ngưỡng dùng để trigger control, không phải bảo đảm zero risk.
**Grammar & collocations:** `exposure-limit value` — value; `stay below a limit` — stay; `workplace concentration` — concentration.
**Examples:** `The monitoring result exceeded the exposure limit, so the shift was stopped while ventilation was repaired.` → Vượt limit thì stop và sửa.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `노출 한계` — exposure limit.

## 4. fit testing /fɪt ˈtɛstɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** testing whether a tight-fitting respirator forms an effective seal on a particular worker's face.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** fit testing kiểm tra mask có seal đúng trên người cụ thể không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `fit-testing protocol` — protocol; `test a respirator seal` — test; `respirator model` — model.
**Examples:** `Fit testing showed that the standard respirator leaked when the worker turned their head.` → Seal fail khi movement.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `밀착도 검사` — fit testing.

## 5. respiratory protection /ˈrɛspərətɔri prəˈtɛkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** equipment, practices, training, and programs that reduce inhalation of harmful dusts, vapors, gases, aerosols, or pathogens.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** respiratory protection là cả program, không chỉ đưa một cái mask.
**Grammar & collocations:** `respiratory-protection program` — program; `protect against inhalation` — protect; `respirator training` — training.
**Examples:** `Respiratory protection combined ventilation, fit testing, maintenance, and medical evaluation.` → Program có nhiều layer.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `호흡기 보호` — respiratory protection.

## 6. ergonomic risk /ˌɜrɡəˈnɑmɪk rɪsk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** risk of injury or strain caused by the fit between a task, tool, posture, force, repetition, and the worker's body.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** ergonomic risk xuất hiện khi task/tool không hợp body và movement.
**Grammar & collocations:** `ergonomic-risk assessment` — assessment; `reduce repetitive strain` — reduce; `workstation design` — design.
**Examples:** `The ergonomic risk assessment changed the lifting height and reduced repeated twisting.` → Sửa task thay vì blame body.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `인체공학적 위험` — ergonomic risk.

## 7. work-related musculoskeletal disorder /wɜrk rɪˈleɪtɪd ˌmʌskjəloʊˈskɛlɪtəl dɪˈsɔrdər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** pain, injury, or functional disorder of muscles, joints, tendons, nerves, or related structures linked partly to work conditions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** work-related musculoskeletal disorder nối symptom với load, posture, repetition hoặc vibration ở job.
**Grammar & collocations:** `work-related-musculoskeletal-disorder prevention` — prevention; `assess repetitive load` — assess; `occupational strain` — strain.
**Examples:** `The work-related musculoskeletal disorder improved after lifting equipment and schedules were redesigned.` → Sửa system giúp recovery.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `업무 관련 근골격계 질환` — work-related musculoskeletal disorder.

## 8. presenteeism /ˌprɛzənˈtiːˌɪzəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** being present at work while illness, fatigue, pain, or distress substantially reduces effective functioning.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** presenteeism là có mặt nhưng capacity thực tế bị giảm.
**Grammar & collocations:** `presenteeism cost` — cost; `measure reduced productivity` — measure; `work while unwell` — work.
**Examples:** `Presenteeism hid the burden of migraine because staff feared using sick leave.` → Có mặt không đồng nghĩa khỏe để làm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `프리젠티즘` — presenteeism.

## 9. sickness absence /ˈsɪknəs ˈæbsəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** time away from work because of illness, injury, treatment, recovery, or health-related caregiving.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** sickness absence là absence có lý do health, cần nhìn cả worker và workplace.
**Grammar & collocations:** `sickness-absence rate` — rate; `return after sick leave` — return; `absence pattern` — pattern.
**Examples:** `Sickness absence fell after the employer improved scheduling and access to early treatment.` → Prevention tốt giảm absence.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `질병 결근` — sickness absence.

## 10. return-to-work plan /rɪˈtɜrn tə wɜrk plæn/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an agreed, staged plan for resuming work safely after illness or injury, with duties and support adjusted as needed.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** return-to-work plan đưa worker trở lại từng bước, không ép full load ngay.
**Grammar & collocations:** `return-to-work-plan review` — review; `resume duties gradually` — resume; `modified workload` — workload.
**Examples:** `The return-to-work plan began with shorter shifts and no overnight lifting.` → Plan theo capacity hiện tại.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `직장 복귀 계획` — return-to-work plan.

## 11. fitness-for-work assessment /ˈfɪtnəs fər wɜrk əˈsɛsmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** evaluation of whether a worker can safely perform specific duties, with or without reasonable restrictions or support.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** fitness-for-work assessment hỏi fit với task cụ thể, không phán xét toàn bộ person.
**Grammar & collocations:** `fitness-for-work-assessment report` — report; `assess functional capacity` — assess; `job-specific demand` — demand.
**Examples:** `The fitness-for-work assessment recommended administrative duties while balance problems were investigated.` → Job được adjust theo ability.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `업무 적합성 평가` — fitness-for-work assessment.

## 12. workplace accommodation /ˈwɜrkˌpleɪs əˌkɑməˈdeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a practical change to a job, schedule, environment, tool, or policy that enables a worker with a health condition or disability to work.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** workplace accommodation đổi context để worker dùng được capacity của mình.
**Grammar & collocations:** `workplace-accommodation request` — request; `adjust a schedule` — adjust; `accessible workstation` — workstation.
**Examples:** `A workplace accommodation allowed remote documentation during treatment appointments.` → Accommodation giữ worker trong job.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `직장 편의 제공` — workplace accommodation.

## 13. occupational injury /ˌɑkjəˈpeɪʃənəl ˈɪndʒəri/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** physical or psychological harm caused or substantially contributed to by work or workplace conditions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** occupational injury gắn harm với work exposure/task/incident.
**Grammar & collocations:** `occupational-injury claim` — claim; `report a work injury` — report; `injury prevention` — prevention.
**Examples:** `The occupational injury investigation examined equipment, training, staffing, and production pressure.` → Không chỉ hỏi worker làm sai gì.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `직업성 손상` — occupational injury.

## 14. needlestick injury /ˈniːdəlˌstɪk ˈɪndʒəri/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** puncture injury from a needle or sharp medical instrument that may expose a worker to bloodborne infection.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** needlestick injury là puncture nhỏ nhưng có exposure risk nghiêm trọng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `needlestick-injury protocol` — protocol; `report a sharps injury` — report; `bloodborne exposure` — exposure.
**Examples:** `The needlestick injury protocol arranged immediate washing, reporting, assessment, and follow-up.` → Response phải nhanh và có sequence.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `주사침 손상` — needlestick injury.

## 15. post-exposure prophylaxis /poʊst ɪkˈspoʊʒər ˌproʊfɪˈlæksɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** preventive treatment offered after a possible exposure to reduce the chance of infection or disease.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** post-exposure prophylaxis là action sau exposure, phụ thuộc đánh giá và thời gian.
**Grammar & collocations:** `post-exposure-prophylaxis protocol` — protocol; `offer preventive treatment` — offer; `exposure assessment` — assessment.
**Examples:** `Post-exposure prophylaxis was considered after the worker's blood exposure was assessed.` → Không tự động dùng; cần assessment.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `노출 후 예방요법` — post-exposure prophylaxis.

## 16. industrial toxicology /ɪnˈdʌstriəl ˌtɑksəˈkɑlədʒi/

**Core meaning (English):** study of how industrial chemicals, processes, and exposures affect workers and how those risks can be controlled.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** industrial toxicology nối chemical/process với biological harm và control.
**Grammar & collocations:** `industrial-toxicology assessment` — assessment; `evaluate chemical toxicity` — evaluate; `workplace toxicant` — toxicant.
**Examples:** `Industrial toxicology guided safer substitution for a solvent linked to nerve symptoms.` → Thay chemical nguy cơ cao.

## 17. psychosocial hazard /ˌsaɪkoʊˈsoʊʃəl ˈhæzərd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a work condition involving organization, relationships, demands, or insecurity that can harm mental or physical health.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** psychosocial hazard là risk từ cách work được tổ chức và quan hệ quyền lực.
**Grammar & collocations:** `psychosocial-hazard assessment` — assessment; `reduce work stressor` — reduce; `organizational demand` — demand.
**Examples:** `The psychosocial hazard assessment identified unpredictable shifts and punitive supervision.` → Hazard nằm trong schedule và management.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `심리사회적 위험` — psychosocial hazard.

## 18. ethical injury /ˈɛθɪkəl ˈɪndʒəri/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** lasting distress or disruption to a worker's moral identity after being forced to participate in, witness, or fail to prevent conduct that violates deeply held values.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** ethical injury là vết thương vào conscience khi work buộc người ta đi ngược value.
**Grammar & collocations:** `ethical-injury support` — support; `violate professional values` — violate; `moral conflict` — conflict.
**Examples:** `Ethical injury increased when staff repeatedly had to ration care without a transparent process.` → Ration không minh bạch làm conscience harm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `윤리적 손상` — ethical injury.

## 19. burnout prevention /ˈbɜrnˌaʊt prɪˈvɛnʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** organizational and individual measures that reduce chronic work exhaustion, detachment, and loss of effectiveness.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** burnout prevention phải sửa workload/system, không chỉ dạy worker self-care.
**Grammar & collocations:** `burnout-prevention strategy` — strategy; `reduce chronic exhaustion` — reduce; `workload redesign` — redesign.
**Examples:** `Burnout prevention combined staffing changes, protected breaks, supervision, and confidential support.` → Nhiều layer thay vì app thiền.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `번아웃 예방` — burnout prevention.

## 22. shift work disorder /ʃɪft wɜrk dɪˈsɔrdər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a sleep-wake disorder associated with work schedules that overlap the usual sleep period and disrupt functioning.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** shift work disorder là sleep/circadian problem gắn với schedule làm việc.
**Grammar & collocations:** `shift-work-disorder assessment` — assessment; `manage shift-related insomnia` — manage; `sleep schedule` — schedule.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `교대근무 수면장애` — shift work disorder.

## 23. circadian misalignment /sɜrˈkeɪdiən ˌmɪsəˈlaɪnmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** mismatch between the body's internal biological timing and the timing demanded by work, light, meals, or social schedules.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** circadian misalignment là body clock lệch với lịch work/life.
**Grammar & collocations:** `circadian-misalignment risk` — risk; `shift circadian timing` — shift; `biological clock` — clock.
**Examples:** `Circadian misalignment made night-shift recovery harder when bright light continued after work.` → Light sau shift cản recovery.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `일주기 불일치` — circadian misalignment.

## 24. heat stress /hiːt strɛs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** physiological strain caused by excessive environmental heat, humidity, workload, clothing, or inadequate recovery.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** heat stress là body không tản nhiệt đủ trong work context.
**Grammar & collocations:** `heat-stress prevention` — prevention; `schedule hydration breaks` — schedule; `thermal load` — load.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `열 스트레스` — heat stress.

## 25. occupational epidemiology /ˌɑkjəˈpeɪʃənəl ˌɛpɪˌdiːmiˈɑlədʒi/

**Core meaning (English):** study of how work exposures and conditions are distributed and associated with health outcomes in worker populations.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `직업 역학` — occupational epidemiology.

## Review in context — Part 1

**Occupational hygiene** uses the **hierarchy of controls** to reduce exposure below an **exposure limit**. **Fit testing** and **respiratory protection** matter when inhalation risk remains. An **ergonomic risk** can cause a **work-related musculoskeletal disorder**, while **presenteeism** and **sickness absence** show different effects of illness on work. A **return-to-work plan**, **fitness-for-work assessment**, and **workplace accommodation** can support recovery after an **occupational injury**. A **needlestick injury** may require rapid assessment and **post-exposure prophylaxis**.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 1:**

**Occupational hygiene** dùng **hierarchy of controls** để đưa exposure xuống dưới **exposure limit**. **Fit testing** và **respiratory protection** quan trọng khi risk hít vào còn lại. **Ergonomic risk** có thể tạo **work-related musculoskeletal disorder**, còn **presenteeism** và **sickness absence** cho thấy hai effect khác nhau của illness lên work. **Return-to-work plan**, **fitness-for-work assessment** và **workplace accommodation** hỗ trợ recovery sau **occupational injury**. **Needlestick injury** có thể cần assessment nhanh và **post-exposure prophylaxis**.

## Review in context — Part 2

**Industrial toxicology** studies chemical harm, while a **psychosocial hazard** may produce **ethical injury** or exhaustion. **Burnout prevention** should address work design. **Workplace violence** requires a **violence prevention plan**. **Shift work disorder** and **circadian misalignment** reveal the health cost of badly timed schedules, and **heat stress** requires environmental controls. **Occupational epidemiology** connects these exposures and conditions to patterns across worker populations.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 2:**

**Industrial toxicology** nghiên cứu harm từ chemical, còn **psychosocial hazard** có thể tạo **ethical injury** hoặc exhaustion. **Burnout prevention** nên sửa work design. **Workplace violence** cần **violence prevention plan**. **Shift work disorder** và **circadian misalignment** cho thấy cost health của schedule sai thời điểm, còn **heat stress** cần environmental control. **Occupational epidemiology** nối exposure/condition với pattern trong worker population.
