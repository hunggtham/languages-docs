# C2+ Vocabulary — Storage, flexibility, and demand forecasting

This lesson extends grid operations into storage duration, flexible demand, renewable uncertainty, and resource adequacy. The network is **capacity credit → firm capacity → seasonal storage → long-duration energy storage → power-to-gas → behind-the-meter storage → depth of discharge → levelized cost of storage → virtual power plant → aggregated distributed energy resource → resource adequacy → curtailment forecasting → renewable forecast error → probabilistic load forecast → net load ramp → duck curve → residual load → flexible demand → load shifting → valley filling**.

## 1. capacity credit /kəˈpæsəti ˈkrɛdɪt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the amount of dependable contribution that a resource can be counted on to provide during periods of system need.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** không phải nameplate capacity; là phần thật sự đáng tin trong giờ stress của grid.
**Grammar & collocations:** `renewable capacity credit` — credit; `capacity-credit value` — value; `assign a credit` — assign.
**Examples:** `The wind farm received a lower capacity credit than the gas plant because output varied during peak hours.` → Wind farm nhận capacity credit thấp hơn gas plant vì output biến động giờ peak.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `용량 기여도` — capacity credit.

## 2. firm capacity /fɜrm kəˈpæsəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** generation or demand-reduction capability that can be relied on to be available at a specified time under defined conditions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** capacity “có thể ký tên đảm bảo” trong condition đã nêu, khác với potential output không chắc.
**Grammar & collocations:** `firm-capacity obligation` — obligation; `procure firm capacity` — procure; `firm-capacity contract` — contract.
**Examples:** `The utility procured firm capacity for the hottest summer evenings.` → Utility procure firm capacity cho các tối mùa hè nóng nhất.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `확정 용량` — firm capacity.

## 3. seasonal storage /ˈsiːzənəl ˈstɔrɪdʒ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** storage that shifts energy across weeks or months to balance seasonal differences between supply and demand.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** giữ energy từ mùa dư sang mùa thiếu, như “kho” nối summer solar với winter demand.
**Grammar & collocations:** `seasonal-storage reservoir` — reservoir; `seasonal storage cycle` — cycle; `store energy seasonally` — store.
**Examples:** `Seasonal storage reduced the need to build fossil capacity for a short winter peak.` → Seasonal storage giảm nhu cầu xây fossil capacity cho winter peak ngắn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `계절 저장` — seasonal storage.

## 4. long-duration energy storage /lɔŋ dʊˈreɪʃən ˈɛnərdʒi ˈstɔrɪdʒ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** energy storage designed to discharge for many hours or longer rather than only for short power bursts.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** storage không chỉ vài phút; nó cover evening, multi-hour shortage hoặc multi-day event.
**Grammar & collocations:** `long-duration energy-storage technology` — technology; `long-duration discharge` — discharge; `deploy long-duration storage` — deploy.
**Examples:** `Long-duration energy storage carried renewable power through a three-day weather system.` → Long-duration energy storage mang renewable power qua weather system kéo dài ba ngày.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `장주기 에너지 저장` — long-duration energy storage.

## 5. power-to-gas /ˈpaʊər tə ɡæs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** conversion of electricity into a gaseous energy carrier such as hydrogen or synthetic methane for storage, industry, or later reconversion.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** biến electron dư thành molecule gas để cất lâu hoặc dùng sector khác.
**Grammar & collocations:** `power-to-gas pathway` — pathway; `power-to-hydrogen` — hydrogen; `convert electricity to gas` — convert.
**Examples:** `Power-to-gas absorbed surplus wind electricity when transmission capacity was constrained.` → Power-to-gas hấp thụ wind electricity dư khi transmission capacity bị constraint.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `전력-가스 전환` — power-to-gas.

## 6. behind-the-meter storage /bɪˈhaɪnd ðə ˈmiːtər ˈstɔrɪdʒ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** energy storage located on the customer's side of the utility meter and operated partly for on-site needs.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** battery nằm “sau meter” của building, có thể self-consume, shave peak hoặc backup.
**Grammar & collocations:** `behind-the-meter battery` — battery; `behind-the-meter storage system` — system; `operate behind the meter` — operate.
**Examples:** `Behind-the-meter storage reduced the factory's demand charge during the afternoon peak.` → Behind-the-meter storage giảm demand charge của factory lúc afternoon peak.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `계량기 후단 저장` — behind-the-meter storage.

## 7. depth of discharge /dɛpθ əv dɪsˈtʃɑrdʒ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the fraction of a battery's usable capacity removed during a discharge cycle.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mỗi cycle rút battery xuống sâu đến đâu; discharge sâu thường ảnh hưởng lifetime và usable energy.
**Grammar & collocations:** `maximum depth of discharge` — depth; `limit discharge depth` — limit; `deep-discharge cycle` — cycle.
**Examples:** `The controller limited depth of discharge to extend the battery's service life.` → Controller giới hạn depth of discharge để kéo dài service life.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `방전 깊이` — depth of discharge.

## 8. levelized cost of storage /ˈlɛvəlaɪzd kɔst əv ˈstɔrɪdʒ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an average cost per unit of energy delivered from a storage asset over its lifetime, including capital, operation, efficiency, and replacement assumptions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** quy đổi toàn bộ lifetime cost thành cost của mỗi kWh discharge để compare technology.
**Grammar & collocations:** `levelized cost of storage estimate` — estimate; `LCOS comparison` — comparison; `calculate storage cost` — calculate.
**Examples:** `The levelized cost of storage fell when the project achieved more annual cycles.` → Levelized cost of storage giảm khi project đạt nhiều cycle mỗi năm hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `균등화 저장 비용` — levelized cost of storage.

## 9. virtual power plant /ˈvɜrtʃuəl ˈpaʊər plænt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a coordinated network of distributed generators, batteries, flexible loads, or electric vehicles operated as if they were one grid resource.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** nhiều asset nhỏ được software gom thành một “nhà máy ảo” có thể bid và respond.
**Grammar & collocations:** `virtual-power-plant platform` — platform; `aggregate a VPP` — aggregate; `VPP dispatch` — dispatch.
**Examples:** `The virtual power plant dispatched home batteries during the system peak.` → Virtual power plant dispatch home battery trong system peak.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `가상 발전소` — virtual power plant.

## 10. aggregated distributed energy resource /ˈæɡrəɡeɪtɪd dɪˈstrɪbjətɪd ˈɛnərdʒi rɪˈsɔrs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a set of geographically dispersed small resources combined under one operational or market representation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** rooftop solar, EV, battery và flexible load được aggregate thành một DER package.
**Grammar & collocations:** `aggregated-DER operator` — operator; `register an aggregated resource` — register; `DER aggregation` — aggregation.
**Examples:** `The aggregated distributed energy resource offered both load reduction and stored energy.` → Aggregated distributed energy resource offer load reduction và stored energy.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `집계된 분산 에너지 자원` — aggregated DER.

## 11. resource adequacy /rɪˈsɔrs ˌædəˈkwəsi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the ability of a power system to have enough available capacity to meet demand and a reliability standard under expected or stressed conditions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** grid có đủ resource “để sống sót” trong peak, outage và weather event theo reliability target không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `resource-adequacy assessment` — assessment; `resource-adequacy margin` — margin; `ensure adequacy` — ensure.
**Examples:** `The adequacy assessment included correlated outages during the cold-weather event.` → Adequacy assessment tính correlated outage trong cold-weather event.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `자원 적정성` — resource adequacy.

## 12. curtailment forecasting /kərˈteɪlmənt ˈfɔrˌkæstɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** prediction of when and how much potential generation will be reduced because of congestion, oversupply, or system limits.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** forecast phần renewable có thể bị “cắt” trước khi event xảy ra để storage/trade chuẩn bị.
**Grammar & collocations:** `renewable-curtailment forecasting` — forecasting; `forecast curtailed energy` — forecast; `curtailment probability` — probability.
**Examples:** `Curtailment forecasting helped the operator schedule charging before the midday solar surplus.` → Curtailment forecasting giúp operator schedule charging trước solar surplus buổi trưa.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `출력 제한 예측` — curtailment forecasting.

## 13. renewable forecast error /rɪˈnuːəbəl ˈfɔrˌkæst ˈɛrər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the difference between predicted and actual renewable generation over a specified horizon.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** gap giữa wind/solar forecast và output thật, cần reserve hoặc storage để cover.
**Grammar & collocations:** `renewable-forecast-error distribution` — distribution; `reduce forecast error` — reduce; `forecast-error reserve` — reserve.
**Examples:** `The battery absorbed the renewable forecast error when clouds crossed the solar field.` → Battery hấp thụ renewable forecast error khi mây đi qua solar field.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `재생에너지 예측 오차` — renewable forecast error.

## 14. probabilistic load forecast /ˌprɑbəbɪˈlɪstɪk loʊd ˈfɔrˌkæst/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a demand forecast expressed as a distribution or range of possible outcomes rather than one point estimate.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** forecast kèm uncertainty band, cho operator biết nhiều khả năng demand và tail risk.
**Grammar & collocations:** `probabilistic-load-forecast model` — model; `prediction interval` — interval; `forecast demand probabilistically` — forecast.
**Examples:** `The probabilistic load forecast showed a wide upper tail during the heat wave.` → Probabilistic load forecast cho thấy upper tail rộng trong heat wave.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `확률적 부하 예측` — probabilistic load forecast.

## 15. net load ramp /nɛt loʊd ræmp/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the rate of change in demand that remains after variable renewable generation has been subtracted from total load.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** operator phải serve phần load còn lại sau solar/wind; net load ramp cho biết nó tăng/giảm nhanh ra sao.
**Grammar & collocations:** `evening net-load ramp` — ramp; `net-load-ramp requirement` — requirement; `manage the ramp` — manage.
**Examples:** `The evening net load ramp required flexible gas units and battery discharge.` → Evening net load ramp cần flexible gas unit và battery discharge.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `순부하 램프` — net load ramp.

## 16. duck curve /dʌk kɜrv/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a daily net-load pattern with a midday dip from solar generation and a steep evening rise as solar output falls.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** đường net load trông như con vịt: bụng thấp buổi trưa, cổ dốc lên buổi tối.
**Grammar & collocations:** `duck-curve effect` — effect; `steepen the duck curve` — steepen; `manage duck-curve ramps` — manage.
**Examples:** `Flexible demand softened the duck curve by moving refrigeration load into midday hours.` → Flexible demand làm mềm duck curve bằng cách chuyển refrigeration load vào midday.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `덕 커브` — duck curve.

## 17. residual load /rɪˈzɪdʒuəl loʊd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** electricity demand remaining after generation from variable renewable resources has been deducted.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** phần load “còn lại” mà dispatchable resource/storage phải phục vụ.
**Grammar & collocations:** `residual-load profile` — profile; `forecast residual load` — forecast; `residual-load variability` — variability.
**Examples:** `The residual load was lowest when solar and wind production overlapped.` → Residual load thấp nhất khi solar và wind production overlap.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `잔여 부하` — residual load.

## 18. flexible demand /ˈflɛksəbəl dɪˈmænd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** electricity consumption that can be shifted, reduced, or increased in response to price, grid conditions, or an operator signal.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** demand có “độ co giãn”: water heater, EV hoặc industrial process đổi timing mà vẫn hoàn thành service.
**Grammar & collocations:** `flexible-demand resource` — resource; `activate flexible demand` — activate; `demand flexibility` — flexibility.
**Examples:** `Flexible demand reduced the need to start an expensive peaker.` → Flexible demand giảm nhu cầu start peaker đắt.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `유연한 수요` — flexible demand.

## 19. load shifting /loʊd ˈʃɪftɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** moving electricity consumption from one time period to another without necessarily reducing total energy use.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** không xóa load mà dời nó khỏi peak sang lúc grid dư/rẻ.
**Grammar & collocations:** `industrial load shifting` — shifting; `load-shifting program` — program; `shift consumption` — shift.
**Examples:** `Load shifting moved the data center's cooling work away from the evening peak.` → Load shifting dời cooling work của data center khỏi evening peak.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `부하 이동` — load shifting.

## 20. valley filling /ˈvæli ˈfɪlɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** increasing electricity use during low-demand periods to make a load profile flatter and use otherwise underutilized capacity.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** “lấp thung lũng” demand bằng EV charging hoặc flexible load lúc grid nhàn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `valley-filling strategy` — strategy; `valley-filling charge` — charge; `fill off-peak demand` — fill.
**Examples:** `Valley filling used overnight charging to improve utilization of the distribution network.` → Valley filling dùng overnight charging để tăng utilization của distribution network.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `골짜기 채우기` — valley filling.

## Review in context

Reliability planners distinguish **capacity credit** from **firm capacity** and use **seasonal storage** or **long-duration energy storage** to cover extended shortages. **Power-to-gas** and **behind-the-meter storage** add flexibility, while **depth of discharge** and **levelized cost of storage** shape project economics. A **virtual power plant** can coordinate an **aggregated distributed energy resource** to improve **resource adequacy**. Operators use **curtailment forecasting**, account for **renewable forecast error**, and build a **probabilistic load forecast**. The **net load ramp**, **duck curve**, and **residual load** reveal when the system needs flexibility. **Flexible demand**, **load shifting**, and **valley filling** move consumption toward hours when renewable power and network capacity are available.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

Planner về reliability phân biệt **capacity credit** với **firm capacity** và dùng **seasonal storage** hoặc **long-duration energy storage** để cover shortage kéo dài. **Power-to-gas** và **behind-the-meter storage** thêm flexibility, còn **depth of discharge** và **levelized cost of storage** định hình economics của project. **Virtual power plant** có thể điều phối **aggregated distributed energy resource** để tăng **resource adequacy**. Operator dùng **curtailment forecasting**, tính **renewable forecast error** và xây **probabilistic load forecast**. **Net load ramp**, **duck curve** và **residual load** cho thấy khi nào system cần flexibility. **Flexible demand**, **load shifting** và **valley filling** dời consumption sang giờ renewable power và network capacity sẵn có.
