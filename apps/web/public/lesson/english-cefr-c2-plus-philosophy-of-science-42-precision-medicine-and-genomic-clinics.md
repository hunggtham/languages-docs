# C2+ Vocabulary — Precision medicine and genomic clinics

This lesson follows how genomic evidence is interpreted, communicated, and connected to individualized clinical decisions. The network is **precision medicine → drug-gene matching → treatment-selection assay → biomarker stratification → molecular tumor board → variant of uncertain significance → unexpected variant finding → clinically actionable mutation → penetrance → polygenic risk score → ancestry bias → genomic comparison panel → genomic privacy → identity inference risk → family follow-up testing → return of results → additional genomic finding → genomic counseling → gene-environment interaction → clinical utility → analytical validity → clinical validity → diagnostic yield → variant classification → therapeutic matching**.

## 1. precision medicine /prɪˈsɪʒən ˈmɛdəsən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an approach that uses individual biological, environmental, and clinical differences to tailor prevention, diagnosis, or treatment.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** precision medicine chọn care theo profile riêng thay vì một treatment cho tất cả.
**Grammar & collocations:** `precision-medicine program` — program; `tailor treatment` — tailor; `individual profile` — profile.
**Examples:** `Precision medicine matched the treatment plan to the patient's tumor profile and kidney function.` → Plan dựa trên nhiều profile.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `정밀의료` — precision medicine.

## 2. drug-gene matching /drʌɡ dʒiː ˈmætʃɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** selection or adjustment of a medicine based on genetic differences that affect response, metabolism, or risk.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** drug-gene matching nối gene profile với thuốc phù hợp.
**Grammar & collocations:** `drug-gene-matching report` — report; `match a medicine to genotype` — match; `metabolism profile` — profile.
**Examples:** `Drug-gene matching warned that the standard dose could accumulate dangerously in this patient.` → Gene ảnh hưởng metabolism và dose.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `약물-유전자 매칭` — drug-gene matching.

## 3. treatment-selection assay /ˈtriːtmənt səˈlɛkʃən ˈæseɪ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a test used to determine whether a patient is likely to benefit from a particular treatment.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** treatment-selection assay giúp chọn treatment trước khi dùng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `treatment-selection-assay result` — result; `select an eligible therapy` — select; `predict treatment response` — predict.
**Examples:** `The treatment-selection assay identified patients likely to respond to the targeted therapy.` → Test chọn nhóm có khả năng response.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `치료 선택 검사` — treatment-selection assay.

## 4. biomarker stratification /ˈbaɪoʊˌmɑrkər ˌstrætəfəˈkeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** division of patients into biologically meaningful groups using measured markers before comparing risk or treatment effects.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** biomarker stratification chia patient thành group theo marker để so sánh đúng hơn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `biomarker-stratification scheme` — scheme; `stratify a patient group` — stratify; `molecular marker` — marker.
**Examples:** `Biomarker stratification showed that the treatment effect was concentrated in one molecular subgroup.` → Effect tập trung ở subgroup.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `바이오마커 층화` — biomarker stratification.

## 5. molecular tumor board /məˈlɛkjələr ˈtuːmər bɔrd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a multidisciplinary team that interprets tumor genomic data and recommends possible targeted treatment or further testing.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** molecular tumor board gom oncologist, pathologist, geneticist và pharmacist để đọc tumor data.
**Grammar & collocations:** `molecular-tumor-board review` — review; `interpret tumor sequencing` — interpret; `multidisciplinary recommendation` — recommendation.
**Examples:** `The molecular tumor board recommended a trial because no approved therapy matched the mutation.` → Board tìm trial khi không có thuốc approved.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `분자 종양 위원회` — molecular tumor board.

## 6. variant of uncertain significance /ˈvɛriənt əv ʌnˈsɜrtən ˈsɪɡnəfɪkəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a genetic variant whose relationship to disease risk or treatment response is not yet established.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** variant of uncertain significance là finding chưa biết có harmful hay không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `variant-of-uncertain-significance report` — report; `classify a variant` — classify; `insufficient evidence` — evidence.
**Examples:** `The report labeled the change a variant of uncertain significance and advised against changing treatment on that basis alone.` → Không đổi treatment chỉ vì VUS.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `임상적 의미 불확실 변이` — variant of uncertain significance.

## 7. unexpected variant finding /ˌʌnɪkˈspɛktɪd ˈvɛriənt ˈfaɪndɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a genetic result discovered during testing for another purpose that was not the original target of the analysis.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** unexpected variant finding là finding ngoài mục tiêu ban đầu.
**Grammar & collocations:** `unexpected-variant-finding protocol` — protocol; `discover an unplanned result` — discover; `return policy` — policy.
**Examples:** `The unexpected variant finding raised a separate question about inherited cardiac risk.` → Finding ngoài mục tiêu mở risk khác.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `예상 밖 변이 발견` — unexpected variant finding.

## 8. clinically actionable mutation /ˈklɪnɪkli ˈækʃənəbəl mjuːˈteɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a genetic change for which an evidence-supported clinical action, such as treatment, surveillance, or prevention, is available.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** clinically actionable mutation là mutation có action thật, không chỉ interesting về biology.
**Grammar & collocations:** `clinically-actionable-mutation report` — report; `act on a mutation` — act; `targeted therapy` — therapy.
**Examples:** `The laboratory confirmed a clinically actionable mutation and expedited specialist referral.` → Mutation có action nên referral nhanh.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `임상적으로 실행 가능한 돌연변이` — clinically actionable mutation.

## 9. penetrance /ˈpɛnətrəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the proportion of people with a particular genetic variant who actually develop the associated trait or disease.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** penetrance hỏi có gene thì bao nhiêu người thật sự biểu hiện disease.
**Grammar & collocations:** `penetrance estimate` — estimate; `high-penetrance variant` — variant; `develop a phenotype` — develop.
**Examples:** `The counseling session explained that a high-risk variant did not guarantee disease because penetrance was incomplete.` → Gene risk cao không phải guarantee.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `침투도` — penetrance.

## 10. polygenic risk score /ˌpɑlidʒɛnɪk rɪsk skɔr/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a score combining the small effects of many genetic variants to estimate relative risk for a trait or disease.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** polygenic risk score cộng nhiều variant nhỏ thành risk estimate.
**Grammar & collocations:** `polygenic-risk-score model` — model; `estimate inherited susceptibility` — estimate; `variant weight` — weight.
**Examples:** `The polygenic risk score was used as one factor alongside family history and blood pressure.` → Score chỉ là một factor.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `다유전자 위험 점수` — polygenic risk score.

## 11. ancestry bias /ˈænsəstri baɪəs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** systematic error that occurs when genomic datasets or risk models represent some ancestral populations better than others.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** ancestry bias làm model chính xác hơn cho group có data nhiều.
**Grammar & collocations:** `ancestry-bias audit` — audit; `underrepresent an ancestry group` — underrepresent; `model performance gap` — gap.
**Examples:** `Ancestry bias made the risk score less reliable for patients missing from the training database.` → Thiếu data làm score kém tin cậy.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `조상 배경 편향` — ancestry bias.

## 12. genomic comparison panel /dʒiˈnoʊmɪk kəmˈpɛrəsən ˈpænəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a reference collection of genetic sequences or variant frequencies used to interpret an individual's genomic result.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** genomic comparison panel là bộ reference để so genome patient.
**Grammar & collocations:** `genomic-comparison-panel database` — database; `compare variant frequencies` — compare; `reference population` — population.
**Examples:** `The genomic comparison panel lacked local data, increasing uncertainty about the rare variant.` → Panel thiếu local data.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `유전체 비교 패널` — genomic comparison panel.

## 13. genomic privacy /dʒiˈnoʊmɪk ˈpraɪvəsi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** protection of a person's genetic information from unauthorized access, use, disclosure, or inference.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** genomic privacy bảo vệ data gene vì nó liên quan cả individual và family.
**Grammar & collocations:** `genomic-privacy policy` — policy; `protect genetic data` — protect; `authorized access` — access.
**Examples:** `Genomic privacy required stricter access controls than an ordinary appointment record.` → Genome nhạy cảm hơn record thường.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `유전체 개인정보 보호` — genomic privacy.

## 14. identity inference risk /aɪˈdɛntəti ˈɪnfərəns rɪsk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the possibility that a person's identity can be deduced from supposedly de-identified genetic or linked data.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** identity inference risk là khả năng ghép data để đoán đúng người.
**Grammar & collocations:** `identity-inference-risk assessment` — assessment; `infer an identity` — infer; `linked dataset` — dataset.
**Examples:** `Identity inference risk remained after names were removed because relatives' records could narrow the match.` → Xóa name chưa đủ vì data family.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `신원 추론 위험` — identity inference risk.

## 15. family follow-up testing /ˈfæməli ˈfɑloʊ ʌp ˈtɛstɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** genetic testing offered to relatives after a clinically relevant familial variant has been identified.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** family follow-up testing mở rộng từ patient index sang người thân có risk.
**Grammar & collocations:** `family-follow-up-testing pathway` — pathway; `test at-risk relatives` — test; `familial variant` — variant.
**Examples:** `Family follow-up testing found two relatives who could benefit from earlier surveillance.` → Testing giúp surveillance sớm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `가족 추적 검사` — family follow-up testing.

## 16. return of results /rɪˈtɜrn əv rɪˈzʌlts/

**Core meaning (English):** the process of communicating a participant's research or clinical findings in a way that is understandable, timely, and ethically appropriate.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** return of results là đưa result trở lại cho người cung cấp sample.

## 17. additional genomic finding /əˈdɪʃənəl dʒiˈnoʊmɪk ˈfaɪndɪŋ/

**Core meaning (English):** a genetic result beyond the primary question that may nevertheless have established medical relevance.

## 18. genomic counseling /dʒiˈnoʊmɪk ˈkaʊnsəlɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** professional guidance that helps people understand genetic information, uncertainty, family implications, and possible choices.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** genomic counseling nối result với meaning, family và decision.
**Grammar & collocations:** `genomic-counseling session` — session; `explain inherited risk` — explain; `family implication` — implication.

## 19. gene-environment interaction /dʒiːn ɪnˈvaɪrənmənt ˌɪntərˈækʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a situation in which genetic differences alter the effect of an environmental exposure, or an exposure changes how genetic risk is expressed.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** gene-environment interaction là gene và environment cùng tạo outcome.
**Grammar & collocations:** `gene-environment-interaction model` — model; `modify genetic risk` — modify; `environmental exposure` — exposure.
**Examples:** `The study examined a gene-environment interaction involving air pollution and asthma susceptibility.` → Gene và pollution cùng ảnh hưởng asthma.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `유전자-환경 상호작용` — gene-environment interaction.

## 20. clinical utility /ˈklɪnɪkəl juːˈtɪləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the extent to which a test or result improves real clinical decisions, outcomes, or patient management.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** clinical utility hỏi test có làm care tốt hơn thật không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `clinical-utility assessment` — assessment; `improve clinical decisions` — improve; `management benefit` — benefit.
**Examples:** `The genomic panel had analytical promise but uncertain clinical utility because no treatment changed after testing.` → Test tốt về lab nhưng chưa đổi care.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `임상적 유용성` — clinical utility.

## 21. analytical validity /ˌænəˈlɪtɪkəl vəˈlɪdəti/

**Core meaning (English):** the accuracy, reliability, and reproducibility with which a test measures or identifies the biological feature it claims to measure.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** analytical validity hỏi test đo đúng gene/variant không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `analytical-validity study` — study; `validate a laboratory assay` — validate; `measurement accuracy` — accuracy.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `분석적 타당도` — analytical validity.

## 22. clinical validity /ˈklɪnɪkəl vəˈlɪdəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the accuracy with which a test result predicts or identifies a clinical condition, risk, or response.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** clinical validity hỏi result có liên hệ đúng với disease/risk/response không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `clinical-validity evidence` — evidence; `predict disease risk` — predict; `clinical association` — association.
**Examples:** `Clinical validity was limited because the variant had been studied in only a small population.` → Data population nhỏ nên validity hạn chế.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `임상적 타당도` — clinical validity.

## 23. diagnostic yield /ˌdaɪəɡˈnɑstɪk jiːld/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the proportion of tested patients for whom a diagnostic test produces a result that explains the condition or changes management.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** diagnostic yield đo test tìm được answer hữu ích cho bao nhiêu patient.
**Grammar & collocations:** `diagnostic-yield estimate` — estimate; `increase diagnostic yield` — increase; `explanatory result` — result.
**Examples:** `Diagnostic yield rose when the panel included structural variants as well as single-letter changes.` → Panel rộng hơn tìm được nhiều answer.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `진단 수율` — diagnostic yield.

## 24. variant classification /ˈvɛriənt ˌklæsəfəˈkeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the process of assigning a genetic variant to a category such as benign, likely pathogenic, or pathogenic using evidence and defined criteria.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** variant classification biến raw variant thành category có ý nghĩa clinical.
**Grammar & collocations:** `variant-classification guideline` — guideline; `classify a genetic change` — classify; `evidence criterion` — criterion.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `변이 분류` — variant classification.

## 25. therapeutic matching /ˌθɛrəˈpjuːtɪk ˈmætʃɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** alignment of a patient's biological or clinical profile with a treatment expected to provide the greatest relevant benefit.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** therapeutic matching nối patient profile với therapy phù hợp nhất.
**Grammar & collocations:** `therapeutic-matching algorithm` — algorithm; `match a patient to therapy` — match; `targeted option` — option.
**Examples:** `Therapeutic matching avoided an ineffective drug and directed the patient toward an appropriate trial.` → Profile giúp tránh thuốc không hiệu quả.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `치료 매칭` — therapeutic matching.

## Review in context — Part 1

**Precision medicine** can use **drug-gene matching**, a **treatment-selection assay**, and **biomarker stratification** to tailor care. A **molecular tumor board** may interpret a **variant of uncertain significance**, an **unexpected variant finding**, or a **clinically actionable mutation**. **Penetrance** and a **polygenic risk score** require careful explanation, especially when **ancestry bias** affects a **genomic comparison panel**. **Genomic privacy** and **identity inference risk** matter because genetic data can reveal information about relatives as well as the tested patient.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 1:**

**Precision medicine** có thể dùng **drug-gene matching**, **treatment-selection assay** và **biomarker stratification** để tailor care. **Molecular tumor board** có thể giải thích **variant of uncertain significance**, **unexpected variant finding** hoặc **clinically actionable mutation**. **Penetrance** và **polygenic risk score** cần được giải thích cẩn thận, nhất là khi **ancestry bias** ảnh hưởng **genomic comparison panel**. **Genomic privacy** và **identity inference risk** quan trọng vì genomic data có thể lộ thông tin về relatives ngoài patient được test.

## Review in context — Part 2

**Family follow-up testing**, **return of results**, and an **additional genomic finding** require thoughtful **genomic counseling**. A **gene-environment interaction** can complicate interpretation, so **clinical utility** must be distinguished from **analytical validity** and **clinical validity**. **Diagnostic yield** describes useful answers, while **variant classification** organizes evidence. Ultimately, **therapeutic matching** asks whether the result improves a real treatment choice rather than merely producing an interesting report.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 2:**

**Family follow-up testing**, **return of results** và **additional genomic finding** cần **genomic counseling** cẩn thận. **Gene-environment interaction** có thể làm interpretation phức tạp, nên phải tách **clinical utility** khỏi **analytical validity** và **clinical validity**. **Diagnostic yield** mô tả số answer hữu ích, còn **variant classification** sắp xếp evidence. Cuối cùng, **therapeutic matching** hỏi result có cải thiện choice treatment thật không, chứ không chỉ tạo một report thú vị.
