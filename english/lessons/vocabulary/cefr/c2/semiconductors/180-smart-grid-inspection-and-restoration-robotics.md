# C2 Vocabulary — Smart-grid inspection and restoration robotics

This lesson follows an electric grid from substation inspection and line maintenance to fault response, storm recovery, and microgrid control. The network is **substation patrol robot → transformer inspection drone → transmission line climbing robot → breaker maintenance robot → switchgear thermal scan → grid battery inspection → underground cable survey robot → power pole climbing robot → insulator cleaning robot → fault locator drone → grid restoration robot → mobile substation robot → reactive power sensor robot → power quality monitoring drone → corona discharge inspection → vegetation clearance robot → line tension measurement robot → storm damage assessment drone → microgrid control assistant → black start support robot**.

## 1. substation patrol robot /ˈsʌbˌsteɪʃən pəˈtroʊl ˈroʊbɑːt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a robot that patrols a substation for equipment alarms, intrusion, heat, or damage.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** robot patrol substation tìm alarm, intrusion, heat hoặc damage.
**Grammar & collocations:** `substation-patrol-robot route` — route; `check a transformer bay` — check transformer bay.
**Examples:** `The substation patrol robot reported heat near a cable termination.` → Robot patrol substation báo heat gần cable termination.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `변전소 순찰 로봇`.

## Review in context

A **substation patrol robot**, **transformer inspection drone**, and **transmission line climbing robot** inspect grid assets, while a **breaker maintenance robot** and **switchgear thermal scan** check switching equipment. **Grid battery inspection**, an **underground cable survey robot**, a **power pole climbing robot**, and an **insulator cleaning robot** support continuity. A **fault locator drone**, **grid restoration robot**, and **mobile substation robot** respond to failure. A **reactive power sensor robot**, **power quality monitoring drone**, and **corona discharge inspection** measure electrical condition. A **vegetation clearance robot**, **line tension measurement robot**, **storm damage assessment drone**, **microgrid control assistant**, and **black start support robot** connect prevention with recovery.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:** **Substation patrol robot**, **transformer inspection drone** và **transmission line climbing robot** inspect grid asset, còn **breaker maintenance robot** và **switchgear thermal scan** check switching equipment. **Grid battery inspection**, **underground cable survey robot**, **power pole climbing robot** và **insulator cleaning robot** hỗ trợ continuity. **Fault locator drone**, **grid restoration robot** và **mobile substation robot** response khi failure. **Reactive power sensor robot**, **power quality monitoring drone** và **corona discharge inspection** đo electrical condition. **Vegetation clearance robot**, **line tension measurement robot**, **storm damage assessment drone**, **microgrid control assistant** và **black start support robot** nối prevention với recovery.
