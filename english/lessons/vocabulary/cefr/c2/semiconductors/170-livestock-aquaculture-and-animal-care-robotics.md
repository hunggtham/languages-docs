# C2 Vocabulary — Livestock, aquaculture, and animal-care robotics

This lesson follows animal production from monitoring and feeding to cleaning, health checks, aquaculture, and welfare review. The network is **livestock monitoring robot → milking parlor robot → feed dispensing robot → barn cleaning robot → herd health scanner → rumination monitoring collar → automated hoof trimming → poultry house robot → egg collection robot → hatchery sorting robot → aquaculture feeding robot → fish health inspection robot → water quality aquaculture robot → manure separation robot → pasture mapping drone → fence inspection robot → veterinary handling robot → livestock weighing platform → disease outbreak monitoring → animal welfare audit robot**.

## 1. livestock monitoring robot /ˈlaɪvˌstɑːk ˈmɑːnətərɪŋ ˈroʊbɑːt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a robot that observes animals, their movement, environment, or routine behavior.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** robot observe animal, movement, environment hoặc routine behavior.
**Grammar & collocations:** `livestock-monitoring-robot camera` — camera; `track a herd movement` — track herd movement.
**Examples:** `The livestock monitoring robot noticed unusual isolation in one calf.` → Livestock monitoring robot notice calf bị isolate bất thường.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `가축 모니터링 로봇`.

## Review in context

A **livestock monitoring robot**, **milking parlor robot**, **feed dispensing robot**, and **barn cleaning robot** support daily herd care. A **herd health scanner**, **rumination monitoring collar**, and **automated hoof trimming** reveal or address individual health needs. A **poultry house robot**, **egg collection robot**, and **hatchery sorting robot** support birds, while an **aquaculture feeding robot**, **fish health inspection robot**, and **water quality aquaculture robot** support fish. A **manure separation robot**, **pasture mapping drone**, and **fence inspection robot** maintain the environment. A **veterinary handling robot**, **livestock weighing platform**, **disease outbreak monitoring**, and **animal welfare audit robot** connect treatment, measurement, prevention, and welfare.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:** **Livestock monitoring robot**, **milking parlor robot**, **feed dispensing robot** và **barn cleaning robot** hỗ trợ chăm herd hằng ngày. **Herd health scanner**, **rumination monitoring collar** và **automated hoof trimming** reveal/xử lý need sức khỏe cá thể. **Poultry house robot**, **egg collection robot** và **hatchery sorting robot** hỗ trợ bird, còn **aquaculture feeding robot**, **fish health inspection robot** và **water quality aquaculture robot** hỗ trợ fish. **Manure separation robot**, **pasture mapping drone** và **fence inspection robot** duy trì environment. **Veterinary handling robot**, **livestock weighing platform**, **disease outbreak monitoring** và **animal welfare audit robot** nối treatment, measurement, prevention với welfare.
