# C2 Vocabulary — Sports robotics and athletic performance analysis

This lesson follows sports robots from training and capture to venue operations and performance evidence. The network is **sports robot → athletic training robot → robotic referee assistant → ball tracking robot → athlete motion capture → biomechanical analysis robot → automated camera robot → stadium inspection robot → arena cleaning robot → event crowd monitoring → sports analytics drone → robotic coaching assistant → training load estimation → pose quality assessment → motion skill assessment → reaction time measurement → force plate robot → wearable sensor calibration → injury risk monitoring → rehabilitation training robot**.

## 1. sports robot /spɔːrts ˈroʊbɑːt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a robot used for sports training, analysis, officiating, venue service, or athlete support.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** robot dùng cho training, analysis, officiating, venue service hoặc athlete support.
**Grammar & collocations:** `sports-robot platform` — platform; `support a training session` — hỗ trợ training session.
**Examples:** `The sports robot returned balls at a consistent speed during the drill.` → Sports robot trả ball với speed đều trong drill.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `스포츠 로봇`.

## 2. athletic training robot /æθˈletɪk ˈtreɪnɪŋ ˈroʊbɑːt/

**Core meaning (English):** a robot that delivers repeatable physical practice for an athlete.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** robot cung cấp physical practice lặp lại, nhất quán cho athlete.
**Grammar & collocations:** `athletic-training-robot drill` — drill; `vary a training challenge` — thay đổi challenge.
**Examples:** `The athletic training robot increased the return angle after the player adapted.` → Athletic training robot tăng return angle sau khi player thích nghi.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `운동 훈련 로봇`.

## Review in context

A **sports robot** or **athletic training robot** can support drills, while a **robotic referee assistant**, **ball tracking robot**, and **athlete motion capture** system measure action. A **biomechanical analysis robot**, **automated camera robot**, **stadium inspection robot**, and **arena cleaning robot** support evidence and venue operations; **event crowd monitoring** protects spectators. A **sports analytics drone** and **robotic coaching assistant** turn footage into feedback. The resulting **training load estimation**, **pose quality assessment**, **motion skill assessment**, and **reaction time measurement** evaluate performance. A **force plate robot**, **wearable sensor calibration**, **injury risk monitoring**, and **rehabilitation training robot** connect analysis to safer training.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:** **Sports robot** hoặc **athletic training robot** hỗ trợ drill, còn **robotic referee assistant**, **ball tracking robot** và **athlete motion capture** đo action. **Biomechanical analysis robot**, **automated camera robot**, **stadium inspection robot** và **arena cleaning robot** hỗ trợ evidence/venue; **event crowd monitoring** bảo vệ spectator. **Sports analytics drone** và **robotic coaching assistant** biến footage thành feedback. Các phép **training load estimation**, **pose quality assessment**, **motion skill assessment** và **reaction time measurement** đánh giá performance. **Force plate robot**, **wearable sensor calibration**, **injury risk monitoring** và **rehabilitation training robot** nối analysis với training an toàn hơn.
