# C2 Vocabulary — Precision-clock interrogation and stability diagnostics

This lesson connects quantum-state preparation, clock interrogation, field shifts, servo behavior, and time-domain stability.

Flow: **spin-squeezing parameter → interrogation contrast → differential Stark shift → Zeeman sensitivity → magnetic-field bias → bias-field reversal → dead-time aliasing → Dick-effect contribution → servo bump → phase accumulator → cycle-slip detector → time-domain stability → overlapping Allan deviation → optical pumping efficiency → state-preparation fidelity → projection-noise floor → interrogation dead time → frequency-error integrator → phase-step response → frequency-step response**.

## 1. spin-squeezing parameter /spɪn ˈskwiːzɪŋ pəˈræmətər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a measure of reduced collective spin noise relative to the standard quantum limit.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** measure spin noise collective giảm so với standard quantum limit.
**Grammar & collocations:** `spin-squeezing-parameter estimate` — estimate; `reduce projection noise` — giảm projection noise.
**Examples:** `The spin-squeezing parameter predicted improved frequency sensitivity for the ensemble.` → Parameter squeezing-spin dự đoán frequency sensitivity tốt hơn cho ensemble.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `스핀 압착 매개변수`.

## 2. interrogation contrast /ɪnˌterəˈɡeɪʃən ˈkɑːntræst/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** amplitude contrast of the measured fringe after a clock interrogation sequence.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** amplitude contrast của fringe đo sau clock interrogation sequence.
**Grammar & collocations:** `interrogation-contrast decay` — decay; `fit a fringe amplitude` — fit fringe amplitude.
**Examples:** `Interrogation contrast decayed when the atoms spent longer in the trap.` → Contrast interrogation giảm khi atom ở trap lâu hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `심문 대비`.

## 3. differential Stark shift /ˌdɪfəˈrenʃəl stɑːrk ʃɪft/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** difference in light-induced energy shift between two clock states.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** khác biệt energy shift do light giữa hai clock state.
**Grammar & collocations:** `differential-Stark-shift coefficient` — coefficient; `cancel probe-light bias` — triệt bias probe-light.
**Examples:** `The differential Stark shift moved the measured transition during high-intensity probing.` → Shift Stark vi sai dịch transition đo trong probing intensity cao.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `차등 스타크 이동`.

## 4. Zeeman sensitivity /ˈzeɪmən ˌsensəˈtɪvəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** frequency sensitivity of a transition to magnetic-field strength.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** frequency sensitivity của transition với magnetic-field strength.
**Grammar & collocations:** `Zeeman-sensitivity coefficient` — coefficient; `reject magnetic perturbation` — loại perturbation từ trường.
**Examples:** `Zeeman sensitivity limited the clock in the unshielded laboratory.` → Sensitivity Zeeman giới hạn clock trong lab không shield.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `제만 민감도`.

## 5. magnetic-field bias /mæɡˈnetɪk fiːld ˈbaɪəs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** intentional static magnetic field used to define a quantization axis or operating point.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** magnetic field tĩnh có chủ ý để định nghĩa quantization axis hoặc operating point.
**Grammar & collocations:** `magnetic-field-bias coil` — coil; `set a quantization axis` — đặt quantization axis.
**Examples:** `Magnetic-field bias stabilized the selected transition against transverse noise.` → Bias field-từ ổn định transition chọn trước noise ngang.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `자기장 바이어스`.

## 6. bias-field reversal /ˈbaɪəs fiːld rɪˈvɜːrsəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** reversing an applied magnetic bias to cancel odd-symmetry frequency shifts.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** đảo magnetic bias để triệt frequency shift có đối xứng lẻ.
**Grammar & collocations:** `bias-field-reversal cycle` — cycle; `average opposite field settings` — average setting field ngược.
**Examples:** `Bias-field reversal removed the first-order magnetic offset from the clock average.` → Reversal field-bias loại offset từ trường bậc nhất khỏi clock average.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `바이어스장 반전`.

## 7. dead-time aliasing /ded taɪm ˈeɪliəsɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** misleading frequency or noise behavior caused by unobserved intervals between measurements.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** frequency hoặc noise behavior sai lệch do interval giữa measurement không observe.
**Grammar & collocations:** `dead-time-aliasing correction` — correction; `model unsampled noise` — model noise không sample.
**Examples:** `Dead-time aliasing hid oscillator noise at harmonics of the measurement cycle.` → Aliasing time-dead che oscillator noise ở harmonic measurement cycle.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `데드타임 앨리어싱`.

## 8. Dick-effect contribution /dɪk ɪˈfekt ˌkɑːntrəˈbjuːʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** clock instability transferred from local-oscillator noise through periodic interrogation and sampling.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** clock instability truyền từ oscillator noise qua interrogation và sampling tuần hoàn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `Dick-effect-contribution budget` — budget; `fold oscillator noise into clock variance` — fold oscillator noise vào clock variance.
**Examples:** `The Dick-effect contribution dominated after the interrogation duty cycle was shortened.` → Contribution effect-Dick chi phối sau khi duty cycle interrogation rút ngắn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `딕 효과 기여도`.

## 9. servo bump /ˈsɜːrvoʊ bʌmp/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a localized noise peak created by the closed-loop servo response.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** noise peak cục bộ do response servo vòng kín tạo.
**Grammar & collocations:** `servo-bump frequency` — frequency; `damp loop peaking` — giảm peaking loop.
**Examples:** `A servo bump appeared just above the clock-control bandwidth.` → Bump servo xuất hiện ngay trên bandwidth control-clock.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `서보 범프`.

## 10. phase accumulator /feɪz əˈkjuːmjəleɪtər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a digital state variable that integrates frequency commands into a continuously updated phase.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** state variable số tích hợp frequency command thành phase cập nhật liên tục.
**Grammar & collocations:** `phase-accumulator word` — word; `synthesize a fractional frequency` — synthesize frequency phân số.
**Examples:** `The phase accumulator applied a fine steering correction without a discontinuous jump.` → Accumulator phase áp correction steering nhỏ mà không jump discontinuous.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `위상 누산기`.

## 11. cycle-slip detector /ˈsaɪkəl slɪp dɪˈtektər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** logic that identifies an abrupt whole-cycle phase error in a tracked oscillator or link.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** logic phát hiện phase error nguyên cycle đột ngột trong oscillator hoặc link track.
**Grammar & collocations:** `cycle-slip-detector flag` — flag; `reacquire phase continuity` — reacquire phase continuity.
**Examples:** `The cycle-slip detector marked one corrupted interval for exclusion.` → Detector slip-cycle đánh dấu một interval corrupt để loại.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `사이클 슬립 검출기`.

## 12. time-domain stability /taɪm doʊˈmeɪn stəˈbɪləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** stability characterized by how frequency or phase fluctuations evolve over averaging time.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** stability characterize theo cách frequency hoặc phase fluctuation đổi qua averaging time.
**Grammar & collocations:** `time-domain-stability plot` — plot; `compare short and long averages` — so average ngắn và dài.
**Examples:** `Time-domain stability improved at long averages but worsened near one-second integration.` → Stability domain-time tốt hơn ở average dài nhưng xấu gần integration một giây.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `시간 영역 안정도`.

## 13. overlapping Allan deviation /ˌoʊvərˈlæpɪŋ ˈælən ˌdiːviˈeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an Allan-deviation estimator that reuses overlapping data segments to improve confidence.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** estimator Allan deviation dùng segment data overlap để tăng confidence.
**Grammar & collocations:** `overlapping-Allan-deviation estimator` — estimator; `reduce finite-sample uncertainty` — giảm uncertainty finite-sample.
**Examples:** `Overlapping Allan deviation resolved the stability minimum with fewer recorded hours.` → Deviation Allan overlap phân giải stability minimum với ít giờ record hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `중첩 앨런 편차`.

## 14. optical pumping efficiency /ˈɑːptɪkəl ˈpʌmpɪŋ ɪˈfɪʃənsi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** fraction of atoms or ions transferred into the desired initial state by optical pumping.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** fraction atom hoặc ion chuyển vào desired initial state bằng optical pumping.

**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** noise floor cơ bản do quantum projection của ensemble hữu hạn.

## 17. interrogation dead time /ɪnˌterəˈɡeɪʃən ded taɪm/

## 18. frequency-error integrator /ˈfriːkwənsi ˈerər ˈɪntəɡreɪtər/

**Core meaning (English):** servo element that accumulates frequency error to remove a steady-state offset.

## 19. phase-step response /feɪz step rɪˈspɑːns/

## 20. frequency-step response /ˈfriːkwənsi step rɪˈspɑːns/
