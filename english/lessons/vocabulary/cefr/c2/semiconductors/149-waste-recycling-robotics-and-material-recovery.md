# C2 Vocabulary — Waste, recycling robotics, and material recovery

This lesson follows discarded material from collection and characterization through robotic sorting, processing, and traceability. The network is **waste sorting robot → recycling robot → material recovery robot → refuse collection robot → autonomous sweeper → street cleaning robot → landfill monitoring robot → compost monitoring robot → waste characterization → object sorting → contamination detection → plastic identification → material classification → conveyor picking → bin picking recycling → gripper selection → crushing line monitoring → baler automation → compact-and-collect → route-aware collection**.

## 1. waste sorting robot /weɪst ˈsɔːrtɪŋ ˈroʊbɑːt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a robot that identifies and separates discarded items into different waste streams.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** robot identify và tách item bỏ đi vào các waste stream khác nhau.
**Grammar & collocations:** `waste-sorting-robot gripper` — gripper; `separate recyclable material` — tách recyclable material.
**Examples:** `The waste sorting robot removed aluminum cans from the mixed stream.` → Waste sorting robot lấy lon aluminum khỏi stream hỗn hợp.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `폐기물 분류 로봇`.

## 2. recycling robot /riːˈsaɪklɪŋ ˈroʊbɑːt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a robot that supports the collection, separation, processing, or reuse of recyclable material.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** robot hỗ trợ collection, separation, processing hoặc reuse recyclable material.
**Grammar & collocations:** `recycling-robot cell` — cell; `recover a reusable component` — thu component dùng lại.
**Examples:** `The recycling robot recovered copper wire before the appliance entered the shredder.` → Recycling robot thu copper wire trước khi appliance vào shredder.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `재활용 로봇`.

## 3. material recovery robot /məˈtɪriəl rɪˈkʌvəri ˈroʊbɑːt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a robot that retrieves valuable materials from a waste stream for reuse or recycling.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** robot thu material có giá trị từ waste stream để reuse/recycle.
**Grammar & collocations:** `material-recovery-robot target` — target; `recover a rare metal` — thu rare metal.
**Examples:** `The material recovery robot extracted stainless parts from the damaged equipment.` → Material recovery robot lấy part stainless khỏi equipment hỏng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `자원 회수 로봇`.

## 4. refuse collection robot /ˈrefjuːs kəˈlekʃən ˈroʊbɑːt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a robot that collects household or commercial waste from containers or collection points.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** robot thu household/commercial waste từ container hoặc collection point.
**Grammar & collocations:** `refuse-collection-robot route` — route; `empty a curbside bin` — làm rỗng curbside bin.
**Examples:** `The refuse collection robot lifted the bin without blocking the bicycle lane.` → Refuse collection robot nhấc bin mà không chặn bike lane.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `폐기물 수거 로봇`.

## 5. autonomous sweeper /ɔːˈtɑːnəməs ˈswiːpər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a self-driving machine that removes dust, leaves, or litter from roads or floors.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** machine tự lái loại dust, lá, litter khỏi road hoặc floor.
**Grammar & collocations:** `autonomous-sweeper route` — route; `sweep a pedestrian plaza` — quét quảng trường đi bộ.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `자율 청소차`.

## 6. street cleaning robot /striːt ˈkliːnɪŋ ˈroʊbɑːt/

## 7. landfill monitoring robot /ˈlændfɪl ˈmɑːnətərɪŋ ˈroʊbɑːt/

**Core meaning (English):** a robot that measures gases, temperature, settlement, or leaks at a landfill.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `매립지 모니터링 로봇`.

## 8. compost monitoring robot /ˈkɑːmpoʊst ˈmɑːnətərɪŋ ˈroʊbɑːt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a robot that measures temperature, moisture, oxygen, or decomposition in compost.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** robot đo temperature, moisture, oxygen hoặc decomposition trong compost.
**Grammar & collocations:** `compost-monitoring-robot probe` — probe; `turn a hot pile` — đảo pile nóng.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `퇴비 모니터링 로봇`.

## 9. waste characterization /weɪst ˌkerəktərɪˈzeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** measuring the types, amounts, properties, and contamination of material in a waste stream.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** đo type, amount, property và contamination của material trong waste stream.
**Grammar & collocations:** `waste-characterization survey` — survey; `estimate a composition profile` — estimate composition profile.
**Examples:** `Waste characterization showed that food residue was reducing paper recovery.` → Waste characterization cho thấy food residue làm giảm paper recovery.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `폐기물 특성 분석`.

## 10. object sorting /ˈɑːbdʒekt ˈsɔːrtɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** assigning individual objects to categories and directing them to corresponding destinations.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** gán từng object vào category và đưa tới destination tương ứng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `object-sorting policy` — policy; `route an item to a bin` — đưa item tới bin.
**Examples:** `Object sorting sent rigid plastic to one chute and flexible film to another.` → Object sorting đưa rigid plastic vào chute này và flexible film vào chute khác.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `객체 분류`.

## 11. contamination detection /kənˌtæməˈneɪʃən dɪˈtekʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** detecting material that makes a waste stream less pure or unsuitable for a process.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** detect material làm waste stream kém tinh khiết hoặc không phù hợp process.
**Grammar & collocations:** `contamination-detection threshold` — threshold; `reject a food-soiled package` — reject package dính food.
**Examples:** `Contamination detection removed a greasy container from the paper line.` → Contamination detection loại container dính dầu khỏi paper line.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `오염 탐지`.

## 13. material classification /məˈtɪriəl ˌklæsəfəˈkeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** assigning material objects to classes based on composition, structure, or intended processing.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** gán object material vào class theo composition, structure hoặc process dự kiến.
**Grammar & collocations:** `material-classification model` — model; `classify a metal fragment` — phân loại metal fragment.
**Examples:** `Material classification marked the fragment as aluminum rather than painted steel.` → Material classification đánh fragment là aluminum chứ không phải steel sơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `재료 분류`.

## 14. conveyor picking /kənˈveɪər ˈpɪkɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** picking moving objects from a conveyor as they pass a robot station.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** pick object đang chạy trên conveyor khi qua robot station.

## 15. bin picking recycling /bɪn ˈpɪkɪŋ riːˈsaɪklɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** robotic picking of randomly piled recyclable objects from a container.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** robot pick recyclable object xếp ngẫu nhiên trong container.

## 16. gripper selection /ˈɡrɪpər səˈlekʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** choosing a gripper or tool suited to an object’s shape, material, and handling risk.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** chọn gripper/tool hợp shape, material và handling risk của object.
**Grammar & collocations:** `gripper-selection policy` — policy; `choose suction for a flat item` — chọn suction cho item phẳng.
**Examples:** `Gripper selection chose a soft finger tool for the crushed carton.` → Gripper selection chọn tool finger mềm cho carton bị ép.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `그리퍼 선택`.

## 18. baler automation /ˈbeɪlər ˌɔːtəˈmeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** automating compression, tying, and handling of recyclable material into compact bales.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** tự động compress, tie và handle recyclable material thành bale gọn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `baler-automation cycle` — cycle; `compress a cardboard load` — nén load cardboard.
**Examples:** `Baler automation tied the cardboard bundle after the density sensor reached its target.` → Baler automation buộc bundle cardboard sau khi density sensor đạt target.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `압축 포장 자동화`.

## 19. compact-and-collect /kəmˈpækt ænd kəˈlekt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a waste operation that compresses material before collecting or transporting it.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** operation nén material trước khi thu hoặc transport.
**Grammar & collocations:** `compact-and-collect route` — route; `reduce container volume` — giảm volume container.
**Examples:** `Compact-and-collect reduced the number of trips from the crowded event site.` → Compact-and-collect giảm số trip từ event site đông.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `압축 수거`.

## 20. route-aware collection /ruːt əˈwer kəˈlekʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** waste collection planned around fill levels, traffic, travel cost, and service priorities.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** waste collection plan theo fill level, traffic, travel cost và priority service.
**Grammar & collocations:** `route-aware-collection planner` — planner; `visit a nearly full bin first` — tới bin gần đầy trước.
**Examples:** `Route-aware collection sent the truck to the full container before visiting the quiet street.` → Route-aware collection đưa truck tới container đầy trước khi tới street vắng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `경로 인지 수거`.

## Review in context

A **waste sorting robot**, **recycling robot**, **material recovery robot**, or **refuse collection robot** can work with an **autonomous sweeper** or **street cleaning robot**. **Landfill monitoring robot** and **compost monitoring robot** support **waste characterization**. **Object sorting**, **contamination detection**, **plastic identification**, and **material classification** guide **conveyor picking** and **bin picking recycling**; **gripper selection** protects difficult items. **Crushing line monitoring**, **baler automation**, and **compact-and-collect** prepare material, while **route-aware collection** coordinates service across the city.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:** **Waste sorting robot**, **recycling robot**, **material recovery robot** hoặc **refuse collection robot** có thể làm việc với **autonomous sweeper** hay **street cleaning robot**. **Landfill monitoring robot** và **compost monitoring robot** hỗ trợ **waste characterization**. **Object sorting**, **contamination detection**, **plastic identification** và **material classification** dẫn **conveyor picking** và **bin picking recycling**; **gripper selection** bảo vệ item khó. **Crushing line monitoring**, **baler automation** và **compact-and-collect** chuẩn bị material, còn **route-aware collection** phối hợp service trong thành phố.
