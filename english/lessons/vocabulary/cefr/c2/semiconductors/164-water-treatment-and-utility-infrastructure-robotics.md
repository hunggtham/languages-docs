# C2 Vocabulary — Water treatment and utility-infrastructure robotics

This lesson follows public water infrastructure from intake and treatment to inspection, maintenance, and environmental discharge. The network is **water treatment plant robot → clarifier inspection robot → sewer inspection robot → storm drain inspection robot → reservoir survey drone → dam face inspection robot → hydropower turbine inspection robot → wastewater sampling robot → sludge handling robot → pipe descaling robot → valve exercise robot → manhole mapping robot → flood response robot → water meter inspection robot → treatment membrane cleaning robot → chlorine dosing robot → odor monitoring robot → outfall inspection drone → wetland monitoring robot → utility control room assistant robot**.

## 1. water treatment plant robot /ˈwɔːtər ˈtriːtmənt plænt ˈroʊbɑːt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a robot that operates, inspects, or maintains equipment in a water treatment facility.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** robot operate, inspect hoặc maintain equipment trong water treatment facility.
**Grammar & collocations:** `water-treatment-plant-robot route` — route; `inspect a process basin` — inspect process basin.
**Examples:** `The water treatment plant robot inspected the intake screen before dawn.` → Robot water treatment plant inspect intake screen trước bình minh.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `정수 처리장 로봇`.

## Review in context

A **water treatment plant robot** and **clarifier inspection robot** support the facility, while a **sewer inspection robot** and **storm drain inspection robot** examine underground flow. A **reservoir survey drone**, **dam face inspection robot**, and **hydropower turbine inspection robot** cover upstream infrastructure. A **wastewater sampling robot**, **sludge handling robot**, **pipe descaling robot**, and **valve exercise robot** support treatment and maintenance; a **manhole mapping robot** records hidden connections. A **flood response robot** and **water meter inspection robot** help during disruption. A **treatment membrane cleaning robot**, **chlorine dosing robot**, and **odor monitoring robot** protect process quality, while an **outfall inspection drone**, **wetland monitoring robot**, and **utility control room assistant robot** connect discharge, ecology, and operations.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:** **Water treatment plant robot** và **clarifier inspection robot** hỗ trợ facility, còn **sewer inspection robot** và **storm drain inspection robot** kiểm tra flow underground. **Reservoir survey drone**, **dam face inspection robot** và **hydropower turbine inspection robot** bao phủ infrastructure upstream. **Wastewater sampling robot**, **sludge handling robot**, **pipe descaling robot** và **valve exercise robot** hỗ trợ treatment/maintenance; **manhole mapping robot** record connection ẩn. **Flood response robot** và **water meter inspection robot** giúp khi disruption. **Treatment membrane cleaning robot**, **chlorine dosing robot** và **odor monitoring robot** bảo vệ process quality, còn **outfall inspection drone**, **wetland monitoring robot** và **utility control room assistant robot** nối discharge, ecology và operation.
