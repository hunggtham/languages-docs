# C2 Vocabulary — Commercial kitchen and restaurant-service robotics

This lesson follows food service from ingredient handling and cooking to sanitation, delivery, and customer flow. The network is **commercial kitchen robot → automated griddle robot → pizza assembly robot → restaurant dishwashing robot → ingredient dispensing robot → bakery proofing robot → dough shaping robot → beverage dispensing robot → restaurant table clearing robot → kitchen inventory robot → allergen separation robot → food allergen inspection → sanitation verification robot → cold storage kitchen robot → menu portioning robot → catering tray assembly robot → restaurant dining delivery robot → kitchen exhaust cleaning robot → kitchen food waste robot → restaurant queue forecasting robot**.

## 1. commercial kitchen robot /kəˈmɜːrʃəl ˈkɪtʃən ˈroʊbɑːt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a robot that prepares, cooks, cleans, or moves food in a professional kitchen.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** robot prepare, cook, clean hoặc move food trong professional kitchen.
**Grammar & collocations:** `commercial-kitchen-robot cell` — cell; `support a busy service` — support service đông.
**Examples:** `The commercial kitchen robot supported the busy service without blocking the cooks.` → Robot commercial kitchen support service đông mà không block cook.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `상업용 주방 로봇`.
