# C2 Vocabulary — Food-processing robotics and packaging quality

This lesson follows food production from hygienic handling and portioning to packaging inspection and quality grading. The network is **food processing robot → food handling robot → packaging robot → palletizing robot → depalletizing robot → case packing robot → cartoning robot → label inspection robot → quality inspection robot → foreign object detection → food-grade robot → hygienic design → washdown robot → sanitary end-effector → soft food handling → delicate product grasping → portioning robot → cutting robot → sorting by quality → defect classification**.

## 1. food processing robot /fuːd ˈprɑːsesɪŋ ˈroʊbɑːt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a robot that prepares, portions, moves, inspects, or packages food products.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** robot prepare, portion, move, inspect hoặc package food product.
**Grammar & collocations:** `food-processing-robot cell` — cell; `handle a prepared product` — handle product đã chuẩn bị.
**Examples:** `The food processing robot moved prepared trays from cooking to cooling.` → Food processing robot chuyển tray đã nấu từ cooking sang cooling.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `식품 가공 로봇`.

## 2. food handling robot /fuːd ˈhændlɪŋ ˈroʊbɑːt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a robot that moves or manipulates food while preserving hygiene and product quality.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** robot move/manipulate food mà vẫn giữ hygiene và quality.
**Grammar & collocations:** `food-handling-robot gripper` — gripper; `transfer a fragile item` — chuyển item dễ hỏng.
**Examples:** `The food handling robot lifted the pastry without crushing its surface.` → Food handling robot nâng pastry mà không làm nát surface.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `식품 취급 로봇`.

## 3. packaging robot /ˈpækɪdʒɪŋ ˈroʊbɑːt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a robot that places products into containers, wraps, seals, or prepares them for shipment.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** robot đặt product vào container, wrap, seal hoặc chuẩn bị shipment.
**Grammar & collocations:** `packaging-robot cycle` — cycle; `place a product in a tray` — đặt product vào tray.
**Examples:** `The packaging robot placed each bottle into the carton with the label facing outward.` → Packaging robot đặt mỗi chai vào carton với label hướng ra ngoài.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `포장 로봇`.

## 4. palletizing robot /ˈpælətaɪzɪŋ ˈroʊbɑːt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a robot that arranges cases or packages into stable layers on a pallet.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** robot xếp case/package thành layer ổn định trên pallet.
**Grammar & collocations:** `palletizing-robot pattern` — pattern; `stack a mixed pallet` — xếp mixed pallet.
**Examples:** `The palletizing robot rotated every case to keep the load balanced.` → Palletizing robot xoay từng case để giữ load cân bằng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `팔레타이징 로봇`.

## 5. depalletizing robot /ˌdiːˈpælətaɪzɪŋ ˈroʊbɑːt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a robot that removes cases or packages from a pallet for downstream processing.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** robot lấy case/package khỏi pallet cho process tiếp theo.
**Grammar & collocations:** `depalletizing-robot sequence` — sequence; `unstack a damaged layer` — dỡ layer hỏng.
**Examples:** `The depalletizing robot skipped the crushed case and reported it for review.` → Depalletizing robot bỏ qua case bị ép và báo để review.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `디팔레타이징 로봇`.

## 6. case packing robot /keɪs ˈpækɪŋ ˈroʊbɑːt/

**Core meaning (English):** a robot that packs individual products into shipping cases in a defined arrangement.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** robot pack product lẻ vào shipping case theo arrangement định.

## 7. cartoning robot /ˈkɑːrtənɪŋ ˈroʊbɑːt/

## 8. label inspection robot /ˈleɪbəl ɪnˈspekʃən ˈroʊbɑːt/

**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** robot check vị trí, content, legibility và attachment của label.

**Examples:** `The label inspection robot rejected a bottle whose date code was unreadable.` → Label inspection robot reject chai có date code không đọc được.

## 9. quality inspection robot /ˈkwɑːləti ɪnˈspekʃən ˈroʊbɑːt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a robot that measures product features to determine whether they meet quality requirements.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** robot đo feature product để xác định đạt quality requirement.

**Examples:** `The quality inspection robot measured the seal width on every pouch.` → Quality inspection robot đo seal width trên mỗi pouch.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `품질 검사 로봇`.

## 10. foreign object detection /ˈfɔːrən ˈɑːbdʒekt dɪˈtekʃən/

**Core meaning (English):** detecting unintended material in food or packaging where it could create a safety or quality problem.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** detect material không mong muốn trong food/package gây safety hoặc quality problem.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `이물 검출`.

## 11. food-grade robot /fuːd ɡreɪd ˈroʊbɑːt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a robot whose materials, lubricants, surfaces, and construction are suitable for food contact or food environments.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** robot có material, lubricant, surface và construction phù hợp food contact/environment.
**Grammar & collocations:** `food-grade-robot coating` — coating; `meet a hygiene requirement` — đạt hygiene requirement.
**Examples:** `The food-grade robot used sealed bearings and a cleanable outer surface.` → Food-grade robot dùng bearing kín và outer surface dễ vệ sinh.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `식품 등급 로봇`.

## 12. hygienic design /haɪˈdʒiːnɪk dɪˈzaɪn/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** designing equipment so it can be cleaned, drained, inspected, and kept free of contamination.

**Grammar & collocations:** `hygienic-design principle` — principle; `remove a dirt-trapping crevice` — loại crevice giữ dirt.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `위생 설계`.

## 14. sanitary end-effector /ˈsænəteri end ɪˈfektər/

**Core meaning (English):** a robot tool designed to contact food while remaining cleanable and resistant to contamination.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** tool robot tiếp xúc food nhưng cleanable và chống contamination.

## 15. soft food handling /sɔːft fuːd ˈhændlɪŋ/

**Core meaning (English):** manipulating food that deforms, bruises, tears, or changes shape under force.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** manipulate food dễ deform, bruise, tear hoặc đổi shape dưới force.
**Grammar & collocations:** `soft-food-handling strategy` — strategy; `limit contact pressure` — giới hạn contact pressure.
**Examples:** `Soft food handling slowed the gripper before it touched the ripe fruit.` → Soft food handling làm gripper chậm trước khi chạm quả chín.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `연성 식품 취급`.

**Core meaning (English):** grasping products whose appearance, texture, or structure can be damaged easily.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `섬세한 제품 파지`.

## 18. cutting robot /ˈkʌtɪŋ ˈroʊbɑːt/

**Core meaning (English):** a robot that cuts food or packaging along a planned shape, path, or size.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** robot cắt food/package theo shape, path hoặc size plan.

## 19. sorting by quality /ˈsɔːrtɪŋ baɪ ˈkwɑːləti/

**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** tách product theo condition, appearance, size hoặc performance đo được.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `품질별 분류`.

## 20. defect classification /dɪˈfekt ˌklæsəfəˈkeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** identifying the type and severity of a product or package defect.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** identify type và severity của defect product/package.
**Grammar & collocations:** `defect-classification label` — label; `distinguish a cosmetic mark from a safety fault` — phân biệt mark cosmetic với safety fault.
**Examples:** `Defect classification separated a harmless wrinkle from a leaking seal.` → Defect classification tách wrinkle vô hại khỏi seal bị rò.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `결함 분류`.

## Review in context

A **food processing robot**, **food handling robot**, or **packaging robot** can feed a **palletizing robot**, **depalletizing robot**, **case packing robot**, and **cartoning robot**. A **label inspection robot** and **quality inspection robot** check packages, while **foreign object detection** protects safety. A **food-grade robot**, **hygienic design**, and **washdown robot** support sanitation; a **sanitary end-effector** enables **soft food handling** and **delicate product grasping**. A **portioning robot** and **cutting robot** prepare servings, then **sorting by quality** and **defect classification** route the finished products.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:** **Food processing robot**, **food handling robot** hoặc **packaging robot** có thể feed **palletizing robot**, **depalletizing robot**, **case packing robot** và **cartoning robot**. **Label inspection robot** và **quality inspection robot** kiểm tra package, còn **foreign object detection** bảo vệ safety. **Food-grade robot**, **hygienic design** và **washdown robot** hỗ trợ sanitation; **sanitary end-effector** cho phép **soft food handling** và **delicate product grasping**. **Portioning robot** và **cutting robot** chuẩn bị serving, rồi **sorting by quality** và **defect classification** route finished product.
