# C2 Vocabulary — Energy-infrastructure robotics and asset inspection

This lesson follows robots and drones that inspect power, pipeline, and renewable-energy assets while operators plan maintenance from the evidence. The network is **energy infrastructure robot → power-line inspection robot → substation inspection robot → transformer monitoring robot → pipeline inspection robot → wind turbine inspection robot → solar panel cleaning robot → blade inspection drone → load forecasting → peak shaving → microgrid control → fault location → line patrol → vegetation encroachment → corona discharge detection → thermal hotspot detection → acoustic partial discharge → magnetic flux leakage → eddy-current inspection → ultrasonic thickness measurement**.

## 1. energy infrastructure robot /ˈenərdʒi ˈɪnfrəˌstrʌktʃər ˈroʊbɑːt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a robot that inspects, maintains, or monitors equipment used to produce, transmit, or store energy.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** robot inspect, maintain hoặc monitor equipment tạo, truyền hoặc lưu energy.
**Grammar & collocations:** `energy-infrastructure-robot mission` — mission; `inspect a remote asset` — kiểm tra asset xa.
**Examples:** `The energy infrastructure robot inspected the remote valve station after the storm.` → Energy infrastructure robot kiểm tra valve station xa sau bão.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `에너지 인프라 로봇`.

## 2. power-line inspection robot /ˈpaʊər laɪn ɪnˈspekʃən ˈroʊbɑːt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a robot that travels along or near electrical lines to inspect wires, towers, and fittings.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** robot di chuyển trên/gần power line để inspect wire, tower và fitting.
**Grammar & collocations:** `power-line-inspection-robot clamp` — clamp; `scan an insulator` — scan insulator.
**Examples:** `The power-line inspection robot found a cracked insulator before the next high-wind event.` → Power-line inspection robot tìm insulator nứt trước đợt gió lớn tiếp theo.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `전력선 검사 로봇`.

## 9. load forecasting /loʊd ˈfɔːrkæstɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** predicting future electrical demand over a defined time horizon.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** dự báo electrical demand tương lai trong time horizon định rõ.
**Grammar & collocations:** `load-forecasting model` — model; `predict an evening peak` — dự đoán peak buổi tối.
**Examples:** `Load forecasting warned that the charging fleet would coincide with the factory peak.` → Load forecasting cảnh báo fleet charging trùng factory peak.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `부하 예측`.

## 10. peak shaving /piːk ˈʃeɪvɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** reducing the highest short-term power demand by shifting or limiting consumption.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** giảm power demand ngắn hạn cao nhất bằng cách shift hoặc limit consumption.
**Grammar & collocations:** `peak-shaving schedule` — schedule; `stagger robot charging` — lệch charging robot.
**Examples:** `Peak shaving delayed noncritical charging until the grid load declined.` → Peak shaving hoãn charging không critical tới khi grid load giảm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `피크 절감`.

## 11. microgrid control /ˈmaɪkroʊɡrɪd kənˈtroʊl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** coordinating local generation, storage, loads, and connections to keep a small grid stable.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** điều phối generation, storage, load và connection để giữ grid nhỏ ổn định.
**Grammar & collocations:** `microgrid-control policy` — policy; `balance local generation and demand` — cân generation và demand local.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `마이크로그리드 제어`.

## 18. magnetic flux leakage /mæɡˈnetɪk flʌks ˈliːkɪdʒ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** magnetic field escaping from a steel component where corrosion or loss of material has altered the path.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** magnetic field thoát khỏi steel component khi corrosion/loss material đổi đường field.
**Grammar & collocations:** `magnetic-flux-leakage scan` — scan; `detect metal loss` — detect metal loss.
**Examples:** `Magnetic flux leakage revealed thinning along the storage tank floor.` → Magnetic flux leakage làm lộ chỗ mỏng ở đáy storage tank.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `자속 누설`.

## 19. eddy-current inspection /ˈedi ˈkɜːrənt ɪnˈspekʃən/

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `와전류 검사`.

## Review in context

An **energy infrastructure robot** may be a **power-line inspection robot**, **substation inspection robot**, **transformer monitoring robot**, or **pipeline inspection robot**; a **wind turbine inspection robot**, **solar panel cleaning robot**, and **blade inspection drone** cover renewable assets. **Load forecasting**, **peak shaving**, and **microgrid control** coordinate energy demand, while **fault location** and **line patrol** guide response. **Vegetation encroachment**, **corona discharge detection**, and **thermal hotspot detection** reveal hazards. **Acoustic partial discharge**, **magnetic flux leakage**, **eddy-current inspection**, and **ultrasonic thickness measurement** provide deeper evidence of asset condition.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:** **Energy infrastructure robot** có thể là **power-line inspection robot**, **substation inspection robot**, **transformer monitoring robot** hoặc **pipeline inspection robot**; **wind turbine inspection robot**, **solar panel cleaning robot** và **blade inspection drone** bao phủ asset renewable. **Load forecasting**, **peak shaving** và **microgrid control** phối hợp energy demand, còn **fault location** và **line patrol** dẫn response. **Vegetation encroachment**, **corona discharge detection** và **thermal hotspot detection** làm lộ hazard. **Acoustic partial discharge**, **magnetic flux leakage**, **eddy-current inspection** và **ultrasonic thickness measurement** cung cấp evidence sâu hơn về condition asset.
