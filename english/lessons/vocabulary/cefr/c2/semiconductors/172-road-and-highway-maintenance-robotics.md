# C2 Vocabulary — Road and highway maintenance robotics

This lesson follows a road network from inspection and marking to winter service, work-zone protection, and incident response. The network is **road inspection robot → pavement crack mapping robot → bridge deck scanning robot → lane marking robot → roadside mowing robot → pothole repair robot → snowplow autonomy → salt spreading robot → traffic cone placement robot → work-zone barrier robot → highway drainage robot → guardrail inspection robot → roadside litter robot → asphalt compaction robot → bridge cable inspection drone → road weather sensor robot → traffic incident response robot → roadside vegetation robot → mobile work-zone camera robot → highway maintenance robot**.

## 1. road inspection robot /roʊd ɪnˈspekʃən ˈroʊbɑːt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a robot that examines roads, shoulders, signs, or roadside assets for defects and hazards.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** robot examine road, shoulder, sign hoặc roadside asset về defect/hazard.
**Grammar & collocations:** `road-inspection-robot route` — route; `inspect a damaged shoulder` — inspect shoulder hỏng.
**Examples:** `The road inspection robot found loose gravel near the merge lane.` → Road inspection robot tìm gravel lỏng gần merge lane.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `도로 검사 로봇`.

## Review in context

A **road inspection robot**, **pavement crack mapping robot**, and **bridge deck scanning robot** find structural problems, while a **lane marking robot**, **roadside mowing robot**, and **pothole repair robot** maintain the surface. **Snowplow autonomy** and a **salt spreading robot** support winter roads. A **traffic cone placement robot**, **work-zone barrier robot**, and **highway drainage robot** protect work areas and water flow. A **guardrail inspection robot**, **roadside litter robot**, and **asphalt compaction robot** improve safety and quality. A **bridge cable inspection drone**, **road weather sensor robot**, **traffic incident response robot**, **roadside vegetation robot**, **mobile work-zone camera robot**, and **highway maintenance robot** connect monitoring with coordinated service.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:** **Road inspection robot**, **pavement crack mapping robot** và **bridge deck scanning robot** tìm structural problem, còn **lane marking robot**, **roadside mowing robot** và **pothole repair robot** maintain surface. **Snowplow autonomy** và **salt spreading robot** hỗ trợ road mùa đông. **Traffic cone placement robot**, **work-zone barrier robot** và **highway drainage robot** bảo vệ work area và water flow. **Guardrail inspection robot**, **roadside litter robot** và **asphalt compaction robot** cải thiện safety/quality. **Bridge cable inspection drone**, **road weather sensor robot**, **traffic incident response robot**, **roadside vegetation robot**, **mobile work-zone camera robot** và **highway maintenance robot** nối monitoring với service phối hợp.
