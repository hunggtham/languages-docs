# C2 Vocabulary — Office-facility and workplace-service robotics

This lesson follows an office from reception and meetings to supplies, safety, sanitation, and secure records. The network is **office reception robot → conference room setup robot → office mailroom robot → document shredding robot → meeting room cleaning robot → office supply inventory robot → elevator delivery robot → workplace occupancy robot → coffee service robot → printer maintenance robot → ergonomic assessment robot → defibrillator delivery robot → building access robot → visitor check-in robot → office plant care robot → meeting transcription robot → secure document courier robot → workspace sanitation robot → office recycling robot → desk equipment delivery robot**.

## 1. office reception robot /ˈɔːfɪs rɪˈsepʃən ˈroʊbɑːt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a robot that greets visitors, provides directions, and contacts office staff.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** robot greet visitor, provide direction và contact office staff.
**Grammar & collocations:** `office-reception-robot kiosk` — kiosk; `announce a visitor` — announce visitor.
**Examples:** `The office reception robot announced the visitor to the host.` → Robot reception office announce visitor cho host.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `사무실 안내 로봇`.
