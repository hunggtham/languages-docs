# C2 Vocabulary — Smart-building inspection and facility robotics

This lesson follows a building from exterior inspection and air quality to maintenance, access, and emergency guidance. The network is **building facade inspection robot → window-cleaning robot → elevator shaft inspection robot → HVAC duct inspection robot → indoor air mapping robot → fire sprinkler inspection robot → leak detection robot → roof inspection drone → structural health monitoring robot → building access patrol robot → floor polishing robot → waste chute inspection robot → parking garage patrol robot → escalator inspection robot → elevator maintenance robot → thermal envelope scan → occupancy mapping robot → emergency evacuation guidance robot → water tank cleaning robot → facility digital twin**.

## 1. building facade inspection robot /ˈbɪldɪŋ fəˈsɑːd ɪnˈspekʃən ˈroʊbɑːt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a robot that examines the outside surface of a building for cracks, corrosion, or loose materials.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** robot examine exterior building để tìm crack, corrosion hoặc material lỏng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `building-facade-inspection-robot scan` — scan; `inspect a curtain wall` — inspect curtain wall.
**Examples:** `The building facade inspection robot found a crack beneath the balcony.` → Building facade inspection robot tìm crack dưới balcony.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `건물 외벽 검사 로봇`.

A **building facade inspection robot** and **window-cleaning robot** examine the exterior, while an **elevator shaft inspection robot** and **HVAC duct inspection robot** reach hidden spaces. An **indoor air mapping robot**, **fire sprinkler inspection robot**, and **leak detection robot** check environmental and safety systems; a **roof inspection drone** surveys above. A **structural health monitoring robot**, **building access patrol robot**, and **floor polishing robot** support condition and daily operations.
