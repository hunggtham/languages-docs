# C2 Vocabulary — Hospitality and hotel operations robotics

This lesson follows a hotel from guest arrival and room service to housekeeping, events, and departure. The network is **hotel housekeeping robot → room-service delivery robot → linen-sorting robot → laundry folding robot → guest check-in robot → room inspection robot → minibar inventory robot → hotel corridor patrol robot → hospitality cleaning robot → luggage storage robot → breakfast service robot → banquet setup robot → pool maintenance robot → hotel energy optimization robot → occupancy-based housekeeping → guest privacy mode → accessible room assistance → keycard delivery robot → lost-and-found retrieval → checkout room inspection**.

## 1. hotel housekeeping robot /hoʊˈtel ˈhaʊsˌkiːpɪŋ ˈroʊbɑːt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a robot that helps clean, reset, or supply guest rooms in a hotel.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** robot giúp clean, reset hoặc supply guest room trong hotel.
**Grammar & collocations:** `hotel-housekeeping-robot cart` — cart; `reset a guest room` — reset guest room.
**Examples:** `The hotel housekeeping robot delivered towels before the next guest arrived.` → Hotel housekeeping robot giao towel trước khi guest tiếp theo tới.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `호텔 하우스키핑 로봇`.

## Review in context

A **hotel housekeeping robot** and **room-service delivery robot** support rooms, while a **linen-sorting robot** and **laundry folding robot** prepare textiles. A **guest check-in robot**, **room inspection robot**, and **minibar inventory robot** support arrival and room readiness; a **hotel corridor patrol robot** and **hospitality cleaning robot** protect shared spaces. A **luggage storage robot**, **breakfast service robot**, and **banquet setup robot** support guest activity. A **pool maintenance robot** and **hotel energy optimization robot** manage facilities. **Occupancy-based housekeeping** respects schedules, and **guest privacy mode** protects personal space. **Accessible room assistance**, a **keycard delivery robot**, **lost-and-found retrieval**, and **checkout room inspection** connect service to departure.
