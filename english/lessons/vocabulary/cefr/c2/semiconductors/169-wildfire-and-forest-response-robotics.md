# C2 Vocabulary — Wildfire and forest-response robotics

This lesson follows a wildfire response from early detection and mapping to crew support, evacuation, and post-fire recovery. The network is **wildfire detection drone → fireline mapping robot → smoke plume analysis drone → forest fuel survey robot → firebreak inspection robot → remote water cannon robot → ash hazard inspection robot → burn severity mapping drone → evacuation route robot → wildland communications robot → tree health scanning robot → canopy survey drone → invasive species robot → soil moisture mapping robot → forest restoration planting robot → wildfire weather station robot → lookout camera robot → fire crew supply robot → snag hazard detection robot → post-fire erosion robot**.

## 1. wildfire detection drone /ˈwaɪldfaɪər dɪˈtekʃən droʊn/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a drone that detects early signs of fire, heat, or smoke in a forest or wildland area.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** drone detect early sign của fire, heat hoặc smoke trong forest/wildland.
**Grammar & collocations:** `wildfire-detection-drone camera` — camera; `spot a fresh ignition` — spot ignition mới.
**Examples:** `The wildfire detection drone spotted a fresh ignition near the ridge.` → Wildfire detection drone spot ignition mới gần ridge.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `산불 탐지 드론`.

## Review in context

A **wildfire detection drone**, **fireline mapping robot**, and **smoke plume analysis drone** identify and map a fire, while a **forest fuel survey robot** and **firebreak inspection robot** show how it may spread. A **remote water cannon robot**, **ash hazard inspection robot**, and **burn severity mapping drone** support safer response. An **evacuation route robot** and **wildland communications robot** guide people and crews. A **tree health scanning robot**, **canopy survey drone**, **invasive species robot**, and **soil moisture mapping robot** support recovery; a **forest restoration planting robot** rebuilds habitat. A **wildfire weather station robot**, **lookout camera robot**, **fire crew supply robot**, **snag hazard detection robot**, and **post-fire erosion robot** connect warning, logistics, and long-term safety.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:** **Wildfire detection drone**, **fireline mapping robot** và **smoke plume analysis drone** identify/map fire, còn **forest fuel survey robot** và **firebreak inspection robot** cho thấy fire có thể spread thế nào. **Remote water cannon robot**, **ash hazard inspection robot** và **burn severity mapping drone** hỗ trợ response an toàn hơn. **Evacuation route robot** và **wildland communications robot** guide người và crew. **Tree health scanning robot**, **canopy survey drone**, **invasive species robot** và **soil moisture mapping robot** hỗ trợ recovery; **forest restoration planting robot** rebuild habitat. **Wildfire weather station robot**, **lookout camera robot**, **fire crew supply robot**, **snag hazard detection robot** và **post-fire erosion robot** nối warning, logistics với safety dài hạn.
