# C2+ Vocabulary — Virus structure, evolution, and assays

This lesson follows viruses from particle structure and cell entry through genome persistence, replication, immune escape, transmission, and laboratory measurement. The network is **capsid assembly → viral envelope → virion → tropism virology → serotype classification → quasispecies → provirus → episome → reverse transcriptase → integrase → viral protease → cytopathic effect → syncytium → influenza antigenic drift → influenza antigenic shift → zoonotic spillover → genome reassortment → plaque assay → multiplicity of infection → neutralization titer**.

## 1. capsid assembly /ˈkæpsɪd əˈsɛmbli/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the process by which viral protein subunits form a protective shell around the genome.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** process protein subunit virus form shell bảo vệ quanh genome.
**Grammar & collocations:** `capsid assembly pathway` — pathway; `block capsid assembly` — block.
**Examples:** `The drug was designed to block capsid assembly inside infected cells.` → Drug được design để block capsid assembly trong infected cell.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `캡시드 조립`.

## 2. viral envelope /ˈvaɪrəl ˈɛnvəloʊp/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a lipid membrane acquired from a host cell and studded with viral proteins.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** lipid membrane lấy từ host cell, có viral protein gắn trên.
**Grammar & collocations:** `fragile viral envelope` — envelope dễ hỏng; `disrupt the viral envelope` — disrupt.
**Examples:** `Soap can disrupt the viral envelope before the particle reaches a cell.` → Soap có thể disrupt viral envelope trước khi particle tới cell.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `바이러스 외피`.

## 3. virion /ˈvɪriən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a complete, infectious virus particle outside a host cell.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** virus particle hoàn chỉnh và infectious bên ngoài host cell.
**Grammar & collocations:** `mature virion` — virion mature; `release a virion` — release.
**Examples:** `The mature virion left the cell when the membrane ruptured.` → Virion mature rời cell khi membrane rupture.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `비리온`.

## 4. tropism virology /ˈtroʊpɪzəm vaɪˈrɑːlədʒi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the study of why a virus preferentially infects particular cells, tissues, or species.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** study vì sao virus ưu tiên infect cell, tissue hoặc species cụ thể.
**Grammar & collocations:** `tissue tropism virology` — tropism tissue; `study tropism` — study.
**Examples:** `Tropism virology explained why the virus concentrated in airway tissue.` → Tropism virology giải thích virus tập trung ở airway tissue.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `바이러스 친화성 연구`.

## 5. serotype classification /ˈsɪroʊtaɪp ˌklæsɪfɪˈkeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** grouping strains according to distinct antigenic reactions with antibodies.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** group strain theo antigenic reaction khác nhau với antibody.
**Grammar & collocations:** `viral serotype classification` — classification; `refine serotype classification` — refine.
**Examples:** `Viral serotype classification helped select the matching diagnostic reagent.` → Serotype classification virus giúp chọn reagent diagnostic phù hợp.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `혈청형 분류`.

## 6. quasispecies /ˌkwɑːziˈspiːʃiːz/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a cloud of closely related viral variants produced by a mutation-prone replication process.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cloud variant virus gần nhau do replication dễ mutation tạo.
**Grammar & collocations:** `viral quasispecies` — quasispecies virus; `track a quasispecies` — track.
**Examples:** `The viral quasispecies shifted after antibody pressure increased.` → Viral quasispecies shift sau khi antibody pressure tăng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `준종`.

## 7. provirus /ˈproʊvaɪrəs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** viral genetic material integrated into a host cell's genome.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** genetic material virus integrate vào genome của host cell.
**Grammar & collocations:** `latent provirus` — provirus latent; `activate a provirus` — activate.
**Examples:** `The latent provirus remained silent until the cell was stressed.` → Provirus latent remain silent đến khi cell bị stress.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `프로바이러스`.

## 8. episome /ˈɛpɪsoʊm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a genetic element that can replicate inside a cell without being integrated into its chromosome.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** genetic element replicate trong cell mà không integrate vào chromosome.
**Grammar & collocations:** `viral episome` — episome virus; `maintain an episome` — maintain.
**Examples:** `The viral episome persisted independently in the host nucleus.` → Viral episome persist độc lập trong host nucleus.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `에피솜`.

## 9. reverse transcriptase /rɪˈvɜːrs trænˈskrɪpteɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an enzyme that makes DNA from an RNA template.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** enzyme tạo DNA từ RNA template.
**Grammar & collocations:** `viral reverse transcriptase` — transcriptase virus; `inhibit reverse transcriptase` — inhibit.
**Examples:** `The inhibitor blocked viral reverse transcriptase during genome copying.` → Inhibitor block reverse transcriptase virus trong genome copying.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `역전사효소`.

## 10. integrase /ˈɪntɪɡreɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a viral enzyme that inserts viral DNA into the host genome.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** enzyme virus insert viral DNA vào host genome.
**Grammar & collocations:** `viral integrase inhibitor` — inhibitor; `block integrase` — block.
**Examples:** `The therapy blocked integrase and prevented stable genome insertion.` → Therapy block integrase và ngăn genome insertion ổn định.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `인테그레이스`.

## 11. viral protease /ˈvaɪrəl ˈproʊteɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an enzyme that cuts viral polyproteins into functional components during maturation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** enzyme cut viral polyprotein thành component functional khi maturation.
**Grammar & collocations:** `viral protease inhibitor` — inhibitor; `inhibit viral protease` — inhibit.
**Examples:** `The viral protease inhibitor produced immature, noninfectious particles.` → Inhibitor viral protease tạo particle immature, không infectious.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `바이러스 프로테아제`.

## 12. cytopathic effect /ˌsaɪtoʊˈpæθɪk ɪˈfɛkt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** visible structural damage that viral infection causes in cultured cells.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** damage visible về structure do virus infection gây trong cultured cell.
**Grammar & collocations:** `distinct cytopathic effect` — effect rõ; `score cytopathic effect` — score.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `세포병변 효과`.

## 13. syncytium /sɪnˈsɪtiəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a large multinucleated cell formed when infected cells fuse with one another.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cell lớn nhiều nucleus tạo khi infected cell fuse nhau.
**Grammar & collocations:** `multinucleated syncytium` — syncytium nhiều nucleus; `form a syncytium` — form.
**Examples:** `The viral protein caused neighboring cells to form a syncytium.` → Viral protein làm cell lân cận form syncytium.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `합포체`.

## 14. influenza antigenic drift /ˌɪnfluˈɛnzə ˌæntɪˈdʒɛnɪk drɪft/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** gradual antigen changes caused by mutations that accumulate in an influenza virus.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** antigen change gradual do mutation tích lũy trong influenza virus.
**Grammar & collocations:** `seasonal influenza antigenic drift` — drift theo mùa; `monitor antigenic drift` — monitor.

## 15. influenza antigenic shift /ˌɪnfluˈɛnzə ˌæntɪˈdʒɛnɪk ʃɪft/
**Part of speech:** noun

**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** antigen change lớn, đột ngột do genome segment reassortment.
**Grammar & collocations:** `pandemic influenza antigenic shift` — shift pandemic; `detect antigenic shift` — detect.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `인플루엔자 항원 대변이`.

## 17. genome reassortment /ˈdʒiːnoʊm ˌriːəˈsɔːrtmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the exchange and reorganization of genome segments when related segmented viruses infect the same cell.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** exchange/reorganize genome segment khi segmented virus related cùng infect cell.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `유전체 재분절`.

## 18. plaque assay /plæk ˈæseɪ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a cell-culture method that counts infectious virus by measuring clear zones formed after cell killing.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** method cell culture count infectious virus qua clear zone do cell killing.
**Grammar & collocations:** `viral plaque assay` — assay virus; `run a plaque assay` — run.
**Examples:** `The laboratory used a viral plaque assay to compare infectivity.` → Lab dùng plaque assay virus để compare infectivity.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `플라크 분석`.

## 19. multiplicity of infection /ˌmʌltəˈplɪsəti əv ɪnˈfɛkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the average number of infectious particles added per target cell in an experiment.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** số infectious particle trung bình thêm trên mỗi target cell trong experiment.
**Grammar & collocations:** `low multiplicity of infection` — multiplicity thấp; `set the multiplicity` — set.
**Examples:** `A low multiplicity of infection revealed the spread between cells.` → Multiplicity infection thấp reveal spread giữa cell.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `감염 다중도`.

## 20. neutralization titer /ˌnuːtrələˈzeɪʃən ˈtaɪtər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the highest dilution of serum that still prevents a virus from infecting cells.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** dilution cao nhất của serum vẫn prevent virus infect cell.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high neutralization titer` — titer cao; `measure a neutralization titer` — measure.
**Examples:** `The vaccinated group showed a high neutralization titer against the variant.` → Group vaccinated có neutralization titer cao với variant.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `중화 역가`.

## Review in context

Virus begins with **capsid assembly**, perhaps gains a **viral envelope**, and exits as a complete **virion**; **tropism virology** explains which cells it enters, while **serotype classification** compares antigenic reactions. A mutating **quasispecies** may persist as a **provirus** or **episome**; **reverse transcriptase**, **integrase**, and **viral protease** support replication. Infection can create a **cytopathic effect** or **syncytium**. **Influenza antigenic drift**, **influenza antigenic shift**, **zoonotic spillover**, and **genome reassortment** shape transmission. A **plaque assay**, **multiplicity of infection**, and **neutralization titer** quantify laboratory behavior.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:** Virus bắt đầu bằng **capsid assembly**, có thể nhận **viral envelope** và rời cell thành **virion** hoàn chỉnh; **tropism virology** giải thích cell nào bị enter, còn **serotype classification** compare antigenic reaction. **Quasispecies** mutation có thể persist thành **provirus** hoặc **episome**; **reverse transcriptase**, **integrase** và **viral protease** support replication. Infection có thể tạo **cytopathic effect** hoặc **syncytium**. **Influenza antigenic drift**, **influenza antigenic shift**, **zoonotic spillover** và **genome reassortment** shape transmission. **Plaque assay**, **multiplicity of infection** và **neutralization titer** quantify behavior trong lab.
