# C2+ Vocabulary — Observational astronomy and detector language

This lesson follows an astronomical observation from atmosphere and optics through calibration, photometry, spectroscopy, and survey interpretation. The network is **adaptive-optics correction → interferometric baseline → spectrograph → echelle grating → radial velocity → transit photometry → coronagraph → aperture synthesis → seeing disk → airmass → sidereal tracking → plate solving → dark frame → flat-field correction → bias frame → detector read noise → point-spread function → redshift survey → occultation light curve → spectral line broadening**.

## 1. adaptive-optics correction /əˈdæptɪv ˈɑːptɪks kəˈrɛkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a real-time adjustment that reduces atmospheric distortion in an astronomical image.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** adjustment real-time giảm atmospheric distortion trong astronomical image.
**Grammar & collocations:** `real-time adaptive-optics correction` — correction real-time; `apply adaptive-optics correction` — apply correction.
**Examples:** `Adaptive-optics correction sharpened the image of the faint companion.` → Adaptive-optics correction làm sắc image của companion mờ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `적응 광학 보정`.

## 2. interferometric baseline /ˌɪntərfəroʊˈmɛtrɪk ˈbeɪslaɪn/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the separation between telescopes whose signals are combined to infer fine spatial detail.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** khoảng cách giữa telescope combine signal để suy ra spatial detail tinh.
**Grammar & collocations:** `long interferometric baseline` — baseline dài; `extend the baseline` — extend baseline.
**Examples:** `A longer interferometric baseline improved the array's angular resolution.` → Baseline interferometric dài hơn cải thiện angular resolution của array.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `간섭계 기준선`.

## 3. spectrograph /ˈspɛktrəɡræf/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an instrument that separates light into wavelengths and records the resulting spectrum.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** instrument tách light thành wavelength và record spectrum.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high-resolution spectrograph` — spectrograph độ phân giải cao; `feed light into a spectrograph` — đưa light vào.
**Examples:** `The high-resolution spectrograph revealed weak absorption lines.` → Spectrograph độ phân giải cao reveal absorption line yếu.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `분광기`.

## 4. echelle grating /eɪˈʃɛl ˈɡreɪtɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a coarse diffraction grating used at high orders to obtain very high spectral resolution.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** diffraction grating thô dùng ở high order để đạt spectral resolution rất cao.
**Grammar & collocations:** `cross-dispersed echelle grating` — grating echelle cross-dispersed; `align an echelle grating` — align grating.
**Examples:** `The instrument used an echelle grating to separate neighboring stellar lines.` → Instrument dùng echelle grating để tách stellar line gần nhau.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `에셸 회절격자`.

## 5. radial velocity /ˈreɪdiəl vəˈlɑːsəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an object's velocity toward or away from the observer, measured along the line of sight.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** velocity của object toward/away observer dọc line of sight.
**Grammar & collocations:** `precise radial velocity` — velocity chính xác; `measure radial velocity` — đo velocity.
**Examples:** `The team measured radial velocity to search for an orbiting planet.` → Team đo radial velocity để tìm planet orbit.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `시선속도`.

## 6. transit photometry /ˈtrænzɪt fəˈtɑːmətri/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** measuring a star's brightness as a planet passes in front of it.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** đo brightness của star khi planet đi qua phía trước.
**Grammar & collocations:** `precision transit photometry` — photometry precision; `analyze transit photometry` — analyze.
**Examples:** `Transit photometry revealed a repeating dip in the star's light.` → Transit photometry reveal dip lặp lại trong light của star.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `통과 측광`.

## 7. coronagraph /kəˈroʊnəɡræf/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an optical device that blocks a bright star's central light so nearby faint objects can be studied.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** device block central light sáng của star để study faint object gần.
**Grammar & collocations:** `stellar coronagraph` — coronagraph stellar; `operate a coronagraph` — operate device.
**Examples:** `The coronagraph suppressed starlight around the young planetary system.` → Coronagraph suppress starlight quanh planetary system trẻ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `코로나그래프`.

## 8. aperture synthesis /ˈæpərtʃər ˈsɪnθəsɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** reconstructing a high-resolution image by combining observations from separated antennas or telescopes.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** reconstruct image resolution cao bằng combine observation từ antenna/telescope tách nhau.
**Grammar & collocations:** `radio aperture synthesis` — synthesis radio; `use aperture synthesis` — dùng synthesis.
**Examples:** `Aperture synthesis produced a detailed image of the radio jet.` → Aperture synthesis tạo image chi tiết của radio jet.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `개구 합성`.

## 9. seeing disk /ˈsiːɪŋ dɪsk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the blurred image of a point source caused mainly by atmospheric turbulence.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** image point source bị blur chủ yếu bởi atmospheric turbulence.
**Grammar & collocations:** `large seeing disk` — disk lớn; `measure the seeing disk` — đo disk.
**Examples:** `A large seeing disk limited the ground-based image.` → Seeing disk lớn giới hạn ground-based image.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `시잉 원반`.

## 10. airmass /ˈɛrˌmæs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the relative amount of atmosphere light travels through when observing an object.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** lượng atmosphere tương đối mà light đi qua khi observe object.
**Grammar & collocations:** `low airmass` — airmass thấp; `observe at high airmass` — observe airmass cao.
**Examples:** `The astronomer observed near the meridian to minimize airmass.` → Astronomer observe gần meridian để minimize airmass.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `대기 질량`.

## 11. sidereal tracking /saɪˈdɪəriəl ˈtrækɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** moving a telescope at the rate of Earth's rotation relative to the stars.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** move telescope theo rate Earth rotate relative với star.
**Grammar & collocations:** `accurate sidereal tracking` — tracking chính xác; `enable sidereal tracking` — enable tracking.
**Examples:** `Accurate sidereal tracking kept the galaxy centered during the exposure.` → Sidereal tracking chính xác giữ galaxy ở center trong exposure.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `항성시 추적`.

## 12. plate solving /pleɪt ˈsɑːlvɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** identifying the exact sky coordinates and orientation of an astronomical image by matching its stars to a catalog.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** identify coordinate/orientation của sky image bằng match star với catalog.
**Grammar & collocations:** `automatic plate solving` — solving tự động; `run plate solving` — chạy solving.
**Examples:** `Automatic plate solving identified the field within seconds.` → Automatic plate solving identify field trong vài giây.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `천문 사진 좌표 해석`.

## 13. dark frame /dɑːrk freɪm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a calibration image recorded without incoming light to measure the detector's electronic signal.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** calibration image record không có incoming light để đo electronic signal của detector.
**Grammar & collocations:** `matched dark frame` — frame matched; `subtract a dark frame` — subtract frame.
**Examples:** `The pipeline subtracted a matched dark frame from every exposure.` → Pipeline subtract matched dark frame khỏi mỗi exposure.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `다크 프레임`.

## 14. flat-field correction /ˈflæt fiːld kəˈrɛkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** correcting an image for uneven pixel sensitivity, illumination, or dust patterns.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** correct image về pixel sensitivity, illumination hoặc dust pattern không đều.
**Grammar & collocations:** `precise flat-field correction` — correction chính xác; `apply flat-field correction` — apply correction.
**Examples:** `Flat-field correction removed the vignetting from the wide-field image.` → Flat-field correction remove vignetting khỏi wide-field image.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `플랫 필드 보정`.

## 15. bias frame /ˈbaɪəs freɪm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a zero-exposure calibration image that measures the detector's baseline electronic offset.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** calibration image zero-exposure đo baseline electronic offset của detector.
**Grammar & collocations:** `master bias frame` — frame bias master; `subtract the bias frame` — subtract frame.
**Examples:** `The reduction script created a master bias frame from twenty short exposures.` → Reduction script tạo master bias frame từ hai mươi exposure ngắn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `바이어스 프레임`.

## 16. detector read noise /dɪˈtɛktər riːd nɔɪz/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** random electronic variation introduced when a detector reads out its accumulated signal.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** variation electronic ngẫu nhiên khi detector read out signal tích lũy.
**Grammar & collocations:** `low detector read noise` — noise thấp; `characterize detector read noise` — characterize noise.
**Examples:** `Low detector read noise made the faint signal measurable.` → Detector read noise thấp làm faint signal đo được.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `검출기 판독 잡음`.

## 17. point-spread function /ˈpɔɪnt sprɛd ˈfʌŋkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the image shape produced by an instrument when observing an ideal point source.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** shape image instrument tạo ra khi observe ideal point source.
**Grammar & collocations:** `measured point-spread function` — function đo được; `model the point-spread function` — model function.
**Examples:** `The team modeled the point-spread function before measuring galaxy sizes.` → Team model point-spread function trước khi đo galaxy size.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `점 확산 함수`.

## 18. redshift survey /ˈrɛdʃɪft ˈsɜːrveɪ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a catalog of galaxy distances and motions inferred from spectral redshifts.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** catalog distance/motion của galaxy suy ra từ spectral redshift.
**Grammar & collocations:** `deep redshift survey` — survey sâu; `analyze a redshift survey` — analyze survey.
**Examples:** `The redshift survey mapped the large-scale structure of the universe.` → Redshift survey map large-scale structure của universe.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `적색편이 탐사`.

## 19. occultation light curve /ˌɑːkəlˈteɪʃən laɪt kɜːrv/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a graph of brightness change recorded when one object passes in front of another.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** graph brightness change khi một object đi trước object khác.
**Grammar & collocations:** `sharp occultation light curve` — curve sắc; `fit an occultation light curve` — fit curve.
**Examples:** `The occultation light curve constrained the moon's atmosphere.` → Occultation light curve constrain atmosphere của moon.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `엄폐 광도곡선`.

## 20. spectral line broadening /ˈspɛktrəl laɪn ˈbrɔːdənɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the widening of a spectral line caused by motion, pressure, temperature, or instrumental effects.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** spectral line widen vì motion, pressure, temperature hoặc instrument effect.
**Grammar & collocations:** `rotation-induced spectral line broadening` — broadening do rotation; `model spectral line broadening` — model broadening.
**Examples:** `Rotation-induced spectral line broadening revealed the star's rapid spin.` → Spectral line broadening do rotation reveal star quay nhanh.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `스펙트럼 선폭 넓어짐`.

## Review in context

An observatory applies **adaptive-optics correction** and chooses an **interferometric baseline** before feeding light to a **spectrograph** with an **echelle grating**. Scientists measure **radial velocity**, **transit photometry**, or use a **coronagraph** and **aperture synthesis**. Atmospheric limits include the **seeing disk** and **airmass**, while **sidereal tracking** and **plate solving** stabilize the image. Calibration uses a **dark frame**, **flat-field correction**, and **bias frame**, with **detector read noise** and the **point-spread function** modeled explicitly. A **redshift survey**, **occultation light curve**, and **spectral line broadening** turn measurements into astrophysical evidence.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:** Observatory apply **adaptive-optics correction** và chọn **interferometric baseline** trước khi đưa light vào **spectrograph** với **echelle grating**. Scientists đo **radial velocity**, **transit photometry**, hoặc dùng **coronagraph** và **aperture synthesis**. Giới hạn atmosphere gồm **seeing disk** và **airmass**, còn **sidereal tracking** và **plate solving** stabilize image. Calibration dùng **dark frame**, **flat-field correction** và **bias frame**, đồng thời model rõ **detector read noise** và **point-spread function**. **Redshift survey**, **occultation light curve** và **spectral line broadening** biến measurement thành astrophysical evidence.
