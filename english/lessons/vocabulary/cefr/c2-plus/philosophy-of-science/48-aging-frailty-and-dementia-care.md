# C2+ Vocabulary — Aging, frailty, and dementia care

This lesson follows how services recognize frailty, preserve function, support cognition, and coordinate care for older adults. The network is **frailty phenotype → intrinsic capacity → geriatric syndromes → cognitive resilience → delirium prevention → dementia-friendly care → advance dementia planning → behavioral and psychological symptoms → decision-specific capacity → medication deprescribing → falls prevention → mobility aid → continence care → sarcopenia → polypharmacy review → care home → ageism → elder abuse → social prescribing → loneliness intervention → caregiver navigation → geriatric assessment → function-first care → hospital-at-home → reablement**.

## 1. frailty phenotype /ˈfreɪlti ˈfiːnətaɪp/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a pattern of reduced physiological reserve, often shown by weakness, slowness, exhaustion, weight loss, or low activity.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** frailty phenotype mô tả cơ thể ít reserve và dễ sụp khi stress.
**Grammar & collocations:** `frailty-phenotype assessment` — assessment; `identify frailty` — identify; `physiological reserve` — reserve.
**Examples:** `The frailty phenotype became visible through slow walking, exhaustion, and unintentional weight loss.` → Nhiều dấu hiệu cùng tạo pattern.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `노쇠 표현형` — frailty phenotype.

## 2. intrinsic capacity /ɪnˈtrɪnsɪk kəˈpæsəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the combination of a person's physical and mental abilities that supports functioning at a given time.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** intrinsic capacity là khả năng bên trong, khác với environment hỗ trợ hay cản trở.
**Grammar & collocations:** `intrinsic-capacity profile` — profile; `preserve intrinsic capacity` — preserve; `physical ability` — ability.
**Examples:** `The program protected intrinsic capacity by combining strength training, nutrition, and social connection.` → Nhiều yếu tố giữ capacity.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `내재적 역량` — intrinsic capacity.

## 3. geriatric syndromes /dʒɛriˈætrɪk ˈsɪndroʊmz/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** common complex conditions in older adults, such as falls, delirium, incontinence, and frailty, that arise from multiple causes.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** geriatric syndromes là condition nhiều nguyên nhân, không gói trong một disease.
**Grammar & collocations:** `geriatric-syndromes screen` — screen; `manage a complex syndrome` — manage; `multifactorial condition` — condition.
**Examples:** `Geriatric syndromes required a team review because medication, vision, mobility, and housing interacted.` → Nhiều factor cùng tạo syndrome.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `노인 증후군` — geriatric syndromes.

## 4. cognitive resilience /ˈkɑɡnətɪv rɪˈzɪliəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the ability to maintain or recover thinking and functioning despite brain changes, illness, stress, or temporary disruption.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cognitive resilience là khả năng brain giữ function hoặc hồi phục khi gặp stress.
**Grammar & collocations:** `cognitive-resilience measure` — measure; `preserve cognitive function` — preserve; `mental recovery` — recovery.
**Examples:** `Social engagement and physical activity were associated with greater cognitive resilience after hospitalization.` → Activity/social connection hỗ trợ brain.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `인지 회복탄력성` — cognitive resilience.

## 5. delirium prevention /dɪˈlɪriəm prɪˈvɛnʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** coordinated measures that reduce the risk of acute, fluctuating confusion during illness, hospitalization, or surgery.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** delirium prevention giữ sleep, orientation, hydration, mobility và hearing/vision.
**Grammar & collocations:** `delirium-prevention bundle` — bundle; `prevent acute confusion` — prevent; `orientation cue` — cue.
**Examples:** `Delirium prevention included daylight, familiar glasses, early mobility, and avoidance of unnecessary sedation.` → Nhiều action nhỏ cùng bảo vệ cognition.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `섬망 예방` — delirium prevention.

## 6. dementia-friendly care /dɪˈmɛnʃə ˈfrɛndli kɛr/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** care adapted in communication, environment, timing, and support so people with dementia can participate with dignity.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** dementia-friendly care điều chỉnh setting và communication, không chỉ yêu cầu patient “nhớ tốt hơn”.
**Grammar & collocations:** `dementia-friendly-care standard` — standard; `support orientation` — support; `person-centered communication` — communication.
**Examples:** `Dementia-friendly care used one question at a time and a familiar object during the examination.` → Giảm cognitive load.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `치매 친화적 돌봄` — dementia-friendly care.

## 7. advance dementia planning /ədˈvæns dɪˈmɛnʃə ˈplænɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** early discussion and documentation of future care preferences, decision-makers, and values before dementia progresses.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** advance dementia planning giữ voice patient khi disease tiến triển.
**Grammar & collocations:** `advance-dementia-planning conversation` — conversation; `document future preferences` — document; `decision-maker` — decision-maker.
**Examples:** `Advance dementia planning recorded the person's wish to remain at home if safe support was available.` → Preference được ghi sớm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `치매 사전 계획` — advance dementia planning.

## 8. behavioral and psychological symptoms /bɪˈheɪvjərəl ænd ˌsaɪkəˈlɑdʒɪkəl ˈsɪmptəmz/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** changes such as agitation, apathy, hallucinations, anxiety, or sleep disruption that occur with dementia or other neurological illness.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** behavioral and psychological symptoms là expression của distress/brain change, không chỉ “behavior xấu”.
**Grammar & collocations:** `behavioral-and-psychological-symptoms plan` — plan; `assess agitation` — assess; `nonpharmacological support` — support.
**Examples:** `The team treated behavioral and psychological symptoms by checking pain, noise, loneliness, and medication effects.` → Tìm nguyên nhân trước khi sedate.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `행동 및 심리 증상` — behavioral and psychological symptoms.

## 9. decision-specific capacity /dɪˈsɪʒən spəˈsɪfɪk kəˈpæsəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** ability to make one particular decision at one particular time, which may differ across decisions and circumstances.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** decision-specific capacity chống lại kết luận “mất capacity toàn bộ”.
**Grammar & collocations:** `decision-specific-capacity assessment` — assessment; `assess a particular choice` — assess; `time-specific ability` — ability.
**Examples:** `Decision-specific capacity allowed the patient to choose meals even while needing help with finances.` → Capacity khác nhau theo decision.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `결정별 능력` — decision-specific capacity.

## 10. medication deprescribing /ˌmɛdɪˈkeɪʃən ˌdiːprɪˈskrɪbɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** planned reduction or stopping of medicines that may no longer help, may cause harm, or no longer match a person's goals.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** medication deprescribing là review rồi giảm thuốc có chủ đích, không phải bỏ thuốc tùy ý.
**Grammar & collocations:** `medication-deprescribing plan` — plan; `taper a medicine` — taper; `treatment burden` — burden.
**Examples:** `Medication deprescribing reduced dizziness after the team reviewed every prescription and goal.` → Review cả list và goal.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `약물 감량 중단` — medication deprescribing.

## 11. falls prevention /fɔlz prɪˈvɛnʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** coordinated action to reduce the likelihood and consequences of falls among people at risk.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** falls prevention kết hợp strength, vision, medication, footwear, environment và support.
**Grammar & collocations:** `falls-prevention program` — program; `prevent a fall` — prevent; `balance training` — training.
**Examples:** `Falls prevention included strength exercises, brighter lighting, and a medication review.` → Không chỉ bảo patient đi cẩn thận.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `낙상 예방` — falls prevention.

## 12. mobility aid /moʊˈbɪləti eɪd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** equipment that helps a person move safely, such as a cane, walker, wheelchair, or transfer device.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mobility aid mở rộng movement và giảm risk khi đi/chuyển.
**Grammar & collocations:** `mobility-aid assessment` — assessment; `use a walker` — use; `safe transfer` — transfer.
**Examples:** `The therapist adjusted the mobility aid so the patient could reach the bathroom without pulling on furniture.` → Aid đúng giúp independent hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `이동 보조기기` — mobility aid.

## 13. continence care /ˈkɑntənəns kɛr/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** assessment and support for bladder or bowel control that protects dignity, skin, health, and participation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** continence care không chỉ là supply; gồm assessment, timing, skin và dignity.
**Grammar & collocations:** `continence-care plan` — plan; `manage bladder symptoms` — manage; `toileting schedule` — schedule.
**Examples:** `Continence care used a toileting schedule and hydration plan instead of unnecessary long-term catheterization.` → Tránh catheter không cần.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `배뇨·배변 관리` — continence care.

## 14. sarcopenia /ˌsɑrkəˈpiːniə/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** age-related loss of muscle mass, strength, and physical performance.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** sarcopenia làm body yếu dần qua muscle mass và strength.
**Grammar & collocations:** `sarcopenia screening` — screening; `preserve muscle strength` — preserve; `resistance exercise` — exercise.
**Examples:** `Sarcopenia made recovery slower, so the plan combined protein support with resistance exercise.` → Nutrition + exercise cùng cần.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `근감소증` — sarcopenia.

## 15. polypharmacy review /ˌpɑliˈfɑrməsi rɪˈvjuː/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** systematic review of a person's multiple medicines to identify unnecessary treatment, interactions, duplication, and burden.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** polypharmacy review nhìn toàn bộ medicine list, không chỉ từng prescription.
**Grammar & collocations:** `polypharmacy-review clinic` — clinic; `reconcile medicines` — reconcile; `drug interaction` — interaction.
**Examples:** `The polypharmacy review found two medicines treating the same symptom and one causing falls.` → Review tìm duplication và side effect.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `다약제 복용 검토` — polypharmacy review.

## 16. care home /kɛr hoʊm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a residential setting providing accommodation and varying levels of personal, nursing, or medical support for older or disabled people.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** care home là nơi ở có support care, không đồng nghĩa hospital.
**Grammar & collocations:** `care-home resident` — resident; `provide residential care` — provide; `care-home staff` — staff.
**Examples:** `The care home created a quiet dining area for residents who became overwhelmed by noise.` → Environment được adapt.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `요양원` — care home.

## 17. ageism /ˈeɪdʒɪzəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** stereotyping, prejudice, or discrimination based on a person's age.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** ageism coi older person luôn yếu, không muốn hoặc không có khả năng quyết định.
**Grammar & collocations:** `ageism-awareness training` — training; `challenge ageist assumptions` — challenge; `age discrimination` — discrimination.
**Examples:** `The clinician challenged ageism by asking the older patient directly about treatment goals.` → Hỏi trực tiếp thay vì nói qua family.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `연령 차별` — ageism.

## 18. elder abuse /ˈɛldər əˈbjuːs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** intentional or neglectful harm, exploitation, or coercion affecting an older person.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** elder abuse gồm physical, emotional, financial, sexual harm hoặc neglect.
**Grammar & collocations:** `elder-abuse screening` — screening; `report suspected abuse` — report; `financial exploitation` — exploitation.
**Examples:** `The team screened privately for elder abuse after noticing unexplained withdrawals and fear around a relative.` → Screen riêng để người già nói thật.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `노인 학대` — elder abuse.

## 19. social prescribing /ˈsoʊʃəl prɪˈskraɪbɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** linking people to community activities, groups, practical help, or social resources as part of a broader health plan.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** social prescribing “kê” connection/community resource cùng medical care.
**Grammar & collocations:** `social-prescribing referral` — referral; `link a patient to community support` — link; `social resource` — resource.
**Examples:** `Social prescribing connected the isolated patient with a walking group and a meal cooperative.` → Community activity hỗ trợ health.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `사회적 처방` — social prescribing.

## 20. loneliness intervention /ˈloʊnlinəs ˌɪntərˈvɛnʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a planned action intended to reduce distress from insufficient meaningful social connection.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** loneliness intervention tạo connection meaningful, không chỉ đưa person vào chỗ đông người.
**Grammar & collocations:** `loneliness-intervention trial` — trial; `reduce social isolation` — reduce; `meaningful connection` — connection.
**Examples:** `The loneliness intervention paired weekly calls with opportunities for reciprocal volunteering.` → Connection có qua lại, không chỉ call một chiều.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `외로움 개입` — loneliness intervention.

## 21. caregiver navigation /ˈkɛrˌɡɪvər ˌnævəˈɡeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** guidance that helps unpaid caregivers understand services, benefits, appointments, decisions, and support options.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** caregiver navigation giúp người chăm sóc không bị lạc trong system.
**Grammar & collocations:** `caregiver-navigation service` — service; `navigate benefits` — navigate; `family support plan` — plan.
**Examples:** `Caregiver navigation helped the daughter arrange transport, respite, and a medication review.` → Navigator nối nhiều service.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `돌봄 제공자 네비게이션` — caregiver navigation.

## 22. geriatric assessment /ˌdʒɛriˈætrɪk əˈsɛsmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** multidimensional assessment of an older person's medical conditions, function, cognition, mood, medicines, support, and environment.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** geriatric assessment nhìn cả person và context, không chỉ disease list.
**Grammar & collocations:** `geriatric-assessment team` — team; `assess function and cognition` — assess; `multidimensional review` — review.
**Examples:** `The geriatric assessment found that a housing hazard mattered as much as the patient's heart disease.` → Housing cũng là health factor.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `노인 포괄 평가` — geriatric assessment.

## 23. function-first care /ˈfʌŋkʃən fɜrst kɛr/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** care organized around preserving or restoring what a person can do and values doing, not merely treating diagnoses.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** function-first care hỏi patient muốn làm gì và care giúp việc đó thế nào.
**Grammar & collocations:** `function-first-care plan` — plan; `restore daily function` — restore; `valued activity` — activity.
**Examples:** `Function-first care focused on walking to the garden rather than achieving a perfect laboratory value.` → Function thực tế làm goal.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `기능 우선 돌봄` — function-first care.

## 24. hospital-at-home /ˈhɑspɪtəl æt hoʊm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** hospital-level assessment and treatment delivered in a person's home with reliable clinical monitoring and escalation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** hospital-at-home đưa intensity của hospital về home khi đủ safe.
**Grammar & collocations:** `hospital-at-home model` — model; `deliver hospital-level care` — deliver; `remote monitoring` — monitoring.
**Examples:** `Hospital-at-home treated the infection while preserving the patient's familiar routines and family support.` → Home giữ routine và support.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `재택 병원 진료` — hospital-at-home.

## 25. reablement /ˌriːəˈbeɪlmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** short-term, goal-focused support that helps a person regain skills and independence after illness, injury, or functional decline.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** reablement tập trung giúp person làm lại được, không tạo dependence dài hạn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `reablement service` — service; `regain daily skills` — regain; `goal-focused support` — support.
**Examples:** `Reablement taught the patient to shower safely and prepare breakfast after the stroke.` → Support hướng tới tự làm lại.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `재활적 자립 지원` — reablement.

## Review in context — Part 1

The **frailty phenotype**, **intrinsic capacity**, and **geriatric syndromes** guide a broad assessment. **Cognitive resilience** may be protected through **delirium prevention** and **dementia-friendly care**. **Advance dementia planning**, attention to **behavioral and psychological symptoms**, and **decision-specific capacity** preserve voice. **Medication deprescribing**, **falls prevention**, a suitable **mobility aid**, **continence care**, and treatment of **sarcopenia** can restore function. A **polypharmacy review** may improve safety for a resident in a **care home**.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 1:**

**Frailty phenotype**, **intrinsic capacity** và **geriatric syndromes** hướng dẫn assessment rộng. **Cognitive resilience** có thể được bảo vệ bằng **delirium prevention** và **dementia-friendly care**. **Advance dementia planning**, chú ý tới **behavioral and psychological symptoms** và **decision-specific capacity** giữ voice của patient. **Medication deprescribing**, **falls prevention**, **mobility aid** phù hợp, **continence care** và xử lý **sarcopenia** có thể phục hồi function. **Polypharmacy review** có thể tăng safety cho resident ở **care home**.

## Review in context — Part 2

**Ageism** can hide **elder abuse**, so clinicians should ask privately and respectfully. **Social prescribing**, a **loneliness intervention**, and **caregiver navigation** connect people to practical and social support. A **geriatric assessment** supports **function-first care**. **Hospital-at-home** can deliver intensive treatment in familiar surroundings, while **reablement** helps a person regain daily skills rather than becoming unnecessarily dependent.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 2:**

**Ageism** có thể che giấu **elder abuse**, nên clinician cần hỏi riêng và tôn trọng. **Social prescribing**, **loneliness intervention** và **caregiver navigation** nối person với support thực tế và xã hội. **Geriatric assessment** hỗ trợ **function-first care**. **Hospital-at-home** có thể cung cấp treatment intensive trong môi trường quen thuộc, còn **reablement** giúp person lấy lại skill hằng ngày thay vì phụ thuộc không cần thiết.
