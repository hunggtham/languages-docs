# C2+ Vocabulary — Health outcomes and value measurement

This lesson follows how health systems define value, measure patient benefit, compare care, and interpret performance data. The network is **value-based care → patient voice measure → patient-reported experience → quality-adjusted life-year → disability-adjusted life-year → incremental benefit → absolute risk reduction → number needed to treat → minimum clinically important difference → proxy endpoint → combined endpoint → practice-based evidence → reference comparison → routine outcome monitoring → risk adjustment → case-mix index → comparative value → minimum quality bar → pay-for-performance → performance measure → denominator exclusion → process indicator → outcome indicator → counterfactual benchmark → implementation outcome**.

## 1. value-based care /ˈvælju beɪst kɛr/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** organization and payment of care around meaningful patient outcomes and responsible use of resources rather than service volume alone.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** value-based care hỏi care tạo outcome gì với resource nào.
**Grammar & collocations:** `value-based-care model` — model; `improve value` — improve; `patient outcome` — outcome.
**Examples:** `Value-based care rewarded the clinic for helping patients control diabetes over time.` → Clinic được reward theo outcome dài hạn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `가치 기반 의료` — value-based care.

## 2. patient voice measure /ˈpeɪʃənt vɔɪs ˈmɛʒər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a measure that captures what patients report about symptoms, goals, burden, or the effect of care on their lives.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** patient voice measure đưa trải nghiệm và goal của patient vào outcome.
**Grammar & collocations:** `patient-voice measure` — measure; `capture patient priorities` — capture; `self-reported symptom` — symptom.
**Examples:** `The patient voice measure showed that pain relief mattered more than a small change in laboratory values.` → Patient coi pain relief quan trọng hơn lab value nhỏ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `환자 목소리 지표` — patient voice measure.

## 3. patient-reported experience /ˈpeɪʃənt rɪˈpɔrtɪd ɪkˈspɪriəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a patient's account of how care was accessed, communicated, coordinated, and experienced.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** patient-reported experience kể patient đã đi qua care journey thế nào.
**Grammar & collocations:** `patient-reported-experience survey` — survey; `report a care experience` — report; `communication quality` — quality.
**Examples:** `Patient-reported experience improved after the hospital explained delays and assigned one contact person.` → Giải thích delay và có contact làm experience tốt hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `환자 보고 경험` — patient-reported experience.

## 4. quality-adjusted life-year /ˈkwɑləti əˈdʒʌstɪd laɪf jɪr/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a unit combining length of life with the quality of health experienced during that time.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** quality-adjusted life-year quy đổi thêm quality vào số năm sống.
**Grammar & collocations:** `quality-adjusted-life-year estimate` — estimate; `gain a QALY` — gain; `health-state utility` — utility.
**Examples:** `The analysis estimated how many quality-adjusted life-years the new treatment could add.` → Analysis tính số năm sống có quality.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `질보정수명` — quality-adjusted life-year.

## 5. disability-adjusted life-year /ˌdɪsəˈbɪləti əˈdʒʌstɪd laɪf jɪr/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a population-health unit combining years lost through early death with years lived with disability or reduced health.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** disability-adjusted life-year cộng premature death và disability burden.
**Grammar & collocations:** `disability-adjusted-life-year estimate` — estimate; `measure disease burden` — measure; `years lived with disability` — years.
**Examples:** `The study used disability-adjusted life-years to compare the burden of injury and infectious disease.` → Dùng unit để so burden khác disease.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `장애보정수명` — disability-adjusted life-year.

## 6. incremental benefit /ˌɪŋkrəˈmɛntl ˈbɛnəfɪt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the additional health gain produced by one intervention compared with a relevant alternative.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** incremental benefit chỉ phần gain thêm so với option đang có.
**Grammar & collocations:** `incremental-benefit estimate` — estimate; `measure additional benefit` — measure; `comparator treatment` — treatment.
**Examples:** `The incremental benefit was modest, but it mattered for patients who had failed standard therapy.` → Gain nhỏ nhưng hữu ích cho nhóm khó trị.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `증분 편익` — incremental benefit.

## 7. absolute risk reduction /ˈæbsəluːt rɪsk rɪˈdʌkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the difference in event risk between a treated group and a comparison group, expressed in absolute terms.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** absolute risk reduction là risk giảm thật bao nhiêu điểm, không chỉ phần trăm tương đối.
**Grammar & collocations:** `absolute-risk-reduction estimate` — estimate; `reduce absolute risk` — reduce; `baseline event rate` — rate.
**Examples:** `The drug lowered relative risk substantially, but its absolute risk reduction was small because the baseline risk was low.` → Relative lớn nhưng absolute nhỏ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `절대 위험 감소` — absolute risk reduction.

## 8. number needed to treat /ˈnʌmbər ˈniːdɪd tə triːt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the number of people who must receive an intervention for one additional person to benefit compared with the alternative.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** number needed to treat hỏi cần treat bao nhiêu người để có thêm một benefit.
**Grammar & collocations:** `number-needed-to-treat estimate` — estimate; `calculate NNT` — calculate; `additional beneficiary` — beneficiary.
**Examples:** `The number needed to treat was favorable for patients at high baseline risk.` → Risk nền cao làm NNT tốt hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `치료필요수` — number needed to treat.

## 9. minimum clinically important difference /ˈmɪnəməm ˈklɪnɪkli ɪmˈpɔrtənt ˈdɪfrəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the smallest change in an outcome that patients or clinicians would regard as meaningful in practice.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** minimum clinically important difference phân biệt thay đổi có ý nghĩa với thay đổi chỉ có trên giấy.
**Grammar & collocations:** `minimum-clinically-important-difference threshold` — threshold; `exceed a meaningful change` — exceed; `patient-perceived benefit` — benefit.
**Examples:** `The average score improved, but it did not reach the minimum clinically important difference.` → Có cải thiện nhưng patient chưa cảm thấy đủ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `최소 임상 중요 차이` — minimum clinically important difference.

## 10. proxy endpoint /ˈprɑksi ˈɛndˌpɔɪnt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an intermediate measure used as a substitute for a more direct outcome that matters to patients.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** proxy endpoint là dấu hiệu thay thế, cần xem nó có thật sự dự báo patient outcome không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `proxy-endpoint validation` — validation; `use a surrogate measure` — use; `patient-important outcome` — outcome.
**Examples:** `Lower cholesterol was a proxy endpoint, so the trial still needed evidence about heart attacks and survival.` → Cholesterol thấp chưa đủ chứng minh sống lâu.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `대리지표 평가변수` — proxy endpoint.

## 11. combined endpoint /kəmˈbaɪnd ˈɛndˌpɔɪnt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an outcome measure that groups several events or conditions into one composite result.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** combined endpoint gộp nhiều event, nhưng từng component có thể quan trọng khác nhau.
**Grammar & collocations:** `combined-endpoint analysis` — analysis; `combine clinical events` — combine; `component outcome` — outcome.
**Examples:** `The combined endpoint was positive because hospitalization fell, even though mortality did not change.` → Composite dương nhờ một component.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `복합 평가변수` — combined endpoint.

## 12. practice-based evidence /ˈpræktɪs beɪst ˈɛvɪdəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** evidence generated from routine care settings and real clinical practice rather than only from tightly controlled experiments.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** practice-based evidence học từ care thật với patient thật và constraint thật.
**Grammar & collocations:** `practice-based-evidence study` — study; `learn from routine care` — learn; `clinical setting` — setting.
**Examples:** `Practice-based evidence showed that the intervention was harder to deliver in understaffed clinics.` → Care thật lộ barrier implementation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `실무 기반 근거` — practice-based evidence.

## 13. reference comparison /ˈrɛfərəns kəmˈpɛrəsən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** comparison with a defined standard, previous period, peer group, or usual-care condition used to interpret performance.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** reference comparison cho biết result phải so với mốc nào mới có nghĩa.
**Grammar & collocations:** `reference-comparison group` — group; `compare against a reference` — compare; `usual care` — care.
**Examples:** `The reference comparison used last year's adjusted outcomes rather than raw averages.` → So với baseline đã adjust.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `기준 비교` — reference comparison.

## 14. routine outcome monitoring /ruːˈtiːn ˈaʊtˌkʌm ˈmɑnətərɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** regular collection and review of outcomes during ordinary service delivery to detect change and guide improvement.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** routine outcome monitoring biến measurement thành vòng feedback liên tục.
**Grammar & collocations:** `routine-outcome-monitoring dashboard` — dashboard; `monitor outcomes routinely` — monitor; `improvement cycle` — cycle.
**Examples:** `Routine outcome monitoring revealed that recovery slowed after the referral pathway changed.` → Monitoring tìm ra recovery chậm hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `정기 결과 모니터링` — routine outcome monitoring.

## 15. risk adjustment /rɪsk əˈdʒʌstmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** statistical adjustment that accounts for differences in patient risk, severity, or circumstance before comparing outcomes or payments.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** risk adjustment làm comparison công bằng hơn giữa patient mix khác nhau.
**Grammar & collocations:** `risk-adjustment model` — model; `adjust for case severity` — adjust; `patient mix` — mix.
**Examples:** `Risk adjustment prevented the safety-net hospital from being judged against a healthier patient population.` → Adjust để không phạt hospital nhận case nặng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `위험 보정` — risk adjustment.

## 16. case-mix index /ˈkeɪs mɪks ˈɪnˌdɛks/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a measure of the average complexity and resource intensity of cases treated by a provider or hospital.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** case-mix index cho biết patient mix nặng và tốn resource tới mức nào.
**Grammar & collocations:** `case-mix-index value` — value; `reflect case complexity` — reflect; `resource intensity` — intensity.
**Examples:** `The hospital's case-mix index rose after it became the regional referral center for severe injuries.` → Referral center nhận case nặng hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `환자 구성 지수` — case-mix index.

## 17. minimum quality bar /ˈmɪnəməm ˈkwɑləti bɑr/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the lowest acceptable level of safety, effectiveness, or service quality that a provider or program must meet.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** minimum quality bar là sàn quality không được thấp hơn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `minimum-quality-bar standard` — standard; `meet the quality bar` — meet; `acceptable performance` — performance.
**Examples:** `The regulator set a minimum quality bar for infection control before allowing the clinic to expand.` → Clinic phải đạt sàn infection control.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `최소 품질 기준` — minimum quality bar.

## 18. pay-for-performance /ˈpeɪ fər pərˈfɔrməns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a payment arrangement that gives providers additional money for meeting specified quality, access, or outcome targets.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** pay-for-performance gắn bonus với target performance.
**Grammar & collocations:** `pay-for-performance contract` — contract; `reward target achievement` — reward; `quality incentive` — incentive.
**Examples:** `Pay-for-performance improved screening rates but had little effect on patients who faced transport barriers.` → Bonus tăng screening nhưng barrier access vẫn còn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `성과 보상제` — pay-for-performance.

## 19. performance measure /pərˈfɔrməns ˈmɛʒər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a defined indicator used to assess how well a person, service, or system performs a specified function.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** performance measure biến expectation thành indicator để theo dõi.
**Grammar & collocations:** `performance-measure set` — set; `track performance` — track; `defined indicator` — indicator.
**Examples:** `The performance measure tracked whether discharge summaries reached primary care within forty-eight hours.` → Measure theo dõi handoff đúng hạn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `성과 지표` — performance measure.

## 20. denominator exclusion /dɪˈnɑməˌneɪtər ɪkˈskluːʒən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** removal of eligible-looking cases from the denominator of a performance measure because a documented exception applies.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** denominator exclusion quyết định case nào không được tính vào mẫu số.
**Grammar & collocations:** `denominator-exclusion rule` — rule; `exclude an exception case` — exclude; `eligible denominator` — denominator.
**Examples:** `Denominator exclusion was audited because broad exclusions could make the clinic's rate look artificially high.` → Exclude quá rộng làm rate đẹp giả.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `분모 제외` — denominator exclusion.

## 21. process indicator /ˈprɑsɛs ˈɪndəˌkeɪtər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a measure of whether a recommended action, workflow, or step of care occurred.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** process indicator đo “đã làm đúng bước chưa”, chưa chắc đo outcome cuối.
**Grammar & collocations:** `process-indicator target` — target; `complete a care step` — complete; `recommended process` — process.
**Examples:** `The process indicator confirmed that high-risk patients received medication reconciliation.` → Indicator xác nhận đã làm reconciliation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `과정 지표` — process indicator.

## 22. outcome indicator /ˈaʊtˌkʌm ˈɪndəˌkeɪtər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a measure of the health result, experience, or condition produced after care or policy is delivered.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** outcome indicator đo result xảy ra sau care.
**Grammar & collocations:** `outcome-indicator dashboard` — dashboard; `measure a health outcome` — measure; `result indicator` — indicator.
**Examples:** `The outcome indicator tracked avoidable admissions rather than merely counting counseling sessions.` → Đo admission tránh được, không chỉ đếm session.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `결과 지표` — outcome indicator.

## 23. counterfactual benchmark /ˌkaʊntərˈfæktʃuəl ˈbɛntʃˌmɑrk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an estimate of what would have happened without the intervention, used as a comparison for judging its effect.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** counterfactual benchmark dựng scenario “nếu không làm intervention thì sao”.
**Grammar & collocations:** `counterfactual-benchmark estimate` — estimate; `compare with no-treatment scenario` — compare; `causal effect` — effect.
**Examples:** `The counterfactual benchmark suggested that only part of the improvement came from the new program.` → Không phải toàn bộ gain do program.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `반사실적 기준` — counterfactual benchmark.

## 24. implementation outcome /ˌɪmpləmɛnˈteɪʃən ˈaʊtˌkʌm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a result describing whether an intervention was adopted, delivered, acceptable, feasible, faithful, or sustainable in practice.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** implementation outcome đo intervention có sống được trong practice không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `implementation-outcome measure` — measure; `assess feasibility` — assess; `sustain a program` — sustain.
**Examples:** `The implementation outcome was weak because staff accepted the protocol but could not fit it into routine visits.` → Staff đồng ý nhưng workflow không fit.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `실행 결과` — implementation outcome.

## 25. comparative value /kəmˈpærətɪv ˈvæljuː/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the value of one intervention relative to relevant alternatives, considering outcomes, harms, costs, and feasibility together.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** comparative value không hỏi treatment có tốt không, mà hỏi tốt hơn option nào và đáng cost không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `comparative-value assessment` — assessment; `compare treatment value` — compare; `relevant alternative` — alternative.
**Examples:** `Comparative value favored the simpler treatment because it delivered similar outcomes at lower cost.` → Option đơn giản hơn nhưng value tốt hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `비교 가치` — comparative value.

## Review in context — Part 1

**Value-based care** combines a **patient voice measure** and **patient-reported experience** with outcomes that can be expressed as a **quality-adjusted life-year** or **disability-adjusted life-year**. Analysts estimate **incremental benefit**, **absolute risk reduction**, and **number needed to treat**, then ask whether the change reaches the **minimum clinically important difference**. A **proxy endpoint** or **combined endpoint** may simplify measurement, while **practice-based evidence** and a **reference comparison** show how care works in context.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 1:**

**Value-based care** kết hợp **patient voice measure** và **patient-reported experience** với outcome có thể biểu diễn bằng **quality-adjusted life-year** hoặc **disability-adjusted life-year**. Analyst tính **incremental benefit**, **absolute risk reduction** và **number needed to treat**, rồi hỏi thay đổi có đạt **minimum clinically important difference** không. **Proxy endpoint** hoặc **combined endpoint** làm measurement đơn giản hơn, còn **practice-based evidence** và **reference comparison** cho thấy care vận hành trong context thật.

## Review in context — Part 2

**Routine outcome monitoring**, **risk adjustment**, and a **case-mix index** improve interpretation; a **minimum quality bar** prevents unsafe performance. **Pay-for-performance** uses a **performance measure**, but **denominator exclusion** can distort it. A **process indicator** shows whether care happened, an **outcome indicator** shows what resulted, and a **counterfactual benchmark** estimates what would have happened otherwise. An **implementation outcome** and **comparative value** complete the assessment.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 2:**

**Routine outcome monitoring**, **risk adjustment** và **case-mix index** làm interpretation tốt hơn; **minimum quality bar** ngăn performance không an toàn. **Pay-for-performance** dùng **performance measure**, nhưng **denominator exclusion** có thể làm méo kết quả. **Process indicator** cho biết bước care có diễn ra, **outcome indicator** cho biết result, và **counterfactual benchmark** ước tính điều sẽ xảy ra nếu không có intervention. **Implementation outcome** và **comparative value** hoàn tất assessment.
