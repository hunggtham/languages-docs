# C2+ Vocabulary — Health financing and provider payment

This lesson follows how health systems raise funds, pool uncertainty, purchase care, and shape provider behavior through payment rules. The network is **contribution pooling → cross-subsidy → strategic purchasing → provider payment reform → capitation adjustment → fee-for-service incentive → bundled payment → global budget → prospective payment → behavioral hazard → selection distortion → cream skimming → uncompensated care → financial protection → premature discontinuation → coverage gap → benefit package → reimbursement lag → claims adjudication → fraud risk → audit sampling → cost shifting → budgetary room → earmarked revenue → sin tax**.

## 1. contribution pooling /ˌkɑntrəˈbjuʃən ˈpuːlɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** combining contributions from many people or sponsors so that health costs are shared across a larger group.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** contribution pooling gom contribution để cả group cùng chia risk.
**Grammar & collocations:** `contribution-pooling arrangement` — arrangement; `pool contributions` — pool; `shared health risk` — risk.
**Examples:** `Contribution pooling protected small households from the full cost of an unexpected admission.` → Nhiều người cùng chia risk.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `기여금 풀링` — contribution pooling.

## 2. cross-subsidy /ˈkrɔs ˈsʌbsədi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** financing in which one group, service, or activity helps pay for another group, service, or activity.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cross-subsidy chuyển surplus từ một nhóm sang hỗ trợ nhóm khác.
**Grammar & collocations:** `cross-subsidy mechanism` — mechanism; `cross-subsidize care` — cross-subsidize; `higher-income contributor` — contributor.
**Examples:** `The cross-subsidy allowed higher-margin services to support low-income primary care.` → Service có margin cao hỗ trợ primary care.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `교차 보조` — cross-subsidy.

## 3. strategic purchasing /strəˈtiːdʒɪk ˈpɜrtʃəsɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** deliberate purchasing of health services using information, contracts, and incentives to improve quality, equity, and value.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** strategic purchasing không chỉ mua service; nó dùng contract để shape value.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strategic-purchasing agency` — agency; `purchase for value` — purchase; `provider contract` — contract.
**Examples:** `Strategic purchasing linked payment to timely follow-up rather than to visit volume alone.` → Payment gắn với outcome hơn số visit.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `전략적 구매` — strategic purchasing.

## 4. provider payment reform /prəˈvaɪdər ˈpeɪmənt rɪˈfɔrm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** redesign of the rules by which clinicians, facilities, or organizations are paid for health services.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** provider payment reform đổi cách trả tiền để đổi behavior và quality.
**Grammar & collocations:** `provider-payment-reform pilot` — pilot; `redesign payment rules` — redesign; `payment incentive` — incentive.
**Examples:** `Provider payment reform rewarded integrated chronic-care management instead of fragmented consultations.` → Trả tiền cho care tích hợp.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `의료 제공자 지불 개혁` — provider payment reform.

## 5. capitation adjustment /ˌkæpəˈteɪʃən əˈdʒʌstmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** modification of a fixed per-person payment to reflect differences in expected need, risk, age, or social circumstance.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** capitation adjustment sửa payment per person theo risk và need khác nhau.
**Grammar & collocations:** `capitation-adjustment formula` — formula; `adjust for risk` — adjust; `enrolled population` — population.
**Examples:** `Capitation adjustment gave rural clinics more funding for older and medically complex populations.` → Rural có need cao nhận nhiều hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `인두제 조정` — capitation adjustment.

## 6. fee-for-service incentive /fiː fər ˈsɜrvɪs ɪnˈsɛntɪv/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the motivation created when providers are paid separately for each visit, test, procedure, or billable service.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** fee-for-service incentive có thể kéo volume lên vì mỗi service tạo thêm payment.
**Grammar & collocations:** `fee-for-service incentive` — incentive; `bill a procedure` — bill; `service volume` — volume.
**Examples:** `The fee-for-service incentive encouraged repeat testing even when a shared care plan would have sufficed.` → Payment theo procedure làm test lặp.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `행위별 수가 인센티브` — fee-for-service incentive.

## 7. bundled payment /ˈbʌndəld ˈpeɪmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** one negotiated payment covering a defined episode of care or group of related services.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** bundled payment gom nhiều service trong một episode thành một package.
**Grammar & collocations:** `bundled-payment contract` — contract; `cover an episode of care` — cover; `episode price` — price.
**Examples:** `The bundled payment covered surgery, rehabilitation, and routine follow-up for ninety days.` → Một bundle cho cả episode.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `묶음 지불` — bundled payment.

## 8. global budget /ˈɡloʊbəl ˈbʌdʒət/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a predetermined total amount available to an institution or service for a defined period.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** global budget đặt trần tổng chi, buộc organization ưu tiên trong envelope.
**Grammar & collocations:** `global-budget cap` — cap; `operate within a budget` — operate; `annual allocation` — allocation.
**Examples:** `A global budget gave the hospital flexibility but required transparent choices when demand exceeded forecasts.` → Có trần nhưng được tự phân bổ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `총액 예산` — global budget.

## 9. prospective payment /prəˈspɛktɪv ˈpeɪmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** payment determined in advance for a defined service, case, or episode rather than calculated only after costs occur.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** prospective payment chốt payment trước khi biết cost thực tế.
**Grammar & collocations:** `prospective-payment rate` — rate; `set payment in advance` — set; `case category` — category.
**Examples:** `Prospective payment encouraged the hospital to manage length of stay within the case rate.` → Hospital phải quản length of stay.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `선불 지불` — prospective payment.

## 10. behavioral hazard /bɪˈheɪvjərəl ˈhæzərd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a change in behavior after protection or payment reduces the perceived cost of taking a risk or using a service.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** behavioral hazard là behavior đổi vì người dùng không còn cảm thấy full cost.
**Grammar & collocations:** `behavioral-hazard risk` — risk; `change use behavior` — change; `insured patient` — patient.
**Examples:** `The plan monitored behavioral hazard without assuming that higher use was automatically wasteful.` → Dùng nhiều chưa chắc lãng phí.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `행태적 위험` — behavioral hazard.

## 11. selection distortion /sɪˈlɛkʃən dɪˈstɔrʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** bias created when the people, providers, or cases entering a payment arrangement differ systematically from those left out.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** selection distortion làm kết quả lệch vì group tham gia không giống population thật.
**Grammar & collocations:** `selection-distortion check` — check; `enter a payment pool` — enter; `systematic difference` — difference.
**Examples:** `Selection distortion made the pilot look successful because the sickest patients were excluded.` → Exclude case nặng làm result đẹp giả.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `선택 왜곡` — selection distortion.

## 12. cream skimming /kriːm ˈskɪmɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** selection of easier, cheaper, or lower-risk patients while avoiding people whose care is more complex or costly.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cream skimming lấy “phần dễ và ngon” rồi né case khó.
**Grammar & collocations:** `cream-skimming risk` — risk; `select low-risk patients` — select; `complex case` — case.
**Examples:** `The regulator audited cream skimming after one network stopped accepting patients with multiple conditions.` → Network né multimorbidity.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `선별적 환자 취급` — cream skimming.

## 13. uncompensated care /ˌʌnkəmˈpɛnsˌeɪtɪd kɛr/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** medically necessary care for which the provider receives no or insufficient payment.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** uncompensated care là care đã làm nhưng payment không tới hoặc không đủ.
**Grammar & collocations:** `uncompensated-care cost` — cost; `provide uncompensated care` — provide; `unpaid service` — service.
**Examples:** `Uncompensated care weakened the clinic's ability to keep evening appointments open.` → Care miễn phí quá nhiều làm clinic hụt sức.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `미보상 진료` — uncompensated care.

## 14. financial protection /faɪˈnænʃəl prəˈtɛkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** protection against health costs that would otherwise force people to reduce basic consumption, borrow heavily, or fall into poverty.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** financial protection giữ medical cost không phá household budget.
**Grammar & collocations:** `financial-protection policy` — policy; `protect households from costs` — protect; `out-of-pocket payment` — payment.
**Examples:** `Financial protection improved when the benefit package covered essential medicines without a large deductible.` → Cover medicine giúp household.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `재정적 보호` — financial protection.

## 15. premature discontinuation /ˌpriːməˈtʃʊr ˌdɪskənˌtɪnjuˈeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** stopping a treatment, service, or program before its clinically or operationally appropriate endpoint.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** premature discontinuation là dừng quá sớm vì cost, access hoặc payment.
**Grammar & collocations:** `premature-discontinuation rate` — rate; `discontinue treatment early` — discontinue; `unfinished course` — course.
**Examples:** `Premature discontinuation rose when patients had to pay for the final visits in a rehabilitation course.` → Không trả nổi nên dừng rehab sớm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `조기 중단` — premature discontinuation.

## 16. coverage gap /ˈkʌvərɪdʒ ɡæp/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a period, population, service, or cost that falls outside available health insurance or public coverage.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** coverage gap là phần rơi ngoài insurance/benefit.
**Grammar & collocations:** `coverage-gap analysis` — analysis; `close a coverage gap` — close; `eligible population` — population.
**Examples:** `A coverage gap left seasonal workers without medicine access between contracts.` → Worker thời vụ mất cover giữa hai contract.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `보장 공백` — coverage gap.

## 17. benefit package /ˈbɛnəfɪt ˈpækɪdʒ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the defined set of health services, medicines, devices, and protections included under a financing arrangement.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** benefit package là danh mục service được cover.
**Grammar & collocations:** `benefit-package design` — design; `define covered services` — define; `excluded item` — item.
**Examples:** `The benefit package added community mental-health visits but excluded high-cost experimental procedures.` → Package mở mental health, loại procedure experimental.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `급여 패키지` — benefit package.

## 18. reimbursement lag /ˌriːɪmˈbɜrsmənt læɡ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the delay between providing an eligible service and receiving the payment owed for it.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** reimbursement lag làm provider phải chờ tiền sau khi đã cung cấp care.
**Grammar & collocations:** `reimbursement-lag problem` — problem; `receive reimbursement` — receive; `cash-flow pressure` — pressure.
**Examples:** `Reimbursement lag forced the small clinic to borrow money for routine supplies.` → Chậm payment gây thiếu cash flow.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `상환 지연` — reimbursement lag.

## 19. claims adjudication /kleɪmz ˌædʒʊdɪˈkeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** review and decision on whether a submitted claim meets rules for payment, including coding, eligibility, and documentation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** claims adjudication là process xét claim có đủ điều kiện trả không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `claims-adjudication system` — system; `adjudicate a claim` — adjudicate; `payment rule` — rule.
**Examples:** `Claims adjudication rejected the submission because the referral code and supporting note disagreed.` → Code và note không khớp.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `청구 심사` — claims adjudication.

## 20. fraud risk /frɔd rɪsk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the possibility that false, inflated, duplicated, or deliberately misleading claims will divert health funds.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** fraud risk là khả năng tiền health bị rút bằng claim gian.
**Grammar & collocations:** `fraud-risk score` — score; `detect fraudulent billing` — detect; `improper claim` — claim.
**Examples:** `Fraud risk increased when one provider submitted identical procedures for patients recorded in different cities.` → Claim trùng location khác nhau.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `사기 위험` — fraud risk.

## 21. audit sampling /ˈɔdɪt ˈsæmplɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** selection of a subset of records or transactions for systematic review so that quality or compliance can be assessed efficiently.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** audit sampling kiểm tra sample đại diện thay vì đọc mọi record.
**Grammar & collocations:** `audit-sampling method` — method; `sample claims for review` — sample; `representative record` — record.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `감사 표본추출` — audit sampling.

## 22. cost shifting /kɔst ˈʃɪftɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** movement of costs from one payer, service, population, or period to another without reducing the underlying resource use.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cost shifting chuyển bill sang nơi khác, không làm cost thật biến mất.
**Grammar & collocations:** `cost-shifting strategy` — strategy; `shift costs to patients` — shift; `hidden burden` — burden.
**Examples:** `Cost shifting moved the hospital's unpaid emergency work into higher prices for insured procedures.` → Cost emergency ẩn trong giá khác.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `비용 전가` — cost shifting.

## 23. budgetary room /ˈbʌdʒəˌtɛri ruːm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** available fiscal capacity to introduce, expand, or sustain a health policy without displacing unacceptable priorities.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** budgetary room là khoảng trống tài chính để thêm policy.
**Grammar & collocations:** `budgetary-room estimate` — estimate; `create fiscal room` — create; `spending constraint` — constraint.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `예산 여력` — budgetary room.

## 24. earmarked revenue /ˈɪrˌmɑrkt ˈrɛvəˌnuː/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** income legally or administratively reserved for a specified health purpose rather than available for general spending.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** earmarked revenue có dòng tiền dành riêng cho một mục tiêu.
**Grammar & collocations:** `earmarked-revenue stream` — stream; `earmark revenue for care` — earmark; `dedicated fund` — fund.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `목적 지정 세입` — earmarked revenue.

## 25. sin tax /ˈsɪn tæks/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a tax on products or activities considered harmful, often intended both to reduce use and to raise health-related revenue.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** sin tax vừa discourage harmful use vừa tạo revenue cho health.
**Grammar & collocations:** `sin-tax policy` — policy; `tax harmful consumption` — tax; `public-health revenue` — revenue.
**Examples:** `A sin tax on sugary drinks funded nutrition programs while signaling the cost of excessive consumption.` → Tax vừa tạo fund vừa đổi signal.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `죄악세` — sin tax.

## Review in context

**Contribution pooling** and **cross-subsidy** spread risk, while **strategic purchasing** and **provider payment reform** shape what care providers deliver. **Capitation adjustment** can reflect need; a **fee-for-service incentive** may increase volume, whereas **bundled payment**, a **global budget**, and **prospective payment** organize spending differently. **Behavioral hazard**, **selection distortion**, and **cream skimming** can undermine fairness. **Uncompensated care** threatens provider stability, while **financial protection** helps households avoid a **coverage gap** or **premature discontinuation**. A clear **benefit package** must be matched by timely payment; otherwise **reimbursement lag** creates cash-flow stress. **Claims adjudication**, **fraud risk**, and **audit sampling** protect funds, but **cost shifting** can hide the burden. **Budgetary room**, **earmarked revenue**, and a **sin tax** determine whether reforms can last.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

**Contribution pooling** và **cross-subsidy** chia risk, còn **strategic purchasing** và **provider payment reform** định hình provider cung cấp care gì. **Capitation adjustment** có thể phản ánh need; **fee-for-service incentive** có thể tăng volume, trong khi **bundled payment**, **global budget** và **prospective payment** tổ chức spending khác nhau. **Behavioral hazard**, **selection distortion** và **cream skimming** có thể làm yếu fairness. **Uncompensated care** đe dọa sức bền provider, còn **financial protection** giúp household tránh **coverage gap** hoặc **premature discontinuation**. **Benefit package** rõ phải đi cùng payment đúng hạn; nếu không, **reimbursement lag** gây cash-flow stress. **Claims adjudication**, **fraud risk** và **audit sampling** bảo vệ quỹ, nhưng **cost shifting** có thể che burden thật. **Budgetary room**, **earmarked revenue** và **sin tax** quyết định reform có thể bền không.
