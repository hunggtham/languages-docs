# C2+ Vocabulary — Immunization program design and equity

This lesson follows how immunization programs build protection, identify access gaps, and turn vaccine policy into reliable community delivery. The network is **population immunity → vaccine confidence → primary series → booster dose → protection marker → post-vaccination infection → waning immunity → heterologous boosting → coadministration → catch-up campaign → ring vaccination → school-entry requirement → vaccination exclusion → vaccination precaution → adverse event following immunization → cold-chain excursion → coverage denominator → zero-dose child → missed opportunity → microplanning → defaulter tracing → community immunity gap → vaccine equity → demand generation → immunization registry**.

## 1. population immunity /ˌpɑpjəˈleɪʃən ɪˈmjuːnəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the level and distribution of protection in a population that reduces transmission or limits the impact of an infection.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** population immunity là protection của cả population, không chỉ một người đã tiêm.
**Grammar & collocations:** `population-immunity threshold` — threshold; `build population immunity` — build; `community protection` — protection.
**Examples:** `Population immunity declined when routine childhood vaccination fell across several districts.` → Protection population giảm theo coverage.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `인구 면역` — population immunity.

## 2. vaccine confidence /vækˈsiːn ˈkɑnfədəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** trust that vaccines are safe, effective, necessary, and fairly provided by credible institutions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** vaccine confidence là trust vào vaccine, provider và process cung cấp.
**Grammar & collocations:** `vaccine-confidence survey` — survey; `build confidence` — build; `trusted provider` — provider.
**Examples:** `Vaccine confidence improved when clinics acknowledged side effects and explained how safety was monitored.` → Minh bạch side effect làm trust tăng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `백신 신뢰` — vaccine confidence.

## 3. primary series /ˈpraɪˌmɛri ˈsɪriz/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the initial set of vaccine doses recommended to establish a basic level of protection.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** primary series là các dose nền trước booster hoặc lịch đặc biệt.
**Grammar & collocations:** `primary-series schedule` — schedule; `complete the primary series` — complete; `initial dose` — dose.
**Examples:** `The clinic used text reminders to help families complete the primary series on time.` → Reminder giữ lịch dose nền.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `기초 접종` — primary series.

## 4. booster dose /ˈbuːstər doʊs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an additional vaccine dose given after an initial series to restore, extend, or strengthen protection.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** booster dose nhắc lại hoặc tăng protection sau primary series.
**Grammar & collocations:** `booster-dose recommendation` — recommendation; `receive a booster` — receive; `restore protection` — restore.
**Examples:** `The booster dose was recommended when protection had declined in older adults.` → Booster tập trung nhóm protection giảm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `추가 접종` — booster dose.

## 5. protection marker /prəˈtɛkʃən ˈmɑrkər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a measurable immune or biological feature used as an indication, though not always a complete guarantee, of protection.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** protection marker là dấu hiệu proxy cho protection, không tự động đồng nghĩa immunity tuyệt đối.
**Grammar & collocations:** `protection-marker study` — study; `measure an immune marker` — measure; `correlate with protection` — correlate.
**Examples:** `The protection marker rose after vaccination but did not fully predict protection against every variant.` → Marker hữu ích nhưng không tuyệt đối.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `보호 지표` — protection marker.

## 6. post-vaccination infection /poʊst væksəˈneɪʃən ɪnˈfɛkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** infection that occurs after a person has received some or all recommended vaccine doses.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** post-vaccination infection có thể xảy ra; cần xem severity, timing và variant.
**Grammar & collocations:** `post-vaccination-infection surveillance` — surveillance; `investigate an infection` — investigate; `vaccine effectiveness` — effectiveness.
**Examples:** `Post-vaccination infection was usually milder, but the program still monitored severe cases.` → Infection vẫn có thể xảy ra nhưng severity khác.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `접종 후 감염` — post-vaccination infection.

## 7. waning immunity /ˈweɪnɪŋ ɪˈmjuːnəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** gradual reduction in immune protection over time after vaccination or infection.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** waning immunity là protection giảm dần, không nhất thiết mất ngay.
**Grammar & collocations:** `waning-immunity estimate` — estimate; `monitor declining protection` — monitor; `time since vaccination` — time.
**Examples:** `Waning immunity informed the timing of the booster recommendation for high-risk groups.` → Time từ dose ảnh hưởng recommendation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `면역력 감소` — waning immunity.

## 8. heterologous boosting /ˌhɛtərəˈlɑɡəs ˈbuːstɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** use of a different vaccine product or platform for a booster than the one used in the initial series.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** heterologous boosting là booster khác platform/product primary.
**Grammar & collocations:** `heterologous-boosting study` — study; `mix vaccine platforms` — mix; `booster response` — response.
**Examples:** `Heterologous boosting offered flexibility when the original product was scarce.` → Khác product giúp supply linh hoạt.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `이종 백신 추가 접종` — heterologous boosting.

## 9. coadministration /ˌkoʊədˌmɪnɪˈstreɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** administration of two or more vaccines during the same visit, according to approved guidance.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** coadministration cho nhiều vaccine trong một visit để giảm missed opportunity.
**Grammar & collocations:** `coadministration policy` — policy; `coadminister vaccines` — coadminister; `same-visit delivery` — delivery.
**Examples:** `Coadministration helped the clinic protect children who might not return for another appointment.` → Một visit bảo vệ nhiều hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `동시 접종` — coadministration.

## 10. catch-up campaign /ˈkætʃ ʌp kæmˈpeɪn/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** organized effort to vaccinate people who missed doses or fell behind the recommended schedule.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** catch-up campaign kéo người bị trễ trở lại lịch protection.
**Grammar & collocations:** `catch-up-campaign plan` — plan; `catch up missed doses` — catch up; `under-immunized group` — group.
**Examples:** `The catch-up campaign used schools and weekend clinics to reach children who had missed routine visits.` → Nhiều địa điểm để catch up.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `따라잡기 접종 캠페인` — catch-up campaign.

## 11. ring vaccination /rɪŋ væksəˈneɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** targeted vaccination of contacts and nearby groups around identified cases to contain transmission.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** ring vaccination tạo vòng protection quanh case.
**Grammar & collocations:** `ring-vaccination response` — response; `vaccinate close contacts` — vaccinate; `contain transmission` — contain.
**Examples:** `Ring vaccination began after the investigation identified household and workplace contacts.` → Target theo contact network.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `환상 예방접종` — ring vaccination.

## 12. school-entry requirement /skuːl ˈɛntri rɪˈkwaɪərmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a rule requiring specified immunization documentation or exemptions before a child enters a school system.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** school-entry requirement dùng enrollment rule để tăng routine coverage.
**Grammar & collocations:** `school-entry-requirement policy` — policy; `document immunization` — document; `enrollment condition` — condition.
**Examples:** `The school-entry requirement included a simple process for families to request legitimate exemptions.` → Requirement đi cùng process rõ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `학교 입학 요건` — school-entry requirement.

## 13. vaccination exclusion /væksəˈneɪʃən ɪkˈskluːʒən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a defined reason a person should not receive a particular vaccine or dose at a particular time.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** vaccination exclusion là lý do cụ thể để chưa tiêm, không phải refusal chung chung.
**Grammar & collocations:** `vaccination-exclusion criterion` — criterion; `exclude a dose temporarily` — exclude; `clinical reason` — reason.
**Examples:** `The nurse checked for a vaccination exclusion before rescheduling the dose.` → Check reason cụ thể trước khi delay.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `예방접종 제외 사유` — vaccination exclusion.

## 14. vaccination precaution /væksəˈneɪʃən prɪˈkɔʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a condition requiring extra assessment, timing, observation, or planning before or after vaccination.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** vaccination precaution cần care thêm nhưng không nhất thiết cấm vaccine.
**Grammar & collocations:** `vaccination-precaution guidance` — guidance; `observe after vaccination` — observe; `assess a precaution` — assess.
**Examples:** `A vaccination precaution required observation after the dose rather than exclusion from vaccination.` → Precaution khác exclusion.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `예방접종 주의사항` — vaccination precaution.

## 15. adverse event following immunization /ˈædvɜrs ɪˈvɛnt ˈfɑloʊɪŋ ɪˌmjuːnəˈzeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** any unfavorable medical occurrence after immunization, whether or not the vaccine caused it.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** adverse event following immunization là event sau tiêm, chưa tự động chứng minh causation.
**Grammar & collocations:** `adverse-event-following-immunization report` — report; `investigate an event` — investigate; `causal assessment` — assessment.
**Examples:** `The team reported the adverse event following immunization and investigated other possible causes.` → Event được report rồi assess causation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `예방접종 후 이상반응` — adverse event following immunization.

## 16. cold-chain excursion /koʊld tʃeɪn ɪkˈskɜrʒən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a period in which a temperature-sensitive vaccine is exposed to conditions outside its required storage range.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cold-chain excursion là vaccine đi ra ngoài temperature range an toàn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `cold-chain-excursion log` — log; `assess a temperature excursion` — assess; `vaccine viability` — viability.
**Examples:** `The cold-chain excursion led staff to quarantine the shipment until its stability was assessed.` → Quarantine trước khi dùng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `콜드체인 이탈` — cold-chain excursion.

## 17. coverage denominator /ˈkʌvərɪdʒ dɪˈnɑməˌneɪtər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the defined population used as the denominator when calculating vaccination coverage.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** coverage denominator quyết định “coverage bao nhiêu phần trăm của ai”.
**Grammar & collocations:** `coverage-denominator definition` — definition; `calculate coverage` — calculate; `eligible population` — population.
**Examples:** `The coverage denominator changed after the district updated its population estimates.` → Denominator đổi làm percentage đổi.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `접종률 분모` — coverage denominator.

## 18. zero-dose child /ˈzɪroʊ doʊs tʃaɪld/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a child who has received no routine vaccine doses through the immunization system.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** zero-dose child là child chưa chạm được routine system, không chỉ trễ một dose.
**Grammar & collocations:** `zero-dose-child estimate` — estimate; `reach zero-dose children` — reach; `routine contact` — contact.
**Examples:** `The outreach team mapped zero-dose children who lived beyond the normal transport route.` → Map theo access gap.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `제로 도스 아동` — zero-dose child.

## 19. missed opportunity /mɪst ˌɑpərˈtuːnəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a health-service contact during which an eligible person could have received a needed vaccine but did not.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** missed opportunity là visit đã có nhưng vaccine không được dùng dù đủ điều kiện.
**Grammar & collocations:** `missed-opportunity audit` — audit; `miss an immunization opportunity` — miss; `eligible visitor` — visitor.
**Examples:** `The audit found a missed opportunity when a child visited for fever follow-up but no vaccination status was checked.` → Visit có nhưng không check vaccine.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `접종 기회 누락` — missed opportunity.

## 20. microplanning /ˈmaɪkroʊˌplænɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** detailed local planning of target populations, teams, routes, supplies, timing, and monitoring for an immunization activity.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** microplanning biến campaign lớn thành route và task cụ thể ở từng địa bàn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `microplanning workshop` — workshop; `map vaccination routes` — map; `local target list` — list.
**Examples:** `Microplanning identified which villages needed boats, evening sessions, and extra carriers.` → Plan theo địa hình thật.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `세부 계획 수립` — microplanning.

## 21. defaulter tracing /dɪˈfɔltər ˈtreɪsɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** organized follow-up to locate and reconnect people who missed scheduled vaccine doses.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** defaulter tracing tìm người trễ lịch và đưa họ quay lại care.
**Grammar & collocations:** `defaulter-tracing list` — list; `trace a missed appointment` — trace; `follow-up worker` — worker.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `미접종자 추적` — defaulter tracing.

## 22. community immunity gap /kəˈmjuːnəti ɪˈmjuːnəti ɡæp/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the difference between the level of protection a community needs and the protection it currently has.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** community immunity gap là khoảng thiếu protection khiến outbreak dễ lan.
**Grammar & collocations:** `community-immunity-gap map` — map; `close an immunity gap` — close; `local susceptibility` — susceptibility.
**Examples:** `The community immunity gap was largest among mobile families who had missed several services.` → Mobility làm gap tăng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `지역사회 면역 격차` — community immunity gap.

## 23. vaccine equity /vækˈsiːn ˈɛkwəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** fair opportunity for people and communities to receive vaccines, information, services, and protection according to their needs.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** vaccine equity không chỉ chia vaccine đều; là đảm bảo access theo barrier và need.
**Grammar & collocations:** `vaccine-equity strategy` — strategy; `advance equitable access` — advance; `underserved population` — population.
**Examples:** `Vaccine equity required mobile teams and interpretation rather than simply opening another central clinic.` → Cần đưa service tới nơi barrier.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `백신 형평성` — vaccine equity.

## 24. demand generation /dɪˈmænd ˌdʒɛnəˈreɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** communication and community-engagement work that builds informed interest in a health service without coercion or manipulation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** demand generation tạo nhu cầu informed, không chỉ ép người dân đi tiêm.
**Grammar & collocations:** `demand-generation campaign` — campaign; `generate informed demand` — generate; `community engagement` — engagement.
**Examples:** `Demand generation used trusted messengers to explain benefits, limits, and where vaccination was available.` → Message rõ và không ép.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `수요 창출` — demand generation.

## 25. immunization registry /ˌɪmjənəˈzeɪʃən ˈrɛdʒɪstri/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a record system that tracks vaccine doses, eligibility, reminders, coverage, and follow-up for individuals or populations.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** immunization registry giữ lịch dose và giúp system nhìn coverage thật.
**Grammar & collocations:** `immunization-registry record` — record; `update a vaccine record` — update; `reminder-recall function` — function.
**Examples:** `The immunization registry generated reminders and showed which neighborhoods had the largest coverage gap.` → Registry vừa nhắc vừa phân tích.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `예방접종 등록부` — immunization registry.

## Review in context — Part 1

**Population immunity** depends on **vaccine confidence**, completion of the **primary series**, and timely **booster dose** delivery. A **protection marker** may help interpret a **post-vaccination infection**, while **waning immunity** can support **heterologous boosting** or **coadministration**. A **catch-up campaign** and **ring vaccination** address different gaps; a **school-entry requirement** can increase routine coverage. **Vaccination exclusion** differs from a **vaccination precaution**, and an **adverse event following immunization** must be assessed rather than assumed to be caused by the vaccine.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 1:**

**Population immunity** phụ thuộc **vaccine confidence**, hoàn thành **primary series** và tiêm **booster dose** đúng lúc. **Protection marker** có thể giúp hiểu **post-vaccination infection**, còn **waning immunity** có thể dẫn tới **heterologous boosting** hoặc **coadministration**. **Catch-up campaign** và **ring vaccination** xử lý các gap khác nhau; **school-entry requirement** có thể tăng routine coverage. **Vaccination exclusion** khác **vaccination precaution**, còn **adverse event following immunization** cần được assess thay vì mặc định do vaccine gây ra.

## Review in context — Part 2

A **cold-chain excursion** can compromise supply, while a correct **coverage denominator** is needed to interpret results. The program should find every **zero-dose child**, reduce each **missed opportunity**, and use **microplanning** and **defaulter tracing** to close the **community immunity gap**. **Vaccine equity** requires service designed around barriers. **Demand generation** builds informed interest, and an **immunization registry** supports reminders, monitoring, and accountability.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 2:**

**Cold-chain excursion** có thể làm hỏng supply, còn **coverage denominator** đúng là điều kiện để hiểu kết quả. Program cần tìm mọi **zero-dose child**, giảm **missed opportunity**, dùng **microplanning** và **defaulter tracing** để lấp **community immunity gap**. **Vaccine equity** cần service thiết kế theo barrier. **Demand generation** tạo interest có informed choice, và **immunization registry** hỗ trợ reminder, monitoring và accountability.
