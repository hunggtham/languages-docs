# C2+ Vocabulary — Health priority setting and governance

This lesson follows how health institutions choose among competing needs, justify difficult allocations, and adapt policy under uncertainty. The network is **priority setting → resource trade-off → disinvestment → health technology assessment → cost-effectiveness threshold → budget impact analysis → evidence value → structured deliberation → stakeholder representation → fair-process test → accountability for reasonableness → ethical matrix → intergenerational equity → risk precaution → least restrictive alternative → renewal clause → adaptive governance → experimental regulation → service fidelity → policy coherence → institutional capacity → frontline judgment → bureaucratic load → feedback effect → distributional impact**.

## 1. priority setting /praɪˈɔrəti ˈsɛtɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the deliberate process of deciding which health goals, services, or populations should receive attention and resources first.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** priority setting biến nhiều need thành thứ tự hành động có lý do.
**Grammar & collocations:** `priority-setting framework` — framework; `set a health priority` — set; `competing need` — need.
**Examples:** `Priority setting became unavoidable when the ministry faced a fixed budget and rising treatment requests.` → Ngân sách cố định buộc phải xếp priority.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `우선순위 설정` — priority setting.

## 2. resource trade-off /rɪˈsɔrs ˈtreɪdˌɔf/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a choice in which gaining resources or benefits for one purpose means giving up some resources or benefits elsewhere.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** resource trade-off là được một phía nhưng mất cơ hội ở phía khác.
**Grammar & collocations:** `resource-trade-off analysis` — analysis; `make a trade-off` — make; `scarce resource` — resource.
**Examples:** `The resource trade-off favored emergency beds but reduced funding for preventive screening.` → Tăng bed cấp cứu làm giảm screening.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `자원 상충` — resource trade-off.

## 3. disinvestment /ˌdɪsɪnˈvɛstmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the deliberate reduction or withdrawal of funding, staff, or support from an existing intervention or service.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** disinvestment là rút resource khỏi service cũ vì value thấp hoặc không còn phù hợp.
**Grammar & collocations:** `disinvestment decision` — decision; `disinvest from a program` — disinvest; `low-value care` — care.
**Examples:** `Disinvestment from an outdated screening program released funds for a more effective test.` → Rút khỏi program cũ để đầu tư test tốt hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `투자 철회` — disinvestment.

## 4. health technology assessment /hɛlθ tɛkˈnɑlədʒi əˈsɛsmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** systematic evaluation of a health technology's clinical effects, safety, costs, ethics, and wider social consequences.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** health technology assessment xem technology qua clinical, cost và ethical lens.
**Grammar & collocations:** `health-technology-assessment report` — report; `assess a technology` — assess; `comparative effectiveness` — effectiveness.
**Examples:** `The health technology assessment compared the device with standard treatment and estimated budget consequences.` → Assessment vừa so sánh vừa tính budget.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `보건의료기술평가` — health technology assessment.

## 5. cost-effectiveness threshold /ˌkɔst ɪˈfɛktɪvnəs ˈθrɛʃˌhoʊld/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a benchmark for judging whether the health benefit gained by an intervention is worth its additional cost.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cost-effectiveness threshold là ngưỡng so benefit với extra cost, không phải giá tuyệt đối.
**Grammar & collocations:** `cost-effectiveness-threshold policy` — policy; `fall below a threshold` — fall; `incremental cost` — cost.
**Examples:** `The medicine exceeded the cost-effectiveness threshold unless its price was negotiated downward.` → Giá cần giảm để dưới threshold.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `비용효과성 임계값` — cost-effectiveness threshold.

## 6. budget impact analysis /ˈbʌdʒət ˈɪmpækt əˈnæləsɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an estimate of how adopting an intervention will change the spending and affordability of a particular payer or health system.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** budget impact analysis hỏi “hệ thống này sẽ phải chi bao nhiêu?” chứ không chỉ hỏi value.
**Grammar & collocations:** `budget-impact-analysis model` — model; `estimate fiscal impact` — estimate; `payer budget` — budget.
**Examples:** `The budget impact analysis showed that a clinically valuable therapy would still require phased adoption.` → Therapy tốt nhưng cần triển khai theo phase.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `예산 영향 분석` — budget impact analysis.

## 7. evidence value /ˈɛvɪdəns ˈvæljuː/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the usefulness of additional information for reducing uncertainty and improving a decision.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** evidence value đo information mới có giúp quyết định tốt hơn không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `evidence-value estimate` — estimate; `reduce decision uncertainty` — reduce; `inform a choice` — inform.
**Examples:** `The evidence value of a small trial was high because the policy decision affected millions of patients.` → Trial nhỏ nhưng information rất valuable.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `근거 가치` — evidence value.

## 8. structured deliberation /ˈstrʌktʃərd dɪˌlɪbəˈreɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a facilitated process in which participants examine evidence, values, and alternatives according to explicit rules.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** structured deliberation biến tranh luận thành process có evidence và rule rõ.
**Grammar & collocations:** `structured-deliberation forum` — forum; `deliberate on an option` — deliberate; `facilitated discussion` — discussion.
**Examples:** `Structured deliberation allowed patients, clinicians, and economists to challenge the draft priority list.` → Nhiều stakeholder cùng challenge list.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `구조화된 숙의` — structured deliberation.

## 9. stakeholder representation /ˈsteɪkˌhoʊldər ˌrɛprɪzɛnˈteɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** inclusion of affected groups and relevant institutions in a decision process in a way that reflects their interests and experiences.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** stakeholder representation bảo đảm người bị ảnh hưởng có voice trong decision.
**Grammar & collocations:** `stakeholder-representation rule` — rule; `represent an affected group` — represent; `lived experience` — experience.
**Examples:** `Stakeholder representation exposed transport barriers that were missing from the economic model.` → Người dùng cho thấy barrier model bỏ sót.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `이해관계자 대표성` — stakeholder representation.

## 10. fair-process test /fɛr ˈprɑsɛs tɛst/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a check that a decision was made through transparent, relevant, revisable, and accountable procedures.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** fair-process test kiểm tra fairness của process, không chỉ outcome.
**Grammar & collocations:** `fair-process-test criterion` — criterion; `apply a fairness test` — apply; `transparent procedure` — procedure.
**Examples:** `The committee passed the fair-process test because its criteria, evidence, and appeals route were public.` → Criteria và appeal đều minh bạch.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `공정 절차 검증` — fair-process test.

## 11. accountability for reasonableness /əˌkaʊntəˈbɪləti fər ˈriːzənəblnəs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a framework requiring priority decisions to use relevant reasons, public procedures, revision opportunities, and enforcement of the rules.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** accountability for reasonableness hỏi decision có lý do relevant, public và có thể review không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `accountability-for-reasonableness framework` — framework; `justify a decision` — justify; `appeal mechanism` — mechanism.
**Examples:** `Accountability for reasonableness made the agency publish why one therapy was deferred.` → Agency phải công khai lý do defer.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `합리성에 대한 책무성` — accountability for reasonableness.

## 12. ethical matrix /ˈɛθɪkəl ˈmeɪtrɪks/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a table that compares policy options across ethical principles, affected groups, and possible benefits or harms.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** ethical matrix đặt option và principle vào cùng bảng để nhìn trade-off.
**Grammar & collocations:** `ethical-matrix worksheet` — worksheet; `compare ethical dimensions` — compare; `affected party` — party.
**Examples:** `The ethical matrix revealed a conflict between efficiency, privacy, and equal access.` → Bảng làm conflict hiện rõ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `윤리 매트릭스` — ethical matrix.

## 13. intergenerational equity /ˌɪntərˌdʒɛnəˈreɪʃənəl ˈɛkwəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** fairness between present and future generations in the distribution of health benefits, risks, costs, and opportunities.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** intergenerational equity cân bằng benefit/cost giữa hôm nay và thế hệ sau.
**Grammar & collocations:** `intergenerational-equity principle` — principle; `protect future generations` — protect; `long-term burden` — burden.
**Examples:** `Intergenerational equity supported prevention spending whose largest benefits would appear decades later.` → Prevention hôm nay giúp người tương lai.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `세대 간 형평성` — intergenerational equity.

## 14. risk precaution /rɪsk prɪˈkɔʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a policy stance that takes protective action when serious harm is plausible even though scientific evidence remains incomplete.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** risk precaution cho phép act trước khi evidence hoàn toàn chắc chắn nếu harm nghiêm trọng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `risk-precaution rule` — rule; `act under uncertainty` — act; `plausible harm` — harm.
**Examples:** `Risk precaution justified temporary restrictions while investigators studied the contaminated water source.` → Tạm hạn chế để tránh harm lớn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `위험 예방 원칙` — risk precaution.

## 15. least restrictive alternative /liːst rɪˈstrɪktɪv ɔlˈtɜrnətɪv/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the option that achieves a legitimate protective goal while limiting liberty, privacy, or access as little as reasonably possible.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** least restrictive alternative đạt goal nhưng giảm restriction tối thiểu.
**Grammar & collocations:** `least-restrictive-alternative analysis` — analysis; `limit liberty` — limit; `protective measure` — measure.
**Examples:** `The board chose remote monitoring as the least restrictive alternative to compulsory admission.` → Monitoring nhẹ hơn admission bắt buộc.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `최소 제한 대안` — least restrictive alternative.

## 16. renewal clause /rɪˈnuəl klɔz/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a contractual or policy provision requiring a program, authority, or funding arrangement to be reviewed before it continues.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** renewal clause tạo điểm review bắt buộc trước khi policy tiếp tục.
**Grammar & collocations:** `renewal-clause review` — review; `renew a mandate` — renew; `expiry date` — date.
**Examples:** `A renewal clause prevented the emergency procurement rule from becoming permanent by inertia.` → Rule khẩn cấp phải được review.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `갱신 조항` — renewal clause.

## 17. adaptive governance /əˈdæptɪv ˈɡʌvərnəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** governance that deliberately learns from monitoring and revises rules as conditions, evidence, or risks change.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** adaptive governance xem policy như hệ thống học và điều chỉnh.
**Grammar & collocations:** `adaptive-governance model` — model; `revise a rule` — revise; `learning cycle` — cycle.
**Examples:** `Adaptive governance allowed the vaccination schedule to change when supply and safety data improved.` → Schedule đổi theo data mới.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `적응형 거버넌스` — adaptive governance.

## 18. experimental regulation /ɪkˌspɛrəˈmɛntl ˌrɛɡjəˈleɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a temporary or limited regulatory arrangement designed to test an approach while monitoring effects and safeguards.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** experimental regulation là regulation có giới hạn để học trước khi scale.
**Grammar & collocations:** `experimental-regulation framework` — framework; `test a regulatory option` — test; `monitor safeguards` — monitor.
**Examples:** `Experimental regulation let rural clinics pilot remote prescribing under enhanced audit.` → Pilot có audit chặt.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `실험적 규제` — experimental regulation.

## 19. service fidelity /ˈsɜrvɪs fɪˈdɛləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the degree to which a delivered program remains faithful to its intended design, population, dosage, and core components.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** service fidelity đo program thực tế có giữ core design không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `service-fidelity audit` — audit; `maintain program fidelity` — maintain; `core component` — component.
**Examples:** `Service fidelity fell when local teams removed the counseling component to shorten visits.` → Bỏ counseling làm program lệch design.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `서비스 충실도` — service fidelity.

## 20. policy coherence /ˈpɑləsi koʊˈhɪrəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the degree to which policies across sectors and levels support rather than undermine one another.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** policy coherence là các policy không tự triệt tiêu nhau.
**Grammar & collocations:** `policy-coherence review` — review; `align policies` — align; `cross-sector objective` — objective.
**Examples:** `Policy coherence required housing, transport, and health plans to share an access goal.` → Ba sector cùng hướng access.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `정책 일관성` — policy coherence.

## 21. institutional capacity /ˌɪnstəˈtuʃənəl kəˈpæsəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an institution's practical ability to plan, finance, implement, monitor, and learn from its responsibilities.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** institutional capacity là khả năng biến policy thành operation bền vững.
**Grammar & collocations:** `institutional-capacity assessment` — assessment; `build implementation capacity` — build; `administrative function` — function.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `제도적 역량` — institutional capacity.

## 22. frontline judgment /ˈfrʌntˌlaɪn ˈdʒʌdʒmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** practical interpretation and decision-making by workers who apply general rules to particular cases in real settings.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** frontline judgment là nơi rule chung gặp tình huống cụ thể.
**Grammar & collocations:** `frontline-judgment guideline` — guideline; `exercise professional judgment` — exercise; `particular case` — case.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `일선 판단` — frontline judgment.

## 23. bureaucratic load /ˌbjʊrəˈkrætɪk loʊd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the time, paperwork, verification, and procedural effort imposed on people or organizations by administrative requirements.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** bureaucratic load là sức nặng giấy tờ và thủ tục làm chậm care.
**Grammar & collocations:** `bureaucratic-load reduction` — reduction; `complete paperwork` — complete; `administrative requirement` — requirement.
**Examples:** `Bureaucratic load discouraged small clinics from applying for preventive-care grants.` → Thủ tục nặng làm clinic nhỏ bỏ cuộc.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `관료적 부담` — bureaucratic load.

## 24. feedback effect /ˈfiːdˌbæk ɪˈfɛkt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a change in behavior, expectations, or future policy caused by the consequences or signals of an earlier policy.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** feedback effect là policy tạo response mới rồi response đó lại đổi system.
**Grammar & collocations:** `feedback-effect model` — model; `alter future behavior` — alter; `policy signal` — signal.
**Examples:** `The reimbursement change produced a feedback effect as providers shifted toward easier-to-code diagnoses.` → Payment đổi làm provider đổi behavior.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `피드백 효과` — feedback effect.

## 25. distributional impact /ˌdɪstrəˈbjuʃənəl ˈɪmpækt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the way a policy's benefits, costs, risks, and opportunities are distributed across different groups.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** distributional impact hỏi ai được lợi, ai chịu cost và risk.
**Grammar & collocations:** `distributional-impact assessment` — assessment; `map who gains` — map; `affected group` — group.
**Examples:** `The distributional impact assessment showed that a uniform co-payment would burden low-income patients most.` → Co-payment đều nhưng burden không đều.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `분배 영향` — distributional impact.

## Review in context

**Priority setting** makes difficult choices visible, especially when a **resource trade-off** or **disinvestment** is required. **Health technology assessment**, a **cost-effectiveness threshold**, and **budget impact analysis** examine value and affordability, while **evidence value** shows whether more information could improve the choice. **Structured deliberation**, **stakeholder representation**, a **fair-process test**, and **accountability for reasonableness** make the process more legitimate. An **ethical matrix** can expose **intergenerational equity** concerns and support **risk precaution** or the **least restrictive alternative**. A **renewal clause** and **adaptive governance** keep policy revisable; **experimental regulation** permits learning under safeguards. **Service fidelity**, **policy coherence**, and **institutional capacity** shape implementation, while **frontline judgment** works under **bureaucratic load**. A policy can create a **feedback effect**, so its **distributional impact** must be monitored rather than assumed.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

**Priority setting** làm các lựa chọn khó trở nên rõ ràng, nhất là khi cần **resource trade-off** hoặc **disinvestment**. **Health technology assessment**, **cost-effectiveness threshold** và **budget impact analysis** xem xét value và affordability, còn **evidence value** cho biết thông tin thêm có thể cải thiện choice hay không. **Structured deliberation**, **stakeholder representation**, **fair-process test** và **accountability for reasonableness** làm process chính danh hơn. **Ethical matrix** có thể lộ vấn đề **intergenerational equity** và hỗ trợ **risk precaution** hoặc **least restrictive alternative**. **Renewal clause** và **adaptive governance** giữ policy có thể sửa; **experimental regulation** cho phép học dưới safeguard. **Service fidelity**, **policy coherence** và **institutional capacity** định hình implementation, còn **frontline judgment** phải hoạt động dưới **bureaucratic load**. Policy có thể tạo **feedback effect**, nên phải theo dõi **distributional impact** thay vì mặc định nó sẽ công bằng.
