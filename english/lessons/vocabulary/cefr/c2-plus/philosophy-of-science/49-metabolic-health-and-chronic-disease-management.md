# C2+ Vocabulary — Metabolic health and chronic-disease management

This lesson follows how clinicians and communities manage metabolic risk, support daily self-care, and prevent long-term complications. The network is **metabolic syndrome → glucose fluctuation → sensor-based glucose tracking → time-in-range → insulin titration → cardiometabolic risk → weight stigma → obesogenic environment → nutrition insecurity → food prescription → dietary pattern → ultra-processed food → metabolic flexibility → nonalcoholic fatty liver → hypertension burden → blood pressure variability → medication adherence → self-management support → therapeutic inertia → clinical inertia → shared lifestyle plan → cardiac rehabilitation → secondary prevention → risk conversation → downstream care chain**.

## 1. metabolic syndrome /ˌmɛtəˈbɑlɪk ˈsɪndroʊm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a combination of metabolic risk factors, commonly including abdominal adiposity, high blood pressure, abnormal blood lipids, and impaired glucose regulation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** metabolic syndrome là nhiều risk factor chuyển hóa cùng xuất hiện, làm risk tim mạch tăng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `metabolic-syndrome assessment` — assessment; `identify metabolic risk` — identify; `cluster of risk factors` — cluster.
**Examples:** `The assessment addressed metabolic syndrome through blood pressure, waist measurement, lipids, and glucose together.` → Không nhìn một chỉ số riêng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `대사 증후군` — metabolic syndrome.

## 2. glucose fluctuation /ˈɡluːkoʊs ˌflʌktʃuˈeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** repeated rises and falls in blood glucose over time, including changes that may not appear in a single spot measurement.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** glucose fluctuation là đường huyết lên xuống, không chỉ một con số tại một thời điểm.
**Grammar & collocations:** `glucose-fluctuation pattern` — pattern; `reduce glucose swings` — reduce; `daily profile` — profile.
**Examples:** `Glucose fluctuation increased after irregular meals and overnight correction doses.` → Meal và dose không đều làm swing tăng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `혈당 변동` — glucose fluctuation.

## 3. sensor-based glucose tracking /ˈsɛnsər beɪst ˈɡluːkoʊs ˈtrækɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** monitoring glucose through a sensor that records repeated readings, often continuously or at short intervals.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** sensor-based glucose tracking cho thấy pattern theo thời gian thay vì vài fingerstick snapshot.
**Grammar & collocations:** `sensor-based-glucose-tracking report` — report; `track glucose continuously` — track; `sensor reading` — reading.
**Examples:** `Sensor-based glucose tracking revealed nighttime lows that the morning test had missed.` → Sensor thấy low ban đêm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `센서 기반 혈당 추적` — sensor-based glucose tracking.

## 4. time-in-range /taɪm ɪn reɪndʒ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the proportion of monitored time that glucose remains within a defined target range.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** time-in-range đo bao nhiêu thời gian glucose ở vùng target.
**Grammar & collocations:** `time-in-range target` — target; `increase time in range` — increase; `glucose target zone` — zone.
**Examples:** `Time-in-range improved after the patient adjusted meal timing and reviewed overnight insulin.` → Không chỉ nhìn average.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `목표 범위 내 시간` — time-in-range.

## 5. insulin titration /ˈɪnsəlɪn taɪˈtreɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** careful adjustment of insulin dose according to glucose patterns, meals, activity, illness, and treatment goals.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** insulin titration điều chỉnh dose từng bước theo pattern và context.
**Grammar & collocations:** `insulin-titration protocol` — protocol; `titrate a dose` — titrate; `glucose response` — response.
**Examples:** `Insulin titration reduced overnight lows without sacrificing daytime control.` → Dose được cân bằng theo nhiều thời điểm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `인슐린 용량 조절` — insulin titration.

## 6. cardiometabolic risk /ˌkɑrdioʊˌmɛtəˈbɑlɪk rɪsk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** combined risk of cardiovascular disease and metabolic conditions such as diabetes, dyslipidemia, or obesity.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cardiometabolic risk nối heart và metabolism trong một risk profile.
**Grammar & collocations:** `cardiometabolic-risk profile` — profile; `reduce cardiometabolic risk` — reduce; `vascular outcome` — outcome.
**Examples:** `The prevention plan addressed cardiometabolic risk through blood pressure, sleep, movement, and nutrition.` → Plan không chỉ tập trung một drug.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `심혈관 대사 위험` — cardiometabolic risk.

## 7. weight stigma /weɪt ˈstɪɡmə/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** stereotyping, shame, discrimination, or unequal treatment directed at people because of body weight or size.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** weight stigma làm patient né care và clinician bỏ qua nguyên nhân khác.
**Grammar & collocations:** `weight-stigma training` — training; `challenge weight stigma` — challenge; `stigmatizing language` — language.
**Examples:** `The clinic removed weight stigma from intake questions and focused on health goals rather than blame.` → Language đổi để care an toàn hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `체중 낙인` — weight stigma.

## 8. obesogenic environment /ˌoʊbəˈsɛdʒənɪk ɪnˈvaɪrənmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an environment whose food, transport, work, marketing, and built conditions make weight gain and inactivity easier.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** obesogenic environment đặt người dân trong context dễ ăn excess và khó move.
**Grammar & collocations:** `obesogenic-environment exposure` — exposure; `shape eating behavior` — shape; `built environment` — environment.
**Examples:** `An obesogenic environment offered cheap sugary drinks but no safe walking route.` → Choice cá nhân bị context định hình.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `비만 유발 환경` — obesogenic environment.

## 9. nutrition insecurity /nuˈtrɪʃən ˌɪnsəˈkjʊrəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** uncertain or inadequate access to affordable food that supports health, cultural needs, and stable eating patterns.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** nutrition insecurity là không chắc có đủ food phù hợp và lành mạnh.
**Grammar & collocations:** `nutrition-insecurity screening` — screening; `secure nutritious food` — secure; `food access` — access.
**Examples:** `Nutrition insecurity made a complex meal plan unrealistic for the family.` → Plan phải hợp resource thật.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `영양 불안정` — nutrition insecurity.

## 10. food prescription /fuːd prɪˈskrɪpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a structured health intervention that connects people with specific nutritious foods, vouchers, or food programs as part of care.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** food prescription đưa food support vào treatment plan như một intervention.
**Grammar & collocations:** `food-prescription program` — program; `prescribe produce vouchers` — prescribe; `nutrition support` — support.
**Examples:** `The food prescription provided produce vouchers and cooking support for patients with diabetes.` → Food support đi cùng clinical care.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `식품 처방` — food prescription.

## 11. dietary pattern /ˈdaɪəˌtɛri ˈpætərn/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the overall combination, frequency, timing, and context of foods and drinks consumed over time.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** dietary pattern nhìn cả pattern ăn, không gắn health vào một food riêng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `dietary-pattern assessment` — assessment; `change a dietary pattern` — change; `eating routine` — routine.
**Examples:** `The counseling focused on the dietary pattern across a week rather than banning one ingredient.` → Nhìn toàn pattern.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `식이 패턴` — dietary pattern.

## 12. ultra-processed food /ˈʌltrə ˈprɑsɛst fuːd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an industrially formulated food made with extensive processing and ingredients uncommon in ordinary home cooking.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** ultra-processed food là category dựa vào mức processing và formulation, không chỉ “đồ ăn đóng gói”.
**Grammar & collocations:** `ultra-processed-food intake` — intake; `limit ultra-processed food` — limit; `food formulation` — formulation.
**Examples:** `The program reduced ultra-processed food by changing the school menu and the neighborhood store supply.` → Cần đổi environment, không chỉ khuyên.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `초가공 식품` — ultra-processed food.

## 13. metabolic flexibility /ˌmɛtəˈbɑlɪk flɛksəˈbɪləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the body's ability to switch efficiently between fuel sources and adjust energy use across feeding, fasting, activity, and rest.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** metabolic flexibility là khả năng đổi fuel và energy mode theo context.
**Grammar & collocations:** `metabolic-flexibility measure` — measure; `switch fuel use` — switch; `energy metabolism` — metabolism.
**Examples:** `Regular movement and sleep supported metabolic flexibility more consistently than a short restrictive diet.` → Routine quan trọng hơn diet cực đoan.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `대사 유연성` — metabolic flexibility.

## 14. nonalcoholic fatty liver /ˌnɑnˌælkəˈhɑlɪk ˈfæti ˈlɪvər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** accumulation of excess fat in the liver in people who do not drink alcohol at levels that explain the condition.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** nonalcoholic fatty liver liên quan metabolism và liver fat, không đơn giản là một hành vi riêng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `nonalcoholic-fatty-liver assessment` — assessment; `monitor liver fat` — monitor; `metabolic risk` — risk.
**Examples:** `The clinician linked nonalcoholic fatty liver to the broader cardiometabolic risk profile.` → Liver nằm trong metabolic network.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `비알코올성 지방간` — nonalcoholic fatty liver.

## 15. hypertension burden /ˌhaɪpərˈtɛnʃən ˈbɜrdən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the cumulative population or individual impact of high blood pressure, including duration, severity, treatment difficulty, and complications.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** hypertension burden không chỉ một reading cao; là exposure dài và consequence.
**Grammar & collocations:** `hypertension-burden estimate` — estimate; `reduce blood-pressure burden` — reduce; `vascular complication` — complication.
**Examples:** `The hypertension burden remained high because many patients had years of untreated elevation.` → Duration untreated rất quan trọng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `고혈압 부담` — hypertension burden.

## 16. blood pressure variability /blʌd ˈprɛʃər ˌvɛriəˈbɪləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** changes in blood pressure across readings, times, settings, or physiological states.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** blood pressure variability cho thấy pressure không cố định giữa clinic và home.
**Grammar & collocations:** `blood-pressure-variability pattern` — pattern; `measure home readings` — measure; `within-day change` — change.
**Examples:** `Blood pressure variability was visible only after home and clinic readings were reviewed together.` → Cần nhiều context.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `혈압 변동성` — blood pressure variability.

## 17. medication adherence /ˌmɛdɪˈkeɪʃən ədˈhɪrəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the extent to which a person takes medicines according to an agreed plan, including timing, dose, and persistence.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** medication adherence mô tả medicine có được dùng như plan trong đời thật không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `medication-adherence support` — support; `adhere to a regimen` — adhere; `persistence gap` — gap.
**Examples:** `Medication adherence improved after the pharmacist simplified the schedule and addressed cost.` → Simplify và giảm cost giúp adherence.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `복약 순응도` — medication adherence.

## 18. self-management support /ˌsɛlf ˈmænɪdʒmənt səˈpɔrt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** education, coaching, tools, and follow-up that help people manage a chronic condition in daily life.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** self-management support biến patient thành người có capability, không bỏ họ tự xoay xở.
**Grammar & collocations:** `self-management-support program` — program; `support daily disease management` — support; `action plan` — plan.
**Examples:** `Self-management support taught the patient how to respond to sick-day glucose changes.` → Patient biết xử lý context khác.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `자가 관리 지원` — self-management support.

## 19. therapeutic inertia /ˌθɛrəˈpjuːtɪk ɪˈnɜrʃə/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** failure to start or intensify appropriate treatment when a condition remains above the agreed target.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** therapeutic inertia là biết target chưa đạt nhưng treatment không đổi.
**Grammar & collocations:** `therapeutic-inertia audit` — audit; `intensify treatment` — intensify; `unmet target` — target.
**Examples:** `Therapeutic inertia left blood pressure uncontrolled for months despite repeated high readings.` → High readings lặp nhưng không action.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `치료 관성` — therapeutic inertia.

## 20. clinical inertia /ˈklɪnɪkəl ɪˈnɜrʃə/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** failure to change clinical assessment, investigation, or management when new information indicates that change is needed.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** clinical inertia rộng hơn therapeutic inertia; cả diagnosis/plan không đổi.
**Grammar & collocations:** `clinical-inertia review` — review; `reassess a changing condition` — reassess; `management delay` — delay.
**Examples:** `Clinical inertia delayed investigation when fatigue and weight loss no longer fit the original explanation.` → New signal nhưng plan cũ giữ nguyên.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `임상 관성` — clinical inertia.

## 21. shared lifestyle plan /ʃɛrd ˈlaɪfˌstaɪl plæn/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a jointly created, realistic plan for food, movement, sleep, stress, and routines that reflects a person's goals and constraints.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** shared lifestyle plan được patient và clinician cùng build, không áp lifestyle lý tưởng từ ngoài.
**Grammar & collocations:** `shared-lifestyle-plan review` — review; `set a realistic routine` — set; `patient constraint` — constraint.
**Examples:** `The shared lifestyle plan focused on a ten-minute walk after dinner rather than an unrealistic exercise target.` → Goal nhỏ nhưng làm được.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `공동 생활습관 계획` — shared lifestyle plan.

## 22. cardiac rehabilitation /ˈkɑrdiæk ˌriːəbɪləˈteɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a supervised program combining exercise, education, risk-factor management, and support after a heart event or procedure.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cardiac rehabilitation nối exercise, education và risk management sau heart event.
**Grammar & collocations:** `cardiac-rehabilitation program` — program; `complete cardiac rehabilitation` — complete; `post-event recovery` — recovery.
**Examples:** `Cardiac rehabilitation improved confidence and exercise tolerance after the infarction.` → Rehab giúp cả body và confidence.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `심장 재활` — cardiac rehabilitation.

## 23. secondary prevention /ˈsɛkənˌdɛri prɪˈvɛnʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** prevention intended to stop an established disease or prior event from worsening, recurring, or causing further complications.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** secondary prevention bắt đầu sau disease/event để ngăn recurrence.
**Grammar & collocations:** `secondary-prevention plan` — plan; `prevent recurrence` — prevent; `established disease` — disease.
**Examples:** `Secondary prevention combined statin therapy, smoking support, movement, and follow-up after the stroke.` → Nhiều layer ngăn recurrence.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `이차 예방` — secondary prevention.

## 24. risk conversation /rɪsk ˌkɑnvərˈseɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a shared discussion of a person's likelihood of harm, uncertainty, options, and priorities without reducing the decision to a single score.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** risk conversation biến score thành decision có context và preference.
**Grammar & collocations:** `risk-conversation guide` — guide; `discuss individualized risk` — discuss; `shared decision` — decision.
**Examples:** `The risk conversation explained that a lower probability did not mean zero risk and explored what the patient valued.` → Risk không phải yes/no.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `위험 대화` — risk conversation.

## 25. downstream care chain /ˈdaʊnˌstriːm kɛr tʃeɪn/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the sequence of referrals, tests, treatments, monitoring, and consequences that follows an initial health finding or decision.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** downstream care chain là các bước tiếp theo kéo dài sau finding ban đầu.
**Grammar & collocations:** `downstream-care-chain map` — map; `trace downstream consequences` — trace; `follow-up burden` — burden.
**Examples:** `The team mapped the downstream care chain before ordering a test that might trigger invasive procedures.` → Nghĩ trước consequence.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `하류 진료 연쇄` — downstream care chain.

## Review in context — Part 1

**Metabolic syndrome** can involve **glucose fluctuation**, so **sensor-based glucose tracking** and **time-in-range** may add information beyond a single reading. **Insulin titration** and a broader **cardiometabolic risk** profile should be discussed without **weight stigma**. An **obesogenic environment** and **nutrition insecurity** can limit choices; a **food prescription**, a realistic **dietary pattern**, and less **ultra-processed food** may help. **Metabolic flexibility**, **nonalcoholic fatty liver**, **hypertension burden**, and **blood pressure variability** connect daily behavior with long-term risk.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 1:**

**Metabolic syndrome** có thể gồm **glucose fluctuation**, nên **sensor-based glucose tracking** và **time-in-range** bổ sung thông tin ngoài một reading. **Insulin titration** và profile **cardiometabolic risk** rộng cần được nói mà không tạo **weight stigma**. **Obesogenic environment** và **nutrition insecurity** có thể giới hạn choice; **food prescription**, **dietary pattern** thực tế và giảm **ultra-processed food** có thể hữu ích. **Metabolic flexibility**, **nonalcoholic fatty liver**, **hypertension burden** và **blood pressure variability** nối daily behavior với long-term risk.

## Review in context — Part 2

**Medication adherence** improves when **self-management support** addresses cost, routine, and understanding. **Therapeutic inertia** and **clinical inertia** can leave risk untreated, so a **shared lifestyle plan** should be reviewed rather than assumed. **Cardiac rehabilitation** and **secondary prevention** reduce recurrence after an event. A respectful **risk conversation** explains uncertainty, and mapping the **downstream care chain** shows what a test or decision may set in motion.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 2:**

**Medication adherence** tốt hơn khi **self-management support** xử lý cost, routine và understanding. **Therapeutic inertia** và **clinical inertia** có thể để risk không được xử lý, nên **shared lifestyle plan** cần được review chứ không mặc định. **Cardiac rehabilitation** và **secondary prevention** giảm recurrence sau event. **Risk conversation** tôn trọng giải thích uncertainty, còn map **downstream care chain** cho thấy một test hoặc decision có thể kéo theo điều gì.
