# C2+ Vocabulary — Theories, explanation, and scientific change

This lesson follows how scientists choose, test, explain, and revise theories, from competing interpretations to paradigm-level change and reproducibility. The network is **underdetermination of theory → instrumental realism → entity realism → constructive empiricism → scientific realism → explanatory pluralism → mechanistic explanation → interventionist explanation → nomological-deductive model → inference to the best explanation → abductive reasoning → theory confirmation → falsification criterion → auxiliary hypothesis → Duhem–Quine thesis → research programme → paradigm shift → normal science → epistemic community → replicability crisis**.

## 1. underdetermination of theory /ˌʌndərdɪˌtɜrməˈneɪʃən əv ˈθɪəri/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the condition in which available evidence is compatible with more than one theory or explanatory framework.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cùng một evidence có thể fit nhiều theory; data không tự chọn toàn bộ interpretation.
**Grammar & collocations:** `underdetermination-of-theory thesis` — thesis; `empirical underdetermination` — underdetermination; `argue for underdetermination` — argue.
**Examples:** `The underdetermination of theory became clear when two models predicted the same observed curve.` → Underdetermination of theory rõ khi hai model dự đoán cùng curve quan sát.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `이론의 미결정성` — underdetermination of theory.

## 2. instrumental realism /ˌɪnstrəˈmɛntl ˈriəlɪzəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a position that treats theories as reliable instruments for prediction and intervention without requiring every theoretical entity to be literally real.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** theory có thể dùng rất tốt như “tool” mà không cần cam kết mọi invisible entity trong nó tồn tại như mô tả.
**Grammar & collocations:** `instrumental-realist position` — position; `instrumental success` — success; `adopt instrumental realism` — adopt.
**Examples:** `Instrumental realism let the engineer use the model without settling the metaphysics of its hidden variables.` → Instrumental realism cho engineer dùng model mà không cần giải quyết metaphysics của hidden variable.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `도구적 실재론` — instrumental realism.

## 3. entity realism /ˈɛntəti ˈriəlɪzəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a position that treats some theoretical entities as real when scientists can manipulate them reliably to produce new effects.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** nếu ta có thể dùng entity để tạo và điều khiển effect mới, entity đó đáng được xem là real hơn chỉ là fiction trong equation.
**Grammar & collocations:** `entity-realist argument` — argument; `manipulable entity` — entity; `commit to entity realism` — commit.
**Examples:** `Entity realism focused on whether the particle could be manipulated rather than merely detected.` → Entity realism tập trung particle có thể manipulate hay không, không chỉ detect.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `실체 실재론` — entity realism.

## 4. constructive empiricism /kənˈstrʌktɪv ɛmˈpɪrɪsɪzəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a view that science aims at empirical adequacy—getting observable phenomena right—without requiring belief that unobservable parts of a theory are true.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mục tiêu science là đúng với observable world; không cần tin mọi unobservable mechanism là true.
**Grammar & collocations:** `constructive-empiricist view` — view; `empirical adequacy` — adequacy; `defend constructive empiricism` — defend.
**Examples:** `Constructive empiricism accepted the model's predictions while suspending judgment about its unobserved structure.` → Constructive empiricism accept prediction nhưng suspend judgment về structure không quan sát.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `구성적 경험주의` — constructive empiricism.

## 5. scientific realism /ˌsaɪənˈtɪfɪk ˈriəlɪzəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the view that successful mature scientific theories are at least approximately true and that many of their unobservable entities and structures are real.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** success lâu dài của theory là evidence cho approximate truth, kể cả về entity không nhìn trực tiếp.
**Grammar & collocations:** `scientific-realist commitment` — commitment; `realist interpretation` — interpretation; `defend realism` — defend.
**Examples:** `Scientific realism interpreted the stable predictive success as evidence for a real underlying mechanism.` → Scientific realism xem predictive success ổn định là evidence cho mechanism thật bên dưới.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `과학적 실재론` — scientific realism.

## 6. explanatory pluralism /ɪkˈsplænətɔri ˈplʊrəlɪzəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the view that a phenomenon may legitimately require multiple kinds of explanation—mechanistic, statistical, functional, historical, or other—rather than one universal form.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** một phenomenon có thể cần nhiều “ống kính” explanation; không phải mọi câu hỏi đều quy về một level.
**Grammar & collocations:** `explanatory-pluralist approach` — approach; `plurality of explanations` — plurality; `defend explanatory pluralism` — defend.
**Examples:** `Explanatory pluralism allowed the biologist to combine a molecular mechanism with an ecological explanation.` → Explanatory pluralism cho biologist kết hợp mechanism molecular với explanation ecological.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `설명 다원주의` — explanatory pluralism.

## 7. mechanistic explanation /ˌmɛkəˈnɪstɪk ˌɛkspləˈneɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an explanation that identifies organized parts, activities, and interactions whose operation produces a phenomenon.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mở hộp đen thành parts + activities + interaction để thấy phenomenon được tạo ra thế nào.
**Grammar & collocations:** `mechanistic explanation of disease` — explanation; `mechanism-based account` — account; `identify component interactions` — identify.
**Examples:** `The mechanistic explanation traced the phenotype from receptor binding through intracellular signaling.` → Mechanistic explanation trace phenotype từ receptor binding qua intracellular signaling.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `기계론적 설명` — mechanistic explanation.

## 8. interventionist explanation /ˌɪntərˈvɛnʃənɪst ˌɛkspləˈneɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an explanation that identifies causal structure through interventions that change one variable and observe the resulting difference.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** hiểu cause bằng cách “động vào” system, không chỉ quan sát correlation.
**Grammar & collocations:** `interventionist account` — account; `interventionist explanation of causation` — explanation; `manipulate a variable` — manipulate.
**Examples:** `The interventionist explanation was stronger after the experiment changed temperature while holding pressure constant.` → Interventionist explanation mạnh hơn sau experiment đổi temperature và giữ pressure constant.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `개입주의적 설명` — interventionist explanation.

## 9. nomological-deductive model /ˌnɑməˌlɑdʒɪkəl dɪˈdʌktɪv ˈmɑdəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a model of explanation in which a phenomenon is deduced from general laws together with specific initial or boundary conditions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** explanation như proof: law + initial condition → phenomenon được deduce.
**Grammar & collocations:** `nomological-deductive explanation` — explanation; `covering law` — law; `deduce an outcome` — deduce.
**Examples:** `The nomological-deductive model represented the orbit as a consequence of a general law and initial position.` → Nomological-deductive model biểu diễn orbit như consequence của law và initial position.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `법칙-연역 모형` — nomological-deductive model.

## 10. inference to the best explanation /ˈɪnfərəns tə ðə bɛst ˌɛkspləˈneɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** reasoning that selects the hypothesis providing the most satisfactory explanation of the available evidence relative to alternatives.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** chọn hypothesis “giải thích tốt nhất” theo fit, simplicity, scope và coherence, không phải deduction chắc chắn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `inference-to-the-best-explanation argument` — argument; `compare explanatory virtues` — compare; `select the best explanation` — select.
**Examples:** `Inference to the best explanation favored the model that explained both the anomaly and the control result.` → Inference to the best explanation ưu tiên model giải thích cả anomaly và control.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `최선의 설명으로의 추론` — inference to the best explanation.

## 11. abductive reasoning /əˈdʌktɪv ˈriːzənɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** reasoning that generates or selects a plausible hypothesis from an observation that requires explanation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** thấy surprising fact rồi hỏi “nếu hypothesis này đúng thì fact có hợp lý không?”
**Grammar & collocations:** `abductive-reasoning step` — step; `abductive inference` — inference; `generate a hypothesis` — generate.
**Examples:** `Abductive reasoning suggested a measurement fault before the team proposed a new physical law.` → Abductive reasoning gợi ý measurement fault trước khi team đề xuất law mới.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `귀추적 추론` — abductive reasoning.

## 12. theory confirmation /ˈθɪəri ˌkɑnfərˈmeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the accumulation and assessment of evidence that increases confidence in a theory relative to competing hypotheses.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** evidence không “prove” theory tuyệt đối nhưng tăng degree of support qua prediction, replication và coherence.
**Grammar & collocations:** `theory-confirmation evidence` — evidence; `confirm a theory` — confirm; `confirmation strength` — strength.
**Examples:** `Theory confirmation grew after independent laboratories reproduced the predicted isotope pattern.` → Theory confirmation tăng sau khi lab độc lập reproduce isotope pattern dự đoán.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `이론 확증` — theory confirmation.

## 13. falsification criterion /ˌfɔlsəfəˈkeɪʃən kraɪˈtɪriən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a proposed standard for identifying whether a theory makes risky empirical claims that could be shown false by observation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** theory tốt phải expose itself to possible failure; nếu mọi outcome đều fit thì criterion yếu.
**Grammar & collocations:** `falsification criterion` — criterion; `risky prediction` — prediction; `falsify a claim` — falsify.
**Examples:** `The falsification criterion required a prediction precise enough to fail under a defined measurement.` → Falsification criterion yêu cầu prediction đủ precise để fail dưới measurement xác định.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `반증 기준` — falsification criterion.

## 14. auxiliary hypothesis /ɔɡˈzɪljəri haɪˈpɑθəsɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a supporting assumption used with a main theory to derive a particular prediction or interpret an observation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** assumption phụ nối theory với experiment; khi prediction fail, lỗi có thể ở auxiliary chứ không main theory.
**Grammar & collocations:** `auxiliary-hypothesis adjustment` — adjustment; `add an auxiliary hypothesis` — add; `test the auxiliary` — test.
**Examples:** `The failed prediction challenged an auxiliary hypothesis about the instrument's calibration.` → Failed prediction challenge auxiliary hypothesis về calibration của instrument.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `보조 가설` — auxiliary hypothesis.

## 15. Duhem–Quine thesis /ˈduːɛm kwaɪn ˈθiːsɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the thesis that empirical tests typically confront a network of theory and auxiliary assumptions rather than an isolated hypothesis.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** experiment test cả “gói” theory + auxiliary + condition, nên failure không chỉ đích danh một proposition.
**Grammar & collocations:** `Duhem–Quine thesis` — thesis; `holistic testing` — testing; `theory–auxiliary network` — network.
**Examples:** `The Duhem–Quine thesis explained why one failed observation did not immediately refute the central model.` → Duhem–Quine thesis giải thích vì sao một observation fail không refute ngay central model.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `뒤엠-콰인 논제` — Duhem–Quine thesis.

## 16. research programme /rɪˈsɜrtʃ ˈproʊɡræm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an organized sequence of theories, problems, methods, and auxiliary commitments pursued as a continuing scientific project.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** không chỉ một paper/theory mà là “chương trình” dài hạn có core idea, problem và protective adjustments.
**Grammar & collocations:** `progressive research programme` — programme; `research-programme tradition` — tradition; `develop a programme` — develop.
**Examples:** `The research programme generated new predictions instead of merely explaining old anomalies.` → Research programme tạo prediction mới thay vì chỉ explain anomaly cũ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `연구 프로그램` — research programme.

## 17. paradigm shift /ˈpærəˌdaɪm ʃɪft/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a major change in the framework, concepts, standards, and problems that organize a scientific field.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** field đổi “kính nhìn”: câu hỏi, evidence quan trọng và cách giải thích đều được sắp lại.
**Grammar & collocations:** `paradigm-shift account` — account; `undergo a paradigm shift` — undergo; `scientific paradigm` — paradigm.
**Examples:** `The paradigm shift replaced a static model of heredity with a molecular and dynamic one.` → Paradigm shift thay model heredity static bằng model molecular và dynamic.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `패러다임 전환` — paradigm shift.

## 18. normal science /ˈnɔrməl ˈsaɪəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** routine puzzle-solving carried out within an accepted scientific paradigm rather than a period of foundational framework change.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** scientist làm puzzle trong rules đã được field chấp nhận, không liên tục hỏi lại foundation.
**Grammar & collocations:** `normal-science practice` — practice; `normal-science puzzle` — puzzle; `conduct normal science` — conduct.
**Examples:** `During normal science, researchers refined measurements and extended the accepted model's range.` → Trong normal science, researcher tinh chỉnh measurement và mở rộng range của model accepted.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `정상 과학` — normal science.

## 19. epistemic community /ɪˈpɪstəmɪk kəˈmjuːnəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a community that shares standards, concepts, methods, and practices for producing and evaluating knowledge.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** group cùng rules of knowing: họ quyết định evidence nào đủ, method nào credible và claim nào đáng tin.
**Grammar & collocations:** `scientific epistemic community` — community; `shared epistemic norms` — norms; `belong to a community` — belong.
**Examples:** `The epistemic community developed a shared protocol for comparing data across laboratories.` → Epistemic community phát triển protocol chung để compare data giữa lab.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `인식론적 공동체` — epistemic community.

## 20. replicability crisis /ˌrɛplɪkəˈbɪləti ˈkraɪsɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a widespread concern that many published findings cannot be reproduced reliably when studies are repeated or reanalyzed.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** kết quả published không lặp lại được như kỳ vọng, làm lộ vấn đề power, bias, flexibility và incentives.
**Grammar & collocations:** `replicability-crisis debate` — debate; `address the crisis` — address; `replication failure` — failure.
**Examples:** `The replicability crisis motivated preregistration, larger samples, and more transparent analysis.` → Replicability crisis thúc đẩy preregistration, sample lớn hơn và analysis minh bạch hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `재현성 위기` — replicability crisis.

## Review in context

Evidence often faces the **underdetermination of theory**: more than one framework may fit the same result. **Instrumental realism**, **entity realism**, **constructive empiricism**, and **scientific realism** offer different attitudes toward unobservable entities, while **explanatory pluralism** permits more than one useful level of explanation. A **mechanistic explanation**, **interventionist explanation**, or **nomological-deductive model** may support **inference to the best explanation** through **abductive reasoning**. **Theory confirmation** and a **falsification criterion** evaluate support and risk, but an **auxiliary hypothesis** and the **Duhem–Quine thesis** remind us that tests confront networks. A long **research programme** can undergo a **paradigm shift**; during **normal science**, an **epistemic community** solves puzzles. The **replicability crisis** asks whether those standards and results survive independent repetition.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

Evidence thường đối mặt **underdetermination of theory**: nhiều framework có thể fit cùng một result. **Instrumental realism**, **entity realism**, **constructive empiricism** và **scientific realism** đưa ra các thái độ khác nhau với unobservable entity, còn **explanatory pluralism** cho phép nhiều level explanation hữu ích. **Mechanistic explanation**, **interventionist explanation** hoặc **nomological-deductive model** có thể hỗ trợ **inference to the best explanation** qua **abductive reasoning**. **Theory confirmation** và **falsification criterion** đánh giá support và risk, nhưng **auxiliary hypothesis** cùng **Duhem–Quine thesis** nhắc rằng test đối mặt cả network. Một **research programme** dài hạn có thể trải qua **paradigm shift**; trong **normal science**, **epistemic community** giải puzzle. **Replicability crisis** hỏi liệu standards và results đó có tồn tại qua repetition độc lập hay không.
