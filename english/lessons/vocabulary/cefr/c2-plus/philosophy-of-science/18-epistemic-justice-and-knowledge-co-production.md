# C2+ Vocabulary — Epistemic justice and knowledge co-production

This lesson follows how communities distribute credibility, interpretive resources, and authority in knowledge-making. The network is **testimonial credibility → hermeneutic resource → epistemic exclusion → credibility excess → credibility deficit → epistemic exploitation → epistemic oppression → epistemic agency → interpretive authority → participatory parity → knowledge co-production → lay expertise → situated testimony → experiential expertise → community validation → epistemic repair → epistemic friction → epistemic trustworthiness → intellectual modesty → cognitive justice → testimonial sensibility → interpretive charity → epistemic marginalization → epistemic democratization → knowledge commons**.

## 1. testimonial credibility /ˌtɛstəˈmoʊniəl ˌkrɛdəˈbɪləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the degree of trust a listener appropriately assigns to a person's report or testimony.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** testimonial credibility là mức listener nên tin report dựa trên evidence và competence, không dựa prejudice.
**Grammar & collocations:** `testimonial-credibility judgment` — judgment; `assign credibility` — assign; `speaker reliability` — reliability.
**Examples:** `The review separated testimonial credibility from the speaker's institutional status.` → Review tách credibility khỏi status.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `증언 신뢰성` — testimonial credibility.

## 2. hermeneutic resource /ˌhɜrməˈnuːtɪk rɪˈsɔrs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a shared concept, vocabulary, or interpretive framework that helps people understand and communicate experience.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** hermeneutic resource là concept/vocabulary chung giúp gọi tên và hiểu experience.
**Grammar & collocations:** `hermeneutic-resource gap` — gap; `develop an interpretive resource` — develop; `name an experience` — name.
**Examples:** `The new term became a hermeneutic resource for describing a previously invisible workplace pattern.` → Term mới giúp gọi tên pattern.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `해석학적 자원` — hermeneutic resource.

## 3. epistemic exclusion /ɪˌpɪstɛmɪk ɪkˈskluːʒən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** exclusion of people or groups from producing, contributing to, interpreting, or being recognized within knowledge practices.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** epistemic exclusion đẩy group ra khỏi quá trình tạo/diễn giải/công nhận knowledge.
**Grammar & collocations:** `epistemic-exclusion mechanism` — mechanism; `exclude a knower` — exclude; `participation barrier` — barrier.
**Examples:** `The meeting's technical rules created epistemic exclusion for residents without formal training.` → Rule kỹ thuật exclude resident.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `인식론적 배제` — epistemic exclusion.

## 4. credibility excess /ˌkrɛdəˈbɪləti ˈɛksɛs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** unwarranted assignment of more credibility to a speaker than their evidence, expertise, or reliability supports.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** credibility excess là tin quá mức vì prestige/identity dù evidence không đủ.
**Grammar & collocations:** `credibility-excess effect` — effect; `overcredit a source` — overcredit; `unwarranted trust` — trust.
**Examples:** `The celebrity's credibility excess made a speculative health claim appear authoritative.` → Celebrity được tin quá mức.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `신뢰성 과잉` — credibility excess.

## 5. credibility deficit /ˌkrɛdəˈbɪləti ˈdɛfəsɪt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an unwarranted reduction in trust assigned to a speaker or report because of prejudice or social position.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** credibility deficit là report bị discount không vì evidence mà vì identity/prejudice.
**Grammar & collocations:** `credibility-deficit pattern` — pattern; `discount testimony` — discount; `prejudicial discount` — discount.
**Examples:** `The patient's credibility deficit delayed recognition of the adverse reaction.` → Deficit làm nhận adverse reaction chậm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `신뢰성 결핍` — credibility deficit.

## 6. epistemic exploitation /ɪˌpɪstɛmɪk ˌɛksplɔɪˈteɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** unfair extraction of knowledge, interpretive labor, or testimony from people without appropriate recognition, compensation, or power.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** epistemic exploitation lấy knowledge/labor của group mà không trả credit, benefit hay authority tương xứng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `epistemic-exploitation critique` — critique; `extract testimony` — extract; `unpaid interpretive labor` — labor.
**Examples:** `The project avoided epistemic exploitation by sharing authorship with community researchers.` → Project share authorship để tránh exploitation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `인식론적 착취` — epistemic exploitation.

## 7. epistemic oppression /ɪˌpɪstɛmɪk əˈprɛʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** systematic restriction of a group's ability to know, interpret, communicate, or shape the knowledge that governs it.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** epistemic oppression khóa khả năng group tạo/diễn giải knowledge và định hình rule.
**Grammar & collocations:** `epistemic-oppression structure` — structure; `suppress a knowledge practice` — suppress; `governing knowledge` — knowledge.
**Examples:** `Epistemic oppression persisted when official categories erased the community's own land terms.` → Official category erase land term của community.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `인식론적 억압` — epistemic oppression.

## 8. epistemic agency /ɪˌpɪstɛmɪk ˈeɪdʒənsi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the capacity to form, communicate, evaluate, and act on one's own knowledge claims.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** epistemic agency là quyền/năng lực tự tạo và dùng knowledge claim.
**Grammar & collocations:** `epistemic-agency framework` — framework; `exercise epistemic agency` — exercise; `make a knowledge claim` — claim.
**Examples:** `The workshop strengthened participants' epistemic agency by letting them set the research questions.` → Workshop cho participant set research question.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `인식론적 행위 주체성` — epistemic agency.

## 9. interpretive authority /ɪnˈtɜrprətɪv əˈθɔrəti/

**Core meaning (English):** recognized standing to define the meaning, significance, or proper interpretation of an experience or body of evidence.

## 10. participatory parity /pɑrˌtɪsəˈpeɪtəri ˈpærəti/

## 11. knowledge co-production /ˈnɑlɪdʒ koʊprəˈdʌkʃən/

**Core meaning (English):** joint production of questions, methods, evidence, and interpretations by researchers and knowledge holders outside research institutions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** knowledge co-production để researcher và community cùng làm question/method/evidence.

## 12. lay expertise /leɪ ˌɛkspərˈtiːz/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** practical or experiential knowledge held by non-credentialed people who closely encounter a phenomenon.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** lay expertise là expertise từ lived/practical experience, không cần credential formal.
**Grammar & collocations:** `lay-expertise contribution` — contribution; `draw on local expertise` — draw; `noncredentialed knower` — knower.
**Examples:** `Lay expertise helped engineers identify a recurring vibration that the sensor missed.` → Local experience lộ vibration sensor miss.

## 13. situated testimony /ˈsɪtʃuˌeɪtɪd ˈtɛstɪˌmoʊni/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** testimony whose meaning and evidential value are shaped by the speaker's position, experience, and relation to the event.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** situated testimony mang context/position của speaker, cần interpret mà không dismiss.
**Grammar & collocations:** `situated-testimony record` — record; `interpret testimony in context` — interpret; `lived position` — position.
**Examples:** `The inquiry treated situated testimony as evidence about daily exposure conditions.` → Inquiry xem testimony là evidence exposure hằng ngày.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `상황적 증언` — situated testimony.

## 16. epistemic repair /ɪˌpɪstɛmɪk rɪˈpɛr/

## Review in context

Knowledge practices assign **testimonial credibility**, depend on a **hermeneutic resource**, and can produce **epistemic exclusion**, **credibility excess**, or **credibility deficit**. **Epistemic exploitation** and **epistemic oppression** restrict **epistemic agency** and **interpretive authority**. **Participatory parity** and **knowledge co-production** make room for **lay expertise**, **situated testimony**, and **experiential expertise**; **community validation** tests whether an interpretation fits. **Epistemic repair** can turn **epistemic friction** into stronger **epistemic trustworthiness** when paired with **intellectual modesty**. **Cognitive justice**, **testimonial sensibility**, and **interpretive charity** resist **epistemic marginalization**. **Epistemic democratization** builds a **knowledge commons** in which access and recognition are shared.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

Knowledge practice phân phối **testimonial credibility**, dựa vào **hermeneutic resource** và có thể tạo **epistemic exclusion**, **credibility excess** hoặc **credibility deficit**. **Epistemic exploitation** và **epistemic oppression** hạn chế **epistemic agency** và **interpretive authority**. **Participatory parity** và **knowledge co-production** mở chỗ cho **lay expertise**, **situated testimony** và **experiential expertise**; **community validation** kiểm tra interpretation có fit không. **Epistemic repair** có thể biến **epistemic friction** thành **epistemic trustworthiness** mạnh hơn nếu có **intellectual modesty**. **Cognitive justice**, **testimonial sensibility** và **interpretive charity** chống **epistemic marginalization**. **Epistemic democratization** xây **knowledge commons** với access và recognition được chia sẻ.
