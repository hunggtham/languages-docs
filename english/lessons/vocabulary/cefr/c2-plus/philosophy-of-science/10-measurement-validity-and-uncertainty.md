# C2+ Vocabulary — Measurement, validity, and uncertainty

This lesson follows how researchers connect constructs to measurements, quantify uncertainty, and judge whether results travel. The network is **cross-group measurement invariance → construct representation validity → convergence validity → discriminant evidence → criterion-linked validity → calibration drift profile → metrological traceability → uncertainty allocation → error propagation → detection limit → quantification limit → measurement error model → unobserved construct → operational definition → domain undercoverage → response shift → measurement noninvariance → field realism → population transportability → internal validity → factorial validity → instrument sensitivity → specificity profile → positive post-test value → negative post-test value**.

## 1. cross-group measurement invariance /krɔs ɡruːp ˈmɛʒərmənt ɪnˈvɛriəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the condition that a measure has the same meaning and measurement structure across specified groups.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cross-group measurement invariance hỏi cùng một score có nghĩa tương đương giữa group không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `cross-group-measurement-invariance test` — test; `establish invariance` — establish; `compare latent means` — compare.
**Examples:** `The survey failed the cross-group measurement invariance test across language versions.` → Survey fail test giữa các language version.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `집단 간 측정 불변성` — cross-group measurement invariance.

## 2. construct representation validity /ˈkɑnstrʌkt ˌrɛprɪzɛnˈteɪʃən vəˈlɪdəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the degree to which a measure adequately represents the conceptual construct it is intended to assess.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** construct representation validity hỏi measure có cover đúng construct hay chỉ một mảnh dễ đo.
**Grammar & collocations:** `construct-representation-validity argument` — argument; `represent a construct` — represent; `content domain` — domain.
**Examples:** `The checklist had weak construct representation validity because it omitted social participation.` → Checklist có validity yếu vì bỏ social participation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `구성개념 표상 타당도` — construct representation validity.

## 3. convergence validity /kənˈvɜrdʒəns vəˈlɪdəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** evidence that measures intended to assess the same construct show the expected degree of agreement.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** convergence validity tăng khi các measure cùng construct converge như theory dự đoán.
**Grammar & collocations:** `convergence-validity evidence` — evidence; `converge with a measure` — converge; `same-construct agreement` — agreement.
**Examples:** `The behavioral score showed convergence validity with the clinician's structured rating.` → Behavioral score có convergence validity với clinician rating.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `수렴 타당도` — convergence validity.

## 4. discriminant evidence /dɪˈskrɪmənənt ˈɛvɪdəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** evidence that a measure remains distinct from measures of theoretically different constructs.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** discriminant evidence cho thấy measure không chỉ capture một construct khác gần đó.
**Grammar & collocations:** `discriminant-evidence test` — test; `distinguish constructs` — distinguish; `low cross-loading` — loading.
**Examples:** `Discriminant evidence showed that the stress scale was not simply a proxy for sleep duration.` → Evidence cho thấy stress scale không chỉ là proxy của sleep duration.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `판별 증거` — discriminant evidence.

## 5. criterion-linked validity /kraɪˈtɪriən lɪŋkt vəˈlɪdəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** evidence that a measure relates to an external criterion in the direction and strength expected.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** criterion-linked validity nối score với external criterion như outcome, diagnosis hoặc behavior.
**Grammar & collocations:** `criterion-linked-validity study` — study; `link a measure to a criterion` — link; `external benchmark` — benchmark.
**Examples:** `Criterion-linked validity was evaluated against later job performance.` → Criterion-linked validity được evaluate với later job performance.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `준거 연계 타당도` — criterion-linked validity.

## 6. calibration drift profile /ˌkælɪˈbreɪʃən drɪft ˈproʊfaɪl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a documented pattern of how an instrument's calibration changes over time, conditions, or use.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** calibration drift profile cho thấy instrument lệch dần ra sao theo time/temperature/use.
**Grammar & collocations:** `calibration-drift profile` — profile; `monitor drift` — monitor; `recalibrate an instrument` — recalibrate.
**Examples:** `The calibration drift profile showed a gradual bias after prolonged heat exposure.` → Profile cho thấy bias tăng dần sau heat exposure.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `교정 드리프트 프로필` — calibration drift profile.

## 7. metrological traceability /ˌmɛtrəˈlɑdʒɪkəl ˌtreɪsəˈbɪləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the property of a measurement result being linked through an unbroken documented chain to a recognized reference.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** metrological traceability nối result qua chain calibration đến reference standard.
**Grammar & collocations:** `metrological-traceability chain` — chain; `trace a result to a standard` — trace; `reference measurement` — measurement.
**Examples:** `Metrological traceability allowed laboratories to compare concentrations measured on different continents.` → Traceability giúp lab compare concentration ở các continent.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `계측학적 소급성` — metrological traceability.

## 8. uncertainty allocation /ʌnˈsɜrtənti ˌæləˈkeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the assignment of portions of a total uncertainty budget to specified sources, components, or stages.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** uncertainty allocation chia total uncertainty cho sensor, sampling, model, processing.
**Grammar & collocations:** `uncertainty-allocation table` — table; `allocate uncertainty` — allocate; `dominant source` — source.
**Examples:** `Uncertainty allocation showed that sampling, not the detector, dominated the final estimate.` → Allocation cho thấy sampling dominate estimate, không phải detector.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `불확실성 할당` — uncertainty allocation.

## 9. error propagation /ˈɛrər ˌprɑpəˈɡeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the transmission and transformation of measurement errors through calculations or model operations.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** error propagation theo dõi noise/error từ input sang derived quantity.
**Grammar & collocations:** `error-propagation formula` — formula; `propagate uncertainty` — propagate; `derived quantity` — quantity.
**Examples:** `The analyst included error propagation when converting concentration into exposure dose.` → Analyst include error propagation khi đổi concentration thành exposure dose.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `오차 전파` — error propagation.

## 10. detection limit /dɪˈtɛkʃən ˈlɪmɪt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the lowest amount of a substance or signal that can be reliably distinguished from background noise.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** detection limit là mức thấp nhất còn phân biệt được signal với background.
**Grammar & collocations:** `detection-limit estimate` — estimate; `detect above background` — detect; `limit of detection` — limit.
**Examples:** `The contaminant remained below the detection limit in half of the samples.` → Contaminant dưới detection limit ở một nửa sample.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `검출 한계` — detection limit.

## 11. quantification limit /ˌkwɑntəfəˈkeɪʃən ˈlɪmɪt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the lowest amount that can be measured quantitatively with a specified level of precision and accuracy.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** quantification limit cao hơn detection limit: thấy được chưa chắc đo chính xác được.
**Grammar & collocations:** `quantification-limit criterion` — criterion; `quantify a concentration` — quantify; `acceptable precision` — precision.
**Examples:** `Values below the quantification limit were reported as detected but not numerically reliable.` → Value dưới limit được report detected nhưng không numeric reliable.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `정량 한계` — quantification limit.

## 12. measurement error model /ˈmɛʒərmənt ˈɛrər ˈmɑdəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a model describing how observed measurements differ from the underlying quantity because of noise, bias, or instrument limitations.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** measurement error model tách true quantity khỏi noise/bias của observation.
**Grammar & collocations:** `measurement-error model` — model; `model observation noise` — model; `latent true value` — value.
**Examples:** `The measurement error model separated sensor noise from real seasonal variation.` → Model tách sensor noise khỏi seasonal variation thật.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `측정 오차 모형` — measurement error model.

## 13. unobserved construct /ˌʌnəbˈzɜrvd ˈkɑnstrʌkt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a theoretical construct that cannot be measured directly and must be inferred from indicators.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** unobserved construct như trust hay resilience được infer qua indicator, không đọc trực tiếp.
**Grammar & collocations:** `unobserved-construct model` — model; `infer a construct` — infer; `indicator variable` — variable.
**Examples:** `The model estimated the unobserved construct of institutional trust from five survey items.` → Model estimate institutional trust qua năm item.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `관찰되지 않은 구성개념` — unobserved construct.

## 14. operational definition /ˌɑpəˈreɪʃənəl ˌdɛfəˈnɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a definition that specifies how an abstract concept will be measured, manipulated, or recognized in practice.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** operational definition biến abstract concept thành rule đo/manipulate cụ thể.
**Grammar & collocations:** `operational-definition choice` — choice; `operationalize a construct` — operationalize; `measurement rule` — rule.
**Examples:** `The operational definition of recovery required both symptom relief and sustained functioning.` → Definition của recovery cần symptom relief và functioning bền.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `조작적 정의` — operational definition.

## 15. domain undercoverage /doʊˈmeɪn ˌʌndərˈkʌvərɪdʒ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** failure of a measure to represent important portions of the conceptual domain it claims to cover.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** domain undercoverage là measure bỏ sót phần quan trọng của domain.
**Grammar & collocations:** `domain-undercoverage critique` — critique; `cover a content domain` — cover; `omitted dimension` — dimension.
**Examples:** `Domain undercoverage made the wellbeing index insensitive to social isolation.` → Undercoverage làm wellbeing index không nhạy với social isolation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `영역 과소 포괄` — domain undercoverage.

## 16. response shift /rɪˈspɑns ʃɪft/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a change in how a person interprets or scales an outcome after adaptation, new information, or a change in internal standards.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** response shift làm cùng score mang meaning khác sau adaptation hoặc experience.
**Grammar & collocations:** `response-shift analysis` — analysis; `shift internal standards` — shift; `recalibrate a rating` — recalibrate.
**Examples:** `A response shift made patients rate moderate pain as acceptable after rehabilitation.` → Response shift làm patient rate moderate pain là acceptable sau rehab.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `응답 변화` — response shift.

## 17. measurement noninvariance /ˈmɛʒərmənt ˌnɑnɪnˈvɛriəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a condition in which a measure functions differently across groups, times, languages, or contexts.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** measurement noninvariance nghĩa score không còn comparable giữa group/time/context.
**Grammar & collocations:** `measurement-noninvariance diagnosis` — diagnosis; `detect noninvariance` — detect; `group-specific item` — item.
**Examples:** `Measurement noninvariance appeared when one item was interpreted differently after translation.` → Noninvariance xuất hiện khi một item được hiểu khác sau translation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `측정 비불변성` — measurement noninvariance.

## 18. field realism /fiːld ˈriəlɪzəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the extent to which a study's tasks, conditions, and interactions resemble those in the real setting of interest.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** field realism hỏi study có giống đời thật đủ để behavior/response transfer không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `field-realism criterion` — criterion; `increase realism` — increase; `real-world condition` — condition.
**Examples:** `The simulation gained field realism by including interruptions and time pressure from actual clinics.` → Simulation tăng field realism bằng interruption và time pressure thật.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `현장 현실성` — field realism.

## 19. population transportability /ˌpɑpjəˈleɪʃən ˌtrænspɔrtəˈbɪləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the extent to which an estimated effect can be applied to a specified target population with justified assumptions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** population transportability hỏi effect từ study sample đi được tới population nào.
**Grammar & collocations:** `population-transportability analysis` — analysis; `transport an estimate` — transport; `target population` — population.
**Examples:** `Population transportability was limited because the trial excluded older adults.` → Transportability hạn chế vì trial exclude older adult.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `모집단 이송 가능성` — population transportability.

## 20. internal validity /ɪnˈtɜrnəl vəˈlɪdəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the credibility of a study's causal or descriptive conclusion for the people, procedures, and setting actually studied.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** internal validity hỏi conclusion có đúng trong sample/setting study không, trước khi generalize.
**Grammar & collocations:** `internal-validity threat` — threat; `protect internal validity` — protect; `study-specific conclusion` — conclusion.
**Examples:** `Loss to follow-up threatened the internal validity of the treatment comparison.` → Loss to follow-up đe dọa internal validity.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `내적 타당도` — internal validity.

## 21. factorial validity /fækˈtɔriəl vəˈlɪdəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** evidence that the observed pattern of items or indicators matches the factor structure proposed by a measurement theory.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** factorial validity hỏi item có group thành factor đúng như theory không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `factorial-validity evidence` — evidence; `test a factor structure` — test; `item loading` — loading.
**Examples:** `Factorial validity supported the proposed two-domain structure of the questionnaire.` → Factorial validity support two-domain structure.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `요인 타당도` — factorial validity.

## 22. instrument sensitivity /ˈɪnstrəmənt ˌsɛnsəˈtɪvəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the ability of an instrument to detect meaningful changes or small differences in the quantity being measured.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** instrument sensitivity nói instrument bắt được change nhỏ đến đâu.
**Grammar & collocations:** `instrument-sensitivity study` — study; `detect a small change` — detect; `sensitive assay` — assay.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `기기 민감도` — instrument sensitivity.

## 23. specificity profile /ˌspɛsəˈfɪsəti ˈproʊfaɪl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the pattern of how well a test avoids false positives across relevant populations or thresholds.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** specificity profile cho thấy test tránh false positive ra sao theo population/threshold.
**Grammar & collocations:** `specificity-profile comparison` — comparison; `avoid false positives` — avoid; `threshold-specific specificity` — specificity.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `특이도 프로필` — specificity profile.

## 24. positive post-test value /ˈpɑzətɪv poʊst tɛst ˈvæljuː/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the probability that a person with a positive test result truly has the target condition in the tested setting.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** positive post-test value phụ thuộc prevalence, sensitivity, specificity và context.
**Grammar & collocations:** `positive-post-test-value estimate` — estimate; `interpret a positive result` — interpret; `disease prevalence` — prevalence.
**Examples:** `The positive post-test value fell when the test was used in a low-prevalence population.` → Value giảm ở population prevalence thấp.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `양성 사후 예측값` — positive post-test value.

## 25. negative post-test value /ˈnɛɡətɪv poʊst tɛst ˈvæljuː/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the probability that a person with a negative test result truly lacks the target condition in the tested setting.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** negative post-test value cho biết negative result đáng tin đến đâu trong context cụ thể.
**Grammar & collocations:** `negative-post-test-value estimate` — estimate; `interpret a negative result` — interpret; `rule out a condition` — rule out.
**Examples:** `The negative post-test value was high enough to support discharge after a low-risk screen.` → Value đủ cao để support discharge sau low-risk screen.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `음성 사후 예측값` — negative post-test value.

## Review in context

**Cross-group measurement invariance** asks whether a score means the same thing across groups. **Construct representation validity**, **convergence validity**, **discriminant evidence**, and **criterion-linked validity** examine different aspects of measurement quality. A **calibration drift profile** and **metrological traceability** document instrument reliability, while **uncertainty allocation** and **error propagation** show how error travels. **Detection limit** and **quantification limit** distinguish seeing a signal from measuring it precisely. A **measurement error model** separates noise from an **unobserved construct**, whose **operational definition** may suffer **domain undercoverage**. **Response shift** and **measurement noninvariance** complicate comparison. **Field realism**, **population transportability**, and **internal validity** describe where a result holds; **factorial validity** checks structure. Finally, **instrument sensitivity**, a **specificity profile**, **positive post-test value**, and **negative post-test value** determine how a test performs in practice.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

**Cross-group measurement invariance** hỏi score có cùng meaning giữa group không. **Construct representation validity**, **convergence validity**, **discriminant evidence** và **criterion-linked validity** xem các mặt khác nhau của measurement quality. **Calibration drift profile** và **metrological traceability** document độ tin cậy của instrument, còn **uncertainty allocation** và **error propagation** cho thấy error lan ra sao. **Detection limit** và **quantification limit** phân biệt thấy signal với đo chính xác. **Measurement error model** tách noise khỏi **unobserved construct**, mà **operational definition** có thể bị **domain undercoverage**. **Response shift** và **measurement noninvariance** làm comparison phức tạp. **Field realism**, **population transportability** và **internal validity** nói result hold ở đâu; **factorial validity** kiểm tra structure. Cuối cùng, **instrument sensitivity**, **specificity profile**, **positive post-test value** và **negative post-test value** quyết định test hoạt động ra sao trong practice.
