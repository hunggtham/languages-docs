# C2+ Vocabulary — Palliative care and serious-illness communication

This lesson follows how clinicians and families plan care, relieve suffering, and make ethically grounded decisions during serious illness and the end of life. The network is **advance care planning → goals-of-care conversation → serious illness communication → palliative sedation → symptom burden → family care strain → prognostic uncertainty → time-limited trial → hospice eligibility → comfort-focused care → life-sustaining treatment → do-not-attempt-resuscitation → surrogate decision-maker → shared mortality awareness → anticipatory grief → bereavement support → grief-informed care → legacy work → spiritual care → existential distress → total pain → home hospice → care transition → advance directive → medical aid in dying**.

## 1. advance care planning /ədˈvæns kɛr ˈplænɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an ongoing process of discussing values, future decisions, preferred treatments, and possible decision-makers before a person loses the ability to speak for themselves.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** advance care planning chuẩn bị choice trước khi crisis lấy mất voice.
**Grammar & collocations:** `advance-care-planning conversation` — conversation; `document care preferences` — document; `future decision` — decision.
**Examples:** `Advance care planning helped the family understand which treatments the patient would consider unacceptable.` → Family biết preference trước crisis.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `사전 돌봄 계획` — advance care planning.

## 2. goals-of-care conversation /ɡoʊlz əv kɛr ˌkɑnvərˈseɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a clinical discussion that connects a person's values and priorities with realistic treatment options and likely outcomes.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** goals-of-care conversation nối treatment với điều patient coi là meaningful.
**Grammar & collocations:** `goals-of-care conversation` — conversation; `clarify treatment goals` — clarify; `realistic option` — option.
**Examples:** `The goals-of-care conversation shifted from extending life at any cost to staying comfortable at home.` → Goal đổi theo value patient.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `치료 목표 대화` — goals-of-care conversation.

## 3. serious illness communication /ˈsɪriəs ˈɪlnəs kəˌmjuːnəˈkeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** communication that explains a serious diagnosis, uncertainty, treatment choices, prognosis, and support needs with honesty and compassion.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** serious illness communication nói thật nhưng vẫn giữ hope thực tế và dignity.
**Grammar & collocations:** `serious-illness-communication training` — training; `discuss a difficult diagnosis` — discuss; `compassionate disclosure` — disclosure.
**Examples:** `Serious illness communication made room for silence before the clinician explained the scan results.` → Communication có cả khoảng lặng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `중증 질환 의사소통` — serious illness communication.

## 4. palliative sedation /ˈpæliətɪv səˈdeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** carefully monitored use of medication to reduce consciousness when otherwise uncontrollable suffering remains near the end of life.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** palliative sedation giảm consciousness để relieve refractory suffering, không phải mục tiêu gây chết.
**Grammar & collocations:** `palliative-sedation protocol` — protocol; `relieve refractory suffering` — relieve; `proportional sedation` — sedation.
**Examples:** `Palliative sedation was considered only after specialist review found the pain and agitation refractory to other treatment.` → Chỉ dùng sau review chuyên khoa.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `완화적 진정` — palliative sedation.

## 5. symptom burden /ˈsɪmptəm ˈbɜrdən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the combined severity, frequency, and impact of symptoms experienced by a person.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** symptom burden là tổng sức nặng symptoms, không chỉ một symptom riêng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `symptom-burden score` — score; `reduce symptom burden` — reduce; `multiple symptom` — symptom.
**Examples:** `The symptom burden included pain, breathlessness, nausea, and fear of being alone.` → Burden gồm body và emotion.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `증상 부담` — symptom burden.

## 6. family care strain /ˈfæməli kɛr streɪn/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** physical, emotional, financial, and logistical pressure experienced by family or friends providing serious-illness care.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** family care strain là sức nặng family gánh khi care kéo dài.
**Grammar & collocations:** `family-care-strain assessment` — assessment; `reduce family strain` — reduce; `care coordination` — coordination.
**Examples:** `Family care strain eased after a home nurse took over overnight symptom checks.` → Nurse đỡ burden ban đêm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `가족 돌봄 부담` — family care strain.

## 7. prognostic uncertainty /ˌprɑɡˈnɑstɪk ʌnˈsɜrtənti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** uncertainty about the likely course, duration, response, or outcome of an illness.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** prognostic uncertainty là không biết bệnh sẽ đi theo trajectory nào.
**Grammar & collocations:** `prognostic-uncertainty discussion` — discussion; `explain an uncertain prognosis` — explain; `likely course` — course.
**Examples:** `Prognostic uncertainty made it reasonable to revisit the treatment plan after two weeks.` → Chưa chắc nên hẹn review.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `예후 불확실성` — prognostic uncertainty.

## 8. time-limited trial /taɪm ˈlɪmətɪd traɪəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a treatment started for a defined period with agreed goals and a plan to reassess whether it is helping.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** time-limited trial cho treatment một khoảng thời gian rồi review theo goal.
**Grammar & collocations:** `time-limited-trial agreement` — agreement; `reassess treatment benefit` — reassess; `trial goal` — goal.
**Examples:** `A time-limited trial of ventilation would continue only if it restored enough alertness for meaningful interaction.` → Tiếp tục nếu đạt goal patient.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `기간 제한 치료 시도` — time-limited trial.

## 9. hospice eligibility /ˈhɑspɪs ˌɛlədʒəˈbɪləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the clinical, functional, and policy criteria used to determine whether a person qualifies for hospice services.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** hospice eligibility xác định ai có thể nhận care tập trung comfort cuối đời.
**Grammar & collocations:** `hospice-eligibility review` — review; `qualify for hospice` — qualify; `terminal prognosis` — prognosis.
**Examples:** `Hospice eligibility was reviewed after repeated hospitalizations and a marked decline in function.` → Decline và admission hỗ trợ review.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `호스피스 자격` — hospice eligibility.

## 10. comfort-focused care /ˈkʌmfərt ˈfoʊkəst kɛr/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** care whose primary aim is relief of pain, distress, breathlessness, and other suffering rather than prolonging life through burdensome intervention.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** comfort-focused care đặt relief và dignity làm main goal.
**Grammar & collocations:** `comfort-focused-care plan` — plan; `relieve distress` — relieve; `burdensome intervention` — intervention.
**Examples:** `Comfort-focused care stopped invasive monitoring but continued medication for pain and anxiety.` → Dừng invasive nhưng vẫn treat suffering.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `안위 중심 돌봄` — comfort-focused care.

## 11. life-sustaining treatment /laɪf səˈsteɪnɪŋ ˈtriːtmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** treatment that replaces, supports, or prolongs essential bodily functions, such as ventilation, dialysis, or artificial nutrition.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** life-sustaining treatment giữ function sống nhưng có burden và goal cần cân nhắc.
**Grammar & collocations:** `life-sustaining-treatment decision` — decision; `continue life support` — continue; `treatment burden` — burden.
**Examples:** `The family discussed whether life-sustaining treatment still matched the patient's stated goals.` → Treatment phải khớp goal.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `생명 유지 치료` — life-sustaining treatment.

## 12. do-not-attempt-resuscitation /duː nɑt əˈtɛmpt rɪˌsʌsəˈteɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a documented medical order stating that cardiopulmonary resuscitation should not be attempted if the person's heart or breathing stops.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** do-not-attempt-resuscitation là order cụ thể về CPR, không phải “bỏ mọi care”.
**Grammar & collocations:** `do-not-attempt-resuscitation order` — order; `document a resuscitation preference` — document; `cardiac arrest` — arrest.
**Examples:** `The do-not-attempt-resuscitation order did not stop antibiotics, oxygen, or comfort medication.` → DNR không đồng nghĩa ngừng mọi care.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `심폐소생술 시도 금지` — do-not-attempt-resuscitation.

## 13. surrogate decision-maker /ˈsɜrəɡət dɪˈsɪʒən ˌmeɪkər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a person legally or ethically authorized to make health decisions for someone who cannot make or communicate them.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** surrogate decision-maker đại diện cho patient, không phải tự chọn theo preference của mình.
**Grammar & collocations:** `surrogate-decision-maker role` — role; `make a substituted judgment` — make; `patient values` — values.
**Examples:** `The surrogate decision-maker used the patient's written wishes rather than personal preference.` → Đại diện theo values của patient.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `대리 의사결정자` — surrogate decision-maker.

## 14. shared mortality awareness /ʃɛrd mɔrˈtæləti əˈwɛrnəs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** shared understanding among patient, family, and clinicians that death is approaching and that this fact should shape planning.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** shared mortality awareness giúp mọi người cùng nhìn thẳng vào limited time.
**Grammar & collocations:** `shared-mortality-awareness conversation` — conversation; `acknowledge approaching death` — acknowledge; `care priority` — priority.
**Examples:** `Shared mortality awareness allowed the family to prioritize a final visit over another burdensome admission.` → Cùng hiểu thời gian còn lại giúp chọn priority.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `공유된 죽음 인식` — shared mortality awareness.

## 15. anticipatory grief /ænˌtɪsəˈpætəri ɡriːf/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** grief experienced before an expected death or major loss has occurred.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** anticipatory grief bắt đầu khi loss đang tới nhưng chưa xảy ra.
**Grammar & collocations:** `anticipatory-grief support` — support; `grieve an expected loss` — grieve; `family counseling` — counseling.
**Examples:** `Anticipatory grief affected the partner's sleep long before the patient's death.` → Grief đến trước loss thật.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `예견된 슬픔` — anticipatory grief.

## 16. bereavement support /bɪˈriːvmənt səˈpɔrt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** emotional, practical, social, or clinical support offered after someone has died.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** bereavement support giúp người sống sau death, theo timeline riêng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `bereavement-support service` — service; `support a grieving family` — support; `post-loss care` — care.
**Examples:** `Bereavement support continued after the funeral with a counselor and a community group.` → Support không kết thúc ở funeral.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `사별 지원` — bereavement support.

## 17. grief-informed care /ɡriːf ɪnˈfɔrmd kɛr/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** care that recognizes how grief can affect attention, health, behavior, relationships, and engagement without treating grief as a simple disorder.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** grief-informed care hiểu grief ảnh hưởng toàn person và điều chỉnh support.
**Grammar & collocations:** `grief-informed-care approach` — approach; `respond to grief sensitively` — respond; `loss experience` — experience.
**Examples:** `Grief-informed care allowed missed appointments to be discussed with curiosity rather than punishment.` → Không vội phạt người đang grief.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `애도 인지 돌봄` — grief-informed care.

## 18. legacy work /ˈlɛɡəsi wɜrk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** activities that help a seriously ill person preserve memories, relationships, values, stories, or messages for others.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** legacy work giúp patient để lại meaning và connection.
**Grammar & collocations:** `legacy-work project` — project; `create a memory archive` — create; `leave a message` — message.
**Examples:** `Legacy work included recording family recipes and letters for a future birthday.` → Patient tạo memory cho family.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `유산 작업` — legacy work.

## 19. spiritual care /ˈspɪrətʃuəl kɛr/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** support for a person's meaning, beliefs, values, connection, hope, or existential concerns, whether religious or not.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** spiritual care không chỉ religion; là meaning và connection khi illness làm worldview lung lay.
**Grammar & collocations:** `spiritual-care referral` — referral; `explore spiritual concerns` — explore; `meaning-making` — making.
**Examples:** `Spiritual care connected the patient with a chaplain who respected the family's nonreligious worldview.` → Care tôn trọng worldview khác nhau.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `영적 돌봄` — spiritual care.

## 20. existential distress /ˌɛɡzəˈstɛnʃəl dɪˈstrɛs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** suffering related to meaning, identity, freedom, isolation, mortality, or the question of whether life remains worthwhile.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** existential distress là distress về meaning và mortality, không chỉ physical symptom.
**Grammar & collocations:** `existential-distress assessment` — assessment; `address loss of meaning` — address; `mortality concern` — concern.
**Examples:** `Existential distress persisted even after pain was controlled, so the team invited a counselor.` → Pain hết nhưng meaning distress còn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `실존적 고통` — existential distress.

## 21. total pain /ˈtoʊtəl peɪn/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** suffering understood as an interaction of physical, emotional, social, and spiritual pain.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** total pain nhìn pain như tổng hợp body, emotion, social và spirit.
**Grammar & collocations:** `total-pain assessment` — assessment; `treat total pain` — treat; `multidimensional suffering` — suffering.
**Examples:** `Total pain improved when the team treated breathlessness, debt, family conflict, and fear together.` → Nhiều dimension cùng ảnh hưởng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `전체적 통증` — total pain.

## 22. home hospice /hoʊm ˈhɑspɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** hospice care delivered in a person's home, with clinical support for comfort, family caregivers, and crisis response.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** home hospice đưa end-of-life care tới nơi patient muốn ở.
**Grammar & collocations:** `home-hospice nurse` — nurse; `receive hospice at home` — receive; `comfort visit` — visit.
**Examples:** `Home hospice gave the family overnight advice and rapid medication adjustment.` → Family được support tại nhà.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `가정 호스피스` — home hospice.

## 23. care transition /kɛr trænˈzɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** movement of a person and responsibility between care settings, teams, or phases, with coordinated information and support.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** care transition là lúc patient đi từ setting/team này sang setting/team khác.
**Grammar & collocations:** `care-transition checklist` — checklist; `coordinate a transition` — coordinate; `handoff information` — information.
**Examples:** `The care transition included a medication list, emergency number, and clear responsibility for follow-up.` → Transition cần ownership rõ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `돌봄 전환` — care transition.

## 24. advance directive /ədˈvæns dəˈrɛktɪv/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a legal document stating a person's future health-care preferences or naming someone to make decisions if the person loses capacity.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** advance directive ghi choice hoặc chỉ định đại diện trước khi mất capacity.
**Grammar & collocations:** `advance-directive form` — form; `complete an advance directive` — complete; `health-care preference` — preference.
**Examples:** `The advance directive named a close friend rather than assuming the oldest relative would decide.` → Patient tự chọn representative.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `사전 의료 지시서` — advance directive.

## 25. medical aid in dying /ˈmɛdɪkəl eɪd ɪn ˈdaɪɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a legally regulated practice, where permitted, in which a qualifying person receives medical assistance to end life under defined safeguards.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** medical aid in dying là practice pháp lý đặc thù, phụ thuộc jurisdiction và safeguard nghiêm ngặt.
**Grammar & collocations:** `medical-aid-in-dying law` — law; `meet eligibility safeguards` — meet; `jurisdictional requirement` — requirement.
**Examples:** `The consultation explained medical aid in dying neutrally alongside palliative options and eligibility limits.` → Giải thích trung lập và đầy đủ option.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `의료적 조력 사망` — medical aid in dying.

## Review in context — Part 1

**Advance care planning**, a **goals-of-care conversation**, and **serious illness communication** connect values to treatment. **Palliative sedation** may be considered for refractory suffering, while **symptom burden** and **family care strain** require ongoing support. **Prognostic uncertainty** can justify a **time-limited trial** before reassessment. **Hospice eligibility** and **comfort-focused care** prioritize relief; decisions about **life-sustaining treatment** and a **do-not-attempt-resuscitation** order should reflect the person's wishes. A **surrogate decision-maker** may act when capacity is lost, and **shared mortality awareness** can help families plan.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 1:**

**Advance care planning**, **goals-of-care conversation** và **serious illness communication** nối values với treatment. **Palliative sedation** có thể được cân nhắc khi suffering refractory, còn **symptom burden** và **family care strain** cần support liên tục. **Prognostic uncertainty** có thể hợp lý hóa **time-limited trial** trước khi reassess. **Hospice eligibility** và **comfort-focused care** ưu tiên relief; decision về **life-sustaining treatment** và order **do-not-attempt-resuscitation** nên phản ánh wishes của person. **Surrogate decision-maker** có thể act khi mất capacity, còn **shared mortality awareness** giúp family plan.

## Review in context — Part 2

**Anticipatory grief** may begin before death, and **bereavement support** continues afterward. **Grief-informed care**, **legacy work**, and **spiritual care** respond to different dimensions of loss, including **existential distress** and **total pain**. **Home hospice** supports comfort at home, while a careful **care transition** protects continuity. An **advance directive** records future choices. Where legal and regulated, **medical aid in dying** must be explained accurately alongside palliative alternatives and safeguards.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 2:**

**Anticipatory grief** có thể bắt đầu trước death, còn **bereavement support** tiếp tục sau đó. **Grief-informed care**, **legacy work** và **spiritual care** đáp ứng các dimension khác nhau của loss, gồm **existential distress** và **total pain**. **Home hospice** hỗ trợ comfort tại nhà, còn **care transition** cẩn thận giữ continuity. **Advance directive** ghi choice tương lai. Ở nơi được pháp luật cho phép và điều chỉnh, **medical aid in dying** phải được giải thích chính xác cùng palliative alternative và safeguard.
