# C2+ Vocabulary — Climate adaptation and resilience governance

This lesson follows how institutions anticipate climate hazards, choose adaptation pathways, and protect essential services. The network is **adaptive pathway → adaptation tipping point → managed retreat → remaining risk → maladaptation → climate loss and harm → climate justice → vulnerability profile → exposure pathway → sensitivity factor → capacity-to-adapt → coping capacity → transformative adaptation → anticipatory action → forecast-based financing → early action protocol → resilience pathway planning → critical service → service redundancy → fail-safe → safe-to-fail → ecological infrastructure measure → ecosystem-based adaptation → co-benefit → adaptation finance**.

## 1. adaptive pathway /əˈdæptɪv ˈpæθweɪ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a sequence of possible adaptation actions arranged so that later choices can change as conditions and evidence evolve.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** adaptive pathway không khóa một solution; nó xếp option theo trigger và future condition.
**Grammar & collocations:** `adaptive-pathway map` — map; `sequence adaptation options` — sequence; `decision trigger` — trigger.
**Examples:** `The coastal plan used an adaptive pathway that began with wetland restoration and reserved barriers for later.` → Plan xếp option theo stage.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `적응 경로` — adaptive pathway.

## 2. adaptation tipping point /ˌædæpˈteɪʃən ˈtɪpɪŋ pɔɪnt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a condition at which an existing adaptation measure no longer achieves its intended protection or performance.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** adaptation tipping point là lúc measure hiện tại không còn protect đủ.
**Grammar & collocations:** `adaptation-tipping-point estimate` — estimate; `exceed a protection threshold` — exceed; `upgrade a measure` — upgrade.
**Examples:** `The drainage system had an adaptation tipping point when hourly rainfall exceeded its design capacity.` → Drainage fail khi rainfall vượt capacity.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `적응 티핑 포인트` — adaptation tipping point.

## 3. managed retreat /ˈmænɪdʒd rɪˈtriːt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** planned relocation or withdrawal from a place where continued occupation becomes unsafe, costly, or unsustainable.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** managed retreat là rút/di dời có plan, không phải abandon hỗn loạn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `managed-retreat program` — program; `relocate a settlement` — relocate; `unsafe exposure` — exposure.
**Examples:** `Managed retreat protected households that could no longer be defended from repeated flooding.` → Retreat bảo vệ household không thể defend nữa.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `관리된 후퇴` — managed retreat.

## 4. remaining risk /rɪˈmeɪnɪŋ rɪsk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** risk that persists after feasible protective, preventive, or adaptive measures have been implemented.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** remaining risk là phần risk còn lại sau measure, cần communicate và finance.
**Grammar & collocations:** `remaining-risk estimate` — estimate; `manage remaining risk` — manage; `residual exposure` — exposure.
**Examples:** `The flood barrier reduced damage but left remaining risk for low-income homes outside the protected zone.` → Barrier giảm damage nhưng risk còn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `잔여 위험` — remaining risk.

## 5. maladaptation /ˌmæləˌdæpˈteɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an adaptation action that increases vulnerability, shifts risk to others, reduces future options, or creates new harm.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** maladaptation là adaptation phản tác dụng: giảm risk nơi này nhưng tăng risk nơi khác/future.
**Grammar & collocations:** `maladaptation risk` — risk; `create a maladaptive outcome` — create; `shift vulnerability` — shift.
**Examples:** `The seawall became maladaptation when it accelerated erosion in the neighboring village.` → Seawall shift erosion sang village bên.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `부적응` — maladaptation.

## 6. climate loss and harm /ˈklaɪmət lɔs ænd hɑrm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** climate-related damage and suffering that remain after adaptation limits are reached or cannot prevent the impact.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** climate loss and harm gồm damage/harm không tránh được bằng adaptation hiện có.
**Grammar & collocations:** `climate-loss-and-harm assessment` — assessment; `document unavoidable harm` — document; `adaptation limit` — limit.
**Examples:** `The report separated adaptation costs from climate loss and harm that could not be avoided.` → Report tách cost và harm không tránh.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `기후 손실과 피해` — climate loss and harm.

## 7. climate justice /ˈklaɪmət ˈdʒʌstɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** attention to fairness in responsibility, exposure, protection, participation, and distribution of climate costs and benefits.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** climate justice hỏi ai gây, ai chịu, ai được protect và ai có voice.
**Grammar & collocations:** `climate-justice framework` — framework; `distribute climate costs` — distribute; `affected community` — community.
**Examples:** `Climate justice shaped the decision to prioritize neighborhoods with the least cooling access.` → Justice ưu tiên neighborhood thiếu cooling.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `기후 정의` — climate justice.

## 8. vulnerability profile /ˌvʌlnərəˈbɪləti ˈproʊfaɪl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a structured description of who or what is susceptible to harm, through which pathways, and under which conditions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** vulnerability profile map group/system nào susceptible và vì pathway nào.
**Grammar & collocations:** `vulnerability-profile map` — map; `profile a community` — profile; `susceptibility factor` — factor.
**Examples:** `The vulnerability profile highlighted older residents living alone during heat waves.` → Profile highlight older resident sống alone.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `취약성 프로필` — vulnerability profile.

## 9. exposure pathway /ɪkˈspoʊʒər ˈpæθweɪ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the route through which a hazard reaches a person, population, ecosystem, or asset.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** exposure pathway là route hazard đi từ source tới receptor.
**Grammar & collocations:** `exposure-pathway analysis` — analysis; `trace a pathway` — trace; `hazard receptor` — receptor.
**Examples:** `The exposure pathway ran from contaminated soil through home-grown vegetables to children.` → Pathway từ soil tới children.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `노출 경로` — exposure pathway.

## 10. sensitivity factor /ˌsɛnsəˈtɪvəti ˈfæktər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a characteristic that changes how strongly a person, system, or ecosystem responds to a hazard or stressor.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** sensitivity factor làm response trước cùng hazard mạnh/nhẹ khác nhau.
**Grammar & collocations:** `sensitivity-factor analysis` — analysis; `increase sensitivity` — increase; `stressor response` — response.
**Examples:** `Poor ventilation was a sensitivity factor that increased indoor heat exposure.` → Ventilation kém làm sensitivity tăng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `민감도 요인` — sensitivity factor.

## 11. capacity-to-adapt /kəˈpæsəti tə əˈdæpt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the resources, institutions, knowledge, and abilities available to adjust to changing conditions and reduce harm.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** capacity-to-adapt gồm resource, institution, knowledge và ability để adjust.
**Grammar & collocations:** `capacity-to-adapt assessment` — assessment; `build adaptive capacity` — build; `institutional resource` — resource.
**Examples:** `Reliable public transport increased the community's capacity-to-adapt during evacuation.` → Transport tăng capacity.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `적응 역량` — capacity-to-adapt.

## 12. coping capacity /ˈkoʊpɪŋ kəˈpæsəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the ability to manage, endure, or respond to an immediate shock using available resources and practices.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** coping capacity xử lý shock hiện tại; khác long-term transformation.
**Grammar & collocations:** `coping-capacity indicator` — indicator; `cope with a shock` — cope; `immediate response` — response.
**Examples:** `Emergency cash transfers strengthened coping capacity after the crop failure.` → Cash transfer giúp cope sau crop failure.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `대처 역량` — coping capacity.

## 13. transformative adaptation /trænsˈfɔrmətɪv ˌædæpˈteɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** adaptation that changes the underlying systems, relationships, or development patterns producing vulnerability.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** transformative adaptation đổi system/root cause, không chỉ patch damage.
**Grammar & collocations:** `transformative-adaptation pathway` — pathway; `change an underlying system` — change; `structural vulnerability` — vulnerability.
**Examples:** `Transformative adaptation replaced flood-prone housing with safer, connected neighborhoods.` → Adaptation đổi housing system.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `변혁적 적응` — transformative adaptation.

## 14. anticipatory action /ænˌtɪsəˈpətɔri ˈækʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** action taken before a forecast hazard occurs, based on predefined triggers and plans.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** anticipatory action làm trước hazard theo forecast/trigger, không chờ damage.
**Grammar & collocations:** `anticipatory-action trigger` — trigger; `act before impact` — act; `pre-agreed measure` — measure.
**Examples:** `Anticipatory action released water and moved livestock before the cyclone arrived.` → Action làm trước cyclone.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `선제적 조치` — anticipatory action.

## 15. forecast-based financing /ˈfɔrˌkæst beɪst ˈfaɪnænsɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** financing released when a forecast reaches an agreed threshold so that protective action can begin before a disaster.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** forecast-based financing release money theo forecast threshold trước disaster.
**Grammar & collocations:** `forecast-based-financing mechanism` — mechanism; `release funds early` — release; `forecast trigger` — trigger.
**Examples:** `Forecast-based financing paid for evacuation supplies before the river reached danger level.` → Finance release trước flood.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `예측 기반 금융` — forecast-based financing.

## 16. early action protocol /ˈɜrli ˈækʃən ˈproʊtəˌkɔl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a pre-agreed sequence specifying who acts, when, and how after a forecast or warning reaches a trigger.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** early action protocol biến forecast thành sequence action có role/time rõ.
**Grammar & collocations:** `early-action protocol` — protocol; `activate a protocol` — activate; `triggered response` — response.
**Examples:** `The early action protocol assigned shelters, transport, and communication tasks before the storm.` → Protocol assign task trước storm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `조기 행동 프로토콜` — early action protocol.

## 17. resilience pathway planning /rɪˈzɪliəns ˈpæθweɪ ˈplænɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** planning that maps multiple routes for maintaining or restoring essential functions under changing shocks and conditions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** resilience pathway planning map nhiều route giữ/restore function dưới shock khác nhau.
**Grammar & collocations:** `resilience-pathway plan` — plan; `maintain an essential function` — maintain; `shock scenario` — scenario.
**Examples:** `Resilience pathway planning compared backup wells, conservation, and demand reduction.` → Planning compare nhiều option.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `회복탄력성 경로 계획` — resilience pathway planning.

## 18. critical service /ˈkrɪtɪkəl ˈsɜrvɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a service whose interruption would create serious harm or prevent a community from functioning safely.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** critical service như water, power, emergency care; fail là harm lớn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `critical-service continuity` — continuity; `protect a service` — protect; `service interruption` — interruption.
**Examples:** `The plan prioritized backup power for critical services such as dialysis and emergency communications.` → Plan ưu tiên backup cho service critical.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `핵심 서비스` — critical service.

## 19. service redundancy /ˈsɜrvɪs rɪˈdʌndənsi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** availability of alternative components, routes, providers, or systems that can maintain a service when one fails.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** service redundancy có backup/alternative để service không single-point fail.
**Grammar & collocations:** `service-redundancy plan` — plan; `provide an alternative route` — provide; `backup capacity` — capacity.
**Examples:** `Service redundancy kept water flowing when one treatment plant flooded.` → Backup giữ water flow.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `서비스 중복성` — service redundancy.

## 20. fail-safe /ˈfeɪl seɪf/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a design that moves a system toward a safer state when a component fails or information becomes unavailable.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** fail-safe khi fail thì system tự về state an toàn hơn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `fail-safe mode` — mode; `default to safety` — default; `safe shutdown` — shutdown.
**Examples:** `The pump entered fail-safe mode when the sensor signal disappeared.` → Pump về safe mode khi sensor mất.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `고장 안전` — fail-safe.

## 21. safe-to-fail /seɪf tə feɪl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a design or intervention structured so that experimentation can occur without causing unacceptable harm when it fails.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** safe-to-fail không giả định không fail; nó giới hạn harm khi fail.
**Grammar & collocations:** `safe-to-fail experiment` — experiment; `contain a failure` — contain; `bounded risk` — risk.
**Examples:** `The pilot was safe-to-fail because it was reversible and limited to one district.` → Pilot reversible và bounded.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `실패 안전` — safe-to-fail.

## 22. ecological infrastructure measure /ˌiːkəˈlɑdʒɪkəl ˈɪnfrəˌstrʌktʃər ˈmɛʒər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an infrastructure intervention that uses ecological processes or living systems to reduce hazard or provide essential functions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** ecological infrastructure measure dùng wetland/tree/soil process như infrastructure.
**Grammar & collocations:** `ecological-infrastructure measure` — measure; `restore an ecosystem function` — restore; `living system` — system.
**Examples:** `The ecological infrastructure measure used mangroves to reduce storm surge.` → Mangrove giảm storm surge.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `생태 인프라 조치` — ecological infrastructure measure.

## 23. ecosystem-based adaptation /ˈiːkoʊˌsɪstəm beɪst ˌædæpˈteɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** adaptation that uses the protection, restoration, or management of ecosystems to reduce climate vulnerability.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** ecosystem-based adaptation dùng ecosystem health để giảm vulnerability.
**Grammar & collocations:** `ecosystem-based-adaptation project` — project; `restore a wetland` — restore; `reduce vulnerability` — reduce.
**Examples:** `Ecosystem-based adaptation restored wetlands that stored floodwater and supported fisheries.` → Wetland vừa giữ floodwater vừa support fishery.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `생태계 기반 적응` — ecosystem-based adaptation.

## 24. co-benefit /koʊˈbɛnəfɪt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an additional positive effect produced by an action aimed primarily at another objective.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** co-benefit là benefit phụ đi cùng main objective.
**Grammar & collocations:** `adaptation co-benefit` — benefit; `generate a co-benefit` — generate; `additional positive effect` — effect.
**Examples:** `The cooling project produced a co-benefit by improving air quality and public space.` → Project vừa cool vừa improve air/public space.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `공동 편익` — co-benefit.

## 25. adaptation finance /ˌædæpˈteɪʃən ˈfaɪnæns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** funding directed toward reducing vulnerability, preparing for climate impacts, and implementing adaptation measures.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** adaptation finance là tiền cho prepare/reduce vulnerability/implement measure.
**Grammar & collocations:** `adaptation-finance gap` — gap; `finance resilience measures` — finance; `climate investment` — investment.
**Examples:** `Adaptation finance reached the neighborhoods with the highest heat vulnerability only after the equity review.` → Finance tới neighborhood vulnerable sau equity review.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `적응 금융` — adaptation finance.

## Review in context

An **adaptive pathway** identifies an **adaptation tipping point** and may eventually require **managed retreat**. Planners track **remaining risk**, avoid **maladaptation**, and address **climate loss and harm** through **climate justice**. A **vulnerability profile** maps an **exposure pathway** and **sensitivity factor**, while **capacity-to-adapt** and **coping capacity** shape response. **Transformative adaptation** changes underlying systems. **Anticipatory action**, **forecast-based financing**, and an **early action protocol** act before impact. **Resilience pathway planning** protects a **critical service** through **service redundancy**, a **fail-safe**, or a **safe-to-fail** pilot. An **ecological infrastructure measure** and **ecosystem-based adaptation** can deliver a **co-benefit**, but both depend on sustained **adaptation finance**.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

**Adaptive pathway** xác định **adaptation tipping point** và có thể cần **managed retreat**. Planner theo dõi **remaining risk**, tránh **maladaptation** và xử lý **climate loss and harm** qua **climate justice**. **Vulnerability profile** map **exposure pathway** và **sensitivity factor**, còn **capacity-to-adapt** và **coping capacity** định hình response. **Transformative adaptation** đổi system gốc. **Anticipatory action**, **forecast-based financing** và **early action protocol** hành động trước impact. **Resilience pathway planning** bảo vệ **critical service** bằng **service redundancy**, **fail-safe** hoặc pilot **safe-to-fail**. **Ecological infrastructure measure** và **ecosystem-based adaptation** có thể tạo **co-benefit**, nhưng cần **adaptation finance** bền.
