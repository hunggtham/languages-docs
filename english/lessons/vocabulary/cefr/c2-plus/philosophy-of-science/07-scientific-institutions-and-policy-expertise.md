# C2+ Vocabulary — Scientific institutions and policy expertise

This lesson follows how research communities organize trust, distribute expertise, and translate evidence into public decisions. The network is **organized skepticism → scientific communalism → scientific universalism → research disinterestedness → credit economy → priority dispute → epistemic division of labor → distributed cognition → collective evidence → structured expert elicitation → consensus statement → minority report → dissenting opinion → boundary organization → transdisciplinary collaboration → knowledge broker → science-policy interface → advisory committee → precautionary reasoning → decision-relevant uncertainty → evidence broker → policy translation → research community norm → expert disagreement → institutional credibility**.

## 1. organized skepticism /ˈɔrɡənaɪzd ˈskɛptəˌsɪzəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a social norm requiring claims to face systematic criticism, checking, and possible revision.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** organized skepticism biến doubt thành quy trình tập thể: review, replicate, challenge và revise.
**Grammar & collocations:** `organized-skepticism norm` — norm; `subject a claim to scrutiny` — subject; `systematic criticism` — criticism.
**Examples:** `Organized skepticism led the panel to request an independent analysis before issuing its recommendation.` → Organized skepticism khiến panel yêu cầu independent analysis trước recommendation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `조직된 회의주의` — organized skepticism.

## 2. scientific communalism /ˌsaɪənˈtɪfɪk kəˈmjuːnəlɪzəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the norm that scientific knowledge should be treated as a shared resource rather than private property.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** scientific communalism coi knowledge là tài sản chung để community inspect và build on.
**Grammar & collocations:** `scientific-communalism norm` — norm; `share findings` — share; `common intellectual resource` — resource.
**Examples:** `Scientific communalism supported releasing the reference dataset for reuse by other laboratories.` → Scientific communalism ủng hộ release reference dataset.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `과학적 공유주의` — scientific communalism.

## 3. scientific universalism /ˌsaɪənˈtɪfɪk ˌjuːnəˈvɜrsəlɪzəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the norm that claims should be judged by generalizable evidence and methods rather than by the status or identity of the claimant.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** scientific universalism yêu cầu đánh giá claim bằng evidence, không bằng prestige hay identity của người nói.
**Grammar & collocations:** `scientific-universalism principle` — principle; `evaluate a claim impartially` — evaluate; `status-independent judgment` — judgment.
**Examples:** `Scientific universalism required the committee to apply the same evidence standard to junior and senior researchers.` → Scientific universalism yêu cầu cùng evidence standard cho junior và senior researcher.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `과학적 보편주의` — scientific universalism.

## 4. research disinterestedness /rɪˈsɜrtʃ dɪsˈɪntərɪdstnəs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the norm that researchers should separate evidential judgment from personal gain, factional loyalty, or concealed interest.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** research disinterestedness không đòi researcher không có interest; đòi disclose và không bóp evidence vì lợi ích.
**Grammar & collocations:** `research-disinterestedness norm` — norm; `separate judgment from interest` — separate; `disclose incentives` — disclose.
**Examples:** `The funding agreement included safeguards intended to protect research disinterestedness.` → Funding agreement có safeguard bảo vệ research disinterestedness.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `연구 사심 없음` — research disinterestedness.

## 5. credit economy /ˈkrɛdɪt ɪˈkɑnəmi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the system through which recognition, authorship, citations, positions, and grants reward scientific contributions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** credit economy phân phối reward và status qua authorship, citation, grant và promotion.
**Grammar & collocations:** `academic-credit economy` — economy; `allocate credit` — allocate; `recognition incentive` — incentive.
**Examples:** `The credit economy rewarded the headline result more visibly than the careful null analysis.` → Credit economy reward headline result rõ hơn null analysis cẩn thận.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `학술 공로 경제` — credit economy.

## 6. priority dispute /praɪˈɔrəti dɪˈspjuːt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a conflict over who first made, documented, or publicly communicated a scientific contribution.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** priority dispute xoay quanh “ai làm trước”, thường gắn với credit và publication timing.
**Grammar & collocations:** `priority-dispute claim` — claim; `establish priority` — establish; `competing discovery` — discovery.
**Examples:** `The archive helped resolve a priority dispute by showing the date of the original notebook.` → Archive giúp resolve priority dispute bằng date của notebook.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `우선권 분쟁` — priority dispute.

## 7. epistemic division of labor /ɪˌpɪstəmɪk dɪˈvɪʒən əv ˈleɪbər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the distribution of different knowledge-making tasks among people, instruments, disciplines, or institutions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** epistemic division of labor chia task quan sát, calibration, modeling, interpretation cho các actor khác nhau.
**Grammar & collocations:** `epistemic-division-of-labor arrangement` — arrangement; `distribute a task` — distribute; `specialized contribution` — contribution.
**Examples:** `The project relied on an epistemic division of labor between field teams, statisticians, and local observers.` → Project dựa vào epistemic division of labor giữa field team, statistician và local observer.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `인식론적 분업` — epistemic division of labor.

## 8. distributed cognition /dɪˈstrɪbjətɪd kɑɡˈnɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** cognition understood as spread across people, tools, representations, and coordinated environments.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** distributed cognition không đặt thinking trong một head; nó nằm giữa team, tool, record và workflow.
**Grammar & collocations:** `distributed-cognition system` — system; `distribute reasoning across tools` — distribute; `cognitive artifact` — artifact.
**Examples:** `The control room used distributed cognition: operators, dashboards, and alarms jointly tracked the process.` → Control room dùng distributed cognition giữa operator, dashboard và alarm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `분산 인지` — distributed cognition.

## 9. collective evidence /kəˈlɛktɪv ˈɛvɪdəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** evidence whose force comes from the convergence and mutual checking of many observations, studies, or contributors.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** collective evidence mạnh nhờ nhiều source cross-check, không phụ thuộc một paper hero.
**Grammar & collocations:** `collective-evidence base` — base; `pool evidence` — pool; `mutually checking studies` — study.
**Examples:** `The guideline rested on collective evidence from hospitals in six regions.` → Guideline dựa trên collective evidence từ hospital ở sáu vùng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `집단적 증거` — collective evidence.

## 10. structured expert elicitation /ˈstrʌktʃərd ˈɛkspɜrt ˌɪlɪsəˈteɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a transparent procedure for obtaining and aggregating expert judgments under explicit questions, uncertainty scales, and calibration rules.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** structured expert elicitation biến opinion chuyên gia thành process có question, probability scale và calibration rõ.
**Grammar & collocations:** `structured-expert-elicitation protocol` — protocol; `elicit a probability` — elicit; `calibrate experts` — calibrate.
**Examples:** `A structured expert elicitation estimated the probability of infrastructure failure under rare conditions.` → Structured expert elicitation estimate probability của infrastructure failure.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `구조화된 전문가 추출` — structured expert elicitation.

## 11. consensus statement /kənˈsɛns ˈsteɪtmənt/

**Core meaning (English):** a formal statement summarizing areas of expert agreement, evidence limits, and practical implications.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** consensus statement không chỉ nói agreement; nó nên nêu limit, disagreement residual và implication.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `합의 성명` — consensus statement.

## 12. minority report /maɪˈnɔrəti rɪˈpɔrt/

**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** minority report giữ dissent có lý do trong hồ sơ, thay vì xóa khác biệt.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `소수 의견 보고서` — minority report.

## 13. dissenting opinion /dɪˈsɛntɪŋ əˈpɪnjən/

**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** dissenting opinion không phải phản đối cảm tính; nó trình bày reasoning và evidence khác.

## 15. transdisciplinary collaboration /ˌtrænzdɪsəˈplɪnəri kəˌlæbəˈreɪʃən/

## 16. knowledge broker /ˈnɑlɪdʒ ˈbroʊkər/

## 19. precautionary reasoning /prɪˈkɔʃəˌnɛri ˈriːzənɪŋ/

**Core meaning (English):** reasoning that supports protective action when serious harm is plausible but evidence remains incomplete or uncertain.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** precautionary reasoning không nói chắc hazard; nó hỏi cost của waiting khi evidence chưa đủ.

**Grammar & collocations:** `evidence-broker service` — service; `synthesize evidence` — synthesize; `decision audience` — audience.

## 24. expert disagreement /ˈɛkspɜrt ˌdɪsəˈɡriːmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a substantive difference among qualified experts about evidence, assumptions, interpretation, or action.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** expert disagreement có thể đến từ evidence, prior, value hoặc model khác; không tự động là incompetence.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `전문가 불일치` — expert disagreement.

## 25. institutional credibility /ˌɪnstəˈtuːʃənəl ˌkrɛdəˈbɪləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the trust an institution earns through competent methods, transparency, consistency, and accountable correction.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** institutional credibility tích lũy từ method tốt, transparency, consistency và correction khi sai.
**Grammar & collocations:** `institutional-credibility assessment` — assessment; `earn credibility` — earn; `accountable correction` — correction.
**Examples:** `Promptly correcting the estimate strengthened the agency's institutional credibility.` → Sửa estimate nhanh củng cố institutional credibility của agency.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `기관 신뢰성` — institutional credibility.

## Review in context

**Organized skepticism**, **scientific communalism**, **scientific universalism**, and **research disinterestedness** describe norms for trustworthy inquiry. The **credit economy** can produce a **priority dispute**, so an **epistemic division of labor** and **distributed cognition** help build **collective evidence**. A **structured expert elicitation** may support a **consensus statement**, while a **minority report** or **dissenting opinion** preserves reasoned disagreement. A **boundary organization** and **transdisciplinary collaboration** connect researchers with practice; a **knowledge broker** works at the **science-policy interface** alongside an **advisory committee**. **Precautionary reasoning** turns serious uncertainty into protective options, and **decision-relevant uncertainty** clarifies trade-offs. An **evidence broker** supports **policy translation** while respecting a **research community norm**. **Expert disagreement** need not destroy **institutional credibility** when methods and corrections remain visible.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

**Organized skepticism**, **scientific communalism**, **scientific universalism** và **research disinterestedness** mô tả norm của inquiry đáng tin. **Credit economy** có thể tạo **priority dispute**, nên **epistemic division of labor** và **distributed cognition** giúp xây **collective evidence**. **Structured expert elicitation** có thể hỗ trợ **consensus statement**, trong khi **minority report** hoặc **dissenting opinion** giữ lại disagreement có lý do. **Boundary organization** và **transdisciplinary collaboration** nối researcher với practice; **knowledge broker** hoạt động ở **science-policy interface** cùng **advisory committee**. **Precautionary reasoning** biến uncertainty nghiêm trọng thành option bảo vệ, còn **decision-relevant uncertainty** làm trade-off rõ. **Evidence broker** hỗ trợ **policy translation** mà vẫn tôn trọng **research community norm**. **Expert disagreement** không nhất thiết phá hỏng **institutional credibility** nếu method và correction vẫn visible.
