# C2+ Vocabulary — Atmospheric chemistry and dynamics

This lesson follows atmospheric particles and gases from radiative effects and chemical loss through stability, convection, mountain winds, and cloud formation. The network is **aerosol optical-depth measurement → radiative forcing estimate → surface albedo → hygroscopic growth → nucleation atmospheric → coagulation aerosol → dry-deposition velocity → atmospheric residence time → photolysis rate → hydroxyl radical → ozone titration → inversion layer → environmental lapse rate → adiabatic lapse rate → CAPE meteorology → CIN meteorology → katabatic wind → foehn wind → rain shadow → cloud condensation nucleus activation**.

## 1. aerosol optical-depth measurement /ˈɛəroʊsɑːl ˈɑːptɪkəl dɛpθ ˈmɛʒərmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** measuring how strongly airborne particles reduce or scatter light through a vertical atmospheric column.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** đo mức particle airborne giảm/scatter light qua atmospheric column dọc.
**Grammar & collocations:** `satellite aerosol optical-depth measurement` — measurement satellite; `calibrate a measurement` — calibrate.
**Examples:** `Satellite aerosol optical-depth measurement revealed smoke over the basin.` → Measurement aerosol optical-depth từ satellite reveal smoke trên basin.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `에어로졸 광학 깊이 측정`.

## 2. radiative forcing estimate /ˈreɪdiətɪv ˈfɔːrsɪŋ ˈɛstəmət/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an estimate of how a change in radiation alters the energy balance of the climate system.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** estimate change radiation làm đổi energy balance của climate system.
**Grammar & collocations:** `net radiative forcing estimate` — estimate net; `refine a forcing estimate` — refine.
**Examples:** `The model produced a lower radiative forcing estimate after cloud feedback was added.` → Model tạo radiative forcing estimate thấp hơn sau thêm cloud feedback.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `복사 강제력 추정`.

## 3. surface albedo /ˈsɜːrfɪs ælˈbiːdoʊ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the fraction of incoming sunlight reflected by a surface.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** fraction sunlight incoming bị surface reflect.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high surface albedo` — albedo cao; `measure surface albedo` — measure.
**Examples:** `Fresh snow increased the surface albedo and reduced absorption.` → Snow fresh tăng surface albedo và giảm absorption.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `지표 반사율`.

## 4. hygroscopic growth /ˌhaɪɡrəˈskɑːpɪk ɡroʊθ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the increase in particle size when an aerosol absorbs water from humid air.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** particle size tăng khi aerosol absorb water từ humid air.
**Grammar & collocations:** `rapid hygroscopic growth` — growth nhanh; `model hygroscopic growth` — model.
**Examples:** `Rapid hygroscopic growth made the particles more efficient at scattering light.` → Hygroscopic growth nhanh làm particle scatter light hiệu quả hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `흡습 성장`.

## 5. nucleation atmospheric /ˌnuːkliˈeɪʃən ˌæt məˈsfɛrɪk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the initial formation of tiny atmospheric particles from gases.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** formation ban đầu của particle atmospheric nhỏ từ gas.
**Grammar & collocations:** `new-particle nucleation atmospheric` — nucleation atmospheric tạo particle mới; `observe nucleation` — observe.
**Examples:** `New-particle nucleation atmospheric increased after the forest released organic vapors.` → Nucleation atmospheric particle mới tăng sau khi forest release organic vapor.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `대기 핵생성`.

## 6. coagulation aerosol /koʊˌæɡjəˈleɪʃən ˈɛəroʊsɑːl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the collision and merging of airborne particles into larger particles.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** particle airborne collide và merge thành particle lớn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `aerosol coagulation rate` — rate; `simulate coagulation` — simulate.
**Examples:** `Aerosol coagulation reduced the number of ultrafine particles.` → Coagulation aerosol giảm number particle ultrafine.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `에어로졸 응집`.

## 7. dry-deposition velocity /draɪ ˌdɛpəˈzɪʃən vəˈlɑːsəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the effective speed at which airborne material is removed onto a surface without precipitation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** speed hiệu dụng particle airborne bị remove lên surface mà không cần precipitation.
**Grammar & collocations:** `particle dry-deposition velocity` — velocity particle; `estimate deposition velocity` — estimate.
**Examples:** `Rough vegetation increased the dry-deposition velocity of the particles.` → Vegetation rough tăng dry-deposition velocity của particle.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `건식 침적 속도`.

## 8. atmospheric residence time /ˌæt məˈsfɛrɪk ˈrɛzɪdəns taɪm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the average time a gas or particle remains in the atmosphere before removal.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** time trung bình gas/particle remain trong atmosphere trước removal.
**Grammar & collocations:** `long atmospheric residence time` — time dài; `calculate residence time` — calculate.
**Examples:** `The pollutant's long atmospheric residence time allowed it to cross oceans.` → Atmospheric residence time dài cho pollutant cross ocean.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `대기 체류 시간`.

## 9. photolysis rate /fəˈtɑːləsɪs reɪt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the rate at which light breaks a molecule into smaller chemical fragments.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** rate light break molecule thành chemical fragment nhỏ.
**Grammar & collocations:** `rapid photolysis rate` — rate nhanh; `measure a photolysis rate` — measure.
**Examples:** `Sunlight increased the photolysis rate of the reactive compound.` → Sunlight tăng photolysis rate của compound reactive.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `광분해 속도`.

## 10. hydroxyl radical /haɪˈdrɑːksəl ˈrædɪkəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a highly reactive atmospheric molecule that helps oxidize and remove many pollutants.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** molecule atmospheric rất reactive giúp oxidize/remove pollutant.
**Grammar & collocations:** `atmospheric hydroxyl radical` — radical atmospheric; `react with hydroxyl radicals` — react.
**Examples:** `The pollutant reacted rapidly with the atmospheric hydroxyl radical.` → Pollutant react nhanh với hydroxyl radical atmospheric.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `수산화 라디칼`.

## 11. ozone titration /ˈoʊzoʊn taɪˈtreɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the removal of ozone when it reacts with nitrogen oxide or another chemical species.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** ozone bị remove khi react với nitrogen oxide hoặc species khác.
**Grammar & collocations:** `ozone titration effect` — effect; `observe ozone titration` — observe.
**Examples:** `Ozone titration lowered concentrations near the busy roadway.` → Ozone titration làm concentration thấp gần roadway đông.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `오존 적정`.

## 12. inversion layer /ɪnˈvɜːrʒən ˈleɪər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a layer in which temperature increases with height and suppresses vertical mixing.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** layer temperature tăng theo height và suppress vertical mixing.
**Grammar & collocations:** `persistent inversion layer` — layer kéo dài; `break an inversion layer` — break.
**Examples:** `A persistent inversion layer trapped pollution near the ground.` → Inversion layer kéo dài trap pollution gần ground.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `역전층`.

## 13. environmental lapse rate /ɪnˌvaɪrənˈmɛntəl læps reɪt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the actual rate at which air temperature changes with altitude in the environment.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** rate thực tế temperature air change theo altitude trong environment.
**Grammar & collocations:** `steep environmental lapse rate` — rate dốc; `measure the environmental lapse rate` — measure.
**Examples:** `The steep environmental lapse rate favored vigorous convection.` → Environmental lapse rate dốc favor convection mạnh.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `환경 기온 감률`.

## 14. adiabatic lapse rate /ˌædiəˈbætɪk læps reɪt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the temperature change of an air parcel as it rises or sinks without exchanging heat with its surroundings.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** temperature change của air parcel rise/sink mà không exchange heat.
**Grammar & collocations:** `moist adiabatic lapse rate` — rate moist; `compare lapse rates` — compare.
**Examples:** `The moist adiabatic lapse rate changed after condensation began.` → Moist adiabatic lapse rate đổi sau khi condensation bắt đầu.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `단열 감률`.

## 15. CAPE meteorology /keɪp miːtiəˈrɑːlədʒi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the atmospheric energy available to accelerate a rising parcel of air.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** energy atmospheric có sẵn để accelerate air parcel rising.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high CAPE meteorology` — CAPE cao; `calculate CAPE` — calculate.
**Examples:** `High CAPE meteorology supported the development of severe thunderstorms.` → CAPE meteorology cao support severe thunderstorm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `대류 가용 잠재에너지`.

## 16. CIN meteorology /sɪn miːtiəˈrɑːlədʒi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the energy barrier that prevents an air parcel from rising freely.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** energy barrier prevent air parcel rise tự do.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong CIN meteorology` — CIN mạnh; `erode CIN` — erode.
**Examples:** `Strong CIN meteorology delayed convection until late afternoon.` → CIN meteorology mạnh delay convection đến late afternoon.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `대류 억제 에너지`.

## 17. katabatic wind /ˌkætəˈbætɪk wɪnd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a downslope wind driven by dense, cooled air flowing downhill.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** wind xuống slope do dense air đã cool flow downhill.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong katabatic wind` — wind mạnh; `measure katabatic winds` — measure.
**Examples:** `A strong katabatic wind accelerated across the glacier at night.` → Katabatic wind mạnh accelerate qua glacier ban đêm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `활강풍`.

## 18. foehn wind /ˈfeɪn wɪnd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a warm, dry downslope wind produced as air descends on the lee side of mountains.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** wind xuống slope ấm và khô ở lee side của mountain.
**Grammar & collocations:** `dry foehn wind` — wind khô; `forecast a foehn wind` — forecast.
**Examples:** `A dry foehn wind rapidly cleared the clouds east of the range.` → Foehn wind khô clear cloud nhanh east range.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `푄풍`.

## 19. rain shadow /reɪn ˈʃædoʊ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a dry region on the sheltered side of a mountain range where descending air loses little additional moisture.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** region khô ở sheltered side mountain nơi descending air mất ít moisture thêm.
**Grammar & collocations:** `mountain rain shadow` — shadow mountain; `lie in a rain shadow` — nằm trong.
**Examples:** `The valley lies in a rain shadow and receives little winter precipitation.` → Valley nằm trong rain shadow và nhận ít winter precipitation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `우풍 지역`.

## 20. cloud condensation nucleus activation /klaʊd ˌkɑːndənˈseɪʃən ˈnuːkliəs ˌæktəˈveɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the process by which an aerosol particle becomes a site for water to condense into a cloud droplet.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** aerosol particle trở thành site để water condense thành cloud droplet.
**Grammar & collocations:** `cloud condensation nucleus activation threshold` — threshold; `model nucleus activation` — model.
**Examples:** `Humidity exceeded the cloud condensation nucleus activation threshold.` → Humidity vượt threshold activation của cloud condensation nucleus.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `구름응결핵 활성화`.
