# C2+ Vocabulary — Marine zones and animal adaptations

This lesson follows marine environments from water-column structure through productivity, animal physiology, development, and survival strategies. The network is **benthic ecology → pelagic zone → nektonic locomotion → planktonic larva → coastal upwelling → thermocline depth → halocline formation → pycnocline gradient → eutrophication event → marine bioluminescence → deep-sea chemosynthesis → fish osmoregulation → countercurrent exchange system → brood pouch → metamorphosis → neoteny → aposematism → cryptobiosis → diapause strategy → benthic recruitment**.

## 1. benthic ecology /ˈbɛnθɪk iˈkɑːlədʒi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the study of organisms and interactions on or in the bottom of aquatic environments.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** study organism và interaction trên/trong đáy aquatic environment.
**Grammar & collocations:** `shallow benthic ecology` — ecology benthic nông; `study benthic ecology` — study.
**Examples:** `Benthic ecology changed after sediment covered the seagrass bed.` → Benthic ecology thay đổi sau khi sediment phủ seagrass bed.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `저서 생태학`.

## 2. pelagic zone /pəˈlædʒɪk zoʊn/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the open-water region away from the bottom and shore.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** vùng open water xa đáy và shore.
**Grammar & collocations:** `open pelagic zone` — zone pelagic open; `cross the pelagic zone` — cross zone.
**Examples:** `The turtle crossed the pelagic zone between two islands.` → Turtle băng qua pelagic zone giữa hai island.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `원양대`.

## 3. nektonic locomotion /nɛkˈtɑːnɪk loʊməˈmoʊʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** active movement by swimming organisms that can overcome surrounding currents.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** movement active của organism bơi, vượt được current xung quanh.
**Grammar & collocations:** `efficient nektonic locomotion` — locomotion hiệu quả; `analyze nektonic locomotion` — analyze.
**Examples:** `Efficient nektonic locomotion helped the squid hold position in the current.` → Nektonic locomotion hiệu quả giúp squid giữ position trong current.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `유영 생물 운동`.

## 4. planktonic larva /plæŋkˈtɑːnɪk ˈlɑːrvə/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an early life stage that drifts with currents rather than swimming strongly against them.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** life stage sớm drift theo current thay vì swim mạnh ngược lại.
**Grammar & collocations:** `dispersing planktonic larva` — larva planktonic dispersing; `sample a planktonic larva` — sample.
**Examples:** `The dispersing planktonic larva eventually settled on the reef.` → Planktonic larva dispersing cuối cùng settle trên reef.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `부유 유생`.

## 5. coastal upwelling /ˈkoʊstəl ʌpˈwɛlɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the upward movement of cold, nutrient-rich deep water near a coast.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** deep water lạnh giàu nutrient move lên gần coast.
**Grammar & collocations:** `seasonal coastal upwelling` — upwelling theo mùa; `track coastal upwelling` — track.
**Examples:** `Seasonal coastal upwelling supported a dense anchovy fishery.` → Coastal upwelling theo mùa support fishery anchovy dày.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `연안 용승`.

## 6. thermocline depth /ˈθɜːrmoʊklaɪn dɛpθ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the depth at which water temperature changes rapidly with depth.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** depth nơi water temperature đổi nhanh theo depth.
**Grammar & collocations:** `seasonal thermocline depth` — depth thermocline theo mùa; `measure thermocline depth` — measure.
**Examples:** `The seasonal thermocline depth moved upward during the calm summer.` → Thermocline depth theo mùa move upward trong summer yên.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `수온약층 깊이`.

## 7. halocline formation /ˈhæləʊklaɪn fɔːrˈmeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the development of a layer where salinity changes sharply with depth.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** hình thành layer salinity đổi mạnh theo depth.
**Grammar & collocations:** `rapid halocline formation` — formation nhanh; `model halocline formation` — model.
**Examples:** `Rapid halocline formation separated the fresh surface water from the sea below.` → Halocline formation nhanh tách fresh surface water khỏi sea bên dưới.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `염분약층 형성`.

## 8. pycnocline gradient /ˈpɪknəklaɪn ˈɡreɪdiənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the rate at which water density changes across a depth zone.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** rate water density change xuyên qua depth zone.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong pycnocline gradient` — gradient mạnh; `estimate the pycnocline gradient` — estimate.
**Examples:** `A strong pycnocline gradient limited vertical mixing in the basin.` → Pycnocline gradient mạnh hạn chế vertical mixing trong basin.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `밀도약층 구배`.

## 9. eutrophication event /ˌjuːtrəfɪˈkeɪʃən ɪˈvɛnt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a period when excess nutrients cause abnormal algal growth and oxygen depletion in water.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** period nutrient dư làm algae grow abnormal và oxygen depletion.
**Grammar & collocations:** `severe eutrophication event` — event nặng; `document an eutrophication event` — document.
**Examples:** `The severe eutrophication event created a temporary dead zone.` → Eutrophication event nặng tạo dead zone tạm thời.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `부영양화 사건`.

## 10. marine bioluminescence /məˈriːn ˌbaɪoʊluːməˈnɛsəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** light produced by living marine organisms through chemical reactions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** light do marine organism tạo bằng chemical reaction.
**Grammar & collocations:** `blue marine bioluminescence` — bioluminescence xanh; `observe marine bioluminescence` — observe.
**Examples:** `Blue marine bioluminescence flashed when the water was disturbed.` → Marine bioluminescence xanh flash khi water bị disturb.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `해양 생물발광`.

## 11. deep-sea chemosynthesis /ˌdiːp siː ˌkiːmoʊˈsɪnθəsɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** producing organic energy from chemical reactions instead of sunlight in deep marine environments.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** tạo energy organic từ chemical reaction thay vì sunlight ở deep sea.
**Grammar & collocations:** `microbial deep-sea chemosynthesis` — chemosynthesis microbial; `support deep-sea chemosynthesis` — support.
**Examples:** `Microbial deep-sea chemosynthesis supports life around the vent.` → Deep-sea chemosynthesis microbial support life quanh vent.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `심해 화학합성`.

## 12. fish osmoregulation /fɪʃ ˌɑːzmoʊrɛɡjəˈleɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the regulation of water and dissolved salts inside a fish's body.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** regulation water và dissolved salt trong body fish.
**Grammar & collocations:** `marine fish osmoregulation` — osmoregulation fish marine; `study fish osmoregulation` — study.
**Examples:** `Marine fish osmoregulation prevents the animal from losing too much water.` → Fish osmoregulation marine ngăn animal mất quá nhiều water.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `어류 삼투조절`.

## 13. countercurrent exchange system /ˌkaʊntərˈkɜːrənt ɪksˈtʃeɪndʒ ˈsɪstəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an arrangement in which fluids or gases flow in opposite directions to improve transfer of heat or substances.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** fluids/gases flow opposite direction để tăng transfer heat hoặc substance.
**Grammar & collocations:** `gill countercurrent exchange system` — system ở gill; `optimize a countercurrent exchange system` — optimize.
**Examples:** `The gill countercurrent exchange system extracts oxygen efficiently.` → Countercurrent exchange system ở gill extract oxygen hiệu quả.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `역류 교환 체계`.

## 14. brood pouch /bruːd paʊtʃ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a body structure in which an animal carries or protects developing young.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** structure body để animal carry/protect young đang develop.
**Grammar & collocations:** `paternal brood pouch` — pouch của male; `protect the brood pouch` — protect.
**Examples:** `The male seahorse protected embryos inside its brood pouch.` → Seahorse đực protect embryo trong brood pouch.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `육아낭`.

## 15. metamorphosis /ˌmɛtəˈmɔːrfəsɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a major change in body form during an animal's development.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** change lớn về body form trong development animal.
**Grammar & collocations:** `complete metamorphosis` — metamorphosis hoàn toàn; `undergo metamorphosis` — undergo.
**Examples:** `The insect underwent complete metamorphosis inside the sealed chamber.` → Insect undergo metamorphosis hoàn toàn trong chamber kín.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `변태`.

## 16. neoteny /niˈɑːtəni/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** retention of juvenile features in an organism that reaches reproductive maturity.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** giữ feature juvenile dù organism đạt reproductive maturity.
**Grammar & collocations:** `evolutionary neoteny` — neoteny tiến hóa; `explain neoteny` — explain.
**Examples:** `Evolutionary neoteny allowed the salamander to retain its aquatic gills.` → Neoteny tiến hóa cho salamander giữ gill aquatic.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `유형성숙`.

## 17. aposematism /ˈæpəˌsiːmətɪzəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** warning coloration or signaling that discourages predators by advertising danger or unpalatability.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** color/signal cảnh báo discourage predator bằng advertise danger hoặc không ăn được.
**Grammar & collocations:** `bright aposematism` — aposematism sáng; `evolve aposematism` — evolve.
**Examples:** `Bright aposematism warned predators that the caterpillar was toxic.` → Aposematism sáng cảnh báo predator caterpillar độc.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `경고색`.

## 18. cryptobiosis /ˌkrɪptoʊbaɪˈoʊsɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a state in which an organism's metabolism becomes almost undetectable during extreme conditions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** state metabolism gần như không detect trong điều kiện extreme.
**Grammar & collocations:** `induced cryptobiosis` — cryptobiosis induced; `enter cryptobiosis` — enter.
**Examples:** `The microscopic animal entered cryptobiosis during prolonged desiccation.` → Animal microscopic enter cryptobiosis trong desiccation kéo dài.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `숨은 생명 상태`.

## 19. diapause strategy /ˈdaɪəpɔːz ˈstrætədʒi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a programmed pause in development that helps an organism survive predictable unfavorable seasons.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** pause development được program để survive season unfavorable có thể predict.
**Grammar & collocations:** `seasonal diapause strategy` — strategy theo mùa; `trigger a diapause strategy` — trigger.
**Examples:** `A seasonal diapause strategy synchronized hatching with spring food.` → Diapause strategy theo mùa synchronize hatching với food mùa xuân.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `휴면 전략`.

## 20. benthic recruitment /ˈbɛnθɪk rɪˈkruːtmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the arrival and establishment of young organisms on the bottom of an aquatic habitat.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** young organism arrive và establish trên đáy aquatic habitat.
**Grammar & collocations:** `seasonal benthic recruitment` — recruitment theo mùa; `measure benthic recruitment` — measure.
**Examples:** `Seasonal benthic recruitment restored the shellfish bed after the storm.` → Benthic recruitment theo mùa restore shellfish bed sau storm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `저서 생물 가입`.

## Review in context

In **benthic ecology**, organisms on the bottom interact with the open **pelagic zone**, where **nektonic locomotion** differs from a drifting **planktonic larva**. **Coastal upwelling**, **thermocline depth**, **halocline formation**, and the **pycnocline gradient** shape productivity, while an **eutrophication event** can remove oxygen. **Marine bioluminescence** and **deep-sea chemosynthesis** reveal unusual energy pathways. **Fish osmoregulation** and a **countercurrent exchange system** support physiology; a **brood pouch**, **metamorphosis**, and **neoteny** shape development. **Aposematism**, **cryptobiosis**, a **diapause strategy**, and **benthic recruitment** connect survival with habitat timing.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:** Trong **benthic ecology**, organism ở đáy tương tác với **pelagic zone** open, nơi **nektonic locomotion** khác với **planktonic larva** drift. **Coastal upwelling**, **thermocline depth**, **halocline formation** và **pycnocline gradient** định hình productivity, còn **eutrophication event** có thể làm mất oxygen. **Marine bioluminescence** và **deep-sea chemosynthesis** reveal energy pathway khác thường. **Fish osmoregulation** và **countercurrent exchange system** support physiology; **brood pouch**, **metamorphosis** và **neoteny** shape development. **Aposematism**, **cryptobiosis**, **diapause strategy** và **benthic recruitment** nối survival với timing của habitat.
