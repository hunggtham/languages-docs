# C2+ Vocabulary — Healthcare-associated infection epidemiology

This lesson follows how hospitals measure preventable infection, identify transmission, and improve infection-control systems. The network is **incidence density → device-associated infection → central-line-associated bloodstream infection → catheter-associated urinary infection → ventilator-associated event → surgical-site infection → standardized infection ratio → unit colonization load → hand hygiene compliance → environmental reservoir → cohorting → isolation room → bundle compliance → infection prevention link → healthcare worker carriage → terminal cleaning → environmental sampling → pathogen typing → outbreak attack rate → infection control assessment → prescribing timeout review → contact precaution audit → hospital-onset infection → laboratory-onset infection → facility transmission chain**.

## 1. incidence density /ˈɪnsɪdəns ˈdɛnsəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the rate of new infection events relative to the amount of time people remain at risk or under observation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** incidence density dùng person-time hoặc device-days để so infection giữa unit khác nhau.
**Grammar & collocations:** `incidence-density rate` — rate; `calculate infections per device-day` — calculate; `person-time denominator` — denominator.
**Examples:** `Incidence density fell after the unit reduced unnecessary catheter-days.` → Denominator gắn với exposure time.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `발생 밀도` — incidence density.

## 2. device-associated infection /dɪˈvaɪs əˈsoʊsiˌeɪtɪd ɪnˈfɛkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an infection occurring in relation to the presence or use of an invasive medical device.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** device-associated infection liên quan tới catheter, line, ventilator hoặc device khác.
**Grammar & collocations:** `device-associated-infection rate` — rate; `remove an unnecessary device` — remove; `device-day denominator` — denominator.
**Examples:** `The device-associated infection rate dropped when daily device necessity was reviewed.` → Review giảm exposure không cần.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `기기 관련 감염` — device-associated infection.

## 3. central-line-associated bloodstream infection /ˈsɛntrəl laɪn əˈsoʊsiˌeɪtɪd ˈblʌdˌstriːm ɪnˈfɛkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a bloodstream infection associated with a central venous catheter under a defined surveillance rule.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** central-line-associated bloodstream infection là bloodstream infection gắn với central line và criteria surveillance.
**Grammar & collocations:** `central-line-associated bloodstream infection` — infection; `maintain central-line sterility` — maintain; `line bundle` — bundle.
**Examples:** `Central-line-associated bloodstream infection declined after insertion and maintenance steps were audited.` → Bundle cần audit liên tục.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `중심정맥관 관련 혈류 감염` — central-line-associated bloodstream infection.

## 4. catheter-associated urinary infection /ˈkæθətər əˈsoʊsiˌeɪtɪd ˈjʊrəˌnɛri ɪnˈfɛkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a urinary infection associated with an indwelling urinary catheter under a defined clinical and surveillance criterion.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** catheter-associated urinary infection nối urinary catheter với infection risk.
**Grammar & collocations:** `catheter-associated urinary infection` — infection; `remove a urinary catheter` — remove; `catheter necessity` — necessity.
**Examples:** `Catheter-associated urinary infection fell after nurses used a daily removal checklist.` → Checklist làm device duration ngắn hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `유치도뇨관 관련 요로감염` — catheter-associated urinary infection.

## 5. ventilator-associated event /ˈvɛntəˌleɪtər əˈsoʊsiˌeɪtɪd ɪˈvɛnt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a defined worsening in respiratory status among a patient receiving mechanical ventilation, used as a surveillance event.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** ventilator-associated event là event respiratory worsening quanh ventilator, rộng hơn một diagnosis infection.
**Grammar & collocations:** `ventilator-associated-event definition` — definition; `monitor ventilated patients` — monitor; `respiratory deterioration` — deterioration.
**Examples:** `The ventilator-associated event rate improved after sedation and mobility practices changed.` → Prevention có thể không chỉ là antibiotic.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `인공호흡기 관련 사건` — ventilator-associated event.

## 6. surgical-site infection /ˈsɜrdʒɪkəl saɪt ɪnˈfɛkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** infection occurring at or near a surgical incision or operated tissue within a defined follow-up period.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** surgical-site infection có thể xuất hiện sau discharge nên cần follow-up đủ dài.
**Grammar & collocations:** `surgical-site infection rate` — rate; `prevent wound infection` — prevent; `postoperative follow-up` — follow-up.
**Examples:** `Surgical-site infection surveillance continued after discharge through a patient call system.` → Follow-up ngoài hospital rất quan trọng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `수술 부위 감염` — surgical-site infection.

## 7. standardized infection ratio /ˈstændərˌdaɪzd ɪnˈfɛkʃən ˈreɪʃioʊ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the ratio of observed healthcare-associated infections to the number expected after adjusting for patient and procedure characteristics.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** standardized infection ratio so observed infection với expected infection công bằng hơn giữa facility.
**Grammar & collocations:** `standardized-infection ratio` — ratio; `compare observed and expected infections` — compare; `risk adjustment` — adjustment.
**Examples:** `The standardized infection ratio was above one after case-mix adjustment.` → Trên one gợi excess so expected.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `표준화 감염비` — standardized infection ratio.

## 8. unit colonization load /ˈjuːnɪt ˌkɑlənəˈzeɪʃən loʊd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the amount of resistant or potentially pathogenic organism carriage present among patients in a clinical unit.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** unit colonization load là reservoir nền trong unit, chưa chắc clinical infection nhưng tạo transmission pressure.
**Grammar & collocations:** `unit-colonization load` — load; `measure carriage burden` — measure; `patient reservoir` — reservoir.
**Examples:** `Unit colonization load rose after transfers brought resistant organisms from several facilities.` → Patient movement đổi reservoir.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `병동 집락화 부하` — unit colonization load.

## 9. hand hygiene compliance /hænd ˈhaɪdʒiːn kəmˈplaɪəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the extent to which staff perform hand cleaning at the required moments and with the required technique.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** hand hygiene compliance đo behavior thật, không chỉ policy trên poster.
**Grammar & collocations:** `hand-hygiene compliance` — compliance; `observe hand hygiene` — observe; `compliance audit` — audit.
**Examples:** `Hand hygiene compliance improved after sanitizer placement and immediate feedback were combined.` → Design và feedback cùng tác động.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `손 위생 준수` — hand hygiene compliance.

## 10. environmental reservoir /ɪnˌvaɪrənˈmɛntəl ˈrɛzərˌvwɑr/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a surface, water source, device, or built environment in which an organism persists and can expose people.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** environmental reservoir là source ngoài patient: sink, drain, surface, equipment hoặc water.
**Grammar & collocations:** `environmental-reservoir investigation` — investigation; `sample a reservoir` — sample; `persistent organism` — organism.
**Examples:** `The sink drain became an environmental reservoir for a resistant organism.` → Reservoir cần source control.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `환경 저장소` — environmental reservoir.

## 11. cohorting /ˈkoʊˌhɔrtɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** grouping patients, staff, or equipment according to infection status or exposure to reduce cross-transmission.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cohorting gom người cùng status để giảm mixing giữa infected và susceptible.
**Grammar & collocations:** `cohorting strategy` — strategy; `cohort infected patients` — cohort; `dedicated staff` — staff.
**Examples:** `Cohorting patients and assigning dedicated equipment reduced cross-unit movement.` → Separation cần logistics.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `코호팅` — cohorting.

## 12. isolation room /ˌaɪsəˈleɪʃən ruːm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a room designed and managed to separate an infectious patient and reduce transmission to others.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** isolation room là physical space cộng ventilation, PPE và workflow.
**Grammar & collocations:** `isolation-room capacity` — capacity; `place a patient in isolation` — place; `airflow control` — control.
**Examples:** `Isolation room capacity was expanded before the seasonal respiratory surge.` → Capacity phải có trước peak.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `격리실` — isolation room.

## 13. bundle compliance /ˈbʌndəl kəmˈplaɪəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the extent to which all required elements of a coordinated prevention bundle are performed together.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** bundle compliance không chỉ làm một step; phải đủ các element cần thiết.
**Grammar & collocations:** `bundle-compliance audit` — audit; `complete every bundle element` — complete; `process reliability` — reliability.
**Examples:** `Bundle compliance was low because staff documented insertion steps but skipped daily review.` → Partial compliance có thể không đủ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `묶음 준수` — bundle compliance.

## 14. infection prevention link /ɪnˈfɛkʃən prɪˈvɛnʃən lɪŋk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a specific connection between a prevention action and the transmission route or infection outcome it is intended to change.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** infection prevention link giải thích action nào chặn route nào, tránh làm checklist máy móc.
**Grammar & collocations:** `infection-prevention link` — link; `link an action to transmission` — link; `mechanism of prevention` — mechanism.
**Examples:** `The infection prevention link between drain cleaning and sink contamination was made explicit.` → Mechanism rõ giúp compliance có meaning.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `감염 예방 연결고리` — infection prevention link.

## 15. healthcare worker carriage /ˈhɛlθˌkɛr ˈwɜrkər ˈkærɪdʒ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** asymptomatic or symptomatic carriage of an organism by a healthcare worker who may transmit it.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** healthcare worker carriage có thể là reservoir hoặc bridge giữa patient và unit.
**Grammar & collocations:** `healthcare-worker carriage` — carriage; `screen staff carriage` — screen; `staff transmission route` — route.
**Examples:** `Healthcare worker carriage was investigated after linked infections appeared across two wards.` → Worker movement có thể nối unit.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `의료 종사자 보균` — healthcare worker carriage.

## 16. terminal cleaning /ˈtɜrmənəl ˈkliːnɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** thorough cleaning and disinfection of a patient space after discharge, transfer, or the end of an isolation period.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** terminal cleaning reset room trước patient mới.
**Grammar & collocations:** `terminal-cleaning checklist` — checklist; `clean a patient room thoroughly` — clean; `disinfection standard` — standard.
**Examples:** `Terminal cleaning was delayed when the unit lacked trained environmental-services staff.` → Staffing ảnh hưởng infection control.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `퇴실 후 최종 청소` — terminal cleaning.

## 17. environmental sampling /ɪnˌvaɪrənˈmɛntəl ˈsæmplɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** collection and testing of surfaces, water, air, or equipment to investigate environmental contamination.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** environmental sampling tìm pathogen ngoài patient khi source chưa rõ.
**Grammar & collocations:** `environmental-sampling plan` — plan; `sample high-touch surfaces` — sample; `contamination mapping` — mapping.
**Examples:** `Environmental sampling found matching organisms on a shared medication cart.` → Equipment có thể là route.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `환경 시료 채취` — environmental sampling.

## 18. pathogen typing /ˈpæθədʒən ˈtaɪpɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** laboratory characterization that distinguishes strains or variants of a pathogen for transmission investigation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** pathogen typing giúp biết isolates có related không, hỗ trợ trace chain.
**Grammar & collocations:** `pathogen-typing result` — result; `type an isolate` — type; `strain comparison` — comparison.
**Examples:** `Pathogen typing linked patient isolates to the environmental sample from the medication cart.` → Lab evidence nối source.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `병원체 유형 분석` — pathogen typing.

## 19. outbreak attack rate /ˈaʊtˌbreɪk əˈtæk reɪt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the proportion of an at-risk group that becomes ill during a defined outbreak period.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** outbreak attack rate cho biết trong group exposed có bao nhiêu người thành case.
**Grammar & collocations:** `outbreak-attack rate` — rate; `calculate attack rate` — calculate; `at-risk denominator` — denominator.
**Examples:** `The outbreak attack rate was higher among patients sharing the same treatment bay.` → Shared space gợi transmission route.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `유행 발병률` — outbreak attack rate.

## 21. prescribing timeout review /prɪˈskraɪbɪŋ ˈtaɪmˌaʊt rɪˈvjuː/

## 23. hospital-onset infection /ˈhɑspɪtəl ˈɑnsɛt ɪnˈfɛkʃən/

**Core meaning (English):** an infection first meeting a defined clinical or surveillance criterion after a patient has been in a hospital for a specified period.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** hospital-onset infection được classify theo timing sau admission, không tự động chứng minh facility causation.

## Review in context — Part 1

**Incidence density** measures new events over time. A **device-associated infection**, **central-line-associated bloodstream infection**, **catheter-associated urinary infection**, **ventilator-associated event**, or **surgical-site infection** can be compared with a **standardized infection ratio**. **Unit colonization load**, **hand hygiene compliance**, an **environmental reservoir**, **cohorting**, and an **isolation room** shape transmission.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 1:**

**Incidence density** đo event mới theo thời gian. **Device-associated infection**, **central-line-associated bloodstream infection**, **catheter-associated urinary infection**, **ventilator-associated event** hoặc **surgical-site infection** có thể được so bằng **standardized infection ratio**. **Unit colonization load**, **hand hygiene compliance**, **environmental reservoir**, **cohorting** và **isolation room** định hình transmission.

## Review in context — Part 2

**Bundle compliance** and an **infection prevention link** connect each practice with a transmission route. **Healthcare worker carriage**, **terminal cleaning**, **environmental sampling**, and **pathogen typing** can clarify an **outbreak attack rate**. An **infection control assessment**, **prescribing timeout review**, and **contact precaution audit** support improvement. **Hospital-onset infection**, **laboratory-onset infection**, and a **facility transmission chain** must be interpreted together rather than treated as automatic proof of source.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 2:**

**Bundle compliance** và **infection prevention link** nối từng practice với transmission route. **Healthcare worker carriage**, **terminal cleaning**, **environmental sampling** và **pathogen typing** có thể làm rõ **outbreak attack rate**. **Infection control assessment**, **prescribing timeout review** và **contact precaution audit** hỗ trợ improvement. **Hospital-onset infection**, **laboratory-onset infection** và **facility transmission chain** phải được diễn giải cùng nhau, không tự động coi là proof của source.
