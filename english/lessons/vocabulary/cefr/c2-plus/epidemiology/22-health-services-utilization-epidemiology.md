# C2+ Vocabulary — Health-services utilization epidemiology

This lesson follows how people reach care, move through services, and experience gaps, delays, and unequal burdens. The network is **care-seeking behavior → service unmet-need profile → service contact rate → referral completion → no-show rate → emergency department boarding → avoidable admission → ambulatory care-sensitive condition → post-discharge readmission risk → length-of-stay distribution → discharge destination → continuity-of-care gap → care fragmentation → unmet treatment need → provider density → travel-time access → health-spending shock → service substitution → telehealth uptake → utilization inequity → health service denominator → service care cascade → treatment cascade → retention in care → linkage-to-care**.

## 1. care-seeking behavior /kɛr ˈsiːkɪŋ bɪˈheɪvjər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the choices and actions through which people recognize a need and seek formal or informal health care.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** care-seeking behavior là route từ symptom → decision → service.
**Grammar & collocations:** `care-seeking behavior` — behavior; `seek formal care` — seek; `help-seeking pattern` — pattern.
**Examples:** `Care-seeking behavior changed after the clinic extended evening hours.` → Convenience ảnh hưởng timing đi care.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `의료 이용 행동` — care-seeking behavior.

## 2. service unmet-need profile /ˈsɜrvɪs ʌnˈmɛt niːd ˈproʊfaɪl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a structured description of health needs for which people do not receive appropriate or timely services.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** service unmet-need profile cho biết ai cần care nhưng chưa nhận được care phù hợp.
**Grammar & collocations:** `service-unmet-need profile` — profile; `assess unmet need` — assess; `care gap` — gap.
**Examples:** `The service unmet-need profile showed high demand for addiction treatment but few local appointments.` → Need và supply không match.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `의료 서비스 미충족 필요 프로필` — service unmet-need profile.

## 3. service contact rate /ˈsɜrvɪs ˈkɑntækt reɪt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the rate at which a defined population has contact with a specified health service during a period.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** service contact rate đo population thực sự chạm vào service bao nhiêu.
**Grammar & collocations:** `service-contact rate` — rate; `measure service contacts` — measure; `population denominator` — denominator.
**Examples:** `The service contact rate rose after mobile clinics visited isolated communities.` → Delivery model đổi contact.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `서비스 접촉률` — service contact rate.

## 4. referral completion /rɪˈfɜrəl kəmˈpliːʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the proportion of referred people who reach and complete the next recommended service or evaluation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** referral completion đo referral có đi đến endpoint thật không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `referral-completion rate` — rate; `complete a referral` — complete; `closed-loop referral` — referral.
**Examples:** `Referral completion improved when the sending clinic scheduled the specialist appointment directly.` → Handoff chủ động giảm drop-off.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `의뢰 완료율` — referral completion.

## 5. no-show rate /noʊ ʃoʊ reɪt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the proportion of scheduled appointments that patients miss without completing the visit.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** no-show rate đo appointment bị mất, thường phản ánh barrier chứ không chỉ “không có trách nhiệm”.
**Grammar & collocations:** `no-show rate` — rate; `reduce missed appointments` — reduce; `appointment reminder` — reminder.
**Examples:** `The no-show rate fell after transportation vouchers and text reminders were offered.` → Barrier thực tế có thể sửa.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `예약 불이행률` — no-show rate.

## 6. emergency department boarding /ɪˈmɜrdʒənsi dɪˈpɑrtmənt ˈbɔrdɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** prolonged time spent in an emergency department after a patient is admitted but before an inpatient bed becomes available.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** emergency department boarding là patient đã cần inpatient nhưng vẫn bị “kẹt” ở ED.
**Grammar & collocations:** `emergency-department boarding` — boarding; `board an admitted patient` — board; `bed-flow delay` — delay.
**Examples:** `Emergency department boarding increased when discharge planning slowed on weekends.` → Flow toàn hospital ảnh hưởng ED.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `응급실 체류 지연` — emergency department boarding.

## 7. avoidable admission /əˈvɔɪdəbəl ədˈmɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a hospital admission that might have been prevented by timely, accessible, and effective earlier care.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** avoidable admission gợi ý upstream care chưa đủ hoặc đến quá muộn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `avoidable-admission measure` — measure; `prevent an admission` — prevent; `early outpatient care` — care.
**Examples:** `Avoidable admission rates were higher where primary-care appointments were scarce.` → Access upstream ảnh hưởng hospital burden.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `예방 가능한 입원` — avoidable admission.

## 8. ambulatory care-sensitive condition /ˈæmbjələtɔri kɛr ˈsɛnsətɪv kənˈdɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a condition for which timely outpatient care can often prevent deterioration, emergency treatment, or hospitalization.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** ambulatory care-sensitive condition dùng hospital admission như signal của primary-care performance.
**Grammar & collocations:** `ambulatory-care-sensitive condition` — condition; `prevent hospitalization` — prevent; `primary-care indicator` — indicator.
**Examples:** `Admissions for an ambulatory care-sensitive condition declined after same-day primary care expanded.` → Outpatient access đổi outcome.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `외래 진료 민감 질환` — ambulatory care-sensitive condition.

## 9. post-discharge readmission risk /poʊst dɪsˈtʃɑrdʒ riːədˈmɪʃən rɪsk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the probability that a patient returns to hospital within a defined period after discharge.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** post-discharge readmission risk bắt đầu sau discharge và phụ thuộc handoff, support, condition.
**Grammar & collocations:** `post-discharge readmission risk` — risk; `predict readmission` — predict; `transition-of-care plan` — plan.
**Examples:** `Post-discharge readmission risk fell after medication reconciliation and follow-up calls.` → Transition care có effect.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `퇴원 후 재입원 위험` — post-discharge readmission risk.

## 10. length-of-stay distribution /lɛŋθ əv steɪ dɪˌstrəˈbjuːʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the distribution of time patients remain in a facility from admission to discharge or transfer.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** length-of-stay distribution cho thấy median, tail và nhóm nằm rất lâu.
**Grammar & collocations:** `length-of-stay distribution` — distribution; `model hospital stay` — model; `long-stay tail` — tail.
**Examples:** `The length-of-stay distribution had a long tail among patients waiting for social support.` → Discharge barrier kéo tail.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `재원 기간 분포` — length-of-stay distribution.

## 11. discharge destination /dɪsˈtʃɑrdʒ ˌdɛstəˈneɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the place or level of care to which a patient goes after leaving a hospital or facility.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** discharge destination có thể là home, rehab, nursing facility hoặc transfer khác.
**Grammar & collocations:** `discharge-destination pattern` — pattern; `plan a discharge destination` — plan; `post-acute care` — care.
**Examples:** `Discharge destination depended on caregiver availability as well as clinical readiness.` → Ready về clinical chưa đủ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `퇴원 목적지` — discharge destination.

## 12. continuity-of-care gap /ˌkɑntəˈnuəti əv kɛr ɡæp/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a break or mismatch in information, responsibility, or treatment as a person moves between services.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** continuity-of-care gap là chỗ handoff bị đứt, khiến care lặp hoặc bỏ sót.
**Grammar & collocations:** `continuity-of-care gap` — gap; `bridge a care gap` — bridge; `handoff failure` — failure.
**Examples:** `A continuity-of-care gap left the patient without a follow-up appointment after discharge.` → Handoff gap tạo risk.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `진료 연속성 격차` — continuity-of-care gap.

## 13. care fragmentation /kɛr ˌfræɡmənˈteɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** organization of care across disconnected providers, records, or decisions without adequate coordination.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** care fragmentation làm mỗi provider thấy một mảnh, không ai giữ toàn picture.
**Grammar & collocations:** `care-fragmentation measure` — measure; `reduce fragmented care` — reduce; `coordinated record` — record.
**Examples:** `Care fragmentation led to duplicate imaging and conflicting medication instructions.` → Fragmentation vừa tốn vừa nguy hiểm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `의료 분절화` — care fragmentation.

## 14. unmet treatment need /ʌnˈmɛt ˈtriːtmənt niːd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a condition for which a person needs effective treatment but does not receive it at all or receives too little.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** unmet treatment need hẹp hơn general need: cần treatment cụ thể nhưng chưa nhận đủ.
**Grammar & collocations:** `unmet-treatment need` — need; `identify treatment gaps` — identify; `effective therapy` — therapy.
**Examples:** `Unmet treatment need remained high among people who could not afford specialist medicines.` → Cost làm treatment gap.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `미충족 치료 필요` — unmet treatment need.

## 15. provider density /prəˈvaɪdər ˈdɛnsəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the number of health professionals or facilities relative to the population they serve.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** provider density đo supply health worker trên population.
**Grammar & collocations:** `provider-density map` — map; `measure clinician density` — measure; `population-adjusted supply` — supply.
**Examples:** `Provider density was lowest in districts with the longest travel times.` → Supply và distance thường đi cùng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `의료 제공자 밀도` — provider density.

## 16. travel-time access /ˈtrævəl taɪm ˈæksɛs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** access measured by the time required to reach an appropriate health service from a person's location.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** travel-time access đo journey thật, không chỉ khoảng cách trên map.
**Grammar & collocations:** `travel-time access measure` — measure; `estimate travel time` — estimate; `geographic access` — access.
**Examples:** `Travel-time access worsened during the rainy season when roads became unreliable.` → Season làm access đổi.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `이동 시간 접근성` — travel-time access.

## 17. health-spending shock /hɛlθ ˈspɛndɪŋ ʃɑk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a sudden health-related expense that substantially disrupts a household's finances or ability to meet basic needs.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** health-spending shock là cost của illness vượt khả năng budget và đẩy household vào hardship.
**Grammar & collocations:** `health-spending shock` — shock; `absorb medical costs` — absorb; `financial hardship` — hardship.
**Examples:** `A health-spending shock caused the family to delay follow-up visits and medication refills.` → Cost làm care cascade đứt.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `의료비 충격` — health-spending shock.

## 18. service substitution /ˈsɜrvɪs ˌsʌbstəˈtuːʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** replacing one type, provider, or location of care with another because of access, cost, preference, or system change.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** service substitution là patient đổi hospital → clinic, in-person → telehealth hoặc formal → informal.
**Grammar & collocations:** `service-substitution pattern` — pattern; `substitute one service for another` — substitute; `alternative provider` — provider.
**Examples:** `Service substitution increased when telehealth became available for routine follow-up.` → Substitute có thể giữ continuity.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `서비스 대체` — service substitution.

## 19. telehealth uptake /ˈtɛləˌhɛlθ ˈʌpˌteɪk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the extent to which eligible people begin using remote clinical or health services.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** telehealth uptake đo ai thật sự chuyển sang remote care.
**Grammar & collocations:** `telehealth-uptake rate` — rate; `adopt telehealth` — adopt; `remote consultation` — consultation.
**Examples:** `Telehealth uptake was lower among patients without private internet access.` → Digital access quyết định uptake.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `원격의료 수용` — telehealth uptake.

## 20. utilization inequity /ˌjuːtələˈzeɪʃən ɪnˈɛkwəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an unfair difference in the use, timeliness, quality, or outcomes of health services across groups.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** utilization inequity là gap trong service use và benefit, không chỉ availability.
**Grammar & collocations:** `utilization-inequity analysis` — analysis; `measure unequal utilization` — measure; `care-use gap` — gap.
**Examples:** `Utilization inequity persisted after insurance coverage expanded because transportation remained limited.` → Coverage không xóa mọi barrier.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `의료 이용 불평등` — utilization inequity.

## 21. health service denominator /hɛlθ ˈsɜrvɪs dɪˈnɑməˌneɪtər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the defined population eligible, exposed, or expected to use a health service when calculating a service rate.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** health service denominator quyết định rate đang chia trên resident, enrolled patient hay eligible group.
**Grammar & collocations:** `health-service denominator` — denominator; `define the eligible population` — define; `service-rate estimate` — estimate.
**Examples:** `The health service denominator changed when the program moved from residents to registered patients.` → Base đổi làm rate đổi.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `보건 서비스 분모` — health service denominator.

## 22. service care cascade /ˈsɜrvɪs kɛr kæˈskeɪd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the sequence from identifying a need through contact, diagnosis, treatment, follow-up, and outcome in a health system.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** service care cascade là chain cần đi qua từng step, rơi ở đâu cũng tạo gap.
**Grammar & collocations:** `service-care cascade` — cascade; `map the care cascade` — map; `cascade completion` — completion.
**Examples:** `The service care cascade showed a large drop between diagnosis and treatment initiation.` → Mỗi step cần measure riêng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `서비스 진료 연쇄` — service care cascade.

## 23. treatment cascade /ˈtriːtmənt kæˈskeɪd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** sequential stages from diagnosis to treatment initiation, adherence, control, or cure.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** treatment cascade tập trung vào stage sau diagnosis.
**Grammar & collocations:** `treatment-cascade measure` — measure; `complete the treatment cascade` — complete; `care-stage drop-off` — drop-off.
**Examples:** `The treatment cascade revealed that many diagnosed patients never started therapy.` → Diagnosis chưa đồng nghĩa treatment.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `치료 연쇄` — treatment cascade.

## 24. retention in care /rɪˈtɛnʃən ɪn kɛr/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** continued participation in a health service over the period needed for monitoring or treatment.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** retention in care đo patient có ở lại system đủ lâu không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `retention-in-care rate` — rate; `retain patients in care` — retain; `longitudinal follow-up` — follow-up.
**Examples:** `Retention in care improved after flexible appointments and reminder outreach were introduced.` → Flexibility giữ patient trong cascade.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `진료 유지` — retention in care.

## 25. linkage-to-care /ˈlɪŋkɪdʒ tə kɛr/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the process of connecting a person with a diagnosis, result, or identified need to an appropriate treatment service.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** linkage-to-care là bridge từ result/diagnosis sang provider và treatment thật.
**Grammar & collocations:** `linkage-to-care program` — program; `link a patient to care` — link; `care-navigation support` — support.
**Examples:** `Linkage-to-care support connected people with positive screens to same-week treatment.` → Link nhanh giảm drop-off.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `치료 연계` — linkage-to-care.

## Review in context — Part 1

**Care-seeking behavior** shapes a **service unmet-need profile** and the **service contact rate**. **Referral completion** and the **no-show rate** affect **emergency department boarding** and **avoidable admission**. An **ambulatory care-sensitive condition** can reveal weak primary care, while **post-discharge readmission risk**, **length-of-stay distribution**, and **discharge destination** describe what happens later.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 1:**

**Care-seeking behavior** định hình **service unmet-need profile** và **service contact rate**. **Referral completion** và **no-show rate** ảnh hưởng **emergency department boarding** và **avoidable admission**. **Ambulatory care-sensitive condition** có thể cho thấy primary care yếu, còn **post-discharge readmission risk**, **length-of-stay distribution** và **discharge destination** mô tả các bước sau.

## Review in context — Part 2

A **continuity-of-care gap** and **care fragmentation** can produce **unmet treatment need**. **Provider density**, **travel-time access**, and a **health-spending shock** influence **service substitution** and **telehealth uptake**. **Utilization inequity** must be calculated with a clear **health service denominator**. The **service care cascade**, **treatment cascade**, **retention in care**, and **linkage-to-care** show where people enter, remain, or fall out of the system.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 2:**

**Continuity-of-care gap** và **care fragmentation** có thể tạo **unmet treatment need**. **Provider density**, **travel-time access** và **health-spending shock** ảnh hưởng **service substitution** và **telehealth uptake**. **Utilization inequity** cần được tính với **health service denominator** rõ ràng. **Service care cascade**, **treatment cascade**, **retention in care** và **linkage-to-care** cho thấy người dân vào, ở lại hay rơi khỏi system ở đâu.
