# C2+ Vocabulary — Nutrition epidemiology and dietary exposure

This lesson follows how researchers measure diet, food access, nutrient status, and population nutrition outcomes. The network is **dietary recall bias → dietary frequency instrument → nutrient density → dietary pattern score → ultra-processed food exposure → nutritional epidemiology → energy misreporting → portion-size estimation → biomarker calibration → usual intake distribution → nutrient adequacy → food insecurity screening → household food access → diet-related risk → population attributable fraction → salt reduction target → fortification coverage → micronutrient shortfall → stunting prevalence → wasting prevalence → obesity surveillance → body-mass index trajectory → school meal exposure → nutrition transition → dietary substitution**.

## 1. dietary recall bias /ˈdaɪəˌtɛri rɪˈkɔl baɪəs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** systematic error caused by inaccurate memory of foods, drinks, amounts, or timing in a dietary report.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** dietary recall bias làm reported diet khác actual diet vì memory và social desirability.
**Grammar & collocations:** `dietary-recall bias` — bias; `underreport food intake` — underreport; `memory error` — error.
**Examples:** `Dietary recall bias was greater when participants reported meals from several months earlier.` → Nhớ xa làm data kém hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `식이 회상 편향` — dietary recall bias.

## 2. dietary frequency instrument /ˈdaɪəˌtɛri ˈfriːkwənsi ˈɪnstrəmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a questionnaire or tool that estimates how often people consume specified foods or food groups over a reference period.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** dietary frequency instrument hỏi “ăn món này bao nhiêu lần” thay vì ghi từng meal.
**Grammar & collocations:** `dietary-frequency instrument` — instrument; `estimate usual frequency` — estimate; `food-frequency item` — item.
**Examples:** `The dietary frequency instrument captured seasonal fruit intake better than a single-day diary.` → Tool khác nhau nhìn exposure khác.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `식이 빈도 도구` — dietary frequency instrument.

## 3. nutrient density /ˈnuːtriənt ˈdɛnsəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the amount of vitamins, minerals, protein, or other useful nutrients relative to the energy or quantity in a food.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** nutrient density hỏi một calorie hoặc một portion mang theo bao nhiêu nutrient hữu ích.
**Grammar & collocations:** `nutrient-density score` — score; `choose nutrient-dense foods` — choose; `nutrient-to-energy ratio` — ratio.
**Examples:** `The program replaced sugary snacks with foods of higher nutrient density.` → Cùng energy nhưng chất lượng khác.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `영양 밀도` — nutrient density.

## 4. dietary pattern score /ˈdaɪəˌtɛri ˈpætərn skɔr/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a composite score representing how closely a person's overall food choices match a defined dietary pattern.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** dietary pattern score nhìn cả pattern meals thay vì một nutrient đơn lẻ.
**Grammar & collocations:** `dietary-pattern score` — score; `score adherence to a diet` — score; `whole-diet measure` — measure.
**Examples:** `The dietary pattern score rose after the intervention increased legumes, vegetables, and whole grains.` → Pattern cho picture tổng thể.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `식이 패턴 점수` — dietary pattern score.

## 5. ultra-processed food exposure /ˌʌltrə ˈprɑsɛst fuːd ɪkˈspoʊʒər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** exposure to industrially formulated foods made with multiple refined ingredients, additives, and processing steps.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** ultra-processed food exposure đo mức diet có nhiều packaged formulation và additive.
**Grammar & collocations:** `ultra-processed-food exposure` — exposure; `classify processed foods` — classify; `food-processing category` — category.
**Examples:** `Ultra-processed food exposure was higher where fresh food outlets were scarce.` → Food environment ảnh hưởng exposure.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `초가공식품 노출` — ultra-processed food exposure.

## 6. nutritional epidemiology /nuˈtrɪʃənəl ˌɛpədəmiˈɑlədʒi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the study of relationships among diet, nutrient status, disease, and health outcomes in populations.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** nutritional epidemiology nối food exposure với disease và population pattern.
**Grammar & collocations:** `nutritional-epidemiology study` — study; `study diet and disease` — study; `population nutrition` — nutrition.
**Examples:** `Nutritional epidemiology combined dietary reports with biomarkers and long-term health records.` → Nhiều data giảm dependence vào một method.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `영양 역학` — nutritional epidemiology.

## 7. energy misreporting /ˈɛnərdʒi ˌmɪsrɪˈpɔrtɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** systematic overreporting or underreporting of food energy intake in a dietary assessment.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** energy misreporting làm calorie reported không khớp requirement hoặc biomarker.
**Grammar & collocations:** `energy-misreporting adjustment` — adjustment; `underreport energy intake` — underreport; `plausible intake` — intake.
**Examples:** `Energy misreporting was checked by comparing reported intake with body-size and activity data.` → Cross-check giúp detect implausible report.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `에너지 오보고` — energy misreporting.

## 8. portion-size estimation /ˈpɔrʃən saɪz ˌɛstəˈmeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the process of estimating how much food or drink was consumed in a reported serving.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** portion-size estimation biến “một bát/một phần” thành amount có thể phân tích.
**Grammar & collocations:** `portion-size estimation` — estimation; `estimate serving size` — estimate; `food photograph aid` — aid.
**Examples:** `Portion-size estimation improved when participants used photographs of familiar bowls.` → Visual aid giảm memory gap.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `섭취량 추정` — portion-size estimation.

## 9. biomarker calibration /ˈbaɪoʊˌmɑrkər ˌkælɪˈbreɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** adjustment or validation linking a biological measurement to the exposure or nutrient level it represents.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** biomarker calibration nối lab value với dietary exposure thật.
**Grammar & collocations:** `biomarker-calibration model` — model; `calibrate a nutrient biomarker` — calibrate; `biological measure` — measure.
**Examples:** `Biomarker calibration corrected the dietary estimate for differences in metabolism.` → Same intake không luôn same biomarker.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `바이오마커 보정` — biomarker calibration.

## 10. usual intake distribution /ˈjuːʒuəl ˈɪnˌteɪk ˌdɪstrəˈbjuːʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the distribution of an individual's long-term typical intake rather than a single day's intake.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** usual intake distribution nhìn diet “thường ngày” qua nhiều day và variation.
**Grammar & collocations:** `usual-intake distribution` — distribution; `estimate usual intake` — estimate; `within-person variation` — variation.
**Examples:** `The usual intake distribution was modeled from repeated recalls rather than one diary.` → Repeated measure giúp tách daily noise.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `평상 섭취량 분포` — usual intake distribution.

## 11. nutrient adequacy /ˈnuːtriənt ˈædəkwəsi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the extent to which intake meets the physiological requirement for a nutrient.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** nutrient adequacy hỏi intake có đủ theo requirement và life stage không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `nutrient-adequacy estimate` — estimate; `meet nutrient requirements` — meet; `adequacy ratio` — ratio.
**Examples:** `Nutrient adequacy was low for iron among adolescents with limited dietary variety.` → Variety và life stage cùng quan trọng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `영양소 적정성` — nutrient adequacy.

## 12. food insecurity screening /fuːd ˌɪnsəˈkjʊrəti ˈskriːnɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** systematic identification of households that lack reliable access to enough affordable and nutritious food.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** food insecurity screening tìm household có food access không ổn định trước khi outcome xấu hơn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `food-insecurity screening` — screening; `screen for food insecurity` — screen; `household food hardship` — hardship.
**Examples:** `Food insecurity screening connected families with vouchers and community food programs.` → Screening phải nối support.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `식량 불안 선별` — food insecurity screening.

## 13. household food access /ˈhaʊsˌhoʊld fuːd ˈæksɛs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the practical ability of a household to obtain sufficient, affordable, safe, and culturally acceptable food.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** household food access rộng hơn “có cửa hàng”, gồm cost, distance, transport và acceptability.
**Grammar & collocations:** `household-food access measure` — measure; `improve food access` — improve; `affordable food` — food.
**Examples:** `Household food access improved when a mobile market visited the neighborhood weekly.` → Distribution đổi access.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `가구 식품 접근성` — household food access.

## 14. diet-related risk /ˈdaɪət rɪˈleɪtɪd rɪsk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** increased probability of disease or poor health associated with a dietary exposure or pattern.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** diet-related risk nối food pattern với disease probability, không quy blame cho individual.
**Grammar & collocations:** `diet-related risk factor` — factor; `estimate dietary risk` — estimate; `modifiable exposure` — exposure.
**Examples:** `Diet-related risk reflected sodium, fiber, food access, and the broader eating pattern.` → Risk thường là network factor.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `식이 관련 위험` — diet-related risk.

## 15. population attributable fraction /ˌpɑpjəˈleɪʃən əˈtrɪbjətəbəl ˈfrækʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the proportion of population cases that could theoretically be prevented if an exposure were eliminated under stated assumptions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** population attributable fraction nhấn mạnh prevention potential ở cấp population.
**Grammar & collocations:** `population-attributable fraction` — fraction; `estimate preventable cases` — estimate; `population exposure` — exposure.
**Examples:** `The population attributable fraction was larger for modest widespread exposure than for a rare extreme exposure.` → Population share matters.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `인구 기여 분율` — population attributable fraction.

## 16. salt reduction target /sɔlt rɪˈdʌkʃən ˈtɑrɡət/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a defined population or product goal for lowering sodium or salt intake.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** salt reduction target biến goal “ăn mặn ít hơn” thành mức đo cụ thể.
**Grammar & collocations:** `salt-reduction target` — target; `reduce sodium exposure` — reduce; `reformulate food` — reformulate.
**Examples:** `The salt reduction target was met after manufacturers reformulated staple foods.` → Product change có reach rộng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `염분 감소 목표` — salt reduction target.

## 17. fortification coverage /ˌfɔrtəfəˈkeɪʃən ˈkʌvərɪdʒ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the proportion of a target population receiving food or supplements fortified with a needed nutrient.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** fortification coverage đo policy có đến được đúng population không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `fortification-coverage estimate` — estimate; `reach fortified foods` — reach; `micronutrient delivery` — delivery.
**Examples:** `Fortification coverage rose when rural retailers received reliable supplies.` → Supply chain quyết định coverage.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `강화식품 보급률` — fortification coverage.

## 18. micronutrient shortfall /ˌmaɪkroʊˈnuːtriənt ˈʃɔrtˌfɔl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** inadequate intake or status of a vitamin or mineral relative to physiological need.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** micronutrient shortfall là thiếu vitamin/mineral dù calorie tổng thể có thể đủ.
**Grammar & collocations:** `micronutrient-shortfall estimate` — estimate; `identify a nutrient shortfall` — identify; `hidden hunger` — hunger.
**Examples:** `Micronutrient shortfall persisted among children whose diets supplied enough calories but little variety.` → Calorie đủ không có nghĩa nutrient đủ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `미량영양소 부족` — micronutrient shortfall.

## 19. stunting prevalence /ˈstʌntɪŋ ˈprɛvələns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the proportion of children whose height-for-age indicates chronic growth restriction.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** stunting prevalence phản ánh chronic nutrition và infection burden trong early life.
**Grammar & collocations:** `stunting-prevalence estimate` — estimate; `measure height-for-age` — measure; `chronic growth restriction` — restriction.
**Examples:** `Stunting prevalence declined after sanitation, maternal nutrition, and infant feeding improved together.` → Outcome cần nhiều system.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `발육부진 유병률` — stunting prevalence.

## 20. wasting prevalence /ˈweɪstɪŋ ˈprɛvələns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the proportion of children with low weight-for-height indicating acute undernutrition or recent illness.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** wasting prevalence phản ánh acute nutrition loss nhanh hơn stunting.
**Grammar & collocations:** `wasting-prevalence estimate` — estimate; `measure weight-for-height` — measure; `acute undernutrition` — undernutrition.
**Examples:** `Wasting prevalence rose after crop failure and repeated diarrheal illness.` → Acute shock có thể đổi nhanh.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `소모성 영양실조 유병률` — wasting prevalence.

## 21. obesity surveillance /oʊˈbiːsəti sərˈveɪləns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** systematic monitoring of body size, weight-related risk, environments, and trends in obesity across populations.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** obesity surveillance theo dõi trend population và environment, không chỉ individual blame.
**Grammar & collocations:** `obesity-surveillance system` — system; `monitor weight trends` — monitor; `population obesity` — obesity.
**Examples:** `Obesity surveillance combined school measurements with food-price and neighborhood data.` → Context giúp hiểu trend.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `비만 감시` — obesity surveillance.

## 22. body-mass index trajectory /ˈbɑdi ˈmæs ˈɪndɛks trəˈdʒɛktəri/

**Core meaning (English):** the pattern of change in body-mass index across age or time rather than a single measurement.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** body-mass index trajectory nhìn direction và speed của change.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `체질량지수 궤적` — body-mass index trajectory.

## 23. school meal exposure /skuːl miːl ɪkˈspoʊʒər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** exposure to meals, snacks, or food standards provided through a school program.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** school meal exposure là dietary environment lặp lại mỗi school day.
**Grammar & collocations:** `school-meal exposure` — exposure; `evaluate a meal program` — evaluate; `school food standard` — standard.

## 24. nutrition transition /nuˈtrɪʃən trænˈzɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** population change from traditional food patterns toward diets shaped by urbanization, processed foods, and changing incomes.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** nutrition transition là society đổi food system và disease pattern cùng lúc.
**Grammar & collocations:** `nutrition-transition pathway` — pathway; `undergo a nutrition transition` — undergo; `urban food environment` — environment.

## 25. dietary substitution /ˈdaɪəˌtɛri ˌsʌbstəˈtuːʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** replacing one food, nutrient, or source of energy with another in a diet.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** dietary substitution hỏi “bỏ món này thì ăn gì thay?” chứ không chỉ giảm một nutrient.
**Grammar & collocations:** `dietary-substitution model` — model; `substitute one food for another` — substitute; `replacement nutrient` — nutrient.
**Examples:** `Dietary substitution modeled replacing refined carbohydrates with legumes rather than simply reducing calories.` → Replacement quyết định effect.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `식이 대체` — dietary substitution.

## Review in context — Part 1

**Dietary recall bias** can affect a **dietary frequency instrument** and distort estimates of **nutrient density** or a **dietary pattern score**. **Ultra-processed food exposure** is studied in **nutritional epidemiology** alongside **energy misreporting** and **portion-size estimation**. **Biomarker calibration** and a **usual intake distribution** improve estimates of **nutrient adequacy**.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 1:**

**Dietary recall bias** có thể ảnh hưởng **dietary frequency instrument** và làm lệch ước tính **nutrient density** hoặc **dietary pattern score**. **Ultra-processed food exposure** được nghiên cứu trong **nutritional epidemiology** cùng với **energy misreporting** và **portion-size estimation**. **Biomarker calibration** và **usual intake distribution** cải thiện ước tính **nutrient adequacy**.

## Review in context — Part 2

**Food insecurity screening** identifies problems with **household food access** and **diet-related risk**. A **population attributable fraction**, **salt reduction target**, and **fortification coverage** guide prevention, while a **micronutrient shortfall** may persist. **Stunting prevalence**, **wasting prevalence**, and **obesity surveillance** track different outcomes. A **body-mass index trajectory**, **school meal exposure**, **nutrition transition**, and **dietary substitution** explain how food environments change health.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 2:**

**Food insecurity screening** tìm vấn đề trong **household food access** và **diet-related risk**. **Population attributable fraction**, **salt reduction target** và **fortification coverage** định hướng prevention, nhưng **micronutrient shortfall** vẫn có thể tồn tại. **Stunting prevalence**, **wasting prevalence** và **obesity surveillance** theo dõi các outcome khác nhau. **Body-mass index trajectory**, **school meal exposure**, **nutrition transition** và **dietary substitution** giải thích food environment đổi health thế nào.
