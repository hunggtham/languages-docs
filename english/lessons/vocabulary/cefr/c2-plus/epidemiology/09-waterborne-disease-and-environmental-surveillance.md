# C2+ Vocabulary — Waterborne disease and environmental surveillance

This lesson follows how contamination moves through water systems, how investigators identify its source, and how communities reduce exposure. The network is **fecal-oral transmission → indicator organism → microbial source tracking → turbidity event → residual chlorine → waterborne outbreak → recreational water exposure → shellfish depuration → sewage overflow → storm-sewer overflow → water safety plan → catchment protection → watershed surveillance → enteric pathogen → environmental persistence → pathogen dose-response relationship → infectious dose → secondary contamination → household water treatment → point-of-use chlorination → sanitary survey → outbreak source attribution → waterborne disease burden → risk-based monitoring → boil-water advisory**.

## 1. fecal-oral transmission /ˈfiːkəl ˈɔrəl trænzˈmɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** transmission that occurs when organisms from fecal contamination reach another person's mouth through water, food, hands, or surfaces.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** fecal-oral transmission nối contamination với ingestion qua nhiều route.
**Grammar & collocations:** `fecal-oral transmission route` — route; `interrupt transmission` — interrupt; `contaminated water` — water.
**Examples:** `Handwashing and safe water interrupted the fecal-oral transmission route in the shelter.` → Hygiene chặn route cơ bản.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `분변-경구 전파` — fecal-oral transmission.

## 2. indicator organism /ˈɪndəˌkeɪtər ˈɔrɡəˌnɪzəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an organism measured as evidence of contamination or treatment performance rather than as the disease-causing agent itself.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** indicator organism là “dấu hiệu đại diện” cho contamination hoặc treatment failure.
**Grammar & collocations:** `indicator-organism count` — count; `measure fecal indicators` — measure; `water-quality marker` — marker.
**Examples:** `The indicator organism count rose after the treatment plant lost pressure.` → Indicator báo problem trước khi biết pathogen cụ thể.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `지표 미생물` — indicator organism.

## 3. microbial source tracking /maɪˈkroʊbiəl sɔrs ˈtrækɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** methods that identify the likely human, animal, or environmental source of microbial contamination.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** microbial source tracking tìm contamination đến từ human, livestock hay wildlife.
**Grammar & collocations:** `microbial-source tracking assay` — assay; `track a contamination source` — track; `source marker` — marker.
**Examples:** `Microbial source tracking linked the creek contamination to a leaking septic system.` → Source rõ giúp chọn intervention.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `미생물 오염원 추적` — microbial source tracking.

## 4. turbidity event /tɜrˈbɪdəti ɪˈvɛnt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a period in which suspended particles make water unusually cloudy and may interfere with treatment or signal contamination.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** turbidity event là lúc nước đục lên vì sediment, runoff hoặc system disturbance.
**Grammar & collocations:** `turbidity-event response` — response; `monitor turbidity` — monitor; `cloudy source water` — water.
**Examples:** `The turbidity event followed a storm and prompted additional filtration checks.` → Cloudiness có thể làm treatment khó hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `탁도 사건` — turbidity event.

## 5. residual chlorine /rɪˈzɪdʒuəl ˈklɔrˌiːn/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** chlorine remaining in treated water after disinfection, providing continuing protection during distribution.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** residual chlorine là “lớp bảo vệ còn lại” khi nước đi qua pipes.
**Grammar & collocations:** `residual-chlorine level` — level; `maintain chlorine residual` — maintain; `distribution protection` — protection.
**Examples:** `Residual chlorine fell near the end of the network after a long pressure interruption.` → Distribution cũng ảnh hưởng safety.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `잔류 염소` — residual chlorine.

## 6. waterborne outbreak /ˈwɔtərˌbɔrn ˈaʊtˌbreɪk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an outbreak in which contaminated water is a plausible vehicle for transmitting an infectious agent.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** waterborne outbreak là outbreak có water như vehicle chung.
**Grammar & collocations:** `waterborne-outbreak investigation` — investigation; `investigate a waterborne outbreak` — investigate; `shared water source` — source.
**Examples:** `The waterborne outbreak affected several neighborhoods supplied by the same reservoir.` → Shared source gợi ý common vehicle.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `수인성 질병 유행` — waterborne outbreak.

## 7. recreational water exposure /ˌrɛkriˈeɪʃənəl ˈwɔtər ɪkˈspoʊʒər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** contact with water used for swimming, play, boating, or other leisure activities.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** recreational water exposure gồm pool, lake, river hoặc splash pad.
**Grammar & collocations:** `recreational-water exposure` — exposure; `swim in treated water` — swim; `water-contact activity` — activity.
**Examples:** `Interviews linked several cases to recreational water exposure at the same lake.` → Leisure activity cũng là exposure route.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `레크리에이션 수역 노출` — recreational water exposure.

## 8. shellfish depuration /ˈʃɛlfɪʃ ˌdiːpjəˈreɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a controlled process in which living shellfish filter clean water to reduce accumulated contaminants.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** shellfish depuration cho shellfish “tự lọc” trong water sạch trước khi bán.
**Grammar & collocations:** `shellfish-depuration facility` — facility; `depurate harvested shellfish` — depurate; `clean-water holding` — holding.
**Examples:** `Shellfish depuration reduced bacterial contamination before the harvest entered the market.` → Depuration giảm nhưng không thay thế mọi control.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `패류 정화` — shellfish depuration.

## 9. sewage overflow /ˈsuːɪdʒ ˈoʊvərˌfloʊ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** release of untreated or partly treated sewage from a drainage or wastewater system into the environment.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** sewage overflow đưa wastewater ra ngoài system và tạo exposure rộng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `sewage-overflow alert` — alert; `report a sewage overflow` — report; `untreated discharge` — discharge.
**Examples:** `The sewage overflow closed the downstream beach until testing and cleanup were complete.` → Public notice cần đi trước exposure.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `하수 범람` — sewage overflow.

## 10. storm-sewer overflow /stɔrm ˈsuːər ˈoʊvərˌfloʊ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** discharge from a storm-drain system when heavy rainfall exceeds its capacity and carries contaminated runoff.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** storm-sewer overflow nối heavy rain với runoff và contamination downstream.
**Grammar & collocations:** `storm-sewer overflow` — overflow; `manage storm runoff` — manage; `overflow discharge` — discharge.
**Examples:** `A storm-sewer overflow carried animal waste into the recreational lake.` → Rain biến land contamination thành water exposure.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `우수 하수관 범람` — storm-sewer overflow.

## 11. water safety plan /ˈwɔtər ˈseɪfti plæn/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a preventive plan that identifies hazards from source to consumer and specifies controls, monitoring, and corrective actions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** water safety plan nhìn toàn chain từ catchment đến tap.
**Grammar & collocations:** `water-safety plan` — plan; `identify water hazards` — identify; `corrective action` — action.
**Examples:** `The water safety plan assigned monitoring points from the intake to household taps.` → Safety là chain, không chỉ plant.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `물 안전 계획` — water safety plan.

## 12. catchment protection /ˈkætʃmənt prəˈtɛkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** measures that protect the land and water area feeding a drinking-water source from contamination.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** catchment protection ngăn contamination ngay trước khi vào treatment system.
**Grammar & collocations:** `catchment-protection zone` — zone; `protect a drinking-water catchment` — protect; `land-use control` — control.
**Examples:** `Catchment protection limited livestock access near the reservoir inlet.` → Source protection giảm burden downstream.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `집수지 보호` — catchment protection.

## 13. watershed surveillance /ˈwɔtərˌʃɛd sərˈveɪləns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** systematic monitoring of water, land, organisms, and activities across a watershed for health threats.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** watershed surveillance theo dõi cả vùng nước chảy về cùng một system.
**Grammar & collocations:** `watershed-surveillance network` — network; `monitor a watershed` — monitor; `upstream signal` — signal.
**Examples:** `Watershed surveillance detected upstream contamination before it reached the treatment intake.` → Upstream signal tạo time để act.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `유역 감시` — watershed surveillance.

## 14. enteric pathogen /ˈɛntərɪk ˈpæθədʒən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a pathogen that infects or passes through the intestinal system and can spread through fecal contamination.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** enteric pathogen gắn với gut và fecal-oral route.
**Grammar & collocations:** `enteric-pathogen surveillance` — surveillance; `detect an enteric pathogen` — detect; `intestinal infection` — infection.
**Examples:** `The laboratory tested the water for an enteric pathogen after the gastroenteritis cluster.` → Symptom cluster gợi ý testing.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `장관 병원체` — enteric pathogen.

## 15. environmental persistence /ɪnˌvaɪrənˈmɛntəl pərˈsɪstəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the ability of an organism or contaminant to remain viable or detectable in the environment over time.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** environmental persistence cho biết pathogen ở water, soil hoặc surface bao lâu.
**Grammar & collocations:** `environmental-persistence study` — study; `persist in water` — persist; `survival time` — time.
**Examples:** `Environmental persistence was longer in cool, shaded water than in direct sunlight.` → Conditions đổi survival.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `환경 내 지속성` — environmental persistence.

## 16. pathogen dose-response relationship /ˈpæθədʒən doʊs rɪˈspɑns rɪˈleɪʃənˌʃɪp/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the relationship between the amount of pathogen exposure and the probability or severity of infection.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** pathogen dose-response relationship nối amount exposure với risk infection.
**Grammar & collocations:** `pathogen dose-response relationship` — relationship; `estimate infection probability` — estimate; `exposure dose` — dose.
**Examples:** `The pathogen dose-response relationship informed the threshold used for recreational-water warnings.` → Dose cao thường đổi risk.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `병원체 용량-반응 관계` — pathogen dose-response relationship.

## 17. infectious dose /ɪnˈfɛkʃəs doʊs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the amount of an infectious agent needed to produce infection with a specified probability in a host.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** infectious dose là lượng pathogen đủ để infection có khả năng xảy ra.
**Grammar & collocations:** `infectious-dose estimate` — estimate; `exceed the infectious dose` — exceed; `dose threshold` — threshold.
**Examples:** `Repeated low-level exposure may still matter when the cumulative dose approaches the infectious dose.` → Exposure có thể cộng dồn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `감염량` — infectious dose.

## 18. secondary contamination /ˈsɛkənˌdɛri kənˌtæməˈneɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** contamination that occurs after an original source has spread through handling, equipment, surfaces, or another pathway.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** secondary contamination là contamination “lây tiếp” sau source ban đầu.
**Grammar & collocations:** `secondary-contamination route` — route; `prevent cross-contamination` — prevent; `contaminated equipment` — equipment.
**Examples:** `Secondary contamination occurred when clean containers were filled with an unwashed hose.` → Workflow sai tạo route mới.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `2차 오염` — secondary contamination.

## 19. household water treatment /ˈhaʊsˌhoʊld ˈwɔtər ˈtriːtmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** treatment performed at or near the household to make water safer for drinking or cooking.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** household water treatment là lớp cuối khi central supply chưa đủ an toàn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `household-water-treatment method` — method; `treat drinking water` — treat; `safe storage` — storage.
**Examples:** `Household water treatment combined filtration with covered storage during the emergency.` → Treat xong vẫn cần store đúng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `가정용 수처리` — household water treatment.

## 20. point-of-use chlorination /pɔɪnt əv juːs ˌklɔrəˈneɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** adding a measured amount of chlorine where water is consumed or collected rather than only at a central plant.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** point-of-use chlorination xử lý nước ngay nơi household lấy hoặc uống.
**Grammar & collocations:** `point-of-use chlorination` — chlorination; `dose chlorine at the tap` — dose; `free-chlorine residual` — residual.
**Examples:** `Point-of-use chlorination protected households while the distribution network was repaired.` → Lớp tạm thời nhưng thiết thực.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `사용 지점 염소 소독` — point-of-use chlorination.

## 21. sanitary survey /ˈsænəˌtɛri ˈsɜrveɪ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a structured inspection of a water system and its surroundings to identify conditions that could cause contamination.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** sanitary survey đi field để tìm pipe break, animal access, drainage và hygiene gap.
**Grammar & collocations:** `sanitary-survey checklist` — checklist; `conduct a sanitary survey` — conduct; `contamination hazard` — hazard.
**Examples:** `The sanitary survey found an uncovered well beside a flooded drainage ditch.` → Field inspection thấy risk mà lab chưa thấy.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `위생 조사` — sanitary survey.

## 22. outbreak source attribution /ˈaʊtˌbreɪk sɔrs ˌætrəˈbjuːʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the process of estimating which source, exposure, or pathway caused an outbreak.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** outbreak source attribution chọn source có evidence mạnh nhất trong nhiều khả năng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `outbreak-source attribution` — attribution; `attribute cases to a source` — attribute; `source evidence` — evidence.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `유행 원인 귀속` — outbreak source attribution.

## 23. waterborne disease burden /ˈwɔtərˌbɔrn dɪˈziːz ˈbɜrdən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the total health impact caused by diseases transmitted through contaminated water.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** waterborne disease burden gồm cases, complications, deaths, cost và lost work.
**Grammar & collocations:** `waterborne-disease burden` — burden; `estimate disease burden` — estimate; `avoidable illness` — illness.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `수인성 질병 부담` — waterborne disease burden.

## 24. risk-based monitoring /rɪsk beɪst ˈmɑnətərɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** monitoring whose locations, frequency, and tests are chosen according to hazard and consequence rather than evenly everywhere.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** risk-based monitoring dồn sampling vào điểm có hazard hoặc consequence cao.
**Grammar & collocations:** `risk-based-monitoring plan` — plan; `prioritize high-risk sites` — prioritize; `monitoring frequency` — frequency.
**Examples:** `Risk-based monitoring increased sampling after heavy rain in vulnerable catchments.` → Event đổi thì monitoring đổi.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `위험 기반 모니터링` — risk-based monitoring.

## 25. boil-water advisory /ˈbɔɪl ˈwɔtər ədˈvaɪzəri/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a public instruction to boil water before drinking or using it when contamination is suspected or confirmed.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** boil-water advisory là action message tạm thời để giảm ingestion risk.
**Grammar & collocations:** `boil-water advisory` — advisory; `issue a boil-water notice` — issue; `safe drinking instruction` — instruction.
**Examples:** `The boil-water advisory remained until pressure was restored and samples were clear.` → Advisory cần tiêu chí kết thúc.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `수돗물 끓이기 권고` — boil-water advisory.

## Review in context — Part 1

**Fecal-oral transmission** can be tracked with an **indicator organism** and **microbial source tracking**. A **turbidity event** or low **residual chlorine** may precede a **waterborne outbreak**. **Recreational water exposure**, **shellfish depuration**, **sewage overflow**, and **storm-sewer overflow** create different pathways. A **water safety plan** combines **catchment protection** with **watershed surveillance** for an **enteric pathogen**.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 1:**

**Fecal-oral transmission** có thể được theo dõi bằng **indicator organism** và **microbial source tracking**. **Turbidity event** hoặc **residual chlorine** thấp có thể đi trước **waterborne outbreak**. **Recreational water exposure**, **shellfish depuration**, **sewage overflow** và **storm-sewer overflow** tạo các route khác nhau. **Water safety plan** kết hợp **catchment protection** với **watershed surveillance** để theo dõi **enteric pathogen**.

## Review in context — Part 2

**Environmental persistence**, the **pathogen dose-response relationship**, and the **infectious dose** shape risk. **Secondary contamination** can occur after treatment, so **household water treatment** and **point-of-use chlorination** may be needed. A **sanitary survey** supports **outbreak source attribution**. Together, these data estimate the **waterborne disease burden**, guide **risk-based monitoring**, and determine when a **boil-water advisory** is necessary.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 2:**

**Environmental persistence**, **pathogen dose-response relationship** và **infectious dose** định hình risk. **Secondary contamination** có thể xảy ra sau treatment, nên cần **household water treatment** và **point-of-use chlorination**. **Sanitary survey** hỗ trợ **outbreak source attribution**. Những data này cùng nhau ước tính **waterborne disease burden**, hướng dẫn **risk-based monitoring** và quyết định khi nào cần **boil-water advisory**.
