# C2+ Vocabulary — Environmental and air-pollution epidemiology

This lesson follows how environmental exposures are measured, assigned, and connected with population health outcomes. The network is **particulate matter → ultrafine particle → exposure assignment error → personal exposure monitor → indoor-outdoor ratio → pollution source apportionment → concentration-response function → acute exposure window → chronic exposure burden → attributable mortality → urban heat-island signal → ozone episode → wildfire smoke plume → ventilation rate → infiltration factor → air-quality index → low-cost sensor calibration → satellite-derived exposure → environmental justice screening → traffic-related pollution → noise exposure → light-at-night exposure → exposure biomonitoring → mixed-exposure model → cumulative environmental burden**.

## 1. particulate matter /pərˈtɪkjələt ˈmætər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a mixture of suspended solid and liquid particles in air, classified partly by aerodynamic size.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** particulate matter là hạt lơ lửng trong air, size quyết định đi sâu đến đâu trong lung.
**Grammar & collocations:** `particulate-matter concentration` — concentration; `measure airborne particles` — measure; `fine-particle exposure` — exposure.
**Examples:** `Particulate matter rose after the inversion trapped emissions near the ground.` → Weather có thể giữ pollution lại.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `미세먼지` — particulate matter.

## 2. ultrafine particle /ˈʌltrəˌfaɪn ˈpɑrtɪkəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an extremely small airborne particle with high surface area and the potential to reach deep into the respiratory system.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** ultrafine particle rất nhỏ, thường gắn với combustion và có thể đi sâu vào lung.
**Grammar & collocations:** `ultrafine-particle count` — count; `measure particle number` — measure; `combustion source` — source.
**Examples:** `Ultrafine particle counts were highest near the busy freight corridor.` → Traffic tạo particle number cao.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `초미세입자` — ultrafine particle.

## 3. exposure assignment error /ɪkˈspoʊʒər əˈsaɪnmənt ˈɛrər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** error caused by assigning an exposure estimate that differs from what a person actually experienced.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** exposure assignment error xảy ra khi area average bị dùng thay cho personal exposure thật.
**Grammar & collocations:** `exposure-assignment error` — error; `assign exposure inaccurately` — assign; `measurement error` — error.
**Examples:** `Exposure assignment error weakened the association when workers moved between buildings.` → Mobility làm average kém chính xác.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `노출 할당 오류` — exposure assignment error.

## 4. personal exposure monitor /ˈpɜrsənəl ɪkˈspoʊʒər ˈmɑnətər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a wearable or portable device that measures a person's exposure to an environmental hazard over time.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** personal exposure monitor đi theo người để đo exposure theo route và timing thật.
**Grammar & collocations:** `personal-exposure monitor` — monitor; `wear a monitor` — wear; `time-resolved exposure` — exposure.
**Examples:** `The personal exposure monitor captured spikes during the commute that the neighborhood station missed.` → Personal data thấy peak riêng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `개인 노출 모니터` — personal exposure monitor.

## 5. indoor-outdoor ratio /ˈɪnˌdɔr ˈaʊtˌdɔr ˈreɪʃioʊ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the ratio between pollutant concentration indoors and the concentration measured outdoors.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** indoor-outdoor ratio cho biết building giảm hay giữ pollution so với ngoài trời.
**Grammar & collocations:** `indoor-outdoor ratio` — ratio; `compare indoor and outdoor concentrations` — compare; `building infiltration` — infiltration.
**Examples:** `The indoor-outdoor ratio fell after filtration and window-sealing improvements.` → Building intervention giảm exposure.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `실내-실외 비율` — indoor-outdoor ratio.

## 6. pollution source apportionment /pəˈluːʃən sɔrs əˌpɔrʃənmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** estimation of how much of observed pollution comes from different sources or source categories.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** pollution source apportionment chia mixture thành traffic, industry, dust, fire hoặc source khác.
**Grammar & collocations:** `pollution-source apportionment` — apportionment; `attribute pollution to a source` — attribute; `source profile` — profile.
**Examples:** `Pollution source apportionment attributed the winter peak to residential heating and traffic.` → Source rõ giúp policy target đúng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `오염원 기여도 분석` — pollution source apportionment.

## 7. concentration-response function /ˌkɑnsənˈtreɪʃən rɪˈspɑns ˈfʌŋkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a function describing how a health outcome changes as environmental concentration changes.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** concentration-response function nối dose ngoài environment với change trong health outcome.
**Grammar & collocations:** `concentration-response function` — function; `estimate the response curve` — estimate; `exposure–outcome relationship` — relationship.
**Examples:** `The concentration-response function predicted higher admissions even below the regulatory threshold.` → Threshold pháp lý không luôn là zero risk.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `농도-반응 함수` — concentration-response function.

## 8. acute exposure window /əˈkjuːt ɪkˈspoʊʒər ˈwɪndoʊ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a short time period in which exposure is evaluated for immediate or near-term effects.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** acute exposure window tập trung vài giờ/ngày trước outcome.
**Grammar & collocations:** `acute-exposure window` — window; `define a lag period` — define; `short-term exposure` — exposure.
**Examples:** `The acute exposure window covered the two days before the asthma visit.` → Timing của exposure cần được chọn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `급성 노출 기간` — acute exposure window.

## 9. chronic exposure burden /ˈkrɑnɪk ɪkˈspoʊʒər ˈbɜrdən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the accumulated health-relevant exposure experienced repeatedly or continuously over a long period.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** chronic exposure burden là tổng burden qua tháng/năm, không phải một spike.
**Grammar & collocations:** `chronic-exposure burden` — burden; `accumulate long-term exposure` — accumulate; `longitudinal exposure` — exposure.
**Examples:** `Chronic exposure burden was higher near the industrial corridor even when daily peaks varied.` → Long-term average vẫn quan trọng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `만성 노출 부담` — chronic exposure burden.

## 10. attributable mortality /əˈtrɪbjətəbəl mɔrˈtæləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** deaths estimated to be caused or contributed to by a specified environmental exposure under stated assumptions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** attributable mortality chuyển association thành ước tính death burden do exposure.
**Grammar & collocations:** `attributable-mortality estimate` — estimate; `attribute deaths to pollution` — attribute; `avoidable mortality` — mortality.
**Examples:** `Attributable mortality was estimated under several plausible concentration-response assumptions.` → Estimate phụ thuộc model.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `기여 사망률` — attributable mortality.

## 11. urban heat-island signal /ˈɜrbən hiːt ˈaɪlənd ˈsɪɡnəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** evidence that built-up areas are warmer than surrounding areas because of surfaces, buildings, and limited vegetation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** urban heat-island signal là nhiệt bị giữ trong city so với rural surroundings.
**Grammar & collocations:** `urban-heat-island signal` — signal; `map urban heat` — map; `built-environment temperature` — temperature.
**Examples:** `The urban heat-island signal was strongest at night in neighborhoods with little tree cover.` → Night và surface làm heat giữ lâu.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `도시 열섬 신호` — urban heat-island signal.

## 12. ozone episode /ˈoʊzoʊn ˈɛpəˌsoʊd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a period of unusually high ground-level ozone concentration caused by atmospheric chemistry and weather.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** ozone episode là vài ngày ozone ground-level tăng đủ để health risk rõ.
**Grammar & collocations:** `ozone-episode alert` — alert; `monitor ground-level ozone` — monitor; `photochemical pollution` — pollution.
**Examples:** `The ozone episode coincided with stagnant air and intense sunlight.` → Weather và chemistry cùng tạo episode.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `오존 고농도 에피소드` — ozone episode.

## 13. wildfire smoke plume /ˈwaɪldˌfaɪər smoʊk pluːm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a moving column or region of smoke and pollutants released by a wildfire.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** wildfire smoke plume di chuyển theo wind và tạo exposure không theo boundary hành chính.
**Grammar & collocations:** `wildfire-smoke plume` — plume; `track smoke movement` — track; `smoke exposure` — exposure.
**Examples:** `The wildfire smoke plume reached the city overnight and sharply worsened visibility.` → Smoke có thể đến rất nhanh.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `산불 연기 기둥` — wildfire smoke plume.

## 14. ventilation rate /ˌvɛntəˈleɪʃən reɪt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the amount of outdoor air supplied to or removed from an indoor space over time.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** ventilation rate quyết định indoor air được thay mới nhanh đến đâu.
**Grammar & collocations:** `ventilation-rate measurement` — measurement; `increase ventilation` — increase; `air changes per hour` — hour.
**Examples:** `The ventilation rate was too low for the room's occupancy and pollutant source.` → Room cần rate phù hợp với load.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `환기율` — ventilation rate.

## 15. infiltration factor /ˌɪnfɪlˈtreɪʃən ˈfæktər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the fraction of outdoor pollution that enters an indoor environment through leaks, openings, or air exchange.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** infiltration factor cho biết outdoor pollution đi vào building bao nhiêu phần.
**Grammar & collocations:** `infiltration-factor estimate` — estimate; `model outdoor entry` — model; `building envelope` — envelope.
**Examples:** `The infiltration factor fell after cracks were sealed and filtration was upgraded.` → Envelope và filter cùng ảnh hưởng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `침투 계수` — infiltration factor.

## 16. air-quality index /ˈɛr ˈkwɑləti ˈɪndɛks/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a communication scale that converts pollutant measurements into categories of health concern and recommended action.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** air-quality index biến concentration kỹ thuật thành màu/category dễ hiểu.
**Grammar & collocations:** `air-quality index` — index; `check the AQI` — check; `health-protective advice` — advice.
**Examples:** `The air-quality index entered the unhealthy range during the smoke episode.` → Index hỗ trợ action nhanh.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `대기질 지수` — air-quality index.

## 17. low-cost sensor calibration /loʊ kɔst ˈsɛnsər ˌkælɪˈbreɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** adjustment of an inexpensive environmental sensor against a trusted reference measurement.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** low-cost sensor calibration làm sensor rẻ usable hơn bằng cách so với reference.
**Grammar & collocations:** `low-cost-sensor calibration` — calibration; `calibrate a sensor` — calibrate; `reference monitor` — monitor.
**Examples:** `Low-cost sensor calibration corrected humidity-related bias in the neighborhood network.` → Sensor cần context correction.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `저가 센서 보정` — low-cost sensor calibration.

## 18. satellite-derived exposure /ˈsætəˌlaɪt dɪˈraɪvd ɪkˈspoʊʒər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an exposure estimate inferred from satellite observations, atmospheric models, or remotely sensed environmental features.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** satellite-derived exposure mở coverage rộng khi ground monitor thưa.
**Grammar & collocations:** `satellite-derived exposure` — exposure; `derive a spatial estimate` — derive; `remote-sensing product` — product.
**Examples:** `Satellite-derived exposure filled monitoring gaps across rural regions.` → Satellite tăng spatial coverage nhưng cần validation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `위성 유래 노출` — satellite-derived exposure.

## 19. environmental justice screening /ɪnˌvaɪrənˈmɛntəl ˈdʒʌstɪs ˈskriːnɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** systematic identification of communities facing disproportionate environmental hazards and fewer protective resources.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** environmental justice screening tìm nơi hazard cao và resource thấp cùng lúc.
**Grammar & collocations:** `environmental-justice screening` — screening; `screen for cumulative burden` — screen; `overburdened community` — community.
**Examples:** `Environmental justice screening prioritized truck corridors beside low-income housing.` → Screening hỗ trợ resource allocation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `환경 정의 선별` — environmental justice screening.

## 20. traffic-related pollution /ˈtræfɪk rɪˈleɪtɪd pəˈluːʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** pollution generated by vehicle exhaust, brakes, tires, road dust, and traffic-related activity.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** traffic-related pollution gồm nhiều source từ vehicle, không chỉ exhaust.
**Grammar & collocations:** `traffic-related-pollution exposure` — exposure; `measure roadside pollution` — measure; `vehicle emission` — emission.
**Examples:** `Traffic-related pollution was highest near schools beside the freight route.` → Location và timing tạo risk cho children.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `교통 관련 오염` — traffic-related pollution.

## 21. noise exposure /nɔɪz ɪkˈspoʊʒər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** exposure to unwanted or excessive sound that may affect hearing, sleep, stress, or cardiovascular health.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** noise exposure không chỉ là loudness; duration và night timing cũng quan trọng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `noise-exposure map` — map; `measure sound levels` — measure; `nighttime noise` — noise.
**Examples:** `Nighttime noise exposure was linked to sleep disruption near the airport.` → Timing đổi health effect.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `소음 노출` — noise exposure.

## 22. light-at-night exposure /laɪt ət naɪt ɪkˈspoʊʒər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** exposure to artificial light during the usual dark period, potentially affecting sleep and circadian timing.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** light-at-night exposure là ánh sáng ban đêm làm body clock lệch.
**Grammar & collocations:** `light-at-night exposure` — exposure; `measure nighttime illumination` — measure; `circadian disruption` — disruption.
**Examples:** `Light-at-night exposure was higher in brightly lit commercial corridors.` → Built environment đổi sleep exposure.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `야간 인공조명 노출` — light-at-night exposure.

## 23. exposure biomonitoring /ɪkˈspoʊʒər ˌbaɪoʊˈmɑnətərɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** measurement of chemicals, metabolites, or biological responses inside the body to assess environmental exposure.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** exposure biomonitoring đo dấu vết exposure bên trong body thay vì chỉ đo ngoài environment.
**Grammar & collocations:** `exposure-biomonitoring study` — study; `measure a metabolite` — measure; `internal exposure` — exposure.
**Examples:** `Exposure biomonitoring detected a pesticide metabolite not captured by the outdoor station.` → Internal marker thấy route cá nhân.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `노출 생체 모니터링` — exposure biomonitoring.

## 24. mixed-exposure model /mɪkst ɪkˈspoʊʒər ˈmɑdəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a model that estimates health effects from several correlated environmental exposures considered together.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mixed-exposure model nhìn mixture pollution thay vì giả định một exposure độc lập.
**Grammar & collocations:** `mixed-exposure model` — model; `model correlated exposures` — model; `exposure mixture` — mixture.
**Examples:** `The mixed-exposure model showed that traffic, noise, and heat acted together in the same neighborhoods.` → Real life thường là mixture.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `복합 노출 모형` — mixed-exposure model.

## 25. cumulative environmental burden /ˈkjuːmjələtɪv ɪnˌvaɪrənˈmɛntəl ˈbɜrdən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the combined health-relevant load of multiple environmental hazards experienced across place and time.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cumulative environmental burden cộng nhiều hazard và social vulnerability trong cùng place.
**Grammar & collocations:** `cumulative-environmental burden` — burden; `map cumulative burden` — map; `multiple-hazard exposure` — exposure.
**Examples:** `Cumulative environmental burden was highest where pollution, heat, noise, and limited green space overlapped.` → Burden là tổng hệ thống.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `누적 환경 부담` — cumulative environmental burden.

## Review in context — Part 1

**Particulate matter**, an **ultrafine particle**, and an **exposure assignment error** affect estimates from a **personal exposure monitor**. The **indoor-outdoor ratio**, **pollution source apportionment**, and **concentration-response function** help interpret an **acute exposure window** and **chronic exposure burden**. **Attributable mortality** can be modeled for an **urban heat-island signal**, an **ozone episode**, or a **wildfire smoke plume**.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 1:**

**Particulate matter**, **ultrafine particle** và **exposure assignment error** ảnh hưởng các ước tính từ **personal exposure monitor**. **Indoor-outdoor ratio**, **pollution source apportionment** và **concentration-response function** giúp diễn giải **acute exposure window** và **chronic exposure burden**. **Attributable mortality** có thể được model cho **urban heat-island signal**, **ozone episode** hoặc **wildfire smoke plume**.

## Review in context — Part 2

**Ventilation rate** and the **infiltration factor** shape indoor exposure. An **air-quality index**, **low-cost sensor calibration**, and **satellite-derived exposure** improve communication and coverage. **Environmental justice screening** identifies **traffic-related pollution**, **noise exposure**, and **light-at-night exposure** in overburdened communities. **Exposure biomonitoring**, a **mixed-exposure model**, and **cumulative environmental burden** connect measurements with lived health risk.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 2:**

**Ventilation rate** và **infiltration factor** định hình indoor exposure. **Air-quality index**, **low-cost sensor calibration** và **satellite-derived exposure** cải thiện communication và coverage. **Environmental justice screening** tìm **traffic-related pollution**, **noise exposure** và **light-at-night exposure** trong các community chịu burden cao. **Exposure biomonitoring**, **mixed-exposure model** và **cumulative environmental burden** nối measurement với health risk thực tế.
