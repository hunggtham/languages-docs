# C2+ Vocabulary — Corrosion and materials processing

This lesson follows materials from surface protection and corrosion damage through heat treatment, deposition, sintering, and microstructural change. The network is **metal passivation → galvanic corrosion → pitting corrosion → crevice corrosion → stress-corrosion cracking → hydrogen embrittlement → carburization → decarburization → nitriding → anodizing → electrophoretic deposition → sol-gel → sputtering → ceramic sintering → grain-boundary segregation → recrystallization → precipitation hardening → dislocation → slip system → phase boundary**.

## 1. metal passivation /ˈmɛtəl ˌpæsəfəˈkeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** formation of a protective surface film that slows further chemical attack on a metal.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** hình thành film bảo vệ trên surface làm chậm chemical attack.
**Grammar & collocations:** `stable metal passivation` — passivation ổn định; `promote metal passivation` — promote.
**Examples:** `Stable metal passivation protected the stainless surface in the humid chamber.` → Metal passivation ổn định bảo vệ stainless surface trong chamber ẩm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `금속 부동태화`.

## 2. galvanic corrosion /ɡælˈvænɪk kəˈroʊʒən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** corrosion caused when dissimilar metals are electrically connected in an electrolyte.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** corrosion khi metal khác nhau connect điện trong electrolyte.
**Grammar & collocations:** `accelerated galvanic corrosion` — corrosion nhanh; `prevent galvanic corrosion` — prevent.
**Examples:** `An insulating washer prevented galvanic corrosion between the steel and aluminum.` → Washer cách điện ngăn galvanic corrosion giữa steel và aluminum.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `갈바닉 부식`.

## 3. pitting corrosion /ˈpɪtɪŋ kəˈroʊʒən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** localized corrosion that creates small holes or cavities in a metal surface.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** corrosion local tạo hole/cavity nhỏ trên metal surface.
**Grammar & collocations:** `localized pitting corrosion` — corrosion local; `inspect for pitting corrosion` — inspect.
**Examples:** `Localized pitting corrosion appeared beneath the salt deposit.` → Pitting corrosion local xuất hiện dưới salt deposit.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `공식 부식`.

## 4. crevice corrosion /ˈkrɛvɪs kəˈroʊʒən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** corrosion concentrated in a narrow gap where stagnant liquid changes the local chemistry.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** corrosion tập trung trong gap hẹp nơi liquid stagnant đổi local chemistry.
**Grammar & collocations:** `under-deposit crevice corrosion` — corrosion trong crevice; `avoid crevice corrosion` — avoid.
**Examples:** `The gasket joint was redesigned to avoid crevice corrosion.` → Gasket joint được redesign để tránh crevice corrosion.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `틈새 부식`.

## 5. stress-corrosion cracking /ˈstrɛs kəˈroʊʒən ˈkrækɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** cracking caused by the combined action of tensile stress and a corrosive environment.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** crack do tensile stress kết hợp với corrosive environment.
**Grammar & collocations:** `stress-corrosion cracking risk` — risk; `detect stress-corrosion cracking` — detect.
**Examples:** `Inspection detected stress-corrosion cracking near the loaded weld.` → Inspection detect stress-corrosion cracking gần loaded weld.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `응력 부식 균열`.

## 6. hydrogen embrittlement /ˈhaɪdrədʒən ɪmˈbrɪtəlmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** loss of ductility caused when hydrogen enters a metal and promotes cracking.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** ductility loss khi hydrogen enter metal và promote crack.
**Grammar & collocations:** `delayed hydrogen embrittlement` — embrittlement trễ; `test for hydrogen embrittlement` — test.
**Examples:** `The high-strength bolt failed after delayed hydrogen embrittlement.` → High-strength bolt fail sau hydrogen embrittlement trễ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `수소 취성`.

## 7. carburization /ˌkɑːrbjəraɪˈzeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** adding carbon to the surface of steel at high temperature to harden it.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** thêm carbon vào steel surface ở nhiệt cao để harden.
**Grammar & collocations:** `case carburization` — carburization bề mặt; `control carburization depth` — control depth.
**Examples:** `Case carburization hardened the gear surface while leaving its core tough.` → Carburization bề mặt harden gear surface và giữ core tough.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `침탄`.

## 8. decarburization /diːˌkɑːrbjəraɪˈzeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** loss of carbon from steel near the surface during heating in an oxidizing atmosphere.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** carbon loss khỏi steel surface khi heat trong atmosphere oxidizing.
**Grammar & collocations:** `surface decarburization` — decarburization surface; `limit decarburization` — limit.
**Examples:** `A protective atmosphere limited surface decarburization during forging.` → Protective atmosphere limit decarburization surface trong forging.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `탈탄`.

## 9. nitriding /ˈnaɪtraɪdɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** introducing nitrogen into a metal surface to increase hardness and wear resistance.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** đưa nitrogen vào metal surface để tăng hardness và wear resistance.
**Grammar & collocations:** `gas nitriding` — nitriding bằng gas; `nitriding treatment` — treatment.
**Examples:** `Gas nitriding improved the wear resistance of the shaft.` → Gas nitriding cải thiện wear resistance của shaft.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `질화`.

## 10. anodizing /ˈænədaɪzɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** electrochemically thickening the oxide layer on a metal, especially aluminum.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** electrochemical làm dày oxide layer trên metal, nhất là aluminum.
**Grammar & collocations:** `aluminum anodizing` — anodizing aluminum; `color by anodizing` — color.
**Examples:** `Aluminum anodizing produced a durable blue surface.` → Anodizing aluminum tạo surface xanh bền.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `양극산화`.

## 11. electrophoretic deposition /ɪˌlɛktroʊfəˈrɛtɪk ˌdɛpəˈzɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** depositing charged particles onto an object by moving them through a liquid under an electric field.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** deposit charged particle lên object bằng electric field trong liquid.
**Grammar & collocations:** `uniform electrophoretic deposition` — deposition đều; `apply electrophoretic deposition` — apply.
**Examples:** `Uniform electrophoretic deposition coated the complex metal frame.` → Electrophoretic deposition đều coat metal frame phức tạp.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `전기영동 증착`.

## 12. sol-gel /ˈsɑːl dʒɛl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a process that converts a liquid chemical solution into a solid inorganic network or coating.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** process chuyển chemical solution lỏng thành network inorganic solid/coating.
**Grammar & collocations:** `sol-gel coating` — coating sol-gel; `prepare a sol-gel` — prepare.
**Examples:** `The lab prepared a sol-gel coating for the optical surface.` → Lab prepare sol-gel coating cho optical surface.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `졸-겔`.

## 13. sputtering /ˈspʌtərɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** depositing a thin film by ejecting atoms from a target with energetic ions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** deposit thin film bằng eject atom khỏi target với energetic ion.
**Grammar & collocations:** `magnetron sputtering` — sputtering magnetron; `sputter a thin film` — sputter.
**Examples:** `Magnetron sputtering produced a conductive thin film on the glass.` → Magnetron sputtering tạo thin film conductive trên glass.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `스퍼터링`.

## 14. ceramic sintering /səˈræmɪk ˈsɪntərɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** heating compacted ceramic powder so particles bond and the body becomes dense.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** heat ceramic powder compact để particle bond và body dense.
**Grammar & collocations:** `pressureless ceramic sintering` — sintering không pressure; `control ceramic sintering` — control.
**Examples:** `Pressureless ceramic sintering produced a dense, fine-grained component.` → Ceramic sintering không pressure tạo component dense, grain mịn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `세라믹 소결`.

## 15. grain-boundary segregation /ɡreɪn ˈbaʊndəri ˌsɛɡrəˈɡeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** concentration of particular atoms or impurities along the boundaries between crystals.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** atom/impurity concentrate dọc boundary giữa crystal.
**Grammar & collocations:** `solute grain-boundary segregation` — segregation solute; `measure segregation` — measure.
**Examples:** `Grain-boundary segregation weakened the alloy after prolonged heating.` → Grain-boundary segregation làm alloy yếu sau heat kéo dài.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `입계 편석`.

## 16. recrystallization /riːˌkrɪstəlaɪˈzeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** formation of new, relatively strain-free grains in a deformed material during heating.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** grain mới ít strain hình thành trong material deform khi heat.
**Grammar & collocations:** `dynamic recrystallization` — recrystallization dynamic; `trigger recrystallization` — trigger.
**Examples:** `Dynamic recrystallization refined the grain structure during hot working.` → Recrystallization dynamic refine grain structure trong hot working.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `재결정`.

## 17. precipitation hardening /prɪˌsɪpɪˈteɪʃən ˈhɑːrdənɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** strengthening an alloy by forming fine particles that obstruct dislocation movement.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** strengthen alloy bằng particle nhỏ chặn dislocation movement.
**Grammar & collocations:** `age precipitation hardening` — hardening aging; `optimize precipitation hardening` — optimize.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `석출 경화`.

## 18. dislocation /ˌdɪsləˈkeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a line defect in a crystal lattice that enables plastic deformation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** line defect trong crystal lattice cho phép plastic deformation.
**Grammar & collocations:** `mobile dislocation` — dislocation mobile; `pin a dislocation` — pin.
**Examples:** `Solute atoms pinned the dislocation and increased resistance to deformation.` → Solute atom pin dislocation và tăng resistance deformation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `전위`.

## 19. slip system /slɪp ˈsɪstəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a combination of crystal plane and direction along which dislocations move.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** combination crystal plane/direction nơi dislocation move.
**Grammar & collocations:** `active slip system` — system active; `identify a slip system` — identify.
**Examples:** `The active slip system determined how the single crystal deformed.` → Slip system active quyết định single crystal deform thế nào.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `슬립계`.

## 20. phase boundary /feɪz ˈbaʊndəri/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the interface separating two distinct phases or microstructural regions in a material.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** interface tách hai phase hoặc microstructural region khác nhau.
**Grammar & collocations:** `sharp phase boundary` — boundary rõ; `inspect a phase boundary` — inspect.
**Examples:** `Microscopy revealed a sharp phase boundary near the precipitates.` → Microscopy reveal phase boundary rõ gần precipitate.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `상 경계`.

## Review in context

Engineers use **metal passivation** to slow attack, but **galvanic corrosion**, **pitting corrosion**, **crevice corrosion**, and **stress-corrosion cracking** remain risks; **hydrogen embrittlement** can cause sudden failure. Heat treatments include **carburization**, **decarburization**, and **nitriding**, while **anodizing**, **electrophoretic deposition**, **sol-gel**, and **sputtering** build protective films. **Ceramic sintering**, **grain-boundary segregation**, and **recrystallization** change microstructure. **Precipitation hardening** obstructs a **dislocation** on an active **slip system**, and microscopy can reveal a **phase boundary**.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:** Engineers dùng **metal passivation** để slow attack, nhưng **galvanic corrosion**, **pitting corrosion**, **crevice corrosion** và **stress-corrosion cracking** vẫn là risk; **hydrogen embrittlement** có thể gây fail đột ngột. Heat treatment gồm **carburization**, **decarburization** và **nitriding**, còn **anodizing**, **electrophoretic deposition**, **sol-gel** và **sputtering** tạo protective film. **Ceramic sintering**, **grain-boundary segregation** và **recrystallization** đổi microstructure. **Precipitation hardening** chặn **dislocation** trên **slip system** active, còn microscopy có thể reveal **phase boundary**.
