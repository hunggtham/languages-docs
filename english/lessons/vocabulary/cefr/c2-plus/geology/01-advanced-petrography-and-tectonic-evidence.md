# C2+ Vocabulary — Advanced petrography and tectonic evidence

This lesson follows rock interpretation from thin-section textures through deformation, plate fragments, and geological dating. The network is **igneous petrography → thin section → euhedral → anhedral → porphyritic texture → phenocryst → groundmass → vesicular → amygdaloidal → foliation → schistosity → cleavage geology → boudinage → mélange geology → ophiolite → mylonite → cataclasite → geochronology → paleomagnetism → tephrochronology**.

## 1. igneous petrography /ˈɪɡniəs pəˈtrɑːɡrəfi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the microscopic study of igneous rocks, their minerals, textures, and history of crystallization.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** study microscopic về igneous rock, mineral, texture và crystallization history.
**Grammar & collocations:** `detailed igneous petrography` — petrography chi tiết; `apply igneous petrography` — áp dụng study.
**Examples:** `Igneous petrography revealed two stages of crystallization in the lava.` → Igneous petrography reveal hai giai đoạn crystallization trong lava.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `화성암 암석학`.

## 2. thin section /θɪn ˈsɛkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a very thin slice of rock mounted on glass for examination under a microscope.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** lát rock rất mỏng gắn trên glass để examine dưới microscope.
**Grammar & collocations:** `polished thin section` — section mỏng polished; `prepare a thin section` — prepare section.
**Examples:** `The laboratory prepared a polished thin section from the core sample.` → Lab prepare thin section polished từ core sample.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `박편`.

## 3. euhedral /juːˈhiːdrəl/
**Part of speech:** adjective
**Core meaning (English):** having well-formed crystal faces because a mineral grew without severe spatial restriction.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** có crystal face rõ vì mineral grow không bị space restriction nặng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `euhedral feldspar` — feldspar euhedral; `euhedral crystal face` — face tinh thể.
**Examples:** `Euhedral feldspar crystals indicate that the magma cooled slowly.` → Feldspar euhedral cho thấy magma cool chậm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `자형의`.

## 4. anhedral /ænˈhiːdrəl/
**Part of speech:** adjective
**Core meaning (English):** lacking well-formed crystal faces because growth was constrained by neighboring minerals.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** thiếu crystal face rõ vì growth bị mineral xung quanh constrain.
**Grammar & collocations:** `anhedral quartz` — quartz anhedral; `anhedral grain boundary` — boundary.
**Examples:** `Anhedral quartz filled the spaces between earlier crystals.` → Quartz anhedral lấp space giữa crystal hình thành trước.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `타형의`.

## 5. porphyritic texture /ˌpɔːrfəˈrɪtɪk ˈtɛkstʃər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a texture with large crystals embedded in a much finer-grained matrix.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** texture có crystal lớn nằm trong matrix hạt mịn hơn nhiều.
**Grammar & collocations:** `distinctive porphyritic texture` — texture rõ; `describe porphyritic texture` — describe texture.
**Examples:** `The sample's porphyritic texture records cooling at two different rates.` → Porphyritic texture của sample ghi nhận cooling ở hai rate khác nhau.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `반상 조직`.

## 6. phenocryst /ˈfiːnəkrɪst/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a relatively large crystal in a fine-grained igneous groundmass.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** crystal tương đối lớn trong groundmass igneous hạt mịn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `feldspar phenocryst` — phenocryst feldspar; `measure a phenocryst` — đo phenocryst.
**Examples:** `A feldspar phenocryst stood out against the dark matrix.` → Feldspar phenocryst nổi bật trên matrix tối.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `반정`.

## 7. groundmass /ˈɡraʊndmæs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the fine-grained or glassy material surrounding larger crystals in an igneous rock.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** material hạt mịn hoặc glass bao quanh crystal lớn trong igneous rock.
**Grammar & collocations:** `glassy groundmass` — groundmass glassy; `analyze the groundmass` — analyze groundmass.
**Examples:** `The glassy groundmass cooled rapidly near the surface.` → Groundmass glassy cool nhanh gần surface.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `석기질`.

## 8. vesicular /vəˈsɪkjələr/
**Part of speech:** adjective
**Core meaning (English):** containing cavities formed by gas bubbles trapped in cooling lava.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** có cavity do gas bubble trap trong lava đang cool.
**Grammar & collocations:** `highly vesicular basalt` — basalt nhiều vesicle; `vesicular lava` — lava.
**Examples:** `The highly vesicular basalt formed near the top of the flow.` → Basalt highly vesicular hình thành gần top flow.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `기공질의`.

## 9. amygdaloidal /ˌæmɪɡdəˈlɔɪdəl/
**Part of speech:** adjective
**Core meaning (English):** describing volcanic cavities later filled by secondary minerals.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cavity volcanic sau đó được fill bằng secondary mineral.
**Grammar & collocations:** `amygdaloidal basalt` — basalt amygdaloidal; `amygdaloidal cavity` — cavity.
**Examples:** `Amygdaloidal basalt contained pale zeolite fillings.` → Basalt amygdaloidal chứa zeolite filling nhạt.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `행인상 조직의`.

## 10. foliation /ˌfoʊliˈeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a planar arrangement of minerals or layers produced by deformation or metamorphism.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** arrangement planar của mineral/layer do deformation hoặc metamorphism.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong foliation` — foliation mạnh; `measure foliation` — đo foliation.
**Examples:** `Strong foliation showed that the rock had experienced directed pressure.` → Foliation mạnh cho thấy rock chịu directed pressure.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `엽리`.

## 11. schistosity /ʃɪˈstɑːsəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the alignment of visible platy minerals that allows a schist to split into thin layers.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** platy mineral align, cho schist split thành layer mỏng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `pronounced schistosity` — schistosity rõ; `record schistosity` — record schistosity.
**Examples:** `Pronounced schistosity made the sample easy to split by hand.` → Schistosity rõ làm sample dễ split bằng tay.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `편암 엽리`.

## 12. cleavage geology /ˈkliːvɪdʒ dʒiˈɑːlədʒi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a planar fabric along which a deformed rock preferentially breaks or separates.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** fabric planar nơi deformed rock break/separate ưu tiên.
**Grammar & collocations:** `slaty cleavage geology` — cleavage geology dạng slate; `map cleavage` — map cleavage.
**Examples:** `Cleavage geology helped the team reconstruct the direction of shortening.` → Cleavage geology giúp team reconstruct hướng shortening.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `지질 쪼개짐면`.

## 13. boudinage /buːdɪˈnɑːʒ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the stretching and necking of a competent rock layer into sausage-shaped segments.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** layer rock competent bị stretch/neck thành segment giống sausage.
**Grammar & collocations:** `asymmetric boudinage` — boudinage bất đối xứng; `recognize boudinage` — recognize boudinage.
**Examples:** `Asymmetric boudinage indicated the direction of tectonic shear.` → Boudinage bất đối xứng chỉ hướng tectonic shear.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `부댕나주`.

## 14. mélange geology /meɪˈlɑːndʒ dʒiˈɑːlədʒi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the study of chaotic rock mixtures formed by tectonic mixing, often in subduction zones.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** study về rock mixture hỗn độn do tectonic mixing, thường ở subduction zone.
**Grammar & collocations:** `tectonic mélange geology` — geology mélange tectonic; `interpret mélange` — interpret mélange.
**Examples:** `Mélange geology linked the blocks to an ancient subduction complex.` → Mélange geology link block với subduction complex cổ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `멜란지 지질학`.

## 15. ophiolite /ˈoʊfiəlaɪt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a fragment of oceanic crust and upper mantle exposed on land.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** fragment oceanic crust và upper mantle lộ trên land.
**Grammar & collocations:** `ophiolite sequence` — sequence ophiolite; `map an ophiolite` — map ophiolite.
**Examples:** `The field party mapped an ophiolite sequence along the mountain road.` → Field party map ophiolite sequence dọc mountain road.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `오피올라이트`.

## 16. mylonite /ˈmaɪlənaɪt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a fine-grained, foliated rock formed by intense ductile deformation in a shear zone.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** rock hạt mịn có foliation do ductile deformation mạnh trong shear zone.
**Grammar & collocations:** `quartz-rich mylonite` — mylonite nhiều quartz; `sample mylonite` — sample mylonite.
**Examples:** `Quartz-rich mylonite marked the deep part of the shear zone.` → Mylonite nhiều quartz đánh dấu phần sâu của shear zone.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `압쇄암`.

## 17. cataclasite /ˌkætəˈklæsaɪt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a fault-zone rock made of broken and crushed fragments formed by brittle deformation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** rock fault-zone từ fragment bị break/crush do brittle deformation.
**Grammar & collocations:** `fault-zone cataclasite` — cataclasite fault-zone; `identify cataclasite` — identify cataclasite.
**Examples:** `Fault-zone cataclasite recorded brittle movement near the surface.` → Cataclasite fault-zone ghi nhận movement brittle gần surface.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `파쇄암`.

## 18. geochronology /ˌdʒiːoʊkrəˈnɑːlədʒi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the science of determining the ages and sequence of geological events.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** science xác định age và sequence của geological event.
**Grammar & collocations:** `isotopic geochronology` — geochronology isotope; `apply geochronology` — áp dụng science.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `지질연대학`.

## 19. paleomagnetism /ˌpeɪlioʊˈmæɡnətɪzəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the study of ancient magnetic directions preserved in rocks.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** study hướng magnetic cổ được preserve trong rock.
**Grammar & collocations:** `paleomagnetism record` — record paleomagnetism; `measure paleomagnetism` — đo paleomagnetism.

## 20. tephrochronology /ˌtɛfroʊkrəˈnɑːlədʒi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** dating and correlating geological or archaeological layers using distinctive volcanic ash deposits.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** date/correlate layer bằng volcanic ash deposit đặc trưng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `regional tephrochronology` — tephrochronology vùng; `use tephrochronology` — dùng method.
**Examples:** `Tephrochronology linked the lake sediments to a known eruption.` → Tephrochronology link lake sediment với eruption đã biết.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `화산재 연대학`.

## Review in context

Researchers begin with **igneous petrography** on a **thin section**, noting **euhedral** and **anhedral** grains, a **porphyritic texture**, each **phenocryst**, and the surrounding **groundmass**. **Vesicular** and **amygdaloidal** textures record gas and later mineral filling. Deformed rocks show **foliation**, **schistosity**, **cleavage geology**, and **boudinage**; a **mélange geology** framework may identify an **ophiolite**. Deep ductile deformation produces **mylonite**, while brittle faulting produces **cataclasite**. **Geochronology**, **paleomagnetism**, and **tephrochronology** establish timing and movement.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:** Researchers bắt đầu bằng **igneous petrography** trên **thin section**, ghi nhận grain **euhedral**, **anhedral**, **porphyritic texture**, từng **phenocryst** và **groundmass** xung quanh. Texture **vesicular** và **amygdaloidal** ghi dấu gas và mineral filling về sau. Rock bị deform có **foliation**, **schistosity**, **cleavage geology** và **boudinage**; framework **mélange geology** có thể identify **ophiolite**. Ductile deformation sâu tạo **mylonite**, còn faulting brittle tạo **cataclasite**. **Geochronology**, **paleomagnetism** và **tephrochronology** xác lập timing và movement.
