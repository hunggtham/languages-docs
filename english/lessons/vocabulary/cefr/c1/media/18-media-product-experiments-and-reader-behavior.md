# C1 Vocabulary 18 — Media product experiments and reader behavior

This lesson follows how a media team tests product changes, protects readers from avoidable harm, and interprets behavior responsibly.

Flow: **baseline metric → guardrail metric → feature flag → rollout plan → user cohort → behavioral signal → friction point → drop-off rate → experiment log → novelty effect → product iteration → holdout group → success criterion → instrumentation plan → product experiment → A/B test → experiment result → reader behavior → baseline period → test exposure**.

## 1. baseline metric /ˈbeɪsˌlaɪn ˈmɛtrɪk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a measurement of existing performance used as the reference point for judging a change.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** measurement performance hiện tại dùng làm reference để judge change.
**Grammar & collocations:** `reliable baseline metric` — metric; `establish a baseline metric` — establish.
**Examples:** `The team recorded a baseline metric before changing the article page.` → Team record metric baseline trước khi đổi article page.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `기준 지표`.

## 2. guardrail metric /ˈɡɑrdˌreɪl ˈmɛtrɪk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a measurement that limits an experiment by showing whether a desired improvement causes an unacceptable side effect.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** measurement giới hạn experiment bằng cách show improvement gây side effect unacceptable không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `critical guardrail metric` — metric; `monitor a guardrail metric` — monitor.
**Examples:** `Time on page rose, but the guardrail metric showed that newsletter complaints also increased.` → Time on page tăng nhưng metric guardrail cho thấy complaint newsletter cũng tăng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `안전 지표`.

## 3. feature flag /ˈfiːtʃər flæɡ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a software control that turns a product feature on or off for selected users without changing the whole system.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** control software bật/tắt feature cho selected user mà không đổi whole system.
**Grammar & collocations:** `temporary feature flag` — flag; `use a feature flag` — use.
**Examples:** `A feature flag limited the new audio player to volunteer readers.` → Flag feature giới hạn audio player mới cho volunteer reader.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `기능 플래그`.

## 4. rollout plan /ˈroʊlˌaʊt plæn/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a plan for releasing a change gradually, monitoring it, and expanding or stopping it at defined points.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** plan release change dần, monitor rồi expand hoặc stop ở point define.
**Grammar & collocations:** `phased rollout plan` — plan theo phase; `approve a rollout plan` — approve.
**Examples:** `The rollout plan began with one regional newsletter before a national release.` → Plan rollout bắt đầu với một regional newsletter trước release national.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `출시 계획`.

## 5. user cohort /ˈjuːzər ˈkoʊˌhɔrt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a group of users who share a relevant starting date, experience, or condition in an analysis.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** group user share starting date, experience hoặc condition liên quan trong analysis.
**Grammar & collocations:** `new user cohort` — cohort; `compare user cohorts` — compare.
**Examples:** `The new user cohort returned more often after receiving topic controls.` → Cohort user mới return thường hơn sau khi nhận topic control.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `사용자 코호트`.

## 6. behavioral signal /bɪˈheɪvjərəl ˈsɪɡnəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an observable action that provides evidence about what a person understands, wants, or is likely to do.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** action observable provide evidence về điều person understand, want hoặc likely do.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong behavioral signal` — signal; `interpret a behavioral signal` — interpret.
**Examples:** `Repeated searches were a behavioral signal that readers needed a clearer index.` → Search lặp lại là signal behavioral reader cần index rõ hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `행동 신호`.

## 7. friction point /ˈfrɪkʃən pɔɪnt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a step or feature that makes it unnecessarily difficult for someone to complete a useful action.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** step hoặc feature làm người khó hoàn tất action hữu ích một cách không cần thiết.
**Grammar & collocations:** `major friction point` — point; `remove a friction point` — remove.
**Examples:** `The extra confirmation screen was a friction point for readers trying to save a story.` → Confirmation screen thêm là point friction cho reader muốn save story.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `마찰 지점`.

## 8. drop-off rate /ˈdrɑp ɔf reɪt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the percentage of people who leave or stop completing a process at a particular stage.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** percentage người leave hoặc stop process ở một stage.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high drop-off rate` — rate cao; `reduce the drop-off rate` — reduce.
**Examples:** `The drop-off rate fell when the subscription form asked fewer questions.` → Rate drop-off giảm khi form subscription hỏi ít question hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `이탈률`.

## 9. experiment log /ɪkˈspɛrəmənt lɔɡ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a dated record of an experiment's purpose, design, changes, observations, and decisions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** record có date về purpose, design, change, observation và decision của experiment.
**Grammar & collocations:** `shared experiment log` — log; `maintain an experiment log` — maintain.
**Examples:** `The experiment log showed when the headline rule had been changed.` → Log experiment show khi headline rule được change.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `실험 기록`.

## 10. novelty effect /ˈnɑvəlti ɪˈfɛkt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a temporary change in behavior caused by people's excitement or curiosity about something new.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** change behavior tạm thời do excitement hoặc curiosity về thứ mới.
**Grammar & collocations:** `short-lived novelty effect` — effect; `control for a novelty effect` — control.
**Examples:** `The initial spike in listening may have been a novelty effect rather than lasting demand.` → Spike listening ban đầu có thể là effect novelty chứ không phải demand lâu dài.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `신기성 효과`.

## 11. product iteration /ˈprɑdʌkt ˌɪtəˈreɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a revised version of a product or feature created from evidence, feedback, and previous testing.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** version product hoặc feature revise từ evidence, feedback và test trước.
**Grammar & collocations:** `rapid product iteration` — iteration; `plan a product iteration` — plan.
**Examples:** `The next product iteration simplified the reading list after interviews with members.` → Iteration product tiếp simplify reading list sau interview với member.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `제품 반복 개선`.

## 12. holdout group /ˈhoʊlˌaʊt ɡruːp/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a group kept out of an intervention so that its results can be compared with those of exposed users.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** group không nhận intervention để compare result với user exposed.
**Grammar & collocations:** `small holdout group` — group; `retain a holdout group` — retain.
**Examples:** `A holdout group helped the team distinguish a real improvement from a seasonal change.` → Group holdout giúp team phân biệt improvement thật với seasonal change.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `대조 보류 집단`.

## 13. success criterion /səkˈsɛs kraɪˈtɪriən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a clearly stated condition that must be met for a project or experiment to count as successful.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** condition rõ phải meet để project hoặc experiment được xem successful.
**Grammar & collocations:** `predefined success criterion` — criterion; `set a success criterion` — set.
**Examples:** `The success criterion combined higher completion with no increase in complaints.` → Criterion success combine completion cao hơn mà complaint không tăng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `성공 기준`.

## 14. instrumentation plan /ˌɪnstrəmənˈteɪʃən plæn/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a plan for recording the events and measurements needed to understand how a product is used.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** plan record event và measurement cần để understand product được use thế nào.
**Grammar & collocations:** `careful instrumentation plan` — plan; `review an instrumentation plan` — review.
**Examples:** `The instrumentation plan recorded skips without storing the article's private text.` → Plan instrumentation record skip mà không store private text của article.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `계측 계획`.

## 15. product experiment /ˈprɑdʌkt ɪkˈspɛrəmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a controlled test of a product change intended to learn how it affects user behavior or service quality.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** test controlled của product change để learn effect lên behavior user hoặc quality service.
**Grammar & collocations:** `low-risk product experiment` — experiment; `design a product experiment` — design.
**Examples:** `The product experiment tested a quieter notification setting for evening readers.` → Experiment product test notification setting yên hơn cho evening reader.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `제품 실험`.

## 16. A/B test /ˈeɪ ˈbiː tɛst/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a comparison in which different users see two versions of a product or message so their outcomes can be evaluated.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** compare nơi user khác nhau thấy hai version product hoặc message để evaluate outcome.
**Grammar & collocations:** `carefully randomized A/B test` — test; `run an A/B test` — run.
**Examples:** `The A/B test compared two donation prompts without changing the article itself.` → Test A/B compare hai prompt donation mà không đổi article.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `A/B 테스트`.

## 17. experiment result /ɪkˈspɛrəmənt rɪˈzʌlt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an observed finding from a test, interpreted in relation to its design, limits, and comparison group.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** finding observe từ test, interpret theo design, limit và comparison group.
**Grammar & collocations:** `mixed experiment result` — result; `interpret an experiment result` — interpret.
**Examples:** `The experiment result was promising but too small to justify a full release.` → Result experiment promising nhưng quá nhỏ để justify release full.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `실험 결과`.

## 18. reader behavior /ˈriːdər bɪˈheɪvjər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the ways readers find, consume, share, return to, and respond to published material.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cách reader find, consume, share, return và respond với published material.
**Grammar & collocations:** `changing reader behavior` — behavior; `study reader behavior` — study.
**Examples:** `Reader behavior changed when the publication added audio to long investigations.` → Behavior reader đổi khi publication thêm audio cho investigation dài.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `독자 행동`.

## 19. baseline period /ˈbeɪsˌlaɪn ˈpɪriəd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a period in which existing behavior is measured before an intervention begins.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** period đo behavior hiện tại trước khi intervention bắt đầu.
**Grammar & collocations:** `two-week baseline period` — period; `define a baseline period` — define.
**Examples:** `The baseline period covered normal traffic before the redesign was announced.` → Period baseline cover traffic normal trước khi announce redesign.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `기준 기간`.

## 20. test exposure /tɛst ɪkˈspoʊʒər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the amount and pattern of contact a user has with an experimental version or message.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** amount và pattern contact user có với version hoặc message experimental.
**Grammar & collocations:** `uneven test exposure` — exposure không đều; `measure test exposure` — measure.
**Examples:** `Uneven test exposure made the comparison less reliable for occasional readers.` → Exposure test không đều làm comparison kém reliable với occasional reader.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `테스트 노출`.

## Review in context

A media team establishes a **baseline metric** during a **baseline period**, then adds a **guardrail metric** before enabling a **feature flag**. Its **rollout plan** may start with one **user cohort**, where a **behavioral signal** reveals a **friction point** or a high **drop-off rate**. An **experiment log** helps explain a temporary **novelty effect** and guides the next **product iteration**. A **holdout group**, a clear **success criterion**, and an **instrumentation plan** make the **product experiment** easier to interpret. In an **A/B test**, uneven **test exposure** can weaken the **experiment result**, so the team studies **reader behavior** before releasing the change widely.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:** Team media establish **baseline metric** trong **baseline period**, rồi thêm **guardrail metric** trước khi enable **feature flag**. **Rollout plan** có thể bắt đầu với một **user cohort**, nơi **behavioral signal** reveal **friction point** hoặc **drop-off rate** cao. **Experiment log** giúp explain **novelty effect** tạm thời và guide **product iteration** tiếp theo. **Holdout group**, **success criterion** rõ và **instrumentation plan** làm **product experiment** dễ interpret hơn. Trong **A/B test**, **test exposure** không đều có thể làm yếu **experiment result**, nên team study **reader behavior** trước khi release change rộng.
