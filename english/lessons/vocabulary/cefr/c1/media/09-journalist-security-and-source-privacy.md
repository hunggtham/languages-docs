# C1 Vocabulary 09 — Journalist security and source privacy

This lesson follows how journalists and sources reduce digital exposure while communicating, publishing, and protecting sensitive material.

Flow: **source encryption → secure drop → metadata stripping → identity protection → consent withdrawal → location obfuscation → biometric privacy → device fingerprinting → tracking pixel → first-party data → third-party data → privacy-preserving analytics → source threat model → journalist security → tracking protection → secure channel → digital safety plan → compartmentalized identity → contact discovery → metadata hygiene**.

## 1. source encryption /sɔrs ɪnˈkrɪpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the use of cryptographic protection to keep communication with a source unreadable to unauthorized people.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** dùng cryptographic protection để communication với source không đọc được bởi người unauthorized.
**Grammar & collocations:** `source-encryption practice` — practice; `use source encryption` — dùng.
**Examples:** `Source encryption protected the interview notes while the reporter traveled through a high-risk region.` → Encryption source bảo vệ note interview khi reporter đi qua region risk cao.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `취재원 암호화`.

## 2. secure drop /sɪˈkjʊr drɑp/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a protected system through which a source can submit documents or messages without directly revealing an identity.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** system protected cho source submit document/message mà không reveal identity trực tiếp.
**Grammar & collocations:** `secure-drop portal` — portal; `submit material through a secure drop` — gửi.
**Examples:** `The secure drop allowed the worker to send records without creating a visible email relationship.` → Drop an toàn cho worker gửi record mà không tạo email relationship visible.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `보안 제보 창구`.

## 3. metadata stripping /ˈmɛtəˌdeɪtə ˈstrɪpɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the removal of hidden file information that might reveal a device, author, location, or editing history.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** remove hidden file information có thể lộ device, author, location hoặc history edit.
**Grammar & collocations:** `metadata-stripping tool` — tool; `strip metadata before publication` — strip.
**Examples:** `Metadata stripping removed the phone model and location from the photograph before release.` → Stripping metadata remove phone model và location khỏi photo trước release.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `메타데이터 제거`.

## 4. identity protection /aɪˈdɛntəti prəˈtɛkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** measures that prevent a person's name, characteristics, or identifying connections from being exposed unnecessarily.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** measure ngăn name, characteristic hoặc connection nhận dạng bị expose không cần.
**Grammar & collocations:** `identity-protection measure` — measure; `strengthen identity protection` — củng cố.
**Examples:** `Identity protection required changing small details that could have identified the only witness.` → Bảo vệ identity cần đổi detail nhỏ có thể identify witness duy nhất.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `신원 보호`.

## 5. consent withdrawal /kənˈsɛnt wɪðˈdrɔəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a person's act of ending previously granted permission for material to be collected, used, or published.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** person chấm dứt permission trước đó cho collect, use hoặc publish material.
**Grammar & collocations:** `consent-withdrawal request` — request; `honor consent withdrawal` — tôn trọng.
**Examples:** `The newsroom honored consent withdrawal before the audio story had been released.` → Newsroom tôn trọng withdrawal consent trước khi story audio release.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `동의 철회`.

## 6. location obfuscation /loʊˈkeɪʃən ˌɑbfəsˈkeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the deliberate blurring, generalizing, or removal of location information to reduce identification risk.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** làm mờ, generalize hoặc remove location để giảm risk identify.
**Grammar & collocations:** `location-obfuscation technique` — technique; `obfuscate a precise location` — che.
**Examples:** `Location obfuscation showed the forest district rather than the village where the source lived.` → Obfuscation location chỉ show district forest thay vì village source sống.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `위치 난독화`.

## 7. biometric privacy /ˌbaɪoʊˈmɛtrɪk ˈpraɪvəsi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** protection of identifying physical or behavioral measurements such as faces, voices, fingerprints, or gait.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** bảo vệ measurement physical/behavior nhận dạng như face, voice, fingerprint hoặc gait.
**Grammar & collocations:** `biometric-privacy risk` — risk; `protect biometric privacy` — bảo vệ.
**Examples:** `Biometric privacy concerns led the editor to blur faces in the crowd video.` → Concern privacy biometric khiến editor blur mặt trong video crowd.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `생체 정보 프라이버시`.

## 8. device fingerprinting /dɪˈvaɪs ˈfɪŋɡərˌprɪntɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** identifying or linking a device by combining technical characteristics such as browser, screen, language, and hardware details.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** identify/link device bằng cách gộp browser, screen, language và hardware detail.
**Grammar & collocations:** `device-fingerprinting script` — script; `block device fingerprinting` — chặn.
**Examples:** `Device fingerprinting could connect a source's supposedly separate visits to the newsroom portal.` → Fingerprinting device có thể nối các visit riêng của source với portal newsroom.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `기기 지문 채취`.

## 9. tracking pixel /ˈtrækɪŋ ˈpɪksəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a tiny invisible image or code element that records whether content was opened or interacted with.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** image/code vô hình nhỏ ghi việc content được open hoặc interact.
**Grammar & collocations:** `tracking-pixel request` — request; `block a tracking pixel` — chặn.
**Examples:** `The newsletter removed the tracking pixel from messages sent to confidential sources.` → Newsletter remove pixel tracking khỏi message gửi source confidential.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `추적 픽셀`.

## 10. first-party data /ˌfɝst ˈpɑrti ˈdeɪtə/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** information collected directly by an organization from its own users, visitors, or sources.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** information organization collect trực tiếp từ user, visitor hoặc source của mình.
**Grammar & collocations:** `first-party-data policy` — policy; `govern first-party data` — quản trị.
**Examples:** `The publisher limited first-party data to what readers had clearly agreed to provide.` → Publisher giới hạn data first-party vào điều reader đồng ý cung cấp rõ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `자사 데이터`.

## 11. third-party data /ˌθɝd ˈpɑrti ˈdeɪtə/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** information obtained from an outside organization rather than collected directly from the people it describes.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** information lấy từ organization ngoài thay vì collect trực tiếp từ người được mô tả.
**Grammar & collocations:** `third-party-data broker` — broker; `audit third-party data` — audit.
**Examples:** `The newsroom rejected third-party data whose collection purpose and consent history were unclear.` → Newsroom reject data third-party có purpose collect và history consent không rõ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `제3자 데이터`.

## 12. privacy-preserving analytics /ˈpraɪvəsi prɪˈzɝvɪŋ ˌænəˈlɪtɪks/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** analysis designed to learn aggregate patterns while limiting the exposure of individual records or identities.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** analysis learn aggregate pattern nhưng limit exposure record/identity cá nhân.
**Grammar & collocations:** `privacy-preserving-analytics system` — system; `run privacy-preserving analytics` — chạy.
**Examples:** `Privacy-preserving analytics showed reading trends without giving editors a list of individual searches.` → Analytics giữ privacy show trend đọc mà không cho editor list search cá nhân.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `프라이버시 보존 분석`.

## 13. source threat model /sɔrs θrɛt ˈmɑdəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an analysis of who might identify, pressure, monitor, or harm a source and through which pathways.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** analysis ai có thể identify, pressure, monitor hoặc harm source và qua pathway nào.
**Grammar & collocations:** `source-threat-model review` — review; `build a source threat model` — xây.
**Examples:** `The source threat model considered workplace logs, shared devices, and predictable travel patterns.` → Model threat source xem xét log workplace, device chung và travel pattern dễ đoán.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `취재원 위협 모델`.

## 14. journalist security /ˈdʒɝnəlɪst sɪˈkjʊrəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the physical, digital, legal, and psychological measures that protect journalists while they work.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** measure physical, digital, legal và psychological bảo vệ journalist khi work.
**Grammar & collocations:** `journalist-security training` — training; `improve journalist security` — cải thiện.
**Examples:** `Journalist security training covered device seizure, hostile crowds, and emergency contacts.` → Training security journalist cover device seizure, crowd hostile và contact khẩn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `기자 보안`.

## 15. tracking protection /ˈtrækɪŋ prəˈtɛkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** settings, tools, or practices that limit unwanted monitoring of a person's online activity.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** setting, tool hoặc practice limit monitoring không muốn về online activity.
**Grammar & collocations:** `tracking-protection setting` — setting; `enable tracking protection` — bật.
**Examples:** `Tracking protection prevented the reporting app from sending unnecessary location signals.` → Protection tracking ngăn app reporting gửi location signal không cần.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `추적 방지`.

## 16. secure channel /sɪˈkjʊr ˈtʃænəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a communication route designed to reduce interception, manipulation, or unauthorized access.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** route communication design để giảm interception, manipulation hoặc access unauthorized.
**Grammar & collocations:** `secure-channel protocol` — protocol; `move to a secure channel` — chuyển.
**Examples:** `The reporter moved the conversation to a secure channel before discussing the unpublished files.` → Reporter chuyển conversation sang channel secure trước khi discuss file chưa publish.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `보안 채널`.

## 17. digital safety plan /ˈdɪdʒətəl ˈseɪfti plæn/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a practical plan for reducing digital risks, responding to incidents, and recovering access or evidence.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** plan thực tế giảm digital risk, respond incident và recover access/evidence.
**Grammar & collocations:** `digital-safety-plan checklist` — checklist; `update a digital safety plan` — update.
**Examples:** `The digital safety plan included backup codes, device updates, and a contact for legal help.` → Plan safety digital gồm backup code, update device và contact legal.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `디지털 안전 계획`.

## 18. compartmentalized identity /kəmˌpɑrtməntəˈlaɪzd aɪˈdɛntəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a deliberately separated identity used in one context so that it cannot easily be linked to another identity.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** identity tách có chủ ý trong một context để khó link với identity khác.
**Grammar & collocations:** `compartmentalized-identity practice` — practice; `maintain a compartmentalized identity` — duy trì.
**Examples:** `A compartmentalized identity kept the source's public professional account separate from confidential contact.` → Identity compartmentalized giữ account professional công tách khỏi contact confidential.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `분리된 신원`.

## 19. contact discovery /ˈkɑntækt dɪˈskʌvəri/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the process by which an application or service identifies which people from an address book use it.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** process app/service identify người trong address book đang dùng nó.
**Grammar & collocations:** `contact-discovery feature` — feature; `disable contact discovery` — tắt.
**Examples:** `The source disabled contact discovery so the messaging service would not expose professional connections.` → Source tắt discovery contact để service message không expose connection professional.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `연락처 검색`.

## 20. metadata hygiene /ˈmɛtəˌdeɪtə ˈhaɪdʒiːn/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the routine practice of checking, limiting, and cleaning hidden information before a file is shared.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** practice thường xuyên check, limit và clean hidden information trước share file.
**Grammar & collocations:** `metadata-hygiene checklist` — checklist; `practice metadata hygiene` — thực hành.
**Examples:** `Metadata hygiene became routine before reporters uploaded photographs from sensitive meetings.` → Hygiene metadata thành routine trước khi reporter upload photo từ meeting nhạy cảm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `메타데이터 위생`.

## Review in context

**Source encryption** and a **secure drop** protect communication, while **metadata stripping**, **identity protection**, and **consent withdrawal** reduce exposure. **Location obfuscation** and **biometric privacy** matter when images or travel can identify people; **device fingerprinting** and a **tracking pixel** can reveal repeated visits. A publisher must distinguish **first-party data** from **third-party data** and consider **privacy-preserving analytics**. A **source threat model**, **journalist security**, **tracking protection**, and a **secure channel** belong in a **digital safety plan**. **Compartmentalized identity**, **contact discovery**, and **metadata hygiene** reduce links that an attacker could exploit.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

**Source encryption** và **secure drop** bảo vệ communication, còn **metadata stripping**, **identity protection** và **consent withdrawal** giảm exposure. **Location obfuscation** và **biometric privacy** quan trọng khi image/travel có thể identify người; **device fingerprinting** và **tracking pixel** có thể lộ visit lặp. Publisher phải phân biệt **first-party data** với **third-party data** và cân nhắc **privacy-preserving analytics**. **Source threat model**, **journalist security**, **tracking protection** và **secure channel** thuộc **digital safety plan**. **Compartmentalized identity**, **contact discovery** và **metadata hygiene** giảm link mà attacker có thể exploit.
