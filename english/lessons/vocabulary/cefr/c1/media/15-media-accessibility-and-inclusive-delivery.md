# C1 Vocabulary 15 — Media accessibility and inclusive delivery

This lesson follows how media teams remove barriers so that people with different sensory, linguistic, and cognitive needs can use public information.

Flow: **captioning standard → audio description → transcript quality → accessible player → color contrast → plain-language summary → multilingual access → translation workflow → localization brief → disability representation → inclusive commissioning → sensory accommodation → accessible transcript → caption quality → audio-described version → closed caption → open caption → accessibility statement → hearing access → visual access**.

## 1. captioning standard /ˈkæpʃənɪŋ ˈstændərd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a set of technical and editorial rules for producing accurate, readable, and well-timed captions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** bộ rule technical và editorial tạo caption accurate, readable và đúng timing.
**Grammar & collocations:** `broadcast captioning standard` — standard; `meet a captioning standard` — đáp ứng.
**Examples:** `The captioning standard required speakers, important sounds, and timing to be identified clearly.` → Standard caption yêu cầu identify rõ speaker, sound quan trọng và timing.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `자막 표준`.

## 2. audio description /ˈɔdioʊ dɪˈskrɪpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** spoken narration that describes important visual information for people who cannot see it fully.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** narration spoken describe visual information quan trọng cho người không xem đầy đủ.
**Grammar & collocations:** `detailed audio description` — description; `provide audio description` — cung cấp.
**Examples:** `Audio description explained the map and the speaker's gestures between lines of dialogue.` → Description audio giải thích map và gesture của speaker giữa các line dialogue.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `화면 해설`.

## 3. transcript quality /ˈtrænskrɪpt ˈkwɑləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the accuracy, completeness, readability, and usefulness of a written record of spoken content.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** accuracy, completeness, readability và usefulness của record written về spoken content.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high transcript quality` — quality cao; `check transcript quality` — check.
**Examples:** `The editor checked transcript quality before publishing the interview for search and study.` → Editor check quality transcript trước khi publish interview để search và study.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `대본 품질`.

## 4. accessible player /əkˈsɛsəbəl ˈpleɪər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a media player designed so that people using different devices, controls, or assistive tools can operate it.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** media player design để người dùng device, control hoặc assistive tool khác nhau operate được.
**Grammar & collocations:** `fully accessible player` — player; `test an accessible player` — test.
**Examples:** `The accessible player let users adjust speed and captions without leaving the video.` → Player accessible cho user adjust speed và caption mà không rời video.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `접근 가능한 플레이어`.

## 5. color contrast /ˈkʌlər ˈkɑntræst/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the difference in lightness and color between text, controls, and their background that affects readability.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** difference lightness và color giữa text, control và background ảnh hưởng readability.
**Grammar & collocations:** `sufficient color contrast` — contrast đủ; `improve color contrast` — cải thiện.
**Examples:** `The redesign improved color contrast so labels remained visible in bright sunlight.` → Redesign cải thiện contrast color để label vẫn visible dưới sunlight mạnh.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `색상 대비`.

## 6. plain-language summary /ˌpleɪn ˈlæŋɡwɪdʒ ˈsʌməri/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a short explanation that presents complex information in direct language and avoids unnecessary specialist terms.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** explanation ngắn trình bày information complex bằng language direct, tránh term specialist không cần.
**Grammar & collocations:** `clear plain-language summary` — summary; `write a plain-language summary` — viết.
**Examples:** `The report opened with a plain-language summary for readers unfamiliar with the policy.` → Report mở đầu bằng summary plain-language cho reader không quen policy.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `쉬운 말 요약`.

## 7. multilingual access /ˌmʌltiˈlɪŋɡwəl ˈæksɛs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the ability to receive and understand a service or public message in more than one language.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** ability receive và understand service hoặc message public bằng nhiều language.
**Grammar & collocations:** `meaningful multilingual access` — access; `expand multilingual access` — mở rộng.
**Examples:** `Multilingual access became essential during the health emergency.` → Access multilingual trở nên essential trong emergency health.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `다언어 접근성`.

## 8. translation workflow /trænzˈleɪʃən ˈwɜrkˌfloʊ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the organized sequence of assigning, translating, reviewing, and publishing material in another language.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** sequence có tổ chức assign, translate, review và publish material bằng language khác.
**Grammar & collocations:** `efficient translation workflow` — workflow; `document a translation workflow` — document.
**Examples:** `The translation workflow included a subject expert who checked emergency terminology.` → Workflow translation gồm subject expert check terminology emergency.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `번역 작업 흐름`.

## 9. localization brief /ˌloʊkələˈzeɪʃən briːf/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** instructions explaining how content should be adapted for the language, culture, laws, and practical context of a particular audience.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** instruction explain adapt content theo language, culture, law và context practical của audience.
**Grammar & collocations:** `detailed localization brief` — brief; `prepare a localization brief` — chuẩn bị.
**Examples:** `The localization brief warned translators about local names and emergency phone numbers.` → Brief localization warn translator về local name và emergency phone number.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `현지화 지침서`.

## 10. disability representation /dɪsəˈbɪləti ˌrɛprɪzənˈteɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the way media portrays disabled people, their lives, identities, and contributions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cách media portray disabled people, life, identity và contribution của họ.
**Grammar & collocations:** `accurate disability representation` — representation; `improve disability representation` — cải thiện.
**Examples:** `The series improved disability representation by giving characters agency and ordinary ambitions.` → Series cải thiện representation disability bằng cách cho character agency và ambition bình thường.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `장애 재현`.

## 11. inclusive commissioning /ɪnˈkluːsɪv kəˈmɪʃənɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the practice of ordering media work from creators and teams with varied backgrounds and perspectives.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** practice order media work từ creator và team có background, perspective đa dạng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `inclusive commissioning policy` — policy; `promote inclusive commissioning` — promote.
**Examples:** `Inclusive commissioning brought local disabled creators into the planning process.` → Commissioning inclusive đưa disabled creator địa phương vào planning process.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `포용적 제작 의뢰`.

## 12. sensory accommodation /ˈsɛnsəri əˌkɑməˈdeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an adjustment that helps people with sensory differences access an event, service, or piece of media.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** adjustment giúp người có sensory difference access event, service hoặc media.
**Grammar & collocations:** `reasonable sensory accommodation` — accommodation; `request sensory accommodation` — yêu cầu.
**Examples:** `The festival offered a sensory accommodation room and lower-volume screenings.` → Festival offer room accommodation sensory và screening volume thấp.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `감각 지원 조정`.

## 13. accessible transcript /əkˈsɛsəbəl ˈtrænskrɪpt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a written transcript structured and formatted so that people using different reading tools can navigate it.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** transcript written structure và format để người dùng reading tool khác nhau navigate.
**Grammar & collocations:** `searchable accessible transcript` — transcript; `publish an accessible transcript` — publish.
**Examples:** `The accessible transcript used headings and speaker labels that worked with screen-reading software.` → Transcript accessible dùng heading và speaker label work với software screen-reading.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `접근 가능한 녹취록`.

## 14. caption quality /ˈkæpʃən ˈkwɑləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the accuracy, timing, completeness, and readability of captions attached to audiovisual content.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** accuracy, timing, completeness và readability của caption gắn với audiovisual content.
**Grammar & collocations:** `poor caption quality` — quality thấp; `evaluate caption quality` — evaluate.
**Examples:** `Viewers reported poor caption quality when names and background sounds were repeatedly omitted.` → Viewer report quality caption thấp khi name và background sound bị bỏ lặp lại.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `자막 품질`.

## 15. audio-described version /ˈɔdioʊ dɪˈskraɪbd ˈvɜrʒən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a version of audiovisual content that includes spoken descriptions of important visual details.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** version audiovisual có spoken description về visual detail quan trọng.

## 16. closed caption /ˌkloʊzd ˈkæpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** text that represents speech and relevant sounds and can usually be turned on or off by the viewer.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** text represent speech và sound relevant, thường viewer bật/tắt được.
**Grammar & collocations:** `accurate closed caption` — caption; `turn on closed captions` — bật.
**Examples:** `The viewer turned on closed captions during the noisy train journey.` → Viewer bật closed caption trong chuyến tàu ồn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `폐쇄 자막`.

## 17. open caption /ˌoʊpən ˈkæpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** text permanently displayed as part of a video or screening and not controlled by the viewer.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** text hiển thị permanent như một phần video hoặc screening, viewer không control.
**Grammar & collocations:** `open-caption screening` — screening; `provide open captions` — cung cấp.
**Examples:** `The cinema scheduled an open-caption screening for the whole audience.` → Cinema schedule screening open-caption cho toàn audience.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `개방 자막`.

## 18. accessibility statement /əkˌsɛsəˈbɪləti ˈsteɪtmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a public explanation of the access features a service provides and how users can report barriers.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** explanation public về feature access service provide và cách user report barrier.
**Grammar & collocations:** `clear accessibility statement` — statement; `publish an accessibility statement` — publish.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `접근성 선언문`.

## 19. hearing access /ˈhɪrɪŋ ˈæksɛs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the ability of people who are deaf or hard of hearing to receive and understand spoken or auditory information.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** ability người deaf hoặc hard of hearing receive và understand information spoken hoặc auditory.
**Grammar & collocations:** `equal hearing access` — access; `improve hearing access` — cải thiện.
**Examples:** `Captions and visual alerts improved hearing access during the live briefing.` → Caption và visual alert cải thiện access hearing trong briefing live.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `청각 접근성`.

## 20. visual access /ˈvɪʒuəl ˈæksɛs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the ability of people who are blind or have low vision to receive and understand visual information.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** ability người blind hoặc low vision receive và understand visual information.
**Grammar & collocations:** `equitable visual access` — access; `design for visual access` — design.
**Examples:** `Audio description and text alternatives improved visual access to the exhibition.` → Description audio và text alternative cải thiện access visual đến exhibition.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `시각 접근성`.

## Review in context

A strong **captioning standard** supports **audio description** and improves **transcript quality** across an **accessible player**. Designers check **color contrast**, add a **plain-language summary**, and plan **multilingual access** through a documented **translation workflow** and a careful **localization brief**. Good **disability representation** is strengthened by **inclusive commissioning** and practical **sensory accommodation**. An **accessible transcript** makes research easier, while **caption quality** must be checked in every **audio-described version**. **Closed captions** can be switched on, whereas **open captions** remain visible for everyone. An **accessibility statement** explains the service's commitments, including **hearing access** and **visual access**.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:** **Captioning standard** tốt hỗ trợ **audio description** và cải thiện **transcript quality** trong **accessible player**. Designer check **color contrast**, thêm **plain-language summary** và plan **multilingual access** qua **translation workflow** được document cùng **localization brief** cẩn thận. **Disability representation** tốt được củng cố bằng **inclusive commissioning** và **sensory accommodation** thực tế. **Accessible transcript** làm research dễ hơn, còn **caption quality** phải được check trong mỗi **audio-described version**. **Closed caption** có thể bật/tắt, còn **open caption** luôn visible cho mọi người. **Accessibility statement** giải thích commitment của service, gồm **hearing access** và **visual access**.
