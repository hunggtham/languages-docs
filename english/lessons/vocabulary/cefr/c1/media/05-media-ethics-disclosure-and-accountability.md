# C1 Vocabulary 05 — Media ethics, disclosure, and accountability

This lesson follows how news organizations separate reporting from influence, disclose conflicts, and repair trust when standards fail.

Flow: **editorial firewall → conflict disclosure → sponsored content → native advertising → product placement → source confidentiality → shield law → right of reply → public-interest test → privacy intrusion → defamation per se → actual malice → fair comment → retraction policy → correction culture → ombuds editor → ethics code → editorial recusal → accountability report → editorial transparency**.

## 1. editorial firewall /ˌɛtəˈrɪəl ˈfaɪərˌwɔl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a formal separation between editorial decisions and commercial, ownership, or political pressure.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** separation formal giữa decision editorial và pressure commercial, ownership hoặc political.
**Grammar & collocations:** `editorial-firewall policy` — policy; `maintain an editorial firewall` — duy trì.
**Examples:** `The editorial firewall prevented an advertiser from changing the investigation's conclusions.` → Firewall editorial ngăn advertiser đổi conclusion investigation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `편집 방화벽`.

## 2. conflict disclosure /ˈkɑnflɪkt dɪˈskloʊʒər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a statement identifying a financial, personal, or institutional interest that could affect how information is produced or received.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** statement nêu interest financial, personal hoặc institutional có thể ảnh hưởng production/reception information.
**Grammar & collocations:** `conflict-disclosure statement` — statement; `require conflict disclosure` — yêu cầu.
**Examples:** `The article included a conflict disclosure because the analyst's firm held the company’s bonds.` → Article có disclosure conflict vì firm analyst giữ bond company.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `이해충돌 공개`.

## 3. sponsored content /ˈspɑnsərd ˈkɑntɛnt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** material paid for or commissioned by an organization whose support is disclosed to the audience.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** material được tổ chức trả tiền/commission và support đó được disclose cho audience.
**Grammar & collocations:** `sponsored-content label` — label; `publish sponsored content` — publish.
**Examples:** `The sponsored content carried a clear label so readers would not mistake it for independent reporting.` → Content sponsored có label rõ để reader không nhầm với reporting độc lập.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `후원 콘텐츠`.

## 4. native advertising /ˈneɪtɪv ˈædvərˌtaɪzɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** paid promotion designed to resemble the surrounding editorial format or style.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** promotion trả tiền được design giống format/style editorial xung quanh.
**Grammar & collocations:** `native-advertising disclosure` — disclosure; `mark native advertising` — đánh dấu.
**Examples:** `Native advertising required a visible label because its layout resembled a reported article.` → Advertising native cần label visible vì layout giống article reporting.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `네이티브 광고`.

## 5. product placement /ˈprɑdʌkt ˈpleɪsmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the deliberate appearance of a branded product in media content as part of a commercial arrangement.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** brand product xuất hiện có chủ ý trong content qua arrangement commercial.
**Grammar & collocations:** `product-placement agreement` — agreement; `disclose product placement` — disclose.
**Examples:** `The review disclosed product placement before discussing the device's performance.` → Review disclose placement product trước khi discuss performance device.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `제품 배치`.

## 6. source confidentiality /sɔrs ˌkɑnfəˌdɛnʃiˈæləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the duty or practice of protecting a source's identity when disclosure could cause retaliation or serious harm.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** duty/practice bảo vệ identity source khi disclosure có thể gây retaliation hoặc harm nghiêm trọng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `source-confidentiality agreement` — agreement; `protect source confidentiality` — bảo vệ.
**Examples:** `Source confidentiality allowed the reporter to publish evidence without exposing the worker.` → Confidentiality source giúp reporter publish evidence mà không lộ worker.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `취재원 비밀 보호`.

## 7. shield law /ˈʃiːld lɔ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a law that limits the power to force journalists to reveal confidential sources or unpublished material.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** law giới hạn power buộc journalist reveal source confidential hoặc material chưa publish.
**Grammar & collocations:** `shield-law protection` — protection; `invoke a shield law` — viện dẫn.
**Examples:** `The reporter invoked the shield law when a court demanded the source's name.` → Reporter viện dẫn law shield khi court đòi tên source.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `취재원 보호법`.

## 8. right of reply /raɪt əv rɪˈplaɪ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an opportunity for a person or organization criticized in a report to respond publicly.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cơ hội cho person/organization bị criticize được response công khai.
**Grammar & collocations:** `right-of-reply request` — request; `offer a right of reply` — offer.
**Examples:** `The editor offered a right of reply before publishing the allegation about the contractor.` → Editor offer quyền reply trước khi publish allegation về contractor.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `반론권`.

## 9. public-interest test /ˈpʌblɪk ˈɪntrəst tɛst/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a test of whether publishing information serves a substantial civic benefit rather than mere curiosity.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** test xem publish information có phục vụ civic benefit đáng kể hay chỉ thỏa curiosity.
**Grammar & collocations:** `public-interest-test analysis` — analysis; `apply a public-interest test` — áp dụng.
**Examples:** `The public-interest test supported publication of the procurement records but not the official's private address.` → Test interest công ủng hộ publish record procurement nhưng không phải address riêng official.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `공익성 심사`.

## 10. privacy intrusion /ˈpraɪvəsi ɪnˈtruːʒən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an unjustified entry into a person's private life, space, data, or communications.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** xâm nhập không justify vào private life, space, data hoặc communication.
**Grammar & collocations:** `privacy-intrusion risk` — risk; `avoid privacy intrusion` — tránh.
**Examples:** `The editor removed a photograph because identifying the victim would create a privacy intrusion.` → Editor bỏ photo vì identify victim tạo intrusion privacy.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `사생활 침해`.

## 11. defamation per se /ˌdɛfəˈmeɪʃən pɝ seɪ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a form of defamatory statement considered so inherently harmful that damage may be presumed under applicable law.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** statement defamatory inherently harmful đến mức law có thể presume damage.
**Grammar & collocations:** `defamation-per-se claim` — claim; `plead defamation per se` — plead.
**Examples:** `The lawyer argued that the false accusation fell within defamation per se.` → Lawyer lập luận accusation false thuộc defamation per se.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `당연 위법 명예훼손`.

## 12. actual malice /ˈæktʃuəl ˈmælɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** knowledge that a published statement is false or reckless disregard for whether it is true.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** biết statement false hoặc reckless disregard việc nó true hay không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `actual-malice standard` — standard; `prove actual malice` — chứng minh.
**Examples:** `The plaintiff had to show actual malice rather than merely prove that the report was mistaken.` → Plaintiff phải show malice thật thay vì chỉ prove report mistaken.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `실제 악의`.

## 13. fair comment /fɛr ˈkɑmɛnt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an honestly held opinion based on disclosed or reasonably known facts about a matter of public concern.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** opinion honestly held dựa fact disclosed/known hợp lý về matter public concern.
**Grammar & collocations:** `fair-comment defense` — defense; `publish fair comment` — publish.
**Examples:** `The column was protected as fair comment because it separated opinion from the verified record.` → Column được bảo vệ như comment fair vì tách opinion khỏi record verified.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `공정 논평`.

## 14. retraction policy /rɪˈtrækʃən ˈpɑləsi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a stated procedure for withdrawing seriously false or unreliable published material and explaining the decision.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** procedure nêu trước để withdraw material false/unreliable nghiêm trọng và explain decision.
**Grammar & collocations:** `retraction-policy page` — page; `follow a retraction policy` — tuân theo.
**Examples:** `The retraction policy required the headline to identify the original error instead of silently deleting the story.` → Policy retract yêu cầu headline nêu error gốc thay vì lặng lẽ xóa story.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `철회 정책`.

## 15. correction culture /kəˈrɛkʃən ˈkʌltʃər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an organizational norm that treats admitting, fixing, and learning from errors as part of responsible work.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** norm organization coi admit, fix và learn từ error là một phần work có trách nhiệm.
**Grammar & collocations:** `correction-culture initiative` — initiative; `build a correction culture` — xây.
**Examples:** `A correction culture made reporters more willing to flag an uncertain figure before publication.` → Culture correction khiến reporter sẵn sàng flag figure uncertain trước publish.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `정정 문화`.

## 16. ombuds editor /ˈɑmˌbʌdz ˈɛtər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an editor who independently reviews audience complaints and evaluates whether newsroom standards were followed.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** editor review complaint audience độc lập và đánh giá newsroom có follow standard không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `ombuds-editor review` — review; `consult the ombuds editor` — tham vấn.
**Examples:** `The ombuds editor published an explanation after readers challenged the headline's fairness.` → Editor ombuds publish explanation sau khi reader challenge fairness headline.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `옴부즈 편집자`.

## 17. ethics code /ˈɛθɪks koʊd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a written set of professional principles governing accuracy, independence, fairness, and treatment of people.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** bộ principle professional bằng văn bản về accuracy, independence, fairness và treatment people.
**Grammar & collocations:** `journalism-ethics code` — code; `follow an ethics code` — tuân theo.
**Examples:** `The ethics code required the newsroom to distinguish reporting from advocacy.` → Code ethics yêu cầu newsroom phân biệt reporting với advocacy.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `윤리 강령`.

## 18. editorial recusal /ˌɛtəˈrɪəl rɪˈkjuːzəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the withdrawal of an editor or journalist from a story because of a personal or financial conflict.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** editor/journalist rút khỏi story vì conflict personal hoặc financial.
**Grammar & collocations:** `editorial-recusal decision` — decision; `request editorial recusal` — yêu cầu.
**Examples:** `Editorial recusal kept the editor from assigning a story about a close relative's company.` → Recusal editorial ngăn editor assign story về company của relative gần.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `편집 회피`.

## 19. accountability report /əˌkaʊntəˈbɪləti rɪˈpɔrt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a public explanation of an organization's decisions, performance, mistakes, and corrective actions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** explanation công về decision, performance, mistake và corrective action của organization.
**Grammar & collocations:** `accountability-report section` — section; `publish an accountability report` — công bố.
**Examples:** `The accountability report described why the investigation was delayed and what the newsroom changed.` → Report accountability mô tả vì sao investigation delay và newsroom đổi gì.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `책무성 보고서`.

## 20. editorial transparency /ˌɛtəˈrɪəl trænˈspɛrənsi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** openness about how stories are selected, reported, corrected, funded, and presented.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** openness về cách story được select, report, correct, fund và present.
**Grammar & collocations:** `editorial-transparency standard` — standard; `increase editorial transparency` — tăng.
**Examples:** `Editorial transparency explained which evidence was still missing from the investigation.` → Transparency editorial giải thích evidence nào investigation vẫn thiếu.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `편집 투명성`.

## Review in context

An **editorial firewall** and **conflict disclosure** separate reporting from influence, while **sponsored content**, **native advertising**, and **product placement** require clear labels. **Source confidentiality** may be supported by a **shield law**, and a **right of reply** gives criticized people a response. A **public-interest test** weighs civic value against **privacy intrusion**; legal issues may include **defamation per se**, **actual malice**, and **fair comment**. A **retraction policy** and **correction culture** support repair, while an **ombuds editor**, an **ethics code**, and **editorial recusal** strengthen oversight. An **accountability report** and **editorial transparency** show how standards work in practice.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

**Editorial firewall** và **conflict disclosure** tách reporting khỏi influence, còn **sponsored content**, **native advertising** và **product placement** cần label rõ. **Source confidentiality** có thể được **shield law** hỗ trợ, và **right of reply** cho người bị criticize cơ hội response. **Public-interest test** cân civic value với **privacy intrusion**; vấn đề legal có thể gồm **defamation per se**, **actual malice** và **fair comment**. **Retraction policy** và **correction culture** hỗ trợ repair, còn **ombuds editor**, **ethics code** và **editorial recusal** củng cố oversight. **Accountability report** và **editorial transparency** cho thấy standard vận hành thực tế.
