# C1 Vocabulary 13 — Public-service media and governance

This lesson follows how public-service media is funded, governed, and protected while serving diverse audiences.

Flow: **charter renewal → license fee → broadcast mandate → public-service remit → governance board → news plurality → universal service → regional bureau → civic media → public-interest media → audience trust → service obligation → funding formula → oversight committee → editorial charter → public media remit → institutional independence → broadcast spectrum → regional service → media regulator**.

## 1. charter renewal /ˈtʃɑrtər rɪˈnuːəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the formal process of extending or revising the legal document that authorizes a public media organization to operate.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** process formal extend hoặc revise legal document cho phép public media organization operate.
**Grammar & collocations:** `periodic charter renewal` — renewal định kỳ; `debate a charter renewal` — tranh luận.
**Examples:** `The charter renewal required the broadcaster to explain how it served younger audiences.` → Renewal charter yêu cầu broadcaster explain cách serve audience trẻ hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `헌장 갱신`.

## 2. license fee /ˈlaɪsəns fiː/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a charge collected under law to help fund a broadcasting service, often from households or device owners.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** khoản charge theo law để fund broadcasting service, thường thu từ household hoặc device owner.
**Grammar & collocations:** `annual license fee` — fee hằng năm; `collect a license fee` — thu.
**Examples:** `The license fee gave the broadcaster a stable source of income outside commercial advertising.` → License fee cho broadcaster nguồn income ổn định ngoài advertising commercial.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `수신료`.

## 3. broadcast mandate /ˈbrɔdˌkæst ˈmændeɪt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the legal or public instruction defining what a broadcaster is expected to provide.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** instruction legal hoặc public định nghĩa broadcaster phải provide gì.
**Grammar & collocations:** `broad broadcast mandate` — mandate rộng; `fulfill a broadcast mandate` — thực hiện.
**Examples:** `The broadcast mandate required accurate news, educational programs, and emergency information.` → Mandate broadcast yêu cầu news accurate, program educational và emergency information.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `방송 책무`.

## 4. public-service remit /ˌpʌblɪk ˈsɜrvɪs rɪˈmɪt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the range of public benefits and responsibilities assigned to a media service.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** phạm vi benefit và responsibility public giao cho media service.
**Grammar & collocations:** `broad public-service remit` — remit rộng; `define the public-service remit` — định nghĩa.
**Examples:** `The public-service remit included programming for minority languages and remote regions.` → Remit public-service gồm program cho minority language và region xa.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `공공 서비스 책무`.

## 5. governance board /ˈɡʌvərnəns bɔrd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a body responsible for supervising an organization, setting high-level policy, and holding executives accountable.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** body supervise organization, set policy cấp cao và hold executive accountable.
**Grammar & collocations:** `independent governance board` — board độc lập; `appoint a governance board` — bổ nhiệm.
**Examples:** `The governance board published minutes from its debate about newsroom priorities.` → Board governance publish minutes debate về priority newsroom.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `거버넌스 이사회`.

## 6. news plurality /ˈnuːz pləˈrælɪti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the presence of many independent news voices, owners, viewpoints, and sources in a media system.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** sự hiện diện của nhiều voice, owner, viewpoint và source news độc lập.
**Grammar & collocations:** `protect news plurality` — bảo vệ; `measure news plurality` — đo.
**Examples:** `The regulator considered whether the merger would reduce news plurality.` → Regulator xem merger có giảm plurality news không.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `뉴스 다원성`.

## 7. universal service /ˌjuːnəˈvɜrsəl ˈsɜrvɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a policy goal that makes a communication or media service available to everyone, including people in remote or disadvantaged areas.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** goal policy làm service communication hoặc media available cho tất cả, kể cả nơi xa hoặc disadvantaged.
**Grammar & collocations:** `universal-service obligation` — obligation; `extend universal service` — mở rộng.
**Examples:** `Universal service kept the radio signal available in mountainous communities.` → Universal service giữ signal radio available ở community miền núi.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `보편적 서비스`.

## 8. regional bureau /ˈriːdʒənəl ˈbjʊroʊ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a local office of a media organization that reports on a particular region.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** office local của media organization report về một region cụ thể.
**Grammar & collocations:** `well-staffed regional bureau` — bureau đủ staff; `open a regional bureau` — mở.
**Examples:** `The regional bureau understood the transport problems that national editors had overlooked.` → Bureau regional hiểu problem transport national editor bỏ sót.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `지역 지국`.

## 9. civic media /ˈsɪvɪk ˈmiːdiə/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** media that helps people understand public affairs, participate in community life, and hold institutions to account.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** media giúp người hiểu public affairs, tham gia community life và hold institution accountable.
**Grammar & collocations:** `local civic media` — media civic địa phương; `support civic media` — support.
**Examples:** `Civic media explained the budget process before residents voted on the proposal.` → Media civic giải thích budget process trước khi resident vote proposal.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `시민 미디어`.

## 10. public-interest media /ˌpʌblɪk ˈɪntrəst ˈmiːdiə/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** media whose primary purpose is to inform and benefit the public rather than maximize private profit.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** media ưu tiên inform và benefit public hơn maximize private profit.
**Grammar & collocations:** `independent public-interest media` — media độc lập; `fund public-interest media` — fund.
**Examples:** `Public-interest media investigated a problem that commercial outlets considered too expensive.` → Media public-interest investigate problem commercial outlet thấy quá expensive.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `공익 미디어`.

## 11. audience trust /ˈɔdiəns trʌst/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the confidence audiences place in a media organization's accuracy, fairness, and intentions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** confidence audience đặt vào accuracy, fairness và intention của media organization.
**Grammar & collocations:** `fragile audience trust` — trust mong manh; `maintain audience trust` — duy trì.
**Examples:** `Transparent corrections helped rebuild audience trust after a serious mistake.` → Correction minh bạch giúp rebuild trust audience sau mistake nghiêm trọng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `시청자 신뢰`.

## 12. service obligation /ˈsɜrvɪs ˌɑbləˈɡeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a required duty to provide a defined service, often to groups or places that markets might underserve.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** duty bắt buộc provide service cho group hoặc place market có thể underserve.
**Grammar & collocations:** `statutory service obligation` — obligation theo statute; `meet a service obligation` — đáp ứng.
**Examples:** `The service obligation required subtitles and sign-language interpretation for major broadcasts.` → Obligation service yêu cầu subtitle và interpretation sign-language cho broadcast lớn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `서비스 의무`.

## 19. regional service /ˈriːdʒənəl ˈsɜrvɪs/

## 20. media regulator /ˈmiːdiə ˈrɛɡjəˌleɪtər/

**Core meaning (English):** a public authority that sets and enforces rules for broadcasters, platforms, or other media services.

## Review in context

During a **charter renewal**, a public broadcaster may defend its **license fee** by explaining how the **broadcast mandate** and **public-service remit** benefit citizens. A **governance board** can protect **news plurality** and **universal service** by funding a **regional bureau**, supporting **civic media**, and preserving **public-interest media**. These choices can strengthen **audience trust** and meet a **service obligation**. A transparent **funding formula** lets an **oversight committee** inspect the arrangement, while an **editorial charter** explains how it serves the wider **public media remit**. **Institutional independence** also depends on fair access to the **broadcast spectrum**, a reliable **regional service**, and an impartial **media regulator**.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:** Trong một đợt **charter renewal**, public broadcaster có thể bảo vệ **license fee** bằng cách giải thích **broadcast mandate** và **public-service remit** benefit citizen thế nào. **Governance board** có thể bảo vệ **news plurality** và **universal service** bằng cách fund **regional bureau**, support **civic media** và duy trì **public-interest media**. Những lựa chọn này có thể strengthen **audience trust** và đáp ứng **service obligation**. **Funding formula** minh bạch cho **oversight committee** inspect arrangement, còn **editorial charter** giải thích cách organization serve **public media remit** rộng hơn. **Institutional independence** cũng phụ thuộc vào access công bằng tới **broadcast spectrum**, một **regional service** đáng tin và **media regulator** impartial.
