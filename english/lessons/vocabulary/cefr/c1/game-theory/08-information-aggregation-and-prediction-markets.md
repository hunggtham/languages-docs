# C1 Vocabulary 08 — Information aggregation and prediction markets

This lesson follows how dispersed signals become collective forecasts, and how strategic behavior can improve or distort that process.

Flow: **information cascade → herding → wisdom of crowds → prediction market → market scoring rule → logarithmic market scoring rule → aggregation bias → Condorcet jury theorem → competence probability → common signal → idiosyncratic signal → signal correlation → information aggregation → information revelation → no-trade theorem → rational expectations → forecast tournament → information mirroring → Bayesian aggregation → consensus forecast**.

## 1. information cascade /ˌɪnfərˈmeɪʃən kæˈskeɪd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a process in which people ignore their private information and follow earlier public actions that appear informative.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** process người sau bỏ qua private information và follow public action trước vì tưởng action đó informative.
**Grammar & collocations:** `information-cascade model` — model; `trigger an information cascade` — kích hoạt.
**Examples:** `An information cascade caused later buyers to copy a product review they had not read.` → Cascade information khiến buyer sau copy review product mà họ chưa đọc.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `정보 연쇄`.

## 2. herding /ˈhɝdɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** behavior in which people converge on the same choice because they observe others making it, rather than because their own evidence agrees.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mọi người converge cùng choice vì thấy người khác chọn, không hẳn vì evidence riêng giống nhau.
**Grammar & collocations:** `investor herding` — herd investor; `measure herding behavior` — đo.
**Examples:** `Herding pushed several funds into the same trade even though their private research differed.` → Herding đẩy nhiều fund vào cùng trade dù research riêng khác nhau.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `군집 행동`.

## 3. wisdom of crowds /ˈwɪzdəm əv kraʊdz/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the idea that combining many independent judgments can produce a better estimate than relying on one expert.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** gộp nhiều judgment độc lập có thể tạo estimate tốt hơn dựa vào một expert.
**Grammar & collocations:** `wisdom-of-crowds estimate` — estimate; `aggregate independent judgments` — gộp.
**Examples:** `The wisdom of crowds produced a useful demand estimate when each participant had a different local signal.` → Wisdom crowds tạo estimate demand hữu ích khi mỗi participant có signal địa phương khác.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `군중의 지혜`.

## 4. prediction market /prɪˈdɪkʃən ˈmɑrkət/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a market in which contracts tied to future events are traded so that prices summarize participants' forecasts.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** market trade contract gắn với future event, để price tóm tắt forecast participant.
**Grammar & collocations:** `prediction-market price` — price; `trade a forecast contract` — trade.
**Examples:** `The prediction market placed a high price on approval after new survey evidence arrived.` → Market prediction đặt price cao cho approval sau survey evidence mới.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `예측 시장`.

## 5. market scoring rule /ˈmɑrkət ˈskɔrɪŋ ruːl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a mechanism that continuously rewards traders for moving a public probability estimate toward their reported belief.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mechanism thưởng trader khi họ move public probability estimate về belief họ report.
**Grammar & collocations:** `market-scoring-rule mechanism` — mechanism; `update a probability quote` — cập nhật.
**Examples:** `The market scoring rule let participants contribute information without waiting for a final buyer.` → Rule scoring market cho participant đóng góp information mà không cần chờ buyer cuối.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `시장 점수 규칙`.

## 6. logarithmic market scoring rule /ˌlɔɡəˈrɪðmɪk ˈmɑrkət ˈskɔrɪŋ ruːl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a market scoring rule whose logarithmic score rewards probability updates while limiting the market maker's worst-case loss.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** rule scoring dùng score logarithmic, thưởng probability update và giới hạn worst-case loss của market maker.
**Grammar & collocations:** `logarithmic-market-scoring-rule subsidy` — subsidy; `quote a probability` — quote.
**Examples:** `A logarithmic market scoring rule kept the forecast open even when only a few traders were active.` → Rule scoring logarithmic giữ forecast mở dù chỉ ít trader active.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `로그 시장 점수 규칙`.

## 7. aggregation bias /ˌæɡrəˈɡeɪʃən baɪəs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** distortion caused when information is combined in a way that hides important differences among sources or groups.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** distortion khi gộp information làm ẩn difference quan trọng giữa source hoặc group.
**Grammar & collocations:** `aggregation-bias correction` — correction; `detect aggregation bias` — phát hiện.
**Examples:** `Aggregation bias hid the fact that the national forecast was accurate only for large cities.` → Bias aggregation che việc forecast quốc gia chỉ chính xác ở city lớn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `집계 편향`.

## 8. Condorcet jury theorem /kənˈdɔrsɛt ˈdʒʊri ˈθiərəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the result that a majority decision becomes more likely to be correct as independent voters become individually more likely than not to be correct.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** majority decision dễ đúng hơn khi voter độc lập có probability đúng lớn hơn 50% và số voter tăng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `Condorcet-jury-theorem assumption` — assumption; `apply the jury theorem` — áp dụng.
**Examples:** `The Condorcet jury theorem supported a larger panel when members' signals were independent.` → Theorem jury Condorcet ủng hộ panel lớn hơn khi signal member độc lập.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `콩도르세 배심 정리`.

## 9. competence probability /ˈkɑmpətən̩s ˌprɑbəˈbɪləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the probability that an individual decision-maker chooses the correct option given the available evidence.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** probability decision-maker cá nhân chọn đúng theo evidence có sẵn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `competence-probability estimate` — estimate; `raise individual competence` — tăng.
**Examples:** `The panel's competence probability rose after members completed domain training.` → Probability competence của panel tăng sau training domain.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `능력 확률`.

## 10. common signal /ˈkɑmən ˈsɪɡnəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** information about a state that is observed by several or all participants.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** information về state được nhiều hoặc mọi participant quan sát.
**Grammar & collocations:** `common-signal precision` — precision; `condition on a common signal` — condition.
**Examples:** `The common signal moved every trader's forecast toward higher inflation.` → Signal chung kéo forecast mọi trader về inflation cao hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `공통 신호`.

## 11. idiosyncratic signal /ˌɪdioʊsɪŋˈkrætɪk ˈsɪɡnəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** information observed by one participant or a small subset that is not directly available to everyone else.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** information chỉ một participant hoặc nhóm nhỏ thấy, không trực tiếp available cho mọi người.
**Grammar & collocations:** `idiosyncratic-signal noise` — noise; `combine idiosyncratic signals` — gộp.
**Examples:** `Each farmer contributed an idiosyncratic signal about rainfall in a different field.` → Mỗi farmer đóng góp signal riêng về rainfall ở field khác nhau.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `특이 신호`.

## 12. signal correlation /ˈsɪɡnəl ˌkɔrəˈleɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the degree to which information signals received by different participants move together.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mức signal information của participant khác nhau move cùng nhau.
**Grammar & collocations:** `signal-correlation estimate` — estimate; `allow correlated signals` — cho phép.
**Examples:** `High signal correlation made the crowd seem large without adding much independent information.` → Correlation signal cao khiến crowd có vẻ lớn nhưng không thêm nhiều information độc lập.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `신호 상관관계`.

## 13. information aggregation /ˌɪnfərˈmeɪʃən ˌæɡrəˈɡeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the process through which dispersed information is combined into a collective belief, price, or decision.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** process gộp information phân tán thành belief, price hoặc decision collective.
**Grammar & collocations:** `information-aggregation mechanism` — mechanism; `improve information aggregation` — cải thiện.
**Examples:** `The exchange improved information aggregation by publishing trades immediately.` → Exchange cải thiện aggregation information bằng cách công bố trade ngay.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `정보 집계`.

## 14. information revelation /ˌɪnfərˈmeɪʃən ˌrɛvəˈleɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the extent to which a market, institution, or interaction makes privately held information observable to others.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mức market/institution/interaction khiến private information trở nên observable cho người khác.
**Grammar & collocations:** `information-revelation mechanism` — mechanism; `promote information revelation` — thúc đẩy.
**Examples:** `Transparent bids increased information revelation but also made coordination easier.` → Bid minh bạch tăng revelation information nhưng cũng làm coordination dễ hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `정보 공개`.

## 15. no-trade theorem /ˈnoʊ treɪd ˈθiərəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the result that rational, risk-neutral agents with a common prior and identical information may have no reason to trade solely because they disagree.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** agent rational, risk-neutral có prior chung và information giống nhau không có lý do trade chỉ vì disagreement.
**Grammar & collocations:** `no-trade-theorem implication` — implication; `explain absent trade` — giải thích.
**Examples:** `The no-trade theorem made the observed volume puzzling unless traders had different signals or needs.` → Theorem no-trade khiến volume quan sát trở nên khó hiểu nếu trader không có signal hoặc need khác.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `거래 없음 정리`.

## 16. rational expectations /ˈræʃənəl ˌɛkspɛkˈteɪʃənz/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** expectations that use the available information and the model's implications without systematic predictable errors.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** expectation dùng information sẵn có và implication model mà không mắc predictable error có hệ thống.
**Grammar & collocations:** `rational-expectations assumption` — assumption; `form rational expectations` — hình thành.
**Examples:** `Rational expectations did not mean every trader predicted the exact outcome, only that errors were not systematic.` → Expectations rational không có nghĩa mọi trader đoán chính xác, chỉ là error không systematic.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `합리적 기대`.

## 17. forecast tournament /ˈfɔrˌkæst ˈtʊrnəmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a structured comparison in which competing forecasters are scored on predictions made over the same period.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** comparison có cấu trúc trong đó forecaster cạnh tranh được score trên prediction cùng period.
**Grammar & collocations:** `forecast-tournament ranking` — ranking; `enter a forecast tournament` — tham gia.
**Examples:** `The forecast tournament rewarded calibrated predictions rather than dramatic but unreliable calls.` → Tournament forecast thưởng prediction calibrated thay vì call dramatic nhưng không reliable.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `예측 대회`.

## 18. information mirroring /ˌɪnfərˈmeɪʃən ˈmɪrərɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** behavior in which an agent's report mainly reproduces public information instead of adding an independent private signal.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** report của agent chủ yếu lặp public information thay vì thêm private signal độc lập.
**Grammar & collocations:** `information-mirroring behavior` — behavior; `measure information mirroring` — đo.
**Examples:** `Information mirroring made the survey appear more precise than the underlying independent evidence justified.` → Mirroring information khiến survey trông precise hơn evidence độc lập thực sự.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `정보 미러링`.

## 19. Bayesian aggregation /beɪˈziən ˌæɡrəˈɡeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** combining prior beliefs and multiple signals by using Bayes' rule to form an updated collective probability.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** gộp prior belief và nhiều signal bằng Bayes để tạo collective probability update.
**Grammar & collocations:** `Bayesian-aggregation formula` — formula; `update a posterior probability` — cập nhật.
**Examples:** `Bayesian aggregation gave more weight to the laboratory signal because its error rate was lower.` → Aggregation Bayesian gán trọng số cao hơn cho signal laboratory vì error rate thấp hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `베이지안 집계`.

## 20. consensus forecast /kənˈsɛns ˈfɔrˌkæst/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a forecast formed by combining the judgments of several analysts or models into one shared prediction.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** forecast tạo bằng cách gộp judgment của nhiều analyst hoặc model thành một prediction chung.
**Grammar & collocations:** `consensus-forecast revision` — revision; `publish a consensus forecast` — công bố.
**Examples:** `The consensus forecast changed only after several independent models revised their inflation paths.` → Forecast consensus chỉ đổi sau khi nhiều model độc lập revise inflation path.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `컨센서스 예측`.

## Review in context

An **information cascade** can create **herding**, while the **wisdom of crowds** depends on independent signals. A **prediction market** may use a **market scoring rule** or a **logarithmic market scoring rule** to turn updates into prices, but **aggregation bias** can remain. The **Condorcet jury theorem** links accuracy to **competence probability**. Analysts distinguish a **common signal** from an **idiosyncratic signal**, measure **signal correlation**, and study **information aggregation** and **information revelation**. The **no-trade theorem** contrasts with **rational expectations** in explaining volume. A **forecast tournament**, **information mirroring**, **Bayesian aggregation**, and a **consensus forecast** provide additional ways to compare collective predictions.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

**Information cascade** có thể tạo **herding**, còn **wisdom of crowds** phụ thuộc signal độc lập. **Prediction market** có thể dùng **market scoring rule** hoặc **logarithmic market scoring rule** để biến update thành price, nhưng **aggregation bias** vẫn có thể tồn tại. **Condorcet jury theorem** nối accuracy với **competence probability**. Analyst phân biệt **common signal** với **idiosyncratic signal**, đo **signal correlation** và nghiên cứu **information aggregation** cùng **information revelation**. **No-trade theorem** đối lập với **rational expectations** khi giải thích volume. **Forecast tournament**, **information mirroring**, **Bayesian aggregation** và **consensus forecast** cung cấp thêm cách so sánh collective prediction.
