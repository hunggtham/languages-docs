# C1 Vocabulary 10 — Experimental game theory and strategic behavior

This lesson follows how researchers test strategic models, elicit beliefs, and separate genuine behavior from artifacts of an experiment.

Flow: **response method → strategy method → belief elicitation → belief report → prediction task → experimenter demand → demand characteristic → subject pool → session effect → random rematching → partner matching → role asymmetry → payoff salience → monetary incentive → participation fee → show-up fee → lab-in-the-field experiment → artefactual field experiment → natural field experiment → strategic sophistication**.

## 1. response method /rɪˈspɑns ˈmɛθəd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the procedure used to record a participant's action, choice, or stated belief in an experiment.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** procedure ghi action, choice hoặc belief mà participant đưa ra trong experiment.
**Grammar & collocations:** `response-method comparison` — comparison; `choose a response method` — chọn.
**Examples:** `The response method let participants submit a decision before seeing the other player's action.` → Method response cho participant submit decision trước khi thấy action player kia.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `응답 방법`.

## 2. strategy method /ˈstrætədʒi ˈmɛθəd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an experimental procedure in which a participant specifies an action for every possible move by another participant.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** procedure participant specify action cho mọi move khả dĩ của participant khác.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strategy-method design` — design; `elicit a complete strategy` — elicitation.
**Examples:** `The strategy method revealed how a responder would react to both fair and unfair offers.` → Method strategy cho thấy responder phản ứng với offer fair và unfair ra sao.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `전략 방법`.

## 3. belief elicitation /bɪˈliːf ɪˌlɪsəˈteɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the process of asking participants to state their beliefs about an event, action, or probability.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** process yêu cầu participant state belief về event, action hoặc probability.
**Grammar & collocations:** `belief-elicitation task` — task; `elicit a probability belief` — elicitation.
**Examples:** `Belief elicitation showed that players expected cooperation more often than their actions suggested.` → Elicitation belief cho thấy player kỳ vọng cooperation thường hơn action thực tế.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `신념 도출`.

## 4. belief report /bɪˈliːf rɪˈpɔrt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a participant's stated probability or forecast about another player's behavior or an uncertain state.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** probability hoặc forecast participant report về behavior player khác hoặc state bất định.
**Grammar & collocations:** `belief-report accuracy` — accuracy; `submit a belief report` — nộp.
**Examples:** `The belief report predicted a high chance of defection after a missed payment.` → Report belief dự đoán chance defect cao sau missed payment.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `신념 보고`.

## 5. prediction task /prɪˈdɪkʃən tæsk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an experimental task in which participants forecast another person's action, a market outcome, or a future event.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** task experiment participant forecast action người khác, market outcome hoặc future event.
**Grammar & collocations:** `prediction-task score` — score; `complete a prediction task` — hoàn thành.
**Examples:** `The prediction task tested whether participants understood the payoff consequences of a new rule.` → Task prediction kiểm tra participant hiểu consequence payoff của rule mới không.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `예측 과제`.

## 6. experimenter demand /ɪkˌspɛrɪˈmɛntər dɪˈmænd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a participant's tendency to change behavior because the study appears to suggest what response the researcher wants.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** participant đổi behavior vì study có vẻ gợi ý researcher muốn response nào.
**Grammar & collocations:** `experimenter-demand concern` — concern; `minimize experimenter demand` — giảm.
**Examples:** `Anonymous instructions reduced experimenter demand in the cooperation task.` → Instruction anonymous giảm demand experimenter trong task cooperation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `실험자 요구 효과`.

## 7. demand characteristic /dɪˈmænd ˌkærəktəˈrɪstɪk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a feature of a study that reveals its apparent purpose and may influence participants' responses.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** feature của study làm lộ apparent purpose và có thể ảnh hưởng response participant.
**Grammar & collocations:** `demand-characteristic bias` — bias; `mask a demand characteristic` — che.
**Examples:** `The neutral cover story hid a demand characteristic that could have encouraged generosity.` → Cover story trung lập che characteristic demand có thể khuyến khích generosity.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `요구 특성`.

## 8. subject pool /ˈsʌbdʒɪkt puːl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the population of people from which an experiment recruits its participants.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** population người mà experiment recruit participant.
**Grammar & collocations:** `diverse subject pool` — pool đa dạng; `recruit from a subject pool` — recruit.
**Examples:** `The subject pool included students, local residents, and experienced traders.` → Pool subject gồm student, resident địa phương và trader có kinh nghiệm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `피험자 풀`.

## 9. session effect /ˈsɛʃən ɪˈfɛkt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a difference in behavior caused by the time, group, facilitator, or setting of an experimental session.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** difference behavior do time, group, facilitator hoặc setting của session experiment.
**Grammar & collocations:** `session-effect control` — control; `estimate session effects` — ước lượng.
**Examples:** `The analysis included session effects because morning groups cooperated more often.` → Analysis gồm effect session vì group buổi sáng cooperate thường hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `세션 효과`.

## 10. random rematching /ˈrændəm riˈmætʃɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** repeatedly pairing participants with different partners according to a random procedure.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** pair participant với partner khác nhiều lần theo procedure random.
**Grammar & collocations:** `random-rematching protocol` — protocol; `rematch participants randomly` — rematch.
**Examples:** `Random rematching prevented a personal reputation from explaining every cooperative choice.` → Rematch random ngăn reputation cá nhân giải thích mọi choice cooperation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `무작위 재매칭`.

## 11. partner matching /ˈpɑrtnər ˈmætʃɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the rule or procedure used to assign participants to one another in an interaction.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** rule/procedure assign participant với nhau trong interaction.
**Grammar & collocations:** `partner-matching rule` — rule; `control partner matching` — kiểm soát.
**Examples:** `Partner matching determined whether participants met the same opponent in every round.` → Matching partner quyết định participant gặp cùng opponent mọi round hay không.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `파트너 매칭`.

## 12. role asymmetry /roʊl eɪˈsɪmətri/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a difference between participants' powers, information, responsibilities, or available actions in an experiment.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** difference về power, information, responsibility hoặc action giữa participant.
**Grammar & collocations:** `role-asymmetry treatment` — treatment; `introduce role asymmetry` — tạo.
**Examples:** `Role asymmetry gave one player the first move and the other player a veto.` → Asymmetry role cho một player đi trước và player kia veto.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `역할 비대칭`.

## 13. payoff salience /ˈpeɪɔf ˈseɪliəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the degree to which the size and consequences of experimental payoffs are noticeable to participants.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mức size và consequence payoff experiment dễ nhận thấy với participant.
**Grammar & collocations:** `payoff-salience manipulation` — manipulation; `increase payoff salience` — tăng.
**Examples:** `Higher payoff salience made participants respond more carefully to a rare penalty.` → Salience payoff cao khiến participant phản ứng cẩn thận hơn với penalty hiếm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `보수 현저성`.

## 14. monetary incentive /ˈmɑnəˌtɛri ɪnˈsɛntɪv/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a payment or financial consequence intended to influence a participant's effort or choice.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** payment hoặc financial consequence nhằm ảnh hưởng effort/choice participant.
**Grammar & collocations:** `monetary-incentive scheme` — scheme; `provide a monetary incentive` — cung cấp.
**Examples:** `The monetary incentive rewarded accurate probability reports rather than fast guesses.` → Incentive tiền thưởng report probability chính xác chứ không phải đoán nhanh.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `금전적 유인`.

## 15. participation fee /pɑrˌtɪsəˈpeɪʃən fiː/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a fixed payment given to a participant for taking part, regardless of the participant's decisions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** payment cố định cho participant tham gia, không phụ thuộc decision.
**Grammar & collocations:** `participation-fee schedule` — schedule; `pay a participation fee` — trả.
**Examples:** `The participation fee compensated volunteers before any performance bonus was calculated.` → Fee participation bù cho volunteer trước khi tính performance bonus.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `참여 수당`.

## 16. show-up fee /ˈʃoʊ ʌp fiː/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a small payment made simply for arriving at a scheduled experimental session.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** payment nhỏ chỉ cần participant đến session theo lịch.
**Grammar & collocations:** `show-up-fee policy` — policy; `receive a show-up fee` — nhận.
**Examples:** `A show-up fee reduced the risk that selected participants would fail to attend.` → Fee show-up giảm risk participant được chọn không đến.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `참석 수당`.

## 17. lab-in-the-field experiment /læb ɪn ðə fiːld ɪkˈspɛrəmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a controlled experiment using a participant group from a real-world population rather than a conventional student laboratory sample.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** experiment controlled dùng participant từ population đời thực thay vì sample student lab thông thường.
**Grammar & collocations:** `lab-in-the-field design` — design; `run a lab-in-the-field experiment` — thực hiện.
**Examples:** `The lab-in-the-field experiment tested bargaining among professional procurement officers.` → Experiment lab-in-field test bargaining giữa procurement officer chuyên nghiệp.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `현장 실험실 실험`.

## 18. artefactual field experiment /ˌɑrtəˈfæktʃuəl fiːld ɪkˈspɛrəmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a controlled experiment conducted with a real-world population while participants know they are taking part in a study.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** experiment controlled với real-world population, participant biết mình đang tham gia study.
**Grammar & collocations:** `artefactual-field-experiment sample` — sample; `conduct an artefactual field experiment` — thực hiện.
**Examples:** `The artefactual field experiment recruited farmers who completed the same decision task as laboratory subjects.` → Experiment field artefactual recruit farmer làm cùng task decision như subject lab.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `인공적 현장 실험`.

## 19. natural field experiment /ˈnætʃərəl fiːld ɪkˈspɛrəmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a field study in which participants usually do not know they are being studied and encounter the intervention in ordinary life.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** field study participant thường không biết đang được study và gặp intervention trong đời sống thường.
**Grammar & collocations:** `natural-field-experiment setting` — setting; `observe behavior unobtrusively` — quan sát kín.
**Examples:** `A natural field experiment tested whether a reminder changed actual charitable giving at a public event.` → Experiment field tự nhiên test reminder đổi donation thật ở event công không.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `자연 현장 실험`.

## 20. strategic sophistication /strəˈtiːdʒɪk səˌfɪstəˈkeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the depth with which a participant anticipates other players' reasoning and adapts a strategy.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** độ sâu participant đoán reasoning của player khác và adapt strategy.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strategic-sophistication measure` — measure; `model strategic sophistication` — mô hình hóa.
**Examples:** `The study linked strategic sophistication to experience in repeated negotiation.` → Study nối sophistication strategic với experience trong negotiation lặp.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `전략적 정교함`.

## Review in context

An experiment can compare a **response method** with a **strategy method**, combine **belief elicitation**, a **belief report**, and a **prediction task**, and guard against **experimenter demand** and a **demand characteristic**. The **subject pool** and **session effect** matter, as do **random rematching**, **partner matching**, and **role asymmetry**. Researchers can vary **payoff salience**, a **monetary incentive**, a **participation fee**, or a **show-up fee**. A **lab-in-the-field experiment**, **artefactual field experiment**, or **natural field experiment** changes realism, while **strategic sophistication** describes how deeply participants reason.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

Experiment có thể so sánh **response method** với **strategy method**, kết hợp **belief elicitation**, **belief report** và **prediction task**, đồng thời kiểm soát **experimenter demand** và **demand characteristic**. **Subject pool** và **session effect** quan trọng, cũng như **random rematching**, **partner matching** và **role asymmetry**. Researcher có thể thay đổi **payoff salience**, **monetary incentive**, **participation fee** hoặc **show-up fee**. **Lab-in-the-field experiment**, **artefactual field experiment** hoặc **natural field experiment** đổi mức realism, còn **strategic sophistication** mô tả độ sâu reasoning của participant.
