# C1 Vocabulary 24 — Behavioral culture, identity, and participation

This lesson follows how people interpret cultural practices, status signals, representation, memory, and belonging when deciding whether to participate.

Flow: **cultural norm perception → symbolic consumption → status signaling → symbolic identity utility → reference group cue → taste hierarchy belief → cultural distance perception → norm enforcement expectation → ritual commitment → authenticity heuristic → heritage value framing → cultural appropriation concern → audience effect → public performance anxiety → collective memory cue → narrative transportation → stereotype threat cue → representation credibility → cultural participation friction → pluralism preference**.

## 1. cultural norm perception /ˈkʌltʃərəl nɔrm pərˈsɛpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a judgment about what behavior, taste, or participation a cultural group considers ordinary or appropriate.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** judgment group cultural xem behavior, taste hoặc participation nào ordinary hay appropriate.
**Grammar & collocations:** `accurate cultural norm perception` — perception; `change cultural norm perception` — change.
**Examples:** `Cultural norm perception shaped whether newcomers felt comfortable speaking during the ceremony.` → Perception norm cultural shape newcomer feel comfortable speak trong ceremony.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `문화 규범 인식`.

## 2. symbolic consumption /sɪmˈbɑlɪk kənˈsʌmpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** using goods, services, or experiences to communicate identity, status, values, or group membership.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** use goods, service hoặc experience để communicate identity, status, value hoặc group.
**Grammar & collocations:** `visible symbolic consumption` — consumption; `study symbolic consumption` — study.
**Examples:** `Symbolic consumption made the handmade bag valuable beyond its practical function.` → Consumption symbolic làm bag handmade valuable vượt function practical.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `상징적 소비`.

## 3. status signaling /ˈsteɪtəs ˈsɪɡnəlɪŋ/

## 4. symbolic identity utility /sɪmˈbɑlɪk aɪˈdɛntəti juˈtɪləti/

**Core meaning (English):** the personal value gained when an object or activity expresses who someone is or wants to become.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** value personal gained khi object hoặc activity express identity hiện tại hay mong muốn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high symbolic identity utility` — utility; `estimate symbolic identity utility` — estimate.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `상징적 정체성 효용`.

## 6. taste hierarchy belief /teɪst ˈhaɪəˌrɑrki bɪˈliːf/

## 7. cultural distance perception /ˈkʌltʃərəl ˈdɪstəns pərˈsɛpʃən/

**Core meaning (English):** a judgment about how different another group's practices, values, or communication style seem from one's own.

## 8. norm enforcement expectation /nɔrm ɪnˈfɔrsmənt ˌɛkspɛkˈteɪʃən/

## 9. ritual commitment /ˈrɪtʃuəl kəˈmɪtmənt/

**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** willingness invest time và effort vào practice lặp lại tạo meaning hoặc continuity shared.

## 10. authenticity heuristic /ˌɔθɛnˈtɪsəti hjʊrɪstɪk/

**Core meaning (English):** a quick judgment that a person, object, or performance is genuine rather than staged or commercialized.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** judgment nhanh person, object hoặc performance genuine chứ không staged hay commercialized.

## 11. heritage value framing /ˈhɛrətɪdʒ ˈvælju ˈfreɪmɪŋ/

## Review in context

People act within a **cultural norm perception** that tells them what feels ordinary, and **symbolic consumption** can communicate identity or status. **Status signaling** may offer **symbolic identity utility**, while a **reference group cue** and a **taste hierarchy belief** shape whose choices appear respectable. **Cultural distance perception** affects openness, and **norm enforcement expectation** can either protect or restrict a practice. **Ritual commitment** preserves continuity, but an **authenticity heuristic** may reject a new version as commercial. **Heritage value framing** can support preservation, while a **cultural appropriation concern** asks who has credit and power. The **audience effect** may create **public performance anxiety**, yet a **collective memory cue** and **narrative transportation** can deepen connection. A **stereotype threat cue** undermines performance, whereas **representation credibility** builds trust. Reducing **cultural participation friction** makes a **pluralism preference** practical rather than merely rhetorical.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

Người ta act trong **cultural norm perception** cho biết điều gì ordinary, và **symbolic consumption** communicate identity hoặc status. **Status signaling** có thể tạo **symbolic identity utility**, còn **reference group cue** và **taste hierarchy belief** shape choice nào trông respectable. **Cultural distance perception** ảnh hưởng openness, còn **norm enforcement expectation** có thể protect hoặc restrict practice. **Ritual commitment** giữ continuity, nhưng **authenticity heuristic** có thể reject version mới như commercial. **Heritage value framing** hỗ trợ preservation, còn **cultural appropriation concern** hỏi ai có credit và power. **Audience effect** có thể tạo **public performance anxiety**, nhưng **collective memory cue** và **narrative transportation** deepen connection. **Stereotype threat cue** undermine performance, còn **representation credibility** build trust. Giảm **cultural participation friction** làm **pluralism preference** trở nên practical thay vì chỉ rhetorical.
