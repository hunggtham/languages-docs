# C1 Vocabulary 30 — Behavioral housing and neighborhood choice

This lesson follows how households search for homes, weigh costs, interpret neighborhoods, and respond to insecurity, maintenance, and shared amenities.

Flow: **housing search friction → rent salience → mortgage payment illusion → homeownership identity → neighborhood choice framing → school access perception → landlord trust → tenant bargaining power belief → housing scarcity expectation → moving cost aversion → renovation optimism → maintenance neglect → energy comfort tradeoff → housing insecurity perception → eviction stigma → property tax salience → shared amenity reciprocity → housing queue fairness → speculative demand cue → stable home value framing**.

## 1. housing search friction /ˈhaʊzɪŋ sɝtʃ ˈfrɪkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** time, effort, uncertainty, and paperwork that make finding and comparing homes difficult.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** time, effort, uncertainty và paperwork làm find và compare home khó.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high housing search friction` — friction; `reduce housing search friction` — reduce.
**Examples:** `Housing search friction forced the couple to decide from incomplete information before the listing disappeared.` → Friction search housing buộc couple decide từ information incomplete trước khi listing disappear.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `주택 탐색 마찰`.

## 2. rent salience /rɛnt ˈseɪliəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** how noticeable and psychologically important a recurring rent payment is in household decisions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mức rent payment recurring noticeable và psychologically important.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high rent salience` — salience; `increase rent salience` — increase.
**Examples:** `Rent salience rose when the tenant compared the monthly charge with take-home income.` → Salience rent tăng khi tenant compare charge monthly với income take-home.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `임대료 현저성`.

## 3. mortgage payment illusion /ˈmɔrɡɪdʒ ˈpeɪmənt ɪˈluːʒən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** underestimating the full cost of owning a home by focusing on the regular mortgage payment alone.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** underestimate full cost own home vì chỉ focus mortgage payment regular.
**Grammar & collocations:** `dangerous mortgage payment illusion` — illusion; `correct a mortgage payment illusion` — correct.
**Examples:** `Mortgage payment illusion hid insurance, repairs, and taxes from the first-time buyer.` → Illusion payment mortgage che insurance, repair và tax với buyer lần đầu.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `주택담보대출 상환액 착시`.

## 4. homeownership identity /ˈhoʊmˌoʊnərˌʃɪp aɪˈdɛntəti/

**Core meaning (English):** a sense that owning a home is a central part of personal success, security, or adulthood.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** sense own home là phần central của success, security hoặc adulthood.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong homeownership identity` — identity; `question homeownership identity` — question.
**Examples:** `Homeownership identity made the family accept a long commute to keep a private yard.` → Identity homeownership khiến family accept commute dài để giữ yard private.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `주택 소유 정체성`.

## 5. neighborhood choice framing /ˈneɪbərˌhʊd tʃɔɪs ˈfreɪmɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the way choosing a neighborhood is presented as a trade-off among safety, status, access, community, and cost.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cách present choose neighborhood như trade-off safety, status, access, community và cost.
**Grammar & collocations:** `balanced neighborhood choice framing` — framing; `change neighborhood choice framing` — change.
**Examples:** `Neighborhood choice framing shifted from prestige toward access to childcare and public transport.` → Framing choice neighborhood shift từ prestige sang access childcare và transport public.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `지역 선택 프레이밍`.

## 6. school access perception /skuːl ˈæksɛs pərˈsɛpʃən/

**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** judgment child neighborhood dễ reach education suitable và quality cao tới đâu.
**Grammar & collocations:** `unequal school access perception` — perception; `improve school access perception` — improve.

## 7. landlord trust /ˈlændˌlɔrd trʌst/

## 10. moving cost aversion /ˈmuːvɪŋ kɔst əˈvɝʒən/

## 11. renovation optimism /ˌrɛnəˈveɪʃən ˈɑptəˌmɪzəm/

## 12. maintenance neglect /ˈmeɪntənəns nəˈɡlɛkt/

## 13. energy comfort tradeoff /ˈɛnərdʒi ˈkʌmfərt ˈtreɪdˌɔf/

**Core meaning (English):** the choice between reducing energy use and preserving a preferred level of warmth, cooling, light, or convenience.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `에너지 편안함 상충`.

## 19. speculative demand cue /ˌspɛkjəˈleɪtɪv dɪˈmænd kjuː/

## 20. stable home value framing /ˈsteɪbəl hoʊm ˈvælju ˈfreɪmɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** presenting a home as a source of security, continuity, and use rather than only as a financial asset.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** present home như source security, continuity và use thay vì chỉ financial asset.
**Grammar & collocations:** `protective stable home value framing` — framing; `use stable home value framing` — use.
**Examples:** `Stable home value framing encouraged the cooperative to prioritize long-term residents over rapid resale.` → Framing value home stable khuyến khích cooperative prioritize resident long-term thay resale nhanh.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `안정적 주택 가치 프레이밍`.

## Review in context

Households face **housing search friction** before they can compare homes, and **rent salience** can make a monthly payment dominate attention. A **mortgage payment illusion** hides taxes and repairs, while **homeownership identity** turns ownership into a measure of adulthood. **Neighborhood choice framing** weighs cost against safety, access, and community; **school access perception** can change the value of a small apartment. **Landlord trust** and **tenant bargaining power belief** shape contract decisions, while **housing scarcity expectation** encourages rushed offers. **Moving cost aversion** keeps people in poor arrangements, and **renovation optimism** can hide delays. **Maintenance neglect** increases the **energy comfort tradeoff**, especially when **housing insecurity perception** makes every expense feel risky. **Eviction stigma** may silence a tenant, while **property tax salience** changes ownership calculations. **Shared amenity reciprocity** and **housing queue fairness** support collective trust; a **speculative demand cue** can inflate prices, whereas **stable home value framing** emphasizes security and long-term use.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

Household đối mặt **housing search friction** trước khi compare home, và **rent salience** làm payment monthly dominate attention. **Mortgage payment illusion** che tax và repair, còn **homeownership identity** biến ownership thành measure adulthood. **Neighborhood choice framing** cân cost với safety, access và community; **school access perception** đổi value apartment nhỏ. **Landlord trust** và **tenant bargaining power belief** shape contract, còn **housing scarcity expectation** khuyến khích offer vội. **Moving cost aversion** giữ người trong arrangement kém, và **renovation optimism** che delay. **Maintenance neglect** làm trade-off **energy comfort tradeoff** lớn, đặc biệt khi **housing insecurity perception** khiến expense nào cũng risky. **Eviction stigma** có thể làm tenant im lặng, còn **property tax salience** đổi calculation ownership. **Shared amenity reciprocity** và **housing queue fairness** support trust collective; **speculative demand cue** inflate price, trong khi **stable home value framing** nhấn security và use dài hạn.
