# C1 Vocabulary 31 — Behavioral travel and tourism choices

This lesson follows how travelers choose destinations, manage uncertainty, interpret reviews, and balance novelty, comfort, cost, and local impact.

Flow: **destination risk perception → itinerary complexity → booking friction → travel default → souvenir identity cue → tourist crowd aversion → hospitality trust → cancellation option value → exchange-rate salience → travel insurance reluctance → cultural novelty appetite → itinerary optimism → local authenticity cue → queue patience tourism → review credibility travel → overtourism norm → vacation memory framing → guide gratuity norm → travel fatigue perception → sustainable tourism preference**.

## 1. destination risk perception /ˌdɛstəˈneɪʃən rɪsk pərˈsɛpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a judgment about the safety, uncertainty, health, and disruption risks associated with a place to visit.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** judgment safety, uncertainty, health và disruption risk của destination.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high destination risk perception` — perception; `calibrate destination risk perception` — calibrate.
**Examples:** `Destination risk perception increased after travelers saw conflicting advice about local transport.` → Perception risk destination tăng sau khi traveler thấy advice transport local conflict.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `여행지 위험 인식`.

## 2. itinerary complexity /aɪˈtɪnəˌrɛri kəmˈplɛksəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the number of connections, reservations, timing constraints, and decisions required by a travel plan.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** number connection, reservation, timing constraint và decision trong travel plan.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high itinerary complexity` — complexity; `reduce itinerary complexity` — reduce.
**Examples:** `Itinerary complexity made a short trip feel risky when one delayed train could break every connection.` → Complexity itinerary khiến trip ngắn trông risky khi một train delay break mọi connection.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `여행 일정 복잡성`.

## 3. booking friction /ˈbʊkɪŋ ˈfrɪkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** extra effort, confusion, or uncertainty involved in comparing and reserving travel services.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** effort, confusion hoặc uncertainty thêm khi compare và reserve travel service.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high booking friction` — friction; `remove booking friction` — remove.
**Examples:** `Booking friction increased when the final price appeared only after several forms were completed.` → Friction booking tăng khi final price chỉ appear sau nhiều form.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `예약 마찰`.

## 4. travel default /ˈtrævəl dɪˈfɔlt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a familiar transport, lodging, or planning option that travelers choose unless they actively search for alternatives.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** option transport, lodging hoặc planning quen được choose nếu không actively search alternative.
**Grammar & collocations:** `convenient travel default` — default; `change a travel default` — change.
**Examples:** `Travel default led the family to book the same chain even though a local guesthouse was cheaper.` → Default travel khiến family book chain quen dù guesthouse local rẻ hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `여행 기본값`.

## 5. souvenir identity cue /ˌsuːvəˈnɪr aɪˈdɛntəti kjuː/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an object that reminds a traveler of a place and communicates a personal connection to it.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** object nhắc traveler về place và communicate connection personal.
**Grammar & collocations:** `meaningful souvenir identity cue` — cue; `choose a souvenir identity cue` — choose.
**Examples:** `A souvenir identity cue made a small handmade bowl more valuable than an expensive mass-produced gift.` → Cue identity souvenir làm bowl handmade nhỏ valuable hơn gift mass-produced đắt.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `기념품 정체성 단서`.

## 6. tourist crowd aversion /ˈtʊrɪst kraʊd əˈvɝʒən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a tendency to avoid attractions, seasons, or routes that feel overcrowded with visitors.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** tendency avoid attraction, season hoặc route cảm thấy visitor quá đông.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong tourist crowd aversion` — aversion; `accommodate tourist crowd aversion` — accommodate.
**Examples:** `Tourist crowd aversion led the couple to visit a smaller museum outside the central district.` → Aversion crowd tourist khiến couple visit museum nhỏ ngoài district central.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `관광객 혼잡 회피`.

## 7. hospitality trust /ˌhɑspəˈtæləti trʌst/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** confidence that a host, hotel, or service provider will deliver care, honesty, and reasonable treatment.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** confidence host, hotel hoặc provider deliver care, honesty và treatment reasonable.
**Grammar & collocations:** `fragile hospitality trust` — trust; `build hospitality trust` — build.
**Examples:** `Hospitality trust improved when the host explained the house rules before arrival.` → Trust hospitality improve khi host explain house rule trước arrival.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `환대 신뢰`.

## 8. cancellation option value /ˌkænsəˈleɪʃən ˈɑpʃən ˈvælju/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the value travelers place on being able to cancel or change a reservation if uncertainty becomes costly.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** value của ability cancel hoặc change reservation khi uncertainty costly.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high cancellation option value` — value; `increase cancellation option value` — increase.
**Examples:** `Cancellation option value was high because the child had an unpredictable medical schedule.` → Value option cancellation cao vì child có schedule medical unpredictable.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `취소 옵션 가치`.

## 9. exchange-rate salience /ɪksˈtʃeɪndʒ reɪt ˈseɪliəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** how noticeable and influential currency conversion is when travelers evaluate a trip's cost.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mức currency conversion noticeable và influential khi evaluate cost trip.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high exchange-rate salience` — salience; `increase exchange-rate salience` — increase.
**Examples:** `Exchange-rate salience increased when the app showed the local amount beside the home-currency total.` → Salience rate exchange tăng khi app show amount local cạnh total currency home.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `환율 현저성`.

## 10. travel insurance reluctance /ˈtrævəl ɪnˈʃʊrəns rɪˈlʌktəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** unwillingness to buy travel insurance because coverage seems confusing, unnecessary, or difficult to claim.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** không willing buy insurance vì coverage confusing, unnecessary hoặc claim difficult.
**Grammar & collocations:** `persistent travel insurance reluctance` — reluctance; `reduce travel insurance reluctance` — reduce.
**Examples:** `Travel insurance reluctance fell after the provider explained exclusions with concrete examples.` → Reluctance insurance travel giảm sau khi provider explain exclusion bằng example cụ thể.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `여행 보험 주저`.

## 11. cultural novelty appetite /ˈkʌltʃərəl ˈnɑvəlti ˈæpəˌtaɪt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** willingness to seek unfamiliar foods, practices, languages, and social experiences while traveling.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** willingness seek food, practice, language và social experience unfamiliar.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high cultural novelty appetite` — appetite; `develop cultural novelty appetite` — develop.
**Examples:** `Cultural novelty appetite encouraged the student to join a local cooking class without knowing anyone.` → Appetite novelty cultural khuyến khích student join class cooking local không biết ai.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `문화적 새로움 욕구`.

## 12. itinerary optimism /aɪˈtɪnəˌrɛri ˈɑptəˌmɪzəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a belief that a travel schedule will run smoothly despite delays, fatigue, and limited transition time.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** belief travel schedule run smooth dù delay, fatigue và transition time limited.
**Grammar & collocations:** `excessive itinerary optimism` — optimism; `temper itinerary optimism` — temper.

## 13. local authenticity cue /ˈloʊkəl ˌɔθɛnˈtɪsəti kjuː/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a signal that a place, activity, or product reflects local life rather than being designed only for visitors.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** signal place, activity hoặc product reflect local life chứ không design chỉ cho visitor.
**Grammar & collocations:** `credible local authenticity cue` — cue; `provide a local authenticity cue` — provide.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `지역 진정성 단서`.

## 14. queue patience tourism /kjuː ˈpeɪʃəns ˈtʊrɪzəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** willingness to wait for a tourist attraction or service when the expected experience seems worthwhile.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** willingness wait attraction hoặc service khi experience expected worthwhile.
**Grammar & collocations:** `limited queue patience tourism` — patience; `increase queue patience tourism` — increase.

## 15. review credibility travel /rɪˈvjuː krɛdəˈbɪləti ˈtrævəl/

## 16. overtourism norm /ˌoʊvərˈtʊrɪzəm nɔrm/
**Part of speech:** noun

**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** expectation shared visitor excessive và effect của họ normal hay unacceptable.

## 18. guide gratuity norm /ɡaɪd ɡrəˈtuːəti nɔrm/

**Core meaning (English):** a shared expectation about when, how much, and why travelers should tip a guide.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** expectation shared khi nào, bao nhiêu và vì sao traveler tip guide.
**Grammar & collocations:** `unclear guide gratuity norm` — norm; `explain a guide gratuity norm` — explain.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `가이드 팁 규범`.

## 19. travel fatigue perception /ˈtrævəl fəˈtiːɡ pərˈsɛpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a judgment about how exhausting travel will feel and how much recovery it will require.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** judgment travel exhausting và cần recovery bao nhiêu.
**Grammar & collocations:** `low travel fatigue perception` — perception; `improve travel fatigue perception` — improve.

## 20. sustainable tourism preference /səˈsteɪnəbəl ˈtʊrɪzəm ˈprɛfərəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a preference for travel options that reduce environmental and social harm while supporting local communities.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** preference travel option reduce harm environmental và social, support community local.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong sustainable tourism preference` — preference; `measure sustainable tourism preferences` — measure.
**Examples:** `Sustainable tourism preference led the group to choose a slower train and a locally owned hotel.` → Preference tourism sustainable khiến group choose train chậm và hotel local.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `지속 가능한 관광 선호`.

## Review in context

Travel decisions begin with **destination risk perception**, then expand as **itinerary complexity** and **booking friction** make uncertainty visible. A familiar **travel default** is convenient, while a **souvenir identity cue** gives a purchase personal meaning. **Tourist crowd aversion** changes timing, and **hospitality trust** determines whether a host feels safe. **Cancellation option value** protects against disruption; **exchange-rate salience** and **travel insurance reluctance** shape the budget. **Cultural novelty appetite** encourages exploration, but **itinerary optimism** can ignore fatigue. A **local authenticity cue** makes an experience feel connected to place, while **queue patience tourism** depends on honest waiting information. **Review credibility travel** affects comparison, and an **overtourism norm** determines whether crowding is tolerated. **Vacation memory framing** shapes what remains meaningful, while a clear **guide gratuity norm** prevents awkwardness. Accurate **travel fatigue perception** supports a **sustainable tourism preference** that balances enjoyment with local impact.
