# C1 Vocabulary 43 — Behavioral civic participation and elections

This lesson follows how citizens interpret political choices, participation costs, trust, identity, and democratic procedures.

Flow: **voting turnout salience → registration friction → ballot choice overload → candidate trust cue → campaign promise credibility → issue importance perception → political efficacy belief → abstention regret → polling influence expectation → partisan identity cue → misinformation correction fatigue → civic norm enforcement → protest participation cost → policy feedback trust → representative similarity cue → coalition fairness belief → deliberation patience → public opinion volatility perception → election administration trust → democratic participation framing**.

## 1. voting turnout salience /ˈvoʊtɪŋ ˈtɝnˌaʊt ˈseɪliəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** how noticeable and important the act of voting feels when a citizen decides whether to participate.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mức act voting noticeable và important khi citizen decide participate.
**Grammar & collocations:** `low voting turnout salience` — salience; `increase voting turnout salience` — increase.
**Examples:** `Voting turnout salience increased when local candidates explained how the ballot affected bus routes.` → Salience turnout voting tăng khi candidate local explain ballot affect bus route.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `투표 참여 현저성`.

## 2. registration friction /ˌrɛdʒəˈstreɪʃən ˈfrɪkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** effort, uncertainty, paperwork, or travel that makes registering to vote difficult.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** effort, uncertainty, paperwork hoặc travel làm register vote khó.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high registration friction` — friction; `remove registration friction` — remove.
**Examples:** `Registration friction discouraged the new resident after the online form rejected a valid address.` → Friction registration discourage resident mới sau form online reject address valid.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `등록 마찰`.

## 3. ballot choice overload /ˈbælət tʃɔɪs ˈoʊvərˌloʊd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** difficulty deciding caused by too many candidates, offices, questions, or unfamiliar alternatives on a ballot.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** difficulty decide vì ballot quá nhiều candidate, office, question hoặc alternative unfamiliar.
**Grammar & collocations:** `severe ballot choice overload` — overload; `reduce ballot choice overload` — reduce.
**Examples:** `Ballot choice overload made the voter skip local contests and focus only on the top office.` → Overload choice ballot khiến voter skip contest local và focus office top.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `투표지 선택 과부하`.

## 4. candidate trust cue /ˈkændəˌdeɪt trʌst kjuː/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a signal that leads voters to infer a candidate's honesty, competence, and concern for the public.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** signal voter infer honesty, competence và concern public của candidate.
**Grammar & collocations:** `credible candidate trust cue` — cue; `provide a candidate trust cue` — provide.
**Examples:** `A candidate trust cue appeared when the speaker admitted a past mistake and described the correction.` → Cue trust candidate xuất hiện khi speaker admit mistake cũ và describe correction.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `후보자 신뢰 단서`.

## 5. campaign promise credibility /kæmˈpeɪn ˈprɑmɪs krɛdəˈbɪləti/

## 6. issue importance perception /ˈɪʃu ɪmˈpɔrtəns pərˈsɛpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a judgment about how much a political issue affects one's life, values, or community.

## 7. political efficacy belief /pəˈlɪtɪkəl ˈɛfəkəsi bɪˈliːf/
**Part of speech:** noun

## 8. abstention regret /əbˈstɛnʃən rɪˈɡrɛt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** disappointment about not participating after an election produces an unwanted result.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** disappointment vì không participate sau election produce result không muốn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong abstention regret` — regret; `anticipate abstention regret` — anticipate.
**Examples:** `Abstention regret motivated the citizen to learn the local voting rules before the next election.` → Regret abstention motivate citizen learn rule voting local trước election sau.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `기권 후회`.

## 9. polling influence expectation /ˈpoʊlɪŋ ˈɪnfluəns ˌɛkspɛkˈteɪʃən/

**Core meaning (English):** a belief that public polls can change candidates, donors, media attention, or one's own vote.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `여론조사 영향 기대`.

## 10. partisan identity cue /ˈpɑrtəzən aɪˈdɛntəti kjuː/

## 11. misinformation correction fatigue /ˌmɪsɪnfərˈmeɪʃən kəˈrɛkʃən fəˈtiːɡ/

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `허위 정보 정정 피로`.

## Review in context

Citizens respond to **voting turnout salience**, but **registration friction** and **ballot choice overload** can suppress participation. A **candidate trust cue** and **campaign promise credibility** shape choice, while **issue importance perception** and **political efficacy belief** determine whether action feels worthwhile. **Abstention regret** may motivate future participation, whereas **polling influence expectation** changes donations and attention. A **partisan identity cue** can simplify judgment, but **misinformation correction fatigue** weakens careful evaluation. **Civic norm enforcement** makes participation visible, and reducing **protest participation cost** broadens voice. **Policy feedback trust** grows when institutions respond, while a **representative similarity cue** shapes personal connection. **Coalition fairness belief** and **deliberation patience** support cooperation; **public opinion volatility perception** should not justify panic. **Election administration trust** protects legitimacy, and **democratic participation framing** turns civic action into a shared responsibility.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

Citizen phản hồi **voting turnout salience**, nhưng **registration friction** và **ballot choice overload** suppress participation. **Candidate trust cue** và **campaign promise credibility** shape choice, còn **issue importance perception** và **political efficacy belief** quyết định action có worthwhile không. **Abstention regret** motivate participation future, trong khi **polling influence expectation** đổi donation và attention. **Partisan identity cue** simplify judgment, nhưng **misinformation correction fatigue** làm evaluation careful yếu. **Civic norm enforcement** làm participation visible, và giảm **protest participation cost** mở rộng voice. **Policy feedback trust** grow khi institution respond, còn **representative similarity cue** shape connection personal. **Coalition fairness belief** và **deliberation patience** support cooperation; **public opinion volatility perception** không nên justify panic. **Election administration trust** protect legitimacy, và **democratic participation framing** biến civic action thành responsibility shared.
