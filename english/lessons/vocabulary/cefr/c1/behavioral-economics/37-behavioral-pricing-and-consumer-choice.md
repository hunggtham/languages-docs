# C1 Vocabulary 37 — Behavioral pricing and consumer choice

This lesson follows how people interpret prices, discounts, bundles, refunds, and seller signals when attention and uncertainty shape consumption.

Flow: **price fairness perception → reference price belief → discount salience → free trial inertia → bundle valuation → willingness-to-pay uncertainty → payment pain → sunk subscription cost → dynamic pricing suspicion → surge price acceptance → loyalty reward framing → coupon redemption friction → scarcity discount effect → product comparison overload → checkout abandonment → refund expectation → seller reputation cue → auction winner curse → bargaining norm → consumer regret anticipation**.

## 1. price fairness perception /praɪs ˈfɛrnəs pərˈsɛpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a judgment about whether a price is justified, consistent, and respectful of buyers and sellers.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** judgment price justified, consistent và respectful với buyer và seller.
**Grammar & collocations:** `low price fairness perception` — perception; `improve price fairness perception` — improve.
**Examples:** `Price fairness perception fell when identical customers received different explanations for the same charge.` → Perception fairness price giảm khi customer giống nhau nhận explanation khác cho charge.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `가격 공정성 인식`.

## 2. reference price belief /ˈrɛfərəns praɪs bɪˈliːf/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a belief about what a product or service normally costs and therefore what price seems high or low.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** belief product hoặc service normally cost bao nhiêu để judge price high hay low.
**Grammar & collocations:** `anchored reference price belief` — belief; `change reference price beliefs` — change.
**Examples:** `Reference price belief made the new fee seem excessive after years of a lower advertised rate.` → Belief price reference làm fee mới trông excessive sau nhiều năm rate advertise thấp.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `기준 가격 신념`.

## 3. discount salience /ˈdɪskaʊnt ˈseɪliəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** how noticeable and motivating a reduction from an original price appears to a buyer.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mức reduction từ original price noticeable và motivating với buyer.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high discount salience` — salience; `increase discount salience` — increase.
**Examples:** `Discount salience made customers focus on the percentage saved rather than the final amount paid.` → Salience discount khiến customer focus percentage save thay final amount pay.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `할인 현저성`.

## 4. free trial inertia /friː ˈtraɪəl ˈɪnɝʒə/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** continuing a paid service after a free trial because cancellation is easy to postpone.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** continue service paid sau trial free vì cancel dễ postpone.
**Grammar & collocations:** `persistent free trial inertia` — inertia; `overcome free trial inertia` — overcome.
**Examples:** `Free trial inertia charged the customer after the product stopped being useful.` → Inertia trial free charge customer sau product không useful.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `무료 체험 관성`.

## 5. bundle valuation /ˈbʌndəl ˌvæljuˈeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the way people judge the value of several products or services offered together.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cách judge value nhiều product hoặc service offer together.
**Grammar & collocations:** `inflated bundle valuation` — valuation; `compare bundle valuations` — compare.
**Examples:** `Bundle valuation was high until the buyer noticed that two included services would never be used.` → Valuation bundle cao tới khi buyer thấy hai service include sẽ không use.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `묶음 가치 평가`.

## 6. willingness-to-pay uncertainty /ˈwɪlɪŋnəs tə peɪ ˌʌnsərˈtənti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** uncertainty about the maximum amount a person would accept paying for a product or outcome.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** uncertainty về maximum amount person willing pay cho product hoặc outcome.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high willingness-to-pay uncertainty` — uncertainty; `reduce willingness-to-pay uncertainty` — reduce.
**Examples:** `Willingness-to-pay uncertainty made the startup test several prices before launching.` → Uncertainty willingness-to-pay khiến startup test nhiều price trước launch.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `지불 의사 불확실성`.

## 7. payment pain /ˈpeɪmənt peɪn/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the immediate emotional discomfort of giving up money during a purchase.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** discomfort emotional immediate khi give up money lúc purchase.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high payment pain` — pain; `reduce payment pain` — reduce.
**Examples:** `Payment pain was lower when the annual fee was divided into predictable monthly amounts.` → Pain payment thấp hơn khi fee annual chia thành amount monthly predictable.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `지불 고통`.

## 8. sunk subscription cost /sʌŋk səbˈskrɪpʃən kɔst/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** money already spent on a subscription that cannot be recovered by continuing or cancelling it.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** money đã spend cho subscription không recover được bằng continue hay cancel.
**Grammar & collocations:** `large sunk subscription cost` — cost; `ignore sunk subscription costs` — ignore.
**Examples:** `Sunk subscription cost made the user keep paying for a course already abandoned.` → Cost subscription sunk khiến user tiếp tục pay course đã abandon.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `매몰 구독 비용`.

## 9. dynamic pricing suspicion /daɪˈnæmɪk ˈpraɪsɪŋ səˈspɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** doubt that changing prices reflect legitimate demand rather than hidden exploitation or discrimination.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** doubt price change reflect demand legitimate hay exploitation/discrimination hidden.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high dynamic pricing suspicion` — suspicion; `address dynamic pricing suspicion` — address.
**Examples:** `Dynamic pricing suspicion increased when the app changed the price without explaining the conditions.` → Suspicion pricing dynamic tăng khi app change price không explain condition.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `동적 가격 의심`.

## 10. surge price acceptance /sɝdʒ praɪs əkˈsɛptəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** willingness to pay a temporarily higher price when demand, urgency, or scarcity is visible.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** willingness pay price temporarily high khi demand, urgency hoặc scarcity visible.
**Grammar & collocations:** `low surge price acceptance` — acceptance; `increase surge price acceptance` — increase.
**Examples:** `Surge price acceptance was higher during the storm because alternatives had disappeared.` → Acceptance price surge cao hơn lúc storm vì alternative biến mất.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `급등 가격 수용`.

## 11. loyalty reward framing /ˈlɔɪəlti rɪˈwɔrd ˈfreɪmɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the way a reward program is presented as savings, recognition, progress, exclusivity, or obligation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cách present reward program như saving, recognition, progress, exclusivity hoặc obligation.
**Grammar & collocations:** `effective loyalty reward framing` — framing; `change loyalty reward framing` — change.
**Examples:** `Loyalty reward framing made points feel like earned progress rather than a discount with conditions.` → Framing reward loyalty làm point trông như progress earned thay discount có condition.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `충성도 보상 프레이밍`.

## 12. coupon redemption friction /ˈkuːpɑn rɪˈdɛmpʃən ˈfrɪkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** effort, restrictions, or confusion that make a customer less likely to use a coupon.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** effort, restriction hoặc confusion làm customer ít use coupon.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high coupon redemption friction` — friction; `remove coupon redemption friction` — remove.
**Examples:** `Coupon redemption friction caused the shopper to abandon a discount that required three separate codes.` → Friction redemption coupon khiến shopper abandon discount cần ba code riêng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `쿠폰 사용 마찰`.

## 13. scarcity discount effect /ˈskɛrsəti ˈdɪskaʊnt ɪˈfɛkt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a change in the appeal of a discount caused by the impression that the product or offer is limited.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** appeal discount đổi vì impression product hoặc offer limited.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong scarcity discount effect` — effect; `test the scarcity discount effect` — test.
**Examples:** `Scarcity discount effect made the offer seem more valuable when only a few units remained.` → Effect discount scarcity làm offer trông valuable hơn khi chỉ còn ít unit.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `희소성 할인 효과`.

## 14. product comparison overload /ˈprɑdʌkt kəmˈpɛrɪsən ˈoʊvərˌloʊd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** difficulty choosing caused by too many product attributes, alternatives, or conflicting reviews.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** difficulty choose do quá nhiều attribute, alternative hoặc review conflict.
**Grammar & collocations:** `severe product comparison overload` — overload; `reduce product comparison overload` — reduce.
**Examples:** `Product comparison overload made the customer choose the first acceptable option to escape research.` → Overload comparison product khiến customer choose option acceptable đầu để escape research.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `제품 비교 과부하`.

## 15. checkout abandonment /ˈtʃɛkˌaʊt əˈbændənmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** leaving a purchase process before payment is completed.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** leave purchase process trước payment complete.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high checkout abandonment` — abandonment; `reduce checkout abandonment` — reduce.
**Examples:** `Checkout abandonment increased after the store added an unexpected delivery fee.` → Abandonment checkout tăng sau store add fee delivery unexpected.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `결제 포기`.

## 16. refund expectation /ˈriːfʌnd ˌɛkspɛkˈteɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a belief about whether, how quickly, and how completely money will be returned after a purchase problem.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** belief money return whether, quickly và completely sau purchase problem.
**Grammar & collocations:** `clear refund expectation` — expectation; `manage refund expectations` — manage.
**Examples:** `Refund expectation was low because the policy used vague language about processing time.` → Expectation refund thấp vì policy dùng language vague về processing time.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `환불 기대`.

## 17. seller reputation cue /ˈsɛlər ˌrɛpjəˈteɪʃən kjuː/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a signal about a seller's reliability, honesty, quality, or treatment of customers.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** signal reliability, honesty, quality hoặc treatment customer của seller.
**Grammar & collocations:** `credible seller reputation cue` — cue; `interpret a seller reputation cue` — interpret.
**Examples:** `A seller reputation cue made buyers accept a slightly higher price from a verified local shop.` → Cue reputation seller khiến buyer accept price cao hơn từ shop local verify.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `판매자 평판 단서`.

## 18. auction winner curse /ˈɔkʃən ˈwɪnər kɝs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the risk that winning a competitive auction means paying more than an asset is actually worth.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** risk win auction nghĩa pay nhiều hơn asset value thật.
**Grammar & collocations:** `classic auction winner curse` — curse; `avoid the auction winner curse` — avoid.
**Examples:** `Auction winner curse appeared when the buyer celebrated winning before checking the renovation cost.` → Curse winner auction xuất hiện khi buyer celebrate trước check renovation cost.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `경매 승자의 저주`.

## 19. bargaining norm /ˈbɑrɡənɪŋ nɔrm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a shared expectation about whether prices, terms, and concessions should be negotiated.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** expectation shared price, term và concession có nên negotiate không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong bargaining norm` — norm; `follow a bargaining norm` — follow.
**Examples:** `Bargaining norm differed across markets, so a polite question sounded demanding in one place.` → Norm bargaining khác giữa market khiến question polite nghe demanding ở place kia.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `흥정 규범`.

## 20. consumer regret anticipation /kənˈsuːmər rɪˈɡrɛt ænˌtɪsəˈpeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** imagining future disappointment about buying, not buying, or choosing the wrong option.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** imagine disappointment future vì buy, not buy hoặc choose option wrong.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong consumer regret anticipation` — anticipation; `reduce consumer regret anticipation` — reduce.
**Examples:** `Consumer regret anticipation kept the shopper comparing phones long after the practical differences were clear.` → Anticipation regret consumer giữ shopper compare phone dù difference practical đã clear.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `소비자 후회 예상`.

## Review in context

Buyers evaluate **price fairness perception** against a **reference price belief**, while **discount salience** can make a reduction seem more important than the final cost. **Free trial inertia** and **sunk subscription cost** keep spending active, and **bundle valuation** may hide unused features. **Willingness-to-pay uncertainty** makes testing useful, but **payment pain** still shapes timing. **Dynamic pricing suspicion** can reduce **surge price acceptance**, even when scarcity is real. **Loyalty reward framing** changes how points feel, while **coupon redemption friction** destroys a promised saving. A **scarcity discount effect** can intensify urgency, and **product comparison overload** leads to **checkout abandonment**. Clear **refund expectation** and a credible **seller reputation cue** repair confidence. The **auction winner curse** warns against celebrating a bid too early; a shared **bargaining norm** changes what seems polite. Finally, **consumer regret anticipation** can delay a reasonable choice long after evidence is sufficient.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

Buyer evaluate **price fairness perception** với **reference price belief**, còn **discount salience** làm reduction trông important hơn final cost. **Free trial inertia** và **sunk subscription cost** giữ spending active, còn **bundle valuation** có thể che feature không use. **Willingness-to-pay uncertainty** làm testing useful, nhưng **payment pain** vẫn shape timing. **Dynamic pricing suspicion** giảm **surge price acceptance** dù scarcity thật. **Loyalty reward framing** đổi cảm nhận point, còn **coupon redemption friction** phá saving promise. **Scarcity discount effect** tăng urgency, và **product comparison overload** dẫn tới **checkout abandonment**. **Refund expectation** rõ và **seller reputation cue** credible repair trust. **Auction winner curse** cảnh báo đừng celebrate bid sớm; **bargaining norm** shared đổi điều gì polite. Cuối cùng, **consumer regret anticipation** delay choice hợp lý dù evidence đủ.
