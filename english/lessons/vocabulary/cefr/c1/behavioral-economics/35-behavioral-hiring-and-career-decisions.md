# C1 Vocabulary 35 — Behavioral hiring and career decisions

This lesson follows how applicants, recruiters, and employees interpret opportunities, signals, fairness, uncertainty, and identity across a working life.

Flow: **job search salience → vacancy framing → resume signal → applicant effort cost → interview anxiety → recruiter stereotype cue → credential inflation belief → wage transparency perception → offer rejection aversion → onboarding default → probation uncertainty → promotion expectation → performance appraisal bias → feedback seeking motive → job security perception → turnover intention → organizational fit belief → referral trust → hiring queue fairness → career identity framing**.

## 1. job search salience /dʒɑb sɝtʃ ˈseɪliəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** how noticeable and urgent finding employment feels when a person evaluates options and effort.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mức find employment noticeable và urgent khi evaluate option và effort.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high job search salience` — salience; `increase job search salience` — increase.
**Examples:** `Job search salience rose after the contract end date became certain.` → Salience search job tăng sau khi contract end date trở nên certain.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `구직 현저성`.

## 2. vacancy framing /ˈveɪkənsi ˈfreɪmɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the way an open role is presented as security, growth, service, status, challenge, or temporary work.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cách present open role như security, growth, service, status, challenge hoặc temporary work.
**Grammar & collocations:** `growth-oriented vacancy framing` — framing; `change vacancy framing` — change.
**Examples:** `Vacancy framing emphasized learning and autonomy rather than only a list of tasks.` → Framing vacancy nhấn learning và autonomy thay vì chỉ list task.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `채용 공고 프레이밍`.

## 3. resume signal /ˈrɛzəˌmeɪ ˈsɪɡnəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a feature of a résumé that leads an employer to infer skill, reliability, ambition, or fit.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** feature resume khiến employer infer skill, reliability, ambition hoặc fit.
**Grammar & collocations:** `credible resume signal` — signal; `interpret a resume signal` — interpret.
**Examples:** `A resume signal about volunteer leadership mattered more than a long list of unrelated courses.` → Signal resume về leadership volunteer quan trọng hơn list course không liên quan dài.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `이력서 신호`.

## 4. applicant effort cost /ˈæplɪkənt ˈɛfərt kɔst/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the time, money, attention, and emotional energy required to apply for a role.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** time, money, attention và energy emotional để apply role.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high applicant effort cost` — cost; `reduce applicant effort costs` — reduce.
**Examples:** `Applicant effort cost discouraged qualified people from repeating a lengthy unpaid assessment.` → Cost effort applicant làm người qualified ngại repeat assessment unpaid dài.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `지원자 노력 비용`.

## 5. interview anxiety /ˈɪntərˌvjuː æŋˈzaɪəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** fear or tension that affects thinking, communication, and behavior during a job interview.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** fear hoặc tension ảnh hưởng thinking, communication và behavior lúc interview job.
**Grammar & collocations:** `severe interview anxiety` — anxiety; `reduce interview anxiety` — reduce.
**Examples:** `Interview anxiety made the experienced technician give shorter answers than the work required.` → Anxiety interview khiến technician experienced give answer ngắn hơn work cần.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `면접 불안`.

## 6. recruiter stereotype cue /rɪˈkruːtər ˈstɛriəˌtaɪp kjuː/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a feature of a candidate or context that activates a stereotype in a recruiter's judgment.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** feature candidate hoặc context activate stereotype trong judgment recruiter.
**Grammar & collocations:** `subtle recruiter stereotype cue` — cue; `remove recruiter stereotype cues` — remove.
**Examples:** `A recruiter stereotype cue influenced how the panel interpreted a confident answer from a nontraditional candidate.` → Cue stereotype recruiter influence panel interpret answer confident từ candidate nontraditional.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `채용 담당자 고정관념 단서`.

## 7. credential inflation belief /krəˈdɛnʃəl ɪnˈfleɪʃən bɪˈliːf/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a belief that increasingly high qualifications are necessary for work that once required less formal education.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** belief qualification increasingly high cần cho work từng cần education formal ít hơn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong credential inflation belief` — belief; `question credential inflation beliefs` — question.
**Examples:** `Credential inflation belief made the experienced assistant consider another degree for a familiar role.` → Belief inflation credential khiến assistant experienced consider degree khác cho role quen.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `자격 인플레이션 신념`.

## 8. wage transparency perception /weɪdʒ trænˈspɛrənsi pərˈsɛpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a judgment about how clearly and fairly pay information is shared within a workplace or labor market.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** judgment pay information share clear và fair trong workplace hoặc labor market.
**Grammar & collocations:** `low wage transparency perception` — perception; `improve wage transparency perception` — improve.
**Examples:** `Wage transparency perception improved when the company published a range and explained progression.` → Perception transparency wage improve khi company publish range và explain progression.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `임금 투명성 인식`.

## 9. offer rejection aversion /ˈɔfər rɪˈdʒɛkʃən əˈvɝʒən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** reluctance to decline a job offer because rejection feels risky, ungrateful, or difficult to reverse.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** reluctance decline offer vì rejection feel risky, ungrateful hoặc khó reverse.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong offer rejection aversion` — aversion; `manage offer rejection aversion` — manage.
**Examples:** `Offer rejection aversion made the applicant accept a role that conflicted with caregiving responsibilities.` → Aversion rejection offer khiến applicant accept role conflict responsibility caregiving.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `제안 거절 회피`.

## 10. onboarding default /ˈɑnˌbɔrdɪŋ dɪˈfɔlt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a preselected training, communication, or work routine that shapes a new employee's first weeks.

**Grammar & collocations:** `supportive onboarding default` — default; `design an onboarding default` — design.
**Examples:** `An onboarding default paired the new analyst with a mentor before the first complex assignment.` → Default onboarding pair analyst mới với mentor trước assignment phức tạp đầu.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `온보딩 기본값`.

## 12. promotion expectation /prəˈmoʊʃən ˌɛkspɛkˈteɪʃən/

## 13. performance appraisal bias /pərˈfɔrməns əˈpreɪzəl baɪəs/

## 14. feedback seeking motive /ˈfiːdbæk ˈsiːkɪŋ ˈmoʊtɪv/

## 19. hiring queue fairness /ˈhaɪərɪŋ kjuː ˈfɛrnəs/

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `경력 정체성 프레이밍`.

## Review in context

Job decisions begin with **job search salience** and the **vacancy framing** that makes a role seem secure, purposeful, or developmental. A **resume signal** can be overread, while **applicant effort cost** and **interview anxiety** hide capable people. A **recruiter stereotype cue** may distort judgment, and **credential inflation belief** raises entry barriers. **Wage transparency perception** shapes trust, while **offer rejection aversion** can make a poor fit feel safer than refusal. An **onboarding default** supports early learning, but **probation uncertainty** limits questions. **Promotion expectation** and **performance appraisal bias** influence effort, while a **feedback seeking motive** can improve performance when questions are safe. **Job security perception** affects **turnover intention**, and **organizational fit belief** changes with lived experience. **Referral trust** opens doors, but **hiring queue fairness** determines whether the process feels legitimate. Finally, **career identity framing** helps people judge a move by purpose and growth rather than title alone.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

Decision job bắt đầu từ **job search salience** và **vacancy framing** làm role trông secure, purposeful hoặc developmental. **Resume signal** có thể bị overread, còn **applicant effort cost** và **interview anxiety** che người capable. **Recruiter stereotype cue** distort judgment, và **credential inflation belief** nâng barrier entry. **Wage transparency perception** shape trust, còn **offer rejection aversion** làm fit kém trông safe hơn refusal. **Onboarding default** hỗ trợ learning sớm, nhưng **probation uncertainty** limit question. **Promotion expectation** và **performance appraisal bias** influence effort, còn **feedback seeking motive** improve performance khi question safe. **Job security perception** ảnh hưởng **turnover intention**, và **organizational fit belief** đổi theo experience thật. **Referral trust** mở door, nhưng **hiring queue fairness** quyết định process legitimate không. Cuối cùng, **career identity framing** giúp judge move bằng purpose và growth thay vì chỉ title.
