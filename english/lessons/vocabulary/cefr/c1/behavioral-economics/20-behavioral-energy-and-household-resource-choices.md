# C1 Vocabulary 20 — Behavioral energy and household resource choices

This lesson follows how households interpret bills, comfort, efficiency, scarcity, and fairness when making energy and resource decisions.

Flow: **energy use salience → appliance default → thermostat inertia → rebound expectation → conservation identity → utility bill framing → energy poverty perception → peak demand avoidance → renewable trust → green premium acceptance → household energy norm → efficiency hassle → comfort preference → heating risk perception → bill shock response → metering feedback → repair procrastination → water-saving habit → resource scarcity cue → energy transition fairness**.

## 1. energy use salience /ˈɛnərdʒi juːs ˈseɪliəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** how noticeable and mentally important household energy consumption is during daily decisions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mức energy consumption household noticeable và important khi decision hằng ngày.
**Grammar & collocations:** `low energy use salience` — salience; `increase energy use salience` — increase.
**Examples:** `Energy use salience rose when the display showed the cost of leaving lights on.` → Salience use energy tăng khi display show cost để light bật.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `에너지 사용 현저성`.

## 2. appliance default /əˈplaɪəns dɪˈfɔlt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a preselected setting or operating mode that an appliance uses unless someone changes it.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** setting hoặc mode preselect appliance use nếu không ai change.
**Grammar & collocations:** `energy-saving appliance default` — default; `set an appliance default` — set.
**Examples:** `An appliance default kept the washing machine on a cooler cycle unless users selected another one.` → Default appliance giữ washing machine ở cycle mát hơn nếu user không chọn khác.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `가전 기본값`.

## 3. thermostat inertia /ˈθɝməˌstæt ɪˈnɝʒə/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the tendency to leave a thermostat setting unchanged because adjustment requires attention or effort.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** tendency để thermostat unchanged vì adjustment cần attention hoặc effort.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong thermostat inertia` — inertia; `overcome thermostat inertia` — overcome.
**Examples:** `Thermostat inertia kept the heating high during a warm afternoon.` → Inertia thermostat giữ heating cao trong afternoon ấm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `온도 조절기 관성`.

## 4. rebound expectation /ˈriːbaʊnd ˌɛkspɛkˈteɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a belief that efficiency gains will be partly offset because people use a service more when it becomes cheaper or easier.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** belief gain efficiency bị offset vì service rẻ hoặc easy khiến người ta use nhiều hơn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `realistic rebound expectation` — expectation; `test rebound expectations` — test.
**Examples:** `Rebound expectation led the team to examine whether cheaper heating encouraged longer showers.` → Expectation rebound khiến team examine heating cheap có khuyến khích shower dài hơn không.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `반동 효과 기대`.

## 5. conservation identity /ˌkɑnsərˈveɪʃən aɪˈdɛntəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a sense that saving energy or resources is part of who a person or household is.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** sense tiết kiệm energy hoặc resource là một phần identity.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong conservation identity` — identity; `build a conservation identity` — build.
**Examples:** `Conservation identity made the household keep repairing an old appliance instead of replacing it quickly.` → Identity conservation khiến household tiếp tục repair appliance cũ thay vì replace nhanh.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `절약 정체성`.

## 6. utility bill framing /juːˈtɪləti bɪl ˈfreɪmɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the way a utility charge is presented as a cost, comparison, warning, or opportunity to save.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cách present utility charge như cost, comparison, warning hoặc opportunity save.
**Grammar & collocations:** `clear utility bill framing` — framing; `change utility bill framing` — change.
**Examples:** `Utility bill framing that compared similar homes made an abstract charge easier to interpret.` → Framing bill utility compare homes similar làm charge abstract dễ interpret hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `공공요금 청구서 프레이밍`.

## 7. energy poverty perception /ˈɛnərdʒi ˈpɑvərti pərˈsɛpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a judgment about whether a household lacks affordable, reliable energy for basic needs.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** judgment household thiếu energy affordable và reliable cho basic need.
**Grammar & collocations:** `accurate energy poverty perception` — perception; `improve energy poverty perception` — improve.
**Examples:** `Energy poverty perception changed when residents saw the health cost of keeping rooms cold.` → Perception poverty energy đổi khi resident thấy health cost của room lạnh.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `에너지 빈곤 인식`.

## 8. peak demand avoidance /piːk dɪˈmænd əˈvɔɪdəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** shifting energy use away from periods when the system is most crowded or expensive.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** shift energy use khỏi period system đông hoặc expensive nhất.
**Grammar & collocations:** `voluntary peak demand avoidance` — avoidance; `encourage peak demand avoidance` — encourage.
**Examples:** `Peak demand avoidance became easier when the dishwasher could start automatically after midnight.` → Avoidance demand peak dễ hơn khi dishwasher tự start sau midnight.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `최대 수요 회피`.

## 9. renewable trust /rɪˈnuːəbəl trʌst/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** confidence that renewable energy technologies and providers will deliver reliable and honest benefits.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** confidence technology và provider renewable deliver benefit reliable và honest.
**Grammar & collocations:** `low renewable trust` — trust; `build renewable trust` — build.
**Examples:** `Renewable trust improved after the cooperative published maintenance records and realistic output estimates.` → Trust renewable improve sau khi cooperative publish record maintenance và estimate output realistic.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `재생에너지 신뢰`.

## 10. green premium acceptance /ɡriːn ˈpriːmiəm əkˈsɛptəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** willingness to pay more for a product or service believed to reduce environmental harm.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** willingness pay more cho product hoặc service believed reduce environmental harm.
**Grammar & collocations:** `limited green premium acceptance` — acceptance; `increase green premium acceptance` — increase.
**Examples:** `Green premium acceptance was higher when buyers could see durable savings rather than only a moral claim.` → Acceptance premium green cao hơn khi buyer thấy saving durable thay vì chỉ moral claim.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `녹색 프리미엄 수용`.

## 11. household energy norm /ˈhaʊsˌhoʊld ˈɛnərdʒi nɔrm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a shared expectation within a household about what level of energy use is normal or responsible.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** expectation shared household về energy use normal hoặc responsible mức nào.
**Grammar & collocations:** `conservative household energy norm` — norm; `change a household energy norm` — change.
**Examples:** `A household energy norm made turning off unused devices feel automatic rather than restrictive.` → Norm energy household khiến turn off device unused trông automatic thay vì restrictive.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `가정 에너지 규범`.

## 12. efficiency hassle /ɪˈfɪʃənsi ˈhæsəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** inconvenience, paperwork, installation effort, or uncertainty that makes an efficiency measure harder to adopt.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** inconvenience, paperwork, installation effort hoặc uncertainty làm efficiency measure khó adopt.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high efficiency hassle` — hassle; `reduce efficiency hassle` — reduce.
**Examples:** `Efficiency hassle discouraged renters from installing equipment they could not take when moving.` → Hassle efficiency làm renter ngại install equipment không thể mang khi move.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `효율성 번거로움`.

## 13. comfort preference /ˈkʌmfərt ˈprɛfərəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a relative desire for a particular level of temperature, lighting, quiet, or convenience.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** desire tương đối cho level temperature, lighting, quiet hoặc convenience.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong comfort preference` — preference; `respect comfort preferences` — respect.
**Examples:** `Comfort preference differed between residents who worked from home and those away all day.` → Preference comfort khác giữa resident work at home và người away cả ngày.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `편안함 선호`.

## 14. heating risk perception /ˈhiːtɪŋ rɪsk pərˈsɛpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a judgment about the health or safety danger of using too little or too much heating.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** judgment danger health hoặc safety khi use heating quá ít hay quá nhiều.
**Grammar & collocations:** `elevated heating risk perception` — perception; `change heating risk perception` — change.

## 15. bill shock response /bɪl ʃɑk rɪˈspɑns/

## 17. repair procrastination /rɪˈpɛr proʊˌkræstəˈneɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** delaying a repair even when the delay increases waste, cost, or risk.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** delay repair dù delay làm waste, cost hoặc risk tăng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `costly repair procrastination` — procrastination; `overcome repair procrastination` — overcome.
**Examples:** `Repair procrastination allowed a small leak to damage the flooring and increase water use.` → Procrastination repair để leak nhỏ damage floor và làm water use tăng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `수리 미루기`.

## 18. water-saving habit /ˈwɔtər ˈseɪvɪŋ ˈhæbɪt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a repeated routine that reduces unnecessary water use with little deliberate effort.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** routine lặp lại giảm water use unnecessary với ít deliberate effort.
**Grammar & collocations:** `stable water-saving habit` — habit; `form a water-saving habit` — form.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `물 절약 습관`.

## 19. resource scarcity cue /rɪˈsɔrs ˈskɛrsəti kjuː/

**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** signal làm supply limited hoặc shortage future prominent trong mind.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `자원 희소성 단서`.

## 20. energy transition fairness /ˈɛnərdʒi trænˈzɪʃən ˈfɛrnəs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a judgment about whether the costs, opportunities, and voice in an energy transition are distributed fairly.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** judgment cost, opportunity và voice trong transition energy phân phối fair không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `credible energy transition fairness` — fairness; `improve energy transition fairness` — improve.
**Examples:** `Energy transition fairness improved when low-income households received support before prices changed.` → Fairness transition energy improve khi household low-income receive support trước price đổi.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `에너지 전환 공정성`.

## Review in context

Household resource choices depend on **energy use salience** and the **appliance default** that quietly shapes routine. **Thermostat inertia** can preserve an old setting, while **rebound expectation** reminds planners that efficiency may change behavior as well as consumption. A **conservation identity** supports action, and **utility bill framing** can make an invisible cost understandable. **Energy poverty perception** matters because saving energy is not the same as accepting unsafe cold. **Peak demand avoidance** becomes easier with automation, but **renewable trust** and **green premium acceptance** determine whether people support new systems. A **household energy norm** can normalize care, while **efficiency hassle** blocks even attractive upgrades. **Comfort preference** and **heating risk perception** shape daily trade-offs, and **bill shock response** may lead to harmful cuts. Timely **metering feedback** can reveal problems before **repair procrastination** becomes expensive. A **water-saving habit** and a visible **resource scarcity cue** support conservation, but **energy transition fairness** determines whether people experience the change as shared progress or an unfair burden.

Choice resource household phụ thuộc **energy use salience** và **appliance default** âm thầm shape routine. **Thermostat inertia** giữ setting cũ, trong khi **rebound expectation** nhắc planner efficiency có thể đổi behavior cùng consumption. **Conservation identity** hỗ trợ action, còn **utility bill framing** làm cost invisible dễ hiểu. **Energy poverty perception** quan trọng vì saving energy không giống accept cold unsafe. **Peak demand avoidance** dễ hơn với automation, nhưng **renewable trust** và **green premium acceptance** quyết định người ta support system mới. **Household energy norm** normalize care, còn **efficiency hassle** block upgrade dù attractive. **Comfort preference** và **heating risk perception** shape trade-off daily, còn **bill shock response** có thể dẫn tới cut harmful. **Metering feedback** timely reveal problem trước khi **repair procrastination** costly. **Water-saving habit** và **resource scarcity cue** visible hỗ trợ conservation, nhưng **energy transition fairness** quyết định change được trải nghiệm như progress shared hay burden unfair.
