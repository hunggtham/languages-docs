# C1 Vocabulary 14 — Behavioral evidence and experiment design

This lesson follows how behavioral researchers measure choices, test mechanisms, and judge whether an intervention travels beyond its original study.

Flow: **behavioral measurement → option abundance effect → participant attention check → comprehension friction → hypothetical response bias → contextual generalization → treatment visibility → study-expectation cue → researcher expectancy → replication incentive → preregistration norm → selective outcome reporting → journal publication bias → p-hacking risk → specification sensitivity → treatment-effect variation → mechanism identification → behavioral heterogeneity → nudge persistence → protocol adherence**.

## 1. behavioral measurement /bɪˈheɪvjərəl ˈmɛʒərmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the systematic recording of choices, actions, or responses that represent a behavioral construct.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** ghi nhận có hệ thống choice, action hoặc response đại diện cho construct behavior.
**Grammar & collocations:** `valid behavioral measurement` — measurement; `improve behavioral measurement` — improve.
**Examples:** `Behavioral measurement was redesigned so that reported intentions were separated from observed choices.` → Measurement behavioral được redesign để tách intention report khỏi choice observed.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `행동 측정`.

## 2. option abundance effect /ˈɑpʃən əˈbʌndəns ɪˈfɛkt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a change in choice behavior caused by presenting an unusually large number of alternatives.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** thay đổi behavior choice do đưa ra quá nhiều alternative.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong option abundance effect` — effect; `test an option abundance effect` — test.
**Examples:** `The option abundance effect reduced completed purchases when the catalog became too broad.` → Effect abundance option làm purchase completed giảm khi catalog quá rộng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `선택지 풍부성 효과`.

## 3. participant attention check /pɑrˈtɪsəpənt əˈtɛnʃən tʃɛk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a task or question used to identify whether a participant is reading and processing study instructions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** task hoặc question kiểm tra participant có đọc và process instruction không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `simple participant attention check` — check; `pass a participant attention check` — pass.
**Examples:** `A participant attention check removed responses that had ignored the central instruction.` → Check attention participant loại response bỏ qua instruction chính.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `참가자 주의 확인`.

## 4. comprehension friction /ˌkɑmprɪˈhɛnʃən ˈfrɪkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** difficulty understanding a question, option, or instruction that can distort a measured choice.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** difficulty hiểu question, option hoặc instruction làm méo choice được đo.
**Grammar & collocations:** `avoidable comprehension friction` — friction; `reduce comprehension friction` — reduce.
**Examples:** `Comprehension friction made a technically neutral survey feel confusing to first-time respondents.` → Friction comprehension khiến survey neutral về kỹ thuật trở nên confusing với respondent lần đầu.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `이해 마찰`.

## 5. hypothetical response bias /ˌhaɪpəˈθɛtɪkəl rɪˈspɑns baɪəs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a systematic difference between what people say they would do and what they do when consequences are real.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** difference có hệ thống giữa điều người ta nói sẽ làm và hành động khi consequence thật.
**Grammar & collocations:** `large hypothetical response bias` — bias; `estimate hypothetical response bias` — estimate.
**Examples:** `Hypothetical response bias inflated stated willingness to pay for the environmental program.` → Bias response hypothetical inflate willingness to pay được state cho program môi trường.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `가상 응답 편향`.

## 6. contextual generalization /kənˈtɛkstʃuəl ˌdʒɛnərələˈzeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the extent to which a behavioral finding remains useful in a different setting, population, or time.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mức finding behavior vẫn useful trong setting, population hoặc time khác.
**Grammar & collocations:** `limited contextual generalization` — generalization; `assess contextual generalization` — assess.
**Examples:** `Contextual generalization was uncertain because the original sample came from one unusually motivated workplace.` → Generalization contextual chưa chắc vì sample gốc từ một workplace có động lực bất thường.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `맥락적 일반화`.

## 7. treatment visibility /ˈtriːtmənt ˌvɪzəˈbɪləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** how noticeable an intervention is to the people who receive or evaluate it.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mức intervention noticeable với người receive hoặc evaluate nó.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high treatment visibility` — visibility; `vary treatment visibility` — vary.
**Examples:** `High treatment visibility allowed participants to infer the study's preferred answer.` → Visibility treatment cao cho participant đoán được answer study preferred.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `처치 가시성`.

## 8. study-expectation cue /ˈstʌdi ˌɛkspɛkˈteɪʃən kjuː/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a cue in a study that leads participants to guess its purpose and adjust their behavior.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cue trong study khiến participant đoán purpose và điều chỉnh behavior.
**Grammar & collocations:** `subtle study-expectation cue` — cue; `minimize study-expectation cues` — minimize.
**Examples:** `A study-expectation cue encouraged respondents to choose the answer that sounded socially responsible.` → Cue expectation study khiến respondent chọn answer nghe có vẻ socially responsible.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `연구 기대 단서`.

## 9. researcher expectancy /rɪˈsɝtʃər ɪkˈspɛktənsi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an investigator's expectation that can unintentionally influence measurement, interaction, or interpretation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** expectation của researcher vô tình influence measurement, interaction hoặc interpretation.
**Grammar & collocations:** `unconscious researcher expectancy` — expectancy; `blind against researcher expectancy` — blind.
**Examples:** `Double coding reduced researcher expectancy during the classification of ambiguous answers.` → Coding double giảm expectancy researcher khi classify answer ambiguous.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `연구자 기대`.

## 10. replication incentive /ˌrɛpləˈkeɪʃən ɪnˈsɛntɪv/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a reward or career motive that makes researchers more willing to repeat and test an earlier finding.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** reward hoặc motive career khiến researcher sẵn sàng repeat và test finding cũ.
**Grammar & collocations:** `weak replication incentive` — incentive; `create a replication incentive` — create.
**Examples:** `A replication incentive encouraged the lab to publish a careful null result.` → Incentive replication khuyến khích lab publish null result cẩn thận.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `반복 연구 인센티브`.

## 11. preregistration norm /ˌpriːˌrɛdʒɪˈstreɪʃən nɔrm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a shared expectation that researchers record hypotheses and analysis plans before seeing the main data.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** expectation shared rằng researcher record hypothesis và plan trước khi xem data chính.
**Grammar & collocations:** `credible preregistration norm` — norm; `follow a preregistration norm` — follow.
**Examples:** `The preregistration norm clarified which outcome was primary before the survey was launched.` → Norm preregistration làm rõ outcome primary trước khi survey launch.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `사전 등록 규범`.

## 12. selective outcome reporting /sɪˈlɛktɪv ˈaʊtˌkʌm rɪˈpɔrtɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** presenting only the measured outcomes that support a preferred story while leaving other outcomes unreported.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** chỉ report outcome ủng hộ story preferred và bỏ outcome khác.
**Grammar & collocations:** `serious selective outcome reporting` — reporting; `detect selective outcome reporting` — detect.
**Examples:** `Selective outcome reporting made a mixed intervention look consistently successful.` → Reporting outcome selective khiến intervention mixed trông như luôn successful.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `선택적 결과 보고`.

## 13. journal publication bias /ˈdʒɝnəl ˌpʌblɪˈkeɪʃən baɪəs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a tendency for journals to favor striking or positive findings over careful null or contradictory results.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** journal ưu tiên finding striking hoặc positive hơn null hoặc contradictory result.
**Grammar & collocations:** `persistent journal publication bias` — bias; `correct for journal publication bias` — correct.
**Examples:** `Journal publication bias can make a small effect appear more reliable than the full evidence suggests.` → Bias publication journal khiến effect nhỏ trông reliable hơn evidence đầy đủ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `학술지 출판 편향`.

## 14. p-hacking risk /ˈpiː ˌhækɪŋ rɪsk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the risk that repeated analytical choices are used until a result crosses a conventional significance threshold.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** risk thử nhiều choice analysis tới khi result vượt threshold significance quen thuộc.
**Grammar & collocations:** `substantial p-hacking risk` — risk; `reduce p-hacking risk` — reduce.
**Examples:** `A transparent analysis plan lowered p-hacking risk in the multi-outcome study.` → Plan analysis transparent giảm risk p-hacking trong study nhiều outcome.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `p-해킹 위험`.

## 15. specification sensitivity /ˌspɛsəfɪˈkeɪʃən ˌsɛnsəˈtɪvəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the degree to which an estimated result changes when reasonable model or coding choices change.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mức estimate thay đổi khi model hoặc coding choice hợp lý thay đổi.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high specification sensitivity` — sensitivity; `report specification sensitivity` — report.
**Examples:** `Specification sensitivity showed that the headline effect depended on one coding decision.` → Sensitivity specification cho thấy effect headline phụ thuộc một coding decision.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `모형 설정 민감도`.

## 16. treatment-effect variation /ˈtriːtmənt ɪˈfɛkt ˌvɛriˈeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** differences in the size or direction of an intervention's effect across people, contexts, or outcomes.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** khác biệt về size hoặc direction effect giữa người, context hoặc outcome.
**Grammar & collocations:** `meaningful treatment-effect variation` — variation; `model treatment-effect variation` — model.
**Examples:** `Treatment-effect variation was larger among new users than among experienced participants.` → Variation treatment-effect lớn hơn ở user mới so với participant experienced.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `처치 효과 변이`.

## 17. mechanism identification /ˈmɛkəˌnɪzəm aɪˌdɛntəfɪˈkeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** establishing the process through which an intervention is expected to change behavior or outcomes.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** xác định process intervention dùng để change behavior hoặc outcome.
**Grammar & collocations:** `careful mechanism identification` — identification; `support mechanism identification` — support.
**Examples:** `Mechanism identification required measuring attention rather than assuming that attention had changed.` → Identification mechanism cần đo attention thay vì assume attention đã đổi.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `메커니즘 식별`.

## 18. behavioral heterogeneity /bɪˈheɪvjərəl ˌhɛtərədʒəˈniəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** meaningful differences in preferences, responses, or strategies among members of a studied population.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** khác biệt meaningful về preference, response hoặc strategy giữa member population.
**Grammar & collocations:** `substantial behavioral heterogeneity` — heterogeneity; `capture behavioral heterogeneity` — capture.
**Examples:** `Behavioral heterogeneity explained why the same reminder helped some users but annoyed others.` → Heterogeneity behavioral giải thích reminder giúp user này nhưng annoy user khác.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `행동 이질성`.

## 19. nudge persistence /nʌdʒ pərˈsɪstəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the extent to which a small choice-design intervention continues to influence behavior after time or attention changes.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mức nudge vẫn influence behavior khi time hoặc attention thay đổi.
**Grammar & collocations:** `limited nudge persistence` — persistence; `test nudge persistence` — test.
**Examples:** `Nudge persistence declined once the reminder became familiar and easy to ignore.` → Persistence nudge giảm khi reminder trở nên quen và dễ ignore.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `넛지 지속성`.

## 20. protocol adherence /ˈproʊtəˌkɔl ədˈhɪrəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the degree to which a study or program is delivered according to its specified procedures.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mức study hoặc program được deliver đúng procedure đã specify.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high protocol adherence` — adherence; `monitor protocol adherence` — monitor.
**Examples:** `Low protocol adherence made it difficult to tell whether the intervention or its implementation had failed.` → Adherence protocol thấp khiến khó biết intervention hay implementation fail.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `프로토콜 준수`.

## Review in context

Sound behavioral research begins with clear **behavioral measurement**. An **option abundance effect** can make a catalog look generous while reducing completion, so a **participant attention check** and a review of **comprehension friction** help distinguish disengagement from preference. Researchers must consider **hypothetical response bias**, ask about **contextual generalization**, and record **treatment visibility** because a visible intervention can create a **study-expectation cue**. Blinding helps limit **researcher expectancy**, while a **replication incentive** and a credible **preregistration norm** make the evidence easier to audit. Without those safeguards, **selective outcome reporting**, **journal publication bias**, and **p-hacking risk** can exaggerate a result. **Specification sensitivity** shows whether the estimate survives reasonable choices, and **treatment-effect variation** prevents an average from hiding important groups. **Mechanism identification** asks how the effect works, whereas **behavioral heterogeneity** asks for whom it works. Finally, **nudge persistence** and **protocol adherence** determine whether a promising laboratory result survives contact with real practice.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

Nghiên cứu hành vi tốt bắt đầu bằng **behavioral measurement** rõ ràng. **Option abundance effect** có thể khiến catalog trông phong phú nhưng làm giảm completion, nên **participant attention check** và việc xem xét **comprehension friction** giúp phân biệt disengagement với preference. Researcher phải tính đến **hypothetical response bias**, hỏi về **contextual generalization**, và ghi nhận **treatment visibility** vì intervention visible có thể tạo **study-expectation cue**. Blinding giúp hạn chế **researcher expectancy**, còn **replication incentive** và **preregistration norm** đáng tin khiến evidence dễ audit hơn. Không có safeguard, **selective outcome reporting**, **journal publication bias** và **p-hacking risk** có thể phóng đại result. **Specification sensitivity** cho thấy estimate có đứng vững trước choice hợp lý không, còn **treatment-effect variation** ngăn average che giấu group quan trọng. **Mechanism identification** hỏi effect hoạt động thế nào, trong khi **behavioral heterogeneity** hỏi nó hiệu quả với ai. Cuối cùng, **nudge persistence** và **protocol adherence** quyết định result promising trong lab có tồn tại khi đi vào thực hành hay không.
