# C1 Vocabulary 17 — Behavioral digital platforms and attention

This lesson follows how people navigate digital platforms when defaults, recommendations, social signals, and privacy concerns compete for limited attention.

Flow: **platform choice architecture → notification fatigue → algorithmic salience → recommender trust → digital default → interface friction → dark pattern perception → privacy trade-off heuristic → data disclosure reluctance → online identity cue → social proof signal → platform switching cost → digital scarcity cue → engagement metric fixation → content moderation trust → algorithm aversion → personalization comfort → search ranking reliance → online outrage contagion → digital well-being policy**.

## 1. platform choice architecture /ˈplætˌfɔrm tʃɔɪs ˈɑrkəˌtɛktʃər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the arrangement of screens, options, defaults, and prompts that shapes decisions on a digital platform.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cách sắp xếp screen, option, default và prompt shape decision trên platform digital.
**Grammar & collocations:** `transparent platform choice architecture` — architecture; `audit platform choice architecture` — audit.
**Examples:** `Platform choice architecture made the privacy-protective setting visible before the user accepted the account.` → Architecture choice platform làm setting bảo vệ privacy visible trước khi user accept account.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `플랫폼 선택 설계`.

## 2. notification fatigue /ˌnoʊtəfɪˈkeɪʃən fəˈtiːɡ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** reduced attention and responsiveness caused by receiving too many digital alerts.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** attention và responsiveness giảm vì nhận quá nhiều alert digital.
**Grammar & collocations:** `severe notification fatigue` — fatigue; `prevent notification fatigue` — prevent.
**Examples:** `Notification fatigue caused users to ignore the one alert that required immediate action.` → Fatigue notification khiến user ignore alert duy nhất cần action ngay.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `알림 피로`.

## 3. algorithmic salience /ˌælgəˈrɪðmɪk ˈseɪliəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the degree to which an algorithmically selected item captures attention because of its placement or presentation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mức item do algorithm select capture attention vì placement hoặc presentation.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high algorithmic salience` — salience; `increase algorithmic salience` — increase.
**Examples:** `Algorithmic salience made a dramatic headline seem more representative than the full news list.` → Salience algorithmic khiến headline dramatic trông representative hơn list news đầy đủ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `알고리즘 현저성`.

## 4. recommender trust /ˌrɛkəˈmɛndər trʌst/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** confidence that a recommendation system understands a user's needs and presents suitable options.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** confidence recommender hiểu need user và present option phù hợp.
**Grammar & collocations:** `calibrated recommender trust` — trust; `build recommender trust` — build.
**Examples:** `Recommender trust fell when the service repeatedly suggested products that contradicted stated preferences.` → Trust recommender giảm khi service liên tục suggest product trái preference đã state.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `추천 시스템 신뢰`.

## 5. digital default /ˈdɪdʒətəl dɪˈfɔlt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a preselected digital setting or action that remains unless a user actively changes it.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** setting hoặc action digital preselect và giữ nguyên nếu user không đổi.
**Grammar & collocations:** `privacy-preserving digital default` — default; `change a digital default` — change.
**Examples:** `A digital default shared activity data until users opened a less visible settings page.` → Default digital share activity data tới khi user mở settings page khó thấy hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `디지털 기본값`.

## 6. interface friction /ˈɪntərˌfeɪs ˈfrɪkʃən/

**Core meaning (English):** extra effort, delay, or confusion created by the design of a digital interface.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** effort, delay hoặc confusion thêm do design interface digital tạo ra.

## 7. dark pattern perception /dɑrk ˈpætərn pərˈsɛpʃən/

**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** judgment interface steer user bằng design manipulative hoặc obscure.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong dark pattern perception` — perception; `measure dark pattern perception` — measure.

## 8. privacy trade-off heuristic /ˈpraɪvəsi ˈtreɪdˌɔf hjʊrɪstɪk/

**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** judgment nhanh benefit digital có đáng information cá nhân phải provide không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `simple privacy trade-off heuristic` — heuristic; `activate a privacy trade-off heuristic` — activate.

## 9. data disclosure reluctance /ˈdeɪtə dɪˈskloʊʒər rɪˈlʌktəns/

**Core meaning (English):** unwillingness to provide personal information because the purpose, safety, or future use is uncertain.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** không willing provide information vì purpose, safety hoặc future use không chắc.
**Grammar & collocations:** `reasonable data disclosure reluctance` — reluctance; `reduce data disclosure reluctance` — reduce.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `데이터 공개 주저`.

## Review in context

Digital services guide behavior through **platform choice architecture**. Too many alerts create **notification fatigue**, while **algorithmic salience** makes selected content feel unusually important. **Recommender trust** can support discovery, but a hidden **digital default** or unnecessary **interface friction** may produce a **dark pattern perception**. Users apply a **privacy trade-off heuristic** when deciding whether a benefit justifies disclosure, and **data disclosure reluctance** rises when purpose is unclear. An **online identity cue** and a **social proof signal** can make one option feel credible, while **platform switching cost** keeps people from leaving. A **digital scarcity cue** accelerates action; **engagement metric fixation** may then reward attention without trust. Consistent rules strengthen **content moderation trust**, whereas one visible error can trigger **algorithm aversion**. **Personalization comfort** helps until it becomes restrictive, and **search ranking reliance** can hide useful alternatives. **Online outrage contagion** exploits these attention patterns, so a serious **digital well-being policy** must protect autonomy rather than merely reduce screen time.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

Digital service hướng behavior qua **platform choice architecture**. Quá nhiều alert tạo **notification fatigue**, còn **algorithmic salience** khiến content được select trông quan trọng bất thường. **Recommender trust** hỗ trợ discovery, nhưng **digital default** hidden hoặc **interface friction** không cần thiết có thể tạo **dark pattern perception**. User dùng **privacy trade-off heuristic** để quyết định benefit có đáng disclosure không, và **data disclosure reluctance** tăng khi purpose không rõ. **Online identity cue** và **social proof signal** khiến một option trông credible, còn **platform switching cost** giữ người ta không rời đi. **Digital scarcity cue** đẩy action nhanh; sau đó **engagement metric fixation** có thể reward attention mà không có trust. Rule consistent củng cố **content moderation trust**, trong khi một error visible có thể trigger **algorithm aversion**. **Personalization comfort** hữu ích tới khi trở nên restrictive, còn **search ranking reliance** có thể che alternative hữu ích. **Online outrage contagion** khai thác pattern attention này, nên **digital well-being policy** nghiêm túc phải bảo vệ autonomy thay vì chỉ giảm screen time.
