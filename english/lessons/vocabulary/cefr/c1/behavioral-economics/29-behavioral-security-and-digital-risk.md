# C1 Vocabulary 29 — Behavioral security and digital risk

This lesson follows how people notice, interpret, and respond to digital threats when convenience, trust, uncertainty, and recovery costs compete.

Flow: **password salience → security fatigue → privacy threat perception → phishing suspicion → default credential behavior → trust transfer cue → breach notification response → authentication friction → risk compensation digital → security norm → incident disclosure credibility → vigilance decay → scam familiarity cue → verification habit → cyber insurance perception → data minimization preference → permission overload → recovery confidence belief → adversarial uncertainty → security-by-design framing**.

## 1. password salience /ˈpæsˌwɝd ˈseɪliəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** how noticeable and important password security is when a person chooses a digital action.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mức password security noticeable và important khi choose action digital.
**Grammar & collocations:** `low password salience` — salience; `increase password salience` — increase.
**Examples:** `Password salience rose after the service showed how a reused credential could expose several accounts.` → Salience password tăng sau khi service show credential reuse expose nhiều account.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `비밀번호 현저성`.

## 2. security fatigue /sɪˈkjʊrəti fəˈtiːɡ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** declining attention and compliance caused by repeated security warnings, prompts, or requirements.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** attention và compliance giảm do warning, prompt hoặc requirement security lặp lại.
**Grammar & collocations:** `severe security fatigue` — fatigue; `prevent security fatigue` — prevent.
**Examples:** `Security fatigue made employees approve a familiar sign-in prompt without checking its source.` → Fatigue security khiến employee approve prompt sign-in quen mà không check source.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `보안 피로`.

## 3. privacy threat perception /ˈpraɪvəsi θrɛt pərˈsɛpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a judgment about how likely and harmful unwanted collection or use of personal information may be.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** judgment collection hoặc use information personal unwanted likely và harmful thế nào.
**Grammar & collocations:** `elevated privacy threat perception` — perception; `calibrate privacy threat perception` — calibrate.
**Examples:** `Privacy threat perception increased when the application requested contacts unrelated to its main function.` → Perception threat privacy tăng khi app request contact không liên quan function chính.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `개인정보 위협 인식`.

## 4. phishing suspicion /ˈfɪʃɪŋ səˈspɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** doubt that a message or website is impersonating a trusted source to obtain information or access.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** doubt message hoặc website impersonate source trusted để lấy information hoặc access.
**Grammar & collocations:** `low phishing suspicion` — suspicion; `raise phishing suspicion` — raise.
**Examples:** `Phishing suspicion grew when the payment link used a slightly altered company name.` → Suspicion phishing tăng khi payment link dùng company name đổi nhẹ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `피싱 의심`.

## 5. default credential behavior /dɪˈfɔlt krəˈdɛnʃəl bɪˈheɪvjər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the tendency to leave preset usernames, passwords, or access settings unchanged.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** tendency để username, password hoặc access setting preset unchanged.
**Grammar & collocations:** `unsafe default credential behavior` — behavior; `change default credential behavior` — change.
**Examples:** `Default credential behavior left the new device exposed until an administrator reviewed it.` → Behavior credential default để device mới exposed tới khi administrator review.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `기본 자격 증명 행동`.

## 6. trust transfer cue /trʌst ˈtrænsfər kjuː/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a signal that makes trust in one person, brand, or system spill over to another connected service.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** signal trust từ person, brand hoặc system này spill sang service connected.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong trust transfer cue` — cue; `use a trust transfer cue` — use.
**Examples:** `A familiar payment logo acted as a trust transfer cue for an unfamiliar marketplace.` → Logo payment quen act như cue transfer trust cho marketplace unfamiliar.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `신뢰 전이 단서`.

## 7. breach notification response /briːtʃ ˌnoʊtəfɪˈkeɪʃən rɪˈspɑns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the emotional and practical actions people take after learning that information or systems were compromised.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** action emotional và practical sau khi biết information hoặc system bị compromise.
**Grammar & collocations:** `rapid breach notification response` — response; `improve breach notification response` — improve.
**Examples:** `Breach notification response was slow because customers could not tell which account required a password change.` → Response notification breach chậm vì customer không biết account nào cần đổi password.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `침해 통지 대응`.

## 8. authentication friction /ɔːˌθɛntɪˈkeɪʃən ˈfrɪkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** extra steps, delay, or confusion involved in proving one's identity before access is granted.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** step, delay hoặc confusion thêm để prove identity trước khi access.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high authentication friction` — friction; `reduce authentication friction` — reduce.
**Examples:** `Authentication friction caused users to reuse a weak backup method during an urgent task.` → Friction authentication khiến user reuse backup method yếu trong task urgent.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `인증 마찰`.

## 9. risk compensation digital /rɪsk ˌkɑmpənˈseɪʃən ˈdɪdʒətəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** taking greater digital risks after adopting a tool or setting believed to provide extra protection.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** take risk digital lớn hơn sau khi adopt tool hoặc setting được tin bảo vệ thêm.
**Grammar & collocations:** `possible risk compensation digital` — compensation; `monitor risk compensation digital` — monitor.
**Examples:** `Risk compensation digital appeared when employees shared more sensitive files after installing a security extension.` → Compensation risk digital xuất hiện khi employee share file sensitive hơn sau install extension security.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `디지털 위험 보상`.

## 10. security norm /sɪˈkjʊrəti nɔrm/

## 11. incident disclosure credibility /ˈɪnsədənt dɪˈskloʊʒər krɛdəˈbɪləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the perceived honesty, completeness, and usefulness of an organization's account of a security incident.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** honesty, completeness và usefulness được perceive của account incident security.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high incident disclosure credibility` — credibility; `protect incident disclosure credibility` — protect.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `사고 공개 신뢰성`.

## 12. vigilance decay /ˈvɪdʒələns dɪˈkeɪ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the gradual decline of careful attention after a person has monitored for threats for a long time.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** attention careful giảm dần sau khi monitor threat lâu.
**Grammar & collocations:** `predictable vigilance decay` — decay; `counter vigilance decay` — counter.
**Examples:** `Vigilance decay made the final review of a long incident less careful than the first.` → Decay vigilance khiến review cuối incident dài kém careful hơn review đầu.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `경계력 저하`.

## 13. scam familiarity cue /skæm fəˌmɪliˈɛrəti kjuː/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a signal that a fraudulent message resembles a known story, brand, or routine and therefore feels less suspicious.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** signal scam resemble story, brand hoặc routine known nên feel ít suspicious.
**Grammar & collocations:** `misleading scam familiarity cue` — cue; `detect a scam familiarity cue` — detect.
**Examples:** `A scam familiarity cue made the fake delivery notice seem ordinary during a busy week.` → Cue familiarity scam làm notice delivery fake trông ordinary trong week busy.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `사기 친숙성 단서`.

## 14. verification habit /ˌvɛrəfəˈkeɪʃən ˈhæbɪt/
**Part of speech:** noun

**Grammar & collocations:** `stable verification habit` — habit; `form a verification habit` — form.

## 18. recovery confidence belief /rɪˈkʌvəri ˈkɑnfədəns bɪˈliːf/

## 19. adversarial uncertainty /ˌædvərˈsɛriəl ˌʌnsərˈtənti/

**Core meaning (English):** uncertainty caused by an opponent deliberately adapting tactics to exploit a system or decision-maker.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** uncertainty do opponent deliberately adapt tactic để exploit system hoặc decision-maker.

**Grammar & collocations:** `credible security-by-design framing` — framing; `use security-by-design framing` — use.
**Examples:** `Security-by-design framing led the team to limit data collection before writing the marketing plan.` → Framing security-by-design khiến team limit data collection trước khi write marketing plan.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `설계 단계 보안 프레이밍`.

## Review in context

Digital safety depends on **password salience**, but repeated prompts can create **security fatigue**. **Privacy threat perception** should rise when data use expands, and **phishing suspicion** helps people question a familiar-looking request. **Default credential behavior** leaves avoidable openings, while a **trust transfer cue** may make an unfamiliar service seem safe. After a breach, **breach notification response** is shaped by **authentication friction** and possible **risk compensation digital**. A shared **security norm** supports reporting, and **incident disclosure credibility** depends on acknowledging uncertainty. Long monitoring produces **vigilance decay**, while a **scam familiarity cue** makes imitation feel ordinary. A stable **verification habit** is more reliable than fear alone. **Cyber insurance perception** and **data minimization preference** shape preparation, but **permission overload** encourages careless approval. **Recovery confidence belief** improves through tested backups; **adversarial uncertainty** requires flexible defenses. Finally, **security-by-design framing** makes protection part of everyday product choices.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

Safety digital phụ thuộc **password salience**, nhưng prompt lặp tạo **security fatigue**. **Privacy threat perception** nên tăng khi data use mở rộng, và **phishing suspicion** giúp question request trông familiar. **Default credential behavior** tạo opening tránh được, còn **trust transfer cue** làm service unfamiliar trông safe. Sau breach, **breach notification response** chịu ảnh hưởng **authentication friction** và khả năng **risk compensation digital**. **Security norm** shared hỗ trợ report, còn **incident disclosure credibility** phụ thuộc acknowledge uncertainty. Monitor lâu tạo **vigilance decay**, trong khi **scam familiarity cue** làm imitation trông ordinary. **Verification habit** stable reliable hơn fear alone. **Cyber insurance perception** và **data minimization preference** shape preparation, nhưng **permission overload** khuyến khích approve careless. **Recovery confidence belief** improve qua backup tested; **adversarial uncertainty** cần defense flexible. Cuối cùng, **security-by-design framing** làm protection thành một phần product choice hằng ngày.
