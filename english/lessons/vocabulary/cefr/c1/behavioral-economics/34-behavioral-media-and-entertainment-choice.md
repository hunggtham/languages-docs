# C1 Vocabulary 34 — Behavioral media and entertainment choice

This lesson follows how people choose, trust, interpret, and remain with media when attention, identity, novelty, and platform design interact.

Flow: **attention scarcity media → genre expectation → spoiler aversion → subscription inertia → streaming default → recommendation fatigue → content completion bias → cliffhanger effect → parasocial trust → celebrity credibility → audience identity cue → entertainment value framing → ad avoidance motive → media multitasking cost → cultural translation friction → news source loyalty → headline curiosity gap → misinformation familiarity → creator reciprocity expectation → public media trust**.

## 1. attention scarcity media /əˈtɛnʃən ˈskɛrsəti ˈmiːdiə/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** limited mental time and focus that force people to choose among competing media messages.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** time và focus mental limited khiến choose giữa media message cạnh tranh.
**Grammar & collocations:** `severe attention scarcity media` — scarcity; `manage attention scarcity media` — manage.
**Examples:** `Attention scarcity media made the reader save several articles but finish none.` → Scarcity attention media khiến reader save nhiều article nhưng finish không cái nào.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `미디어 주의 희소성`.

## 2. genre expectation /ˈʒɑnrə ˌɛkspɛkˈteɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a belief about the style, pace, emotion, or outcome normally associated with a type of media.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** belief style, pace, emotion hoặc outcome thường gắn với genre.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong genre expectation` — expectation; `violate genre expectations` — violate.
**Examples:** `Genre expectation made the quiet ending seem disappointing to viewers expecting a thriller.` → Expectation genre khiến ending quiet disappoint viewer expect thriller.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `장르 기대`.

## 3. spoiler aversion /ˈspɔɪlər əˈvɝʒən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a strong desire to avoid information that reveals a story's important events or outcomes.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** desire mạnh avoid information reveal event hoặc outcome story.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong spoiler aversion` — aversion; `respect spoiler aversion` — respect.
**Examples:** `Spoiler aversion led the fan to mute several keywords before watching the final episode.` → Aversion spoiler khiến fan mute keyword trước xem episode cuối.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `스포일러 회피`.

## 4. subscription inertia /səbˈskrɪpʃən ˈɪnɝʒə/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** continuing to pay for a subscription because cancellation requires attention, even when use has declined.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** tiếp tục pay subscription vì cancel cần attention dù use giảm.
**Grammar & collocations:** `persistent subscription inertia` — inertia; `overcome subscription inertia` — overcome.
**Examples:** `Subscription inertia kept three unused services on the household card.` → Inertia subscription giữ ba service không use trên card household.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `구독 관성`.

## 5. streaming default /ˈstriːmɪŋ dɪˈfɔlt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a preselected platform, quality, autoplay, or recommendation setting that shapes viewing unless changed.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** platform, quality, autoplay hoặc recommendation setting preselect shape viewing nếu không change.
**Grammar & collocations:** `convenient streaming default` — default; `change a streaming default` — change.
**Examples:** `A streaming default started the next episode before the viewer decided whether to continue.` → Default streaming start episode next trước viewer decide continue không.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `스트리밍 기본값`.

## 6. recommendation fatigue /ˌrɛkəmənˈdeɪʃən fəˈtiːɡ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** reduced interest or trust caused by receiving too many recommendations that feel repetitive or irrelevant.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** interest hoặc trust giảm vì nhận nhiều recommendation repetitive hay irrelevant.
**Grammar & collocations:** `severe recommendation fatigue` — fatigue; `prevent recommendation fatigue` — prevent.
**Examples:** `Recommendation fatigue made the user browse an unfamiliar library instead of following the homepage.` → Fatigue recommendation khiến user browse library unfamiliar thay homepage.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `추천 피로`.

## 7. content completion bias /ˈkɑntɛnt kəmˈpliːʃən baɪəs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a tendency to finish a piece of media because it has been started, even when interest or quality has fallen.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** tendency finish media đã start dù interest hoặc quality giảm.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong content completion bias` — bias; `counter content completion bias` — counter.
**Examples:** `Content completion bias kept the viewer watching a weak series through its final season.` → Bias completion content giữ viewer xem series yếu tới season cuối.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `콘텐츠 완주 편향`.

## 8. cliffhanger effect /ˈklɪfˌhæŋər ɪˈfɛkt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** increased curiosity or continued attention caused by ending a story before a significant uncertainty is resolved.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** curiosity hoặc attention tăng vì end story trước resolve uncertainty significant.
**Grammar & collocations:** `powerful cliffhanger effect` — effect; `use the cliffhanger effect` — use.
**Examples:** `The cliffhanger effect increased next-week viewing but frustrated people who preferred closure.` → Effect cliffhanger tăng viewing tuần sau nhưng frustrate người prefer closure.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `클리프행어 효과`.

## 9. parasocial trust /ˌpærəˈsoʊʃəl trʌst/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** confidence in a media figure who feels familiar despite there being no mutual personal relationship.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** confidence vào media figure cảm thấy familiar dù không có relationship personal mutual.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong parasocial trust` — trust; `exploit parasocial trust` — exploit.
**Examples:** `Parasocial trust made followers accept product advice that had not been independently tested.` → Trust parasocial khiến follower accept advice product chưa test independent.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `의사사회적 신뢰`.

## 10. celebrity credibility /səˈlɛbrəti krɛdəˈbɪləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the perceived expertise, honesty, or relevance of a famous person when they make a claim.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** expertise, honesty hoặc relevance được perceive của celebrity khi make claim.
**Grammar & collocations:** `limited celebrity credibility` — credibility; `question celebrity credibility` — question.
**Examples:** `Celebrity credibility was high among fans but low among specialists evaluating the medical claim.` → Credibility celebrity cao với fan nhưng thấp với specialist evaluate claim medical.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `유명인 신뢰성`.

## 11. audience identity cue /ˈɔdiəns aɪˈdɛntəti kjuː/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a signal that tells viewers what kind of person or group the media is meant to represent.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** signal cho viewer biết media represent person hoặc group kiểu nào.
**Grammar & collocations:** `clear audience identity cue` — cue; `provide an audience identity cue` — provide.
**Examples:** `An audience identity cue made the documentary feel designed for local families rather than distant experts.` → Cue identity audience làm documentary trông design cho family local thay expert xa.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `관객 정체성 단서`.

## 12. entertainment value framing /ˌɛntərˈteɪnmənt ˈvælju ˈfreɪmɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the way media is presented as fun, relaxation, learning, status, escape, or emotional connection.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cách present media như fun, relaxation, learning, status, escape hoặc connection emotional.
**Grammar & collocations:** `relaxation-focused entertainment value framing` — framing; `change entertainment value framing` — change.
**Examples:** `Entertainment value framing made a serious documentary attractive as a shared evening activity.` → Framing value entertainment làm documentary serious attractive như activity evening shared.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `엔터테인먼트 가치 프레이밍`.

## 13. ad avoidance motive /æd əˈvɔɪdəns ˈmoʊtɪv/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a reason to skip, block, or pay to remove advertising because it interrupts attention or feels intrusive.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** reason skip, block hoặc pay remove ad vì interrupt attention hoặc intrusive.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong ad avoidance motive` — motive; `understand an ad avoidance motive` — understand.
**Examples:** `Ad avoidance motive led the listener to choose a more expensive plan with uninterrupted playback.` → Motive avoidance ad khiến listener choose plan đắt hơn với playback không interrupt.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `광고 회피 동기`.

## 14. media multitasking cost /ˈmiːdiə ˌmʌltiˈtæskɪŋ kɔst/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** lost comprehension, memory, and attention caused by consuming several media streams at once.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** comprehension, memory và attention mất do consume nhiều media stream cùng lúc.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high media multitasking cost` — cost; `reduce media multitasking costs` — reduce.
**Examples:** `Media multitasking cost made the lecture feel familiar without leaving a clear memory.` → Cost multitasking media khiến lecture feel familiar mà không để memory rõ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `미디어 멀티태스킹 비용`.

## 15. cultural translation friction /ˈkʌltʃərəl trænzˈleɪʃən ˈfrɪkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** difficulty caused when humor, references, values, or emotion do not transfer smoothly across languages or cultures.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** difficulty humor, reference, value hoặc emotion không transfer smooth qua language hay culture.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high cultural translation friction` — friction; `reduce cultural translation friction` — reduce.
**Examples:** `Cultural translation friction weakened the joke even though every word was technically accurate.` → Friction translation cultural làm joke yếu dù từng word accurate kỹ thuật.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `문화 번역 마찰`.

## 16. news source loyalty /nuːz sɔrs ˈlɔɪəlti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a tendency to continue using one news source because it feels familiar, trustworthy, or identity-consistent.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** tendency continue use source news vì familiar, trustworthy hoặc match identity.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong news source loyalty` — loyalty; `challenge news source loyalty` — challenge.
**Examples:** `News source loyalty made readers dismiss a correction from a publication they usually disliked.` → Loyalty source news khiến reader dismiss correction từ publication thường dislike.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `뉴스 출처 충성도`.

## 17. headline curiosity gap /ˈhɛdˌlaɪn ˌkjʊriˈɑsəti ɡæp/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the gap between what a headline reveals and what a reader wants to know, which encourages a click.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** gap giữa headline reveal và điều reader muốn know khiến click.
**Grammar & collocations:** `large headline curiosity gap` — gap; `exploit a headline curiosity gap` — exploit.
**Examples:** `A headline curiosity gap increased clicks even though the article offered little new evidence.` → Gap curiosity headline tăng click dù article ít evidence mới.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `헤드라인 호기심 격차`.

## 18. misinformation familiarity /ˌmɪsɪnfərˈmeɪʃən fəˌmɪliˈɛrəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the tendency for repeated exposure to make false information feel more believable or true.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** exposure lặp làm information false feel believable hoặc true hơn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `dangerous misinformation familiarity` — familiarity; `counter misinformation familiarity` — counter.
**Examples:** `Misinformation familiarity made the repeated claim seem credible despite the absence of evidence.` → Familiarity misinformation làm claim lặp trông credible dù thiếu evidence.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `허위 정보 친숙성`.

## 19. creator reciprocity expectation /kriˈeɪtər ˌrɛsəˈprɑsəti ˌɛkspɛkˈteɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a belief that creators who receive attention, money, or loyalty should respond with honesty, access, or care.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** belief creator receive attention, money hoặc loyalty nên respond honesty, access hoặc care.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong creator reciprocity expectation` — expectation; `meet creator reciprocity expectations` — meet.
**Examples:** `Creator reciprocity expectation increased when subscribers funded the new production directly.` → Expectation reciprocity creator tăng khi subscriber fund production mới trực tiếp.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `창작자 상호성 기대`.

## 20. public media trust /ˈpʌblɪk ˈmiːdiə trʌst/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** confidence that media serving a broad public is accurate, independent, accountable, and socially useful.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** confidence media serve public broad accurate, independent, accountable và useful social.
**Grammar & collocations:** `low public media trust` — trust; `restore public media trust` — restore.
**Examples:** `Public media trust improved when the newsroom explained corrections and separated reporting from opinion.` → Trust media public improve khi newsroom explain correction và separate report khỏi opinion.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `공공 미디어 신뢰`.

## Review in context

With **attention scarcity media**, people rely on **genre expectation** and **spoiler aversion** to choose quickly. **Subscription inertia** keeps unused services active, while a **streaming default** encourages continuation. Too many suggestions create **recommendation fatigue**, and **content completion bias** can keep weak stories alive. A **cliffhanger effect** captures attention, while **parasocial trust** and **celebrity credibility** transfer confidence beyond evidence. An **audience identity cue** and **entertainment value framing** determine whether a program feels relevant. An **ad avoidance motive** may justify payment, but **media multitasking cost** reduces understanding. **Cultural translation friction** changes how stories travel, and **news source loyalty** shapes correction. A **headline curiosity gap** increases clicks; repeated exposure creates **misinformation familiarity**. **Creator reciprocity expectation** and **public media trust** determine whether attention becomes a durable relationship.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

Với **attention scarcity media**, người ta rely **genre expectation** và **spoiler aversion** để choose nhanh. **Subscription inertia** giữ service không use active, còn **streaming default** encourage continue. Quá nhiều suggestion tạo **recommendation fatigue**, và **content completion bias** giữ story yếu. **Cliffhanger effect** capture attention, còn **parasocial trust** và **celebrity credibility** transfer confidence vượt evidence. **Audience identity cue** và **entertainment value framing** quyết định program relevant không. **Ad avoidance motive** có thể justify payment, nhưng **media multitasking cost** giảm understanding. **Cultural translation friction** đổi cách story travel, còn **news source loyalty** shape correction. **Headline curiosity gap** tăng click; exposure lặp tạo **misinformation familiarity**. **Creator reciprocity expectation** và **public media trust** quyết định attention thành relationship lâu dài.
