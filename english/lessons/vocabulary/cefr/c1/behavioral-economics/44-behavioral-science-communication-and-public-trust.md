# C1 Vocabulary 44 — Behavioral science communication and public trust

This lesson follows how people interpret evidence, uncertainty, disagreement, correction, and participation in scientific and public-information systems.

Flow: **risk communication salience → expert disagreement perception → evidence uncertainty framing → trust repair science → source diversity cue → statistic numeracy → causal story appeal → visual evidence credibility → false balance perception → correction timing effect → scientific consensus belief → uncertainty tolerance public → public question burden → media source switching → explanation completeness expectation → evidence accessibility friction → research participation motive → consent comprehension → data donation trust → open science credibility**.

## 1. risk communication salience /rɪsk kəˌmjuːnəˈkeɪʃən ˈseɪliəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** how noticeable and behaviorally important a message about a threat or precaution becomes.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mức message threat hoặc precaution noticeable và important cho behavior.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high risk communication salience` — salience; `increase risk communication salience` — increase.
**Examples:** `Risk communication salience rose when the warning showed one clear action before background detail.` → Salience communication risk tăng khi warning show action rõ trước detail background.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `위험 소통 현저성`.

## 2. expert disagreement perception /ˈɛkspɝt ˌdɪsəˈɡriːmənt pərˈsɛpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a judgment about how much qualified experts disagree and what that disagreement means for action.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** judgment expert qualified disagree bao nhiêu và disagreement mean gì cho action.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high expert disagreement perception` — perception; `calibrate expert disagreement perception` — calibrate.
**Examples:** `Expert disagreement perception increased after headlines presented a minority view as equal to the evidence.` → Perception disagreement expert tăng sau headline present minority view equal evidence.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `전문가 불일치 인식`.

## 3. evidence uncertainty framing /ˈɛvədəns ˌʌnsərˈtənti ˈfreɪmɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the way limits, confidence ranges, and unknowns are presented alongside a claim.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cách present limit, confidence range và unknown cạnh claim.
**Grammar & collocations:** `honest evidence uncertainty framing` — framing; `improve evidence uncertainty framing` — improve.
**Examples:** `Evidence uncertainty framing helped readers understand what the study could not establish.` → Framing uncertainty evidence giúp reader hiểu study không establish được gì.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `증거 불확실성 프레이밍`.

## 4. trust repair science /trʌst rɪˈpɛr ˈsaɪəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** actions that rebuild confidence in scientific institutions after error, misconduct, or confusing communication.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** action rebuild confidence science institution sau error, misconduct hoặc communication confusing.
**Grammar & collocations:** `credible trust repair science` — repair; `begin trust repair science` — begin.
**Examples:** `Trust repair science required a public correction, accessible data, and an explanation of the new safeguard.` → Repair trust science cần correction public, data accessible và explain safeguard mới.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `과학 신뢰 회복`.

## 5. source diversity cue /sɔrs daɪˈvɝsəti kjuː/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a signal that information has been checked across independent sources rather than repeated from one origin.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** signal information check qua source independent thay repeat từ một origin.
**Grammar & collocations:** `visible source diversity cue` — cue; `provide a source diversity cue` — provide.
**Examples:** `A source diversity cue made the report seem less dependent on one institution's press release.` → Cue diversity source làm report trông ít dependent vào press release một institution.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `출처 다양성 단서`.

## 6. statistic numeracy /stəˈtɪstɪk ˈnuːmərəsi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the ability to understand and reason with probabilities, rates, comparisons, and uncertainty.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** ability understand và reason với probability, rate, comparison và uncertainty.
**Grammar & collocations:** `limited statistic numeracy` — numeracy; `improve statistic numeracy` — improve.
**Examples:** `Limited statistic numeracy made a relative increase sound larger than the absolute change.` → Numeracy statistic hạn chế làm increase relative nghe lớn hơn change absolute.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `통계 수리력`.

## 7. causal story appeal /ˈkɔzəl ˈstɔri əˈpiːl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the persuasive attraction of a simple story that links events through a clear cause and effect.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** attraction persuasive của story simple link event qua cause và effect clear.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong causal story appeal` — appeal; `resist causal story appeal` — resist.
**Examples:** `Causal story appeal made one vivid anecdote outweigh a larger but less dramatic dataset.` → Appeal story causal khiến anecdote vivid outweigh dataset lớn nhưng ít dramatic.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `인과 이야기 매력`.

## 8. visual evidence credibility /ˈvɪʒuəl ˈɛvədəns krɛdəˈbɪləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** confidence that a chart, image, map, or visualization accurately represents the underlying evidence.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** confidence chart, image, map hoặc visualization accurately represent evidence gốc.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high visual evidence credibility` — credibility; `question visual evidence credibility` — question.
**Examples:** `Visual evidence credibility fell when the chart omitted the zero point and exaggerated a small difference.` → Credibility evidence visual giảm khi chart omit zero và exaggerate difference nhỏ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `시각적 증거 신뢰성`.

## 9. false balance perception /fɔls ˈbæləns pərˈsɛpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a judgment that two positions deserve equal weight even when the evidence supporting them is highly unequal.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** judgment hai position deserve weight equal dù evidence unequal rõ.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high false balance perception` — perception; `correct false balance perception` — correct.
**Examples:** `False balance perception made a fringe claim seem as credible as a broad scientific conclusion.` → Perception balance false làm claim fringe trông credible như conclusion khoa học rộng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `거짓 균형 인식`.

## 10. correction timing effect /kəˈrɛkʃən ˈtaɪmɪŋ ɪˈfɛkt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a change in how effective a correction is depending on when it appears relative to the original claim.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** effectiveness correction đổi tùy timing so với claim original.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong correction timing effect` — effect; `study the correction timing effect` — study.
**Examples:** `Correction timing effect was strongest when the clarification appeared before the false claim spread widely.` → Effect timing correction mạnh khi clarification appear trước claim false spread rộng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `정정 시점 효과`.

## 11. scientific consensus belief /ˌsaɪənˈtɪfɪk kənˈsɛnsəs bɪˈliːf/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a belief about whether qualified researchers broadly agree on a claim or conclusion.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** belief researcher qualified broadly agree claim hoặc conclusion không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `weak scientific consensus belief` — belief; `communicate scientific consensus beliefs` — communicate.
**Examples:** `Scientific consensus belief rose when the summary distinguished active research from settled findings.` → Belief consensus scientific tăng khi summary distinguish research active với finding settled.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `과학적 합의 신념`.

## 12. uncertainty tolerance public /ˌʌnsərˈtənti ˈtɑlərəns ˈpʌblɪk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** willingness to make reasonable decisions while accepting that public evidence may remain incomplete.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** willingness decide reasonable khi evidence public vẫn incomplete.
**Grammar & collocations:** `low uncertainty tolerance public` — tolerance; `build uncertainty tolerance public` — build.
**Examples:** `Uncertainty tolerance public improved when officials explained why guidance could change with new data.` → Tolerance uncertainty public improve khi official explain guidance đổi theo data mới.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `공공 불확실성 인내`.

## 13. public question burden /ˈpʌblɪk ˈkwɛstʃən ˈbɝdən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the effort and knowledge required for ordinary people to formulate useful questions about a public issue.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** effort và knowledge cần để public formulate question useful về issue.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high public question burden` — burden; `reduce public question burden` — reduce.
**Examples:** `Public question burden fell when the briefing offered examples of decisions residents actually faced.` → Burden question public giảm khi briefing offer example decision resident face.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `공공 질문 부담`.

## 14. media source switching /ˈmiːdiə sɔrs ˈswɪtʃɪŋ/

## 15. explanation completeness expectation /ˌɛkspləˈneɪʃən kəmˈpliːtnəs ˌɛkspɛkˈteɪʃən/

## 19. data donation trust /ˈdeɪtə doʊˈneɪʃən trʌst/

## 20. open science credibility /ˈoʊpən ˈsaɪəns krɛdəˈbɪləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** confidence that research is transparent, reproducible, accountable, and open to independent checking.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** confidence research transparent, reproducible, accountable và open check independent.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `오픈 사이언스 신뢰성`.

## Review in context

Public decisions depend on **risk communication salience** and how people interpret **expert disagreement perception**. Honest **evidence uncertainty framing** supports **trust repair science**, while a **source diversity cue** and **statistic numeracy** help readers evaluate claims. **Causal story appeal** can overpower careful data, and **visual evidence credibility** falls when charts mislead. **False balance perception** gives fringe views too much weight; the **correction timing effect** determines whether clarification arrives in time. **Scientific consensus belief** and **uncertainty tolerance public** shape action, but a high **public question burden** excludes ordinary voices. **Media source switching** follows trust, while **explanation completeness expectation** determines whether a message feels adequate. Lower **evidence accessibility friction** supports participation, and a clear **research participation motive** improves recruitment. **Consent comprehension**, **data donation trust**, and **open science credibility** make public research more accountable.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

Decision public phụ thuộc **risk communication salience** và cách người ta interpret **expert disagreement perception**. **Evidence uncertainty framing** honest support **trust repair science**, còn **source diversity cue** và **statistic numeracy** giúp evaluate claim. **Causal story appeal** có thể overpower data careful, và **visual evidence credibility** giảm khi chart mislead. **False balance perception** cho view fringe quá nhiều weight; **correction timing effect** quyết định clarification có đến kịp không. **Scientific consensus belief** và **uncertainty tolerance public** shape action, nhưng **public question burden** cao exclude voice ordinary. **Media source switching** follow trust, còn **explanation completeness expectation** quyết message adequate không. Giảm **evidence accessibility friction** support participation, và **research participation motive** rõ improve recruitment. **Consent comprehension**, **data donation trust** và **open science credibility** làm public research accountable hơn.
