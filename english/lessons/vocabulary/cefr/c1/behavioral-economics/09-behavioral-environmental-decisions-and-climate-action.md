# C1 Vocabulary 09 — Behavioral environmental decisions and climate action

This lesson follows how people perceive environmental risk, respond to climate policies, and choose sustainable behavior under uncertainty and social pressure.

Flow: **climate risk perception → environmental salience → green default → energy conservation bias → present bias climate → status quo energy → proenvironmental preference → green premium → moral suasion → carbon tax framing → climate optimism → climate pessimism → behavioral environmental economics → social norm energy → energy-use feedback → conservation norm → recycling norm → eco-label attention → sustainable choice architecture → greenwashing belief**.

## 1. climate risk perception /ˈklaɪmət rɪsk pərˈsɛpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a person's judgment about the likelihood, seriousness, and personal relevance of climate-related harm.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** judgment về likelihood, seriousness và personal relevance của climate harm.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high climate risk perception` — perception; `change climate risk perception` — change.
**Examples:** `Climate risk perception increased after repeated floods affected nearby neighborhoods.` → Perception risk climate tăng sau flood lặp lại affect neighborhood gần.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `기후 위험 인식`.

## 2. environmental salience /ɪnˌvaɪrənˈmɛntl ˈseɪliəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** how noticeable and attention-grabbing an environmental consequence is when a person makes a choice.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mức environmental consequence noticeable và attention-grabbing khi choose.
**Grammar & collocations:** `low environmental salience` — salience; `increase environmental salience` — increase.
**Examples:** `Environmental salience rose when the receipt displayed the meal's carbon footprint.` → Salience environmental tăng khi receipt display carbon footprint meal.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `환경 현저성`.

## 3. green default /ɡriːn dɪˈfɔlt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a preselected option that favors lower environmental harm unless a person actively chooses another option.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** option preselected favor environmental harm thấp hơn nếu person không choose khác.
**Grammar & collocations:** `energy-saving green default` — default; `set a green default` — set.
**Examples:** `The green default enrolled households in renewable electricity unless they opted out.` → Default green enroll household vào renewable electricity nếu không opt out.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `친환경 기본값`.

## 4. energy conservation bias /ˈɛnərdʒi ˌkɑnsərˈveɪʃən baɪəs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a predictable distortion that makes people underestimate or misjudge the benefits of saving energy.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** distortion predictable làm underestimate hoặc misjudge benefit save energy.
**Grammar & collocations:** `persistent energy conservation bias` — bias; `correct energy conservation bias` — correct.
**Examples:** `Energy conservation bias led tenants to ignore small savings that accumulated over a year.` → Bias conservation energy làm tenant ignore saving nhỏ tích lũy một năm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `에너지 절약 편향`.

## 5. present bias climate /ˈprɛzənt baɪəs ˈklaɪmət/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a tendency to favor immediate comfort or savings over delayed climate benefits.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** favor comfort hoặc saving immediate hơn climate benefit delayed.
**Grammar & collocations:** `present-bias climate decision` — decision; `address present bias in climate choices` — address.
**Examples:** `Present bias climate reduced interest in insulation that paid for itself only after several years.` → Bias present climate giảm interest insulation chỉ pay off sau vài năm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `기후 현재 편향`.

## 6. status quo energy /ˈsteɪtəs kwoʊ ˈɛnərdʒi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** continued use of an existing energy arrangement because changing suppliers, devices, or habits feels costly.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** tiếp tục energy arrangement cũ vì change supplier, device hoặc habit thấy costly.
**Grammar & collocations:** `status quo energy choice` — choice; `move beyond status quo energy` — move.
**Examples:** `Status quo energy kept the building on an inefficient heating contract.` → Energy status quo giữ building trong heating contract inefficient.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `현상 유지 에너지`.

## 7. proenvironmental preference /ˌproʊɪnˌvaɪrənˈmɛntl ˈprɛfərəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a preference for choices that protect the environment, even when they involve extra effort or cost.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** preference choice protect environment dù extra effort hoặc cost.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong proenvironmental preference` — preference; `measure proenvironmental preferences` — measure.
**Examples:** `A proenvironmental preference made shoppers accept slower delivery for shared transport.` → Preference proenvironmental làm shopper accept delivery chậm hơn cho transport chung.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `친환경 선호`.

## 8. green premium /ɡriːn ˈpriːmiəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the additional price people pay for a product or service with lower environmental impact.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** extra price pay cho product hoặc service impact environment thấp.
**Grammar & collocations:** `small green premium` — premium; `accept a green premium` — accept.
**Examples:** `The green premium was acceptable when the label explained durable savings.` → Premium green acceptable khi label explain saving durable.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `친환경 프리미엄`.

## 9. moral suasion /ˈmɔrəl sweɪˈʒən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an appeal to people's values or sense of responsibility rather than a financial reward or punishment.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** appeal vào value hoặc responsibility thay vì reward hoặc punishment financial.
**Grammar & collocations:** `moral-suasian message` — message; `use moral suasion` — use.
**Examples:** `Moral suasion encouraged residents to conserve water during the drought.` → Suasion moral encourage resident conserve water trong drought.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `도덕적 설득`.

## 10. carbon tax framing /ˈkɑrbən tæks ˈfreɪmɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the way a carbon tax is described, which can alter perceptions of fairness, cost, and purpose.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cách describe carbon tax đổi perception fairness, cost và purpose.
**Grammar & collocations:** `revenue-recycling carbon tax framing` — framing; `test carbon tax framing` — test.
**Examples:** `Carbon tax framing as a household dividend gained more support than framing as a penalty.` → Framing tax carbon như dividend household gain support hơn penalty.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `탄소세 프레이밍`.

## 11. climate optimism /ˈklaɪmət ˈɑptəˌmɪzəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an overly favorable belief that climate risks will be limited or solved without substantial personal or collective change.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** belief quá favorable climate risk sẽ limited hoặc solved không cần change lớn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `unrealistic climate optimism` — optimism; `temper climate optimism` — temper.
**Examples:** `Climate optimism reduced support for expensive adaptation in flood-prone areas.` → Optimism climate giảm support adaptation đắt ở vùng flood.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `기후 낙관주의`.

## 12. climate pessimism /ˈklaɪmət ˈpɛsəˌmɪzəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a belief that climate damage is unavoidable or that individual and collective action will not matter.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** belief climate damage unavoidable hoặc action không matter.
**Grammar & collocations:** `paralyzing climate pessimism` — pessimism; `counter climate pessimism` — counter.
**Examples:** `Climate pessimism made some residents reject a community solar project as pointless.` → Pessimism climate làm resident reject solar project vì thấy pointless.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `기후 비관주의`.

## 13. behavioral environmental economics /bɪˈheɪvjərəl ɪnˌvaɪrənˈmɛntl ˌiːkəˈnɑmɪks/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the study of how psychological tendencies and social preferences shape environmental choices and policy responses.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** study tendency psychological và social preference shape environmental choice và policy response.
**Grammar & collocations:** `behavioral-environmental-economics policy` — policy; `study behavioral environmental economics` — study.
**Examples:** `Behavioral environmental economics compared how households responded to visible and invisible savings.` → Economics environmental behavioral compare household respond với saving visible/invisible.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `행동 환경경제학`.

## 14. social norm energy /ˈsoʊʃəl nɔrm ˈɛnərdʒi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a shared expectation about how much energy people in a group should use.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** expectation shared về energy group member nên use bao nhiêu.
**Grammar & collocations:** `descriptive social norm energy` — norm; `activate a social norm about energy` — activate.
**Examples:** `The social norm energy message compared a household's use with its neighbors' use.` → Message norm social energy compare use household với neighbor.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `에너지 사회 규범`.

## 15. energy-use feedback /ˈɛnərdʒi juːs ˈfiːdbæk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** information showing a person how much energy they use and how that compares with a meaningful reference.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** information show energy use và compare với reference meaningful.
**Grammar & collocations:** `real-time energy-use feedback` — feedback; `provide energy-use feedback` — provide.
**Examples:** `Energy-use feedback helped residents notice waste during unoccupied hours.` → Feedback use energy giúp resident notice waste giờ không occupied.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `에너지 사용 피드백`.

## 16. conservation norm /ˌkɑnsərˈveɪʃən nɔrm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a shared expectation that people should avoid unnecessary use of scarce environmental resources.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** expectation shared tránh use không cần resource environment scarce.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong conservation norm` — norm; `build a conservation norm` — build.
**Examples:** `A conservation norm made turning off unused lights part of the team's routine.` → Norm conservation làm turn off light không use thành routine team.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `절약 규범`.

## 17. recycling norm /riːˈsaɪklɪŋ nɔrm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a shared expectation that people should sort and recycle materials responsibly.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** expectation shared sort và recycle material responsibly.
**Grammar & collocations:** `local recycling norm` — norm; `reinforce a recycling norm` — reinforce.
**Examples:** `The recycling norm weakened when collection rules changed without explanation.` → Norm recycling weaken khi collection rule change không explain.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `재활용 규범`.

## 18. eco-label attention /ˈiːkoʊ ˈleɪbəl əˈtɛnʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the degree to which people notice and use environmental labels when evaluating products.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mức người notice và use environmental label khi evaluate product.
**Grammar & collocations:** `limited eco-label attention` — attention; `increase eco-label attention` — increase.
**Examples:** `Eco-label attention rose when the symbol appeared beside the product price.` → Attention eco-label tăng khi symbol appear cạnh price.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `환경 라벨 주의`.

## 19. sustainable choice architecture /səˈsteɪnəbəl tʃɔɪs ˈɑrkəˌtɛktʃər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the design of options and environments that makes sustainable choices easier without removing alternatives.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** design option và environment làm sustainable choice dễ hơn mà không remove alternative.
**Grammar & collocations:** `effective sustainable choice architecture` — architecture; `design sustainable choice architecture` — design.
**Examples:** `Sustainable choice architecture placed plant-based meals first on the menu.` → Architecture choice sustainable place plant-based meal đầu menu.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `지속 가능한 선택 설계`.

## 20. greenwashing belief /ˈɡriːnˌwɑʃɪŋ bɪˈliːf/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the belief that environmental claims are exaggerated, misleading, or mainly promotional.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** belief environmental claim exaggerated, misleading hoặc mainly promotional.
**Grammar & collocations:** `widespread greenwashing belief` — belief; `counter a greenwashing belief` — counter.
**Examples:** `A greenwashing belief made customers ignore a genuine emissions label.` → Belief greenwashing làm customer ignore emissions label genuine.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `그린워싱 믿음`.

## Review in context

**Climate risk perception** and **environmental salience** shape whether a **green default** works, while **energy conservation bias**, **present bias climate**, and **status quo energy** can block change. A **proenvironmental preference** may justify a **green premium**, and **moral suasion** can complement **carbon tax framing**. **Climate optimism** and **climate pessimism** are central to **behavioral environmental economics**. A **social norm energy**, **energy-use feedback**, **conservation norm**, and **recycling norm** support action, while **eco-label attention**, **sustainable choice architecture**, and a **greenwashing belief** determine whether environmental information changes behavior.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:** **Climate risk perception** và **environmental salience** shape green default work không, còn **energy conservation bias**, **present bias climate** và **status quo energy** có thể block change. **Proenvironmental preference** có thể justify **green premium**, còn **moral suasion** complement **carbon tax framing**. **Climate optimism** và **climate pessimism** central trong **behavioral environmental economics**. **Social norm energy**, **energy-use feedback**, **conservation norm** và **recycling norm** support action, còn **eco-label attention**, **sustainable choice architecture** và **greenwashing belief** quyết định environmental information có đổi behavior không.
