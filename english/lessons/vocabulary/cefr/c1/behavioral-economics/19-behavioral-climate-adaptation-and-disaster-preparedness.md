# C1 Vocabulary 19 — Behavioral climate adaptation and disaster preparedness

This lesson follows how people interpret hazards, prepare for disruption, and decide whether to adapt, insure, evacuate, or relocate.

Flow: **hazard risk perception → preparedness intention → evacuation friction → warning credibility → normalcy bias → optimism bias disaster → social amplification risk → shelter choice → insurance take-up reluctance → recovery expectation → climate adaptation identity → resilience framing → emergency default → warning fatigue → collective efficacy belief → precautionary behavior → disaster memory cue → relocation aversion → loss threshold → adaptive capacity perception**.

## 1. hazard risk perception /ˈhæzərd rɪsk pərˈsɛpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a person's judgment about how likely, severe, and personally relevant a hazard is.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** judgment về hazard likely, severe và personally relevant thế nào.
**Grammar & collocations:** `elevated hazard risk perception` — perception; `measure hazard risk perception` — measure.
**Examples:** `Hazard risk perception rose after residents saw how quickly the river reached the road.` → Perception risk hazard tăng sau khi resident thấy river reach road nhanh thế nào.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `위험 인식`.

## 2. preparedness intention /prɪˈpɛrədnəs ɪnˈtɛnʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a stated willingness to take steps that could reduce harm before a disruption occurs.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** willingness được state để take step reduce harm trước disruption.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong preparedness intention` — intention; `convert preparedness intention into action` — convert.
**Examples:** `Preparedness intention remained high, but few households actually stored clean water.` → Intention preparedness vẫn cao nhưng ít household thật sự store nước sạch.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `대비 의도`.

## 3. evacuation friction /ɪˌvækjuˈeɪʃən ˈfrɪkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** practical effort, uncertainty, or delay that makes leaving a dangerous area harder.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** effort, uncertainty hoặc delay làm việc leave area dangerous khó hơn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `serious evacuation friction` — friction; `remove evacuation friction` — remove.
**Examples:** `Evacuation friction increased because residents did not know which roads allowed pets.` → Friction evacuation tăng vì resident không biết road nào cho phép pet.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `대피 마찰`.

## 4. warning credibility /ˈwɔrnɪŋ krɛdəˈbɪləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the perceived trustworthiness and accuracy of a warning about an approaching threat.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** trustworthiness và accuracy được perceive của warning về threat approaching.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high warning credibility` — credibility; `protect warning credibility` — protect.
**Examples:** `Warning credibility declined after several urgent messages predicted events that never appeared.` → Credibility warning giảm sau nhiều message urgent predict event không xảy ra.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `경보 신뢰성`.

## 5. normalcy bias /ˈnɔrməlsi baɪəs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a tendency to assume that familiar conditions will continue despite evidence of unusual danger.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** tendency assume condition familiar continue dù evidence danger unusual.
**Grammar & collocations:** `dangerous normalcy bias` — bias; `counter normalcy bias` — counter.
**Examples:** `Normalcy bias kept shoppers inside the market after the first evacuation notice.` → Bias normalcy giữ shopper trong market sau notice evacuation đầu.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `정상성 편향`.

## 6. optimism bias disaster /ˈɑptəˌmɪzəm baɪəs dɪˈzæstər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a tendency to believe that a disaster is less likely to affect oneself than other people.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** tendency believe disaster less likely affect self hơn người khác.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong optimism bias disaster` — bias; `correct optimism bias disaster` — correct.
**Examples:** `Optimism bias disaster made the owner postpone flood insurance until after the next season.` → Bias optimism disaster khiến owner postpone insurance flood tới sau season tiếp.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `재난 낙관 편향`.

## 7. social amplification risk /ˈsoʊʃəl ˌæmpləfɪˈkeɪʃən rɪsk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the process by which communication, institutions, and social reactions make a risk seem much larger or smaller.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** communication và social reaction làm risk appear lớn hoặc nhỏ hơn thực tế.
**Grammar & collocations:** `rapid social amplification risk` — amplification; `study social amplification risk` — study.
**Examples:** `Social amplification risk spread when a frightening image was shared without location or date.` → Risk amplification social lan rộng khi image đáng sợ share không có location hay date.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `사회적 위험 증폭`.

## 8. shelter choice /ˈʃɛltər tʃɔɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a person's selection among available places for temporary safety during or after a hazard.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** selection giữa các place available cho safety temporary lúc hoặc sau hazard.
**Grammar & collocations:** `constrained shelter choice` — choice; `support shelter choice` — support.
**Examples:** `Shelter choice depended on transport, privacy, and whether the site accepted medical equipment.` → Choice shelter phụ thuộc transport, privacy và site có accept equipment medical không.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `대피소 선택`.

## 9. insurance take-up reluctance /ɪnˈʃʊrəns teɪk ʌp rɪˈlʌktəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** unwillingness to buy insurance even when coverage could reduce the financial consequences of a hazard.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** không willing buy insurance dù coverage reduce financial consequence.
**Grammar & collocations:** `persistent insurance take-up reluctance` — reluctance; `reduce insurance take-up reluctance` — reduce.
**Examples:** `Insurance take-up reluctance remained after premiums were subsidized because claims seemed difficult.` → Reluctance take-up insurance vẫn còn dù premium subsidize vì claim trông difficult.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `보험 가입 주저`.

## 10. recovery expectation /rɪˈkʌvəri ˌɛkspɛkˈteɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a belief about how quickly and fairly people, services, or places will return after disruption.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** belief recovery quick và fair thế nào sau disruption.
**Grammar & collocations:** `realistic recovery expectation` — expectation; `raise recovery expectations` — raise.
**Examples:** `Recovery expectation shaped whether families accepted temporary relocation or waited for repairs.` → Expectation recovery shape family accept relocation temporary hay đợi repair.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `회복 기대`.

## 11. climate adaptation identity /ˈklaɪmət ˌædəpˈteɪʃən aɪˈdɛntəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a sense that preparing for climate impacts is part of who a person or community is.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** sense preparation climate impact là một phần identity của person hoặc community.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong climate adaptation identity` — identity; `build climate adaptation identity` — build.
**Examples:** `Climate adaptation identity made neighborhood volunteers maintain the cooling center each summer.` → Identity adaptation climate khiến volunteer neighborhood maintain cooling center mỗi summer.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `기후 적응 정체성`.

## 12. resilience framing /rɪˈzɪliəns ˈfreɪmɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** presenting preparation as a way to preserve capability, connection, and recovery rather than only avoid loss.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** present preparation như cách preserve capability, connection và recovery thay vì chỉ avoid loss.
**Grammar & collocations:** `empowering resilience framing` — framing; `use resilience framing` — use.
**Examples:** `Resilience framing increased support for backup power because it emphasized community continuity.` → Framing resilience tăng support backup power vì nhấn mạnh continuity community.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `회복탄력성 프레이밍`.

## 13. emergency default /ɪˈmɝdʒənsi dɪˈfɔlt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a prearranged action or setting that takes effect automatically when a crisis limits time or attention.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** action hoặc setting prearrange tự động activate khi crisis limit time hay attention.
**Grammar & collocations:** `safe emergency default` — default; `activate an emergency default` — activate.
**Examples:** `An emergency default sent medication reminders to the designated caregiver when the patient stopped responding.` → Default emergency gửi reminder medicine cho caregiver designate khi patient ngừng response.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `비상 기본값`.

## 14. warning fatigue /ˈwɔrnɪŋ fəˈtiːɡ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** declining attention to warnings after people receive too many messages or alarms.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** attention warning giảm sau khi nhận quá nhiều message hoặc alarm.
**Grammar & collocations:** `serious warning fatigue` — fatigue; `prevent warning fatigue` — prevent.
**Examples:** `Warning fatigue made residents mute the phone that later carried the evacuation order.` → Fatigue warning khiến resident mute phone sau đó lại carry order evacuation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `경보 피로`.

## 15. collective efficacy belief /kəˈlɛktɪv ˈɛfəkəsi bɪˈliːf/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a shared belief that a group can coordinate effectively to solve a common problem.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** belief shared rằng group có thể coordinate effective để solve problem chung.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong collective efficacy belief` — belief; `strengthen collective efficacy beliefs` — strengthen.
**Examples:** `Collective efficacy belief helped neighbors check on isolated residents before the storm arrived.` → Belief efficacy collective giúp neighbor check resident isolated trước storm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `집단 효능감 신념`.

## 16. precautionary behavior /prɪˈkɔʃəˌnɛri bɪˈheɪvjər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an action taken to reduce possible harm before the probability or severity of danger is certain.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** action reduce harm possible trước khi probability hoặc severity chắc chắn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `low-cost precautionary behavior` — behavior; `encourage precautionary behavior` — encourage.
**Examples:** `Precautionary behavior included moving documents upstairs before forecasts became precise.` → Behavior precautionary gồm move document lên tầng trên trước forecast precise.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `예방 행동`.

## 17. disaster memory cue /dɪˈzæstər ˈmɛməri kjuː/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a reminder of a previous disaster that changes how people interpret current warnings or prepare.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** reminder disaster cũ change cách interpret warning hiện tại hoặc prepare.
**Grammar & collocations:** `powerful disaster memory cue` — cue; `activate a disaster memory cue` — activate.
**Examples:** `A disaster memory cue made older residents react sooner to a familiar cloud pattern.` → Cue memory disaster khiến resident lớn tuổi react sớm hơn với cloud pattern quen.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `재난 기억 단서`.

## 18. relocation aversion /ˌriːloʊˈkeɪʃən əˈvɝʒən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a tendency to resist moving from a familiar home or community even when the move reduces exposure to danger.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** tendency resist move khỏi home hoặc community quen dù move reduce danger exposure.
**Grammar & collocations:** `deep relocation aversion` — aversion; `understand relocation aversion` — understand.
**Examples:** `Relocation aversion reflected attachment to schools, neighbors, and local support rather than simple stubbornness.` → Aversion relocation phản ánh attachment tới school, neighbor và support local chứ không chỉ stubborn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `이주 회피`.

## 19. loss threshold /lɔs ˈθrɛʃˌhoʊld/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the level of expected or experienced loss at which a person changes strategy or accepts a costly adaptation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mức loss expected hoặc experienced khiến person đổi strategy hoặc accept adaptation costly.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high loss threshold` — threshold; `cross a loss threshold` — cross.
**Examples:** `The family crossed its loss threshold when repeated repairs exceeded the cost of moving.` → Family cross threshold loss khi repair lặp lại vượt cost move.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `손실 임계값`.

## 20. adaptive capacity perception /əˈdæptɪv kəˈpæsəti pərˈsɛpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a judgment about how able a person, household, or institution is to adjust to changing conditions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** judgment person, household hoặc institution có thể adjust condition changing tới đâu.
**Grammar & collocations:** `low adaptive capacity perception` — perception; `improve adaptive capacity perception` — improve.
**Examples:** `Adaptive capacity perception improved when residents saw that assistance was available after the first warning.` → Perception capacity adaptive improve khi resident thấy assistance available sau warning đầu.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `적응 역량 인식`.

## Review in context

Preparation starts with **hazard risk perception**, but a high **preparedness intention** does not guarantee action. **Evacuation friction** can block a willing household, and weak **warning credibility** encourages **normalcy bias**. **Optimism bias disaster** makes personal exposure seem lower than other people's, while **social amplification risk** can make a distant image feel immediate. A family's **shelter choice** depends on transport, care, and privacy, and **insurance take-up reluctance** may persist when claims seem difficult. **Recovery expectation** shapes whether people repair, wait, or move. A **climate adaptation identity** and constructive **resilience framing** can make preparation feel communal rather than fearful. An **emergency default** helps when attention collapses, but repeated messages create **warning fatigue**. **Collective efficacy belief** supports **precautionary behavior**, especially when a **disaster memory cue** makes risk vivid. **Relocation aversion** may remain until losses cross a **loss threshold**; fair support can improve **adaptive capacity perception** and make change seem possible.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

Preparation bắt đầu từ **hazard risk perception**, nhưng **preparedness intention** cao không guarantee action. **Evacuation friction** có thể block household willing, còn **warning credibility** yếu khuyến khích **normalcy bias**. **Optimism bias disaster** khiến exposure cá nhân trông thấp hơn người khác, trong khi **social amplification risk** làm image xa trông immediate. **Shelter choice** của family phụ thuộc transport, care và privacy, còn **insurance take-up reluctance** có thể vẫn còn khi claim trông difficult. **Recovery expectation** định hình việc người ta repair, wait hay move. **Climate adaptation identity** và **resilience framing** constructive khiến preparation mang tính community thay vì fear. **Emergency default** giúp khi attention collapse, nhưng message lặp lại tạo **warning fatigue**. **Collective efficacy belief** hỗ trợ **precautionary behavior**, đặc biệt khi **disaster memory cue** làm risk vivid. **Relocation aversion** có thể còn tới khi loss vượt **loss threshold**; support fair có thể nâng **adaptive capacity perception** và khiến change trở nên possible.
