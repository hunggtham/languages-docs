# C1 Vocabulary 12 — Retirement, household finance, and aging decisions

This lesson follows how households plan for later life, share resources, and make long-term financial decisions under uncertainty.

Flow: **retirement present bias → pension framing → retirement optimism → longevity perception → saving inertia → automatic enrollment pension → annuity aversion → retirement decumulation → elderly risk perception → health expectation aging → intergenerational transfer belief → family altruism → bequest motive → inheritance framing → household bargaining belief → financial caregiving → aging decision bias → retirement planning friction → pension complexity → behavioral household finance**.

## 1. retirement present bias /rɪˈtaɪərmənt ˈprɛzənt baɪəs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a tendency to value current spending more than saving for retirement benefits that arrive much later.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** value spending hiện tại hơn saving cho retirement benefit đến rất later.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong retirement present bias` — bias; `address retirement present bias` — address.
**Examples:** `Retirement present bias made the worker delay contributions despite an employer match.` → Bias present retirement làm worker delay contribution dù employer match.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `은퇴 현재 편향`.

## 2. pension framing /ˈpɛnʃən ˈfreɪmɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the way a pension contribution or future benefit is described, which affects how people evaluate it.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cách describe pension contribution hoặc benefit future ảnh hưởng evaluation.
**Grammar & collocations:** `future-security pension framing` — framing; `change pension framing` — change.
**Examples:** `Pension framing as future income increased participation more than framing as a payroll deduction.` → Framing pension như income future tăng participation hơn payroll deduction.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `연금 프레이밍`.

## 3. retirement optimism /rɪˈtaɪərmənt ˈɑptəˌmɪzəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an overly favorable belief that one's future retirement resources will be sufficient.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** belief quá favorable resource retirement future sẽ đủ.
**Grammar & collocations:** `unrealistic retirement optimism` — optimism; `temper retirement optimism` — temper.
**Examples:** `Retirement optimism led the household to underestimate medical and housing costs.` → Optimism retirement làm household underestimate medical và housing cost.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `은퇴 낙관주의`.

## 4. longevity perception /lɑnˈdʒɛvəti pərˈsɛpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a person's belief about how long they and people like them are likely to live.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** belief person và người giống mình likely live bao lâu.
**Grammar & collocations:** `biased longevity perception` — perception; `update longevity perceptions` — update.
**Examples:** `Longevity perception changed after the family discussed relatives who lived into their nineties.` → Perception longevity đổi sau family discuss relative sống tới ninety.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `수명 인식`.

## 5. saving inertia /ˈseɪvɪŋ ˈɪnɝʒə/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the tendency to leave saving arrangements unchanged despite opportunities to improve them.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** leave saving arrangement không đổi dù có opportunity improve.
**Grammar & collocations:** `persistent saving inertia` — inertia; `overcome saving inertia` — overcome.
**Examples:** `Saving inertia kept the employee's contribution at the default rate for years.` → Inertia saving giữ contribution employee ở default rate nhiều năm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `저축 관성`.

## 6. automatic enrollment pension /ˌɔtəˈmætɪk ɪnˈroʊlmənt ˈpɛnʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a pension arrangement that enrolls eligible workers automatically unless they actively opt out.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** pension arrangement enroll worker eligible tự động nếu không actively opt out.
**Grammar & collocations:** `automatic-enrollment pension plan` — plan; `adopt automatic enrollment pension` — adopt.
**Examples:** `Automatic enrollment pension increased participation among employees who rarely changed paperwork.` → Pension enrollment automatic tăng participation employee hiếm khi change paperwork.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `자동 가입 연금`.

## 7. annuity aversion /əˈnuəti əˈvɝʒən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** reluctance to exchange savings for a regular lifelong income, often because the product feels complex or inflexible.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** reluctance đổi saving thành income lifelong vì product complex hoặc inflexible.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong annuity aversion` — aversion; `explain annuity aversion` — explain.
**Examples:** `Annuity aversion made retirees prefer a visible account balance to guaranteed monthly income.` → Aversion annuity làm retiree thích account balance visible hơn income monthly guaranteed.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `연금 상품 회피`.

## 8. retirement decumulation /rɪˈtaɪərmənt ˌdiːˌkjuːmjəˈleɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the process of drawing down accumulated retirement assets to finance later-life spending.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** process draw down retirement asset tích lũy để finance spending later life.
**Grammar & collocations:** `cautious retirement decumulation` — decumulation; `plan retirement decumulation` — plan.
**Examples:** `Retirement decumulation was slower than expected because the couple feared outliving their savings.` → Decumulation retirement chậm hơn expect vì couple fear outlive saving.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `은퇴 자산 인출`.

## 9. elderly risk perception /ˈɛldərli rɪsk pərˈsɛpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an older person's judgment about the likelihood and seriousness of future financial, health, or safety risks.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** judgment người older về likelihood và seriousness risk financial, health hoặc safety future.
**Grammar & collocations:** `accurate elderly risk perception` — perception; `measure elderly risk perception` — measure.
**Examples:** `Elderly risk perception increased after a neighbor experienced a costly home repair.` → Perception risk elderly tăng sau neighbor gặp home repair costly.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `노인 위험 인식`.

## 10. health expectation aging /hɛlθ ˌɛkspɛkˈteɪʃən ˈeɪdʒɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a belief about how one's health will change with age and what care will be needed.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** belief health change theo age và care cần thế nào.
**Grammar & collocations:** `realistic health expectation aging` — expectation; `update health expectations about aging` — update.
**Examples:** `Health expectation aging influenced whether the family bought long-term-care coverage.` → Expectation health aging influence family buy long-term-care coverage không.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `노화 건강 기대`.

## 11. intergenerational transfer belief /ˌɪntərˌdʒɛnəˈreɪʃənəl ˈtrænsfər bɪˈliːf/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a belief about how much financial or practical support one generation should give another.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** belief generation nên give financial hoặc practical support cho generation khác bao nhiêu.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong intergenerational transfer belief` — belief; `examine transfer beliefs` — examine.
**Examples:** `The intergenerational transfer belief shaped whether parents funded a child's graduate study.` → Belief transfer intergenerational shape parent fund graduate study child không.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `세대 간 이전 믿음`.

## 12. family altruism /ˈfæməli ˈæltruˌɪzəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** concern for relatives' welfare that motivates costly support or resource sharing.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** concern welfare relative motivate support hoặc share resource có cost.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong family altruism` — altruism; `model family altruism` — model.
**Examples:** `Family altruism led the older couple to reduce travel so they could help with childcare.` → Altruism family làm couple older giảm travel để help childcare.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `가족 이타주의`.

## 13. bequest motive /bɪˈkwɛst ˈmoʊtɪv/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a desire to leave assets, property, or opportunities to others after one's death.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** desire leave asset, property hoặc opportunity cho người khác sau death.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong bequest motive` — motive; `estimate a bequest motive` — estimate.
**Examples:** `A bequest motive made the household save beyond what was needed for its own retirement.` → Motive bequest làm household save hơn mức cần cho retirement.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `유산 동기`.

## 14. inheritance framing /ɪnˈhɛrətəns ˈfreɪmɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the way a transfer of wealth is described, influencing whether recipients see it as help, duty, reward, or entitlement.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cách describe wealth transfer làm recipient xem help, duty, reward hoặc entitlement.
**Grammar & collocations:** `family-duty inheritance framing` — framing; `change inheritance framing` — change.
**Examples:** `Inheritance framing as educational support changed how the child used the money.` → Framing inheritance như education support đổi cách child use money.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `상속 프레이밍`.

## 15. household bargaining belief /ˈhaʊsˌhoʊld ˈbɑrɡənɪŋ bɪˈliːf/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a belief about how household members negotiate, share power, and divide resources.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** belief member household negotiate, share power và divide resource thế nào.
**Grammar & collocations:** `unequal household bargaining belief` — belief; `study household bargaining beliefs` — study.
**Examples:** `The household bargaining belief affected who controlled retirement accounts.` → Belief bargaining household affect ai control retirement account.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `가구 협상 믿음`.

## 16. financial caregiving /faɪˈnænʃəl ˈkɛrˌɡɪvɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** providing money, financial management, or material support to a relative who needs care.

## 17. aging decision bias /ˈeɪdʒɪŋ dɪˈsɪʒən baɪəs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a predictable distortion in choices about aging caused by limited attention, stereotypes, or inaccurate expectations.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** distortion predictable trong choice aging do attention limited, stereotype hoặc expectation sai.
**Grammar & collocations:** `age-related aging decision bias` — bias; `correct aging decision bias` — correct.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `노화 의사결정 편향`.

## 18. retirement planning friction /rɪˈtaɪərmənt ˈplænɪŋ ˈfrɪkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an administrative, informational, or psychological obstacle that makes retirement planning difficult.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** obstacle administrative, informational hoặc psychological làm retirement planning khó.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high retirement planning friction` — friction; `reduce retirement planning friction` — reduce.
**Examples:** `Retirement planning friction prevented the worker from comparing contribution options.` → Friction planning retirement prevent worker compare contribution option.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `은퇴 계획 마찰`.

## 19. pension complexity /ˈpɛnʃən kəmˈplɛksəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the difficulty created by complicated pension rules, choices, fees, and projections.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** difficulty do pension rule, choice, fee và projection phức tạp.
**Grammar & collocations:** `administrative pension complexity` — complexity; `simplify pension complexity` — simplify.
**Examples:** `Pension complexity made employees ignore a valuable employer contribution.` → Complexity pension làm employee ignore employer contribution valuable.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `연금 복잡성`.

## 20. behavioral household finance /bɪˈheɪvjərəl ˈhaʊsˌhoʊld faɪˌnæns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the study of how household financial choices are shaped by biases, beliefs, emotions, and social preferences.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** study household financial choice shape bias, belief, emotion và social preference.
**Grammar & collocations:** `behavioral-household-finance model` — model; `study behavioral household finance` — study.
**Examples:** `Behavioral household finance explains why similar families choose different saving paths.` → Finance household behavioral explain family similar choose saving path khác.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `행동 가계금융`.

## Review in context

**Retirement present bias**, **pension framing**, and **retirement optimism** shape **longevity perception** and **saving inertia**. **Automatic enrollment pension** can increase saving, while **annuity aversion** affects **retirement decumulation** and **elderly risk perception**. **Health expectation aging** influences an **intergenerational transfer belief**, **family altruism**, and the **bequest motive**. **Inheritance framing**, **household bargaining belief**, and **financial caregiving** affect resources across generations. **Aging decision bias**, **retirement planning friction**, and **pension complexity** are central to **behavioral household finance**.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:** **Retirement present bias**, **pension framing** và **retirement optimism** shape **longevity perception** và **saving inertia**. **Automatic enrollment pension** có thể tăng saving, còn **annuity aversion** ảnh hưởng **retirement decumulation** và **elderly risk perception**. **Health expectation aging** influence **intergenerational transfer belief**, **family altruism** và **bequest motive**. **Inheritance framing**, **household bargaining belief** và **financial caregiving** ảnh hưởng resource giữa generation. **Aging decision bias**, **retirement planning friction** và **pension complexity** central trong **behavioral household finance**.
