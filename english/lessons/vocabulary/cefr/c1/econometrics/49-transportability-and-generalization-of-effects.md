# C1 Vocabulary 49 — Transportability and generalization of effects

This lesson follows how researchers move evidence from one study setting to a different population, site, or policy context.

Flow: **source population → trial sample → selection diagram → trial participation → effect transport → site heterogeneity → target-site effect → reweighting function → overlap region → trial-to-target → generalization error → target-site heterogeneity → sampling mechanism → transported effect → population bridge → trial representativeness → site-specific effect → target weighting → generalization target → transport analysis**.

## 1. source population /sɔrs ˌpɑpjəˈleɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the population from which a study's participants or observations are drawn.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** population nơi participant hoặc observation của study được draw.
**Grammar & collocations:** `well-defined source population` — population; `describe the source population` — describe.
**Examples:** `The source population consisted of adults visiting six urban clinics.` → Population source gồm adult visit sáu clinic urban.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `원천 모집단`.

## 2. trial sample /traɪəl ˈsæmpəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the people or units actually enrolled and measured in a trial or experimental study.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** người hoặc unit thực sự enroll và measure trong trial hoặc experimental study.
**Grammar & collocations:** `representative trial sample` — sample; `recruit a trial sample` — recruit.
**Examples:** `The trial sample was smaller than the eligible population because many people declined.` → Sample trial nhỏ hơn eligible population vì nhiều người decline.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `시험 표본`.

## 3. selection diagram /səˈlɛkʃən ˈdaɪəˌɡræm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a graph that shows how study participation or inclusion is related to variables and possible causal pathways.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** graph show participation hoặc inclusion liên quan variable và causal pathway thế nào.
**Grammar & collocations:** `causal selection diagram` — diagram; `draw a selection diagram` — draw.
**Examples:** `The selection diagram showed why clinic attendance could distort the apparent treatment effect.` → Diagram selection show clinic attendance có thể distort treatment effect apparent.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `선택 도표`.

## 4. trial participation /traɪəl pɑrˌtɪsəˈpeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the process or act of an eligible person entering and remaining in a trial.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** process hoặc act eligible person enter và remain trong trial.
**Grammar & collocations:** `voluntary trial participation` — participation; `model trial participation` — model.
**Examples:** `Trial participation was lower among workers with irregular schedules.` → Participation trial thấp hơn ở worker có schedule irregular.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `시험 참여`.

## 5. effect transport /ɪˈfɛkt ˈtrænspɔrt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the process of using study evidence to estimate how an intervention would affect a different target population.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** process dùng study evidence estimate intervention affect target population khác thế nào.
**Grammar & collocations:** `causal effect transport` — transport; `perform effect transport` — perform.
**Examples:** `Effect transport required information about how the target population differed from trial participants.` → Transport effect cần information target population khác trial participant thế nào.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `효과 이전`.

## 6. site heterogeneity /saɪt ˌhɛtərədʒəˈniəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** variation in effects, conditions, or implementation across locations where a study or policy operates.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** variation effect, condition hoặc implementation giữa location study hoặc policy operate.
**Grammar & collocations:** `substantial site heterogeneity` — heterogeneity; `account for site heterogeneity` — account.
**Examples:** `Site heterogeneity reflected differences in staffing, prices, and local referral rules.` → Heterogeneity site reflect difference staffing, price và referral rule local.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `사이트 이질성`.

## 7. target-site effect /ˈtɑrɡət saɪt ɪˈfɛkt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the estimated intervention effect in the particular site or setting where a policy is being considered.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** intervention effect estimate ở site hoặc setting cụ thể nơi policy được consider.
**Grammar & collocations:** `estimated target-site effect` — effect; `predict a target-site effect` — predict.
**Examples:** `The target-site effect was smaller because the local baseline risk was already low.` → Effect target-site nhỏ hơn vì baseline risk local đã thấp.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `대상 사이트 효과`.

## 8. reweighting function /ˌriːˈweɪtɪŋ ˈfʌŋkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a rule that changes the contribution of observations so that a study better reflects a target population.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** rule đổi contribution observation để study reflect target population tốt hơn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `estimated reweighting function` — function; `apply a reweighting function` — apply.
**Examples:** `The reweighting function gave more influence to participants from underrepresented neighborhoods.` → Function reweighting give influence hơn cho participant từ neighborhood underrepresented.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `재가중 함수`.

## 9. overlap region /ˈoʊvərˌlæp ˈriːdʒən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the part of the covariate space in which both the study and target populations have comparable observations.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** phần covariate space nơi study và target population có observation comparable.
**Grammar & collocations:** `limited overlap region` — region; `restrict analysis to the overlap region` — restrict.
**Examples:** `The overlap region excluded extremely remote patients who had no trial counterpart.` → Region overlap exclude patient remote extreme không có counterpart trial.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `중첩 영역`.

## 10. trial-to-target /traɪəl tə ˈtɑrɡət/
**Part of speech:** adjective
**Core meaning (English):** relating to the movement of evidence or estimates from a trial to a specified target population.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** liên quan move evidence hoặc estimate từ trial sang target population cụ thể.
**Grammar & collocations:** `trial-to-target weighting` — weighting; `perform trial-to-target inference` — infer.
**Examples:** `The trial-to-target analysis adjusted for age, transport access, and disease severity.` → Analysis trial-to-target adjust age, access transport và severity disease.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `시험에서 대상으로`.

## 11. generalization error /ˌdʒɛnərələˈzeɪʃən ˈɛrər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the difference between a model's performance or effect estimate in a study and its performance in the wider population of interest.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** difference estimate hoặc performance trong study với wider population interest.
**Grammar & collocations:** `anticipated generalization error` — error; `measure generalization error` — measure.
**Examples:** `Generalization error increased when the target population had unfamiliar risk factors.` → Error generalization tăng khi target population có risk factor unfamiliar.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `일반화 오차`.

## 12. target-site heterogeneity /ˈtɑrɡət saɪt ˌhɛtərədʒəˈniəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** variation across the specific sites or settings that make up the target population.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** variation giữa site hoặc setting cụ thể tạo thành target population.
**Grammar & collocations:** `unmodeled target-site heterogeneity` — heterogeneity; `study target-site heterogeneity` — study.
**Examples:** `Target-site heterogeneity made one national average misleading for local planners.` → Heterogeneity target-site làm national average misleading với local planner.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `대상 사이트 이질성`.

## 13. sampling mechanism /ˈsæmplɪŋ ˈmɛkəˌnɪzəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the process that determines which units are selected into a study or dataset.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** process determine unit nào selected vào study hoặc dataset.
**Grammar & collocations:** `nonrandom sampling mechanism` — mechanism; `describe the sampling mechanism` — describe.
**Examples:** `The sampling mechanism favored people who had reliable transportation.` → Mechanism sampling favor người có transportation reliable.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `표본 추출 메커니즘`.

## 14. transported effect /trænˈspɔrtɪd ɪˈfɛkt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an effect estimate adapted from study evidence to the characteristics of a different population.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** effect estimate adapt từ study evidence theo characteristic population khác.
**Grammar & collocations:** `transported treatment effect` — effect; `report a transported effect` — report.
**Examples:** `The transported effect was smaller than the trial estimate after target weighting.` → Effect transported nhỏ hơn trial estimate sau target weighting.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `이전된 효과`.

## 15. population bridge /ˌpɑpjəˈleɪʃən brɪdʒ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** data or assumptions that connect the characteristics of a study population to those of a target population.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** data hoặc assumption connect characteristic study population với target population.
**Grammar & collocations:** `valid population bridge` — bridge; `construct a population bridge` — construct.
**Examples:** `A national registry provided the population bridge between the trial and local service users.` → Registry national provide bridge population giữa trial và service user local.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `모집단 연결`.

## 16. trial representativeness /traɪəl ˌrɛprɪzɛnˈteɪʃənəs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the degree to which trial participants resemble the population to which the findings may be applied.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mức trial participant giống population mà finding có thể apply.
**Grammar & collocations:** `limited trial representativeness` — representativeness; `assess trial representativeness` — assess.
**Examples:** `Trial representativeness was limited because older adults were under-enrolled.` → Representativeness trial limited vì older adult under-enroll.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `시험 대표성`.

## 17. site-specific effect /saɪt spəˈsɪfɪk ɪˈfɛkt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an intervention effect estimated for one particular location or implementation setting.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** effect intervention estimate cho location hoặc implementation setting cụ thể.
**Grammar & collocations:** `uncertain site-specific effect` — effect; `compare site-specific effects` — compare.
**Examples:** `The site-specific effect varied with the availability of trained counselors.` → Effect site-specific vary theo availability counselor trained.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `사이트별 효과`.

## 18. target weighting /ˈtɑrɡət ˈweɪtɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the use of weights that make study observations resemble the composition of a target population.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** dùng weight làm study observation giống composition target population.
**Grammar & collocations:** `calibrated target weighting` — weighting; `perform target weighting` — perform.
**Examples:** `Target weighting increased the influence of rural participants in the final estimate.` → Weighting target tăng influence participant rural trong estimate cuối.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `대상 가중`.

## 19. generalization target /ˌdʒɛnərələˈzeɪʃən ˈtɑrɡət/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the clearly defined population, setting, or decision context to which a study's conclusions are intended to apply.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** population, setting hoặc decision context define rõ nơi conclusion study intended apply.
**Grammar & collocations:** `narrow generalization target` — target; `define a generalization target` — define.
**Examples:** `The report stated a generalization target of public clinics in three coastal provinces.` → Report state target generalization là public clinic ở ba province coastal.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `일반화 대상`.

## 20. transport analysis /ˈtrænspɔrt əˈnæləsɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an analysis that adapts evidence from one population or setting to answer a question about another.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** analysis adapt evidence từ population hoặc setting này để answer question về population khác.
**Grammar & collocations:** `careful transport analysis` — analysis; `conduct a transport analysis` — conduct.
**Examples:** `The transport analysis made its assumptions about measurement and treatment effect explicit.` → Analysis transport nêu rõ assumption về measurement và treatment effect.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `이전 분석`.

## Review in context

A **source population** may produce a **trial sample**, but a **selection diagram** is needed to understand **trial participation** before attempting **effect transport**. **Site heterogeneity** can make a **target-site effect** differ from the trial result, so a **reweighting function** should be checked within the **overlap region**. A **trial-to-target** analysis must consider **generalization error**, **target-site heterogeneity**, and the **sampling mechanism**. The resulting **transported effect** depends on a credible **population bridge** and adequate **trial representativeness**. Analysts can compare a **site-specific effect**, apply **target weighting**, define the **generalization target**, and report a transparent **transport analysis**.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:** **Source population** có thể tạo **trial sample**, nhưng cần **selection diagram** để hiểu **trial participation** trước khi làm **effect transport**. **Site heterogeneity** có thể làm **target-site effect** khác trial result, nên cần check **reweighting function** trong **overlap region**. **Trial-to-target** analysis phải xét **generalization error**, **target-site heterogeneity** và **sampling mechanism**. **Transported effect** phụ thuộc **population bridge** credible và **trial representativeness** đủ. Analyst có thể compare **site-specific effect**, apply **target weighting**, define **generalization target** và report **transport analysis** minh bạch.
