# C1 Vocabulary 38 — Urban and regional economics

This lesson follows how cities, local public finance, land markets, and regional policy shape productivity and household location decisions.

Flow: **agglomeration effect → urban wage premium → congestion externality → land-rent gradient → spatial mismatch → commuting cost → housing elasticity → zoning constraint → amenity value → capitalization effect → local multiplier → place-based policy → enterprise zone → tax increment financing → public goods provision → fiscal federalism → intergovernmental transfer → tax competition → Tiebout sorting → regional convergence**.

## 1. agglomeration effect /əˌɡlɑməˈreɪʃən ɪˈfɛkt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a productivity or innovation benefit created when firms and workers locate near one another.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** benefit productivity/innovation do firm và worker ở gần nhau.
**Grammar & collocations:** `agglomeration-effect estimate` — estimate; `share knowledge locally` — chia sẻ.
**Examples:** `The agglomeration effect raised productivity in districts with dense networks of specialized suppliers.` → Effect agglomeration tăng productivity ở district có supplier chuyên môn dày.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `집적 효과`.

## 2. urban wage premium /ˈɝbən weɪdʒ ˈpriːmiəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the earnings advantage associated with working in a larger or denser urban labor market.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** lợi thế earnings khi làm trong labor market đô thị lớn hoặc dày.
**Grammar & collocations:** `urban-wage-premium estimate` — estimate; `compare urban and rural earnings` — so sánh.
**Examples:** `The urban wage premium remained after controlling for education and occupation.` → Premium vẫn còn sau khi kiểm soát education và occupation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `도시 임금 프리미엄`.

## 3. congestion externality /kənˈdʒɛstʃən ˌɛkstərˈnæləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a delay or cost imposed on other travelers when one additional user joins a crowded road or network.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** delay/cost một traveler thêm gây cho traveler khác khi road/network đông.
**Grammar & collocations:** `congestion-externality cost` — cost; `price peak-hour traffic` — định giá.
**Examples:** `The congestion externality was largest on the bridge during the morning peak.` → Externality lớn nhất trên cầu giờ peak sáng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `혼잡 외부효과`.

## 4. land-rent gradient /lænd rɛnt ˈɡreɪdiənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the pattern of land or property rents changing with distance from a central business area or other valued location.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** rent đất/property đổi theo distance từ trung tâm business hoặc location có giá trị.
**Grammar & collocations:** `land-rent-gradient estimate` — estimate; `model distance decay` — mô hình hóa.
**Examples:** `The land-rent gradient flattened after a new rail station improved access to the center.` → Gradient phẳng hơn sau khi station mới cải thiện access.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `지대 구배`.

## 5. spatial mismatch /ˈspeɪʃəl mɪsˈmætʃ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a situation in which workers and suitable jobs are separated by location, transport, or information barriers.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** worker và job phù hợp bị tách bởi location, transport hoặc information barrier.
**Grammar & collocations:** `spatial-mismatch hypothesis` — hypothesis; `connect workers to vacancies` — kết nối.
**Examples:** `Spatial mismatch kept unemployment high in neighborhoods far from expanding employers.` → Mismatch giữ unemployment cao ở neighborhood xa employer mở rộng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `공간적 불일치`.

## 6. commuting cost /kəˈmjuːtɪŋ kɔst/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the money, time, effort, and risk required to travel between home and work.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** money, time, effort và risk đi từ nhà đến chỗ làm.
**Grammar & collocations:** `commuting-cost estimate` — estimate; `reduce travel time` — giảm.
**Examples:** `Lower commuting costs expanded the set of jobs available to low-income workers.` → Cost đi lại thấp mở rộng tập job cho worker income thấp.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `통근 비용`.

## 7. housing elasticity /ˈhaʊzɪŋ ɪˈlæstəsəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the percentage response of housing supply or demand to a percentage change in price or income.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** phần trăm supply/demand housing phản ứng với phần trăm đổi price/income.
**Grammar & collocations:** `housing-elasticity estimate` — estimate; `respond to higher rents` — phản ứng.
**Examples:** `Housing elasticity was low because zoning limited new construction.` → Elasticity thấp vì zoning hạn chế xây mới.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `주택 탄력성`.

## 8. zoning constraint /ˈzoʊnɪŋ kənˈstreɪnt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a land-use rule that limits where, how densely, or for what purpose construction may occur.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** rule land-use giới hạn nơi, mật độ hoặc mục đích xây dựng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `zoning-constraint reform` — reform; `permit higher density` — cho phép.
**Examples:** `The zoning constraint prevented apartments from being built near the transit corridor.` → Constraint ngăn xây apartment gần corridor transit.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `용도지역 제약`.

## 9. amenity value /əˈmɛnəti ˈvælju/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the value households place on desirable local features such as parks, safety, climate, or cultural services.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** value household đặt vào feature local tốt như park, safety, climate hoặc service culture.
**Grammar & collocations:** `amenity-value capitalization` — capitalization; `price neighborhood quality` — định giá.
**Examples:** `Amenity value raised rents near the waterfront park even without better job access.` → Value amenity tăng rent gần park ven nước.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `편의시설 가치`.

## 10. capitalization effect /ˌkæpɪtələˈzeɪʃən ɪˈfɛkt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the extent to which a public investment, service, or amenity is reflected in land or housing prices.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** investment/service/amenity công đi vào giá đất hoặc nhà đến mức nào.
**Grammar & collocations:** `capitalization-effect estimate` — estimate; `capitalize a transit improvement` — phản ánh.
**Examples:** `The capitalization effect appeared in property prices before the new station opened.` → Effect xuất hiện trong giá property trước station mở.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `자본화 효과`.

## 11. local multiplier /ˈloʊkəl ˈmʌltəˌplaɪər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the total local change in income or employment generated by one additional unit of local demand or activity.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** thay đổi tổng local về income/employment do thêm một unit demand/activity.
**Grammar & collocations:** `local-multiplier estimate` — estimate; `trace indirect spending` — truy.
**Examples:** `The local multiplier was larger when new workers bought services from nearby businesses.` → Multiplier lớn hơn khi worker mới mua service local.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `지역 승수`.

## 12. place-based policy /pleɪs beɪst ˈpɑləsi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a policy targeted at a particular geographic area rather than at people or firms regardless of location.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** policy nhắm một địa bàn cụ thể thay vì người/firm bất kể location.
**Grammar & collocations:** `place-based-policy evaluation` — evaluation; `target a distressed region` — nhắm.
**Examples:** `The place-based policy subsidized firms that opened in neighborhoods with persistent unemployment.` → Policy hỗ trợ firm mở ở neighborhood unemployment dai dẳng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `장소 기반 정책`.

## 13. enterprise zone /ˈɛntərˌpraɪz zoʊn/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a designated area where firms receive tax, regulatory, or infrastructure incentives to encourage investment and employment.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** khu vực được chỉ định nhận incentive tax, regulation hoặc infrastructure để hút investment/job.
**Grammar & collocations:** `enterprise-zone incentive` — incentive; `attract firms into a distressed area` — thu hút.
**Examples:** `The enterprise zone attracted warehouses but produced few permanent jobs.` → Zone thu warehouse nhưng tạo ít job lâu dài.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `기업 구역`.

## 14. tax increment financing /tæks ˈɪnkrəmənt faɪˈnænsɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a public-finance method that uses future increases in property-tax revenue to fund current redevelopment.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** dùng tax property tăng trong tương lai để tài trợ redevelopment hiện tại.
**Grammar & collocations:** `tax-increment-financing district` — district; `pledge future tax growth` — cam kết.
**Examples:** `Tax increment financing funded the station-area redevelopment from expected future property values.` → TIF tài trợ redevelopment bằng property value tương lai.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `세금 증가분 금융`.

## 15. public goods provision /ˈpʌblɪk ɡʊdz prəˈvɪʒən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the supply of services or infrastructure whose benefits are broadly shared and difficult to restrict to paying users.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cung cấp service/infrastructure benefit rộng và khó giới hạn người dùng trả tiền.
**Grammar & collocations:** `public-goods-provision decision` — decision; `finance a shared service` — tài trợ.
**Examples:** `Public goods provision improved after neighboring municipalities shared a regional tax base.` → Provision cải thiện sau khi municipality chia sẻ tax base.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `공공재 공급`.

## 16. fiscal federalism /ˈfɪskəl ˈfɛdərəlˌɪzəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the study and design of how taxing, spending, and borrowing responsibilities are divided across levels of government.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cách chia trách nhiệm tax, spending và borrowing giữa các cấp government.
**Grammar & collocations:** `fiscal-federalism arrangement` — arrangement; `assign a service to the local level` — phân công.
**Examples:** `Fiscal federalism assigned primary education to local governments but equalized funding nationally.` → Federalism giao education cho local nhưng equalize funding toàn quốc.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `재정 연방주의`.

## 17. intergovernmental transfer /ˌɪntərˌɡʌvərnˈmɛntəl ˈtrænsfɝ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a payment from one level of government to another to finance services, equalize resources, or pursue policy goals.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** payment giữa cấp government để finance service, equalize resource hoặc đạt policy goal.
**Grammar & collocations:** `intergovernmental-transfer formula` — formula; `equalize local fiscal capacity` — cân bằng.
**Examples:** `The intergovernmental transfer helped poorer districts maintain the same school staffing standard.` → Transfer giúp district nghèo giữ chuẩn staffing.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `정부 간 이전`.

## 18. tax competition /tæks ˌkɑmpəˈtɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** competition among jurisdictions to attract residents, workers, or firms by changing tax rates, bases, or public services.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** jurisdiction cạnh tranh hút resident/worker/firm bằng tax rate/base hoặc service.
**Grammar & collocations:** `tax-competition pressure` — pressure; `undercut a neighboring jurisdiction` — hạ thấp.
**Examples:** `Tax competition pushed nearby towns to lower business rates and reduce discretionary services.` → Competition khiến town giảm rate business và service tùy ý.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `조세 경쟁`.

## 19. Tiebout sorting /ˈtiːbaʊt ˈsɔrtɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the movement of households among jurisdictions to match their preferred taxes and public services.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** household di chuyển giữa jurisdiction để khớp tax và public service ưa thích.
**Grammar & collocations:** `Tiebout-sorting mechanism` — mechanism; `choose a tax-service package` — chọn.
**Examples:** `Tiebout sorting made high-service suburbs more attractive to families willing to pay higher property taxes.` → Sorting làm suburb service cao hấp dẫn family chịu tax property cao.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `Tiebout 정렬`.

## 20. regional convergence /ˈriːdʒənəl kənˈvɝdʒəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a process in which poorer regions grow faster than richer regions and their incomes or productivity become more similar.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** vùng nghèo tăng nhanh hơn vùng giàu, income/productivity trở nên giống nhau hơn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `regional-convergence test` — test; `close an income gap` — thu hẹp.
**Examples:** `Regional convergence slowed when skilled workers concentrated in a few metropolitan areas.` → Convergence chậm khi skilled worker tập trung ở ít metro.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `지역 수렴`.

## Review in context

An **agglomeration effect** can create an **urban wage premium**, but a **congestion externality**, **land-rent gradient**, and **spatial mismatch** shape location choices alongside **commuting cost**. **Housing elasticity**, a **zoning constraint**, and **amenity value** determine the **capitalization effect** of investment. A **local multiplier** may motivate **place-based policy** or an **enterprise zone**, financed through **tax increment financing**. **Public goods provision** and **fiscal federalism** organize services; an **intergovernmental transfer** can reduce inequality, while **tax competition** and **Tiebout sorting** influence location. Over time, **regional convergence** may narrow income differences.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:** **Agglomeration effect** tạo **urban wage premium**, nhưng **congestion externality**, **land-rent gradient**, **spatial mismatch** và **commuting cost** cùng định hình lựa chọn location. **Housing elasticity**, **zoning constraint** và **amenity value** quyết định **capitalization effect** của investment. **Local multiplier** có thể thúc đẩy **place-based policy** hoặc **enterprise zone**, tài trợ bằng **tax increment financing**. **Public goods provision** và **fiscal federalism** tổ chức service; **intergovernmental transfer** giảm bất bình đẳng, còn **tax competition** và **Tiebout sorting** ảnh hưởng location. Theo thời gian, **regional convergence** có thể thu hẹp chênh income.
