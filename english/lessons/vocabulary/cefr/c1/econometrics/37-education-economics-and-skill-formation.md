# C1 Vocabulary 37 — Education economics and skill formation

This lesson follows how economists estimate schooling decisions, classroom inputs, skill formation, and labor-market returns to education.

Flow: **schooling attainment → enrollment elasticity → teacher value-added → class-size effect → peer composition → tracking system → test-score gain → learning loss → skill formation → early-childhood intervention → education production function → school choice → voucher take-up → attendance incentive → credential signal → sheepskin effect → overeducation → skill mismatch → human-capital investment → intergenerational mobility**.

## 1. schooling attainment /ˈskuːlɪŋ əˈteɪnmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the highest level of formal education completed by a person or population group.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** level education chính thức cao nhất một người/group hoàn tất.
**Grammar & collocations:** `schooling-attainment distribution` — distribution; `complete secondary education` — hoàn tất.
**Examples:** `Schooling attainment increased after secondary fees were removed.` → Attainment tăng sau khi bỏ fee secondary.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `교육 성취도`.

## 2. enrollment elasticity /ɪnˈroʊlmənt ɪˈlæstəsəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the percentage response of school enrollment to a percentage change in cost, income, distance, or another enrollment incentive.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** phần trăm enrollment phản ứng với phần trăm đổi cost, income, distance hoặc incentive.
**Grammar & collocations:** `enrollment-elasticity estimate` — estimate; `measure participation response` — đo.
**Examples:** `Enrollment elasticity was largest for students living close to the new school.` → Elasticity lớn nhất với student sống gần school mới.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `등록 탄력성`.

## 3. teacher value-added /ˈtiːtʃər ˈvælju ædɪd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an estimated contribution of a teacher to student outcomes after accounting for prior achievement and other student characteristics.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** contribution teacher ước lượng vào outcome student sau khi tính achievement trước và characteristic khác.
**Grammar & collocations:** `teacher-value-added estimate` — estimate; `rank instructional contributions` — xếp hạng.
**Examples:** `Teacher value-added varied substantially even among instructors in the same school.` → Value-added khác đáng kể giữa teacher cùng school.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `교사 부가가치`.

## 4. class-size effect /ˈklæs saɪz ɪˈfɛkt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the estimated change in student outcomes associated with having more or fewer students in a class.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** outcome student đổi theo số student trong class.
**Grammar & collocations:** `class-size-effect estimate` — estimate; `reduce pupils per classroom` — giảm.
**Examples:** `The class-size effect was larger in early grades than in advanced courses.` → Effect class size lớn hơn ở grade đầu.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `학급 규모 효과`.

## 5. peer composition /pɪr ˌkɑmpəˈzɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the demographic, academic, or social characteristics of the students or coworkers surrounding a person.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** characteristic demographic, academic hoặc social của peer xung quanh.
**Grammar & collocations:** `peer-composition measure` — measure; `change classroom mix` — đổi thành phần.
**Examples:** `Peer composition affected participation even after individual ability was controlled.` → Composition peer ảnh hưởng participation sau khi kiểm soát ability.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `동료 구성`.

## 6. tracking system /ˈtrækɪŋ ˈsɪstəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an education system that assigns students to different academic pathways or levels.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** system xếp student vào pathway/level academic khác nhau.
**Grammar & collocations:** `tracking-system assignment` — assignment; `place students into ability groups` — xếp.
**Examples:** `The tracking system placed students into vocational or academic programs at age fourteen.` → System xếp student vào vocational/academic lúc 14 tuổi.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `학생 트래킹 제도`.

## 7. test-score gain /tɛst skɔr ɡeɪn/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an increase in measured performance on a standardized assessment after an intervention or period of instruction.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** điểm standardized tăng sau intervention hoặc thời gian học.
**Grammar & collocations:** `test-score-gain estimate` — estimate; `measure instructional impact` — đo.
**Examples:** `The tutoring program produced a test-score gain that persisted for two years.` → Program tạo gain điểm kéo dài hai năm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `시험 점수 향상`.

## 8. learning loss /ˈlɝnɪŋ lɔs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a decline or missed growth in knowledge and skills relative to the learning path expected without a disruption.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** knowledge/skill giảm hoặc không tăng như path kỳ vọng do disruption.
**Grammar & collocations:** `learning-loss estimate` — estimate; `recover missed instruction` — bù.
**Examples:** `Learning loss was greatest for children without reliable internet access during the closure.` → Loss lớn nhất ở trẻ không có internet ổn định.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `학습 손실`.

## 9. skill formation /skɪl fɔrˈmeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the process through which cognitive, technical, social, and noncognitive abilities develop over time.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** process ability cognitive, technical, social và noncognitive phát triển theo thời gian.
**Grammar & collocations:** `skill-formation process` — process; `build early capabilities` — xây dựng.
**Examples:** `Skill formation depended on both early stimulation and later workplace practice.` → Formation phụ thuộc stimulation sớm và practice nơi làm việc.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `기술 형성`.

## 10. early-childhood intervention /ˌɝli ˈtʃaɪldˌhʊd ˌɪntərˈvɛnʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a program provided during a child's early years to improve health, development, learning, or family support.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** program những năm đầu cải thiện health, development, learning hoặc family support.
**Grammar & collocations:** `early-childhood-intervention evaluation` — evaluation; `support preschool development` — hỗ trợ.
**Examples:** `The early-childhood intervention improved language skills and reduced later grade repetition.` → Intervention cải thiện language và giảm repeat grade.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `유아기 개입`.

## 11. education production function /ˌɛdʒəˈkeɪʃən prəˈdʌkʃən ˈfʌŋkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a function relating student learning or achievement to school inputs, family resources, peers, and teaching.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** hàm nối learning/achievement với input school, family, peer và teaching.
**Grammar & collocations:** `education-production-function estimate` — estimate; `map resources into learning` — ánh xạ.
**Examples:** `The education production function treated instructional time and peer composition as separate inputs.` → Function xem time instruction và peer composition là input riêng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `교육 생산 함수`.

## 12. school choice /skuːl tʃɔɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the ability of families to select among schools rather than being assigned solely by residence.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** family được chọn giữa school thay vì chỉ bị xếp theo residence.
**Grammar & collocations:** `school-choice policy` — policy; `select a preferred school` — chọn.
**Examples:** `School choice changed enrollment patterns when families could cross district boundaries.` → Choice đổi enrollment khi family qua boundary district.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `학교 선택`.

## 13. voucher take-up /ˈvaʊtʃər teɪk ʌp/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the share of eligible families that use an education voucher to enroll in a participating school.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** phần family đủ điều kiện dùng voucher để enroll school tham gia.
**Grammar & collocations:** `voucher-take-up rate` — rate; `redeem an education voucher` — sử dụng.
**Examples:** `Voucher take-up was low because families lacked information about participating schools.` → Take-up thấp vì family thiếu information.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `바우처 수혜율`.

## 14. attendance incentive /əˈtɛndəns ɪnˈsɛntɪv/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a payment or benefit designed to increase regular participation in school or another service.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** payment/benefit nhằm tăng participation đều ở school/service.
**Grammar & collocations:** `attendance-incentive program` — program; `reward regular presence` — thưởng.
**Examples:** `The attendance incentive raised weekly presence but had little effect on test scores.` → Incentive tăng presence nhưng ít ảnh hưởng test score.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `출석 인센티브`.

## 15. credential signal /krɪˈdɛnʃəl ˈsɪɡnəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** information conveyed by a degree, certificate, or qualification about a worker's expected ability to employers.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** degree/certificate cho employer information về ability kỳ vọng của worker.
**Grammar & collocations:** `credential-signal effect` — effect; `signal unobserved ability` — báo hiệu.
**Examples:** `The credential signal raised starting wages even when measured skills were similar.` → Signal credential tăng starting wage dù skill đo tương tự.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `자격 신호`.

## 16. sheepskin effect /ˈʃiːpˌskɪn ɪˈfɛkt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an extra earnings premium associated with completing a credential beyond the return to the years of study themselves.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** premium earnings thêm do hoàn tất credential, ngoài return của số năm học.
**Grammar & collocations:** `sheepskin-effect estimate` — estimate; `reward diploma completion` — thưởng.
**Examples:** `The sheepskin effect appeared when graduates earned more than near-graduates with similar coursework.` → Effect xuất hiện khi graduate kiếm nhiều hơn người gần tốt nghiệp.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `졸업장 효과`.

## 17. overeducation /ˌoʊvərˌɛdʒəˈkeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a situation in which a worker's formal education exceeds what the current job requires or rewards.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** education worker có vượt mức job cần hoặc thưởng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `overeducation measure` — measure; `work below qualification` — làm dưới.
**Examples:** `Overeducation was common among graduates entering a weak local labor market.` → Overeducation phổ biến khi graduate vào labor market địa phương yếu.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `과잉 교육`.

## 18. skill mismatch /skɪl mɪsˈmætʃ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a gap between the skills workers possess and the skills employers require.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** gap giữa skill worker có và skill employer cần.
**Grammar & collocations:** `skill-mismatch indicator` — indicator; `align training with vacancies` — căn chỉnh.
**Examples:** `Skill mismatch kept vacancies high even though unemployment remained elevated.` → Mismatch giữ vacancy cao dù unemployment cao.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `기술 불일치`.

## 19. human-capital investment /ˈhjuːmən ˈkæpɪtəl ɪnˈvɛstmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** spending time or resources to increase a person's future productivity, health, knowledge, or skills.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** dành time/resource tăng productivity, health, knowledge hoặc skill tương lai.
**Grammar & collocations:** `human-capital-investment decision` — decision; `invest in training` — đầu tư.
**Examples:** `Human-capital investment continued after graduation through employer-sponsored training.` → Investment tiếp tục sau graduation qua training employer.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `인적 자본 투자`.

## 20. intergenerational mobility /ˌɪntərˌdʒɛnəˈreɪʃənəl moʊˈbɪləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the degree to which a person's economic or educational position differs from that of their parents.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mức economic/education position của người khác position của cha mẹ.
**Grammar & collocations:** `intergenerational-mobility estimate` — estimate; `move beyond parental status` — vượt.
**Examples:** `Intergenerational mobility was higher in regions with affordable universities and diverse employers.` → Mobility cao hơn nơi university affordable và employer đa dạng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `세대 간 이동성`.

## Review in context

**Schooling attainment** responds to **enrollment elasticity**, while **teacher value-added**, a **class-size effect**, and **peer composition** shape learning within a **tracking system**. A **test-score gain** may reverse as **learning loss** occurs; **skill formation** and an **early-childhood intervention** can be studied with an **education production function**. **School choice**, **voucher take-up**, and an **attendance incentive** change participation. A degree can act as a **credential signal** and create a **sheepskin effect**, but **overeducation** and **skill mismatch** may reduce returns. **Human-capital investment** influences **intergenerational mobility**.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:** **Schooling attainment** phản ứng với **enrollment elasticity**, còn **teacher value-added**, **class-size effect** và **peer composition** định hình learning trong **tracking system**. **Test-score gain** có thể đảo ngược khi xảy ra **learning loss**; **skill formation** và **early-childhood intervention** có thể nghiên cứu bằng **education production function**. **School choice**, **voucher take-up** và **attendance incentive** đổi participation. Degree có thể là **credential signal** và tạo **sheepskin effect**, nhưng **overeducation** và **skill mismatch** làm giảm return. **Human-capital investment** ảnh hưởng **intergenerational mobility**.
