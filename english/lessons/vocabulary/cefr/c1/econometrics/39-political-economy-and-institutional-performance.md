# C1 Vocabulary 39 — Political economy and institutional performance

This lesson follows how political incentives, institutions, and administrative choices shape public policy and economic outcomes.

Flow: **public-choice → median voter theorem → political budget cycle → vote share → constituent service → institutional quality → corruption premium → bribery incidence → procurement risk → legislative bargaining → agenda setting → veto player → constitutional constraint → political accountability → electoral volatility → coalition formation → executive constraint → party system fragmentation → policy reversal → bureaucratic discretion**.

## 1. public-choice /ˈpʌblɪk tʃɔɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the analysis of political decisions using the idea that voters, politicians, and officials respond to incentives.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** phân tích decision chính trị bằng giả định voter, politician và official phản ứng với incentive.
**Grammar & collocations:** `public-choice analysis` — analysis; `model political incentives` — mô hình hóa.
**Examples:** `Public-choice analysis asked why a costly subsidy survived despite weak evidence of its benefits.` → Phân tích hỏi vì sao subsidy đắt vẫn tồn tại dù evidence benefit yếu.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `공공 선택론`.

## 2. median voter theorem /ˈmiːdiən ˈvoʊtər ˈθiərəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the proposition that majority voting can favor the policy preferred by the voter located at the median of a one-dimensional preference distribution.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** majority voting có thể chọn policy được voter nằm giữa preference distribution ưa thích.
**Grammar & collocations:** `median-voter-theorem prediction` — prediction; `target the pivotal voter` — nhắm.
**Examples:** `The median voter theorem predicted moderate tax policy when preferences were single-peaked.` → Theorem dự đoán tax policy vừa phải khi preference một đỉnh.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `중위 투표자 정리`.

## 3. political budget cycle /pəˈlɪtɪkəl ˈbʌdʒət ˈsaɪkəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a pattern in which public spending, taxes, or deficits change systematically around elections.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** spending, tax hoặc deficit đổi có hệ thống quanh election.
**Grammar & collocations:** `political-budget-cycle effect` — effect; `expand pre-election spending` — mở rộng.
**Examples:** `The political budget cycle produced a temporary rise in visible infrastructure projects before voting.` → Cycle tạo tăng tạm thời project thấy được trước vote.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `정치적 예산 순환`.

## 4. vote share /voʊt ʃɛr/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the proportion of valid votes received by a candidate or party.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** phần trăm valid vote candidate/party nhận.
**Grammar & collocations:** `vote-share estimate` — estimate; `gain support among undecided voters` — giành.
**Examples:** `The reform increased the incumbent's vote share in districts with visible job gains.` → Reform tăng share của incumbent ở district có job gain thấy rõ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `득표율`.

## 5. constituent service /kənˈstɪtʃuənt ˈsɝvɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** assistance a representative provides to individual residents or local groups seeking help from government.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** assistance representative cung cấp cho resident/group địa phương cần government giúp.
**Grammar & collocations:** `constituent-service activity` — activity; `resolve a benefits case` — giải quyết.
**Examples:** `Constituent service helped residents navigate a delayed immigration application.` → Service giúp resident xử lý application immigration chậm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `유권자 서비스`.

## 6. institutional quality /ˌɪnstəˈtuːʃənəl ˈkwɑləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the reliability, impartiality, and effectiveness of rules and organizations that govern economic and political activity.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** độ reliable, impartial và effective của rule/organization điều hành activity.
**Grammar & collocations:** `institutional-quality index` — index; `enforce contracts predictably` — thực thi.
**Examples:** `Institutional quality attracted investment by making property rights more predictable.` → Quality thu investment nhờ property rights predictable hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `제도적 품질`.

## 7. corruption premium /kəˈrʌpʃən ˈpriːmiəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an additional cost or return demanded because corruption makes a transaction or investment uncertain.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cost/return thêm do corruption làm transaction/investment bất định.
**Grammar & collocations:** `corruption-premium estimate` — estimate; `price unofficial payments` — định giá.
**Examples:** `The corruption premium raised construction costs for firms bidding on public contracts.` → Premium làm cost xây dựng tăng với firm đấu thầu.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `부패 프리미엄`.

## 8. bribery incidence /ˈbraɪbəri ˈɪnsɪdəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the frequency or share of interactions in which a bribe is requested, offered, or paid.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** tần suất/tỉ lệ interaction có yêu cầu, đề nghị hoặc trả hối lộ.
**Grammar & collocations:** `bribery-incidence survey` — survey; `report an unofficial payment` — báo cáo.
**Examples:** `Bribery incidence fell after permit applications became traceable online.` → Incidence giảm sau khi application permit traceable online.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `뇌물 발생률`.

## 9. procurement risk /prəˈkjʊrmənt rɪsk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the risk that public purchasing will suffer from inflated prices, weak competition, fraud, or poor delivery.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** risk mua sắm công bị giá inflated, cạnh tranh yếu, fraud hoặc delivery kém.
**Grammar & collocations:** `procurement-risk score` — score; `screen a public tender` — sàng lọc.
**Examples:** `Procurement risk was high when one supplier submitted every technically acceptable bid.` → Risk cao khi một supplier nộp mọi bid acceptable.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `조달 위험`.

## 10. legislative bargaining /ˈlɛdʒəsˌleɪtɪv ˈbɑrɡənɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** negotiation among legislators over policy content, spending, amendments, and coalition support.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** legislator thương lượng content policy, spending, amendment và coalition support.
**Grammar & collocations:** `legislative-bargaining model` — model; `trade votes across issues` — trao đổi.
**Examples:** `Legislative bargaining added a regional investment clause to secure final approval.` → Bargaining thêm clause investment vùng để được duyệt.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `입법 협상`.

## 11. agenda setting /əˈdʒɛndə ˈsɛtɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the process of deciding which issues receive formal attention, debate, and a chance of a policy vote.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** quyết định issue nào được đưa vào attention, debate và vote policy.
**Grammar & collocations:** `agenda-setting power` — power; `bring a bill to the floor` — đưa ra.
**Examples:** `Agenda setting kept the tax proposal out of committee despite broad public interest.` → Agenda setting giữ proposal ngoài committee dù public interest rộng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `의제 설정`.

## 12. veto player /ˈviːtoʊ ˈpleɪər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an individual or institution whose agreement is necessary for a policy change to occur.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cá nhân/institution phải đồng ý thì policy change mới xảy ra.
**Grammar & collocations:** `veto-player theory` — theory; `block a reform` — chặn.
**Examples:** `A coalition partner became a veto player when the government lost its majority.` → Partner thành veto player khi government mất majority.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `거부권 행사자`.

## 13. constitutional constraint /ˌkɑnstəˈtuːʃənəl kənˈstreɪnt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a constitutional rule that limits what public officials may legislate, spend, or enforce.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** rule hiến pháp giới hạn official được legislate, spend hoặc enforce gì.
**Grammar & collocations:** `constitutional-constraint review` — review; `limit executive action` — giới hạn.
**Examples:** `The constitutional constraint required legislative approval before emergency borrowing.` → Constraint yêu cầu approval trước borrowing khẩn cấp.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `헌법적 제약`.

## 14. political accountability /pəˈlɪtɪkəl əˌkaʊntəˈbɪləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the ability of citizens, legislatures, courts, or oversight bodies to evaluate and sanction public officials.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** citizen, legislature, court hoặc oversight đánh giá và sanction official.
**Grammar & collocations:** `political-accountability mechanism` — mechanism; `punish poor performance` — trừng phạt.
**Examples:** `Political accountability improved when spending data were published before the election.` → Accountability cải thiện khi data spending công bố trước election.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `정치적 책무성`.

## 15. electoral volatility /ɪˈlɛktərəl vɑləˈtɪləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the extent to which vote shares shift among parties or candidates between elections.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mức vote share chuyển giữa party/candidate qua các election.
**Grammar & collocations:** `electoral-volatility index` — index; `measure party switching` — đo.
**Examples:** `Electoral volatility increased after a new party entered with a strong anti-corruption platform.` → Volatility tăng khi party mới vào với platform chống corruption.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `선거 변동성`.

## 16. coalition formation /ˌkoʊəˈlɪʃən fɔrˈmeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the process through which parties or legislators assemble enough support to control a government or pass a proposal.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** party/legislator gom đủ support để control government hoặc pass proposal.
**Grammar & collocations:** `coalition-formation model` — model; `assemble a governing majority` — tập hợp.
**Examples:** `Coalition formation delayed the budget until smaller parties received regional concessions.` → Formation trì hoãn budget đến khi party nhỏ nhận concession.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `연립 형성`.

## 17. executive constraint /ɪɡˈzɛkjətɪv kənˈstreɪnt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the extent to which courts, legislatures, or other institutions limit an executive's ability to act unilaterally.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** court, legislature hoặc institution giới hạn executive hành động một mình.
**Grammar & collocations:** `executive-constraint index` — index; `check unilateral power` — kiểm soát.
**Examples:** `Executive constraint reduced the risk that a new administration would reverse contracts overnight.` → Constraint giảm risk administration đảo contract qua đêm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `행정부 제약`.

## 18. party system fragmentation /ˈpɑrti ˈsɪstəm ˌfræɡmənˈteɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the degree to which votes or legislative seats are divided among many parties rather than concentrated in a few.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** vote/seat bị chia giữa nhiều party thay vì tập trung ít party.
**Grammar & collocations:** `party-system-fragmentation measure` — measure; `form a multiparty legislature` — hình thành.
**Examples:** `Party system fragmentation made coalition formation necessary after the election.` → Fragmentation khiến cần coalition sau election.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `정당 체계 파편화`.

## 19. policy reversal /ˈpɑləsi rɪˈvɝsəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a change that abandons, removes, or substantially redirects an earlier policy.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** policy cũ bị bỏ, gỡ hoặc chuyển hướng đáng kể.
**Grammar & collocations:** `policy-reversal risk` — risk; `undo a regulatory reform` — đảo.
**Examples:** `Policy reversal discouraged investment in infrastructure with long payback periods.` → Reversal làm nản investment infrastructure hoàn vốn dài.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `정책 반전`.

## 20. bureaucratic discretion /ˌbjʊrəˈkrætɪk dɪˈskrɛʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the freedom public officials have to interpret rules, prioritize cases, or choose how to implement a policy.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** freedom official diễn giải rule, ưu tiên case hoặc chọn cách implement policy.
**Grammar & collocations:** `bureaucratic-discretion margin` — margin; `apply a rule flexibly` — áp dụng.
**Examples:** `Bureaucratic discretion helped officials adapt the program to remote communities but created uneven treatment.` → Discretion giúp thích nghi nhưng tạo đối xử không đều.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `관료 재량`.

## Review in context

**Public-choice** analysis can use the **median voter theorem** and examine a **political budget cycle** through changes in **vote share** and **constituent service**. **Institutional quality**, a **corruption premium**, **bribery incidence**, and **procurement risk** affect performance. **Legislative bargaining**, **agenda setting**, a **veto player**, and a **constitutional constraint** shape **political accountability**. **Electoral volatility** influences **coalition formation**, while **executive constraint** and **party system fragmentation** affect stability. A **policy reversal** may follow weak commitment, and **bureaucratic discretion** changes implementation on the ground.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:** **Public-choice** có thể dùng **median voter theorem** và xem **political budget cycle** qua thay đổi **vote share** và **constituent service**. **Institutional quality**, **corruption premium**, **bribery incidence** và **procurement risk** ảnh hưởng performance. **Legislative bargaining**, **agenda setting**, **veto player** và **constitutional constraint** định hình **political accountability**. **Electoral volatility** ảnh hưởng **coalition formation**, còn **executive constraint** và **party system fragmentation** ảnh hưởng stability. **Policy reversal** có thể theo sau commitment yếu, còn **bureaucratic discretion** đổi cách implement thực tế.
