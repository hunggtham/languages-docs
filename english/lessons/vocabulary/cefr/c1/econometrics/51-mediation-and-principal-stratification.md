# C1 Vocabulary 51 — Mediation and principal stratification

This lesson follows how causal analysts study mechanisms, intermediate outcomes, and treatment effects defined for latent response groups.

Flow: **mediation estimand → exposure-mediator interaction → path-specific effect → cross-world assumption → principal stratum → truncation by death → survivor average causal effect → complier average effect → instrumental principal stratum → mediation sensitivity → unmeasured mediator confounding → interventional effect → stochastic mediator intervention → mechanistic pathway → mediation decomposition → indirect pathway → direct pathway → mediator ignorability → mediation bound → mechanism-specific effect**.

## 1. mediation estimand /miːdiˈeɪʃən ˈɛstəmənd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a precisely defined causal quantity describing how an exposure affects an outcome through an intermediate variable or pathway.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** causal quantity define rõ exposure affect outcome qua intermediate variable hoặc pathway thế nào.
**Grammar & collocations:** `well-defined mediation estimand` — estimand; `identify a mediation estimand` — identify.
**Examples:** `The mediation estimand separated the policy's effect through attendance from its other effects.` → Estimand mediation tách effect policy qua attendance khỏi effect khác.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `매개 추정 대상`.

## 2. exposure-mediator interaction /ɪkˈspoʊʒər ˈmiːdiˌeɪtər ˌɪntərˈækʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a situation in which the effect of an intermediate variable differs depending on the exposure level.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** effect của intermediate variable khác nhau theo exposure level.
**Grammar & collocations:** `exposure-mediator interaction term` — term; `model an exposure-mediator interaction` — model.
**Examples:** `An exposure-mediator interaction meant that tutoring helped more after a difficult transition.` → Interaction exposure-mediator nghĩa tutoring help hơn sau transition khó.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `노출-매개변수 상호작용`.

## 3. path-specific effect /pæθ spəˈsɪfɪk ɪˈfɛkt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a causal effect attributed to a selected pathway through a causal system.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** causal effect assign cho pathway được select trong causal system.
**Grammar & collocations:** `identified path-specific effect` — effect; `estimate a path-specific effect` — estimate.
**Examples:** `The path-specific effect through job training excluded the effect through relocation.` → Effect path-specific qua job training exclude effect qua relocation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `경로별 효과`.

## 4. cross-world assumption /ˌkrɔs ˈwɜrld əˈsʌmpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an assumption linking potential outcomes that refer to different hypothetical treatment or mediator conditions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** assumption link potential outcome thuộc các treatment hoặc mediator condition hypothetical khác nhau.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong cross-world assumption` — assumption; `question a cross-world assumption` — question.
**Examples:** `The cross-world assumption was too strong for the authors' preferred mediation claim.` → Assumption cross-world quá mạnh cho mediation claim author prefer.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `교차 세계 가정`.

## 5. principal stratum /ˈprɪnsəpəl ˈstrætəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a latent subgroup defined by how a unit would respond under different treatment or measurement conditions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** subgroup latent define theo cách unit sẽ respond dưới treatment hoặc measurement condition khác.
**Grammar & collocations:** `latent principal stratum` — stratum; `compare principal strata` — compare.
**Examples:** `The principal stratum of always-attenders could not be observed directly.` → Stratum principal của người luôn attend không observe trực tiếp được.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `주요 층화`.

## 6. truncation by death /trʌŋˈkeɪʃən baɪ dɛθ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the problem that an outcome cannot be measured for units who die before the measurement time.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** outcome không measure được cho unit chết trước measurement time.
**Grammar & collocations:** `bias from truncation by death` — truncation; `address truncation by death` — address.
**Examples:** `Truncation by death made a quality-of-life comparison ambiguous.` → Truncation by death làm quality-of-life comparison ambiguous.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `사망에 의한 절단`.

## 7. survivor average causal effect /sərˈvaɪvər ˈævərɪdʒ ˈkɔzəl ɪˈfɛkt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the average causal effect among units who would survive under each treatment being compared.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** average causal effect trong unit sẽ survive dưới mỗi treatment compare.
**Grammar & collocations:** `survivor average causal effect estimate` — estimate; `interpret a survivor average causal effect` — interpret.
**Examples:** `The survivor average causal effect concerned only patients expected to survive either treatment.` → Effect causal average survivor chỉ concern patient expected survive cả hai treatment.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `생존자 평균 인과 효과`.

## 8. complier average effect /kəmˈplaɪər ˈævərɪdʒ ɪˈfɛkt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the average causal effect among units who would follow the treatment assignment encouraged by an instrument.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** average causal effect trong unit sẽ follow treatment assignment được instrument encourage.
**Grammar & collocations:** `local complier average effect` — effect; `estimate a complier average effect` — estimate.
**Examples:** `The complier average effect applied to patients whose treatment choice changed with the lottery.` → Effect average complier apply cho patient choice treatment đổi theo lottery.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `순응자 평균 효과`.

## 9. instrumental principal stratum /ˌɪnstrəˈmɛntəl ˈprɪnsəpəl ˈstrætəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a latent subgroup defined by potential treatment behavior under different values of an instrument.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** subgroup latent define theo treatment behavior potential dưới value instrument khác nhau.
**Grammar & collocations:** `instrumental principal stratum analysis` — analysis; `define an instrumental principal stratum` — define.
**Examples:** `The instrumental principal stratum distinguished always-users from people persuaded by the offer.` → Stratum principal instrumental tách always-user khỏi người bị offer thuyết phục.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `도구변수 주요 층화`.

## 10. mediation sensitivity /miːdiˈeɪʃən ˌsɛnsəˈtɪvəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an analysis of how a mediation conclusion changes when untestable assumptions are weakened.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** analysis xem mediation conclusion đổi thế nào khi assumption untestable weaken.
**Grammar & collocations:** `mediation-sensitivity analysis` — analysis; `report mediation sensitivity` — report.
**Examples:** `Mediation sensitivity showed that the indirect pathway disappeared under modest confounding.` → Sensitivity mediation show indirect pathway disappear với confounding vừa phải.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `매개 민감도`.

## 11. unmeasured mediator confounding /ˌʌnˈmɛʒərd ˈmiːdiˌeɪtər kənˈfaʊndɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** confounding of the mediator-outcome relationship by a variable that was not measured.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mediator-outcome relationship bị confound bởi variable không measure.
**Grammar & collocations:** `possible unmeasured mediator confounding` — confounding; `allow for unmeasured mediator confounding` — allow.
**Examples:** `Unmeasured mediator confounding weakened the claim that stress reduction caused the outcome.` → Confounding mediator không measure làm claim stress reduction cause outcome yếu hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `측정되지 않은 매개 교란`.

## 12. interventional effect /ˌɪntərˈvɛnʃənəl ɪˈfɛkt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a causal effect defined by setting or changing a variable through a realistic intervention distribution.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** causal effect define bằng set hoặc change variable qua realistic intervention distribution.
**Grammar & collocations:** `interventional indirect effect` — effect; `estimate an interventional effect` — estimate.
**Examples:** `The interventional effect asked what would happen if the mediator followed the program's observed distribution.` → Effect interventional hỏi điều gì xảy ra nếu mediator follow distribution observe của program.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `개입 효과`.

## 13. stochastic mediator intervention /stəˈkæstɪk ˈmiːdiˌeɪtər ˌɪntərˈvɛnʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an intervention that changes a mediator by drawing it from a specified probability distribution rather than assigning one fixed value.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** intervention change mediator bằng draw từ probability distribution thay vì assign fixed value.
**Grammar & collocations:** `realistic stochastic mediator intervention` — intervention; `simulate a stochastic mediator intervention` — simulate.
**Examples:** `A stochastic mediator intervention represented a feasible range of counseling intensity.` → Intervention mediator stochastic represent range counseling intensity feasible.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `확률적 매개 개입`.

## 14. mechanistic pathway /ˌmɛkəˈnɪstɪk ˈpæθweɪ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a sequence of processes through which an intervention is expected to produce an outcome.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** sequence process qua đó intervention expected produce outcome.
**Grammar & collocations:** `plausible mechanistic pathway` — pathway; `trace a mechanistic pathway` — trace.
**Examples:** `The proposed mechanistic pathway ran from sleep improvement to better concentration.` → Pathway mechanistic đề xuất đi từ sleep improvement tới concentration tốt hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `기전 경로`.

## 15. mediation decomposition /miːdiˈeɪʃən ˌdiːkɑmpəˈzɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a breakdown of a total effect into components associated with intermediate variables or pathways.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** breakdown total effect thành component liên quan intermediate variable hoặc pathway.
**Grammar & collocations:** `additive mediation decomposition` — decomposition; `interpret a mediation decomposition` — interpret.
**Examples:** `The mediation decomposition attributed most of the gain to faster referral.` → Decomposition mediation attribute phần lớn gain cho referral nhanh hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `매개 분해`.

## 16. indirect pathway /ˌɪndəˈrɛkt ˈpæθweɪ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a causal route from an exposure to an outcome that passes through one or more intermediate variables.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** route causal từ exposure tới outcome đi qua intermediate variable.
**Grammar & collocations:** `plausible indirect pathway` — pathway; `test an indirect pathway` — test.
**Examples:** `The indirect pathway ran through training attendance rather than equipment quality.` → Pathway indirect đi qua training attendance chứ không phải equipment quality.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `간접 경로`.

## 17. direct pathway /dəˈrɛkt ˈpæθweɪ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a causal route from an exposure to an outcome that does not pass through the specified mediator.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** route causal từ exposure tới outcome không đi qua mediator được specify.
**Grammar & collocations:** `remaining direct pathway` — pathway; `estimate a direct pathway` — estimate.
**Examples:** `The direct pathway remained after the analysis accounted for attendance.` → Pathway direct vẫn còn sau khi analysis account attendance.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `직접 경로`.

## 18. mediator ignorability /ˈmiːdiˌeɪtər ˌɪɡnərˈæbɪləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the assumption that, after measured variables are controlled, mediator assignment is unrelated to the relevant potential outcome.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** assumption sau control measured variable, mediator assignment không liên quan potential outcome.
**Grammar & collocations:** `conditional mediator ignorability` — ignorability; `question mediator ignorability` — question.
**Examples:** `Mediator ignorability was doubtful because motivation was not measured.` → Ignorability mediator đáng doubt vì motivation không được measure.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `매개변수 무시 가능성`.

## 19. mediation bound /miːdiˈeɪʃən baʊnd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a range of values within which a mediation effect must lie when point identification is not justified.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** range value mà mediation effect phải nằm trong khi point identification không justified.
**Grammar & collocations:** `informative mediation bound` — bound; `derive a mediation bound` — derive.
**Examples:** `The mediation bound allowed a positive indirect effect but ruled out a large one.` → Bound mediation allow indirect effect positive nhưng rule out effect lớn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `매개 경계`.

## 20. mechanism-specific effect /ˈmɛkəˌnɪzəm spəˈsɪfɪk ɪˈfɛkt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a causal effect defined for one particular mechanism through which an intervention may operate.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** causal effect define cho một mechanism cụ thể mà intervention operate qua.
**Grammar & collocations:** `mechanism-specific effect estimate` — estimate; `compare mechanism-specific effects` — compare.
**Examples:** `The mechanism-specific effect focused on improved access rather than changed preferences.` → Effect mechanism-specific focus access improved chứ không phải preference đổi.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `기전별 효과`.

## Review in context

A **mediation estimand** may depend on an **exposure-mediator interaction** and a **path-specific effect**, but a **cross-world assumption** can be difficult to defend. A **principal stratum** clarifies latent response groups, while **truncation by death** complicates a **survivor average causal effect**. An instrument may identify a **complier average effect** for an **instrumental principal stratum**. **Mediation sensitivity** tests **unmeasured mediator confounding**, and an **interventional effect** can use a **stochastic mediator intervention**. Analysts trace a **mechanistic pathway**, perform a **mediation decomposition**, and distinguish an **indirect pathway** from a **direct pathway**. **Mediator ignorability**, a **mediation bound**, and a **mechanism-specific effect** make the remaining assumptions explicit.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:** **Mediation estimand** có thể phụ thuộc **exposure-mediator interaction** và **path-specific effect**, nhưng **cross-world assumption** khó defend. **Principal stratum** clarify latent response group, còn **truncation by death** làm **survivor average causal effect** phức tạp. Instrument có thể identify **complier average effect** cho **instrumental principal stratum**. **Mediation sensitivity** test **unmeasured mediator confounding**, và **interventional effect** có thể dùng **stochastic mediator intervention**. Analyst trace **mechanistic pathway**, làm **mediation decomposition** và phân biệt **indirect pathway** với **direct pathway**. **Mediator ignorability**, **mediation bound** và **mechanism-specific effect** làm assumption còn lại rõ hơn.
