# C1 Vocabulary 58 — Network formation and strategic links

This lesson follows how econometricians model the creation of relationships when agents choose links, respond to incentives, and shape one another's opportunities.

Flow: **network formation model → link formation → homophily parameter → stochastic block model → latent space model → network equilibrium → link externality → strategic network formation → network formation shock → degree heterogeneity → network sampling → graph sampling → edge formation → dyadic outcome → social adjacency → network treatment → peer selection → network utility → link cost → formation probability**.

## 1. network formation model /ˈnɛtˌwɜrk fɔrˈmeɪʃən ˈmɑdəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a model describing how agents create, maintain, or remove relationships in a network.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** model describe agent create, maintain hoặc remove relationship trong network.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strategic network formation model` — model; `estimate a network formation model` — estimate.
**Examples:** `The network formation model explained why firms formed links to nearby suppliers.` → Model formation network explain vì sao firm link với supplier gần.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `네트워크 형성 모형`.

## 2. link formation /lɪŋk fɔrˈmeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the process through which a connection between two units is created.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** process connection giữa hai unit được create.
**Grammar & collocations:** `bilateral link formation` — formation; `study link formation` — study.
**Examples:** `Link formation was more common when two researchers shared a language.` → Formation link phổ biến hơn khi hai researcher share language.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `연결 형성`.

## 3. homophily parameter /hoʊˈmɑfəli pəˈræmətər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a parameter measuring how strongly agents prefer links with others who have similar characteristics.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** parameter measure agent thích link với người có characteristic tương tự mạnh thế nào.
**Grammar & collocations:** `positive homophily parameter` — parameter; `estimate a homophily parameter` — estimate.
**Examples:** `The homophily parameter was high for education and occupation.` → Parameter homophily cao với education và occupation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `동질성 매개변수`.

## 4. stochastic block model /stəˈkæstɪk blɑk ˈmɑdəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a network model in which link probabilities depend on latent groups to which nodes belong.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** model network nơi link probability phụ thuộc latent group của node.
**Grammar & collocations:** `degree-corrected stochastic block model` — model; `fit a stochastic block model` — fit.
**Examples:** `The stochastic block model found communities with dense internal links.` → Model block stochastic tìm community có link internal dense.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `확률적 블록 모형`.

## 5. latent space model /ˈleɪtənt speɪs ˈmɑdəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a network model in which links are more likely when nodes are close in an unobserved geometric space.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** model link likely hơn khi node gần nhau trong geometric space unobserved.
**Grammar & collocations:** `distance-based latent space model` — model; `estimate a latent space model` — estimate.
**Examples:** `The latent space model placed similar research teams near one another.` → Model space latent place research team tương tự gần nhau.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `잠재 공간 모형`.

## 6. network equilibrium /ˈnɛtˌwɜrk ˌiːkwəˈlɪbriəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a stable network outcome in which no agent has a profitable change in links given others' links.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** network outcome stable nơi không agent có change link profitable khi biết link của người khác.
**Grammar & collocations:** `stable network equilibrium` — equilibrium; `solve for a network equilibrium` — solve.
**Examples:** `The network equilibrium contained fewer links after maintenance costs increased.` → Equilibrium network có ít link hơn sau khi maintenance cost tăng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `네트워크 균형`.

## 7. link externality /lɪŋk ˌɛkstərˈnæləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an effect of one relationship on the benefits or costs of other relationships in the network.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** effect của một relationship lên benefit hoặc cost của relationship khác trong network.
**Grammar & collocations:** `positive link externality` — externality; `model link externality` — model.
**Examples:** `A new rail link created a link externality for nearby warehouses.` → Link mới tạo externality link cho warehouse gần.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `연결 외부성`.

## 8. strategic network formation /strəˈtiːdʒɪk ˈnɛtˌwɜrk fɔrˈmeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** network formation in which agents choose links while anticipating how others will respond.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** formation network nơi agent choose link và anticipate người khác respond.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strategic network-formation game` — game; `analyze strategic network formation` — analyze.
**Examples:** `Strategic network formation led firms to avoid links that would strengthen a rival.` → Formation network strategic làm firm avoid link strengthen rival.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `전략적 네트워크 형성`.

## 9. network formation shock /ˈnɛtˌwɜrk fɔrˈmeɪʃən ʃɑk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an unexpected event that changes the incentives or opportunities for creating network links.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** event unexpected đổi incentive hoặc opportunity create network link.
**Grammar & collocations:** `exogenous network formation shock` — shock; `identify a network formation shock` — identify.
**Examples:** `The platform merger was a network formation shock for independent sellers.` → Merger platform là shock formation network cho seller independent.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `네트워크 형성 충격`.

## 10. degree heterogeneity /dɪˈɡriː ˌhɛtərədʒəˈniəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** variation in the number of links held by different nodes in a network.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** variation số link mỗi node hold trong network.
**Grammar & collocations:** `substantial degree heterogeneity` — heterogeneity; `allow for degree heterogeneity` — allow.
**Examples:** `Degree heterogeneity separated a few highly connected hospitals from small clinics.` → Heterogeneity degree tách vài hospital highly connected khỏi clinic nhỏ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `차수 이질성`.

## 11. network sampling /ˈnɛtˌwɜrk ˈsæmplɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the process of selecting nodes, links, or subgraphs for observation from a larger network.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** process select node, link hoặc subgraph để observe từ network lớn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `biased network sampling` — sampling; `design network sampling` — design.
**Examples:** `Network sampling missed isolated firms that had no visible platform links.` → Sampling network miss firm isolated không có platform link visible.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `네트워크 표본 추출`.

## 12. graph sampling /ɡræf ˈsæmplɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the selection of a smaller graph or set of nodes and edges to represent a larger graph.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** select graph nhỏ hoặc node và edge để represent graph lớn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `subgraph graph sampling` — sampling; `evaluate graph sampling` — evaluate.
**Examples:** `Graph sampling preserved dense communities but underrepresented rare bridges.` → Sampling graph preserve community dense nhưng underrepresent bridge hiếm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `그래프 표본 추출`.

## 13. edge formation /ɛdʒ fɔrˈmeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the creation of a specific connection, represented as an edge, between two nodes.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** create connection cụ thể represent bằng edge giữa hai node.
**Grammar & collocations:** `directed edge formation` — formation; `model edge formation` — model.
**Examples:** `Edge formation accelerated after the messaging feature was introduced.` → Formation edge accelerate sau khi introduce messaging feature.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `간선 형성`.

## 14. dyadic outcome /daɪˈædɪk ˈaʊtˌkʌm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an outcome defined for a pair of units, such as whether they collaborate or exchange resources.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** outcome define cho pair unit, như collaborate hoặc exchange resource không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `binary dyadic outcome` — outcome; `model a dyadic outcome` — model.
**Examples:** `The dyadic outcome recorded whether two firms signed a supply agreement.` → Outcome dyadic record hai firm ký supply agreement không.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `쌍대 결과`.

## 15. social adjacency /ˈsoʊʃəl əˈdʒeɪsənsi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the relation of being connected or directly reachable within a social network.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** relationship connected hoặc reachable trực tiếp trong social network.
**Grammar & collocations:** `measured social adjacency` — adjacency; `define social adjacency` — define.
**Examples:** `Social adjacency determined whose messages appeared in the participant's feed.` → Adjacency social determine message của ai appear trong feed participant.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `사회적 인접성`.

## 16. network treatment /ˈnɛtˌwɜrk ˈtriːtmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a treatment defined over a unit and the treatment status of connected units.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** treatment define theo unit và treatment status của unit connected.
**Grammar & collocations:** `neighborhood network treatment` — treatment; `assign network treatment` — assign.
**Examples:** `Network treatment included both a household's subsidy and its neighbors' subsidies.` → Treatment network gồm subsidy household và subsidy neighbor.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `네트워크 처치`.

## 17. peer selection /pɪr səˈlɛkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the process through which units choose or become connected to particular peers.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** process unit choose hoặc become connected với peer cụ thể.
**Grammar & collocations:** `nonrandom peer selection` — selection; `adjust for peer selection` — adjust.
**Examples:** `Peer selection made high-achieving students more likely to study together.` → Selection peer làm high-achieving student likely study cùng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `동료 선택`.

## 18. network utility /ˈnɛtˌwɜrk juːˈtɪləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the benefit an agent receives from its own links, others' links, and the network position created by them.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** benefit agent receive từ link own, link người khác và network position.
**Grammar & collocations:** `expected network utility` — utility; `maximize network utility` — maximize.
**Examples:** `Network utility increased when a firm gained access to a reliable supplier cluster.` → Utility network tăng khi firm access supplier cluster reliable.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `네트워크 효용`.

## 19. link cost /lɪŋk kɔst/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the time, money, effort, or risk required to create or maintain a network connection.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** time, money, effort hoặc risk cần create hoặc maintain network connection.
**Grammar & collocations:** `fixed link cost` — cost; `estimate link cost` — estimate.
**Examples:** `A high link cost discouraged small firms from joining the formal network.` → Cost link cao discourage firm nhỏ join formal network.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `연결 비용`.

## 20. formation probability /fɔrˈmeɪʃən ˌprɑbəˈbɪləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the estimated probability that a particular relationship or edge will be created.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** probability estimate một relationship hoặc edge cụ thể sẽ được create.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high formation probability` — probability; `estimate formation probability` — estimate.
**Examples:** `Formation probability rose when firms shared a director.` → Probability formation tăng khi firm share director.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `형성 확률`.

## Review in context

A **network formation model** explains **link formation** through a **homophily parameter**, a **stochastic block model**, or a **latent space model**. A **network equilibrium** may reflect a **link externality** and **strategic network formation**, while a **network formation shock** can change **degree heterogeneity**. **Network sampling** and **graph sampling** determine which **edge formation** events are observed. A **dyadic outcome** may depend on **social adjacency**, a **network treatment**, and **peer selection**. Agents compare **network utility** with **link cost**, and the resulting **formation probability** describes which relationships are likely to appear.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:** **Network formation model** explain **link formation** qua **homophily parameter**, **stochastic block model** hoặc **latent space model**. **Network equilibrium** có thể reflect **link externality** và **strategic network formation**, còn **network formation shock** có thể đổi **degree heterogeneity**. **Network sampling** và **graph sampling** quyết định edge **edge formation** nào được observe. **Dyadic outcome** có thể phụ thuộc **social adjacency**, **network treatment** và **peer selection**. Agent compare **network utility** với **link cost**, còn **formation probability** mô tả relationship nào likely xuất hiện.
