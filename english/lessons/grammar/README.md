# English Grammar — A Natural American English Learning Path

This grammar library is meant to be read continuously, not scanned like a formula sheet. The previous band-based files were useful as coverage checklists, but they encouraged the learner to jump from one pattern to another before building a stable mental model. The canonical curriculum now follows conceptual dependency: first understand how an English sentence is organized, then how speakers identify things and quantities, then how English builds time and viewpoint, then modality and clause relationships, and only after that move into counterfactuality, compression, information structure, register, and advanced rhetorical choices.

The default variety is contemporary General American English. British English differences are connected directly to the relevant concept whenever they affect grammar, vocabulary, collocation, meaning, or real-world interpretation. The goal is to keep one stable American production standard while building enough British-English links that international books, media, exams, and coworkers do not require a second lookup process.

The explanations are layered inside ordinary prose. When a technical term is necessary, the lesson gives a precise grammar-reference-style definition and immediately turns it into plain language, often with the kind of concrete explanation that would also make sense to a child. These layers are deliberately mixed into the same explanation rather than split into separate “technical,” “dictionary,” or “easy” sections. The wording is self-contained and paraphrased so the reader does not need an outside grammar dictionary to understand the idea.

The first reading sequence is `01-foundations/01-how-an-english-sentence-works.md`, followed by `02-reference-nouns-articles-and-quantity.md`, `03-time-tense-and-aspect.md`, `04-modality-negation-and-questions.md`, and `05-verb-patterns-and-connecting-ideas.md`. This establishes sentence architecture, reference, tense/aspect, modality, questions, negation, complementation, passive voice, reported speech, relative clauses, conditions, and the basic logic for joining ideas.

After that, continue through `02-core/01-perfect-meaning-and-narrative-time.md`, `02-conditionals-counterfactuality-and-wish.md`, `03-modal-perfects-deduction-and-evaluation.md`, `04-voice-reporting-and-clause-compression.md`, and `05-focus-emphasis-and-information-flow.md`. These lessons deepen the same systems rather than introducing a new “band”: temporal reference points, unreal alternatives, modal stance toward past events, passive/reporting systems, reduced clauses, clefts, inversion, focus, and discourse flow.

The final sequence is `03-advanced/01-register-subjunctive-and-formal-choices.md`, `02-hedging-stance-and-evidential-grammar.md`, `03-marked-word-order-and-rhetorical-focus.md`, `04-ellipsis-compression-and-nominalization.md`, and `05-american-british-bridges-and-native-choice.md`. These lessons are about control rather than complexity for its own sake: choosing the correct register, using the American mandative subjunctive naturally, calibrating certainty to evidence, recognizing rare or literary syntax without overusing it, compressing information without making prose opaque, and mapping important American/British differences into one connected grammar system.

The library is intentionally not divided into A1, A2, B1, B2, C1, or C2 files. Those labels can still be useful for assessment, but they are poor conceptual boundaries. A learner may understand an advanced conditional only after understanding tense as viewpoint, and may understand a C1 inversion only after understanding ordinary auxiliary movement. The files therefore follow prerequisites rather than exam bands.

No lesson treats “advanced” as “use the longest or rarest structure.” Native control means choosing the simplest form that expresses the intended meaning, evidence, focus, and social relationship precisely. Marked inversion, nominalization, passive voice, formal linkers, and dense syntax are presented as tools with costs and purposes, not as upgrades over ordinary English.
