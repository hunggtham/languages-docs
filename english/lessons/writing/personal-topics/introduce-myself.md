# Writing: Introduce myself

Target: 80–100 words.

## Questions to answer

- Where are you from and where do you live now?
- What kind of work do you do?
- What do you enjoy outside work?
- Why are you studying English?

## Suggested structure

1. Basic personal information.
2. Work and daily life.
3. English goal and one reason.

## Useful sentence starters

- I am originally from …, but I currently live in …
- I work as …, and my main responsibility is …
- In my free time, I usually …
- I am studying English because …
