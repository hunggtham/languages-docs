# Answer 07 — Decisions and regret

1. `take the plunge` = make a brave decision; `have second thoughts` = doubt it later; `weigh up the pros and cons` = compare advantages/disadvantages; `play it safe` = choose less risk.
2. `could`
3. `should`
4. `would`
5. Answers vary; the sentence must describe a sudden decision.
6. `Mai might have stayed because her mother was ill.`
7. `would` — second conditional: `if + past`, then `would + base verb`.
