# Answer 01 — Tense map

1. `work / am studying` — routine versus temporary activity.
2. `was watching / called` — background action interrupted by a completed event.
3. `had left` — earlier past event.
4. `is going to rain` — prediction from visible evidence.
5. `will send` — promise.
6. `will have finished` — complete before a future deadline.
7. `became` — finished event with a specific past year.
8. `have already sent` — recent experience/result without a finished time.
