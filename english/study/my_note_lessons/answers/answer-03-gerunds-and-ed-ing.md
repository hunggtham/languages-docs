# Answer 03 — Gerunds and adjective endings

1. `I enjoy studying English.`
2. `I finished doing my work.`
3. `The lesson was interesting, so I was interested.`
4. `It was a tiring day, and I felt tired.`
5. `Reading is important.`
6. `After finishing work, I went home.`
7. `I want to study English.`
8. In the first sentence `studying` is the verb after `am`; in the second it is a gerund functioning as the subject.
