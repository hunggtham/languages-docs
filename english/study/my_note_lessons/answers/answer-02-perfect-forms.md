# Answer 02 — Perfect forms and deductions

1. `have been learning` — activity continuing from a starting point.
2. `have been running` — recent activity with present evidence.
3. `had been studying` — duration before a past moment.
4. `have visited / went` — life experience versus finished time.
5. `must have left` — strong deduction about the past.
6. `might have forgotten` — possible deduction.
7. `can’t have left` — strong negative deduction.
8. `should have checked` — regret/criticism.
