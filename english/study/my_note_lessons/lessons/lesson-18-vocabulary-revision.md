# Lesson 18 — Vocabulary revision: July–August set

**Level:** B1–B2  
**Source:** `20-vocabulary-revision-homework.docx`.  
**Goals:** retrieve vocabulary in context, form words correctly, and avoid false collocations.

## Revision groups

Travel: `itinerary`, `off the beaten track`. Work: `burnout`, `upskill`. Crime: `accomplice`, `loot`. Beliefs: `jinx`, `faux pas`. Digital safety: `red flag`, `digital footprint`. Materials: `brittle`, `translucent`.

Remember: `lead to burnout`, not `lead to upskill`; `upskill` is a verb. `A glass window can be translucent`; a person who believes everything online is `gullible`.
