# Lesson 23 — Travel writing and speaking task

**Level:** B1  
**Source:** travel homework/catch-up sheets.  
**Goals:** combine travel vocabulary, perfect forms and polite requests in a short output.

Write 6–8 sentences about a real or imaginary trip. Include where/when, who travelled with you, what happened, two present-perfect sentences and three travel words. Then record 1–2 minutes about a dream holiday and one problem that happened (or could happen).
