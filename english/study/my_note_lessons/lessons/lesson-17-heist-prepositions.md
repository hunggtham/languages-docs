# Lesson 17 — Prepositions in a heist story

**Level:** B1  
**Source:** `16-heist-prepositions-homework-pht.docx`.  
**Goals:** separate position from movement and use dependent prepositions accurately.

## Position vs movement

Position: `in front of the vault`, `between two statues`, `inside his pocket`. Movement: `through a window`, `past the station`, `down the stairs`, `out of the building`, `onto the roof`.

## Dependent prepositions

`wait for`, `accused of`, `prevented from`, `confessed to`, `keen on`, `made from`, `congratulations on`, `apologised for`, `depend on`, `explain to`, `good at`. `Discuss` and `phone` do not need an extra preposition: `discuss the problem`, `phone the restaurant`.
