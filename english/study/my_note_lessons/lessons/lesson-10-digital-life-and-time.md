# Lesson 10 — Digital life, time zones and past habits

**Level:** B1  
**Goals:** talk about online privacy, compare clocks, and distinguish `used to`, `would` and past simple.

## Digital-footprint vocabulary

**digital footprint, track, leak data, permanent, personal data, screenshot, erase, anonymous, compromising, consent**.

Good practice: do not share passwords or urgent transfers from an unverified message; obtain consent before posting someone else’s image; remember that deletion may not be permanent.

## Time-zone vocabulary

**local time, standard time, time zone, prime meridian, GMT/UTC, longitude, ahead of/behind, time difference, set/synchronise a clock, daylight saving time**.

`Japan is two hours ahead of Vietnam.` `My phone and laptop are out of sync.`

## Past habits

- `used to + verb`: a past habit or state that is no longer true: `I used to be afraid of flying.`
- `would + verb`: a repeated past action inside an established story: `People would set their clocks by the sun.`
- past simple: a single event or a counted number of events: `The railroads divided the continent in 1883.`

Do not use `would` for a state such as `be`, `have`, `know`, or `own`.

## Pronunciation checkpoint

The export also contains minimal-pair practice (`right/light`, `lead/read`,
`collect/correct`, `sip/ship`, `seat/sheet`, `think/sink`, `three/tree`).
Say the two words slowly, then in a short sentence; the goal is a contrast in
sound, not perfect spelling.
