# Lesson 13 — Passive voice through the glass lesson

**Level:** B1–B2  
**Source:** `glass-lesson-homework*.docx`.  
**Goals:** describe processes when the action/result matters more than the agent.

## Forms

`be + past participle`: `Glass is made from sand.` · `The road is being repaired.` · `The account has been deleted.` · `The molten glass can be shaped.`

Use `made of` when the material remains recognisable (`a bottle made of glass`), `made from` when it changes (`glass made from sand`), `made into` for the result, and `made by` for the maker.

## Glass vocabulary

`fragile`, `brittle`, `transparent`, `translucent`, `opaque`, `molten`, `shard`, `pane`, `lens`, `furnace`.
