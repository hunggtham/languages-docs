# Lesson 01 — Tense map: present, past and future

**Level:** A2–B1  
**Goals:** choose a tense from the time reference; describe routines, actions in progress, finished events and plans.

## 1. Present forms

| Form | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Present simple: `subject + base verb` (`-s` for he/she/it) | habits, facts, schedules | `I work from home on Fridays.` |
| Present continuous: `am/is/are + V-ing` | now, around now, a temporary situation, an arranged plan | `I am studying English this week.` |

Use a time marker to make the choice clear: **every day/usually** → present simple; **now/this week/at the moment** → present continuous.

## 2. Past forms

| Form | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Past simple | finished event at a finished time; a sequence; a past habit | `I ate a cake yesterday.` |
| Past continuous: `was/were + V-ing` | background action, interruption, two actions in progress | `I was walking home when I saw a dog.` |
| Past perfect: `had + past participle` | the earlier of two past events | `They had left by the time I arrived.` |

## 3. Future forms

| Form | Use | Example |
|---|---|---|
| `will + verb` | decision now, promise, offer, prediction | `I’ll call you.` |
| `be going to + verb` | prior plan or prediction from evidence | `The clouds are black; it’s going to rain.` |
| Future continuous: `will be + V-ing` | action in progress at a future time | `This time tomorrow I’ll be working.` |
| Future perfect: `will have + past participle` | finished before a future deadline | `By Friday, I’ll have finished the report.` |

## 4. A reliable decision process

1. Circle the time phrase.
2. Ask whether the action is finished, in progress, earlier than another action, or a plan.
3. Check the auxiliary (`do`, `be`, `have`, or `will`).
