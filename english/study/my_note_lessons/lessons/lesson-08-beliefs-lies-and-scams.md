# Lesson 08 — Beliefs, lies, warnings and scams

**Level:** B1–B2  
**Goals:** describe uncertainty, report dishonest behaviour, and give safe advice.

## Belief vocabulary

**superstition, charm, curse, jinx, ritual, coincidence, fortune-teller, sceptical, gullible, rational**.

`Must be` expresses a strong positive deduction, `might/could be` a possibility, and `can’t be` a strong negative deduction.

## Lying vocabulary

**cover up** a mistake; **betray** trust; tell a **white lie**; **come clean**; **see through** someone; **bend the truth**; **let something slip**.

## Scam-safety language

- Warning: `Watch out for that.`, `Whatever you do, don’t click it.`, `I’d steer clear of that.`
- Advice: `If I were you, I’d hang up.`, `You’d be better off calling the bank yourself.`
- Past regret: `I shouldn’t have clicked the link.`, `I wish I’d asked someone.`

Do not trust a message just because it uses a logo. Check the sender, URL, urgency, spelling, and payment request through an official channel.
