# Lesson 05 — Place, time and movement prepositions

**Level:** A2–B1  
**Goals:** choose the preposition that describes a location, time or path.

## Core groups

- **Time:** `on Friday`, `on Christmas Day`, `in the 13th century`, `at 8 p.m.`
- **Place:** `in the refrigerator`, `in the armchair`, `near the fireplace`, `between two statues`, `opposite the museum`.
- **Movement:** `into`, `out of`, `through`, `across`, `along`, `past`, `over`, `under`, `around`, `towards`, `onto`, `off`, `up`, `down`.

Contrast `in` (inside a place) with `into` (movement to the inside), and `on` (position) with `onto` (movement to a surface).

Useful location language from the notes: `near your home` (home as a place), `far from`, and `Japan is two hours ahead of Vietnam`.
