# Lesson 20 — Reporting verbs: deny, suggest, claim and admit

**Level:** B2  
**Source:** review and vocabulary-revision worksheets.  
**Goals:** choose the correct complement after a reporting verb.

- `deny + V-ing`: `He denied stealing the loot.`
- `suggest + V-ing` or `suggest that + subject + base verb`: `She suggested checking the reviews.`
- `admit + V-ing` or `admit that + clause`: `He admitted breaking the window.`
- `claim/point out + that-clause`: `The scientist claimed that stripes confused insects.`
- `insist that + subject + (should) + base verb`: `She insisted that I stay.`
