# Lesson 14 — Say, tell, talk and speak

**Level:** B1  
**Source:** `say-tell-talk-worksheet.docx` and review worksheet.  
**Goals:** choose the verb by focusing on words, listener, conversation or language ability.

- `say + words/clause`: `She said she was tired.` If a listener is named, use `say to`: `He said it to me.`
- `tell + person + information/instruction`: `Tell me the truth.`
- `talk + to/with + person + about + topic`: `We talked about the project.`
- `speak + to/with + person` or `speak + language`: `Can I speak to the manager? Do you speak Vietnamese?`
