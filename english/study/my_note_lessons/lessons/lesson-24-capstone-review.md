# Lesson 24 — Capstone review

**Level:** B1–B2  
**Source:** the two revision worksheets and the standardized lessons in this folder.  
**Goals:** demonstrate what is ready for web study and identify the next review target.

The capstone covers: one vocabulary gap-fill, one passive transformation, one embedded question, one reported statement, one perfect-modal deduction, one pronunciation pair and a one-minute speaking response. Record the score and the three errors that still repeat.
