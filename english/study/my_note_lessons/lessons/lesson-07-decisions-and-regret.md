# Lesson 07 — Decisions, possibilities and regret

**Level:** B1–B2  
**Goals:** discuss difficult choices and evaluate a past decision.

## Decision idioms

| Expression | Meaning |
|---|---|
| make up your mind | finally decide |
| have second thoughts | start to doubt a decision |
| weigh up the pros and cons | compare advantages and disadvantages |
| take the plunge | make a brave decision after hesitation |
| play it safe | choose the less risky option |
| decide on the spur of the moment | decide suddenly |
| be in two minds | be unable to choose |
| live to regret it | regret a decision later |

The reading *The Job She Didn’t Take* contrasts money and travel with family concerns. The key grammar is: `I could have gone` (past possibility), `I should probably have gone` (regret/criticism), and `I would have been a different person` (unreal result).

## Hypothetical choices

For an imagined present/future choice, use `if + past simple, would + base verb`:

`If I could become a professional in one unusual skill, I would become a flower arranger.`

Here `would` is the conditional form of `will`; it does not describe a real plan yet.
