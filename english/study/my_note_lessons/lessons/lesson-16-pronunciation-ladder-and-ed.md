# Lesson 16 — The pronunciation ladder and `-ed` endings

**Level:** B1  
**Source:** `17-speaking-homework.docx` and review worksheet.  
**Goals:** distinguish nasal `-nd` from final `-t` and pronounce regular past endings.

## Ladder

`bow–bound–bout`, `row–round–rout`, `sew–sound–sought`, `fine–find–fight`, `pan–panned–pant`.

In `-ound`, keep the nasal /n/ before /d/. Do not collapse `sound` into `sout`.

## Regular `-ed`

- `/ɪd/` after /t/ or /d/: `wanted, needed, decided`.
- `/t/` after a voiceless sound: `walked, washed, missed`.
- `/d/` after a voiced sound: `played, called, loved`.
