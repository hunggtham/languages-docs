# Lesson 04 — Rules, advice and etiquette

**Level:** B1  
**Goals:** express obligation, prohibition, choice and advice without changing the meaning.

| Function | Forms | Example |
|---|---|---|
| necessary | `must`, `have to` | `You have to book a table.` |
| forbidden | `mustn’t`, `can’t`, `not allowed to` | `You mustn’t take photos.` |
| no obligation | `don’t have to`, `don’t need to` | `You don’t have to tip.` |
| advice/expectation | `should`, `be supposed to` | `You should try the local food.` |

`Mustn’t` means “it is forbidden”; `don’t have to` means “it is optional”. They are not interchangeable.

## Etiquette vocabulary

**etiquette** = polite behaviour; **be frowned upon** = be disapproved of; **a faux pas** = an embarrassing social mistake; **savour** = enjoy slowly; **a staple** = basic everyday food; **seasonal** = available only in a particular season.

The Italian-food reading says that cappuccino after dinner is an unwritten rule, pasta is normally a first course, and eating quickly without noticing the food is the real faux pas.
