# Lesson 15 — Pronunciation foundations

**Level:** A2–B1  
**Source:** `speaking-homework.docx`.  
**Goals:** release final consonants, distinguish L/R and TH, and apply third-person `-s`.

## Practice sets

- final sounds: `wait/what`, `like/book`, `food/need`
- L/R: `light/right`, `long/wrong`, `glass/grass`
- TH: `think`, `three`, `this` (tongue lightly between the teeth)
- third-person `-s`: `I work`; `he works`; `we can swim`; `she wants to stay`.

Record once slowly and once at normal speed. The goal is to finish the word and keep the small grammar sounds audible.
