# Lesson 06 — Reported speech and indirect questions

**Level:** B1–B2  
**Goals:** report statements/questions accurately and keep indirect-question word order.

## Backshift after a past reporting verb

| Direct speech | Reported speech |
|---|---|
| present simple | past simple |
| present continuous | past continuous |
| past simple | past perfect |
| will | would |
| can | could |
| tonight/today | that night/that day |
| here | there |

Example: `She said, “I will publish it next year.”` → `She said that she would publish it the following year.`

## Say, tell and ask

- `say + (that) clause`: `He said that the theory was wrong.`
- `tell + object + (that) clause`: `She told me that lions hunt at night.`
- `ask + wh-word + subject + verb`: `He asked what I thought.`

An indirect question is not a direct question: `Do you know how many emus there were?` (not `how many emus were there`).
