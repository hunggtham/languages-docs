# Lesson 12 — The future of work

**Level:** B1–B2  
**Source:** `tham-homework.docx` and `quynh-homework.docx`.  
**Goals:** discuss employment trends and select the correct future form.

## Vocabulary

`automation`, `upskill`, `gig economy`, `burnout`, `hybrid model`, `digital nomad`, `work–life balance`, `redundant`, `remote work`.

## Future choices

- prior plan: `I’m going to apply for a remote job.`
- fixed arrangement: `I’m meeting the client on Friday.`
- evidence-based prediction: `The printer is going to run out of paper.`
- instant decision/promise: `I’ll answer it.`
- uncertain prediction: `AI might change my job.`
