# Lesson 11 — Travel vocabulary and airport English

**Level:** B1  
**Source:** travel catch-up/homework DOCX files.  
**Goals:** describe a trip, choose present perfect or past simple, and report an airport problem politely.

## Core vocabulary

`itinerary` (trip plan), `destination`, `budget`, `sightseeing`, `souvenir`, `jet lag`, `baggage`.

## Grammar reminder

Use present perfect for life experience or a result now: `I have visited six countries.` Use past simple with a finished time: `We travelled to Vietnam last year.`

## Airport phrases

`I’m afraid there’s a problem with my flight.` · `My suitcase didn’t arrive.` · `Would it be possible to change my seat?` · `Could you check whether it was left at the departure airport?` · `Could I have the delivery reference number?`
