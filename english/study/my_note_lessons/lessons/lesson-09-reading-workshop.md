# Lesson 09 — Reading workshop: history, science and narrative

**Level:** B1–B2  
**Goals:** scan for dates and causes, infer meaning, and answer evidence-based questions.

## Reading set from the export

1. **The History of the Umbrella** — parasols began as sun protection; waterproof versions developed in China; Jonas Hanway helped popularize umbrellas in Europe (`media/notion-image-015.png`).
2. **The Landlady** — Billy Weaver enters a bed-and-breakfast; details about the stuffed pets create an uneasy inference (`media/notion-image-018.png`–`media/notion-image-020.png`).
3. **Reaching for the Stars** — Sputnik (1957), Gagarin (1961), the Moon landing (1969), later probes and private space travel (`media/notion-image-041.png`–`media/notion-image-042.png`).
4. **Glass** — glass is an amorphous solid; old windows are uneven because of manufacture, not centuries of flow (`media/notion-image-047.png`–`media/notion-image-048.png`).
5. **The Emu War** — rough terrain, fast emus and a jammed gun made the operation ineffective; fencing and a bounty worked better (`media/notion-image-060.png`–`media/notion-image-063.png`).
6. **The 100-Year Mystery** — zebra stripes were tested as camouflage, cooling and social recognition; the strongest evidence supports confusing horseflies (`media/notion-image-072.png`–`media/notion-image-074.png`).
7. **The Book Nobody Can Read** — the Voynich manuscript is old and statistically language-like, but its meaning is unknown (`media/notion-image-079.png`–`media/notion-image-080.png`).
8. **The Day With Two Noons** — railways needed standard time; companies adopted zones before governments formalized them (`media/notion-image-083.png`–`media/notion-image-085.png`).

## Reading method

Read the question first, underline the date/name/number, then find the sentence that proves the answer. Separate a fact from an inference: “the tea tasted of bitter almonds” is evidence; “the landlady was dangerous” is an inference.

For an oral recap, use linking phrases from the notes: `in short`, `to recap`,
`the main point is…`.
