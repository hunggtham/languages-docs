# Lesson 19 — Embedded questions and dependent prepositions

**Level:** B1–B2  
**Source:** `21-pht-intermediate-review-worksheet.docx`.  
**Goals:** keep statement word order inside polite/indirect questions.

Direct: `Where does she live?` → Embedded: `Do you know where she lives?`

Yes/no questions use `if/whether`: `I wonder whether he passed.` Wh-questions keep the subject before the verb: `Could you tell me what time the film starts?` Do not use `where is`, `what does`, or `why did` after the introductory clause.
