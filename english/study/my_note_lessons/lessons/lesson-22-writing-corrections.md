# Lesson 22 — Corrections from your writing

**Level:** B1  
**Source:** travel, future-of-work and daily-routine homework.  
**Goals:** turn understandable drafts into natural, accurate English without changing the intended meaning.

Focus patterns: `a medium-sized suitcase`, `didn’t appear`, `I would appreciate it if…`, `my work starts`, `my dishes are getting cold`, `right now`, `I am doing my homework`, `I am going to become a manager`, `I chose to learn`, and `I hope I will not regret it`.
