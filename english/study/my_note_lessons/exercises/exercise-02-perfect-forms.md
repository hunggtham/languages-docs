# Exercise 02 — Perfect forms and deductions

Choose the best form.

1. I ___ (learn) English since I was five.
2. You are out of breath. You ___ (run).
3. She was tired because she ___ (study) for hours before the test.
4. I ___ (visit) Korea three times, but I ___ (go) there last year.
5. I’m sure they left early. They ___ (leave) before us.
6. Perhaps he forgot the meeting. He ___ (forget) the meeting.
7. It is impossible that they left by boat; the boat was not there. They ___ (leave) by boat.
8. I regret clicking the link. I ___ (check) it first.
