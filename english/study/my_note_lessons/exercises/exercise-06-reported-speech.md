# Exercise 06 — Reported speech and indirect questions

Rewrite each sentence.

1. “I will publish it next year,” she said.
2. “The theory is wrong,” he said.
3. “What did you do in the experiment?” she asked.
4. “Are all the old theories dead?” she asked.
5. Correct: `He said me that the theory was wrong.`
6. Correct: `She told that lions hunt at night.`
7. Correct: `He asked what did I think.`
8. Correct: `Do you know how many emus were there?`
