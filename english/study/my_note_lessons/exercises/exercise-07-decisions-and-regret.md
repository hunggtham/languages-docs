# Exercise 07 — Decisions and regret

1. Match: `take the plunge`, `have second thoughts`, `weigh up the pros and cons`, `play it safe` with their meanings.
2. Complete: `I ___ have gone` (past possibility).
3. Complete: `I ___ have checked first` (regret).
4. Complete: `I ___ have been a different person` (unreal result).
5. Write one sentence about a decision you made `on the spur of the moment`.
6. Rewrite with a perfect modal: `Perhaps Mai stayed because her mother was ill.`
7. Complete: `If I could become a professional in one unusual skill, I ___ become a flower arranger.`
