# Exercise 15 — Pronunciation foundations

1. Read aloud: `wait/what`, `like/book`, `food/need`.
2. Read aloud: `light/right`, `long/wrong`, `glass/grass`.
3. Read: `I think there are three things. This is my mother.`
4. Correct the verb: `He work every day`; `She want to stay`; `We can swims`.
5. Record the slow and normal versions of one sentence.
