# C2+ Vocabulary — Restoration and population dynamics

This lesson follows ecological restoration from succession and plant interactions through genetic risk, dispersal, food webs, and rewilding. The network is **seral stage → facilitation ecology → inhibition ecology → priority effect ecology → nurse plant → soil seed bank → propagule pressure → founder effect ecology → genetic rescue → outbreeding depression → inbreeding depression → population bottleneck → metapopulation rescue effect → source-sink habitat → trophic cascade restoration → mesopredator release → keystone species ecology → ecological succession trajectory → assisted migration policy → rewilding ecology**.

## 1. seral stage /ˈsɪrəl steɪdʒ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a recognizable phase in the sequence of ecological communities during succession.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** phase có thể nhận diện trong sequence community khi succession.
**Grammar & collocations:** `early seral stage` — stage sớm; `identify a seral stage` — identify.
**Examples:** `The recovering grassland had reached an early seral stage.` → Grassland phục hồi đã đạt seral stage sớm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `천이 단계`.

## 2. facilitation ecology /fəˌsɪləˈteɪʃən iˈkɑːlədʒi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the study of cases in which one organism makes conditions more suitable for another.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** study trường hợp một organism làm condition phù hợp hơn cho organism khác.
**Grammar & collocations:** `positive facilitation ecology` — ecology facilitation tích cực; `apply facilitation ecology` — apply.
**Examples:** `Facilitation ecology explained why shrubs helped tree seedlings survive.` → Facilitation ecology giải thích vì sao shrub giúp tree seedling survive.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `생태 촉진 연구`.

## 3. inhibition ecology /ˌɪnhɪˈbɪʃən iˈkɑːlədʒi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the study of cases in which established organisms prevent or slow the arrival of others.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** study khi organism established prevent/slow organism khác arrive.
**Grammar & collocations:** `competitive inhibition ecology` — ecology inhibition cạnh tranh; `model inhibition` — model.
**Examples:** `Inhibition ecology accounted for the slow recovery beneath the dense canopy.` → Inhibition ecology giải thích recovery chậm dưới canopy dày.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `생태 억제 연구`.

## 4. priority effect ecology /praɪˈɔːrəti ɪˈfɛkt iˈkɑːlədʒi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the influence of arrival order on later community composition and interactions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** ảnh hưởng của arrival order lên community composition/interaction về sau.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong priority effect ecology` — effect mạnh; `test priority effects` — test.
**Examples:** `Priority effect ecology showed that the first colonists changed later competition.` → Priority effect ecology cho thấy colonist đầu đổi competition về sau.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `선점 효과 생태학`.

## 5. nurse plant /nɜːrs plænt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a plant that protects or improves conditions for the growth of nearby seedlings.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** plant protect hoặc improve condition cho seedling gần đó grow.
**Grammar & collocations:** `native nurse plant` — plant nurse bản địa; `plant a nurse plant` — plant.
**Examples:** `The native nurse plant shaded seedlings from the hottest afternoon sun.` → Nurse plant bản địa shade seedling khỏi nắng chiều nóng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `보호 식물`.

## 6. soil seed bank /sɔɪl siːd bæŋk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** dormant seeds stored naturally in soil and available for future germination.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** seed dormant tự nhiên store trong soil, sẵn sàng germinate về sau.
**Grammar & collocations:** `native soil seed bank` — bank seed bản địa; `sample a soil seed bank` — sample.
**Examples:** `The restoration relied on the native soil seed bank after grazing stopped.` → Restoration dựa vào soil seed bank bản địa sau khi grazing stop.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `토양 종자은행`.

## 7. propagule pressure /ˈprɑːpjəɡjuːl ˈprɛʃər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the number and frequency of reproductive units introduced into a new area.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** number/frequency của reproductive unit được introduce vào area mới.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high propagule pressure` — pressure cao; `reduce propagule pressure` — reduce.
**Examples:** `High propagule pressure increased the chance that the invasive plant would establish.` → Propagule pressure cao tăng chance invasive plant establish.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `번식체 압력`.

## 8. founder effect ecology /ˈfaʊndər ɪˈfɛkt iˈkɑːlədʒi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the loss of genetic variation that can occur when a new population begins from a few individuals.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** genetic variation loss khi population mới bắt đầu từ vài individual.
**Grammar & collocations:** `severe founder effect ecology` — effect nặng; `model the founder effect` — model.
**Examples:** `Founder effect ecology explained the unusual allele frequency on the island.` → Founder effect ecology giải thích allele frequency lạ trên island.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `창시자 효과 생태학`.

## 9. genetic rescue /dʒəˈnɛtɪk ˈrɛskjuː/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** increasing a small population's genetic health by introducing individuals from another population.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** tăng genetic health của population nhỏ bằng introduce individual từ population khác.
**Grammar & collocations:** `careful genetic rescue` — rescue cẩn thận; `plan genetic rescue` — plan.
**Examples:** `Careful genetic rescue increased fertility without erasing local adaptation.` → Genetic rescue cẩn thận tăng fertility mà không xóa local adaptation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `유전적 구제`.

## 10. outbreeding depression /ˈaʊtˌbriːdɪŋ dɪˈprɛʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** reduced fitness caused by crossing individuals from genetically too-distant populations.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** fitness giảm do cross individual từ population genetic quá xa.
**Grammar & collocations:** `risk of outbreeding depression` — risk; `monitor outbreeding depression` — monitor.
**Examples:** `The plan monitored for outbreeding depression after the translocation.` → Plan monitor outbreeding depression sau translocation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `이교배 약세`.

## 11. inbreeding depression /ˈɪnˌbriːdɪŋ dɪˈprɛʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** reduced survival or reproduction caused by mating among close relatives.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** survival/reproduction giảm do mating giữa close relative.
**Grammar & collocations:** `severe inbreeding depression` — depression nặng; `avoid inbreeding depression` — avoid.
**Examples:** `The isolated herd showed signs of inbreeding depression.` → Herd isolated có dấu hiệu inbreeding depression.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `근친교배 약세`.

## 12. population bottleneck /ˌpɑːpjəˈleɪʃən ˈbɑːtəlnek/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a sharp temporary reduction in population size that removes genetic variation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** population size giảm sharp tạm thời, làm mất genetic variation.
**Grammar & collocations:** `severe population bottleneck` — bottleneck nặng; `survive a bottleneck` — survive.
**Examples:** `The species survived a severe population bottleneck after the epidemic.` → Species survive population bottleneck nặng sau epidemic.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `개체군 병목`.

## 13. metapopulation rescue effect /ˌmɛtəpɑːpjəˈleɪʃən ˈrɛskjuː ɪˈfɛkt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the reduction of local extinction risk when individuals repeatedly arrive from other habitat patches.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** local extinction risk giảm khi individual từ habitat patch khác arrive lặp lại.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong metapopulation rescue effect` — effect mạnh; `measure a rescue effect` — measure.
**Examples:** `The corridor created a strong metapopulation rescue effect.` → Corridor tạo metapopulation rescue effect mạnh.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `메타개체군 구제 효과`.

## 14. source-sink habitat /sɔːrs sɪŋk ˈhæbɪtæt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a landscape containing productive patches that export individuals and poorer patches that require immigrants to persist.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** landscape có patch productive export individual và patch kém cần immigrant để persist.
**Grammar & collocations:** `connected source-sink habitat` — habitat source-sink connected; `map source-sink habitat` — map.
**Examples:** `The reserve protected source-sink habitat across the floodplain.` → Reserve protect source-sink habitat trên floodplain.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `공급원-싱크 서식지`.

## 15. trophic cascade restoration /ˈtroʊfɪk kæˈskeɪd ˌrɛstəˈreɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** restoring a food-web interaction so changes at one trophic level affect several lower levels.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** restore food-web interaction để change ở một trophic level ảnh hưởng nhiều level dưới.
**Grammar & collocations:** `trophic cascade restoration project` — project; `test trophic cascade restoration` — test.
**Examples:** `Trophic cascade restoration began when wolves returned to the valley.` → Trophic cascade restoration bắt đầu khi wolf trở lại valley.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `영양 단계 연쇄 복원`.

## 16. mesopredator release /ˌmɛsoʊˈprɛdətər rɪˈliːs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an increase in medium-sized predators after larger predators decline or disappear.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** medium predator tăng sau khi large predator giảm hoặc biến mất.
**Grammar & collocations:** `mesopredator release effect` — effect; `prevent mesopredator release` — prevent.
**Examples:** `Mesopredator release increased pressure on ground-nesting birds.` → Mesopredator release tăng pressure lên bird ground-nesting.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `중형 포식자 방출`.

## 17. keystone species ecology /ˈkiːstoʊn ˈspiːʃiːz iˈkɑːlədʒi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the study of species whose ecological effects are much larger than their abundance suggests.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** study species có ecological effect lớn hơn nhiều so với abundance.
**Grammar & collocations:** `keystone species ecology indicator` — indicator; `apply keystone species ecology` — apply.
**Examples:** `Keystone species ecology guided the decision to protect the beaver colony.` → Keystone species ecology guide decision protect beaver colony.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `핵심종 생태학`.

## 18. ecological succession trajectory /ˌiːkəˈlɑːdʒɪkəl səkˈsɛʃən trəˈdʒɛktəri/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the expected direction and sequence of community change through time.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** direction/sequence expected của community change theo time.
**Grammar & collocations:** `divergent succession trajectory` — trajectory khác hướng; `project a trajectory` — project.
**Examples:** `Invasive grass diverted the ecological succession trajectory away from forest.` → Invasive grass divert trajectory succession khỏi forest.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `생태 천이 궤적`.

## 19. assisted migration policy /əˈsɪstɪd maɪˈɡreɪʃən ˈpɑːləsi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a policy for deliberately moving species or populations to areas expected to remain suitable under environmental change.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** policy move species/population có chủ ý tới area dự kiến còn suitable khi environment change.
**Grammar & collocations:** `cautious assisted migration policy` — policy thận trọng; `debate assisted migration policy` — debate.
**Examples:** `The cautious assisted migration policy required disease screening and monitoring.` → Assisted migration policy thận trọng cần disease screening và monitoring.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `보조 이주 정책`.

## 20. rewilding ecology /ˌriːˈwaɪldɪŋ iˈkɑːlədʒi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the study and practice of restoring self-sustaining ecological processes and wild species interactions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** study/practice restore ecological process tự sustain và wild species interaction.
**Grammar & collocations:** `large-scale rewilding ecology` — ecology rewilding quy mô lớn; `evaluate rewilding` — evaluate.
**Examples:** `Rewilding ecology favored natural grazing over constant mechanical mowing.` → Rewilding ecology favor grazing tự nhiên thay mowing mechanical liên tục.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `재야생화 생태학`.

## Review in context

Restoration tracks an **ecological succession trajectory** through each **seral stage**, asking whether **facilitation ecology**, **inhibition ecology**, or a **priority effect ecology** shapes recovery. A **nurse plant** and **soil seed bank** can help, but **propagule pressure** and a **founder effect ecology** influence establishment. Managers consider **genetic rescue**, **outbreeding depression**, **inbreeding depression**, and a **population bottleneck**. Connected patches may show a **metapopulation rescue effect** and **source-sink habitat** dynamics. Food-web work includes **trophic cascade restoration**, **mesopredator release**, and **keystone species ecology**, while an **assisted migration policy** or **rewilding ecology** defines long-term intervention.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:** Restoration theo dõi **ecological succession trajectory** qua từng **seral stage**, hỏi **facilitation ecology**, **inhibition ecology** hay **priority effect ecology** định hình recovery. **Nurse plant** và **soil seed bank** có thể giúp, nhưng **propagule pressure** và **founder effect ecology** ảnh hưởng establishment. Manager cân nhắc **genetic rescue**, **outbreeding depression**, **inbreeding depression** và **population bottleneck**. Patch kết nối có thể cho thấy **metapopulation rescue effect** và dynamics **source-sink habitat**. Food-web work gồm **trophic cascade restoration**, **mesopredator release** và **keystone species ecology**, còn **assisted migration policy** hoặc **rewilding ecology** xác định intervention dài hạn.
