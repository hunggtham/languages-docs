# C1 Vocabulary 18 — Behavioral food, health, and nutrition choices

This lesson follows how people interpret food, symptoms, treatment options, and preventive advice when habits, identity, and limited attention interact.

Flow: **portion perception → health halo heuristic → menu salience → calorie neglect → taste expectation → food identity cue → healthy default → grocery temptation → present-biased eating → satiety belief → labeling credibility → nutrition numeracy → risk compensation health → adherence friction → treatment preference → preventive action gap → symptom interpretation bias → medical waiting aversion → caregiving burden perception → population health framing**.

## 1. portion perception /ˈpɔrʃən pərˈsɛpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a person's subjective judgment about how large or satisfying a serving of food appears.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** judgment subjective về serving food trông lớn hoặc satisfying thế nào.
**Grammar & collocations:** `distorted portion perception` — perception; `change portion perception` — change.
**Examples:** `Portion perception changed when the same meal was served on a smaller plate.` → Perception portion đổi khi meal giống nhau được serve trên plate nhỏ hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `분량 인식`.

## 2. health halo heuristic /hɛlθ ˈheɪloʊ hjʊrɪstɪk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a quick judgment that a food or product is healthy overall because one feature appears beneficial.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** judgment nhanh food hoặc product healthy overall vì một feature có vẻ beneficial.
**Grammar & collocations:** `misleading health halo heuristic` — heuristic; `activate a health halo heuristic` — activate.
**Examples:** `A health halo heuristic made shoppers underestimate the sugar in a cereal labeled natural.` → Heuristic halo health khiến shopper underestimate sugar trong cereal label natural.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `건강 후광 휴리스틱`.

## 3. menu salience /ˈmɛnjuː ˈseɪliəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** how noticeable and mentally prominent a menu item is when someone chooses what to eat.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mức menu item noticeable và prominent khi chọn món ăn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high menu salience` — salience; `increase menu salience` — increase.
**Examples:** `Menu salience made the brightly described dessert seem central to the meal.` → Salience menu khiến dessert được describe bright trông central với meal.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `메뉴 현저성`.

## 4. calorie neglect /ˈkæləri nəˈɡlɛkt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a tendency to overlook or underweight calorie information when making a food choice.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** tendency overlook hoặc underweight information calorie khi choose food.
**Grammar & collocations:** `persistent calorie neglect` — neglect; `reduce calorie neglect` — reduce.
**Examples:** `Calorie neglect increased when the label used unfamiliar serving units.` → Neglect calorie tăng khi label dùng unit serving unfamiliar.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `칼로리 무시`.

## 5. taste expectation /teɪst ˌɛkspɛkˈteɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a belief about how food will taste before it is eaten, shaped by description, appearance, and prior experience.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** belief food taste thế nào trước khi eat, do description, appearance và experience.
**Grammar & collocations:** `positive taste expectation` — expectation; `shape taste expectations` — shape.
**Examples:** `Taste expectation improved after the menu used familiar names for the unfamiliar grain.` → Expectation taste improve sau khi menu dùng name quen cho grain unfamiliar.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `맛 기대`.

## 6. food identity cue /fuːd aɪˈdɛntəti kjuː/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a signal that connects a food choice with a person's values, culture, group, or desired self-image.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** signal connect food choice với value, culture, group hoặc self-image mong muốn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong food identity cue` — cue; `use a food identity cue` — use.
**Examples:** `A food identity cue made the local dish feel like participation in community life.` → Cue identity food khiến local dish trông như participation vào community life.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `음식 정체성 단서`.

## 7. healthy default /ˈhɛlθi dɪˈfɔlt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a preselected food or health option that makes the beneficial choice easiest unless someone changes it.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** option food hoặc health beneficial được preselect và dễ nhất nếu không change.
**Grammar & collocations:** `supportive healthy default` — default; `set a healthy default` — set.
**Examples:** `A healthy default placed fruit beside the register and required an extra step for sugary drinks.` → Default healthy đặt fruit cạnh register và cần thêm step để lấy drink sugary.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `건강한 기본값`.

## 8. grocery temptation /ˈɡroʊsəri tɛm(p)ˈteɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a shopping cue or situation that makes an immediately attractive food harder to resist.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cue hoặc situation shopping khiến food attractive ngay khó resist.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong grocery temptation` — temptation; `manage grocery temptation` — manage.
**Examples:** `Grocery temptation rose when small sweets were displayed beside the checkout.` → Temptation grocery tăng khi sweet nhỏ display cạnh checkout.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `식료품 유혹`.

## 9. present-biased eating /ˈprɛzənt baɪəst ˈiːtɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** choosing immediate taste or convenience over delayed health or energy benefits.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** chọn taste hoặc convenience immediate hơn health hoặc energy benefit delay.
**Grammar & collocations:** `habitual present-biased eating` — eating; `limit present-biased eating` — limit.
**Examples:** `Present-biased eating made the worker choose a fast snack before a long afternoon meeting.` → Eating present-biased khiến worker chọn snack fast trước meeting afternoon dài.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `현재 편향적 식사`.

## 10. satiety belief /səˈtaɪəti bɪˈliːf/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a belief about how full or satisfied a food will make someone feel.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** belief food sẽ make người ta full hoặc satisfied thế nào.
**Grammar & collocations:** `accurate satiety belief` — belief; `update a satiety belief` — update.
**Examples:** `The satiety belief was wrong because the small portion contained little protein.` → Belief satiety sai vì portion nhỏ có ít protein.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `포만감 신념`.

## 11. labeling credibility /ˈleɪbəlɪŋ krɛdəˈbɪləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the perceived trustworthiness of information printed on a food, medicine, or health product label.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** trustworthiness được perceive của information trên label food, medicine hoặc health product.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high labeling credibility` — credibility; `protect labeling credibility` — protect.
**Examples:** `Labeling credibility declined after several products used vague claims without measurable definitions.` → Credibility labeling giảm sau khi product dùng claim vague không có definition measurable.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `표시 신뢰성`.

## 12. nutrition numeracy /nuˈtrɪʃən ˈnuːmərəsi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the ability to understand and use numbers in nutrition information and health decisions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** ability hiểu và use number trong nutrition information và health decision.
**Grammar & collocations:** `limited nutrition numeracy` — numeracy; `improve nutrition numeracy` — improve.
**Examples:** `Limited nutrition numeracy made the daily percentage seem smaller than the actual serving total.` → Numeracy nutrition hạn chế khiến percentage daily trông nhỏ hơn serving total thật.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `영양 수리력`.

## 13. risk compensation health /rɪsk ˌkɑmpənˈseɪʃən hɛlθ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an increase in risky behavior after a person adopts a measure believed to make them safer.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** risky behavior tăng sau khi adopt measure được tin là làm safe hơn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `possible risk compensation health effect` — effect; `monitor risk compensation health` — monitor.
**Examples:** `Risk compensation health appeared when drivers sped up after receiving better protective equipment.` → Effect compensation risk health xuất hiện khi driver speed up sau khi nhận equipment bảo vệ tốt hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `건강 위험 보상`.

## 14. adherence friction /ədˈhɪrəns ˈfrɪkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** practical effort, confusion, or inconvenience that makes it harder to follow a treatment or health routine.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** effort, confusion hoặc inconvenience làm khó follow treatment hay routine health.
**Grammar & collocations:** `avoidable adherence friction` — friction; `remove adherence friction` — remove.
**Examples:** `Adherence friction increased when the medicine required an inconvenient meal schedule.` → Friction adherence tăng khi medicine cần schedule meal bất tiện.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `복약 순응 마찰`.

## 15. treatment preference /ˈtriːtmənt ˈprɛfərəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a person's relative liking for one medical treatment, delivery method, or care plan over another.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** liking tương đối cho treatment, delivery method hoặc care plan này hơn option khác.
**Grammar & collocations:** `informed treatment preference` — preference; `elicit treatment preferences` — elicit.
**Examples:** `Treatment preference favored a shorter course even though its daily side effects were stronger.` → Preference treatment favor course ngắn dù side effect daily mạnh hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `치료 선호`.

## 16. preventive action gap /prɪˈvɛntɪv ˈækʃən ɡæp/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the difference between knowing that prevention is useful and actually taking the preventive step.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** gap giữa biết prevention useful và thật sự take preventive step.
**Grammar & collocations:** `large preventive action gap` — gap; `close the preventive action gap` — close.
**Examples:** `A preventive action gap remained even after residents understood the value of screening.` → Gap action preventive còn lại dù resident hiểu value screening.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `예방 행동 격차`.

## 17. symptom interpretation bias /ˈsɪmptəm ɪnˌtɝprəˈteɪʃən baɪəs/
**Part of speech:** noun

**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** tendency explain body sign serious hơn hoặc nhẹ hơn evidence support.
**Grammar & collocations:** `anxious symptom interpretation bias` — bias; `correct symptom interpretation bias` — correct.

## 18. medical waiting aversion /ˈmɛdɪkəl ˈweɪtɪŋ əˈvɝʒən/

**Core meaning (English):** a tendency to avoid care because waiting for an appointment or result feels especially costly.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** tendency avoid care vì chờ appointment hoặc result cảm thấy costly.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong medical waiting aversion` — aversion; `reduce medical waiting aversion` — reduce.

## 19. caregiving burden perception /ˈkɛrˌɡɪvɪŋ ˈbɝdən pərˈsɛpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a person's judgment about how heavy, disruptive, or unfair caring for another person will be.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** judgment care cho người khác heavy, disruptive hoặc unfair thế nào.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high caregiving burden perception` — perception; `change caregiving burden perception` — change.

## 20. population health framing /ˌpɑpjəˈleɪʃən hɛlθ ˈfreɪmɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the way a health issue is presented as affecting a whole population rather than only individual patients.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cách present health issue ảnh hưởng population tổng thể thay vì chỉ individual patient.
**Grammar & collocations:** `equity-focused population health framing` — framing; `use population health framing` — use.
**Examples:** `Population health framing shifted attention from individual blame to safer food environments.` → Framing health population chuyển attention từ blame cá nhân sang food environment safe hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `인구 건강 프레이밍`.

## Review in context

Food choices begin with **portion perception**, not only with measured quantity. A **health halo heuristic** and high **menu salience** can make a product appear better, while **calorie neglect** hides relevant information. **Taste expectation** and a **food identity cue** influence whether a dish feels attractive, and a **healthy default** can make the better option convenient. Yet **grocery temptation** and **present-biased eating** pull attention toward immediate reward. A person's **satiety belief** may be inaccurate, especially when **labeling credibility** is weak or **nutrition numeracy** is limited. A safety intervention can produce **risk compensation health**, and **adherence friction** can undermine a treatment that people otherwise prefer. **Treatment preference** does not always close the **preventive action gap**; **symptom interpretation bias** and **medical waiting aversion** can delay care. Families also judge **caregiving burden perception** before accepting support, while **population health framing** can move discussion from individual blame toward healthier environments.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

Choice food bắt đầu từ **portion perception**, không chỉ từ quantity đo được. **Health halo heuristic** và **menu salience** cao khiến product trông tốt hơn, còn **calorie neglect** che information relevant. **Taste expectation** và **food identity cue** influence dish có attractive không, còn **healthy default** làm option tốt thuận tiện hơn. Tuy vậy **grocery temptation** và **present-biased eating** kéo attention về reward immediate. **Satiety belief** có thể sai, đặc biệt khi **labeling credibility** yếu hoặc **nutrition numeracy** hạn chế. Safety intervention có thể tạo **risk compensation health**, còn **adherence friction** làm yếu treatment dù người ta otherwise prefer. **Treatment preference** không luôn close **preventive action gap**; **symptom interpretation bias** và **medical waiting aversion** có thể delay care. Family cũng judge **caregiving burden perception** trước khi accept support, trong khi **population health framing** chuyển discussion từ blame cá nhân sang environment healthy hơn.
