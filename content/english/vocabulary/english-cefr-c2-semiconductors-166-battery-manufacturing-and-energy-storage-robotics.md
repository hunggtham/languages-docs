# C2 Vocabulary — Battery manufacturing and energy-storage robotics

This lesson follows a battery from electrode preparation and cell assembly to pack testing, recycling, and second-life deployment. The network is **battery manufacturing robot → cell assembly robot → electrode coating robot → battery formation tester → module stacking robot → pack welding robot → electrolyte filling robot → dry-room transfer robot → battery inspection robot → thermal runaway detection → battery recycling robot → cathode mixing robot → anode slurry robot → separator handling robot → state-of-charge calibration → battery pack leak testing → cell grading robot → module balancing system → second-life battery sorting → energy storage safety robot**.

## 1. battery manufacturing robot /ˈbætəri ˌmænjəˈfæktʃərɪŋ ˈroʊbɑːt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a robot used to make, handle, inspect, or package rechargeable batteries.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** robot dùng để make, handle, inspect hoặc package rechargeable battery.
**Grammar & collocations:** `battery-manufacturing-robot cell` — cell; `handle a fragile electrode` — handle electrode dễ hỏng.
**Examples:** `The battery manufacturing robot moved cells between dry-room stations.` → Battery manufacturing robot chuyển cell giữa các station trong dry room.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `배터리 제조 로봇`.

## 2. cell assembly robot /sel əˈsembli ˈroʊbɑːt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a robot that places, aligns, and joins the components of an individual battery cell.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** robot place, align và join component của một battery cell.
**Grammar & collocations:** `cell-assembly-robot gripper` — gripper; `align a jelly roll` — align jelly roll.
**Examples:** `The cell assembly robot aligned the tabs before sealing the pouch.` → Cell assembly robot align tab trước khi seal pouch.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `셀 조립 로봇`.

## 3. electrode coating robot /ɪˈlektroʊd ˈkoʊtɪŋ ˈroʊbɑːt/

**Core meaning (English):** a robot that applies an active material evenly to a battery electrode.

**Grammar & collocations:** `electrode-coating-robot head` — head; `control a wet thickness` — control wet thickness.
**Examples:** `The electrode coating robot maintained a uniform layer across the foil.` → Electrode coating robot giữ layer đều trên foil.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `전극 코팅 로봇`.

## 4. battery formation tester /ˈbætəri fɔːrˈmeɪʃən ˈtestər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a system that charges and discharges new cells in a controlled sequence to establish their initial performance.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** system charge/discharge cell mới theo sequence control để establish initial performance.
**Grammar & collocations:** `battery-formation-tester channel` — channel; `run a first charge cycle` — run first charge cycle.
**Examples:** `The battery formation tester recorded a stable voltage curve.` → Battery formation tester record voltage curve ổn định.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `배터리 포메이션 테스터`.

## 10. thermal runaway detection /ˈθɜːrməl ˈrʌnəweɪ dɪˈtekʃən/

**Core meaning (English):** detecting the accelerating heat release that can cause a battery to fail violently.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** detect heat release tăng tốc có thể khiến battery fail nguy hiểm.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `열 폭주 감지`.

## 19. second-life battery sorting /ˈsekənd laɪf ˈbætəri ˈsɔːrtɪŋ/

**Core meaning (English):** classifying used batteries for reuse in a less demanding application instead of immediate recycling.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `배터리 2차 사용 분류`.

## Review in context

A **battery manufacturing robot** feeds a **cell assembly robot**, **electrode coating robot**, and **battery formation tester**. A **module stacking robot**, **pack welding robot**, **electrolyte filling robot**, and **dry-room transfer robot** build and protect the pack, while a **battery inspection robot** and **thermal runaway detection** check risk. A **battery recycling robot**, **cathode mixing robot**, **anode slurry robot**, and **separator handling robot** connect recovery with new production. **State-of-charge calibration**, **battery pack leak testing**, and a **cell grading robot** establish quality; a **module balancing system**, **second-life battery sorting**, and an **energy storage safety robot** extend safe use.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:** **Battery manufacturing robot** feed **cell assembly robot**, **electrode coating robot** và **battery formation tester**. **Module stacking robot**, **pack welding robot**, **electrolyte filling robot** và **dry-room transfer robot** build/bảo vệ pack, còn **battery inspection robot** và **thermal runaway detection** check risk. **Battery recycling robot**, **cathode mixing robot**, **anode slurry robot** và **separator handling robot** nối recovery với production mới. **State-of-charge calibration**, **battery pack leak testing** và **cell grading robot** thiết lập quality; **module balancing system**, **second-life battery sorting** và **energy storage safety robot** kéo dài việc dùng an toàn.
