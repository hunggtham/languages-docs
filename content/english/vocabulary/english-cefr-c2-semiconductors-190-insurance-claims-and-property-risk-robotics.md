# C2 Vocabulary — Insurance claims and property-risk robotics

This lesson follows an insurance claim from document intake and damage capture to triage, estimation, payment, and recovery coordination. The network is **insurance damage inspection drone → claims document intake robot → property survey robot → roof claims inspection drone → vehicle damage imaging robot → fraud evidence review robot → policy document extraction robot → claims triage assistant robot → repair estimate robot → disaster photo geolocation robot → underwriting property scan robot → risk engineering inspection robot → catastrophe mapping drone → water damage sensor robot → fire damage assessment robot → contents inventory robot → claim payment verification robot → adjuster field robot → insurance correspondence robot → recovery contractor coordination robot**.

## 1. insurance damage inspection drone /ɪnˈʃʊrəns ˈdæmɪdʒ ɪnˈspekʃən droʊn/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a drone that records property damage for an insurance claim.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** drone record property damage cho insurance claim.
**Grammar & collocations:** `insurance-damage-inspection-drone camera` — camera; `survey a damaged roof` — survey roof hỏng.
**Examples:** `The insurance damage inspection drone surveyed the damaged roof after the storm.` → Drone inspection insurance damage survey roof hỏng sau storm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `보험 피해 검사 드론`.

## Review in context

An **insurance damage inspection drone**, **claims document intake robot**, **property survey robot**, and **roof claims inspection drone** capture a claim, while a **vehicle damage imaging robot**, **fraud evidence review robot**, and **policy document extraction robot** support review. A **claims triage assistant robot**, **repair estimate robot**, and **disaster photo geolocation robot** prioritize and explain damage. An **underwriting property scan robot**, **risk engineering inspection robot**, **catastrophe mapping drone**, **water damage sensor robot**, **fire damage assessment robot**, and **contents inventory robot** connect risk with evidence. A **claim payment verification robot**, **adjuster field robot**, **insurance correspondence robot**, and **recovery contractor coordination robot** carry the claim through payment and recovery.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:** **Insurance damage inspection drone**, **claims document intake robot**, **property survey robot** và **roof claims inspection drone** capture claim, còn **vehicle damage imaging robot**, **fraud evidence review robot** và **policy document extraction robot** hỗ trợ review. **Claims triage assistant robot**, **repair estimate robot** và **disaster photo geolocation robot** ưu tiên/giải thích damage. **Underwriting property scan robot**, **risk engineering inspection robot**, **catastrophe mapping drone**, **water damage sensor robot**, **fire damage assessment robot** và **contents inventory robot** nối risk với evidence. **Claim payment verification robot**, **adjuster field robot**, **insurance correspondence robot** và **recovery contractor coordination robot** đưa claim qua payment/recovery.
