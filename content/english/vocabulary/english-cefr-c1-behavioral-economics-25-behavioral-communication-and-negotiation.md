# C1 Vocabulary 25 — Behavioral communication and negotiation

This lesson follows how people interpret messages, disagreement, concessions, and repair when conversations involve uncertainty and competing interests.

Flow: **conversation repair → listening effort → misunderstanding probability → politeness norm → directness preference → silence interpretation → turn-taking cue → speaker confidence signal → trust calibration dialogue → apology acceptance → disagreement cost perception → negotiation anchor → concession reciprocity → bargaining patience → fairness offer perception → deadline threat effect → conflict avoidance motive → mediation trust → face-saving strategy → communication overload**.

## 1. conversation repair /ˌkɑnvərˈseɪʃən rɪˈpɛr/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an attempt to correct misunderstanding, restore cooperation, or reopen a conversation after a problem.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** attempt correct misunderstanding, restore cooperation hoặc reopen conversation sau problem.
**Grammar & collocations:** `timely conversation repair` — repair; `initiate conversation repair` — initiate.
**Examples:** `Conversation repair began when the manager restated the concern without blaming the analyst.` → Repair conversation bắt đầu khi manager restate concern không blame analyst.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `대화 복구`.

## 2. listening effort /ˈlɪsənɪŋ ˈɛfərt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the mental attention and work required to understand another person's words, intentions, and context.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** attention mental và work cần để hiểu word, intention và context người khác.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high listening effort` — effort; `reduce listening effort` — reduce.
**Examples:** `Listening effort increased when the speaker used unfamiliar technical terms without examples.` → Effort listening tăng khi speaker dùng term technical unfamiliar không có example.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `듣기 노력`.

## 3. misunderstanding probability /ˌmɪsʌndərˈstændɪŋ ˌprɑbəˈbɪləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an estimate of how likely a message is to be interpreted differently from what the speaker intended.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** estimate message likely bị interpret khác intention speaker bao nhiêu.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high misunderstanding probability` — probability; `lower misunderstanding probability` — lower.
**Examples:** `Misunderstanding probability was high because the deadline was described as flexible to one team and fixed to another.` → Probability misunderstanding cao vì deadline được describe flexible với team này và fixed với team kia.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `오해 확률`.

## 4. politeness norm /pəˈlaɪtnəs nɔrm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a shared expectation about how respect, disagreement, requests, and refusals should be expressed.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** expectation shared về cách express respect, disagreement, request và refusal.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strict politeness norm` — norm; `violate a politeness norm` — violate.
**Examples:** `The politeness norm made the reviewer soften a necessary criticism with a specific suggestion.` → Norm politeness khiến reviewer soften criticism cần thiết bằng suggestion specific.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `예의 규범`.

## 5. directness preference /dɪˈrɛktnəs ˈprɛfərəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a relative preference for expressing requests and disagreement explicitly rather than indirectly.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** preference express request và disagreement explicit thay vì indirect.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong directness preference` — preference; `respect directness preferences` — respect.
**Examples:** `Directness preference differed between the teams, so a brief message sounded efficient to one and rude to the other.` → Preference directness khác giữa team khiến message ngắn trông efficient với team này nhưng rude với team kia.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `직접성 선호`.

## 6. silence interpretation /ˈsaɪləns ɪnˌtɝprəˈteɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the meaning a listener assigns to someone not speaking, such as agreement, confusion, respect, or resistance.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** meaning listener assign cho silence như agreement, confusion, respect hoặc resistance.
**Grammar & collocations:** `ambiguous silence interpretation` — interpretation; `check silence interpretation` — check.
**Examples:** `Silence interpretation caused the chair to mistake uncertainty for support.` → Interpretation silence khiến chair nhầm uncertainty với support.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `침묵 해석`.

## 7. turn-taking cue /ˈtɝn ˌteɪkɪŋ kjuː/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a verbal or nonverbal signal indicating when another person may begin, continue, or finish speaking.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** signal verbal hoặc nonverbal cho biết người khác begin, continue hay finish speaking.
**Grammar & collocations:** `subtle turn-taking cue` — cue; `miss a turn-taking cue` — miss.
**Examples:** `A missed turn-taking cue made two participants speak at once and lose the question.` → Cue turn-taking bị miss khiến hai participant nói cùng lúc và mất question.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `발화 순서 단서`.

## 8. speaker confidence signal /ˈspiːkər ˈkɑnfədəns ˈsɪɡnəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a feature of speech or delivery that leads listeners to infer competence or certainty.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** feature speech hoặc delivery khiến listener infer competence hoặc certainty.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong speaker confidence signal` — signal; `interpret a speaker confidence signal` — interpret.
**Examples:** `A speaker confidence signal made the estimate sound more certain than the supporting data justified.` → Signal confidence speaker làm estimate nghe chắc hơn data support justify.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `화자 자신감 신호`.

## 9. trust calibration dialogue /trʌst ˌkæləˈbreɪʃən ˈdaɪəˌlɔɡ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a conversation that adjusts how much confidence people should place in one another's knowledge, promises, or tools.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** conversation adjust mức confidence nên place vào knowledge, promise hoặc tool của nhau.
**Grammar & collocations:** `honest trust calibration dialogue` — dialogue; `hold a trust calibration dialogue` — hold.
**Examples:** `The trust calibration dialogue clarified which predictions were tested and which remained provisional.` → Dialogue calibration trust clarify prediction nào test và prediction nào provisional.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `신뢰 조정 대화`.

## 10. apology acceptance /əˈpɑlədʒi əkˈsɛptəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the extent to which a person believes an apology is sincere, accountable, and connected to repair.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mức person tin apology sincere, accountable và gắn với repair.
**Grammar & collocations:** `conditional apology acceptance` — acceptance; `increase apology acceptance` — increase.
**Examples:** `Apology acceptance improved after the organization named the harm and changed the procedure.` → Acceptance apology improve sau khi organization name harm và change procedure.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `사과 수용`.

## 11. disagreement cost perception /ˌdɪsəˈɡriːmənt kɔst pərˈsɛpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a judgment about the social, emotional, or career cost of expressing a different view.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** judgment social, emotional hoặc career cost khi express view different.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high disagreement cost perception` — perception; `lower disagreement cost perception` — lower.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `불일치 비용 인식`.

## 12. negotiation anchor /nɪˌɡoʊʃiˈeɪʃən ˈæŋkər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an initial number, proposal, or reference point that influences later judgments in a negotiation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** number, proposal hoặc reference point đầu influence judgment sau trong negotiation.
**Grammar & collocations:** `ambitious negotiation anchor` — anchor; `set a negotiation anchor` — set.
**Examples:** `The first negotiation anchor made later offers seem generous even though the final price was unchanged.` → Anchor negotiation đầu khiến offer sau trông generous dù final price không đổi.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `협상 기준점`.

## 13. concession reciprocity /kənˈsɛʃən ˌrɛsəˈprɑsəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the tendency to return a concession after the other side has given ground.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** tendency return concession sau khi bên kia give ground.
**Grammar & collocations:** `expected concession reciprocity` — reciprocity; `use concession reciprocity` — use.
**Examples:** `Concession reciprocity helped the parties exchange delivery time for a simpler reporting requirement.` → Reciprocity concession giúp hai bên đổi delivery time lấy reporting requirement đơn giản hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `양보 상호성`.

## 14. bargaining patience /ˈbɑrɡənɪŋ ˈpeɪʃəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** willingness to wait, gather information, or reject a poor offer instead of settling immediately.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** willingness wait, gather information hoặc reject offer poor thay vì settle ngay.
**Grammar & collocations:** `limited bargaining patience` — patience; `maintain bargaining patience` — maintain.
**Examples:** `Bargaining patience weakened when the supplier knew the buyer's deadline was near.` → Patience bargaining yếu khi supplier biết deadline buyer gần.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `협상 인내`.

## 15. fairness offer perception /ˈfɛrnəs ˈɔfər pərˈsɛpʃən/

## 16. deadline threat effect /ˈdɛdˌlaɪn θrɛt ɪˈfɛkt/

**Core meaning (English):** a change in judgment or behavior caused by the perceived threat of losing an opportunity when time expires.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** change judgment hoặc behavior do threat mất opportunity khi time hết.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong deadline threat effect` — effect; `measure the deadline threat effect` — measure.

## 17. conflict avoidance motive /ˈkɑnflɪkt əˈvɔɪdəns ˈmoʊtɪv/

**Core meaning (English):** a reason to choose silence, compromise, or withdrawal in order to prevent open disagreement.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** reason choose silence, compromise hoặc withdrawal để prevent disagreement open.

## 18. mediation trust /miːdiˈeɪʃən trʌst/

**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** confidence mediator neutral, competent và committed process fair.

## 19. face-saving strategy /ˈfeɪs ˌseɪvɪŋ ˈstrætədʒi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a way of responding that protects a person's dignity, status, or social relationship while allowing change.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cách response bảo vệ dignity, status hoặc relationship khi vẫn cho phép change.
**Grammar & collocations:** `subtle face-saving strategy` — strategy; `use a face-saving strategy` — use.
**Examples:** `The face-saving strategy let the director revise the forecast without announcing a humiliating reversal.` → Strategy saving face cho director revise forecast không announce reversal humiliating.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `체면 유지 전략`.

## 20. communication overload /kəˌmjuːnəˈkeɪʃən ˈoʊvərˌloʊd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a situation in which the amount, speed, or variety of messages exceeds people's ability to process them well.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** amount, speed hoặc variety message vượt ability process tốt.
**Grammar & collocations:** `severe communication overload` — overload; `reduce communication overload` — reduce.
**Examples:** `Communication overload buried the one update that changed the project's legal requirement.` → Overload communication bury update duy nhất change legal requirement project.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `의사소통 과부하`.

## Review in context

Good negotiation often begins with **conversation repair** and enough **listening effort** to reduce **misunderstanding probability**. A shared **politeness norm** may conflict with a person's **directness preference**, and **silence interpretation** can be wrong without a clear **turn-taking cue**. A confident delivery is only a **speaker confidence signal**, so a **trust calibration dialogue** should clarify evidence and limits. **Apology acceptance** depends on repair, while **disagreement cost perception** determines who speaks first. A **negotiation anchor** shapes the range, **concession reciprocity** encourages movement, and **bargaining patience** prevents panic. **Fairness offer perception** matters even when the numbers are equal; a **deadline threat effect** can make an unfair clause look acceptable. A **conflict avoidance motive** may delay resolution, but **mediation trust** makes a neutral process possible. A **face-saving strategy** can preserve dignity, while reducing **communication overload** keeps the agreement visible.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

Negotiation tốt thường bắt đầu bằng **conversation repair** và đủ **listening effort** để giảm **misunderstanding probability**. **Politeness norm** shared có thể conflict với **directness preference**, còn **silence interpretation** có thể sai nếu thiếu **turn-taking cue** rõ. Delivery tự tin chỉ là **speaker confidence signal**, nên **trust calibration dialogue** cần clarify evidence và limit. **Apology acceptance** phụ thuộc repair, còn **disagreement cost perception** quyết định ai speak trước. **Negotiation anchor** shape range, **concession reciprocity** encourage movement, và **bargaining patience** ngăn panic. **Fairness offer perception** quan trọng dù number equal; **deadline threat effect** có thể làm clause unfair trông acceptable. **Conflict avoidance motive** delay resolution, nhưng **mediation trust** làm process neutral possible. **Face-saving strategy** preserve dignity, còn giảm **communication overload** giữ agreement visible.
