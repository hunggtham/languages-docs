# C2+ Vocabulary — Complexity, systems, and emergence

This lesson follows how interacting systems organize, destabilize, and transform across scales. The network is **complex adaptive system → self-organization → critical transition → critical threshold → attractor basin → bifurcation point → historical lock-in → path-dependent hysteresis → tipping element → cascade failure → network centrality → community modularity → small-world topology → heavy-tailed network → multilayer network → percolation threshold → robustness margin → resilience co-benefit → early-warning signal → critical slowing down → state-regime transition → multistability → coevolutionary feedback → adaptive response capacity → nested hierarchy**.

## 1. complex adaptive system /kəmˈplɛks əˈdæptɪv ˈsɪstəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a system of interacting agents that changes its behavior through adaptation, learning, selection, or feedback.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** complex adaptive system có agent tương tác và tự đổi rule/behavior theo feedback.
**Grammar & collocations:** `complex-adaptive-system model` — model; `adapt to feedback` — adapt; `interacting agent` — agent.
**Examples:** `A city is a complex adaptive system whose traffic patterns change as commuters learn new routes.` → City là system adaptive với traffic đổi theo learning.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `복잡 적응계` — complex adaptive system.

## 2. self-organization /ˌsɛlf ˌɔrɡənəˈzeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** formation of coherent structure or order through local interactions without central direction.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** self-organization tạo order từ local interaction, không cần central controller.
**Grammar & collocations:** `self-organization process` — process; `self-organize into a pattern` — self-organize; `local interaction` — interaction.
**Examples:** `Self-organization produced lanes in the pedestrian flow without an assigned coordinator.` → Self-organization tạo lane trong pedestrian flow.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `자기 조직화` — self-organization.

## 3. critical transition /ˈkrɪtɪkəl trænˈzɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a change from one qualitative regime to another as a control parameter crosses a critical region.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** critical transition là đổi regime về chất khi parameter đi qua vùng critical.
**Grammar & collocations:** `critical-transition analysis` — analysis; `cross a critical point` — cross; `qualitative regime` — regime.
**Examples:** `The lake underwent a critical transition from clear water to persistent algal dominance.` → Lake đổi regime sang algae dominance.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `임계 전이` — critical transition.

## 4. critical threshold /ˈkrɪtɪkəl ˈθrɛʃhoʊld/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a boundary value beyond which a system's behavior changes sharply or becomes difficult to reverse.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** critical threshold là line mà qua đó system đổi mạnh hoặc khó quay lại.
**Grammar & collocations:** `critical-threshold estimate` — estimate; `cross a threshold` — cross; `irreversible change` — change.
**Examples:** `The reef approached a critical threshold after repeated heat stress.` → Reef tiến gần critical threshold sau heat stress lặp.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `임계 임계값` — critical threshold.

## 5. attractor basin /əˈtræktər ˈbeɪsən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the set of starting states that evolve toward the same attractor in a dynamical system.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** attractor basin là vùng initial state cùng bị hút về một stable pattern.
**Grammar & collocations:** `attractor-basin geometry` — geometry; `fall into an attractor` — fall; `state-space region` — region.
**Examples:** `Small disturbances kept the economy inside the same attractor basin until a crisis shifted it.` → Disturbance nhỏ vẫn trong basin cho tới crisis.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `끌개 분지` — attractor basin.

## 6. bifurcation point /ˌbaɪfərˈkeɪʃən pɔɪnt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a point at which a small parameter change causes a dynamical system to develop qualitatively different possible behaviors.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** bifurcation point là chỗ một system tách thành behavior/pathway khác.
**Grammar & collocations:** `bifurcation-point analysis` — analysis; `branch into states` — branch; `parameter change` — change.
**Examples:** `The model reached a bifurcation point when birth rates fell below replacement.` → Model đạt bifurcation point khi birth rate dưới replacement.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `분기점` — bifurcation point.

## 7. historical lock-in /hɪˈstɔrɪkəl lɑk ɪn/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** persistence of a technology, institution, or pattern because earlier choices make alternatives increasingly costly or difficult.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** historical lock-in khiến choice cũ khóa future dù alternative có thể tốt hơn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `historical-lock-in effect` — effect; `lock in a pathway` — lock in; `switching cost` — cost.
**Examples:** `Historical lock-in kept the region dependent on a rail gauge chosen a century ago.` → Lock-in giữ region phụ thuộc rail gauge cũ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `역사적 고착` — historical lock-in.

## 8. path-dependent hysteresis /pæθ dɪˈpɛndənt ˌhɪstəˈriːsɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** dependence of a system's current state on the direction and history of previous changes, so reversing an input does not simply reverse the outcome.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** path-dependent hysteresis nghĩa output phụ thuộc history; đưa input về cũ chưa chắc state về cũ.
**Grammar & collocations:** `path-dependent-hysteresis loop` — loop; `trace a return path` — trace; `history-dependent response` — response.
**Examples:** `The material showed path-dependent hysteresis: cooling did not retrace the heating curve.` → Material không retrace curve khi cooling.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `경로 의존 이력 현상` — path-dependent hysteresis.

## 9. tipping element /ˈtɪpɪŋ ˈɛləmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a component of a larger system that can shift abruptly after passing a threshold and trigger wider change.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** tipping element là component có thể tip và kéo theo system khác.
**Grammar & collocations:** `tipping-element risk` — risk; `trigger a cascade` — trigger; `threshold-sensitive component` — component.
**Examples:** `The ice sheet was treated as a tipping element in the climate assessment.` → Ice sheet là tipping element trong assessment.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `티핑 요소` — tipping element.

## 10. cascade failure /kæˈskeɪd ˈfeɪljər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a sequence in which failure in one component overloads or destabilizes others, spreading through a connected system.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cascade failure lan từ component này sang component khác qua network connection.
**Grammar & collocations:** `cascade-failure scenario` — scenario; `propagate a failure` — propagate; `interdependent system` — system.
**Examples:** `A transformer fault triggered cascade failure across three substations.` → Transformer fault gây cascade failure qua ba substation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `연쇄 고장` — cascade failure.

## 11. network centrality /ˈnɛtˌwɜrk sɛnˈtræləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a measure of how structurally important or influential a node is within a network.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** network centrality cho biết node nằm trung tâm, nối nhiều hoặc bridge quan trọng đến đâu.
**Grammar & collocations:** `network-centrality measure` — measure; `rank nodes by centrality` — rank; `central node` — node.
**Examples:** `High network centrality made the port a critical point for disruption planning.` → Port centrality cao nên là point critical.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `네트워크 중심성` — network centrality.

## 12. community modularity /kəˈmjuːnəti ˌmɑdʒəˈlærəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the degree to which a network separates into densely connected communities with fewer links between them.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** community modularity cho thấy network chia thành cluster/community rõ đến đâu.
**Grammar & collocations:** `community-modularity score` — score; `detect network communities` — detect; `within-community link` — link.
**Examples:** `Community modularity was high because suppliers clustered by region.` → Modularity cao vì supplier cluster theo region.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `커뮤니티 모듈성` — community modularity.

## 13. small-world topology /ˌsmɔl wɜrld təˈpɑlədʒi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** network structure combining dense local clustering with short paths between distant nodes.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** small-world topology vừa có local cluster vừa có shortcut ngắn giữa vùng xa.
**Grammar & collocations:** `small-world-topology metric` — metric; `short path length` — length; `clustered network` — network.
**Examples:** `The collaboration network had small-world topology, linking specialized labs through a few brokers.` → Network có local cluster và broker bridge.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `좁은 세상 위상` — small-world topology.

## 14. heavy-tailed network /ˈhɛvi teɪld ˈnɛtˌwɜrk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a network whose node-degree distribution has many ordinary nodes and a small number of extremely connected hubs.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** heavy-tailed network có rất nhiều node ít link và vài hub cực lớn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `heavy-tailed-network degree distribution` — distribution; `connected hub` — hub; `degree tail` — tail.
**Examples:** `The citation graph was a heavy-tailed network dominated by a few highly connected journals.` → Citation graph bị dominate bởi vài journal hub.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `두꺼운 꼬리 네트워크` — heavy-tailed network.

## 15. multilayer network /ˈmʌltɪˌleɪər ˈnɛtˌwɜrk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a network in which the same nodes are connected through different relation types or layers.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** multilayer network chồng nhiều relation: trade, friendship, information, transport trên cùng node.
**Grammar & collocations:** `multilayer-network analysis` — analysis; `stack network layers` — stack; `cross-layer link` — link.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `다층 네트워크` — multilayer network.

## 16. percolation threshold /ˌpɜrkəˈleɪʃən ˈθrɛʃhoʊld/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the critical level of connectivity or occupancy at which a system gains a large spanning pathway.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** percolation threshold là line network bắt đầu có connected pathway xuyên system.
**Grammar & collocations:** `percolation-threshold estimate` — estimate; `form a spanning cluster` — form; `connectivity level` — level.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `침투 임계값` — percolation threshold.

## 17. robustness margin /roʊˈbʌstnəs ˈmɑrdʒɪn/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the amount of disturbance or parameter change a system can absorb before its performance or regime fails.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** robustness margin là khoảng an toàn trước khi disturbance làm system fail.
**Grammar & collocations:** `robustness-margin estimate` — estimate; `absorb a perturbation` — absorb; `failure margin` — margin.
**Examples:** `The bridge had little robustness margin after corrosion reduced its load capacity.` → Bridge còn ít robustness margin.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `강건성 여유` — robustness margin.

**Core meaning (English):** an additional resilience gain produced by an intervention aimed at another objective.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** resilience co-benefit là benefit resilience đi kèm mục tiêu chính như energy saving hay fairness.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `회복탄력성 공동 편익` — resilience co-benefit.

## 19. early-warning signal /ˈɜrli ˈwɔrnɪŋ ˈsɪɡnəl/

**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** early-warning signal là dấu hiệu trước khi regime đổi, như variance tăng hoặc recovery chậm.

## 20. critical slowing down /ˈkrɪtɪkəl ˈsloʊɪŋ daʊn/

**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** critical slowing down nghĩa system mất lâu hơn để return sau disturbance trước tipping.

## 21. state-regime transition /steɪt rɪˈʒiːm trænˈzɪʃən/

**Core meaning (English):** the capacity of a system to maintain multiple stable states under similar external conditions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** multistability cho phép cùng condition nhưng system nằm ở nhiều stable state tùy history.

## 23. coevolutionary feedback /ˌkoʊˌɛvəˈluːʃəˌnɛri ˈfiːdbæk/

**Core meaning (English):** feedback in which interacting populations, institutions, or technologies change in response to one another over time.

## 25. nested hierarchy /ˈnɛstɪd ˈhaɪəˌrɑrki/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a structure in which units are organized within larger units across multiple levels or scales.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** nested hierarchy xếp cell trong tissue, tissue trong organ, tạo interaction xuyên scale.
**Grammar & collocations:** `nested-hierarchy structure` — structure; `nest units within levels` — nest; `cross-level interaction` — interaction.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `중첩 계층` — nested hierarchy.

## Review in context

A **complex adaptive system** can display **self-organization**, a **critical transition**, and a **critical threshold**. States may flow into an **attractor basin** or split at a **bifurcation point**; **historical lock-in** and **path-dependent hysteresis** make reversal difficult. A **tipping element** can trigger **cascade failure**. Network structure matters: **network centrality**, **community modularity**, **small-world topology**, a **heavy-tailed network**, and a **multilayer network** shape contagion and control. A **percolation threshold** determines connectivity, while a **robustness margin** and **resilience co-benefit** influence recovery. An **early-warning signal**, **critical slowing down**, and a **state-regime transition** reveal instability. **Multistability**, **coevolutionary feedback**, **adaptive response capacity**, and a **nested hierarchy** explain why similar systems can follow different paths.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

**Complex adaptive system** có thể thể hiện **self-organization**, **critical transition** và **critical threshold**. State có thể đi vào **attractor basin** hoặc tách ở **bifurcation point**; **historical lock-in** và **path-dependent hysteresis** làm reversal khó. **Tipping element** có thể gây **cascade failure**. Network structure quan trọng: **network centrality**, **community modularity**, **small-world topology**, **heavy-tailed network** và **multilayer network** định hình contagion/control. **Percolation threshold** quyết định connectivity, còn **robustness margin** và **resilience co-benefit** ảnh hưởng recovery. **Early-warning signal**, **critical slowing down** và **state-regime transition** cho thấy instability. **Multistability**, **coevolutionary feedback**, **adaptive response capacity** và **nested hierarchy** giải thích vì sao system tương tự đi theo path khác nhau.
