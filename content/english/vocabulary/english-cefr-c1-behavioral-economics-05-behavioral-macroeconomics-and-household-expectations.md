# C1 Vocabulary 05 — Behavioral macroeconomics and household expectations

This lesson follows how households interpret prices, debt, wages, and policy signals when making macroeconomic decisions.

Flow: **money illusion → nominal loss aversion → fiscal illusion → debt aversion → precautionary motive → nominal rigidity → wage illusion → fiscal fatigue → policy credibility → expectation anchoring → adaptive expectations → rational inattention → inattentive consumer → inflation salience → savings buffer → behavioral macroeconomics → expectations management → debt rollover aversion → tax refund spending → real-balance neglect**.

## 1. money illusion /ˈmʌni ɪˈluːʒən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the tendency to think in nominal amounts while overlooking how inflation changes their real purchasing power.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** nghĩ theo nominal amount mà bỏ qua inflation đổi real purchasing power.
**Grammar & collocations:** `money-illusion effect` — effect; `correct for money illusion` — hiệu chỉnh.
**Examples:** `Money illusion made the larger paycheck feel like a raise even though prices had risen faster.` → Illusion tiền khiến paycheck lớn hơn trông như raise dù price tăng nhanh hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `화폐 환상`.

## 2. nominal loss aversion /ˈnɑmənəl lɔs əˈvɝʒən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the tendency to dislike a fall in the stated amount of money even when its real value has not fallen.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** không thích nominal amount giảm dù real value không giảm.
**Grammar & collocations:** `nominal-loss-aversion response` — response; `avoid a nominal loss` — tránh.
**Examples:** `Nominal loss aversion made the investor reject a bond whose payment was indexed to inflation.` → Ác cảm loss nominal khiến investor reject bond payment index theo inflation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `명목 손실 회피`.

## 3. fiscal illusion /ˈfɪskəl ɪˈluːʒən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a misunderstanding of the true cost or benefit of public spending and taxation because financing is complex or indirect.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** hiểu sai cost/benefit thật của spending và tax vì financing phức tạp hoặc indirect.
**Grammar & collocations:** `fiscal-illusion hypothesis` — hypothesis; `reduce fiscal illusion` — giảm.
**Examples:** `Fiscal illusion made residents support a visible subsidy without noticing the future levy that financed it.` → Illusion fiscal khiến resident support subsidy visible mà không thấy levy future fund nó.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `재정 환상`.

## 4. debt aversion /dɛt əˈvɝʒən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a strong dislike of borrowing that leads people to avoid debt even when borrowing could improve long-term welfare.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** không thích borrowing mạnh đến mức tránh debt dù borrowing có thể cải thiện welfare dài hạn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `debt-aversion parameter` — parameter; `measure debt aversion` — đo.
**Examples:** `Debt aversion kept the household from financing a repair that would have lowered energy bills.` → Ác cảm debt khiến household không finance repair vốn giảm energy bill.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `부채 회피`.

## 5. precautionary motive /prɪˈkɔʃəˌnɛri ˈmoʊtɪv/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a reason for saving or delaying spending in order to protect against uncertain future income or expenses.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** lý do save hoặc delay spending để bảo vệ trước income/expense future bất định.
**Grammar & collocations:** `precautionary-motive saving` — saving; `act on a precautionary motive` — hành động.
**Examples:** `The precautionary motive strengthened after workers heard rumors about layoffs.` → Động cơ precautionary mạnh hơn sau khi worker nghe rumor layoff.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `예방 동기`.

## 6. nominal rigidity /ˈnɑmənəl rɪˈdʒɪdəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the tendency for stated prices or wages to adjust slowly even when economic conditions change.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** price hoặc wage stated adjust chậm dù condition economic đổi.
**Grammar & collocations:** `nominal-rigidity assumption` — assumption; `model nominal rigidity` — mô hình hóa.
**Examples:** `Nominal rigidity delayed the wage adjustment after demand fell.` → Rigidity nominal trì hoãn wage adjustment sau khi demand giảm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `명목 경직성`.

## 7. wage illusion /weɪdʒ ɪˈluːʒən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the tendency to judge a wage change by its stated amount rather than by the purchasing power it represents.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** đánh giá wage change theo stated amount thay vì purchasing power.
**Grammar & collocations:** `wage-illusion response` — response; `distinguish nominal and real wages` — phân biệt.
**Examples:** `Wage illusion hid a real pay cut when the contract raise was smaller than inflation.` → Illusion wage che real pay cut khi raise contract nhỏ hơn inflation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `임금 환상`.

## 8. fiscal fatigue /ˈfɪskəl fəˈtiːɡ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** declining willingness to accept further taxes, spending cuts, or fiscal reforms after repeated policy pressure.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** willingness giảm với tax, cut spending hoặc reform sau pressure policy lặp lại.
**Grammar & collocations:** `fiscal-fatigue threshold` — threshold; `experience fiscal fatigue` — trải qua.
**Examples:** `Fiscal fatigue made another emergency levy politically difficult even after a new shock.` → Mệt mỏi fiscal khiến levy khẩn cấp khác khó về politics sau shock mới.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `재정 피로`.

## 9. policy credibility /ˈpɑləsi ˌkrɛdəˈbɪləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the degree to which people believe that policymakers will follow through on announced commitments.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mức người ta tin policymaker sẽ thực hiện commitment đã announce.
**Grammar & collocations:** `policy-credibility gap` — gap; `restore policy credibility` — khôi phục.
**Examples:** `Policy credibility improved after the central bank published a consistent reaction rule.` → Credibility policy cải thiện sau khi central bank công bố reaction rule nhất quán.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `정책 신뢰성`.

## 10. expectation anchoring /ˌɛkspɛkˈteɪʃən ˈæŋkərɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the tendency for forecasts to remain close to a familiar reference value even after relevant evidence changes.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** forecast vẫn gần reference value quen dù evidence liên quan đổi.
**Grammar & collocations:** `expectation-anchoring measure` — measure; `break expectation anchoring` — phá.
**Examples:** `Expectation anchoring kept households' inflation forecasts below the latest survey evidence.` → Anchoring expectation giữ forecast inflation household thấp hơn survey evidence mới.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `기대 앵커링`.

## 11. adaptive expectations /əˈdæptɪv ˌɛkspɛkˈteɪʃənz/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** expectations updated gradually from past errors or past observed outcomes.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** expectation update dần từ error quá khứ hoặc outcome quan sát quá khứ.
**Grammar & collocations:** `adaptive-expectations model` — model; `update expectations backward-looking` — update.
**Examples:** `Adaptive expectations made the forecast respond slowly to the sudden change in inflation.` → Expectations adaptive khiến forecast phản ứng chậm với inflation đổi đột ngột.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `적응적 기대`.

## 12. rational inattention /ˈræʃənəl ˌɪnəˈtɛnʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the choice to ignore some information because acquiring and processing all available information is costly.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** chọn ignore một số information vì acquire/process toàn bộ quá tốn cost.
**Grammar & collocations:** `rational-inattention model` — model; `allocate limited attention` — phân bổ.
**Examples:** `Rational inattention explained why households did not update a loan decision after every small rate change.` → Inattention rational giải thích household không update loan decision sau mỗi rate change nhỏ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `합리적 부주의`.

## 13. inattentive consumer /ˌɪnəˈtɛntɪv kənˈsuːmər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a consumer who overlooks relevant prices, terms, or information because attention is limited or costly.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** consumer bỏ qua price, term hoặc information vì attention limited/costly.
**Grammar & collocations:** `inattentive-consumer model` — model; `target inattentive consumers` — nhắm.
**Examples:** `The inattentive consumer missed the automatic fee listed below the headline rate.` → Consumer không chú ý bỏ lỡ fee tự động dưới headline rate.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `부주의한 소비자`.

## 14. inflation salience /ɪnˈfleɪʃən ˈseɪliəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the degree to which price increases are visible and mentally prominent to households.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mức price increase visible và prominent trong mind household.
**Grammar & collocations:** `inflation-salience effect` — effect; `increase inflation salience` — tăng.
**Examples:** `Inflation salience rose when grocery receipts displayed the previous year's prices beside current ones.` → Salience inflation tăng khi receipt grocery hiển thị price năm trước cạnh price hiện tại.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `인플레이션 현저성`.

## 15. savings buffer /ˈseɪvɪŋz ˈbʌfər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a stock of liquid savings kept to absorb temporary income loss or unexpected expenses.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** stock saving liquid giữ để absorb income loss tạm thời hoặc expense unexpected.
**Grammar & collocations:** `savings-buffer target` — target; `build a savings buffer` — xây.
**Examples:** `The household built a savings buffer before accepting a less predictable job.` → Household xây buffer saving trước khi nhận job khó predict hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `저축 완충`.

## 16. behavioral macroeconomics /bɪˈheɪvjərəl ˌmækroʊˌɛkəˈnɑmɪks/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** macroeconomic analysis that incorporates realistic limits, biases, preferences, and learning into household and market behavior.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** macroeconomics đưa limit, bias, preference và learning realistic vào behavior household/market.
**Grammar & collocations:** `behavioral-macroeconomics model` — model; `apply behavioral macroeconomics` — áp dụng.
**Examples:** `Behavioral macroeconomics linked household attention limits to aggregate inflation dynamics.` → Macroeconomics hành vi nối limit attention household với inflation dynamics tổng thể.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `행동 거시경제학`.

## 17. expectations management /ˌɛkspɛkˈteɪʃənz ˈmænɪdʒmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** deliberate communication intended to shape what people expect policymakers, firms, or institutions to do.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** communication có chủ ý để định hình điều người ta kỳ vọng policymaker/firm/institution làm.
**Grammar & collocations:** `expectations-management strategy` — strategy; `manage public expectations` — quản lý.
**Examples:** `Clear expectations management reduced panic before the bank published its rate decision.` → Management expectation rõ giảm panic trước khi bank công bố rate decision.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `기대 관리`.

## 18. debt rollover aversion /dɛt ˈroʊlˌoʊvər əˈvɝʒən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** reluctance to refinance or extend maturing debt because future borrowing conditions feel uncertain.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** ngại refinance hoặc extend debt đến hạn vì future borrowing condition bất định.
**Grammar & collocations:** `debt-rollover-aversion measure` — measure; `reduce rollover aversion` — giảm.
**Examples:** `Debt rollover aversion led the firm to hold cash instead of funding a profitable expansion.` → Ác cảm rollover debt khiến firm giữ cash thay vì fund expansion profit.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `부채 차환 회피`.

## 19. tax refund spending /tæks rɪˈfʌnd ˈspɛndɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** household spending behavior observed after receiving a tax refund or other predictable lump-sum payment.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** behavior spending household sau tax refund hoặc lump-sum payment predictable.
**Grammar & collocations:** `tax-refund-spending response` — response; `track refund spending` — theo dõi.
**Examples:** `Tax refund spending rose on durable goods even though the payment represented delayed income.` → Spending refund tax tăng ở durable goods dù payment là income bị delay.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `세금 환급 지출`.

## 20. real-balance neglect /ˈriːəl ˈbæləns nəˈɡlɛkt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the tendency to overlook how inflation changes the real value of money balances, contracts, or nominal wealth.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** bỏ qua inflation đổi real value của money balance, contract hoặc nominal wealth.
**Grammar & collocations:** `real-balance-neglect assumption` — assumption; `correct for real-balance neglect` — hiệu chỉnh.
**Examples:** `Real-balance neglect made the household underestimate the cost of holding cash during high inflation.` → Bỏ qua balance real khiến household đánh giá thấp cost giữ cash lúc inflation cao.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `실질 잔액 무시`.

## Review in context

**Money illusion**, **nominal loss aversion**, and **fiscal illusion** shape how households read prices and public finance; **debt aversion** and a **precautionary motive** shape borrowing and saving. **Nominal rigidity** and **wage illusion** slow adjustment, while **fiscal fatigue** and **policy credibility** affect reform. **Expectation anchoring** and **adaptive expectations** contrast with **rational inattention**, which may produce an **inattentive consumer**. **Inflation salience**, a **savings buffer**, and **behavioral macroeconomics** help explain **expectations management**, **debt rollover aversion**, **tax refund spending**, and **real-balance neglect**.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

**Money illusion**, **nominal loss aversion** và **fiscal illusion** định hình cách household đọc price và public finance; **debt aversion** và **precautionary motive** định hình borrowing/saving. **Nominal rigidity** và **wage illusion** làm adjustment chậm, còn **fiscal fatigue** và **policy credibility** ảnh hưởng reform. **Expectation anchoring** và **adaptive expectations** đối lập với **rational inattention**, có thể tạo **inattentive consumer**. **Inflation salience**, **savings buffer** và **behavioral macroeconomics** giúp giải thích **expectations management**, **debt rollover aversion**, **tax refund spending** và **real-balance neglect**.
