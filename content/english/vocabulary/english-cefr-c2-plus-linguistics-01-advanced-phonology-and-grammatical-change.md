# C2+ Vocabulary — Advanced phonology and grammatical change

This lesson follows sound structure and language change from phonetic detail through morphology, evidential meaning, and multilingual repertoires. The network is **prosodic phrasing → intonation contour → phoneme inventory → allophonic variation → minimal pair contrast → acoustic formant → voice-onset lag → coarticulatory overlap → assimilation phonology → lenition → fortition → epenthesis → metathesis phonology → reduplicative morphology → clitic placement → grammaticalization pathway → evidentiality system → switch-reference → diglossic repertoire → morphophonemic alternation**.

## 1. prosodic phrasing /prəˈsɑːdɪk ˈfreɪzɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** organizing speech into rhythmically and intonationally meaningful units.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** organize speech thành unit có rhythm và intonation meaning.
**Grammar & collocations:** `prosodic phrasing boundary` — boundary phrasing; `analyze prosodic phrasing` — analyze phrasing.
**Examples:** `Prosodic phrasing made the contrast between the two interpretations clear.` → Prosodic phrasing làm contrast giữa hai interpretation rõ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `운율 구분`.

## 2. intonation contour /ˌɪntəˈneɪʃən ˈkɑːntʊr/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the changing pattern of pitch across an utterance.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** pattern pitch thay đổi xuyên suốt utterance.
**Grammar & collocations:** `rising intonation contour` — contour rising; `trace an intonation contour` — trace contour.
**Examples:** `The rising intonation contour made the statement sound like a question.` → Rising intonation contour làm statement nghe như question.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `억양 곡선`.

## 3. phoneme inventory /ˈfoʊniːm ˈɪnvəntɔːri/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the complete set of contrastive speech sounds in a language or dialect.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** toàn bộ sound speech có tính contrast trong language/dialect.
**Grammar & collocations:** `small phoneme inventory` — inventory nhỏ; `compare phoneme inventories` — compare inventories.
**Examples:** `The language's phoneme inventory includes a contrastive vowel length.` → Phoneme inventory của language có contrast vowel length.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `음소 목록`.

## 4. allophonic variation /ˌæləˈfɑːnɪk ˌvɛəriˈeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** predictable pronunciation differences among forms of the same phoneme.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** pronunciation difference có thể predict giữa các form của cùng phoneme.
**Grammar & collocations:** `contextual allophonic variation` — variation theo context; `describe allophonic variation` — describe.
**Examples:** `Allophonic variation explained why the stop sounded unreleased in that position.` → Allophonic variation giải thích vì sao stop không release ở position đó.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `이음 변이`.

## 5. minimal pair contrast /ˈmɪnəməl pɛr ˈkɑːntræst/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a difference in meaning shown by changing only one sound in otherwise similar words.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** meaning difference khi chỉ đổi một sound trong các word gần như giống nhau.
**Grammar & collocations:** `vowel minimal pair contrast` — contrast vowel; `demonstrate a minimal pair contrast` — demonstrate.
**Examples:** `The teacher used a minimal pair contrast to establish the new vowel.` → Teacher dùng minimal pair contrast để establish vowel mới.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `최소대립쌍 대조`.

## 6. acoustic formant /əˈkuːstɪk ˈfɔːrmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a prominent resonance frequency in the acoustic spectrum of speech.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** resonance frequency nổi bật trong acoustic spectrum của speech.
**Grammar & collocations:** `first acoustic formant` — formant đầu; `measure an acoustic formant` — đo formant.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `음향 포먼트`.

## 7. voice-onset lag /vɔɪs ˈɑːnsɛt læɡ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the interval between releasing a consonant and beginning vocal-fold vibration.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** interval giữa consonant release và vocal-fold vibration bắt đầu.
**Grammar & collocations:** `short voice-onset lag` — lag ngắn; `measure voice-onset lag` — measure lag.
**Examples:** `A short voice-onset lag identified the stop as usually voiced.` → Voice-onset lag ngắn identify stop là thường voiced.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `발성 개시 지연`.

## 8. coarticulatory overlap /koʊɑrˈtɪkjələtɔːri ˈoʊvərlæp/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the simultaneous influence of neighboring speech sounds on one another.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** các sound lân cận ảnh hưởng đồng thời lên nhau.
**Grammar & collocations:** `extensive coarticulatory overlap` — overlap lớn; `model coarticulatory overlap` — model overlap.
**Examples:** `Coarticulatory overlap made the vowel slightly nasal before the consonant.` → Coarticulatory overlap làm vowel hơi nasal trước consonant.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `공동 조음 중첩`.

## 9. assimilation phonology /əˌsɪməˈleɪʃən fəˈnɑːlədʒi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the study of sound changes in which one segment becomes more like a neighboring segment.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** study sound change khi một segment trở nên giống segment kế bên hơn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `regressive assimilation phonology` — phonology assimilation lùi; `analyze assimilation` — analyze.
**Examples:** `Assimilation phonology accounted for the place change across the word boundary.` → Assimilation phonology giải thích place change qua word boundary.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `동화 음운론`.

## 10. lenition /ləˈnɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a sound change that makes a consonant weaker or less constricted.

**Grammar & collocations:** `intervocalic lenition` — lenition giữa vowel; `undergo lenition` — undergo lenition.
**Examples:** `Intervocalic lenition turned the stop into a softer fricative.` → Intervocalic lenition biến stop thành fricative mềm hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `약화`.

## 11. fortition /fɔːrˈtɪʃən/

## 12. epenthesis /ɛˈpɛnθəsɪs/

**Core meaning (English):** inserting an extra sound into a word, often to make pronunciation easier.

## 18. switch-reference /ˈswɪtʃ ˌrɛfərəns/

**Core meaning (English):** the range of language varieties speakers use when a high and a low variety serve different social functions.

## Review in context

An analyst describes **prosodic phrasing** and an **intonation contour**, inventories the language's **phoneme inventory**, and separates **allophonic variation** from a **minimal pair contrast**. An **acoustic formant**, **voice-onset lag**, and **coarticulatory overlap** reveal fine phonetic timing. Historical patterns include **assimilation phonology**, **lenition**, **fortition**, **epenthesis**, and **metathesis phonology**. In grammar, **reduplicative morphology**, **clitic placement**, a **grammaticalization pathway**, an **evidentiality system**, **switch-reference**, a **diglossic repertoire**, and **morphophonemic alternation** connect form with discourse and social use.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:** Analyst mô tả **prosodic phrasing** và **intonation contour**, lập **phoneme inventory**, rồi tách **allophonic variation** khỏi **minimal pair contrast**. **Acoustic formant**, **voice-onset lag** và **coarticulatory overlap** reveal timing phonetic tinh. Pattern lịch sử gồm **assimilation phonology**, **lenition**, **fortition**, **epenthesis** và **metathesis phonology**. Trong grammar, **reduplicative morphology**, **clitic placement**, **grammaticalization pathway**, **evidentiality system**, **switch-reference**, **diglossic repertoire** và **morphophonemic alternation** nối form với discourse và social use.
