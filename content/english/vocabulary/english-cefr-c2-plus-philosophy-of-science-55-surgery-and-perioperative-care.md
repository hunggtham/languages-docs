# C2+ Vocabulary — Surgery and perioperative care

This lesson follows how surgical services prepare patients, reduce avoidable harm, manage recovery, and organize capacity. The network is **enhanced recovery after surgery → prehabilitation → surgical site infection → perioperative risk → anesthesia assessment → airway plan → regional anesthesia → intraoperative monitoring → surgical checklist → wrong-site surgery → postoperative ileus → venous thromboembolism prophylaxis → pain stewardship → opioid-sparing analgesia → drain management → wound dehiscence → anastomotic leak → readmission risk → day-case surgery → ambulatory surgery → surgical backlog → operating-room turnover → case cancellation → perioperative frailty → shared surgical decision**.

## 1. enhanced recovery after surgery /ɪnˈhænst rɪˈkʌvəri ˈæftər ˈsɜrdʒəri/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a coordinated, evidence-based approach that prepares patients before surgery, reduces avoidable stress during it, and supports earlier safe recovery afterward.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** enhanced recovery after surgery nối pre-op, intra-op và post-op thành một pathway.
**Grammar & collocations:** `enhanced-recovery-after-surgery pathway` — pathway; `accelerate safe recovery` — accelerate; `multimodal care` — care.
**Examples:** `Enhanced recovery after surgery combined early movement, nutrition, careful fluids, and clear discharge criteria.` → Nhiều action cùng giảm recovery delay.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `수술 후 향상된 회복` — enhanced recovery after surgery.

## 2. prehabilitation /ˌpriːhæbɪləˈteɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** preparation before surgery that improves physical condition, nutrition, smoking status, mental readiness, or self-management.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** prehabilitation làm patient “vào surgery” với reserve tốt hơn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `prehabilitation program` — program; `prepare before surgery` — prepare; `functional reserve` — reserve.
**Examples:** `Prehabilitation used walking practice and nutrition support before the planned joint replacement.` → Chuẩn bị trước giúp recovery.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `수술 전 재활` — prehabilitation.

## 3. surgical site infection /ˈsɜrdʒɪkəl saɪt ɪnˈfɛkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** infection occurring in the incision, organ, or space involved in a surgical procedure.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** surgical site infection là infection gắn với nơi/space surgery.
**Grammar & collocations:** `surgical-site-infection rate` — rate; `prevent an incision infection` — prevent; `postoperative surveillance` — surveillance.
**Examples:** `The surgical site infection review examined skin preparation, antibiotics, temperature, and handoff.` → Nhiều step ảnh hưởng infection.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `수술 부위 감염` — surgical site infection.

## 4. perioperative risk /ˌpɛriˈɑpərətɪv rɪsk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** risk of complications or poor outcome across the period before, during, and after an operation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** perioperative risk trải dài cả journey, không chỉ risk trên operating table.
**Grammar & collocations:** `perioperative-risk assessment` — assessment; `stratify surgical risk` — stratify; `postoperative complication` — complication.
**Examples:** `Perioperative risk reflected heart function, frailty, home support, and the urgency of surgery.` → Risk gồm medical và social context.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `수술 주위 위험` — perioperative risk.

## 5. anesthesia assessment /ˌænəsˈθiʒə əˈsɛsmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** preoperative evaluation of a patient's health, medicines, airway, prior reactions, and readiness for anesthesia.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** anesthesia assessment chuẩn bị plan gây mê theo patient-specific risk.
**Grammar & collocations:** `anesthesia-assessment clinic` — clinic; `assess anesthesia risk` — assess; `prior anesthetic reaction` — reaction.
**Examples:** `The anesthesia assessment identified untreated sleep apnea and changed the monitoring plan.` → Finding pre-op đổi plan.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `마취 평가` — anesthesia assessment.

## 6. airway plan /ˈɛrˌweɪ plæn/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** predefined strategy for maintaining or securing a patient's airway during anesthesia or a procedure, including backup options.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** airway plan có primary và backup để không bị bất ngờ khi airway khó.
**Grammar & collocations:** `airway-plan briefing` — briefing; `secure the airway` — secure; `backup airway option` — option.
**Examples:** `The airway plan included an alternative device and a senior clinician available before induction.` → Chuẩn bị backup trước.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `기도 계획` — airway plan.

## 7. regional anesthesia /ˈriːdʒənəl ˌænəsˈθiʒə/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** anesthesia that numbs a larger body region while the patient may remain awake or lightly sedated.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** regional anesthesia block sensation ở vùng cần surgery thay vì luôn làm unconscious toàn thân.
**Grammar & collocations:** `regional-anesthesia technique` — technique; `numb a body region` — numb; `nerve block` — block.
**Examples:** `Regional anesthesia reduced the need for deep general anesthesia during the limb procedure.` → Chọn technique theo context.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `부위 마취` — regional anesthesia.

## 8. intraoperative monitoring /ˌɪntrəˈɑpərətɪv ˈmɑnətərɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** continuous observation of physiological signals and surgical conditions during an operation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** intraoperative monitoring theo dõi body và procedure trong lúc operation.
**Grammar & collocations:** `intraoperative-monitoring plan` — plan; `monitor physiological changes` — monitor; `operative signal` — signal.
**Examples:** `Intraoperative monitoring detected a change early enough for the team to pause and reassess.` → Monitor giúp pause sớm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `수술 중 모니터링` — intraoperative monitoring.

## 9. surgical checklist /ˈsɜrdʒɪkəl ˈtʃɛkˌlɪst/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a structured pause used to confirm identity, procedure, site, risks, equipment, roles, and communication before key stages of surgery.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** surgical checklist là safety pause để team cùng nhìn một page sự thật.
**Grammar & collocations:** `surgical-checklist pause` — pause; `confirm the operative site` — confirm; `team briefing` — briefing.
**Examples:** `The surgical checklist caught a mismatch between the consent form and the scheduled procedure.` → Checklist chặn mismatch.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `수술 체크리스트` — surgical checklist.

## 10. wrong-site surgery /rɔŋ saɪt ˈsɜrdʒəri/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** surgery performed on the wrong body part, side, level, or patient.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** wrong-site surgery là serious preventable event cần nhiều safeguard.
**Grammar & collocations:** `wrong-site-surgery prevention` — prevention; `verify the surgical site` — verify; `never event` — event.
**Examples:** `Wrong-site surgery prevention required site marking, consent verification, and a team time-out.` → Nhiều lớp cùng ngăn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `수술 부위 오류` — wrong-site surgery.

## 11. postoperative ileus /ˌpoʊstˈɑpərətɪv ˈɪliəs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** temporary reduction or absence of bowel movement after surgery that delays feeding, comfort, and recovery.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** postoperative ileus làm gut “chậm lại” sau surgery và kéo dài recovery.
**Grammar & collocations:** `postoperative-ileus risk` — risk; `monitor bowel function` — monitor; `delayed feeding` — feeding.
**Examples:** `Early movement and careful pain management were part of the plan to reduce postoperative ileus.` → Mobility và pain strategy hỗ trợ gut recovery.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `수술 후 장폐색` — postoperative ileus.

## 12. venous thromboembolism prophylaxis /ˈviːnəs ˌθrɑmboʊˈɛmbəˌlɪzəm ˌproʊfɪˈlæksɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** measures used to reduce the risk of blood clots in veins and clot-related embolism around surgery.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** venous thromboembolism prophylaxis kết hợp movement, devices và medicine theo risk.
**Grammar & collocations:** `venous-thromboembolism-prophylaxis plan` — plan; `prevent postoperative clots` — prevent; `risk-adjusted measure` — measure.
**Examples:** `Venous thromboembolism prophylaxis was adjusted for bleeding risk and mobility after surgery.` → Cân bằng clot và bleeding risk.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `정맥 혈전색전증 예방` — venous thromboembolism prophylaxis.

## 13. pain stewardship /peɪn ˈstuərdʃɪp/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** balanced management of pain that aims for relief, function, safety, and responsible use of analgesic medicines.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** pain stewardship không undertreat pain và không dùng medicine vô kiểm soát.
**Grammar & collocations:** `pain-stewardship program` — program; `manage pain responsibly` — manage; `functional recovery` — recovery.
**Examples:** `Pain stewardship combined communication, movement, non-drug methods, and individualized medication review.` → Pain plan đa phương thức.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `통증 관리 책임` — pain stewardship.

## 14. opioid-sparing analgesia /ˈoʊpiˌɔɪd ˈspɛrɪŋ ˌænəlˈdʒiʒə/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** pain treatment that reduces reliance on opioids by combining other medicines, regional techniques, and non-drug approaches.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** opioid-sparing analgesia giảm opioid mà vẫn cần treat pain đủ.
**Grammar & collocations:** `opioid-sparing-analgesia plan` — plan; `reduce opioid exposure` — reduce; `multimodal pain control` — control.
**Examples:** `Opioid-sparing analgesia supported mobility while limiting sedation and constipation.` → Mobility tốt hơn khi side effect giảm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `오피오이드 절감 진통` — opioid-sparing analgesia.

## 15. drain management /dreɪn ˈmænɪdʒmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** monitoring, care, documentation, and timely removal of a surgical drain according to the clinical purpose and risk.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** drain management là theo dõi purpose, output, infection và removal.
**Grammar & collocations:** `drain-management protocol` — protocol; `monitor drain output` — monitor; `remove a drain` — remove.
**Examples:** `Drain management included a clear output threshold and a documented removal plan.` → Có threshold và ownership rõ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `배액관 관리` — drain management.

## 16. wound dehiscence /wuːnd dɪˈhɪsəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** partial or complete separation of a surgical wound after closure.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** wound dehiscence là wound tách ra sau closure, cần assessment.
**Grammar & collocations:** `wound-dehiscence risk` — risk; `assess wound separation` — assess; `incision care` — care.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `창상 벌어짐` — wound dehiscence.

## 17. anastomotic leak /əˌnæstəˈmɑtɪk liːk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** leakage from a surgical connection between two hollow organs.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** anastomotic leak là complication ở connection sau surgery, cần vigilance.
**Grammar & collocations:** `anastomotic-leak surveillance` — surveillance; `detect a postoperative leak` — detect; `surgical connection` — connection.

## 18. readmission risk /ˌriːədˈmɪʃən rɪsk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** likelihood that a patient will need unplanned hospital admission again after discharge.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** readmission risk cho thấy discharge plan có bền trong home context không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `readmission-risk model` — model; `reduce unplanned readmission` — reduce; `post-discharge support` — support.
**Examples:** `Readmission risk fell after medication reconciliation and a follow-up call were added to discharge.` → Handoff tốt giảm readmission.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `재입원 위험` — readmission risk.

## 19. day-case surgery /ˈdeɪ keɪs ˈsɜrdʒəri/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** surgery planned so that a patient is assessed, treated, and discharged on the same day when safe.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** day-case surgery cần selection, recovery và home support phù hợp.
**Grammar & collocations:** `day-case-surgery pathway` — pathway; `discharge the same day` — discharge; `postoperative escort` — escort.

## 20. ambulatory surgery /ˈæmbjələtɔri ˈsɜrdʒəri/

## 23. case cancellation /keɪs ˌkænsəˈleɪʃən/

## 24. perioperative frailty /ˌpɛriˈɑpərətɪv ˈfreɪlti/
**Part of speech:** noun

**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** perioperative frailty cho thấy patient dễ bị stress surgery và cần preparation/support thêm.

## 25. shared surgical decision /ʃɛrd ˈsɜrdʒɪkəl dɪˈsɪʒən/

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `공동 수술 결정` — shared surgical decision.

## Review in context — Part 1

**Enhanced recovery after surgery** begins with **prehabilitation** and a plan to prevent **surgical site infection**. **Perioperative risk** informs the **anesthesia assessment** and **airway plan**; **regional anesthesia** and **intraoperative monitoring** may be selected for the individual case. A **surgical checklist** helps prevent **wrong-site surgery**. Afterward, teams monitor **postoperative ileus**, use **venous thromboembolism prophylaxis**, and apply **pain stewardship** with **opioid-sparing analgesia**. **Drain management**, surveillance for **wound dehiscence** and **anastomotic leak**, and attention to **readmission risk** support recovery.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 1:**

**Enhanced recovery after surgery** bắt đầu bằng **prehabilitation** và plan ngăn **surgical site infection**. **Perioperative risk** định hướng **anesthesia assessment** và **airway plan**; **regional anesthesia** và **intraoperative monitoring** được chọn theo case. **Surgical checklist** giúp ngăn **wrong-site surgery**. Sau đó, team theo dõi **postoperative ileus**, dùng **venous thromboembolism prophylaxis**, và áp dụng **pain stewardship** với **opioid-sparing analgesia**. **Drain management**, surveillance **wound dehiscence** và **anastomotic leak**, cùng chú ý **readmission risk** hỗ trợ recovery.

## Review in context — Part 2

**Day-case surgery** and **ambulatory surgery** depend on safe selection and follow-up. A **surgical backlog** can grow when **operating-room turnover** is slow or **case cancellation** is frequent. **Perioperative frailty** may change preparation and support. A **shared surgical decision** brings evidence, alternatives, risk, and patient goals into the plan rather than treating surgery as an automatic answer.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 2:**

**Day-case surgery** và **ambulatory surgery** cần selection và follow-up an toàn. **Surgical backlog** có thể tăng khi **operating-room turnover** chậm hoặc **case cancellation** thường xuyên. **Perioperative frailty** có thể làm đổi preparation và support. **Shared surgical decision** đưa evidence, alternatives, risk và goal patient vào plan thay vì coi surgery là đáp án tự động.
