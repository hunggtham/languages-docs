# C2+ Vocabulary — Diagnostic testing and screening strategy

This lesson follows how tests detect disease, change probability, and fit into screening and treatment pathways. The network is **test sensitivity → test specificity → positive predictive fraction → negative predictive fraction → diagnostic likelihood ratio → assay detection floor → analytical sensitivity → clinical sensitivity → pretest probability → posttest probability → serial testing → parallel testing → reflex testing → orthogonal confirmation assay → orthogonal testing → pooled testing → screening interval → repeat positivity → false-negative window → specimen adequacy → invalid result → test-to-treat pathway → confirmatory testing threshold → screening yield → diagnostic stewardship**.

## 1. test sensitivity /tɛst ˌsɛnsəˈtɪvəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the proportion of people who truly have a condition and receive a positive test result.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** test sensitivity hỏi test bắt được bao nhiêu người thật sự có disease.
**Grammar & collocations:** `test-sensitivity estimate` — estimate; `maximize sensitivity` — maximize; `missed case` — case.
**Examples:** `The screening test had high test sensitivity during the symptomatic phase.` → Sensitivity cao giúp ít bỏ sót.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `검사 민감도` — test sensitivity.

## 2. test specificity /tɛst ˌspɛsəˈfɪsəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the proportion of people who truly do not have a condition and receive a negative test result.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** test specificity hỏi test loại đúng bao nhiêu người không có disease.
**Grammar & collocations:** `test-specificity estimate` — estimate; `maximize specificity` — maximize; `false-positive result` — result.
**Examples:** `High test specificity reduced unnecessary referrals after a low-risk screen.` → Specificity cao giảm false positive.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `검사 특이도` — test specificity.

## 3. positive predictive fraction /ˈpɑzətɪv prɪˈdɪktɪv ˈfrækʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the proportion of positive results that come from people who truly have the condition.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** positive predictive fraction hỏi “trong những người test positive, bao nhiêu người thật sự có disease?”.
**Grammar & collocations:** `positive-predictive fraction` — fraction; `interpret a positive result` — interpret; `base-rate effect` — effect.
**Examples:** `The positive predictive fraction fell when testing expanded into a very low-risk population.` → Base rate ảnh hưởng ý nghĩa positive.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `양성 예측 분율` — positive predictive fraction.

## 4. negative predictive fraction /ˈnɛɡətɪv prɪˈdɪktɪv ˈfrækʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the proportion of negative results that come from people who truly do not have the condition.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** negative predictive fraction hỏi “negative này đáng tin đến đâu trong context này?”.
**Grammar & collocations:** `negative-predictive fraction` — fraction; `interpret a negative result` — interpret; `residual risk` — risk.
**Examples:** `The negative predictive fraction was lower during the early false-negative window.` → Negative sớm chưa chắc an toàn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `음성 예측 분율` — negative predictive fraction.

## 5. diagnostic likelihood ratio /ˌdaɪəɡˈnɑstɪk ˈlaɪkliˌhʊd ˈreɪʃioʊ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a ratio describing how much a test result changes the odds of a condition compared with the pretest odds.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** diagnostic likelihood ratio đo test result đẩy probability lên hoặc xuống bao nhiêu.
**Grammar & collocations:** `diagnostic-likelihood ratio` — ratio; `update disease odds` — update; `likelihood-ratio interpretation` — interpretation.
**Examples:** `The positive diagnostic likelihood ratio was strong enough to justify confirmatory treatment.` → Result đổi odds đáng kể.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `진단 우도비` — diagnostic likelihood ratio.

## 6. assay detection floor /ˈæseɪ dɪˈtɛkʃən flɔr/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the lowest amount of target material that an assay can reliably detect under defined conditions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** assay detection floor là mức thấp nhất mà assay còn “nhìn thấy” target.
**Grammar & collocations:** `assay-detection floor` — floor; `detect low target levels` — detect; `limit-of-detection study` — study.
**Examples:** `The assay detection floor explained why weak samples produced inconsistent results.` → Signal dưới floor không đáng tin.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `검사 검출 하한` — assay detection floor.

## 7. analytical sensitivity /ˌænəˈlɪtɪkəl ˌsɛnsəˈtɪvəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an assay's ability to detect small amounts of a target under controlled laboratory conditions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** analytical sensitivity nói về performance của assay trong lab, không phải toàn clinical pathway.
**Grammar & collocations:** `analytical-sensitivity study` — study; `detect low concentrations` — detect; `laboratory performance` — performance.
**Examples:** `Analytical sensitivity improved after the extraction step was redesigned.` → Lab process ảnh hưởng detection.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `분석 민감도` — analytical sensitivity.

## 8. clinical sensitivity /ˈklɪnɪkəl ˌsɛnsəˈtɪvəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an assay's ability to identify a condition in real patients across symptoms, timing, specimens, and care settings.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** clinical sensitivity rộng hơn lab performance vì gồm patient và timing thật.
**Grammar & collocations:** `clinical-sensitivity estimate` — estimate; `evaluate clinical sensitivity` — evaluate; `real-world detection` — detection.
**Examples:** `Clinical sensitivity varied with specimen type and the day of illness.` → Same assay có thể khác performance ngoài field.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `임상 민감도` — clinical sensitivity.

## 9. pretest probability /ˌpriːˈtɛst prɑbəˈbɪləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the estimated probability that a person has a condition before the current test result is considered.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** pretest probability là starting point trước khi test update belief.
**Grammar & collocations:** `pretest-probability estimate` — estimate; `assess pretest probability` — assess; `clinical context` — context.
**Examples:** `The clinician raised the pretest probability after a compatible exposure history.` → History đổi starting point.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `검사 전 확률` — pretest probability.

## 10. posttest probability /ˌpoʊstˈtɛst prɑbəˈbɪləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the estimated probability of a condition after incorporating the test result and the pretest probability.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** posttest probability là belief sau khi result đã update odds.
**Grammar & collocations:** `posttest-probability estimate` — estimate; `calculate posttest probability` — calculate; `residual disease risk` — risk.
**Examples:** `A negative result lowered the posttest probability but did not remove risk during early illness.` → Negative không luôn bằng zero.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `검사 후 확률` — posttest probability.

## 11. serial testing /ˈsɪriəl ˈtɛstɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** testing the same person more than once at planned intervals to improve detection across time.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** serial testing dùng nhiều lần test theo thời gian để bắt case bị miss lần đầu.
**Grammar & collocations:** `serial-testing schedule` — schedule; `repeat testing` — repeat; `time-dependent detection` — detection.
**Examples:** `Serial testing improved detection when viral levels rose gradually after exposure.` → Time giúp signal vượt floor.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `연속 검사` — serial testing.

## 12. parallel testing /ˈpærəˌlɛl ˈtɛstɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** using two or more tests at the same time to increase the chance that at least one detects a condition.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** parallel testing chạy nhiều assay cùng lúc để giảm bỏ sót.
**Grammar & collocations:** `parallel-testing strategy` — strategy; `test in parallel` — test; `combined sensitivity` — sensitivity.
**Examples:** `Parallel testing used two specimen types for patients with high clinical concern.` → Nhiều route tăng chance detect.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `병렬 검사` — parallel testing.

## 13. reflex testing /ˈriːflɛks ˈtɛstɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an automatic follow-up test triggered by a predefined initial result or condition.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** reflex testing là test tiếp theo tự động chạy khi result đầu thỏa trigger.
**Grammar & collocations:** `reflex-testing rule` — rule; `trigger a reflex test` — trigger; `automatic follow-up` — follow-up.
**Examples:** `A reactive screen triggered reflex testing for a more specific marker.` → Workflow giảm delay và lost follow-up.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `반사 검사` — reflex testing.

## 14. orthogonal confirmation assay /ɔrˈθɑɡənəl ˌkɑnfərˈmeɪʃən ˈæseɪ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a confirmatory assay based on a different target, method, or biological principle from the initial test.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** orthogonal confirmation assay dùng evidence độc lập hơn để kiểm tra positive ban đầu.
**Grammar & collocations:** `orthogonal-confirmation assay` — assay; `confirm with an independent method` — confirm; `second target` — target.
**Examples:** `The laboratory used an orthogonal confirmation assay before reporting a rare resistance marker.` → Method khác tăng confidence.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `직교 확인 검사` — orthogonal confirmation assay.

## 15. orthogonal testing /ɔrˈθɑɡənəl ˈtɛstɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** use of tests that measure different features or targets so their errors are less likely to be identical.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** orthogonal testing tạo hai góc nhìn ít phụ thuộc cùng một error.
**Grammar & collocations:** `orthogonal-testing design` — design; `combine independent tests` — combine; `error dependence` — dependence.
**Examples:** `Orthogonal testing paired antigen detection with a molecular method.` → Independent evidence mạnh hơn lặp cùng assay.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `직교 검사` — orthogonal testing.

## 16. pooled testing /puːld ˈtɛstɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** testing a combined sample from several people and testing individuals separately only if the pool is positive or unclear.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** pooled testing tiết kiệm reagent khi prevalence thấp.
**Grammar & collocations:** `pooled-testing algorithm` — algorithm; `pool specimens` — pool; `deconvolute a positive pool` — deconvolute.
**Examples:** `Pooled testing expanded screening capacity when most samples were expected to be negative.` → Pool hiệu quả khi prevalence thấp.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `혼합 검체 검사` — pooled testing.

## 17. screening interval /ˈskriːnɪŋ ˈɪntərvəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the planned time between repeated screening tests for a population or condition.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** screening interval quyết định test lại sau bao lâu để bắt disease kịp thời.
**Grammar & collocations:** `screening-interval recommendation` — recommendation; `shorten the interval` — shorten; `periodic screening` — screening.
**Examples:** `The screening interval was shortened during the period of fastest transmission.` → Transmission nhanh cần test dày hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `검사 간격` — screening interval.

## 18. repeat positivity /rɪˈpiːt ˌpɑzəˈtɪvəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a pattern in which a person or sample remains positive or tests positive again after an earlier result.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** repeat positivity cần phân biệt persistent signal, reinfection và test artifact.
**Grammar & collocations:** `repeat-positivity pattern` — pattern; `investigate repeat positivity` — investigate; `persistent detection` — detection.
**Examples:** `Repeat positivity prompted review of specimen timing and the possibility of reinfection.` → Positive lại chưa tự nói nguyên nhân.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `반복 양성` — repeat positivity.

## 19. false-negative window /fɔls ˈnɛɡətɪv ˈwɪndoʊ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a period after infection or exposure when a test may remain negative because the target is not yet detectable.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** false-negative window là khoảng thời gian test chưa “nhìn thấy” infection.
**Grammar & collocations:** `false-negative window` — window; `test during the window` — test; `early infection` — infection.
**Examples:** `A negative result inside the false-negative window did not end monitoring.` → Timing quyết định meaning của negative.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `위음성 기간` — false-negative window.

## 20. specimen adequacy /ˈspɛsəmən ˈædəkwəsi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the extent to which a collected specimen has the right type, amount, quality, and timing for a reliable test.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** specimen adequacy hỏi sample có đủ và đúng để test không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `specimen-adequacy check` — check; `collect an adequate specimen` — collect; `sample quality` — quality.
**Examples:** `Specimen adequacy was questioned after a shallow swab produced an unexpected negative result.` → Sample kém có thể tạo false negative.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `검체 적합성` — specimen adequacy.

## 21. invalid result /ɪnˈvælɪd rɪˈzʌlt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a test outcome that cannot be interpreted because controls, specimen quality, equipment, or procedure failed.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** invalid result không phải positive hay negative; cần xử lý lại.
**Grammar & collocations:** `invalid-result rate` — rate; `repeat an invalid test` — repeat; `assay control failure` — failure.
**Examples:** `The invalid result was repeated after the instrument control failed.` → Invalid cần repeat, không nên classify.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `무효 결과` — invalid result.

## 22. test-to-treat pathway /tɛst tə triːt ˈpæθweɪ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an integrated process that links testing, result communication, clinical eligibility, and timely treatment.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** test-to-treat pathway bảo đảm result positive dẫn tới treatment nhanh.
**Grammar & collocations:** `test-to-treat pathway` — pathway; `link testing to treatment` — link; `same-day therapy` — therapy.
**Examples:** `The test-to-treat pathway reserved appointments for people with newly positive results.` → Test chỉ hữu ích khi nối được care.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `검사-치료 경로` — test-to-treat pathway.

## 23. confirmatory testing threshold /ˌkɑnfərˈmeɪtəri ˈtɛstɪŋ ˈθrɛʃhoʊld/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the predefined level of concern or consequence at which an initial result requires an additional confirmatory test.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** confirmatory testing threshold quyết định positive nào cần thêm evidence trước khi act.
**Grammar & collocations:** `confirmatory-testing threshold` — threshold; `trigger confirmation` — trigger; `high-consequence result` — result.
**Examples:** `A low-prevalence positive crossed the confirmatory testing threshold because treatment carried major risk.` → Consequence ảnh hưởng threshold.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `확인 검사 임계치` — confirmatory testing threshold.

## 24. screening yield /ˈskriːnɪŋ jiːld/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the number or proportion of meaningful cases detected by a screening program.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** screening yield cho biết effort screening tìm được bao nhiêu case có giá trị.
**Grammar & collocations:** `screening-yield estimate` — estimate; `measure case yield` — measure; `high-yield population` — population.
**Examples:** `Screening yield was highest in clinics serving people with recent exposure.` → Target đúng làm yield tăng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `선별검사 수율` — screening yield.

## 25. diagnostic stewardship /ˌdaɪəɡˈnɑstɪk ˈstjuərdʃɪp/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** coordinated effort to order, perform, interpret, and act on diagnostic tests appropriately.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** diagnostic stewardship tránh test thừa, test sai timing và result không được dùng đúng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `diagnostic-stewardship program` — program; `order an appropriate test` — order; `result interpretation` — interpretation.
**Examples:** `Diagnostic stewardship reduced repeat testing that added cost without changing clinical decisions.` → Test tốt cần workflow tốt.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `진단 검사 관리` — diagnostic stewardship.

## Review in context — Part 1

**Test sensitivity** and **test specificity** describe basic performance, but a **positive predictive fraction** and **negative predictive fraction** depend on context. A **diagnostic likelihood ratio** updates the **pretest probability** into a **posttest probability**. The **assay detection floor**, **analytical sensitivity**, and **clinical sensitivity** explain why timing and setting matter.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 1:**

**Test sensitivity** và **test specificity** mô tả performance cơ bản, nhưng **positive predictive fraction** và **negative predictive fraction** phụ thuộc context. **Diagnostic likelihood ratio** cập nhật **pretest probability** thành **posttest probability**. **Assay detection floor**, **analytical sensitivity** và **clinical sensitivity** giải thích vì sao timing và setting quan trọng.

## Review in context — Part 2

**Serial testing**, **parallel testing**, and **reflex testing** can improve detection. An **orthogonal confirmation assay** and broader **orthogonal testing** provide independent evidence, while **pooled testing** saves resources. A **screening interval**, **repeat positivity**, the **false-negative window**, **specimen adequacy**, and an **invalid result** determine what happens next. The **test-to-treat pathway**, **confirmatory testing threshold**, **screening yield**, and **diagnostic stewardship** connect laboratory decisions to useful care.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 2:**

**Serial testing**, **parallel testing** và **reflex testing** có thể tăng detection. **Orthogonal confirmation assay** và **orthogonal testing** rộng hơn cung cấp evidence độc lập, còn **pooled testing** tiết kiệm resource. **Screening interval**, **repeat positivity**, **false-negative window**, **specimen adequacy** và **invalid result** quyết định bước tiếp theo. **Test-to-treat pathway**, **confirmatory testing threshold**, **screening yield** và **diagnostic stewardship** nối quyết định lab với care hữu ích.
