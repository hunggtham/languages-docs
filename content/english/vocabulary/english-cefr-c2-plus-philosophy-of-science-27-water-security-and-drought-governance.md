# C2+ Vocabulary — Water security and drought governance

This lesson follows how institutions measure water stress, allocate scarce supplies, and combine conservation with reuse. The network is **water stress → drought severity index → hydrological drought → agricultural drought → ecological drought → groundwater depletion → safe yield → recharge rate → aquifer storage → conjunctive use → water allocation → prior appropriation → environmental flow → demand management → water banking → drought contingency plan → water rationing → tiered tariff → distribution-loss reduction → non-revenue water → desalination brine → water reuse → fit-for-purpose treatment → potable reuse → water-energy nexus**.

## 1. water stress /ˈwɔtər strɛs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** pressure on available water caused by demand, scarcity, pollution, timing, or competition among users and ecosystems.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** water stress không chỉ thiếu volume; còn do demand, pollution, timing và competition.
**Grammar & collocations:** `water-stress indicator` — indicator; `experience water stress` — experience; `competing demand` — demand.
**Examples:** `Water stress increased when tourism demand coincided with a dry farming season.` → Stress tăng khi tourism trùng dry season.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `물 스트레스` — water stress.

## 2. drought severity index /draʊt səˈvɛrəti ˈɪndɛks/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a standardized indicator that summarizes the intensity, duration, or spatial extent of drought conditions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** drought severity index gom intensity/duration/extent thành indicator compare được.
**Grammar & collocations:** `drought-severity index` — index; `classify drought conditions` — classify; `severity category` — category.
**Examples:** `The drought severity index triggered conservation measures after three dry months.` → Index trigger conservation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `가뭄 심각도 지수` — drought severity index.

## 3. hydrological drought /ˌhaɪdrəˈlɑdʒɪkəl draʊt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** drought expressed through unusually low streamflow, reservoir storage, groundwater, or other hydrological conditions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** hydrological drought nhìn water system low: stream/reservoir/groundwater.
**Grammar & collocations:** `hydrological-drought signal` — signal; `monitor streamflow` — monitor; `low storage` — storage.
**Examples:** `Hydrological drought persisted after rainfall returned because reservoirs remained depleted.` → Mưa về nhưng reservoir vẫn low.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `수문학적 가뭄` — hydrological drought.

## 4. agricultural drought /ˌæɡrɪˈkʌltʃərəl draʊt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** drought expressed through inadequate soil moisture or water supply for crops, pasture, and agricultural production.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** agricultural drought là water không đủ cho crop/pasture dù river chưa quá low.
**Grammar & collocations:** `agricultural-drought assessment` — assessment; `stress a crop` — stress; `soil-moisture deficit` — deficit.
**Examples:** `Agricultural drought reduced yields before the city experienced a drinking-water shortage.` → Crop fail trước drinking shortage.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `농업 가뭄` — agricultural drought.

## 5. ecological drought /ˌiːkəˈlɑdʒɪkəl draʊt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** drought conditions that disrupt ecosystem functions, species, habitats, or ecological flows.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** ecological drought nhìn ecosystem function/species/habitat bị water deficit.
**Grammar & collocations:** `ecological-drought indicator` — indicator; `stress an ecosystem` — stress; `ecological flow` — flow.
**Examples:** `Ecological drought reduced spawning habitat even though farms retained emergency allocations.` → Habitat fish giảm dù farm còn allocation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `생태학적 가뭄` — ecological drought.

## 6. groundwater depletion /ˈɡraʊndˌwɔtər dɪˈpliːʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** decline in groundwater storage caused by withdrawals exceeding natural or managed recharge.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** groundwater depletion là bơm ra nhanh hơn recharge.
**Grammar & collocations:** `groundwater-depletion trend` — trend; `deplete an aquifer` — deplete; `withdrawal rate` — rate.
**Examples:** `Groundwater depletion lowered wells and increased pumping costs for small farms.` → Depletion làm well low và cost tăng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `지하수 고갈` — groundwater depletion.

## 7. safe yield /seɪf jiːld/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a withdrawal level considered sustainable under specified assumptions about recharge, storage, quality, and ecological effects.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** safe yield là mức withdrawal sustainable theo assumption rõ, không phải một number vĩnh viễn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `safe-yield estimate` — estimate; `withdraw within safe yield` — withdraw; `sustainable pumping` — pumping.
**Examples:** `The basin exceeded its safe yield during the fifth consecutive dry year.` → Basin pump vượt safe yield.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `안전 취수량` — safe yield.

## 8. recharge rate /riːˈtʃɑrdʒ reɪt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the rate at which water enters and replenishes an aquifer, reservoir, soil store, or other water body.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** recharge rate là tốc độ water quay vào storage.
**Grammar & collocations:** `recharge-rate estimate` — estimate; `recharge an aquifer` — recharge; `infiltration input` — input.
**Examples:** `Paved surfaces lowered the recharge rate by diverting rainfall into drains.` → Paved surface làm recharge giảm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `재충전율` — recharge rate.

## 9. aquifer storage /ˈækwəfər ˈstɔrɪdʒ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the amount of water held in an aquifer that can potentially support ecosystems, wells, or future use.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** aquifer storage là “kho” water dưới đất có thể dùng/giữ ecosystem.
**Grammar & collocations:** `aquifer-storage estimate` — estimate; `store groundwater` — store; `underground reserve` — reserve.
**Examples:** `Aquifer storage buffered the town during the first months of the drought.` → Aquifer buffer town lúc đầu drought.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `대수층 저장량` — aquifer storage.

## 10. conjunctive use /kənˈdʒʌŋktɪv juːs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** coordinated management of surface water and groundwater as connected parts of one supply system.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** conjunctive use quản lý surface + groundwater cùng nhau thay vì tách silo.
**Grammar & collocations:** `conjunctive-use strategy` — strategy; `coordinate surface and groundwater` — coordinate; `integrated supply` — supply.
**Examples:** `Conjunctive use stored winter river flows underground for summer demand.` → Store river water dưới đất cho summer.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `연계 이용` — conjunctive use.

## 11. water allocation /ˈwɔtər ˌæləˈkeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** distribution of available water among households, farms, industries, ecosystems, and jurisdictions under stated rules.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** water allocation quyết định water đi cho ai/theo rule nào.
**Grammar & collocations:** `water-allocation rule` — rule; `allocate a quota` — allocate; `competing user` — user.
**Examples:** `The water allocation protected drinking supply before lower-priority irrigation deliveries.` → Drinking priority trước irrigation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `물 배분` — water allocation.

## 12. prior appropriation /ˈpraɪər əˌproʊpriˈeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a water-rights principle in which earlier recognized beneficial use receives priority over later claims during shortage.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** prior appropriation ưu tiên người có right/use được recognize trước.
**Grammar & collocations:** `prior-appropriation doctrine` — doctrine; `priority water right` — right; `beneficial use` — use.
**Examples:** `Prior appropriation determined which farms received water during the emergency restriction.` → Doctrine quyết định farm priority.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `선취 수리권` — prior appropriation.

## 13. environmental flow /ɪnˌvaɪrənˈmɛntl floʊ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the quantity, timing, and quality of water needed to sustain river, wetland, estuary, or groundwater ecosystem functions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** environmental flow giữ water đủ về quantity/timing/quality cho ecosystem.
**Grammar & collocations:** `environmental-flow requirement` — requirement; `maintain a river flow` — maintain; `ecosystem function` — function.
**Examples:** `The permit reserved an environmental flow for fish migration before diversion began.` → Permit giữ flow cho fish.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `환경 유량` — environmental flow.

## 14. demand management /dɪˈmænd ˈmænɪdʒmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** policies and practices that reduce, shift, or smooth water demand rather than relying only on new supply.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** demand management giảm/shift demand thay vì chỉ build supply mới.
**Grammar & collocations:** `demand-management program` — program; `shift consumption` — shift; `conservation measure` — measure.
**Examples:** `Demand management reduced peak irrigation through scheduling and soil-moisture sensors.` → Program giảm peak irrigation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `수요 관리` — demand management.

## 15. water banking /ˈwɔtər ˈbæŋkɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** storing, crediting, or trading water rights or saved water for later use under defined rules.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** water banking lưu/credit/trade water để dùng sau.
**Grammar & collocations:** `water-banking program` — program; `bank a water right` — bank; `carry over a credit` — carry over.
**Examples:** `Water banking allowed farmers to carry unused allocations into a dry year.` → Farmer carry allocation chưa dùng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `물 은행` — water banking.

## 16. drought contingency plan /draʊt kənˈtɪndʒənsi plæn/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a staged plan specifying actions, priorities, triggers, and responsibilities during worsening drought.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** drought contingency plan chia stage và action khi drought xấu dần.
**Grammar & collocations:** `drought-contingency plan` — plan; `activate a stage` — activate; `priority use` — use.
**Examples:** `The drought contingency plan moved from voluntary conservation to mandatory limits.` → Plan đi từ voluntary đến mandatory.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `가뭄 비상 계획` — drought contingency plan.

## 17. water rationing /ˈwɔtər ˈræʃənɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** controlled limitation of water use through quotas, schedules, restrictions, or emergency distribution.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** water rationing chia quota/schedule/restriction khi supply khẩn cấp.
**Grammar & collocations:** `water-rationing schedule` — schedule; `ration household use` — ration; `emergency quota` — quota.
**Examples:** `Water rationing limited outdoor irrigation to two mornings each week.` → Ration giới hạn irrigation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `물 배급` — water rationing.

## 18. tiered tariff /tɪrd ˈtærɪf/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a pricing structure in which the unit price changes across increasing levels of consumption.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** tiered tariff làm unit price đổi theo mức consumption.
**Grammar & collocations:** `tiered-tariff schedule` — schedule; `charge a higher tier` — charge; `consumption block` — block.
**Examples:** `The tiered tariff protected basic household use while charging more for excessive consumption.` → Tariff bảo vệ basic use.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `누진 요금` — tiered tariff.

## 19. distribution-loss reduction /ˌdɪstrəˈbjuːʃən lɔs rɪˈdʌkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** reduction of water lost through leaks, illegal connections, metering errors, or operational problems between supply and users.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** distribution-loss reduction tìm water thất thoát giữa plant và user.
**Grammar & collocations:** `distribution-loss-reduction program` — program; `repair a leak` — repair; `physical loss` — loss.
**Examples:** `Distribution-loss reduction created more supply than drilling a new emergency well.` → Giảm loss tạo supply hơn khoan well.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `배수 손실 감소` — distribution-loss reduction.

## 20. non-revenue water /nɑn ˈrɛvəˌnuː ˈwɔtər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** water produced and distributed by a utility but not billed because of leaks, theft, inaccurate meters, or unbilled use.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** non-revenue water là water utility deliver nhưng không thu tiền vì leak/theft/meter.
**Grammar & collocations:** `non-revenue-water audit` — audit; `measure unbilled water` — measure; `utility loss` — loss.
**Examples:** `The utility reduced non-revenue water by replacing broken meters and repairing mains.` → Utility giảm water không thu được.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `무수익 수량` — non-revenue water.

## 21. desalination brine /diːˌsælɪˈneɪʃən braɪn/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** concentrated salty water left after freshwater is separated from seawater or brackish water.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** desalination brine là phần salt concentrate còn lại sau tách freshwater.
**Grammar & collocations:** `desalination-brine discharge` — discharge; `concentrate salt` — concentrate; `marine impact` — impact.
**Examples:** `The plant needed a plan for monitoring desalination brine near the marine outfall.` → Plant cần monitor brine.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `담수화 농축 염수` — desalination brine.

## 22. water reuse /ˈwɔtər riˈjuːs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** use of treated wastewater or previously used water for another beneficial purpose.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** water reuse đưa used water qua treatment để dùng lại.
**Grammar & collocations:** `water-reuse scheme` — scheme; `reuse treated water` — reuse; `nonpotable application` — application.
**Examples:** `Water reuse supplied cooling towers and reduced demand on the drinking-water network.` → Reuse cấp cooling tower.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `물 재이용` — water reuse.

## 23. fit-for-purpose treatment /fɪt fər ˈpɜrpəs ˈtriːtmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** treatment designed to meet the quality needed for a specified use rather than applying the same standard to every use.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** fit-for-purpose treatment xử lý đúng quality cần cho use, tránh over/under treatment.
**Grammar & collocations:** `fit-for-purpose-treatment standard` — standard; `treat for a use` — treat; `quality requirement` — requirement.
**Examples:** `Fit-for-purpose treatment made reclaimed water suitable for irrigation but not drinking.` → Treatment đủ cho irrigation, không drinking.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `목적 적합 처리` — fit-for-purpose treatment.

## 24. potable reuse /ˈpoʊtəbəl riˈjuːs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** reuse of highly treated water for drinking-water supply, either directly or after environmental buffering.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** potable reuse đưa treated water vào drinking supply với safeguard cao.
**Grammar & collocations:** `potable-reuse facility` — facility; `produce drinking water` — produce; `advanced treatment barrier` — barrier.
**Examples:** `Potable reuse required multiple treatment barriers and continuous monitoring.` → Potable reuse cần nhiều barrier và monitor.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `음용수 재이용` — potable reuse.

## 25. water-energy nexus /ˈwɔtər ˈɛnərdʒi ˈnɛksəs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the interdependence between water systems and energy systems, including energy needed to extract, treat, move, or cool water.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** water-energy nexus nối water với energy dùng để pump/treat/move/cool.
**Grammar & collocations:** `water-energy-nexus assessment` — assessment; `energy-intensive treatment` — treatment; `interdependent system` — system.
**Examples:** `The water-energy nexus made desalination more expensive during an electricity shortage.` → Electricity shortage làm desalination đắt.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `물-에너지 넥서스` — water-energy nexus.

## Review in context

**Water stress** can be measured with a **drought severity index**, while **hydrological drought**, **agricultural drought**, and **ecological drought** affect different systems. **Groundwater depletion**, **safe yield**, **recharge rate**, and **aquifer storage** shape **conjunctive use**. **Water allocation**, **prior appropriation**, and **environmental flow** determine who receives water; **demand management**, **water banking**, and a **drought contingency plan** support shortages. **Water rationing**, a **tiered tariff**, and **distribution-loss reduction** can reduce **non-revenue water**. Supply options include managing **desalination brine**, **water reuse**, **fit-for-purpose treatment**, and **potable reuse**, but all interact through the **water-energy nexus**.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

**Water stress** có thể đo bằng **drought severity index**, còn **hydrological drought**, **agricultural drought** và **ecological drought** ảnh hưởng system khác nhau. **Groundwater depletion**, **safe yield**, **recharge rate** và **aquifer storage** định hình **conjunctive use**. **Water allocation**, **prior appropriation** và **environmental flow** quyết định ai nhận water; **demand management**, **water banking** và **drought contingency plan** hỗ trợ shortage. **Water rationing**, **tiered tariff** và **distribution-loss reduction** giúp giảm **non-revenue water**. Supply option gồm quản lý **desalination brine**, **water reuse**, **fit-for-purpose treatment** và **potable reuse**, nhưng tất cả liên quan tới **water-energy nexus**.
