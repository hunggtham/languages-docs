# C2+ Vocabulary — Health innovation, implementation, and scale

This lesson follows how a health innovation moves from an attractive idea into routine, equitable, and sustainable practice. The network is **implementation climate → readiness for change → adoption barrier → adaptation envelope → core component → peripheral component → co-design → co-production → implementation strategy → technical assistance → implementation facilitation → audit and feedback → champion role → opinion leader → implementation dosage → penetration rate → reach equity → sustainability mechanism → de-implementation → scale-up pathway → context-mechanism-outcome → normalization process → workflow congruence → task-technology fit → innovation legitimacy**.

## 1. implementation climate /ˌɪmpləmɛnˈteɪʃən ˈklaɪmət/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the shared organizational expectations, incentives, and support that signal whether a new practice is valued and likely to be used.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** implementation climate là “khí hậu” trong organization cho biết innovation có được ủng hộ thật không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `implementation-climate survey` — survey; `support implementation` — support; `organizational expectation` — expectation.
**Examples:** `The implementation climate was weak because managers praised the new protocol but measured only speed.` → Manager nói ủng hộ nhưng metric không support.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `실행 분위기` — implementation climate.

## 2. readiness for change /ˈrɛdinəs fər tʃeɪndʒ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the extent to which people and organizations are willing, able, and prepared to adopt a new way of working.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** readiness for change gồm muốn đổi, có capability và đã chuẩn bị.
**Grammar & collocations:** `readiness-for-change assessment` — assessment; `prepare for change` — prepare; `organizational capacity` — capacity.
**Examples:** `Readiness for change increased after staff received protected time to practice the new workflow.` → Có time practice làm readiness tăng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `변화 준비도` — readiness for change.

## 3. adoption barrier /əˈdɑpʃən ˈbæriər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a factor that prevents or slows a person, team, or organization from beginning to use an innovation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** adoption barrier là vật cản trước bước “bắt đầu dùng”.
**Grammar & collocations:** `adoption-barrier map` — map; `remove an adoption barrier` — remove; `uptake decision` — decision.
**Examples:** `The main adoption barrier was not cost but the absence of an after-hours support line.` → Barrier là support, không phải cost.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `도입 장벽` — adoption barrier.

## 4. adaptation envelope /ˌædæpˈteɪʃən ˈɛnvəˌloʊp/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the range of changes an intervention can tolerate while still preserving the functions and effects that define it.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** adaptation envelope vẽ giới hạn được phép đổi mà innovation vẫn còn là chính nó.
**Grammar & collocations:** `adaptation-envelope boundary` — boundary; `adapt an intervention` — adapt; `preserve core function` — preserve.
**Examples:** `The adaptation envelope allowed local languages but not removal of the safety check.` → Đổi language được, bỏ safety check thì không.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `적응 범위` — adaptation envelope.

## 5. core component /kɔr kəmˈpoʊnənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an essential element of an intervention whose presence is necessary for its intended mechanism or effect.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** core component là phần lõi; bỏ nó thì intervention mất mechanism.
**Grammar & collocations:** `core-component checklist` — checklist; `preserve a core component` — preserve; `causal mechanism` — mechanism.
**Examples:** `Weekly coaching was a core component because it converted written guidance into clinical skill.` → Coaching là phần tạo skill.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `핵심 구성요소` — core component.

## 6. peripheral component /pəˈrɪfərəl kəmˈpoʊnənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a nonessential element that may be changed, replaced, or removed without destroying the intervention's central mechanism.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** peripheral component là phần rìa có thể localize.
**Grammar & collocations:** `peripheral-component option` — option; `modify a peripheral component` — modify; `local adaptation` — adaptation.
**Examples:** `The color of the reminder card was a peripheral component, so each clinic could choose its own design.` → Màu card có thể đổi.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `주변 구성요소` — peripheral component.

## 7. co-design /ˌkoʊ dɪˈzaɪn/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** collaborative design of a service, tool, or intervention by professionals together with the people who will use or experience it.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** co-design để user và provider cùng thiết kế từ đầu.
**Grammar & collocations:** `co-design workshop` — workshop; `co-design a service` — co-design; `user insight` — insight.
**Examples:** `Co-design exposed a privacy concern that engineers had not noticed in the prototype.` → User phát hiện privacy issue.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `공동 설계` — co-design.

## 8. co-production /ˌkoʊ prəˈdʌkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** joint creation and delivery of a service by professionals, communities, and users who contribute knowledge and effort.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** co-production không chỉ design chung mà còn cùng làm và vận hành.
**Grammar & collocations:** `co-production model` — model; `co-produce a service` — co-produce; `community contribution` — contribution.
**Examples:** `Co-production trained local residents to maintain the peer-support network after launch.` → Community cùng duy trì service.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `공동 생산` — co-production.

## 9. implementation strategy /ˌɪmpləmɛnˈteɪʃən ˈstrætədʒi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a planned set of actions used to introduce, support, monitor, and sustain an intervention in a real setting.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** implementation strategy là plan để innovation đi vào practice, không chỉ là plan nội dung.
**Grammar & collocations:** `implementation-strategy map` — map; `introduce a practice` — introduce; `support uptake` — support.
**Examples:** `The implementation strategy combined training, coaching, audit, and local problem-solving.` → Strategy có nhiều layer support.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `실행 전략` — implementation strategy.

## 10. technical assistance /ˈtɛknɪkəl əˈsɪstəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** specialized practical help that enables an organization to install, operate, troubleshoot, or improve a program or system.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** technical assistance là help cụ thể để system chạy được.
**Grammar & collocations:** `technical-assistance team` — team; `provide technical assistance` — provide; `troubleshoot a system` — troubleshoot.
**Examples:** `Technical assistance helped the clinic connect the reporting tool to its existing records system.` → Assistance giải quyết integration.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `기술 지원` — technical assistance.

## 11. implementation facilitation /ˌɪmpləmɛnˈteɪʃən fəˌsɪləˈteɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** structured support that helps a team understand, adapt, coordinate, and use an intervention in its own context.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** implementation facilitation là người/process giúp team tự biến change thành workflow.
**Grammar & collocations:** `implementation-facilitation plan` — plan; `facilitate local adoption` — facilitate; `contextual problem-solving` — problem-solving.
**Examples:** `Implementation facilitation helped nurses redesign the handoff rather than simply telling them to comply.` → Facilitation cùng redesign, không chỉ ra lệnh.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `실행 촉진` — implementation facilitation.

## 12. audit and feedback /ˈɔdɪt ænd ˈfiːdˌbæk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** provision of performance information to a team, usually with comparison to a target or peers, to encourage improvement.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** audit and feedback biến performance thành thông tin quay lại cho team sửa.
**Grammar & collocations:** `audit-and-feedback cycle` — cycle; `feed performance data back` — feed; `target comparison` — comparison.
**Examples:** `Audit and feedback showed that weekend follow-up lagged behind weekday performance.` → Data chỉ ra gap cuối tuần.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `감사 및 피드백` — audit and feedback.

## 13. champion role /ˈtʃæmpiən roʊl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a role in which a respected person actively advocates for an innovation, removes obstacles, and keeps attention on its use.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** champion role là người có uy tín kéo innovation qua resistance.
**Grammar & collocations:** `champion-role description` — description; `champion an innovation` — champion; `remove an obstacle` — remove.
**Examples:** `The champion role rotated so that implementation did not depend on one charismatic manager.` → Rotate để giảm dependence.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `변화 옹호자 역할` — champion role.

## 14. opinion leader /əˈpɪnjən ˈliːdər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a person whose knowledge, status, or relationships strongly influence the attitudes and behavior of peers.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** opinion leader ảnh hưởng peer dù không nhất thiết là manager.
**Grammar & collocations:** `opinion-leader network` — network; `influence peer practice` — influence; `trusted clinician` — clinician.
**Examples:** `An opinion leader persuaded skeptical physicians to test the pathway for one month.` → Peer trust giúp thử pathway.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `의견 지도자` — opinion leader.

## 15. implementation dosage /ˌɪmpləmɛnˈteɪʃən ˈdoʊsɪdʒ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the amount, frequency, intensity, and duration of implementation support delivered to a setting.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** implementation dosage hỏi support được “dùng” bao nhiêu và trong bao lâu.
**Grammar & collocations:** `implementation-dosage schedule` — schedule; `deliver support at high intensity` — deliver; `coaching frequency` — frequency.
**Examples:** `Implementation dosage was reduced after teams became self-sufficient but increased again during staff turnover.` → Support giảm rồi tăng khi turnover.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `실행 지원 용량` — implementation dosage.

## 16. penetration rate /ˌpɛnəˈtreɪʃən reɪt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the proportion of eligible sites, teams, or users in which an intervention has become established.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** penetration rate đo innovation đã vào được bao nhiêu nơi/user.
**Grammar & collocations:** `penetration-rate estimate` — estimate; `reach eligible sites` — reach; `adoption proportion` — proportion.
**Examples:** `The penetration rate was high in urban clinics but low in remote facilities.` → Adoption không đều theo geography.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `침투율` — penetration rate.

## 17. reach equity /riːtʃ ˈɛkwəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** fairness in which groups and communities are actually reached by an intervention, not merely included in its formal target.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** reach equity hỏi innovation có tới nhóm khó tiếp cận không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `reach-equity audit` — audit; `reach underserved groups` — reach; `access distribution` — distribution.
**Examples:** `Reach equity fell when the digital program excluded people without reliable internet.` → Digital program không reach được người thiếu internet.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `도달 형평성` — reach equity.

## 18. sustainability mechanism /səˌsteɪnəˈbɪləti ˈmɛkənɪzəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a feature that allows an intervention to continue through stable funding, ownership, skills, routines, or institutions after initial support ends.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** sustainability mechanism là cái giữ innovation sống sau pilot.
**Grammar & collocations:** `sustainability-mechanism review` — review; `sustain a program` — sustain; `stable ownership` — ownership.
**Examples:** `A reimbursement code became the sustainability mechanism for the new counseling service.` → Code payment giữ service sống.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `지속 가능 메커니즘` — sustainability mechanism.

## 19. de-implementation /ˌdiːɪmpləmɛnˈteɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** deliberate reduction or stopping of a low-value, harmful, or no-longer-useful practice.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** de-implementation là đưa practice cũ ra khỏi routine có chủ đích.
**Grammar & collocations:** `de-implementation plan` — plan; `de-implement low-value care` — de-implement; `practice reduction` — reduction.
**Examples:** `De-implementation removed routine imaging that added cost without improving decisions.` → Bỏ imaging low-value.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `실행 중단` — de-implementation.

## 20. scale-up pathway /skeɪl ʌp ˈpæθweɪ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a planned route for expanding an intervention from a pilot or small setting to wider populations and systems.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** scale-up pathway là đường đi từ pilot tới system rộng hơn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `scale-up-pathway map` — map; `scale up a pilot` — scale up; `wider deployment` — deployment.
**Examples:** `The scale-up pathway added regional training hubs before national rollout.` → Scale theo từng layer.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `확대 경로` — scale-up pathway.

## 21. context-mechanism-outcome /ˈkɑntɛkst ˈmɛkənɪzəm ˈaʊtˌkʌm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an analytic model linking the setting, the process triggered by an intervention, and the result that follows.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** context-mechanism-outcome giải thích “ở đâu, vì sao, tạo result gì”.
**Grammar & collocations:** `context-mechanism-outcome configuration` — configuration; `link context to outcome` — link; `causal explanation` — explanation.
**Examples:** `The context-mechanism-outcome analysis explained why coaching worked in one clinic but not another.` → Khác context nên mechanism khác.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `맥락-기제-결과` — context-mechanism-outcome.

## 22. normalization process /ˌnɔrmələˈzeɪʃən ˈprɑsɛs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the gradual process by which a new practice becomes an ordinary, taken-for-granted part of everyday work.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** normalization process là lúc innovation không còn “mới” mà thành routine.
**Grammar & collocations:** `normalization-process assessment` — assessment; `normalize a practice` — normalize; `routine work` — work.
**Examples:** `The normalization process was visible when new staff learned the checklist from peers without a special campaign.` → Checklist thành routine tự nhiên.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `정상화 과정` — normalization process.

## 23. workflow congruence /ˈwɜrkˌfloʊ kənˈɡruəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the degree to which a new intervention fits the timing, sequence, roles, and information flow of existing work.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** workflow congruence đo innovation có khớp flow đang chạy không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `workflow-congruence test` — test; `fit an existing workflow` — fit; `role sequence` — sequence.
**Examples:** `Workflow congruence improved after the alert appeared inside the chart rather than in a separate inbox.` → Alert vào đúng nơi làm flow tốt hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `업무 흐름 일치성` — workflow congruence.

## 24. task-technology fit /tæsk tɛkˈnɑlədʒi fɪt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the degree to which a technology's capabilities match the requirements and constraints of the task it is meant to support.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** task-technology fit hỏi tool có đúng việc và đúng context không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `task-technology-fit assessment` — assessment; `match technology to a task` — match; `user requirement` — requirement.
**Examples:** `Task-technology fit was poor because the tablet could not work offline in the outreach area.` → Tablet không fit vì không offline được.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `업무-기술 적합성` — task-technology fit.

## 25. innovation legitimacy /ˌɪnəˈveɪʃən ləˈdʒɪtəməsi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the perceived appropriateness, fairness, credibility, and moral acceptability of a new intervention among affected people and institutions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** innovation legitimacy là lý do khiến people thấy innovation đáng tin và đáng dùng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `innovation-legitimacy assessment` — assessment; `build legitimacy` — build; `affected community` — community.
**Examples:** `Innovation legitimacy grew when residents saw their data concerns reflected in the final design.` → Lắng nghe data concern làm legitimacy tăng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `혁신 정당성` — innovation legitimacy.

## Review in context — Part 1

An **implementation climate** and **readiness for change** determine whether an **adoption barrier** can be addressed. An **adaptation envelope** protects the **core component** while allowing a **peripheral component** to change. **Co-design** and **co-production** bring users into the work, and an **implementation strategy** can combine **technical assistance** with **implementation facilitation**. **Audit and feedback**, a visible **champion role**, and a trusted **opinion leader** reinforce the new practice; the right **implementation dosage** supports teams without creating dependence.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 1:**

**Implementation climate** và **readiness for change** quyết định có xử lý được **adoption barrier** hay không. **Adaptation envelope** bảo vệ **core component** nhưng cho phép đổi **peripheral component**. **Co-design** và **co-production** đưa user vào process, còn **implementation strategy** có thể kết hợp **technical assistance** với **implementation facilitation**. **Audit and feedback**, **champion role** rõ và **opinion leader** đáng tin củng cố practice mới; **implementation dosage** phù hợp hỗ trợ team mà không tạo dependence.

## Review in context — Part 2

The **penetration rate** shows how widely a practice is established, while **reach equity** asks who is actually reached. A **sustainability mechanism** keeps useful work alive, and **de-implementation** removes low-value work. A **scale-up pathway** can use a **context-mechanism-outcome** explanation before expansion. The **normalization process** makes a practice ordinary; **workflow congruence** and **task-technology fit** make it workable. Finally, **innovation legitimacy** determines whether people regard the change as credible and fair.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 2:**

**Penetration rate** cho biết practice đã được thiết lập rộng đến đâu, còn **reach equity** hỏi nó thật sự tới nhóm nào. **Sustainability mechanism** giữ work hữu ích tiếp tục, và **de-implementation** loại work low-value. **Scale-up pathway** có thể dùng giải thích **context-mechanism-outcome** trước khi mở rộng. **Normalization process** làm practice thành routine; **workflow congruence** và **task-technology fit** làm nó chạy được. Cuối cùng, **innovation legitimacy** quyết định mọi người có coi change là credible và fair không.
