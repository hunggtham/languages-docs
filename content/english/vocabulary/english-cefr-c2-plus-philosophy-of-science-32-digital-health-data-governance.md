# C2+ Vocabulary — Digital health data governance

This lesson follows how health systems collect, connect, evaluate, and protect data while deploying digital tools. The network is **collection restraint → use-boundary rule → consent retraction → secondary pathway → distributed computation → privacy-preserving linkage → privacy-enhancing simulation → lineage trace → interoperability layer → semantic harmonization → observation-gap process → clinical recommendation engine → notification overload → model drift → calibration error → generalizability check → operator override → traceability record → data trust → stewardship agreement → public-interest data use → digital exclusion → connectivity gap → supplier dependence → cyber incident response**.

## 1. collection restraint /kəˈlɛkʃən rɪˈstreɪnt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** deliberate limitation of the amount, detail, or duration of data collection to what is necessary for a legitimate purpose.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** collection restraint thu thập vừa đủ thay vì gom mọi thứ có thể gom.
**Grammar & collocations:** `collection-restraint principle` — principle; `limit data collection` — limit; `necessary field` — field.
**Examples:** `Collection restraint reduced privacy risk by excluding location details that the screening program did not need.` → Không thu thập location thừa.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `수집 절제` — collection restraint.

## 2. use-boundary rule /juːz ˈbaʊndəri ruːl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a rule that defines which purposes, users, settings, or decisions a dataset may and may not support.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** use-boundary rule vẽ ranh giới cho việc dùng data.
**Grammar & collocations:** `use-boundary-rule register` — register; `restrict a data use` — restrict; `authorized purpose` — purpose.
**Examples:** `The use-boundary rule barred insurance pricing from a dataset collected for outbreak monitoring.` → Data outbreak không được dùng định giá bảo hiểm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `사용 경계 규칙` — use-boundary rule.

## 3. consent retraction /kənˈsɛnt rɪˈtrækʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a participant's withdrawal of previously granted permission for data collection, storage, sharing, or research use.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** consent retraction là patient rút permission đã cho trước đó.
**Grammar & collocations:** `consent-retraction workflow` — workflow; `retract consent` — retract; `participant request` — request.
**Examples:** `The registry honored consent retraction by stopping new analyses and flagging retained records.` → Registry dừng phân tích mới khi patient rút consent.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `동의 철회` — consent retraction.

## 4. secondary pathway /ˈsɛkənˌdɛri ˈpæθweɪ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a later, separately governed route through which data collected for one activity may support another approved activity.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** secondary pathway là route dùng lại data với governance riêng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `secondary-pathway approval` — approval; `authorize a new use` — authorize; `downstream project` — project.
**Examples:** `A secondary pathway allowed de-identified clinic data to support a vaccine-effectiveness study.` → Data cũ hỗ trợ study mới có approval.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `2차 활용 경로` — secondary pathway.

## 5. distributed computation /dɪˈstrɪbjətɪd ˌkɑmpjəˈteɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** analysis performed across multiple data locations without moving all underlying records into one central repository.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** distributed computation đưa analysis tới nơi data nằm thay vì kéo data về một chỗ.
**Grammar & collocations:** `distributed-computation protocol` — protocol; `run a computation locally` — run; `federated node` — node.
**Examples:** `Distributed computation let hospitals compare outcomes while keeping identifiable records inside each institution.` → Hospital giữ record tại chỗ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `분산 계산` — distributed computation.

## 6. privacy-preserving linkage /ˈpraɪvəsi prɪˈzɜrvɪŋ ˈlɪŋkɪdʒ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** matching records across sources while reducing the exposure of direct identifiers and sensitive information.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** privacy-preserving linkage nối record nhưng giảm lộ identity.
**Grammar & collocations:** `privacy-preserving-linkage protocol` — protocol; `link records securely` — link; `direct identifier` — identifier.
**Examples:** `Privacy-preserving linkage connected laboratory and mortality records without sharing names with analysts.` → Analyst không nhận names.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `개인정보 보호 연계` — privacy-preserving linkage.

## 7. privacy-enhancing simulation /ˈpraɪvəsi ɪnˈhænsɪŋ ˌsɪmjəˈleɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** generation or use of simulated data and environments to test methods while limiting exposure of real individuals' records.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** privacy-enhancing simulation cho phép test mà không mở toàn bộ real record.
**Grammar & collocations:** `privacy-enhancing-simulation dataset` — dataset; `simulate sensitive records` — simulate; `testing environment` — environment.
**Examples:** `Privacy-enhancing simulation let trainees practice the dashboard without seeing real patient histories.` → Học viên dùng data giả lập.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `프라이버시 강화 시뮬레이션` — privacy-enhancing simulation.

## 8. lineage trace /ˈlɪniɪdʒ treɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a record showing where a data value came from, which transformations changed it, and where it was used.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** lineage trace kể đường đi của value từ source đến output.
**Grammar & collocations:** `lineage-trace record` — record; `trace a data value` — trace; `transformation step` — step.
**Examples:** `The lineage trace showed that a coding change had altered the hospital's trend indicator.` → Trace tìm ra code change.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `데이터 계보 추적` — lineage trace.

## 9. interoperability layer /ˌɪntərˌɑpərəˈbɪləti ˈleɪər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a technical and organizational layer that enables distinct systems to exchange and use information consistently.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** interoperability layer là lớp nối các system khác nhau.
**Grammar & collocations:** `interoperability-layer standard` — standard; `exchange structured data` — exchange; `system interface` — interface.
**Examples:** `The interoperability layer allowed emergency clinicians to view medication histories from another network.` → ER xem được medication history khác network.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `상호운용성 계층` — interoperability layer.

## 10. semantic harmonization /sɪˈmæntɪk ˌhɑrmənəˈzeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** alignment of terms, definitions, codes, and meanings so that data from different sources can be interpreted together.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** semantic harmonization làm các label khác nhau cùng nghĩa dùng chung được.
**Grammar & collocations:** `semantic-harmonization table` — table; `harmonize clinical terminology` — harmonize; `shared definition` — definition.
**Examples:** `Semantic harmonization revealed that three labels described the same respiratory symptom.` → Ba label được gộp đúng nghĩa.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `의미 조화` — semantic harmonization.

## 11. observation-gap process /ˌɑbzərˈveɪʃən ɡæp ˈprɑsɛs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the mechanism that determines why measurements are absent, delayed, censored, or recorded only for selected cases.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** observation-gap process hỏi vì sao data missing chứ không coi missing là ngẫu nhiên.
**Grammar & collocations:** `observation-gap-process model` — model; `explain missing observations` — explain; `selective recording` — recording.
**Examples:** `The observation-gap process was not random because the sickest patients had incomplete follow-up.` → Missing tập trung ở case nặng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `관측 공백 과정` — observation-gap process.

## 12. clinical recommendation engine /ˈklɪnɪkəl ˌrɛkəmənˈdeɪʃən ˈɛndʒən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a software system that generates patient-specific suggestions by applying rules or models to clinical information.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** clinical recommendation engine biến data patient thành suggestion cho clinician.
**Grammar & collocations:** `clinical-recommendation engine` — engine; `generate a recommendation` — generate; `decision support` — support.
**Examples:** `The clinical recommendation engine flagged a possible interaction but left the final choice to the physician.` → Engine gợi ý, doctor quyết định.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `임상 추천 엔진` — clinical recommendation engine.

## 13. notification overload /ˌnoʊtəfəˈkeɪʃən ˈoʊvərˌloʊd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an excessive volume of alerts or messages that makes it difficult to notice, interpret, and act on important ones.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** notification overload làm alert quan trọng chìm trong quá nhiều alert.
**Grammar & collocations:** `notification-overload audit` — audit; `suppress low-value alerts` — suppress; `clinician attention` — attention.
**Examples:** `Notification overload caused nurses to silence reminders that later proved clinically important.` → Quá nhiều alert làm mất alert cần.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `알림 과부하` — notification overload.

## 14. model drift /ˈmɑdəl drɪft/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** deterioration or change in a model's performance because the data, population, behavior, or environment has shifted.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** model drift là model cũ lệch khi world/data đổi.
**Grammar & collocations:** `model-drift monitor` — monitor; `detect performance drift` — detect; `changing data distribution` — distribution.
**Examples:** `Model drift appeared after referral patterns changed and the original training population no longer matched current patients.` → Data mới khác training data.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `모델 드리프트` — model drift.

## 15. calibration error /ˌkælɪˈbreɪʃən ˈɛrər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the difference between predicted probabilities and the actual frequency of outcomes among cases assigned those probabilities.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** calibration error đo prediction 70% có thật xảy ra khoảng 70% không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `calibration-error plot` — plot; `calibrate a risk score` — calibrate; `predicted probability` — probability.
**Examples:** `Calibration error made the risk score appear more confident than the observed outcomes justified.` → Score tự tin quá mức.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `보정 오차` — calibration error.

## 16. generalizability check /ˌdʒɛnərəlɪˈzæbələti tʃɛk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an assessment of whether findings, rules, or model performance remain valid in a different population, setting, or time period.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** generalizability check hỏi result có chạy được ngoài nơi train/test không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `generalizability-check protocol` — protocol; `test external validity` — test; `new care setting` — setting.
**Examples:** `A generalizability check found that the triage model underperformed in smaller rural hospitals.` → Model yếu ở rural hospital.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `일반화 가능성 점검` — generalizability check.

## 17. operator override /ˈɑpəˌreɪtər ˈoʊvərˌraɪd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an authorized human decision to reject, modify, or bypass an automated recommendation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** operator override giữ quyền can thiệp của human khi machine sai hoặc context khác.
**Grammar & collocations:** `operator-override log` — log; `override an automated suggestion` — override; `human review` — review.
**Examples:** `The operator override was recorded when the clinician had information that the model could not see.` → Doctor có context ngoài model.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `운영자 재정의` — operator override.

## 18. traceability record /ˌtreɪsəˈbɪləti ˈrɛkərd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a durable record of who changed, approved, accessed, or acted on a data product or automated decision.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** traceability record cho biết ai làm gì với data/output.
**Grammar & collocations:** `traceability-record requirement` — requirement; `record an access event` — record; `audit history` — history.
**Examples:** `The traceability record showed which analyst approved the revised mortality dashboard.` → Có lịch sử approval rõ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `추적성 기록` — traceability record.

## 19. data trust /ˈdeɪtə trʌst/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an arrangement in which data is managed by a trusted steward for specified purposes, with duties to affected people and institutions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** data trust là cơ chế giao data cho steward đáng tin với duty rõ.
**Grammar & collocations:** `data-trust charter` — charter; `act as a trusted steward` — act; `purpose-limited access` — access.
**Examples:** `A data trust gave community representatives oversight of how local health records were reused.` → Community có quyền oversight.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `데이터 신탁` — data trust.

## 20. stewardship agreement /ˈstuərdʃɪp əˈɡriːmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a formal agreement defining responsibilities for data quality, security, access, retention, sharing, and harm response.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** stewardship agreement chia duty giữ data an toàn và hữu ích.
**Grammar & collocations:** `stewardship-agreement clause` — clause; `assign data responsibility` — assign; `retention period` — period.
**Examples:** `The stewardship agreement required partners to report breaches and correct unsafe data immediately.` → Partner phải report breach.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `데이터 관리 협약` — stewardship agreement.

## 21. public-interest data use /ˈpʌblɪk ˈɪntrəst ˈdeɪtə juːz/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** use of data to advance a demonstrable collective benefit under safeguards that protect people from unjustified harm.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** public-interest data use cần collective benefit và safeguard, không phải cứ “public” là đủ.
**Grammar & collocations:** `public-interest-data-use case` — case; `demonstrate public benefit` — demonstrate; `protective safeguard` — safeguard.
**Examples:** `Public-interest data use supported outbreak forecasting when the project minimized identifiers and published its methods.` → Public benefit đi cùng safeguard.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `공익적 데이터 이용` — public-interest data use.

## 22. digital exclusion /ˈdɪdʒɪtəl ɪkˈskluːʒən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** inability to access or effectively use digital services because of cost, skills, disability, language, trust, or infrastructure.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** digital exclusion khiến người cần service nhất không vào được kênh số.
**Grammar & collocations:** `digital-exclusion audit` — audit; `bridge a digital gap` — bridge; `offline alternative` — alternative.
**Examples:** `Digital exclusion worsened when appointment booking moved online without a telephone alternative.` → Chỉ online làm nhiều người mất access.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `디지털 배제` — digital exclusion.

## 23. connectivity gap /ˌkɑnɛkˈtɪvəti ɡæp/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** unequal access to reliable internet, devices, electricity, or network quality needed for digital participation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** connectivity gap là khoảng thiếu network/device/power, không chỉ thiếu skill.
**Grammar & collocations:** `connectivity-gap map` — map; `close a network gap` — close; `reliable broadband` — broadband.
**Examples:** `The connectivity gap made video consultations unreliable in mountainous districts.` → Video call fail vì network.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `연결성 격차` — connectivity gap.

## 24. supplier dependence /səˈplaɪər dɪˈpɛndəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** reliance on one vendor or provider for a critical system, service, interface, or technical capability.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** supplier dependence làm hệ thống dễ bị khóa hoặc gián đoạn khi vendor gặp vấn đề.
**Grammar & collocations:** `supplier-dependence risk` — risk; `diversify suppliers` — diversify; `critical vendor` — vendor.
**Examples:** `Supplier dependence limited the ministry's ability to move patient data to a safer platform.` → Một vendor làm switching khó.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `공급업체 의존` — supplier dependence.

## 25. cyber incident response /ˈsaɪbər ˈɪnsədənt rɪˈspɑns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the organized process of detecting, containing, investigating, communicating, and recovering from a cyberattack or serious security event.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cyber incident response nối detect, contain, investigate, communicate và recover.
**Grammar & collocations:** `cyber-incident-response plan` — plan; `contain a breach` — contain; `recover a service` — recover.
**Examples:** `Cyber incident response kept emergency care operating while the appointment platform was isolated.` → Isolate platform nhưng giữ ER chạy.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `사이버 사고 대응` — cyber incident response.

## Review in context

**Collection restraint** and a **use-boundary rule** limit unnecessary collection and inappropriate reuse, while **consent retraction** gives participants a route to withdraw permission. A **secondary pathway** may authorize a new project through **distributed computation**, **privacy-preserving linkage**, or **privacy-enhancing simulation**. A **lineage trace**, an **interoperability layer**, and **semantic harmonization** make data understandable; an **observation-gap process** explains what is missing. A **clinical recommendation engine** can create **notification overload**, suffer **model drift**, show **calibration error**, or fail a **generalizability check**. An **operator override** and **traceability record** preserve human accountability. **Data trust** and a **stewardship agreement** can govern **public-interest data use**, but **digital exclusion** and a **connectivity gap** may still block access. **Supplier dependence** increases fragility, so **cyber incident response** must protect continuity.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

**Collection restraint** và **use-boundary rule** giới hạn việc thu thập thừa và dùng lại sai mục đích, còn **consent retraction** cho participant đường rút permission. **Secondary pathway** có thể cho phép project mới bằng **distributed computation**, **privacy-preserving linkage** hoặc **privacy-enhancing simulation**. **Lineage trace**, **interoperability layer** và **semantic harmonization** làm data dễ hiểu; **observation-gap process** giải thích phần bị thiếu. **Clinical recommendation engine** có thể tạo **notification overload**, gặp **model drift**, có **calibration error** hoặc fail **generalizability check**. **Operator override** và **traceability record** giữ human accountability. **Data trust** và **stewardship agreement** có thể quản trị **public-interest data use**, nhưng **digital exclusion** và **connectivity gap** vẫn chặn access. **Supplier dependence** làm system mong manh hơn, nên **cyber incident response** phải bảo vệ continuity.
