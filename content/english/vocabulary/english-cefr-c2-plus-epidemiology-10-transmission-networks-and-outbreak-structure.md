# C2+ Vocabulary — Transmission networks and outbreak structure

This lesson follows how contact networks shape spread, how investigators reconstruct chains, and how targeted interventions use network structure. The network is **transmission-network centrality → degree distribution → assortative mixing → contact heterogeneity → transmission bottleneck → serial founder effect → superspreading potential → epidemic percolation threshold → transmission community detection → time-varying contact network → multilayer transmission network → contact-edge weight → network intervention → contact-tracing yield → cluster growth rate → transmission chain depth → source-case inference → outbreak reconstruction → phylogenetic cluster → genomic epidemiology → transmission pair → household secondary risk → network seeding → bridge node → contact saturation**.

## 1. transmission-network centrality /trænzˈmɪʃən ˈnɛtˌwɜrk sɛnˈtræləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the extent to which a person, place, or connection occupies a strategically important position in a transmission network.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** transmission-network centrality cho biết node nào nằm ở vị trí nối nhiều route hoặc nhiều group.
**Grammar & collocations:** `transmission-network centrality` — centrality; `rank network nodes` — rank; `high-centrality setting` — setting.
**Examples:** `Transmission-network centrality was high for the transport hub connecting several neighborhoods.` → Hub quan trọng hơn node isolated.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `전파 네트워크 중심성` — transmission-network centrality.

## 2. degree distribution /dɪˈɡriː ˌdɪstrəˈbjuːʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the distribution of how many connections each node has in a network.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** degree distribution cho thấy network có nhiều node ít contact hay một số hub rất nhiều contact.
**Grammar & collocations:** `degree-distribution curve` — curve; `estimate node degree` — estimate; `heavy-tailed distribution` — distribution.
**Examples:** `The degree distribution was highly uneven, with a small number of venues hosting many contacts.` → Một số venue tạo nhiều opportunity.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `차수 분포` — degree distribution.

## 3. assortative mixing /əˈsɔrtətɪv ˈmɪksɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a pattern in which people or entities preferentially connect with others who share a characteristic such as age, occupation, or location.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** assortative mixing là “người giống nhau gặp nhau” nhiều hơn ngẫu nhiên.
**Grammar & collocations:** `assortative-mixing pattern` — pattern; `mix assortatively by age` — mix; `group-specific contact` — contact.
**Examples:** `Assortative mixing by age concentrated transmission within schools and households.` → Mixing theo age làm chain khác đi.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `동류 혼합` — assortative mixing.

## 4. contact heterogeneity /ˈkɑntækt ˌhɛtərədʒəˈniəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** variation in the number, intensity, duration, or setting of contacts across people or groups.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** contact heterogeneity nhắc rằng average contact không đại diện cho tất cả.
**Grammar & collocations:** `contact heterogeneity` — heterogeneity; `model heterogeneous contacts` — model; `contact-intensity variation` — variation.
**Examples:** `Contact heterogeneity made a small group of highly connected workers disproportionately important.` → Average che mất tail risk.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `접촉 이질성` — contact heterogeneity.

## 5. transmission bottleneck /trænzˈmɪʃən ˈbɑtlˌnɛk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a restriction in the number or diversity of pathogen variants that successfully pass from one host to another.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** transmission bottleneck là “cửa hẹp” khiến chỉ một phần pathogen population đi qua.
**Grammar & collocations:** `transmission-bottleneck estimate` — estimate; `cross a bottleneck` — cross; `founder variant` — variant.
**Examples:** `The transmission bottleneck explained why only a small subset of variants appeared in the recipient.` → Host transfer làm diversity giảm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `전파 병목` — transmission bottleneck.

## 6. serial founder effect /ˈsɪriəl ˈfaʊndər ɪˈfɛkt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** repeated reduction in genetic diversity as a population is founded successively by small groups moving through a chain of locations or hosts.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** serial founder effect là mỗi lần spread lại “mang theo” một sample nhỏ của population trước.
**Grammar & collocations:** `serial-founder effect` — effect; `found successive populations` — found; `genetic diversity loss` — loss.
**Examples:** `A serial founder effect suggested that the pathogen reached islands through successive small introductions.` → Mỗi bước làm diversity hẹp hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `연속 창시자 효과` — serial founder effect.

## 7. superspreading potential /ˈsuːpərˌsprɛdɪŋ pəˈtɛnʃəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the tendency of a person, setting, or event to generate an unusually large number of secondary infections under particular conditions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** superspreading potential là potential của event trước khi nó thực sự tạo superspreading.
**Grammar & collocations:** `superspreading potential` — potential; `assess event potential` — assess; `high-contact setting` — setting.
**Examples:** `Crowding and poor ventilation increased the superspreading potential of the indoor event.` → Conditions làm potential tăng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `슈퍼전파 가능성` — superspreading potential.

## 8. epidemic percolation threshold /ˌɛpɪˈdɛmɪk ˌpɜrkəˈleɪʃən ˈθrɛʃhoʊld/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the network connectivity level above which transmission can form a large connected outbreak rather than dying out locally.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** epidemic percolation threshold là mức connectedness để chain đi xuyên network lớn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `epidemic-percolation threshold` — threshold; `cross a connectivity threshold` — cross; `giant component` — component.
**Examples:** `The intervention pushed the contact network below the epidemic percolation threshold.` → Giảm link đủ để chain không nối rộng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `유행 침투 임계치` — epidemic percolation threshold.

## 9. transmission community detection /trænzˈmɪʃən kəˈmjuːnəti dɪˈtɛkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** methods for identifying densely connected groups within a transmission network.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** transmission community detection tìm các “cụm” có contact dày hơn phần còn lại.
**Grammar & collocations:** `transmission-community detection` — detection; `detect a network community` — detect; `dense subnetwork` — subnetwork.
**Examples:** `Transmission community detection revealed separate workplace and household clusters.` → Cụm khác nhau cần intervention khác.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `전파 공동체 탐지` — transmission community detection.

## 10. time-varying contact network /taɪm ˈvɛriɪŋ ˈkɑntækt ˈnɛtˌwɜrk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a network whose connections appear, disappear, or change strength over time.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** time-varying contact network phản ánh contact không đứng yên.
**Grammar & collocations:** `time-varying contact network` — network; `update network edges` — update; `temporal contact pattern` — pattern.
**Examples:** `The time-varying contact network changed sharply when schools closed.` → Policy làm topology đổi.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `시간 변화 접촉 네트워크` — time-varying contact network.

## 11. multilayer transmission network /ˈmʌltiˌleɪər trænzˈmɪʃən ˈnɛtˌwɜrk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a network model containing different kinds of connections, such as household, work, school, and travel links.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** multilayer transmission network xếp nhiều loại contact lên cùng một population.
**Grammar & collocations:** `multilayer transmission network` — network; `combine network layers` — combine; `cross-layer pathway` — pathway.
**Examples:** `The multilayer transmission network showed that travel links connected otherwise separate communities.` → Travel là bridge giữa layer.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `다층 전파 네트워크` — multilayer transmission network.

## 12. contact-edge weight /ˈkɑntækt ɛdʒ weɪt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a numerical value representing the frequency, duration, intensity, or risk associated with a network connection.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** contact-edge weight cho biết một link mạnh hay yếu, không chỉ có/không có.
**Grammar & collocations:** `contact-edge weight` — weight; `assign an edge weight` — assign; `weighted network` — network.
**Examples:** `The model gave a higher contact-edge weight to prolonged indoor interactions.` → Duration và setting đổi weight.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `접촉 간선 가중치` — contact-edge weight.

## 13. network intervention /ˈnɛtˌwɜrk ˌɪntərˈvɛnʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an intervention that targets network structure, connections, or strategically positioned nodes rather than treating everyone equally.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** network intervention đánh vào link hoặc node có leverage cao.
**Grammar & collocations:** `network-intervention strategy` — strategy; `target central nodes` — target; `break a connection` — break.
**Examples:** `The network intervention focused on transport hubs instead of blanket restrictions.` → Targeted action có thể ít burden hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `네트워크 개입` — network intervention.

## 14. contact-tracing yield /ˈkɑntækt ˈtreɪsɪŋ jiːld/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the number or proportion of useful contacts identified per case, interview, or tracing effort.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** contact-tracing yield đo tracing tạo được bao nhiêu contact thật sự hữu ích.
**Grammar & collocations:** `contact-tracing yield` — yield; `measure tracing yield` — measure; `useful contact` — contact.
**Examples:** `Contact-tracing yield fell when interviews occurred several days after symptom onset.` → Delay làm yield giảm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `접촉자 추적 수율` — contact-tracing yield.

## 15. cluster growth rate /ˈklʌstər ɡroʊθ reɪt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the rate at which the number of cases in a connected cluster increases over time.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cluster growth rate cho biết cụm đang phình nhanh hay chậm.
**Grammar & collocations:** `cluster-growth rate` — rate; `estimate cluster growth` — estimate; `case-doubling time` — time.
**Examples:** `The cluster growth rate slowed after the workplace closed and contacts were tested.` → Intervention đổi tốc độ growth.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `집단 증가율` — cluster growth rate.

## 16. transmission chain depth /trænzˈmɪʃən tʃeɪn dɛpθ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the number of successive transmission generations connecting an initial source to later cases.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** transmission chain depth là chain đã đi qua bao nhiêu generation.
**Grammar & collocations:** `transmission-chain depth` — depth; `trace generations` — trace; `deep chain` — chain.
**Examples:** `Transmission chain depth was uncertain because several early contacts were never tested.` → Missing data làm depth khó biết.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `전파 사슬 깊이` — transmission chain depth.

## 17. source-case inference /sɔrs keɪs ˈɪnfərəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** reasoning used to identify the most likely earlier case or source from which a later infection originated.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** source-case inference chọn source likely từ timing, contact và genetic evidence.
**Grammar & collocations:** `source-case inference` — inference; `infer an upstream case` — infer; `source probability` — probability.
**Examples:** `Source-case inference favored the visitor whose onset preceded the household cluster.` → Timing giúp xác định direction.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `감염원 사례 추론` — source-case inference.

## 18. outbreak reconstruction /ˈaʊtˌbreɪk ˌriːkənˈstrʌkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a retrospective analysis that rebuilds how an outbreak unfolded from incomplete epidemiological, clinical, and genomic evidence.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** outbreak reconstruction ghép các mảnh rời để kể lại outbreak theo time và route.
**Grammar & collocations:** `outbreak-reconstruction model` — model; `reconstruct an outbreak` — reconstruct; `evidence timeline` — timeline.
**Examples:** `Outbreak reconstruction combined interview dates with sequence data and venue records.` → Reconstruction cần nhiều layer data.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `유행 재구성` — outbreak reconstruction.

## 19. phylogenetic cluster /ˌfaɪloʊdʒəˈnɛtɪk ˈklʌstər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a group of pathogen sequences that are genetically more similar to one another than expected under the broader population structure.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** phylogenetic cluster là nhóm sequence gần nhau, có thể gợi common transmission nhưng không tự chứng minh contact.
**Grammar & collocations:** `phylogenetic-cluster analysis` — analysis; `identify a sequence cluster` — identify; `genetic relatedness` — relatedness.
**Examples:** `The phylogenetic cluster supported a common introduction but did not identify the exact venue.` → Gene không thay thế mọi field evidence.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `계통발생학적 군집` — phylogenetic cluster.

## 20. genomic epidemiology /dʒiˈnoʊmɪk ˌɛpədəmiˈɑlədʒi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the use of pathogen genome data together with epidemiological information to study transmission and spread.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** genomic epidemiology ghép sequence với who/where/when để hiểu chain.
**Grammar & collocations:** `genomic-epidemiology analysis` — analysis; `integrate sequence data` — integrate; `transmission reconstruction` — reconstruction.
**Examples:** `Genomic epidemiology narrowed the likely transmission window between the two facilities.` → Sequence tăng resolution của timeline.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `유전체 역학` — genomic epidemiology.

## 21. transmission pair /trænzˈmɪʃən pɛr/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a linked pair consisting of a likely source case and the person infected by that case.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** transmission pair là một link cụ thể trong chain, source → recipient.
**Grammar & collocations:** `transmission-pair analysis` — analysis; `identify a pair` — identify; `source-recipient link` — link.
**Examples:** `The study compared symptom timing within each transmission pair.` → Pair giúp estimate interval.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `전파 쌍` — transmission pair.

## 22. household secondary risk /ˈhaʊsˌhoʊld ˈsɛkənˌdɛri rɪsk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the risk that a susceptible household member becomes infected after exposure to an infected household member.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** household secondary risk tập trung risk trong shared living environment.
**Grammar & collocations:** `household-secondary risk` — risk; `estimate household transmission` — estimate; `shared-air exposure` — exposure.
**Examples:** `Household secondary risk was higher when sleeping rooms were crowded and poorly ventilated.` → Home context làm risk khác.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `가구 내 2차 감염 위험` — household secondary risk.

## 23. network seeding /ˈnɛtˌwɜrk ˈsiːdɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the introduction of a pathogen into one or more strategic locations or groups from which wider spread may begin.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** network seeding là đặt “hạt giống” vào node có thể lan ra nhiều route.
**Grammar & collocations:** `network-seeding event` — event; `seed a transmission network` — seed; `initial introduction` — introduction.
**Examples:** `Network seeding occurred when infected travelers entered several connected cities.` → Travel có thể seed nhiều node.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `네트워크 파종` — network seeding.

## 24. bridge node /brɪdʒ noʊd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a person, place, or organization that connects otherwise weakly connected groups in a network.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** bridge node nối hai community vốn ít contact trực tiếp.
**Grammar & collocations:** `bridge-node analysis` — analysis; `identify a bridge node` — identify; `cross-community connection` — connection.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `브리지 노드` — bridge node.

## 25. contact saturation /ˈkɑntækt ˌsætʃəˈreɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a point at which additional tracing effort yields few new useful contacts because most reachable links have already been identified.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** contact saturation là lúc tracing thêm nhưng yield mới rất ít.
**Grammar & collocations:** `contact-saturation point` — point; `reach tracing saturation` — reach; `marginal yield` — yield.
**Examples:** `Contact saturation suggested that resources should shift from tracing to prevention outreach.` → Saturation giúp đổi chiến lược.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `접촉 포화` — contact saturation.

## Review in context — Part 1

**Transmission-network centrality**, **degree distribution**, **assortative mixing**, and **contact heterogeneity** determine how links shape spread. A **transmission bottleneck** or **serial founder effect** can narrow pathogen diversity, while **superspreading potential** increases under certain settings. An **epidemic percolation threshold** marks the connectivity needed for a large outbreak. **Transmission community detection** and a **time-varying contact network** reveal changing structure.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 1:**

**Transmission-network centrality**, **degree distribution**, **assortative mixing** và **contact heterogeneity** quyết định links định hình spread thế nào. **Transmission bottleneck** hoặc **serial founder effect** có thể thu hẹp diversity của pathogen, còn **superspreading potential** tăng trong một số setting. **Epidemic percolation threshold** đánh dấu mức connectedness cần cho outbreak lớn. **Transmission community detection** và **time-varying contact network** cho thấy structure thay đổi.

## Review in context — Part 2

A **multilayer transmission network** can assign a **contact-edge weight** to each connection. A **network intervention** may improve **contact-tracing yield** and slow the **cluster growth rate**. Investigators estimate **transmission chain depth**, use **source-case inference**, and perform **outbreak reconstruction**. A **phylogenetic cluster**, **genomic epidemiology**, and each **transmission pair** add evidence. **Household secondary risk**, **network seeding**, a **bridge node**, and **contact saturation** guide the next response choice.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 2:**

**Multilayer transmission network** có thể gán **contact-edge weight** cho từng connection. **Network intervention** có thể tăng **contact-tracing yield** và làm chậm **cluster growth rate**. Điều tra viên ước tính **transmission chain depth**, dùng **source-case inference** và thực hiện **outbreak reconstruction**. **Phylogenetic cluster**, **genomic epidemiology** và từng **transmission pair** bổ sung evidence. **Household secondary risk**, **network seeding**, **bridge node** và **contact saturation** định hướng lựa chọn response tiếp theo.
