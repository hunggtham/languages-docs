# C2+ Vocabulary — Food-system resilience and agrobiodiversity

This lesson follows how farming communities conserve genetic diversity, manage ecological tradeoffs, and connect production to nutrition, markets, and resilience. The network is **seed-system resilience → farmer-managed seed system → postharvest loss → cold-chain gap → food-environment exposure → nutrition-sensitive agriculture → land-sparing strategy → land-sharing strategy → ecosystem service valuation → payment for ecosystem services → common-pool resource → tragedy of the commons → water-energy-food nexus → climate-smart agriculture → agricultural biodiversity → functional agrobiodiversity → landrace conservation → on-farm conservation → crop diversification → intercropping ratio**.

## 1. seed-system resilience /siːd ˈsɪstəm rɪˈzɪliəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the ability of a seed system to maintain access to diverse, suitable planting material despite shocks, market changes, climate stress, or supply disruption.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** seed network vẫn có giống phù hợp khi drought, conflict, disease hoặc market shock xảy ra.
**Grammar & collocations:** `local seed-system resilience` — resilience; `strengthen resilience` — strengthen; `seed-system vulnerability` — vulnerability.
**Examples:** `Seed-system resilience improved when farmers stored several locally adapted varieties.` → Seed-system resilience tăng khi farmer lưu nhiều variety thích nghi local.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `종자 체계 회복탄력성` — seed-system resilience.

## 2. farmer-managed seed system /ˈfɑrmər ˈmænɪdʒd siːd ˈsɪstəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a seed network in which farmers select, save, exchange, and distribute planting material through local practices and relationships.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** farmer không chỉ mua seed; họ tự select, save, exchange và duy trì diversity trong community.
**Grammar & collocations:** `farmer-managed seed system` — system; `seed exchange network` — network; `manage seed locally` — manage.
**Examples:** `The farmer-managed seed system preserved varieties that commercial catalogs no longer carried.` → Farmer-managed seed system giữ variety commercial catalog không còn bán.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `농민 관리 종자 체계` — farmer-managed seed system.

## 3. postharvest loss /ˌpoʊstˈhɑrvəst lɔs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the quantity or quality of food lost between harvest and consumption through damage, spoilage, pests, poor handling, or inadequate storage.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** food đã harvest nhưng mất trước khi ăn vì moisture, heat, pest, transport hoặc storage.
**Grammar & collocations:** `postharvest-loss assessment` — assessment; `reduce postharvest loss` — reduce; `postharvest handling` — handling.
**Examples:** `Improved drying reduced postharvest loss in the grain cooperative.` → Drying tốt hơn giảm postharvest loss trong cooperative grain.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `수확 후 손실` — postharvest loss.

## 4. cold-chain gap /koʊld tʃeɪn ɡæp/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a missing or unreliable link in the temperature-controlled chain needed to keep perishable food safe and usable.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** một “mắt xích lạnh” bị đứt từ farm tới market, làm food spoil dù production tốt.
**Grammar & collocations:** `cold-chain gap analysis` — analysis; `close the gap` — close; `cold-chain infrastructure` — infrastructure.
**Examples:** `The cold-chain gap between the collection point and the city caused major losses in leafy vegetables.` → Cold-chain gap giữa collection point và city gây loss lớn ở leafy vegetable.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `콜드체인 격차` — cold-chain gap.

## 5. food-environment exposure /fuːd ɪnˈvaɪrənmənt ɪkˈspoʊʒər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the set of foods, prices, marketing, retail access, and social conditions that shape what people can obtain and consume.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** không chỉ individual choice; neighborhood food environment “phơi” người dân trước option, price và marketing khác nhau.
**Grammar & collocations:** `food-environment exposure measure` — measure; `local food environment` — environment; `assess exposure` — assess.
**Examples:** `Food-environment exposure differed sharply between the transit-rich center and the remote settlement.` → Food-environment exposure khác rõ giữa center có transit và settlement remote.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `식품 환경 노출` — food-environment exposure.

## 6. nutrition-sensitive agriculture /nuˈtrɪʃən ˈsɛnsətɪv ˈæɡrɪˌkʌltʃər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** agriculture designed and evaluated not only for production but also for improving diet quality, nutrition, health, and equitable access.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** farm project tính cả nutrient, diet và access, không chỉ tonnes/ha.
**Grammar & collocations:** `nutrition-sensitive-agriculture program` — program; `nutrition-sensitive value chain` — value chain; `design interventions` — design.
**Examples:** `Nutrition-sensitive agriculture added diverse vegetables to a program previously focused only on calories.` → Nutrition-sensitive agriculture thêm vegetable đa dạng vào program trước chỉ focus calories.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `영양 민감형 농업` — nutrition-sensitive agriculture.

## 7. land-sparing strategy /lænd ˈspɛrɪŋ ˈstrætədʒi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a conservation strategy that concentrates intensive production on some land in order to leave other land relatively undisturbed for nature.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** intensify một phần để “nhường” phần khác cho habitat; success phụ thuộc yield, governance và leakage.
**Grammar & collocations:** `land-sparing strategy` — strategy; `spare habitat` — spare; `land-sparing tradeoff` — tradeoff.
**Examples:** `The land-sparing strategy protected a large forest block but required careful fertilizer management.` → Land-sparing strategy bảo vệ forest block lớn nhưng cần fertilizer management cẩn thận.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `토지 절약 전략` — land-sparing strategy.

## 8. land-sharing strategy /lænd ˈʃɛrɪŋ ˈstrætədʒi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a strategy that integrates food production and habitat or ecological functions within the same agricultural landscape.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** farm và nature “share” cùng landscape qua hedgerow, shade, mosaic và low-intensity practice.
**Grammar & collocations:** `land-sharing strategy` — strategy; `wildlife-friendly farming` — farming; `share habitat with crops` — share.
**Examples:** `The land-sharing strategy retained pollinator habitat inside the productive fields.` → Land-sharing strategy giữ pollinator habitat ngay trong productive field.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `토지 공유 전략` — land-sharing strategy.

## 9. ecosystem service valuation /ˈiːkoʊˌsɪstəm ˈsɜrvɪs ˌvæljuˈeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** assessment of the benefits people receive from ecological processes, including provisioning, regulating, cultural, and supporting services.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** làm rõ value của pollination, flood control, soil formation hoặc recreation, không chỉ output market.
**Grammar & collocations:** `ecosystem-service valuation method` — method; `value regulating services` — value; `nonmarket valuation` — valuation.
**Examples:** `Ecosystem service valuation showed that wetland protection reduced downstream flood costs.` → Ecosystem service valuation cho thấy wetland protection giảm flood cost hạ lưu.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `생태계 서비스 가치 평가` — ecosystem service valuation.

## 10. payment for ecosystem services /ˈpeɪmənt fər ˈiːkoʊˌsɪstəm ˈsɜrvɪsɪz/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a scheme that compensates land managers for maintaining or providing ecological benefits valued by others.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** người hưởng lợi trả incentive cho farmer giữ forest, water, carbon, biodiversity hoặc service khác.
**Grammar & collocations:** `payment-for-ecosystem-services scheme` — scheme; `pay land managers` — pay; `ecosystem-service contract` — contract.
**Examples:** `The payment for ecosystem services rewarded upstream farmers for maintaining stream buffers.` → Payment for ecosystem services reward farmer thượng nguồn giữ stream buffer.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `생태계 서비스 지불` — payment for ecosystem services.

## 11. common-pool resource /ˌkɑmən puːl rɪˈsɔrs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a resource from which it is difficult to exclude users but where one person's use reduces what remains for others.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** resource chung nhưng finite: groundwater, pasture, fishery; dùng thêm làm phần người khác còn ít.
**Grammar & collocations:** `common-pool-resource governance` — governance; `manage a resource` — manage; `resource-user group` — group.
**Examples:** `The irrigation aquifer was a common-pool resource requiring shared extraction rules.` → Aquifer irrigation là common-pool resource cần rule extraction chung.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `공유지 자원` — common-pool resource.

## 12. tragedy of the commons /ˈtrædʒədi əv ðə ˈkɑmənz/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the degradation of a shared resource when individually rational use accumulates beyond the resource's capacity to regenerate.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** từng người có incentive lấy thêm, nhưng tổng extraction làm resource collapse.
**Grammar & collocations:** `tragedy-of-the-commons scenario` — scenario; `avoid a tragedy` — avoid; `commons governance` — governance.
**Examples:** `Without enforceable rules, overpumping created a tragedy of the commons in the basin.` → Không có rule enforceable, overpumping tạo tragedy of the commons trong basin.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `공유지의 비극` — tragedy of the commons.

## 13. water-energy-food nexus /ˈwɔtər ˈɛnərdʒi fuːd ˈnɛksəs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an approach that analyzes the interdependence and tradeoffs among water, energy, and food systems.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** water cần energy, food cần water, energy policy đổi food system; giải một sector có thể làm sector khác xấu đi.
**Grammar & collocations:** `water-energy-food nexus analysis` — analysis; `nexus tradeoff` — tradeoff; `model interdependence` — model.
**Examples:** `The water-energy-food nexus revealed that cheap desalination could increase agricultural electricity demand.` → Water-energy-food nexus cho thấy desalination rẻ có thể tăng electricity demand nông nghiệp.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `물-에너지-식량 넥서스` — water-energy-food nexus.

## 14. climate-smart agriculture /ˈklaɪmət smɑrt ˈæɡrɪˌkʌltʃər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** agriculture designed to increase productivity, adapt to climate change, and reduce or remove greenhouse-gas emissions where feasible.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cùng lúc pursue productivity, adaptation và mitigation, nhưng phải kiểm tra tradeoff thật.
**Grammar & collocations:** `climate-smart-agriculture practice` — practice; `climate-smart investment` — investment; `adapt farming` — adapt.
**Examples:** `Climate-smart agriculture combined drought-tolerant seed with improved soil water retention.` → Climate-smart agriculture kết hợp drought-tolerant seed với soil water retention tốt hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `기후 스마트 농업` — climate-smart agriculture.

## 15. agricultural biodiversity /ˌæɡrɪˈkʌltʃərəl ˌbaɪoʊdaɪˈvɜrsəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the diversity of crops, livestock, genes, associated organisms, and ecological processes within agricultural systems.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** diversity của seed, breed, pollinator, soil organism và landscape function trong farm.
**Grammar & collocations:** `agricultural-biodiversity conservation` — conservation; `maintain diversity` — maintain; `agrobiodiversity indicator` — indicator.
**Examples:** `Agricultural biodiversity reduced the chance that one pathogen would destroy the entire harvest.` → Agricultural biodiversity giảm khả năng một pathogen phá toàn bộ harvest.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `농업 생물다양성` — agricultural biodiversity.

## 16. functional agrobiodiversity /ˈfʌŋkʃənəl ˌæɡroʊbaɪoʊdaɪˈvɜrsəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the diversity of organisms and traits that directly support ecological functions such as pollination, pest control, nutrient cycling, and soil structure.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** diversity được nhìn qua function farm cần, không chỉ đếm species.
**Grammar & collocations:** `functional-agrobiodiversity design` — design; `support pest control` — support; `functional trait` — trait.
**Examples:** `Functional agrobiodiversity increased when flowering margins supported predators of crop pests.` → Functional agrobiodiversity tăng khi flower margin hỗ trợ predator của pest.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `기능적 농업 생물다양성` — functional agrobiodiversity.

## 17. landrace conservation /ˈlændreɪs ˌkɑnsərˈveɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** protection and continued maintenance of locally adapted traditional crop populations and their genetic diversity.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** giữ landrace sống và evolving trong local condition, thay vì chỉ lưu seed chết.
**Grammar & collocations:** `landrace-conservation program` — program; `conserve a landrace` — conserve; `local adaptation` — adaptation.
**Examples:** `Landrace conservation preserved drought tolerance that was absent from the commercial cultivar.` → Landrace conservation giữ drought tolerance không có trong commercial cultivar.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `재래종 보전` — landrace conservation.

## 18. on-farm conservation /ɑn fɑrm ˌkɑnsərˈveɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** conservation of genetic resources through continued cultivation and management in farmers' fields rather than only in storage collections.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** conservation diễn ra ngay trong farm, nơi variety tiếp tục adapt và được community dùng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `on-farm conservation strategy` — strategy; `maintain diversity on farm` — maintain; `in situ conservation` — conservation.
**Examples:** `On-farm conservation kept the variety connected to local taste and climate selection.` → On-farm conservation giữ variety gắn với taste local và climate selection.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `농가 현지 보전` — on-farm conservation.

## 19. crop diversification /krɑp daɪˌvɜrsəfəˈkeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** increasing the number or functional diversity of crops grown across time, space, or production enterprises.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** không đặt mọi risk vào một crop; diversification có thể là rotation, mixture, mosaic hoặc enterprise.
**Grammar & collocations:** `crop-diversification strategy` — strategy; `diversify production` — diversify; `diversified rotation` — rotation.
**Examples:** `Crop diversification reduced income volatility when one commodity price collapsed.` → Crop diversification giảm income volatility khi giá một commodity collapse.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `작물 다양화` — crop diversification.

## 20. intercropping ratio /ˈɪntərˌkrɑpɪŋ ˈreɪʃioʊ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the relative proportion of component crops in a mixture or intercropping arrangement.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** tỷ lệ crop A:B quyết định competition, complementarity, light capture và harvest balance.
**Grammar & collocations:** `optimal intercropping ratio` — ratio; `adjust the ratio` — adjust; `row intercropping` — intercropping.
**Examples:** `The intercropping ratio favored legumes enough to improve nitrogen supply without suppressing the cereal.` → Intercropping ratio đủ favor legume để tăng nitrogen mà không suppress cereal.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `간작 비율` — intercropping ratio.

## Review in context

**Seed-system resilience** depends on a **farmer-managed seed system**, while **postharvest loss** and a **cold-chain gap** can erase production after harvest. **Food-environment exposure** and **nutrition-sensitive agriculture** connect farm choices to diets. A **land-sparing strategy** concentrates production, whereas a **land-sharing strategy** combines production and habitat. **Ecosystem service valuation** can support a **payment for ecosystem services** scheme. A watershed is a **common-pool resource**, and weak governance can produce a **tragedy of the commons**. The **water-energy-food nexus** and **climate-smart agriculture** reveal cross-sector tradeoffs. **Agricultural biodiversity**, **functional agrobiodiversity**, **landrace conservation**, **on-farm conservation**, **crop diversification**, and an appropriate **intercropping ratio** strengthen resilience.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

**Seed-system resilience** phụ thuộc **farmer-managed seed system**, còn **postharvest loss** và **cold-chain gap** có thể xóa lợi ích production sau harvest. **Food-environment exposure** và **nutrition-sensitive agriculture** nối lựa chọn farm với diet. **Land-sparing strategy** tập trung production, trong khi **land-sharing strategy** kết hợp production và habitat. **Ecosystem service valuation** có thể hỗ trợ scheme **payment for ecosystem services**. Watershed là **common-pool resource**, và governance yếu có thể tạo **tragedy of the commons**. **Water-energy-food nexus** và **climate-smart agriculture** cho thấy tradeoff liên sector. **Agricultural biodiversity**, **functional agrobiodiversity**, **landrace conservation**, **on-farm conservation**, **crop diversification** và **intercropping ratio** phù hợp làm resilience mạnh hơn.
