# C2 Vocabulary — Live event production and stage automation robotics

This lesson follows a live show from construction and load-in to synchronized performance and safe teardown. The network is **live-event production robot → stage automation robot → robotic lighting rig → automated scene change → robotic camera dolly → programmable camera crane → theater projection robot → automated curtain system → robotic prop handler → set construction robot → scenic painting robot → stage rigging inspection → concert load-in robot → backstage delivery robot → performer tracking system → cue synchronization → show-control network → pyrotechnic safety robot → venue access control robot → event teardown robot**.

## 1. live-event production robot /laɪv ɪˈvent prəˈdʌkʃən ˈroʊbɑːt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a robot used to build, operate, document, or dismantle a live performance or public event.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** robot dùng để build, operate, document hoặc dismantle live performance/event công cộng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `live-event-production-robot workflow` — workflow; `support a touring show` — hỗ trợ show lưu diễn.
**Examples:** `The live-event production robot moved heavy scenery between venues.` → Live-event production robot chuyển scenery nặng giữa các venue.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `라이브 행사 제작 로봇`.

## 2. stage automation robot /steɪdʒ ˌɔːtəˈmeɪʃən ˈroʊbɑːt/

**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** robot di chuyển scenery, platform hoặc stage element theo cue đã lập trình.
**Grammar & collocations:** `stage-automation-robot controller` — controller; `move a platform on cue` — di chuyển platform đúng cue.
**Examples:** `The stage automation robot raised the platform during the chorus.` → Stage automation robot nâng platform trong chorus.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `무대 자동화 로봇`.

## Review in context

A **live-event production robot** and **stage automation robot** move scenery while a **robotic lighting rig** supports an **automated scene change**. A **robotic camera dolly**, **programmable camera crane**, and **theater projection robot** capture or shape the audience’s view; an **automated curtain system** marks transitions. A **robotic prop handler**, **set construction robot**, and **scenic painting robot** prepare the physical world, while **stage rigging inspection** checks what hangs above it. A **concert load-in robot** and **backstage delivery robot** move equipment. A **performer tracking system**, **cue synchronization**, and **show-control network** coordinate the show. A **pyrotechnic safety robot**, **venue access control robot**, and **event teardown robot** keep the venue safe before, during, and after the performance.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:** **Live-event production robot** và **stage automation robot** di chuyển scenery, còn **robotic lighting rig** hỗ trợ **automated scene change**. **Robotic camera dolly**, **programmable camera crane** và **theater projection robot** ghi hoặc định hình góc nhìn của khán giả; **automated curtain system** đánh dấu transition. **Robotic prop handler**, **set construction robot** và **scenic painting robot** chuẩn bị thế giới vật lý, còn **stage rigging inspection** kiểm tra những thứ treo phía trên. **Concert load-in robot** và **backstage delivery robot** chuyển equipment. **Performer tracking system**, **cue synchronization** và **show-control network** phối hợp show. **Pyrotechnic safety robot**, **venue access control robot** và **event teardown robot** giữ venue an toàn trước, trong và sau performance.
