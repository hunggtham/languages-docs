# C2+ Vocabulary — Visualization and evidence communication

This lesson follows how visual displays encode, distort, and communicate scientific evidence. The network is **evidence visualization → perceptual encoding → graphical integrity → decorative clutter → uncertainty display → interval band → color map → sequential palette → diverging palette → perceptual consistency → repeated-panel display → faceted display → manifold reduction → projection artifact → representation space → glyph encoding → annotated trace → explanatory graphic → exploratory visualization → narrative visualization → graphical inference → graphical model → causal diagram → axis truncation → denominator neglect**.

## 1. evidence visualization /ˈɛvɪdəns ˌvɪʒuələˈzeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** visual presentation designed to let viewers inspect patterns, uncertainty, comparisons, or relationships in evidence.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** evidence visualization dùng visual để người xem inspect pattern và uncertainty, không chỉ làm đẹp.
**Grammar & collocations:** `evidence-visualization workflow` — workflow; `visualize a distribution` — visualize; `inspect a pattern` — inspect.
**Examples:** `The evidence visualization showed both the estimated effect and the range of plausible values.` → Visualization cho effect và plausible range.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `증거 시각화` — evidence visualization.

## 2. perceptual encoding /pərˈsɛptʃuəl ɛnˈkoʊdɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** mapping data values to visual properties such as position, length, color, shape, or area.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** perceptual encoding map value data vào position/length/color/shape để mắt đọc.
**Grammar & collocations:** `perceptual-encoding choice` — choice; `encode a magnitude` — encode; `visual channel` — channel.
**Examples:** `Position was a more accurate perceptual encoding of magnitude than area.` → Position encode magnitude chính xác hơn area.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `지각적 부호화` — perceptual encoding.

## 3. graphical integrity /ˈɡræfɪkəl ɪnˈtɛɡrəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** faithful correspondence between a graphic's visual structure and the quantitative relationships in the data.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** graphical integrity yêu cầu hình ảnh không exaggerate hoặc hide quantitative relation.
**Grammar & collocations:** `graphical-integrity audit` — audit; `preserve visual proportion` — preserve; `faithful graphic` — graphic.
**Examples:** `The redesign improved graphical integrity by restoring a proportional baseline.` → Redesign tăng integrity bằng baseline proportional.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `그래픽 무결성` — graphical integrity.

## 4. decorative clutter /ˈdɛkərətɪv ˈklʌtər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** visual elements that add ornament or noise without helping viewers interpret the data.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** decorative clutter là ornament/noise làm chart nặng mà không thêm evidence.
**Grammar & collocations:** `decorative-clutter reduction` — reduction; `remove visual clutter` — remove; `nondata ink` — ink.
**Examples:** `Removing decorative clutter made the small difference between groups easier to see.` → Bỏ clutter làm difference nhỏ rõ hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `장식적 혼잡` — decorative clutter.

## 5. uncertainty display /ʌnˈsɜrtənti dɪˈspleɪ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a visual method for showing the range, probability, or structure of uncertainty around an estimate or prediction.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** uncertainty display làm range/probability/structure của uncertainty nhìn thấy.
**Grammar & collocations:** `uncertainty-display convention` — convention; `display a credible interval` — display; `uncertainty band` — band.
**Examples:** `The uncertainty display prevented readers from treating the forecast line as exact.` → Display ngăn đọc forecast như exact.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `불확실성 표시` — uncertainty display.

## 6. interval band /ˈɪntərvəl bænd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a shaded or bounded region around a plotted estimate representing an interval of uncertainty or variation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** interval band là dải quanh line, biểu diễn interval chứ không phải decoration.
**Grammar & collocations:** `interval-band width` — width; `plot an interval band` — plot; `uncertainty ribbon` — ribbon.
**Examples:** `The interval band widened during the years with sparse observations.` → Band rộng hơn ở năm ít observation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `구간 밴드` — interval band.

## 7. color map /ˈkʌlər mæp/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a rule or palette that assigns colors to numeric or categorical data values in a graphic.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** color map map value vào color; lựa chọn map ảnh hưởng interpretation.
**Grammar & collocations:** `color-map design` — design; `map values to color` — map; `legend` — legend.
**Examples:** `The color map used darker tones for larger concentration values.` → Color map dùng dark tone cho concentration lớn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `색상 맵` — color map.

## 8. sequential palette /sɪˈkwɛnʃəl ˈpælət/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an ordered color palette used to represent progression from low to high values.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** sequential palette phù hợp value tăng/giảm một chiều từ light đến dark.
**Grammar & collocations:** `sequential-palette scale` — scale; `encode ordered magnitude` — encode; `light-to-dark ramp` — ramp.
**Examples:** `A sequential palette made the rainfall gradient legible without implying a midpoint.` → Palette làm rainfall gradient rõ, không imply midpoint.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `순차 색상 팔레트` — sequential palette.

## 9. diverging palette /daɪˈvɜrdʒɪŋ ˈpælət/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a two-sided color palette that emphasizes departures in opposite directions from a meaningful midpoint.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** diverging palette dùng hai hướng màu quanh midpoint như zero hoặc target.
**Grammar & collocations:** `diverging-palette midpoint` — midpoint; `encode positive and negative deviation` — encode; `two-sided scale` — scale.
**Examples:** `The diverging palette distinguished warming from cooling relative to the baseline.` → Palette tách warming/cooling quanh baseline.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `발산 색상 팔레트` — diverging palette.

## 10. perceptual consistency /pərˈsɛptʃuəl kənˈsɪstənsi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** stability in how viewers perceive equal data differences across a visual scale or display.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** perceptual consistency giúp equal numeric step trông tương đương, tránh color/area gây bias.
**Grammar & collocations:** `perceptual-consistency criterion` — criterion; `preserve equal visual steps` — preserve; `uniform scale` — scale.
**Examples:** `The revised map improved perceptual consistency across the full temperature range.` → Map mới làm visual step nhất quán.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `지각적 일관성` — perceptual consistency.

## 11. repeated-panel display /rɪˈpiːtɪd ˈpænəl dɪˈspleɪ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a set of comparable panels that repeat the same visual structure for different groups, times, or conditions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** repeated-panel display lặp cùng scale/layout để compare nhiều subgroup.
**Grammar & collocations:** `repeated-panel display` — display; `compare panels` — compare; `shared axis` — axis.
**Examples:** `The repeated-panel display showed the same outcome for each region without changing the scale.` → Display giữ cùng scale cho region.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `반복 패널 표시` — repeated-panel display.

## 12. faceted display /ˈfæsətɪd dɪˈspleɪ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a visualization split into panels according to one or more conditioning variables.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** faceted display chia chart theo condition để pattern từng context hiện ra.
**Grammar & collocations:** `faceted-display layout` — layout; `facet by region` — facet; `conditioning variable` — variable.
**Examples:** `A faceted display separated weekday and weekend traffic patterns.` → Display tách weekday và weekend.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `패싯 표시` — faceted display.

## 13. manifold reduction /ˈmænəˌfoʊld rɪˈdʌkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** reduction of high-dimensional data by representing its presumed lower-dimensional geometric structure.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** manifold reduction tìm geometry thấp chiều ẩn trong data nhiều chiều.
**Grammar & collocations:** `manifold-reduction method` — method; `embed high-dimensional data` — embed; `local geometry` — geometry.
**Examples:** `Manifold reduction revealed a curved trajectory that ordinary principal components missed.` → Reduction lộ trajectory cong bị PCA bỏ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `다양체 축소` — manifold reduction.

## 14. projection artifact /prəˈdʒɛkʃən ˈɑrtəˌfækt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a pattern created or exaggerated by mapping high-dimensional data into a lower-dimensional visual space.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** projection artifact là pattern do projection tạo, không nhất thiết tồn tại trong data gốc.
**Grammar & collocations:** `projection-artifact warning` — warning; `distort distances` — distort; `low-dimensional embedding` — embedding.
**Examples:** `The apparent cluster was a projection artifact caused by compressing several dimensions.` → Cluster apparent là artifact do compress dimension.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `투영 인공물` — projection artifact.

## 15. representation space /ˌrɛprɪzɛnˈteɪʃən speɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a coordinate space in which objects, observations, or concepts are positioned according to selected features or learned representations.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** representation space là “bản đồ” feature nơi item gần nhau nếu representation tương tự.
**Grammar & collocations:** `representation-space geometry` — geometry; `embed an observation` — embed; `feature distance` — distance.
**Examples:** `In representation space, the two dialects formed overlapping rather than separate regions.` → Hai dialect overlap trong representation space.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `표상 공간` — representation space.

## 16. glyph encoding /ɡlɪf ɛnˈkoʊdɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** representing multiple data attributes through the shape, size, orientation, or internal marks of a single visual symbol.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** glyph encoding nhét nhiều attribute vào một symbol, như arrow vừa direction vừa magnitude.
**Grammar & collocations:** `glyph-encoding scheme` — scheme; `encode multiple attributes` — encode; `visual glyph` — glyph.
**Examples:** `Glyph encoding showed wind direction, speed, and uncertainty at each station.` → Glyph cho direction, speed và uncertainty.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `글리프 부호화` — glyph encoding.

## 17. annotated trace /ˈænəˌteɪtɪd treɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a plotted signal or time series marked with labels that identify events, phases, or interpretive landmarks.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** annotated trace thêm label/event marker lên signal để nối data với story.
**Grammar & collocations:** `annotated-trace figure` — figure; `mark an event` — mark; `time-series trace` — trace.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `주석이 달린 추적선` — annotated trace.

## 18. explanatory graphic /ɪkˈsplænəˌtɔri ˈɡræfɪk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a graphic designed to clarify a mechanism, process, relationship, or argument rather than merely display raw data.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** explanatory graphic giúp hiểu mechanism/process, không chỉ plot observation.
**Grammar & collocations:** `explanatory-graphic sequence` — sequence; `illustrate a mechanism` — illustrate; `annotated diagram` — diagram.
**Examples:** `The explanatory graphic showed how runoff moved through the treatment system.` → Graphic cho runoff đi qua treatment system.

## 19. exploratory visualization /ɪkˈsplɔrəˌtɔri ˌvɪʒuələˈzeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** visualization used during analysis to discover patterns, anomalies, or questions before formal claims are fixed.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** exploratory visualization phục vụ analyst tìm pattern/anomaly, chưa phải polished evidence claim.
**Grammar & collocations:** `exploratory-visualization notebook` — notebook; `inspect an anomaly` — inspect; `generate a question` — generate.

## 20. narrative visualization /ˈnærətɪv ˌvɪʒuələˈzeɪʃən/

## 23. causal diagram /ˈkɔzəl ˈdaɪəˌɡræm/

## 24. axis truncation /ˈæksɪs trʌŋˈkeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** omission of part of an axis range, often making small differences appear larger than they are.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** axis truncation cắt axis để difference nhỏ trông dramatic hơn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `axis-truncation warning` — warning; `truncate the y-axis` — truncate; `visual exaggeration` — exaggeration.

## 25. denominator neglect /dɪˈnɑməˌneɪtər nɪˈɡlɛkt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** failure to consider the population or exposure count underlying a reported numerator or event count.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** denominator neglect chỉ nhìn số case mà quên tổng population/exposure để tính rate.
**Grammar & collocations:** `denominator-neglect error` — error; `report a rate` — report; `population at risk` — population.
**Examples:** `Denominator neglect made the small hospital seem less safe despite its lower rate per patient.` → Neglect làm hospital nhỏ trông kém an toàn dù rate thấp.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `분모 무시` — denominator neglect.

## Review in context

**Evidence visualization** depends on **perceptual encoding** and **graphical integrity**; **decorative clutter** can obscure the signal. An **uncertainty display** may use an **interval band**, a **color map**, a **sequential palette**, or a **diverging palette**, but it should preserve **perceptual consistency**. A **repeated-panel display** or **faceted display** supports comparison. **Manifold reduction** can reveal structure in **representation space**, yet a **projection artifact** may mislead. **Glyph encoding**, an **annotated trace**, and an **explanatory graphic** serve different purposes. **Exploratory visualization** finds questions, while **narrative visualization** guides an audience; **graphical inference** interprets a **graphical model** or **causal diagram**. Finally, **axis truncation** and **denominator neglect** can distort an otherwise careful display.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

**Evidence visualization** dựa vào **perceptual encoding** và **graphical integrity**; **decorative clutter** có thể che signal. **Uncertainty display** có thể dùng **interval band**, **color map**, **sequential palette** hoặc **diverging palette**, nhưng phải giữ **perceptual consistency**. **Repeated-panel display** hoặc **faceted display** hỗ trợ comparison. **Manifold reduction** có thể lộ structure trong **representation space**, nhưng **projection artifact** có thể đánh lừa. **Glyph encoding**, **annotated trace** và **explanatory graphic** phục vụ purpose khác nhau. **Exploratory visualization** tìm question, còn **narrative visualization** dẫn audience; **graphical inference** diễn giải **graphical model** hoặc **causal diagram**. Cuối cùng, **axis truncation** và **denominator neglect** có thể làm méo display cẩn thận.
