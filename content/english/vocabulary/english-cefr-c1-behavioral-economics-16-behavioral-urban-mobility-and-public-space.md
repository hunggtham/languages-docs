# C1 Vocabulary 16 — Behavioral urban mobility and public space

This lesson follows how people choose routes, modes, neighborhoods, and shared spaces when time, safety, cost, and identity interact.

Flow: **travel time perception → route choice bias → peak-load avoidance → parking search cost → transit default → mobility salience → pedestrian risk perception → cycling identity → congestion norm → fare salience → service reliability belief → last-mile friction → neighborhood stigma → place attachment → crowding aversion → public-space appropriation → accessibility heuristic → greenway preference → transport equity perception → urban behavioral policy**.

## 1. travel time perception /ˈtrævəl taɪm pərˈsɛpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a person's subjective estimate of how long a journey feels or will take.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** estimate subjective về journey feel lâu bao nhiêu hoặc sẽ take bao lâu.
**Grammar & collocations:** `distorted travel time perception` — perception; `measure travel time perception` — measure.
**Examples:** `Travel time perception worsened when the station offered no information during the delay.` → Perception time travel tệ hơn khi station không có information lúc delay.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `이동 시간 인식`.

## 2. route choice bias /ruːt tʃɔɪs baɪəs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a predictable tendency to choose some routes because of familiarity, salience, or habit rather than total efficiency.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** tendency chọn route do familiarity, salience hoặc habit thay vì efficiency tổng thể.
**Grammar & collocations:** `persistent route choice bias` — bias; `correct for route choice bias` — correct.
**Examples:** `Route choice bias kept commuters on a familiar road after a faster connection opened.` → Bias choice route giữ commuter trên road quen dù connection nhanh hơn đã mở.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `경로 선택 편향`.

## 3. peak-load avoidance /piːk loʊd əˈvɔɪdəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the tendency to change travel time or activity in order to avoid crowded periods.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** tendency đổi time travel hoặc activity để tránh period đông.
**Grammar & collocations:** `voluntary peak-load avoidance` — avoidance; `encourage peak-load avoidance` — encourage.
**Examples:** `Peak-load avoidance spread demand across the morning after flexible tickets were introduced.` → Avoidance peak-load phân tán demand buổi sáng sau khi ticket flexible được introduce.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `혼잡 시간 회피`.

## 4. parking search cost /ˈpɑrkɪŋ sɝtʃ kɔst/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the time, effort, uncertainty, and stress involved in finding a place to park.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** time, effort, uncertainty và stress để tìm parking.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high parking search cost` — cost; `reduce parking search costs` — reduce.
**Examples:** `High parking search cost made drivers circle the block instead of using the nearby garage.` → Cost search parking cao khiến driver chạy vòng thay vì dùng garage gần.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `주차 탐색 비용`.

## 5. transit default /ˈtrænzɪt dɪˈfɔlt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a transport arrangement that makes public transit the easiest or preselected option.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** arrangement khiến public transit là option dễ nhất hoặc được preselect.
**Grammar & collocations:** `convenient transit default` — default; `design a transit default` — design.
**Examples:** `A transit default sent new residents a route plan and a loaded travel card on arrival.` → Default transit gửi resident mới route plan và travel card loaded khi đến.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `대중교통 기본값`.

## 6. mobility salience /moʊˈbɪləti ˈseɪliəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** how visible and mentally prominent travel options are when people plan daily activities.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mức travel option visible và prominent khi plan activity hằng ngày.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high mobility salience` — salience; `increase mobility salience` — increase.
**Examples:** `Mobility salience rose when the city displayed walking times beside driving times.` → Salience mobility tăng khi city display walking time cạnh driving time.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `이동성 현저성`.

## 7. pedestrian risk perception /pəˈdɛstriən rɪsk pərˈsɛpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a person's judgment about the danger of walking in a particular street or traffic environment.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** judgment về danger khi walk trên street hoặc traffic environment cụ thể.
**Grammar & collocations:** `elevated pedestrian risk perception` — perception; `change pedestrian risk perception` — change.
**Examples:** `Pedestrian risk perception increased after vehicles began turning quickly across the crossing.` → Perception risk pedestrian tăng sau khi vehicle turn nhanh qua crossing.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `보행자 위험 인식`.

## 8. cycling identity /ˈsaɪklɪŋ aɪˈdɛntəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the sense that cycling is part of who a person is and how they belong to a social group.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** sense cycling là một phần identity và belonging social group.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong cycling identity` — identity; `develop a cycling identity` — develop.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `자전거 정체성`.

## 9. congestion norm /kənˈdʒɛstʃən nɔrm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a shared expectation about whether crowding and delay are normal or unacceptable in a transport system.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** expectation shared về crowd và delay là normal hay unacceptable.
**Grammar & collocations:** `tolerant congestion norm` — norm; `shift a congestion norm` — shift.
**Examples:** `A tolerant congestion norm reduced pressure to improve buses during the busiest hour.` → Norm congestion tolerant giảm pressure improve bus vào giờ bận nhất.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `혼잡 규범`.

## 10. fare salience /fɛr ˈseɪliəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** how noticeable the price of a trip is when a traveler compares transport options.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mức price trip noticeable khi traveler compare transport option.

## 11. service reliability belief /ˈsɝvɪs rɪˌlaɪəˈbɪləti bɪˈliːf/

## 12. last-mile friction /læst maɪl ˈfrɪkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the practical difficulty of completing the final part of a journey after the main transport leg.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** difficulty practical hoàn thành đoạn cuối sau main transport leg.
**Grammar & collocations:** `serious last-mile friction` — friction; `remove last-mile friction` — remove.
**Examples:** `Last-mile friction made the train attractive on paper but inconvenient for parents with strollers.` → Friction last-mile khiến train attractive trên paper nhưng inconvenient với parent có stroller.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `라스트 마일 마찰`.

## 13. neighborhood stigma /ˈneɪbərˌhʊd ˈstɪɡmə/

**Core meaning (English):** a negative social label attached to a neighborhood that affects how outsiders judge it and its residents.

## 15. crowding aversion /ˈkraʊdɪŋ əˈvɝʒən/

**Core meaning (English):** a tendency to avoid spaces or times that feel physically or socially overcrowded.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** xu hướng tránh space hoặc time cảm thấy quá đông về physical hay social.

## 19. transport equity perception /ˈtrænspɔrt ˈɛkwəti pərˈsɛpʃən/

## 20. urban behavioral policy /ˈɝbən bɪˈheɪvjərəl ˈpɑləsi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a city policy designed around how people actually notice, choose, and use urban systems.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** policy city design dựa trên cách người thật notice, choose và use urban system.
**Grammar & collocations:** `evidence-based urban behavioral policy` — policy; `evaluate urban behavioral policy` — evaluate.
**Examples:** `Urban behavioral policy combined clearer signs with safer crossings instead of relying on warnings alone.` → Policy behavioral urban kết hợp sign rõ với crossing an toàn thay vì chỉ dựa warning.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `도시 행동 정책`.

## Review in context

Travel decisions begin with **travel time perception**, not with a clock alone. A **route choice bias** can keep people on familiar roads, while **peak-load avoidance** and **parking search cost** change when and how they travel. A convenient **transit default** raises **mobility salience**, but **pedestrian risk perception** and **cycling identity** shape whether the option feels safe and socially fitting. A tolerant **congestion norm** can hide poor service, whereas **fare salience** and a weak **service reliability belief** make public transport seem costly and uncertain. **Last-mile friction** explains why an efficient line may still lose riders. **Neighborhood stigma** affects investment, while **place attachment** protects familiar public features. **Crowding aversion** and **public-space appropriation** show that users experience shared places differently. An **accessibility heuristic** can be corrected by better design, and **greenway preference** may justify a longer but calmer route. Finally, **transport equity perception** determines whether people see change as fair; an **urban behavioral policy** must account for these judgments rather than merely publish a map.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

Quyết định di chuyển bắt đầu từ **travel time perception**, không chỉ từ đồng hồ. **Route choice bias** có thể giữ người ta trên road quen, còn **peak-load avoidance** và **parking search cost** thay đổi thời điểm và cách họ đi. **Transit default** thuận tiện nâng **mobility salience**, nhưng **pedestrian risk perception** và **cycling identity** quyết định option có cảm thấy safe và hợp social không. **Congestion norm** tolerant có thể che giấu service kém, trong khi **fare salience** và **service reliability belief** yếu khiến public transport trông costly và uncertain. **Last-mile friction** giải thích vì sao line efficient vẫn mất rider. **Neighborhood stigma** ảnh hưởng investment, còn **place attachment** bảo vệ feature quen thuộc. **Crowding aversion** và **public-space appropriation** cho thấy user trải nghiệm shared place khác nhau. **Accessibility heuristic** có thể được sửa bằng design tốt hơn, và **greenway preference** có thể justify route dài hơn nhưng calm hơn. Cuối cùng, **transport equity perception** quyết định người ta xem change có fair không; **urban behavioral policy** phải tính tới các judgment này thay vì chỉ publish map.
