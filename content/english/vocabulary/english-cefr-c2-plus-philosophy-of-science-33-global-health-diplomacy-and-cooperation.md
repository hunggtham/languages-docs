# C2+ Vocabulary — Global health diplomacy and cooperation

This lesson follows how governments and institutions coordinate across borders when health risks, supplies, data, and responsibilities are shared. The network is **health diplomacy → cross-border surveillance → mutual assistance pact → reciprocity principle → sovereign consent → jurisdictional overlap → extraterritorial effect → travel health measure → border health screening → mutual recognition → data-sharing compact → benefit sharing → access-and-benefit sharing → know-how transfer → local manufacturing → regional stockpile → pooled procurement → advance market commitment → tiered licensing → humanitarian exemption → global public good → collective action dilemma → policy diffusion → norm cascade → transnational externality**.

## 1. health diplomacy /hɛlθ dɪˈploʊməsi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** negotiation and relationship-building among governments and institutions to advance health goals across political borders.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** health diplomacy dùng negotiation để xử lý health issue vượt biên giới.
**Grammar & collocations:** `health-diplomacy channel` — channel; `conduct health diplomacy` — conduct; `bilateral negotiation` — negotiation.
**Examples:** `Health diplomacy secured agreement on sharing outbreak samples before a regional summit.` → Ngoại giao health mở đường cho sample sharing.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `보건 외교` — health diplomacy.

## 2. cross-border surveillance /ˌkrɔs ˈbɔrdər sərˈveɪləns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** coordinated monitoring of health signals that move across or affect more than one national or administrative border.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cross-border surveillance nối signal ở hai hay nhiều jurisdiction.
**Grammar & collocations:** `cross-border-surveillance network` — network; `monitor a transnational signal` — monitor; `shared alert` — alert.
**Examples:** `Cross-border surveillance detected the same respiratory cluster on both sides of the river.` → Hai bên biên giới thấy cùng cluster.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `국경 간 감시` — cross-border surveillance.

## 3. mutual assistance pact /ˈmjuːtʃuəl əˈsɪstəns pækt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a formal agreement in which parties promise to provide specified help to one another during emergencies or shared risks.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mutual assistance pact biến lời hứa hỗ trợ thành commitment giữa các bên.
**Grammar & collocations:** `mutual-assistance-pact clause` — clause; `provide emergency assistance` — provide; `request support` — request.
**Examples:** `The mutual assistance pact allowed neighboring states to lend laboratory staff during the surge.` → Nước láng giềng cho mượn lab staff.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `상호 지원 협정` — mutual assistance pact.

## 4. reciprocity principle /ˌrɛprəˈsɑsəti ˈprɪnsəpəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the principle that cooperation should involve fair exchange of assistance, recognition, duties, or protections among participants.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** reciprocity principle nhấn mạnh hỗ trợ qua lại công bằng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `reciprocity-principle test` — test; `reciprocate assistance` — reciprocate; `fair exchange` — exchange.
**Examples:** `The reciprocity principle balanced the host country's costs with promised vaccine deliveries.` → Host country chịu cost và nhận vaccine.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `상호주의 원칙` — reciprocity principle.

## 5. sovereign consent /ˈsɑvrən kənˈsɛnt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** explicit permission by a sovereign state for an external actor or agreement to operate within its authority or territory.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** sovereign consent là sự đồng ý chính thức của state có chủ quyền.
**Grammar & collocations:** `sovereign-consent requirement` — requirement; `grant consent` — grant; `territorial authority` — authority.
**Examples:** `The field team could enter only after sovereign consent was recorded through the ministry.` → Team phải có consent của state.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `주권 국가의 동의` — sovereign consent.

## 6. jurisdictional overlap /ˌdʒʊrəsˈdɪkʃənəl ˈoʊvərˌlæp/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a situation in which two or more authorities have partly shared power, duties, or legal claims over the same health activity.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** jurisdictional overlap là vùng quyền hạn chồng lên nhau.
**Grammar & collocations:** `jurisdictional-overlap dispute` — dispute; `share regulatory authority` — share; `legal mandate` — mandate.
**Examples:** `Jurisdictional overlap delayed the decision because customs and health authorities used different rules.` → Hải quan và health không cùng rule.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `관할권 중복` — jurisdictional overlap.

## 7. extraterritorial effect /ˌɛkstrətɛrəˈtɔriəl ɪˈfɛkt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an effect of a law, policy, or decision that reaches people, firms, or conduct outside the authority's own territory.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** extraterritorial effect là policy vượt ra ngoài biên giới pháp lý ban đầu.
**Grammar & collocations:** `extraterritorial-effect analysis` — analysis; `reach foreign conduct` — reach; `cross-border obligation` — obligation.
**Examples:** `The regulation had an extraterritorial effect because foreign suppliers had to meet its safety standard.` → Supplier nước ngoài cũng phải theo standard.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `역외 효과` — extraterritorial effect.

## 8. travel health measure /ˈtrævəl hɛlθ ˈmɛʒər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a health-related requirement or intervention applied to travelers, transport, or movement between places.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** travel health measure quản lý risk gắn với movement.
**Grammar & collocations:** `travel-health-measure review` — review; `apply a travel measure` — apply; `cross-border movement` — movement.
**Examples:** `The travel health measure combined targeted testing with clear exemptions for essential workers.` → Measure target đúng risk.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `여행 보건 조치` — travel health measure.

## 9. border health screening /ˈbɔrdər hɛlθ ˈskriːnɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** assessment of travelers or goods at a border to identify signs, risks, or documentation relevant to health protection.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** border health screening là lớp check health tại cửa khẩu.
**Grammar & collocations:** `border-health-screening protocol` — protocol; `screen arriving travelers` — screen; `health declaration` — declaration.
**Examples:** `Border health screening was paired with referral arrangements rather than automatic detention.` → Screening đi cùng referral, không detain tự động.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `국경 보건 선별` — border health screening.

## 10. mutual recognition /ˈmjuːtʃuəl ˌrɛkəɡˈnɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** acceptance by one authority that another authority's certificates, tests, qualifications, or controls meet an agreed standard.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mutual recognition giúp certificate/test của bên này được bên kia chấp nhận.
**Grammar & collocations:** `mutual-recognition agreement` — agreement; `recognize a certificate` — recognize; `equivalent standard` — standard.
**Examples:** `Mutual recognition reduced duplicated testing for medical devices moving across the regional market.` → Không phải test lại hai lần.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `상호 인정` — mutual recognition.

## 11. data-sharing compact /ˈdeɪtə ˈʃɛrɪŋ ˈkɑmpækt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a negotiated agreement defining what data parties will share, for which purposes, with what safeguards, and on what schedule.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** data-sharing compact biến việc share data thành rule và obligation rõ.
**Grammar & collocations:** `data-sharing-compact clause` — clause; `share data under safeguards` — share; `reporting schedule` — schedule.
**Examples:** `The data-sharing compact required rapid alerts but prohibited publication of identifiable case lists.` → Alert nhanh nhưng cấm list lộ identity.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `데이터 공유 협약` — data-sharing compact.

## 12. benefit sharing /ˈbɛnəfɪt ˈʃɛrɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** fair distribution of gains, resources, credit, or access generated from shared biological materials, knowledge, or data.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** benefit sharing bảo đảm nơi đóng góp cũng nhận benefit.
**Grammar & collocations:** `benefit-sharing agreement` — agreement; `share research benefits` — share; `contributing community` — community.
**Examples:** `Benefit sharing returned diagnostic royalties to the communities that supported specimen collection.` → Community nhận một phần royalty.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `이익 공유` — benefit sharing.

## 13. access-and-benefit sharing /ˌæksɛs ænd ˈbɛnəfɪt ˈʃɛrɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** governance of access to biological resources or associated knowledge together with fair sharing of resulting benefits.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** access-and-benefit sharing nối permission lấy resource với nghĩa vụ share benefit.
**Grammar & collocations:** `access-and-benefit-sharing framework` — framework; `authorize resource access` — authorize; `share resulting benefits` — share.
**Examples:** `Access-and-benefit sharing required consent before researchers exported a locally collected pathogen sample.` → Export sample cần consent và benefit rule.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `접근 및 이익 공유` — access-and-benefit sharing.

## 14. know-how transfer /ˈnoʊ haʊ ˈtrænsfər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** transfer of practical technical knowledge, methods, training, and experience needed to perform or scale an activity.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** know-how transfer chuyển “cách làm được” chứ không chỉ gửi blueprint.
**Grammar & collocations:** `know-how-transfer program` — program; `transfer technical know-how` — transfer; `hands-on training` — training.
**Examples:** `Know-how transfer enabled local technicians to maintain the oxygen-generation equipment after foreign engineers left.` → Local staff tự maintain được.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `노하우 이전` — know-how transfer.

## 15. local manufacturing /ˈloʊkəl ˌmænjəˈfæktʃərɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** production of health products, equipment, or inputs within the region or country where they are needed.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** local manufacturing đưa production gần nơi demand để giảm supply risk.
**Grammar & collocations:** `local-manufacturing capacity` — capacity; `manufacture essential supplies` — manufacture; `regional plant` — plant.
**Examples:** `Local manufacturing shortened replenishment times for basic protective equipment.` → Sản xuất gần làm lead time ngắn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `현지 제조` — local manufacturing.

## 16. regional stockpile /ˈriːdʒənəl ˈstɑkˌpaɪl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a shared reserve of medicines, equipment, or supplies held for coordinated use across a defined region.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** regional stockpile là kho dự trữ chung của nhiều jurisdiction.
**Grammar & collocations:** `regional-stockpile inventory` — inventory; `release reserve supplies` — release; `shared warehouse` — warehouse.
**Examples:** `The regional stockpile released oxygen concentrators to districts facing simultaneous surges.` → Kho chung phân bổ tới nơi surge.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `지역 비축` — regional stockpile.

## 17. pooled procurement /puːld prəˈkjʊrmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** joint purchasing by multiple buyers to combine demand, improve bargaining power, and reduce duplication.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** pooled procurement gom demand để mua chung tốt hơn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `pooled-procurement mechanism` — mechanism; `pool purchasing demand` — pool; `negotiated price` — price.
**Examples:** `Pooled procurement secured lower prices for diagnostic cartridges than each country could obtain alone.` → Mua chung có bargaining power.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `공동 조달` — pooled procurement.

## 18. advance market commitment /ədˈvæns ˈmɑrkət kəˈmɪtmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a promise by buyers or funders to purchase a product that meets specified requirements, creating an incentive for development or production.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** advance market commitment hứa trước demand để kéo innovation/supply.
**Grammar & collocations:** `advance-market-commitment contract` — contract; `guarantee future demand` — guarantee; `product requirement` — requirement.
**Examples:** `An advance market commitment encouraged manufacturers to develop a vaccine for a neglected disease.` → Hứa mua trước làm manufacturer dám đầu tư.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `선구매 약정` — advance market commitment.

## 19. tiered licensing /tɪrd ˈlaɪsənsɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** licensing terms that vary by income level, market, volume, or public-health need so access conditions are not identical everywhere.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** tiered licensing đặt giá/terms khác nhau theo context để mở access.
**Grammar & collocations:** `tiered-licensing agreement` — agreement; `license at a reduced rate` — license; `low-income market` — market.
**Examples:** `Tiered licensing made the diagnostic affordable in lower-income settings while preserving commercial returns elsewhere.` → Terms khác nhau theo market.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `차등 라이선싱` — tiered licensing.

## 20. humanitarian exemption /hjuˌmænɪˈtɛriən ɪɡˈzɛmpʃən/

**Core meaning (English):** a legal or policy exception allowing essential relief, care, or supplies despite a general restriction or sanction.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** humanitarian exemption mở cửa cho relief cần thiết trong rule hạn chế chung.
**Grammar & collocations:** `humanitarian-exemption clause` — clause; `exempt essential aid` — exempt; `humanitarian corridor` — corridor.
**Examples:** `The humanitarian exemption allowed insulin shipments to continue during the financial restrictions.` → Insulin vẫn được đi qua restriction.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `인도적 면제` — humanitarian exemption.

## 21. global public good /ˈɡloʊbəl ˈpʌblɪk ɡʊd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a benefit whose protection or availability crosses borders and cannot be secured efficiently by one country acting alone.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** global public good cần cooperation vì benefit vượt biên giới.
**Grammar & collocations:** `global-public-good investment` — investment; `protect a shared good` — protect; `cross-border benefit` — benefit.
**Examples:** `Reliable outbreak information is a global public good because every country benefits from earlier warning.` → Cả thế giới hưởng lợi từ early warning.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `글로벌 공공재` — global public good.

## 22. collective action dilemma /kəˈlɛktɪv ˈækʃən dɪˈlɛmə/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a situation in which everyone could benefit from cooperation but each actor has incentives to delay, free-ride, or protect its own short-term interest.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** collective action dilemma là ai cũng cần hợp tác nhưng mỗi bên muốn người khác chịu cost.
**Grammar & collocations:** `collective-action dilemma` — dilemma; `free-ride on cooperation` — free-ride; `shared incentive` — incentive.
**Examples:** `The collective action dilemma slowed reporting because governments feared economic stigma.` → Sợ stigma làm chậm report.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `집단행동 딜레마` — collective action dilemma.

## 23. policy diffusion /ˈpɑləsi dɪˈfjuːʒən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** spread of policy ideas, instruments, standards, or practices from one jurisdiction to others.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** policy diffusion là policy lan từ nơi này sang nơi khác qua learning/competition.
**Grammar & collocations:** `policy-diffusion pathway` — pathway; `adopt a neighboring policy` — adopt; `policy lesson` — lesson.
**Examples:** `Policy diffusion accelerated after several cities published evidence that wastewater monitoring worked.` → Thành phố khác học theo evidence.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `정책 확산` — policy diffusion.

## 24. norm cascade /nɔrm kæˈskeɪd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** rapid spread of a social or institutional norm after enough actors accept and reinforce it.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** norm cascade là chuẩn mực tăng tốc khi nhiều actor cùng adopt.
**Grammar & collocations:** `norm-cascade effect` — effect; `reinforce a norm` — reinforce; `social expectation` — expectation.
**Examples:** `A norm cascade made transparent vaccine allocation the expected practice across the region.` → Minh bạch trở thành expectation chung.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `규범 연쇄` — norm cascade.

## 25. transnational externality /ˌtrænznæʃənəl ˌɛkstərˈnæləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a cost or benefit created in one jurisdiction that affects people or systems in another without being fully reflected in the original decision.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** transnational externality là effect spill over qua biên giới.
**Grammar & collocations:** `transnational-externality assessment` — assessment; `spill across borders` — spill; `unpriced harm` — harm.
**Examples:** `Antimicrobial resistance is a transnational externality because local antibiotic use can reduce treatment options worldwide.` → Dùng thuốc một nơi ảnh hưởng toàn cầu.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `초국가적 외부효과` — transnational externality.

## Review in context

**Health diplomacy** builds cooperation, while **cross-border surveillance** and a **mutual assistance pact** organize shared risk response. The **reciprocity principle** can balance duties, but **sovereign consent**, **jurisdictional overlap**, and **extraterritorial effect** complicate authority. A **travel health measure** and **border health screening** should be proportionate, and **mutual recognition** can reduce duplication. A **data-sharing compact**, **benefit sharing**, and **access-and-benefit sharing** govern information and biological resources. **Know-how transfer**, **local manufacturing**, a **regional stockpile**, and **pooled procurement** strengthen supply security; an **advance market commitment** and **tiered licensing** can expand access. A **humanitarian exemption** protects essential relief. Health cooperation creates a **global public good**, but a **collective action dilemma** may delay it. **Policy diffusion** and a **norm cascade** spread workable practice, while a **transnational externality** reminds every jurisdiction that its choices can affect others.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

**Health diplomacy** xây cooperation, còn **cross-border surveillance** và **mutual assistance pact** tổ chức response cho risk chung. **Reciprocity principle** có thể cân bằng duty, nhưng **sovereign consent**, **jurisdictional overlap** và **extraterritorial effect** làm authority phức tạp. **Travel health measure** và **border health screening** cần proportionate, còn **mutual recognition** giảm duplication. **Data-sharing compact**, **benefit sharing** và **access-and-benefit sharing** quản trị information và biological resource. **Know-how transfer**, **local manufacturing**, **regional stockpile** và **pooled procurement** tăng supply security; **advance market commitment** và **tiered licensing** mở access. **Humanitarian exemption** bảo vệ relief thiết yếu. Cooperation tạo **global public good**, nhưng **collective action dilemma** có thể làm chậm. **Policy diffusion** và **norm cascade** lan practice tốt, còn **transnational externality** nhắc rằng choice của một jurisdiction có thể ảnh hưởng nơi khác.
