# C2+ Vocabulary 03 — Viral capsid self-assembly thermodynamics, quasi-equivalence triangulation, and conformational maturation kinetics

## Contextual Network
This lesson explores the biophysics and structural biology of **viral capsid self-assembly**, focusing on thermodynamic equilibria, subunit interactions, and conformational maturation. It details how proteins organize into **pentamer and hexamer capsomers**, adhering to the **Caspar-Klug triangulation number** and utilizing **quasi-equivalence conformational flexibility** to construct closed shells. The framework examines energetic drivers including **electrostatic condensation free energy** and **hydrophobic subunit interfaces**, counterbalanced by the **enthalpy-entropy assembly balance**. Mechanics are dictated by **capsid mechanical stiffness Young's modulus** and the **capsid buckling pressure threshold**, while structural architecture involves **icosahedral symmetry elements**, **viral portal complex vertices**, and precise **viral shell thickness calibration**. During packaging, the **genome packaging ATP motor** acts against internal repulsion, supported by **viral scaffold protein catalysis**. Maturation pathways feature **viral procapsid metastable intermediates**, **allosteric capsomer coordination**, and **proteolytic cleavage triggering** that drives irreversible **capsid maturation conformational transitions**. Finally, the chapter addresses exterior coats like **enveloped viral tegument matrices**, targeted disruption via **viral genome uncoating disassembly**, imaging through **cryo-EM capsid reconstruction**, and biotechnological applications in **engineered nanocapsid drug delivery** and **non-covalent protein oligomerization**, forming a comprehensive structural virology model.

## 1. viral capsid self-assembly /ˌvaɪrəl ˈkæpsɪd sɛlf-əˈsɛmbli/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the spontaneous thermodynamic process by which individual coat proteins organize into a symmetrical viral shell.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** quá trình thermodynamic spontaneous mà các protein áo (coat protein) tự tổ chức thành lớp vỏ virus đối xứng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `spontaneous viral capsid self-assembly` — tự lắp ráp vỏ capsid tự phát; `study viral capsid self-assembly` — nghiên cứu sự tự lắp ráp vỏ capsid; `mediate viral capsid self-assembly` — trung gian cho sự tự lắp ráp capsid.
**Examples:** `Spontaneous viral capsid self-assembly proceeds without external energy input when subunit concentration exceeds a critical threshold.` → Quá trình tự lắp ráp vỏ capsid tự phát diễn ra không cần đầu tư năng lượng bên ngoài khi nồng độ tiểu đơn vị vượt quá ngưỡng tới hạn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `바이러스 캡시드 자가조립` (viral capsid self-assembly).

## 2. Caspar-Klug triangulation number /ˈkæspər kluːɡ traɪˌæŋɡjʊˈleɪʃən ˈnʌmbər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a geometric classification number (T) describing the organization of protein subunits in icosahedral viral capsids.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** số phân loại hình học (T) mô tả sự tổ chức của các tiểu đơn vị protein trong lớp vỏ capsid hình khối 2D/3D (icosahedral).
**Grammar & collocations:** `calculate the Caspar-Klug triangulation number` — tính toán số tam giác hóa Caspar-Klug; `higher triangulation number` — số tam giác hóa cao hơn; `triangulation number T = 3` — số tam giác hóa T = 3.
**Examples:** `The picornavirus exhibits a Caspar-Klug triangulation number of 3, accommodating 180 structural subunits.` → Virus bại liệt hiển thị số tam giác hóa Caspar-Klug là 3, chứa 180 tiểu đơn vị cấu trúc.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `카스퍼-클루그 삼각분할수` (Caspar-Klug triangulation number).
