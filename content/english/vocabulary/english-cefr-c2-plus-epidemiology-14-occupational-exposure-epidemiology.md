# C2+ Vocabulary — Occupational exposure epidemiology

This lesson follows how researchers reconstruct workplace exposure, compare worker populations, and connect hazards with long-term outcomes. The network is **job-exposure matrix → cumulative exposure index → task-based exposure assessment → personal sampling → area sampling → exposure reconstruction → dose reconstruction → healthy-worker effect → survivor bias → latency period → attributable fraction → standardized incidence ratio → standardized mortality ratio → sentinel occupation → work-relatedness → return-to-work surveillance → ergonomic risk factor → shift-work exposure → workplace psychosocial stressor → heat strain → respiratory fit factor → control hierarchy sequence → occupational exposure limit → occupational cohort → internal comparison group**.

## 1. job-exposure matrix /dʒɑb ɪkˈspoʊʒər ˈmeɪtrɪks/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a table linking occupations or job periods with estimated exposure levels to specific hazards.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** job-exposure matrix nối job title/time với hazard exposure, kể cả khi không có sample từng người.
**Grammar & collocations:** `job-exposure matrix` — matrix; `assign exposure by occupation` — assign; `historical exposure estimate` — estimate.
**Examples:** `The job-exposure matrix estimated solvent exposure for workers hired before monitoring began.` → Matrix giúp nhìn lịch sử thiếu sample.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `직업-노출 행렬` — job-exposure matrix.

## 2. cumulative exposure index /ˈkjuːmjələtɪv ɪkˈspoʊʒər ˈɪndɛks/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a summary measure combining exposure intensity and duration across time.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cumulative exposure index cộng intensity × time để biểu diễn burden exposure dài hạn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `cumulative-exposure index` — index; `accumulate exposure` — accumulate; `exposure-duration score` — score.
**Examples:** `The cumulative exposure index was highest among maintenance workers with repeated shutdown assignments.` → Repeated task làm burden tích lũy.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `누적 노출 지수` — cumulative exposure index.

## 3. task-based exposure assessment /tæsk beɪst ɪkˈspoʊʒər əˈsɛsmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** evaluation of exposure by examining the specific tasks, materials, tools, and conditions involved in work.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** task-based exposure assessment nhìn task thật thay vì chỉ nhìn job title.
**Grammar & collocations:** `task-based exposure assessment` — assessment; `assess task exposure` — assess; `work-process observation` — observation.
**Examples:** `Task-based exposure assessment found short high-intensity cleaning steps hidden inside a low-risk job title.` → Title trung bình có thể che peak.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `작업 기반 노출 평가` — task-based exposure assessment.

## 4. personal sampling /ˈpɜrsənəl ˈsæmplɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** collection of air, dust, noise, or biological samples near a worker's breathing zone or body during work.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** personal sampling đo exposure gần người worker hơn area monitor.
**Grammar & collocations:** `personal-sampling pump` — pump; `collect a breathing-zone sample` — collect; `worker exposure` — exposure.
**Examples:** `Personal sampling showed that exposure peaked during loading rather than routine production.` → Timing của task quan trọng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `개인 시료 채취` — personal sampling.

## 5. area sampling /ˈɛriə ˈsæmplɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** collection of environmental samples at fixed locations to characterize conditions in a workplace or zone.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** area sampling đo exposure của không gian, không trực tiếp của từng worker.
**Grammar & collocations:** `area-sampling station` — station; `sample a work zone` — sample; `fixed-location monitor` — monitor.
**Examples:** `Area sampling mapped a contaminated corner before workers were assigned new tasks.` → Map không gian giúp control source.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `지역 시료 채취` — area sampling.

## 6. exposure reconstruction /ɪkˈspoʊʒər ˌriːkənˈstrʌkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** retrospective estimation of what hazards, intensities, and durations a person or group experienced.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** exposure reconstruction dựng lại exposure quá khứ từ records, interviews và measurements.
**Grammar & collocations:** `exposure-reconstruction model` — model; `reconstruct historical exposure` — reconstruct; `work-history record` — record.
**Examples:** `Exposure reconstruction combined work histories with archived ventilation data.` → Past exposure cần ghép nhiều evidence.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `노출 재구성` — exposure reconstruction.

## 7. dose reconstruction /doʊs ˌriːkənˈstrʌkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** estimation of the amount of a hazard that entered or affected a person's body over time.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** dose reconstruction đi xa hơn “có exposure” để ước amount vào cơ thể.
**Grammar & collocations:** `dose-reconstruction estimate` — estimate; `reconstruct absorbed dose` — reconstruct; `internal dose` — dose.
**Examples:** `Dose reconstruction used inhalation rates and protective-equipment records to estimate internal dose.` → Route và protection cùng quan trọng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `용량 재구성` — dose reconstruction.

## 8. healthy-worker effect /ˈhɛlθi ˈwɜrkər ɪˈfɛkt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** apparent lower disease rates among employed people because workers are generally healthier than the wider population at entry or remain employed while healthier.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** healthy-worker effect làm occupational cohort trông khỏe hơn general population vì selection.
**Grammar & collocations:** `healthy-worker effect` — effect; `compare workers with the general population` — compare; `employment selection` — selection.
**Examples:** `The healthy-worker effect masked a modest excess of respiratory disease among exposed staff.` → Comparator sai có thể che harm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `건강한 근로자 효과` — healthy-worker effect.

## 9. survivor bias /sərˈvaɪvər baɪəs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** bias caused by analyzing only people who remain employed, alive, enrolled, or observable after earlier selection or loss.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** survivor bias chỉ nhìn người còn lại và quên người đã rời system vì harm.
**Grammar & collocations:** `survivor-bias risk` — risk; `condition on survival` — condition; `loss from follow-up` — loss.
**Examples:** `Survivor bias understated symptoms because workers who became ill had already left the job.` → Người rời đi cũng là data.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `생존자 편향` — survivor bias.

## 10. latency period /ˈleɪtənsi ˈpɪriəd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the time between an exposure or initiating event and the appearance of a disease or measurable outcome.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** latency period là khoảng chờ giữa exposure và disease.
**Grammar & collocations:** `latency-period estimate` — estimate; `long disease latency` — latency; `time since exposure` — time.
**Examples:** `The long latency period required employment records from several decades.` → Follow-up dài mới thấy outcome.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `잠복 기간` — latency period.

## 11. attributable fraction /əˈtrɪbjətəbəl ˈfrækʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the proportion of cases or outcomes in a specified population that can be attributed to a particular exposure under stated assumptions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** attributable fraction ước phần burden có thể quy cho exposure.
**Grammar & collocations:** `attributable-fraction estimate` — estimate; `attribute disease burden` — attribute; `preventable fraction` — fraction.
**Examples:** `The attributable fraction suggested that reducing dust exposure could prevent a meaningful share of cases.` → Fraction giúp nghĩ về prevention.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `기여 분율` — attributable fraction.

## 12. standardized incidence ratio /ˈstændərˌdaɪzd ˈɪnsɪdəns ˈreɪʃioʊ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the ratio of observed incident cases in a study group to the number expected from a reference population after standardization.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** standardized incidence ratio so observed case với expected case theo population chuẩn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `standardized-incidence ratio` — ratio; `compare observed and expected cases` — compare; `reference rate` — rate.
**Examples:** `The standardized incidence ratio exceeded one for a rare cancer among exposed workers.` → Trên one gợi excess so với expected.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `표준화 발생비` — standardized incidence ratio.

## 13. standardized mortality ratio /ˈstændərˌdaɪzd mɔrˈtæləti ˈreɪʃioʊ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the ratio of observed deaths in a study group to the number expected from a reference population after standardization.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** standardized mortality ratio so death observed với death expected.
**Grammar & collocations:** `standardized-mortality ratio` — ratio; `estimate expected mortality` — estimate; `reference mortality rate` — rate.
**Examples:** `The standardized mortality ratio was interpreted alongside age, tenure, and exposure history.` → Ratio cần context.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `표준화 사망비` — standardized mortality ratio.

## 14. sentinel occupation /ˈsɛntənəl ˌɑkjəˈpeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an occupation monitored because its workers may provide an early signal of a hazard or emerging disease.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** sentinel occupation là nghề “canh” signal exposure sớm.
**Grammar & collocations:** `sentinel-occupation surveillance` — surveillance; `monitor a sentinel occupation` — monitor; `early occupational signal` — signal.
**Examples:** `Healthcare aides served as a sentinel occupation for emerging respiratory exposure.` → Nghề gần hazard báo sớm hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `감시 표지 직업` — sentinel occupation.

## 15. work-relatedness /ˈwɜrk rɪˈleɪtɪdnəs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the degree to which a disease, injury, or exposure is caused or materially contributed to by work.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** work-relatedness hỏi outcome có liên quan causal tới job/work condition không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `work-relatedness assessment` — assessment; `establish work-relatedness` — establish; `occupational contribution` — contribution.
**Examples:** `The work-relatedness review considered both workplace exposure and nonwork sources.` → Attribution cần nhìn toàn context.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `업무 관련성` — work-relatedness.

## 16. return-to-work surveillance /rɪˈtɜrn tə wɜrk sərˈveɪləns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** monitoring health, symptoms, and safety after workers resume duties following illness, injury, or exposure.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** return-to-work surveillance theo dõi worker sau khi quay lại, không coi recovery là one-time event.
**Grammar & collocations:** `return-to-work surveillance` — surveillance; `monitor work reintegration` — monitor; `fitness-for-duty review` — review.
**Examples:** `Return-to-work surveillance identified recurrent symptoms after a gradual schedule began.` → Return cần follow-up.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `복귀 후 감시` — return-to-work surveillance.

## 17. ergonomic risk factor /ˌɜrɡəˈnɑmɪk rɪsk ˈfæktər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a work feature such as force, repetition, awkward posture, or vibration that increases musculoskeletal risk.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** ergonomic risk factor là feature của task làm cơ thể chịu load sai hoặc lặp quá nhiều.
**Grammar & collocations:** `ergonomic-risk factor` — factor; `assess repetitive lifting` — assess; `musculoskeletal strain` — strain.
**Examples:** `The ergonomic risk factor was repeated overhead lifting without adjustable equipment.` → Task design ảnh hưởng injury.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `인체공학적 위험 요인` — ergonomic risk factor.

## 18. shift-work exposure /ʃɪft wɜrk ɪkˈspoʊʒər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** health-relevant exposure associated with working at night, rotating schedules, or irregular hours.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** shift-work exposure gồm sleep disruption, light timing, meal timing và schedule instability.
**Grammar & collocations:** `shift-work exposure` — exposure; `rotate night shifts` — rotate; `circadian disruption` — disruption.
**Examples:** `Shift-work exposure was evaluated together with sleep duration and recovery time.` → Schedule không chỉ là clock time.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `교대근무 노출` — shift-work exposure.

## 19. workplace psychosocial stressor /ˈwɜrkˌpleɪs ˌsaɪkoʊˈsoʊʃəl ˈstrɛsər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a work-related social or organizational condition that can produce stress, such as low control, harassment, or chronic understaffing.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** workplace psychosocial stressor đến từ cách work được tổ chức và quan hệ trong workplace.
**Grammar & collocations:** `workplace-psychosocial stressor` — stressor; `assess job control` — assess; `organizational strain` — strain.
**Examples:** `Chronic understaffing was a workplace psychosocial stressor linked to sleep problems and turnover.` → Work design ảnh hưởng health.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `직장 심리사회적 스트레스 요인` — workplace psychosocial stressor.

## 20. heat strain /hiːt streɪn/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the physiological stress produced when heat exposure and workload challenge the body's ability to regulate temperature.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** heat strain là effect trên body, khác với chỉ temperature ngoài môi trường.
**Grammar & collocations:** `heat-strain assessment` — assessment; `monitor core temperature` — monitor; `thermal workload` — workload.
**Examples:** `Heat strain increased during peak production when workers wore impermeable protective clothing.` → Clothing và workload cộng với heat.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `열 부담` — heat strain.

## 21. respiratory fit factor /rɪˈspɪrəˌtɔri fɪt ˈfæktər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a measure of how well a respirator seals and reduces contaminated air reaching the wearer.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** respiratory fit factor đo seal của respirator giữa mặt và mask.
**Grammar & collocations:** `respiratory-fit factor` — factor; `perform a fit test` — perform; `respirator seal` — seal.
**Examples:** `A low respiratory fit factor led the team to replace the respirator size.` → Seal kém làm protection giảm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `호흡기 밀착 계수` — respiratory fit factor.

## 22. control hierarchy sequence /kənˈtroʊl ˈhaɪəˌrɑrki ˈsiːkwəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the ordered preference for eliminating a hazard, substituting it, engineering it out, using administrative controls, and finally relying on personal protection.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** control hierarchy sequence nhắc ưu tiên control ở source trước khi đẩy burden cho worker.
**Grammar & collocations:** `control-hierarchy sequence` — sequence; `apply the hierarchy` — apply; `engineering control` — control.
**Examples:** `The control hierarchy sequence favored enclosure before adding more personal protective equipment.` → Source control bền hơn PPE đơn lẻ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `통제 계층 순서` — control hierarchy sequence.

## 23. occupational exposure limit /ˌɑkjəˈpeɪʃənəl ɪkˈspoʊʒər ˈlɪmət/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a defined concentration or level of workplace exposure intended to protect workers over a specified period.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** occupational exposure limit là benchmark để so measured exposure với mức acceptable.
**Grammar & collocations:** `occupational-exposure limit` — limit; `compare exposure with a limit` — compare; `time-weighted average` — average.
**Examples:** `The eight-hour result exceeded the occupational exposure limit despite a short peak being missed.` → Average và peak đều cần xem.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `직업성 노출 한계` — occupational exposure limit.

## 24. occupational cohort /ˌɑkjəˈpeɪʃənəl ˈkoʊˌhɔrt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a defined group of workers followed to study relationships between workplace exposures and health outcomes.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** occupational cohort là group worker được follow theo exposure và outcome.
**Grammar & collocations:** `occupational-cohort study` — study; `follow a worker cohort` — follow; `health outcome` — outcome.
**Examples:** `The occupational cohort linked employment records with cancer registry data.` → Cohort cần record linkage dài hạn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `직업 코호트` — occupational cohort.

## 25. internal comparison group /ɪnˈtɜrnəl kəmˈpærəsən ɡruːp/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a comparison group drawn from the same organization or workforce but with lower or different exposure.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** internal comparison group giúp so worker với worker, giảm khác biệt health cơ bản giữa worker và general population.
**Grammar & collocations:** `internal-comparison group` — group; `compare exposure strata` — compare; `lower-exposure worker` — worker.
**Examples:** `The internal comparison group included office staff with similar employment benefits but little solvent exposure.` → Comparator gần hơn về background.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `내부 비교 집단` — internal comparison group.

## Review in context — Part 1

A **job-exposure matrix**, **cumulative exposure index**, and **task-based exposure assessment** estimate historical burden. **Personal sampling** measures a worker's breathing zone, while **area sampling** maps a space. **Exposure reconstruction** and **dose reconstruction** address missing records. The **healthy-worker effect**, **survivor bias**, and a long **latency period** can distort associations. An **attributable fraction**, **standardized incidence ratio**, and **standardized mortality ratio** summarize population impact.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 1:**

**Job-exposure matrix**, **cumulative exposure index** và **task-based exposure assessment** ước tính burden quá khứ. **Personal sampling** đo vùng thở của worker, còn **area sampling** lập bản đồ không gian. **Exposure reconstruction** và **dose reconstruction** xử lý record thiếu. **Healthy-worker effect**, **survivor bias** và **latency period** dài có thể làm association lệch. **Attributable fraction**, **standardized incidence ratio** và **standardized mortality ratio** tóm tắt impact ở population.

## Review in context — Part 2

A **sentinel occupation** may reveal a hazard early, while **work-relatedness** connects an outcome to work conditions. **Return-to-work surveillance** follows recovery. An **ergonomic risk factor**, **shift-work exposure**, **workplace psychosocial stressor**, or **heat strain** can affect health through different pathways. A **respiratory fit factor**, **control hierarchy sequence**, and **occupational exposure limit** guide prevention. An **occupational cohort** becomes more credible when it uses an **internal comparison group**.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 2:**

**Sentinel occupation** có thể phát hiện hazard sớm, còn **work-relatedness** nối outcome với điều kiện work. **Return-to-work surveillance** theo dõi recovery. **Ergonomic risk factor**, **shift-work exposure**, **workplace psychosocial stressor** hoặc **heat strain** ảnh hưởng health qua các route khác nhau. **Respiratory fit factor**, **control hierarchy sequence** và **occupational exposure limit** định hướng prevention. **Occupational cohort** đáng tin hơn khi dùng **internal comparison group**.
