# C1 Vocabulary 08 — Behavioral health insurance and care decisions

This lesson follows how people perceive medical risk, insurance choices, treatment costs, and health decisions under uncertainty.

Flow: **insurance framing → ambiguity aversion insurance → insurance inertia → coverage lapse → deductible salience → premium salience → health insurance choice → adverse selection belief → moral hazard belief → health behavior bias → status quo insurance → default insurance → preventive care salience → present bias health → health optimism → risk compensation → behavioral health economics → medical debt perception → financial toxicity → care avoidance**.

## 1. insurance framing /ɪnˈʃʊrəns ˈfreɪmɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the way an insurance option, risk, or payment is described, which can influence how people evaluate it.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cách describe insurance option, risk hoặc payment ảnh hưởng evaluation.
**Grammar & collocations:** `loss-focused insurance framing` — framing; `change insurance framing` — change.
**Examples:** `Insurance framing as protection against bills increased enrollment more than framing as a monthly cost.` → Framing insurance như bảo vệ bill tăng enrollment hơn framing như monthly cost.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `보험 프레이밍`.

## 2. ambiguity aversion insurance /ˌæmbəˈɡjuəti əˈvɝʒən ɪnˈʃʊrəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** reluctance to buy coverage when the probability or consequences of future loss are unclear.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** reluctance buy coverage khi probability hoặc consequence loss future unclear.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong ambiguity aversion insurance` — aversion; `model ambiguity aversion in insurance` — model.
**Examples:** `Ambiguity aversion insurance made households reject coverage for a poorly understood illness.` → Aversion ambiguity insurance làm household reject coverage illness không hiểu rõ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `보험 모호성 회피`.

## 3. insurance inertia /ɪnˈʃʊrəns ˈɪnɝʒə/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the tendency to keep an existing insurance plan despite changes that make another plan preferable.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** giữ insurance plan cũ dù change làm plan khác preferable hơn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `persistent insurance inertia` — inertia; `overcome insurance inertia` — overcome.
**Examples:** `Insurance inertia kept families in a plan with a higher premium and narrower network.` → Inertia insurance giữ family trong plan premium cao và network hẹp.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `보험 관성`.

## 4. coverage lapse /ˈkʌvərɪdʒ læps/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a period during which a person has no active insurance protection.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** period person không có insurance protection active.
**Grammar & collocations:** `temporary coverage lapse` — lapse; `prevent a coverage lapse` — prevent.
**Examples:** `A paperwork error caused a coverage lapse just before the scheduled surgery.` → Error paperwork gây lapse coverage ngay trước surgery.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `보장 공백`.

## 5. deductible salience /dɪˈdʌktəbəl ˈseɪliəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** how noticeable the amount a patient must pay before insurance coverage begins is when making a choice.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mức deductible patient phải pay trước coverage trở nên noticeable khi choose.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high deductible salience` — salience; `increase deductible salience` — increase.
**Examples:** `Deductible salience discouraged visits even though the annual plan was affordable.` → Salience deductible discourage visit dù annual plan affordable.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `공제액 현저성`.

## 6. premium salience /ˈpriːmiəm ˈseɪliəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** how visible and psychologically important the regular insurance premium feels to a buyer.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mức regular premium visible và psychologically important với buyer.
**Grammar & collocations:** `monthly premium salience` — salience; `raise premium salience` — raise.
**Examples:** `Premium salience made the low-deductible plan feel more expensive than its total cost justified.` → Salience premium làm low-deductible plan trông đắt hơn total cost.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `보험료 현저성`.

## 7. health insurance choice /hɛlθ ɪnˈʃʊrəns tʃɔɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a decision about which insurance plan, coverage level, or provider network to select.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** decision choose insurance plan, coverage level hoặc provider network.
**Grammar & collocations:** `complex health insurance choice` — choice; `simplify health insurance choice` — simplify.
**Examples:** `The health insurance choice required comparing premiums, deductibles, and specialist access.` → Choice insurance health cần compare premium, deductible và access specialist.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `건강보험 선택`.

## 8. adverse selection belief /ˈædvɝs səˈlɛkʃən bɪˈliːf/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a belief that people with higher expected losses are more likely to seek or retain insurance.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** belief người expected loss cao likely seek hoặc retain insurance hơn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `accurate adverse-selection belief` — belief; `test an adverse selection belief` — test.
**Examples:** `An adverse selection belief made healthy workers underestimate the value of broad coverage.` → Belief selection adverse làm healthy worker underestimate value coverage rộng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `역선택 믿음`.

## 9. moral hazard belief /ˈmɔrəl ˈhæzərd bɪˈliːf/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a belief that insurance changes people's incentives to take risks or use services after coverage begins.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** belief insurance đổi incentive take risk hoặc use service sau coverage.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong moral-hazard belief` — belief; `challenge a moral hazard belief` — challenge.
**Examples:** `A moral hazard belief led the employer to favor a higher copayment.` → Belief hazard moral làm employer favor copayment cao hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `도덕적 해이 믿음`.

## 10. health behavior bias /hɛlθ bɪˈheɪvjər baɪəs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a systematic distortion in health decisions caused by limited attention, inaccurate beliefs, or predictable preferences.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** distortion systematic trong health decision do attention limited, belief sai hoặc preference predictable.
**Grammar & collocations:** `persistent health behavior bias` — bias; `correct health behavior bias` — correct.
**Examples:** `Health behavior bias made people delay preventive tests while responding quickly to symptoms.` → Bias behavior health làm người delay test preventive nhưng phản ứng nhanh với symptom.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `건강 행동 편향`.

## 11. status quo insurance /ˈsteɪtəs kwoʊ ɪnˈʃʊrəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an existing insurance arrangement that people keep because changing plans requires effort or feels risky.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** insurance arrangement cũ giữ vì change cần effort hoặc thấy risky.
**Grammar & collocations:** `status quo insurance plan` — plan; `remain with status quo insurance` — remain.
**Examples:** `Status quo insurance persisted after the employer offered a cheaper equivalent plan.` → Insurance status quo persist sau employer offer plan rẻ equivalent.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `현상 유지 보험`.

## 12. default insurance /dɪˈfɔlt ɪnˈʃʊrəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** insurance coverage assigned automatically unless a person actively chooses another option.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** coverage assign automatically nếu person không actively choose option khác.
**Grammar & collocations:** `protective default insurance` — insurance; `set default insurance` — set.
**Examples:** `Default insurance increased coverage among workers who rarely checked benefit forms.` → Insurance default tăng coverage ở worker hiếm khi check benefit form.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `기본 보험`.

## 13. preventive care salience /prɪˈvɛntɪv kɛr ˈseɪliəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** how visible and attention-grabbing preventive health benefits or risks are when people decide what care to use.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** preventive benefit hoặc risk visible, attention-grabbing khi decide care.
**Grammar & collocations:** `low preventive care salience` — salience; `increase preventive care salience` — increase.
**Examples:** `Preventive care salience rose when the clinic sent a personalized reminder.` → Salience care preventive tăng khi clinic send reminder cá nhân.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `예방 진료 현저성`.

## 14. present bias health /ˈprɛzənt baɪəs hɛlθ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a tendency to value immediate comfort over future health benefits when choosing care or habits.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** value comfort immediate hơn health benefit future khi choose care hoặc habit.
**Grammar & collocations:** `present-bias health decision` — decision; `address present bias in health` — address.
**Examples:** `Present bias health made the patient postpone rehabilitation that would prevent future pain.` → Bias present health làm patient postpone rehab prevent pain future.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `건강 현재 편향`.

## 15. health optimism /hɛlθ ˈɑptəˌmɪzəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an overly favorable belief about one's future health or likelihood of avoiding illness.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** belief quá favorable về health future hoặc chance avoid illness.
**Grammar & collocations:** `unrealistic health optimism` — optimism; `correct health optimism` — correct.
**Examples:** `Health optimism reduced demand for screening among people with a family history of disease.` → Optimism health giảm demand screening ở người có family history.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `건강 낙관주의`.

## 16. risk compensation /rɪsk ˌkɑmpənˈseɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a tendency to take more risks after a safety improvement reduces perceived danger.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** take risk nhiều hơn sau safety improvement làm perceived danger giảm.
**Grammar & collocations:** `risk-compensation behavior` — behavior; `observe risk compensation` — observe.
**Examples:** `Risk compensation offset part of the protection provided by the safety equipment.` → Compensation risk offset một phần protection safety equipment.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `위험 보상`.

## 17. behavioral health economics /bɪˈheɪvjərəl hɛlθ ˌiːkəˈnɑmɪks/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the study of how psychological tendencies and social preferences shape health choices, care use, and medical spending.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** study tendency psychological và preference social shape health choice, care use và medical spending.
**Grammar & collocations:** `behavioral-health-economics model` — model; `study behavioral health economics` — study.
**Examples:** `Behavioral health economics explains why identical copayments affect patients differently.` → Economics health behavioral explain copayment giống nhau affect patient khác.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `행동 보건경제학`.

## 18. medical debt perception /ˈmɛdɪkəl dɛt pərˈsɛpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** how a person understands the size, fairness, and future burden of debt caused by medical care.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cách person understand size, fairness và burden future của debt medical.
**Grammar & collocations:** `serious medical debt perception` — perception; `change medical debt perception` — change.
**Examples:** `Medical debt perception influenced whether the family returned for follow-up care.` → Perception debt medical ảnh hưởng family return follow-up không.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `의료 부채 인식`.

## 19. financial toxicity /faɪˈnænʃəl tɑkˈsɪsəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the financial strain and distress caused by medical costs, treatment decisions, or illness-related loss of income.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** financial strain và distress do medical cost, treatment decision hoặc income loss vì illness.
**Grammar & collocations:** `severe financial toxicity` — toxicity; `screen for financial toxicity` — screen.
**Examples:** `Financial toxicity made the patient reduce medication before discussing the problem with a doctor.` → Toxicity financial làm patient giảm medicine trước khi discuss với doctor.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `재정 독성`.

## 20. care avoidance /kɛr əˈvɔɪdəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** choosing not to seek or continue needed medical care because of cost, fear, inconvenience, or beliefs.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** không seek hoặc continue care cần thiết vì cost, fear, inconvenience hoặc belief.
**Grammar & collocations:** `delayed care avoidance` — avoidance; `reduce care avoidance` — reduce.
**Examples:** `Clear cost information reduced care avoidance among patients with chronic symptoms.` → Information cost rõ giảm avoidance care ở patient symptom chronic.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `진료 회피`.

## Review in context

**Insurance framing**, **ambiguity aversion insurance**, and **insurance inertia** shape coverage decisions, while a **coverage lapse** may follow high **deductible salience** or **premium salience**. A complex **health insurance choice** reflects an **adverse selection belief** and a **moral hazard belief**. **Health behavior bias**, **status quo insurance**, and **default insurance** affect preventive decisions. **Preventive care salience**, **present bias health**, **health optimism**, and **risk compensation** are central to **behavioral health economics**. **Medical debt perception**, **financial toxicity**, and **care avoidance** show how costs alter health behavior.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:** **Insurance framing**, **ambiguity aversion insurance** và **insurance inertia** shape coverage decision, còn **coverage lapse** có thể theo sau **deductible salience** hoặc **premium salience** cao. **Health insurance choice** phức tạp reflect **adverse selection belief** và **moral hazard belief**. **Health behavior bias**, **status quo insurance** và **default insurance** ảnh hưởng preventive decision. **Preventive care salience**, **present bias health**, **health optimism** và **risk compensation** central trong **behavioral health economics**. **Medical debt perception**, **financial toxicity** và **care avoidance** cho thấy cost đổi health behavior thế nào.
