# C2+ Vocabulary — Health-system resilience and surge response

This lesson follows how health systems absorb shocks, maintain care, detect outbreaks, and recover capacity. The network is **health-system resilience → surge capacity → critical care capacity → referral pathway → continuity of care → care delivery interruption → workforce absenteeism → task shifting → stockout → buffer inventory → cold-chain failure → sentinel monitoring → symptom-based surveillance → excess mortality → all-cause mortality → health emergency operations center → incident command system → public health laboratory network → case fatality ratio → demand surge → care rationing → triage protocol → community health worker → primary care gatekeeping → recovery backlog**.

## 1. health-system resilience /hɛlθ ˈsɪstəm rɪˈzɪliəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a health system's ability to absorb shocks, maintain essential functions, adapt operations, and recover without unacceptable loss.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** health-system resilience là absorb shock, giữ function, adapt và recover.
**Grammar & collocations:** `health-system-resilience assessment` — assessment; `absorb a shock` — absorb; `maintain essential care` — maintain.
**Examples:** `Health-system resilience depended on staffing, supply chains, referral routes, and trusted communication.` → Resilience phụ thuộc nhiều layer.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `보건의료 체계 회복탄력성` — health-system resilience.

## 2. surge capacity /sɜrdʒ kəˈpæsəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the ability to expand staff, beds, equipment, space, and services quickly when demand rises sharply.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** surge capacity là khả năng scale nhanh khi demand đột ngột tăng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `surge-capacity plan` — plan; `expand capacity` — expand; `overflow unit` — unit.
**Examples:** `The hospital activated surge capacity by opening a temporary respiratory ward.` → Hospital mở ward tạm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `급증 대응 능력` — surge capacity.

## 3. critical care capacity /ˈkrɪtɪkəl kɛr kəˈpæsəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the available combination of intensive-care beds, trained staff, equipment, medicines, and support services needed for critically ill patients.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** critical care capacity không chỉ là bed; cần staff/equipment/medicine/support.
**Grammar & collocations:** `critical-care-capacity limit` — limit; `provide intensive care` — provide; `trained clinician` — clinician.
**Examples:** `Critical care capacity became the limiting factor during the respiratory outbreak.` → Capacity ICU là bottleneck.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `중환자 치료 역량` — critical care capacity.

## 4. referral pathway /rɪˈfɜrəl ˈpæθweɪ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an organized route through which a patient moves from one service or level of care to another.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** referral pathway nối patient từ primary tới specialist/emergency care.
**Grammar & collocations:** `referral-pathway map` — map; `refer a patient` — refer; `higher level of care` — care.
**Examples:** `The referral pathway sent severe cases from rural clinics to the regional hospital.` → Pathway chuyển case nặng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `의뢰 경로` — referral pathway.

## 5. continuity of care /ˌkɑntəˈnuəti əv kɛr/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** coordinated and uninterrupted care across time, providers, settings, and changes in a patient's condition.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** continuity of care giữ care liền mạch khi patient đổi provider/setting/time.
**Grammar & collocations:** `continuity-of-care plan` — plan; `maintain continuity` — maintain; `handoff record` — record.
**Examples:** `Telehealth preserved continuity of care when travel to the clinic was restricted.` → Telehealth giữ care continuity.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `진료 연속성` — continuity of care.

## 6. care delivery interruption /kɛr dɪˈlɪvəri ˌɪntəˈrʌpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a break or reduction in the delivery of planned health services caused by disruption, shortage, or unsafe conditions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** care delivery interruption là care plan bị break/reduce vì crisis/shortage.
**Grammar & collocations:** `care-delivery interruption` — interruption; `interrupt treatment` — interrupt; `service continuity` — continuity.
**Examples:** `Care delivery interruption delayed cancer treatment for patients outside the emergency zone.` → Cancer treatment bị delay.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `의료 제공 중단` — care delivery interruption.

## 7. workforce absenteeism /ˈwɜrkˌfɔrs ˌæbsənˈtiːˌɪzəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** absence of workers from scheduled duties, often because of illness, caregiving, transport failure, fear, or exhaustion.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** workforce absenteeism làm thiếu staff vì illness/caregiving/transport/fatigue.
**Grammar & collocations:** `workforce-absenteeism rate` — rate; `miss a shift` — miss; `staff shortage` — shortage.
**Examples:** `Workforce absenteeism reduced operating hours even before beds filled.` → Staff absent làm hours giảm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `인력 결근` — workforce absenteeism.

## 8. task shifting /tæsk ˈʃɪftɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** redistribution of tasks from more specialized workers to other trained workers when appropriate safeguards are in place.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** task shifting chuyển task sang worker khác đã train để mở capacity.
**Grammar & collocations:** `task-shifting protocol` — protocol; `shift a clinical task` — shift; `supervised training` — training.
**Examples:** `Task shifting allowed community nurses to provide routine follow-up during the surge.` → Nurse cộng đồng làm follow-up.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `업무 전환` — task shifting.

## 9. stockout /ˈstɑkˌaʊt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a situation in which an essential medicine, supply, or product is temporarily unavailable when needed.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** stockout là hết supply cần dùng đúng lúc.
**Grammar & collocations:** `stockout duration` — duration; `prevent a stockout` — prevent; `essential medicine` — medicine.
**Examples:** `The stockout forced clinics to substitute a less suitable antibiotic.` → Clinic phải substitute antibiotic.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `재고 고갈` — stockout.

## 10. buffer inventory /ˈbʌfər ˈɪnvənˌtɔri/

**Core meaning (English):** extra stock held to absorb delays, demand spikes, supplier failures, or uncertainty in replenishment.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** buffer inventory là stock reserve chống delay/demand spike.
**Grammar & collocations:** `buffer-inventory policy` — policy; `hold reserve stock` — hold; `replenishment delay` — delay.

## 11. cold-chain failure /koʊld tʃeɪn ˈfeɪljər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** loss of required temperature control during storage or transport of temperature-sensitive health products.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cold-chain failure làm vaccine/medicine mất temperature safety.
**Grammar & collocations:** `cold-chain-failure alert` — alert; `maintain temperature control` — maintain; `temperature excursion` — excursion.
**Examples:** `Cold-chain failure spoiled the vaccine shipment after the backup generator ran out of fuel.` → Vaccine hỏng khi generator hết fuel.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `콜드체인 실패` — cold-chain failure.

## 12. sentinel monitoring /ˈsɛntənəl ˈmɑnətərɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** monitoring at selected sites or populations chosen to provide early indication of a wider health event.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** sentinel monitoring dùng site được chọn làm “điểm canh” cho event rộng hơn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `sentinel-monitoring site` — site; `detect an early signal` — detect; `selected population` — population.
**Examples:** `Sentinel monitoring detected unusual respiratory illness before routine reporting changed.` → Monitoring detect sớm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `감시 지점 모니터링` — sentinel monitoring.

## 13. symptom-based surveillance /ˈsɪmptəm beɪst sərˈveɪləns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** surveillance based on reported symptoms, syndromes, or care-seeking patterns rather than confirmed laboratory diagnoses.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** symptom-based surveillance dùng symptom pattern trước khi lab confirm.
**Grammar & collocations:** `symptom-based-surveillance signal` — signal; `track a syndrome` — track; `care-seeking pattern` — pattern.
**Examples:** `Symptom-based surveillance gave the response team an earlier warning than laboratory confirmation.` → Symptom signal tới trước lab.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `증상 기반 감시` — symptom-based surveillance.

## 14. excess mortality /ˈɛksɛs mɔrˈtæləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** deaths above the number expected from historical or modeled baseline conditions during a specified period.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** excess mortality so sánh death thật với baseline expected.
**Grammar & collocations:** `excess-mortality estimate` — estimate; `compare with baseline deaths` — compare; `mortality surge` — surge.
**Examples:** `Excess mortality captured both direct infections and deaths from disrupted care.` → Measure gồm direct và indirect death.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `초과 사망률` — excess mortality.

## 15. all-cause mortality /ˌɔl kɔz mɔrˈtæləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** deaths from all causes combined in a defined population and period.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** all-cause mortality không chỉ đếm disease target; gồm mọi cause.
**Grammar & collocations:** `all-cause-mortality series` — series; `estimate deaths from all causes` — estimate; `population baseline` — baseline.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `전체 원인 사망률` — all-cause mortality.

## 19. case fatality ratio /keɪs fəˈtæləti ˈreɪʃioʊ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the proportion of identified cases of a condition that result in death within a defined period or case definition.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** case fatality ratio là death trong confirmed/identified case, phụ thuộc case definition.
**Grammar & collocations:** `case-fatality ratio estimate` — estimate; `calculate a ratio` — calculate; `identified case` — case.
**Examples:** `The case fatality ratio fell after testing expanded to detect milder cases.` → Ratio giảm vì denominator mở rộng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `치명률` — case fatality ratio.

## 20. demand surge /dɪˈmænd sɜrdʒ/

## 25. recovery backlog /rɪˈkʌvəri ˈbækˌlɔɡ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** accumulated unmet care, appointments, procedures, or preventive services left after a disruption.

## Review in context

**Health-system resilience** depends on **surge capacity**, **critical care capacity**, a clear **referral pathway**, and **continuity of care**. **Care delivery interruption**, **workforce absenteeism**, **task shifting**, a **stockout**, **buffer inventory**, and **cold-chain failure** shape operations. **Sentinel monitoring** and **symptom-based surveillance** can reveal danger before **excess mortality** or **all-cause mortality** rises. A **health emergency operations center**, an **incident command system**, and a **public health laboratory network** coordinate response; the **case fatality ratio** may change as detection changes. A **demand surge** can force **care rationing** under a **triage protocol**. **Community health workers** and **primary care gatekeeping** preserve access, while a **recovery backlog** measures the work left after the crisis.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

**Health-system resilience** phụ thuộc **surge capacity**, **critical care capacity**, **referral pathway** rõ và **continuity of care**. **Care delivery interruption**, **workforce absenteeism**, **task shifting**, **stockout**, **buffer inventory** và **cold-chain failure** định hình operation. **Sentinel monitoring** và **symptom-based surveillance** có thể lộ danger trước khi **excess mortality** hoặc **all-cause mortality** tăng. **Health emergency operations center**, **incident command system** và **public health laboratory network** phối hợp response; **case fatality ratio** có thể đổi khi detection đổi. **Demand surge** có thể buộc **care rationing** theo **triage protocol**. **Community health workers** và **primary care gatekeeping** giữ access, còn **recovery backlog** đo công việc còn lại sau crisis.
