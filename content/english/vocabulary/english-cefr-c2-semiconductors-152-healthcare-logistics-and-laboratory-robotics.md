# C2 Vocabulary — Healthcare logistics and laboratory robotics

This lesson follows hospital and laboratory robots from supply delivery to clean, traceable sample handling. The network is **hospital logistics robot → pharmacy dispensing robot → medication delivery robot → linen delivery robot → meal delivery robot → hospital disinfection robot → ultraviolet disinfection robot → patient transport robot → bed transport robot → telepresence robot → wayfinding assistant robot → care companion robot → fall detection robot → patient lifting robot → clinical sample robot → laboratory automation robot → specimen sorting robot → automated pipetting robot → liquid handling robot → plate handling robot**.

## 1. hospital logistics robot /ˈhɑːspɪtəl ləˈdʒɪstɪks ˈroʊbɑːt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a robot that moves supplies, waste, equipment, or other materials through a hospital.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** robot chuyển supply, waste, equipment hoặc material khác trong hospital.
**Grammar & collocations:** `hospital-logistics-robot route` — route; `deliver a sealed cart` — giao cart kín.
**Examples:** `The hospital logistics robot delivered sterile packs to the operating suite.` → Hospital logistics robot giao sterile pack tới operating suite.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `병원 물류 로봇`.

## 2. pharmacy dispensing robot /ˈfɑːrməsi dɪˈspensɪŋ ˈroʊbɑːt/

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `약국 조제 로봇`.

## Review in context

A **hospital logistics robot** can work beside a **pharmacy dispensing robot**, **medication delivery robot**, **linen delivery robot**, and **meal delivery robot**. A **hospital disinfection robot** or **ultraviolet disinfection robot** treats rooms; a **patient transport robot**, **bed transport robot**, and **telepresence robot** support care. A **wayfinding assistant robot**, **care companion robot**, **fall detection robot**, and **patient lifting robot** support patients and caregivers. In laboratories, a **clinical sample robot**, **laboratory automation robot**, **specimen sorting robot**, **automated pipetting robot**, **liquid handling robot**, and **plate handling robot** preserve accurate, traceable workflows.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:** **Hospital logistics robot** có thể làm việc cạnh **pharmacy dispensing robot**, **medication delivery robot**, **linen delivery robot** và **meal delivery robot**. **Hospital disinfection robot** hoặc **ultraviolet disinfection robot** xử lý room; **patient transport robot**, **bed transport robot** và **telepresence robot** hỗ trợ care. **Wayfinding assistant robot**, **care companion robot**, **fall detection robot** và **patient lifting robot** hỗ trợ patient/caregiver. Trong laboratory, **clinical sample robot**, **laboratory automation robot**, **specimen sorting robot**, **automated pipetting robot**, **liquid handling robot** và **plate handling robot** giữ workflow chính xác, traceable.
