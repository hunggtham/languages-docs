# C2 Vocabulary — Airport operations and ground-support robotics

This lesson follows an airport from passenger arrival and baggage movement to aircraft servicing and disruption recovery. The network is **airport service robot → baggage handling robot → autonomous baggage cart → aircraft cabin cleaning robot → runway inspection robot → airport runway debris detection → aircraft exterior inspection drone → gate assistance robot → passenger wayfinding robot → boarding queue monitoring → airport wheelchair robot → catering loading robot → de-icing support robot → airfield lighting inspection → baggage reconciliation → security screening robot → airport fire response robot → jet bridge inspection robot → ground support coordination → irregular operations rerouting**.

## 1. airport service robot /ˈerpɔːrt ˈsɜːrvɪs ˈroʊbɑːt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a robot that provides information, transport, cleaning, or other operational support in an airport.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** robot cung cấp information, transport, cleaning hoặc operational support trong airport.
**Grammar & collocations:** `airport-service-robot station` — station; `assist a departing traveler` — hỗ trợ traveler sắp khởi hành.
**Examples:** `The airport service robot answered questions about the delayed flight.` → Airport service robot trả lời câu hỏi về flight bị delay.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `공항 서비스 로봇`.

## Review in context

An **airport service robot**, **baggage handling robot**, and **autonomous baggage cart** support passenger and bag movement, while an **aircraft cabin cleaning robot** prepares the aircraft. A **runway inspection robot** and **airport runway debris detection** protect the surface, and an **aircraft exterior inspection drone** checks the airframe. At the terminal, a **gate assistance robot**, **passenger wayfinding robot**, **boarding queue monitoring**, and **airport wheelchair robot** help travelers. A **catering loading robot**, **de-icing support robot**, and **airfield lighting inspection** support the aircraft; **baggage reconciliation** matches bags to passengers. A **security screening robot**, **airport fire response robot**, and **jet bridge inspection robot** reduce risk. **Ground support coordination** organizes the turnaround, while **irregular operations rerouting** keeps disrupted journeys moving.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:** **Airport service robot**, **baggage handling robot** và **autonomous baggage cart** hỗ trợ di chuyển passenger/bag, còn **aircraft cabin cleaning robot** chuẩn bị aircraft. **Runway inspection robot** và **airport runway debris detection** bảo vệ mặt runway, còn **aircraft exterior inspection drone** kiểm tra airframe. Ở terminal, **gate assistance robot**, **passenger wayfinding robot**, **boarding queue monitoring** và **airport wheelchair robot** giúp traveler. **Catering loading robot**, **de-icing support robot** và **airfield lighting inspection** hỗ trợ aircraft; **baggage reconciliation** ghép bag với passenger. **Security screening robot**, **airport fire response robot** và **jet bridge inspection robot** giảm risk. **Ground support coordination** tổ chức turnaround, còn **irregular operations rerouting** giữ journey bị gián đoạn tiếp tục.
