# C1 Vocabulary 54 — Continuous treatment and dose-response analysis

This lesson follows how causal analysts study interventions whose intensity varies rather than taking only treated and untreated values.

Flow: **continuous treatment → dose-response function → generalized propensity score → treatment intensity → dose-response estimand → continuous exposure → dose positivity → dose overlap → dose-response surface → dose contrast → marginal structural model → dynamic dose → dose-response heterogeneity → continuous intervention → treatment level → dose assignment → dose-response calibration → dose-response confounding → continuous treatment effect → incremental propensity score**.

## 1. continuous treatment /kənˈtɪnjuəs ˈtriːtmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an intervention whose amount, intensity, or duration can take many values rather than only treated or untreated.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** intervention có amount, intensity hoặc duration nhiều value, không chỉ treated/untreated.
**Grammar & collocations:** `continuous-treatment model` — model; `analyze continuous treatment` — analyze.
**Examples:** `The continuous treatment was the number of counseling hours received.` → Treatment continuous là số giờ counseling receive.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `연속 처치`.

## 2. dose-response function /doʊs rɪˈspɑns ˈfʌŋkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a function describing how an expected outcome changes as treatment dose changes.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** function describe expected outcome đổi theo treatment dose.
**Grammar & collocations:** `smooth dose-response function` — function; `estimate a dose-response function` — estimate.
**Examples:** `The dose-response function rose quickly and then leveled off.` → Function dose-response tăng nhanh rồi level off.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `용량-반응 함수`.

## 3. generalized propensity score /ˈdʒɛnərələzd prəˈpɛnsəti skɔr/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the conditional density or probability of receiving a particular continuous treatment level given observed characteristics.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** density hoặc probability receive treatment level continuous theo observed characteristic.
**Grammar & collocations:** `estimated generalized propensity score` — score; `model a generalized propensity score` — model.
**Examples:** `The generalized propensity score balanced observed health differences across dose levels.` → Score propensity generalized balance health difference observed giữa dose level.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `일반화 성향 점수`.

## 4. treatment intensity /ˈtriːtmənt ɪnˈtɛnsəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the amount, frequency, strength, or duration of an intervention applied to a unit.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** amount, frequency, strength hoặc duration intervention apply cho unit.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high treatment intensity` — intensity cao; `vary treatment intensity` — vary.
**Examples:** `Treatment intensity differed because clinics offered different numbers of visits.` → Intensity treatment khác vì clinic offer số visit khác.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `처치 강도`.

## 5. dose-response estimand /doʊs rɪˈspɑns ˈɛstəmənd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a precisely defined causal quantity describing an outcome under a specified dose or dose contrast.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** causal quantity define rõ outcome dưới dose hoặc dose contrast cụ thể.
**Grammar & collocations:** `average dose-response estimand` — estimand; `identify a dose-response estimand` — identify.
**Examples:** `The dose-response estimand compared ten hours of training with five hours.` → Estimand dose-response compare ten giờ training với five giờ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `용량-반응 추정 대상`.

## 6. continuous exposure /kənˈtɪnjuəs ɪkˈspoʊʒər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an exposure measured on a scale with many possible values rather than a simple category.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** exposure measure trên scale nhiều value, không phải category đơn giản.
**Grammar & collocations:** `environmental continuous exposure` — exposure; `model continuous exposure` — model.

## 7. dose positivity /doʊs ˌpɑzəˈtɪvəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the condition that relevant units have a non-negligible chance of receiving the dose levels being compared.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** unit liên quan có chance không negligible receive dose level được compare.
**Grammar & collocations:** `dose-positivity assumption` — assumption; `check dose positivity` — check.
**Examples:** `Dose positivity failed for patients whose clinics never offered intensive therapy.` → Positivity dose fail ở patient clinic không bao giờ offer intensive therapy.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `용량 양성`.

## 8. dose overlap /doʊs ˈoʊvərˌlæp/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the range of treatment doses observed for comparable units across the groups or conditions being contrasted.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** range dose observe ở unit comparable giữa group hoặc condition contrast.
**Grammar & collocations:** `limited dose overlap` — overlap; `restrict analysis to dose overlap` — restrict.
**Examples:** `Limited dose overlap made the highest intensity difficult to compare.` → Overlap dose limited làm intensity cao nhất khó compare.

## 9. dose-response surface /doʊs rɪˈspɑns ˈsɜrfəs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a representation of how an outcome varies jointly with dose and other effect-modifying characteristics.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** representation outcome vary cùng dose và characteristic modify effect.
**Grammar & collocations:** `three-dimensional dose-response surface` — surface; `plot a dose-response surface` — plot.
**Examples:** `The dose-response surface showed larger gains for people starting with lower skills.` → Surface dose-response show gain lớn hơn ở người starting skill thấp.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `용량-반응 표면`.

## 12. dynamic dose /daɪˈnæmɪk doʊs/

## 13. dose-response heterogeneity /doʊs rɪˈspɑns ˌhɛtərədʒəˈniəti/

## Review in context

A **continuous treatment** requires a **dose-response function** and a **generalized propensity score** to represent changing **treatment intensity**. The analyst defines a **dose-response estimand** for a **continuous exposure**, checks **dose positivity** and **dose overlap**, and studies a **dose-response surface** under a clear **dose contrast**. A **marginal structural model** can represent a **dynamic dose** and **dose-response heterogeneity**. A **continuous intervention** contains several possible **treatment levels**, while **dose assignment** may be confounded. **Dose-response calibration** tests the model, **dose-response confounding** threatens interpretation, and a **continuous treatment effect** can be expressed through an **incremental propensity score**.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:** **Continuous treatment** cần **dose-response function** và **generalized propensity score** để represent **treatment intensity** đổi. Analyst define **dose-response estimand** cho **continuous exposure**, check **dose positivity** và **dose overlap**, rồi study **dose-response surface** dưới **dose contrast** rõ. **Marginal structural model** có thể represent **dynamic dose** và **dose-response heterogeneity**. **Continuous intervention** có nhiều **treatment level**, còn **dose assignment** có thể confound. **Dose-response calibration** test model, **dose-response confounding** đe dọa interpretation, và **continuous treatment effect** có thể express qua **incremental propensity score**.
