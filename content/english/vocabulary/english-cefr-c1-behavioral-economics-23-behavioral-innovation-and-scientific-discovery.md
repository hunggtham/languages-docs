# C1 Vocabulary 23 — Behavioral innovation and scientific discovery

This lesson follows how researchers, inventors, funders, and communities judge novelty, uncertainty, collaboration, and the value of new knowledge.

Flow: **innovation optimism → research risk perception → inventor identity → novelty discount → sunk research cost → patent salience → collaborative trust → knowledge spillover belief → science communication cue → expert credibility → innovation ambiguity tolerance → experimentation norm → failure stigma → grant deadline effect → discovery serendipity → technology adoption expectation → innovation diffusion cue → standardization friction → open-source reciprocity → public value framing**.

## 1. innovation optimism /ˌɪnəˈveɪʃən ˈɑptəˌmɪzəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a belief that a new idea or technology will produce benefits more quickly or widely than evidence warrants.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** belief idea hoặc technology new create benefit nhanh hoặc rộng hơn evidence support.
**Grammar & collocations:** `excessive innovation optimism` — optimism; `temper innovation optimism` — temper.
**Examples:** `Innovation optimism made the founders promise immediate savings before the prototype was tested.` → Optimism innovation khiến founder promise saving immediate trước khi prototype test.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `혁신 낙관주의`.

## 2. research risk perception /rɪˈsɝtʃ rɪsk pərˈsɛpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a judgment about the chance that a research project will fail, cause harm, or produce an uncertain result.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** judgment project research fail, cause harm hoặc result uncertain chance bao nhiêu.
**Grammar & collocations:** `accurate research risk perception` — perception; `calibrate research risk perception` — calibrate.
**Examples:** `Research risk perception influenced whether the lab invested in a bold but unproven method.` → Perception risk research influence lab invest method bold nhưng unproven không.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `연구 위험 인식`.

## 3. inventor identity /ɪnˈvɛntər aɪˈdɛntəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a sense that creating and improving new solutions is part of who a person is.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** sense create và improve solution new là một phần identity.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong inventor identity` — identity; `develop an inventor identity` — develop.
**Examples:** `Inventor identity kept the engineer refining a device after the first market test disappointed buyers.` → Identity inventor khiến engineer tiếp tục refine device sau market test đầu làm buyer thất vọng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `발명가 정체성`.

## 4. novelty discount /ˈnɑvəlti ˈdɪskaʊnt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a tendency to undervalue an idea because it is unfamiliar, unusual, or not yet supported by established routines.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** tendency undervalue idea vì unfamiliar, unusual hoặc chưa có routine established.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong novelty discount` — discount; `overcome novelty discount` — overcome.
**Examples:** `Novelty discount delayed adoption of a simple tool that did not resemble existing software.` → Discount novelty delay adoption tool simple không giống software cũ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `참신성 할인`.

## 5. sunk research cost /sʌŋk rɪˈsɝtʃ kɔst/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** resources already spent on a research direction that cannot be recovered by changing course.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** resource đã spend cho direction research không recover được bằng change course.
**Grammar & collocations:** `large sunk research cost` — cost; `ignore sunk research costs` — ignore.
**Examples:** `Sunk research cost made the team continue collecting weak data instead of redesigning the study.` → Cost research sunk khiến team tiếp tục collect data yếu thay vì redesign study.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `매몰 연구 비용`.

## 6. patent salience /ˈpætənt ˈseɪliəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** how noticeable and motivational the possibility of patent protection is to an innovator.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mức possibility patent protection noticeable và motivational với innovator.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high patent salience` — salience; `increase patent salience` — increase.
**Examples:** `Patent salience encouraged the researcher to document the process before sharing the design publicly.` → Salience patent khuyến khích researcher document process trước khi share design public.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `특허 현저성`.

## 7. collaborative trust /kəˈlæbərətɪv trʌst/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** confidence that partners will contribute honestly, share credit, and protect one another's work.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** confidence partner contribute honest, share credit và protect work của nhau.
**Grammar & collocations:** `fragile collaborative trust` — trust; `build collaborative trust` — build.
**Examples:** `Collaborative trust allowed two labs to share a difficult result without fearing blame.` → Trust collaborative cho hai lab share result khó mà không fear blame.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `협업 신뢰`.

## 8. knowledge spillover belief /ˈnɑlɪdʒ ˈspɪlˌoʊvər bɪˈliːf/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a belief that ideas created in one project, firm, or field can benefit others beyond the original setting.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** belief idea từ project, firm hoặc field này benefit người khác ngoài setting gốc.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong knowledge spillover belief` — belief; `encourage knowledge spillover beliefs` — encourage.
**Examples:** `Knowledge spillover belief justified publishing the method even though the first application was narrow.` → Belief spillover knowledge justify publish method dù application đầu narrow.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `지식 파급 신념`.

## 9. science communication cue /ˈsaɪəns kəˌmjuːnəˈkeɪʃən kjuː/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a feature of a message that signals whether scientific information is careful, relevant, and understandable.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** feature message signal information science careful, relevant và understandable.
**Grammar & collocations:** `clear science communication cue` — cue; `provide a science communication cue` — provide.
**Examples:** `A science communication cue explained uncertainty instead of presenting a single number as a promise.` → Cue communication science explain uncertainty thay vì present một number như promise.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `과학 소통 단서`.

## 10. expert credibility /ˈɛkspɝt krɛdəˈbɪləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the perceived reliability, competence, and honesty of a person presented as an expert.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** reliability, competence và honesty được perceive của người được present như expert.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high expert credibility` — credibility; `protect expert credibility` — protect.
**Examples:** `Expert credibility fell when the spokesperson hid the limits of the evidence.` → Credibility expert giảm khi spokesperson hide limit evidence.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `전문가 신뢰성`.

## 11. innovation ambiguity tolerance /ˌɪnəˈveɪʃən ˌæmbɪˈɡjuəti ˈtɑlərəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** willingness to support experimentation when the eventual use, outcome, or business model remains unclear.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** willingness support experiment khi use, outcome hoặc business model chưa rõ.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high innovation ambiguity tolerance` — tolerance; `cultivate innovation ambiguity tolerance` — cultivate.
**Examples:** `Innovation ambiguity tolerance gave the team room to test a design before choosing its final market.` → Tolerance ambiguity innovation cho team room test design trước khi choose market final.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `혁신 모호성 인내`.

## 12. experimentation norm /ɪkˌspɛrəmənˈteɪʃən nɔrm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a shared expectation that small tests and evidence should guide improvement instead of speculation alone.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** expectation shared dùng test nhỏ và evidence guide improve thay vì chỉ speculation.
**Grammar & collocations:** `constructive experimentation norm` — norm; `establish an experimentation norm` — establish.
**Examples:** `An experimentation norm made it acceptable to stop a feature after a careful trial.` → Norm experimentation làm việc stop feature sau trial cẩn thận trở nên acceptable.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `실험 규범`.

## 13. failure stigma /ˈfeɪljər ˈstɪɡmə/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a negative social label attached to unsuccessful experiments, projects, or entrepreneurial attempts.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** label social negative gắn với experiment, project hoặc attempt thất bại.
**Grammar & collocations:** `severe failure stigma` — stigma; `reduce failure stigma` — reduce.
**Examples:** `Failure stigma made junior researchers hide a result that could have improved the next study.` → Stigma failure khiến researcher junior hide result có thể improve study tiếp.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `실패 낙인`.

## 14. grant deadline effect /ɡrænt ˈdɛdˌlaɪn ɪˈfɛkt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a change in research effort, project choice, or risk-taking caused by a funding deadline.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** change effort, project choice hoặc risk-taking do deadline funding.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong grant deadline effect` — effect; `measure the grant deadline effect` — measure.
**Examples:** `The grant deadline effect pushed the lab toward a familiar study with a faster visible result.` → Effect deadline grant đẩy lab về study quen có result visible nhanh.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `연구비 마감 효과`.

**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** belief technology adopted nhanh và rộng tới đâu.

## 17. innovation diffusion cue /ˌɪnəˈveɪʃən dɪˈfjuːʒən kjuː/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a signal that makes a new idea appear accepted, useful, or ready to spread through a community.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** signal khiến idea new trông accepted, useful hoặc ready spread community.
**Grammar & collocations:** `visible innovation diffusion cue` — cue; `create an innovation diffusion cue` — create.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `혁신 확산 단서`.

## 18. standardization friction /ˌstændərdəˈzeɪʃən ˈfrɪkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** difficulty created when a new practice must fit shared standards, formats, rules, or technical interfaces.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** difficulty khi practice new phải fit standard, format, rule hoặc interface technical.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high standardization friction` — friction; `reduce standardization friction` — reduce.
**Examples:** `Standardization friction delayed the pilot because different hospitals stored the same data differently.` → Friction standardization delay pilot vì hospital khác store data khác nhau.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `표준화 마찰`.

## 19. open-source reciprocity /ˈoʊpən sɔrs ˌrɛsəˈprɑsəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the expectation that people who benefit from shared code or knowledge will contribute improvements back to the community.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** expectation người benefit từ code hoặc knowledge shared sẽ contribute improvement lại community.
**Grammar & collocations:** `healthy open-source reciprocity` — reciprocity; `sustain open-source reciprocity` — sustain.
**Examples:** `Open-source reciprocity encouraged the company to publish a fix instead of keeping it private.` → Reciprocity open-source khuyến khích company publish fix thay vì giữ private.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `오픈 소스 상호성`.

## 20. public value framing /ˈpʌblɪk ˈvælju ˈfreɪmɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** presenting innovation as a contribution to shared social benefit rather than only private profit or prestige.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** present innovation như contribution shared social benefit thay vì chỉ profit hoặc prestige private.
**Grammar & collocations:** `credible public value framing` — framing; `use public value framing` — use.
**Examples:** `Public value framing helped residents support a research center whose benefits would arrive gradually.` → Framing value public giúp resident support research center benefit đến dần.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `공공 가치 프레이밍`.

## Review in context

Innovation begins with **innovation optimism**, but responsible teams calibrate **research risk perception** before promising results. An **inventor identity** can sustain effort, while a **novelty discount** makes unfamiliar ideas seem less valuable. Leaders should distinguish a **sunk research cost** from a reason to continue, and consider how **patent salience** changes sharing. **Collaborative trust** supports a **knowledge spillover belief**, especially when a clear **science communication cue** protects **expert credibility**. High **innovation ambiguity tolerance** and an **experimentation norm** create room for learning, while **failure stigma** suppresses useful evidence. A **grant deadline effect** may narrow choices just when **discovery serendipity** requires patience. **Technology adoption expectation** and an **innovation diffusion cue** shape whether a prototype spreads, but **standardization friction** can slow even a valuable tool. **Open-source reciprocity** keeps shared improvements moving, and **public value framing** helps communities judge innovation by benefits that extend beyond the original inventor.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

Innovation bắt đầu với **innovation optimism**, nhưng team responsible calibrate **research risk perception** trước khi promise result. **Inventor identity** sustain effort, trong khi **novelty discount** làm idea unfamiliar trông ít value. Leader nên distinguish **sunk research cost** khỏi reason continue, và xem **patent salience** đổi việc share thế nào. **Collaborative trust** hỗ trợ **knowledge spillover belief**, đặc biệt khi **science communication cue** rõ bảo vệ **expert credibility**. **Innovation ambiguity tolerance** cao và **experimentation norm** tạo room learning, còn **failure stigma** suppress evidence hữu ích. **Grant deadline effect** có thể narrow choice đúng lúc **discovery serendipity** cần patience. **Technology adoption expectation** và **innovation diffusion cue** shape prototype spread, nhưng **standardization friction** làm tool valuable slow. **Open-source reciprocity** giữ improvement shared tiếp tục, và **public value framing** giúp community judge innovation bằng benefit vượt original inventor.
