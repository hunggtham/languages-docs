# C1 Vocabulary 11 — Behavioral education and learning decisions

This lesson follows how students, families, and teachers make education decisions under limited attention, uncertainty, and social influence.

Flow: **student present bias → learning mindset → effort belief → teacher expectation → peer achievement norm → grade salience → test anxiety bias → education choice architecture → default enrollment education → college application friction → education aspiration → school quality perception → tuition salience → student debt framing → academic procrastination → study habit formation → learning goal framing → behavioral education economics → feedback framing → growth mindset cue**.

## 1. student present bias /ˈstuːdənt ˈprɛzənt baɪəs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a tendency for students to value immediate comfort over future learning, grades, or opportunities.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** student value comfort immediate hơn learning, grade hoặc opportunity future.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong student present bias` — bias; `address student present bias` — address.
**Examples:** `Student present bias made the learner postpone a difficult reading until the night before the exam.` → Bias present student làm learner postpone reading khó tới đêm trước exam.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `학생 현재 편향`.

## 2. learning mindset /ˈlɝnɪŋ ˈmaɪnˌsɛt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a person's beliefs about whether ability can develop through practice, strategy, and support.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** belief ability develop qua practice, strategy và support không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `adaptive learning mindset` — mindset; `cultivate a learning mindset` — cultivate.
**Examples:** `A learning mindset helped students treat a difficult draft as information about what to practice.` → Mindset learning giúp student xem draft khó là information cần practice.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `학습 사고방식`.

## 3. effort belief /ˈɛfərt bɪˈliːf/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a belief about whether additional work is likely to improve performance or understanding.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** belief effort thêm có likely improve performance hoặc understanding không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `accurate effort belief` — belief; `change an effort belief` — change.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `노력 믿음`.

## 4. teacher expectation /ˈtiːtʃər ˌɛkspɛkˈteɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a teacher's belief about a student's likely performance that can influence instruction and student behavior.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** belief teacher về performance likely của student, có thể influence instruction và behavior.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `교사 기대`.

## 5. peer achievement norm /pɪr əˈtʃiːvmənt nɔrm/

**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** expectation shared giữa classmate về study hoặc perform serious thế nào.
**Grammar & collocations:** `positive peer achievement norm` — norm; `strengthen a peer achievement norm` — strengthen.

## 6. grade salience /ɡreɪd ˈseɪliəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** how visible and psychologically important grades are when a student chooses effort or strategy.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mức grade visible và psychologically important khi student choose effort hoặc strategy.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high grade salience` — salience; `reduce grade salience` — reduce.

## 7. test anxiety bias /tɛst æŋˈzaɪəti baɪəs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a systematic performance distortion caused by anxiety during an assessment rather than by the underlying skill being measured.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** distortion performance do anxiety trong assessment, không phải skill thật.
**Grammar & collocations:** `test-anxiety bias effect` — effect; `adjust for test anxiety bias` — adjust.
**Examples:** `Test anxiety bias lowered the score of a capable student during timed testing.` → Bias anxiety test làm score student capable giảm trong timed test.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `시험 불안 편향`.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `교육 선택 설계`.

## Review in context

**Student present bias**, a **learning mindset**, and an **effort belief** shape how students respond to a **teacher expectation** and a **peer achievement norm**. **Grade salience** and **test anxiety bias** can distort performance, while **education choice architecture** and **default enrollment education** lower **college application friction**. **Education aspiration** depends partly on **school quality perception**, **tuition salience**, and **student debt framing**. **Academic procrastination** can be reduced through **study habit formation**, **learning goal framing**, and **feedback framing**. **Behavioral education economics** uses a **growth mindset cue** to make persistence more likely.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:** **Student present bias**, **learning mindset** và **effort belief** shape cách student respond với **teacher expectation** và **peer achievement norm**. **Grade salience** và **test anxiety bias** có thể distort performance, còn **education choice architecture** và **default enrollment education** giảm **college application friction**. **Education aspiration** partly phụ thuộc **school quality perception**, **tuition salience** và **student debt framing**. **Academic procrastination** có thể giảm qua **study habit formation**, **learning goal framing** và **feedback framing**. **Behavioral education economics** dùng **growth mindset cue** làm persistence likely hơn.
