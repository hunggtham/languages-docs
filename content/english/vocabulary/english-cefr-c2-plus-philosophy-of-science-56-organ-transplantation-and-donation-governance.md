# C2+ Vocabulary — Organ transplantation and donation governance

This lesson follows how transplant systems obtain organs, preserve them, allocate them fairly, and support recipients and donors. The network is **organ allocation → waiting-list mortality → donor risk index → deceased donation → living donation → donation after circulatory death → donation after brain death → organ preservation → ex vivo perfusion → ischemic time → transplant immunosuppression → rejection surveillance → graft dysfunction → HLA matching → crossmatch → desensitization → paired exchange → donor chain → transplant tourism → organ trafficking → consent registry → donor family support → post-transplant adherence → graft survival → allocation equity**.

## 1. organ allocation /ˈɔrɡən ˌæləˈkeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the process of assigning donated organs to people waiting for transplant according to medical, ethical, logistical, and legal criteria.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** organ allocation biến resource cực hiếm thành rule phân bổ có lý do.
**Grammar & collocations:** `organ-allocation policy` — policy; `allocate a donated organ` — allocate; `eligibility criterion` — criterion.
**Examples:** `Organ allocation balanced urgency, expected benefit, compatibility, geography, and fairness.` → Allocation phải cân nhiều value.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `장기 배분` — organ allocation.

## 2. waiting-list mortality /ˈweɪtɪŋ lɪst mɔrˈtæləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** deaths occurring among people waiting for an organ before a transplant becomes available.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** waiting-list mortality cho thấy cost của việc phải chờ organ.
**Grammar & collocations:** `waiting-list-mortality rate` — rate; `die while waiting` — die; `transplant delay` — delay.
**Examples:** `Waiting-list mortality was highest for patients whose disease progressed faster than the organ supply.` → Supply và disease speed tạo gap.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `대기자 명단 사망률` — waiting-list mortality.

## 3. donor risk index /ˈdoʊnər rɪsk ˈɪnˌdɛks/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a measure estimating how donor and organ characteristics may affect transplant outcomes.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** donor risk index mô tả risk của organ theo donor/organ features.
**Grammar & collocations:** `donor-risk-index estimate` — estimate; `assess donor risk` — assess; `organ quality` — quality.
**Examples:** `The donor risk index supported transparent discussion rather than an automatic rejection of the organ.` → Index hỗ trợ decision, không thay decision.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `기증자 위험 지수` — donor risk index.

## 4. deceased donation /dɪˈsiːst doʊˈneɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** donation of organs from a person who has died under conditions that permit lawful recovery.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** deceased donation lấy organ sau death theo consent và legal process.
**Grammar & collocations:** `deceased-donation pathway` — pathway; `recover a donated organ` — recover; `donor authorization` — authorization.
**Examples:** `Deceased donation required coordination among the family, hospital, registry, and recovery team.` → Nhiều actor cùng process.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `사후 장기 기증` — deceased donation.

## 5. living donation /ˈlɪvɪŋ doʊˈneɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** donation of an organ or organ segment by a living person who undergoes evaluation and surgery.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** living donation có donor còn sống nên cần protect autonomy và long-term health.
**Grammar & collocations:** `living-donation evaluation` — evaluation; `donate an organ segment` — donate; `donor protection` — protection.
**Examples:** `Living donation required independent counseling so the donor could decide without family pressure.` → Independent counseling bảo vệ choice.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `생체 장기 기증` — living donation.

## 6. donation after circulatory death /doʊˈneɪʃən ˈæftər ˈsɜrkjələtɔri dɛθ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** organ donation after death is determined by permanent cessation of circulation and breathing under a defined protocol.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** donation after circulatory death có pathway xác định death bằng circulation/breathing.
**Grammar & collocations:** `donation-after-circulatory-death protocol` — protocol; `recover organs after death` — recover; `withdrawal of support` — withdrawal.
**Examples:** `Donation after circulatory death required a clear separation between end-of-life care and donation decisions.` → Care decision phải độc lập.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `순환기 사망 후 기증` — donation after circulatory death.

## 7. donation after brain death /doʊˈneɪʃən ˈæftər breɪn dɛθ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** organ donation after death is determined by legally recognized irreversible loss of all brain functions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** donation after brain death dùng tiêu chuẩn brain death theo pháp luật và protocol.
**Grammar & collocations:** `donation-after-brain-death determination` — determination; `confirm brain death` — confirm; `organ recovery` — recovery.
**Examples:** `Donation after brain death followed independent testing and a separate family discussion.` → Determination độc lập trước donation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `뇌사 후 장기 기증` — donation after brain death.

## 8. organ preservation /ˈɔrɡən ˌprɛzərˈveɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** methods used to protect an organ's structure and function between recovery from a donor and implantation in a recipient.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** organ preservation giữ organ sống được trong khoảng thời gian giữa donor và recipient.
**Grammar & collocations:** `organ-preservation method` — method; `preserve organ viability` — preserve; `transport solution` — solution.
**Examples:** `Organ preservation began immediately after recovery and continued through controlled transport.` → Time và temperature đều quan trọng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `장기 보존` — organ preservation.

## 9. ex vivo perfusion /ɛks ˈvaɪvoʊ pərˈfjuːʒən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** circulation of oxygenated fluid through an organ outside the body to preserve, assess, or sometimes improve its condition before transplant.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** ex vivo perfusion đưa organ ra ngoài body nhưng vẫn cung cấp flow/oxygen để assess.
**Grammar & collocations:** `ex-vivo-perfusion platform` — platform; `perfuse an organ` — perfuse; `assess organ function` — assess.
**Examples:** `Ex vivo perfusion allowed the team to assess an organ that had initially seemed too risky to use.` → Platform mở thêm information.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `체외 관류` — ex vivo perfusion.

## 10. ischemic time /ɪˈskiːmɪk taɪm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** time during which an organ receives insufficient blood flow and oxygen between recovery and restoration of circulation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** ischemic time là đồng hồ từ mất flow đến revascularization.
**Grammar & collocations:** `ischemic-time limit` — limit; `minimize ischemic time` — minimize; `organ transport` — transport.
**Examples:** `The coordination center shortened ischemic time by aligning the aircraft, surgical team, and recipient preparation.` → Logistics trực tiếp ảnh hưởng organ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `허혈 시간` — ischemic time.

## 11. transplant immunosuppression /ˈtrænsˌplænt ˌɪmjənoʊsəˈprɛʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** medicines and monitoring used to reduce immune attack against a transplanted organ.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** transplant immunosuppression bảo vệ graft nhưng tăng infection và other risk cần cân bằng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `transplant-immunosuppression regimen` — regimen; `suppress immune rejection` — suppress; `drug monitoring` — monitoring.
**Examples:** `Transplant immunosuppression required regular laboratory monitoring and careful infection prevention.` → Benefit đi cùng monitoring.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `이식 면역억제` — transplant immunosuppression.

## 12. rejection surveillance /rɪˈdʒɛkʃən sərˈveɪləns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** systematic monitoring for signs that the recipient's immune system is damaging the transplanted organ.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** rejection surveillance tìm signal graft bị immune attack trước khi loss lớn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `rejection-surveillance schedule` — schedule; `monitor graft function` — monitor; `immune injury` — injury.
**Examples:** `Rejection surveillance combined laboratory tests, symptoms, imaging, and biopsy when indicated.` → Nhiều signal cùng nhau.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `거부반응 감시` — rejection surveillance.

## 13. graft dysfunction /ɡræft dɪsˈfʌŋkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** impaired function of a transplanted organ, whether caused by rejection, ischemia, infection, medication, or another problem.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** graft dysfunction là graft hoạt động kém, cần tìm nhiều possible cause.
**Grammar & collocations:** `graft-dysfunction workup` — workup; `investigate declining function` — investigate; `transplant complication` — complication.
**Examples:** `Graft dysfunction prompted a review of drug levels, blood flow, infection, and rejection markers.` → Workup đa nguyên nhân.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `이식편 기능 장애` — graft dysfunction.

## 14. HLA matching /ˌeɪtʃ ɛl eɪ ˈmætʃɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** comparison of donor and recipient human leukocyte antigen profiles to inform compatibility and immune risk.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** HLA matching so profile immune của donor và recipient.
**Grammar & collocations:** `HLA-matching result` — result; `compare antigen profiles` — compare; `compatibility assessment` — assessment.
**Examples:** `HLA matching informed the allocation discussion but did not override urgency and other criteria.` → Matching chỉ là một factor.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `HLA 적합성 검사` — HLA matching.

## 15. crossmatch /ˈkrɔsˌmætʃ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** laboratory test mixing donor and recipient material to detect immune reactions that could threaten a transplant.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** crossmatch test trực tiếp phản ứng immune giữa donor và recipient.
**Grammar & collocations:** `crossmatch result` — result; `perform a crossmatch` — perform; `immune reaction` — reaction.
**Examples:** `A positive crossmatch required specialist review before the transplant could proceed.` → Positive result cần review kỹ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `교차 적합성 검사` — crossmatch.

## 16. desensitization /diːˌsɛnsətəˈzeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** treatment intended to reduce harmful preexisting immune reactivity so a transplant may become possible or safer.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** desensitization giảm immune barrier trước transplant trong selected cases.
**Grammar & collocations:** `desensitization protocol` — protocol; `reduce antibody reactivity` — reduce; `highly sensitized recipient` — recipient.
**Examples:** `Desensitization was considered for a highly sensitized recipient with few compatible offers.` → Strategy mở cơ hội nhưng có risk.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `탈감작` — desensitization.

## 17. paired exchange /pɛrd ɪksˈtʃeɪndʒ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** arrangement in which incompatible donor-recipient pairs exchange donors so each recipient can receive a compatible organ.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** paired exchange nối hai cặp incompatible để tạo match chéo.
**Grammar & collocations:** `paired-exchange program` — program; `exchange donor pairs` — exchange; `compatible match` — match.
**Examples:** `Paired exchange allowed two willing donors to help their intended recipients through a cross-match.` → Pair đổi để cả hai nhận organ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `교환 이식` — paired exchange.

## 18. donor chain /ˈdoʊnər tʃeɪn/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a sequence of linked donations in which one donor's action enables a series of transplants across multiple recipients.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** donor chain là chuỗi donation kéo nhiều transplant.
**Grammar & collocations:** `donor-chain coordination` — coordination; `start a donation chain` — start; `linked transplant` — transplant.
**Examples:** `A donor chain began with an altruistic donor and enabled several compatible exchanges.` → Một donor mở chain dài.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `기증자 연쇄` — donor chain.

## 19. transplant tourism /ˈtrænsˌplænt ˈtʊrɪzəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** travel across borders to obtain a transplant, often outside the person's ordinary health system and sometimes involving unsafe or exploitative practices.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** transplant tourism có thể tạo risk y tế, pháp lý và đạo đức khi đi tìm organ ngoài system.
**Grammar & collocations:** `transplant-tourism risk` — risk; `travel for a transplant` — travel; `cross-border practice` — practice.
**Examples:** `The clinic explained transplant tourism risks, including uncertain follow-up and unclear organ provenance.` → Risk không chỉ ở surgery.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `장기이식 관광` — transplant tourism.

**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** organ trafficking biến organ và vulnerable people thành hàng hóa bất hợp pháp.
**Grammar & collocations:** `organ-trafficking investigation` — investigation; `combat illegal organ trade` — combat; `exploitation risk` — risk.

## 21. consent registry /kənˈsɛnt ˈrɛdʒɪstri/

## 22. donor family support /ˈdoʊnər ˈfæməli səˈpɔrt/

## 23. post-transplant adherence /poʊst ˈtrænsˌplænt ədˈhɪrəns/

**Core meaning (English):** continued following of medicines, monitoring, appointments, and self-care needed to protect a transplanted organ.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** post-transplant adherence giữ graft bằng routine dài hạn.

## 24. graft survival /ɡræft sərˈvaɪvəl/

**Core meaning (English):** continued functioning and survival of a transplanted organ over a defined period.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** graft survival đo graft sống và hoạt động theo thời gian.

## 25. allocation equity /ˌæləˈkeɪʃən ˈɛkwəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** fairness in how scarce organs and transplant opportunities are distributed across people and populations.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** allocation equity hỏi rule có công bằng với group khác nhau không.

## Review in context — Part 1

**Organ allocation** must consider **waiting-list mortality** and a **donor risk index** while distinguishing **deceased donation** from **living donation**. **Donation after circulatory death** and **donation after brain death** require clear definitions and independent safeguards. **Organ preservation**, **ex vivo perfusion**, and limited **ischemic time** affect whether an organ can be used. After transplant, **transplant immunosuppression**, **rejection surveillance**, and evaluation of **graft dysfunction** protect the recipient. **HLA matching** and a **crossmatch** inform compatibility; **desensitization** may be considered in selected cases.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 1:**

**Organ allocation** phải cân **waiting-list mortality** và **donor risk index**, đồng thời phân biệt **deceased donation** với **living donation**. **Donation after circulatory death** và **donation after brain death** cần định nghĩa rõ và safeguard độc lập. **Organ preservation**, **ex vivo perfusion** và **ischemic time** giới hạn khả năng dùng organ. Sau transplant, **transplant immunosuppression**, **rejection surveillance** và đánh giá **graft dysfunction** bảo vệ recipient. **HLA matching** và **crossmatch** cung cấp thông tin compatibility; **desensitization** có thể được cân nhắc trong case phù hợp.

## Review in context — Part 2

**Paired exchange** and a **donor chain** can create matches when direct donation is incompatible. **Transplant tourism** and **organ trafficking** show why provenance, law, and protection matter. A **consent registry** records a person's decision, while **donor family support** recognizes grief and choice. **Post-transplant adherence** supports **graft survival**. Finally, **allocation equity** asks whether the whole system distributes opportunity fairly rather than merely applying a neutral-looking rule.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 2:**

**Paired exchange** và **donor chain** có thể tạo match khi donation trực tiếp không compatible. **Transplant tourism** và **organ trafficking** cho thấy provenance, law và protection quan trọng. **Consent registry** ghi decision của person, còn **donor family support** thừa nhận grief và choice. **Post-transplant adherence** hỗ trợ **graft survival**. Cuối cùng, **allocation equity** hỏi toàn system có phân phối opportunity công bằng không, chứ không chỉ apply một rule trông có vẻ neutral.
