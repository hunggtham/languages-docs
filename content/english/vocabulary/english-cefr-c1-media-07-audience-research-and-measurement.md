# C1 Vocabulary 07 — Audience research and measurement

This lesson follows how publishers measure reach, understand reader journeys, and distinguish useful engagement from superficial traffic.

Flow: **audience panel → reach estimate → unique visitor → returning visitor → conversion funnel → retention cohort → cohort analysis → attribution window → referral source → direct traffic → organic search → engagement rate → completion rate → bounce rate → click-through rate → open rate → unsubscribe rate → audience segmentation → user journey → survey weighting**.

## 1. audience panel /ˈɔdiəns ˈpænəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a selected group of people whose media behavior is measured to estimate patterns in a larger audience.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** group người được chọn để đo media behavior và estimate pattern audience lớn hơn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `audience-panel data` — data; `recruit an audience panel` — recruit.
**Examples:** `The audience panel showed that older readers preferred email updates to social alerts.` → Panel audience cho thấy reader lớn tuổi thích email update hơn social alert.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `수용자 패널`.

## 2. reach estimate /riːtʃ ˈɛstəmət/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an estimate of how many people were exposed to a piece of content during a specified period.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** estimate số người exposed với content trong period cụ thể.
**Grammar & collocations:** `reach-estimate uncertainty` — uncertainty; `calculate a reach estimate` — tính.
**Examples:** `The reach estimate included readers who received the article through partner newsletters.` → Estimate reach gồm reader nhận article qua newsletter partner.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `도달 추정치`.

## 3. unique visitor /juˈniːk ˈvɪzətər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** one distinct person or device counted once during a defined measurement period.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** một person/device distinct được count một lần trong period đo.
**Grammar & collocations:** `unique-visitor count` — count; `estimate unique visitors` — estimate.
**Examples:** `The report separated unique visitors from total page views to avoid counting repeat visits as new people.` → Report tách visitor unique khỏi total page view để không coi visit lặp là người mới.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `순 방문자`.

## 4. returning visitor /rɪˈtɝnɪŋ ˈvɪzətər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a person or device that comes back to a site or service after an earlier visit.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** person/device quay lại site/service sau visit trước.
**Grammar & collocations:** `returning-visitor rate` — rate; `identify returning visitors` — identify.
**Examples:** `Returning visitors increased after the newsroom added a weekly local briefing.` → Visitor quay lại tăng sau khi newsroom thêm briefing địa phương hàng tuần.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `재방문자`.

## 5. conversion funnel /kənˈvɝʒən ˈfʌnəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the sequence of stages through which a visitor moves from first contact to a desired action such as subscribing.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** sequence stage từ contact đầu đến action mong muốn như subscribe.
**Grammar & collocations:** `conversion-funnel stage` — stage; `analyze a conversion funnel` — phân tích.
**Examples:** `The conversion funnel lost many readers between registration and the first payment.` → Funnel conversion mất nhiều reader giữa registration và payment đầu.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `전환 퍼널`.

## 6. retention cohort /rɪˈtɛnʃən ˈkoʊˌhɔrt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a group of users who began at a similar time and are tracked to see whether they continue returning.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** group user bắt đầu cùng time và track xem có tiếp tục return.
**Grammar & collocations:** `retention-cohort curve` — curve; `track a retention cohort` — theo dõi.
**Examples:** `The January retention cohort remained more active than the cohort acquired through a short promotion.` → Cohort retention January active hơn cohort thu qua promotion ngắn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `유지 코호트`.

## 7. cohort analysis /ˈkoʊˌhɔrt əˈnæləsɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** analysis that compares groups formed by a shared starting date, behavior, or characteristic over time.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** analysis so group có start date, behavior hoặc characteristic chung theo thời gian.
**Grammar & collocations:** `cohort-analysis table` — table; `run cohort analysis` — thực hiện.
**Examples:** `Cohort analysis showed that annual subscribers stayed longer than monthly subscribers acquired in the same week.` → Analysis cohort cho thấy subscriber annual stay lâu hơn monthly cùng tuần.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `코호트 분석`.

## 8. attribution window /ˌætrəˈbjuːʃən ˈwɪndoʊ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the period during which a later action is credited to an earlier advertisement, message, or referral.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** period action sau được credit cho ad, message hoặc referral trước.
**Grammar & collocations:** `attribution-window rule` — rule; `set an attribution window` — đặt.
**Examples:** `A seven-day attribution window credited the newsletter with subscriptions made after the initial click.` → Window attribution bảy ngày credit newsletter cho subscription sau click đầu.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `기여 기간`.

## 9. referral source /rɪˈfɝəl sɔrs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the site, message, person, or channel that sends a visitor to a piece of content.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** site, message, person hoặc channel gửi visitor đến content.
**Grammar & collocations:** `referral-source report` — report; `identify a referral source` — identify.
**Examples:** `The referral source was a community newsletter rather than a search engine.` → Source referral là newsletter cộng đồng chứ không phải search engine.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `추천 출처`.

## 10. direct traffic /dəˈrɛkt ˈtræfɪk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** visits that arrive without a measurable external referral, often through a typed address, bookmark, or saved link.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** visit không có external referral đo được, thường qua typed address, bookmark hoặc saved link.
**Grammar & collocations:** `direct-traffic share` — share; `measure direct traffic` — đo.
**Examples:** `Direct traffic rose after the paper printed its short web address on every receipt.` → Traffic direct tăng sau khi paper in web address ngắn trên mọi receipt.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `직접 트래픽`.

## 11. organic search /ɔrˈɡænɪk sɝtʃ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** visits that arrive through unpaid search results rather than advertising placement.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** visit qua search result không trả tiền, không phải ad placement.
**Grammar & collocations:** `organic-search traffic` — traffic; `grow organic search` — tăng.
**Examples:** `Organic search brought new readers to the archive's explanation of local water rules.` → Search organic đưa reader mới đến explanation archive về rule water địa phương.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `자연 검색`.

## 12. engagement rate /ɪnˈɡeɪdʒmənt reɪt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the share of an audience that performs a defined interaction such as clicking, commenting, sharing, or saving.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** share audience thực hiện interaction như click, comment, share hoặc save.
**Grammar & collocations:** `engagement-rate denominator` — denominator; `compare engagement rates` — so sánh.
**Examples:** `The engagement rate was high, but most interactions came from a small group of loyal readers.` → Rate engagement cao nhưng đa số interaction đến từ group nhỏ reader loyal.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `참여율`.

## 13. completion rate /kəmˈpliːʃən reɪt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the share of people who finish a defined item, process, video, form, or task after starting it.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** share người finish item, process, video, form hoặc task sau khi start.
**Grammar & collocations:** `completion-rate comparison` — comparison; `raise the completion rate` — tăng.
**Examples:** `The completion rate for the data lesson rose after the introduction was shortened.` → Rate completion lesson data tăng sau khi intro ngắn hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `완료율`.

## 14. bounce rate /baʊns reɪt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the share of visits that leave after viewing only one page or taking no measured further action.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** share visit rời sau một page hoặc không có action tiếp theo đo được.
**Grammar & collocations:** `bounce-rate interpretation` — interpretation; `lower the bounce rate` — giảm.
**Examples:** `The bounce rate fell when the article linked directly to the underlying documents.` → Rate bounce giảm khi article link thẳng đến document gốc.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `이탈률`.

## 15. click-through rate /ˈklɪk θruː reɪt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the share of people who click a link after seeing it in an email, message, page, or advertisement.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** share người click link sau khi thấy trong email, message, page hoặc ad.
**Grammar & collocations:** `click-through-rate test` — test; `improve click-through rate` — cải thiện.
**Examples:** `The click-through rate increased when the newsletter used a specific description instead of “read more.”` → Rate click-through tăng khi newsletter dùng description cụ thể thay vì “read more.”
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `클릭률`.

## 16. open rate /ˈoʊpən reɪt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the share of delivered messages that recipients open or are recorded as opening.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** share message deliver mà recipient open hoặc được record là đã open.
**Grammar & collocations:** `newsletter open rate` — rate; `compare open rates` — so sánh.
**Examples:** `The open rate improved after the subject line stated the local issue clearly.` → Rate open cải thiện sau khi subject line nêu issue địa phương rõ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `열람률`.

## 17. unsubscribe rate /ˌʌnsəbˈskraɪb reɪt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the share of recipients who stop receiving a newsletter, alert, or other recurring message.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** share recipient stop nhận newsletter, alert hoặc message lặp.
**Grammar & collocations:** `unsubscribe-rate spike` — spike; `monitor the unsubscribe rate` — monitor.
**Examples:** `The unsubscribe rate spiked after the service doubled the number of daily alerts.` → Rate unsubscribe spike sau khi service gấp đôi alert hằng ngày.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `구독 해지율`.

## 18. audience segmentation /ˈɔdiəns ˌsɛɡmənˈteɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the division of an audience into groups with different needs, behaviors, locations, or preferences.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** chia audience thành group khác về need, behavior, location hoặc preference.
**Grammar & collocations:** `audience-segmentation model` — model; `segment an audience` — chia nhóm.
**Examples:** `Audience segmentation helped the newsroom send a farming bulletin only to interested regions.` → Phân khúc audience giúp newsroom gửi bulletin farming chỉ cho region interested.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `수용자 세분화`.

## 19. user journey /ˈjuːzər ˈdʒɝni/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the sequence of steps and experiences a person has while discovering, using, and returning to a service.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** sequence step/experience khi person discover, use và return service.
**Grammar & collocations:** `user-journey map` — map; `trace a user journey` — trace.
**Examples:** `The user journey showed that readers abandoned the subscription form after the identity check.` → Journey user cho thấy reader bỏ form subscription sau identity check.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `사용자 여정`.

## 20. survey weighting /ˈsɝveɪ ˈweɪtɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** adjusting survey responses so the sample better represents the target population's known composition.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** điều chỉnh response survey để sample đại diện target population tốt hơn theo composition đã biết.
**Grammar & collocations:** `survey-weighting adjustment` — adjustment; `apply survey weighting` — áp dụng.
**Examples:** `Survey weighting corrected for the overrepresentation of frequent online readers.` → Weighting survey sửa việc frequent online reader bị overrepresent.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `설문 가중`.

## Review in context

An **audience panel** supports a **reach estimate** by comparing a **unique visitor** with a **returning visitor**. A **conversion funnel**, **retention cohort**, and **cohort analysis** show whether attention becomes durable support, while an **attribution window** and **referral source** clarify where visitors came from. **Direct traffic** and **organic search** describe discovery; **engagement rate**, **completion rate**, **bounce rate**, **click-through rate**, **open rate**, and **unsubscribe rate** describe different behaviors. **Audience segmentation**, a **user journey**, and **survey weighting** help interpret the numbers responsibly.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

**Audience panel** hỗ trợ **reach estimate** bằng cách so **unique visitor** với **returning visitor**. **Conversion funnel**, **retention cohort** và **cohort analysis** cho thấy attention có thành support lâu dài không, còn **attribution window** và **referral source** làm rõ visitor đến từ đâu. **Direct traffic** và **organic search** mô tả discovery; **engagement rate**, **completion rate**, **bounce rate**, **click-through rate**, **open rate** và **unsubscribe rate** mô tả behavior khác nhau. **Audience segmentation**, **user journey** và **survey weighting** giúp diễn giải số liệu có trách nhiệm.
