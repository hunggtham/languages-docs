# C1 Vocabulary 20 — Media law and rights management

This lesson follows how journalists and editors manage legal risks while protecting reporting, sources, and the public's right to know.

Flow: **libel standard → privacy tort → copyright license → fair use analysis → takedown notice → legal threat → prepublication review → source privilege → confidentiality clause → contempt risk → injunction request → copyright clearance → legal hold → press freedom → publication ban → copyright exception → defamation defense → legal correspondent → right to reply → court reporting**.

## 1. libel standard /ˈlaɪbəl ˈstændərd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the legal test used to decide whether a published statement is unlawfully damaging and false.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** legal test decide statement publish có unlawfully damaging và false không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strict libel standard` — standard; `apply a libel standard` — apply.
**Examples:** `The editor checked the libel standard before naming the contractor in the investigation.` → Editor check standard libel trước khi name contractor trong investigation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `명예훼손 기준`.

## 2. privacy tort /ˈpraɪvəsi tɔrt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a civil wrong involving an unjustified intrusion into a person's private life or personal information.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** civil wrong liên quan intrusion không justified vào private life hoặc personal information.
**Grammar & collocations:** `possible privacy tort` — tort; `allege a privacy tort` — allege.
**Examples:** `The newsroom removed a detail that could create a privacy tort without adding public value.` → Newsroom remove detail có thể tạo tort privacy mà không thêm public value.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `사생활 불법행위`.

## 3. copyright license /ˈkɑpiˌraɪt ˈlaɪsəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** permission from a rights holder to use protected creative material under specified conditions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** permission từ rights holder use creative material protected theo condition cụ thể.
**Grammar & collocations:** `limited copyright license` — license; `negotiate a copyright license` — negotiate.
**Examples:** `The producer obtained a copyright license for the archive photographs.` → Producer obtain license copyright cho archive photo.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `저작권 라이선스`.

## 4. fair use analysis /ˌfɛr ˈjuːs əˈnæləsɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a reasoned assessment of whether a use of copyrighted material may qualify for a legal exception.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** assessment có reasoning xem use copyrighted material có qualify legal exception không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `documented fair use analysis` — analysis; `conduct a fair use analysis` — conduct.
**Examples:** `The fair use analysis considered the purpose, amount, and market effect of the quotation.` → Analysis fair use xem purpose, amount và market effect của quotation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `공정 이용 분석`.

## 5. takedown notice /ˈteɪkˌdaʊn ˈnoʊtɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a formal demand that material be removed from a service because it allegedly violates a legal right or rule.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** demand formal remove material vì alleged violate legal right hoặc rule.
**Grammar & collocations:** `copyright takedown notice` — notice; `receive a takedown notice` — receive.
**Examples:** `The publisher received a takedown notice but kept the disputed excerpt while reviewing it.` → Publisher receive notice takedown nhưng giữ excerpt đang dispute trong lúc review.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `삭제 통지`.

## 6. legal threat /ˈliːɡəl θrɛt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a warning that someone may begin legal action if a demand is not met.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** warning someone may begin legal action nếu demand không được meet.
**Grammar & collocations:** `credible legal threat` — threat; `respond to a legal threat` — respond.
**Examples:** `The editor treated the legal threat seriously without changing the verified facts.` → Editor treat threat legal serious nhưng không đổi verified fact.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `법적 위협`.

## 7. prepublication review /ˌpriːpʌbləˈkeɪʃən rɪˈvjuː/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a check of planned material before release for accuracy, fairness, security, and avoidable legal risk.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** check material trước release về accuracy, fairness, security và legal risk avoidable.
**Grammar & collocations:** `senior prepublication review` — review; `request prepublication review` — request.
**Examples:** `Prepublication review caught an incorrect date and a revealing detail about a source.` → Review prepublication catch date sai và detail reveal về source.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `출판 전 검토`.

## 8. source privilege /sɔrs ˈprɪvəlɪdʒ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a legal protection that may allow a journalist to resist revealing a confidential source in specified circumstances.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** protection legal có thể cho journalist resist reveal confidential source trong circumstance cụ thể.
**Grammar & collocations:** `limited source privilege` — privilege; `claim source privilege` — claim.
**Examples:** `The lawyer explained that source privilege depended on the jurisdiction and facts.` → Lawyer explain privilege source phụ thuộc jurisdiction và fact.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `취재원 특권`.

## 9. confidentiality clause /ˌkɑnfəˌdɛnʃiˈæləti klɔz/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a contract term that restricts how specified information may be shared or disclosed.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** term trong contract restrict cách share hoặc disclose information cụ thể.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strict confidentiality clause` — clause; `include a confidentiality clause` — include.
**Examples:** `The interview agreement contained a confidentiality clause covering unpublished medical records.` → Agreement interview có clause confidentiality cover medical record chưa publish.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `비밀 유지 조항`.

## 10. contempt risk /kənˈtɛmpt rɪsk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the possibility that publication or conduct could disobey or improperly interfere with a court's authority.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** possibility publication hoặc conduct disobey hoặc interfere improper với authority court.
**Grammar & collocations:** `serious contempt risk` — risk; `assess contempt risk` — assess.
**Examples:** `The legal desk assessed contempt risk before publishing material covered by a court order.` → Desk legal assess risk contempt trước khi publish material bị court order cover.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `법정 모독 위험`.

## 11. injunction request /ɪnˈdʒʌŋkʃən rɪˈkwɛst/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a formal request for a court order requiring someone to stop or take a particular action.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** request formal xin court order yêu cầu someone stop hoặc take action cụ thể.
**Grammar & collocations:** `emergency injunction request` — request; `file an injunction request` — file.
**Examples:** `The publisher prepared for an injunction request but continued checking every allegation.` → Publisher prepare cho request injunction nhưng tiếp tục check mọi allegation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `금지명령 신청`.

## 12. copyright clearance /ˈkɑpiˌraɪt ˈklɪrəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the process of confirming permission to reproduce, adapt, display, or distribute copyrighted material.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** process confirm permission reproduce, adapt, display hoặc distribute material copyrighted.
**Grammar & collocations:** `complete copyright clearance` — clearance; `delay publication for copyright clearance` — delay.
**Examples:** `Copyright clearance delayed the documentary until the music licenses were confirmed.` → Clearance copyright delay documentary tới khi music license confirm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `저작권 허가 확인`.

## 13. legal hold /ˈliːɡəl hoʊld/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an instruction to preserve potentially relevant records because litigation or an investigation may occur.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** instruction preserve record liên quan vì litigation hoặc investigation có thể xảy ra.
**Grammar & collocations:** `formal legal hold` — hold; `issue a legal hold` — issue.
**Examples:** `The company issued a legal hold covering emails, drafts, and interview notes.` → Company issue hold legal cover email, draft và interview note.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `법적 보존 명령`.

## 14. press freedom /ˈprɛs ˈfriːdəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the ability of journalists and media organizations to report and publish without improper interference or censorship.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** ability journalist và media organization report, publish không bị interference hoặc censorship improper.
**Grammar & collocations:** `protect press freedom` — freedom; `threaten press freedom` — threaten.
**Examples:** `The court recognized that a broad restriction could threaten press freedom.` → Court recognize restriction rộng có thể threaten freedom press.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `언론의 자유`.

## 15. publication ban /ˌpʌbləˈkeɪʃən bæn/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an official restriction that prevents certain information from being published or broadcast.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** restriction official prevent information cụ thể publish hoặc broadcast.
**Grammar & collocations:** `temporary publication ban` — ban; `challenge a publication ban` — challenge.
**Examples:** `The editor explained the publication ban and removed only the restricted details.` → Editor explain ban publication và chỉ remove detail bị restrict.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `보도 금지`.

## 16. copyright exception /ˈkɑpiˌraɪt ɪkˈsɛpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a legal rule that permits a limited use of protected work without ordinary permission.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** rule legal permit limited use work protected không cần permission ordinary.
**Grammar & collocations:** `narrow copyright exception` — exception; `rely on a copyright exception` — rely.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `저작권 예외`.

## 17. defamation defense /ˌdɛfəˈmeɪʃən dɪˈfɛns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a legal argument used to show that a challenged statement should not create liability for defamation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** legal argument show statement bị challenge không nên tạo liability defamation.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong defamation defense` — defense; `prepare a defamation defense` — prepare.
**Examples:** `Accurate documentation gave the newspaper a stronger defamation defense.` → Documentation accurate cho newspaper defense defamation mạnh hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `명예훼손 방어`.

## 18. legal correspondent /ˈliːɡəl ˌkɔrəˈspɑndənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a journalist who regularly reports on courts, legislation, legal institutions, and significant cases.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** journalist thường xuyên report court, legislation, legal institution và case quan trọng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `experienced legal correspondent` — correspondent; `brief a legal correspondent` — brief.
**Examples:** `The legal correspondent translated the ruling's technical language for general readers.` → Correspondent legal translate language technical của ruling cho general reader.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `법률 담당 기자`.

## 19. right to reply /raɪt tə rɪˈplaɪ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an opportunity for a person or organization criticized in a report to respond to the relevant claims.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** opportunity cho person hoặc organization bị criticize respond với claim liên quan.
**Grammar & collocations:** `offer a right to reply` — offer; `honor the right to reply` — respect.
**Examples:** `The reporter offered the company a right to reply before publication.` → Reporter offer company right to reply trước publication.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `반론권`.

## 20. court reporting /ˈkɔrt rɪˌpɔrtɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** journalism that accurately explains court proceedings, evidence, arguments, and decisions to the public.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** journalism explain accurately proceeding, evidence, argument và decision của court cho public.
**Grammar & collocations:** `careful court reporting` — reporting; `practice court reporting` — practice.
**Examples:** `Careful court reporting separated allegations from findings established by the judge.` → Reporting court cẩn thận tách allegation khỏi finding judge establish.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `법정 보도`.

## Review in context

A newsroom applies a **libel standard** and considers a possible **privacy tort** before relying on a **copyright license** or completing a **fair use analysis**. A **takedown notice** or **legal threat** may trigger **prepublication review**, while **source privilege** and a **confidentiality clause** protect sensitive reporting. Editors assess **contempt risk** if an **injunction request** or **copyright clearance** dispute is active, and they preserve records under a **legal hold**. **Press freedom** must be weighed against a **publication ban** and any relevant **copyright exception**. A documented **defamation defense**, a careful **legal correspondent**, a genuine **right to reply**, and accurate **court reporting** help the public understand both the story and its limits.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:** Newsroom áp dụng **libel standard** và xem xét **privacy tort** có thể có trước khi rely vào **copyright license** hoặc hoàn tất **fair use analysis**. **Takedown notice** hoặc **legal threat** có thể trigger **prepublication review**, còn **source privilege** và **confidentiality clause** bảo vệ reporting nhạy cảm. Editor assess **contempt risk** nếu **injunction request** hoặc dispute **copyright clearance** đang active, và preserve record theo **legal hold**. **Press freedom** phải được cân nhắc với **publication ban** và **copyright exception** liên quan. **Defamation defense** có documentation, **legal correspondent** cẩn thận, **right to reply** thật sự và **court reporting** accurate giúp public hiểu story và limit của nó.
