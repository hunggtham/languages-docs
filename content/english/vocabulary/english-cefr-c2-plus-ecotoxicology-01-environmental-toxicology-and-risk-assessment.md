# C2+ Vocabulary — Environmental toxicology and risk assessment

This lesson follows how contaminants move through food webs, interact with organisms, and become evidence in ecological risk assessment. The network is **trophic transfer → ecological exposure → point-of-departure → benchmark dose → no-observed-adverse-effect level → lowest-observed-adverse-effect level → adverse outcome pathway → toxicokinetic model → toxicodynamic model → speciation analysis → redox cycling → reactive oxygen species → oxidative stress → endocrine disruption → receptor agonism → receptor antagonism → mixture toxicity → concentration addition → independent action → species sensitivity distribution**.

## 1. trophic transfer /ˈtroʊfɪk ˈtrænsfɜr/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the movement of a chemical, element, or biological agent from one feeding level to another in a food web.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** contaminant đi từ prey sang predator qua việc ăn, như một “vé” chuyển giữa các tầng food web.
**Grammar & collocations:** `trophic-transfer pathway` — pathway; `food-web trophic transfer` — transfer; `quantify transfer` — quantify.
**Examples:** `Trophic transfer moved the contaminant from plankton into predatory fish.` → Trophic transfer đưa contaminant từ plankton vào cá săn mồi.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `영양단계 전이` — chuyển qua trophic level.

## 2. ecological exposure /ˌiːkəˈlɑdʒɪkəl ɪkˈspoʊʒər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the contact of organisms, populations, or ecosystems with a stressor at a concentration, route, and duration relevant to ecological effects.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** exposure trong bối cảnh ecology: species gặp chemical ở đâu, bao lâu, qua nước, sediment hay food.
**Grammar & collocations:** `chronic ecological exposure` — exposure mạn; `exposure pathway` — pathway; `estimate ecological exposure` — estimate.
**Examples:** `Ecological exposure was highest in benthic invertebrates living in the contaminated sediment.` → Ecological exposure cao nhất ở invertebrate đáy sống trong sediment contaminated.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `생태학적 노출` — exposure sinh thái.

## 3. point-of-departure /ˌpɔɪnt əv dɪˈpɑrtʃər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a dose or concentration from a study, such as a threshold or benchmark, used as the starting point for deriving a safe or protective level.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** “điểm xuất phát” của risk calculation, lấy từ evidence rồi điều chỉnh uncertainty và exposure.
**Grammar & collocations:** `toxicological point-of-departure` — P-O-D; `select a point of departure` — select; `POD uncertainty factor` — factor.
**Examples:** `The lowest reliable point-of-departure was used to derive a protective water criterion.` → Point-of-departure đáng tin thấp nhất được dùng để derive water criterion bảo vệ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `출발점` — point-of-departure trong assessment.

## 4. benchmark dose /ˈbɛntʃmɑrk doʊs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a modeled dose associated with a specified change in the probability or magnitude of an adverse response.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** dose lấy từ dose–response curve tại mức response định trước, thay vì chỉ lấy một dose thử nghiệm duy nhất.
**Grammar & collocations:** `benchmark-dose modeling` — modeling; `benchmark dose lower confidence limit` — BMDL; `derive a benchmark dose` — derive.
**Examples:** `The benchmark dose was estimated from all response levels rather than from one observed treatment group.` → Benchmark dose được estimate từ mọi response level thay vì một treatment group.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `기준 용량` — benchmark dose.

## 5. no-observed-adverse-effect level /noʊ əbˈzɜrvd ˈædvɜrs ɪˈfɛkt ˈlɛvəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the highest tested exposure at which a study does not observe a statistically or biologically meaningful adverse effect under its conditions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mức cao nhất trong study mà chưa thấy adverse effect đáng kể; “không thấy” không đồng nghĩa zero risk.
**Grammar & collocations:** `NOAEL value` — value; `identify a NOAEL` — identify; `NOAEL-based assessment` — assessment.
**Examples:** `The no-observed-adverse-effect level was higher in adults than in developing organisms.` → No-observed-adverse-effect level cao hơn ở adult so với organism đang phát triển.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `무관찰 유해영향 수준` — NOAEL.

## 6. lowest-observed-adverse-effect level /ˈloʊəst əbˈzɜrvd ˈædvɜrs ɪˈfɛkt ˈlɛvəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the lowest tested exposure at which a defined adverse effect is observed under the study conditions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mức thấp nhất đã thấy adverse effect; nó thường nằm ngay trên NOAEL nhưng phụ thuộc thiết kế dose.
**Grammar & collocations:** `LOAEL estimate` — estimate; `lowest-observed-adverse-effect level` — LOAEL; `LOAEL-to-NOAEL interval` — interval.
**Examples:** `The lowest-observed-adverse-effect level occurred at the first concentration that impaired larval development.` → Lowest-observed-adverse-effect level xuất hiện ở concentration đầu tiên làm suy giảm phát triển larva.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `최저 관찰 유해영향 수준` — LOAEL.

## 7. adverse outcome pathway /ˈædvɜrs ˈaʊtkʌm ˈpæθweɪ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a structured chain linking a molecular initiating event through cellular and organismal changes to an adverse effect relevant to an individual or population.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** pathway từ “chạm” phân tử đầu tiên đến endpoint xấu ở organism/population, như một flowchart cơ chế toxicity.
**Grammar & collocations:** `adverse-outcome-pathway framework` — framework; `molecular initiating event` — event; `map an AOP` — map.
**Examples:** `The adverse outcome pathway linked receptor activation to impaired reproduction in the fish population.` → Adverse outcome pathway nối receptor activation với suy giảm reproduction ở fish population.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `유해 결과 경로` — adverse outcome pathway.

## 8. toxicokinetic model /ˌtɑksɪkoʊkəˈnɛtɪk ˈmɑdəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a model describing how an external chemical enters, distributes through, transforms within, and leaves an organism.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** toxicokinetics hỏi “cơ thể làm gì với chemical”: absorption, distribution, metabolism và excretion theo thời gian.
**Grammar & collocations:** `physiologically based toxicokinetic model` — model PBK; `toxicokinetic parameter` — parameter; `fit a model` — fit.
**Examples:** `The toxicokinetic model predicted higher internal concentrations after repeated exposure.` → Toxicokinetic model dự đoán internal concentration cao hơn sau exposure lặp lại.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `독성동태 모델` — model toxicokinetic.

## 9. toxicodynamic model /ˌtɑksɪkoʊdaɪˈnæmɪk ˈmɑdəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a model describing how an internal chemical concentration produces molecular, physiological, or ecological effects.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** toxicodynamics hỏi “chemical làm gì với cơ thể”: internal dose biến thành response ra sao.
**Grammar & collocations:** `toxicodynamic response model` — model; `link concentration to effect` — link; `toxicodynamic parameter` — parameter.
**Examples:** `The toxicodynamic model connected tissue concentration with reduced feeding activity.` → Toxicodynamic model nối tissue concentration với feeding activity giảm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `독성동력학 모델` — model toxicodynamic.

## 10. speciation analysis /ˌspiːʃiˈeɪʃən əˈnæləsɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** analysis of the distinct chemical forms in which an element occurs, including their oxidation states, complexes, and physical phases.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** không chỉ hỏi “có bao nhiêu metal” mà hỏi metal đang ở form nào, vì mỗi species có mobility và toxicity khác nhau.
**Grammar & collocations:** `metal speciation analysis` — analysis; `aqueous speciation` — speciation trong nước; `determine chemical species` — determine.
**Examples:** `Speciation analysis showed that the dissolved metal was mostly bound to organic ligands.` → Speciation analysis cho thấy metal hòa tan chủ yếu gắn với organic ligand.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `종분석` — phân tích chemical species.

## 11. redox cycling /ˈriːdɑks ˈsaɪklɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** repeated oxidation and reduction of a chemical that transfers electrons and can continuously generate reactive intermediates.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** chemical cứ đổi oxidation state qua lại, như một “bánh xe” electron tạo stress liên tục.
**Grammar & collocations:** `redox-cycling compound` — compound; `redox cycling generates radicals` — generate; `interrupt redox cycling` — interrupt.
**Examples:** `Redox cycling generated radicals that damaged membranes in the exposed cells.` → Redox cycling tạo radical làm hỏng membrane trong cell exposed.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `산화환원 순환` — redox cycling.

## 12. reactive oxygen species /riˈæktɪv ˈɑksɪdʒən ˈspiːʃiːz/
**Part of speech:** plural noun
**Core meaning (English):** chemically reactive oxygen-containing molecules or radicals that can act as signals or damage cellular components.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** các oxygen species phản ứng mạnh; ít có thể signal, nhiều có thể tấn công lipid, protein và DNA.
**Grammar & collocations:** `reactive-oxygen-species production` — production; `ROS scavenger` — scavenger; `measure ROS` — measure.
**Examples:** `The pollutant increased reactive oxygen species in the gill tissue.` → Pollutant làm tăng reactive oxygen species trong mô mang.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `활성 산소종` — reactive oxygen species.

## 13. oxidative stress /ˈɑksɪdeɪtɪv strɛs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a state in which oxidant production exceeds antioxidant defenses and causes harmful molecular damage.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cán cân oxidant–antioxidant nghiêng quá xa, khiến cell bị damage vì không dọn kịp reactive species.
**Grammar & collocations:** `cellular oxidative stress` — stress; `oxidative-stress marker` — marker; `induce oxidative stress` — induce.
**Examples:** `Oxidative stress impaired mitochondrial respiration before the cells died.` → Oxidative stress làm suy giảm mitochondrial respiration trước khi cell chết.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `산화 스트레스` — oxidative stress.

## 14. endocrine disruption /ˈɛndəkrɪn dɪsˈrʌpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** interference with hormone synthesis, transport, receptor signaling, or metabolism that alters endocrine function.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** chemical làm lệch hệ hormone, có thể bắt chước, chặn hoặc đổi timing của signal nội tiết.
**Grammar & collocations:** `endocrine-disruption activity` — activity; `endocrine-disrupting chemical` — chemical; `screen for disruption` — screen.
**Examples:** `The study examined endocrine disruption in fish exposed during early development.` → Study kiểm tra endocrine disruption ở cá exposed trong giai đoạn phát triển sớm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `내분비계 교란` — endocrine disruption.

## 15. receptor agonism /rɪˈsɛptər ˈæɡənɪzəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** activation of a receptor by a chemical that mimics or enhances the action of its natural ligand.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** chemical đóng vai “chìa khóa giả” nhưng bật receptor lên như hormone thật.
**Grammar & collocations:** `partial receptor agonism` — agonism một phần; `agonist activity` — activity; `test receptor agonism` — test.
**Examples:** `Receptor agonism triggered a transcriptional response at concentrations below those causing cell death.` → Receptor agonism trigger transcriptional response ở concentration thấp hơn mức gây cell death.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `수용체 작용제 활성` — hoạt tính agonist receptor.

## 16. receptor antagonism /rɪˈsɛptər ænˈtæɡənɪzəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** inhibition of receptor signaling by a chemical that blocks the natural ligand or prevents receptor activation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** chemical chiếm chỗ hoặc khóa receptor nhưng không bật signal, ngăn hormone thật hoạt động.
**Grammar & collocations:** `competitive receptor antagonism` — antagonism cạnh tranh; `antagonist activity` — activity; `block receptor signaling` — block.
**Examples:** `Receptor antagonism prevented the normal hormone from activating the developmental pathway.` → Receptor antagonism ngăn hormone bình thường bật developmental pathway.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `수용체 길항` — antagonism receptor.

## 17. mixture toxicity /ˈmɪkstʃər tɑkˈsɪsəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** harmful effects produced by exposure to two or more chemicals together, which may differ from the effects of each chemical alone.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** hỗn hợp có thể cộng, tăng cường hoặc triệt tiêu effect; không thể luôn suy ra từ từng chemical riêng lẻ.
**Grammar & collocations:** `combined mixture toxicity` — toxicity; `mixture-toxicity assessment` — assessment; `test a mixture` — test.
**Examples:** `Mixture toxicity appeared even though each component was below its individual effect threshold.` → Mixture toxicity xuất hiện dù từng component dưới threshold effect riêng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `혼합물 독성` — toxicity của mixture.

## 18. concentration addition /ˌkɑnsənˈtreɪʃən əˈdɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a mixture model that treats chemicals as having similar modes of action and predicts their combined effect by adding scaled concentrations.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** nếu chemical cùng mode of action, quy đổi mỗi concentration về potency tương đương rồi cộng lại.
**Grammar & collocations:** `concentration-addition model` — model; `predict combined effects` — predict; `scaled concentration` — concentration đã scale.
**Examples:** `Concentration addition accurately predicted the joint effect of the compounds sharing one receptor.` → Concentration addition dự đoán đúng joint effect của các compound cùng receptor.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `농도 첨가` — concentration addition.

## 19. independent action /ˌɪndɪˈpɛndənt ˈækʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a mixture model that predicts combined effects by treating chemicals as acting through sufficiently different modes or pathways.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mỗi chemical tác động độc lập; xác suất có ít nhất một effect được tính từ các effect riêng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `independent-action model` — model; `Bliss independence` — independence; `assume independent action` — assume.
**Examples:** `Independent action fit the mixture better because the compounds targeted different biological pathways.` → Independent action fit mixture tốt hơn vì compound nhắm các pathway biology khác nhau.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `독립 작용` — independent action.

## 20. species sensitivity distribution /ˈspiːʃiːz ˌsɛnsəˈtɪvəti ˌdɪstrəˈbjuːʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a distribution describing how sensitive different species are to a chemical, often used to derive a protective concentration for most of the community.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** xếp species từ nhạy đến chịu được, rồi chọn concentration bảo vệ tỷ lệ community mong muốn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `species-sensitivity-distribution curve` — curve; `protective concentration` — concentration; `fit an SSD` — fit.
**Examples:** `The species sensitivity distribution supported a concentration protective for ninety-five percent of tested species.` → Species sensitivity distribution hỗ trợ concentration bảo vệ 95% species đã test.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `종 민감도 분포` — phân bố sensitivity theo species.

## Review in context

Contaminants move through food webs by **trophic transfer**, and **ecological exposure** depends on route, duration, and concentration. Risk assessors choose a **point-of-departure**, such as a **benchmark dose**, a **no-observed-adverse-effect level**, or a **lowest-observed-adverse-effect level**. An **adverse outcome pathway** links molecular events to ecological effects, while a **toxicokinetic model** follows internal concentration and a **toxicodynamic model** links that concentration to response. **Speciation analysis** determines which chemical form is present; **redox cycling**, **reactive oxygen species**, and **oxidative stress** can then damage cells. **Endocrine disruption** may involve **receptor agonism** or **receptor antagonism**. Real ecosystems face **mixture toxicity**: **concentration addition** models similar modes of action, **independent action** models different ones, and a **species sensitivity distribution** estimates community protection.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

Contaminant đi qua food web bằng **trophic transfer**, còn **ecological exposure** phụ thuộc route, duration và concentration. Risk assessor chọn **point-of-departure**, như **benchmark dose**, **no-observed-adverse-effect level** hoặc **lowest-observed-adverse-effect level**. **Adverse outcome pathway** nối molecular event với ecological effect, trong khi **toxicokinetic model** theo dõi internal concentration và **toxicodynamic model** nối concentration đó với response. **Speciation analysis** xác định chemical form hiện diện; sau đó **redox cycling**, **reactive oxygen species** và **oxidative stress** có thể làm hỏng cell. **Endocrine disruption** có thể liên quan **receptor agonism** hoặc **receptor antagonism**. Ecosystem thực tế đối mặt **mixture toxicity**: **concentration addition** model mode of action tương tự, **independent action** model mode khác nhau, còn **species sensitivity distribution** ước lượng mức bảo vệ community.
