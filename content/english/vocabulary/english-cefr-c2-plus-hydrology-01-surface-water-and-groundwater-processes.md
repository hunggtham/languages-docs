# C2+ Vocabulary — Surface-water and groundwater processes

This lesson follows rainfall through runoff, infiltration, aquifer storage, well testing, and exchange between streams and groundwater. The network is **baseflow recession → hydrograph separation → hyetograph → actual evapotranspiration → infiltration capacity → Hortonian overland flow → macropore flow → perched aquifer interface → vadose-zone profile → phreatic surface → transmissivity → storativity → drawdown cone → pumping test → Darcy flux → capillary fringe → aquitard → gaining stream → losing stream → hyporheic exchange zone**.

## 1. baseflow recession /ˈbeɪs floʊ rɪˈsɛʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the gradual decline of streamflow supplied by groundwater after rainfall or snowmelt ends.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** streamflow giảm dần do groundwater supply sau rain/snowmelt kết thúc.
**Grammar & collocations:** `slow baseflow recession` — recession chậm; `model baseflow recession` — model.
**Examples:** `A slow baseflow recession indicated substantial groundwater storage.` → Baseflow recession chậm cho thấy groundwater storage đáng kể.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `기저유출 감쇠`.

## 2. hydrograph separation /ˈhaɪdrəɡræf ˌsɛpəˈreɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** estimating the proportions of streamflow that come from quick runoff and delayed groundwater flow.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** estimate phần streamflow từ quick runoff và groundwater flow delayed.
**Grammar & collocations:** `digital hydrograph separation` — separation digital; `perform hydrograph separation` — perform.
**Examples:** `Hydrograph separation attributed most of the late flow to groundwater.` → Hydrograph separation quy phần lớn late flow cho groundwater.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `수문곡선 분리`.

## 3. hyetograph /ˈhaɪətoʊɡræf/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a graph showing rainfall intensity or depth over time.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** graph thể hiện rainfall intensity/depth theo time.
**Grammar & collocations:** `design-storm hyetograph` — hyetograph design storm; `plot a hyetograph` — plot.
**Examples:** `The design-storm hyetograph placed the heaviest rain in the first hour.` → Hyetograph design storm đặt heavy rain trong hour đầu.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `우량주상도`.

## 4. actual evapotranspiration /ˈæktʃuəl ɪˌvæpoʊtrænspəˈreɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the water actually transferred to the atmosphere by evaporation and plant transpiration under existing conditions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** water thực tế đi vào atmosphere qua evaporation và plant transpiration.
**Grammar & collocations:** `seasonal actual evapotranspiration` — evapotranspiration theo mùa; `estimate actual evapotranspiration` — estimate.
**Examples:** `Satellite data estimated actual evapotranspiration across the irrigated valley.` → Satellite data estimate actual evapotranspiration trên valley irrigated.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `실제 증발산`.

## 5. infiltration capacity /ˌɪnfɪlˈtreɪʃən kəˈpæsəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the maximum rate at which soil can absorb water at a given time.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** rate tối đa soil absorb water tại một time.
**Grammar & collocations:** `low infiltration capacity` — capacity thấp; `exceed infiltration capacity` — exceed.
**Examples:** `Compacted soil had a low infiltration capacity during the storm.` → Soil compact có infiltration capacity thấp trong storm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `침투능`.

## 6. Hortonian overland flow /hɔːrˈtoʊniən ˈoʊvərlænd floʊ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** surface runoff generated when rainfall exceeds the soil's infiltration capacity.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** runoff surface tạo khi rainfall vượt infiltration capacity của soil.
**Grammar & collocations:** `rapid Hortonian overland flow` — flow nhanh; `simulate overland flow` — simulate.
**Examples:** `Rapid Hortonian overland flow carried sediment into the channel.` → Hortonian overland flow nhanh carry sediment vào channel.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `호턴식 지표 유출`.

## 7. macropore flow /ˈmækroʊpɔːr floʊ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** rapid water movement through large soil channels created by roots, cracks, or burrowing organisms.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** water move nhanh qua channel soil lớn do root, crack hoặc burrow tạo.
**Grammar & collocations:** `preferential macropore flow` — flow ưu tiên; `measure macropore flow` — measure.
**Examples:** `Preferential macropore flow moved nitrate below the root zone.` → Macropore flow ưu tiên move nitrate dưới root zone.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `대공극 흐름`.

## 8. perched aquifer interface /pɜːrtʃt ˈækwəfər ˈɪntərfeɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the boundary around a small saturated zone resting above the main groundwater table.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** boundary quanh saturated zone nhỏ nằm trên main groundwater table.
**Grammar & collocations:** `shallow perched aquifer interface` — interface nông; `map an interface` — map.
**Examples:** `The shallow perched aquifer interface disappeared after a dry summer.` → Perched aquifer interface nông biến mất sau summer khô.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `렌즈형 대수층 경계`.

## 9. vadose-zone profile /veɪˈdoʊz zoʊn ˈproʊfaɪl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a vertical description of the unsaturated soil and rock above the groundwater table.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** description dọc của soil/rock unsaturated trên groundwater table.
**Grammar & collocations:** `detailed vadose-zone profile` — profile chi tiết; `sample a profile` — sample.
**Examples:** `The detailed vadose-zone profile showed a clay lens that slowed recharge.` → Vadose-zone profile chi tiết cho thấy clay lens làm chậm recharge.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `통기대 단면`.

## 10. phreatic surface /friˈætɪk ˈsɜːrfɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the upper surface of a saturated groundwater zone, commonly called the water table.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** upper surface của saturated groundwater zone, tức water table.
**Grammar & collocations:** `seasonal phreatic surface` — surface theo mùa; `measure the phreatic surface` — measure.
**Examples:** `The seasonal phreatic surface rose after snowmelt.` → Phreatic surface theo mùa rise sau snowmelt.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `지하수면`.

## 11. transmissivity /ˌtrænzmɪˈsɪvəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the capacity of an aquifer to transmit groundwater through its saturated thickness.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** capacity aquifer transmit groundwater qua saturated thickness.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high transmissivity` — transmissivity cao; `estimate transmissivity` — estimate.
**Examples:** `The fractured limestone had high transmissivity near the well.` → Limestone fractured có transmissivity cao gần well.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `투수량계수`.

## 12. storativity /ˌstɔːrəˈtɪvəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the volume of water an aquifer releases or stores in response to a change in hydraulic head.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** volume water aquifer release/store khi hydraulic head change.
**Grammar & collocations:** `confined-aquifer storativity` — storativity aquifer confined; `calculate storativity` — calculate.
**Examples:** `The confined-aquifer storativity was estimated from the pumping data.` → Storativity aquifer confined estimate từ pumping data.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `저류계수`.

## 13. drawdown cone /ˈdrɔːdaʊn koʊn/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the cone-shaped decline in groundwater level around a pumping well.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** groundwater level decline hình cone quanh pumping well.
**Grammar & collocations:** `expanding drawdown cone` — cone đang expand; `map a drawdown cone` — map.
**Examples:** `The expanding drawdown cone reached the neighboring monitoring well.` → Drawdown cone expand tới monitoring well bên cạnh.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `수위 저하 원추`.

## 14. pumping test /ˈpʌmpɪŋ tɛst/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a controlled well experiment used to estimate aquifer properties from water-level responses.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** experiment well có control estimate aquifer property qua water-level response.
**Grammar & collocations:** `constant-rate pumping test` — test rate constant; `conduct a pumping test` — conduct.
**Examples:** `The constant-rate pumping test revealed a leaky boundary.` → Pumping test rate constant reveal boundary bị leak.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `양수 시험`.

## 15. Darcy flux /ˈdɑːrsi flʌks/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the volumetric groundwater flow rate per unit area calculated from hydraulic gradient and conductivity.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** flow groundwater per area, calculate từ hydraulic gradient và conductivity.
**Grammar & collocations:** `vertical Darcy flux` — flux dọc; `calculate Darcy flux` — calculate.
**Examples:** `The model calculated vertical Darcy flux through the clay layer.` → Model calculate Darcy flux dọc qua clay layer.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `다르시 플럭스`.

## 16. capillary fringe /ˈkæpəˌlɛri frɪndʒ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the zone just above the water table where water rises through small pores by capillary forces.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** zone ngay trên water table nơi water rise qua pore nhỏ nhờ capillary force.
**Grammar & collocations:** `thick capillary fringe` — fringe dày; `sample the capillary fringe` — sample.
**Examples:** `The thick capillary fringe supported wetland roots above the water table.` → Capillary fringe dày support wetland root trên water table.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `모세관대`.

## 17. aquitard /ˈækwəˌtɑːrd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a relatively low-permeability layer that slows groundwater flow between aquifers.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** layer permeability thấp làm chậm groundwater flow giữa aquifer.
**Grammar & collocations:** `clayey aquitard` — aquitard clay; `cross an aquitard` — cross.
**Examples:** `A clayey aquitard slowed leakage from the upper aquifer.` → Aquitard clay làm chậm leakage từ upper aquifer.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `저투수층`.

## 18. gaining stream /ˈɡeɪnɪŋ striːm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a stream that receives groundwater through its bed and banks.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** stream nhận groundwater qua bed và bank.
**Grammar & collocations:** `perennial gaining stream` — stream gaining quanh năm; `identify a gaining stream` — identify.
**Examples:** `The perennial gaining stream stayed cool during the drought.` → Gaining stream quanh năm vẫn mát trong drought.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `유입 하천`.

## 19. losing stream /ˈluːzɪŋ striːm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a stream that loses water downward into the surrounding groundwater system.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** stream mất water xuống groundwater system xung quanh.
**Grammar & collocations:** `intermittent losing stream` — stream losing gián đoạn; `map a losing stream` — map.
**Examples:** `The intermittent losing stream disappeared into the gravel plain.` → Losing stream gián đoạn biến mất vào gravel plain.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `손실 하천`.

## 20. hyporheic exchange zone /ˌhaɪpəˈriːɪk ɪksˈtʃeɪndʒ zoʊn/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the region beneath and beside a stream where surface water and groundwater mix through the sediment.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** region dưới/cạnh stream nơi surface water và groundwater mix qua sediment.
**Grammar & collocations:** `active hyporheic exchange zone` — zone active; `sample a zone` — sample.
**Examples:** `The active hyporheic exchange zone supported intense microbial activity.` → Hyporheic exchange zone active support microbial activity mạnh.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `하천 지하 교환대`.

## Review in context

Hydrologists interpret a **baseflow recession**, perform **hydrograph separation**, and compare a **hyetograph** with **actual evapotranspiration** and **infiltration capacity**. Excess rain creates **Hortonian overland flow**, while roots and cracks enable **macropore flow**. A **perched aquifer interface**, **vadose-zone profile**, and **phreatic surface** describe vertical storage; **transmissivity**, **storativity**, a **drawdown cone**, and a **pumping test** characterize wells. **Darcy flux**, the **capillary fringe**, and an **aquitard** explain movement. A **gaining stream**, **losing stream**, and **hyporheic exchange zone** connect surface water with groundwater.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:** Hydrologist interpret **baseflow recession**, thực hiện **hydrograph separation** và compare **hyetograph** với **actual evapotranspiration** và **infiltration capacity**. Rain dư tạo **Hortonian overland flow**, còn root/crack enable **macropore flow**. **Perched aquifer interface**, **vadose-zone profile** và **phreatic surface** mô tả storage dọc; **transmissivity**, **storativity**, **drawdown cone** và **pumping test** characterize well. **Darcy flux**, **capillary fringe** và **aquitard** giải thích movement. **Gaining stream**, **losing stream** và **hyporheic exchange zone** nối surface water với groundwater.
