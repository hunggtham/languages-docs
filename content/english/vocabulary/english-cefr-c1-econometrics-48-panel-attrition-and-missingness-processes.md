# C1 Vocabulary 48 — Panel attrition and missingness processes

This lesson follows how longitudinal studies lose observations, diagnose the pattern, and adjust inference without pretending that missing data are harmless.

Flow: **panel attrition → unit dropout → missing completely at random → attrition weight → follow-up subsample → panel conditioning → survivorship bias → joint missingness model → coarsened observation → informative dropout → observation process → participation process → attrition correction → longitudinal dropout → unit nonresponse → item nonresponse → panel replacement → sample refresh → tracking loss → wave-specific response**.

## 1. panel attrition /ˈpænəl əˈtrɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the gradual loss of units from a longitudinal study across repeated waves of data collection.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** việc mất dần unit khỏi longitudinal study qua nhiều wave collect data.
**Grammar & collocations:** `substantial panel attrition` — attrition; `measure panel attrition` — measure.
**Examples:** `Panel attrition was highest among households that moved frequently.` → Attrition panel cao nhất ở household move thường xuyên.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `패널 탈락`.

## 2. unit dropout /ˈjuːnɪt ˈdrɑpˌaʊt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the loss of one sampled person, household, firm, or other unit before the study ends.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** loss của một person, household, firm hoặc unit sample trước study end.
**Grammar & collocations:** `early unit dropout` — dropout; `model unit dropout` — model.
**Examples:** `Unit dropout left fewer observations for the final employment wave.` → Dropout unit để ít observation hơn cho employment wave cuối.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `단위 탈락`.

## 3. missing completely at random /ˈmɪsɪŋ kəmˈpliːtli æt ˈrændəm/
**Part of speech:** adjective
**Core meaning (English):** describing missingness unrelated to observed or unobserved data under the assumed study process.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** missingness không liên quan data observe hoặc unobserved theo process assume.
**Grammar & collocations:** `missing-completely-at-random assumption` — assumption; `assume missing completely at random` — assume.
**Examples:** `The analysis could not defend a missing-completely-at-random assumption after the device failure was identified.` → Analysis không defend assumption missing completely at random sau khi identify device failure.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `완전 무작위 결측`.

## 4. attrition weight /əˈtrɪʃən weɪt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a weight that compensates for unequal chances that study units remain observed over time.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** weight compensate chance không đều unit remain observed theo time.
**Grammar & collocations:** `inverse attrition weight` — weight; `calibrate an attrition weight` — calibrate.
**Examples:** `The attrition weight gave more influence to participants likely to leave the panel.` → Weight attrition give influence hơn cho participant likely leave panel.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `탈락 가중치`.

## 5. follow-up subsample /ˈfɑloʊ ʌp ˈsʌmˌsæmpəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a selected part of the original sample contacted again to collect information that was missing or to study later outcomes.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** phần sample original được contact lại để collect information missing hoặc study outcome later.
**Grammar & collocations:** `targeted follow-up subsample` — subsample; `draw a follow-up subsample` — draw.
**Examples:** `A follow-up subsample clarified why some participants stopped reporting income.` → Subsample follow-up clarify vì sao vài participant stop report income.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `추적 하위 표본`.

## 6. panel conditioning /ˈpænəl kənˈdɪʃənɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** changes in participants' answers or behavior caused by being measured repeatedly.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** change answer hoặc behavior do participant được measure repeated.
**Grammar & collocations:** `panel-conditioning effect` — effect; `test for panel conditioning` — test.
**Examples:** `Panel conditioning made long-term respondents more familiar with the survey's financial terms.` → Conditioning panel làm respondent lâu năm quen term financial survey hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `패널 조건화`.

## 7. survivorship bias /sərˈvaɪvərˌʃɪp baɪəs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a distortion caused by analyzing only units that remain visible while ignoring those that disappeared or failed.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** distortion do chỉ analyze unit còn visible và ignore unit disappear hoặc fail.
**Grammar & collocations:** `serious survivorship bias` — bias; `avoid survivorship bias` — avoid.
**Examples:** `Survivorship bias made the firms that stayed in the market look unusually successful.` → Bias survivorship làm firm ở lại market trông successful bất thường.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `생존자 편향`.

## 8. joint missingness model /dʒɔɪnt ˈmɪsɪŋnəs ˈmɑdəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a model that represents the outcome process and the process causing values to be missing together.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** model represent cùng lúc outcome process và process cause value missing.
**Grammar & collocations:** `joint missingness model` — model; `fit a joint missingness model` — fit.
**Examples:** `The joint missingness model allowed health status to affect both earnings and response.` → Model missingness joint cho health status affect earnings và response.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `공동 결측 모형`.

## 9. coarsened observation /ˈkɔrsənd ˌɑbzərˈveɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an observation recorded with less detail than the underlying value, such as a range instead of an exact amount.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** observation record ít detail hơn underlying value, như range thay exact amount.
**Grammar & collocations:** `coarsened observation process` — process; `analyze coarsened observations` — analyze.
**Examples:** `A coarsened observation reported income bands instead of exact income.` → Observation coarsened report band income thay vì income exact.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `범주화 관측`.

## 10. informative dropout /ɪnˈfɔrmətɪv ˈdrɑpˌaʊt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** dropout related to the outcome or unobserved condition being studied rather than occurring harmlessly.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** dropout liên quan outcome hoặc condition unobserved đang study, không harmless.
**Grammar & collocations:** `informative dropout mechanism` — mechanism; `model informative dropout` — model.
**Examples:** `Informative dropout was plausible because people became ill before leaving the survey.` → Dropout informative plausible vì người sick trước khi leave survey.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `정보성 탈락`.

## 11. observation process /ˌɑbzərˈveɪʃən ˈprɑsɛs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the mechanism determining when, how, and with what accuracy a value becomes recorded.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mechanism determine khi nào, thế nào và accuracy nào value được record.
**Grammar & collocations:** `irregular observation process` — process; `model the observation process` — model.
**Examples:** `The observation process changed when clinics switched from paper to online forms.` → Process observation đổi khi clinic switch paper sang online form.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `관측 과정`.

## 12. participation process /pɑrˌtɪsəˈpeɪʃən ˈprɑsɛs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the mechanism determining who joins, remains in, responds to, or leaves a study.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mechanism determine ai join, remain, respond hoặc leave study.
**Grammar & collocations:** `unequal participation process` — process; `describe the participation process` — describe.
**Examples:** `The participation process favored respondents with stable internet access.` → Process participation favor respondent có internet stable.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `참여 과정`.

## 13. attrition correction /əˈtrɪʃən kəˈrɛkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an adjustment intended to reduce bias caused by losing observations during a longitudinal study.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** adjustment giảm bias do mất observation trong longitudinal study.
**Grammar & collocations:** `robust attrition correction` — correction; `apply an attrition correction` — apply.
**Examples:** `The attrition correction changed the estimated wage trend only slightly.` → Correction attrition chỉ đổi wage trend estimate một chút.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `탈락 보정`.

## 14. longitudinal dropout /ˌlɑndʒəˈtuːdənəl ˈdrɑpˌaʊt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the loss of a unit from repeated observations over the course of a longitudinal study.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** loss unit khỏi repeated observation trong longitudinal study.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high longitudinal dropout` — dropout; `track longitudinal dropout` — track.
**Examples:** `Longitudinal dropout increased after the interview schedule became more demanding.` → Dropout longitudinal tăng sau khi interview schedule demanding hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `종단 탈락`.

## 15. unit nonresponse /ˈjuːnɪt ˌnɑn rɪˈspɑns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** failure of an entire sampled unit to provide any usable response for a data collection occasion.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cả sampled unit không provide usable response cho một occasion collect data.
**Grammar & collocations:** `unit nonresponse rate` — rate; `adjust for unit nonresponse` — adjust.
**Examples:** `Unit nonresponse was higher among businesses contacted during the holiday season.` → Nonresponse unit cao hơn ở business contact trong holiday season.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `단위 무응답`.

## 16. item nonresponse /ˈaɪtəm ˌnɑn rɪˈspɑns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** failure to answer one or more questions even though the sampled unit provides other responses.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** không answer một hoặc nhiều question dù unit provide response khác.
**Grammar & collocations:** `item nonresponse pattern` — pattern; `impute item nonresponse` — impute.
**Examples:** `Item nonresponse concentrated in questions about debt and household transfers.` → Nonresponse item tập trung ở question về debt và household transfer.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `항목 무응답`.

## 17. panel replacement /ˈpænəl rɪˈpleɪsmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the addition of new units to maintain a panel when original units leave or become unavailable.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** thêm unit mới để maintain panel khi unit original leave hoặc unavailable.
**Grammar & collocations:** `planned panel replacement` — replacement; `manage panel replacement` — manage.
**Examples:** `Panel replacement kept the survey's age distribution close to its target.` → Replacement panel giữ age distribution survey gần target.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `패널 교체`.

## 18. sample refresh /ˈsæmpəl rɪˈfrɛʃ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the planned introduction of new sampled units to update coverage and reduce the aging of a panel.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** introduce unit sample mới để update coverage và giảm panel aging.
**Grammar & collocations:** `regular sample refresh` — refresh; `schedule a sample refresh` — schedule.
**Examples:** `The sample refresh added younger households that the original frame had missed.` → Refresh sample thêm household trẻ mà frame original miss.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `표본 갱신`.

## 19. tracking loss /ˈtrækɪŋ lɔs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the loss of contact with a study unit that cannot be reached or located for a later wave.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** loss contact với unit study vì không reach hoặc locate ở wave later.
**Grammar & collocations:** `geographic tracking loss` — loss; `reduce tracking loss` — reduce.
**Examples:** `Tracking loss rose when seasonal workers moved between regions.` → Loss tracking tăng khi seasonal worker move giữa region.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `추적 손실`.

## 20. wave-specific response /weɪv spəˈsɪfɪk rɪˈspɑns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a response pattern that differs across particular rounds or waves of a longitudinal study.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** response pattern khác nhau ở từng round hoặc wave của longitudinal study.
**Grammar & collocations:** `wave-specific response rate` — rate; `model wave-specific response` — model.
**Examples:** `Wave-specific response fell during the year when interviews moved to a new platform.` → Response wave-specific giảm trong year interview chuyển platform mới.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `파동별 응답`.

## Review in context

**Panel attrition** begins when a **unit dropout** changes who remains in a study; a **missing completely at random** assumption is often too strong. An **attrition weight** or a **follow-up subsample** can help, but **panel conditioning** and **survivorship bias** still require attention. A **joint missingness model** can represent a **coarsened observation** and **informative dropout** through an explicit **observation process** and **participation process**. An **attrition correction** may address **longitudinal dropout**, while **unit nonresponse** and **item nonresponse** need different adjustments. **Panel replacement** and **sample refresh** maintain coverage, but **tracking loss** and **wave-specific response** can still change the target population.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:** **Panel attrition** bắt đầu khi **unit dropout** đổi người còn lại trong study; assumption **missing completely at random** thường quá mạnh. **Attrition weight** hoặc **follow-up subsample** có thể giúp, nhưng **panel conditioning** và **survivorship bias** vẫn cần chú ý. **Joint missingness model** có thể represent **coarsened observation** và **informative dropout** qua **observation process** và **participation process** rõ. **Attrition correction** có thể xử lý **longitudinal dropout**, còn **unit nonresponse** và **item nonresponse** cần adjustment khác nhau. **Panel replacement** và **sample refresh** duy trì coverage, nhưng **tracking loss** và **wave-specific response** vẫn có thể đổi target population.
