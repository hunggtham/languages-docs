# C2+ Vocabulary — Trauma systems and emergency response

This lesson follows how emergency and trauma systems triage, stabilize, transfer, and learn from high-acuity events. The network is **damage control resuscitation → mass casualty triage → disaster medical assistance team → prehospital care → trauma registry → trauma activation → golden hour → permissive hypotension → massive transfusion protocol → tourniquet conversion → airway compromise → spinal motion restriction → crush syndrome → compartment syndrome → trauma-informed emergency → field hospital → surge triage → reverse triage → decontamination corridor → hazmat exposure → ambulance diversion → bypass protocol → emergency medical dispatch → time-critical transfer → trauma system regionalization**.

## 1. damage control resuscitation /ˈdæmɪdʒ kənˈtroʊl rɪˌsʌsəˈteɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** early trauma resuscitation focused on controlling bleeding, maintaining essential physiology, and avoiding interventions that worsen coagulopathy or hypothermia before definitive repair.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** damage control resuscitation ưu tiên giữ patient sống và ổn định trước definitive surgery.
**Grammar & collocations:** `damage-control-resuscitation protocol` — protocol; `control traumatic bleeding` — control; `definitive repair` — repair.
**Examples:** `Damage control resuscitation prioritized hemorrhage control and balanced blood products before transfer to the operating room.` → Stabilize trước rồi repair.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `손상 통제 소생술` — damage control resuscitation.

## 2. mass casualty triage /mæs ˈkæʒuəlti ˈtriːɑʒ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** sorting many injured or ill people according to urgency, expected benefit, and available resources during a major incident.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mass casualty triage phải phân bổ scarce resource khi số patient vượt capacity.
**Grammar & collocations:** `mass-casualty-triage protocol` — protocol; `sort casualties` — sort; `limited resource` — resource.
**Examples:** `Mass casualty triage changed as additional ambulances and operating teams arrived.` → Triage phụ thuộc resource động.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `대량 사상자 분류` — mass casualty triage.

## 3. disaster medical assistance team /dɪˈzæstər ˈmɛdɪkəl əˈsɪstəns tiːm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a deployable multidisciplinary team that provides medical support during disasters or emergencies beyond local capacity.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** disaster medical assistance team là team có thể deploy tới nơi capacity bị vượt.
**Grammar & collocations:** `disaster-medical-assistance-team roster` — roster; `deploy a medical team` — deploy; `surge support` — support.
**Examples:** `The disaster medical assistance team established a treatment area while local hospitals restored power.` → Team hỗ trợ tạm thời.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `재난 의료 지원팀` — disaster medical assistance team.

## 4. prehospital care /ˌpriːˈhɑspɪtəl kɛr/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** assessment, stabilization, treatment, and transport provided before a patient reaches a hospital.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** prehospital care là care trên đường hoặc tại scene trước hospital.
**Grammar & collocations:** `prehospital-care protocol` — protocol; `stabilize before transport` — stabilize; `field assessment` — assessment.
**Examples:** `Prehospital care controlled bleeding and transmitted vital information before arrival.` → Scene team chuẩn bị hospital trước.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `병원 전 처치` — prehospital care.

## 5. trauma registry /ˈtrɔmə ˈrɛdʒɪstri/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a structured database of trauma cases, treatments, outcomes, and system factors used for surveillance and improvement.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** trauma registry lưu case để thấy pattern và cải thiện system.
**Grammar & collocations:** `trauma-registry record` — record; `analyze trauma outcomes` — analyze; `case surveillance` — surveillance.
**Examples:** `The trauma registry revealed longer transfer times for rural patients after midnight.` → Registry lộ gap geography/time.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `외상 등록체계` — trauma registry.

## 6. trauma activation /ˈtrɔmə ˌæktəˈveɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** activation of a predefined hospital response involving specialized staff, equipment, space, and coordination for a suspected severe trauma.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** trauma activation gọi đúng team trước khi patient tới hoặc ngay khi tới.
**Grammar & collocations:** `trauma-activation criterion` — criterion; `activate the trauma team` — activate; `specialist response` — response.
**Examples:** `Trauma activation brought surgery, anesthesia, radiology, and blood-bank staff to the emergency bay.` → Nhiều team cùng đến.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `외상 대응 활성화` — trauma activation.

## 7. golden hour /ˈɡoʊldən ˈaʊər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an early period after serious injury when rapid recognition, stabilization, and definitive planning may strongly affect outcome.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** golden hour nhấn mạnh giá trị của thời gian sớm, không phải một đồng hồ cứng cho mọi case.
**Grammar & collocations:** `golden-hour response` — response; `use the early treatment window` — use; `time-sensitive injury` — injury.
**Examples:** `The team used the golden hour to control bleeding and arrange transfer rather than delay for nonessential testing.` → Ưu tiên action time-sensitive.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `골든타임` — golden hour.

## 8. permissive hypotension /pərˈmɪsɪv ˌhaɪpoʊˈtɛnʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a context-specific resuscitation strategy that avoids aggressively raising blood pressure before bleeding is controlled in selected trauma situations.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** permissive hypotension là strategy chuyên môn có điều kiện, không phải mục tiêu chung cho mọi patient.
**Grammar & collocations:** `permissive-hypotension strategy` — strategy; `avoid excessive pressure` — avoid; `uncontrolled hemorrhage` — hemorrhage.
**Examples:** `The team considered permissive hypotension while preparing definitive control of internal bleeding.` → Strategy gắn với hemorrhage control.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `허용적 저혈압` — permissive hypotension.

## 9. massive transfusion protocol /ˈmæsɪv trænˈsfjuːʒən ˈproʊtəˌkɔl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an organized protocol for rapidly delivering coordinated blood components, monitoring physiology, and managing severe hemorrhage.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** massive transfusion protocol biến blood delivery thành response có sequence và coordination.
**Grammar & collocations:** `massive-transfusion-protocol activation` — activation; `deliver balanced blood components` — deliver; `hemorrhage response` — response.
**Examples:** `The massive transfusion protocol linked the trauma bay, blood bank, laboratory, and operating room.` → Nhiều unit phải phối hợp.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `대량 수혈 프로토콜` — massive transfusion protocol.

## 10. tourniquet conversion /ˈtʊrnɪkət kənˈvɜrʒən/

**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** tourniquet conversion là chuyển từ temporary control sang definitive method dưới giám sát chuyên môn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `tourniquet-conversion decision` — decision; `convert temporary control` — convert; `definitive hemorrhage control` — control.

## 11. airway compromise /ˈɛrˌweɪ ˈkɑmprəˌmaɪz/

**Grammar & collocations:** `airway-compromise alert` — alert; `protect the airway` — protect; `breathing threat` — threat.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `기도 손상` — airway compromise.

## 12. spinal motion restriction /ˈspaɪnəl ˈmoʊʃən rɪˈstrɪkʃən/

## 13. crush syndrome /ˈkrʌʃ ˌsɪndroʊm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** systemic illness caused by prolonged compression of muscle, with release of damaged tissue and risk to circulation and kidney function after release.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** crush syndrome có thể xuất hiện sau giải phóng khỏi compression, cần system response.
**Grammar & collocations:** `crush-syndrome risk` — risk; `assess prolonged compression` — assess; `muscle injury` — injury.
**Examples:** `The team anticipated crush syndrome when rescuers prepared medical support before lifting the debris.` → Chuẩn bị trước khi release.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `압궤 증후군` — crush syndrome.

## 14. compartment syndrome /kəmˈpɑrtmənt ˌsɪndroʊm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** dangerous pressure rise within a confined muscle compartment that can impair blood flow and tissue function.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** compartment syndrome là pressure trong space kín làm tissue thiếu perfusion.
**Grammar & collocations:** `compartment-syndrome assessment` — assessment; `monitor limb pressure` — monitor; `tissue perfusion` — perfusion.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `구획 증후군` — compartment syndrome.

## 15. trauma-informed emergency /ˈtrɔmə ɪnˈfɔrmd ɪˈmɜrdʒənsi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** emergency care that recognizes prior trauma, preserves control and dignity, and avoids unnecessary frightening or coercive practices.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** trauma-informed emergency làm emergency care an toàn hơn cho người từng trauma.
**Grammar & collocations:** `trauma-informed-emergency approach` — approach; `preserve patient control` — preserve; `avoid retraumatization` — avoid.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `트라우마 인지 응급 진료` — trauma-informed emergency.

## 17. surge triage /sɜrdʒ ˈtriːɑʒ/

## 18. reverse triage /rɪˈvɜrs ˈtriːɑʒ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** prioritization of which current patients can safely be discharged, transferred, or stepped down to create capacity for a surge.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** reverse triage nhìn từ người đang ở system để giải phóng capacity an toàn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `reverse-triage review` — review; `step down a stable patient` — step down; `create surge capacity` — create.

## Review in context — Part 1

**Damage control resuscitation** and **mass casualty triage** guide early response, while a **disaster medical assistance team** can reinforce local capacity. **Prehospital care**, a **trauma registry**, and **trauma activation** connect scene, hospital, and learning. The **golden hour** emphasizes time, but strategies such as **permissive hypotension** and a **massive transfusion protocol** require specialized judgment. **Tourniquet conversion**, **airway compromise**, **spinal motion restriction**, **crush syndrome**, and **compartment syndrome** illustrate different time-sensitive risks.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 1:**

**Damage control resuscitation** và **mass casualty triage** định hướng response sớm, còn **disaster medical assistance team** tăng capacity địa phương. **Prehospital care**, **trauma registry** và **trauma activation** nối scene, hospital và learning. **Golden hour** nhấn mạnh time, nhưng strategy như **permissive hypotension** và **massive transfusion protocol** cần chuyên môn. **Tourniquet conversion**, **airway compromise**, **spinal motion restriction**, **crush syndrome** và **compartment syndrome** cho thấy các risk time-sensitive khác nhau.

## Review in context — Part 2

**Trauma-informed emergency** care protects dignity, while a **field hospital** can add capacity. **Surge triage** prioritizes incoming patients and **reverse triage** creates space by stepping down people who can safely continue elsewhere. A **decontamination corridor** controls **hazmat exposure**. **Ambulance diversion** and a **bypass protocol** match patients to safe capacity; **emergency medical dispatch** begins support before arrival. A **time-critical transfer** depends on preparation, and **trauma system regionalization** defines the network's roles.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 2:**

**Trauma-informed emergency** bảo vệ dignity, còn **field hospital** bổ sung capacity. **Surge triage** ưu tiên patient đến và **reverse triage** tạo chỗ bằng cách step down người có thể tiếp tục care an toàn nơi khác. **Decontamination corridor** kiểm soát **hazmat exposure**. **Ambulance diversion** và **bypass protocol** ghép patient với capacity an toàn; **emergency medical dispatch** bắt đầu support trước arrival. **Time-critical transfer** cần preparation, còn **trauma system regionalization** xác định role của network.
