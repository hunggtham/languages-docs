# C1 Vocabulary 39 — Behavioral language learning and multilingual choice

This lesson follows how learners and communities manage language switching, confidence, identity, access, and social judgment across languages.

Flow: **language switching cost → accent perception → vocabulary confidence belief → translation default → conversational repair norm → code-switching identity → language learning salience → immersion expectation → pronunciation anxiety → grammar feedback framing → bilingual advantage belief → linguistic discrimination expectation → interpreter trust → language access friction → word retrieval pressure → fluency illusion → communication accommodation → dialect prestige perception → multilingual opportunity framing → language maintenance habit**.

## 1. language switching cost /ˈlæŋɡwɪdʒ ˈswɪtʃɪŋ kɔst/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** mental effort, delay, or error caused by moving between languages during communication or thought.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** effort mental, delay hoặc error do move giữa language.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high language switching cost` — cost; `reduce language switching costs` — reduce.
**Examples:** `Language switching cost increased when the meeting changed languages without warning.` → Cost switching language tăng khi meeting đổi language không warning.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `언어 전환 비용`.

## 2. accent perception /ˈæksɛnt pərˈsɛpʃən/

**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** judgment competence, identity, warmth hoặc credibility dựa partly pronunciation pattern.
**Grammar & collocations:** `biased accent perception` — perception; `challenge accent perception` — challenge.
**Examples:** `Accent perception influenced the interviewer's confidence even though the explanation was accurate.` → Perception accent influence confidence interviewer dù explanation accurate.

## Review in context

Learners manage **language switching cost** and **accent perception** while developing a **vocabulary confidence belief**. A **translation default** can slow response, but a **conversational repair norm** makes mistakes useful. **Code-switching identity** connects language to belonging, and **language learning salience** grows when communication has a real purpose. An **immersion expectation** may be too optimistic; **pronunciation anxiety** can hide progress. Helpful **grammar feedback framing** supports a **bilingual advantage belief**, while **linguistic discrimination expectation** can silence capable speakers. **Interpreter trust** and low **language access friction** support participation. **Word retrieval pressure** exposes the **fluency illusion**, whereas **communication accommodation** makes interaction cooperative. **Dialect prestige perception** shapes whose speech is valued, and **multilingual opportunity framing** can broaden access. Finally, a **language maintenance habit** protects ability across time and generations.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

Learner manage **language switching cost** và **accent perception** khi develop **vocabulary confidence belief**. **Translation default** làm response chậm, nhưng **conversational repair norm** biến mistake thành useful. **Code-switching identity** connect language với belonging, và **language learning salience** tăng khi communication có purpose thật. **Immersion expectation** có thể optimistic, còn **pronunciation anxiety** che progress. **Grammar feedback framing** helpful support **bilingual advantage belief**, trong khi **linguistic discrimination expectation** silence speaker capable. **Interpreter trust** và **language access friction** thấp support participation. **Word retrieval pressure** expose **fluency illusion**, còn **communication accommodation** làm interaction cooperative. **Dialect prestige perception** shape speech ai được value, và **multilingual opportunity framing** mở access. Cuối cùng, **language maintenance habit** bảo vệ ability qua time và generation.
