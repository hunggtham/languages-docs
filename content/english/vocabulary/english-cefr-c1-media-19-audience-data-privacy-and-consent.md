# C1 Vocabulary 19 — Audience data, privacy, and consent

This lesson follows how media organizations collect audience information while explaining choices, limiting risks, and respecting personal control.

Flow: **consent record → retention limit → sensitive attribute → reidentification risk → deletion request → data breach notification → third-party cookie → consent banner → audience data → data access request → identity resolution → data inventory → processing purpose → tracking consent → data subject → consent interface → privacy preference → collection notice → analytics opt-out → data lifecycle**.

## 1. consent record /kənˈsɛnt ˈrɛkərd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a record showing what permission a person gave, when it was given, and what information the permission covered.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** record show person give permission gì, khi nào và cover information nào.
**Grammar & collocations:** `dated consent record` — record; `maintain a consent record` — maintain.
**Examples:** `The platform stored a consent record for each newsletter preference.` → Platform store record consent cho mỗi preference newsletter.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `동의 기록`.

## 2. retention limit /rɪˈtɛnʃən ˈlɪmət/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a rule that sets the longest period for keeping a particular type of data.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** rule set thời gian dài nhất được giữ một loại data.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strict retention limit` — limit; `set a retention limit` — set.
**Examples:** `The retention limit required old contact records to be removed after two years.` → Limit retention yêu cầu remove contact record cũ sau hai năm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `보유 기간 제한`.

## 3. sensitive attribute /ˈsɛnsətɪv ˈætrəˌbjuːt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a personal characteristic that can create serious privacy or discrimination risks if exposed or misused.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** characteristic cá nhân có thể tạo risk privacy hoặc discrimination serious nếu lộ hoặc misuse.
**Grammar & collocations:** `protected sensitive attribute` — attribute; `exclude a sensitive attribute` — exclude.
**Examples:** `The analysis removed a sensitive attribute before sharing the audience report.` → Analysis remove attribute sensitive trước khi share audience report.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `민감한 속성`.

## 4. reidentification risk /ˌriːaɪˌdɛntəfəˈkeɪʃən rɪsk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the possibility that supposedly anonymous data can be linked back to a specific person.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** possibility data tưởng anonymous bị link trở lại một person cụ thể.
**Grammar & collocations:** `low reidentification risk` — risk thấp; `assess reidentification risk` — assess.
**Examples:** `Rare reading patterns created a reidentification risk for a small community.` → Reading pattern hiếm tạo risk reidentification cho community nhỏ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `재식별 위험`.

## 5. deletion request /dɪˈliːʃən rɪˈkwɛst/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a person's formal request that an organization remove personal information it holds about them.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** request formal yêu cầu organization remove personal information của person.
**Grammar & collocations:** `verified deletion request` — request; `process a deletion request` — process.
**Examples:** `The support team routed the deletion request to the privacy officer.` → Support team route request deletion tới privacy officer.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `삭제 요청`.

## 6. data breach notification /ˈdeɪtə briːtʃ ˌnoʊtəfəˈkeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a message explaining that protected information was exposed, lost, or accessed without authorization.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** message explain protected information bị expose, lose hoặc access without authorization.
**Grammar & collocations:** `prompt data breach notification` — notification; `issue a data breach notification` — issue.
**Examples:** `The data breach notification explained what was exposed and what readers should do next.` → Notification breach data explain thứ bị expose và reader nên làm gì.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `데이터 유출 통지`.

## 7. third-party cookie /ˌθɜrd ˈpɑrti ˈkʊki/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a small data file placed by a domain different from the site a person is visiting, often for tracking or advertising.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** file data nhỏ do domain khác place, thường để tracking hoặc advertising.
**Grammar & collocations:** `block third-party cookies` — cookies; `phase out third-party cookies` — phase out.
**Examples:** `The publication removed third-party cookies from its donation page.` → Publication remove cookie third-party khỏi donation page.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `제3자 쿠키`.

## 8. consent banner /kənˈsɛnt ˈbænər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a notice on a website that asks visitors to accept, reject, or manage specific data practices.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** notice trên website ask visitor accept, reject hoặc manage data practice cụ thể.
**Grammar & collocations:** `clear consent banner` — banner; `design a consent banner` — design.
**Examples:** `The consent banner let visitors refuse advertising trackers without refusing essential cookies.` → Banner consent cho visitor refuse advertising tracker mà không refuse essential cookie.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `동의 배너`.

## 9. audience data /ˈɔdiəns ˈdeɪtə/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** information about how people use, understand, or respond to a media service.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** information về cách người use, understand hoặc respond với media service.
**Grammar & collocations:** `aggregated audience data` — data; `protect audience data` — protect.
**Examples:** `The newsroom used aggregated audience data to plan coverage, not to identify individual readers.` → Newsroom dùng data audience aggregated để plan coverage, không identify từng reader.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `청중 데이터`.

## 10. data access request /ˈdeɪtə ˈæksɛs rɪˈkwɛst/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a formal request for a copy of personal information an organization stores about the requester.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** request formal xin copy personal information organization store về requester.
**Grammar & collocations:** `formal data access request` — request; `answer a data access request` — answer.
**Examples:** `The data access request showed which preference records were attached to the account.` → Request access data show record preference nào attach với account.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `데이터 열람 요청`.

## 11. identity resolution /aɪˈdɛntəti ˌrɛzəˈluːʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the process of deciding whether records from different systems refer to the same person or account.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** process decide record từ system khác có refer cùng person hoặc account không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `cautious identity resolution` — resolution; `limit identity resolution` — limit.
**Examples:** `The team avoided identity resolution when a shared device could represent several readers.` → Team avoid resolution identity khi shared device có thể represent nhiều reader.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `신원 식별`.

## 12. data inventory /ˈdeɪtə ˈɪnvənˌtɔri/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an organized list of the data an organization holds, where it is stored, and why it is used.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** list có tổ chức về data organization hold, store ở đâu và use vì sao.
**Grammar & collocations:** `current data inventory` — inventory; `maintain a data inventory` — maintain.
**Examples:** `The data inventory revealed that an old analytics tool still received newsletter events.` → Inventory data reveal analytics tool cũ vẫn receive newsletter event.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `데이터 목록`.

## 13. processing purpose /ˈprɑsɛsɪŋ ˈpɜrpəs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the specific reason an organization collects, analyzes, stores, or shares a type of personal data.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** reason cụ thể organization collect, analyze, store hoặc share loại personal data.
**Grammar & collocations:** `specified processing purpose` — purpose; `document a processing purpose` — document.
**Examples:** `The form explained the processing purpose before asking for a phone number.` → Form explain purpose processing trước khi ask phone number.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `처리 목적`.

## 14. tracking consent /ˈtrækɪŋ kənˈsɛnt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** permission for a service to record a person's activity across pages, sessions, or sites for a stated purpose.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** permission cho service record activity qua page, session hoặc site theo purpose nêu rõ.
**Grammar & collocations:** `optional tracking consent` — consent; `withdraw tracking consent` — withdraw.
**Examples:** `Tracking consent was separate from the agreement needed to deliver the article.` → Consent tracking separate với agreement cần để deliver article.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `추적 동의`.

## 15. data subject /ˈdeɪtə ˈsʌbdʒɪkt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the person whose personal information is collected, used, or stored.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** person có personal information được collect, use hoặc store.
**Grammar & collocations:** `rights of the data subject` — rights; `inform a data subject` — inform.
**Examples:** `The notice told each data subject how to contact the privacy team.` → Notice nói cho data subject cách contact privacy team.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `정보 주체`.

## 16. consent interface /kənˈsɛnt ˈɪntərˌfeɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the screen or controls through which a person chooses whether and how data may be collected.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** screen hoặc control nơi person choose data được collect thế nào.
**Grammar & collocations:** `accessible consent interface` — interface; `improve a consent interface` — improve.
**Examples:** `The redesigned consent interface used equal buttons for accepting and refusing optional tracking.` → Interface consent redesign dùng button equal cho accept và refuse tracking optional.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `동의 인터페이스`.

## 17. privacy preference /ˈpraɪvəsi ˈprɛfərəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a person's chosen setting about what kinds of data collection or communication they allow.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** setting person choose về loại data collection hoặc communication được allow.
**Grammar & collocations:** `saved privacy preference` — preference; `change a privacy preference` — change.
**Examples:** `The account page saved each privacy preference instead of resetting it after every visit.` → Account page save preference privacy thay vì reset sau mỗi visit.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `개인정보 선호 설정`.

## 18. collection notice /kəˈlɛkʃən ˈnoʊtɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an explanation given when information is collected, stating what is gathered and how it will be used.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** explanation lúc collect information, state collect gì và use thế nào.
**Grammar & collocations:** `plain-language collection notice` — notice; `display a collection notice` — display.
**Examples:** `The collection notice appeared beside the form rather than in a hidden legal page.` → Notice collection xuất hiện cạnh form chứ không ở legal page ẩn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `수집 고지`.

## 19. analytics opt-out /ˌænəˈlɪtɪks ɑpt ˈaʊt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a choice that stops a person's activity from being included in specified measurement or analytics systems.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** choice stop activity của person include trong analytics system cụ thể.
**Grammar & collocations:** `easy analytics opt-out` — opt-out; `offer an analytics opt-out` — offer.
**Examples:** `The analytics opt-out took effect immediately and did not require an account.` → Opt-out analytics có hiệu lực ngay và không cần account.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `분석 거부`.

## 20. data lifecycle /ˈdeɪtə ˈlaɪfˌsaɪkəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the stages through which data passes from collection and use to storage, sharing, archiving, or deletion.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** stages data đi qua từ collection, use tới storage, sharing, archive hoặc deletion.
**Grammar & collocations:** `complete data lifecycle` — lifecycle; `map a data lifecycle` — map.
**Examples:** `The policy mapped the data lifecycle from signup to final deletion.` → Policy map lifecycle data từ signup tới deletion cuối.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `데이터 생명주기`.

## Review in context

A **consent record** should show the **retention limit** for each field and explain whether a **sensitive attribute** creates **reidentification risk**. A **deletion request** and a **data breach notification** follow different procedures, especially when a **third-party cookie** was involved. A clear **consent banner** lets a reader decide whether **audience data** may be used, while a **data access request** can reveal how **identity resolution** works. A **data inventory** links each field to a **processing purpose** and distinguishes ordinary use from **tracking consent**. The **data subject** should understand the **consent interface**, change a **privacy preference**, read the **collection notice**, choose an **analytics opt-out**, and follow the **data lifecycle** from collection to deletion.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:** **Consent record** nên show **retention limit** cho mỗi field và explain **sensitive attribute** có tạo **reidentification risk** không. **Deletion request** và **data breach notification** follow procedure khác nhau, nhất là khi có **third-party cookie**. **Consent banner** rõ cho reader decide **audience data** có được use không, còn **data access request** có thể reveal **identity resolution** hoạt động thế nào. **Data inventory** link mỗi field với **processing purpose** và phân biệt ordinary use với **tracking consent**. **Data subject** nên hiểu **consent interface**, change **privacy preference**, đọc **collection notice**, chọn **analytics opt-out** và follow **data lifecycle** từ collection đến deletion.
