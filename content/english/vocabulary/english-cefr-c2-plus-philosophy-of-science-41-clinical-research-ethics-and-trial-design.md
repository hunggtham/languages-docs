# C2+ Vocabulary — Clinical research ethics and trial design

This lesson follows how clinical studies balance scientific validity, participant protection, uncertainty, and public responsibility. The network is **clinical equipoise → randomization concealment → pragmatic trial → cluster randomization → response-adaptive study → platform trial → early-stop criterion → interim analysis → data safety monitoring board → informed refusal → therapeutic misconception → research exception → vulnerable participant → minimal risk → risk-benefit ratio → protocol departure → adverse event → severe safety event → signal detection → trial registration → selective reporting → selective publication → participant withdrawal → post-trial access → research compensation**.

## 1. clinical equipoise /ˈklɪnɪkəl ɪˈkwɪpɔɪz/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** genuine professional uncertainty about which of two or more interventions is better for the population being studied.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** clinical equipoise là uncertainty thật của giới chuyên môn, tạo nền tảng đạo đức cho randomization.
**Grammar & collocations:** `clinical-equipoise requirement` — requirement; `maintain equipoise` — maintain; `comparative uncertainty` — uncertainty.
**Examples:** `The trial was ethical because clinical equipoise remained about which rehabilitation schedule produced better recovery.` → Chưa biết schedule nào tốt hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `임상적 균형` — clinical equipoise.

## 2. randomization concealment /ˌrændəməˈzeɪʃən kənˈsiːlmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** protection of the upcoming treatment assignment from researchers and recruiters until a participant is irreversibly enrolled.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** randomization concealment ngăn researcher đoán assignment trước khi enroll.
**Grammar & collocations:** `randomization-concealment procedure` — procedure; `conceal treatment assignment` — conceal; `enrollment decision` — decision.
**Examples:** `Randomization concealment prevented recruiters from directing healthier patients toward the experimental arm.` → Recruiter không chọn arm theo patient.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `무작위 배정 은폐` — randomization concealment.

## 3. pragmatic trial /præɡˈmætɪk traɪəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a trial designed to estimate how an intervention works in ordinary practice with typical patients, providers, and constraints.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** pragmatic trial hỏi intervention chạy thế nào trong care thật, không chỉ lab lý tưởng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `pragmatic-trial protocol` — protocol; `test effectiveness in practice` — test; `routine-care setting` — setting.
**Examples:** `The pragmatic trial included older patients and community clinics that an explanatory study would have excluded.` → Trial gần với population thật.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `실용적 임상시험` — pragmatic trial.

## 4. cluster randomization /ˈklʌstər ˌrændəməˈzeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** assignment of whole groups, such as clinics, schools, or villages, rather than individual participants to study conditions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cluster randomization randomize cả group để tránh contamination giữa individuals.
**Grammar & collocations:** `cluster-randomization design` — design; `randomize a clinic` — randomize; `intracluster correlation` — correlation.
**Examples:** `Cluster randomization assigned entire clinics to the new referral system to avoid mixed workflows.` → Cả clinic cùng một workflow.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `군집 무작위 배정` — cluster randomization.

## 5. response-adaptive study /rɪˈspɑns əˈdæptɪv ˈstʌdi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a study design that changes future allocation, sampling, or enrollment in response to accumulating outcome information.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** response-adaptive study điều chỉnh design khi data mới xuất hiện.
**Grammar & collocations:** `response-adaptive-study rule` — rule; `adapt allocation probabilities` — adapt; `accumulating evidence` — evidence.
**Examples:** `The response-adaptive study assigned more participants to the dose showing better early tolerance.` → Data sớm ảnh hưởng allocation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `반응 적응형 연구` — response-adaptive study.

## 6. platform trial /ˈplætˌfɔrm traɪəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a continuing trial infrastructure that evaluates multiple interventions under a shared master protocol, allowing arms to enter or leave over time.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** platform trial là “sân” dài hạn cho nhiều intervention, không phải trial rời từng lần.
**Grammar & collocations:** `platform-trial master protocol` — protocol; `add a treatment arm` — add; `shared control` — control.
**Examples:** `The platform trial added a new antiviral without rebuilding the entire recruitment system.` → Thêm arm mà giữ platform.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `플랫폼 임상시험` — platform trial.

## 7. early-stop criterion /ˈɜrli stɑp kraɪˈtɪriən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a predefined rule for stopping a study or treatment arm early because of benefit, harm, futility, or other decisive evidence.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** early-stop criterion là rule định trước để dừng khi evidence đủ mạnh.
**Grammar & collocations:** `early-stop-criterion rule` — rule; `stop an arm for harm` — stop; `futility boundary` — boundary.
**Examples:** `The early-stop criterion halted the arm after repeated serious reactions exceeded the safety limit.` → Safety limit kích hoạt dừng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `조기 중단 기준` — early-stop criterion.

## 8. interim analysis /ˈɪntərɪm əˈnæləsɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a planned analysis conducted before a study is complete to assess accumulating evidence, safety, or the need to modify the study.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** interim analysis là checkpoint giữa trial để xem evidence đang đi đâu.
**Grammar & collocations:** `interim-analysis plan` — plan; `review interim data` — review; `accrued outcome` — outcome.
**Examples:** `The interim analysis found promising efficacy but too little follow-up to change the primary conclusion.` → Signal tốt nhưng chưa đủ follow-up.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `중간 분석` — interim analysis.

## 9. data safety monitoring board /ˈdeɪtə ˈseɪfti ˈmɑnətərɪŋ bɔrd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an independent group that periodically reviews unblinded trial data for safety, benefit, and study conduct.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** data safety monitoring board là nhóm độc lập canh safety khi trial đang chạy.
**Grammar & collocations:** `data-safety-monitoring-board charter` — charter; `review unblinded data` — review; `independent oversight` — oversight.
**Examples:** `The data safety monitoring board recommended continuing because harms remained balanced and manageable.` → Board xem harm và balance.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `데이터 안전성 모니터링 위원회` — data safety monitoring board.

## 10. informed refusal /ɪnˈfɔrmd rɪˈfjuːzəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a person's deliberate decision not to join or continue research after understanding its relevant information and alternatives.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** informed refusal là quyền nói không sau khi hiểu đủ, không phải không hiểu rồi từ chối.
**Grammar & collocations:** `informed-refusal documentation` — documentation; `decline participation` — decline; `understand the alternative` — understand.
**Examples:** `The coordinator respected informed refusal and offered routine care without pressure to enroll.` → Từ chối research không mất care.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `충분한 설명에 따른 거부` — informed refusal.

## 11. therapeutic misconception /ˌθɛrəˈpjuːtɪk ˌmɪskənˈsɛpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the mistaken belief that the primary purpose of a research study is to provide individualized clinical treatment rather than produce generalizable knowledge.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** therapeutic misconception nhầm research với treatment cá nhân chắc chắn có benefit.
**Grammar & collocations:** `therapeutic-misconception risk` — risk; `confuse research with care` — confuse; `individual benefit` — benefit.
**Examples:** `The consent discussion addressed therapeutic misconception by explaining that assignment might offer no personal benefit.` → Consent nói rõ không guarantee benefit.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `치료적 오해` — therapeutic misconception.

## 12. research exception /rɪˈsɜrtʃ ɪkˈsɛpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a narrowly defined legal or ethical exception that permits specified research activity without the usual form of prospective individual permission.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** research exception là ngoại lệ hẹp, có safeguard, không phải bỏ consent tùy ý.
**Grammar & collocations:** `research-exception review` — review; `authorize an exception` — authorize; `minimal practicability` — practicability.
**Examples:** `The research exception applied only to emergency data collection when asking first was impossible.` → Chỉ dùng khi emergency không thể hỏi trước.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `연구 예외` — research exception.

## 13. vulnerable participant /ˈvʌlnərəbəl pɑrˈtɪsəpənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a research participant whose autonomy, freedom, health, or ability to refuse may be limited by circumstance or power imbalance.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** vulnerable participant cần protection thêm vì khó tự do và khó cân nhắc.
**Grammar & collocations:** `vulnerable-participant safeguard` — safeguard; `protect a participant` — protect; `power imbalance` — imbalance.
**Examples:** `The study added an independent advocate for each vulnerable participant recruited from institutional care.` → Có advocate độc lập.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `취약한 연구 참여자` — vulnerable participant.

## 14. minimal risk /ˈmɪnəməl rɪsk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a level of research-related harm or discomfort that is no greater than what people ordinarily encounter in daily life or routine examinations.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** minimal risk so sánh burden research với risk đời thường/routine care.
**Grammar & collocations:** `minimal-risk determination` — determination; `qualify as minimal risk` — qualify; `ordinary daily harm` — harm.
**Examples:** `The survey was classified as minimal risk because it asked about service use without collecting names.` → Survey không name nên risk thấp.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `최소 위험` — minimal risk.

## 15. risk-benefit ratio /rɪsk ˈbɛnəfɪt ˈreɪʃioʊ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the relationship between the possible harms of an intervention or study and the benefits it may produce.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** risk-benefit ratio cân harm với benefit, không chỉ nhìn một phía.
**Grammar & collocations:** `risk-benefit-ratio assessment` — assessment; `weigh risks against benefits` — weigh; `potential harm` — harm.
**Examples:** `The committee judged the risk-benefit ratio acceptable only after the dose was reduced.` → Giảm dose làm ratio tốt hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `위험-편익 비율` — risk-benefit ratio.

## 16. protocol departure /ˈproʊtəˌkɔl dɪˈpɑrtʃər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a documented action or circumstance in which study conduct differs from the approved protocol.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** protocol departure là đi lệch protocol, có thể nhỏ hoặc ảnh hưởng validity/safety.
**Grammar & collocations:** `protocol-departure log` — log; `depart from the protocol` — depart; `document a deviation` — document.
**Examples:** `The protocol departure occurred when a clinic used a substitute device during a supply interruption.` → Thiếu supply nên dùng device khác.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `시험계획서 이탈` — protocol departure.

## 17. adverse event /ˈædvɜrs ɪˈvɛnt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an unfavorable medical occurrence during a study, whether or not it was caused by the intervention.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** adverse event là event xấu xảy ra trong trial, chưa khẳng định do treatment.
**Grammar & collocations:** `adverse-event report` — report; `record an adverse event` — record; `causal relationship` — relationship.
**Examples:** `The participant reported an adverse event, but investigators found no evidence that the study drug caused it.` → Event xảy ra nhưng causal chưa chắc.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `이상반응 사건` — adverse event.

## 18. severe safety event /səˈvɪr ˈseɪfti ɪˈvɛnt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a serious or clinically important harmful occurrence requiring urgent review, intervention, reporting, or possible study modification.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** severe safety event là adverse event đủ nặng để kích hoạt safety response.
**Grammar & collocations:** `severe-safety-event review` — review; `escalate a safety event` — escalate; `urgent medical response` — response.
**Examples:** `A severe safety event prompted the board to pause enrollment while the dosing error was investigated.` → Dừng enroll để investigate.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `중증 안전 사건` — severe safety event.

## 19. signal detection /ˈsɪɡnəl dɪˈtɛkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** systematic search for unusual patterns that may indicate a safety problem, benefit, fraud, or important effect.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** signal detection tìm pattern bất thường trước khi biết chắc nguyên nhân.
**Grammar & collocations:** `signal-detection algorithm` — algorithm; `detect a safety signal` — detect; `pattern review` — review.
**Examples:** `Signal detection identified a cluster of liver abnormalities that required blinded adjudication.` → Cluster cần review kỹ hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `신호 탐지` — signal detection.

## 20. trial registration /ˈtraɪəl ˌrɛdʒəˈstreɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** public recording of a planned clinical study's design, outcomes, sponsors, and key methods before or near enrollment.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** trial registration tạo dấu vết public trước khi result xuất hiện.
**Grammar & collocations:** `trial-registration record` — record; `register a trial` — register; `prespecified outcome` — outcome.
**Examples:** `Trial registration made the original primary outcome visible before the sponsor reported later analyses.` → Public thấy outcome gốc.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `임상시험 등록` — trial registration.

## 21. selective reporting /sɪˈlɛktɪv rɪˈpɔrtɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** reporting only some measured outcomes, analyses, or time points while leaving other relevant results undisclosed.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** selective reporting chọn result có lợi để kể, giấu phần còn lại.
**Grammar & collocations:** `selective-reporting audit` — audit; `report a prespecified outcome` — report; `undisclosed analysis` — analysis.
**Examples:** `Selective reporting made the intervention appear more effective than the complete outcome set showed.` → Chọn outcome làm efficacy bị phóng đại.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `선택적 보고` — selective reporting.

## 22. selective publication /sɪˈlɛktɪv ˌpʌblɪˈkeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a pattern in which studies or results are more likely to be published when they are positive, novel, or statistically significant.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** selective publication làm literature nghiêng về finding “đẹp”.
**Grammar & collocations:** `selective-publication risk` — risk; `publish positive findings` — publish; `unpublished study` — study.
**Examples:** `Selective publication left the evidence base with too few null trials to estimate the true effect.` → Thiếu null trial làm effect nhìn lớn hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `선택적 출판` — selective publication.

## 23. participant withdrawal /pɑrˈtɪsəpənt wɪðˈdrɔəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a participant's decision to stop taking part in a study, either with or without giving a reason.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** participant withdrawal là quyền dừng participation bất cứ lúc nào theo consent.
**Grammar & collocations:** `participant-withdrawal rate` — rate; `withdraw from a study` — withdraw; `retention plan` — plan.
**Examples:** `Participant withdrawal increased after travel reimbursement was delayed for several months.` → Reimbursement delay làm retention giảm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `참여자 철회` — participant withdrawal.

## 24. post-trial access /poʊst traɪəl ˈæksɛs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** continued access to a beneficial intervention or care after a participant's study involvement has formally ended.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** post-trial access hỏi patient được tiếp tục treatment tốt sau trial không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `post-trial-access plan` — plan; `provide continued access` — provide; `beneficial intervention` — intervention.
**Examples:** `The post-trial access plan covered the medicine until a public reimbursement decision was made.` → Patient không bị bỏ giữa chừng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `시험 후 접근` — post-trial access.

## 25. research compensation /rɪˈsɜrtʃ ˌkɑmpənˈseɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** payment or other remedy for a participant's time, inconvenience, injury, or research-related loss, under ethically appropriate rules.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** research compensation bù time/burden hoặc harm, không được biến thành pressure mua consent.
**Grammar & collocations:** `research-compensation policy` — policy; `compensate a participant` — compensate; `injury remedy` — remedy.
**Examples:** `Research compensation covered transport and time without being large enough to pressure economically vulnerable participants.` → Compensation hợp lý, không ép consent.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `연구 보상` — research compensation.

## Review in context — Part 1

**Clinical equipoise** supports ethical comparison, while **randomization concealment** protects assignment before enrollment. A **pragmatic trial** can use **cluster randomization**, a **response-adaptive study**, or a **platform trial** to answer practical questions. An **early-stop criterion**, **interim analysis**, and an independent **data safety monitoring board** protect participants as evidence accumulates. **Informed refusal** must be respected, especially when **therapeutic misconception** could make research look like guaranteed treatment. A **research exception** requires narrow safeguards for a **vulnerable participant**, and **minimal risk** must be judged alongside the **risk-benefit ratio**.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 1:**

**Clinical equipoise** hỗ trợ so sánh có đạo đức, còn **randomization concealment** bảo vệ assignment trước enrollment. **Pragmatic trial** có thể dùng **cluster randomization**, **response-adaptive study** hoặc **platform trial** để trả lời câu hỏi thực tế. **Early-stop criterion**, **interim analysis** và **data safety monitoring board** độc lập bảo vệ participant khi evidence tích lũy. **Informed refusal** phải được tôn trọng, nhất là khi **therapeutic misconception** khiến research trông như treatment chắc chắn có benefit. **Research exception** cần safeguard hẹp cho **vulnerable participant**, và **minimal risk** phải được cân cùng **risk-benefit ratio**.

## Review in context — Part 2

A **protocol departure** should be documented, while an **adverse event** and a **severe safety event** require proportionate review. **Signal detection** can reveal a pattern before causation is certain. **Trial registration** preserves the planned design; **selective reporting** and **selective publication** can otherwise distort the evidence. **Participant withdrawal** is a protected choice, and **post-trial access** may be needed when an intervention helps. **Research compensation** should address time or harm without becoming undue pressure.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 2:**

**Protocol departure** cần được ghi lại, còn **adverse event** và **severe safety event** cần review proportionate. **Signal detection** có thể lộ pattern trước khi biết chắc causation. **Trial registration** giữ lại design ban đầu; nếu không, **selective reporting** và **selective publication** có thể làm méo evidence. **Participant withdrawal** là choice được bảo vệ, và **post-trial access** có thể cần khi intervention có ích. **Research compensation** nên bù time hoặc harm mà không trở thành pressure quá mức.
