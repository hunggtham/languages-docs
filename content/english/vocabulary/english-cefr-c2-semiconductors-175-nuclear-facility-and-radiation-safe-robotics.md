# C2 Vocabulary — Nuclear-facility and radiation-safe robotics

This lesson follows a nuclear facility from inspection and fuel handling to radiation mapping, emergency work, waste processing, and decommissioning. The network is **nuclear plant inspection robot → reactor vessel inspection robot → nuclear radiation mapping robot → fuel pool handling robot → remote maintenance manipulator → nuclear decontamination robot → spent fuel cask inspection → radiation shielding robot → steam generator inspection → containment survey robot → reactor coolant leak detection → nuclear emergency response robot → radioactive waste sorting robot → dose rate mapping drone → nuclear valve actuator robot → turbine hall inspection robot → fuel assembly handling robot → radiation-hardened crawler → nuclear decommissioning survey robot → nuclear security patrol robot**.

## 1. nuclear plant inspection robot /ˈnuːkliər plænt ɪnˈspekʃən ˈroʊbɑːt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a robot that inspects equipment and structures in a nuclear power or research plant.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** robot inspect equipment/structure trong nuclear power hoặc research plant.
**Grammar & collocations:** `nuclear-plant-inspection-robot route` — route; `inspect a restricted cell` — inspect restricted cell.
**Examples:** `The nuclear plant inspection robot checked the service gallery after the shutdown.` → Robot inspection nuclear plant check service gallery sau shutdown.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `원자력 발전소 검사 로봇`.

## Review in context

A **nuclear plant inspection robot**, **reactor vessel inspection robot**, and **nuclear radiation mapping robot** examine the facility, while a **fuel pool handling robot** and **remote maintenance manipulator** work where people cannot. A **nuclear decontamination robot**, **spent fuel cask inspection**, and **radiation shielding robot** reduce exposure; **steam generator inspection** and a **containment survey robot** check critical systems. **Reactor coolant leak detection**, a **nuclear emergency response robot**, and a **radioactive waste sorting robot** support abnormal conditions.
