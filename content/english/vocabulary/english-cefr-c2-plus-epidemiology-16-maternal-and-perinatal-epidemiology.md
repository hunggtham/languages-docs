# C2+ Vocabulary — Maternal and perinatal epidemiology

This lesson follows how maternal, newborn, and pregnancy outcomes are measured, reviewed, and improved across care systems. The network is **stillbirth rate → perinatal mortality ratio → maternal near-miss review → obstetric deterioration score → severe maternal morbidity → birth cohort → gestational age → preterm birth → low birth weight → neonatal infection signal → antenatal care coverage → skilled birth attendance → postpartum bleeding burden → maternal death review → verbal autopsy → perinatal audit → dignity-centered maternity care → reproductive-age denominator → pregnancy-associated death → obstetric case fatality → perinatal surveillance → facility birth rate → obstetric referral delay → maternal health inequity → newborn outcome**.

## 1. stillbirth rate /ˈstɪlˌbɜrθ reɪt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the number of stillbirths relative to a defined number of births in a population and period.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** stillbirth rate đo fetal death ở một mốc gestation được quy định.
**Grammar & collocations:** `stillbirth-rate estimate` — estimate; `monitor stillbirths` — monitor; `birth denominator` — denominator.
**Examples:** `The stillbirth rate fell after fetal monitoring and referral pathways improved.` → Care pathway đổi outcome.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `사산율` — stillbirth rate.

## 2. perinatal mortality ratio /ˌpɛrəˈneɪtəl mɔrˈtæləti ˈreɪʃioʊ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a measure combining stillbirths and early neonatal deaths in relation to births.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** perinatal mortality ratio nhìn cả death quanh birth thay vì chỉ một phase.
**Grammar & collocations:** `perinatal-mortality ratio` — ratio; `estimate perinatal deaths` — estimate; `birth outcome` — outcome.
**Examples:** `The perinatal mortality ratio highlighted losses that a neonatal-only measure missed.` → Scope rộng hơn cho picture khác.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `주산기 사망비` — perinatal mortality ratio.

## 3. maternal near-miss review /məˈtɜrnəl nɪr mɪs rɪˈvjuː/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** structured review of a life-threatening maternal complication from which the patient survived.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** maternal near-miss review học từ người suýt tử vong để tìm system failure trước death tiếp theo.
**Grammar & collocations:** `maternal-near-miss review` — review; `review a life-threatening complication` — review; `survivor case` — case.
**Examples:** `The maternal near-miss review found a delay in blood-product availability.` → Survivor case mở cơ hội sửa system.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `산모 중증 생존 사례 검토` — maternal near-miss review.

## 4. obstetric deterioration score /əbˈstɛtrɪk dɪˌtɪriəˈreɪʃən skɔr/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a structured score used to identify pregnant or postpartum patients whose condition may be worsening.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** obstetric deterioration score gom vital sign và symptom để bắt decline sớm.
**Grammar & collocations:** `obstetric-deterioration score` — score; `trigger escalation` — trigger; `maternal early warning` — warning.
**Examples:** `The obstetric deterioration score triggered senior review before shock became obvious.` → Score hỗ trợ escalation sớm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `산과 악화 점수` — obstetric deterioration score.

## 5. severe maternal morbidity /səˈvɪr məˈtɜrnəl mɔrˈbɪdəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** serious maternal complications that threaten life or require major intervention but do not necessarily result in death.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** severe maternal morbidity là harm nặng nhưng survivor, cần đo để thấy burden rộng hơn mortality.
**Grammar & collocations:** `severe-maternal-morbidity measure` — measure; `monitor major complications` — monitor; `life-threatening event` — event.
**Examples:** `Severe maternal morbidity remained high even after maternal mortality improved.` → Mortality giảm chưa đủ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `중증 모성 이환` — severe maternal morbidity.

## 6. birth cohort /bɜrθ ˈkoʊˌhɔrt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a group of people born during a defined period and followed for outcomes or exposures.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** birth cohort theo một nhóm trẻ từ cùng thời kỳ birth để nhìn trajectory.
**Grammar & collocations:** `birth-cohort study` — study; `follow a birth cohort` — follow; `longitudinal outcome` — outcome.
**Examples:** `The birth cohort was followed to compare early nutrition with later development.` → Cohort nối early exposure với outcome sau.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `출생 코호트` — birth cohort.

## 7. gestational age /dʒɛˈsteɪʃənəl eɪdʒ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the estimated duration of pregnancy at a specified time, usually measured from the last menstrual period or ultrasound.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** gestational age đặt pregnancy ở tuần nào để interpret growth và outcome.
**Grammar & collocations:** `gestational-age estimate` — estimate; `assess gestational age` — assess; `week of gestation` — week.
**Examples:** `Gestational age was revised after an early ultrasound provided a more reliable estimate.` → Dating đúng ảnh hưởng classification.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `재태 연령` — gestational age.

## 8. preterm birth /ˌpriːˈtɜrm bɜrθ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** birth occurring before the standard gestational-age threshold for term pregnancy.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** preterm birth là birth quá sớm, liên quan đến lung, brain và infection risk.
**Grammar & collocations:** `preterm-birth rate` — rate; `prevent preterm birth` — prevent; `preterm infant` — infant.
**Examples:** `The preterm birth rate was higher in areas with limited prenatal care and high stress exposure.` → Social condition liên quan outcome.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `조산` — preterm birth.

## 9. low birth weight /loʊ bɜrθ weɪt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** birth weight below a defined threshold, regardless of whether the infant was preterm or growth-restricted.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** low birth weight là measure theo weight, cần phân biệt với gestational age.
**Grammar & collocations:** `low-birth-weight infant` — infant; `measure birth weight` — measure; `growth restriction` — restriction.
**Examples:** `Low birth weight was analyzed separately from prematurity to avoid conflating mechanisms.` → Weight và age không identical.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `저체중 출생` — low birth weight.

## 10. neonatal infection signal /ˌniːoʊˈneɪtəl ɪnˈfɛkʃən ˈsɪɡnəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a clinical, laboratory, or service pattern suggesting infection among newborns.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** neonatal infection signal có thể là fever, culture, antibiotic use hoặc mortality pattern.
**Grammar & collocations:** `neonatal-infection signal` — signal; `detect an infection cluster` — detect; `newborn sepsis indicator` — indicator.
**Examples:** `A neonatal infection signal appeared when several infants required antibiotics within one ward.` → Pattern ward-level cần investigate.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `신생아 감염 신호` — neonatal infection signal.

## 11. antenatal care coverage /ˌæntɪˈneɪtəl kɛr ˈkʌvərɪdʒ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the proportion of pregnant people receiving a defined level or number of prenatal care contacts.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** antenatal care coverage đo service có đến được pregnant population không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `antenatal-care coverage` — coverage; `measure prenatal contacts` — measure; `care-contact indicator` — indicator.
**Examples:** `Antenatal care coverage improved after mobile clinics reached remote villages.` → Service model đổi coverage.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `산전 관리 보장률` — antenatal care coverage.

## 12. skilled birth attendance /skɪld bɜrθ əˈtɛndəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** childbirth assisted by a trained health professional able to recognize and manage complications.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** skilled birth attendance là birth có người đủ skill để detect và xử lý danger.
**Grammar & collocations:** `skilled-birth attendance` — attendance; `attend a delivery` — attend; `birth attendant` — attendant.
**Examples:** `Skilled birth attendance increased when transport and referral support were funded together.` → Skill cần access và referral.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `숙련 분만 보조` — skilled birth attendance.

## 13. postpartum bleeding burden /ˌpoʊstˈpɑrtəm ˈbliːdɪŋ ˈbɜrdən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the frequency and health impact of significant bleeding after childbirth across a population or care system.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** postpartum bleeding burden gồm event, transfusion, disability và death sau birth.
**Grammar & collocations:** `postpartum-bleeding burden` — burden; `measure postpartum bleeding` — measure; `hemorrhage response` — response.
**Examples:** `The postpartum bleeding burden fell after blood storage and rapid-response protocols improved.` → System readiness đổi survival.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `산후 출혈 부담` — postpartum bleeding burden.

## 14. maternal death review /məˈtɜrnəl dɛθ rɪˈvjuː/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** structured examination of the circumstances, causes, delays, and preventability of a maternal death.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** maternal death review nhìn toàn chain từ home tới facility và system decision.
**Grammar & collocations:** `maternal-death review` — review; `review a maternal death` — review; `preventable factor` — factor.
**Examples:** `The maternal death review identified referral delay and fragmented records as preventable factors.` → Review tìm system cause.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `모성 사망 검토` — maternal death review.

## 15. verbal autopsy /ˈvɜrbəl ˈɔtɑpsi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an interview-based method for inferring probable cause of death when no medical certification or autopsy is available.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** verbal autopsy dùng narrative của family/caregiver để infer cause.
**Grammar & collocations:** `verbal-autopsy interview` — interview; `assign a probable cause` — assign; `death narrative` — narrative.
**Examples:** `Verbal autopsy expanded maternal-death data in districts without complete civil registration.` → Interview lấp data gap nhưng có uncertainty.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `구두 부검` — verbal autopsy.

## 16. perinatal audit /ˌpɛrəˈneɪtəl ˈɔdɪt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** systematic review of stillbirths, newborn deaths, care processes, and preventable factors within a service.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** perinatal audit học từ từng outcome để đổi practice, không chỉ đếm death.
**Grammar & collocations:** `perinatal-audit meeting` — meeting; `audit perinatal care` — audit; `modifiable factor` — factor.
**Examples:** `The perinatal audit found inconsistent recognition of fetal distress during overnight shifts.` → Audit chỉ ra training gap.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `주산기 감사` — perinatal audit.

## 17. dignity-centered maternity care /ˈdɪɡnəti ˈsɛntərd məˈtɜrnəti kɛr/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** maternity care organized around respectful treatment, informed choice, privacy, and freedom from abuse or humiliation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** dignity-centered maternity care coi safety và dignity là cùng một quality goal.
**Grammar & collocations:** `dignity-centered maternity care` — care; `provide respectful care` — provide; `informed choice` — choice.
**Examples:** `Dignity-centered maternity care included consent before examination and a private communication space.` → Respect là part của quality.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `존엄 중심 모성 돌봄` — dignity-centered maternity care.

## 18. reproductive-age denominator /ˌriːprəˈdʌktɪv eɪdʒ dɪˈnɑməˌneɪtər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the defined population of people within a specified reproductive-age range used to calculate a maternal or reproductive-health rate.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** reproductive-age denominator quyết định population base của rate reproductive health.
**Grammar & collocations:** `reproductive-age denominator` — denominator; `define the denominator` — define; `age-specific rate` — rate.
**Examples:** `The reproductive-age denominator was updated after census estimates changed.` → Denominator đổi thì rate cần recalculate.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `가임 연령 분모` — reproductive-age denominator.

## 19. pregnancy-associated death /ˈprɛɡnənsi əˈsoʊsiˌeɪtɪd dɛθ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a death during pregnancy or within a defined period after pregnancy, regardless of whether pregnancy caused it.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** pregnancy-associated death rộng hơn maternal death vì chỉ cần xảy ra quanh pregnancy.
**Grammar & collocations:** `pregnancy-associated death` — death; `classify pregnancy-relatedness` — classify; `postpartum period` — period.
**Examples:** `The review separated pregnancy-associated deaths from deaths directly caused by obstetric complications.` → Classification rõ giúp prevention đúng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `임신 관련 사망` — pregnancy-associated death.

## 20. obstetric case fatality /əbˈstɛtrɪk keɪs fəˈtæləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the proportion of people with a specified obstetric complication who die from it within a defined period.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** obstetric case fatality đo survival trong những case complication đã xảy ra.
**Grammar & collocations:** `obstetric-case fatality` — fatality; `compare complication survival` — compare; `fatal obstetric outcome` — outcome.
**Examples:** `Obstetric case fatality was lower where referral surgery was available around the clock.` → Access emergency care đổi fatality.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `산과 치명률` — obstetric case fatality.

## 21. perinatal surveillance /ˌpɛrəˈneɪtəl sərˈveɪləns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** systematic monitoring of fetal, newborn, and perinatal deaths, complications, and care indicators.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** perinatal surveillance theo dõi cả fetus, newborn và care process quanh birth.
**Grammar & collocations:** `perinatal-surveillance system` — system; `monitor perinatal outcomes` — monitor; `birth-outcome signal` — signal.
**Examples:** `Perinatal surveillance detected a rise in early neonatal deaths after a supply interruption.` → System data báo service failure.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `주산기 감시` — perinatal surveillance.

## 22. facility birth rate /fəˈsɪləti bɜrθ reɪt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the proportion of births occurring in a health facility within a defined population and period.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** facility birth rate đo births có đi vào formal care system không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `facility-birth rate` — rate; `deliver in a facility` — deliver; `institutional delivery` — delivery.
**Examples:** `The facility birth rate increased after transport vouchers removed a distance barrier.` → Access policy đổi coverage.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `의료기관 분만율` — facility birth rate.

## 23. obstetric referral delay /əbˈstɛtrɪk rɪˈfɜrəl deɪ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** harmful time lost while recognizing a complication, deciding to refer, arranging transport, or receiving care at the next facility.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** obstetric referral delay chia thành nhiều đoạn, không chỉ “xe đến chậm”.
**Grammar & collocations:** `obstetric-referral delay` — delay; `reduce referral delay` — reduce; `emergency transport` — transport.
**Examples:** `Obstetric referral delay included late recognition and a missing blood-transfer agreement.` → Delay có nhiều owner.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `산과 의뢰 지연` — obstetric referral delay.

## 24. maternal health inequity /məˈtɜrnəl hɛlθ ɪnˈɛkwəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an unfair difference in maternal exposure, care, complications, survival, or recovery across social groups.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** maternal health inequity là gap maternal outcome do social và system condition.
**Grammar & collocations:** `maternal-health inequity` — inequity; `measure a care gap` — measure; `unequal maternal outcome` — outcome.
**Examples:** `Maternal health inequity persisted despite national improvement because rural referral access lagged.` → Average progress chưa đủ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `모성 건강 불평등` — maternal health inequity.

## 25. newborn outcome /ˈnuːˌbɔrn ˈaʊtˌkʌm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a measured health result for a newborn, such as survival, infection, growth, disability, or early development.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** newborn outcome mở rộng beyond alive/dead để nhìn quality của survival.
**Grammar & collocations:** `newborn-outcome measure` — measure; `track newborn outcomes` — track; `early developmental outcome` — outcome.
**Examples:** `The program tracked newborn outcome beyond discharge to include feeding and early development.` → Follow-up làm outcome đầy đủ hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `신생아 결과` — newborn outcome.

## Review in context — Part 1

The **stillbirth rate** and **perinatal mortality ratio** show population loss, while a **maternal near-miss review**, **obstetric deterioration score**, and **severe maternal morbidity** describe serious survival events. A **birth cohort** uses **gestational age**, **preterm birth**, and **low birth weight** to interpret a **neonatal infection signal**. **Antenatal care coverage** and **skilled birth attendance** influence prevention.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 1:**

**Stillbirth rate** và **perinatal mortality ratio** cho thấy mất mát ở population, còn **maternal near-miss review**, **obstetric deterioration score** và **severe maternal morbidity** mô tả các event nặng nhưng sống sót. **Birth cohort** dùng **gestational age**, **preterm birth** và **low birth weight** để diễn giải **neonatal infection signal**. **Antenatal care coverage** và **skilled birth attendance** ảnh hưởng prevention.

## Review in context — Part 2

The **postpartum bleeding burden** can be examined through a **maternal death review**, **verbal autopsy**, and **perinatal audit**. **Dignity-centered maternity care** protects choice and trust. A **reproductive-age denominator**, **pregnancy-associated death**, and **obstetric case fatality** clarify rates. **Perinatal surveillance** tracks a **facility birth rate**, **obstetric referral delay**, **maternal health inequity**, and the final **newborn outcome**.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 2:**

**Postpartum bleeding burden** có thể được xem xét qua **maternal death review**, **verbal autopsy** và **perinatal audit**. **Dignity-centered maternity care** bảo vệ choice và trust. **Reproductive-age denominator**, **pregnancy-associated death** và **obstetric case fatality** làm rõ các rate. **Perinatal surveillance** theo dõi **facility birth rate**, **obstetric referral delay**, **maternal health inequity** và **newborn outcome** cuối cùng.
