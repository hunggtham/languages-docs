# C1 Vocabulary 16 — Audience participation and community listening

This lesson follows how newsrooms listen to communities, invite useful participation, and turn audience knowledge into accountable reporting.

Flow: **listening session → callout → participatory reporting → community advisory → reader council → comment moderation → constructive disagreement → source callout → audience insight → trust survey → correction request → response protocol → civic feedback → participation barrier → outreach partner → reader forum → community listening → audience feedback → engagement survey → public editor**.

## 1. listening session /ˈlɪsənɪŋ ˈsɛʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a planned meeting in which an organization hears people's experiences, concerns, and priorities before deciding what to do.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** meeting được plan để organization hear experience, concern và priority trước khi decide.
**Grammar & collocations:** `community listening session` — session; `hold a listening session` — tổ chức.
**Examples:** `The newsroom held a listening session before assigning a series about housing.` → Newsroom tổ chức session listening trước khi assign series về housing.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `경청 모임`.

## 2. callout /ˈkɔlˌaʊt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a public invitation asking people to share information, experiences, questions, or documents related to a reporting project.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** invitation public mời người share information, experience, question hoặc document cho reporting project.
**Grammar & collocations:** `public callout` — callout; `publish a callout` — publish.
**Examples:** `The callout asked tenants to describe repairs that had been delayed.` → Callout mời tenant describe repair bị delay.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `제보 요청`.

## 3. participatory reporting /pɑrˈtɪsəpəˌtɔri rɪˈpɔrtɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** reporting that involves community members in identifying questions, gathering evidence, or shaping the final work.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** reporting involve community member identify question, gather evidence hoặc shape final work.
**Grammar & collocations:** `participatory-reporting project` — project; `practice participatory reporting` — thực hành.
**Examples:** `Participatory reporting helped residents map unsafe crossings near schools.` → Reporting participatory giúp resident map crossing nguy hiểm gần school.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `참여형 보도`.

## 4. community advisory /kəˈmjuːnəti ədˈvaɪzəri/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** advice provided by people from a community to help an organization understand local needs and consequences.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** advice từ người community giúp organization hiểu need và consequence local.
**Grammar & collocations:** `independent community advisory` — advisory; `seek community advisory` — seek.
**Examples:** `Community advisory changed the language used in the health coverage.` → Advisory community thay đổi language dùng trong health coverage.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `지역사회 자문`.

## 5. reader council /ˈriːdər ˈkaʊnsəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a group of readers who regularly discuss a publication's priorities, practices, and relationship with its audience.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** group reader thường xuyên discuss priority, practice và relationship của publication với audience.
**Grammar & collocations:** `diverse reader council` — council đa dạng; `convene a reader council` — triệu tập.
**Examples:** `The reader council questioned whether rural voices were receiving enough attention.` → Council reader hỏi rural voice có nhận đủ attention không.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `독자 협의회`.

## 6. comment moderation /ˈkɑmɛnt ˌmɑdəˈreɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the process of reviewing and managing audience comments according to published rules.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** process review và manage audience comment theo rule publish.
**Grammar & collocations:** `consistent comment moderation` — moderation nhất quán; `explain comment moderation` — explain.
**Examples:** `Clear comment moderation removed threats without silencing firm disagreement.` → Moderation comment rõ remove threat mà không silence disagreement mạnh.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `댓글 관리`.

## 7. constructive disagreement /kənˈstrʌktɪv ˌdɪsəˈɡriːmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a respectful difference of opinion that tests ideas and improves understanding rather than attacking people.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** difference opinion respectful test idea và improve understanding, không attack người.
**Grammar & collocations:** `encourage constructive disagreement` — encourage; `model constructive disagreement` — làm mẫu.
**Examples:** `The forum rules encouraged constructive disagreement about the proposed transit plan.` → Rule forum encourage disagreement constructive về transit plan đề xuất.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `건설적 불일치`.

## 8. source callout /sɔrs ˈkɔlˌaʊt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a public request for people with relevant knowledge or documents to contact a reporting team.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** request public mời người có knowledge hoặc document liên quan contact reporting team.
**Grammar & collocations:** `targeted source callout` — callout; `issue a source callout` — đưa ra.
**Examples:** `A source callout reached former inspectors who had worked on the project.` → Callout source reach former inspector từng work trong project.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `취재원 제보 요청`.

## 9. audience insight /ˈɔdiəns ˈɪnˌsaɪt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a useful understanding of what an audience knows, needs, values, or finds difficult.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** understanding hữu ích về audience know, need, value hoặc find difficult gì.
**Grammar & collocations:** `actionable audience insight` — insight; `gather audience insight` — gather.
**Examples:** `Audience insight showed that readers wanted a glossary beside the investigation.` → Insight audience cho thấy reader muốn glossary cạnh investigation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `독자 통찰`.

## 10. trust survey /trʌst ˈsɜrveɪ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a study that asks people how much they trust an organization, source, or type of information.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** study hỏi người trust organization, source hoặc information type đến mức nào.
**Grammar & collocations:** `independent trust survey` — survey; `conduct a trust survey` — conduct.
**Examples:** `The trust survey found that corrections mattered more than a polished visual identity.` → Survey trust thấy correction quan trọng hơn visual identity polished.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `신뢰 조사`.

## 11. correction request /kəˈrɛkʃən rɪˈkwɛst/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a message asking a publication to review and amend a factual error in its work.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** message yêu cầu publication review và amend factual error trong work.
**Grammar & collocations:** `formal correction request` — request formal; `submit a correction request` — submit.
**Examples:** `The reader sent a correction request with a scan of the original public record.` → Reader gửi request correction cùng scan original public record.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `정정 요청`.

## 12. response protocol /rɪˈspɑns ˈproʊtəˌkɔl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a defined procedure for acknowledging, investigating, and answering messages or concerns from the public.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** procedure định nghĩa acknowledge, investigate và answer message hoặc concern của public.
**Grammar & collocations:** `clear response protocol` — protocol; `follow a response protocol` — follow.
**Examples:** `The response protocol required the desk to acknowledge every correction request within two days.` → Protocol response yêu cầu desk acknowledge mọi request correction trong hai ngày.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `응답 절차`.

## 13. civic feedback /ˈsɪvɪk ˈfiːdbæk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** information and opinions that people provide about public issues, institutions, and community life.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** information và opinion người provide về public issue, institution và community life.
**Grammar & collocations:** `useful civic feedback` — feedback; `collect civic feedback` — collect.
**Examples:** `The project collected civic feedback before the council voted on the redevelopment.` → Project collect feedback civic trước council vote redevelopment.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `시민 피드백`.

## 14. participation barrier /pɑrˌtɪsəˈpeɪʃən ˈbæriər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a practical, social, linguistic, or technical obstacle that prevents people from taking part.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** obstacle practical, social, linguistic hoặc technical ngăn người tham gia.
**Grammar & collocations:** `remove a participation barrier` — remove; `identify participation barriers` — identify.
**Examples:** `Evening childcare removed a participation barrier for working parents.` → Childcare buổi tối remove barrier participation cho working parent.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `참여 장벽`.

## 15. outreach partner /ˈaʊtˌriːtʃ ˈpɑrtnər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a trusted organization or person that helps a project reach communities it might otherwise miss.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** organization hoặc person đáng tin giúp project reach community có thể bị miss.
**Grammar & collocations:** `local outreach partner` — partner; `work with an outreach partner` — work.
**Examples:** `A library became an outreach partner for the survey in neighborhoods without stable internet.` → Library thành partner outreach cho survey ở neighborhood không có internet stable.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `대외 협력 파트너`.

## 16. reader forum /ˈriːdər ˈfɔrəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an organized online or in-person space where readers discuss a publication's work with one another or its staff.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** space online hoặc in-person tổ chức để reader discuss work với nhau hoặc staff.
**Grammar & collocations:** `moderated reader forum` — forum; `host a reader forum` — host.
**Examples:** `The editor hosted a reader forum after publishing the investigation's final installment.` → Editor host forum reader sau khi publish installment cuối của investigation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `독자 포럼`.

## 17. community listening /kəˈmjuːnəti ˈlɪsənɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** sustained attention to what a community says and experiences, especially before or during public-interest work.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** attention sustained đến điều community nói và trải nghiệm, nhất là trước hoặc trong public-interest work.
**Grammar & collocations:** `ongoing community listening` — listening liên tục; `practice community listening` — thực hành.
**Examples:** `Community listening revealed that residents used a different name for the disputed site.` → Listening community reveal resident dùng tên khác cho site tranh chấp.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `지역사회 경청`.

## 18. audience feedback /ˈɔdiəns ˈfiːdbæk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** comments, questions, reactions, and suggestions that an audience gives about published work or service.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** comment, question, reaction và suggestion audience give về work hoặc service publish.
**Grammar & collocations:** `specific audience feedback` — feedback; `respond to audience feedback` — respond.
**Examples:** `Audience feedback prompted the newsroom to add a data download.` → Feedback audience prompt newsroom thêm data download.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `독자 피드백`.

## 19. engagement survey /ɪnˈɡeɪdʒmənt ˈsɜrveɪ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a study of how people interact with, understand, and feel connected to a media service or project.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** study về cách người interact, understand và feel connected với media service hoặc project.
**Grammar & collocations:** `annual engagement survey` — survey hằng năm; `run an engagement survey` — run.
**Examples:** `The engagement survey showed that newsletters reached people who rarely opened the main site.` → Survey engagement cho thấy newsletter reach người hiếm khi mở main site.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `참여도 조사`.

## 20. public editor /ˈpʌblɪk ˈɛdətər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a person inside a media organization who represents audience concerns and examines editorial decisions publicly.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** person trong media organization represent concern audience và examine editorial decision public.
**Grammar & collocations:** `independent public editor` — editor; `consult the public editor` — consult.
**Examples:** `The public editor explained why the headline had been changed after publication.` → Editor public explain vì sao headline đổi sau publish.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `공공 편집자`.

## Review in context

A **listening session** may begin with a public **callout** for a **participatory reporting** project. **Community advisory** can guide a **reader council**, while careful **comment moderation** protects **constructive disagreement**. A targeted **source callout** produces useful **audience insight**, and a **trust survey** can show whether people feel heard. A **correction request** should follow a clear **response protocol**. Newsrooms can collect **civic feedback**, remove a **participation barrier**, and work with an **outreach partner**. A **reader forum** supports ongoing **community listening**, turns **audience feedback** into action, and uses an **engagement survey** to learn what changed. A **public editor** can explain the process openly.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:** Một **listening session** có thể bắt đầu bằng **callout** public cho project **participatory reporting**. **Community advisory** có thể guide **reader council**, còn **comment moderation** cẩn thận bảo vệ **constructive disagreement**. **Source callout** có mục tiêu tạo **audience insight** hữu ích, và **trust survey** cho thấy người có cảm thấy được lắng nghe không. **Correction request** nên follow **response protocol** rõ. Newsroom có thể collect **civic feedback**, remove **participation barrier** và work với **outreach partner**. **Reader forum** hỗ trợ **community listening** liên tục, biến **audience feedback** thành action và dùng **engagement survey** để learn điều gì đã đổi. **Public editor** có thể explain process công khai.
