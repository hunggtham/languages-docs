# C1 Vocabulary 06 — Implementation and incentive mechanisms

This lesson follows how institutions turn private information and individual incentives into a desired collective outcome.

Flow: **implementation theory → social choice correspondence → monotonicity condition → no-veto power → Nash implementation → Bayesian implementation → dominant-strategy implementation → ex post incentive compatibility → interim incentive compatibility → ex post individual rationality → budget balance → voluntary participation → participation constraint → transfer scheme → Groves mechanism → Vickrey–Clarke–Groves mechanism → pivot payment → Clarke tax → Myerson–Satterthwaite theorem → double auction**.

## 1. implementation theory /ˌɪmpləmɛnˈteɪʃən ˈθɪəri/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the study of whether rules can make rational participants produce a specified social choice despite their private information.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** nghiên cứu liệu rule có thể khiến participant rational tạo social choice mong muốn dù information của họ private.
**Grammar & collocations:** `implementation-theory result` — result; `study implementation` — nghiên cứu.
**Examples:** `Implementation theory asked whether the agency could achieve the target allocation without observing each firm's costs.` → Lý thuyết implementation hỏi agency có thể đạt allocation mục tiêu mà không biết cost từng firm không.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `메커니즘 구현 이론`.

## 2. social choice correspondence /ˈsoʊʃəl tʃɔɪs ˌkɔrəˈspɑndəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a rule that maps preferences and relevant information to a set of acceptable collective outcomes.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** rule map preference và information thành một set outcome collective chấp nhận được.
**Grammar & collocations:** `social-choice correspondence` — correspondence; `implement a correspondence` — implement.
**Examples:** `The social choice correspondence allowed several efficient allocations when preferences were tied.` → Correspondence choice xã hội cho phép nhiều allocation efficient khi preference hòa.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `사회적 선택 대응`.

## 3. monotonicity condition /ˌmɑnətəˈnɪsəti kənˈdɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a condition requiring an outcome not to become less favored when its support in the reported preferences increases.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** outcome không được kém favored khi support của nó trong reported preference tăng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `monotonicity-condition test` — test; `satisfy monotonicity` — thỏa.
**Examples:** `The proposed rule failed the monotonicity condition when extra support caused the project to lose funding.` → Rule thất bại condition monotonicity khi support thêm khiến project mất funding.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `단조성 조건`.

## 4. no-veto power /ˌnoʊ ˈviːtoʊ ˈpaʊər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a property of a group decision rule in which no single participant can block every outcome preferred by the others.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** property rule collective mà không participant đơn lẻ nào có thể chặn mọi outcome được người khác thích.
**Grammar & collocations:** `no-veto-power condition` — condition; `remove veto power` — loại.
**Examples:** `The constitution preserved no-veto power by requiring broad rather than unanimous approval.` → Constitution giữ no-veto power bằng cách yêu cầu approval rộng thay vì unanimous.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `거부권 없음`.

## 5. Nash implementation /ˈnɑːʃ ˌɪmpləmɛnˈteɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the design of a mechanism whose equilibrium outcomes coincide with the desired social choice outcomes.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** thiết kế mechanism mà equilibrium outcome trùng với social choice outcome mong muốn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `Nash-implementation result` — result; `Nash-implement a social choice` — implement.
**Examples:** `Nash implementation required every undesired allocation to be unstable under the proposed rules.` → Implementation Nash yêu cầu mọi allocation không mong muốn đều unstable dưới rule đề xuất.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `내시 구현`.

## 6. Bayesian implementation /beɪˈziən ˌɪmpləmɛnˈteɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** implementation of a social choice rule when participants have private types and form beliefs about one another.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** implementation social choice khi participant có type riêng và hình thành belief về nhau.
**Grammar & collocations:** `Bayesian-implementation condition` — condition; `Bayesian-implement a rule` — implement.
**Examples:** `Bayesian implementation used the common prior to predict how private cost reports would affect the outcome.` → Implementation Bayesian dùng prior chung để dự đoán report cost private ảnh hưởng outcome.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `베이지안 구현`.

## 7. dominant-strategy implementation /ˈdɑmənənt ˈstrætədʒi ˌɪmpləmɛnˈteɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** implementation in which truthful or desired behavior is optimal regardless of what other participants report.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** implementation mà behavior truthful hoặc mong muốn optimal bất kể participant khác report gì.
**Grammar & collocations:** `dominant-strategy implementation` — implementation; `implement truth-telling in dominant strategies` — implement.
**Examples:** `Dominant-strategy implementation protected the allocation even when bidders had unusual beliefs about one another.` → Implementation strategy trội bảo vệ allocation dù bidder có belief unusual về nhau.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `지배 전략 구현`.

## 8. ex post incentive compatibility /ˌɛks poʊst ɪnˈsɛntɪv kəmˌpætəˈbɪləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a condition under which truthful reporting remains optimal after all private information is realized.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** truthful report vẫn optimal sau khi toàn bộ private information đã realized.
**Grammar & collocations:** `ex-post incentive-compatibility constraint` — constraint; `verify truthful reporting ex post` — kiểm tra.
**Examples:** `The contract achieved ex post incentive compatibility for every realized demand state.` → Contract đạt compatibility incentive ex post cho mọi demand state realized.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `사후 유인 적합성`.

## 9. interim incentive compatibility /ˈɪntərəm ɪnˈsɛntɪv kəmˌpætəˈbɪləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a condition under which truthful reporting is optimal given a participant's private information and beliefs about what remains unknown.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** truthful report optimal theo private information và belief về phần chưa biết.
**Grammar & collocations:** `interim incentive-compatibility condition` — condition; `check incentives after a signal` — kiểm tra.
**Examples:** `Interim incentive compatibility held after each supplier observed its own production cost.` → Compatibility incentive interim đúng sau khi mỗi supplier thấy production cost của mình.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `중간 유인 적합성`.

## 10. ex post individual rationality /ˌɛks poʊst ˌɪndəˈvɪdʒuəl ˌræʃəˈnæləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a condition ensuring that participation gives each type at least as much value as refusing after uncertainty is resolved.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** participation cho mỗi type ít nhất bằng refusal sau khi uncertainty được giải quyết.
**Grammar & collocations:** `ex-post individual-rationality guarantee` — guarantee; `preserve participation value` — giữ.
**Examples:** `Ex post individual rationality protected a buyer whose realized valuation was unexpectedly low.` → Rationality cá nhân ex post bảo vệ buyer có valuation realized thấp bất ngờ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `사후 개별 합리성`.

## 11. budget balance /ˈbʌdʒət ˈbæləns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a condition under which the mechanism's transfers neither require outside funding nor leave an unexplained surplus.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** condition transfer của mechanism không cần funding ngoài và không để surplus không giải thích.
**Grammar & collocations:** `budget-balance condition` — condition; `maintain budget balance` — duy trì.
**Examples:** `The public exchange pursued budget balance by returning fees after operating costs were covered.` → Exchange công giữ balance budget bằng cách trả fee sau khi bù operating cost.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `예산 균형`.

## 12. voluntary participation /ˈvɑlənˌtɛri pɑrˌtɪsəˈpeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** participation chosen freely rather than imposed, usually because joining is expected to benefit the participant.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** participation được chọn tự do vì participant kỳ vọng joining có lợi.
**Grammar & collocations:** `voluntary-participation condition` — condition; `ensure voluntary participation` — bảo đảm.
**Examples:** `Voluntary participation made the platform publish its fees before suppliers enrolled.` → Participation tự nguyện khiến platform công bố fee trước khi supplier enroll.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `자발적 참여`.

## 13. participation constraint /pɑrˌtɪsəˈpeɪʃən kənˈstreɪnt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an inequality requiring a participant's expected payoff from joining to be at least as high as the payoff from staying out.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** inequality yêu cầu expected payoff khi join ít nhất bằng payoff khi stay out.
**Grammar & collocations:** `participation-constraint slack` — khoảng đệm; `bind the participation constraint` — làm constraint binding.
**Examples:** `A higher entry fee violated the participation constraint for small suppliers.` → Entry fee cao hơn vi phạm constraint participation với supplier nhỏ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `참여 제약`.

## 14. transfer scheme /ˈtrænsfɝ skim/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a rule specifying how money or other resources move among participants after the mechanism selects an outcome.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** rule quy định tiền hoặc resource chuyển giữa participant sau khi mechanism chọn outcome.
**Grammar & collocations:** `transfer-scheme formula` — công thức; `design a transfer scheme` — thiết kế.
**Examples:** `The transfer scheme compensated the losing bidder for verified preparation costs.` → Scheme transfer bù preparation cost đã verify cho bidder thua.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `이전 방식`.

## 15. Groves mechanism /ˈɡroʊvz ˈmɛkəˌnɪzəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a truthful mechanism that chooses an efficient outcome using reported values and assigns transfers based on others' reported welfare.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mechanism truthful chọn efficient outcome từ reported value và gán transfer dựa welfare người khác report.
**Grammar & collocations:** `Groves-mechanism transfer` — transfer; `apply a Groves mechanism` — áp dụng.
**Examples:** `The Groves mechanism used reported valuations to allocate the shared spectrum efficiently.` → Mechanism Groves dùng valuation report để phân bổ spectrum chung hiệu quả.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `그로브스 메커니즘`.

## 16. Vickrey–Clarke–Groves mechanism /ˈvɪkri klɑrk ɡroʊvz ˈmɛkəˌnɪzəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a Groves mechanism in which each participant's payment reflects the external effect of that participant on everyone else.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mechanism Groves mà payment của participant phản ánh external effect participant đó gây cho người khác.
**Grammar & collocations:** `Vickrey–Clarke–Groves mechanism` — mechanism; `calculate an externality payment` — tính.
**Examples:** `The Vickrey–Clarke–Groves mechanism charged the winning bidder for the opportunity cost imposed on others.` → Mechanism VCG charge bidder thắng theo opportunity cost gây cho người khác.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `비크리-클라크-그로브스 메커니즘`.

## 17. pivot payment /ˈpɪvət ˈpeɪmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a payment equal to the harm a participant's presence causes to the other participants' best achievable outcome.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** payment bằng harm participant gây cho best outcome khả dĩ của participant khác.
**Grammar & collocations:** `pivot-payment rule` — rule; `charge a pivot payment` — charge.
**Examples:** `The platform charged a pivot payment equal to the value of the capacity displaced by the selected job.` → Platform charge pivot payment bằng value capacity bị job chọn chiếm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `피벗 지불`.

## 18. Clarke tax /ˈklɑrk tæks/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a payment in a pivotal mechanism that makes a participant bear the external cost of changing the collective decision.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** payment trong mechanism pivotal khiến participant chịu external cost do đổi collective decision.
**Grammar & collocations:** `Clarke-tax payment` — payment; `levy a Clarke tax` — đánh.
**Examples:** `The Clarke tax made the project's lead beneficiary account for the capacity others lost.` → Tax Clarke khiến beneficiary chính tính capacity người khác mất.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `클라크 세금`.

## 19. Myerson–Satterthwaite theorem /ˈmaɪərsən ˈsætərˌweɪt ˈθiərəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the theorem that no mechanism for bilateral trade can simultaneously guarantee efficiency, truthful incentives, voluntary participation, and budget balance under private values.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** theorem nói không mechanism trade song phương nào đồng thời bảo đảm efficiency, truthful incentive, voluntary participation và budget balance khi value private.
**Grammar & collocations:** `Myerson–Satterthwaite theorem` — theorem; `face an impossibility result` — gặp.
**Examples:** `The Myerson–Satterthwaite theorem warned that a perfectly fair trade rule was impossible under the stated assumptions.` → Theorem cảnh báo rule trade hoàn toàn fair là impossible dưới assumption đã nêu.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `마이어슨-새터스웨이트 정리`.

## 20. double auction /ˈdʌbəl ˈɔkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a market mechanism in which multiple buyers and sellers submit bids and offers that are jointly matched.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mechanism nhiều buyer và seller cùng submit bid/offer để match.
**Grammar & collocations:** `double-auction market` — market; `clear a double auction` — clear.
**Examples:** `The double auction matched electricity buyers and sellers after sorting bids and offers.` → Double auction match buyer/seller điện sau khi sort bid và offer.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `이중 경매`.

## Review in context

**Implementation theory** studies whether a **social choice correspondence** can be achieved under a **monotonicity condition** and **no-veto power**. **Nash implementation**, **Bayesian implementation**, and **dominant-strategy implementation** differ in their information and incentive requirements. A mechanism may require **ex post incentive compatibility**, **interim incentive compatibility**, and **ex post individual rationality**, together with **budget balance** and **voluntary participation**. A **participation constraint** shapes the **transfer scheme**. A **Groves mechanism**, especially a **Vickrey–Clarke–Groves mechanism**, may use a **pivot payment** or **Clarke tax**, but the **Myerson–Satterthwaite theorem** limits what is possible in a **double auction**.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

**Implementation theory** nghiên cứu liệu **social choice correspondence** có thể đạt dưới **monotonicity condition** và **no-veto power** không. **Nash implementation**, **Bayesian implementation** và **dominant-strategy implementation** khác nhau về yêu cầu information và incentive. Mechanism có thể cần **ex post incentive compatibility**, **interim incentive compatibility** và **ex post individual rationality**, cùng **budget balance** và **voluntary participation**. **Participation constraint** định hình **transfer scheme**. **Groves mechanism**, đặc biệt **Vickrey–Clarke–Groves mechanism**, có thể dùng **pivot payment** hoặc **Clarke tax**, nhưng **Myerson–Satterthwaite theorem** giới hạn điều có thể làm trong **double auction**.
