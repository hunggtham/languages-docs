# C1 Vocabulary 41 — Behavioral child development and learning choice

This lesson follows how children, families, and educators interpret autonomy, effort, fairness, identity, and learning opportunities.

Flow: **parental expectation effect → child autonomy cue → homework friction → screen-time default → play salience → developmental risk perception → teacher-parent trust → peer norm learning → feedback praise framing → growth attribution belief → school choice overload → learning transfer illusion → curiosity gap learning → classroom belonging cue → test score identity → discipline fairness perception → educational aspiration framing → sibling learning comparison → study habit inertia → child policy compliance**.

## 1. parental expectation effect /pəˈrɛntəl ˌɛkspɛkˈteɪʃən ɪˈfɛkt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a change in a child's behavior or performance caused by what adults expect and communicate.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** change behavior hoặc performance child do expectation adult communicate.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong parental expectation effect` — effect; `measure the parental expectation effect` — measure.
**Examples:** `Parental expectation effect encouraged the child to persist after adults praised careful effort rather than quick talent.` → Effect expectation parental khuyến khích child persist khi adult praise effort careful thay talent nhanh.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `부모 기대 효과`.

## 2. child autonomy cue /tʃaɪld ɔːˈtɑnəmi kjuː/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a signal that a child has meaningful choice, voice, and control within safe limits.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** signal child có choice, voice và control meaningful trong limit safe.
**Grammar & collocations:** `clear child autonomy cue` — cue; `provide a child autonomy cue` — provide.
**Examples:** `A child autonomy cue let the student choose which book to read while keeping the learning goal fixed.` → Cue autonomy child cho student choose book trong khi goal learning giữ fixed.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `아동 자율성 단서`.

## 3. homework friction /ˈhoʊmˌwɝk ˈfrɪkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** practical, emotional, or organizational difficulty that makes completing homework harder.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** difficulty practical, emotional hoặc organizational làm homework khó.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high homework friction` — friction; `reduce homework friction` — reduce.
**Examples:** `Homework friction increased when instructions required materials unavailable at home.` → Friction homework tăng khi instruction cần material không available ở nhà.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `숙제 마찰`.

## 4. screen-time default /skriːn taɪm dɪˈfɔlt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a preselected limit, schedule, or setting that shapes how much time a child spends with screens.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** limit, schedule hoặc setting preselect shape screen time child.
**Grammar & collocations:** `protective screen-time default` — default; `set a screen-time default` — set.
**Examples:** `A screen-time default paused entertainment apps during the child's planned sleep period.` → Default screen-time pause app entertainment trong sleep period plan.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `화면 시간 기본값`.

## 5. play salience /pleɪ ˈseɪliəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** how visible and important play feels when a child or adult chooses among activities.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mức play visible và important khi child hoặc adult choose activity.
**Grammar & collocations:** `low play salience` — salience; `increase play salience` — increase.
**Examples:** `Play salience rose when the schedule displayed outdoor activity beside academic tasks.` → Salience play tăng khi schedule display activity outdoor cạnh task academic.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `놀이 현저성`.

## 6. developmental risk perception /dɪˌvɛləpˈmɛntl rɪsk pərˈsɛpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a judgment about how likely a situation is to harm a child's learning, health, or social development.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** judgment situation harm learning, health hoặc development social child likely thế nào.
**Grammar & collocations:** `elevated developmental risk perception` — perception; `calibrate developmental risk perception` — calibrate.
**Examples:** `Developmental risk perception increased when the child lost sleep for several weeks.` → Perception risk developmental tăng khi child mất sleep nhiều tuần.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `발달 위험 인식`.

## 7. teacher-parent trust /ˈtiːtʃər ˈpɛrənt trʌst/

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `교사-부모 신뢰`.

## 8. peer norm learning /pɪr nɔrm ˈlɝnɪŋ/

## 10. growth attribution belief /ɡroʊθ ˌætrəˈbjuːʃən bɪˈliːf/

## 11. school choice overload /skuːl tʃɔɪs ˈoʊvərˌloʊd/

**Core meaning (English):** difficulty choosing a school caused by too many programs, rankings, requirements, and uncertain trade-offs.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** difficulty choose school vì quá nhiều program, ranking, requirement và trade-off uncertain.

## 12. learning transfer illusion /ˈlɝnɪŋ ˈtrænsfər ɪˈluːʒən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** believing that success on a practiced task automatically transfers to a different context.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** tin success task practiced tự động transfer sang context khác.
**Grammar & collocations:** `persistent learning transfer illusion` — illusion; `correct a learning transfer illusion` — correct.
**Examples:** `Learning transfer illusion disappeared when the learner had to use the formula in an unfamiliar problem.` → Illusion transfer learning biến khi learner use formula trong problem unfamiliar.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `학습 전이 착시`.

## 13. curiosity gap learning /ˌkjʊriˈɑsəti ɡæp ˈlɝnɪŋ/

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `학습 호기심 격차`.

## 20. child policy compliance /tʃaɪld ˈpɑləsi kəmˈplaɪəns/

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `아동 정책 준수`.

## Review in context

Children respond to the **parental expectation effect** and look for a **child autonomy cue** within clear limits. **Homework friction** and a poorly designed **screen-time default** can crowd out learning and play, while **play salience** protects development. **Developmental risk perception** guides adult decisions, and **teacher-parent trust** makes information usable. **Peer norm learning** shapes participation; **feedback praise framing** and a **growth attribution belief** determine how setbacks are interpreted. **School choice overload** can delay action, while a **learning transfer illusion** hides gaps between practice and real use. A **curiosity gap learning** keeps attention alive, and a **classroom belonging cue** invites contribution. **Test score identity** can turn one result into a judgment of worth; **discipline fairness perception** determines whether rules feel legitimate. **Educational aspiration framing**, **sibling learning comparison**, and **study habit inertia** shape long-term choices, while **child policy compliance** improves when rules have meaning.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

Child phản hồi **parental expectation effect** và tìm **child autonomy cue** trong limit rõ. **Homework friction** và **screen-time default** design kém có thể crowd learning và play, còn **play salience** protect development. **Developmental risk perception** guide decision adult, và **teacher-parent trust** làm information usable. **Peer norm learning** shape participation; **feedback praise framing** và **growth attribution belief** quyết định interpret setback. **School choice overload** delay action, còn **learning transfer illusion** che gap giữa practice và use thật. **Curiosity gap learning** giữ attention, và **classroom belonging cue** mời contribution. **Test score identity** biến result thành judgment worth; **discipline fairness perception** quyết định rule legitimate. **Educational aspiration framing**, **sibling learning comparison** và **study habit inertia** shape choice dài hạn, còn **child policy compliance** improve khi rule có meaning.
