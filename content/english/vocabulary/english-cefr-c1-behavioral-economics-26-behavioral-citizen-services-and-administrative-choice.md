# C1 Vocabulary 26 — Behavioral citizen services and administrative choice

This lesson follows how people navigate forms, queues, eligibility rules, and public services when effort, trust, and uncertainty shape take-up.

Flow: **service queue perception → administrative burden perception → form completion friction → eligibility stigma → trust in government service → street-level discretion belief → public complaint salience → benefit take-up gap → bureaucratic language cue → policy feedback expectation → citizen efficacy belief → service co-production → procedural transparency cue → queue abandonment → appointment scarcity perception → public consultation fatigue → compliance cost perception → administrative reciprocity → policy implementation confidence → civic service framing**.

## 1. service queue perception /ˈsɝvɪs kjuː pərˈsɛpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a person's judgment about how long, fair, and worthwhile waiting for a public service will be.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** judgment wait public service long, fair và worthwhile thế nào.
**Grammar & collocations:** `negative service queue perception` — perception; `improve service queue perception` — improve.
**Examples:** `Service queue perception worsened when people could not see how many applications were ahead of them.` → Perception queue service tệ hơn khi người ta không thấy bao nhiêu application phía trước.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `서비스 대기열 인식`.

## 2. administrative burden perception /ədˈmɪnəˌstreɪtɪv ˈbɝdən pərˈsɛpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a judgment about the learning, compliance, and psychological effort required to obtain or keep a public benefit.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** judgment effort learning, compliance và psychological để obtain hoặc keep benefit public.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high administrative burden perception` — perception; `reduce administrative burden perception` — reduce.
**Examples:** `Administrative burden perception discouraged eligible families from applying for support.` → Perception burden administrative khiến family eligible ngại apply support.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `행정 부담 인식`.

## 3. form completion friction /fɔrm kəmˈpliːʃən ˈfrɪkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** extra effort, confusion, or interruption that makes completing an official form difficult.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** effort, confusion hoặc interruption thêm làm complete official form khó.
**Grammar & collocations:** `avoidable form completion friction` — friction; `remove form completion friction` — remove.
**Examples:** `Form completion friction increased when the portal timed out before users could save their answers.` → Friction completion form tăng khi portal timeout trước khi user save answer.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `양식 작성 마찰`.

## 4. eligibility stigma /ˌɛlədʒəˈbɪləti ˈstɪɡmə/

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `자격 낙인`.

## 5. trust in government service /trʌst ɪn ˈɡʌvərnmənt ˈsɝvɪs/

## 6. street-level discretion belief /striːt ˈlɛvəl dɪˈskrɛʃən bɪˈliːf/

**Core meaning (English):** how noticeable and politically important a citizen complaint becomes to an institution or community.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mức complaint citizen noticeable và politically important với institution hoặc community.

## 8. benefit take-up gap /ˈbɛnəfɪt teɪk ʌp ɡæp/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the difference between the people eligible for a benefit and the smaller group that actually receives it.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** difference giữa người eligible và group nhỏ hơn thật sự receive benefit.
**Grammar & collocations:** `large benefit take-up gap` — gap; `close the benefit take-up gap` — close.
**Examples:** `The benefit take-up gap persisted because eligible workers did not understand the renewal rule.` → Gap take-up benefit còn vì worker eligible không hiểu renewal rule.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `복지 수혜 격차`.

## Review in context

People decide whether a public service is worth pursuing through **service queue perception** and **administrative burden perception**. **Form completion friction** and **eligibility stigma** can widen the gap even when **trust in government service** is reasonable. A **street-level discretion belief** shapes whether applicants ask questions, while **public complaint salience** determines which problems receive attention. The **benefit take-up gap** persists when a **bureaucratic language cue** makes a simple rule seem inaccessible. **Policy feedback expectation** and **citizen efficacy belief** influence whether people return to participate, and **service co-production** can turn users into practical partners. A **procedural transparency cue** reduces suspicion, but **queue abandonment** rises with high **appointment scarcity perception**. Repeated requests without visible change create **public consultation fatigue**; high **compliance cost perception** further weakens cooperation. **Administrative reciprocity** and **policy implementation confidence** rebuild trust, while **civic service framing** presents public administration as a shared relationship rather than a one-way demand.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

Người ta quyết định public service có đáng pursue không qua **service queue perception** và **administrative burden perception**. **Form completion friction** và **eligibility stigma** mở rộng gap dù **trust in government service** reasonable. **Street-level discretion belief** shape applicant có hỏi không, còn **public complaint salience** quyết định problem nào được attention. **Benefit take-up gap** tồn tại khi **bureaucratic language cue** làm rule đơn giản trông inaccessible. **Policy feedback expectation** và **citizen efficacy belief** influence việc người ta return participate, còn **service co-production** biến user thành partner thực tế. **Procedural transparency cue** giảm suspicion, nhưng **queue abandonment** tăng với **appointment scarcity perception** cao. Request lặp lại không có change visible tạo **public consultation fatigue**; **compliance cost perception** cao làm cooperation yếu. **Administrative reciprocity** và **policy implementation confidence** rebuild trust, còn **civic service framing** present administration như relationship shared thay vì demand một chiều.
