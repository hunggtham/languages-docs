# C2 Vocabulary — Mining robotics and geological survey

This lesson follows autonomous machines from extraction and drilling to geological measurement and mine-safety monitoring. The network is **mining robot → autonomous haul truck → drilling robot → underground mining robot → tunnel boring robot → geological survey robot → ore grade estimation → ore sorting robot → rock fragmentation → blast-hole drilling → mine ventilation monitoring → gas monitoring robot → dust monitoring robot → slope stability monitoring → rockfall detection → subsurface mapping → ground penetrating radar robot → core logging robot → mineral spectroscopy → hyperspectral geology**.

## 1. mining robot /ˈmaɪnɪŋ ˈroʊbɑːt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a robot that extracts material, inspects a mine, transports loads, or monitors underground conditions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** robot extract material, inspect mine, transport load hoặc monitor condition underground.
**Grammar & collocations:** `mining-robot platform` — platform; `operate in a hazardous heading` — hoạt động trong heading nguy hiểm.
**Examples:** `The mining robot inspected the abandoned heading before workers entered.` → Mining robot kiểm tra heading bỏ hoang trước khi worker vào.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `채굴 로봇`.

## 2. autonomous haul truck /ɔːˈtɑːnəməs hɔːl trʌk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a self-driving truck that carries mined material between loading and dumping locations.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** truck tự lái chở mined material giữa loading và dumping location.
**Grammar & collocations:** `autonomous-haul-truck route` — route; `carry a full load` — chở full load.
**Examples:** `The autonomous haul truck slowed before the blind bend on the haul road.` → Autonomous haul truck giảm tốc trước khúc cua khuất trên haul road.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `자율 운반 트럭`.

## 3. drilling robot /ˈdrɪlɪŋ ˈroʊbɑːt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a robot that drills holes for exploration, blasting, anchoring, or sampling.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** robot khoan lỗ để exploration, blasting, anchoring hoặc sampling.
**Grammar & collocations:** `drilling-robot mast` — mast; `align a borehole` — căn borehole.
**Examples:** `The drilling robot aligned the borehole with the planned mineral section.` → Drilling robot căn borehole với mineral section plan.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `시추 로봇`.

## 4. underground mining robot /ˌʌndərˈɡraʊnd ˈmaɪnɪŋ ˈroʊbɑːt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a robot designed to work below ground in tunnels, stopes, or mine chambers.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** robot làm việc dưới đất trong tunnel, stope hoặc mine chamber.
**Grammar & collocations:** `underground-mining-robot chassis` — chassis; `navigate a narrow drift` — đi qua drift hẹp.
**Examples:** `The underground mining robot mapped the drift after the blast fumes cleared.` → Underground mining robot map drift sau khi blast fumes tan.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `지하 채굴 로봇`.

## 5. tunnel boring robot /ˈtʌnəl ˈbɔːrɪŋ ˈroʊbɑːt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a robot or automated machine that excavates and advances a tunnel through rock or soil.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** robot/machine tự động excavate và advance tunnel qua rock/soil.
**Grammar & collocations:** `tunnel-boring-robot cutterhead` — cutterhead; `advance the excavation face` — đẩy excavation face.
**Examples:** `The tunnel boring robot adjusted its cutter speed when the rock layer hardened.` → Tunnel boring robot chỉnh cutter speed khi rock layer cứng hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `터널 굴착 로봇`.

## 6. geological survey robot /ˌdʒiːəˈlɑːdʒɪkəl ˈsɜːrveɪ ˈroʊbɑːt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a robot that collects spatial, chemical, or physical observations about geological formations.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** robot thu observation spatial, chemical hoặc physical về geological formation.
**Grammar & collocations:** `geological-survey-robot route` — route; `sample a mineral outcrop` — sample mineral outcrop.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `지질 조사 로봇`.

## 7. ore grade estimation /ɔːr ɡreɪd ˌestəˈmeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** estimating the concentration of a valuable material in mined rock.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** estimate concentration vật liệu giá trị trong mined rock.
**Grammar & collocations:** `ore-grade-estimation model` — model; `rank a high-grade section` — xếp hạng section grade cao.
**Examples:** `Ore grade estimation separated the promising core from ordinary host rock.` → Ore grade estimation tách core tiềm năng khỏi host rock thường.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `광석 품위 추정`.

## 8. ore sorting robot /ɔːr ˈsɔːrtɪŋ ˈroʊbɑːt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a robot that separates mined material according to composition, grade, size, or value.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** robot tách mined material theo composition, grade, size hoặc value.
**Grammar & collocations:** `ore-sorting-robot gripper` — gripper; `reject low-grade fragments` — reject fragment grade thấp.
**Examples:** `The ore sorting robot diverted high-grade rocks to the processing stream.` → Ore sorting robot chuyển rock grade cao vào processing stream.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `광석 선별 로봇`.

## 9. rock fragmentation /rɑːk ˌfræɡmənˈteɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** breaking rock into pieces through blasting, cutting, crushing, or natural processes.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** phá rock thành mảnh bằng blasting, cutting, crushing hoặc process tự nhiên.
**Grammar & collocations:** `rock-fragmentation pattern` — pattern; `control fragment size` — control fragment size.
**Examples:** `Rock fragmentation produced pieces small enough for the conveyor to carry.` → Rock fragmentation tạo mảnh đủ nhỏ để conveyor chở.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `암석 파쇄`.

## 10. blast-hole drilling /blæst hoʊl ˈdrɪlɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** drilling holes that will receive explosives for controlled rock breaking.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** khoan lỗ để đặt explosive phá rock có kiểm soát.
**Grammar & collocations:** `blast-hole-drilling pattern` — pattern; `position a borehole accurately` — đặt borehole chính xác.
**Examples:** `Blast-hole drilling followed the designed spacing to control the break line.` → Blast-hole drilling theo spacing design để control break line.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `발파공 천공`.

## 11. mine ventilation monitoring /maɪn ˌventəˈleɪʃən ˈmɑːnətərɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** measuring airflow, gases, temperature, and pressure to keep mine air safe.

**Grammar & collocations:** `mine-ventilation-monitoring sensor` — sensor; `track airflow direction` — theo dõi hướng airflow.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `광산 환기 모니터링`.

## 14. slope stability monitoring /sloʊp stəˈbɪləti ˈmɑːnətərɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** observing movement, cracks, water, and stress to assess whether a slope may fail.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** quan sát movement, crack, water và stress để đánh giá slope có thể fail.
**Grammar & collocations:** `slope-stability-monitoring station` — station; `track wall displacement` — theo dõi wall displacement.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `사면 안정성 모니터링`.

## 15. rockfall detection /ˈrɑːkfɔːl dɪˈtekʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** detecting falling or recently displaced rock that threatens people, vehicles, or infrastructure.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** detect rock rơi hoặc displacement mới đe dọa người, vehicle hoặc infrastructure.
**Grammar & collocations:** `rockfall-detection alert` — alert; `close a haul road` — đóng haul road.

## 16. subsurface mapping /ˈsʌbsɜːrfɪs ˈmæpɪŋ/

## 19. mineral spectroscopy /ˈmɪnərəl spɛkˈtrɑːskəpi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** identifying or estimating minerals from how material interacts with measured wavelengths of light.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** identify/estimate mineral từ cách material tương tác với wavelength light đo.
**Grammar & collocations:** `mineral-spectroscopy signature` — signature; `classify an ore surface` — phân loại ore surface.
**Examples:** `Mineral spectroscopy distinguished the altered rock from the surrounding basalt.` → Mineral spectroscopy phân biệt rock altered với basalt xung quanh.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `광물 분광학`.

## 20. hyperspectral geology /ˌhaɪpərˈspektrəl dʒiˈɑːlədʒi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** geological analysis using many narrow wavelength bands to identify materials and alteration.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** geological analysis dùng nhiều wavelength band hẹp để identify material và alteration.
**Grammar & collocations:** `hyperspectral-geology map` — map; `detect a mineral alteration zone` — detect alteration zone.
**Examples:** `Hyperspectral geology mapped an alteration zone that was invisible in ordinary color imagery.` → Hyperspectral geology map alteration zone không thấy trong color image thường.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `초분광 지질학`.

## Review in context

A **mining robot**, **autonomous haul truck**, **drilling robot**, **underground mining robot**, or **tunnel boring robot** performs extraction and access work. A **geological survey robot** supports **ore grade estimation**, an **ore sorting robot**, **rock fragmentation**, and **blast-hole drilling**. **Mine ventilation monitoring**, a **gas monitoring robot**, and a **dust monitoring robot** protect air quality; **slope stability monitoring** and **rockfall detection** protect terrain. **Subsurface mapping**, a **ground penetrating radar robot**, and a **core logging robot** reveal hidden structure, while **mineral spectroscopy** and **hyperspectral geology** identify material composition.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:** **Mining robot**, **autonomous haul truck**, **drilling robot**, **underground mining robot** hoặc **tunnel boring robot** thực hiện extraction và access work. **Geological survey robot** hỗ trợ **ore grade estimation**, **ore sorting robot**, **rock fragmentation** và **blast-hole drilling**. **Mine ventilation monitoring**, **gas monitoring robot** và **dust monitoring robot** bảo vệ air quality; **slope stability monitoring** và **rockfall detection** bảo vệ terrain. **Subsurface mapping**, **ground penetrating radar robot** và **core logging robot** làm lộ structure ẩn, còn **mineral spectroscopy** và **hyperspectral geology** xác định composition material.
