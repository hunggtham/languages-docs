# C2+ Vocabulary — Health security and pandemic preparedness

This lesson follows how institutions prepare for large outbreaks, allocate scarce countermeasures, and test whether plans work under pressure. The network is **public health emergency of international concern → pandemic preparedness → epidemic intelligence → strategic stockpile → countermeasure allocation → medical countermeasure → surge staffing → interjurisdictional support compact → continuity of operations → border health measure → travel health notice → point of entry screening → quarantine capacity → isolation capacity → emergency use authorization → vaccine platform → cold-chain resilience → scenario exercise → facilitated simulation drill → trigger-action framework → risk appetite statement → preparedness indicator → after-action improvement plan → health security governance → pandemic supply chain**.

## 1. public health emergency of international concern /ˈpʌblɪk hɛlθ ɪˈmɜrdʒənsi əv ˌɪntərˈnæʃənəl kənˈsɜrn/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a formal international alert for an extraordinary health event that may require coordinated action across countries.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** public health emergency of international concern là một status chính thức để gọi attention và coordination quốc tế.
**Grammar & collocations:** `declare a public health emergency of international concern` — declare; `international alert` — alert; `extraordinary event` — event.
**Examples:** `The declaration of a public health emergency of international concern accelerated information sharing among countries.` → Status chung giúp phối hợp nhanh hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `국제적 공중보건 비상사태` — public health emergency of international concern.

## 2. pandemic preparedness /pænˈdɛmɪk prɪˈpɛrdnəs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the capabilities, plans, relationships, and resources maintained before a pandemic occurs.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** pandemic preparedness là năng lực chuẩn bị trước, không phải chỉ viết một response plan.
**Grammar & collocations:** `pandemic-preparedness plan` — plan; `invest in preparedness` — invest; `preparedness capability` — capability.
**Examples:** `Pandemic preparedness included laboratory networks, trained staff, procurement rules, and public communication.` → Preparedness là một system.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `팬데믹 대비` — pandemic preparedness.

## 3. epidemic intelligence /ˌɛpɪˈdɛmɪk ɪnˈtɛlədʒəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the systematic collection, interpretation, and sharing of signals about current or emerging health threats.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** epidemic intelligence biến nhiều signal rời thành risk picture có thể hành động.
**Grammar & collocations:** `epidemic-intelligence unit` — unit; `gather intelligence` — gather; `threat signal` — signal.
**Examples:** `Epidemic intelligence combined clinical reports, laboratory data, and local media monitoring.` → Intelligence ghép nhiều source.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `유행 정보 분석` — epidemic intelligence.

## 4. strategic stockpile /strəˈtiːdʒɪk ˈstɑkˌpaɪl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a deliberately maintained reserve of medicines, equipment, supplies, or protective materials for emergencies.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** strategic stockpile là reserve được mua, bảo quản và xoay vòng trước crisis.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strategic-stockpile inventory` — inventory; `maintain a stockpile` — maintain; `release emergency supplies` — release.
**Examples:** `The strategic stockpile released respirators while new manufacturing capacity came online.` → Stockpile mua thời gian cho supply chain.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `전략 비축물자` — strategic stockpile.

## 5. countermeasure allocation /ˈkaʊntərˌmɛʒər ˌæləˈkeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the process of deciding where scarce protective or therapeutic resources should be sent and in what quantities.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** countermeasure allocation biến resource ít thành decision có tiêu chí về need và impact.
**Grammar & collocations:** `countermeasure-allocation rule` — rule; `allocate scarce doses` — allocate; `priority population` — population.
**Examples:** `Countermeasure allocation prioritized hospitals facing the fastest rise in severe cases.` → Allocation phải dựa trên risk và need.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `대응수단 배분` — countermeasure allocation.

## 6. medical countermeasure /ˈmɛdɪkəl ˈkaʊntərˌmɛʒər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a medical product or intervention used to prevent, diagnose, treat, or reduce harm from a health threat.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** medical countermeasure có thể là vaccine, therapeutic, diagnostic hoặc protective product.
**Grammar & collocations:** `medical-countermeasure portfolio` — portfolio; `deploy a countermeasure` — deploy; `threat-specific product` — product.
**Examples:** `The agency evaluated the diagnostic as a medical countermeasure for early case detection.` → Countermeasure không chỉ là drug.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `의료 대응수단` — medical countermeasure.

## 7. surge staffing /sɜrdʒ ˈstæfɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** rapid expansion or redeployment of personnel to meet exceptional service demand.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** surge staffing đưa thêm người đúng skill vào nơi workload tăng đột ngột.
**Grammar & collocations:** `surge-staffing roster` — roster; `activate surge staffing` — activate; `redeploy personnel` — redeploy.
**Examples:** `Surge staffing covered contact calls without closing routine vaccination services.` → Surge cần tránh làm sập service khác.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `비상 인력 증원` — surge staffing.

## 8. interjurisdictional support compact /ˌɪntərˌdʒʊrɪsˈdɪkʃənəl səˈpɔrt ˈkɑmpækt/

**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** interjurisdictional support compact biến “giúp nhau khi crisis” thành commitment có rule.
**Grammar & collocations:** `interjurisdictional-support compact` — compact; `activate mutual support` — activate; `request personnel` — request.
**Examples:** `The interjurisdictional support compact enabled neighboring states to share epidemiologists.` → Agreement trước giúp mobilize nhanh.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `관할 간 지원 협약` — interjurisdictional support compact.

## 9. continuity of operations /ˌkɑntəˈnuəti əv ˌɑpəˈreɪʃənz/

**Core meaning (English):** the capability to keep essential functions running during disruption, staffing loss, or infrastructure failure.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** continuity of operations giữ service critical không dừng khi crisis xảy ra.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `업무 연속성` — continuity of operations.

## 10. border health measure /ˈbɔrdər hɛlθ ˈmɛʒər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a health-related action applied at or across a border to reduce importation or spread of a threat.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** border health measure can thiệp ở điểm người, hàng hóa hoặc vehicle đi qua border.

## 13. quarantine capacity /ˈkwɔrəntiːn kəˈpæsəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the space, staffing, logistics, and support available to separate exposed people who may become infectious.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** quarantine capacity là khả năng thực tế để người exposed ở riêng an toàn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `quarantine-capacity plan` — plan; `expand quarantine capacity` — expand; `support exposed travelers` — support.
**Examples:** `Quarantine capacity included accommodation, food delivery, mental-health support, and monitoring.` → Capacity không chỉ là số phòng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `격리 수용 능력` — quarantine capacity.

## 14. isolation capacity /ˌaɪsəˈleɪʃən kəˈpæsəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the space, equipment, staffing, and procedures available to separate people who are infectious.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** isolation capacity dành cho người infectious, khác với quarantine của người exposed.
**Grammar & collocations:** `isolation-capacity assessment` — assessment; `expand isolation beds` — expand; `infection-control room` — room.
**Examples:** `Isolation capacity was increased by converting a ward and training additional staff.` → Capacity cần cả space và people.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `격리 병상 수용 능력` — isolation capacity.

## 17. cold-chain resilience /koʊld tʃeɪn rɪˈzɪliəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the ability of temperature-controlled storage and transport systems to continue functioning during disruption.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cold-chain resilience giữ product còn usable dù power, transport hoặc demand bị shock.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `콜드체인 회복탄력성` — cold-chain resilience.

## 18. scenario exercise /sɪˈnɛrioʊ ˈɛksərˌsaɪz/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a structured activity that explores how people, systems, and decisions would respond to a plausible future event.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** scenario exercise thử “nếu xảy ra thì sao?” trước khi crisis thật đến.
**Grammar & collocations:** `scenario-exercise design` — design; `run a scenario exercise` — run; `plausible threat` — threat.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `시나리오 훈련` — scenario exercise.

## 20. trigger-action framework /ˈtrɪɡər ˈækʃən ˈfreɪmˌwɜrk/

## 21. risk appetite statement /rɪsk ˈæpəˌtaɪt ˈsteɪtmənt/

**Core meaning (English):** an explicit statement of how much uncertainty, exposure, or operational risk an organization is willing to accept.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** risk appetite statement nói rõ organization chấp nhận risk nào và không chấp nhận risk nào.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `위험 선호 성명` — risk appetite statement.

## 22. preparedness indicator /prɪˈpɛrdnəs ˈɪndəˌkeɪtər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a measurable sign of whether a system has the capability and resources needed for a future emergency.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** preparedness indicator đo capability trước crisis, không chờ đến lúc response fail.
**Grammar & collocations:** `preparedness-indicator dashboard` — dashboard; `track readiness` — track; `capability measure` — measure.
**Examples:** `A preparedness indicator tracked the percentage of laboratories that could report within one day.` → Indicator phải gắn capability thật.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `대비 지표` — preparedness indicator.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `사후 개선 계획` — after-action improvement plan.

## 24. health security governance /hɛlθ sɪˈkjʊrəti ˈɡʌvərnəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the institutions, rules, accountability, and coordination arrangements used to manage population health threats.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** health security governance quyết định ai có authority, resource và accountability trong threat response.
**Grammar & collocations:** `health-security governance` — governance; `govern a preparedness system` — govern; `accountability arrangement` — arrangement.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `보건 안보 거버넌스` — health security governance.

## 25. pandemic supply chain /pænˈdɛmɪk səˈplaɪ tʃeɪn/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the interconnected system that produces, procures, transports, and distributes goods needed during a pandemic.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** pandemic supply chain nối raw material, factory, logistics và frontline demand.
**Grammar & collocations:** `pandemic-supply-chain resilience` — resilience; `secure critical inputs` — secure; `distribution bottleneck` — bottleneck.
**Examples:** `The pandemic supply chain failed when one specialized filter became unavailable worldwide.` → Một bottleneck nhỏ có thể ảnh hưởng toàn system.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `팬데믹 공급망` — pandemic supply chain.

## Review in context — Part 1

An **emergency use authorization** may accelerate a **vaccine platform**, but monitoring continues. **Cold-chain resilience** protects the product in transit. A **scenario exercise** and **facilitated simulation drill** test the **trigger-action framework** before a real event. A **risk appetite statement** guides difficult choices, while a **preparedness indicator** shows whether capability is improving. The **after-action improvement plan**, **health security governance**, and **pandemic supply chain** must turn each exercise into durable capacity.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 2:**
