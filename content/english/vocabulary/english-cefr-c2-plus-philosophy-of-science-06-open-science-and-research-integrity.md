# C2+ Vocabulary — Open science and research integrity

This lesson follows how research communities make evidence auditable, reproducible, and ethically accountable. The network is **preregistration protocol → registered-report format → exploratory analysis → confirmatory analysis → file drawer problem → direct replication → conceptual replication → reproducibility standard → provenance record → code availability → methodological transparency → analytical latitude → researcher choice latitude → outcome switching → citation bias → retraction notice → correction of record → peer-review cartel → editorial screening → competing-interest statement → open materials → data sharing agreement → fabrication allegation → questionable research practice → replication portfolio**.

## 1. preregistration protocol /priːˌrɛdʒɪˈstreɪʃən ˈproʊtəˌkɔl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a time-stamped public plan specifying a study's questions, design, outcomes, and analysis before data collection or outcome inspection.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** preregistration protocol ghi plan trước khi thấy data, giúp phân biệt confirmatory với post-hoc.
**Grammar & collocations:** `preregister a protocol` — preregister; `time-stamped protocol` — protocol; `deviate from the plan` — deviate.
**Examples:** `The team uploaded a preregistration protocol before recruiting participants.` → Team upload preregistration protocol trước khi recruit participant.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `사전등록 프로토콜` — preregistration protocol.

## 2. registered-report format /ˈrɛdʒɪstərd rɪˈpɔrt ˈfɔrmæt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a publication format in which a study is peer-reviewed and provisionally accepted before its results are known.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** registered-report format review design trước result, giảm incentive chỉ publish positive finding.
**Grammar & collocations:** `registered-report submission` — submission; `accept a protocol in principle` — accept; `results-blind review` — review.
**Examples:** `The journal accepted the registered-report format after reviewing the methods rather than the expected effect.` → Journal accept format sau khi review method, không phải expected effect.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `등록 보고서 형식` — registered-report format.

## 3. exploratory analysis /ɪkˈsplɔrəˌtɔri əˈnæləsɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** analysis used to discover patterns or generate hypotheses without treating the findings as pre-specified tests.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** exploratory analysis dùng để tìm pattern và tạo hypothesis; không nên trình bày như confirmatory test đã plan.
**Grammar & collocations:** `exploratory-analysis phase` — phase; `explore a pattern` — explore; `generate a hypothesis` — generate.
**Examples:** `The exploratory analysis suggested a subgroup effect that required a new confirmatory study.` → Exploratory analysis gợi ý subgroup effect cần confirmatory study mới.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `탐색적 분석` — exploratory analysis.

## 4. confirmatory analysis /kənˈfɜrmətɔri əˈnæləsɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** analysis designed to test a hypothesis or outcome specified before the relevant data were examined.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** confirmatory analysis test hypothesis đã định trước, với decision rule rõ.
**Grammar & collocations:** `confirmatory-analysis plan` — plan; `test a preregistered hypothesis` — test; `specified outcome` — outcome.
**Examples:** `The confirmatory analysis used the outcome and threshold stated in the protocol.` → Confirmatory analysis dùng outcome và threshold ghi trong protocol.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `확증적 분석` — confirmatory analysis.

## 5. file drawer problem /faɪl drɔr ˈprɑbləm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the hidden accumulation of completed studies that remain unpublished because their results are null, negative, or inconvenient.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** study null bị cất trong “drawer”, khiến literature trông positive hơn reality.
**Grammar & collocations:** `file-drawer problem in publication` — problem; `leave a study unpublished` — leave; `missing null result` — result.
**Examples:** `The review corrected for the file drawer problem by searching trial registries.` → Review sửa file drawer problem bằng cách search trial registry.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `파일 서랍 문제` — file drawer problem.

## 6. direct replication /dəˈrɛkt ˌrɛplɪˈkeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a repeated study that follows the original procedures as closely as practical to test whether the result recurs.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** direct replication cố giữ procedure, sample logic và measurement giống original để xem result có lặp.
**Grammar & collocations:** `direct-replication study` — study; `replicate a procedure` — replicate; `repeat the original effect` — repeat.
**Examples:** `The direct replication used the same stimulus timing but a larger sample.` → Direct replication dùng cùng stimulus timing nhưng sample lớn hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `직접 반복 연구` — direct replication.

## 7. conceptual replication /kənˈsɛptʃuəl ˌrɛplɪˈkeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a repeated test of the same theoretical claim using different materials, measures, or procedures.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** conceptual replication giữ claim nhưng đổi method/material để test generality chứ không chỉ lặp máy móc.
**Grammar & collocations:** `conceptual-replication design` — design; `test the same construct` — test; `vary the operationalization` — vary.
**Examples:** `A conceptual replication tested the cooperation claim in an online marketplace.` → Conceptual replication test cooperation claim trong online marketplace.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `개념적 반복 연구` — conceptual replication.

## 8. reproducibility standard /rɪˌproʊdjuːsəˈbɪləti ˈstændərd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a stated requirement for rerunning an analysis or procedure and obtaining the same or appropriately comparable result.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** reproducibility standard nói rõ người khác cần code, data, environment gì để rerun.
**Grammar & collocations:** `reproducibility-standard checklist` — checklist; `meet a standard` — meet; `rerun an analysis` — rerun.
**Examples:** `The repository adopted a reproducibility standard requiring versioned code and documented dependencies.` → Repository adopt standard cần code versioned và dependency document.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `재현성 기준` — reproducibility standard.

## 9. provenance record /ˈprɑvənəns ˈrɛkərd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a traceable record of where data, materials, transformations, and decisions came from.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** provenance record là “lý lịch” của data từ source đến final analysis.
**Grammar & collocations:** `data-provenance record` — record; `trace provenance` — trace; `document a transformation` — document.
**Examples:** `The provenance record showed when each sensor stream was cleaned and merged.` → Provenance record cho thấy từng sensor stream được clean và merge khi nào.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `출처 기록` — provenance record.

## 10. code availability /koʊd əˌveɪləˈbɪləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the accessibility of software scripts needed to inspect, reproduce, or extend an analysis.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** code availability cho phép người khác inspect và rerun analysis, nhưng cần version và documentation.
**Grammar & collocations:** `code-availability statement` — statement; `make code available` — make; `archived source code` — code.
**Examples:** `Code availability allowed the reviewer to reproduce the reported confidence interval.` → Code availability cho reviewer reproduce confidence interval.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `코드 이용 가능성` — code availability.

## 11. methodological transparency /ˌmɛθədəˈlɑdʒɪkəl trænsˈpɛrənsi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** clear disclosure of study procedures, decisions, exclusions, limitations, and analytic choices.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** methodological transparency làm lộ procedure, exclusion, limit và decision để người đọc đánh giá.
**Grammar & collocations:** `methodological-transparency policy` — policy; `disclose a decision` — disclose; `transparent reporting` — reporting.
**Examples:** `Methodological transparency made the sample exclusions visible instead of burying them in a supplement.` → Methodological transparency làm sample exclusion visible.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `방법론적 투명성` — methodological transparency.

## 12. analytical latitude /ˌænəˈlɪtɪkəl ˈlætəˌtuːd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the range of reasonable analytic choices available to a researcher when the protocol does not fix them in advance.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** analytical latitude là khoảng lựa chọn hợp lý; nếu không disclose, nó có thể tạo researcher degrees of freedom.
**Grammar & collocations:** `analytical-latitude decision` — decision; `exercise latitude` — exercise; `pre-specify choices` — pre-specify.
**Examples:** `The authors reported every major analytical latitude they used during model selection.` → Authors report mọi analytical latitude chính khi chọn model.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `분석적 재량` — analytical latitude.

## 13. researcher choice latitude /rɪˈsɜrtʃər tʃɔɪs ˈlætəˌtuːd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the number and range of discretionary decisions researchers can make about measures, samples, models, and reporting.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** researcher choice latitude là “nhiều ngã rẽ” trong workflow, có thể vô tình favor result.
**Grammar & collocations:** `researcher-choice latitude` — latitude; `reduce discretionary choices` — reduce; `choice-sensitive result` — result.
**Examples:** `A registered report narrowed researcher choice latitude before the outcome was known.` → Registered report thu hẹp researcher choice latitude trước khi biết outcome.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `연구자 선택 재량` — researcher choice latitude.

## 14. outcome switching /ˈaʊtkʌm ˈswɪtʃɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** changing the designated primary outcome or its status after seeing results, without a transparent justification.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** outcome switching đổi primary outcome sau khi thấy result, làm claim dễ bị post-hoc.
**Grammar & collocations:** `outcome-switching risk` — risk; `switch a primary outcome` — switch; `document a justified change` — document.
**Examples:** `The audit detected outcome switching between the protocol and the published article.` → Audit phát hiện outcome switching giữa protocol và article.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `결과 변경` — outcome switching.

## 15. citation bias /saɪˈteɪʃən baɪəs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a systematic tendency for some findings, authors, or journals to receive more citations for reasons unrelated to evidential quality.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** citation bias làm literature visibility lệch vì novelty, prestige hoặc positive result chứ không chỉ quality.
**Grammar & collocations:** `citation-bias analysis` — analysis; `cite positive findings` — cite; `visibility advantage` — advantage.
**Examples:** `Citation bias made the dramatic but fragile result appear more established than it was.` → Citation bias làm result dramatic nhưng fragile trông established hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `인용 편향` — citation bias.

## 16. retraction notice /rɪˈtrækʃən ˈnoʊtɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a formal public statement that a published work should no longer be treated as part of the reliable scholarly record.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** retraction notice sửa status của paper trong scholarly record, không xóa lịch sử paper.
**Grammar & collocations:** `issue a retraction notice` — issue; `retracted article` — article; `integrity concern` — concern.
**Examples:** `The publisher released a retraction notice after confirming fabricated images.` → Publisher phát hành retraction notice sau khi confirm fabricated image.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `철회 공지` — retraction notice.

## 17. correction of record /kəˈrɛkʃən əv ˈrɛkərd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a formal amendment that fixes an error while preserving the publication and its traceable history.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** correction of record sửa lỗi minh bạch mà không pretend bản cũ chưa từng tồn tại.
**Grammar & collocations:** `publish a correction of record` — publish; `correct the scholarly record` — correct; `minor factual error` — error.
**Examples:** `A correction of record updated the mislabeled unit without withdrawing the study.` → Correction of record sửa unit mislabeled mà không withdraw study.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `기록 정정` — correction of record.

## 18. peer-review cartel /pɪr rɪˈvjuː kɑrˈtɛl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a coordinated group that manipulates peer review, citations, or editorial decisions for collective advantage.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** peer-review cartel biến review/citation thành coordinated exchange thay vì independent scrutiny.
**Grammar & collocations:** `peer-review-cartel investigation` — investigation; `manipulate review` — manipulate; `coordinated citation` — citation.
**Examples:** `The journal suspended several reviewers during a peer-review cartel investigation.` → Journal suspend reviewer trong peer-review cartel investigation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `동료 심사 카르텔` — peer-review cartel.

## 19. editorial screening /ˌɛdəˈtɔriəl ˈskriːnɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** preliminary editorial checking for scope, basic validity, ethical compliance, and serious reporting defects before full peer review.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** editorial screening là cửa đầu để lọc scope, ethics và defect lớn trước peer review.
**Grammar & collocations:** `editorial-screening criteria` — criteria; `screen a submission` — screen; `desk rejection` — rejection.
**Examples:** `Editorial screening caught the missing consent statement before external review.` → Editorial screening phát hiện thiếu consent statement trước external review.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `편집 심사` — editorial screening.

## 20. competing-interest statement /kəmˈpiːtɪŋ ˈɪntrəst ˈsteɪtmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a disclosure describing financial, professional, personal, or institutional interests that could affect judgment.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** competing-interest statement cho reader biết incentive hoặc relation nào có thể influence judgment.
**Grammar & collocations:** `competing-interest statement in a paper` — statement; `disclose an interest` — disclose; `financial tie` — tie.
**Examples:** `The competing-interest statement disclosed the author's consulting relationship with the device maker.` → Statement disclose consulting relationship với device maker.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `이해 상충 진술` — competing-interest statement.

## 21. open materials /ˈoʊpən məˈtɪriəlz/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** publicly accessible instruments, stimuli, protocols, templates, or other study materials needed to inspect or repeat research.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** open materials cho người khác xem và dùng lại questionnaire, stimulus, protocol hoặc template.
**Grammar & collocations:** `open-materials repository` — repository; `share study materials` — share; `reusable instrument` — instrument.
**Examples:** `Open materials let a second team reproduce the interview prompts exactly.` → Open materials giúp team thứ hai reproduce interview prompt chính xác.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `공개 연구 자료` — open materials.

## 22. data sharing agreement /ˈdeɪtə ˈʃɛrɪŋ əˈɡriːmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a formal arrangement specifying how data may be accessed, protected, reused, and attributed.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** data sharing agreement cân bằng reuse với privacy, security, consent và attribution.
**Grammar & collocations:** `data-sharing agreement` — agreement; `govern data access` — govern; `controlled-access dataset` — dataset.
**Examples:** `The data sharing agreement allowed qualified researchers to use the de-identified records.` → Agreement cho qualified researcher dùng de-identified record.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `데이터 공유 협약` — data sharing agreement.

## 23. fabrication allegation /ˌfæbrɪˈkeɪʃən ˌæləˈɡeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a formal claim that research data, observations, or materials were invented rather than recorded from actual work.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** fabrication allegation cáo buộc data/observation được bịa ra, cần inquiry công bằng và evidence.
**Grammar & collocations:** `fabrication-allegation inquiry` — inquiry; `allege fabricated data` — allege; `investigative finding` — finding.
**Examples:** `The university opened an inquiry after a fabrication allegation about the clinical dataset.` → University mở inquiry sau fabrication allegation về clinical dataset.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `날조 혐의` — fabrication allegation.

## 24. questionable research practice /ˈkwɛstʃənəbəl rɪˈsɜrtʃ ˈpræktɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a research behavior that may distort evidence or reporting without necessarily meeting the formal threshold for misconduct.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** questionable research practice nằm vùng xám: không luôn là fraud nhưng làm evidence kém đáng tin.
**Grammar & collocations:** `questionable-research-practice audit` — audit; `selectively report` — report; `distort evidence` — distort.
**Examples:** `Selective exclusion was classified as a questionable research practice rather than proven fabrication.` → Selective exclusion được classify là questionable research practice, chưa phải proven fabrication.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `의심스러운 연구 관행` — questionable research practice.

## 25. replication portfolio /ˌrɛplɪˈkeɪʃən pɔrtˈfoʊlio/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a coordinated set of replications varying sites, samples, methods, or analysts to assess how broadly a finding holds.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** replication portfolio gom nhiều replication khác nhau để đánh giá robustness và boundary của finding.
**Grammar & collocations:** `replication-portfolio project` — project; `assemble replications` — assemble; `cross-site evidence` — evidence.
**Examples:** `The replication portfolio showed that the effect was reliable in laboratories but weaker online.` → Replication portfolio cho thấy effect đáng tin ở lab nhưng yếu hơn online.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `반복 연구 포트폴리오` — replication portfolio.

## Review in context

A **preregistration protocol** fixes a plan before data collection, while a **registered-report format** reviews that plan before results are known. **Exploratory analysis** generates ideas; **confirmatory analysis** tests specified claims. The **file drawer problem** hides null studies, so **direct replication** and **conceptual replication** are both needed. A **reproducibility standard** depends on a **provenance record**, **code availability**, and **methodological transparency**. Researchers should disclose **analytical latitude**, **researcher choice latitude**, and any **outcome switching**. **Citation bias** can distort visibility. A **retraction notice** differs from a **correction of record**. Editorial safeguards address **peer-review cartel** risks through **editorial screening** and a **competing-interest statement**. **Open materials** and a **data sharing agreement** support reuse. A **fabrication allegation** requires investigation, while a **questionable research practice** may be corrected without proving fraud. A **replication portfolio** shows where a finding travels and where it fails.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

**Preregistration protocol** cố định plan trước data collection, còn **registered-report format** review plan trước khi biết result. **Exploratory analysis** tạo idea; **confirmatory analysis** test claim đã chỉ định. **File drawer problem** che giấu null study, nên cần cả **direct replication** và **conceptual replication**. **Reproducibility standard** dựa vào **provenance record**, **code availability** và **methodological transparency**. Researcher nên disclose **analytical latitude**, **researcher choice latitude** và mọi **outcome switching**. **Citation bias** có thể làm visibility lệch. **Retraction notice** khác **correction of record**. Editorial safeguard xử lý risk của **peer-review cartel** qua **editorial screening** và **competing-interest statement**. **Open materials** và **data sharing agreement** hỗ trợ reuse. **Fabrication allegation** cần inquiry, còn **questionable research practice** có thể được sửa mà chưa cần chứng minh fraud. **Replication portfolio** cho thấy finding đi được tới đâu và thất bại ở đâu.
