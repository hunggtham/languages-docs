# C2+ Vocabulary — Pathogen genomics and phylodynamics

This lesson follows how sequence data reveal pathogen history, movement, selection, and transmission over time. The network is **molecular clock → substitution rate → time-scaled phylogeny → pathogen phylogeography → ancestral state reconstruction → lineage turnover → genomic surveillance coverage → sequence completeness → consensus genome → minor variant → within-host diversity → genomic transmission bottleneck → recombination breakpoint → reassortment event → pathogen convergent evolution → antigenic drift trajectory → reassortment-driven antigenic shift → immune escape → host adaptation → selection coefficient → pathogen effective population size → phylodynamic inference → importation event → cryptic transmission → lineage designation**.

## 1. molecular clock /məˈlɛkjələr klɑk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a model that uses the accumulation of genetic changes to estimate the timing of evolutionary events.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** molecular clock dùng mutation tích lũy như “đồng hồ” để ước thời gian divergence.
**Grammar & collocations:** `molecular-clock model` — model; `date a common ancestor` — date; `evolutionary timing` — timing.
**Examples:** `The molecular clock estimated when the lineage diverged from its closest sampled relative.` → Sequence giúp date history.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `분자 시계` — molecular clock.

## 2. substitution rate /ˌsʌbstəˈtuːʃən reɪt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the estimated rate at which one nucleotide or amino acid replaces another in a lineage over time.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** substitution rate đo tốc độ sequence đổi theo generation hoặc year.
**Grammar & collocations:** `substitution-rate estimate` — estimate; `estimate sequence evolution` — estimate; `mutation accumulation` — accumulation.
**Examples:** `The substitution rate was recalculated after older archived samples were added.` → Sample time span ảnh hưởng rate.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `치환율` — substitution rate.

## 3. time-scaled phylogeny /taɪm skeɪld faɪˈlɑdʒəni/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a phylogenetic tree in which branch lengths or nodes are interpreted in calendar time.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** time-scaled phylogeny biến tree thành timeline có date.
**Grammar & collocations:** `time-scaled phylogeny` — phylogeny; `date a phylogenetic node` — date; `branch-time estimate` — estimate.
**Examples:** `The time-scaled phylogeny placed the earliest local sequences before the reported outbreak.` → Timeline có thể gợi cryptic spread.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `시간 척도 계통수` — time-scaled phylogeny.

## 4. pathogen phylogeography /ˈpæθədʒən ˌfaɪloʊdʒiˈɑɡrəfi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** study of how pathogen lineages are distributed and move across geographic locations.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** pathogen phylogeography nối genetic lineage với geography và movement.
**Grammar & collocations:** `pathogen-phylogeography analysis` — analysis; `map lineage movement` — map; `geographic transition` — transition.
**Examples:** `Pathogen phylogeography suggested repeated introductions along the coastal transport route.` → Geography và sequence bổ sung nhau.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `병원체 계통지리학` — pathogen phylogeography.

## 5. ancestral state reconstruction /ænˈsɛstrəl steɪt ˌriːkənˈstrʌkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** inference of the likely historical trait, location, or host state at an internal node of a phylogenetic tree.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** ancestral state reconstruction đoán location/host/trait của ancestor chưa được sample.
**Grammar & collocations:** `ancestral-state reconstruction` — reconstruction; `infer an ancestral location` — infer; `internal node` — node.
**Examples:** `Ancestral state reconstruction placed the likely source region near the river corridor.` → Inference có uncertainty.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `조상 상태 재구성` — ancestral state reconstruction.

## 6. lineage turnover /ˈliːniɪdʒ ˈtɜrnˌoʊvər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** replacement of one pathogen lineage by another in a population over time.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** lineage turnover là lineage cũ giảm và lineage mới chiếm network.
**Grammar & collocations:** `lineage-turnover analysis` — analysis; `track lineage replacement` — track; `dominant lineage` — lineage.
**Examples:** `Lineage turnover accelerated after the new variant gained a transmission advantage.` → Fitness advantage đổi composition.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `계통 교체` — lineage turnover.

## 7. genomic surveillance coverage /dʒiˈnoʊmɪk sərˈveɪləns ˈkʌvərɪdʒ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the proportion and geographic or demographic breadth of cases represented by pathogen sequencing.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** genomic surveillance coverage cho biết sequence có đại diện cho population thật không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `genomic-surveillance coverage` — coverage; `sequence a representative sample` — sequence; `coverage gap` — gap.
**Examples:** `Genomic surveillance coverage was lowest in rural clinics with limited laboratory capacity.` → Coverage gap tạo blind spot.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `유전체 감시 보장 범위` — genomic surveillance coverage.

## 8. sequence completeness /ˈsiːkwəns kəmˈpliːtnəs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the proportion of a genome or target sequence successfully recovered and usable for analysis.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** sequence completeness cho biết genome có đủ đoạn để compare không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `sequence-completeness threshold` — threshold; `assess genome completeness` — assess; `low-coverage sequence` — sequence.
**Examples:** `Sequence completeness was too low for reliable lineage assignment in several degraded samples.` → Genome thiếu làm classification yếu.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `염기서열 완전성` — sequence completeness.

## 9. consensus genome /kənˈsɛns ˈdʒiːnoʊm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a representative genome sequence constructed from the most supported base at each position in a sample.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** consensus genome là sequence đại diện, không phải mọi minor variant trong sample.
**Grammar & collocations:** `consensus-genome sequence` — sequence; `build a consensus` — build; `major allele` — allele.
**Examples:** `The consensus genome was uploaded after low-quality positions were masked.` → Quality control trước sharing.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `합의 유전체` — consensus genome.

## 10. minor variant /ˈmaɪnər ˈvɛriənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a genetic variant present at a lower frequency within a sample than the dominant sequence.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** minor variant là signal nhỏ trong một host/sample, có thể mất hoặc expand sau.
**Grammar & collocations:** `minor-variant frequency` — frequency; `detect a minor variant` — detect; `within-sample diversity` — diversity.
**Examples:** `The minor variant was below the reporting threshold but became dominant in a later sample.` → Variant nhỏ có thể grow.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `소수 변이` — minor variant.

## 11. within-host diversity /wɪˈðɪn hoʊst daɪˈvɜrsəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the range of genetic variants present in a pathogen population within one infected host.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** within-host diversity là pathogen population bên trong một host không luôn đồng nhất.
**Grammar & collocations:** `within-host diversity` — diversity; `measure intrahost variants` — measure; `within-host evolution` — evolution.
**Examples:** `Within-host diversity increased during prolonged infection and complicated transmission inference.` → One sample có nhiều variant.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `숙주 내 다양성` — within-host diversity.

## 12. genomic transmission bottleneck /dʒiˈnoʊmɪk trænzˈmɪʃən ˈbɑtlˌnɛk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the narrow genetic sampling of a pathogen population that successfully passes from one host to another.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** genomic transmission bottleneck là chỉ một phần nhỏ diversity đi qua transmission event.
**Grammar & collocations:** `genomic-transmission bottleneck` — bottleneck; `estimate founding diversity` — estimate; `recipient population` — population.
**Examples:** `The genomic transmission bottleneck left only a few donor variants in the recipient.` → Transmission làm diversity hẹp.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `유전체 전파 병목` — genomic transmission bottleneck.

## 13. recombination breakpoint /ˌriːkəm bəˈneɪʃən ˈbreɪkˌpɔɪnt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a genomic position where sequence segments appear to have been exchanged between related genomes.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** recombination breakpoint là vị trí “đổi nguồn” trong genome mosaic.
**Grammar & collocations:** `recombination-breakpoint analysis` — analysis; `detect a breakpoint` — detect; `mosaic genome` — genome.
**Examples:** `The recombination breakpoint separated the genome into regions with different evolutionary histories.` → Một genome có nhiều history.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `재조합 절단점` — recombination breakpoint.

## 14. reassortment event /ˌriːəˈsɔrtmənt ɪˈvɛnt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** exchange of whole genome segments between viruses with segmented genomes during coinfection.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** reassortment event tráo cả segment, khác point mutation nhỏ.
**Grammar & collocations:** `reassortment-event reconstruction` — reconstruction; `exchange genome segments` — exchange; `segmented virus` — virus.
**Examples:** `The reassortment event produced a lineage with a new combination of surface proteins.` → Segment mới đổi phenotype có thể.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `재분절 사건` — reassortment event.

## 15. pathogen convergent evolution /ˈpæθədʒən kənˈvɜrdʒənt ˌɛvəˈluːʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** independent evolution of similar genetic or phenotypic changes in separate pathogen lineages.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** pathogen convergent evolution là nhiều lineage tự đi đến mutation/function giống nhau.
**Grammar & collocations:** `pathogen-convergent evolution` — evolution; `evolve similar mutations independently` — evolve; `parallel adaptation` — adaptation.
**Examples:** `Pathogen convergent evolution produced the same receptor-binding change in distant lineages.` → Similar mutation chưa chắc cùng lineage.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `병원체 수렴 진화` — pathogen convergent evolution.

## 16. antigenic drift trajectory /ˌæntɪˈdʒɛnɪk drɪft trəˈdʒɛktəri/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the time-ordered pattern of small antigenic changes that gradually alter recognition by prior immunity.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** antigenic drift trajectory là đường đi tích lũy của antigen change.
**Grammar & collocations:** `antigenic-drift trajectory` — trajectory; `track antigenic change` — track; `immune recognition` — recognition.
**Examples:** `The antigenic drift trajectory informed which strains were prioritized for the next formulation.` → Drift dần dần đổi match.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `항원성 소변이 궤적` — antigenic drift trajectory.

## 17. reassortment-driven antigenic shift /ˌriːəˈsɔrtmənt ˈdrɪvən ˌæntɪˈdʒɛnɪk ʃɪft/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a substantial antigenic change produced by exchange of genome segments between compatible viruses.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** reassortment-driven antigenic shift là jump lớn do segment exchange, không phải drift nhỏ.
**Grammar & collocations:** `reassortment-driven antigenic shift` — shift; `exchange antigenic segments` — exchange; `novel antigenic profile` — profile.
**Examples:** `A reassortment-driven antigenic shift could reduce existing population immunity abruptly.` → Shift có thể tạo vulnerability rộng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `재분절 유발 항원성 변이` — reassortment-driven antigenic shift.

## 18. immune escape /ɪˈmjuːn ɪˈskeɪp/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the ability of a pathogen variant to reduce recognition or control by prior immunity.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** immune escape là pathogen né một phần immune memory, không nhất thiết né toàn bộ.
**Grammar & collocations:** `immune-escape mutation` — mutation; `reduce antibody recognition` — reduce; `immune evasion` — evasion.
**Examples:** `Immune escape reduced protection against infection while protection against severe disease persisted.` → Outcome cần tách.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `면역 회피` — immune escape.

## 19. host adaptation /hoʊst ˌædəpˈteɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** evolutionary change that improves a pathogen's ability to infect, replicate, or transmit in a particular host species.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** host adaptation là pathogen dần hợp với biology của host mới.
**Grammar & collocations:** `host-adaptation signal` — signal; `adapt to a new host` — adapt; `host-specific trait` — trait.
**Examples:** `Host adaptation was investigated after sustained transmission appeared in a new mammal population.` → Sustained spread cần evidence.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `숙주 적응` — host adaptation.

## 20. selection coefficient /səˈlɛkʃən ˌkoʊəˈfɪʃənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a parameter describing the relative reproductive or transmission advantage of one variant over another.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** selection coefficient lượng hóa variant có advantage bao nhiêu.
**Grammar & collocations:** `selection-coefficient estimate` — estimate; `estimate a transmission advantage` — estimate; `relative fitness` — fitness.
**Examples:** `The selection coefficient suggested a modest advantage that accumulated across many generations.` → Advantage nhỏ vẫn có effect.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `선택 계수` — selection coefficient.

## 21. pathogen effective population size /ˈpæθədʒən ɪˈfɛktɪv ˌpɑpjəˈleɪʃən saɪz/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the size of an idealized population that would show the observed amount of genetic drift in a pathogen population.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** pathogen effective population size phản ánh diversity/evolution, không đơn giản là số host nhiễm.
**Grammar & collocations:** `pathogen-effective population size` — size; `infer effective population size` — infer; `genetic drift` — drift.
**Examples:** `The pathogen effective population size fell after transmission chains fragmented.` → Network fragmentation giảm diversity.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `병원체 유효 개체군 크기` — pathogen effective population size.

## 22. phylodynamic inference /ˌfaɪloʊdaɪˈnæmɪk ˈɪnfərəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** inference about transmission, population dynamics, or selection from genetic sequences combined with sampling dates and epidemiological models.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** phylodynamic inference nối phylogeny với epidemic dynamics theo time.
**Grammar & collocations:** `phylodynamic-inference model` — model; `infer transmission dynamics` — infer; `sequence-based inference` — inference.
**Examples:** `Phylodynamic inference suggested that growth began before the first clinical cluster was recognized.` → Genetic data có thể thấy early growth.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `계통동역학 추론` — phylodynamic inference.

## 23. importation event /ˌɪmpɔrˈteɪʃən ɪˈvɛnt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** introduction of a pathogen lineage into a population from another geographic or transmission network.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** importation event là lineage đi vào từ ngoài, có thể tạo local chain hoặc die out.
**Grammar & collocations:** `importation-event estimate` — estimate; `identify an introduction` — identify; `travel-associated lineage` — lineage.
**Examples:** `The importation event was linked to travel records and a closely related external sequence.` → Travel và sequence cùng hỗ trợ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `유입 사건` — importation event.

## 24. cryptic transmission /ˈkrɪptɪk trænzˈmɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** transmission that occurs without being recognized or documented by routine surveillance at the time.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cryptic transmission là spread âm thầm ngoài detection system.
**Grammar & collocations:** `cryptic-transmission period` — period; `infer hidden transmission` — infer; `unrecognized spread` — spread.
**Examples:** `Cryptic transmission was inferred from sequences collected before the official outbreak date.` → Sequence có thể lộ hidden spread.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `은밀한 전파` — cryptic transmission.

## 25. lineage designation /ˈliːniɪdʒ ˌdɛzɪɡˈneɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the formal naming or classification assigned to a pathogen genetic lineage under a defined nomenclature system.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** lineage designation biến cluster genetic thành tên dùng chung giữa labs và agencies.
**Grammar & collocations:** `lineage-designation rule` — rule; `assign a lineage designation` — assign; `nomenclature system` — system.
**Examples:** `The lineage designation was updated after additional sequences clarified the branch structure.` → Name có thể đổi khi evidence tăng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `계통 지정` — lineage designation.

## Review in context — Part 1

A **molecular clock**, **substitution rate**, and **time-scaled phylogeny** estimate when lineages changed. **Pathogen phylogeography** and **ancestral state reconstruction** trace movement, while **lineage turnover** shows replacement. **Genomic surveillance coverage**, **sequence completeness**, and a **consensus genome** determine how reliable the picture is. A **minor variant** and **within-host diversity** reveal variation hidden inside one sample.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 1:**

**Molecular clock**, **substitution rate** và **time-scaled phylogeny** ước tính khi lineage đổi. **Pathogen phylogeography** và **ancestral state reconstruction** truy movement, còn **lineage turnover** cho thấy replacement. **Genomic surveillance coverage**, **sequence completeness** và **consensus genome** quyết định picture đáng tin đến đâu. **Minor variant** và **within-host diversity** cho thấy variation ẩn trong một sample.

## Review in context — Part 2

A **genomic transmission bottleneck**, **recombination breakpoint**, or **reassortment event** can reshape a lineage. **Pathogen convergent evolution**, an **antigenic drift trajectory**, and a **reassortment-driven antigenic shift** affect recognition. **Immune escape**, **host adaptation**, a **selection coefficient**, and **pathogen effective population size** inform **phylodynamic inference**. An **importation event**, **cryptic transmission**, and **lineage designation** complete the public-health interpretation.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 2:**

**Genomic transmission bottleneck**, **recombination breakpoint** hoặc **reassortment event** có thể định hình lại lineage. **Pathogen convergent evolution**, **antigenic drift trajectory** và **reassortment-driven antigenic shift** ảnh hưởng recognition. **Immune escape**, **host adaptation**, **selection coefficient** và **pathogen effective population size** hỗ trợ **phylodynamic inference**. **Importation event**, **cryptic transmission** và **lineage designation** hoàn thiện interpretation cho public health.
