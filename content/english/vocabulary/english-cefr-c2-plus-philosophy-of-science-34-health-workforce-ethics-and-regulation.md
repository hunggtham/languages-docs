# C2+ Vocabulary — Health workforce ethics and regulation

This lesson follows how health professions define competence, distribute responsibility, protect judgment, and maintain trust under pressure. The network is **scope-of-practice → credential portability → licensure reciprocity → continuing competence → moral distress → exhaustion spillover → caregiver strain → duty of care → standard of care → professional indemnity → speak-up protection → interest declaration → clinical autonomy → conscientious objection → dual loyalty → peer review privilege → fitness to practice → supervision ratio → team psychological safety → handover reliability → competency framework → rural retention → task saturation → on-call burden → workforce pipeline**.

## 1. scope-of-practice /skoʊp əv ˈpræktɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the set of activities, decisions, and procedures that a professional is educated, authorized, and competent to perform.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** scope-of-practice vẽ ranh giới việc một professional được làm.
**Grammar & collocations:** `scope-of-practice rule` — rule; `work within scope` — work; `authorized procedure` — procedure.
**Examples:** `The nurse expanded her scope-of-practice only after supervised training and formal approval.` → Mở scope cần train và approve.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `업무 범위` — scope-of-practice.

## 2. credential portability /krəˈdɛnʃəl pɔrˈtæbələti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the ability to use a professional qualification across institutions, regions, or jurisdictions without repeating unnecessary verification.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** credential portability giúp qualification đi theo người qua nhiều nơi.
**Grammar & collocations:** `credential-portability agreement` — agreement; `transfer a qualification` — transfer; `recognized credential` — credential.
**Examples:** `Credential portability helped emergency clinicians move quickly to hospitals facing staffing gaps.` → Clinician chuyển nơi dễ hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `자격 이동성` — credential portability.

## 3. licensure reciprocity /ˈlaɪsəns ˌrɛprəˈsɑsəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** mutual acceptance of a professional license by different jurisdictions under agreed conditions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** licensure reciprocity cho license của nơi này được nơi khác công nhận.
**Grammar & collocations:** `licensure-reciprocity compact` — compact; `recognize a license` — recognize; `jurisdictional board` — board.
**Examples:** `Licensure reciprocity allowed physicians to practice across the border during the disaster response.` → Bác sĩ được hành nghề cross-border.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `면허 상호 인정` — licensure reciprocity.

## 4. continuing competence /kənˈtɪnjuɪŋ ˈkɑmpɪtəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** ongoing ability to perform professional duties safely as knowledge, technology, standards, and situations change.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** continuing competence là competence phải được duy trì và cập nhật.
**Grammar & collocations:** `continuing-competence requirement` — requirement; `maintain competence` — maintain; `periodic assessment` — assessment.
**Examples:** `Continuing competence required the team to rehearse the new ventilator protocol each year.` → Skill cần cập nhật định kỳ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `지속적 역량` — continuing competence.

## 5. moral distress /ˈmɔrəl dɪˈstrɛs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** distress that arises when a person knows the ethically appropriate action but feels unable to take it because of constraints or authority.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** moral distress là biết điều đúng nhưng bị system chặn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `moral-distress debriefing` — debriefing; `experience moral distress` — experience; `ethical constraint` — constraint.
**Examples:** `Moral distress grew when clinicians had to delay essential treatment because supplies were rationed.` → Clinician biết cần treat nhưng thiếu resource.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `도덕적 고통` — moral distress.

## 6. exhaustion spillover /ɪɡˈzɔstʃən ˈspɪlˌoʊvər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the spread of fatigue and reduced cognitive or emotional capacity from one task, shift, or worker into other parts of work.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** exhaustion spillover làm mệt từ shift này lan sang decision và team khác.
**Grammar & collocations:** `exhaustion-spillover effect` — effect; `carry fatigue into a shift` — carry; `cognitive reserve` — reserve.
**Examples:** `Exhaustion spillover increased prescribing errors after several nights of emergency duty.` → Mệt tích lũy làm error tăng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `피로 파급` — exhaustion spillover.

## 7. caregiver strain /ˈkɛrˌɡɪvər streɪn/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** physical, emotional, financial, and time pressure experienced by a person who provides ongoing care to another.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** caregiver strain là gánh nặng tổng hợp của người chăm sóc.
**Grammar & collocations:** `caregiver-strain screening` — screening; `assess caregiver strain` — assess; `respite support` — support.
**Examples:** `Caregiver strain rose when home-care visits were reduced and family members filled the gap.` → Gia đình phải gánh care thay system.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `돌봄 제공자 부담` — caregiver strain.

## 8. duty of care /ˈduːti əv kɛr/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a professional or legal obligation to act with reasonable competence and attention toward a person who may be harmed by neglect.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** duty of care là nghĩa vụ không bỏ mặc risk có thể phòng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `duty-of-care standard` — standard; `owe a duty` — owe; `prevent foreseeable harm` — prevent.
**Examples:** `The duty of care required the clinic to arrange follow-up after an abnormal result.` → Result bất thường phải có follow-up.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `주의 의무` — duty of care.

## 9. standard of care /ˈstændərd əv kɛr/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the level and type of practice reasonably expected from a competent professional in a comparable situation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** standard of care là mức thực hành được kỳ vọng trong context tương đương.
**Grammar & collocations:** `standard-of-care review` — review; `meet the standard` — meet; `comparable clinical situation` — situation.
**Examples:** `The review found that the treatment matched the standard of care available at the time.` → Đánh giá theo standard của thời điểm đó.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `진료 표준` — standard of care.

## 10. professional indemnity /prəˈfɛʃənəl ɪnˈdɛmnəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** insurance or legal protection covering a professional's liability for claims arising from work-related error, omission, or harm.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** professional indemnity bảo vệ professional trước liability nghề nghiệp.
**Grammar & collocations:** `professional-indemnity policy` — policy; `cover professional liability` — cover; `negligence claim` — claim.
**Examples:** `Professional indemnity allowed the clinic to compensate the patient while the investigation continued.` → Compensation không chờ investigation kết thúc.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `전문직 배상책임` — professional indemnity.

## 11. speak-up protection /spiːk ʌp prəˈtɛkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** safeguards against retaliation for workers who report unsafe practice, misconduct, or risks in good faith.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** speak-up protection giúp staff nói về risk mà không sợ bị trả đũa.
**Grammar & collocations:** `speak-up-protection policy` — policy; `report a safety concern` — report; `retaliation safeguard` — safeguard.
**Examples:** `Speak-up protection encouraged a junior pharmacist to report a dangerous dispensing shortcut.` → Junior staff dám report shortcut.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `내부고발자 보호` — speak-up protection.

## 12. interest declaration /ˈɪntrəst ˌdɛkləˈreɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a formal statement of financial, personal, professional, or institutional interests that could influence a decision.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** interest declaration công khai relationship có thể làm decision bias.
**Grammar & collocations:** `interest-declaration form` — form; `declare an interest` — declare; `potential influence` — influence.
**Examples:** `The reviewer filed an interest declaration before assessing a device made by a former employer.` → Reviewer disclose employer cũ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `이해관계 신고` — interest declaration.

## 13. clinical autonomy /ˈklɪnɪkəl ɔˈtɑnəmi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a professional's justified ability to use clinical judgment without improper coercion, while remaining accountable to standards and patients.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** clinical autonomy là quyền phán đoán chuyên môn đi cùng accountability.
**Grammar & collocations:** `clinical-autonomy safeguard` — safeguard; `exercise clinical judgment` — exercise; `professional independence` — independence.
**Examples:** `Clinical autonomy allowed the physician to depart from the default pathway when the patient's condition differed.` → Case khác thì doctor được adapt.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `임상 자율성` — clinical autonomy.

## 14. conscientious objection /ˌkɑnʃiˈɛnʃəs əbˈdʒɛkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a refusal to perform or assist with a lawful professional action because it conflicts with deeply held moral or religious convictions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** conscientious objection là refusal vì conviction, nhưng không được làm patient mất access hợp pháp.
**Grammar & collocations:** `conscientious-objection policy` — policy; `raise an objection` — raise; `lawful clinical service` — service.
**Examples:** `The conscientious objection policy required timely referral so the patient could still obtain lawful care.` → Refuse nhưng phải referral.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `양심적 거부` — conscientious objection.

## 15. dual loyalty /ˈduəl ˈlɔɪəlti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a conflict in which a professional owes duties both to an individual patient and to another authority, institution, or public mission.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** dual loyalty kéo professional giữa patient duty và institutional/public duty.
**Grammar & collocations:** `dual-loyalty conflict` — conflict; `balance competing duties` — balance; `institutional demand` — demand.
**Examples:** `Dual loyalty became visible when the prison clinician had to protect patient confidentiality while reporting a security threat.` → Patient privacy và prison security conflict.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `이중 충성` — dual loyalty.

## 16. peer review privilege /pɪr rɪˈvju ˈprɪvəlɪdʒ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** legal protection that limits disclosure or use of certain materials created for confidential professional quality review.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** peer review privilege bảo vệ discussion nội bộ để team học từ error.
**Grammar & collocations:** `peer-review-privilege rule` — rule; `protect review materials` — protect; `quality-improvement meeting` — meeting.
**Examples:** `Peer review privilege encouraged candid analysis of the near miss without turning every comment into litigation evidence.` → Team nói thật hơn về near miss.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `동료 심사 특권` — peer review privilege.

## 17. fitness to practice /ˈfɪtnəs tə ˈpræktɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a professional's current physical, mental, ethical, and technical ability to perform duties safely.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** fitness to practice hỏi người này hiện có đủ an toàn để hành nghề không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `fitness-to-practice review` — review; `assess fitness` — assess; `safe professional performance` — performance.
**Examples:** `The board initiated a fitness-to-practice review after repeated medication errors and untreated illness.` → Review để bảo vệ patient và worker.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `직무 수행 적합성` — fitness to practice.

## 18. supervision ratio /ˌsuːpərˈvɪʒən ˈreɪʃioʊ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the number and level of trainees or less-experienced workers assigned to each supervisor.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** supervision ratio cho biết một supervisor gánh bao nhiêu người cần guide.
**Grammar & collocations:** `supervision-ratio standard` — standard; `supervise a trainee` — supervise; `senior clinician` — clinician.
**Examples:** `The supervision ratio became unsafe when one senior clinician covered three overloaded wards.` → Một senior không đủ cover ba ward.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `감독 비율` — supervision ratio.

## 19. team psychological safety /tiːm ˌsaɪkəˈlɑdʒɪkəl ˈseɪfti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a shared belief that team members can ask questions, admit mistakes, and raise concerns without humiliation or punishment.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** team psychological safety cho phép nói “tôi chưa chắc” mà không bị shame.
**Grammar & collocations:** `team-psychological-safety survey` — survey; `raise a concern` — raise; `learning climate` — climate.
**Examples:** `Team psychological safety helped a student nurse question an apparently incorrect infusion rate.` → Student dám hỏi và ngăn error.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `팀 심리적 안전` — team psychological safety.

## 20. handover reliability /ˈhændˌoʊvər rɪˌlaɪəˈbɪləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the consistency and completeness with which responsibility and critical information are transferred between workers or teams.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** handover reliability giữ information không rơi khi đổi shift/team.
**Grammar & collocations:** `handover-reliability checklist` — checklist; `transfer responsibility` — transfer; `critical information` — information.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `인계 신뢰성` — handover reliability.

## 22. rural retention /ˈrʊrəl rɪˈtɛnʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the ability of rural or remote health services to keep trained workers over time.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** rural retention là giữ worker ở vùng xa, không chỉ tuyển được lúc đầu.
**Grammar & collocations:** `rural-retention incentive` — incentive; `retain a rural clinician` — retain; `remote service` — service.
**Examples:** `Rural retention improved when housing, career progression, and partner employment were addressed together.` → Retention cần package, không chỉ bonus.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `농촌 인력 유지` — rural retention.

## 23. task saturation /tæsk sætʃəˈreɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a condition in which the volume and complexity of tasks exceed a worker's available attention and time.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** task saturation là task vượt quá bandwidth của worker.
**Grammar & collocations:** `task-saturation threshold` — threshold; `exceed cognitive capacity` — exceed; `workload signal` — signal.
**Examples:** `Task saturation caused the coordinator to miss a transfer request hidden among routine messages.` → Quá tải làm miss request.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `업무 포화` — task saturation.

## 24. on-call burden /ˈɑn kɔl ˈbɜrdən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the cumulative disruption, fatigue, responsibility, and personal cost associated with being available for urgent work outside routine hours.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** on-call burden gồm call, fatigue, trách nhiệm và cost đời sống.
**Grammar & collocations:** `on-call-burden review` — review; `carry overnight responsibility` — carry; `rest disruption` — disruption.
**Examples:** `On-call burden became unsustainable when the same specialists covered every weekend.` → Một nhóm cover mãi sẽ kiệt sức.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `당직 부담` — on-call burden.

## 25. workforce pipeline /ˈwɜrkˌfɔrs ˈpaɪpˌlaɪn/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the sequence through which people are recruited, educated, licensed, supported, and retained for future roles.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** workforce pipeline nối recruitment tới education, license và retention.
**Grammar & collocations:** `workforce-pipeline strategy` — strategy; `build a training pipeline` — build; `future staffing need` — need.
**Examples:** `The workforce pipeline weakened when training places expanded without enough supervisors or rural jobs.` → Mở school mà thiếu supervisor/job thì pipeline gãy.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `보건의료 인력 파이프라인` — workforce pipeline.

## Review in context

**Scope-of-practice**, **credential portability**, and **licensure reciprocity** define where and how professionals can work, while **continuing competence** keeps skills current. **Moral distress**, **exhaustion spillover**, and **caregiver strain** can undermine the **duty of care** and the **standard of care**; **professional indemnity** addresses liability. **Speak-up protection** and an **interest declaration** support trust. **Clinical autonomy**, **conscientious objection**, and **dual loyalty** require explicit safeguards, while **peer review privilege** supports learning. **Fitness to practice**, a safe **supervision ratio**, **team psychological safety**, and **handover reliability** protect patients. A **competency framework** can strengthen **rural retention**, but **task saturation** and **on-call burden** reveal capacity limits. The long-term **workforce pipeline** must connect education, support, and retention.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

**Scope-of-practice**, **credential portability** và **licensure reciprocity** xác định professional được làm ở đâu và thế nào, còn **continuing competence** giữ skill luôn cập nhật. **Moral distress**, **exhaustion spillover** và **caregiver strain** có thể làm yếu **duty of care** và **standard of care**; **professional indemnity** xử lý liability. **Speak-up protection** và **interest declaration** hỗ trợ trust. **Clinical autonomy**, **conscientious objection** và **dual loyalty** cần safeguard rõ, còn **peer review privilege** hỗ trợ learning. **Fitness to practice**, **supervision ratio** an toàn, **team psychological safety** và **handover reliability** bảo vệ patient. **Competency framework** có thể tăng **rural retention**, nhưng **task saturation** và **on-call burden** cho thấy giới hạn capacity. **Workforce pipeline** dài hạn phải nối education, support và retention.
