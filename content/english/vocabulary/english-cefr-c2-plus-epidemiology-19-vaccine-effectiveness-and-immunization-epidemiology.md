# C2+ Vocabulary — Vaccine effectiveness and immunization epidemiology

This lesson follows how vaccination changes individual and population risk, how programs find missed people, and how safety is monitored. The network is **vaccine effectiveness estimate → test-negative control → waning protection → post-immunization infection → dose-response schedule → booster response → correlates of protection → immune imprinting → herd effect → indirect protection → vaccine failure → primary series completion → uptake denominator → immunization coverage shortfall → never-vaccinated child → delayed-dose recovery campaign → local immunization microplan → cold-chain dropout → vaccination missed-contact event → post-immunization adverse event → vaccine safety signal → background incidence → risk-benefit communication → vaccination registry → immunization equity audit**.

## 1. vaccine effectiveness estimate /vækˈsiːn ɪˈfɛktɪvnəs ˈɛstəˌmeɪt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an estimate of how much vaccination reduces a specified outcome under real-world conditions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** vaccine effectiveness estimate đo protection trong field, khác efficacy trong trial lý tưởng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `vaccine-effectiveness estimate` — estimate; `estimate real-world protection` — estimate; `effectiveness study` — study.
**Examples:** `The vaccine effectiveness estimate was higher against hospitalization than against mild infection.` → Outcome khác thì estimate khác.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `백신 효과 추정치` — vaccine effectiveness estimate.

## 2. test-negative control /tɛst ˈnɛɡətɪv kənˈtroʊl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a comparison group of symptomatic people who test negative for the target condition in a test-negative effectiveness design.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** test-negative control giúp compare vaccinated và unvaccinated trong cùng care-seeking context.
**Grammar & collocations:** `test-negative control` — control; `select a test-negative control` — select; `effectiveness design` — design.
**Examples:** `The test-negative control reduced differences in healthcare-seeking behavior between groups.` → Control gần hơn về context.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `검사 음성 대조군` — test-negative control.

## 3. waning protection /ˈweɪnɪŋ prəˈtɛkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** gradual reduction in protection after vaccination or infection over time.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** waning protection là protection giảm dần theo time, không phải mất ngay.
**Grammar & collocations:** `waning-protection curve` — curve; `measure protection over time` — measure; `time since dose` — time.
**Examples:** `Waning protection was most visible among older adults many months after the primary series.` → Time since dose matters.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `감소하는 보호 효과` — waning protection.

## 4. post-immunization infection /poʊst ˌɪmjənəˈzeɪʃən ɪnˈfɛkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** infection occurring after a person has received an immunization, regardless of whether the vaccine failed.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** post-immunization infection chỉ nói timing; cần thêm data để biết severity và protection.
**Grammar & collocations:** `post-immunization infection` — infection; `investigate an infection after vaccination` — investigate; `breakthrough case` — case.
**Examples:** `Most post-immunization infections were mild, while protection against hospitalization remained strong.` → Infection không tự chứng minh failure.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `예방접종 후 감염` — post-immunization infection.

## 5. dose-response schedule /doʊs rɪˈspɑns ˈskɛdʒuːl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the planned timing and number of doses used to produce a desired immune response.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** dose-response schedule nối số dose và khoảng cách với immune response.
**Grammar & collocations:** `dose-response schedule` — schedule; `space vaccine doses` — space; `immune priming` — priming.
**Examples:** `The dose-response schedule was adjusted for infants whose immune systems mature over time.` → Age ảnh hưởng schedule.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `용량-반응 일정` — dose-response schedule.

## 6. booster response /ˈbuːstər rɪˈspɑns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the immune response produced by an additional dose after a primary vaccination series.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** booster response là response được “nhắc lại” sau primary series.
**Grammar & collocations:** `booster-response study` — study; `measure antibody response` — measure; `additional dose` — dose.
**Examples:** `The booster response restored antibody levels that had declined after the initial series.` → Booster có thể restore protection.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `추가접종 반응` — booster response.

## 7. correlates of protection /ˈkɔrəˌleɪts əv prəˈtɛkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** measurable immune markers associated with protection against infection or disease.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** correlates of protection là marker giúp infer protection khi outcome trực tiếp khó đo.
**Grammar & collocations:** `correlate of protection` — correlate; `identify immune correlates` — identify; `protective antibody level` — level.
**Examples:** `The study examined correlates of protection before deciding whether a booster was needed.` → Marker hỗ trợ policy nhưng không luôn causal.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `보호 상관 지표` — correlates of protection.

## 8. immune imprinting /ɪˈmjuːn ɪmˈprɪntɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the tendency of earlier immune experiences to shape later responses to related antigens.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** immune imprinting là immune system “nhớ” exposure cũ và phản ứng mới qua memory đó.
**Grammar & collocations:** `immune-imprinting hypothesis` — hypothesis; `shape later immunity` — shape; `antigenic history` — history.
**Examples:** `Immune imprinting was considered when researchers compared responses to a changed variant.` → History ảnh hưởng response hiện tại.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `면역 각인` — immune imprinting.

## 9. herd effect /hɜrd ɪˈfɛkt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** indirect reduction in infection risk for some people because enough others are immune or protected.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** herd effect là benefit gián tiếp do network transmission khó hơn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `herd-effect estimate` — estimate; `measure indirect benefit` — measure; `population immunity` — immunity.
**Examples:** `The herd effect was strongest in settings with high coverage and dense contact networks.` → Coverage và contact cùng quyết định.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `집단 효과` — herd effect.

## 10. indirect protection /ˌɪndəˈrɛkt prəˈtɛkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** protection received because vaccination or another intervention reduces exposure from other people.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** indirect protection đến qua người khác được protected, không chỉ qua own dose.
**Grammar & collocations:** `indirect-protection effect` — effect; `benefit from community coverage` — benefit; `reduced exposure` — exposure.
**Examples:** `Indirect protection helped shield infants too young for the full schedule.` → Community action bảo vệ người chưa đủ dose.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `간접 보호` — indirect protection.

## 11. vaccine failure /vækˈsiːn ˈfeɪljər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** occurrence of the target outcome despite vaccination, after considering timing, immune response, exposure, and measurement.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** vaccine failure cần investigation, không kết luận chỉ vì post-immunization infection.
**Grammar & collocations:** `vaccine-failure investigation` — investigation; `classify a failure` — classify; `primary nonresponse` — nonresponse.
**Examples:** `The vaccine-failure investigation found that several cases occurred before immunity had fully developed.` → Timing loại bớt false failure.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `백신 실패` — vaccine failure.

## 12. primary series completion /ˈpraɪˌmɛri ˈsɪriz kəmˈpliːʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** receipt of all doses required for the initial vaccination series by the recommended timing.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** primary series completion đo người bắt đầu có đi đến đủ series không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `primary-series completion` — completion; `complete the series` — complete; `dose schedule` — schedule.
**Examples:** `Primary series completion improved after reminder calls and evening clinics were added.` → Convenience làm completion tăng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `기초 접종 완료` — primary series completion.

## 13. uptake denominator /ˈʌpˌteɪk dɪˈnɑməˌneɪtər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the defined population eligible or expected to receive an intervention, used as the denominator for uptake.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** uptake denominator quyết định coverage đang tính trên ai.
**Grammar & collocations:** `uptake-denominator definition` — definition; `define the eligible population` — define; `coverage estimate` — estimate.
**Examples:** `The uptake denominator changed after eligibility expanded to include adolescents.` → Denominator đổi thì rate cần đọc lại.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `수용률 분모` — uptake denominator.

## 14. immunization coverage shortfall /ˌɪmjənəˈzeɪʃən ˈkʌvərɪdʒ ˈʃɔrtˌfɔl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the gap between the vaccination coverage needed for program goals and the coverage actually achieved.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** immunization coverage shortfall là phần coverage còn thiếu để đạt protection goal.
**Grammar & collocations:** `immunization-coverage shortfall` — shortfall; `close a coverage gap` — close; `under-immunized group` — group.
**Examples:** `The immunization coverage shortfall was concentrated in remote districts with irregular outreach.` → Geography tạo gap.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `예방접종 보장 부족` — immunization coverage shortfall.

## 15. never-vaccinated child /ˈnɛvər ˈvæksəˌneɪtɪd tʃaɪld/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a child who has received no doses of a specified routine vaccine or vaccine series.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** never-vaccinated child khác với child bắt đầu nhưng chưa complete.
**Grammar & collocations:** `never-vaccinated child` — child; `find never-vaccinated children` — find; `zero-dose status` — status.
**Examples:** `The outreach team mapped every never-vaccinated child before planning mobile sessions.` → Mapping giúp target đúng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `미접종 아동` — never-vaccinated child.

## 16. delayed-dose recovery campaign /dɪˈleɪd doʊs rɪˈkʌvəri kæmˈpeɪn/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an organized effort to help people receive doses that were missed or delayed during disruption.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** delayed-dose recovery campaign kéo người bị lỡ schedule trở lại protection pathway.
**Grammar & collocations:** `delayed-dose recovery campaign` — campaign; `recover missed doses` — recover; `catch-up visit` — visit.
**Examples:** `The delayed-dose recovery campaign used school lists and community health workers.` → Recovery cần data và local trust.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `지연 접종 회복 캠페인` — delayed-dose recovery campaign.

## 17. local immunization microplan /ˈloʊkəl ˌɪmjənəˈzeɪʃən ˈmaɪkroʊˌplæn/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a detailed local plan specifying target people, sites, staff, routes, supplies, and timing for immunization delivery.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** local immunization microplan biến national goal thành route và schedule của từng district.
**Grammar & collocations:** `local-immunization microplan` — plan; `microplan outreach` — microplan; `session map` — map.
**Examples:** `The local immunization microplan scheduled evening sessions for seasonal farm workers.` → Local detail tăng reach.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `지역 예방접종 세부계획` — local immunization microplan.

## 18. cold-chain dropout /koʊld tʃeɪn ˈdrɑpˌaʊt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** loss of vaccine quality or usability because temperature-controlled storage or transport fails.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cold-chain dropout là vaccine rời khỏi temperature range và có thể mất potency.
**Grammar & collocations:** `cold-chain dropout` — dropout; `detect a temperature excursion` — detect; `discard compromised doses` — discard.
**Examples:** `A cold-chain dropout spoiled doses after a refrigerator alarm was ignored.` → Monitoring và response cùng quan trọng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `콜드체인 이탈` — cold-chain dropout.

## 19. vaccination missed-contact event /væk səˈneɪʃən mɪst ˈkɑntækt ɪˈvɛnt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a care encounter at which an eligible person could have been vaccinated but was not.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** vaccination missed-contact event là cơ hội đã có nhưng dose không được offer hoặc complete.
**Grammar & collocations:** `vaccination-missed-contact event` — event; `capture a missed opportunity` — capture; `offer vaccination` — offer.
**Examples:** `The audit found a vaccination missed-contact event during repeated pediatric visits.` → Hệ thống có thể sửa ngay tại visit.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `예방접종 놓친 접촉 사건` — vaccination missed-contact event.

## 20. post-immunization adverse event /poʊst ˌɪmjənəˈzeɪʃən ˈædvɜrs ɪˈvɛnt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** any unfavorable health event occurring after immunization, whether or not the vaccine caused it.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** post-immunization adverse event chỉ nói timing, chưa nói causality.
**Grammar & collocations:** `post-immunization adverse event` — event; `report an adverse event` — report; `causality assessment` — assessment.
**Examples:** `The post-immunization adverse event was reported before a causality assessment was completed.` → Report rộng hơn causal conclusion.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `예방접종 후 이상반응` — post-immunization adverse event.

## 21. vaccine safety signal /vækˈsiːn ˈseɪfti ˈsɪɡnəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a pattern of reports or data suggesting a possible association between a vaccine and an adverse outcome that requires evaluation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** vaccine safety signal là “dấu hiệu cần investigate”, chưa phải proof vaccine gây harm.
**Grammar & collocations:** `vaccine-safety signal` — signal; `investigate a safety signal` — investigate; `signal detection` — detection.
**Examples:** `The vaccine safety signal prompted record review and comparison with background rates.` → Signal cần comparator.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `백신 안전성 신호` — vaccine safety signal.

## 22. background incidence /ˈbækˌɡraʊnd ˈɪnsɪdəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the expected rate of a condition in a population without assuming that an intervention caused it.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** background incidence là mức disease/event vốn xảy ra tự nhiên để so safety signal.
**Grammar & collocations:** `background-incidence estimate` — estimate; `compare observed and expected events` — compare; `baseline rate` — rate.
**Examples:** `The observed event rate did not exceed background incidence after age adjustment.` → Temporal association chưa đủ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `기저 발생률` — background incidence.

## 23. risk-benefit communication /rɪsk ˈbɛnəfɪt kəˌmjuːnəˈkeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** communication that explains expected benefits, known risks, uncertainty, and alternatives for an intervention.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** risk-benefit communication cho người nghe cả benefit, risk và uncertainty để tự cân nhắc.
**Grammar & collocations:** `risk-benefit communication` — communication; `explain a benefit–risk balance` — explain; `transparent uncertainty` — uncertainty.
**Examples:** `Risk-benefit communication compared a rare adverse event with the much larger risk of severe disease.` → Context giúp hiểu magnitude.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `위험-편익 소통` — risk-benefit communication.

## 24. vaccination registry /væk səˈneɪʃən ˈrɛdʒɪstri/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a record system that tracks administered vaccine doses, dates, products, eligibility, and follow-up.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** vaccination registry giúp biết ai đã dose nào và ai cần follow-up.
**Grammar & collocations:** `vaccination-registry record` — record; `update the registry` — update; `dose history` — history.
**Examples:** `The vaccination registry identified children who had started but not completed the series.` → Registry hỗ trợ recall.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `예방접종 등록부` — vaccination registry.

## 25. immunization equity audit /ˌɪmjənəˈzeɪʃən ˈɛkwəti ˈɔdɪt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a structured review of whether immunization access, coverage, safety, and benefits are distributed fairly across groups.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** immunization equity audit tìm group bị bỏ lại và nguyên nhân system tạo gap.
**Grammar & collocations:** `immunization-equity audit` — audit; `audit coverage by group` — audit; `distributional gap` — gap.
**Examples:** `The immunization equity audit showed lower coverage among migrants despite nearby clinics.` → Proximity không luôn là access.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `예방접종 형평성 감사` — immunization equity audit.

## Review in context — Part 1

A **vaccine effectiveness estimate** may use a **test-negative control** to compare protection under real-world conditions. **Waning protection**, **post-immunization infection**, a **dose-response schedule**, and a **booster response** shape interpretation. **Correlates of protection** and **immune imprinting** help explain immune differences. A **herd effect** and **indirect protection** can benefit people who are not fully vaccinated, while **vaccine failure** requires careful investigation.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 1:**

**Vaccine effectiveness estimate** có thể dùng **test-negative control** để so protection trong real world. **Waning protection**, **post-immunization infection**, **dose-response schedule** và **booster response** định hình interpretation. **Correlates of protection** và **immune imprinting** giúp giải thích khác biệt immune. **Herd effect** và **indirect protection** có thể bảo vệ người chưa đủ dose, còn **vaccine failure** cần investigation cẩn thận.

## Review in context — Part 2

**Primary series completion** depends on the **uptake denominator** and the **immunization coverage shortfall**. A **never-vaccinated child** may need a **delayed-dose recovery campaign** guided by a **local immunization microplan**. A **cold-chain dropout** can invalidate doses, while a **vaccination missed-contact event** reveals a fixable workflow gap. A **post-immunization adverse event** may create a **vaccine safety signal**, which must be compared with **background incidence**. **Risk-benefit communication**, a reliable **vaccination registry**, and an **immunization equity audit** support trust.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 2:**

**Primary series completion** phụ thuộc **uptake denominator** và **immunization coverage shortfall**. **Never-vaccinated child** có thể cần **delayed-dose recovery campaign** theo **local immunization microplan**. **Cold-chain dropout** có thể làm dose không còn usable, còn **vaccination missed-contact event** cho thấy gap workflow có thể sửa. **Post-immunization adverse event** có thể tạo **vaccine safety signal**, cần được so với **background incidence**. **Risk-benefit communication**, **vaccination registry** đáng tin và **immunization equity audit** hỗ trợ trust.
