# C2 Vocabulary — Transit robotics and rail operations

This lesson follows public-transit automation from passenger safety and inspection to dispatch and station accessibility. The network is **transit robot → autonomous shuttle → driverless bus → rail inspection robot → track inspection robot → platform safety robot → station cleaning robot → ticketing robot → passenger flow monitoring → crowd density estimation → platform edge detection → door obstruction detection → train coupling robot → rail grinding robot → rail flaw detection → track geometry measurement → overhead line inspection → tunnel inspection robot → transit depot automation → vehicle dispatch optimization**.

## 1. transit robot /ˈtrænzɪt ˈroʊbɑːt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a robot that supports passenger transport, station service, infrastructure inspection, or transit operations.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** robot hỗ trợ passenger transport, station service, infrastructure inspection hoặc transit operation.
**Grammar & collocations:** `transit-robot deployment` — deployment; `operate in a public station` — hoạt động trong station công cộng.
**Examples:** `The transit robot guided passengers toward the accessible platform entrance.` → Transit robot hướng hành khách tới lối vào platform accessible.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `교통 로봇`.

## 2. autonomous shuttle /ɔːˈtɑːnəməs ˈʃʌtəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a small automated vehicle that carries passengers along a defined route.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** vehicle nhỏ tự động chở passenger theo route định.
**Grammar & collocations:** `autonomous-shuttle route` — route; `serve a campus loop` — phục vụ campus loop.
**Examples:** `The autonomous shuttle slowed before the pedestrian crossing and announced its approach.` → Autonomous shuttle giảm tốc trước pedestrian crossing và báo approaching.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `자율 셔틀`.

## 3. driverless bus /ˈdraɪvərləs bʌs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a bus that operates without a human driver controlling its normal motion.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** bus chạy mà không có human driver control motion bình thường.
**Grammar & collocations:** `driverless-bus supervisor` — supervisor; `monitor a route remotely` — monitor route từ xa.
**Examples:** `The driverless bus completed the loop while a supervisor watched the exception channel.` → Driverless bus hoàn tất loop khi supervisor theo dõi exception channel.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `무인 버스`.

## 4. rail inspection robot /reɪl ɪnˈspekʃən ˈroʊbɑːt/

## 9. passenger flow monitoring /ˈpæsəndʒər floʊ ˈmɑːnətərɪŋ/

## 11. platform edge detection /ˈplætfɔːrm edʒ dɪˈtekʃən/

## 14. rail grinding robot /reɪl ˈɡraɪndɪŋ ˈroʊbɑːt/

## 19. transit depot automation /ˈtrænzɪt ˈdiːpoʊ ˌɔːtəˈmeɪʃən/

**Core meaning (English):** automating vehicle parking, charging, cleaning, inspection, and dispatch inside a transit depot.

## 20. vehicle dispatch optimization /ˈviːɪkəl dɪˈspætʃ ˌɑːptəməˈzeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** optimizing which vehicles serve which trips and when they leave a depot.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** tối ưu vehicle nào phục vụ trip nào và rời depot khi nào.

**Examples:** `Vehicle dispatch optimization assigned the newest bus to the route with the steepest climb.` → Vehicle dispatch optimization gán bus mới nhất cho route dốc nhất.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `차량 배차 최적화`.

## Review in context

A **transit robot**, **autonomous shuttle**, or **driverless bus** supports passenger service, while a **rail inspection robot**, **track inspection robot**, and **platform safety robot** protect infrastructure and people. A **station cleaning robot** and **ticketing robot** work in stations; **passenger flow monitoring**, **crowd density estimation**, **platform edge detection**, and **door obstruction detection** manage safety. A **train coupling robot**, **rail grinding robot**, **rail flaw detection**, and **track geometry measurement** maintain vehicles and track. **Overhead line inspection**, a **tunnel inspection robot**, **transit depot automation**, and **vehicle dispatch optimization** complete the operational network.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:** **Transit robot**, **autonomous shuttle** hoặc **driverless bus** hỗ trợ passenger service, còn **rail inspection robot**, **track inspection robot** và **platform safety robot** bảo vệ infrastructure và người. **Station cleaning robot** và **ticketing robot** làm việc trong station; **passenger flow monitoring**, **crowd density estimation**, **platform edge detection** và **door obstruction detection** quản lý safety. **Train coupling robot**, **rail grinding robot**, **rail flaw detection** và **track geometry measurement** duy trì vehicle/track. **Overhead line inspection**, **tunnel inspection robot**, **transit depot automation** và **vehicle dispatch optimization** hoàn thiện operational network.
