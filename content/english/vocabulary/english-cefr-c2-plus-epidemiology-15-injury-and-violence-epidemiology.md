# C2+ Vocabulary — Injury and violence epidemiology

This lesson follows how injury events are classified, counted, and linked to prevention across emergency, community, and surveillance systems. The network is **injury mechanism → external cause coding → severity score → injury surveillance registry → case fatality → years of potential life lost → injury pyramid → near-miss event → sentinel injury → violence interruption → firearm injury surveillance → road injury surveillance → poison center signal → overdose cluster → syndromic overdose surveillance → injury intent classification → unintentional injury → self-harm event → assault-related injury → fall-risk profile → crash severity → prevention opportunity → injury inequity → trauma burden**.

## 1. injury mechanism /ˈɪndʒəri ˈmɛkəˌnɪzəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the physical, chemical, or behavioral process that produces an injury.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** injury mechanism giải thích injury xảy ra bằng force, object, exposure hoặc action nào.
**Grammar & collocations:** `injury-mechanism classification` — classification; `identify the mechanism` — identify; `mechanical force` — force.
**Examples:** `The injury mechanism was a fall from height rather than a traffic collision.` → Mechanism khác thì prevention khác.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `손상 기전` — injury mechanism.

## 2. external cause coding /ɪkˈstɜrnəl kɔz ˈkoʊdɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** coding of the event, circumstance, place, and activity that caused an injury rather than the injury diagnosis alone.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** external cause coding ghi lại “injury xảy ra vì chuyện gì” ngoài diagnosis.
**Grammar & collocations:** `external-cause coding` — coding; `code the circumstance` — code; `injury classification` — classification.
**Examples:** `External cause coding showed that many emergency visits followed workplace machinery incidents.` → Coding mở đường cho prevention.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `외인 코드화` — external cause coding.

## 3. severity score /səˈvɛrəti skɔr/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a standardized measure summarizing the seriousness of an injury or illness using clinical findings and outcomes.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** severity score biến nhiều clinical sign thành mức severity để compare và triage.
**Grammar & collocations:** `injury-severity score` — score; `calculate severity` — calculate; `high-severity trauma` — trauma.
**Examples:** `The severity score predicted which patients needed transfer to a trauma center.` → Score hỗ trợ triage.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `중증도 점수` — severity score.

## 4. injury surveillance registry /ˈɪndʒəri sərˈveɪləns ˈrɛdʒɪstri/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an organized database that captures injury events, circumstances, treatment, and outcomes over time.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** injury surveillance registry giữ event và outcome để nhìn pattern dài hạn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `injury-surveillance registry` — registry; `maintain an injury registry` — maintain; `registry case` — case.
**Examples:** `The injury surveillance registry linked emergency care with later disability records.` → Registry nối acute với long-term outcome.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `손상 감시 등록부` — injury surveillance registry.

## 5. case fatality /ˈkeɪs fəˈtæləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the proportion of identified cases of a condition or injury that result in death within a defined period.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** case fatality đo mức nguy hiểm trong những case đã được nhận diện.
**Grammar & collocations:** `case-fatality estimate` — estimate; `compare case fatality` — compare; `fatal outcome` — outcome.
**Examples:** `Case fatality fell after faster transfer and hemorrhage control were introduced.` → Care system đổi outcome.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `치명률` — case fatality.

## 6. years of potential life lost /jɪrz əv pəˈtɛnʃəl laɪf lɔst/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** years lost when a death occurs before a chosen reference age or expected lifespan.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** years of potential life lost nhấn mạnh premature death và age lúc death xảy ra.
**Grammar & collocations:** `years-of-potential-life-lost estimate` — estimate; `count premature deaths` — count; `reference age` — age.
**Examples:** `Young-adult overdose deaths produced many years of potential life lost despite fewer total deaths.` → Age làm burden khác.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `잠재 수명 손실 연수` — years of potential life lost.

## 7. injury pyramid /ˈɪndʒəri ˈpɪrəmɪd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a model showing that fatal injuries are the small visible top of a larger number of nonfatal and untreated events.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** injury pyramid nhắc death chỉ là top; bên dưới là hospital, clinic và unreported injuries.
**Grammar & collocations:** `injury-pyramid model` — model; `visualize the injury burden` — visualize; `nonfatal injury` — injury.
**Examples:** `The injury pyramid showed why death certificates alone underestimated community harm.` → Fatal count không đủ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `손상 피라미드` — injury pyramid.

## 8. near-miss event /ˈnɪr mɪs ɪˈvɛnt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an event that could have caused injury but did not, often revealing a preventable hazard.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** near-miss event là warning trước injury thật.
**Grammar & collocations:** `near-miss event report` — report; `investigate a near miss` — investigate; `latent hazard` — hazard.
**Examples:** `The near-miss event exposed a slippery loading ramp before anyone was seriously injured.` → Near miss là cơ hội prevention.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `아차 사고` — near-miss event.

## 9. sentinel injury /ˈsɛntənəl ˈɪndʒəri/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an injury whose occurrence signals a serious hazard, system failure, or possible abuse requiring investigation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** sentinel injury là injury báo động cần tìm cause rộng hơn individual case.
**Grammar & collocations:** `sentinel-injury review` — review; `investigate a sentinel injury` — investigate; `warning signal` — signal.
**Examples:** `A sentinel injury in a child triggered a safeguarding review.` → Injury signal có thể mở investigation khác.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `경고성 손상` — sentinel injury.

## 10. violence interruption /ˈvaɪələns ˌɪntəˈrʌpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** community-based action that detects, mediates, and prevents retaliatory violence before it spreads.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** violence interruption can thiệp vào chain bạo lực trước event tiếp theo.
**Grammar & collocations:** `violence-interruption program` — program; `mediate a conflict` — mediate; `retaliatory violence` — violence.
**Examples:** `Violence interruption workers mediated the conflict and connected injured people with support.` → Prevention cần trusted local actors.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `폭력 중단 개입` — violence interruption.

## 11. firearm injury surveillance /ˈfaɪərˌɑrm ˈɪndʒəri sərˈveɪləns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** systematic monitoring of firearm-related injuries, deaths, circumstances, and affected populations.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** firearm injury surveillance nhìn cả injury, death, intent và context.
**Grammar & collocations:** `firearm-injury surveillance` — surveillance; `monitor firearm injuries` — monitor; `weapon-related event` — event.
**Examples:** `Firearm injury surveillance identified a rise in nonfatal injuries not visible in mortality data.` → Nonfatal data bổ sung picture.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `총기 손상 감시` — firearm injury surveillance.

## 12. road injury surveillance /roʊd ˈɪndʒəri sərˈveɪləns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** monitoring of traffic-related injuries, deaths, locations, road users, and contributing conditions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** road injury surveillance nối crash với người, road, vehicle và timing.
**Grammar & collocations:** `road-injury surveillance` — surveillance; `monitor traffic injuries` — monitor; `road-user group` — group.
**Examples:** `Road injury surveillance showed that pedestrian injuries clustered around poorly lit crossings.` → Location gợi prevention.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `도로 손상 감시` — road injury surveillance.

## 13. poison center signal /ˈpɔɪzən ˈsɛntər ˈsɪɡnəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an unusual change in calls or reports to a poison center that may indicate a new exposure or cluster.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** poison center signal là early signal từ call data trước hospital confirmation.
**Grammar & collocations:** `poison-center signal` — signal; `detect an exposure cluster` — detect; `toxicology call` — call.
**Examples:** `A poison center signal revealed misuse of a newly marketed cleaning product.` → Call system giúp thấy hazard sớm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `중독관리센터 신호` — poison center signal.

## 14. overdose cluster /ˈoʊvərˌdoʊs ˈklʌstər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an unusual concentration of overdose events in time, place, population, or substance pattern.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** overdose cluster là group overdose liên kết bởi time, location hoặc supply.
**Grammar & collocations:** `overdose-cluster alert` — alert; `investigate an overdose cluster` — investigate; `toxic supply` — supply.
**Examples:** `The overdose cluster followed a sudden change in the local drug supply.` → Supply change có thể tạo cluster nhanh.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `과다복용 군집` — overdose cluster.

## 15. syndromic overdose surveillance /sɪnˈdrɑmɪk ˈoʊvərˌdoʊs sərˈveɪləns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** near-real-time monitoring of symptoms, emergency treatment, or naloxone use to detect overdose patterns before final coding.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** syndromic overdose surveillance dùng early clinical signal thay vì chờ death certificate.
**Grammar & collocations:** `syndromic-overdose surveillance` — surveillance; `monitor naloxone use` — monitor; `near-real-time signal` — signal.
**Examples:** `Syndromic overdose surveillance detected a spike in respiratory depression within hours.` → Signal nhanh giúp response sớm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `증후군 기반 과다복용 감시` — syndromic overdose surveillance.

## 16. injury intent classification /ˈɪndʒəri ɪnˈtɛnt ˌklæsəfəˈkeɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** classification of whether an injury was unintentional, self-inflicted, inflicted by another person, or of undetermined intent.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** injury intent classification tách accident, self-harm, assault và unknown.
**Grammar & collocations:** `injury-intent classification` — classification; `classify intent` — classify; `intent category` — category.
**Examples:** `Injury intent classification changed after interview data clarified that the event was self-inflicted.` → Context sửa coding.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `손상 의도 분류` — injury intent classification.

## 17. unintentional injury /ˌʌnɪnˈtɛnʃənəl ˈɪndʒəri/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** injury occurring without an intention to harm oneself or another person.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** unintentional injury vẫn có preventable cause dù không có intent gây harm.
**Grammar & collocations:** `unintentional-injury prevention` — prevention; `classify an injury` — classify; `accidental event` — event.
**Examples:** `Unintentional injury prevention focused on safer storage, road design, and fall reduction.` → “Accidental” không có nghĩa không prevent được.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `비의도적 손상` — unintentional injury.

## 18. self-harm event /sɛlf hɑrm ɪˈvɛnt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an act or episode in which a person intentionally causes injury or poisoning to themselves, regardless of suicidal intent.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** self-harm event mô tả intentional self-injury, không tự động kết luận về motive.
**Grammar & collocations:** `self-harm event` — event; `assess self-harm` — assess; `intent clarification` — clarification.
**Examples:** `The self-harm event prompted immediate safety planning and a careful psychosocial assessment.` → Response cần safety và context.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `자해 사건` — self-harm event.

## 19. assault-related injury /əˈsɔlt rɪˈleɪtɪd ˈɪndʒəri/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an injury caused by intentional physical force from another person.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** assault-related injury là injury do interpersonal violence.
**Grammar & collocations:** `assault-related injury` — injury; `document an assault` — document; `interpersonal violence` — violence.
**Examples:** `Assault-related injury surveillance identified repeat harm around the same nightlife corridor.` → Pattern location có thể dẫn prevention.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `폭행 관련 손상` — assault-related injury.

## 20. fall-risk profile /fɔl rɪsk ˈproʊfaɪl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a structured description of personal, environmental, and medication factors associated with falling.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** fall-risk profile gom balance, medication, vision và home hazard.
**Grammar & collocations:** `fall-risk profile` — profile; `assess fall risk` — assess; `home hazard` — hazard.

## 21. crash severity /kræʃ səˈvɛrəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the seriousness of injury or damage resulting from a transport collision.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** crash severity nối speed, vehicle, road và protection với outcome injury.
**Grammar & collocations:** `crash-severity model` — model; `estimate crash severity` — estimate; `serious collision` — collision.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `충돌 중증도` — crash severity.

## 22. prevention opportunity /prɪˈvɛnʃən ˌɑpərˈtuːnəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a point in a causal chain where a feasible action could prevent an injury or reduce its harm.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** prevention opportunity là chỗ intervention có leverage trong chain.
**Grammar & collocations:** `prevention-opportunity analysis` — analysis; `identify a prevention opportunity` — identify; `modifiable factor` — factor.
**Examples:** `The review found a prevention opportunity in safer medication packaging.` → Opportunity chuyển case thành design change.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `예방 기회` — prevention opportunity.

## 23. injury inequity /ˈɪndʒəri ɪnˈɛkwəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an unfair difference in injury exposure, treatment, recovery, or outcome across social groups.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** injury inequity là injury burden phân bố không công bằng do social condition.
**Grammar & collocations:** `injury-inequity analysis` — analysis; `measure injury inequity` — measure; `unequal recovery` — recovery.
**Examples:** `Injury inequity was visible in slower rehabilitation access for low-income patients.` → Care access ảnh hưởng recovery.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `손상 불평등` — injury inequity.

## 24. trauma burden /ˈtrɔmə ˈbɜrdən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the combined health, disability, emotional, financial, and service impact of traumatic injury.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** trauma burden rộng hơn death: gồm disability, caregiver cost và system load.
**Grammar & collocations:** `trauma-burden estimate` — estimate; `measure trauma burden` — measure; `long-term disability` — disability.
**Examples:** `The trauma burden remained high because many survivors needed years of rehabilitation.` → Survivors cũng tạo burden dài.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `외상 부담` — trauma burden.

## 25. injury surveillance case definition /ˈɪndʒəri sərˈveɪləns keɪs ˌdɛfəˈnɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the operational criteria used to decide which injury events enter a surveillance system.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** injury surveillance case definition quyết định event nào được count và compare.
**Grammar & collocations:** `injury-surveillance case definition` — definition; `apply a case definition` — apply; `reportable injury` — injury.
**Examples:** `The injury surveillance case definition was broadened to include emergency-treated poisoning.` → Definition rộng hơn bắt thêm burden.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `손상 감시 사례 정의` — injury surveillance case definition.

## Review in context — Part 1

An **injury mechanism**, **external cause coding**, and a **severity score** organize an **injury surveillance registry**. **Case fatality**, **years of potential life lost**, and the **injury pyramid** describe different parts of harm. A **near-miss event** can prevent a **sentinel injury**, while **violence interruption** addresses risk before another event. **Firearm injury surveillance** and **road injury surveillance** reveal patterns not visible in routine clinical summaries.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 1:**

**Injury mechanism**, **external cause coding** và **severity score** tổ chức **injury surveillance registry**. **Case fatality**, **years of potential life lost** và **injury pyramid** mô tả các phần khác nhau của harm. **Near-miss event** có thể ngăn **sentinel injury**, còn **violence interruption** xử lý risk trước event tiếp theo. **Firearm injury surveillance** và **road injury surveillance** cho thấy pattern mà clinical summary thường không thấy.

## Review in context — Part 2

A **poison center signal** may precede an **overdose cluster**, and **syndromic overdose surveillance** can respond quickly. **Injury intent classification** separates **unintentional injury**, a **self-harm event**, and **assault-related injury**. A **fall-risk profile** and **crash severity** identify different prevention needs. Each case creates a **prevention opportunity**, while **injury inequity**, **trauma burden**, and the **injury surveillance case definition** determine whose harm is counted and addressed.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 2:**

**Poison center signal** có thể xuất hiện trước **overdose cluster**, còn **syndromic overdose surveillance** có thể response nhanh. **Injury intent classification** tách **unintentional injury**, **self-harm event** và **assault-related injury**. **Fall-risk profile** và **crash severity** tạo nhu cầu prevention khác nhau. Mỗi case mở một **prevention opportunity**, còn **injury inequity**, **trauma burden** và **injury surveillance case definition** quyết định harm của ai được count và xử lý.
