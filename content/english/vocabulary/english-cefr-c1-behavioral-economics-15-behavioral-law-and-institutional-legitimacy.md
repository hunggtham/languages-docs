# C1 Vocabulary 15 — Behavioral law and institutional legitimacy

This lesson follows how people interpret rules, sanctions, evidence, and official responses when deciding whether institutions deserve trust and compliance.

Flow: **legal ambiguity aversion → procedural justice cue → sanction salience → punishment preference → restorative justice belief → rule legitimacy → authority acceptance → due-process heuristic → compliance intention → legal cynicism → deterrence perception → fairness norm → trust repair → institutional apology → compensation framing → grievance escalation → dispute resolution choice → evidence credibility → witness confidence bias → regulatory behavior**.

## 1. legal ambiguity aversion /ˈliːɡəl ˌæmbɪˈɡjuəti əˈvɝʒən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a tendency to avoid choices or obligations when legal consequences are unclear or difficult to predict.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** xu hướng tránh choice hoặc obligation khi consequence pháp lý không rõ và khó predict.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong legal ambiguity aversion` — aversion; `reduce legal ambiguity aversion` — reduce.
**Examples:** `Legal ambiguity aversion made the small firm delay an innovative contract.` → Aversion ambiguity legal khiến firm nhỏ trì hoãn contract innovative.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `법적 모호성 회피`.

## 2. procedural justice cue /prəˈsiːdʒərəl ˈdʒʌstɪs kjuː/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a signal that a decision process is respectful, neutral, and open to a person's voice.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** signal rằng process decision respectful, neutral và cho người involved được nói.
**Grammar & collocations:** `visible procedural justice cue` — cue; `provide a procedural justice cue` — provide.
**Examples:** `A procedural justice cue increased acceptance even when the appeal was unsuccessful.` → Cue justice procedural tăng acceptance dù appeal không thành công.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `절차적 정의 단서`.

## 3. sanction salience /ˈsæŋkʃən ˈseɪliəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** how noticeable and psychologically important a possible penalty is when someone considers violating a rule.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mức penalty possible noticeable và psychologically important khi cân nhắc vi phạm rule.
**Grammar & collocations:** `low sanction salience` — salience; `increase sanction salience` — increase.
**Examples:** `Sanction salience rose after the agency published a clear example of enforcement.` → Salience sanction tăng sau khi agency publish example enforcement rõ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `제재 현저성`.

## 4. punishment preference /ˈpʌnɪʃmənt ˈprɛfərəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a person's tendency to favor penalties over education, repair, or support after wrongdoing.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** xu hướng thích penalty hơn education, repair hoặc support sau wrongdoing.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strict punishment preference` — preference; `explain a punishment preference` — explain.
**Examples:** `A strong punishment preference overlooked the chance to prevent the same harm from recurring.` → Preference punishment mạnh bỏ qua cơ hội ngăn harm lặp lại.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `처벌 선호`.

## 5. restorative justice belief /rɪˈstɔrətɪv ˈdʒʌstɪs bɪˈliːf/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a belief that justice should repair harm, involve affected people, and rebuild relationships where possible.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** belief justice nên repair harm, involve người affected và rebuild relationship nếu possible.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong restorative justice belief` — belief; `support restorative justice beliefs` — support.
**Examples:** `Her restorative justice belief favored a mediated conversation over an automatic fine.` → Belief justice restorative của cô ấy favor conversation mediated hơn fine automatic.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `회복적 사법 신념`.

## 6. rule legitimacy /ruːl lɪˈdʒɪtəməsi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the perceived rightfulness of a rule, making people more willing to follow it even without immediate monitoring.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cảm nhận rule là rightful khiến người ta follow dù không bị monitor ngay.
**Grammar & collocations:** `perceived rule legitimacy` — legitimacy; `strengthen rule legitimacy` — strengthen.
**Examples:** `Rule legitimacy increased when residents helped design the neighborhood restriction.` → Legitimacy rule tăng khi resident tham gia design restriction khu phố.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `규칙 정당성`.

## 7. authority acceptance /əˈθɔrəti əkˈsɛptəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** willingness to recognize an institution's right to make and enforce decisions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** willingness công nhận institution có quyền make và enforce decision.
**Grammar & collocations:** `conditional authority acceptance` — acceptance; `maintain authority acceptance` — maintain.
**Examples:** `Authority acceptance weakened after officials applied the rule inconsistently.` → Acceptance authority yếu đi sau khi official apply rule không nhất quán.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `권위 수용`.

## 8. due-process heuristic /ˌduː ˈprɑsɛs hjʊrɪstɪk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a quick judgment that a decision is trustworthy because the process appears fair and properly followed.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** judgment nhanh rằng decision trustworthy vì process có vẻ fair và đúng procedure.
**Grammar & collocations:** `reliable due-process heuristic` — heuristic; `activate a due-process heuristic` — activate.
**Examples:** `The written hearing record supported a due-process heuristic among people who lost the case.` → Record hearing written hỗ trợ heuristic due-process ở người thua case.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `적법 절차 휴리스틱`.

## 9. compliance intention /kəmˈplaɪəns ɪnˈtɛnʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a stated or inferred willingness to follow a rule, request, or official recommendation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** willingness được state hoặc infer để follow rule, request hoặc recommendation official.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high compliance intention` — intention; `measure compliance intention` — measure.
**Examples:** `Clear reasons for the rule raised compliance intention more than a threatening slogan did.` → Reason rõ cho rule tăng intention compliance hơn slogan đe dọa.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `준수 의도`.

## 10. legal cynicism /ˈliːɡəl ˈsɪnəˌsɪzəm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a belief that laws and legal authorities are unworthy of respect or cannot be trusted to act fairly.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** belief law và legal authority không đáng respect hoặc không act fair.
**Grammar & collocations:** `deep legal cynicism` — cynicism; `reduce legal cynicism` — reduce.
**Examples:** `Legal cynicism made residents ignore a legitimate safety notice.` → Cynicism legal khiến resident ignore safety notice legitimate.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `법적 냉소주의`.

## 11. deterrence perception /dɪˈtɝrəns pərˈsɛpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a person's belief about how likely and effective a penalty would be if a rule were broken.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** belief penalty likely và effective thế nào nếu break rule.
**Grammar & collocations:** `accurate deterrence perception` — perception; `shift deterrence perception` — shift.
**Examples:** `Deterrence perception changed when inspections became regular rather than occasional.` → Perception deterrence đổi khi inspection regular thay vì occasional.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `억지력 인식`.

## 12. fairness norm /ˈfɛrnəs nɔrm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a shared expectation about how benefits, burdens, and procedures should be distributed.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** expectation shared về cách distribute benefit, burden và procedure.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong fairness norm` — norm; `violate a fairness norm` — violate.

## 13. trust repair /trʌst rɪˈpɛr/
**Part of speech:** noun

**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** action restore confidence sau khi institution hoặc person gây thất vọng hay harm.

## 18. evidence credibility /ˈɛvədəns krɛdəˈbɪləti/

**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** reliability và relevance được perceive của information dùng support legal hoặc policy decision.

## 19. witness confidence bias /ˈwɪtnəs ˈkɑnfədəns baɪəs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a tendency to treat a witness's confidence as stronger evidence of accuracy than it actually is.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** tendency xem confidence witness là evidence accuracy mạnh hơn thực tế.
**Grammar & collocations:** `dangerous witness confidence bias` — bias; `guard against witness confidence bias` — guard.

## 20. regulatory behavior /ˈrɛɡjələtɔri bɪˈheɪvjər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the actions people or organizations take in response to rules, inspections, incentives, and enforcement institutions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** action của người hoặc organization đáp lại rule, inspection, incentive và enforcement institution.
**Grammar & collocations:** `adaptive regulatory behavior` — behavior; `observe regulatory behavior` — observe.
**Examples:** `Regulatory behavior changed after firms received predictable guidance instead of surprise penalties.` → Behavior regulatory đổi sau khi firm nhận guidance predictable thay vì penalty bất ngờ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `규제 행동`.

## Review in context

People often avoid unclear obligations because **legal ambiguity aversion** makes unpredictable consequences feel dangerous. A **procedural justice cue** can improve acceptance, while **sanction salience** and **punishment preference** shape whether an institution responds through penalties or repair. A **restorative justice belief** supports dialogue, and **rule legitimacy** encourages compliance even when monitoring is weak. **Authority acceptance** grows when a **due-process heuristic** suggests that the process was neutral. Clear reasons can raise **compliance intention**, whereas repeated unfairness can produce **legal cynicism**. **Deterrence perception** depends on whether enforcement seems likely, but a shared **fairness norm** also matters. After a failure, **trust repair**, an honest **institutional apology**, and respectful **compensation framing** may prevent **grievance escalation**. People then make a **dispute resolution choice** based partly on **evidence credibility**; decision-makers must guard against **witness confidence bias**. In the long run, these judgments shape **regulatory behavior** and determine whether rules are experienced as legitimate guidance or arbitrary control.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

Mọi người thường tránh obligation không rõ vì **legal ambiguity aversion** khiến consequence không predict được trở nên nguy hiểm. **Procedural justice cue** có thể cải thiện acceptance, trong khi **sanction salience** và **punishment preference** định hình institution phản hồi bằng penalty hay repair. **Restorative justice belief** ủng hộ dialogue, còn **rule legitimacy** khuyến khích compliance ngay cả khi monitoring yếu. **Authority acceptance** tăng khi **due-process heuristic** cho thấy process neutral. Reason rõ có thể nâng **compliance intention**, còn unfairness lặp lại có thể tạo **legal cynicism**. **Deterrence perception** phụ thuộc enforcement có vẻ likely không, nhưng **fairness norm** shared cũng quan trọng. Sau failure, **trust repair**, **institutional apology** chân thành và **compensation framing** respectful có thể ngăn **grievance escalation**. Sau đó, người ta chọn **dispute resolution choice** partly dựa trên **evidence credibility**; decision-maker phải cảnh giác với **witness confidence bias**. Về lâu dài, các judgment này định hình **regulatory behavior** và quyết định rule được trải nghiệm như guidance legitimate hay control arbitrary.
