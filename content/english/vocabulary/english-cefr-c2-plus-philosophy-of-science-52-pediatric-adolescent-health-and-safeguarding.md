# C2+ Vocabulary — Pediatric, adolescent, and safeguarding care

This lesson follows how services support development, protect children, respect adolescent autonomy, and coordinate complex pediatric care. The network is **developmental surveillance → early intervention → growth faltering → failure to thrive → pediatric feeding disorder → neurodevelopmental delay → school health → adolescent-friendly service → confidential adolescent care → pubertal delay → eating disorder screening → self-harm assessment → consent maturity → transition to adult care → family-centered care → play therapy → child safeguarding → mandatory reporting → nonaccidental injury → medically complex child → care coordination → home nursing → pediatric palliative care → caregiver partnership → shared pediatric decision-making**.

## 1. developmental surveillance /dɪˌvɛləpˈmɛntl sərˈveɪləns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** ongoing observation and discussion of a child's development, behavior, and functioning to identify concerns early.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** developmental surveillance là theo dõi liên tục qua family, clinic và daily context.
**Grammar & collocations:** `developmental-surveillance visit` — visit; `monitor developmental progress` — monitor; `parent concern` — concern.
**Examples:** `Developmental surveillance noticed a language concern before the child entered school.` → Phát hiện sớm mở cơ hội support.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `발달 감시` — developmental surveillance.

## 2. early intervention /ˈɜrli ˌɪntərˈvɛnʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** support provided early in a child's development to address delays, disability, health needs, or family barriers.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** early intervention đưa support trước khi delay tạo gap lớn hơn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `early-intervention service` — service; `intervene early` — intervene; `developmental support` — support.
**Examples:** `Early intervention combined speech therapy, parent coaching, and adapted play at home.` → Support đi vào daily life.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `조기 개입` — early intervention.

## 3. growth faltering /ɡroʊθ ˈfɔltərɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** slower or inadequate physical growth that requires assessment of health, nutrition, feeding, development, and social context.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** growth faltering là pattern tăng trưởng chậm, cần tìm nhiều nguyên nhân.
**Grammar & collocations:** `growth-faltering assessment` — assessment; `monitor growth trajectory` — monitor; `nutrition concern` — concern.
**Examples:** `Growth faltering led the team to review feeding mechanics, food access, and chronic symptoms together.` → Không quy mọi thứ cho parent.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `성장 지연` — growth faltering.

## 4. failure to thrive /ˈfeɪljər tə θraɪv/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a clinical description of inadequate growth or development in a child that prompts broad evaluation and support.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** failure to thrive là clinical label cần investigation, không phải phán xét gia đình.
**Grammar & collocations:** `failure-to-thrive workup` — workup; `evaluate poor growth` — evaluate; `feeding history` — history.
**Examples:** `The failure-to-thrive workup included observation of a meal rather than assumptions about parental effort.` → Observe thực tế trước khi kết luận.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `성장 장애` — failure to thrive.

## 5. pediatric feeding disorder /ˌpiːdiˈætrɪk ˈfiːdɪŋ dɪˈsɔrdər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** impaired oral intake that is not age-appropriate and is associated with medical, nutritional, feeding-skill, or psychosocial dysfunction.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** pediatric feeding disorder có thể đến từ body, skill, nutrition hoặc relationship với food.
**Grammar & collocations:** `pediatric-feeding-disorder team` — team; `assess feeding skills` — assess; `oral intake` — intake.
**Examples:** `The pediatric feeding disorder plan addressed swallowing safety, sensory tolerance, and family stress.` → Plan nhiều dimension.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `소아 섭식 장애` — pediatric feeding disorder.

## 6. neurodevelopmental delay /ˌnʊroʊdɪˈvɛləpməntl dɪˈleɪ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** slower-than-expected development in one or more domains such as language, motor skills, learning, behavior, or social communication.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** neurodevelopmental delay mô tả tốc độ phát triển, không phải giới hạn cố định của child.
**Grammar & collocations:** `neurodevelopmental-delay assessment` — assessment; `support developmental domains` — support; `developmental profile` — profile.
**Examples:** `Neurodevelopmental delay prompted an assessment of hearing, language, motor skills, and learning context.` → Assessment nhiều domain.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `신경발달 지연` — neurodevelopmental delay.

## 7. school health /skuːl hɛlθ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** health services, prevention, education, and support delivered through or in partnership with schools.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** school health đưa health support tới nơi child học và sống hàng ngày.
**Grammar & collocations:** `school-health program` — program; `deliver school health services` — deliver; `student well-being` — well-being.
**Examples:** `School health linked hearing checks, vaccination reminders, counseling, and safe meal access.` → School là platform health rộng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `학교 보건` — school health.

## 8. adolescent-friendly service /ˌædəˈlɛsənt ˈfrɛndli ˈsɜrvɪs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a service designed around adolescents' privacy, communication, accessibility, developmental stage, and need for respectful participation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** adolescent-friendly service làm teen thấy safe, được nghe và không bị infantilize.
**Grammar & collocations:** `adolescent-friendly-service standard` — standard; `adapt youth services` — adapt; `confidential setting` — setting.
**Examples:** `The adolescent-friendly service offered after-school hours and explained confidentiality before asking sensitive questions.` → Access và privacy cùng quan trọng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `청소년 친화적 서비스` — adolescent-friendly service.

## 9. confidential adolescent care /ˌkɑnfəˈdɛnʃəl ˌædəˈlɛsənt kɛr/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** health care for adolescents that protects privacy within legal and safety limits and explains when information must be shared.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** confidential adolescent care xây trust bằng privacy rõ ràng và giới hạn minh bạch.
**Grammar & collocations:** `confidential-adolescent-care policy` — policy; `explain confidentiality limits` — explain; `private consultation` — consultation.
**Examples:** `Confidential adolescent care made clear that imminent danger would require protective disclosure.` → Privacy có safety limit.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `청소년 비밀 보장 진료` — confidential adolescent care.

## 10. pubertal delay /ˈpjuːbərtəl dɪˈleɪ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** later-than-expected onset or progression of puberty that may warrant assessment of normal variation, nutrition, chronic illness, or endocrine function.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** pubertal delay cần phân biệt variation bình thường với health condition.
**Grammar & collocations:** `pubertal-delay assessment` — assessment; `assess pubertal development` — assess; `normal variation` — variation.
**Examples:** `Pubertal delay was discussed sensitively because the adolescent was already worried about being different.` → Communication tránh shame.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `사춘기 지연` — pubertal delay.

## 11. eating disorder screening /ˈiːtɪŋ dɪˈsɔrdər ˈskriːnɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** structured assessment for restrictive, bingeing, purging, or other eating-related patterns that may threaten health.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** eating disorder screening tìm risk sớm và cần hỏi không phán xét.
**Grammar & collocations:** `eating-disorder-screening tool` — tool; `screen for disordered eating` — screen; `nonjudgmental question` — question.
**Examples:** `Eating disorder screening included private questions about control, fear, exercise, and compensatory behavior.` → Hỏi riêng và rộng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `섭식 장애 선별` — eating disorder screening.

## 12. self-harm assessment /ˌsɛlf hɑrm əˈsɛsmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** structured evaluation of self-injury, suicidal thoughts, intent, access to means, protective factors, and immediate safety.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** self-harm assessment nhìn behavior, intent, context và safety, không chỉ hỏi yes/no.
**Grammar & collocations:** `self-harm-assessment protocol` — protocol; `assess immediate safety` — assess; `protective factor` — factor.
**Examples:** `The self-harm assessment included a private conversation and a concrete safety plan.` → Assessment nối trực tiếp với plan.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `자해 평가` — self-harm assessment.

## 13. consent maturity /kənˈsɛnt məˈtʃʊrəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a young person's developing ability to understand information, appreciate consequences, and participate meaningfully in a health decision.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** consent maturity đánh giá capability thực tế, không chỉ tuổi trên giấy.
**Grammar & collocations:** `consent-maturity assessment` — assessment; `understand a health decision` — understand; `developing autonomy` — autonomy.
**Examples:** `The clinician assessed consent maturity by asking the adolescent to explain the options in their own words.` → Không chỉ hỏi “hiểu chưa?”.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `동의 성숙도` — consent maturity.

## 14. transition to adult care /trænˈzɪʃən tə əˈdʌlt kɛr/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** planned movement from pediatric services to adult services with preparation, information transfer, and continued support.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** transition to adult care là process chuẩn bị, không phải ngày sinh nhật đổi clinic.
**Grammar & collocations:** `transition-to-adult-care plan` — plan; `transfer medical information` — transfer; `self-management skill` — skill.
**Examples:** `The transition to adult care began years early with medication knowledge and appointment planning.` → Chuẩn bị dần, không đột ngột.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `성인 진료로의 전환` — transition to adult care.

## 15. family-centered care /ˈfæməli ˈsɛntərd kɛr/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** care that treats the family as a partner while respecting the child's voice, rights, development, and individual needs.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** family-centered care hợp tác với family nhưng không xóa voice của child.
**Grammar & collocations:** `family-centered-care model` — model; `partner with a family` — partner; `child voice` — voice.
**Examples:** `Family-centered care invited the child to choose which explanation tool felt easiest.` → Family và child cùng tham gia.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `가족 중심 돌봄` — family-centered care.

## 16. play therapy /pleɪ ˈθɛrəpi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** therapeutic use of play, toys, stories, and creative expression to help children communicate, regulate emotion, and process experience.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** play therapy dùng language tự nhiên của child là play.
**Grammar & collocations:** `play-therapy session` — session; `process emotion through play` — process; `therapeutic play` — play.
**Examples:** `Play therapy helped the child show fear about the procedure without needing an adult vocabulary.` → Play mở communication.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `놀이 치료` — play therapy.

## 17. child safeguarding /tʃaɪld ˈseɪfˌɡɑrdɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** coordinated action to protect children from abuse, neglect, exploitation, violence, and preventable harm.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** child safeguarding là responsibility của cả system, không chỉ một clinician.
**Grammar & collocations:** `child-safeguarding policy` — policy; `protect a child from harm` — protect; `multiagency response` — response.
**Examples:** `Child safeguarding required the clinic, school, and social service to share a clear protection plan.` → Nhiều agency cùng action.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `아동 보호` — child safeguarding.

## 18. mandatory reporting /ˈmændəˌtɔri rɪˈpɔrtɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** legally required reporting of suspected abuse, neglect, or serious danger to a designated authority.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mandatory reporting là duty pháp lý khi có suspicion đủ ngưỡng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `mandatory-reporting duty` — duty; `report suspected abuse` — report; `designated authority` — authority.
**Examples:** `Mandatory reporting was explained to the family as a protection process rather than a punishment.` → Giải thích để giảm fear.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `의무 신고` — mandatory reporting.

## 19. nonaccidental injury /ˌnɑnˌæksəˈdɛntl ˈɪndʒəri/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** injury caused intentionally or through abuse rather than by an ordinary accident.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** nonaccidental injury là injury cần xem mechanism và safety context kỹ.
**Grammar & collocations:** `nonaccidental-injury evaluation` — evaluation; `assess injury pattern` — assess; `protective investigation` — investigation.
**Examples:** `The nonaccidental injury evaluation compared the explanation with the child's developmental ability and injury pattern.` → Không chỉ nghe một explanation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `비우발적 손상` — nonaccidental injury.

## 20. medically complex child /ˈmɛdɪkəli ˈkɑmplɛks tʃaɪld/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a child with multiple significant health conditions, technology dependence, or coordinated medical and developmental needs.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** medically complex child cần nhiều team và care plan tích hợp.
**Grammar & collocations:** `medically-complex-child program` — program; `coordinate complex care` — coordinate; `technology dependence` — dependence.
**Examples:** `The medically complex child needed one shared plan for feeding, respiratory support, school, and transport.` → Plan chung giảm fragmentation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `의학적으로 복잡한 아동` — medically complex child.

## 21. care coordination /kɛr koʊˌɔrdəˈneɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** deliberate organization of people, services, information, and timing so that care works as one connected process.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** care coordination nối specialist, school, family và community service.
**Grammar & collocations:** `care-coordination plan` — plan; `coordinate appointments` — coordinate; `shared information` — information.
**Examples:** `Care coordination prevented three specialists from giving conflicting instructions about feeding.` → Một plan chung tránh conflict.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `돌봄 조정` — care coordination.

## 22. home nursing /hoʊm ˈnɜrsɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** nursing care delivered in a child's home to support treatment, technology, development, and caregiver skill.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** home nursing đưa clinical support về nhà, nơi care diễn ra mỗi ngày.
**Grammar & collocations:** `home-nursing visit` — visit; `provide skilled home care` — provide; `caregiver training` — training.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `가정 간호` — home nursing.

## 23. pediatric palliative care /ˌpiːdiˈætrɪk ˈpæliətɪv kɛr/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** supportive care for a child with serious illness that relieves suffering and supports the child and family alongside disease-directed treatment.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** pediatric palliative care có thể song song với treatment, không chỉ ở cuối đời.
**Grammar & collocations:** `pediatric-palliative-care team` — team; `relieve child suffering` — relieve; `family support` — support.
**Examples:** `Pediatric palliative care addressed pain, sleep, school participation, and sibling questions.` → Care nhìn cả family life.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `소아 완화의료` — pediatric palliative care.

## 24. caregiver partnership /ˈkɛrˌɡɪvər ˈpɑrtnərˌʃɪp/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** collaborative relationship in which caregivers' knowledge and effort are included while the child's rights and voice remain protected.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** caregiver partnership coi family là partner có expertise, không phải người làm thay vô danh.
**Grammar & collocations:** `caregiver-partnership model` — model; `partner with caregivers` — partner; `family expertise` — expertise.
**Examples:** `Caregiver partnership used the parent's detailed observations to adjust the child's symptom plan.` → Parent có knowledge về daily pattern.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `돌봄 제공자 파트너십` — caregiver partnership.

## 25. shared pediatric decision-making /ʃɛrd ˌpiːdiˈætrɪk dɪˈsɪʒən ˌmeɪkɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a decision process that combines clinical evidence, caregiver knowledge, and the child's developing preferences and participation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** shared pediatric decision-making chia decision giữa clinician, caregiver và child theo capacity.
**Grammar & collocations:** `shared-pediatric-decision-making tool` — tool; `include a child's preference` — include; `caregiver input` — input.
**Examples:** `Shared pediatric decision-making used pictures so the child could choose between two acceptable options.` → Child có voice trong scope phù hợp.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `소아 공동 의사결정` — shared pediatric decision-making.

## Review in context — Part 1

**Developmental surveillance** can identify a need for **early intervention** when there is **growth faltering**, **failure to thrive**, a **pediatric feeding disorder**, or **neurodevelopmental delay**. **School health** and an **adolescent-friendly service** extend support beyond the clinic. **Confidential adolescent care**, **pubertal delay** assessment, **eating disorder screening**, and **self-harm assessment** should explain privacy and safety. **Consent maturity** helps guide participation, and a planned **transition to adult care** prevents abrupt loss of support.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 1:**

**Developmental surveillance** có thể phát hiện need **early intervention** khi có **growth faltering**, **failure to thrive**, **pediatric feeding disorder** hoặc **neurodevelopmental delay**. **School health** và **adolescent-friendly service** mở rộng support ra ngoài clinic. **Confidential adolescent care**, assessment **pubertal delay**, **eating disorder screening** và **self-harm assessment** cần giải thích privacy/safety. **Consent maturity** giúp định hướng participation, còn plan **transition to adult care** tránh mất support đột ngột.

## Review in context — Part 2

**Family-centered care** partners with caregivers without silencing the child; **play therapy** can make communication possible. **Child safeguarding**, **mandatory reporting**, and evaluation of **nonaccidental injury** protect safety. A **medically complex child** may need **care coordination**, **home nursing**, and **pediatric palliative care**. **Caregiver partnership** values family expertise, while **shared pediatric decision-making** includes the child's developing voice.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 2:**

**Family-centered care** hợp tác với caregiver mà không làm child mất voice; **play therapy** có thể mở communication. **Child safeguarding**, **mandatory reporting** và evaluation **nonaccidental injury** bảo vệ safety. **Medically complex child** có thể cần **care coordination**, **home nursing** và **pediatric palliative care**. **Caregiver partnership** coi trọng expertise của family, còn **shared pediatric decision-making** đưa voice đang phát triển của child vào decision.
