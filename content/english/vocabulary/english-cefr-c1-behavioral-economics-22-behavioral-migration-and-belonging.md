# C1 Vocabulary 22 — Behavioral migration and belonging

This lesson follows how people evaluate moving, staying, sending support, and building belonging under uncertainty, distance, and social judgment.

Flow: **migration aspiration → destination image → departure cost perception → migrant network cue → remittance motive → return migration expectation → integration norm → language learning investment → belonging uncertainty → host-community trust → status loss aversion → credential recognition belief → border risk perception → temporary permanence → family reunification preference → migration information gap → diaspora identity cue → settlement friction → discrimination expectation → mobility opportunity framing**.

## 1. migration aspiration /maɪˈɡreɪʃən ˌæspəˈreɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a person's desired possibility of moving to another place for safety, work, study, or family reasons.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** possibility mong muốn move place khác vì safety, work, study hoặc family.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong migration aspiration` — aspiration; `translate migration aspiration into a plan` — translate.
**Examples:** `Migration aspiration grew after the graduate saw a realistic path to training abroad.` → Aspiration migration tăng sau khi graduate thấy path training abroad realistic.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `이주 열망`.

## 2. destination image /ˌdɛstəˈneɪʃən ˈɪmɪdʒ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a person's mental picture of the opportunities, risks, people, and lifestyle associated with a possible destination.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** picture mental về opportunity, risk, people và lifestyle của destination possible.
**Grammar & collocations:** `attractive destination image` — image; `correct a destination image` — correct.
**Examples:** `Destination image was shaped by relatives' stories rather than by official labor statistics.` → Image destination shape bởi story relative thay vì statistic labor official.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `목적지 이미지`.

## 3. departure cost perception /dɪˈpɑrtʃər kɔst pərˈsɛpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a judgment about the financial, emotional, and social losses involved in leaving a familiar place.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** judgment về financial, emotional và social loss khi leave place quen.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high departure cost perception` — perception; `change departure cost perception` — change.
**Examples:** `Departure cost perception increased when the family considered leaving elderly parents behind.` → Perception cost departure tăng khi family nghĩ để parent lớn tuổi ở lại.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `출발 비용 인식`.

## 4. migrant network cue /ˈmaɪɡrənt ˈnɛtˌwɝk kjuː/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** information that signals relatives, friends, or community contacts can help someone move and settle.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** information signal relative, friend hoặc community contact có thể help move và settle.
**Grammar & collocations:** `reassuring migrant network cue` — cue; `provide a migrant network cue` — provide.
**Examples:** `A migrant network cue reduced uncertainty about housing during the first month abroad.` → Cue network migrant giảm uncertainty về housing trong month đầu abroad.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `이주민 네트워크 단서`.

## 5. remittance motive /rɪˈmɪtəns ˈmoʊtɪv/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a reason for sending money or resources to people in another place, such as support, investment, or obligation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** reason send money hoặc resource cho người nơi khác vì support, investment hoặc obligation.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong remittance motive` — motive; `understand a remittance motive` — understand.
**Examples:** `A remittance motive led the worker to accept extra shifts during the first year abroad.` → Motive remittance khiến worker accept shift extra trong year đầu abroad.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `송금 동기`.

## 6. return migration expectation /rɪˈtɝn maɪˈɡreɪʃən ˌɛkspɛkˈteɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a belief about whether and when a migrant will eventually move back to the place they left.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** belief migrant có return place cũ không và khi nào.
**Grammar & collocations:** `uncertain return migration expectation` — expectation; `revise return migration expectations` — revise.
**Examples:** `Return migration expectation changed after the temporary job became a stable career.` → Expectation migration return đổi sau khi job temporary thành career stable.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `귀환 이주 기대`.

## 7. integration norm /ˌɪntəˈɡreɪʃən nɔrm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a shared expectation about how newcomers and established residents should participate in social, civic, and economic life.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** expectation shared về newcomer và resident established participate social, civic và economic life thế nào.
**Grammar & collocations:** `inclusive integration norm` — norm; `negotiate an integration norm` — negotiate.
**Examples:** `An inclusive integration norm allowed newcomers to keep traditions while joining local associations.` → Norm integration inclusive cho newcomer giữ tradition và join association local.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `통합 규범`.

## 8. language learning investment /ˈlæŋɡwɪdʒ ˈlɝnɪŋ ɪnˈvɛstmənt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** time, money, and effort devoted to learning a language in expectation of future social or economic benefits.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** time, money và effort đầu tư học language vì benefit social hoặc economic future.
**Grammar & collocations:** `substantial language learning investment` — investment; `make a language learning investment` — make.
**Examples:** `Language learning investment increased when the employer offered paid practice hours.` → Investment learning language tăng khi employer offer practice hour được pay.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `언어 학습 투자`.

## 9. belonging uncertainty /bɪˈlɔŋɪŋ ˌʌnˈsɝtənti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** doubt about whether one will be accepted, recognized, and treated as a legitimate member of a community.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** doubt có được accept, recognize và treat như member legitimate không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `persistent belonging uncertainty` — uncertainty; `reduce belonging uncertainty` — reduce.
**Examples:** `Belonging uncertainty made the student hesitate before joining a neighborhood group.` → Uncertainty belonging khiến student hesitate trước khi join group neighborhood.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `소속 불확실성`.

## 10. host-community trust /hoʊst kəˈmjuːnəti trʌst/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** confidence between newcomers and the established community that each side will behave fairly and cooperatively.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** confidence giữa newcomer và community established về fair và cooperative behavior.
**Grammar & collocations:** `fragile host-community trust` — trust; `build host-community trust` — build.
**Examples:** `Host-community trust improved after residents and newcomers solved a practical transport problem together.` → Trust community-host improve sau khi resident và newcomer solve transport problem cùng nhau.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `주최 지역사회 신뢰`.

## 11. status loss aversion /ˈsteɪtəs lɔs əˈvɝʒən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** reluctance to move or change because a person fears losing social position, professional recognition, or autonomy.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** reluctance move vì sợ mất social position, professional recognition hoặc autonomy.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong status loss aversion` — aversion; `understand status loss aversion` — understand.
**Examples:** `Status loss aversion discouraged the engineer from accepting a job below the title previously held.` → Aversion loss status làm engineer ngại accept job title thấp hơn trước.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `지위 손실 회피`.

## 12. credential recognition belief /krəˈdɛnʃəl ˌrɛkəɡˈnɪʃən bɪˈliːf/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a belief about whether education or professional qualifications earned elsewhere will be accepted.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** belief qualification education hoặc professional từ nơi khác có được accept không.
**Grammar & collocations:** `low credential recognition belief` — belief; `strengthen credential recognition beliefs` — strengthen.
**Examples:** `Credential recognition belief influenced whether the nurse invested in local licensing exams.` → Belief recognition credential influence nurse đầu tư exam licensing local.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `자격 인정 신념`.

## 13. border risk perception /ˈbɔrdər rɪsk pərˈsɛpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a judgment about the danger, uncertainty, and possible consequences of crossing a border.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** judgment danger, uncertainty và consequence possible khi cross border.
**Grammar & collocations:** `elevated border risk perception` — perception; `change border risk perception` — change.
**Examples:** `Border risk perception rose after the route became associated with sudden document checks.` → Perception risk border tăng sau khi route gắn với check document bất ngờ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `국경 위험 인식`.

## 14. temporary permanence /ˈtɛmpəˌrɛri ˈpɝmənəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the process by which a move intended to be short-term gradually becomes a long-term or settled arrangement.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** move intended short-term dần thành arrangement long-term hoặc settled.
**Grammar & collocations:** `unexpected temporary permanence` — permanence; `experience temporary permanence` — experience.
**Examples:** `Temporary permanence emerged when the worker renewed the contract and enrolled a child in local school.` → Permanence temporary xuất hiện khi worker renew contract và enroll child ở school local.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `임시적 영속성`.

## 15. family reunification preference /ˈfæməli ˌriːjuːnəfɪˈkeɪʃən ˈprɛfərəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a preference for migration or settlement decisions that keep close family members together.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** preference decision migration hoặc settlement giữ close family together.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong family reunification preference` — preference; `respect family reunification preferences` — respect.
**Examples:** `Family reunification preference outweighed a slightly higher salary in a distant city.` → Preference reunification family lấn át salary cao hơn một chút ở city xa.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `가족 재결합 선호`.

## 16. migration information gap /maɪˈɡreɪʃən ˌɪnfərˈmeɪʃən ɡæp/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a lack of reliable, usable information about routes, rights, costs, and opportunities related to moving.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** thiếu information reliable và usable về route, right, cost và opportunity migration.
**Grammar & collocations:** `serious migration information gap` — gap; `close the migration information gap` — close.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `이주 정보 격차`.

## 17. diaspora identity cue /daɪˈæspərə aɪˈdɛntəti kjuː/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a symbol, story, or practice that connects dispersed people with a shared origin and community.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** symbol, story hoặc practice connect người dispersed với origin và community shared.
**Grammar & collocations:** `powerful diaspora identity cue` — cue; `share a diaspora identity cue` — share.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `디아스포라 정체성 단서`.

## 18. settlement friction /ˈsɛtəlmənt ˈfrɪkʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** practical difficulty involving housing, documents, transport, work, or services after arrival.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** difficulty practical về housing, document, transport, work hoặc service sau arrival.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high settlement friction` — friction; `reduce settlement friction` — reduce.

**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `정착 마찰`.

## 19. discrimination expectation /dɪˌskrɪməˈneɪʃən ˌɛkspɛkˈteɪʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a belief that one will be treated unfairly because of origin, language, appearance, or group membership.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** belief sẽ bị treat unfair vì origin, language, appearance hoặc group.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high discrimination expectation` — expectation; `reduce discrimination expectations` — reduce.
**Examples:** `Discrimination expectation kept qualified applicants from applying for visible customer-facing roles.` → Expectation discrimination giữ applicant qualified không apply role customer-facing visible.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `차별 기대`.

## 20. mobility opportunity framing /moʊˈbɪləti ˌɑpərˈtuːnəti ˈfreɪmɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the way moving is presented as escape, investment, sacrifice, adventure, or a route to wider opportunity.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cách present move như escape, investment, sacrifice, adventure hoặc route tới opportunity rộng hơn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `balanced mobility opportunity framing` — framing; `use mobility opportunity framing` — use.
**Examples:** `Mobility opportunity framing acknowledged both the chance to advance and the cost of leaving support networks.` → Framing opportunity mobility acknowledge chance advance cùng cost leave support network.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `이동 기회 프레이밍`.

## Review in context

Migration begins with a **migration aspiration** and a **destination image**, but a person also weighs **departure cost perception**. A **migrant network cue** can make housing and work seem reachable, while a **remittance motive** may justify separation. **Return migration expectation** changes as opportunities develop, and an inclusive **integration norm** can make **language learning investment** feel worthwhile. Without it, **belonging uncertainty** weakens **host-community trust**. **Status loss aversion** and a low **credential recognition belief** can make a qualified worker accept less than their capacity. **Border risk perception** shapes timing, while **temporary permanence** explains why a short move becomes a settled life. A **family reunification preference** competes with a **migration information gap**, and a **diaspora identity cue** can preserve connection across distance. **Settlement friction** and **discrimination expectation** may delay participation; fair **mobility opportunity framing** should recognize both the possibility of advancement and the social costs of leaving.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

Migration bắt đầu từ **migration aspiration** và **destination image**, nhưng person cũng cân nhắc **departure cost perception**. **Migrant network cue** làm housing và work trông reachable, còn **remittance motive** có thể justify separation. **Return migration expectation** đổi khi opportunity phát triển, và **integration norm** inclusive khiến **language learning investment** đáng giá. Không có nó, **belonging uncertainty** làm yếu **host-community trust**. **Status loss aversion** và **credential recognition belief** thấp khiến worker qualified accept ít hơn capacity. **Border risk perception** shape timing, còn **temporary permanence** giải thích move ngắn thành life settled. **Family reunification preference** cạnh tranh với **migration information gap**, và **diaspora identity cue** giữ connection qua distance. **Settlement friction** và **discrimination expectation** có thể delay participation; **mobility opportunity framing** fair nên acknowledge cả opportunity advance lẫn social cost leave.
