# C1 Vocabulary 21 — Behavioral time, work, and recovery

This lesson follows how people allocate attention, effort, and rest when deadlines, workplace norms, and competing roles shape their experience of time.

Flow: **time scarcity perception → deadline salience → calendar default → task switching cost → planning fallacy work → temporal discounting work → meeting overload → interruption cost → rest guilt → leisure framing → productivity norm → overtime reciprocity → flexible schedule trust → remote-work visibility → commute recovery → work-family boundary → burnout risk perception → recovery self-efficacy → vacation carryover bias → time sovereignty**.

## 1. time scarcity perception /taɪm ˈskɛrsəti pərˈsɛpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a judgment that available time is insufficient for the tasks, relationships, or recovery a person values.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** judgment time available không đủ cho task, relationship hoặc recovery quan trọng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `acute time scarcity perception` — perception; `reduce time scarcity perception` — reduce.
**Examples:** `Time scarcity perception made the analyst rush routine decisions and overlook a useful alternative.` → Perception scarcity time khiến analyst rush decision routine và bỏ qua alternative hữu ích.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `시간 부족 인식`.

## 2. deadline salience /ˈdɛdˌlaɪn ˈseɪliəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** how noticeable and motivational a deadline is when someone chooses what to do next.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mức deadline noticeable và motivational khi chọn việc tiếp theo.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high deadline salience` — salience; `increase deadline salience` — increase.
**Examples:** `Deadline salience rose after the project dashboard showed the remaining hours rather than only the date.` → Salience deadline tăng sau khi dashboard show giờ còn lại thay vì chỉ date.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `마감일 현저성`.

## 3. calendar default /ˈkælən dər dɪˈfɔlt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a preselected duration, reminder, or scheduling rule that shapes how appointments are arranged.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** duration, reminder hoặc scheduling rule preselect shape cách arrange appointment.
**Grammar & collocations:** `helpful calendar default` — default; `change a calendar default` — change.
**Examples:** `A calendar default left ten minutes between meetings so participants could prepare and recover.` → Default calendar để mười phút giữa meeting để participant prepare và recover.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `달력 기본값`.

## 4. task switching cost /tæsk ˈswɪtʃɪŋ kɔst/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** lost time, attention, and accuracy caused by moving repeatedly between different tasks.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** time, attention và accuracy mất do move liên tục giữa task khác nhau.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high task switching cost` — cost; `reduce task switching costs` — reduce.
**Examples:** `Task switching cost made a short email interrupt a complex analysis for much longer than expected.` → Cost switching task khiến email ngắn interrupt analysis phức tạp lâu hơn expected.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `업무 전환 비용`.

## 5. planning fallacy work /ˈplænɪŋ ˈfæləsi wɝk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a tendency to underestimate the time, coordination, and obstacles involved in completing work.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** tendency underestimate time, coordination và obstacle để complete work.
**Grammar & collocations:** `persistent planning fallacy work` — fallacy; `correct planning fallacy work` — correct.
**Examples:** `Planning fallacy work caused the team to promise a polished report before the data were ready.` → Fallacy planning work khiến team promise report polished trước khi data ready.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `업무 계획 오류`.

## 6. temporal discounting work /ˈtɛmpərəl dɪˈskaʊntɪŋ wɝk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** valuing immediate workplace rewards or relief more than delayed benefits from careful effort.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** value reward hoặc relief immediate hơn benefit delay từ effort cẩn thận.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong temporal discounting work` — discounting; `limit temporal discounting work` — limit.
**Examples:** `Temporal discounting work encouraged the designer to patch a visible issue instead of improving the underlying system.` → Discounting temporal work khuyến khích designer patch issue visible thay vì improve system gốc.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `업무 시간 할인`.

## 7. meeting overload /ˈmiːtɪŋ ˈoʊvərˌloʊd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** an excessive number or duration of meetings that leaves too little time for focused work.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** number hoặc duration meeting quá mức để lại ít time cho focused work.
**Grammar & collocations:** `severe meeting overload` — overload; `reduce meeting overload` — reduce.
**Examples:** `Meeting overload pushed important writing into evenings and reduced recovery time.` → Overload meeting đẩy writing quan trọng vào evening và giảm recovery time.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `회의 과부하`.

## 8. interruption cost /ˌɪntəˈrʌpʃən kɔst/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the hidden loss of concentration and restart time caused by an unexpected interruption.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** loss hidden của concentration và restart time do interruption bất ngờ.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high interruption cost` — cost; `estimate interruption cost` — estimate.
**Examples:** `Interruption cost was greatest when the message arrived during a safety-critical calculation.` → Cost interruption lớn nhất khi message đến trong calculation critical về safety.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `중단 비용`.

## 9. rest guilt /rɛst ɡɪlt/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a feeling that taking necessary rest is selfish, unproductive, or evidence of weak commitment.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** feeling rest cần thiết là selfish, unproductive hoặc dấu hiệu commitment yếu.
**Grammar & collocations:** `persistent rest guilt` — guilt; `reduce rest guilt` — reduce.
**Examples:** `Rest guilt kept the nurse answering messages during a scheduled break.` → Guilt rest khiến nurse trả lời message trong break đã schedule.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `휴식 죄책감`.

## 10. leisure framing /ˈliːʒər ˈfreɪmɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the way free time is described as indulgence, recovery, learning, connection, or waste.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** cách describe free time như indulgence, recovery, learning, connection hoặc waste.
**Grammar & collocations:** `restorative leisure framing` — framing; `change leisure framing` — change.
**Examples:** `Leisure framing that emphasized recovery helped the team protect one evening without apology.` → Framing leisure nhấn mạnh recovery giúp team bảo vệ một evening không cần apologize.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `여가 프레이밍`.

## 11. productivity norm /ˌproʊdʌkˈtɪvəti nɔrm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a shared expectation about how much visible activity counts as responsible work.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** expectation shared về mức activity visible được xem là work responsible.
**Grammar & collocations:** `unrealistic productivity norm` — norm; `challenge a productivity norm` — challenge.
**Examples:** `An unrealistic productivity norm rewarded fast replies instead of careful problem solving.` → Norm productivity unrealistic reward reply nhanh thay vì solve problem cẩn thận.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `생산성 규범`.

## 12. overtime reciprocity /ˈoʊvərˌtaɪm ˌrɛsəˈprɑsəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the expectation that extra work should be returned through support, recognition, flexibility, or future help.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** expectation extra work được đáp lại bằng support, recognition, flexibility hoặc future help.
**Grammar & collocations:** `perceived overtime reciprocity` — reciprocity; `strengthen overtime reciprocity` — strengthen.
**Examples:** `Overtime reciprocity weakened when the extra hours received no explanation or acknowledgment.` → Reciprocity overtime yếu khi extra hour không nhận explanation hay acknowledgment.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `초과근무 상호성`.

## 13. flexible schedule trust /ˈflɛksəbəl ˈskɛdʒuːl trʌst/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** confidence that flexible working time will be judged by outcomes rather than constant visibility.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** confidence schedule flexible được judge bởi outcome thay vì visibility constant.
**Grammar & collocations:** `high flexible schedule trust` — trust; `build flexible schedule trust` — build.
**Examples:** `Flexible schedule trust allowed the parent to begin early and leave before the school closed.` → Trust schedule flexible cho parent start sớm và leave trước school close.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `유연 근무 일정 신뢰`.

## 14. remote-work visibility /rɪˈmoʊt wɝk ˌvɪzəˈbɪləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** how easily a person's remote effort and contribution can be noticed by colleagues and managers.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** mức effort và contribution remote dễ notice với colleague và manager.
**Grammar & collocations:** `low remote-work visibility` — visibility; `improve remote-work visibility` — improve.
**Examples:** `Remote-work visibility improved when the team documented decisions instead of relying on hallway conversations.` → Visibility work remote improve khi team document decision thay vì rely conversation hallway.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `원격 근무 가시성`.

## 15. commute recovery /kəˈmjuːt rɪˈkʌvəri/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the time and mental process needed to recover from the demands of traveling to and from work.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** time và process mental cần để recover sau travel đi về work.
**Grammar & collocations:** `limited commute recovery` — recovery; `protect commute recovery` — protect.
**Examples:** `Long traffic delays reduced commute recovery and left the parent irritable at home.` → Recovery commute giảm vì traffic delay dài khiến parent irritable ở nhà.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `통근 회복`.

## 16. work-family boundary /wɝk ˈfæməli ˈbaʊndəri/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a practical or psychological limit separating work demands from family and personal life.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** limit practical hoặc psychological tách work demand khỏi family và personal life.
**Grammar & collocations:** `clear work-family boundary` — boundary; `protect work-family boundaries` — protect.
**Examples:** `A clear work-family boundary kept urgent but nonessential messages out of dinner.` → Boundary work-family rõ giữ message urgent nhưng không essential khỏi dinner.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `일-가족 경계`.

## 17. burnout risk perception /ˈbɝnˌaʊt rɪsk pərˈsɛpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a judgment about how likely sustained demands are to exhaust a person's energy, attention, and motivation.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** judgment demand sustained likely exhaust energy, attention và motivation.
**Grammar & collocations:** `accurate burnout risk perception` — perception; `improve burnout risk perception` — improve.
**Examples:** `Burnout risk perception rose when the team saw that recovery had been postponed for months.` → Perception risk burnout tăng khi team thấy recovery bị postpone nhiều tháng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `번아웃 위험 인식`.

## 18. recovery self-efficacy /rɪˈkʌvəri ˌsɛlfˈɛfəkəsi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** confidence that one can take effective actions to restore energy and functioning after demanding work.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** confidence có thể take action effective để restore energy và functioning sau work demanding.
**Grammar & collocations:** `strong recovery self-efficacy` — self-efficacy; `strengthen recovery self-efficacy` — strengthen.
**Examples:** `Recovery self-efficacy improved after the employee learned how to protect a predictable sleep routine.` → Self-efficacy recovery improve sau khi employee học protect sleep routine predictable.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `회복 자기효능감`.

## 19. vacation carryover bias /veɪˈkeɪʃən ˈkæriˌoʊvər baɪəs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a tendency to postpone taking leave because future work feels more urgent or deserving of attention.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** tendency postpone leave vì future work trông urgent hoặc deserving attention hơn.
**Grammar & collocations:** `persistent vacation carryover bias` — bias; `counter vacation carryover bias` — counter.
**Examples:** `Vacation carryover bias left the employee with unused days after another intense quarter.` → Bias carryover vacation để employee còn day unused sau quarter intense.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `휴가 이월 편향`.

## 20. time sovereignty /taɪm ˈsɑvrənti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the practical ability to decide when and how one's time is used within real obligations.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** ability practical decide time dùng khi nào và thế nào trong obligation thật.
**Grammar & collocations:** `greater time sovereignty` — sovereignty; `protect time sovereignty` — protect.
**Examples:** `Time sovereignty increased when the worker could decline low-value meetings without penalty.` → Sovereignty time tăng khi worker có thể decline meeting value thấp mà không bị penalty.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `시간 주권`.

## Review in context

People often feel **time scarcity perception** when every task competes for attention, and high **deadline salience** can make the nearest obligation dominate. A sensible **calendar default** protects transition time, while **task switching cost** and **planning fallacy work** explain why apparently small requests expand. **Temporal discounting work** favors immediate relief over careful long-term improvement. **Meeting overload** and **interruption cost** then reduce space for focused work, while **rest guilt** makes recovery feel undeserved. A healthier **leisure framing** can challenge an unrealistic **productivity norm**, and fair **overtime reciprocity** shows that extra effort is noticed. **Flexible schedule trust** and **remote-work visibility** allow contribution to be judged by outcomes. Outside work, **commute recovery** and a clear **work-family boundary** protect energy. Accurate **burnout risk perception**, stronger **recovery self-efficacy**, and resistance to **vacation carryover bias** help people maintain **time sovereignty** instead of treating exhaustion as proof of commitment.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt:**

Người ta thường cảm thấy **time scarcity perception** khi mọi task cạnh tranh attention, và **deadline salience** cao khiến obligation gần nhất dominate. **Calendar default** hợp lý bảo vệ transition time, còn **task switching cost** và **planning fallacy work** giải thích vì sao request nhỏ lại expand. **Temporal discounting work** favor relief immediate hơn improvement dài hạn. **Meeting overload** và **interruption cost** làm giảm không gian focused work, còn **rest guilt** khiến recovery trông không deserved. **Leisure framing** healthy có thể challenge **productivity norm** unrealistic, và **overtime reciprocity** fair cho thấy extra effort được notice. **Flexible schedule trust** và **remote-work visibility** cho phép judge contribution bằng outcome. Ngoài work, **commute recovery** và **work-family boundary** rõ bảo vệ energy. **Burnout risk perception** accurate, **recovery self-efficacy** mạnh hơn và chống **vacation carryover bias** giúp duy trì **time sovereignty** thay vì xem exhaustion là proof commitment.
