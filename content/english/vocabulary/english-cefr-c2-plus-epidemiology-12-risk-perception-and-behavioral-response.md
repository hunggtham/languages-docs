# C2+ Vocabulary — Risk perception and behavioral response

This lesson follows how people interpret threat, decide whether to act, and respond to public-health communication during an outbreak. The network is **risk perception → optimism-gap bias → precaution uptake behavior → behavioral fatigue → perceived protective efficacy → response efficacy → response self-efficacy → perceived susceptibility → perceived severity → outbreak message framing → reactance → trust heuristic → social norm signal → descriptive health norm → injunctive health norm → rumor cascade → misinformation exposure → information avoidance → outbreak uncertainty tolerance → protective decision threshold → behavioral surveillance → adherence trajectory → intervention uptake → demand elasticity → communication fatigue**.

## 1. risk perception /rɪsk pərˈsɛpʃən/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a person's or group's judgment about the likelihood, seriousness, and controllability of a threat.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** risk perception là cách người ta cảm nhận threat, không chỉ probability được tính bằng model.
**Grammar & collocations:** `risk-perception survey` — survey; `shape risk perception` — shape; `perceived threat` — threat.
**Examples:** `Risk perception rose after local hospitals began reporting severe cases.` → Local evidence làm threat gần hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `위험 인식` — risk perception.

## 2. optimism-gap bias /ˈɑptəˌmɪzəm ɡæp baɪəs/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a tendency to judge one's own risk as lower than the risk faced by comparable others.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** optimism-gap bias là “người khác dễ gặp hơn tôi” dù evidence tương tự.
**Grammar & collocations:** `optimism-gap bias` — bias; `underestimate personal risk` — underestimate; `comparative optimism` — optimism.
**Examples:** `Optimism-gap bias reduced testing among people who considered themselves healthier than their peers.` → So sánh bản thân làm risk bị hạ.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `낙관 격차 편향` — optimism-gap bias.

## 3. precaution uptake behavior /prɪˈkɔʃən ˈʌpˌteɪk bɪˈheɪvjər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the actions through which people begin, maintain, or stop recommended protective measures.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** precaution uptake behavior là behavior thật sau khi nghe recommendation.
**Grammar & collocations:** `precaution-uptake behavior` — behavior; `adopt a precaution` — adopt; `protective practice` — practice.
**Examples:** `Precaution uptake behavior improved when masks were affordable and easy to find.` → Access ảnh hưởng uptake.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `예방 행동 수용` — precaution uptake behavior.

## 4. behavioral fatigue /bɪˈheɪvjərəl fəˈtiːɡ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** declining motivation or ability to maintain a demanding protective behavior over time.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** behavioral fatigue là sức bền behavior giảm sau nhiều tuần hoặc nhiều đợt rule.
**Grammar & collocations:** `behavioral-fatigue effect` — effect; `reduce fatigue` — reduce; `sustained adherence` — adherence.
**Examples:** `Behavioral fatigue appeared when restrictions continued without clear milestones or support.` → Burden và uncertainty làm fatigue tăng.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `행동 피로` — behavioral fatigue.

## 5. perceived protective efficacy /pərˈsiːvd prəˈtɛktɪv ˈɛfəkəsi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a person's belief about how well a recommended action will protect against infection or severe harm.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** perceived protective efficacy là niềm tin “action này có giúp thật không?”.
**Grammar & collocations:** `perceived-protective efficacy` — efficacy; `judge protection` — judge; `protective action` — action.
**Examples:** `Perceived protective efficacy fell after people saw vaccinated friends become infected but recover quickly.` → Người ta có thể nhầm infection với failure.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `인지된 보호 효과` — perceived protective efficacy.

## 6. response efficacy /rɪˈspɑns ˈɛfəkəsi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the belief that a recommended response will effectively reduce a threat or its consequences.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** response efficacy là niềm tin về hiệu quả của action nói chung.
**Grammar & collocations:** `response-efficacy belief` — belief; `increase response efficacy` — increase; `effective measure` — measure.
**Examples:** `Clear evidence increased response efficacy for ventilation improvements.` → Evidence làm action đáng tin hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `대응 효능감` — response efficacy.

## 7. response self-efficacy /rɪˈspɑns sɛlf ˈɛfəkəsi/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a person's belief that they can successfully perform the recommended protective action.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** response self-efficacy là “tôi có làm được action này đúng không?”.
**Grammar & collocations:** `response-self-efficacy measure` — measure; `build self-efficacy` — build; `perform a protective action` — perform.
**Examples:** `Response self-efficacy rose after the clinic demonstrated how to use the test kit.` → Hướng dẫn cụ thể làm “tôi làm được”.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `대응 자기효능감` — response self-efficacy.

## 8. perceived susceptibility /pərˈsiːvd səˌsɛptəˈbɪləti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a person's belief about how likely they are to acquire a disease or experience a health threat.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** perceived susceptibility là self-estimate về khả năng mình bị ảnh hưởng.
**Grammar & collocations:** `perceived-susceptibility scale` — scale; `feel susceptible` — feel; `personal risk estimate` — estimate.
**Examples:** `Perceived susceptibility increased after the outbreak reached the person's workplace.` → Exposure gần làm risk có vẻ thật hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `인지된 감수성` — perceived susceptibility.

## 9. perceived severity /pərˈsiːvd səˈvɛrəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a person's belief about how serious the disease or its consequences would be for them or others.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** perceived severity là judgment về mức nặng, cost hoặc disruption.
**Grammar & collocations:** `perceived-severity rating` — rating; `judge disease severity` — judge; `serious consequence` — consequence.
**Examples:** `Perceived severity was higher among people caring for medically vulnerable relatives.` → Responsibility làm severity feel nặng hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `인지된 심각성` — perceived severity.

## 10. outbreak message framing /ˈaʊtˌbreɪk ˈmɛsɪdʒ ˈfreɪmɪŋ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the way an outbreak message emphasizes gains, losses, responsibility, uncertainty, or collective protection.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** outbreak message framing chọn “góc kể” để người nghe hiểu và act.
**Grammar & collocations:** `outbreak-message framing` — framing; `frame a recommendation` — frame; `gain-framed message` — message.
**Examples:** `Outbreak message framing emphasized protecting a shared workplace rather than blaming individuals.` → Framing đổi emotional response.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `유행 메시지 프레이밍` — outbreak message framing.

## 11. reactance /riˈæktəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** resistance that arises when people feel a recommendation or rule threatens their freedom of choice.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** reactance là phản ứng “đừng bắt tôi” khi autonomy bị perceived threat.
**Grammar & collocations:** `reactance effect` — effect; `trigger reactance` — trigger; `perceived coercion` — coercion.
**Examples:** `A punitive message triggered reactance and reduced voluntary testing.` → Tone có thể làm action ngược lại.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `심리적 반발` — reactance.

## 12. trust heuristic /trʌst hjʊˈrɪstɪk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a shortcut in which people judge information partly by whether they trust its source.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** trust heuristic là “nguồn này đáng tin không?” trước khi kiểm tra hết content.
**Grammar & collocations:** `trust-heuristic effect` — effect; `use a trusted source` — use; `source credibility` — credibility.
**Examples:** `The trust heuristic made local nurses more persuasive than anonymous online advertisements.` → Messenger ảnh hưởng message.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `신뢰 휴리스틱` — trust heuristic.

## 13. social norm signal /ˈsoʊʃəl nɔrm ˈsɪɡnəl/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** information indicating what other people commonly do or approve of in a social setting.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** social norm signal nói “mọi người quanh tôi đang làm gì/ủng hộ gì?”.
**Grammar & collocations:** `social-norm signal` — signal; `signal common behavior` — signal; `peer behavior` — behavior.
**Examples:** `The clinic displayed a social norm signal showing that most visitors completed testing.` → Peer behavior làm action bình thường hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `사회 규범 신호` — social norm signal.

## 14. descriptive health norm /dɪˈskrɪptɪv hɛlθ nɔrm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a belief about what people in a group actually do regarding a health behavior.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** descriptive health norm mô tả behavior đang phổ biến, không nói nó đúng hay nên làm.
**Grammar & collocations:** `descriptive-health norm` — norm; `describe common behavior` — describe; `normative message` — message.
**Examples:** `The descriptive health norm message reported that most households tested after exposure.` → “Most people do” là descriptive.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `기술적 건강 규범` — descriptive health norm.

## 15. injunctive health norm /ɪnˈdʒʌŋktɪv hɛlθ nɔrm/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** a belief about which health behaviors a group approves or expects people to perform.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** injunctive health norm nói group nghĩ behavior nào “nên” làm.
**Grammar & collocations:** `injunctive-health norm` — norm; `signal social approval` — signal; `expected behavior` — behavior.
**Examples:** `The injunctive health norm encouraged people to stay home when symptomatic.` → “Nên làm” khác “đang làm”.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `명령적 건강 규범` — injunctive health norm.

## 16. rumor cascade /ˈruːmər kæˈskeɪd/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** rapid spread of an unverified claim through repeated social sharing and reinforcement.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** rumor cascade là rumor chạy qua network và lớn lên qua mỗi lần forward.
**Grammar & collocations:** `rumor-cascade analysis` — analysis; `interrupt a rumor cascade` — interrupt; `unverified claim` — claim.
**Examples:** `A rumor cascade falsely linked the vaccine to a local illness cluster.` → Rumor cần correction nhanh và credible.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `소문 연쇄` — rumor cascade.

## 17. misinformation exposure /ˌmɪsɪnfərˈmeɪʃən ɪkˈspoʊʒər/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** contact with false or misleading information that may influence health beliefs or actions.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** misinformation exposure là mức người học/nhóm gặp content sai hoặc misleading.
**Grammar & collocations:** `misinformation-exposure measure` — measure; `reduce exposure` — reduce; `misleading claim` — claim.
**Examples:** `Misinformation exposure was higher in networks with little access to official updates.` → Information gap mở chỗ cho misinformation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `허위정보 노출` — misinformation exposure.

## 18. information avoidance /ˌɪnfərˈmeɪʃən əˈvɔɪdəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** deliberate or habitual choice not to seek, read, or process information about a threat.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** information avoidance là tránh xem data vì sợ, mệt hoặc không muốn đổi behavior.
**Grammar & collocations:** `information-avoidance pattern` — pattern; `avoid health information` — avoid; `uncertainty burden` — burden.
**Examples:** `Information avoidance increased after repeated alerts offered no practical action.` → Alert không actionable làm người ta bỏ qua.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `정보 회피` — information avoidance.

## 19. outbreak uncertainty tolerance /ˈaʊtˌbreɪk ʌnˈsɜrtənti ˈtɑlərəns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the degree to which a person or institution can make decisions while evidence remains incomplete or changing.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** outbreak uncertainty tolerance là khả năng sống với “chưa biết hết” mà vẫn act có trách nhiệm.
**Grammar & collocations:** `outbreak-uncertainty tolerance` — tolerance; `act under uncertainty` — act; `evidence gap` — gap.
**Examples:** `Low outbreak uncertainty tolerance led leaders to delay action until the peak had already passed.` → Chờ certainty có thể quá muộn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `유행 불확실성 인내도` — outbreak uncertainty tolerance.

## 20. protective decision threshold /prəˈtɛktɪv dɪˈsɪʒən ˈθrɛʃhoʊld/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the level of perceived risk or evidence at which a person decides to adopt a protective action.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** protective decision threshold là mức risk cần đạt trước khi người ta quyết định act.
**Grammar & collocations:** `protective-decision threshold` — threshold; `cross a decision threshold` — cross; `adopt protection` — adopt.
**Examples:** `A trusted local alert lowered the protective decision threshold for testing.` → Trust làm người ta act sớm hơn.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `보호 행동 결정 임계치` — protective decision threshold.

## 21. behavioral surveillance /bɪˈheɪvjərəl sərˈveɪləns/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** systematic monitoring of beliefs, behaviors, intentions, and barriers relevant to health protection.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** behavioral surveillance theo dõi people đang nghĩ và làm gì, không chỉ pathogen data.
**Grammar & collocations:** `behavioral-surveillance survey` — survey; `monitor protective behavior` — monitor; `behavioral indicator` — indicator.
**Examples:** `Behavioral surveillance detected declining mask use before case numbers rose.` → Behavior signal đi trước outcome.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `행동 감시` — behavioral surveillance.

## 22. adherence trajectory /ədˈhɪrəns trəˈdʒɛktəri/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the pattern of maintaining, increasing, or decreasing compliance with a recommended behavior over time.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** adherence trajectory là đường đi của compliance chứ không chỉ một snapshot.
**Grammar & collocations:** `adherence-trajectory analysis` — analysis; `track adherence over time` — track; `declining adherence` — adherence.
**Examples:** `The adherence trajectory improved after the program provided reminders and paid leave.` → Support đổi trajectory.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `순응 궤적` — adherence trajectory.

## 23. intervention uptake /ˌɪntərˈvɛnʃən ˈʌpˌteɪk/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the extent to which a population begins using or participating in a recommended intervention.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** intervention uptake đo bao nhiêu người thật sự nhận hoặc dùng intervention.
**Grammar & collocations:** `intervention-uptake rate` — rate; `increase uptake` — increase; `program participation` — participation.
**Examples:** `Intervention uptake rose when appointments were available outside working hours.` → Convenience ảnh hưởng participation.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `중재 수용` — intervention uptake.

## 24. demand elasticity /dɪˈmænd ɪˈlæstəsəti/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** the degree to which demand for a health service changes when price, access, waiting time, or perceived benefit changes.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** demand elasticity cho biết demand phản ứng mạnh hay yếu khi điều kiện service đổi.
**Grammar & collocations:** `demand-elasticity estimate` — estimate; `measure service demand` — measure; `price sensitivity` — sensitivity.
**Examples:** `Demand elasticity was high when testing fees increased for low-income households.` → Cost nhỏ cũng có thể làm uptake giảm.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `수요 탄력성` — demand elasticity.

## 25. communication fatigue /kəˌmjuːnəˈkeɪʃən fəˈtiːɡ/
**Part of speech:** noun
**Core meaning (English):** declining attention or responsiveness caused by repeated, lengthy, or inconsistent health messages.
**Nghĩa cốt lõi & hình ảnh ghi nhớ:** communication fatigue là mệt vì quá nhiều message, dù threat chưa hết.
**Grammar & collocations:** `communication-fatigue effect` — effect; `reduce message burden` — reduce; `repeated alert` — alert.
**Examples:** `Communication fatigue made people ignore later alerts unless the action was specific and urgent.` → Message phải ngắn, rõ và actionable.
**Liên kết tiếng Hàn:** `소통 피로` — communication fatigue.

## Review in context — Part 1

**Risk perception** can be distorted by **optimism-gap bias**. **Precaution uptake behavior** depends on **behavioral fatigue**, **perceived protective efficacy**, **response efficacy**, and **response self-efficacy**. **Perceived susceptibility** and **perceived severity** influence whether a person crosses a protective threshold. **Outbreak message framing** may motivate action or trigger **reactance**. A **trust heuristic** and a **social norm signal** also shape interpretation.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 1:**

**Risk perception** có thể bị lệch bởi **optimism-gap bias**. **Precaution uptake behavior** phụ thuộc **behavioral fatigue**, **perceived protective efficacy**, **response efficacy** và **response self-efficacy**. **Perceived susceptibility** và **perceived severity** ảnh hưởng việc người ta có vượt qua threshold để act không. **Outbreak message framing** có thể thúc đẩy action hoặc tạo **reactance**. **Trust heuristic** và **social norm signal** cũng định hình interpretation.

## Review in context — Part 2

A **descriptive health norm** reports what people do, while an **injunctive health norm** signals what they approve. A **rumor cascade** and **misinformation exposure** can produce **information avoidance**. **Outbreak uncertainty tolerance** affects the **protective decision threshold**. **Behavioral surveillance** tracks an **adherence trajectory** and **intervention uptake**. **Demand elasticity** helps explain access-related changes, while **communication fatigue** shows why repeated messages eventually lose attention.

**Bản dịch tiếng Việt — Phần 2:**

**Descriptive health norm** báo người ta đang làm gì, còn **injunctive health norm** báo group ủng hộ điều gì. **Rumor cascade** và **misinformation exposure** có thể tạo **information avoidance**. **Outbreak uncertainty tolerance** ảnh hưởng **protective decision threshold**. **Behavioral surveillance** theo dõi **adherence trajectory** và **intervention uptake**. **Demand elasticity** giúp giải thích thay đổi do access, còn **communication fatigue** cho thấy vì sao message lặp lại cuối cùng mất attention.
