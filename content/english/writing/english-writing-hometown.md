# Writing: My hometown

Target: 100–150 words.

## Questions to answer

- Where is your hometown?
- What does it look and feel like?
- What is one memorable place there?
- How has it changed?

## Suggested structure

1. Location and general description.
2. One place or feature with a specific example.
3. A change or personal feeling.

## Useful sentence starters

- My hometown is located in …
- It is best known for …
- One place I strongly remember is …
- Compared with the past, it has become …
- What I like most about it is …
